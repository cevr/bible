The people are the plain figures of the films' IDEA pages, each exactly the plain lead as his model sheet draws him: a round head sitting right on a trapezoid tunic with no neck, two short rounded stub legs under the hem; no hair, no ears, no nose, no shoes, no clothes detail. One pale cool grey paper with a faint lilac cast for head, hands, tunic and legs (lit side #dbd6dc, shade side turned lilac-violet #b5a9d0, never a darker grey). A face of three marks: two short arched brows, two small dark oval dot eyes and a one-line mouth; feeling is in the brows' tilt, the mouth's bend and the head's tilt. Arms in sleeves of the same grey paper to the wrist, each round grey hand at the end of its sleeve, no thumbs. A child or a baby is the same figure smaller, its head a larger share of its height; nothing else changes. What they hold, and only where a pose names it: a stub of white chalk.
