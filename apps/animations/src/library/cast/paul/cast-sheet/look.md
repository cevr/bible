The person is the apostle Paul as the films draw him: a man of middle age, slight and narrow in build, a little shorter than the other grown men ("his bodily presence is weak", 2 Corinthians 10:10), no halo and no glow. Short dark-brown hair going thin at the crown, with no headcloth; a short dark-brown beard touched with grey, his mouth inside it, parted from the hair by the skin of the cheek; warm tan skin; small dark oval eyes, short dark brows. A plain linen tunic (#d1c6b4) to the ankles with its sleeves to the wrist, and over it a camel travel cloak (#8c7762) about his shoulders, falling to his shins, with no clasp, pattern or trim; brown sandals. Each round hand at the end of its sleeve, no thumbs. His head is one simple shape. What he carries, and only where a pose names it: a reed pen, a sheet or roll of parchment, a small clay oil lamp, a plain staff.
