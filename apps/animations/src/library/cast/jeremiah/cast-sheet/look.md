The person is the prophet Jeremiah as the films draw him: a young-ish man, of ordinary build, no halo and no glow. Dark-brown hair to just below his ears, with no headcloth; a short, close dark-brown beard, his mouth inside it, parted from the hair by the skin of the cheek; warm tan skin; small dark oval eyes, short dark brows. A plain linen tunic (#d1c6b4) to the ankles with its sleeves to the wrist, and a camel mantle (#8c7762), the prophet's, over one shoulder and across his body to his knees, with no pattern or trim; brown sandals. Each round hand at the end of its sleeve, no thumbs. His head is one simple shape. What he carries: one scroll of pale parchment on two plain wooden rollers, with no writing shown on it.
