# Righteousness by Faith (v3): sources

The film is made for every Christian (CRAFT rule 2): it quotes only Scripture, and it never quotes or names Ellen G. White or the pioneers in speech. Their thought is said in the narrator’s own words, and every paraphrase stays inside its source’s words; the credits name them. The Ellen G. White and pioneer texts are records in `quotes.jsonl`, checked against the local corpus with `python3 .claude/skills/film/frame/verify-quotes.py apps/animations/src/films/righteousness-by-faith/v3/quotes.jsonl` (46 records, all EXACT, 2026-10-14). The KJV, the only words the film quotes, was checked with `bible verse`; its supplied-word brackets are dropped for speech.

Doctrine follows [the frame](../../../../../../.claude/skills/film/frame/righteousness-by-faith.md): justification first, then the walk; Christ now in the heavenly courts; Zechariah 3 with the accuser at the close of the great day of atonement; the Sabbath as rest by faith; and the latter rain. Two of the frame’s anchors are not cited, by the owner’s decisions (no Adventist history in the film; neither the 1889 Fundamental Principles nor Ms 24, 1888 in the credits). FP1889 150.6’s order, justified from past sins first and then grace to obey, is carried by Scripture’s own order in Zechariah 3:4 and 3:7, with 3SM 195.2. Lt 57, 1895, par. 44’s Christ “pleading for the church in the heavenly courts above” is carried by Hebrews 7:25 with GC 489.1.

The nature of Christ follows [its frame](../../../../../../.claude/skills/film/frame/nature-of-christ.md): our sins are laid on Him at the cross, never a stain in Him, and `grain` calls the cross His sacrifice, never the atonement finished.

Bylines. “The Seed-sowing of the Gospel” (_The Signs of the Times_, November 29, 1899) is signed “By E. J. Waggoner”. The _General Conference Daily Bulletin_ of March 8, 1897, pages 297–303, is Waggoner’s sermon on Hebrews (the corpus’s author field names the editor, A. T. Jones). Jones’s _Lessons on Faith_ (LOF_ATJ) is a later compilation: its chapters first ran as unsigned editorials in the _Review and Herald_ while he edited it, and the compilation names him as the writer. Items tagged “E. J. Waggoner” only by an editor (_The Present Truth_ 1897–1899; _The Signs of the Times_ 1890) are not cited.
