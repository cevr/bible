# Delivery: audit

The owner's rule (2026-10-14): "id like to change my own manner of speakign to more closely resemble that", "no tracking, but as part of the scripts for all our planned series". It is the film skill's [voice-counsel.md](../../../.claude/skills/film/reference/voice-counsel.md), **In the scripts**, applied to the README's **Language**:

- one idea a sentence, joined by "and", "so" and "but", the stressed word last;
- the beauty in the picture, never the adjective; plain statements, no exclamations, no hedges;
- a few points, the refrain back in fresh words; a light line only where it makes the point;
- Scripture in the KJV, word for word; no Ellen White or pioneer named in speech; no chapter and verse spoken;
- the marks written in the line: `{*}` before the stressed word, `{/}` a breath, `{//}` a hold (0.6 s, or `{//1.5}` its seconds), as [elevenlabs-delivery.md](../../../.claude/skills/film/reference/elevenlabs-delivery.md) gives them;
- timed at 150–165 words a minute (CRAFT rule 9), the marks' breaths and holds counted.

Where the counsel and the owner's habit differ, the counsel wins: his habits are in [owner-voice.md](../../../.claude/skills/film/reference/owner-voice.md).

Audited 2026-10-15: the 15 draft scripts in `series/*/scripts/`. Every KJV line was printed again by `bible verse` that day. ## Round 1 review (2026-10-15)

A taste review and a doctrine and source check read the drafts after the voice revision. The fixes thinned the stress marks from 564 to 370 (from about 0.87 a sentence to 0.58), moving the rest onto the word that carries the sense; moved five landing holds off function words to the phrase boundary (education 01, 17 and 52, health 01, theology 01); restored contractions in the talk lines where the verb carries nothing ("That's the price", "It's how", "you're sick"), keeping a full negation that carries sense ("He did not rebel"); and cut the remaining signposts and numbered heads ("Look at this verse", "Notice when", "Watch the first lesson", "Watch the third mother", "Here the whole book turns", theology 02's "One", "Two", "Three"). The doctrine fixes: theology 02 says God keeps those who trust him near the truth, not that they understood it well; theology 05's heaven gives glory to both, as Revelation 5:13 has it; country 01 keeps God's plan as "still" the home school after the fall (Ed 33); health 01 has the body working to put itself right; health 06 has breath filling the lungs, not the blood, and carrying off some of the body's waste. Theology 08, Righteousness by Faith, joined the drafts on 2026-10-15. It is the film's v3 rewrite, marked to the same counsel by its own unit, not by this audit. Its words did not change when it was marked. Its landing's 3 s held beat is the seam between its last two beats (`court`'s tail and the thesis's lead), so no line holds it.

The marks follow the counsel: a breath at the end of most sentences, a hold after a quotation that needs room, and the held beat before a landing's last words, at a phrase boundary and never after a function word (3 s in a film, CRAFT rule 7; 1.5 to 2 s in a talk, whose cut is shorter). A stress goes on the word that carries the sense: a contrast, the turn, the landing; not a pronoun, a word just said, or the end of a list that falls there anyway. Each draft's Length line and beat timings are retimed to these. The talk plans' word ranges assumed about 190 words a minute; `education/series.md` and `health/series.md` now give them at 155 (about 140–160 for One Question, 330–410 for an Episode).
