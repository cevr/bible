BINDING OWNER RULES FOR THESE FILMS:
- Films are for every Christian. On screen and in narration they quote ONLY Scripture, KJV, verbatim.
  Pioneers and Ellen White are paraphrased in the narrator's words, never named or quoted; the credits name them.
- No Adventism, no denominations, no church history (no 1844 movement story, no Millerites, no 1888,
  no named churches, no pioneers drawn). Doctrines themselves are fine (sanctuary, Sabbath, judgment,
  state of the dead, prophecy dates as prophecy). Secular history of empires is fine.
  For each episode, FLAG whether it can be told without church history / naming a denomination; if not,
  say which part needs it and whether the episode survives without that part.
- Corpus, not memory: every refcode and snippet you report must have been printed by es.py or the frame
  file in THIS session, copied verbatim. A gap stays a gap: write "not found" rather than recall.
- Typology vs spiritualizing: doctrine rests on plain didactic Scripture; types are text-authorized overlays.

noticed between sources (dated, both sides), and anything not found.
