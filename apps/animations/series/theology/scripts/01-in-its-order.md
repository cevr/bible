## Header (the `script.ts` comment, house style)

The order: the joke (`cold`: a grown man at a feast with a baby's bottle; Hebrews says that to its readers), the title, then what milk is (`milk`: the plain word), which truths are the milk (`stones`: the six first principles of Heb 6:1–2, the series' first tier), the problem in one small dose (`bottle`, `teeth`: some never chew), its other side (`rush`: some skip the first steps and twist the rest, owner 2026-10-20), the turn (`math`: meat leaves out nothing that was in the milk), what the meat is (`priest`: more of Christ, his priesthood now), how growth happens (`grain`, `eat`), the series map (`path`), and the landing back at the feast.

Motifs (CRAFT rule 4):

- The cup. A baby's cup of milk in `cold`; filled in `milk` from an open book (the milk is the word); in the landing the grown figure sets the bottle down at a set table, and the cup stands on the table among the bread and the dishes, still there (`math` paid off: nothing in the milk is left behind).
- The six stones. Laid as a foundation in `stones`, each with its small gold icon; they reappear as the first rise of the path in `path`. They are the series' episode map for Tier One.
- The grain. A seed in `grain` grows blade, ear, full corn; in the landing the bread on the table is that corn.
- The light from the cross. A small gold cross on the page in `priest`, whose light widens over the stones; in `path` the whole path lies inside its light.

Tent poles (CRAFT rule 11), with their act's light and the question that opens the act (the chapters):

- `cold`: the feast table, a grown grey figure holding a baby's bottle, teal day. "What are milk and meat?"
- `stones`: six stones set in a row on parchment, peach. "Which truths are the milk?"
- `math`: the slate of sums combining into one great sum, peach to dawn. "Do we leave the milk behind?"
- `priest`: the veil with gold light behind it, dawn gold. "So what is the meat?"
- `name`: the set table under the teal landing sky. "How does anyone grow?"

Registers: IDEA (parchment, flat figures, one gold prop) for `milk`, `stones`, `math`, `path`; STORY (cardboard diorama, faces) for `cold`, `bottle` (Corinth), `grain` (a field), `eat` (Jeremiah), `name`. The black moment is not in this film; its valley is a short dusk in `bottle`.

Quoted Scripture: 11 passages, 161 of 884 spoken words (18.2 %; recounted 2026-10-20 after Proverbs 4:18 joined `path` and 2 Peter 3:16 `rush`; sources.md's verifier table holds the earlier count). One quotation per beat. 2 Peter 3:18 (`thesis`) is quoted from "Grow", its opening "But" dropped.
