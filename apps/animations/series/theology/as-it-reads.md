# As It Reads (draft, owner's idea, 2026-10-19)

The owner's idea for a foundational film, set against _The Path of the Just_ ([series.md](series.md)) and its episode 02 ([scripts/02-the-oracles-of-god.md](scripts/02-the-oracles-of-god.md)). Nothing here is a script yet, and no episode number has moved. Every verse below was read in the KJV with `bible verse` on 2026-10-19; every pioneer line was read in the corpus the same day (`bible egw lookup`) and is a lead, not yet verified into `sources.md`.

## The idea, in the owner's words

- **The root of all error is a choice between two ways of reading.** Read the Bible literally, as it reads, or spiritually, as you read it. Spiritual readings are infinite in number, and that is today's problem.
- **The Bible has its own dictionary.** It must cross the boundaries of time and culture, and speak to people in widely different times and circumstances. It is countercultural by nature, so it always goes against the grain, yet it must reach people who do not share its writers' mindset, time or culture.
- **So it builds its own small universe of meaning,** and draws its definitions from inside it: Hebrew parallelism, direct translations and the like.
- **The goal:** build that understanding ourselves, and hear what the Bible says through its literal reading.

## What the text and the pioneers give it

- **One meaning, not many:** "no prophecy of the scripture is of any private interpretation" (2 Pet 1:20). "Add thou not unto his words" (Prov 30:6).
- **The two ways, in the pioneers' words** (paraphrased in a film, never named):
  - The plain way: "If it makes good sense as it stands, and does no violence to the simple laws of nature, then it must be understood literally, if not, figuratively" (William Miller, MWV1 22.3, rule XI). "The language of the Bible should be explained according to its obvious meaning, unless a symbol or figure is employed" (GC 598.3).
  - The other way: some "teach that the Scriptures have a mystical, a secret, spiritual meaning not apparent in the language employed" (GC 598.3). "To spiritualize our Lord's return is to pervert the obvious meaning of His promise" (BR-ASI9 229.3); many "spiritualize away the very truths" of the promised home (GC 674, via LDE 286.2).
- **The Bible's own dictionary, as a rule:** "To learn the true meaning of figures, trace your figurative word through your Bible, and where you find it explained, put it on your figure, and if it makes good sense you need look no further, if not, look again" (Miller, MWV1 22.5, rule XII). Its examples: "mountains, meaning governments; beasts, meaning kingdoms" (MWV1 21.6). "The Bible is its own expositor" (Ed 190.2).
- **The dictionary at work (KJV):**
  - Definitions given in the text: "The waters which thou sawest … are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues" (Rev 17:15). "The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches" (Rev 1:20). "These great beasts … are four kings" (Dan 7:17).
  - Direct translations: "Emmanuel, which being interpreted is, God with us" (Matt 1:23); "the Messias, which is, being interpreted, the Christ" (John 1:41).
  - Hebrew parallelism, one line defining the next: "My doctrine shall drop as the rain, my speech shall distil as the dew" (Deut 32:2); "a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path" (Ps 119:105).
  - The method named: "comparing spiritual things with spiritual" (1 Cor 2:13); "precept upon precept; line upon line" (Isa 28:10).
- **Across time and culture:** "whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning" (Rom 15:4); the gospel goes "to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people" (Rev 14:6).
- **Countercultural:** "be not conformed to this world" (Rom 12:2).

## How it meets the series

- **Episode 02 already holds half of it.** 02's `plain` beat says "Read it as it reads … unless the Bible itself shows you a symbol", and its `figure` beat looks the Lamb up in Exodus. But 02's spine is the Bereans and the one measuring rule, so the two ways and the dictionary get about 50 seconds there.
- **The owner's idea is a sharper spine:** the root of error is the way of reading, and the Bible defines its own words. It is the tool every later episode uses to "unravel common theology, indirectly" (series.md): death as sleep (12), the fire that ends (36), the beasts as kingdoms (21b), the sanctuary as a real place (16). Each later film can open its dictionary on screen.

## A proposed shape (the owner decides)

- **Option A, a new episode after 02, "As It Reads":** the two ways (as it reads, against as you read it), why spiritual readings never end, the Bible's own dictionary and how to use it. 02 keeps the Bereans, the measuring rule and the centre (Emmaus), and its `plain` and `figure` beats become a one-line handoff to the new film.
- **Option B, rework 02 around this spine:** one film, the Bereans as its cold open, the two ways as its problem, and the dictionary as its turn. It is shorter for the series, but 02 is already about 6 minutes and drafted.
- **A possible series motif:** the dictionary as an object, a small book that each later episode opens to one entry (waters → peoples; sleep → death; rain → doctrine).

## Cautions

- **"Spiritual" is a Bible word, used for good.** "They are spiritually discerned" (1 Cor 2:14). The film must not set the Spirit against the letter. The fault is not reading by the Spirit; it is reading in a meaning the words do not give. Name the two ways by what they do ("as it reads" against "as you read it"), not as "literal" against "spiritual".
- **Typology stays.** A type the text itself sets up (the Lamb and the Passover, John 1:29 with Ex 12:13) is the dictionary at work, not spiritualizing. Spiritualizing is drawing a doctrine from a reading the text does not authorize.
- **Figures are real and literal in what they mean.** Miller's rule reads the figure through the Bible's own explanation, and then the explained meaning is read literally (MWV1 22.6: the prophecy, "after the figures are understood", is "literally fulfilled").
- **House rules.** Quote only Scripture; paraphrase Miller and Ellen White unnamed, credits name them; no church history and no denominations; one narrator.
