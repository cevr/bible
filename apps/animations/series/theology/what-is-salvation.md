# What Is Salvation? (draft, owner's ideas, 2026-10-19)

The owner's ideas for scripts, set against _The Path of the Just_ ([series.md](series.md)). Nothing here is a script yet, and no episode number has moved. Every verse below was read in the KJV with `bible verse` on 2026-10-19; every pioneer line was found with `bible egw search` the same day and is a lead, not yet copied into `sources.md` or verified by the frame's verifier.

## The question

"What is salvation?" is first answered by "what are we saved from?", which goes back to creation: what was made, and what was lost. The owner names four losses, in this order:

1. **The image.** Man was made in God's image (Gen 1:26–27). The image is God's form and God's character, and it was lost at the fall.
2. **Separation.** Sin separates us from God (Isa 59:2), and God keeps the separation because His presence would kill a sinner (Ex 33:20; Isa 33:14; Heb 12:29). He separates so that we do not die, and gives a mediator.
3. **Death.** Separation from the source of life leads to death (Rom 6:23).
4. **Bondage.** We cannot stop sinning (Rom 7:18–19; Jer 13:23). Even if death's price were paid, we would sin again at once, and the cycle would never end. The owner calls this the biggest problem.

## The image, in the owner's words

- **God is a person, with a form.** We are made in His image, so God is a being like us: a face, hands and feet, a body (a heavenly one), and feelings. A film could be about the personality of God.
- **The image is also character.** Moses asks "shew me thy glory" (Ex 33:18). God answers that he cannot see His face and live (33:20), and gives him the equal of it, His name (33:19; 34:5–7). The name is His character.
- **The image was lost.** Adam was made "in the likeness of God" (Gen 5:1), but Seth was born "in his own likeness, after his image" (Gen 5:3): Adam's, not God's. Seth inherits the fallen flesh. That flesh wars against the character and wears it down until we sin ourselves, and then our own sins wear us down further.
- **The law is an image of God too.** Sin is "the transgression of the law" (1 John 3:4), the Bible's only definition. Jesus sums up the law as love to God and to the neighbour (Matt 22:37–40; Luke 10:27), so the law is love (Rom 13:10). "God is love" (1 John 4:8). God is holy, righteous and just (Ps 145:17; Deut 32:4), and so is the law (Rom 7:12; Ps 119:172). So the law is a picture of God's character.
- **Jesus is the express image.** "The express image of his person" (Heb 1:3). The owner has Bible studies that walk this progression.

## Cautions from the frame

- **Showing God.** The god-christ frame shows the Father as a definite human form veiled in a haze of glorious light, never a face or a sharp outline (owner, 2026-10-13). This fits Ex 33 exactly: Moses in the cleft, the face not seen. It must not reduce the Father to formless light.
- **What Seth inherits.** "The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father" (Ezek 18:20). Seth inherits the fallen nature, not Adam's guilt; the owner's wording already says this (we fall when "we sin ourselves"). Keep it so.
- **Two small wording points.** "Heart, soul, strength and mind" is Luke 10:27, spoken by the lawyer and approved by Jesus; Jesus says it Himself in Matt 22:37–40 (heart, soul, mind). "Might" is Deut 6:5. In Ex 33 the name comes one verse before the face (33:19, then 33:20).
- **House rules.** Quote only Scripture; paraphrase the pioneers unnamed; no denominational sourcing; one narrator; the cast in the background only.

## Eternal life: three ways, one source (owner, 2026-10-19)

The owner's study: Jesus gives a few ways to obtain eternal life, and the Old Testament gives the same ones. In the end they are one source, faith, which comes by the word. Every verse was read with `bible verse` on 2026-10-19.

| Way                  | Jesus                                                                                                                                                         | The Old Testament                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Know Him             | "this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent" (John 17:3)                                        | "let him that glorieth glory in this, that he understandeth and knoweth me" (Jer 9:24)                                                       |
| Keep the law         | "What is written in the law? how readest thou? … this do, and thou shalt live" (Luke 10:26–28)                                                                | "keep his commandments … that thou mayest live" (Deut 30:16); "which if a man do, he shall live in them" (Lev 18:5)                          |
| Him, Himself         | "I am the way, the truth, and the life" (John 14:6); "I am the resurrection, and the life" (John 11:25); "this life is in his Son" (1 John 5:11–12)           | "cleave unto him: for he is thy life, and the length of thy days" (Deut 30:20)                                                               |
| One source: the word | "the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life" (John 6:63); "Search the scriptures … they are they which testify of me" (John 5:39–40) | "man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD" (Deut 8:3); "it is your life" (Deut 32:47) |

- **Faith joins them:** "faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God" (Rom 10:17). To know Him, to keep His law and to have Him are one life, received by faith from His word.
- **Corpus leads (not yet verified into sources.md):** ST August 24, 1891, par. 1 ("The knowledge of God and of Christ is the only knowledge which can lead to true and eternal" life); RH February 22, 1898, par. 7; DA 391.1.
