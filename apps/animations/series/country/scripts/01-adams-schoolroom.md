# 01 · Adam's Schoolroom

Series: Dwell in the Land · Arc I (What) · 1 of 19
Promise: the viewer sees that God's first home was also His first school (a garden for a room, nature for a book, work for a lesson, and God for the teacher), that the deepest lesson in it was trust, and that any home can begin to be a little of both.
Prerequisites: none.
Length: about 5:15 (615 spoken words ≈ 3:58 at 155 wpm, plus the title, held pauses, about 30 s of music alone and 25 s of credits).
Status: draft for the owner, 2026-10-13. Every KJV line was printed by `bible verse`, and every paraphrase source by `bible egw lookup`, on 2026-10-13 (`../sources.md` §2–§3). Nothing narrated or rendered.

## Header

The order:

- `cold`: a city flat at evening. A girl asks why she has to learn this; the one green thing in the room is a plant on the sill. What did God build first, a house or a school?
- The title.
- `garden`: Eden, the first home with no walls.
- `school`: it was his dwelling and his schoolroom.
- `book`: the first lesson, looking and naming.
- `work`: work before anything went wrong.
- `teacher`: the Father came Himself; a family school.
- `trees`, the deepest turn: two trees and one word. The lesson was trust.
- `lost`: the school did not close; the tree's branches hang over the wall.
- `home`: back to the kitchen table, and what a city family can begin.
- `seedling`, the landing: the grandmother's seedling, and the answer to the girl's question.

Motifs:

- **The seedling.** On the sill in `cold`, a sickly potted plant, the one green thing; Mila reaches for it. In `home`, a seed goes into a pot. In the landing, Rosa's seedling, wrapped in newspaper, is pressed into Nina's hands. It is the series motif and grows through all 19 films.
- **The window.** In `cold` it shows only walls and other lit windows. In the landing, far past the walls, the faint line of hills shows at dusk.
- **Two trees.** In `trees` they stand in the middle of the garden. In `lost` one is lifted out of reach, and its branches still hang over a wall of light.
- **The open book.** In `school` the garden is drawn as a book whose pages are fields and leaves. In `book` its page turns to the animals.

Tent poles, act light, chapter question:

- `cold`: Nina at the table, chin up, pencil down, in lamp-yellow against a blue city dusk. "Why do we have to learn this?"
- `garden`: Eden at morning, peach light, deep cardboard planes. "What did God build first?"
- `trees`: the two trees at noon, gold and shadow. "What was the deepest lesson?"
- `seedling` (landing): the same kitchen, lamp-yellow and teal; the seedling in Nina's hands. "So who is the teacher?"

Registers: STORY for `garden`, `book`, `work`, `teacher`, `trees`, `lost`. IDEA for `school` (it opens STORY and turns to IDEA). The cast's own frame for `cold`, `home` and the landing.

Cast (ensemble, ledger: the household in the city flat, Rosa's cottage visited): Clara and Nina lead; Leo at the window; Mila reaching; Rosa at the landing. No cast member enters Eden (ensemble: never inside a Bible scene). The narration never names them: "a mother", "a little girl", "her grandmother".

Frame (god-christ): the Father is never pictured with a face or body. In `teacher` His presence is a warm light coming along the garden path at evening, and it never becomes a figure. Adam and Eve are grey paper figures with faces at a third of the frame.

## Notes for the owner

- **The author of "Adam's schoolroom"** is Ellen White. The 1899 Bulletin prints the line in a testimony that G. A. Irwin read to the session on Sabbath, March 4, 1899, under the heading "The Tree of Life and the Tree of Knowledge". The "—P.C." on its reprint is the reprinting manual's abbreviation for "Private Collection" (its own key, PH140 p. 4), not a person's initials. The credits name Ellen G. White and the Bulletin, so the line can be used as the series' opening idea. The evidence is in `../sources.md` §1.
- **Genesis 3:8** is the evening of the fall, when they hid (theology 03 shows the hiding). This film only alludes to "the cool of the day" (`teacher`), as Ed 21.1 does for the walks before the fall. It does not quote the verse, and the picture shows an earlier evening with no hiding.
- **The tree of life as a figure of Christ** is illustration over plain texts (John 6:35; Rev 2:7; 22:2), never a doctrine of its own (typology rule). The doctrine `lost` states is the one John 6:35 states.
- **"Not yet, and not that way"** (`trees`) is a gentle gloss: the knowledge of good and evil was not wrong to have, but wrong to take against God's word. If the owner prefers the plain text, cut it to "Of one, not."
- **Nothing in this film tells anyone to move.** It shows the pattern only, and the seed pot is the step any family can take (episode 16 returns to it). Episode 04 carries the call.
