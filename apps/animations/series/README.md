# Series

Research, plans and draft scripts for three content series. Nothing here is narrated, rendered or checked yet.

- [`theology/`](theology/series.md): **From Milk to Meat**, cut-paper films that walk from the first principles of the faith (Heb 5:12; 6:1–2) to its deepest truths, in the order the Bible serves them.
- [`education/`](education/series.md): talk videos on true education from Scripture and Ellen White's education books.
- [`health/`](health/series.md): talk videos on health and the eight laws.

Each folder holds `series.md` (the plan and episode list), `sources.md` (every citation and how it was verified against the local corpus with the `bible` CLI) and `scripts/` (drafts).

Third-party source material stays out of git, because the repo is public: video transcripts, the OCR of scanned books and the PDFs. It lives in the git-ignored `tmp/content-series/` of the main checkout.
