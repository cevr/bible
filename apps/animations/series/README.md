# Series

Research, plans and draft scripts for four content series. Nothing here is narrated, rendered or checked yet.

**Names (owner, 2026-10-13: "I like the names you chose for all the series"):** _The Path of the Just_ (theology; renamed from _From Milk to Meat_, owner 2026-10-20, ask a879ff5af83), _Dwell in the Land_ (country), _Taught of the Lord_ (education), _The True Remedies_ (health). The channel is **First the Blade** (owner, 2026-10-13: "my wife really likes first the blade"), at firsttheblade.com; its site is `apps/firsttheblade`.

- [`theology/`](theology/series.md): **The Path of the Just**, cut-paper films that walk from the first principles of the faith (Heb 5:12; 6:1–2) to its deepest truths, in the order the Bible serves them.
- [`education/`](education/series.md): **Taught of the Lord**, talk videos on true education from Scripture and Ellen White's education books.
- [`health/`](health/series.md): **The True Remedies**, talk videos on health and the eight laws.
- [`country/`](country/series.md): **Dwell in the Land**, cut-paper films on country living (what, why, where and how), doctrine and education side by side, interleaved with the education series.

## Voice and audience (owner, 2026-10-13, binding on every series)

- **Audience:** all Christians, Adventist and not alike, and the interested non-believer too (owner, 2026-10-11: "the audience is all christians and even interested non believers"). The one exception is a talk on Adventist history. Write for a viewer who has never heard our terms: say what a term means, or don't use it.
- **Aim: persuade.** Use reason and logic (logos), feeling (pathos), and the speaker's evident trustworthiness (ethos). Never bare assertion, and never an insider's shorthand.
- **Language:** charming, melodic, earnest, fervent. Sentences that sound well aloud, warm and urgent, never cold or clever. In the owner's voice the melody is the parallel run and the refrain in plain words, never ornament: no amazing, beautiful or wonderful ([owner-voice.md](../../../.claude/skills/film/reference/owner-voice.md), sections 4–6). The urgency lands in the closing choice, one dose, with its way out in the same breath. The words come from how Christ spoke (owner, 2026-10-14: "i mainly take this from egw's explanation of how Jesus spoke"), and each shapes the sentence:
  - **Clear as a running stream** (DA 253.5): one idea a sentence, in the order it happened, joined by "and", "so" and "but", never stacked "which" and "whereby" clauses.
  - **No high-sounding words; figures the hearer knows** (CT 240.2; Ev 56.2, "clothed with such beauty"): the beauty is the picture (a seed, a field, a robe), never the adjective.
  - **Slowly, with emphasis, never "sentence upon sentence without a pause"** (CT 240.1): the stressed word ends the sentence, two or three longer lines settle on a short one, and the pause is written in (CRAFT rule 9's 150–165 words a minute).
  - **Not many things at once; old truths repeated** (Ev 56.1): one point a beat, and the refrain comes back.
  - **Earnest rather than vehement; with authority, never doubt** (DA 254.1, DA 253.5): plain statements, no exclamations, no "perhaps" or "some say".
  - The full counsel on the voice, by practice, and how every script is marked for delivery (owner, 2026-10-14: "as part of the scripts for all our planned series"): the film skill's [voice-counsel.md](../../../.claude/skills/film/reference/voice-counsel.md). [`delivery.md`](delivery.md) is the audit of the drafts against it; the lab reads each draft with its marks under **Series**, a staging take ready. `bun cli.ts drafts` reads every draft as the reader does (its spoken beats, words, marks and time at 155 wpm, or why it does not read); the app's tests run it.
- **The owner's own voice:** how he preaches (his openings, how he builds and lands, his tone, cadence and words) and how each habit becomes a film's line, measured on nine of his sermons, is the film skill's [owner-voice.md](../../../.claude/skills/film/reference/owner-voice.md).
- **Method: Christ's method.** "Christ's method alone will give true success in reaching the people. The Saviour mingled with men as one who desired their good. He showed His sympathy for them, ministered to their needs, and won their confidence. Then He bade them, 'Follow Me'" (MH 143.3, verified). Meet the viewer's need first, then win their confidence, and only then call.
- **No overt Adventist sourcing (owner, 2026-10-13, all series):** we reach a wide audience and avoid prejudice. Speech quotes Scripture (KJV); Ellen White and the pioneers are paraphrased without being named, and the credits name them. A plain idea needs no name on it. Draft scripts that quote Ellen White by name in speech are revised to this rule before narration.
- **A recurring cast in the background, plain leads in front (owner, 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-15):** every film shares an ensemble of characters besides its main figures, for continuity across films and series, and the cast stands only in the background (2026-10-15: "let's make the recurring cast only background characters, and the main characters non descriptive so they can be better related"). A film's main characters are Bible figures, or plain figures with no name and no mark that tells who they are, so any viewer can stand in their place. The cast bible is [`docs/ensemble.md`](../docs/ensemble.md): who they are, how they look and where they may appear. The plain lead's sheet is the film skill's [character-design.md](../../../.claude/skills/film/reference/character-design.md) 2.3. The film skill's Ensemble rule applies both.
- **Every script lists its cast (owner, 2026-10-19):** a script carries a `## Cast` section after its header, so the concept stage starts from cast sheets, then rigs, the Board and the render, "kind of like a real studio pass". It names each figure (a Bible person by its one id in [`docs/casting.md`](../docs/casting.md), a plain lead, a supporting role, the recurring cast in the background, a crowd from the base rigs), the beats it appears in, and the angles and poses its sheet must show; then the settings and the props. Each item carries its scope: film, series, or shared across series. The library grows from these lists (owner: "this library will continuously grow and grow and grow until it becomes very easy to make videos"), so a script names an existing item before it proposes a new one. `scripts.test.ts` holds every script to it.
- **Bookend the setting (owner, 2026-10-13):** a film closes in the place it opened, changed. Open in a courtroom and close in that courtroom. Talk videos do the same with their frame scene. The film skill's CRAFT rule 4 has the rule and its check. Draft scripts are revised to it before narration; [`bookends.md`](bookends.md) is the audit.
- **"The Almighty" (owner, 2026-10-13: "dont call Jesus the Almighty unless to help clarify old testament/new testament references"):** no line calls Jesus "the Almighty" or "Lord God Almighty", except once, to show how an Old Testament text reads in the New (Isa 6:1 with John 12:41; Ex 3:14 with John 8:58), never as a title of address. The sources and the texts left unused are in [`theology/series.md`](theology/series.md), question 11.
- **Fiction (owner, 2026-10-13: "Fiction as a rule should be avoided, but it can be used allegorically"):** no invented story told as true. A story may carry a Bible truth only as an allegory, framed openly as one, as a parable is (_The Pilgrim's Progress_, GC 252.2). The plain leads and the cast are parable figures ("Picture a father…"), never real people. The sources are in [`education/series.md`](education/series.md).
- See also the owner's rules in [`theology/series.md`](theology/series.md) (Audience).

Each folder holds `series.md` (the plan and episode list), `sources.md` (every citation and how it was verified against the local corpus with the `bible` CLI) and `scripts/` (drafts).

Third-party source material stays out of git, because the repo is public: video transcripts, the OCR of scanned books and the PDFs. It lives in the git-ignored `tmp/content-series/` of the main checkout.
