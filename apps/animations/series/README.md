# Series

Research, plans and draft scripts for four content series. Nothing here is narrated, rendered or checked yet.

**Names (owner, 2026-10-13: "I like the names you chose for all the series"):** _From Milk to Meat_ (theology), _Dwell in the Land_ (country), _Taught of the Lord_ (education), _The True Remedies_ (health). The channel is **First the Blade** (owner, 2026-10-13: "my wife really likes first the blade"), at firsttheblade.com; its site is `apps/firsttheblade`.

- [`theology/`](theology/series.md): **From Milk to Meat**, cut-paper films that walk from the first principles of the faith (Heb 5:12; 6:1–2) to its deepest truths, in the order the Bible serves them.
- [`education/`](education/series.md): **Taught of the Lord**, talk videos on true education from Scripture and Ellen White's education books.
- [`health/`](health/series.md): **The True Remedies**, talk videos on health and the eight laws.
- [`country/`](country/series.md): **Dwell in the Land**, cut-paper films on country living (what, why, where and how), doctrine and education side by side, interleaved with the education series.

## Voice and audience (owner, 2026-10-13, binding on every series)

- **Audience:** all Christians, Adventist and not alike, and the interested non-believer too (owner, 2026-10-11: "the audience is all christians and even interested non believers"). The one exception is a talk on Adventist history. Write for a viewer who has never heard our terms: say what a term means, or don't use it.
- **Aim: persuade.** Use reason and logic (logos), feeling (pathos), and the speaker's evident trustworthiness (ethos). Never bare assertion, and never an insider's shorthand.
- **Language:** charming, melodic, earnest, fervent. Sentences that sound well aloud, warm and urgent, never cold or clever. In the owner's voice the melody is the parallel run and the refrain in plain words, never ornament: no amazing, beautiful or wonderful ([owner-voice.md](../../../.claude/skills/film/reference/owner-voice.md), sections 4–6). The urgency lands in the closing choice, one dose, with its way out in the same breath.
- **The owner's own voice:** how he preaches (his openings, how he builds and lands, his tone, cadence and words) and how each habit becomes a film's line, measured on nine of his sermons, is the film skill's [owner-voice.md](../../../.claude/skills/film/reference/owner-voice.md).
- **Method: Christ's method.** "Christ's method alone will give true success in reaching the people. The Saviour mingled with men as one who desired their good. He showed His sympathy for them, ministered to their needs, and won their confidence. Then He bade them, 'Follow Me'" (MH 143.3, verified). Meet the viewer's need first, then win their confidence, and only then call.
- **No overt Adventist sourcing (owner, 2026-10-13, all series):** we reach a wide audience and avoid prejudice. Speech quotes Scripture (KJV); Ellen White and the pioneers are paraphrased without being named, and the credits name them. A plain idea needs no name on it. Draft scripts that quote Ellen White by name in speech are revised to this rule before narration.
- **A recurring cast (owner, 2026-10-13):** every film shares an ensemble of characters besides its main figures, for continuity across films and series. The cast bible is [`docs/ensemble.md`](../docs/ensemble.md): who they are, how they look, where they may appear, and each one's part in every series. The film skill's Ensemble rule applies it.
- **Bookend the setting (owner, 2026-10-13):** a film closes in the place it opened, changed. Open in a courtroom and close in that courtroom. Talk videos do the same with their frame scene. The film skill's CRAFT rule 4 has the rule and its check. Draft scripts are revised to it before narration; [`bookends.md`](bookends.md) is the audit.
- **"The Almighty" (owner, 2026-10-13: "dont call Jesus the Almighty unless to help clarify old testament/new testament references"):** no line calls Jesus "the Almighty" or "Lord God Almighty", except once, to show how an Old Testament text reads in the New (Isa 6:1 with John 12:41; Ex 3:14 with John 8:58), never as a title of address. The sources and the texts left unused are in [`theology/series.md`](theology/series.md), question 11.
- **Fiction (owner, 2026-10-13: "Fiction as a rule should be avoided, but it can be used allegorically"):** no invented story told as true. A story may carry a Bible truth only as an allegory, framed openly as one, as a parable is (_The Pilgrim's Progress_, GC 252.2). The cast are parable figures ("Picture a father…"), never real people. The sources are in [`education/series.md`](education/series.md).
- See also the owner's rules in [`theology/series.md`](theology/series.md) (Audience).

Each folder holds `series.md` (the plan and episode list), `sources.md` (every citation and how it was verified against the local corpus with the `bible` CLI) and `scripts/` (drafts).

Third-party source material stays out of git, because the repo is public: video transcripts, the OCR of scanned books and the PDFs. It lives in the git-ignored `tmp/content-series/` of the main checkout.
