## Owner notes

- Beat 5 keeps the context honest: Daniel's refusal was first about idol worship,
  and also about health (PK 481–482 gives both). The episode does not claim Daniel 1
  forbids meat for everyone; the Eden-diet argument is its own video (see series.md, Proper diet).
- Beat 13 is the only "today" line; it stays with what is well established and does not prescribe.
- Beat 14 rests on Dan 1:9–14 and PK 483.2–483.3 (verified): no wish to be singular,
  a request received with respect, then a test.
