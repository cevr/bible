## Owner notes

- The Sabbath belongs in the Rest strand as its own Episode, framed as God's gift and command. A pioneer writer warns that physical rest is not the Sabbath's object (AMS Jan. 30, 1896, A. T. Jones), so this video does not sell the Sabbath as a health habit.
- The sources' "an hour before midnight is worth two after" is left out. Beat 6 keeps only what is established: regular hours, and early enough to get enough.
