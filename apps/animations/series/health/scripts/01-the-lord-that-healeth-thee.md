# 01 · The Lord That Healeth Thee (series opener, Episode)

**Promise.** In under three minutes: where the Bible's healing promise starts,
how Jesus healed, and the eight remedies the series will walk through one at a time.

**Standing line (on screen at 0–3 s, small, bottom safe area; and first line of every description):**
_Principles from Scripture, not medical advice. Don't stop or change any treatment without your doctor._

Speech rules for this script: Scripture quoted from the KJV, verbatim (an ellipsis
marks words skipped, and the card on screen shows the full verse). Everything else is
said in the owner's own words; the writers behind it are credited in the description
(see **Credits** at the end), never named aloud.

## Word count and timing

About 430 spoken words. At 175–185 wpm with the one long pause before the payoff, 2:20–2:30, inside the Episode band (2:15–2:50). If the owner wants more room, beat 6 (Elim) can carry one more sentence of his own.
Speaker on screen: beats 1, 2, 5, 9, 12, 14, 15, 17 in short holds (about 25%).

## Owner notes

- Beat 13 says the eight in plain words. If he would rather say the original
  wording ("pure air, sunlight, abstemiousness, rest, exercise, proper diet, the
  use of water, trust in divine power"), it is the same list, and the word card
  explains _abstemiousness_ either way. Saying the original as one unbroken
  sentence is close to quoting the source, which the series' rule avoids; the plain
  version keeps it his own voice.
- Beat 17 sets up the Medicine strand (God works through means; the means are not the healer).
