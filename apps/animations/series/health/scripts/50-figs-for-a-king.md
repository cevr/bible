# Figs for a King (Medicine, One Question)

**Question.** If I really trust God to heal, is using a remedy, or seeing a doctor, a lack of faith?

**Standing line (on screen 0–3 s; first line of the description):**
_Principles from Scripture, not medical advice. Don't stop or change any treatment without your doctor._
In this video the standing line is also spoken (beat 8), because the subject is treatment itself.

## Owner notes (Medicine strand)

- **What this strand needs from you.** Your message broke off at "whats very interesting
  is medicine…". The research found five likely threads; series.md lists them under
  _Medicine_ (M0) so you can say which you meant. This pilot takes the safest one first
  (faith and means), because every other Medicine video leans on it.
- **What this video does not do.** It does not discuss the sources' warnings against
  "drug medication." Those warnings named the drugs of their day (mercury/calomel,
  strychnine, arsenic, opium and morphine in patent medicines) and have their own
  Episode in the strand, told as medical history.
- Beat 7 paraphrases two passages that say almost the same thing 40 years apart
  (2SG 135.1, 1860; 2SM 286.2, Letter 182, 1899). It names no one aloud.
