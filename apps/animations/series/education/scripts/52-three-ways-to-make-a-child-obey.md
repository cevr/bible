## Pre-shoot checks

- The book's word is "authority". Keep it, and keep "kind" next to it. The episode does not discuss spanking. Abbott's chapter 21 on the rod splits Christian audiences, so leave it out (see `series.md`, "Steer around").
- Dialogue is paraphrased and shortened, not read from the book. Only the second mother's plea is spoken as her own words; the other mothers' words are told.
- "Mommy" stands in for the book's "mamma". The modern word is fine.
