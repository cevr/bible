## Pre-shoot checks

- Beat 8 quotes only the last clause of 2 Corinthians 10:12. The caption shows those words only.
- Leave out the Credits booklet's lines that call degrees "papal" and its school-history asides (see `series.md`, "Steer around").
- The speaker should not single out any named school, test or country's grading system.
- Don't say the old writers "never mention grades". It is a seminar claim (Ritsema) that nobody has checked. _Education_ 225–226 is about emulation and rivalry, and CT 177.1's "grading" means sorting by class level, not marks.
- Optional backup for beat 9 (2026-10-12): offering prizes and rewards does more harm than good, because it pushes the most driven pupils too hard (CT 270.1). Paraphrase only, and add it to the credits if used.
