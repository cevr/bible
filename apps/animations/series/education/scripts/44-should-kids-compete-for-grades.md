## Pre-shoot checks

- Beats 6, 8 and 11 quote part of a verse (Mark 10:43 from "whosoever"; the last clause of 2 Corinthians 10:12; Colossians 3:23 from "heartily"). Each caption shows those words only.
- Leave out the Credits booklet's lines that call degrees "papal" and its school-history asides (see `series.md`, "Steer around").
- The speaker should not single out any named school, test or country's grading system.
- Don't say the old writers "never mention grades". It is a seminar claim (Ritsema) that nobody has checked. _Education_ 225–226 is about emulation and rivalry, and CT 177.1's "grading" means sorting by class level, not marks.
- Optional backup for beat 9 (2026-10-12): offering prizes and rewards does more harm than good, because it pushes the most driven pupils too hard (CT 270.1). Paraphrase only, and add it to the credits if used. The take has no room for it inside 65 s.
