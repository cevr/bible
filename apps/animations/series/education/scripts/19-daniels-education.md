## Pre-shoot checks

- **No date is spoken.** Haskell gives 607 B.C. (SDP 14.1) and 606 B.C. (booklet p. 11) in two places, so the script names no year.
- The "old Jewish saying" is credited to Haskell's text, which calls it a Hebrew maxim. It is attributed as a saying, never as Scripture. It matches the Babylonian Talmud, Shabbat 119b ("Jerusalem was destroyed only because they neglected the schoolchildren" in one translation). Check the wording in a printed edition before the shoot (`transcripts/white/verification.md`).
- Do not say what the Hebrew names mean. The corpus pages above do not give the meanings.
- Captions show only the quoted parts of Daniel 1:4, 1:8, 1:12, 1:15, 1:17 and 1:20.
- Beats 8 and 21 were added 2026-10-12. Beat 8 quotes the last clause of Daniel 1:4 and beat 21 three words of Proverbs 22:29; both captions show only those words. Both were printed by `bible verse` on 2026-10-12. Beat 21 paraphrases Ed 262.2 in the owner's words.
