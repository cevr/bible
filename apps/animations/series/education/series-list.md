# Series list: _Taught of the Lord_

A proposed sequence of episodes that walks a parent through `guide.md` in order. Built 2026-10-13. The 127 draft topics in `series.md` stay the catalogue; this list only orders and groups them. Most episodes fold several draft topics into one talk, and some draft topics feed more than one episode.

**How to read it.**

- **Guide** is the section of `guide.md` the episode carries.
- **Draws on** lists the catalogue numbers in `series.md` it is built from; the first is the spine.
- **Fmt** is the catalogue's format: **Q** a One Question short (45–65 s), **E** an Episode (2:15–2:50).
- **Audience.** Every episode is for all Christians, Adventist or not (`../README.md`, "Voice and audience"). An episode on Adventist history may speak to Adventists as insiders; it is marked **AH**. None of the episodes below is one: the guide keeps institutional and denominational history out, and the one history-heavy episode (no. 78) is general history of schooling.
- **Method.** Each episode meets the parent's need first, wins confidence, then calls (MH 143.3). The order of arcs follows the same logic: the why and the home before the hard questions of school and place.
