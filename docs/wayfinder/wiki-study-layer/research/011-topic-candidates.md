## Method and receipts

Three mechanical sources, weighed together:

1. **The user's own teaching material** — titles, frontmatter (`topic:`/`title:`), and
   file names across:
   - `/Users/cvr/Developer/personal/bible-tools/packages/cli/outputs/teachings/` (great-controversy/reading-102)
   - `/Users/cvr/Developer/personal/bible-tools/packages/cli/outputs/studies/` (33 dated
     studies, the 33-part `the-way-home/` series, `laws-of-health/`, `chain-of-truth/`,
     `godhead/`, `seven-times-2520/`, `two-classes/`, `last-generation-theology/`,
     `why-he-tarries/`, `peace-faith-works/`, `dress-reform/`, `understanding-prophecy/`,
     `daniel-revelation/`, `sections/`)
   - `/Users/cvr/Developer/personal/bible-tools/packages/cli/outputs/decks/`
     (seven-trumpets, what-is-truth, reading-102, why-suffering-scripture-narrative)
2. **Catalog topical index** — `~/.bible/bible.db`, tables `topics` (5,322 rows, all ids
   prefixed `naves-topical-bible.` — the catalog is Nave's Topical Bible),
   `topic_sections`, `topic_references`. Weight = `COUNT(topic_references)` per topic,
   read-only via `bun:sqlite`.
3. **EGW/pioneer corpus phrase frequency** — `~/.bible/egw-paragraphs.db`, virtual table
   `paragraphs_fts` (FTS5 over `paragraphs.content_text`; 3,012,004 paragraphs across
   1,486 books, the post-2026-08-15 sync). Each candidate phrase ran as
   `paragraphs_fts MATCH 'content_text : "<phrase>"'`. Two numbers per phrase:
   **total** (whole corpus, pioneers included) and **EGW** (books whose `books.book_author`
   matches `Ellen%White` or `%White Estate%` — 615 books, ~962k paragraphs). A large
   total with a tiny EGW share flags a pioneer-dominated topic (e.g. `little horn`,
   `2520`, `Eastern Question`).

Caveats: FTS counts are phrase-literal — `judgment`, `Babylon`, `Sabbath` include mundane
and Old-Testament-narrative uses; `the daily` includes "the daily paper". Counts rank
candidates; they do not measure doctrinal weight by themselves. Catalog "none" means
Nave's has no topic of that name (Nave's predates SDA vocabulary — no SECOND COMING,
SPIRITUALISM, REMNANT, or SEAL OF GOD entries).
