## Introduction — The Question Beneath the Questions (5 min)

- Name the moment honestly: the cross is preached — the health message, country
  living, prophecy come up — and a question rises in the heart: "Must I know
  this? Must I do this — to be saved?"
- Today's subject is not those topics; it is that QUESTION — because the
  question is about our DESIRES, and desires are exactly what Jesus came to
  save
- Grant the truth hiding inside it: only Jesus saves — Matthew 1:21 — "he shall
  save his people FROM their sins" — and the law names a wrong WANT as sin
  (Exod 20:17 — "thou shalt not covet"), so the wants are the very thing He
  saves us from
- Two questions divide every hearer of truth:
  - "What must I do?" — the negotiator's question
  - "Search me, O God" — the surrendered heart's question
- Psalm 139:23–24 — "Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my
  thoughts: and see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way
  everlasting"
- [WB]: Two columns, left blank for now — "MUST I?" / "SEARCH ME" — the message
  fills them in (Ps 139:23–24)
- [RQ]: When new light reaches you, which of the two questions rises first in
  your heart? (Ps 139:23)
- [EB]: Deuteronomy 5:29 (God's own longing — "O that there were such an heart
  in them" — the problem was never the commandments; it was always the heart)

## Section 1 — "Must I?": The Negotiator's Question (7 min)

- Micah 6:6–7 — the bargaining ladder: burnt offerings → thousands of rams →
  ten thousands of rivers of oil → "shall I give my firstborn?" — the price
  climbs and climbs, and the heart never moves an inch
- Micah 6:8 — God's answer is not a price at all: "what doth the LORD require
  of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy
  God?" — a WALK, not a payment
- Luke 10:25 — the lawyer "tempted him": "what shall I do to inherit eternal
  life?" — inheritance priced as a transaction
- Matthew 19:16, 20 — the rich young ruler: "what good thing shall I do";
  "All these things have I kept from my youth up: what lack I yet?" — ledger
  obedience that still feels the lack
- "The ruler said, No, I cannot give You all." (DA 523.1) — when the answer
  touched his treasure, the question already had its real answer waiting
- The diagnosis: "Must I?" measures the fee, not the Physician — only an
  unchanged want negotiates with its Healer; the question IS the symptom
- [Aside]: A patient on the surgeon's table, bargaining: "How much of the
  tumor may I keep?" — no one who believes the diagnosis talks that way
- The critical nuance — same words, opposite heart:
  - Acts 16:30–31 — the Philippian jailer: "Sirs, what must I do to be saved?"
    — and the answer is pure grace: "Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and
    thou shalt be saved"
  - The difference is DIRECTION, not vocabulary: the jailer was running
    TOWARD; the negotiator is edging AWAY
  - God never scolds the seeker's question — He exposes the bargainer's
- [EGW]: "Those who feel the constraining love of God, do not ask how little
  may be given to meet the requirements of God; they do not ask for the lowest
  standard, but aim at perfect conformity to the will of their Redeemer."
  (SC 44.2)
- Why the unchanged heart cannot ask anything else: Romans 8:7 — "the carnal
  mind is enmity against God: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither
  indeed can be" — what cannot submit, negotiates

## Section 2 — No Neutral Ground: Why the Minimum Does Not Exist (6 min) [*]

- The Bible is counter-cultural BY DEFINITION — not by accident, not by era
- Romans 8:7 said it of the mind; James 4:4 says it of the world: "the
  friendship of the world is enmity with God"
- 1 John 2:15–16 — the world's whole catalog in three lines: "the lust of the
  flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life" — what we do, what
  we watch, what we boast
- Matthew 12:30 — "He that is not with me is against me" — no neutral PERSON,
  no divided allegiance: the heart is never on the fence
- 1 Corinthians 10:31 — "whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye
  do, do all to the glory of God" — every doing falls under one banner:
  everything is either training the want or untraining it
- Guard: this hands you no list and names no hobby — it removes the fiction of
  neutral ground; the Spirit applies the principle to your feed, your shelf,
  your calendar (John 16:13 — "he will guide you into all truth")
- Romans 12:2 — therefore the command is never "trim" — "be not conformed to
  this world: but be ye TRANSFORMED by the renewing of your mind"
- So the minimum the negotiator hunts for does not exist — and hunting it while
  claiming the promise has a name: "Presumption is Satan's counterfeit of
  faith. Faith claims God's promises, and brings forth fruit in obedience.
  Presumption also claims the promises, but uses them as Satan did, to excuse
  transgression." (DA 126.1)
- [RQ]: If the whole carnal mind is enmity, what exactly would a "minimum"
  surrender be keeping back? (Rom 8:7)

## Section 3 — "Search Me": The Prayer of the New Heart (7 min)

- The regenerate heart asks the opposite question — Psalm 139:23–24 — "see if
  there be ANY wicked way in me" — ANY: no floor, no minimum, no fenced-off
  room
- That question cannot be self-generated; it belongs to a heart God creates:
  Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God" — creation, not renovation
- [EB]: Jeremiah 24:7 (Even the heart that seeks Him is His gift — "I will
  give them an heart to know me")
- How the exchange happens — the way God drew on the floor of His own house
  (Ps 77:13 — "Thy way, O God, is in the sanctuary"):
  - [WB]: GATE → ALTAR → LAVER → HOLY PLACE → ARK — titled: THE WAY THE WANT
    IS CHANGED (Ps 77:13)
  - The court in one breath: faith is a gift (Eph 2:8), brought by your "own
    voluntary will" to the door (Lev 1:3), exchanged for a heart He gives
    (Ezek 36:26 — "a new heart also will I GIVE you")
  - The laver — Ezekiel 36:25 — "Then will I sprinkle clean water upon you,
    and ye shall be clean" — the cleansing comes BEFORE the new heart of v.26,
    and it is His act, not ours (Titus 3:5 — "the washing of regeneration")
  - "We cannot change our hearts... But we can choose to serve God, we can
    give Him our will; then He will work in us to will and to do according to
    His good pleasure." (MH 176.2)
  - "Everything depends on the right action of the will." (SC 47.1) — the will
    given, God working the want in: Philippians 2:12–13 — "work out your own
    salvation... for it is God which worketh in you both to WILL and to do"
- The holy place — where the new want is fed daily; "the right exercise of the
  will": "By yielding up your will to Christ, you ally yourself with the power
  that is above all principalities and powers" (SC 48.1) — means of grace, not
  a willpower program:
  - FEED it — Matthew 4:4 — "man shall not live by bread alone, but by every
    word"
  - UNITE it — Matthew 26:41 — "Watch and pray... the spirit indeed is
    willing, but the flesh is weak"
  - EXERCISE it — Luke 16:10 — "faithful in that which is least"
  - EXAMINE it — Psalm 139:23–24, prayed daily — hand the search to God
- The constant audit — not a list this pulpit hands you, but a search you hand
  to the Spirit: what else do I DO, THINK, WATCH, HEAR that is offensive to
  God?
  - The eyes — Psalm 101:3 — "I will set no wicked thing before mine eyes"
  - The thoughts — Philippians 4:8 — "whatsoever things are true... pure...
    lovely... think on these things"; 2 Corinthians 10:5 — "bringing into
    captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ"
  - The ears — Isaiah 33:14–16 — "Who among us shall dwell with the devouring
    fire?" — "he that... stoppeth his ears from hearing of blood, and
    shutteth his eyes from seeing evil... bread shall be given him; his
    waters shall be sure"
  - [EB]: Job 31:1 (The audit reaches upstream of the act — "I made a covenant
    with mine eyes")
  - [EB]: Proverbs 4:23 (Every gate guards one spring — "Keep thy heart with
    all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life")
- Guardrail — this audit is not scrupulosity, and it does not terminate in
  self: 2 Corinthians 13:5 — "Examine yourselves... Jesus Christ is IN you" —
  the search ends in finding Christ within, never in anxiety

## FINAL PEAK SECTION — For Joy He Sold All: A Want Heaven Can Receive (8 min)

- Why the changed heart never asks the minimum — Matthew 13:44 — "for JOY
  thereof goeth and selleth ALL that he hath, and buyeth that field" — no
  calculation, no haggling: glad liquidation
- Matthew 13:45–46 — the pearl of great price: one thing worth everything —
  "went and sold all that he had, and bought it"
- "He is a gift, but only to those who give themselves, soul, body, and
  spirit, to Him without reserve... When we thus give ourselves wholly to Him,
  Christ, with all the treasures of heaven, gives Himself to us. We obtain the
  pearl of great price." (COL 116.2)
- And the parable runs both directions: Christ the merchantman "collected all
  the riches of the universe, and laid them down in order to buy the pearl"
  (COL 118.2) — He never asked how little; why would His people?
- [EB]: Luke 14:33 (Christ's own terms — "whosoever he be of you that
  forsaketh not all that he hath, he cannot be my disciple" — there is no
  least-I-can-do tier)
- The last refuge of "Must I?" is "God will still let me in" — so test it
  against what heaven IS:
  - "It is no arbitrary decree on the part of God that excludes the wicked
    from heaven; they are shut out by their own unfitness for its
    companionship." (SC 17.2)
  - [EGW]: "A life of rebellion against God has unfitted them for heaven. Its
    purity, holiness, and peace would be torture to them... Their exclusion
    from heaven is voluntary with themselves, and just and merciful on the
    part of God." (GC 542.2)
  - Letting the unchanged want in would not save it — it would immortalize the
    disease and call it heaven (Isa 33:14)
- So the real question was never "will God let me in" — at the end of the way
  stands the ARK: the law under the mercy seat, and its text is Psalm 40:8 —
  "I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is WITHIN my heart"
- Hebrews 8:10 — "I will put my laws into their mind, and write them in their
  hearts" — the finish line is the want rewritten as YOUR want
- [RQ]: When the veil parts, the question will not be "will God let me in" —
  will there be a WANT in you that heaven can receive? (Ps 40:8; Heb 8:10)
- The pastoral turn — to the one who honestly does not want:
  - The want cannot be manufactured — but the unwilling will can be handed
    over; He works the want in (MH 176.2)
  - Willing to be made willing — Psalm 110:3 — "Thy people shall be WILLING in
    the day of thy power"
- [EGW]: "Will you not say, 'I will give my will to Jesus, and I will do it
  now,' and from this moment be wholly on the Lord's side?... It will take, at
  times, every particle of will power which you possess; but it is God that is
  working for you, and you will come forth from the molding process a vessel
  unto honor." (5T 514.1)

### Call to Action — Trade Your Question, and Pray the Dangerous Prayer

- Say it where you sit, and mean it for eternity: "I will give my will to
  Jesus, and I will do it now" (5T 514.1)
- Retire the negotiator's question forever — stop asking how little; ask Him
  for the joy that sells all (Matt 13:44; SC 44.2)
- Covenant to pray Psalm 139:23–24 every day — and mean the word ANY: search
  my doing, my thinking, my watching, my hearing (Ps 139:23–24)
- Keep praying it — He has already signed the answer: "I WILL put my laws into
  their mind" (Heb 8:10); "he which hath begun a good work in you WILL perform
  it" (Phil 1:6) — until "I delight to do thy will, O my God" is written within
  (Ps 40:8)
