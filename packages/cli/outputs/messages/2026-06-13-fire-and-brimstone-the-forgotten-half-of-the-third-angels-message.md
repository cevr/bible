## 1. The Forgotten Half of the Message

> We have forgotten what the third angel's message is about. For a century the Western question seemed impossible — now many see it. But the message's own penalty-words — fire, brimstone, smoke, torment — are the sixth trumpet's words, and they remain forgotten.

- _Rev. 14:9._ "If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark" — the Western question: beast, image, mark. **[Slide 1]**
- _GC 440.2._ "What nation of the New World was in 1798 rising into power, giving promise of strength and greatness...?" — "One nation, and only one, meets the specifications of this prophecy; it points unmistakably to the United States of America" — the prophecy named the nation.
- _GC 442.1._ The lamblike horns and dragon voice "point to a striking contradiction between the professions and the practice of the nation thus represented" — foretold of a power whose lamblike horns "indicate youth, innocence, and gentleness, fitly representing the character of the United States" (_GC 441.1_). **(if time)**
- _GC 442.2._ "Such action would be directly contrary to the principles of this government... and to the Constitution" — why generations could NOT believe it; the prediction was made when it looked impossible. **[Slide 2]**
- _GC 579.1._ "In the events now taking place is seen a rapid advance toward the fulfillment of the prediction" — many now see its veracity.
- _Rev. 14:10-11._ "tormented with **fire and brimstone**... the **smoke** of their torment ascendeth up for ever and ever" — the OTHER half of the very same message.
- _Rev. 9:17-18._ "out of their mouths issued **fire and smoke and brimstone**... by these three was the third part of men killed" — the penalty-words are the SIXTH TRUMPET'S exact words: minted one by one across the trumpets, assembled together in the sixth.
- _Hos. 4:6._ "My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge" — to recover the forgotten half we must learn the trumpets: define the figure, then walk them one by one.

## 2. What Is a Trumpet?

> Let Scripture define its own figure: the trumpet is the Bible's standing alarm of war and of coming judgment. Fix that, and every trumpet of Revelation reads as a war. **[Slide 3]**

- _Num. 10:9._ "If ye go to war in your land against the enemy... then ye shall blow an alarm with the trumpets" — God's own definition; the trumpet is the war-signal. **[Board: TRUMPET = WAR-ALARM]**
- _Amos 3:6._ "Shall a trumpet be blown in the city, and the people not be afraid? shall there be evil in a city, and the LORD hath not done it?" — the trumpet warns of judgment, and the judgment is God's doing. **(if time)**
- _Jer. 4:19-21._ "the sound of the trumpet, the alarm of war... Destruction upon destruction is cried" — the prophet hears the trumpet and names it outright: the alarm of war. **(if time)**
- _1 Cor. 14:8._ "if the trumpet give an uncertain sound, who shall prepare himself to the battle?" — Paul takes trumpet = battle-call as a thing already understood.
- _Joel 2:1._ "Blow ye the trumpet in Zion... let all the inhabitants of the land tremble: for the day of the LORD cometh" — the trumpet is the alarm that the day of the LORD is at hand. **(if time)**
- _Zeph. 1:14-16._ "The great day of the LORD is near... A day of the trumpet and alarm against the fenced cities, and against the high towers" — the day of the LORD is itself a day of the trumpet, a war on walled cities. **(if time)**
- _Eze. 33:2-3._ "When I bring the sword upon a land... if when he seeth the sword come... he blow the trumpet, and warn the people" — the trumpet is the watchman's warning that the sword is coming.
- _Eze. 33:6._ "if the watchman see the sword come, and blow not the trumpet... his blood will I require at the watchman's hand" — the silent watchman is guilty of the slaughter he did not announce. **DEFINITION — TRUMPET =** the Bible's own alarm of war and coming judgment, the watchman's warning that the sword is at the door; the seven trumpets of Revelation are therefore seven successive wars sent of God as judgment.

## 3. The First Four Trumpets

> Four barbarian wars destroyed Western Rome; each prophetic word met by a named campaign — trumpet = war, four times over.

- _Rev. 8:6._ "the seven angels which had the seven trumpets prepared themselves to sound" — seven war-alarms, sounded in sequence against Rome.
- _Rev. 8:7._ "hail and fire mingled with blood... the third part of trees was burnt up, and all green grass was burnt up" — the Goths under Alaric break on Rome from the north; **hail** = sweeping destruction (_Isa. 28:17_) — the invaders' northern, Baltic origin; **blood** = the slaughter, **trees** = great men, **green grass** = the common people. **[Slide 4]**
- _Joel 2:3._ "A fire devoureth before them" — **FIRE = devouring war**: its first trumpet-appearance owns the definition — **THESIS WORD #1: FIRE.** **[Board: FIRE]**
- _SSTR 3.1._ "The first sore and heavy judgment which fell on western Rome in its downward course, was the war with the Goths under Alaric" — invaded Italy in 408, and "in 410 he besieged, took, and sacked Rome" (_SSTR 9.1_). **[Board: A.D. 410]**
- _Rev. 8:8-9._ "a great mountain burning with fire was cast into the sea: and the third part of the sea became blood" — **mountain** = a kingdom, **sea** = peoples — Genseric's Vandal naval war on the maritime coasts. **[Slide 5]**
- _SSTR 12.2._ "Italy continued to be long afflicted by the incessant depredations of the Vandal pirates. In the spring of each year they equipped a formidable navy in the port of Carthage." **(if time)**
- _Rev. 8:10-11._ "there fell a great star from heaven, burning as it were a lamp... and the name of the star is called Wormwood" — Attila the meteor-star, bitterness and ruin. **[Slide 6]**
- _SSTR 17.1._ "There fell a great star from heaven. The name of Attila is to this day a memorial of his greatness" — "It was the boast of Attila, that the grass never grew on the spot which his horse had trod. 'The scourge of God,' was a name that he appropriated to himself" (_SSTR 22.4_). **(if time)**
- _Rev. 8:12._ "the third part of the sun was smitten, and the third part of the moon, and the third part of the stars" — **sun / moon / stars** = a government's luminaries — Rome's emperors, senators, and consuls put out. **[Slide 7]** **[Board: A.D. 476]**
- _SSTR 25.2._ "Extinction of the western empire, A. D. 476..." — and "Every token of royalty disappeared from the imperial city... The sun of Rome was smitten" (_SSTR 26.1_). **(if time)**
- _Rev. 8:7-12._ "the third part," repeated every trumpet — the empire was "divided into three parts; and hence the frequent remark, 'a third part of men,' etc., in allusion to the third part of the empire which was under the scourge" (_SSTR 2.1_). **(if time)**
- _Rev. 8:6-12._ **DEFINITION — FIRST FOUR TRUMPETS =** four barbarian wars that destroyed Western Rome — Goths (Alaric), Vandals (Genseric), Huns (Attila), Heruli (Odoacer) — each prophetic word met by a real campaign; trumpet = war, four times over.

## 4. The First Woe — the Fifth Trumpet

> The last three trumpets are named WOES: God's rod now on apostate Christendom. The first woe is the Saracens, the locust-people of the east. Say it plainly before walking in: the indictment in these woes falls on the apostate church, not on the rod God used — and the rod is itself judged afterward (_Isa. 10:5, 12_).

- _Rev. 8:13._ "Woe, woe, woe, to the inhabiters of the earth by reason of the other voices of the trumpet of the three angels, which are yet to sound" — the last three trumpets re-named woes: God's rod now on apostate Christendom, not pagan Rome. **[Slide 8]**
- _Rev. 9:1-2._ "a star fall from heaven... the key of the bottomless pit... there arose a smoke out of the pit, as the smoke of a great furnace" — the Arabian wastes opened, and Mohammedanism rises out of the desert.
- _Rev. 9:2._ "there arose a smoke out of the pit, as the smoke of a great furnace" — **SMOKE** = the visible going-up of a burning judgment (_Gen. 19:28_, Sodom's furnace-smoke; _Rev. 14:11_), here carrying error up out of the pit: "Like the noxious and even deadly vapor which the winds... diffuse in Arabia" (_SSTR 37.1_) — **THESIS WORD #2: SMOKE.** **[Board: SMOKE]**
- _Rev. 9:3._ "there came out of the smoke locusts upon the earth... as the scorpions of the earth have power" — **locusts** = the innumerable army of the children of the east, laid "like grasshoppers for multitude" (_Judg. 6:5; 7:12_). **[Slide 9]**
- _1 Kgs. 12:11._ "I will chastise you with scorpions" — **scorpions** = power to sting and chastise, not yet to slay.
- _SSTR 38.2._ "swarms of Saracens, like locusts, overspread the earth, and speedily extended their ravages over the Roman empire, from east to west" — "the locusts (the fit symbol of Arabs) issued from Arabia, their native region." **[Slide 10]**
- _Rev. 9:4._ "they should not hurt the grass... neither any tree; but only those men which have not the seal of God" — Islam commissioned against apostate Christendom, sparing God's sealed.
- _Rev. 9:5._ "they should not kill them, but that they should be **tormented** five months" — **TORMENT** = the anguish of God's war-judgments, here LEASHED to the scorpion's sting-not-slay commission — **THESIS WORD #3: TORMENT** — and day = year (_Num. 14:34_): five months = 150 prophetic years. **[Board: TORMENT]**
- _SSTR 49.5._ "to torment, by constant depredations, but not politically to kill them... 'Five months;' that is, one hundred and fifty years. Commencing July 27th, 1299, the one hundred and fifty years reach to 1449." **[Board: 1299 → 1449]**
- _Rev. 9:7-9._ "like unto horses prepared unto battle... crowns like gold... faces of men... hair of women... teeth of lions... breastplates of iron" — **horses** = the mounted war-power: "the barbed Arabs, swift as locusts and armed like scorpions, ready to dart away in a moment" (_SSTR 44.1_). **(if time)**
- _Rev. 9:11._ "they had a king over them" — natural locusts "have no king" (_Prov. 30:27_), so the text's own signal that the symbol is men, not insects. **(if time)**
- _SSTR 47.5._ "Near the close of the 13th century, Othman founded a government, which has since been known as the Ottoman government" — Abaddon/Apollyon, "in both languages... a destroyer," the Ottoman government's standing character (_SSTR 48.3_). **[Slide 11]**
- _Rev. 9:12._ "One woe is past; and, behold, there come two woes more hereafter" — **DEFINITION — WOE / FIRST WOE =** a trumpet (war) by which God scourges apostate Christendom; the first woe = the Saracens, the locust-people, commissioned to torment 150 years (1299-1449).
