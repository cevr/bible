## Introduction — The Question No One Asks (4 min)

- He said He would come quickly. He has not. The reverent question: **what is He waiting for?**
- Matthew 24:14 — the advent was hung on a condition: "this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world... and THEN shall the end come"
- [RQ]: If the timing turns on a finished work, then the delay is not God's slackness — whose is it? (2 Pet. 3:9)
- [EGW]: "Christ is waiting with longing desire for the manifestation of Himself in His church. When the character of Christ shall be perfectly reproduced in His people, then He will come to claim them as His own." (COL 69.1)
- [WB]: Write the question at the top — **WHAT IS CHRIST WAITING FOR?** — leave it open; the message answers it
- The answer is not a date. It is a message — and it is one we already heard, and refused.

## Section 1 — The Wilderness: a Delay of Our Own Making (4 min)

- The pattern is old. Israel stood eleven days from Canaan (Deut. 1:2) — and took forty years
- Numbers 14:33–34 — "a year for a day"; the delay was unbelief, not God
- [EB]: Hebrews 3:19 (They could not enter in because of unbelief — the door was open the whole time)
- [RQ]: Was God unwilling to bring them in — or were they unwilling to go? (Num. 14:3–4)
- [EGW]: "We may have to remain here in this world because of insubordination many more years, as did the children of Israel; but... His people should not add sin to sin by charging God." ("Charge It Not to God," Ev 696.3)
- [WB]: 11 DAYS → 40 YEARS. Caption: _the delay was ours_
- The wilderness is the type of the whole question: a people kept out of the promised rest by their own refusal to go forward

## Section 2 — The Two Lines in Noah's Day: How Separation Was Lost (7 min)

- Christ sends us to "the days of Noe" (Luke 17:26) — so go back and see what was really there: not first a flood, but a MARKED separation between those who worshiped God and those who did not
- Genesis 4:26 — "then began men to call upon the name of the LORD" — the faithful had always worshiped, but now the line drew itself openly
- [EGW]: "As men increased, the distinction between the two classes became more marked. There was an open profession of loyalty to God on the part of one, as there was of contempt and disobedience on the part of the other." (PP 80.2) — THAT is what "calling on the name" represents: a visible, professed separation
- [WB]: TWO LINES — Seth (loyal, the Sabbath kept, PP 80.3) / Cain (built a city, chose his own times, Gen. 4:17)
- And mark WHERE each dwelt: Cain "founded a city," his race "dispersed over the plains and valleys"; the children of Seth "withdrew to the MOUNTAINS, and there made their home" (PP 81.1–2) — the godly in the heights, the worldly in the cities of the plain
- [EGW]: "So long as this separation continued, they maintained the worship of God in its purity." (PP 81.2) — separation was not legalism; it was the very thing that KEPT them
- **But what broke it?** — they came down. [EGW]: "In the lapse of time they ventured, little by little, to mingle with the inhabitants of the valleys. This association was productive of the worst results." (PP 81.2)
- [WB]: Arrow — MOUNTAINS → down into the VALLEYS. "Little by little." No single fall; a drift
- The steps of the drift: they MINGLED → they INTERMARRIED ("the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair," Gen. 6:2; PP 81.2) → they desired the world ("fixed their minds upon worldly prosperity and enjoyment and neglected the commandments," PP 81.2) → they "went in the way of Cain" (Jude 11)
- [RQ]: They did not set out to apostatize — they set out for the conveniences and advantages of the valley. Is the pull on us any different? (PP 81.2)
- **And what did they eat and drink?** — Christ marks them by it: "they did eat, they drank" (Luke 17:27). God had appointed the diet of Eden — Genesis 1:29, "every herb... every tree... to you it shall be for meat"
- [EGW]: "Grains, fruits, nuts, and vegetables constitute the diet chosen for us by our Creator." (CD 81.2) — flesh was not granted until AFTER the flood (Gen. 9:3; CD 373.3)
- So Cain's line, despising God's commands, gave themselves to appetite and excess — the eating and drinking of self-indulgence, not the simple fare God appointed
- [WB]: GOD'S TABLE (fruits, grains — Gen. 1:29) / CAIN'S TABLE (appetite, excess, flesh, wine). What is on yours?
- [Aside]: The diet is a parable of the whole life — the body taken down off God's mountain and fed at the world's table. The first compromise of the worshipers was to eat and drink and live like those they once came out from.

## Section 3 — The Ark Today: Noah and Lot, One Type (7 min)

- Christ binds the two together — same warning, same age, same world: "as it was in the days of Noe... likewise also as it was in the days of Lot" (Luke 17:26–30)
- Both turn on ONE thing: a refuge entered by separation while the world ate, drank, bought, sold, planted, builded — heedless "until" the day judgment fell (Luke 17:27, 29)
- Noah's refuge was an **ark** he built by faith (Heb. 11:7 — "moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house"); Lot's was the **mountain** the angels commanded, "Escape to the mountain" (Gen. 19:17)
- [WB]: ONE TYPE → Noah's ARK + Lot's MOUNTAIN = our **CHARACTER + COUNTRY**. The ark today is not wood — it is a settled character and a separated place
- [RQ]: Noah's ark was both a place of safety AND the proof of his faith — what is ours? (Heb. 11:7)
- **The ark is character first, country second** — Christ's character reproduced in us (the very thing He waits for, COL 69.1), lived out in a separated, self-provisioning life
- **Now the why — this is not symbolism, it is coming:**
- The cities will be destroyed: [EGW]: "The time is near when the large cities will be visited by the judgments of God... these cities will be terribly shaken." (CH 268.2; AH 136.5)
- Buying and selling will become a snare: Revelation 13:17 — "no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark"; the Sunday-law decree that no dissenter be tolerated (GC 615.2)
- [EGW]: "The time is fast coming when the controlling power of the labor unions will be very oppressive... in the future the problem of buying and selling will be a very serious one. Get out of the cities into rural districts... where you can raise your own provisions." (CL 9.5)
- [EGW]: "Get out of the cities as soon as possible, and purchase a little piece of land, where you can have a garden." (CL 17.3)
- [Aside]: As in Noah's day, the world is "absorbed in amusements and pleasure seeking" while the last warning sounds — the antediluvian indifference, repeated (AH 524.2)
- **The logic is inescapable**: IF we believe the cities fall, that buying and selling becomes a trap, that wars and trouble are coming — THEN we MUST be outside now, making provision, before the day we cannot
- [RQ]: Noah did not begin building the day the rain fell. If we believe what is coming, when is the ark built? (Heb. 11:7; Prov. 22:3)

## Section 4 — Why Remember Lot's Wife: the Loitering That Hardens (5 min) [*]

- **The sharpest word Christ left on the whole question** — Luke 17:32: "Remember Lot's wife"
- Why her? She did not want to leave — and neither did Lot. Her heart stayed in Sodom though her feet left it (Gen. 19:26)
- But hear the deeper cause: it was **Lot's own loitering that hardened her**
- Lot drifted IN by degrees: pitched his tent toward Sodom (Gen. 13:12), dwelt in Sodom (Gen. 14:12), sat in its gate (Gen. 19:1); his faith "had grown dim" living there (PP 161.1)
- And at the end he "lingered," pleading not to flee to the mountain but to keep the little city of Zoar — bargaining with the command (Gen. 19:16–20)
- [EGW]: "If Lot himself had manifested no hesitancy to obey the angels' warning, but had earnestly fled toward the mountains, without one word of pleading or remonstrance, his wife also would have made her escape. The influence of his example would have saved her... But his hesitancy and delay caused her to lightly regard the divine warning." (PP 161.2 / Hvn 106.2)
- [WB]: Draw the valley and the mountain. Mark where they LINGERED. Had they fled straight up and away, she could not even have SEEN the destruction to look back at it
- **This is the whole lesson**: had Lot moved directly to the mountains, trusting God to keep him, his wife would have been beyond sight of the valley — no city left to turn to, no fire to gaze upon, no chance to look back
- The half-step — out of the city but lingering near it, heart still tied to "the wealth that it had taken years to accumulate" (PP 161.2) — is the deadly place
- [RQ]: Is your delay your own — or is it teaching those who follow you to "lightly regard the warning"? (PP 161.2; 1 Cor. 11:1)

## Section 5 — What We Actually Refused: Righteousness by Faith Is the Ark (4 min)

- Step back: what message embodies coming out, calling on the Name, building the ark, not looking back? It is one message with two faces
- Revelation 14:12 — the third angel ends in a people who keep "the commandments of God, AND the faith of Jesus" — law and gospel fused
- That fusion is **righteousness by faith** — what salvation really is: not pardon only, but Christ's own character reproduced in us (the very thing of COL 69.1)
- [EGW]: "Several have written to me, inquiring if the message of justification by faith is the third angel's message, and I have answered, 'It is the third angel's message, in verity.'" (1SM 372.2)
- [EB]: Jeremiah 23:6 (THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS — the righteousness is a Person received, not a record kept)
- This is where it ties to the ground: a faith that truly receives Christ OBEYS — it comes out, it separates, it trusts God to provide
- **So the ark of character and the ark of country are ONE faith** — RBF is the inside of the ark, country living is the outside; Noah's ark was both his refuge and the proof of his faith, and so is ours (Heb. 11:7)
- [WB]: RIGHTEOUSNESS BY FAITH (the character) → (the same faith, lived out) → COME OUT, MAKE PROVISION (the country). One ark, two faces
- The country-living call was exactly Noah's ark and Lot's flight for our day — leave the marked stream of buying and selling, depend on God, raise your own provision
- And like Lot, the flight is TO THE MOUNTAINS — the same move the Sethites made (PP 81), the same the angels commanded Lot (Gen. 19:17), spelled out for OUR day:
- [EGW]: "It will then be time to leave the large cities, preparatory to leaving the smaller ones for retired homes in secluded places among the mountains... instead of seeking expensive dwellings here, we should be preparing to move to a better country, even a heavenly." (CL 32.1)
- [RQ]: The Sunday-law decree is named as the signal to flee (CL 32.1) — but Noah did not start the ark when the rain fell. If THEN is too late, what is the preparation now? (Rev. 13:17; Heb. 11:7)
- Like Laodicea, we said "I am rich, and increased with goods, and have need of nothing" — and would not pay the price of separation

## FINAL PEAK SECTION — This Is What We Rejected (4 min)

- Now the question is answered. What is Christ waiting for? **For a people who will build the ark and come out.**
- One line runs through it all — the wilderness generation, Noah's world, Lot lingering, his wife looking back, and us: each heard the call to separate, and would not go
- We were told the cities would fall, that buying and selling would become a snare, that we must get out and make provision now (CL 9.5; CH 268.2) — and like Lot we lingered, like his wife our heart stayed in Sodom
- We rejected the country-living message; and in rejecting it we rejected what it EMBODIED — the righteousness by faith that is the third angel in verity, the very character of Christ He waits to see (1SM 372.2; COL 69.1)
- Revelation 14:12 — the harvest ripens only in a people who keep the commandments AND the faith of Jesus; until then, He waits
- [RQ]: If Christ is waiting for His character to appear in you — and the ark of character and country is how that faith is made visible — what is your answer tonight? (Rev. 18:4)
- Luke 17:32 — "Remember Lot's wife." Flee to the mountain; do not linger, and do not look back.

### Call to Action — Eternal Decision

- **Come out** — answer the call your fathers refused; separate fully, body and heart, from the doomed city (Rev. 18:4; 2 Cor. 6:17)
- **Receive the righteousness of Christ by faith** — not His pardon only, but His character formed in you (Rev. 14:12; Gal. 2:20)
- **Do not look back** — no hand on the plough turning back is fit for the kingdom (Luke 9:62; Luke 17:32)
- **Stop charging the delay to God** — own it, and be the people for whom He need wait no longer (Ev 696.3; COL 69.1)
