# CONTEXT — "What is Truth?" Evangelistic Series

Glossary of resolved series vocabulary. (Series-scoped context; the repo root has no
CONTEXT.md and this domain is specific to this series, so the glossary lives here.)

## Terms

**The Series** — A 3-night evangelistic series for a non-Christian audience whose
single goal is to show the divine hand behind Scripture. Night 1: _Beyond Opinion_.
Night 2: _Just Another Book?_ Night 3: _Why Suffering?_

**The Criteria Spec** — The five qualities any candidate for absolute moral truth
must have, established night 1 and scored across the series:

1. _Consistent written record_ — unchanging, universally accessible, internally consistent.
2. _Above human origins_ — not subject to human limitation, bias, or cultural drift.
3. _Practical_ — livable, real-world principles with proven results.
4. _Universal_ — applies to all people, all cultures, all times.
5. _Benevolent_ — seeks **your** good, not the author's gain — and it must actually
   **fix our problems**, not merely diagnose them.

**The Wager** — Night 1's climax question: "If a source existed that met all five
criteria — above human origin, benevolent, proven — would you follow it?" Asked
_before_ the full proof exists, so the audience commits to a standard of evidence,
not to a religion. Nights 2-3 then deliver the proof.

**The Verdict Deferral** — Criterion 5 (benevolent / fixes our problems) is
deliberately left only _preliminarily_ evidenced on night 1 (golden rule, legal
frameworks, literacy, reforms); its full verdict is night 3's job. This is the
structural thread that makes night 3 necessary.

**Series Grammar** — The scientific register the series argues in:
hypothesis → assumptions → testable predictions → historical verification.
Night 2 already uses it explicitly; nights 1 and 3 adopt it.

**The Source Policy** — On-slide citations are Scripture, historians, newspapers,
and archaeology **only**. EGW and pioneers shape arguments and speaker notes but
never appear on a slide. Exception: **Josiah Litch is named** in the 1840 segment —
he is the historical predictor; his published prediction is the evidence itself.

**The 1840 Capstone** — Night 2's closing proof: Litch's 1838 published prediction
(from Rev 9:15 via the day-year principle) that Ottoman independence would fall
August 11, 1840 — fulfilled to the day, attested by hostile and secular witnesses.
Its unique value: the prediction-before-fulfillment gap is verifiable in modern
documents, closing the "written after the fact" escape hatch.

**The GC Demonstration Arc (v3 — re-spined)** — Night 3's spine, rebuilt around a
single thesis: **humanity was created because it was needed — as third-party
witnesses to vindicate God's character in a trial that predates us.** The arc:
created for His glory (to vindicate) → the angelic order before sin → Lucifer the
covering cherub over the law → "perfect _until_ iniquity" → he covers himself, so
"the law must be wrong" → sin as a foreign/unaccountable element → God is love,
proven by free will (that sin _could_ arise is itself proof of granted freedom) →
why love cannot destroy Lucifer immediately ("a seed would be planted") → the
**Two Falls** → the necessity of a case → Deuteronomy's two-or-three witnesses →
humanity created as the jury/witnesses → the tree of knowledge as the prosecutor's
platform → Satan recruits witnesses (the fall) → Satan thinks he has won → the plan
of salvation (redeem humanity AND conclude the case) → why sin/suffering (diverging
lines — the case must mature to be seen) → "affliction shall not rise a second
time" (Nah 1:9) → the cross as bridge (reconnects us to God) AND vindication
(answers the charge before the universe). This v3 carries criteria 3/4/5 — practical,
universal, **benevolent** — to their verdict.

**The Two Falls** — The double casting-down of Satan, each proven from a DIFFERENT
text set so neither overreaches: (1) the **first fall** (original rebellion,
pre-humanity) — Ezekiel 28:15-16 ("cast out... covering cherub"), Isaiah 14:12,
Jude 6, Luke 10:18. (2) the **second casting-down** (at the cross) — Revelation
12:7-12, anchored to "the blood of the Lamb" (v.11), which dates this war/casting
AFTER Calvary, plus John 12:31 ("now shall the prince of this world be cast out,"
spoken days before the cross). Key move: Rev 12's "war in heaven" is the SECOND
event, not the first — the blood-of-the-Lamb anchor is the proof. The first fall
rests on Ezekiel/Isaiah/Jude/Luke.

**The Covering Cherub Chain** — The proof that Lucifer stood over the law of love,
built as a verse chain with a full plain-language visual (the audience has zero
sanctuary background): the earthly sanctuary is an explicit COPY of a heavenly
original (Heb 8:5, Ex 25:40 — "the pattern shewed to thee in the mount") → so its
furniture pictures heavenly realities → the ark held the law/testimony (Ex 25:21,
Deut 10:5) → two cherubim "covered" the mercy seat above it (Ex 25:20, 22) → Lucifer
was "the anointed cherub that covereth" (Eze 28:14) → therefore what he covered/
guarded was the LAW, and the law is love (Rom 13:10). The turn: when he fell "by
reason of his brightness" he moved to cover HIMSELF — so "the law must be wrong."
The earthly-as-shadow-of-heaven point (Heb 8:5) is what licenses reading the ark
as a window into the heavenly throne room.

**Created as Witnesses (the thesis)** — Night 3's central claim, framed to DISARM
the narcissist-deity caricature: humanity was created not as worshippers to flatter
God but as **third-party witnesses** the trial required (Deut 17:6 / 19:15 — a case
is established only by two or three witnesses). "For His glory" is therefore
reframed as "for the vindication of His character under accusation" — glory = a
cleared name, not ego. The "you are on the jury" reveal is held back as a late
payoff (the night opens on the felt problem of evil, not the thesis).

**The Tree as Fair-Trial Provision** — The tree of knowledge reframed from
arbitrary test to the venue justice required: a real trial lets BOTH sides reach
the jury, so the tree is the one place the accuser was granted ACCESS to make his
case to humanity. God provided access and fully warned the jury of the stakes
(Gen 2:17), but compelled nothing — barring the defense would itself be injustice.
The risk was the price of a real trial, not a trap. (Pairs with Gen 2:16 "of every
tree freely eat" — total abundance, one boundary.)

**The Diverging Lines** — Night 3's PRIMARY answer to "why is sin/suffering
permitted to run so long": two lines a single degree apart look identical at the
start and only show their true separation when extended far enough — so evil's real
nature becomes visible only after its principles are fully developed. The case must
MATURE to be judged. Its goal is Nahum 1:9 — "affliction shall not rise up the
second time": God digs the seed out by the root (lets the consequences be fully
seen) so rebellion can never regrow. This is THE answer; harvest / hostile-author /
God-suffers-with-us are secondary support, not co-leads.

**The Cross (vindication-first)** — Night 3's payload does two jobs: VINDICATION
(the accuser is unmasked — he kills the innocent Author — and God's character is
cleared before the universe; the case concludes) and BRIDGE (the same act
reconnects the individual to God). Order: stay in the courtroom, lead with
vindication, then reveal the bridge as the personal payoff. The night ends on "this
same act reconnects YOU." Carries criterion 5 (benevolent) to its verdict — "what
manner of love" (1 John 3:1).

**Night 3 v3 — settled build decisions:** fresh rebuild from the new spine
(salvage strong v2 slides where they fit); thesis = created-as-witnesses (disarms
the narcissist-deity caricature), revealed LATE (open on the felt problem, Epicurus);
criteria 3/4/5 embodied throughout and scored only at the close; covering-cherub a
full plain-language visual build including sanctuary-as-shadow-of-heaven (Heb 8:5);
Two Falls proven from separate text sets; tree = fair-trial provision; diverging
lines as the primary why-suffering answer; cross vindication-first; source policy
unchanged (Scripture on slides, EGW shapes notes only); target ~90-105 slides.

**Whose Standard?** — A second axis of the controversy at the human level: a trial
cannot run on the defendant's self-grading, and a universe cannot be secured on
billions of conflicting private standards ("I've done my best," "I'm a good person,"
"God sees my effort"). There must be ONE standard, and the Bible reveals it — the
law of love (Matt 22:37-40, "on these hang all the law"), the very thing the
covering cherub guarded then attacked. Every human "my own standard is fine" echoes
the first rebellion ("the law must be wrong"). Texts: Prov 14:12 / 21:2 (the way
that _seems_ right; right in his own eyes) → the standard named (Matt 22:37-40) →
all fall short of it (Rom 3:23; Isa 64:6 "filthy rags"; Rom 3:20) → past/stakes/
future (Eccl 12:13-14, "God shall bring every work into judgment") → rescue is
grace not achievement (Titus 3:5). PLACEMENT: held until AFTER the cross has
redefined the standard as love itself, so the insufficiency lands as good news
("none of us meets it — which is exactly why the bridge exists"), not condemnation.
This is the hinge between vindication (God cleared by HIS standard) and the bridge
(we're measured by that same standard, can't meet it, so the cross is grace).

**The Ask** — Night 3's appeal: a personal Bible-study invitation (the formal
version of night 2's "come talk to me after"), plus a zero-pressure "start reading
the Gospel of John" challenge for those not ready for contact.

survives untouched. Deck 3 is built fresh (seven-trumpets pipeline).
