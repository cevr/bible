# "What is Truth?" — Speaker One-Pagers

Podium reference, one page per night. Print double-sided or as three cards.
Times are targets; the bracketed deck cues are the slides to land hard.
Source policy: **Scripture / historians / newspapers / archaeology only on slides.**

# NIGHT 1 — _Beyond Opinion_ (≈78 slides, ~45 min)

**One-line goal:** Truth is either relative or absolute; relativism self-destructs; so a real standard must come from _outside_ us — and here are the five tests it must pass. End on the wager, before any proof.

**Central move:** hypothesis → assumptions → testable criteria. Don't prove the Bible tonight; _qualify the candidate_ and get the commitment.

### Key verses / anchors

- John 18:38 — "What is truth?" (Pilate) — the series title.
- The five criteria (verbatim): **1. Consistent written record · 2. Above human origins · 3. Practical · 4. Universal · 5. Benevolent.**
- Criterion 5 has two halves: _seeks your good_ AND _must FIX the problem, not just diagnose it._ **Tell them this gets its own night** — that's the hook into Night 3.

### Do NOT

- Don't prove prophecy tonight (that's Night 2 — the block was deliberately moved out).
- Don't over-claim the New Age stat: it's **62% hold ≥1 New Age belief, Pew 2018** [s74]. Not "most Americans are pagan."
- Don't name EGW/pioneers.

### Hook to Night 2 (say it): _"It claims divine origin. Can a book prove that? There's exactly one test no human author can fake — telling the future, in writing, with dates. That's tomorrow."_

# NIGHT 2 — _Just Another Book?_ (≈109 slides, ~55 min)

**Central move:** the day-year key, applied twice — ancient (Messiah, AD 27) and modern (1840). Same arithmetic, public record.

- Rev 9:13-15 → day-year → **391 years + 15 days**; hour = 15 days [s97-98].
- Clock starts **July 27, 1299** (Gibbon); +150 yr 5th trumpet → **1449** [s99].
- **Josiah Litch, 1838** — published from the prophecy; refined to **Aug 11, 1840**, _in print before the event_ (Signs of the Times, Mar 20, 1840) [s100]. ← **This is the only pioneer you name.**
- Convention of London, **July 15, 1840**, four powers [s101]; ultimatum sails Aug 1840 [s102]; **Aug 11, 1840** — Ottoman sovereignty passes to the four Christian powers [s103].
- Witnesses: **Goodell** (missionary, _Missionary Herald_ Apr 1841) [s104]; **Balch** (hostile — _attacking_ the prediction, conceded the fact) [s105].

### The ONE sentence that survives any fact-check (memorize) [s107]

# NIGHT 3 — _Why Suffering?_ (88 slides, ~73 min)

**One-line goal:** Now hear what the proven-divine Book _says_. Suffering is an **intruder**, not a feature — a cosmic accusation against God's character, answered not by force but by **demonstration** (the cross, then the end). Criterion 5 — _benevolent / fixes our problem_ — gets its verdict, and the wager comes due.

**Central verse:** **Romans 3:4** — "…that thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged." _God consents to be judged, and wins by evidence — not by silencing the court._

### Verses to land hard

- **1 John 4:8** (the axiom) · **Rev 12:7-9** (explicit anchor — name "the Devil, and Satan" without caveat) · **Rom 3:4** (central) · **Isa 53:5-6** (scroll ~125 BC predates the cross — _valid here, unlike Cyrus_) · **John 11:35** ("Jesus wept" — let it breathe) · **Nah 1:9** · **Rev 21:4** (the payoff verse) · **Acts 17:11** (Bereans — "check me") · **Ps 34:8** (close).

### Three handling rules (don't skip)

1. **Job 1:21 + Job 2:7 — always paired.** Job credits God in grief; the narration says _Satan_ struck. Pair them or you teach the opposite of the thesis.
2. **Epicurus** — say the attribution caveat (survives only via Lactantius; authorship debated). The challenge stands; your honesty protects you.
3. **Worldviews (§2)** — score atheism/Buddhism/Hinduism/Islam/New Age _fairly_, same two-question scale. **No mockery, no "vibrations" laugh line** — seekers are in the room.
