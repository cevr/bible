## SECTION MANIFEST (~96 slides)

### §1 — The Hook: the question (8) [open on the problem, NOT the thesis]

Epicurus riddle (salvage s6-7, keep attribution caveat) → the two questions every
worldview must answer → "a different shape of answer" (salvage s15) → but to answer
it we have to go back _before_ us. Plant: "the answer starts before humanity existed."

### §2 — Before Us: the angelic order before sin (6)

There was a created order before humanity (Job 38:4-7 — "morning stars sang… sons of
God shouted for joy," angels present at creation; Col 1:16 — thrones, dominions,
principalities created). Peace and harmony, no sin (baseline). The highest of these:
Lucifer. Eze 28:12-15 "perfect… _till_ iniquity." Sin began in the HIGHEST, perfectly
made being — not a design flaw.

### §3 — The Covering Cherub & the Law (10) [FULL VISUAL BUILD]

Eze 28:14 "the anointed cherub that covereth" → what is a "covering cherub"? →
Israel's sanctuary was a COPY of heaven (Heb 8:5, Ex 25:40 "the pattern shewed thee
in the mount") → [IMAGE: sanctuary] → zoom to the ark [IMAGE: ark] → two cherubim
covered the mercy seat (Ex 25:20, 22) → inside the ark: the law/testimony (Ex 25:21,
Deut 10:5) → so the cherub stood guarding the LAW → and the law is love (Rom 13:10,
Matt 22:37-40) → **Lucifer covered the law of love.**

### §4 — God Is Love, Proven by Free Will (7)

1 John 4:8 "God is love." The proof of love = free will (PP 34.3 notes; Josh 24:15 on
slide; salvage s18 "love cannot be compelled"). **The hard turn:** that sin _could_
arise at all is itself proof God grants real freedom — a universe where rebellion is
impossible is a universe without love. Sin was a _foreign, unknown element_ (Gen 3 as
the first reaction; nothing in creation had seen it).

### §5 — The Cover-Up: "the law must be wrong" (6)

By reason of his brightness (Eze 28:17) he fell → first instinct of sin = cover
yourself (Gen 3:7-10, Adam & Eve hide — read as the universal first reaction) → the
covering cherub now covers HIMSELF → to justify self, the standard must be the
problem: **"the law must be wrong."** The accusation is born (PP 41.3 method notes —
projection, manufactured doubt).

### §6 — How Does Love Respond? (6)

New element, no precedent. If God destroys Lucifer the instant sin appears — what does
the watching universe conclude? A seed is planted ("maybe the tyrant feared the
truth"). If God destroys him once rebellion is open — same seed ("was God unjust?").
GC 498.3 notes (LOAD-BEARING): force produces fear-service, leaves the doubt alive.
**Love cannot answer an accusation with force.**

### §7 — The Two Falls (8)

Setup: people think Satan fell once. The Bible shows two castings-down.

- **First fall** (original rebellion, pre-humanity): Eze 28:16 "cast out… covering
  cherub" + Isa 14:12 "fallen from heaven" + Jude 6 "left their first estate" + Luke
  10:18 "I beheld Satan as lightning fall."
- **Second casting-down** (at the cross): Rev 12:7-12 — read the sequence (child caught
  up to God v5 = ascension → war → cast out → "they overcame him by **the blood of the
  Lamb**" v11) → the blood-of-the-Lamb anchor DATES this AFTER Calvary → + John 12:31
  "_now_ shall the prince of this world be cast out" (spoken days before the cross).
- The turn: Rev 12's war is the SECOND event, not the first. (This sets up that the
  cross is when the case is _decided_.)

### §8 — The Necessity of a Case (8) [thesis reveal lands here]

Once hardened, Satan won't recant → the charge stands unanswered → a charge can't be
settled by force, only disproven before witnesses. Deut 19:15 / 17:6: a case is
established only by **two or three witnesses**. Heaven's angels are parties to the
dispute — you need a THIRD party. **REVEAL:** humanity was created _because it was
needed_ — impartial witnesses, made after the conflict, given the facts, to help the
universe see the case for itself. _You were made for the jury._ (Reframe "glory" =
vindicated character, here.) 1 Cor 4:9 "a spectacle… to angels."

### §9 — The Tree & the Fall: the prosecutor gets his platform (9)

Justice requires BOTH sides reach the jury. The tree of knowledge = the one venue the
accuser was granted ACCESS to make his case (Gen 2:9, 16-17 — "of EVERY tree freely
eat," one boundary, stakes fully disclosed). Not a trap — a fair trial. The prosecutor's
best move: recruit witnesses, gain a base of operations. Gen 3:1-6 — the same lie ("ye
shall be as gods," God is holding out) → humanity believes the accuser → dominion
changes hands (Luke 4:6; salvage s46-47 "the wages / whose world is this").

### §10 — Satan Thinks He Has Won (4)

The fall looks like the prosecutor's victory — he has a kingdom, witnesses, a base
(2 Cor 4:4 "god of this world"). But he did not know the depths of the Father's and
the Son's love. Pivot to the plan.

### §11 — Why Sin & Suffering Is Permitted to Run (10)

**THE answer — diverging lines:** [IMAGE: two lines 1° apart, near vs far]. Evil's true
nature is invisible at the start; only when its principles are fully extended does the
divergence show. The case must MATURE to be judged (GC 499.1 notes — history is the
evidence; Rom 8:22 creation groaning on slide). **Why not destroy Satan immediately?**
Affliction shall not rise up the second time (Nah 1:9) — God digs the seed out by the
root so rebellion never regrows; destroying without proving only replants the seed
(GC 504.1 notes — same act means opposite things at the start vs. the end).
_Secondary support_ (brief): God doesn't watch from outside — Isa 63:9 (salvage s54),
"Jesus wept" (salvage s55); the delay is a rescue window (salvage s69, 2 Pet 3:9).

### §12 — The Cross: Vindication First (11)

The plan of salvation does two things: redeem humanity AND conclude the case. Stay in
the courtroom. The prosecutor's sharpest charge: justice and mercy can't coexist —
pick one (salvage s58). God did not waive the law — He absorbed it (salvage s61;
2 Cor 5:19). At the cross the accuser, given a free hand, **tortures and kills the
innocent Author** — his own work condemns him; the deceiver is unmasked before the
universe (Col 2:15 "made a shew of them openly"; GC 498.2 notes). The charge "God is
unjust / the law is bondage" collapses — the Lawgiver pays the law's own penalty.
**This is the verdict the whole trial was for.** (Isa 53:5-6 — keep the scroll-
predates-cross point, DROP "~7 centuries early.")

### §13 — Whose Standard? (7) [hinge: vindication → bridge]

God's character was just vindicated by HIS standard — love, demonstrated. But we run
on private standards: "I've done my best," "I'm a good person," "God sees my effort"
(Prov 14:12 "a way which _seems_ right"; Prov 21:2 "right in his own eyes"). A trial
can't run on the defendant's self-grading; a universe can't rest on billions of
conflicting standards. There is ONE standard — the law of love (Matt 22:37-40, the
same law the cherub guarded). And **none of us meets it** (Rom 3:23; Isa 64:6 "filthy
rags"; Rom 3:20). The future is real (Eccl 12:13-14, "God shall bring every work into
judgment"). **The good news:** that's exactly why the bridge exists — rescue is grace,
not achievement (Titus 3:5). Every human "my standard is fine" was the first rebellion
in miniature ("the law must be wrong") — and the cross answers it.

### §14 — The Bridge & How It Ends (7)

The same act that vindicated God reconnects YOU (1 John 3:1 "what manner of love";
the bridge — [IMAGE: the gulf closed]). A transparent verdict (salvage s76, Rev 20:12).
No more curse, Eden restored, affliction gone for good (salvage s78, Rev 21:4; Rev
22:3). The universe — and you — secured by demonstration, not force.

### §15 — The Verdict & Your Move (6)

The scorecard, scored ONCE: criterion 3 practical ✓, 4 universal ✓ (one controversy,
every culture, every heart), 5 benevolent ✓ (it cost the Author everything — what
manner of love). Three nights ago you took a wager; the case is in — **you judge**
(rebuild the close; NO "you said yes"). He knocks, does not break in (salvage s87,
Rev 3:20). The ask (study with me / read John). Acts 17:11 (check me) → Ps 34:8.
