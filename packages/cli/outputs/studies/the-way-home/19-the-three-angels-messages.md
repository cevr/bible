# Study 19: The Three Angels' Messages — Heaven's Last Warning

**Revelation 10's angel had just sworn that time should be no longer and commissioned John, "Thou must prophesy again." Turn the page, and there the renewed proclamation stands: three angels flying in the midst of heaven, each with a loud voice, each carrying one message to the whole earth. This is not a private sermon to one congregation — it is a literal, datable, worldwide commission God set going from 1844 onward: the everlasting gospel preached in the very hour of judgment, a judgment-hour call back to the Creator's seventh-day Sabbath, the announcement that Babylon is fallen, and the most fearful threat in all Scripture against the beast's mark. Three messages, one banner — and under that banner the rest of this series marches.**

## First — Where We Stand on the Map

**Before we read a single angel, fix the place. The previous studies have already laid the ground these three messages stand on; without it, they hang in the air. With it, they fall like dominoes.**

- **The timeline has already landed on 1844 (Studies 13-18)**
  - The same year-day rule that closed the sixth trumpet on August 11, 1840 (Study 13) reckons the 2300 days of Daniel 8:14 to the autumn of 1844 (Study 14). At that terminus the heavenly sanctuary's cleansing — the antitypical Day of Atonement, the pre-advent judgment — began (Study 18).
  - "Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed" (Dan 8:14)
  - [STR] Hold that date in your hand. Revelation 14's first angel says "the hour of his judgment IS come" — present tense, a fact about a particular hour. Unless an hour of judgment actually arrives somewhere on the calendar, the first angel is preaching a date that never comes. Study 18 supplied the date; Study 19 hears the angel announce it to the world.

- **The vocabulary of the third angel is already in hand (Rev 12-13, Studies 20-21 unpack it fully)**
  - The third angel warns against "the beast and his image" and "his mark" (Rev 14:9). Those terms are not coined here — they belong to the two beasts of Revelation 13, and they get their full identification in the studies on Revelation 12, 13, and 17. Here we read the WARNING; there we read the WANTED-poster.
  - [→] So Revelation 14 sits at the hinge of the whole book: behind it lies the judgment (ch. 7-9, dated to 1844); ahead of it lie the beast, the mark, Babylon, and the plagues. The three angels are heaven's official announcement that the judgment has begun and the final test is being set.

- **The book itself tells you these angels are a message, not literal winged beings**
  - The "angel" of each of the seven churches was its human messenger — its ministry (Rev 1:20, established in Study 10). The same book uses the same word the same way here.
  - [STR] An angel "flying in the midst of heaven... to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people" (Rev 14:6) is the Bible's own picture of a worldwide PROCLAMATION — the gospel given to men to carry, lifted up where all can see it. Three angels = three successive movements of teachers, each bearing a special message, sounding in order across the earth. We are not told to wait for a literal flight; we are told to listen for a message and join the proclaiming.

[DYK🔎] The Greek word here is _angelos_ — "messenger." In Revelation 14:6 the message is named outright: "having the everlasting gospel to preach." A being is described by what he CARRIES. The everlasting gospel does not get preached by inaccessible spirits in the sky; "how shall they preach, except they be sent?" (Rom 10:15). The angel is the symbol; the church carrying the message is the substance — exactly as the candlestick was the church (Rev 1:20). This is why the same body of believers can be commissioned, "Thou must prophesy again" (Rev 10:11), and then SEEN as three angels: the command and the symbol are the same proclamation, viewed from two sides.

## The First Angel — The Everlasting Gospel in the Judgment Hour

**Read the first message in full, then take it apart clause by clause. Every phrase is loaded, and each one is anchored somewhere else in Scripture.**

- **The whole message — one verse and a half**
  - "And I saw another angel fly in the midst of heaven, having the everlasting gospel to preach unto them that dwell on the earth, and to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people" (Rev 14:6)
  - "Saying with a loud voice, Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come: and worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters" (Rev 14:7)

- **It is the EVERLASTING gospel — the same gospel from Eden, not a new one**
  - The gospel preached here is "everlasting." It is not a different message for the last days; it is the one and only gospel, as old as the fall.
  - The first promise of a Saviour was given in Eden: "I will put enmity... it shall bruise thy head" (Gen 3:15) — the Seed who crushes the serpent, the gospel in seed-form.
  - That same gospel was preached to Abraham: "the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham" (Gal 3:8).
  - And it was preached to Israel in the wilderness: "unto us was the gospel preached, as well as unto them" (Heb 4:2).
  - [→] So the first angel does not invent a doctrine; it lifts up the ancient gospel — salvation by faith in the crucified Seed — and preaches it in the judgment hour. The judgment does not replace the gospel; it is the setting in which the gospel is proclaimed with new urgency.

- **"Fear God, and give glory to him" — the everlasting gospel summarized as Scripture summarizes it**
  - When the whole Bible is gathered to one conclusion, it lands on the same two words: "Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man. For God shall bring every work into judgment" (Eccl 12:13-14).
  - [STR] Notice that Ecclesiastes ties "fear God" directly to JUDGMENT — "for God shall bring every work into judgment." The first angel does the very same: "Fear God... for the hour of his judgment is come." The angel is preaching nothing other than the Bible's own summary of human duty, now that the judgment has actually begun.

- **"The hour of his judgment IS come" — a present fact, true only after 1844**
  - This is the clause that DATES the message. Not "judgment is coming someday" — "the hour of his judgment IS come," a particular hour that has arrived.
  - The judgment scene was shown in advance to Daniel: "the Ancient of days did sit... the judgment was set, and the books were opened" (Dan 7:9-10). That sitting court is the hour the angel announces.
  - That same vision dates when the court convenes: it follows the 2300 days, "then shall the sanctuary be cleansed" (Dan 8:14) — 1844 (Studies 14, 18).
  - [→] So the first angel could not have been TRUE before 1844. No one could rightly cry "the hour of his judgment is come" while the 2300 days were still running. The message is welded to a date: it becomes a present, literal fact at the precise moment the longest time-prophecy expires. This is why the message arose exactly when it did, and not a century earlier.

[Q] **"Doesn't the gospel proclaim mercy, not judgment? Why frame the everlasting gospel as a judgment-hour message?"**
→ Because in Scripture the two are never opposites. The same God who "by no means clear the guilty" is "the just, and the justifier of him which believeth in Jesus" (the cross, Study 4). Judgment is not the enemy of the gospel — it is the gospel's vindication: the moment the cases are reviewed and every name written in the Lamb's book is confirmed and cleared. For the believer "there is therefore now no condemnation" (Rom 8:1); the judgment is FOR the saints (Dan 7:22). The first angel does not preach "tremble, you are doomed." It preaches "the hour has come when God settles every case — so fear Him, give Him glory, take refuge in the everlasting gospel NOW." The urgency comes from the date; the safety comes from the cross.

## The First Angel's Call to Worship — and Why It Names the Sabbath

**The first message does not end with "the hour of his judgment is come." It immediately tells the world WHOM to worship — and it does so in words lifted straight out of the fourth commandment. This is the most important single observation in the study: the judgment-hour gospel restores the Creator's Sabbath, by quoting it.**

- **The call: worship the Creator, named by His creation**
  - "and worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters" (Rev 14:7)
  - The ground of worship the angel names is not redemption only, not providence only — it is CREATION. We are to worship Him because He MADE heaven and earth.

- **Now lay the fourth commandment beside it — the wording is the same**
  - "Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy... For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is, and rested the seventh day: wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it" (Ex 20:8,11)
  - [STR] Set the two texts side by side:
    - Rev 14:7 — "worship him that **made heaven, and earth, and the sea**, and the fountains of waters."
    - Ex 20:11 — "the LORD **made heaven and earth, the sea**, and all that in them is... wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day."
  - The first angel is not alluding vaguely to "God the Creator." It is quoting the reason-clause of the fourth commandment almost word for word. Of all ten commandments, ONLY the Sabbath commandment grounds worship in God's making of heaven, earth, and sea. The angel reaches into the law and pulls out exactly that one commandment — the only one that points to the Creator by His creative work.

- **The Sabbath was given as the sign of the Creator and Sanctifier**
  - "Moreover also I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them" (Eze 20:12)
  - "hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign between me and you, that ye may know that I am the LORD your God" (Eze 20:20)
  - "the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God" (Ex 20:10) — a literal day, a literal rest, the literal seal of the literal Creator.
  - [→] God Himself names the Sabbath the SIGN that distinguishes the true worship of the Creator from every counterfeit. So a final message commissioned to call the world back to worshiping the Creator MUST lift up the Sabbath — not as a "trust-rest attitude" disconnected from the day, but as the literal seventh-day rest God blessed and hallowed at creation (Gen 2:1-3). The literal day is the thing the literal commandment commands.

- **Why creation is the only sufficient ground of worship**
  - Heaven's own anthem grounds worship in creation: "Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honour and power: for thou hast created all things, and for thy pleasure they are and were created" (Rev 4:11).
  - The reason a creature owes the Creator everything is simply that the Creator MADE him. "It is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves" — that is the deepest ground of all worship; and the test that exposes a false god is the same: "the LORD made the heavens" (Ps 96:5). The idols did not.
  - [STR] So the first angel's logic is airtight: the hour of judgment has come → therefore worship the Judge → and the Judge is worthy because He is the CREATOR → and the memorial He set up to keep that fact before the human race is the seventh-day Sabbath. Restore worship to the Creator and you cannot help restoring His Sabbath; the message and the day are one.

[DYK🔎] The clearest preaching of "the living God, which made heaven, and earth, and the sea" in the New Testament is on the lips of Paul and Barnabas at Lystra, turning idolaters from their vanities (Acts 14:15) — and it is exactly the phrasing the first angel uses (Rev 14:7). The everlasting gospel has always called men FROM the made things TO the Maker. In the last days that call becomes the dividing line, because the world will be asked to worship a power that "thinks to change times and laws" (Dan 7:25, Study 8) — and the only command joining a time and a law is the fourth. To call men back to the Creator's Sabbath is to undo the very change the little horn attempted.

[Q] **"Isn't this just reading the Sabbath into a verse that only says 'worship the Creator'? Where is the word 'Sabbath' in Revelation 14?"**
→ The word "Sabbath" is not there — but the SENTENCE is. Compare spiritual with spiritual (1 Cor 2:13, the method of Study 0). Revelation 14:7 quotes the operative clause of Exodus 20:11; the two read almost identically, and Exodus 20:11 is the reason God gives for the Sabbath. We are not importing a hidden meaning — we are letting Scripture's own cross-reference do the identifying. The angel did not have to spell "S-a-b-b-a-t-h"; he quoted the commandment that contains it, the way one quotes "love thy neighbour" and everyone knows which command is meant. This is identification by the text's own words, not allegory laid over the text.

## The Second Angel — "Babylon Is Fallen"

**The first angel calls the world to worship the Creator. The instant that call goes out, a counter-system is exposed — the religious power that demands a different worship. The second angel announces its fall.**

- **The message**
  - "And there followed another angel, saying, Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication" (Rev 14:8)

- **"Babylon is fallen, is fallen" — Scripture's own announcement, reused**
  - This very cry is borrowed from the Old Testament's announcement over literal Babylon: "Babylon is fallen, is fallen; and all the graven images of her gods he hath broken unto the ground" (Isa 21:9).
  - [→] So Revelation takes the historical fall of literal Babylon — the city that held God's people captive (the exile of the Way Home story) — and makes it the figure of a religious system that holds souls captive at the end. The figure is the Bible's own: spiritual Babylon, named on her own forehead, "MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS" (Rev 17:5, unpacked in Study 22).

- **"The wine of her fornication" — false doctrine that intoxicates the nations**
  - Babylon "made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication" (Rev 14:8). The same figure runs through Scripture: "Babylon hath been a golden cup in the LORD'S hand, that made all the earth drunken... therefore the nations are mad" (Jer 51:7).
  - [→] Wine that makes nations "mad" and "drunken" is a Bible figure for doctrine that intoxicates — the false teaching she pours out: a counterfeit priesthood, a counterfeit salvation, a counterfeit day of worship, a false view of the dead. To drink her wine is to be drugged out of clear judgment. Her "fornication" is the unlawful union of the apostate church with the kings of the earth (the woman riding the beast, Rev 17, Study 22).

- **A "fall" that is moral, not yet physical — and announced before it is complete**
  - The angel says Babylon "IS fallen" — a moral fall, a fall in character, the moment a church that once held truth rejects the present message and the Spirit of God. The PHYSICAL destruction of Babylon comes later, in the seventh plague: "great Babylon came in remembrance before God, to give unto her the cup of the wine of the fierceness of his wrath" (Rev 16:19).
  - [STR] So the second angel's "is fallen" is the announcement of a MORAL collapse already underway, which deepens and completes over time — and which sets up the urgent call of Revelation 18:4, "Come out of her, my people" (Study 23). The fall is declared so that God's people, still inside, will hear and leave before the plagues land.

[DYK🔎] Why does the second angel follow so closely on the first? Because the moment a body of believers is called to worship the Creator on His own Sabbath, the religious power that teaches a different day and a different worship is, by that very contrast, shown to have fallen from the truth. The first angel lifts up the standard; the second names what fell short of it. You cannot raise the Creator's Sabbath without simultaneously exposing the system that changed it. The two messages are cause and effect.

## The Third Angel — The Most Fearful Threat in Scripture

**The first angel restores true worship. The second exposes the false system. The third draws the line between them and pronounces the most solemn warning God has ever uttered. This is the climax of the whole chapter.**

- **The message — read it whole**
  - "And the third angel followed them, saying with a loud voice, If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark in his forehead, or in his hand, The same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation; and he shall be tormented with fire and brimstone in the presence of the holy angels, and in the presence of the Lamb: And the smoke of their torment ascendeth up for ever and ever: and they have no rest day nor night, who worship the beast and his image, and whosoever receiveth the mark of his name" (Rev 14:9-11)

- **It is the only threat in the Bible of God's wrath WITHOUT mixture**
  - Everywhere else, God's wrath is mingled with mercy, restrained, mixed with grace. Here, to worship the beast is to "drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out WITHOUT MIXTURE" — undiluted wrath, mercy withdrawn.
  - [STR] Note the answering symmetry: Babylon made the nations drink "the wine of... her fornication" (v. 8); whoever takes her mark must drink "the wine of the wrath of God" (v. 10). Drink her cup willingly now, and you will drink His cup at the end. There is no third cup. Everyone drinks one or the other.

- **The warning is against a WORSHIP — the mark is the badge of allegiance**
  - The threat falls on those who "worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark." The whole controversy is over WORSHIP — exactly the issue the first angel raised. The mark is received "in his forehead, or in his hand" (Rev 14:9; cf. Rev 13:16) — in the forehead (the mind, conviction) or in the hand (the act, compliance).
  - The beast "causeth all... to receive a mark... that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark" (Rev 13:16-17) — economic coercion enforcing a false worship (the full identification and the Sunday-law application are Studies 21 and 28).
  - [→] So the third angel is the dark twin of the first. The first calls: worship the CREATOR (whose sign is His Sabbath). The third warns: do not worship the BEAST (whose mark is the counterfeit). One forehead bears "his Father's name written" (Rev 14:1); the other bears "the mark of his name" (Rev 14:11). Every human being ends with one mark or the other. There is no neutral ground in the last crisis.

[Q] **"Doesn't 'tormented for ever and ever' (Rev 14:11) prove eternal conscious torment — the very immortal-soul error this series rejects?"**
→ No — and the phrase must be read by Scripture's own usage, not by importing a Greek-philosophy meaning (the warning of Study 0). "For ever" (Heb. _olam_, Gk. _aiōn_) regularly means "as long as the thing lasts" — Jonah was in the fish's belly "for ever" (Jonah 2:6), a servant served "for ever" (Ex 21:6, i.e. for life). The "smoke" that "ascendeth up for ever" is a fixed Bible figure for a destruction whose RESULT is permanent: Edom's land burns and "the smoke thereof shall go up for ever" (Isa 34:10) — Edom is not still burning today; the smoke testifies that the destruction was final and irreversible. So the third angel threatens a real, literal, final destruction in the lake of fire (Rev 20:9) whose effects are eternal — not an endless torturing of immortal souls. The doctrine of the dead is established fully in Study 27; here it is enough to read "for ever" as Scripture reads it, and refuse to break Scripture against itself (John 10:35). The threat is no less fearful for being final; it is the SECOND death, and it is forever.

## The Dividing Line — Revelation 14:12

**Immediately after the third angel's threat comes the single verse that describes the people the three messages produce. This is the verse the whole Way Home series has been driving toward — the answer to both of Satan's original charges.**

- **The verse**
  - "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (Rev 14:12)

- **It draws the final line of the whole controversy**
  - Over against those who worship the beast and take his mark (v. 9-11) stand "they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." The whole human race is sorted into two companies by this one verse: those marked by the beast, and those who keep God's commandments and Jesus' faith.
  - This is the same remnant the dragon hates: "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ" (Rev 12:17, Study 20). The three angels' message PRODUCES exactly the people Revelation 12 said the dragon would make war with.

- **The two halves of the verse answer the two charges of the cosmic trial (Study 1)**
  - Recall the charges Satan filed:
    - **Charge A — "they only obey for reward":** "Doth Job fear God for nought?" (Job 1:9). The accusation that no creature loves God for Himself; obedience is bought.
    - **Charge B — "the law cannot be kept":** "the carnal mind is enmity against God: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be" (Rom 8:7). The accusation that God's law is impossible, His standard unjust.
  - [STR] Now watch Revelation 14:12 answer both, in order:
    - **"They that keep the commandments of God"** — a people who DO keep the law, in the face of a death-decree that offers them everything for breaking it and threatens everything for keeping it (Rev 13:15-17). They obey when obedience costs them their lives and pays them nothing on earth. That silences Charge A — they plainly do NOT obey for reward; they obey for love, under loss. And it silences Charge B — the law manifestly CAN be kept, for here is a whole company keeping it.
    - **"And the faith of Jesus"** — and they do it not by their own strength but by the faith OF Jesus, the same dependence by which He kept the law in fallen flesh (Study 4). Not law without grace, not grace without law — the two joined in one people.
  - [→] So the last verse of the three angels' message is the verdict-exhibit of the entire cosmic trial. The great controversy that opened in Study 1 with two charges closes its evidence here: a people, in the final crisis, keeping God's commandments by the faith of Jesus, under penalty of death and with no earthly reward — God's living answer to Satan, written in human lives. This is why the three angels' message is "Heaven's last warning": it is also Heaven's last EXHIBIT.

[DYK🔎] Verse 12 calls this "the PATIENCE of the saints" — the same word (_hupomonē_, "patient endurance") that earlier described the saints under the beast's persecution (Rev 13:10). The commandment-keeping of Revelation 14:12 is not comfortable, casual obedience; it is endurance under fire. The people the three angels produce are tested people — which is exactly what an exhibit in a trial must be: evidence that has been examined under pressure and held.

## The Harvest — Where the Three Messages End (Rev 14:14-16)

**The chapter does not stop at verse 12. It carries the three messages straight to their result: the literal second coming, pictured as a harvest. The proclamation has an end-point, and the text names it.**

- **The reaper on the white cloud**
  - "And I looked, and behold a white cloud, and upon the cloud one sat like unto the Son of man, having on his head a golden crown, and in his hand a sharp sickle" (Rev 14:14)
  - "And another angel came out of the temple, crying with a loud voice to him that sat on the cloud, Thrust in thy sickle, and reap: for the time is come for thee to reap; for the harvest of the earth is ripe. And he that sat on the cloud thrust in his sickle on the earth; and the earth was reaped" (Rev 14:15-16)

- **The harvest is Christ's OWN figure for the end of the world**
  - We do not have to guess what the harvest means; Christ defined it: "the harvest is the end of the world; and the reapers are the angels" (Matt 13:39). "In the time of harvest I will say to the reapers, Gather... the wheat into my barn" (Matt 13:30).
  - The same sickle-and-ripeness language was already prophetic shorthand for the day of the Lord: "Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe" (Joel 3:13).
  - [→] So the One "like unto the Son of man" on the white cloud, crowned, with a sharp sickle, is the literal returning Christ (the second coming, Study 31) — and the reaping is the literal gathering of the saved at His coming. Author-marked typology: the harvest is not a vague spiritual ripening; it is the literal end of the world, named by Christ Himself.

- **Why this caps the three angels' message exactly**
  - The three messages are preached "to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people" (Rev 14:6) — and Christ said the harvest waits on exactly that: "this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and THEN shall the end come" (Matt 24:14).
  - [STR] So Revelation 14 lays out the whole final program on one page: three messages go to all the world (v. 6-12) → the warning closes, the field is ripe → the Son of man reaps (v. 14-16). The three angels are the worldwide proclamation that ripens the harvest; the white-cloud reaping is the harvest itself. The message has a deadline and a destination — and they are the literal return of Christ.

[Q] **"If 'the hour of his judgment is come' was already true in 1844, why hasn't the harvest happened yet — why the long delay?"**
→ Because the judgment hour and the harvest are two distinct events in the right order: first the cases are reviewed in the heavenly court (the pre-advent judgment that began in 1844, Study 18), THEN the verdict is executed at the coming ("his reward is with him," Rev 22:12). The three angels' message is precisely the work of that interval — the worldwide warning that runs between the opening of the court and the thrusting-in of the sickle. The delay is not failure; it is the time God gives for the message to reach "every nation" (Matt 24:14) before the field is reaped. The same patience that wrote the tarrying into the Midnight Cry (Study 17) writes it here: the harvest waits for the message to finish its rounds.

## Three Messages, One Order, One Banner

**Step back and see the whole. The three angels are not three disconnected slogans; they are one rising message, sounding in a fixed sequence, building to the final test — and they are the banner this very series marches under.**

- **They sound in a fixed order, each "following" the last**
  - "There FOLLOWED another angel" (v. 8); "the third angel FOLLOWED them" (v. 9). The text fixes the sequence: first, then second, then third — a building proclamation, not a simultaneous shout.
  - [STR] And the order is logical, not arbitrary:
    - **First:** lift up the everlasting gospel and the Creator's worship in the judgment hour (the positive standard — worship the Maker, keep His Sabbath).
    - **Second:** announce that the rival system has fallen (the negative — Babylon, that taught a false worship, is fallen).
    - **Third:** draw the line and warn of the consequence (the test — do not take the beast's mark; the wrath is without mixture).
  - You cannot warn against the counterfeit (third) until you have named the true (first) and exposed the false (second). The order is the order of any honest reformation: raise the standard, expose the substitute, then call for decision.

- **Every thread of this series converges here**
  - The CREATOR'S SABBATH (the seal, Studies 6, 24) — restored by the first angel.
  - The CALL OUT OF BABYLON (Studies 22-23) — opened by the second angel.
  - The WARNING AGAINST THE MARK (the beast, Studies 20-21, 28) — sounded by the third angel.
  - The TWO CHARGES of the cosmic trial (Study 1) — answered by Revelation 14:12.
  - The SECOND COMING (Study 31) — the harvest of Revelation 14:14.
  - [→] This is why the spec calls it "the banner under which the rest of the series marches." Revelation 14:6-12 is not one more doctrine among many; it is the COMMISSION that gathers every doctrine in this series into one final, worldwide, datable message. Everything before it prepared the message; everything after it unpacks the message.

- **This is the renewed proclamation of "thou must prophesy again"**
  - Revelation 10's angel ended with the command, "Thou must prophesy again before many peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings" (Rev 10:11) — after the bitter Disappointment (Study 16).
  - Turn one chapter and there is the renewed proclamation: an angel "to preach unto them that dwell on the earth, and to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people" (Rev 14:6). The wording of the commission (Rev 10:11) and the wording of the message (Rev 14:6) match — "many peoples, and nations, and tongues" / "every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people."
  - [STR] So the three angels' message is literally the answer to "prophesy again." The bitterness of 1844 was not the end of the movement but the birth of its world message. The Way Home series you are reading is itself part of this proclamation — the three angels still flying.

## Document from History — When the Three Angels Began to Sound

**The doctrine above is proved from Scripture alone. History's only job here is the historicist's one job: to record that the predicted worldwide message actually began to sound on schedule. (Dates and facts only; the meaning stands on the Bible.)**

- **The first angel — the judgment-hour cry, A.D. 1840-1844**
  - The first angel's message, "the hour of his judgment is come," is the very proclamation the Advent movement carried across the world from roughly 1840 to 1844 — the same movement that sounded the August 11, 1840 fulfillment (Study 13) and the Midnight Cry (Study 17). It could not be literally true before 1844, because the 2300 days had not expired (the math of Study 14); the close of the 2300 days at the autumn of 1844 made "the hour is come" a present fact, and the message arose to announce it — exactly when, and only when, the prophecy allowed.

- **The second angel — first preached in the summer of 1844**
  - "Babylon is fallen" was first sounded in the summer of 1844, applied to the popular churches in the United States that rejected the Advent warning and shut their doors against it. That was the BEGINNING of the second angel's work — its moral application to the bodies that turned away the present truth. The COMPLETE fulfillment is still future: the worldwide moral fall of the whole apostate system, finished under the loud cry of the other angel of Revelation 18:1 (Study 23). History records the start; prophecy reserves the climax.

- **The third angel — the message now sounding to the world**
  - The third angel followed the first two and is the message proclaimed from 1844 onward, gathering "they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (Rev 14:12) — a Sabbath-keeping, judgment-hour people. Its final, loud-cry application (the universal Sunday-law crisis) is still future and is treated in Study 28; here history records only that the third message began to sound when the first two had done their work, in the exact order Revelation 14 fixes.

- **The form of the proof (the historicist lane)**
  - [STR] Note carefully what history is and is NOT doing here. It is NOT proving that the Sabbath is the seal, or that Babylon is the apostate church, or that the mark is the counterfeit — those rest on Exodus 20, Ezekiel 20, Isaiah 21, Jeremiah 51, and Revelation 13 (all above). History does ONE thing: it confirms that the worldwide message Revelation 14 predicted actually began to be preached, in the predicted order, beginning at the predicted date. Bible predicts the message → history records the message sounded on schedule. The meaning is the Bible's; the fulfillment is the record's.

## Appeal

- Three angels are flying in the midst of heaven, and their message is the loudest, plainest, most urgent God has ever sent: the hour of His judgment is come; worship the One who made heaven and earth and the sea; come out of Babylon; refuse the mark of the beast.
- This is not a message for some distant generation. The judgment court has been sitting since 1844. The angels are flying NOW. The harvest is the next event. There is no fourth angel and no third cup — every soul drinks the wine of Babylon or the wine of God's wrath; every forehead bears the Father's name or the mark of the beast.
- The whole controversy comes down to one question of worship: will you worship the Creator on His own day, by the faith of Jesus — or will you bow to the system that changed His times and His law? Revelation 14:12 is not a burden laid on you; it is the portrait of a free, loved, enduring people, God's living answer to the lie that His law cannot be kept and that no one loves Him for Himself.
- You were created to be a witness in the cosmic trial (Study 1). Here is your stand: "they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." Not for reward — under loss. Not in your own strength — by His faith.
- The first lie was "hath God said?" (Study 0). The last message is "Fear God, and give glory to him." Will you join the three angels — and let the message fly through you?

**Central Verse:** "Saying with a loud voice, Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come: and worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters." (Revelation 14:7)

**[→ Study 20]** The third angel warns against "the beast and his image" and "his mark" — but who is the beast, where did he come from, and why does he hate the commandment-keeping remnant of Revelation 14:12? To answer that, we go back behind the whole war to its origin. Revelation 12 draws the entire great controversy in one chapter: a woman, a dragon, a man-child, a war in heaven, a flight into the wilderness for 1260 years, and a dragon left "wroth with the woman" — turning at last "to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God." The three angels named the remnant; Revelation 12 shows us why the dragon wants them dead.
