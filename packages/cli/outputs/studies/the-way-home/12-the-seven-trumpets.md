# Study 12: The Seven Trumpets — Rome Falls, Byzantium Falls

**The seals showed the inward state of the church across the ages; the trumpets show what God did to the empires that touched her. A trumpet, in Scripture's own dictionary, is the alarm of war — and the seven trumpets sound the political and military judgments that broke Western Rome under the barbarian floods and then loosed the Saracen and the Turk upon the East. They are not random catastrophe: they are God's gavel falling on the nations, struck from behind the golden altar where Christ still ministers. And the fifth trumpet hangs out a 150-year clock — a dry run for the single most dramatic prophetic date in all this series, the one the next study will set before you.**

## First, What Is a Trumpet? — Scripture Hands Us the Symbol

**We will not guess at the meaning of the seven trumpets. Study 0 settled the rule: where a figure is used, the Bible itself supplies the figure's meaning — comparing spiritual things with spiritual (1 Cor 2:13). So before we ask what the seven trumpets DO, we ask what a trumpet IS. And the Bible answers in one voice from Numbers to Joel: a trumpet is the alarm of war.**

- **A trumpet is the alarm of war — the prophet says so himself**
  - "My bowels, my bowels! I am pained at my very heart; my heart maketh a noise in me; I cannot hold my peace, because thou hast heard, O my soul, the sound of the trumpet, the alarm of war." (Jer 4:19)
  - Jeremiah does not need an interpreter; the verse defines its own figure. "The sound of the trumpet" IS "the alarm of war." That is the Bible's dictionary entry for the symbol.

- **The trumpet is the watchman's warning of the invading sword**
  - "If when he seeth the sword come upon the land, he blow the trumpet, and warn the people;" (Eze 33:3)
  - "He heard the sound of the trumpet, and took not warning; his blood shall be upon him. But he that taketh warning shall deliver his soul." (Eze 33:5)
  - In Ezekiel's parable the trumpet means one thing only: the sword is coming. Sound it, and the nation is warned of invasion and war.

- **The trumpet announces the day of the LORD's judgment on the land**
  - "Blow ye the trumpet in Zion, and sound an alarm in my holy mountain: let all the inhabitants of the land tremble: for the day of the LORD cometh, for it is nigh at hand;" (Joel 2:1)
  - "A day of the trumpet and alarm against the fenced cities, and against the high towers." (Zeph 1:16)
  - Israel was commanded: "if ye go to war in your land against the enemy that oppresseth you, then ye shall blow an alarm with the trumpets" (Num 10:9). The trumpet is the literal instrument of war, and so the prophetic symbol of war.

- **And behind the war stands the hand of God**
  - "Shall a trumpet be blown in the city, and the people not be afraid? shall there be evil in a city, and the LORD hath not done it?" (Amos 3:6)
  - [STR] This is the whole key to Revelation 8 and 9 in a single verse. The trumpet sounds; war comes; and it does not come by chance — "the LORD hath not done it?" The judgments of the trumpets are not Rome's bad luck or the barbarian's wild appetite. They are God's gavel falling on a guilty civilization, on His own schedule. Hold Amos 3:6 in your hand and the trumpets stop being chaos and start being a courtroom.

[→] So before we touch a single date, the Bible has already told us what the seven trumpets are. Seven war-alarms. Seven judgments by sword and fire on the empire that had drunk the blood of the saints. The seals looked inside the church; the trumpets look at the nations outside her — and at what the Judge did to them.

## Where the Trumpets Stand — At the Altar Where Christ Ministers

**Before the first trumpet sounds, John shows us a scene at the golden altar. This is not decoration. It tells us WHO is behind the judgments and WHY they fall — and it ties the whole vision back to the sanctuary you have been walking through since Study 5.**

- **The seventh seal opens onto silence, and then seven trumpets are given**
  - "And when he had opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven about the space of half an hour." (Rev 8:1)
  - "And I saw the seven angels which stood before God; and to them were given seven trumpets." (Rev 8:2)
  - The trumpets are the unrolling of the seventh seal — the same era as the churches and the seals, now viewed from a third vantage: not the church's inner condition, not her providential agencies, but the warfare on the nations that surrounded her. One era, three windows. Precept upon precept (Isa 28:10).

- **An angel stands at the golden altar, offering incense with the prayers of the saints**
  - "And another angel came and stood at the altar, having a golden censer; and there was given unto him much incense, that he should offer it with the prayers of all saints upon the golden altar which was before the throne." (Rev 8:3)
  - "And the smoke of the incense, which came with the prayers of the saints, ascended up before God out of the angel's hand." (Rev 8:4)
  - This is the altar of incense from the Holy Place — the very piece of furniture Study 5 named as prayer ascending through Christ (Ps 141:2; Rev 5:8). The persecuted saints have been crying out (Rev 6:9-10); here their prayers go up before the throne.

- **Then the SAME fire from that altar is cast down — and the trumpets sound**
  - "And the angel took the censer, and filled it with fire of the altar, and cast it into the earth: and there were voices, and thunderings, and lightnings, and an earthquake." (Rev 8:5)
  - "And the seven angels which had the seven trumpets prepared themselves to sound." (Rev 8:6)
  - [STR] Mark the sequence. The saints' prayers rise; the censer is filled with the altar's own fire; that fire is hurled to the earth; and immediately the trumpets begin. The judgments on Rome are the ANSWER to the prayers of the martyrs. The fire that falls on the empire is kindled at the altar of intercession.

[→] So the trumpets do not stand apart from Christ's ministry — they stand BEHIND it. Hebrews 8:1-2 has already told us where Christ is: "We have such an high priest, who is set on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens; a minister of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man." The Lamb who opens the seals and the High Priest at the golden altar are one and the same. The hand that judges the nations is the hand that bears the prayers of His people. This is text-authorized typology, not overlay: John deliberately places the war-trumpets at the altar of intercession to show us that even the falling of empires is the work of the Mediator. The Judge of Rome is the Saviour of the saints.

[DYK🔎] The "silence in heaven about the space of half an hour" (Rev 8:1) caught the attention of the early Adventists for a different reason than the trumpets. Reckoned by the year-day key (half an hour of prophetic time, a prophetic day being a year), some read it as roughly a week of literal time and connected it to the Second Coming scene. But the trumpets themselves do not depend on that reading — they begin in verse 6 and run their course on dates history can check. We note the silence; we build nothing on it here.

## The First Four Trumpets — The Barbarian Floods That Broke Western Rome

**Daniel 2 said the legs of iron — Rome — would be broken and divided (Dan 2:40-43). Daniel 7 said the same fourth beast would be torn into ten horns (Dan 7:7,24). The first four trumpets are that breaking, told a third time, blow by blow. They fall on the same "third part" again and again — the western, Roman third of the world — by hail and fire, by a burning mountain hurled into the sea, by a blazing star poisoning the rivers, by the sun and moon darkened. Four judgments; four barbarian floods; one empire dismembered.**

- **The first trumpet: hail, fire, and blood on the land**
  - "The first angel sounded, and there followed hail and fire mingled with blood, and they were cast upon the earth: and the third part of trees was burnt up, and all green grass was burnt up." (Rev 8:7)
  - Hail comes from the frozen north; fire and blood are the marks of war. The blow falls on the LAND — and it strikes "the third part," the Roman west.

- **The second trumpet: a burning mountain cast into the SEA**
  - "And the second angel sounded, and as it were a great mountain burning with fire was cast into the sea: and the third part of the sea became blood;" (Rev 8:8)
  - "And the third part of the creatures which were in the sea, and had life, died; and the third part of the ships were destroyed." (Rev 8:9)
  - A mountain, in prophecy, is a kingdom or great power (the stone that becomes "a great mountain," Dan 2:35; "the mountain of the LORD's house," Isa 2:2). Here a burning power is hurled into the SEA, and ships are destroyed — a maritime war, a naval scourge on the Mediterranean.

- **The third trumpet: a blazing star poisons the RIVERS and fountains**
  - "And the third angel sounded, and there fell a great star from heaven, burning as it were a lamp, and it fell upon the third part of the rivers, and upon the fountains of waters;" (Rev 8:10)
  - "And the name of the star is called Wormwood: and the third part of the waters became wormwood; and many men died of the waters, because they were made bitter." (Rev 8:11)
  - A meteor-like leader falls, blazing across the empire, and his path embitters the very sources of the Roman heartland — the rivers and fountains of central Europe and Italy.

- **The fourth trumpet: the sun, moon, and stars of the empire DARKENED**
  - "And the fourth angel sounded, and the third part of the sun was smitten, and the third part of the moon, and the third part of the stars; so as the third part of them was darkened, and the day shone not for a third part of it, and the night likewise." (Rev 8:12)
  - Sun, moon, and stars are the ruling lights — the governing powers, emperors and dignitaries (compare Joseph's dream, Gen 37:9-10, where sun, moon, and stars are rulers of a house). The fourth trumpet puts out the lights of Western Rome: the imperial line itself is extinguished.

[→] Four trumpets, four spheres — land, sea, rivers, the lights of heaven — and one steady target: "the third part." The Roman world is struck in every part until its government goes dark. This is exactly the breaking Daniel foresaw, now sounded as four war-alarms.

> ### Document from History — The Four Floods (FULFILLMENT ONLY)
>
> Here, and ONLY here, do we step into the historical record. The Bible predicted the breaking of Rome's "third part"; history records who broke it, and when. The meaning was proved from Scripture above (a trumpet = war, Jer 4:19; a mountain/star = a power; sun-moon-stars = rulers); the events below are simply the receipt that the prediction came true. Historians from Edward Gibbon onward — no friend of prophecy — chronicle these four invasions in the very order John gave them.
>
> 1. **First trumpet (land) — Alaric and the Goths**, from about A.D. 395, the first great Gothic flood pouring over the Roman provinces from the north, culminating in the sack of Rome itself in A.D. 410.
> 2. **Second trumpet (sea) — Genseric and the Vandals**, c. A.D. 428-468, whose naval power ravaged the Mediterranean from North Africa, destroyed Roman fleets, and sacked Rome from the sea in A.D. 455 — the "burning mountain cast into the sea."
> 3. **Third trumpet (rivers) — Attila and the Huns**, the "scourge of God," whose campaigns of the 440s-450s blazed across the Alpine and Italian river country before burning out, the meteor "Wormwood" whose passage embittered central Europe.
> 4. **Fourth trumpet (lights darkened) — the extinction of the Western imperial line**, A.D. 476, when Odoacer deposed the last emperor, Romulus Augustulus, and through the decades to c. A.D. 493 the Roman sun, moon, and stars — the whole apparatus of Western government — went dark. The legs of iron lay broken.
>
> Four invasions, four spheres, in John's exact sequence. The empire Daniel 2 said would be "divided" (Dan 2:41) was divided precisely as the first four trumpets sounded.

[DYK🔎] You do not have to take the date-keepers on faith here, because they are HOSTILE witnesses. Edward Gibbon's _Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire_ set out to explain Rome's collapse by purely natural causes and had no interest whatever in confirming the Apocalypse. Yet his narrative of these four barbarian nations, in this order, reads like a commentary on Revelation 8 — an unconscious commentator who would have been astonished to be quoted in a prophecy class. When the prosecution's own historian confirms the prophet's outline, the receipt is hard to argue with.

## The Fifth Trumpet — The First Woe: The Saracen Locusts of Arabia

**The four trumpets that broke Western Rome are spent; the angel cries "Woe, woe, woe" (Rev 8:13), warning that the last three trumpets are of a heavier order. The fifth trumpet — the first woe — turns east. Where the first four were judgments on the WESTERN Roman third, the fifth and sixth fall on the EASTERN third, the surviving Greek empire at Constantinople. And out of Arabia comes a swarm the prophecy calls locusts: the rise of Islam and the Saracen conquests.**

- **A fallen star opens the bottomless pit, and smoke darkens the air**
  - "And the fifth angel sounded, and I saw a star fall from heaven unto the earth: and to him was given the key of the bottomless pit." (Rev 9:1)
  - "And he opened the bottomless pit; and there arose a smoke out of the pit, as the smoke of a great furnace; and the sun and the air were darkened by reason of the smoke of the pit." (Rev 9:2)
  - A new power rises out of the desert "pit," and a darkening smoke — a false religion — spreads over the East, obscuring the light.

- **Out of the smoke come locusts with the power of scorpions**
  - "And there came out of the smoke locusts upon the earth: and unto them was given power, as the scorpions of the earth have power." (Rev 9:3)
  - Locusts are Scripture's symbol of a vast, devouring ARMY. Joel calls the locust swarm "my great army which I sent among you" (Joel 2:25). A locust horde out of the Arabian desert is the perfect figure for the Arab cavalry — countless, sudden, stripping the land.

- **They are commanded to hurt only men, not vegetation — and not to KILL but to TORMENT five months**
  - "And it was commanded them that they should not hurt the grass of the earth, neither any green thing, neither any tree; but only those men which have not the seal of God in their foreheads." (Rev 9:4)
  - "And to them it was given that they should not kill them, but that they should be tormented five months: and their torment was as the torment of a scorpion, when he striketh a man." (Rev 9:5)
  - Strange locusts — they spare the grass and the trees. This is no natural plague; it is an army under restraint, sent to torment and harass the apostate Roman world, not to make a final end of it.

- **Their appearance: crowned horsemen with the faces of men, hair of women, teeth of lions, iron breastplates**
  - "And the shapes of the locusts were like unto horses prepared unto battle; and on their heads were as it were crowns like gold, and their faces were as the faces of men." (Rev 9:7)
  - "And they had hair as the hair of women, and their teeth were as the teeth of lions." (Rev 9:8)
  - "And they had breastplates, as it were breastplates of iron; and the sound of their wings was as the sound of chariots of many horses running to battle." (Rev 9:9)
  - Horsemen (Arab cavalry), turbaned crowns, the long hair of their riders, lion-fierce, armoured — a precise portrait of a mounted desert army.

- **Their king is named — and the name is read literally, not allegorized**
  - "And they had a king over them, which is the angel of the bottomless pit, whose name in the Hebrew tongue is Abaddon, but in the Greek tongue hath his name Apollyon." (Rev 9:11)
  - Abaddon and Apollyon both mean DESTROYER. The Bible itself names the spirit behind this power: a destroying one. We do not invent a hidden meaning; we take the name the text gives — this is the destroyer's army, loosed on the East.

[→] So before any history is consulted, the Bible has drawn the picture: an army like locusts, out of the desert pit, under a new dark religion, mounted, fierce, restrained from total destruction, sent to torment the apostate East for "five months." Now two things must be settled from Scripture — how long is "five months," and then we may look at the record.

### "Five Months" — The Year-Day Key, Demonstrated Again

**This is the second time in Revelation we have met a prophetic period (Smyrna's "ten days," Rev 2:10, was the first). And we will not invent the conversion. Study 9 proved the key from God's own mouth; here we simply apply it.**

- **God Himself fixed the rule: each prophetic day stands for a year**
  - "After the number of the days in which ye searched the land, even forty days, each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities, even forty years" (Num 14:34)
  - "And when thou hast accomplished them, lie again on thy right side, and thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: I have appointed thee each day for a year." (Eze 4:6)
  - Two witnesses, God's own words: in symbolic prophecy, a day is a year (Deut 19:15 requires two or three witnesses; here are two).

- **The arithmetic of "five months"**
  - A prophetic month is 30 days (Scripture reckons 1260 days = 42 months = "time, times, and a half," comparing Rev 11:2-3; 12:6,14; 13:5 — so a month = 30 days).
  - Five months = 5 × 30 = **150 prophetic days**.
  - By the year-day key: 150 days = **150 literal years**.
  - [STR] Note the discipline. We are NOT free-handing a number. The "month = 30 days" comes from Scripture's own reckoning of the 1260; the "day = year" comes from Numbers 14:34 and Ezekiel 4:6. The 150 years is forced on us by the Bible's own self-defined units — line upon line, not numerology.

[→] Twice now ("five months" tormenting, Rev 9:5,10) the prophecy nails down the length: 150 years of harassing warfare against the Eastern empire before the torment turns into the slaying of the sixth trumpet. A period that long, fixed in advance, is a clock — and a clock can be checked.

> ### Document from History — The Saracen Locusts and the 150 Years (FULFILLMENT ONLY)
>
> The Bible drew the picture and set the clock; history supplies the dates. The meaning ("locusts" = a devouring army, Joel 2:25; "five months" = 150 years by the year-day key) was proved above from Scripture alone. The record below is only the receipt.
>
> - **The smoke from the pit** — the rise of Islam under Mohammed in the seventh century, the new religion spreading like a darkening smoke over Arabia and the East from c. A.D. 612 onward, followed by the explosive Saracen (Arab) conquests across the Eastern Roman provinces, Syria, Egypt, and North Africa.
> - **"Tormented, not killed"** — for centuries the Saracen power harassed and tormented the Eastern (Greek) empire without ever finally destroying it; Constantinople stood. That is exactly the prophecy's restraint: torment, not the death-blow.
> - **The 150-year clock** — the historicist expositors (Keith, and after them the Millerite preachers) reckoned the specific 150 years of the locust-torment from **July 27, A.D. 1299**, when Othman (Osman) made his first organized assault on the territory of the Greek empire, founding the Ottoman power — to **A.D. 1449**, when (as the next study documents) the Greek empire's independence ended and the "torment" gave way to the outright "slaying" of the sixth trumpet. 1299 + 150 = 1449. The 150 years run out exactly.
>
> The torment of the fifth trumpet ends precisely where the slaying of the sixth begins — and that hand-off, July 27, 1449, is the starting gun for the most famous prophetic date in this series.

[Q] **"Isn't this just reading current events back into an old book — making locusts mean Arabs because it's convenient?"**
→ No — and the order of operations is the whole answer. We did not start with history and hunt for a text to match it. We started with Scripture and let it draw the picture FIRST: an army like locusts (Joel 2:25 defines the figure), out of a desert pit, under a darkening new religion, mounted and fierce, restrained to torment for a fixed 150 years (the year-day key, proved in Study 9, not assumed). Only AFTER the Bible had specified all of that did we open the record — and there stood the Saracen-Ottoman power, in the desert, on the new religion, mounted, tormenting the East, on the very century the clock named. When the prediction is on the page before the fulfillment is consulted, that is not reading back; that is reading forward. The test of Isaiah 8:20 is met: it speaks according to the word.

## How the Trumpets Lock Into the Whole Map

**Step back and see that the trumpets are not a stray vision. They are the same history Daniel drew, sounded a third time — precept upon precept upon precept.**

- **Daniel 2: the iron legs of Rome are broken and the feet divided** (Dan 2:40-43) — "the kingdom shall be divided."
- **Daniel 7: the fourth beast is torn into ten horns** (Dan 7:7,24) — Rome dismembered.
- **The first four trumpets: that same dismembering, blow by blow** (Rev 8:7-12) — the four floods on the western third.
- **The fifth and sixth trumpets: the EASTERN third falls** (Rev 9) — what Daniel 2 and 7 traced in the West, the trumpets complete in the East.

[STR] This is the Bible reading the Bible. The breakup of Rome is told in Daniel 2 (image), Daniel 7 (beasts), and Revelation 8-9 (trumpets) — three independent witnesses to one history. A reader who has walked Studies 7 and 8 does not meet the trumpets as a stranger; he meets them as the third confirmation of a map he already trusts. "In the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word be established" (compare Deut 19:15; 2 Cor 13:1).

[→] And the trumpets are not finished. The fifth was the FIRST woe (Rev 9:12); "the second woe is past" still lay ahead (Rev 11:14). The sixth trumpet — the second woe — is where the torment becomes the slaying, and where the prophecy hangs out not a 150-year clock but a clock measured to the very day.

## The Sixth Trumpet — The Bridge to the Keystone

**We will not open the sixth trumpet fully here; the next study is built on it. But see where the fifth has set us down, so the hand-off is clear.**

- **The sixth angel sounds, and a voice comes from the golden altar — the altar where the trumpets began**
  - "And the sixth angel sounded, and I heard a voice from the four horns of the golden altar which is before God," (Rev 9:13)
  - "Saying to the sixth angel which had the trumpet, Loose the four angels which are bound in the great river Euphrates." (Rev 9:14)
  - The same golden altar of Revelation 8:3-5 speaks again. Christ's ministry still stands behind the judgment. And now four bound powers on the Euphrates — the seat of the Ottoman Turk — are to be loosed.

- **And the loosing is timed to an exact appointment**
  - "And the four angels were loosed, which were prepared for an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year, for to slay the third part of men." (Rev 9:15)
  - [STR] Where the fifth trumpet gave "five months" (150 years), the sixth gives "an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year" — four units stacked together. By the same year-day key, that converts to an exact span of years and days, with a beginning the next study will fix at July 27, 1449 (where the fifth trumpet's torment ended) and an end that a man named Josiah Litch published two years before it arrived — and history kept the appointment to the day.

[→] That is the keystone exhibit, and it gets its own study. Here it is enough to see that the fifth trumpet's 150-year clock was the rehearsal. If the Bible can fix 150 years on the Saracen and have it fall out exactly, the question for the sixth trumpet — and for the 2300 days that reckon to 1844 by the SAME rule — is no longer "does the method work?" It is "will you trust the method when it points at YOU?"

## Appeal

- The trumpets are God's gavel. "Shall there be evil in a city, and the LORD hath not done it?" (Amos 3:6). The barbarian floods that broke Rome and the locust-swarm that tormented the East did not fall by accident; they fell by appointment, struck from the altar where the prayers of the persecuted saints went up.
- That altar is the same one your prayers reach today. The God who timed the rise and fall of empires to the year is not careless of your small life. The hand that judged Rome bears your name (Heb 8:1-2).
- And there is a sober word in the locust's commission: the torment touched "only those men which have not the seal of God in their foreheads" (Rev 9:4). When judgment is loosed, the seal of God is the only shelter. The studies ahead will show you what that seal is and how it is received — but the question it poses is already in front of you: when the trumpets of the last days sound, will you be found inside the shelter or outside it?
- The God who keeps a 150-year appointment with the Saracen keeps every appointment He has made with you. He is precise because He is faithful. Trust the One whose prophecies never miss — and let that precision answer the old charge that God cannot be relied upon.
- Will you take heed to the trumpet while it is still a warning and not yet the woe?
