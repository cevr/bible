# The Way Home — Series Index

A 33-study (00–32) historicist, literal, Bible-only study chain that recreates the
SDA pioneers' chain of truth, proved from the Bible itself, inside the Way Home
narrative frame (cosmic trial → Satan's two charges → broken bridge → exile in
Babylon → the call home → restoration).

Contract: [`_BLUEPRINT.md`](./_BLUEPRINT.md). Doctrinal line: mature SDA (Uriah
Smith _DAR_ + settled Ellen White positions). Every doctrine is established from
Scripture; history appears only in the fulfillment lane; pioneers/Ellen White
stand only as confirming echoes after the Bible proof.

## The 33 Studies

### Part I — Foundation

**0. Hath God Said?** — _`00-hath-god-said.md`_ — Before any doctrine, settle how to read the Book: take God at His plain word; every error begins where interpretation is surrendered.

**1. The Cosmic Trial — Why You Exist** — _`01-the-cosmic-trial.md`_ — Lucifer filed literal charges against God's character; humanity was created as living evidence in a real trial, settled by truth, not force — the series runs on two charges ("they only obey for reward"; "the law cannot be kept").

**2. The Bridge — Destroyed and Restored** — _`02-the-bridge-destroyed-and-restored.md`_ — The fall severed heaven from earth and brought literal (unconscious) death, met at once by the proto-gospel, the first blood sacrifice, and a sanctuary built after the heavenly pattern.

**3. What Salvation Really Is — The Image Restored** — _`03-what-salvation-really-is.md`_ — Salvation is not merely forgiveness but the restoration of God's image (His character, His law of love) in man, by the Spirit, through beholding Christ.

**4. Why It Had to Be Christ** — _`04-why-it-had-to-be-christ.md`_ — Only Christ — fully God, fully man — could restore the image: His death makes forgiveness just, His sinless life in fallen flesh proves the law can be kept — both charges answered.

**5. Thy Way, O God** — _`05-thy-way-o-god.md`_ — Because the text itself makes Jesus the Ladder (John 1:51), the Gate (John 10:9), and the House/Sanctuary (1 Chr 28:10), the three apartments map the plan of salvation and Christ's literal heavenly ministry — one author-marked diagram.

**6. Two Laws — The Image and the Shadow** — _`06-two-laws.md`_ — Scripture distinguishes the eternal moral law (God's character, on stone, inside the ark) from the ceremonial shadow (written by Moses, beside the ark); only the shadow was nailed to the cross — the moral law, with the literal seventh-day Sabbath, stands.

### Part II — Prophecy Names the System

**7. The Counterfeit System (Daniel 2)** — _`07-the-counterfeit-system.md`_ — Daniel 2 is history written in advance — four literal empires to a stone kingdom — and the same sequence is the frame that exposes Satan's single layered counterfeit.

**8. Prophecy Names the System (Daniel 7)** — _`08-prophecy-names-the-system.md`_ — Daniel 7 shows the four empires as beasts, then specifies a little horn among divided Rome by character, deeds, and a 1260-year calendar — every identity Scripture-fixed — and opens the judgment that ends it.

**9. A Day for a Year** — _`09-a-day-for-a-year.md`_ — Before any date can be trusted, the year-day key is proved from Scripture: God states it twice ("each day for a year"), builds the seven-year week into His calendar, and the seventy weeks run out to the very year of Messiah.

**10. The Seven Churches** — _`10-the-seven-churches.md`_ — The seven letters are one continuous prophecy: seven successive epochs of the whole Christian church, apostolic Ephesus to judgment-hour Laodicea, each name and condition matched by history.

**11. The Seven Seals** — _`11-the-seven-seals.md`_ — The Lamb unrolls the church's outward providence across the era (white-horse purity to pale papal death), climaxing in the literal sixth-seal signs: Lisbon 1755, the Dark Day 1780, the falling stars 1833.

**12. The Seven Trumpets** — _`12-the-seven-trumpets.md`_ — The trumpets sound the political/military judgments on the nations that touched the church — the barbarian floods that broke Western Rome, then the Saracen woe on the East — setting the stage for the sixth-trumpet date.

**13. August 11, 1840** — _`13-august-11-1840.md`_ — The sixth trumpet's "hour, day, month, and year" = 391 years 15 days of Ottoman supremacy (July 27 1449 → Aug 11 1840), a date Litch published two years in advance and history kept to the day — the public receipt on the rule that also reckons 1844.

**14. The Timeline Unlocked (Daniel 8 & 9, the 2300 Days, 1844)** — _`14-the-timeline-unlocked.md`_ — Daniel 8's 2300 years and Daniel 9's seventy weeks, cut from the same start, date the decree, Messiah's anointing, His death, and the gospel's turn to the Gentiles to the exact year — three verified dates on which the fourth, 1844, stands.

**15. Every Clock Agrees (the Daily, the 1290, the 1335, the 2520)** — _`15-every-clock-agrees.md`_ — The 1290 and 1335 of Daniel 12 (from the taking away of the daily = paganism, 508) and the 2520 of Leviticus 26 run out on the same two dates, 1798 and 1843/44 — independent clocks striking the same hour.

**16. The Little Book (Revelation 10)** — _`16-the-little-book.md`_ — The mighty angel brings Daniel's book now open, swears "time shall be no longer," and gives a message honey-sweet then bitter (the Great Disappointment) — both written into the prophecy beforehand — then commissions "thou must prophesy again."

**17. The Midnight Cry (the Chart, the Tarrying, the Seventh-Month)** — _`17-the-midnight-cry.md`_ — Habakkuk 2 framed the whole experience (chart, tarrying, faith) and the ten-virgins parable maps the road; the autumn Day-of-Atonement type fixes the date — October 22, 1844. The time was right; only the event was misread.

### Part III — The Judgment and the Final Crisis

**18. 1844 and the Judgment** — _`18-1844-and-the-judgment.md`_ — When the 2300 days ended, Christ entered the Most Holy Place and the antitypical Day of Atonement — the pre-advent investigative judgment — began, exactly as Dan 8:14, Dan 7, Lev 16, and Heb 8–9 require.

**19. The Three Angels' Messages (Revelation 14)** — _`19-the-three-angels-messages.md`_ — Rev 14:6–12 is the literal, datable, worldwide commission from 1844 onward: the everlasting gospel in the judgment hour, the call to the Creator's Sabbath, Babylon's fall, and the warning against the mark — the banner the rest of the series marches under.

**20. The Woman and the Dragon (Revelation 12)** — _`20-the-woman-and-the-dragon.md`_ — Revelation 12 tells the whole cosmic trial in one sweep: the true church bears Christ; the dragon (Satan through pagan Rome) is defeated at the cross, persecutes the woman 1260 years (538–1798), then makes war on the remnant who keep the commandments.

**21. The Two Beasts (Revelation 13, USA named)** — _`21-the-two-beasts.md`_ — Rev 13 shows the dragon's two agents: the sea-beast (papal Rome, wounded 1798) matching Daniel's little horn, and the two-horned earth-beast which by its textual specifications can only be the United States.

**22. Babylon the Great (Revelation 17)** — _`22-babylon-the-great.md`_ — The scarlet woman on the seven-headed beast is Babylon the Great — the apostate church wielding civil power, mother of harlot daughters, the same wounded-and-revived Roman power, named by the Bible's own dictionary of symbols.

**23. Come Out of Her, My People (Revelation 18:4)** — _`23-come-out-of-her.md`_ — Rev 18:4 is a literal command to physically separate from a literal apostate religious system before its plagues fall — a real exodus into the truth, not an inner change of attitude.

**24. The Sabbath — God's Literal Seal in Time** — _`24-the-sabbath.md`_ — The seventh-day Sabbath is a literal day instituted at creation, carrying the seal of God's authority (name, title, territory); the little horn moved to change it, so its literal observance — not a restful attitude — is the seal of God in the final test.

**25. The Temple of the Holy Spirit** — _`25-the-temple-of-the-holy-spirit.md`_ — The body is literally the temple of the Holy Spirit, bought with a price; how it is treated bears on the clear mind God uses for last-day faithfulness — proved from the plain NT temple texts and Daniel 1, not numerology.

**26. Conquering Canaan — How Transformation Really Works (Two Covenants)** — _`26-conquering-canaan.md`_ — Progressive sanctification is God's literal work by the new covenant — His law written on the heart, righteousness by faith, the Spirit's fruit — with the conquest of Canaan as the God-given framework, the doctrine anchored in the plain NT texts underneath.

**27. The State of the Dead and Spiritualism** — _`27-the-state-of-the-dead-and-spiritualism.md`_ — The dead are literally asleep and unconscious until the resurrection, and the immortal-soul lie (Gen 3:4) is the foundation of spiritualism — Satan's masterpiece for the final crisis; the truth about death is the one bulwark against it.

**28. The Final Crisis (USA, the Image, the Mark)** — _`28-the-final-crisis.md`_ — The two-horned beast — the United States — will speak as a dragon by forming an image to the papacy, a church-state union enforcing Sunday (the mark) by boycott and death decree, while a literal heavenly angel's glory lightens the earth and the latter rain empowers God's last witness.

### Part IV — The Consummation

**29. The 144,000 and the Great Multitude — The Sealed Company** — _`29-the-144000-and-the-great-multitude.md`_ — The sealing of Revelation 7 marks a literal company, the 144,000, who pass through the final crisis alive having answered both charges — distinct from yet joined by the great multitude out of every nation.

**30. The Close of Probation, Jacob's Trouble, and the Seven Last Plagues** — _`30-close-of-probation-jacobs-trouble-plagues.md`_ — When the investigative judgment ends, Christ rises from His mediation, probation closes, and the seven literal plagues fall unmingled with mercy on the mark-bearers — while God's sealed people pass through Jacob's trouble, sheltered though not exempted.

**31. Armageddon, the Second Coming, and Deliverance** — _`31-armageddon-second-coming-deliverance.md`_ — The sixth plague gathers the world to Armageddon, where the literal, visible, audible second coming shatters the last muster, delivers the sealed people, and raises the dead in Christ — the brightest morning after the darkest hour.

**32. The Image Restored — The Millennium and the New Earth** — _`32-the-image-restored.md`_ — The literal millennium with its two resurrections vindicates God before every intelligence, sin and its author are destroyed (not eternally tormented), and a literal new earth restores Eden — the image fully restored, the cosmic trial closed, the Way Home complete.
