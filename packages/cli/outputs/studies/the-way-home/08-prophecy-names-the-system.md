# Study 8: Prophecy Names the System

**Daniel 2 sketched the same four empires as metal; Daniel 7 shows them as beasts — and then specifies a fifth power, a little horn rising among divided Rome, identified by the angel's own definitions, by three marks the Bible itself defines, and by a calendar of "a time and times and the dividing of time." Every identity in this chapter is fixed by Scripture, not supplied by the reader. And the same vision that names the power opens the judgment that ends it.**

## Prep Reading

- Daniel 7 (whole chapter)
- Daniel 2:36-45
- Exodus 20:8-11
- Numbers 14:34 and Ezekiel 4:6

## Daniel 7 — The Same Empires, Unmasked

**Before a single identity is asserted, notice the method: Daniel 7 interprets itself in the same chapter. We do not bring meanings to the beasts — the angel hands them to us (Dan 7:16-17). This is "Scripture its own expositor" working inside one vision.**

- **Four beasts from the sea — the same sequence as Daniel 2, a new lens**
  - "Daniel spake and said, I saw in my vision by night, and, behold, the four winds of the heaven strove upon the great sea. And four great beasts came up from the sea, diverse one from another" (Dan 7:2-3)
  - Daniel 2 showed political succession through metals — how empires look from the outside
  - Daniel 7 reveals beastly character — how empires look from heaven's perspective
  - [→] Man sees kingdoms as gold and silver. God sees them as predators.

- **Lion — Babylon**
  - "The first was like a lion, and had eagle's wings: I beheld till the wings thereof were plucked, and it was lifted up from the earth, and made stand upon the feet as a man, and a man's heart was given to it" (Dan 7:4)
  - Winged lions guarded Babylon's gates — the symbol is unmistakable
  - Wings plucked, made to stand as a man, a man's heart given to it — Nebuchadnezzar's humiliation and restoration (Dan 4)

- **Bear — Medo-Persia**
  - "And behold another beast, a second, like to a bear, and it raised up itself on one side, and it had three ribs in the mouth of it between the teeth of it" (Dan 7:5)
  - Raised on one side — Persia dominated the alliance, the later and stronger of the two
  - "Three ribs in the mouth of it" — three chief conquests devoured (Babylon, Lydia, Egypt)

- **Leopard — Greece**
  - "After this I beheld, and lo another, like a leopard, which had upon the back of it four wings of a fowl; the beast had also four heads; and dominion was given to it" (Dan 7:6)
  - Four wings — the speed of Alexander's conquest (he reached the known world's edge by age 32)
  - Four heads — the four divisions the empire broke into after his death

- **The terrible beast — Rome**
  - "After this I saw in the night visions, and behold a fourth beast, dreadful and terrible, and strong exceedingly; and it had great iron teeth" (Dan 7:7)
  - No animal in nature could represent it — beyond classification, "diverse from all the beasts that were before it"
  - Iron teeth — connecting directly to the iron legs of Daniel 2's image
  - "It devoured and brake in pieces, and stamped the residue with the feet of it... and it had ten horns" (Dan 7:7)

[DYK🔎] Daniel 7 is the only chapter in the Bible that draws four different animals for four successive kingdoms AND supplies the interpretation within the same chapter (Dan 7:17, 23-24). God was not being cryptic. He gave the picture and the key together, so that no man would have to guess.

## The Bible Defines Its Own Symbols — Beast = Kingdom, Horn = King

**This is the hinge of the whole study, and it must be settled before any identity is claimed. We are not assigning meanings to the symbols. The angel does it for us, in the chapter, in plain words. Read the definitions before you read the application.**

- **A beast is a KINGDOM — the angel says so**
  - "These great beasts, which are four, are four kings, which shall arise out of the earth" (Dan 7:17)
  - "Thus he said, The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon earth" (Dan 7:23)
  - The "kings" and the "kingdom" are used interchangeably — a king and the kingdom he heads (compare Dan 2:38-39, "Thou art this head of gold... another kingdom shall arise after thee")
  - [STR] We do not have to argue that a beast means a kingdom. Verse 17 and verse 23 say it. The symbol is unlocked by the text, not by the interpreter.

- **A horn is a KING / kingdom — the angel says so**
  - "And the ten horns out of this kingdom are ten kings that shall arise: and another shall rise after them" (Dan 7:24)
  - Ten horns = ten kings; the little horn that follows = another king/kingdom of the same order
  - [→] The same rule we met in Study 0 — let the Author keep the meaning. Here the Author hands it over directly: beast = kingdom, horn = king. Every identification below stands on these two verses.

- **The four beasts ARE the four kingdoms of Daniel 2 — same sequence, in-chapter proof**
  - Babylon was named for us already: "Thou art this head of gold" (Dan 2:38) — the gold and the lion are the same kingdom
  - Daniel 7's fourth beast has the same "iron" as Daniel 2's iron legs (Dan 7:7; 2:40), divides into the same ten parts (Dan 7:7,24; 2:41-42), and is crushed by the same everlasting kingdom of God (Dan 7:27; 2:44)
  - [→] Two prophecies, two lenses, one history — "precept upon precept" (Isa 28:10). Daniel 7 does not start a new subject; it deepens Daniel 2.

## Ten Horns and One Little Horn

- **Ten horns — the breakup of Western Rome**
  - "And it had... ten horns" (Dan 7:7); "the ten horns out of this kingdom are ten kings that shall arise" (Dan 7:24)
  - Ten divisions arising OUT OF the fourth kingdom — the nations into which Western Rome fragmented
  - The same ten toes of Daniel 2's image — iron mixed with clay, "partly strong, and partly broken" (Dan 2:42)

- **The little horn rises among the ten**
  - "I considered the horns, and, behold, there came up among them another little horn, before whom there were three of the first horns plucked up by the roots" (Dan 7:8)
  - "And another shall rise after them" (Dan 7:24) — it comes up AFTER the ten, not before; later than the divisions, and AMONG them, so geographically within Western Europe
  - "He shall subdue three kings" (Dan 7:24) — three of the ten are plucked up by the roots to make room for it
  - [→] Not one political power simply replacing another. The text marks this horn as different in kind — "diverse from the first" (Dan 7:24).

- **"Eyes like the eyes of man, and a mouth speaking great things" (Dan 7:8)**
  - Eyes of a man — human intelligence, a human leadership at the head
  - A mouth speaking great things — claims of authority that exceed any earthly government
  - [→] A power with a man at its head making superhuman claims. The text itself fuses the human and the more-than-human, the political and the religious.

[Q] **"Why does Daniel call it 'little'?"**

> Because it did not begin as a military empire. It rose small and grew — not by the sword at the first, but by a religious influence that gathered political authority to itself until it could pluck up kings. The "little" describes its origin, not its outcome. By verse 20 the same horn's "look was more stout than his fellows."

## Three Identifying Marks — Daniel 7:25

**This is the core of the study. Three marks, one verse, and every one of them defined elsewhere in Scripture — not by us. Read the verse, then let the Bible say what each phrase means.**

> "And he shall speak great words against the most High, and shall wear out the saints of the most High, and think to change times and laws: and they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time." (Dan 7:25)

- **"And he shall speak great words against the most High" (Dan 7:25a)**

- **Mark 1: Blasphemy — and the Bible defines blasphemy**
  - "Great words against the most High" — not merely criticizing God, but assuming His prerogatives
  - We do not get to decide what blasphemy is. Christ's own accusers tell us, twice:
  - "Why doth this man thus speak blasphemies? who can forgive sins but God only?" (Mark 2:7) — claiming authority to forgive sins is blasphemy, _unless you are God_
  - "For a good work we stone thee not; but for blasphemy; and because that thou, being a man, makest thyself God" (John 10:33) — a man putting himself in God's place is blasphemy, _unless you are God_
  - [→] So Scripture has defined the mark objectively: a human institution that claims to forgive sins and to speak with God's own authority on earth has met it. This is not a slur; it is the Bible's own definition laid over the prophecy.

- **"And shall wear out the saints of the most High" (Dan 7:25b)**

- **Mark 2: Persecution — sustained, institutional, prolonged**
  - "Wear out" — not a single outburst, but a long grinding pressure on God's people
  - The same horn "made war with the saints, and prevailed against them" (Dan 7:21) — until the judgment intervenes (Dan 7:22)
  - Scripture itself ties this to a religious power "drunken with the blood of the saints" (Rev 17:6) — the same wearing-out seen from Revelation's vantage
  - [→] A power that BOTH claims divine authority AND wears out God's saints over centuries. Two marks together narrow the field to almost nothing.

- **"And think to change times and laws" (Dan 7:25c)**

- **Mark 3: Presuming to change God's law — and one commandment joins "times and laws"**
  - "Think to change" — to presume the right to alter (God's law itself is immutable, Ps 89:34; the claim is the offense)
  - "Times and laws" — and there is exactly one place in the moral law where TIME and LAW are joined together: the commandment that governs sacred time
  - "Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy... the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God... For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth... and rested the seventh day: wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it" (Exod 20:8-11)
  - This is the only commandment that fixes a TIME (the seventh day) by a LAW (a "Remember" command) — so a power presuming to "change times and laws" strikes precisely here
  - The Sabbath is God's own sign of authority within the law: "it is a sign between me and you... that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you" (Exod 31:13, 17)
  - [→] This is an objective textual claim, not yet a history lesson: the only command in which "times" and "laws" meet is the fourth. _(How that day was actually shifted, and by whose admitted authority, is documented from the record in Studies 24 and 28 — here we only fix what the prophecy aims at.)_

[ILL] If someone broke into a courthouse, removed the judge's signed ruling, and posted their own in its place, that would not be mere vandalism. It would be a claim of authority OVER the judge. To presume to change God's law is not a minor doctrinal adjustment. It is a claim to sit above the Lawgiver — which is exactly Mark 1 again, in another form.

## The Judgment Scene — The Court That Ends the Horn

**The chapter does not stop at naming the power. In the same vision Daniel is carried up to a courtroom — a real, future judgment that sits, opens books, and takes the horn's dominion away. This is the thread the whole prophetic slice will follow to its date.**

- **The court is convened — the Ancient of days sits and the books are opened**
  - "I beheld till the thrones were cast down, and the Ancient of days did sit, whose garment was white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool: his throne was like the fiery flame" (Dan 7:9)
  - "Thousand thousands ministered unto him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him: the judgment was set, and the books were opened" (Dan 7:10)
  - [STR] A seated judge, attending hosts, opened books — this is the language of a trial. Not the end of the world, but a court convened in heaven _before_ the end.

- **The Son of man is brought before the Ancient of days and receives the kingdom**
  - "I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him" (Dan 7:13)
  - "And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom... his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away" (Dan 7:14)
  - [→] The Son of man does not here come TO the earth — He comes TO the Ancient of days, in heaven, to receive a kingdom by the verdict of a court. A coronation that follows a trial.

- **The verdict goes for the saints and against the horn**
  - "Until the Ancient of days came, and judgment was given to the saints of the most High; and the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom" (Dan 7:22)
  - "But the judgment shall sit, and they shall take away his dominion, to consume and to destroy it unto the end" (Dan 7:26)
  - "And the kingdom and dominion... shall be given to the people of the saints of the most High, whose kingdom is an everlasting kingdom" (Dan 7:27)
  - [→] The same horn that "made war with the saints, and prevailed" (Dan 7:21) is overruled by a higher court. The persecuted are vindicated; the persecutor is stripped.

[STR] Hold this in mind through the whole slice: Daniel 7 opens a JUDGMENT that has a beginning ("the judgment was set"), a work (the books opened, examined), and a verdict (dominion taken from the horn, given to the saints). _When_ that court sits is the question Daniel 8-9 will answer with a date. The same chapter that names the power opens the trial that ends it — and that trial is the courtroom of the cosmic trial from Study 1, now set on a calendar.

## The 1260 Years — A Prophetic Period Introduced

**The horn is also given a calendar. We introduce it here; the rule that converts prophetic days into literal years is proved in the very next study, from Scripture's own mouth.**

- **"Until a time and times and the dividing of time" (Dan 7:25)**
  - Time (1) + times (2, the plural minimum) + the dividing of time (½) = 3½ prophetic "times"
  - A prophetic "time" is a year — Scripture's own usage; Nebuchadnezzar's "seven times" (Dan 4:25, 32) are seven years over one man — so 3½ times = 3½ years
  - At 360 days to the prophetic year, 3½ years = 1260 days
  - [→] The same period is marked SEVEN times across Daniel and Revelation, under three matching expressions — a sevenfold witness that this is one fixed period:
    - "a time and times and the dividing of time" (Dan 7:25)
    - "a time, times, and an half" (Dan 12:7)
    - "forty and two months" (Rev 11:2; 13:5)
    - "a thousand two hundred and threescore days" (Rev 11:3; 12:6)
    - "a time, and times, and half a time" (Rev 12:14)
  - 42 months × 30 days = 1260 days; 1260 days = "time, times, and half a time" — Scripture converts its own units, the figures lock together

[STR] But how do 1260 prophetic DAYS become 1260 literal YEARS? That conversion is not assumed here — it is the entire subject of Study 9, proved from God's own words: "each day for a year" (Num 14:34); "I have appointed thee each day for a year" (Eze 4:6). We introduce the period now; we earn the conversion next. No date in this series rests on a rule the Bible has not first stated.

## How History Identifies the Horn — The Fulfillment Lane

**Everything above was established from the Bible alone — the angel's definitions, the Scripture-defined marks, the period. Now, in its own clearly-marked lane, we lay the prophecy beside the historical record to see whether the predicted power actually arose. This is the historicist proof-form: the Bible predicts; history records.**

- **The ten horns — the divisions of Western Rome**
  - The prophecy: ten kingdoms arise out of the fourth empire (Dan 7:7, 24)
  - The record: as the Western Roman Empire dissolved (4th-5th centuries A.D.), it broke into the successor kingdoms of the barbarian nations — the divisions never reunited (compare Dan 2:43, "they shall not cleave one to another")

- **The three plucked horns — three Arian kingdoms uprooted**
  - The prophecy: three of the ten "plucked up by the roots" before the little horn (Dan 7:8, 24)
  - The record names three that were removed as the rising power consolidated: the **Heruli (A.D. 493)**, the **Vandals (A.D. 534)**, and the **Ostrogoths (A.D. 538)** — three kingdoms that stood in the way were eliminated one after another

- **The start of the 1260 — A.D. 538**
  - The prophecy: the saints given into the horn's hand for "a time and times and the dividing of time" (Dan 7:25)
  - The record: with the last opposing power (the Ostrogoths) driven from Rome in **A.D. 538**, the way was clear for the prophesied supremacy to begin — the opening of the 1260-year period

- **The close of the 1260 — A.D. 1798**
  - The arithmetic the next study will earn: 538 + 1260 = **1798**
  - The record: in **1798** the French general Berthier entered Rome and carried the pope away captive — supremacy interrupted on schedule, "to the year"
  - [→] Prophecy named a power that would rise among divided Rome, after the ten, plucking up three, and hold the saints for 1260 years. History supplied a power that did exactly that, on exactly that calendar.

[Q] **"But couldn't this fit several powers in history?"**

> Test it against ALL the marks at once, the way Scripture is built — "line upon line" (Isa 28:10). Only ONE power in history rises after Rome's ten divisions, AMONG them in Western Europe, uproots three kingdoms, claims to forgive sins and to stand in God's place, wears out the saints for centuries, presumes to alter the law touching sacred time, and runs exactly 1260 years from 538 to 1798. Any one mark might fit several powers. All of them together fit one. That is the method protecting us from guessing.

- **The deadly wound — previewed here, documented later**
  - The 1798 captivity is the same event Revelation calls a wound: "I saw one of his heads as it were wounded to death; and his deadly wound was healed" (Rev 13:3)
  - The wound was dealt in 1798; Revelation foretells its HEALING — meaning this prophecy is not merely past history
  - [→] If the wound heals, you are living inside the prophecy, not merely reading about it. _(The full deadly-wound-and-healing account, and the modern revival of the power, are documented at Revelation 13 in Study 21.)_

> "Paganism, while appearing to be vanquished, became the conqueror." — _The Great Controversy_, p. 49. _(A summary echo of what the Scriptures above have already established — the power that wore Rome's iron carried Rome's spirit forward. The proof is the prophecy; this only confirms it.)_

## Secondary: The Counterfeit System Consolidated

**Everything essential is now proved — the power is named by definition, mark, and date. What follows is a secondary application that draws Study 7 forward: the same horn that fulfills the marks also gathered the counterfeit doctrines that passed through the earlier empires. Each counterfeit is named only where Scripture states the original truth it displaces. This enriches the picture; it does not carry the identification, which already stands.**

- **Every counterfeit traces back to Satan's two original charges (Study 1)**
  - Charge A: "they only obey for reward." Charge B: "the law cannot be kept."
  - The horn's three marks serve those charges: blasphemy misrepresents God's character (so the image cannot be rightly restored); wearing out the saints tests whether loyalty survives without comfort (Charge A); presuming to change the law implies it was never the immovable standard (Charge B)
  - [→] Prophecy does not merely identify a system; it exposes the accuser working _through_ a system — the little horn is the cosmic-trial strategy of Study 1 given institutional form.

- **One Mediator displaced — the truth that names the counterfeit**
  - The Bible: "For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus" (1 Tim 2:5)
  - The counterfeit: a human priest-class inserted between the sinner and God

- **Grace displaced by merit**
  - The Bible: "For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God" (Eph 2:8)
  - The counterfeit: salvation as a transaction of accumulated human merit

- **The sleep of death displaced by the immortal soul**
  - The Bible: "For the living know that they shall die: but the dead know not any thing" (Eccl 9:5)
  - The counterfeit: a naturally immortal soul — the very lie of Eden, "ye shall not surely die" (Gen 3:4) — which opens the door to prayers for the dead and, later, to spiritualism _(treated fully in Study 27)_

- **The Sabbath displaced — the mark already examined**
  - The Bible: the seventh-day Sabbath, sealed in the law as God's sign (Exod 20:8-11; 31:13)
  - The counterfeit: a substituted day, resting on claimed authority rather than "Thus saith the LORD" _(documented in Studies 24 and 28)_

- **Daniel foresaw the merger — iron mixed with clay**
  - "Thou sawest the feet and toes, part of potters' clay, and part of iron, the kingdom shall be divided" (Dan 2:41)
  - "They shall mingle themselves with the seed of men: but they shall not cleave one to another" (Dan 2:43)
  - Iron (Rome's civil power) mixed with clay (a religious element) — church and state fused
  - [→] Every counterfeit that passed through the pagan empires found a permanent home in one consolidated system. Not coincidence — consolidation.

[TANGENT] Seen separately, these look like four unrelated theological disputes. Seen through the sanctuary lens of Study 5, they form one system, each piece blocking a different door the sanctuary opens — Mediator, grace, the state of the dead, the day of worship. Together they obscure the entire Way Home. But note well: the IDENTIFICATION of the power does not rest on this overlay. It rests on Daniel 7's own definitions, marks, and calendar. The overlay only shows _why_ this power matters.

[Q] **"Are we attacking the people inside this system?"**

> Never. Prophecy identifies the SYSTEM, not the individuals within it. God has faithful children in every communion: "And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring" (John 10:16). The later call is "Come out of her, my people" (Rev 18:4) — He calls them His people _while they are still inside_. To name the system is not to condemn a soul; it is the first kindness required before anyone can be called home out of it.

## Appeal

- God did not leave the identity of the consolidating power to speculation, prejudice, or guesswork
- He defined His own symbols in the chapter — beast is kingdom, horn is king (Dan 7:17, 23, 24) — so the reader assigns nothing
- He gave three marks, each defined elsewhere in His own word — blasphemy (Mark 2:7; John 10:33), wearing out the saints (Rev 17:6; Dan 7:21), presuming to change the law touching time (Exod 20:8-11)
- He set a calendar — 1260 years — and history kept it from 538 to 1798
- And in the same vision He opened a JUDGMENT — a court that sits, opens books, and gives the kingdom to the saints (Dan 7:9-10, 22, 26-27)
- This is not conspiracy and not cleverness. It is Daniel 7, read plainly, checked against history, and measured by Scripture's own definitions
- The question is no longer _whether_ prophecy has been fulfilled. The question is: now that the court has opened and the verdict is coming, on which side of it will you stand?
