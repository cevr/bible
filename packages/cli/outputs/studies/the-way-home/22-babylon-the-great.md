# Study 22: Babylon the Great — The Woman Who Rides the Beast

**The whole series has named the place of exile "Babylon" — but until now it has been a word. Revelation 17 pulls back the curtain and shows the thing itself: a scarlet woman, drunk with the blood of saints, riding a seven-headed beast and steering it with the reins of the kings of the earth. By the Bible's own dictionary — a woman is a church, waters are peoples, a beast is a kingdom — Heaven hands us the identification in plain symbols it has already defined: Babylon the Great is the apostate church wielding the civil power, mother of a brood of harlot daughters, the same wounded-and-revived Roman power of Revelation 13, "that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth." This is the city you were born into. This is the city you are being called out of.**

## The Bible Hands Us a Dictionary Before It Hands Us the Riddle

**Revelation 17 is the one chapter where the angel does not leave us to guess. The same scene that shows the woman, the beast, and the waters STOPS and defines each one. This is the rule of Study 0 — Scripture its own expositor — applied to the hardest chapter in the book. We do not import a meaning; we let the angel supply it.**

- **"I will tell thee the mystery" — the angel offers to interpret his own symbol**
  - "And the angel said unto me, Wherefore didst thou marvel? I will tell thee the mystery of the woman, and of the beast that carrieth her" (Rev 17:7)
  - John "wondered with great admiration" (Rev 17:6) — and the angel's answer to wonder is not silence but explanation. The chapter is built to be understood.
  - [STR] Three symbols, three God-given definitions inside the chapter: the WATERS (v.15), the WOMAN (v.18), the BEAST (vv.8-11, cross-referenced to ch. 13). We never have to invent a single one.

- **"The waters... are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues"**
  - "And he saith unto me, The waters which thou sawest, where the whore sitteth, are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues" (Rev 17:15)
  - The woman "sitteth upon many waters" (Rev 17:1) — that is, she rules over a vast population. Heaven defines its own figure before we can mistake it.
  - [→] This is the same self-defining method that read "candlestick = church, star = its messenger" in Rev 1:20 (Study 10). The Author always keeps the key.

- **"The woman... is that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth"**
  - "And the woman which thou sawest is that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth" (Rev 17:18)
  - Not a future city, not a mystery to be cracked by clever men — the angel says plainly: she IS a great city that, at the time of the vision, REIGNS over kings. One city in John's day reigned over the kings of the earth — and it has reigned in a new form ever since.

- **"A beast... shall be a fourth kingdom"** — the standing definition from Daniel
  - "Thus he said, The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon earth" (Dan 7:23)
  - Revelation does not redefine its own grammar; it inherits Daniel's. A beast is a kingdom / political power. We carry that definition straight in from Studies 8 and 21.
  - [→] So before the woman is even described, the dictionary is already on the table: waters = peoples, woman = a ruling religious-city power, beast = a kingdom. The riddle reads itself.

## A Woman Is a Church — And There Are Only Two of Them

**The single most important symbol to fix is the WOMAN. The Bible uses a woman, consistently, for a church or covenant-people — and it uses a PURE woman for the faithful church and a CORRUPT woman for the apostate one. Revelation gives us both, deliberately, as a matched pair. Get this one definition from Scripture and the whole chapter falls open.**

- **The PURE woman = the true church, espoused as a chaste virgin to Christ**
  - "For I am jealous over you with godly jealousy: for I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ" (2 Cor 11:2)
  - Paul calls the church a betrothed virgin. The relationship between Christ and His people is a marriage — so unfaithfulness to Him is pictured as adultery.
  - "the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready... arrayed in fine linen, clean and white: for the fine linen is the righteousness of saints" (Rev 19:7-8) — the true church is the Lamb's clean-clothed bride.
  - This is the woman of Revelation 12 — "clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars" (Rev 12:1), the faithful church of Study 20.

- **The CORRUPT woman = the apostate church — a wife turned harlot**
  - This is no abstract figure. The prophets used it of God's own people when they were unfaithful: "How is the faithful city become an harlot! it was full of judgment; righteousness lodged in it; but now murderers" (Isa 1:21).
  - Ezekiel makes the picture exact: "But thou didst trust in thine own beauty, and playedst the harlot because of thy renown" (Eze 16:15) — and defines what spiritual harlotry IS: "But as a wife that committeth adultery, which taketh strangers instead of her husband!" (Eze 16:32). A harlot-church is a covenant-people who has taken another lover in the place of God.
  - [DYK🔎] Ezekiel 23 sets two harlot-sisters side by side — "there were two women, the daughters of one mother... Aholah the elder, and Aholibah her sister... Samaria is Aholah, and Jerusalem Aholibah" (Eze 23:2-4). The Bible is entirely comfortable picturing apostate CHURCHES as women, even naming MULTIPLE harlot-churches who share one mother. Hold that thought — Revelation 17 will give us a mother and her daughters.

- **[STR] Two women, the whole controversy:** the pure woman of Revelation 12 (the faithful church the dragon hates, Study 20) and the scarlet woman of Revelation 17 (the apostate church the dragon RIDES). They are the same symbol — a church — in two opposite conditions. Revelation set them as bookends on purpose. To read Rev 17's woman as anything other than a church is to break the Bible's own consistent figure (John 10:35).

[Q] **"Isn't calling a church a 'whore' just inflammatory name-calling?"**
→ No — it is the Bible's own precise vocabulary, and it is a MERCY, because it tells you exactly what went wrong. A virgin betrothed to one husband (2 Cor 11:2) who "taketh strangers instead of her husband" (Eze 16:32) is, by definition, unfaithful. The charge is not "she has bad people in her"; the charge is that the church which should have been wedded to Christ alone has gone after another lover. The next section names the lover — and it is not who you might expect.

## What She Did: She Married the Kings of the Earth

**A wife's faithfulness is to her husband. The church's husband is Christ (2 Cor 11:2). So her "fornication" is not vague immorality — Scripture defines it as an UNLAWFUL UNION with the powers of this world. Babylon's signature sin is the union of the church with the STATE. That is the meaning the text itself supplies; nothing here is spiritualized in.**

- **The woman commits fornication WITH KINGS — church wedded to state**
  - "With whom the kings of the earth have committed fornication, and the inhabitants of the earth have been made drunk with the wine of her fornication" (Rev 17:2)
  - A church that should cling to Christ as her only husband has instead embraced the civil rulers. The "friendship of the world is enmity with God... whosoever therefore will be a friend of the world is the enemy of God" (James 4:4) — the New Testament's own word for an unlawful church-world union.
  - [→] This is exactly what the PURE woman did NOT do. The faithful church of Rev 12 was persecuted BY the kings of the earth; the harlot of Rev 17 is in bed WITH them. That is the whole difference between a martyr-church and Babylon.

- **She RIDES the beast — the church steering the civil power**
  - "I saw a woman sit upon a scarlet coloured beast... having seven heads and ten horns" (Rev 17:3)
  - A beast is a kingdom (Dan 7:23). A woman riding a beast is a church controlling, directing, mounted upon a political power. She is not the beast — she is the rider who tells the beast where to go. This is the picture of a church that wields the sword of the state to do her bidding.
  - [STR] The whole anatomy of Babylon is in that one image: a religious power (the woman) using a civil/political power (the beast) to enforce her will. Church-state union — the ecclesiastical riding the civil — is the literal doctrine the symbol teaches.

- **The golden cup = false doctrine she makes the nations drink**
  - "having a golden cup in her hand full of abominations and filthiness of her fornication" (Rev 17:4)
  - The cup is gorgeous on the outside — gold — and poison within. Jeremiah used the very same figure of literal Babylon: "Babylon hath been a golden cup in the LORD'S hand, that made all the earth drunken: the nations have drunken of her wine; therefore the nations are mad" (Jer 51:7).
  - [→] What is the wine? The teaching that turns sober nations mad. The cup is an APPLICATION of a doctrine proved elsewhere in this series — Sunday for Sabbath (Dan 7:25, Studies 8/24), the immortal soul for the resurrection (Eccl 9:5, Study 27), a human priesthood for the one Mediator (1 Tim 2:5). The doctrine that those are errors is proved literally in their own studies; here the cup simply pictures HOW Babylon spreads them — beautifully packaged, intoxicating, world-wide.

[ILL] A wedding ring on the right hand is honour; the same ring slipped onto another man's hand in secret is adultery. The metal has not changed — the LOYALTY has. The church was given every gift to adorn the bride of Christ; Babylon took those gifts and used them to court the kings of the earth instead. Her glory is real — purple, scarlet, gold, pearls — which is precisely why the harlotry is so deadly: it does not look like ruin. It looks like a queen.

## The Name on Her Forehead — And the Beast Underneath Her

**Now the angel writes the identification across the woman's own forehead, and then turns to the beast she rides and gives it a fingerprint we have already taken twice in this series. Two witnesses — the name above and the beast below — converge on one power.**

- **Her name: MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS**
  - "And upon her forehead was a name written, MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS AND ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH" (Rev 17:5)
  - **MYSTERY.** Paul named "the mystery of iniquity" already at work in his day, the apostasy that would head up in "that man of sin" (2 Thess 2:7; cf. 2:3-4, Studies 7-8). Revelation puts that same word on the woman's brow. Babylon is the mystery of iniquity grown to its full stature.
  - **THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS.** A mother implies daughters. If the woman is a fallen church, her daughters are other fallen churches sprung from her — bodies that came out of her but carried her errors with them. (Remember Ezekiel's "two women, the daughters of one mother," Eze 23:4 — the Bible's own picture of a mother-church with harlot daughters.)
  - [→] This is the verse that DEFINES the Babylon of the rest of Revelation. When the second angel cries "Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication" (Rev 14:8), and when the voice says "Come out of her, my people" (Rev 18:4) — THIS woman, here named, is who they mean. Revelation 17 is the master-key to Revelation 14 and 18.

- **The beast under her = the same beast as Revelation 13**
  - Compare the fingerprints. Rev 13's sea-beast: "a beast rise up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns... upon his heads the name of blasphemy" (Rev 13:1). Rev 17's beast: "a scarlet coloured beast, full of names of blasphemy, having seven heads and ten horns" (Rev 17:3). Seven heads, ten horns, full of blasphemy — the SAME beast.
  - And the decisive match — the wound-and-revival: "The beast that thou sawest was, and is not; and shall ascend out of the bottomless pit... when they behold the beast that was, and is not, and yet is" (Rev 17:8). That is Rev 13:3 in other words: "one of his heads as it were wounded to death; and his deadly wound was healed: and all the world wondered after the beast."
  - [STR] **"Was, and is not, and yet is"** = the three phases the world watched: the Roman power that WAS in full supremacy, that for a season IS NOT (the deadly wound), and that YET IS (revived). Same power, same biography as Revelation 13. By Scripture's own cross-reference (John 10:35), the woman is riding the papal-Roman power of Revelation 13 (Study 21).

- **"That great city which reigneth over the kings of the earth"** — the city named
  - "And the woman which thou sawest is that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth" (Rev 17:18)
  - Spoken to John, in the first century, ONE city reigned over the kings of the earth — Rome. And the woman is not pagan Rome with its emperors but the RELIGIOUS power that succeeded the empire on the same seven hills and "reigneth over the kings of the earth" in the spiritual realm — Rome in its papal phase. The angel hands us the city; we do not have to deduce it.
  - [DYK🔎] The angel adds, "The seven heads are seven mountains, on which the woman sitteth" (Rev 17:9). The Bible literally pictures her seated on seven mountains — a detail every reader in John's day would have read in one direction, toward the city famous through all antiquity as the city on seven hills.

[Q] **"How can the SAME power be both the beast AND the woman? Isn't that a contradiction?"**
→ It is two true things about one apostasy seen from two angles. As a persecuting CIVIL/political power, Rome is a BEAST (the meaning we carried from Daniel 7 and Rev 13). As a corrupt CHURCH wielding that power, the same apostasy is a WOMAN. The genius of Revelation 17 is to show you both at once: the religious system (woman) MOUNTED ON and steering the political system (beast). That is not a contradiction — it is the very definition of church-state union, which is the one thing the whole vision is about. The beast carries; the woman rides.

## Drunk With the Blood of the Saints

**One mark above all others nails the identification beyond mistake, because it cannot be faked and cannot be hidden — a documented record of persecution. The woman is not merely doctrinally corrupt; she has killed. And the power she rides was foretold to do exactly this.**

- **"Drunken with the blood of the saints"**
  - "And I saw the woman drunken with the blood of the saints, and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus" (Rev 17:6)
  - Not "stained" — DRUNKEN. So saturated with the blood of the faithful that she staggers with it. This is a persecuting religious power, not a private sect of bad theology.
  - This matches, exactly, the prophetic line we have been tracing: Daniel's little horn "shall wear out the saints of the most High" (Dan 7:25), "made war with the saints, and prevailed against them" (Dan 7:21); Revelation 13's beast was given "to make war with the saints, and to overcome them" (Rev 13:7). One predicted persecutor, one historical record of blood — Daniel, Revelation 13, and Revelation 17 name a single power (John 10:35).
  - [→] And Revelation 18 totals the account: "And in her was found the blood of prophets, and of saints, and of all that were slain upon the earth" (Rev 18:24). The blood-guilt of Babylon is summed at the end exactly as it is described here at the start.

[Document from history — the blood is on the record] The Bible PREDICTED a persecuting power "drunken with the blood of the saints" (Rev 17:6) and stretching across the 1260 years (538-1798, Studies 8/21). History RECORDS the fulfillment in detail:

- The Albigensian crusades of the 13th century and the suppression of the Waldenses in the Piedmont valleys (the wilderness-church of Study 20) — whole communities exterminated for refusing the Roman cup.
- The medieval and Spanish **Inquisitions**, institutions created expressly to find, try, and deliver "heretics" to the civil arm for execution — the very mechanism of a woman riding a beast: the church judging, the state burning.
- The Reformation-era martyrdoms — Hus burned at Constance (1415) despite a safe-conduct, Tyndale strangled and burned (1536), the Marian fires in England, the St. Bartholomew's Day massacre of French Protestants (1572).
- Historians' tallies of those slain over the centuries of the supremacy run into the millions. (The DATE-and-FACT of these events belongs to the historical record; the MEANING — that this is the predicted persecuting woman — is proved from the Bible above.)

- **The scarlet and the purple — the colors are on the record too**
  - "And the woman was arrayed in purple and scarlet colour, and decked with gold and precious stones and pearls" (Rev 17:4)
  - [DYK🔎] Purple and scarlet are, by long and public custom, the chief colors of the highest Roman ecclesiastical robes — the scarlet of the cardinalate, the purple of prelacy. The Bible drew the portrait; history supplies the matching wardrobe. (Color = labeled corroboration, not the proof; the proof is the blood, the cup, and the beast underneath.)

## The Mother and Her Daughters

**"THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS" (Rev 17:5) is not an insult thrown in passing — it is a structural claim. It tells us Babylon is not ONE church but a FAMILY: a mother-church and the daughter-churches that came out of her yet kept her fatal errors. This is the verse that lets the call "Come out of her" reach far beyond a single communion.**

- **A mother of harlots implies a brood of harlot daughters**
  - "THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS AND ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH" (Rev 17:5)
  - Ezekiel already gave the pattern: harlot-churches as "the daughters of one mother" (Eze 23:2-4). A harlot's daughters are themselves harlots if they walk in her ways. The daughters are not the world at large — they are CHURCHES, born of the mother, bearing the family likeness.
  - [→] What makes a daughter a HARLOT like her mother? The same sin: union with the world/state in the place of fidelity to Christ (Rev 17:2; James 4:4), and the same cup of error in her hand. A church that left the mother but kept the mother's wine — Sunday for Sabbath, the immortal soul, a worldly alliance — is a daughter who never left Babylon in heart.

- **"Babylon" is therefore broader than one communion**
  - The second angel says she "made ALL NATIONS drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication" (Rev 14:8) — a world-wide system, not a single building.
  - Revelation 16 shows "the great city... divided into three parts" (Rev 16:19) and Rev 16:13 names a THREEFOLD confederacy (dragon, beast, false prophet, Studies 21/27/28). Babylon at the end is a coalition: the mother-church, her fallen daughters, and the powers of spiritualism — all sharing one cup, all riding one beast.
  - [STR] So "Babylon the Great" = the whole apostate religious system of earth — the mother-church AND every daughter-church that drinks and serves the same wine of false doctrine and world-union. That is precisely why the final call is not "leave that ONE church" but "Come out of HER, my people" (Rev 18:4) — out of the whole intoxicated family.

[Q] **"Then is everyone inside these churches lost? Is Babylon just a slur for everyone who isn't us?"**
→ No — and the next verse forbids that reading. The voice from heaven says, "Come out of her, MY PEOPLE" (Rev 18:4). God's own sincere children are INSIDE Babylon at the moment of the call. The system is fallen; the people in it are precious — many of them are the lost sheep whose "shepherds have caused them to go astray" (Jer 50:6). "Babylon" condemns a SYSTEM of error and world-union, never the honest hearts trapped in it. That is the entire reason there is a call at all: you do not summon people out of a place where there is no one to save.

## Why It Was Always Called "Babylon"

**The series has used "Babylon" as the name of the place of exile from the start. Now we can see it was never an arbitrary label. The literal city of Babylon, in the Bible's own history, is the exact pattern of the end-time apostate system — and Revelation borrows her name on purpose.**

- **Babylon = the original captor of God's people**
  - The historical Babylon carried God's people into literal exile — "By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat down, yea, we wept, when we remembered Zion" (Ps 137:1). She held the saints captive, far from the city of God. The spiritual Babylon does the same: holds God's people captive in a system far from the truth.

- **Babylon = the proud "lady of kingdoms" who said "I sit a queen"**
  - "Sit thou silent, and get thee into darkness, O daughter of the Chaldeans: for thou shalt no more be called, The lady of kingdoms" (Isa 47:5); "thou saidst, I shall be a lady for ever... I am, and none else beside me; I shall not sit as a widow" (Isa 47:7-8).
  - [→] Revelation echoes that exact boast in the fall of the great city: "she saith in her heart, I sit a queen, and am no widow" (the language of Rev 18:7 picks up Isaiah's Babylon). The spiritual Babylon speaks her literal ancestor's very lines. The name was chosen because the CHARACTER matches.

- **Babylon = the golden cup that made the nations drunk**
  - "Babylon hath been a golden cup in the LORD'S hand, that made all the earth drunken... therefore the nations are mad" (Jer 51:7) — and Revelation puts that identical cup into the woman's hand (Rev 17:4; 14:8). Same cup, same intoxication, same madness.
  - And the same command of escape: of literal Babylon, "Flee out of the midst of Babylon, and deliver every man his soul" (Jer 51:6); of spiritual Babylon, "Come out of her, my people" (Rev 18:4). The pattern is deliberate from end to end.

[DYK🔎] The name "Babel/Babylon" first appears at the tower where God "did there confound the language" (Gen 11:9) — confusion. The literal city was the fountainhead of organized rebellion against God, of false worship, of pride that "sits a queen." Revelation does not need a clever etymology to make the point; it needs the BIBLE'S OWN HISTORY of Babylon — captor, proud queen, golden cup of madness — laid alongside the woman of Revelation 17. The match is the proof. The spiritual Babylon is the literal Babylon's true heir.

[TANGENT] Notice the symmetry of the whole Bible: it opens with Babel rising on the plain of Shinar (Gen 11) and closes with "Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen" (Rev 18:2). The first city built against God and the last system built against God carry the same name. The story of redemption runs from Babylon to the New Jerusalem — from the city of confusion to the city of God. Every reader in this series is, at this moment, somewhere on the road between those two cities. Revelation 17 is the map that tells you which one you are standing in.

## What Happens to Her — The Beast Turns on Its Rider

**The vision does not leave Babylon enthroned. The same beast she rode turns and devours her. This is the certainty the whole "come out" appeal rests on: the system you are called to leave is a system already sentenced to fall.**

- **The kings she seduced will turn and destroy her**
  - "And the ten horns which thou sawest upon the beast, these shall hate the whore, and shall make her desolate and naked, and shall eat her flesh, and burn her with fire" (Rev 17:16)
  - The very civil powers she married will at last loathe and destroy her — the adulterous union ends in betrayal. "For God hath put in their hearts to fulfil his will... until the words of God shall be fulfilled" (Rev 17:17). Even Babylon's downfall serves God's word.
  - [→] The rider is thrown and trampled by her own mount. The church that trusted the sword of the state perishes by it. This is the end the second angel already announced as good as done: "Babylon is fallen, is fallen" (Rev 14:8).

- **They make war with the Lamb — and lose**
  - "These shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings: and they that are with him are called, and chosen, and faithful" (Rev 17:14)
  - The final issue is never in doubt. Babylon and her beast make their last stand against the Lamb, and the Lamb wins — and those WITH Him are "called, and chosen, and faithful." That is the company on the other side of the call to come out.

[ILL] Imagine being warned that the grand hotel you are lodging in is built over a fault line that engineers have already condemned — and the warning comes not from a stranger but from the architect Himself, who tells you the exact day the order to demolish was signed. You would not finish unpacking. The certainty of Babylon's fall is not a threat held over God's people; it is the kindness behind the call. He shows you the condemnation notice (Rev 17:16; 14:8; 18:2) precisely so you will not be in the building when it comes down.

## Appeal

- Revelation 17 was given so that you would not be deceived about WHERE you are. The whole series called the exile "Babylon"; this chapter shows you her face — a church that left her Husband to ride the kings of the earth, a golden cup of intoxicating error, a forehead marked MOTHER OF HARLOTS, robes stained with the blood of the saints.
- And yet — "Come out of her, MY PEOPLE" (Rev 18:4). The most stunning word in the chapter is that God still has PEOPLE inside her. Honest, loved, sincere people, drinking from a beautiful cup, never told what was in it.
- The cosmic trial (Study 1) asked whether God's people serve Him for truth or only for reward, and whether the world can be made to drink a lie and call it gold. Babylon is the proof of how far the deception goes — and the call to come out is the proof of how far the mercy goes.
- You cannot ride the beast and follow the Lamb. You cannot drink Babylon's cup and keep the wedding garment "clean and white" (Rev 19:8). At some point the wine has to be set down.
- The woman has been unmasked. The city has been named. The order of demolition has been signed and shown to you in advance. The only question Revelation 17 leaves on the table is the question of Study 23: when the voice from heaven says "Come out of her, my people" — will you go home?
