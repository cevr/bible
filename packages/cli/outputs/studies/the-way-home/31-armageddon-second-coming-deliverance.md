# Study 31: Armageddon, the Second Coming, and Deliverance

**Probation has closed, the seal is set, and the seven last plagues are falling — and now the sixth plague gathers the whole world for one last muster. Three frog-spirits go forth to assemble the kings of the earth to the battle of the great day of God Almighty, a place called Armageddon. But the host that gathers to destroy the sealed remnant never lands its blow. The sky splits, and the King appears — literal, visible, audible, global. The last confederacy is shattered, the dead in Christ rise incorruptible, the living saints are caught up, and the travelers of the long road home are delivered and gathered to go home with the One they waited for. The darkest hour breaks into the brightest morning.**

## Where We Stand — One Line of Bridge

**Study 30 left the traveler in the dark: probation is shut (Michael has stood up, Dan 12:1; the decree is fixed, Rev 22:11; the temple is filled with smoke and no man can enter, Rev 15:8), the saints are sealed and pass through the time of Jacob's trouble under the death decree, and the seven last plagues are falling unmingled with mercy on the mark-bearers. We do not re-walk that ground. We pick up at the SIXTH plague — the one that gathers the world for its final assault — and follow it through to the deliverance.**

- The five plagues have already fallen (Rev 16:2-11). The world is in agony, and it blames the people of God for the calamity.
- **Central beat:** the next vial does not bring the wicked to their knees in repentance — it musters them for war. And that war has a name.

## The Sixth Plague — The World Gathered to Armageddon

**Here is the single thing the original study lacked: Armageddon. Revelation names a final, global gathering of the nations against God — and the Bible itself tells us what the imagery means. We prove the meaning from Scripture, then note where the established historical interpretation of "Euphrates" comes from.**

- **The vial that dries the river**
  - "And the sixth angel poured out his vial upon the great river Euphrates; and the water thereof was dried up, that the way of the kings of the east might be prepared" (Rev 16:12)
  - The Euphrates is dried up — and the drying "prepares the way." Something is being CLEARED so something else can come.
  - [STR] To read this without spiritualizing, we let Scripture define its own water-symbol — and it does.

- **The Bible's own dictionary: many waters = peoples; the overflowing river = the invading power**
  - "the waters which thou sawest... are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues" (Rev 17:15) — Scripture's plain definition of a prophetic flood of "waters."
  - "the Lord bringeth up upon them the waters of the river, strong and many, even the king of Assyria, and all his glory: and he shall come up over all his channels, and go over all his banks" (Isa 8:7) — here God Himself calls an invading EMPIRE "the waters of the river."
  - "Woe to the multitude of many people, which make a noise like the noise of the seas; and to the rushing of nations, that make a rushing like the rushing of mighty waters!" (Isa 17:12)
  - [→] So "the great river Euphrates" stands for the peoples and powers that sustain Babylon — the political and military support of the harlot system. When that "river" dries up, the human support of the persecuting power collapses; its confidence fails and its strength evaporates. This is not allegory imported from outside; it is the Bible's own figure (Rev 17:15; Isa 8:7), applied by the Bible's own rule (compare spiritual with spiritual, 1 Cor 2:13).

[DYK🔎] **Why "Euphrates," and why "kings of the east"?** In the older prophecy of the sixth TRUMPET, "the four angels which are bound in the great river Euphrates" (Rev 9:14) were identified — and history confirmed — as the Ottoman / Turkish power seated on the literal Euphrates (Studies 12-13, the keystone 1840 exhibit). The series has already established that lane; we invoke it, not re-prove it. Just as ancient Babylon fell the night Cyrus diverted the literal Euphrates and "the kings of the east" (the Medo-Persians, who came from the east) marched in on the dry channel, so the drying of mystical Babylon's "river" prepares the way for the true King of the east. For "the Sun of righteousness" rises in the east: "as the lightning cometh out of the east, and shineth even unto the west; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" (Matt 24:27). The drying river clears the road for the advent.

- **The three frog-spirits — the confederacy gathers the world**
  - "And I saw three unclean spirits like frogs come out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet" (Rev 16:13)
  - "For they are the spirits of devils, working miracles, which go forth unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty" (Rev 16:14)
  - Three mouths, three powers — the dragon, the beast, the false prophet: the very threefold confederacy unmasked in Study 28. "Spirits like frogs" working miracles — note the **mouth**: this is gathered by a lying message, by "the spirits of devils, working miracles." The same lying wonders the reader was armed against in Study 27 here muster the entire planet.
  - [→] Notice WHO does the gathering and WHY. It is not God assembling the nations to be blessed — it is demonic spirits assembling them to make war on "that great day of God Almighty." The world is united at last, but united against heaven.

- **The name of the muster**
  - "And he gathered them together into a place called in the Hebrew tongue Armageddon" (Rev 16:16)
  - The Hebrew tongue gives the name: _Har-Magedon_ — most literally "the mountain of Megiddo," the great battle-plain of Israel where God's enemies were so often broken (Judg 5; 2 Kgs 23). Scripture itself locates the meaning in a battlefield, not a metaphor: this is a real, final, global confrontation between the powers of earth and the King of heaven.
  - [STR] **Armageddon in this prophecy is the LAST MUSTER of the living wicked against the living saints, BEFORE the millennium** — gathered under the sixth plague, broken at the appearing of Christ. Hold that timing; we will sharply distinguish it from a different battle in a moment.

[Q] **"Isn't Armageddon a literal battle between modern armies in the Middle East?"**
→ The text refuses that reading on its own terms. The gatherers are "the spirits of devils, working miracles" (Rev 16:14) — a SPIRITUAL, deceiving agency, not geopolitics. The ones gathered are "the kings of the earth AND of the WHOLE WORLD" (Rev 16:14) — global, not regional. And the One they finally face is not a rival nation but "that great day of God Almighty" — Christ Himself at His coming (Rev 17:14; 19:11-21). So Armageddon is literal in that it is a real, final assembling of the world's powers under a demonic deception — but its opponent is heaven, and its outcome is decided not by armies but by the appearing of the King. The meaning rests on the chain of plain texts (Rev 16:13-16 → 17:14 → 19:11-21), not on a newspaper.

## Armageddon Is NOT the Battle After the Thousand Years

**This is the defect the spec names most pointedly: Revelation describes TWO final assaults, a thousand years apart, and they must never be merged. Scripture keeps them distinct by their own markers.**

- **The pre-millennial muster — Armageddon (Rev 16-19)**
  - Gathered under the sixth of the seven last plagues, while probation has just closed and the living wicked still cover the earth (Rev 16:12-16).
  - Broken at the SECOND COMING: "These shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings" (Rev 17:14).
  - The slaughter is by "the sword of him that sat upon the horse, which sword proceeded out of his mouth" (Rev 19:21) — the brightness and word of His coming.

- **The post-millennial assault — Gog and Magog (Rev 20:7-9)**
  - "And when the thousand years are expired, Satan shall be loosed out of his prison" (Rev 20:7) — note the time-stamp: AFTER the 1000 years.
  - "And shall go out to deceive the nations which are in the four quarters of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them together to battle: the number of whom is as the sand of the sea" (Rev 20:8) — these are the RESURRECTED wicked of the second resurrection, mustered against the New Jerusalem.
  - "And they went up on the breadth of the earth, and compassed the camp of the saints about, and the beloved city: and fire came down from God out of heaven, and devoured them" (Rev 20:9) — destroyed by fire from heaven around the descended city.

- **The distinction, side by side**

|                     | Armageddon (Rev 16:16; 17:14; 19:11-21)          | Gog and Magog (Rev 20:7-9)                                             |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **When**            | Under the 6th plague, BEFORE the millennium      | AFTER the 1000 years expire                                            |
| **Who is gathered** | The LIVING wicked, while probation just closed   | The RESURRECTED wicked (2nd resurrection), "as the sand of the sea"    |
| **The target**      | The sealed LIVING saints on earth                | "the camp of the saints... the beloved city" (descended New Jerusalem) |
| **What ends it**    | The SECOND COMING of Christ (sword of His mouth) | FIRE from heaven, after the final judgment                             |
| **Satan**           | Working through the threefold confederacy        | "loosed out of his prison" for a little season                         |

- [→] The two battles bracket the thousand years — one closes the present age, one closes earth's history. Merge them and the millennium vanishes and the whole consummation is thrown into confusion; keep them distinct (by their own time-stamps, Rev 16:12 vs Rev 20:7) and the picture is one harmonious whole. This is exactly the "never break Scripture against itself" rule of Study 0 (John 10:35) applied to the last two battles of the Book. The post-millennial assault belongs to Study 32; here we follow Armageddon to its breaking.

[DYK🔎] Revelation 19 supplies the bridge from Armageddon to the advent. The same beast and false prophet who sent out the frog-spirits (Rev 16:13) are named again at the climax: "the beast was taken, and with him the false prophet... These both were cast alive into a lake of fire" (Rev 19:20), "and the remnant were slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse" (Rev 19:21). The muster of chapter 16 and the breaking of chapter 19 are one event seen from two angles — gathering, then judgment — separated by the appearing of the rider called Faithful and True.

## The Last Confederacy Shattered — The King Appears

**Armageddon has no second act. The world's last army assembles to annihilate the defenceless remnant — and the heavens open. The deliverance is not won by the saints; it is won FOR them, by the literal appearing of the King.**

- **The rider on the white horse**
  - "And I saw heaven opened, and behold a white horse; and he that sat upon him was called Faithful and True, and in righteousness he doth judge and make war" (Rev 19:11)
  - "And out of his mouth goeth a sharp sword, that with it he should smite the nations... And he hath on his vesture and on his thigh a name written, KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS" (Rev 19:15-16)
  - The very title that overcomes Armageddon in Rev 17:14 ("Lord of lords, and King of kings") is the name written on the rider's thigh. The gathered powers "make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them" (Rev 17:14) — not the saints overcoming, the LAMB overcoming.

- **The saints do not fight — they stand still and are delivered**
  - The deliverance follows the oldest pattern in the Book: "Fear ye not, stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD... The LORD shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace" (Ex 14:13-14).
  - "Then shall the LORD go forth, and fight against those nations, as when he fought in the day of battle" (Zech 14:3).
  - [→] At the Red Sea the people had a wall of water before them and Pharaoh's chariots behind — no weapon, no escape, only the command to stand still. The sealed remnant at Armageddon are in the same place: a death decree behind them, a confederate world before them, no power to defend themselves. And as at the Sea, the Lord fights and the enemy that they see today they "shall see them again no more for ever" (Ex 14:13).

[ILL] One night the army of Sennacherib lay encamped against Jerusalem, 185,000 strong, certain of the kill. "And it came to pass that night, that the angel of the LORD went out, and smote in the camp of the Assyrians an hundred fourscore and five thousand: and when they arose early in the morning, behold, they were all dead corpses" (2 Kgs 19:35). Not one Israelite lifted a sword. The host that had gathered to destroy the city of God was broken in a single night by one angel. Armageddon is that night written across the whole earth — the last confederacy mustered to destroy the people of God, shattered not by their hand but by the appearing of their King.

[Q] **"Why gather the whole world only to be destroyed? Isn't that pointless?"**
→ It is the final exhibit of the cosmic trial (Study 1). Satan's charge was that God rules by force and self-interest. At Armageddon Satan is allowed to muster every power, every weapon, every deceived nation, with no restraint left — and God's people, sealed and defenceless, do not retaliate, do not abandon their Lord under the death decree. Charge A ("they only obey for reward") is answered: every reward has been stripped, every threat is real, and still they trust. Charge B ("the law cannot be kept") is answered: under the worst conditions earth has ever known they keep "the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (Rev 14:12). The whole universe watches the confederacy of evil exhaust itself against a people who will not be moved — and then watches the King deliver them. Armageddon is not pointless; it is the verdict, demonstrated.

## The Second Coming — Literal, Visible, Audible, Global

**This is the heart the original study treated well, and we keep its prose: against every counterfeit "coming" — secret, inner, spiritual — the Bible piles up unmistakable, sense-bound language. The advent is an EVENT, not an experience.**

- **Every eye sees Him**
  - "Behold, he cometh with clouds; and every eye shall see him, and they also which pierced him: and all kindreds of the earth shall wail because of him. Even so, Amen" (Rev 1:7)
  - "For as the lightning cometh out of the east, and shineth even unto the west; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" (Matt 24:27)
  - [→] _Every_ eye. Lightning from horizon to horizon. Not secret, not spiritual, not symbolic. Visible. Audible. Global. The whole planet sees the same Christ at the same moment.

- **Christ Himself fenced off every counterfeit in advance**
  - "Then if any man shall say unto you, Lo, here is Christ, or there; believe it not" (Matt 24:23)
  - "Wherefore if they shall say unto you, Behold, he is in the desert; go not forth: behold, he is in the secret chambers; believe it not" (Matt 24:26)
  - "And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven... and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory" (Matt 24:30)
  - [STR] Jesus warned us BEFORE the fact: every "coming" that is local ("in the desert"), private ("in the secret chambers"), or inward is a forgery by definition. The same lying wonders that gather Armageddon (Rev 16:14) will counterfeit a "Christ" — but the real One comes the only way Scripture allows: in the clouds, seen by all, with power and great glory.

- **The shout, the voice, the trumpet**
  - "For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God" (1 Thess 4:16)
  - "And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other" (Matt 24:31)
  - "Our God shall come, and shall not keep silence: a fire shall devour before him, and it shall be very tempestuous round about him" (Ps 50:3)
  - [→] A shout, the archangel's voice, the trump of God — heaven empties of its angels and the sky is full of sound and glory. There is nothing quiet, secret, or interior about it.

## The First Resurrection and the Translation — The Image Completed

**Now the deliverance reaches the dead. Because death is an unconscious sleep (Study 27), the righteous dead are not already in heaven — they are raised. The translation of the living and the resurrection of the dead are ONE event, and the spec is firm: it must rest on 1 Thessalonians 4 and 1 Corinthians 15, not on assertion. So we let those two passages carry it.**

- **The dead in Christ rise FIRST**
  - "the dead in Christ shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air: and so shall we ever be with the Lord" (1 Thess 4:16-17)
  - The order is exact: the sleeping saints are raised, THEN the living are gathered, and the two companies ascend TOGETHER. No one is taken ahead of the other; no one was already in heaven waiting.
  - [→] This is the answer to every claim that the dead are already with the Lord. Paul's whole comfort to the bereaved Thessalonians is that the dead will be raised AT the coming and caught up WITH the living — which would be no comfort, and no sense, if they were already there. The state-of-the-dead truth of Study 27 and the advent are one seamless doctrine.

- **In a moment — the living changed, the dead raised incorruptible**
  - "Behold, I shew you a mystery; We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed, In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump: for the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed" (1 Cor 15:51-52)
  - "For this corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal must put on immortality" (1 Cor 15:53)
  - [→] Immortality is not something the saints already possess — it is "put on" here, at the last trump (1 Cor 15:53; 1 Tim 6:16 gives it to God alone until now). The living do not die; they are _changed_. The dead do not stay in the dust; they are raised incorruptible. Both, in the twinkling of an eye.

- **The body fashioned like His glorious body — the image of God completed**
  - "Who shall change our vile body, that it may be fashioned like unto his glorious body" (Phil 3:21)
  - [STR] The image of God in man was broken in TWO dimensions — character and body (Study 3). The seal restored the character before probation closed; the second coming now restores the BODY. The whole arc of the series — image marred at the fall (Study 2), image being restored across the bridge (Study 3) — reaches its physical completion here: a glorified body fashioned like Christ's own. The image is whole.

- **Death itself swallowed up**
  - "then shall be brought to pass the saying that is written, Death is swallowed up in victory. O death, where is thy sting? O grave, where is thy victory?" (1 Cor 15:54-55)
  - [→] Death — the curse of Eden (Gen 2:17; Rom 6:23), the weapon of the accuser, the thing the serpent said would never come ("ye shall not surely die," Gen 3:4) — is swallowed up. The Way Home does not detour around the grave; it passes straight THROUGH death and comes out the other side, alive forever.

[DYK🔎] **The special resurrection.** Alongside the general first resurrection, Daniel names a smaller, special raising: "And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt" (Dan 12:2). Around the advent a select company of the wicked dead — those who pierced Him — are raised to BEHOLD His coming: "every eye shall see him, AND THEY ALSO WHICH PIERCED HIM" (Rev 1:7). The men who crucified Christ are not left sleeping; they are raised to see, with their own eyes, the One they nailed to the cross now coming as King — a final vindication of the Crucified before His murderers.

## The Two Responses — The Saved and the Lost See the Same Christ

**One Christ appears; two reactions divide the human race. The difference is not information — both see the same face — but what each did with the invitation while it was open.**

- **The response of the saved: "Lo, this is our God"**
  - "And it shall be said in that day, Lo, this is our God; we have waited for him, and he will save us: this is the LORD; we have waited for him, we will be glad and rejoice in his salvation" (Isa 25:9)
  - "we have waited for him" — twice. This is "the patience of the saints" (Rev 14:12) come to its reward; the endurance that outlasted Jacob's trouble and Armageddon now breaks into joy.
  - [→] This is the moment the whole series has been building toward. The cosmic trial opened in Study 1; the way home was mapped in Study 5; the crisis was endured in Studies 28-30. And now, on the broken plain of Armageddon, the delivered remnant lift their eyes and say: _Lo, this is our God._

- **The response of the lost: "hide us from the face"**
  - "And the kings of the earth, and the great men... hid themselves in the dens and in the rocks of the mountains; And said to the mountains and rocks, Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb" (Rev 6:15-16)
  - "For the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?" (Rev 6:17)
  - [→] The very face they could have beheld for transformation (2 Cor 3:18, Study 3) they now beg the rocks to hide them from. They would rather be crushed by a mountain than meet the eyes of the Lamb. Nothing has changed about Christ — the difference is entirely in what they did with His mercy when it was offered.

- **The wicked slain by the brightness of His coming**
  - "And then shall that Wicked be revealed, whom the Lord shall consume with the spirit of his mouth, and shall destroy with the brightness of his coming" (2 Thess 2:8)
  - "when the Lord Jesus shall be revealed from heaven with his mighty angels, In flaming fire taking vengeance on them that know not God... Who shall be punished with everlasting destruction from the presence of the Lord" (2 Thess 1:7-9)
  - "And the slain of the LORD shall be at that day from one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth: they shall not be lamented, neither gathered, nor buried; they shall be dung upon the ground" (Jer 25:33)
  - [STR] The same brightness that is _life_ to the waiting saints is _death_ to the confederate world — not because Christ brings two different glories, but because the unprepared cannot endure the unveiled presence of God (Isa 33:14; Heb 12:29). The living wicked who gathered to Armageddon are consumed "with the spirit of his mouth" (2 Thess 2:8; Rev 19:21), the slain lie unburied from end to end of the earth (Jer 25:33), and the planet is left desolate — emptied of the living, for the millennium that follows (Study 32).

[ILL] Two students sit in the same exam room, under the same lamp, and the same proctor walks in. To the one who studied, the proctor's arrival is the long-awaited green light — _finally, let me show what I've learned._ To the one who cheated and hid it, the same footsteps are dread. The proctor has not changed; the lamp has not changed. The difference was settled long before the door opened. So it is with the face of the Lamb: "Lo, this is our God" or "hide us from his face" — and the verdict was written, by each one's own choice, while mercy still lingered.

## Typology — The Final Passover and the Final Harvest

**Two text-authorized types frame the deliverance, and we keep both because the text itself authorizes them and the doctrine rests on plain Scripture underneath.**

- **The Passover — the destroyer passes over the blood-marked house**
  - "the blood shall be to you for a token upon the houses where ye are: and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I smite the land of Egypt" (Ex 12:13)
  - Paul marks the type himself: "Christ our passover is sacrificed for us" (1 Cor 5:7); the Exodus events "are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come" (1 Cor 10:11).
  - [→] In Egypt the plagues fell on the houses without the blood; the blood-marked passed unharmed. At the end, the seven last plagues fall on the mark of the BEAST; those who bear the blood of the Lamb — sealed by His character — are passed over (the doctrine itself rests on Rev 16:2 and the sealing of Studies 29-30, not merely on the figure). The Passover deliverance is the dress rehearsal; Armageddon's morning is the antitype.

- **The harvest — Christ's own figure for the advent**
  - "And I looked, and behold a white cloud, and upon the cloud one sat like unto the Son of man, having on his head a golden crown, and in his hand a sharp sickle... Thrust in thy sickle, and reap: for the time is come for thee to reap; for the harvest of the earth is ripe" (Rev 14:14-15)
  - Jesus authorizes the type Himself: "the harvest is the end of the world; and the reapers are the angels" (Matt 13:39).
  - [→] The advent is the reaping of a ripened field — wheat gathered into the barn, tares bound for burning (Matt 13:30,39-43). This is not an imported allegory; it is Christ's own marked figure for His coming, and it rests on the plain second-coming texts (1 Thess 4; Matt 24:31) underneath.

## Appeal

- The world's last army gathers at Armageddon — under a lying message, by the spirits of devils, against a people who will not fight back. And it never lands its blow.
- The sky splits. The King appears — every eye sees Him; not secret, not spiritual, but with a shout, the archangel's voice, and the trump of God.
- The dead in Christ rise first, incorruptible. The living are changed in the twinkling of an eye. The body is fashioned like His glorious body. The image of God, marred at the fall, is whole.
- And on the broken plain, the delivered lift their eyes and say the words the whole long road has been bending toward: _Lo, this is our God; we have waited for him._
- The same face is dread to one and home to the other. The difference is not what you will SEE that day — everyone sees the same Christ. The difference is what you do with His mercy now, while the invitation is still open.
- You cannot fight your way through Armageddon. No one can. You can only be found, that day, on the blood-marked side of the door — sealed, waiting, holding on.
- "Stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD." The question is not whether He will come. The question is whether you will be among those who waited.

**Central Verse:** "These shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings: and they that are with him are called, and chosen, and faithful." (Revelation 17:14)

**[→ Study 32]** The confederacy is broken, the wicked slain by the brightness of His coming, the saints caught up to meet the Lord in the air. The earth lies desolate and empty — and Satan is bound, with no one left to deceive. A thousand years of review begins, a final judgment, the descent of the New Jerusalem, one last assault (Gog and Magog — NOT Armageddon), a lake of fire, and then: "Behold, I make all things new." The image of God is fully restored. The Way Home is complete.
