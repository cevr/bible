# Study 23: Come Out of Her, My People — Babylon and the Literal Call

**The judgment is underway and Babylon has been unmasked — the scarlet woman of Revelation 17, an apostate church riding the civil power, drunk with the blood of the saints. Now heaven gives the demanded response, and it is not an attitude but a command: "Come out of her, my people." This is a literal call to a literal people to physically leave a literal apostate religious system — a real exodus out of false churches and into the truth — before her plagues fall. It is the same word God spoke to His captives in literal Babylon — "Flee out of the midst of Babylon" — sounding again at the end of the world. To come out is to leave the false system and take up the trodden-down truths the system buried. The inner work follows; it does not soften the word "come out."**

## Prep Reading

- Revelation 18:1-5
- Jeremiah 51:6, 45
- Isaiah 52:11
- 2 Corinthians 6:14-18
- Acts 17:10-11

---

## The Call Is Literal — A Voice Says "Come OUT"

**Before we ask what Babylon teaches, settle what the command does: it tells real people to physically leave. Read it as plainly as Lot was told to leave Sodom.**

- **Revelation 18:4 — the command itself**
  - "And I heard another voice from heaven, saying, Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues" (Rev 18:4)
  - The verb is _come out_ — a movement, a departure, an exit. Not "be at peace within her," not "improve her," not "feel differently about her." Leave.
  - The reason given is concrete: "that ye receive not of her plagues." Plagues are coming on a place; the way to escape them is to not be in that place.
  - [STR] Take the command at the plain literal sense the way Study 0 taught. "Come out" means come out. The moment we read it as merely an inner change of attitude while staying put, we have done to this verse exactly what the serpent did to "ye shall surely die" — kept the word and emptied it of its plain force.

- **"My people" — there are real people inside to call out**
  - The voice is addressed to "my people." The call presupposes that God owns sincere worshippers who are, at this moment, _inside_ Babylon.
  - These are not pagans, not atheists, not the indifferent. They are God's people — sincere believers — held within a system that has lied to them.
  - [→] If there were no people of God inside Babylon, there would be no one to call out. The command is its own proof that the sincere are still within. The urgency of the call measures the sincerity of those still inside.

- **He calls because He loves them — the call is rescue, not condemnation**
  - "As many as I love, I rebuke and chasten: be zealous therefore, and repent" (Rev 3:19)
  - A judge does not warn the people he means to destroy without notice. The warning is the proof of mercy.
  - The call goes out _before_ the plagues, not after — heaven empties the building before it falls.

[ILL] A prisoner of war is fed his captors' propaganda day after day until he repeats it as his own conviction. He is sincere. He is certain. He is still captive. And the first step of his rescue is not a debate — it is extraction. You get him out first; the unlearning comes after. "Come out of her, my people" is a rescue operation for prisoners who have come to believe the captor's story — and step one is to walk out the gate.

## This Is the Old Command Sounding Again — Flee Babylon

**Revelation 18:4 is not a new idea. God said the very same thing, in the very same words, to His people held captive in literal ancient Babylon. That literal command is the key to this one — Scripture is its own expositor (Study 0).**

- **God's standing command to His captives in Babylon: physically leave**
  - "Flee out of the midst of Babylon, and deliver every man his soul: be not cut off in her iniquity" (Jer 51:6)
  - "My people, go ye out of the midst of her, and deliver ye every man his soul from the fierce anger of the LORD" (Jer 51:45)
  - "Remove out of the midst of Babylon, and go forth out of the land of the Chaldeans" (Jer 50:8)
  - "Go ye forth of Babylon, flee ye from the Chaldeans, with a voice of singing... say ye, The LORD hath redeemed his servant Jacob" (Isa 48:20)
  - "Depart ye, depart ye, go ye out from thence, touch no unclean thing; go ye out of the midst of her; be ye clean, that bear the vessels of the LORD" (Isa 52:11)
  - [→] Notice the exact match: "my people," "go ye out of the midst of her," "deliver every man his soul." Revelation 18:4 quotes these prophets almost word for word. The literal command God gave to literal captives in literal Babylon is the dictionary entry for the end-time call. The original was a real exodus of real people out of a real city — and the figure is built on that literal fact.

[DYK🔎] When the call first sounded in Babylon, it was answered with literal feet. Cyrus issued a literal decree, and a literal company of Israel literally walked out of literal Babylon and back toward Jerusalem to rebuild the literal temple (Ezra 1). The ancient call did not produce an attitude — it produced an exodus. The Bible's own precedent for "come out of her" is people on the road home. The last call asks the same: not a better feeling about the captivity, but the road out of it.

- **The pattern runs back to Sodom — get out before the fire**
  - "Escape for thy life; look not behind thee, neither stay thou in all the plain; escape to the mountain, lest thou be consumed" (Gen 19:17)
  - The angels did not counsel Lot to reform Sodom or to keep a pure heart inside it. They "laid hold upon his hand" and "brought him forth, and set him without the city" (Gen 19:16) — because the city was about to burn.
  - [→] When judgment is fixed on a place, mercy takes the form of evacuation. Sodom, Babylon, and the Babylon of Revelation are the same lesson: the saved are the ones who got _out_.

- **Why a flat call to leave, and not a call to stay and reform**
  - Because the building is condemned: "Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen, and is become the habitation of devils, and the hold of every foul spirit, and a cage of every unclean and hateful bird" (Rev 18:2)
  - You do not renovate a condemned building. You evacuate it. Reform assumes the structure can be corrected; God has already pronounced it _fallen_.
  - [→] "Come out" and "stay and fix it" are different commands, and heaven gave the first one. To turn "come out" into "stay and improve" is to disobey the plain word while feeling devout about it.

## What "Babylon" Is — A Corrupt Church (Revelation Says So)

**The call is only as clear as the thing we are called out of. So fix Babylon's identity the Bible's way — by its own symbol — not by an etymology. Revelation has already named her.**

- **A woman in prophecy = a church — Scripture's own symbol**
  - "I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ" (2 Cor 11:2) — the faithful church is a pure woman, betrothed to Christ.
  - So a corrupt, fornicating woman = a corrupt, apostate church. The Bible holds the same key for both, and we read it consistently (Study 0).
  - This is the symbol Revelation 12 used for the true church (the woman clothed with the sun) and Revelation 17 uses for the false (the woman drunk with blood). Same key, opposite woman.

- **Revelation 17 names Babylon outright — she is the apostate church**
  - "MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS AND ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH" (Rev 17:5)
  - "The woman is that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth" (Rev 17:18) — a religious power that rules over civil powers.
  - "I saw the woman drunken with the blood of the saints, and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus" (Rev 17:6) — a persecuting church, the very power Daniel 7 said would "wear out the saints" (Dan 7:25).
  - [→] Babylon is not a mood or a vague "worldliness." It is a specific, identifiable, persecuting _church-system_ — and her "harlot" daughters are the churches that share her errors. That is what the voice calls God's people out of.

- **Her "fornication" = church-state union**
  - "With whom the kings of the earth have committed fornication, and the inhabitants of the earth have been made drunk with the wine of her fornication" (Rev 17:2)
  - A pure woman is wedded to Christ. A harlot church couples instead with the kings of the earth — trading the husband (Christ) for the civil power, and enforcing her doctrine by the sword of the state.
  - [→] This is the union Study 22 unmasked: the woman who _rides_ the beast. The fornication is literal church-state union; "come out of her" is a call to leave a system that wields political power to compel worship.

[Q] **"Isn't 'Babylon' just from _Babel_ = 'confusion'? So isn't 'come out of Babylon' just 'come out of confusion' — a state of mind?"**
→ No. Etymology is not interpretation. The Bible never tells us to read its symbol off a Hebrew word-origin; it tells us to let Scripture define its own figures (Study 0). And Revelation does exactly that: it shows us a _woman_ (a church, 2 Cor 11:2), names her "BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS" (Rev 17:5), seats her over "the kings of the earth" (Rev 17:18), and shows her "drunken with the blood of the saints" (Rev 17:6). That is not a feeling of confusion; it is an identifiable apostate church wielding civil power. Read the symbol off the text, not off a dictionary, and Babylon is a real system you can walk out of — which is the whole point of being told to walk out.

## What She Teaches — Real Institutionalized Error to Leave

**To "come out" means leaving real doctrines, not vague vibes. Babylon's errors are the trodden-down truths of this whole series, turned upside down. These are the specific things left behind at the door.**

- **Doctrines of devils dressed as Christian teaching**
  - "Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils" (1 Tim 4:1)
  - "For such are false apostles, deceitful workers, transforming themselves into the apostles of Christ. And no marvel; for Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light" (2 Cor 11:13-14)
  - [→] The danger is not crude paganism the eye can spot. It is error wearing the vocabulary of Christ — the angel-of-light disguise. That is why the call is needed: sincere people cannot see the bars of a cage painted to look like a sanctuary.

- **Sunday for Sabbath — a changed law**
  - The little horn would "think to change times and laws" (Dan 7:25) — and the only command that joins time and law is the fourth (Ex 20:8-11).
  - "Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy... the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God... For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth" (Ex 20:8-11) — a literal day, sealed with the Creator's signature.
  - To trade the seventh day for the first is to act on Babylon's claim that the law itself is changeable. (The Sabbath's full seal is Study 24.)

- **The immortal soul for the resurrection — a denied judgment**
  - "For the living know that they shall die: but the dead know not any thing" (Eccl 9:5)
  - "Our friend Lazarus sleepeth; but I go, that I may awake him out of sleep... Then said Jesus unto them plainly, Lazarus is dead" (John 11:11, 14) — Christ calls death a sleep, ended by resurrection, not a doorway the soul walks through at the last breath.
  - If the dead are already in bliss or torment, then the judgment is a formality and the resurrection is unnecessary — and the door stands open for "spirits of the dead" to deceive the living. (Study 27 follows this lie to its end.)

- **A human priesthood for the one Mediator — a barred door**
  - "For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus" (1 Tim 2:5)
  - "Who can forgive sins but God only?" (Mark 2:7) — every earthly confessor pronouncing absolution stands where only Christ may stand.
  - Babylon inserts a human priesthood between the sinner and his one High Priest, who is not on earth (Heb 8:1-2, from Study 5). To come out is to come back to direct access through Christ alone.

[→] Each of these is the inversion of a truth this series has already proved. Sunday says the law can be changed; the immortal soul says there is no real death and so no real judgment; the human priest says you cannot approach God directly. Strip the religious vocabulary and Babylon's doctrines line up as the accuser's old argument — God's law is arbitrary, His judgment unnecessary, His access closed. (That is the narrative thread of this whole series; here it is named, not leaned on. The doctrines are proved from the texts above, plain and literal.)

## What She Has Done — Why Her Cup Is Full

**Heaven does not call this an overreaction. The reason for the flat command is the magnitude of the system's guilt — measured, again, from Revelation's own words.**

- **Her sins are full and remembered**
  - "For her sins have reached unto heaven, and God hath remembered her iniquities" (Rev 18:5)
  - "In her was found the blood of prophets, and of saints, and of all that were slain upon the earth" (Rev 18:24)
  - This is the persecuting record of Revelation 17:6 — a church drunk with the blood of the martyrs. The call to "come out" is a call to be no longer numbered with the perpetrators when the account is settled.

- **Her fall is fixed and her plagues are dated**
  - "Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication" (Rev 14:8) — the second angel's cry, already sounded.
  - "Therefore shall her plagues come in one day, death, and mourning, and famine; and she shall be utterly burned with fire: for strong is the Lord God who judgeth her" (Rev 18:8)
  - [→] The plagues are real and timed (the seven last plagues, Study 30). "That ye receive not of her plagues" (Rev 18:4) is therefore a literal deliverance from a literal outpouring — which makes the coming-out literal too. You cannot escape a literal fire by a figurative move.

[DYK🔎] Heaven itself once tested whether the prophesied fall of Babylon was sure. Daniel told Belshazzar his kingdom was numbered and finished — and "in that night was Belshazzar the king of the Chaldeans slain" and "Darius the Median took the kingdom" (Dan 5:30-31). The literal city fell exactly as the word said. That standing receipt is why "Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen" (Rev 18:2) can be trusted to the letter: the God who kept His word against the first Babylon will keep it against the last. (This is an illustration of certainty, not a proof of doctrine — the doctrine stands on Rev 18 itself.)

## What They Take Up — The Repairer of the Breach

**Coming out is not merely leaving; it is _arriving_. The same people who walk out of the false system walk into the work of rebuilding the truths it buried. Separation has a destination.**

- **Where the call sends them: into the truth, separated unto God**
  - "Wherefore come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you, And will be a Father unto you, and ye shall be my sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty" (2 Cor 6:17-18)
  - The same Berean nobility that proves all things is the means of coming out: "they received the word with all readiness of mind, and searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so" (Acts 17:11)
  - [→] Coming out is not blind departure; it is testing every doctrine by the open Bible and following the result with your feet. Paul calls the result _sonship_: leave the harlot's house, and God receives you as His own. The two halves are one act — out of her, into Him.

- **The mission of those who come out: restore the trodden-down paths**
  - "And they that shall be of thee shall build the old waste places: thou shalt raise up the foundations of many generations; and thou shalt be called, The repairer of the breach, The restorer of paths to dwell in" (Isa 58:12)
  - The "breach" is what was broken when the apostate power "cast down the truth to the ground" (Dan 8:12). The very next verse names the chief stone to be relaid: the Sabbath — "if thou turn away thy foot from the sabbath... and call the sabbath a delight, the holy of the LORD, honourable" (Isa 58:13).
  - [→] "Repairer of the breach" is not a poetic flourish — it is the job description of the people who come out. They are the remnant who relay the exact stones Babylon tore out: the Sabbath, the heavenly sanctuary and its one Mediator, the truth about death, salvation by faith that keeps the commandments. (The conquest and rebuilding theme is Study 26's; here the destination is named.)

[STR] Put it together: the call has two literal sides, and both are doctrine. (1) _Out of_ — leave the apostate church-system and its institutionalized errors (Sunday, the immortal soul, the human priesthood). (2) _Into_ — take up and rebuild the trodden truths (the Sabbath, the sanctuary, the state of the dead, salvation by faith). A reader can verify every line of that with an open Bible. This is the non-negotiable core of "come out of her."

## The Inner Work Follows — It Does Not Soften the Word

**One last thing must be kept in its place. God does indeed want His people transformed, not merely relocated — but that truth comes _after_ the command, as its fruit, never _before_ it as an excuse to stay or to wait.**

- **Transformation is the goal of the exodus — but does not delay it**
  - You can leave the false church on Sabbath morning and carry its theology in your heart all week. Geography is the beginning of the work, not the whole of it.
  - But notice the order Scripture gives: God said _come out_ first — to Israel from Babylon, to Lot from Sodom, to His people now. The heart-work happened _on the road out_, never as a reason to stay in the burning city.
  - [→] "Come out" is the command; "be changed" is the promised result. Never let the result swallow the command. The captor's last trick, once the gate is open, is to whisper, "wait until you feel ready" — and so keep the prisoner inside until the plagues fall.

- **Then let Him finish the work in those who have come out**
  - "But who may abide the day of his coming?... for he is like a refiner's fire... and he shall purify the sons of Levi" (Mal 3:2-3) — the Refiner watches the silver until His own face is reflected in it.
  - "Be patient therefore, brethren, unto the coming of the Lord. Behold, the husbandman waiteth for the precious fruit of the earth" (James 5:7) — God waits for a harvest of character in a people already gathered out.
  - [→] The refining and the harvest happen in those who have already left — they are the destiny of the gathered remnant, not the entrance exam at the gate.

[TANGENT] This is why correct doctrine alone is not the finish line: Satan himself knows every true doctrine and could pass any theology exam. The difference between a demon who knows the truth and a saint who lives it is transformation, not information. But mark the order once more — that transformation is what God does _in_ the people He has gathered out, by the truths they took up. It is the reward of obedience to "come out," never a substitute for it. First the exodus; then the refining; then home.

## Appeal

- A voice from heaven is speaking right now, and it is not vague: "Come out of her, my people" (Rev 18:4). It owns sincere, devout, deceived people inside Babylon — and it loves them enough to call them out before the fire.
- It is the same command God has always given His captives — "Flee out of the midst of Babylon, and deliver every man his soul" (Jer 51:6); "go ye out of the midst of her" (Jer 51:45). His people have always answered it with their feet.
- What you leave is real: an apostate church-system that traded the Sabbath for Sunday, the resurrection for the immortal soul, the one Mediator for a human priest. What you take up is real: the trodden truths, relaid by "the repairer of the breach" (Isa 58:12).
- "Come out from among them, and be ye separate... and I will receive you, And will be a Father unto you" (2 Cor 6:17-18). The road out of Babylon is the road home to a Father.
- Test it yourself, Berean — search the Scriptures daily whether these things are so (Acts 17:11) — and then walk. The inner work will follow you down the road; it was never meant to keep you in the city.
- The question is not first "Will you let truth change you?" It is "Will you come out?" Answer that, and the Refiner does the rest in those who have left.
