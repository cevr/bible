# Study 7: The Counterfeit System

**Daniel 2 is God's sketch of all history written in advance — four literal empires from Babylon to divided Rome, ending in a stone kingdom no human hand sets up. That map is also the frame that exposes a fraud: across the very sequence God charted, Satan could not destroy the sanctuary or the law at its center, so he buried both under a counterfeit. The prophecy that maps history is also the prophecy that unmasks the imitation — four empires, one stone, and a single layered substitute built over the top of God's truth.**

## Prep Reading

- Daniel 2:28-45
- 2 Thessalonians 2:3-7
- 1 Timothy 2:5
- Hebrews 8:1-2
- Isaiah 8:20

---

## God Drew the Map First — Daniel 2 Is Prophecy Written in Advance

**Before any application can be trusted, the foundation must be laid: Daniel 2 is literal predictive prophecy. God told a pagan king the future of the world centuries before it unfolded — and said so plainly. Establish that, and the rest stands on rock.**

- **Daniel says it is the future, not a guess**
  - "But there is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets, and maketh known to the king Nebuchadnezzar what shall be in the latter days. Thy dream, and the visions of thy head upon thy bed, are these" (Dan 2:28)
  - "He that revealeth secrets maketh known to thee what shall come to pass" (Dan 2:29)
  - [STR] This is the Bible's own claim: not philosophy, not symbol-for-symbol's-sake, but history-before-it-happened. If it comes true on schedule, the God who wrote it is the God He claims to be.

- **The image: one statue, four metals, one stone**
  - "Thou, O king, sawest, and behold a great image... This great image, whose brightness was excellent, stood before thee; and the form thereof was terrible" (Dan 2:31)
  - "This image's head was of fine gold, his breast and his arms of silver, his belly and his thighs of brass, His legs of iron, his feet part of iron and part of clay" (Dan 2:32-33)
  - "Thou sawest till that a stone was cut out without hands, which smote the image upon his feet that were of iron and clay, and brake them to pieces" (Dan 2:34)

- **The interpretation is given inside the chapter — no outside key needed**
  - "This is the dream; and we will tell the interpretation thereof before the king" (Dan 2:36)
  - [→] We are not importing a meaning. Daniel hands the king the meaning verse by verse. We only have to read it.

## The Text Says Metal = Kingdom, and Names the First Empire

**The single most important rule for the whole prophetic series is established right here, by Daniel himself: each metal is a literal kingdom. The Bible defines its own symbol — we never have to invent one.**

- **"Thou art this head of gold" — the symbol is decoded for us**
  - "Thou, O king, art a king of kings: for the God of heaven hath given thee a kingdom, power, and strength, and glory" (Dan 2:37)
  - "Thou art this head of gold" (Dan 2:38)
  - [STR] Metal = literal KINGDOM, because the text says so. Daniel does not say "the gold represents wisdom" or "the gold is your soul." He says: _this metal is a kingdom, and it is yours._ That is the literal, plain-sense reading Study 0 demanded — Scripture its own expositor.

- **Four kingdoms in fixed order, each succeeding the last**
  - "And after thee shall arise another kingdom inferior to thee, and another third kingdom of brass, which shall bear rule over all the earth" (Dan 2:39)
  - "And the fourth kingdom shall be strong as iron: forasmuch as iron breaketh in pieces and subdueth all things: and as iron that breaketh all these, shall it break in pieces and bruise" (Dan 2:40)
  - Gold (Babylon, named) → silver (second) → brass (third) → iron (fourth). Four world powers, in succession, "after thee" — not contemporaries, but a relay across history.

- **The fourth kingdom divides — and the divisions never reunite**
  - "And whereas thou sawest the feet and toes, part of potters' clay, and part of iron, the kingdom shall be divided" (Dan 2:41)
  - "And as the toes of the feet were part of iron, and part of clay, so the kingdom shall be partly strong, and partly broken" (Dan 2:42)
  - "And whereas thou sawest iron mixed with miry clay, they shall mingle themselves with the seed of men: but they shall not cleave one to another, even as iron is not mixed with clay" (Dan 2:43)
  - [→] God prophesied not only that the fourth empire would break apart, but that the pieces would _never fuse back into one_. "They shall not cleave one to another." Every attempt to reunite the broken empire by force or by marriage runs into a wall God built into the prophecy.

## Document from History — The Map Came True

> _History is the one place the study looks outside the Bible — and only to confirm a fulfillment. The MEANING was proved above from Daniel's own words; here we simply note that the events arrived on schedule._

- **Gold = Babylon.** Named in the text itself (Dan 2:38) — Nebuchadnezzar's empire, the one kingdom God identifies by name, removing all guesswork about the starting point.
- **Silver, brass, iron = the succession the historians record.** Babylon fell to the Medes and Persians; Persia fell to Greece under Alexander; Greece was absorbed by Rome. The full naming proof — where Scripture itself names Medo-Persia and Greece — comes in Studies 8 and 14 (Dan 8:20-21 spells them out by name). Here it is enough that the sequence of world-empires matches the metals in order.
- **The feet of iron and clay = divided Rome that never reunified.** Western Rome broke into the nations of Europe and stayed broken. Charlemagne, Charles V, Louis XIV, Napoleon, the Kaiser, Hitler — every bid to re-forge one empire out of the fragments failed. Iron will not mix with clay (Dan 2:43). The prophecy is still holding today.

[DYK🔎] Napoleon and others even tried the very thing verse 43 names — "they shall mingle themselves with the seed of men" — uniting the royal houses of Europe by intermarriage. The thrones intermarried for centuries; the continent never became one kingdom. A line written 2,500 years ago is still vindicated every time a would-be conqueror's Europe comes apart in his hands.

## The Stone That Ends the Statue

**The map does not stop at divided Rome. It ends with a fifth kingdom that is not a metal at all — a stone "cut out without hands," set up by God, that does not reform the statue but destroys it and fills the earth. This is the destination of the whole series.**

- **A kingdom God sets up, in the days of the divided fourth empire**
  - "And in the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed: and the kingdom shall not be left to other people, but it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand for ever" (Dan 2:44)
  - "In the days of these kings" — during the era of divided Rome (where we still live). Not a kingdom built by gradual human progress; a kingdom God _sets up_.

- **"Cut out without hands" — divine, not human**
  - "Forasmuch as thou sawest that the stone was cut out of the mountain without hands, and that it brake in pieces the iron, the brass, the clay, the silver, and the gold; the great God hath made known to the king what shall come to pass hereafter: and the dream is certain, and the interpretation thereof sure" (Dan 2:45)
  - "Without hands" = without human agency. The four kingdoms were raised by armies and treaties; the fifth is raised by God alone. [STR] Stone = the kingdom of God — Daniel's own equation, the partner of "metal = kingdom." Both ends of the prophecy are decoded for us by the text.

- **The stone does not patch the statue — it pulverizes it**
  - "Then was the iron, the clay, the brass, the silver, and the gold, broken to pieces together, and became like the chaff of the summer threshingfloors; and the wind carried them away, that no place was found for them: and the stone that smote the image became a great mountain, and filled the whole earth" (Dan 2:35)
  - [→] You cannot reform a counterfeit kingdom into the true one. The stone does not become a sixth metal. It grinds the whole image to chaff and replaces it entirely. Remember that line — it is the verdict on everything this study is about to expose.

**Central Verse:** "And in the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed... it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand for ever." (Daniel 2:44)

## The Same Map Exposes a Counterfeit _(secondary application)_

> _Everything above is the spine — literal predictive prophecy, proved from Daniel's own words and confirmed by history. What follows is a SECONDARY application: across the very empires God charted, Satan layered an imitation of the sanctuary. The four-empire prophecy is the FRAME; the counterfeit pattern is what God's map lets us see laid over the top of history. The doctrines below rest on plain texts about priesthood, blood, death, and the Sabbath — not on the layering itself._

- **Satan's method is replacement, not open destruction**
  - "I will ascend into heaven, I will exalt my throne above the stars of God... I will be like the most High" (Isa 14:13-14)
  - Not "I will destroy the Most High" — "I will be _like_ the most High." The aim is imitation: a copy familiar enough to be mistaken for the original.
  - [→] Satan could not abolish the sanctuary (Study 5) or the moral law at its heart (Study 6). So he builds a parallel system that looks the same and leads nowhere.

- **The man of sin sits in the temple — occupying, not razing it**
  - "Who opposeth and exalteth himself above all that is called God, or that is worshipped; so that he as God sitteth in the temple of God, shewing himself that he is God" (2 Thess 2:4)
  - "For the mystery of iniquity doth already work" (2 Thess 2:7)
  - [STR] _Sitteth in the temple_ — not destroying it, occupying its place. And _already worketh_ — not a single future invention but a slow construction running across centuries. That is exactly the timescale of Daniel's four empires.

- **The beast blasphemes the sanctuary itself**
  - "And he opened his mouth in blasphemy against God, to blaspheme his name, and his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven" (Rev 13:6)
  - Against His _name_ — misrepresenting His character. Against His _tabernacle_ — replacing His ministry. Against _them that dwell in heaven_ — usurping the role of heaven's true ministers.

[→] Each empire below follows the same spine: (1) the sanctuary truth God gave — proved from a plain text; (2) where the counterfeit of it appears; (3) where that counterfeit still lives today; (4) how Christ restores the true thing.

> _Echo (devotional, not proof):_ "The system of heathen worship was Satan's method of obscuring the plan of salvation" (Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 91). The Bible above has already proved the method — Isaiah 14, 2 Thessalonians 2, Revelation 13. Ellen White only puts a name on what the texts establish.

## Babylon — The Head of Gold: Counterfeit Priesthood

- **The sanctuary truth: one High Priest mediates between God and man**
  - "We have such an high priest, who is set on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens; a minister of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man" (Heb 8:1-2)
  - "For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus" (1 Tim 2:5)
  - [STR] The doctrine rests here: _one_ Mediator, and access to God through Him alone. This is the plain text — not the layering.

- **Babylon's counterfeit: a human priest-king standing in God's place**
  - "Thou art this head of gold" (Dan 2:38)
  - Scripture's record of Babylon's beginning: "And Cush begat Nimrod: he began to be a mighty one in the earth. He was a mighty hunter before the LORD... And the beginning of his kingdom was Babel, and Erech, and Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar" (Gen 10:8-10)
  - Scripture names the false worship that grew from that soil — the cult of the "queen of heaven": "The children gather wood, and the fathers kindle the fire, and the women knead their dough, to make cakes to the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto other gods, that they may provoke me to anger" (Jer 7:18)
  - "But we will certainly do whatsoever thing goeth forth out of our own mouth, to burn incense unto the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto her" (Jer 44:17)
  - [→] From Babel forward: a human power stands where only God's appointed Priest should stand, and a counterfeit mediatress is offered worship the true God forbids.

[DYK🔎] _(Historical background — color, not proof.)_ The later Roman title _Pontifex Maximus_, "greatest bridge-builder," named the chief priest as the bridge between gods and men. It passed from the pagan Caesars into a Christian office and survives as a papal title still. Read it for atmosphere, not doctrine: the _doctrine_ — that there is one Mediator — stands on 1 Timothy 2:5 and Hebrews 8:1-2 alone. The bridge a human priesthood offers was always a counterfeit of the ladder Jacob saw, which is Christ (John 1:51, Study 5).

- **Where it lives today**
  - Human priesthood treated as a necessary mediator
  - Confession of sin directed to a man instead of to God
  - Veneration of a "queen of heaven" intercessor — the old title in new language (Jer 7:18; 44:17)
  - Any system that places a human gatekeeper between the sinner and Christ

- **Christ restores the true thing**
  - "Seeing then that we have a great high priest, that is passed into the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast our profession" (Heb 4:14)
  - "Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need" (Heb 4:16)
  - [→] You do not need permission from any man to approach God. The High Priest already knows your name.

## Medo-Persia — The Silver Arms: Counterfeit Atonement

- **The sanctuary truth: atonement is by substitutionary blood alone**
  - "For the life of the flesh is in the blood: and I have given it to you upon the altar to make an atonement for your souls: for it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul" (Lev 17:11)
  - "And almost all things are by the law purged with blood; and without shedding of blood is no remission" (Heb 9:22)
  - [STR] The doctrine rests here: sin costs life, and only a substitute's blood can cover it. The whole sanctuary taught this one lesson relentlessly (Study 5).

- **Medo-Persia's counterfeit: purification by human effort instead of blood**
  - "And after thee shall arise another kingdom inferior to thee" (Dan 2:39)
  - Where the altar required a substitute's blood, the counterfeit offers righteousness by weighing one's own good deeds against the bad — acceptance earned rather than received. No substitute, no shed blood, no remission.
  - [→] An atonement without blood is no atonement. It quietly moves the cost of sin from the substitute back onto the sinner.

[Q] **"Isn't obedience important?"**
→ Absolutely — but obedience is the _fruit_ of salvation, not the _root_. The error was never in valuing good works; it was in making them the _payment_ instead of the _response_. The sanctuary never taught "do good and live." It taught "a substitute dies so that you may live — and then walk in good works." Strip the blood, and grace becomes a wage.

- **Where it lives today**
  - Salvation by works — good deeds outweighing bad on some cosmic scale
  - Moral improvement treated as the path to God's acceptance
  - Purification through ritual, penance, or human effort instead of through the blood of Christ
  - Any gospel that says "do enough good and God will accept you"

- **Christ restores the true thing**
  - "But this man, after he had offered one sacrifice for sins for ever, sat down on the right hand of God" (Heb 10:12)
  - "For by one offering he hath perfected for ever them that are sanctified" (Heb 10:14)
  - One sacrifice. Once. Forever. No scales, no penance, no earning — finished.

## Greece — The Bronze Belly: Counterfeit Immortality

- **Recap of what Study 2 established about death** _(text-anchored, not assumed)_
  - Only God possesses immortality inherently: "Who only hath immortality" (1 Tim 6:16)
  - The dead are not conscious: "the dead know not any thing" (Eccl 9:5); death is sleep — "Our friend Lazarus sleepeth; but I go, that I may awake him out of sleep... Then said Jesus unto them plainly, Lazarus is dead" (John 11:11,14)
  - Without resurrection there is no hope: "Then they also which are fallen asleep in Christ are perished" (1 Cor 15:18)
  - [STR] The doctrine rests on these plain texts. The serpent's first lie — "Ye shall not surely die" (Gen 3:4) — is the seed of every false doctrine about death.

- **Greece made the old lie respectable**
  - "And after thee shall arise... another third kingdom of brass, which shall bear rule over all the earth" (Dan 2:39)
  - The counterfeit recasts death as the soul's liberation rather than an unconscious sleep awaiting resurrection — the serpent's "ye shall not surely die" dressed in philosophy.
  - [→] Satan told the first lie in a garden; the counterfeit gave it an academy. Same lie, more respectable address.

- **Where it lives today**
  - The natural-immortal-soul doctrine, embedded across most of Christianity
  - Prayers to dead saints, ever-burning conscious torment, death as "going home" already
  - Every one of these builds on the assumption that the dead are conscious — the very assumption Scripture denies (Eccl 9:5; John 11:11-14)

- **Christ restores the true thing**
  - "For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in Christ shall rise first" (1 Thess 4:16)
  - The _resurrection_ — not a disembodied departure — is the hope. The counterfeit buried it under a deathless soul; Christ restores it. (Full treatment in Study 27.)

## Rome — The Iron Legs: Counterfeit Worship

- **The sanctuary truth: the seventh-day Sabbath, the Creator's memorial**
  - "Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy. Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work: But the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God" (Ex 20:8-10)
  - "For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is, and rested the seventh day: wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it" (Ex 20:11)
  - [STR] The doctrine rests here, in the literal fourth commandment: a literal seventh day, kept holy by literal rest, because the LORD literally made heaven and earth and rested. The Sabbath identifies WHO is worshipped (the LORD), WHAT He did (created), and WHAT is His (heaven and earth). This is no "rest-attitude" — it is a dated, weekly observance God blessed and hallowed.

- **Rome's counterfeit: a rival day of worship grafted in**
  - "And the fourth kingdom shall be strong as iron: forasmuch as iron breaketh in pieces and subdueth all things" (Dan 2:40)
  - The counterfeit substitutes a different day for the one God blessed — a day for which Scripture gives no command. The change came by custom and decree, not by any "thus saith the LORD."
  - [→] Iron does not reason — it crushes. The change of the day was legislated and absorbed, never argued from Scripture.

- **God's own assessment of sun-facing worship in His house**
  - "And he brought me into the inner court of the LORD's house, and, behold, at the door of the temple of the LORD, between the porch and the altar, were about five and twenty men, with their backs toward the temple of the LORD, and their faces toward the east; and they worshipped the sun toward the east" (Ezek 8:16)
  - "Then he said unto me, Hast thou seen this, O son of man?... they have filled the land with violence, and have returned to provoke me to anger" (Ezek 8:17)
  - [→] The location is the point: these are God's own people, in God's own house, with their _backs toward the temple_ and their _faces toward the east_ — sun-ward. God calls it abomination. A counterfeit worship inside the true sanctuary's own courts.

[TANGENT] The seventh day was never neutral ground to be reassigned. "And on the seventh day God ended his work... And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it" (Gen 2:2-3) — before sin, before Israel, before Sinai. The Sabbath is not Jewish; it is Edenic, given to the human race because it was given to the _first_ human, Adam. And the counterfeit targets the one commandment that opens with "Remember" — the command most easily forgotten, and therefore the one Satan most wants forgotten.

- **Where it lives today**
  - First-day sacredness assumed as Christian practice
  - No biblical command for it exists — the change was institutional, not scriptural
  - "But in vain they do worship me, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men" (Matt 15:9)

- **Christ restores the true thing**
  - "For the Son of man is Lord even of the sabbath day" (Matt 12:8)
  - "If ye love me, keep my commandments" (John 14:15)
  - "And he came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up: and, as his custom was, he went into the synagogue on the sabbath day" (Luke 4:16)
  - [→] The Sabbath is not abolished — it is restored to its meaning: literal rest in the Creator, trust in the Redeemer, the memorial of the God who made you and is remaking you. (Full study: Study 24.)

## The Complete Counterfeit — One System, One Babylon

- **Four empires, four substitutions, one stacked system**
  - Babylon — replaced the **Priest** (who you go through): 1 Tim 2:5; Heb 8:1-2
  - Medo-Persia — replaced the **Sacrifice** (how you are saved): Lev 17:11; Heb 9:22
  - Greece — replaced the **truth about death** (what you hope for): Eccl 9:5; 1 Cor 15:18
  - Rome — replaced the **Sabbath** (whom you worship, and when): Ex 20:8-11
  - Stack them and you have a complete counterfeit sanctuary: wrong mediator, wrong means of salvation, wrong understanding of death, wrong day of worship.

- **Revelation names the finished system "Babylon"**
  - "And upon her forehead was a name written, MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS AND ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH" (Rev 17:5)
  - The last earthly system carries the name of the first — because it carries all four layers. (Babylon unmasked in full: Study 22.)
  - [→] Any institution that holds all four counterfeits is not a random pile of errors. It is the completed statue.

- **Every counterfeit serves the two charges of the cosmic trial**
  - Study 1 filed two charges: "they only obey for reward" (Charge A) and "the law cannot be kept" (Charge B).
  - Replace the **Priest** → man can't access God directly → Charge B reinforced (you cannot know God, so you cannot bear His image).
  - Replace the **Sacrifice** → salvation becomes transactional → Charge A reinforced (they serve God for what they get).
  - Replace the **truth about death** → no real judgment, no real accountability → both charges shielded from exposure.
  - Replace the **Sabbath** → the law is shown changeable → Charge B confirmed (if God's law can be altered, it was never the fixed standard).
  - [→] The counterfeit system is not random error. It is Satan's charges institutionalized as doctrine — a thread Studies 8, 21, and 28 will draw all the way to the final crisis.

- **But the stone strikes** _(back to the spine)_
  - "And in the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed... and it shall stand for ever" (Dan 2:44)
  - The stone is "cut out without hands" (Dan 2:34) — divine, not human. It does not reform the statue; it grinds it to chaff (Dan 2:35).
  - [→] You cannot fix a counterfeit system. You can only let the true kingdom replace it.

## The Test — One Question Exposes Every Counterfeit

- **The Bible's own diagnostic**
  - "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them" (Isa 8:20)
  - Applied to each layer: Does this teaching point me to Christ in heaven, or to an earthly substitute?
    - Does it send me to a human priest — or to Christ my High Priest? (Heb 8:1-2)
    - Does it tell me to earn salvation — or to receive a finished sacrifice? (Heb 10:14)
    - Does it tell me the dead are alive — or that Christ will raise them? (1 Thess 4:16)
    - Does it honor a day men appointed — or the day God blessed? (Ex 20:11)
  - [→] Four questions, four counterfeits, one diagnostic. Every doctrine in this series — and every teaching you will ever meet — must answer to Isaiah 8:20.

## Appeal

- The counterfeits are not ancient history — they are present reality, walking under Christian names.
- Babylon's priesthood still stands between sinners and Christ. Persia's scales still weigh human works against divine grace. Greece's lie still tells the dying they will not really die. Rome's rival day still displaces the Creator's Sabbath.
- But the stone is coming — cut without hands, unstoppable, final. Daniel 2:44 has already settled _whether_ the counterfeits fall.
- The only open question is _where you are standing_ when they do: on the statue, or on the Rock.
- "To the law and to the testimony" — let every doctrine you hold be tested by this word, and let every counterfeit it exposes be abandoned. God drew the whole map in advance so that you could read your way home off of it.
- Will you stand on the stone?
