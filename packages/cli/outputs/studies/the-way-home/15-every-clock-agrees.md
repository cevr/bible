# Study 15: Every Clock Agrees — The Daily, the 1290, the 1335, and the 2520

**The 2300 days do not stand alone. The 1290 and the 1335 of Daniel 12, both reckoned from the taking away of "the daily" — and the "seven times" of Leviticus 26 — were set running on different anchors, in different books, by different prophets, and yet they all strike the same two hours: 1798 and 1843/44. One date might be a coincidence. Independent clocks, started centuries apart, agreeing to the year, are a signature. The God the accuser calls careless and self-serving turns out to be the most exact keeper of time in the universe — and every clock He set is telling the same hour.**

## Where We Stand — and What Is Left to Prove

**Study 14 brought us to the threshold of 1844 by one road: the 2300 days of Daniel 8:14, cut from the same starting point as the seventy weeks, dating the decree, the Messiah, the cross, and the gospel to the Gentiles to the very year. Three of four dates were already verified by history. The fourth — 1844 — stands on the credibility of the three. This study asks a further question: is 1844 hanging on a single thread, or is it braided into a cord?**

## First, "The Daily" — What Is Being Taken Away?

**Two of our three Daniel-clocks are anchored to the moment "the daily" is "taken away." So before we can date anything, we must let Scripture tell us what "the daily" is. Notice at once: this must be settled from the Bible, by the Bible's own clues — not asserted on any man's authority. And the very first clue is sitting in the English text in front of you.**

- **"Sacrifice" is not in the original — the translators supplied it**
  - "Yea, he magnified himself even to the prince of the host, and by him the daily sacrifice was taken away, and the place of his sanctuary was cast down" (Dan 8:11)
  - In a King James Bible the word "sacrifice" is printed in _italics_ every time it appears with "the daily" (Dan 8:11,12,13; 11:31; 12:11). Italics in the KJV mark a word the translators _added_ for smoothness that is **not in the Hebrew**. The Hebrew reads simply "the daily" — "the continual."
  - [STR] So the first rule of Study 0 applies hard here: do not add to the word. The text does not say "the daily _sacrifice_." It says "the _continual_." We must find from the context what continual thing is meant — not import the meaning "Jewish sacrifice" the added word suggests.

- **The Hebrew word is _tamid_ — "continual," and it is everywhere**
  - _Tamid_ simply means "continual, perpetual, that which goes on always." It occurs roughly a hundred times in the Old Testament and by itself names no single object — it is an adjective looking for its noun.
  - [→] The question is therefore not "which sacrifice?" but "which _continual_ desolating thing is in view in this vision?" And the verse answers by pairing it with another.

- **The context names TWO desolating powers, side by side**
  - "Then I heard one saint speaking, and another saint said unto that certain saint which spake, How long shall be the vision concerning the daily sacrifice, and the transgression of desolation, to give both the sanctuary and the host to be trodden under foot?" (Dan 8:13)
  - Read it without the supplied word: "How long shall be the vision concerning **the continual**, _and_ **the transgression of desolation**?" The angel's own question sets two things side by side — two powers that successively tread down the sanctuary and the host.
  - One of them, "the transgression of desolation," is the little horn of Daniel 8 — the **papal** power proved in Studies 8 and 14 (the abomination that follows). It is the _second_ desolating power.
  - [→] So "the continual" is the _first_ desolating power — the one that occupies the ground _before_ the abomination is set up and must be "taken away" to make room for it. In the march of Daniel 2 and 7, the power that held the field before the papacy is **pagan Rome**. "The continual" = the **continual / perpetual desolating power of paganism** — pagan worship and pagan Rome, the desolator that ruled before the papal desolator rose.

- **The vision itself shows the sequence: pagan first, then papal**
  - "And by him the daily was taken away, and the place of his sanctuary was cast down" (Dan 8:11) — the continual (pagan) supremacy is removed so the horn's own "sanctuary" can be established
  - "And from the time that the daily shall be taken away, and the abomination that maketh desolate set up..." (Dan 12:11) — **first** the continual is _taken away_, **then** the abomination is _set up_. Two distinct, dated steps. Paganism does not vanish on its own; it is _taken away_ to clear the way for the abomination.
  - [→] This is exactly the order of history the earlier studies proved: pagan Rome (the continual desolation) → papal Rome (the abomination of desolation). Daniel 12:11 dates the hinge between them.

[Q] **"But isn't 'the daily' obviously the Jewish daily sacrifice? Every commentary says so."**

> Test it by the text, not by the crowd. (1) The word "sacrifice" is _supplied_ — it is in italics, not in the Hebrew. (2) The Jewish sacrifices were not "taken away" by the little horn / the abomination; they ceased at the cross and at AD 70, _before_ the papal abomination ever rose — so they cannot be the thing removed to make room for it. (3) The angel pairs "the continual" with "the transgression of desolation" as two _desolating_ powers treading down the sanctuary (Dan 8:13) — and a holy sacrifice ordained by God is not a desolating power. The only reading that fits all three facts is the continual _desolation_ of paganism, taken away so the papal abomination could be set up. The plain text, line upon line, forces it.

[DYK🔎] This is the point the mature Adventist position settled on after Miller's day, and it is the reading carried in Uriah Smith's _Daniel and the Revelation_: "the daily" is not the Hebrew ritual but the _continual_ (paganism), the desolating power that held the field until it was taken away in favor of the papal abomination. We arrive at it here, though, the only legitimate way — from the italics, the Hebrew, and the angel's own pairing in Daniel 8:13 — not on Smith's say-so. Read the verse with the added word stripped out and the conclusion is the verse's own.

## Clock One Re-Anchored — and Clocks Two and Three Open: Daniel 12

**With "the daily" identified, Daniel 12 hands us a date-stamp and two periods hung on it. Daniel has just been told the time, times, and a half (the 1260, Dan 12:7 — same period proved in Study 8). Now two more numbers are given, both reckoned from the same event: the taking away of the continual.**

- **The anchor verse — two events, one starting line**
  - "And from the time that the daily sacrifice shall be taken away, and the abomination that maketh desolate set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days" (Dan 12:11)
  - The clock starts when "the daily" (paganism, the continual) "shall be taken away" — and the abomination is "set up." 1290 prophetic days from that hinge.

- **The 1290 — 1290 years by the same key**
  - 1290 prophetic days = 1290 prophetic years (Num 14:34; Eze 4:6 — the one key, applied consistently across every Daniel period)
  - The start is the taking away of the daily; history fixes that at **AD 508** (documented below)
  - 508 + 1290 = **1798** — the close of the 1290, falling on the very year the 1260 also closes (538 + 1260 = 1798, Study 8)
  - [STR] The 1290 and the 1260 _end together_ in 1798 but _begin thirty years apart_ — 508 and 538. Why thirty? Because the daily was _taken away_ (508) thirty years before the abomination was fully _set up_ (538). The text put both events in one verse (Dan 12:11) and the calendar keeps the thirty-year gap exactly. Two clocks, two start-dates, one finish line.

- **The 1335 — the blessing on those who wait**
  - "Blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days" (Dan 12:12)
  - 1335 prophetic days = 1335 prophetic years, from the same 508 anchor (the verse follows immediately on the 1290's start, sharing its starting line)
  - 508 + 1335 = **1843/44** — the close of the 1335 falls on the same terminus as the 2300 days
  - [→] Read what lands on that date: a _blessing_, pronounced on "him that waiteth" — those who, at the end of these days, are **waiting** for something and _come to_ it. The 2300 days end in 1844 with the cleansing of the sanctuary; the 1335 days end in 1844 with a blessing on a _waiting people_. Two clocks, two books, one date, and the second tells you what kind of people will be standing there: not the careless, but the _waiting_.

- **The 45-year gap, accounted for**
  - 1335 − 1290 = **45 years** — the distance from the close of the 1290 (1798) to the close of the 1335 (1843)
  - 1798 + 45 = 1843. The same 45 years that separate the two numbers in Daniel 12 separate the two dates in history. The text's own arithmetic predicts the interval, and the calendar keeps it.
  - [STR] This is "Scripture converts its own units" again. We did not choose 45; Daniel did (1335 minus 1290). We did not choose 1843; the calendar did (1798 plus 45). The numbers were written down centuries before either date arrived.

[ILL] Picture three stopwatches lying on a table, each started by a different hand at a different moment — one in 508 (the 1290), one in 508 (the 1335), one back in 457 BC (the 2300). They were never synchronized; the men who read them did not set them. Yet the 1290 stops on 1798, the 2300 and the 1335 both stop on 1843/44, and the 1290 and the 1260 — started thirty years apart — stop on the very same 1798. No watchmaker sets unsynchronized clocks to chime together by accident. Either this is the most extraordinary coincidence in the history of numbers, or one Hand wound them all.

## "The Time of the End" — Why 1798 Opens the Book

**The 1290 closing in 1798 is not a bare arithmetic result; Daniel was told what that hour would do. It would unseal his own book. So the very years in which these calculations could finally be made are the years the prophecy said the calculations would be made.**

- **The book is sealed UNTIL the time of the end**
  - "But thou, O Daniel, shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end: many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased" (Dan 12:4)
  - "And he said, Go thy way, Daniel: for the words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end" (Dan 12:9)
  - Daniel himself did not understand: "And I heard, but I understood not: then said I, O my Lord, what shall be the end of these things?" (Dan 12:8). The book was deliberately closed — _until_ a specific era.

- **Daniel 8's vision pointed to the same era**
  - "Understand, O son of man: for at the time of the end shall be the vision" (Dan 8:17)
  - "Wherefore shut thou up the vision; for it shall be for many days" (Dan 8:26)
  - The 2300-day vision and Daniel 12's numbers were both sealed "for many days," to be opened "at the time of the end."

- **1798 — the close of the 1290 — opens that time of the end**
  - When the 1290 ran out in 1798, the power that had "closed up" understanding for 1260 years was itself broken (Study 8; full account at Study 21). The seal came off the book.
  - "Many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased" (Dan 12:4) — and from 1798 onward students of prophecy across the world independently arrived at the 2300-day reckoning and the 1843/44 date.
  - "Many shall be purified, and made white, and tried... and none of the wicked shall understand; but the wise shall understand" (Dan 12:10) — understanding the book is itself a sign of the era it pointed to.
  - [→] So the 1290 is not only a clock that strikes 1798; it is the clock whose striking _opened the case_ of all the others. The God of Daniel 12 told you in advance not only _when_ the prophecy would be understood, but that it could not be understood before. He sealed it, dated the unsealing, and the dating held.

## Clock Four — The "Seven Times" of Leviticus 26 (a Corroborating Witness)

**Now an older clock, set running long before Daniel — on Sinai, by Moses, in the covenant warnings of Leviticus 26. It is presented here exactly for what it is: a corroborating, secondary witness, a line Miller taught and that points to the same 1843, which the later Adventist leaders (Smith and the _Daniel and the Revelation_ school) set respectfully aside as not airtight. We give it honest weight — strong enough to corroborate, not strong enough to lean a doctrine on. And it is a witness with a remarkable feature: it lands on the same year by a completely separate road.**

- **Four times, God threatens "seven times" of chastisement**
  - "And if ye will not yet for all this hearken unto me, then I will punish you seven times more for your sins" (Lev 26:18)
  - "And if ye walk contrary unto me, and will not hearken unto me; I will bring seven times more plagues upon you according to your sins" (Lev 26:21)
  - "Then will I also walk contrary unto you, and will punish you yet seven times for your sins" (Lev 26:24)
  - "Then I will walk contrary unto you also in fury; and I, even I, will chastise you seven times for your sins" (Lev 26:28)
  - The covenant warns of a national chastisement measured as "seven times."

- **The chastisement is a SCATTERING among the nations**
  - "And I will scatter you among the heathen, and will draw out a sword after you: and your land shall be desolate, and your cities waste" (Lev 26:33)
  - This is not a brief, literal seven-year discipline; it is the long _national_ dispersion of Israel among the heathen — a period, not a moment.

- **"Seven times" as prophetic time = 2520 years**
  - A prophetic "time" = a year (Study 8/9); a prophetic year = 360 days; so "seven times" = 7 × 360 = **2520** prophetic days = **2520 prophetic years** by the day-year key (Num 14:34; Eze 4:6)
  - This reads "seven times" as a _prophetic period of scattering_ — consistent with the year-day key applied everywhere else in this series.
  - [→] The honest caution: Leviticus 26 uses "seven times" to mean _sevenfold intensity_ ("seven times _more_"), and reading it instead as a 2520-year _period_ is a step the text does not force the way Daniel 12:11 forces its numbers. This is why it is corroborating, not keystone.

- **The anchor — Manasseh's captivity, 677 BC**
  - "Wherefore the LORD brought upon them the captains of the host of the king of Assyria, which took Manasseh among the thorns, and bound him with fetters, and carried him to Babylon" (2 Chron 33:11)
  - Miller dated the beginning of the dispersion-chastisement from this captivity of Judah's king, **677 BC**.
  - 2520 years from 677 BC lands on **AD 1843/44** — the very window in which the 2300 days and the 1335 days close.
  - [→] _Why 1843 and not 1844?_ The bare subtraction (2520 − 677, +1 across the year-zero seam) gives 1844, exactly as the 2300 does from 457 BC. Miller, however, reckoned the captivity to the _spring_ of 677 BC and counted to the _spring_ terminus — so his "Jewish year 1843" actually ran from spring 1843 into spring 1844. It is the **same prophetic-year window** the 2300 closes in; the "1843" and "1844" labels are two ends of one year, not a one-year disagreement. The clocks converge; they do not contradict. (This spring-to-spring detail is one more reason the later students treated the 2520 as corroborating rather than keystone — its anchor and its exact terminus both carry a margin the 2300 does not.)
  - [STR] A fourth clock, started on Sinai, anchored at 677 BC, running by a wholly separate route — and it stops in the same 1843/44 window as the others. Even discounted to its proper, secondary weight, a witness that independently names the same year is worth hearing.

[Q] **"If the 2520 is doubtful, why bring it up at all?"**

> Because honesty cuts both ways. To _hide_ a witness that agrees would be as dishonest as to _overclaim_ one that does not. We are not building the 1844 date on the 2520 — the 2300 days built it, by themselves, beyond dispute (Study 14). We mention the 2520 the way you would mention a fourth eyewitness whose testimony is consistent but whose vantage was partly obstructed: it adds weight, it does not bear the case. Miller proclaimed it; the later Adventist students examined it and, judging it not airtight, leaned their teaching on the surer Daniel-8 reckoning instead. That is sound method — and we follow it here.

[DYK🔎] It is a matter of record that William Miller himself preached the "seven times" of Leviticus 26 as 2520 years to 1843, and that as the movement matured, Uriah Smith and the _Daniel and the Revelation_ tradition quietly set that line aside — not because it pointed to the wrong year, but because its _premises_ (reading sevenfold-intensity as a fixed period) could not be nailed down as tightly as Daniel 8:14. The mature position keeps it as a secondary corroboration only. We have done the same — establishing 1844 on the 2300 days, and letting the 2520 stand behind them as a quieter voice saying the same thing.

## Keeping the "Seven Times" Distinct — Three Different Clocks, Not One Blur

**A warning the spec of sound interpretation demands. There are three different "seven times" / "time, times, and a half" reckonings in this Bible, and the careless reader melts them into one mush. They are NOT the same. Holding them apart is itself part of taking God at His exact word.**

- **Daniel 4 — Nebuchadnezzar's SEVEN LITERAL YEARS (the source of the unit)**
  - "That they shall drive thee from men... and seven times shall pass over thee, till thou know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men" (Dan 4:25; repeated Dan 4:32)
  - These "seven times" passed literally over one man, a king reduced to a beast and restored. They are **seven literal years** — not a prophetic period at all.
  - [STR] But notice the immense service this passage renders: it is the Bible's own proof that one "time" = one year. Nebuchadnezzar's "seven times" are seven _years_ (the narrative shows it — a season of madness and restoration measured in years, Dan 4:34-36). This is where the unit "a time = a year" is **defined by Scripture**, so that when prophetic "times" appear elsewhere we already know the unit from God's own usage.
  - [→] Do NOT do what some over-eager teachers do and turn Daniel 4's literal seven years into "the 2520 prophecy." That blur is itself an error. Daniel 4 supplies the _unit_ (a time = a year); it is not itself a 2520-year prophecy.

- **Leviticus 26 — the NATION'S 2520 prophetic years (the dispersion)**
  - "Seven times" of national scattering (Lev 26:18,21,24,28,33) — read as prophetic time, 7 × 360 = 2520 years (the corroborating witness above)
  - Subject: the nation of Israel, scattered among the heathen. A distinct reckoning, on a distinct anchor (677 BC), to a distinct end (1843).

- **Daniel 7 / Revelation 12-13 — the PAPACY'S 1260-year half (a "time, times, and half a time")**
  - "And they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time" (Dan 7:25); "for a time, times, and an half" (Dan 12:7); "forty and two months" (Rev 11:2; 13:5); "a thousand two hundred and threescore days" (Rev 11:3; 12:6); "a time, and times, and half a time" (Rev 12:14)
  - This is **three-and-a-half** times = 1260 years — the _half_ of a "seven times" — and it is the **papal** supremacy, 538-1798 (Study 8). A wholly different subject (the little horn), a different length (1260, not 2520), a different anchor (538).

[STR] **Lay them in a row so the lines never cross:**

- **Daniel 4** — _seven literal years_, over **one man** (Nebuchadnezzar) → supplies the unit "a time = a year." NOT a prophecy of a period.
- **Leviticus 26** — _2520 prophetic years_ (seven full "times"), over **the nation** → 677 BC to 1843 (corroborating witness).
- **Daniel 7 / Revelation 12-13** — _1260 prophetic years_ (three-and-a-half "times," the _half_ of seven), over **the papal power** → 538 to 1798 (keystone, Study 8).
  Three "sevens-language" reckonings, three subjects, three lengths, three anchors. To collapse them is to break Scripture against itself; to keep them distinct is to honor the exactness of the God who wrote them.

  - [STR] So the four clocks of this study are not numerology smuggled in by enthusiasts. They are the chronology of the prophet Christ vouched for, in the book Christ told us to open, understood by the people Daniel said would understand. The authority behind the reckoning is the authority of the Author of Study 0 — the Spirit who wrote the word keeping the right to interpret it.

## The 6000-Year Frame — Backdrop, Never a Date

**The 1843 chart that carried these clocks also sketched a six-thousand-year frame for the world's history — six millennial "days" of labor, then a seventh of rest, after the pattern of the creation week. A word of discipline is owed here, because this is exactly the kind of frame a careless mind turns into a date — and the spec of honest interpretation forbids it.**

- **The frame is a descriptive pattern, not a clock**
  - The week of creation (Gen 2:2-3) and "one day is with the Lord as a thousand years" (2 Pet 3:8) suggested to the pioneers a great-week shape to history — six "days" of toil, a sabbath of rest. It served as a _backdrop_ on the chart, a way of picturing where in history the advent fell.
  - [STR] But **no date in this series is built on it**, and none may be. The chronology of the world's age before Christ is not fixed precisely enough in Scripture to yield a year, and 2 Peter 3:8 ("one day is with the Lord as a thousand years") is a statement about God's patience — the very next verse explains it, "The Lord is not slack concerning his promise" (2 Pet 3:9) — not a measuring-rod for setting dates. To compute an advent date from a 6000-year frame would be to do the very thing Christ forbade: "of that day and hour knoweth no man" (Matt 24:36).
  - [→] Keep it where it belongs: as the descriptive frame of the old chart, a sermon-illustration of history's great week — _never_ as a fifth clock. The four clocks above all rest on **stated** anchors (a decree, a removal, a captivity) and a **proved** key (the year-day). The 6000-year frame rests on neither. It is backdrop, and backdrop only.

[Q] **"Then doesn't admitting the 6000-year frame is loose undercut the whole study?"**

> No — it _strengthens_ it, by showing where the line is. The four time-clocks stand on hard anchors: a dated decree (457 BC, Ezra 7), a dated removal of the continual (508), a dated captivity (677 BC), and a key God spoke with His own mouth (Num 14:34). The 6000-year frame stands on none of these, so we do not build on it. A reckoning honest enough to _refuse_ the frame that doesn't qualify is a reckoning you can trust on the frames that do. We are not grabbing every number that points to 1844; we are testing each one and keeping only what the Bible nails down.

## How History Kept Every Clock — The Fulfillment Lane

**Everything above was established from the Bible: "the daily" from the italics and Daniel 8:13; the 1290 and 1335 from Daniel 12:11-12; the 2520 from Leviticus 26 (corroborating); the distinctions from comparing Daniel 4, Leviticus 26, and Daniel 7. Now, in its own clearly-marked lane, we lay the dated anchors beside the historical record. This is the historicist proof-form: the Bible sets the period and the rule; history supplies the events that start and stop the clock.**

- **AD 508 — the daily (paganism) taken away**
  - The prophecy: "from the time that the daily shall be taken away..." (Dan 12:11) sets the start of the 1290 and 1335.
  - The record: through the late fifth and early sixth centuries the pagan power that had held Western Rome was broken and the field cleared for the papal supremacy. Clovis, king of the Franks, converted and was baptized into the Catholic faith (commonly dated 496), and by **AD 508** the decisive Catholic ascendancy over the remaining pagan and Arian opposition in the West was secured — paganism, "the continual," effectively _taken away_ as the desolating power in possession. This is the start-date the two clocks need.

- **AD 538 — the abomination set up**
  - The prophecy: "and the abomination that maketh desolate set up" (Dan 12:11) — the second step, after the daily is removed.
  - The record: **AD 538**, the year the last opposing power (the Ostrogoths) was driven from Rome and the papal supremacy began (the start of the 1260, documented in Study 8). The thirty years from 508 to 538 are the gap Daniel 12:11 marks between the _taking away_ and the _setting up_.

- **AD 1798 — the close of the 1290 (and the 1260)**
  - The arithmetic: 508 + 1290 = **1798**; and 538 + 1260 = **1798** (Study 8). Two periods, started thirty years apart, ending the same year.
  - The record: in **1798** the papal power received its "deadly wound" — the pope carried captive by the armies of France (the full deadly-wound account and its healing belong to Revelation 13, Study 21). Supremacy interrupted on schedule, and the sealed book of Daniel thrown open to "the time of the end."

- **AD 1843/44 — the close of the 1335, the 2300, and the 2520**
  - The arithmetic: 508 + 1335 = **1843/44**; 457 BC + 2300 = **1844**; 677 BC + 2520 = **1843/44** (Miller's spring-to-spring reckoning of the 2520, the same prophetic-year window — see the note above). Three reckonings (and the 2520 a fourth), three anchors, one terminus.
  - The record: the great **Advent awakening** of 1843-1844 — a worldwide movement of "him that waiteth" (Dan 12:12), looking for the cleansing of the sanctuary (the chart, the midnight cry, and October 22 are documented in Study 17). The blessing of the 1335 fell on a _waiting people_ precisely when Daniel said it would.

- **677 BC — the 2520 anchor (corroborating)**
  - The prophecy: the "seven times" of national scattering (Lev 26), read as 2520 years.
  - The record: **677 BC**, the captivity of King Manasseh of Judah carried to Babylon (2 Chron 33:11) — the anchor Miller used. Noted as a _debated_ starting point (it is one of several captivities), which is the honest reason the 2520 is held as secondary.

[→] Lay the lanes side by side and the pattern is unmistakable: the Bible set four periods by stated anchors and one proved key; history started each clock on a datable event and stopped each on a datable event; and they converge — 1798 (twice), 1843/44 (three or four times over). The prophecy predicted; history recorded; the dates matched. That is the whole historicist proof-form, run four times in one study.

## What This Says About the God on Trial

**Step back from the arithmetic to the courtroom. Remember the two charges of Study 1 — that God is careless of His creatures and serves only Himself, and that His government cannot really be kept or trusted. Look at what these four clocks say about His character.**

- **A God this exact is not a careless God** — He did not leave His people to grope. He wrote down the periods, sealed them until the hour they could be understood, dated the unsealing, and made history keep every appointment to the year. Carelessness does not produce four independent clocks striking the same hour.
- **A God this open is not a self-serving God** — He could have kept the timing to Himself. Instead He told Daniel, told the reader through Daniel ("the wise shall understand," Dan 12:10), and pointed there by His own Son ("whoso readeth, let him understand," Matt 24:15). He _wants_ to be checked; He hands you the receipts.
- **A God this faithful answers the second charge by His own constancy** — the same God who keeps a 2520-year appointment from Sinai to 1843/44, and a 1290-year appointment from 508 to 1798, is not a God whose government quietly fails. The clocks are exhibits in the trial: they are the testimony of His exactness, His openness, and His faithfulness, all at once.

[STR] This is why the Daniel block closes here. We have walked from the four kingdoms (Study 7) to the named power (Study 8), proved the key (Study 9), watched the churches, seals, and trumpets keep their schedule (Studies 10-13), and dated the cleansing by the 2300 days (Study 14). Now four clocks, set going on Sinai and in 508 and in 677 BC, all strike the same two hours — and Christ Himself signs the reckoning. The God the accuser called careless is the most exact Being in the universe; the God he called secretive hands you every figure to check. The case for trusting Him is overwhelming. **What remains is not whether the hour has struck, but what the cleansing the hour announces actually MEANS.**

## Appeal

- God did not hang the date of the judgment on a single thread. He braided it into a cord that cannot be quickly broken.
- Four clocks — the 2300 days from a decree, the 1290 and 1335 from the taking away of the daily, the 2520 from a captivity — started centuries apart, in different books, on different anchors, and they strike the same two hours: 1798 and 1843/44.
- He sealed the book until "the time of the end," dated the unsealing, and made history open it on schedule — and then His own Son told you to read it and understand.
- A God this exact, this open, this faithful, is not the God the charges describe. Every clock He set is a witness _for_ His character and against the accuser.
- The hour has struck. The only question left is the one Study 18 will answer: now that 1844 has come and the sanctuary is being cleansed — _what is happening there, and what does it mean for you?_
- Will you trust the God whose every clock agrees?
