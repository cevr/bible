# Study 11: The Seven Seals — and the Signs in Sun, Moon, and Stars

**The same church-age Daniel mapped from the outside, the Lamb now unrolls from the inside: seven sealed sections of a scroll, opened one at a time, tracing the outward providence of the church across the Christian era — white-horse purity, red persecution, black corruption, pale papal death — until between the fifth seal and the sixth the symbols suddenly stop and the language turns plainly literal: a great earthquake, a darkened sun, a blood-red moon, and stars falling like figs from a shaken tree. Those were not poetry. They fell on Lisbon in 1755, on New England in 1780, and over the United States in 1833 — God's own alarm clock hung over the world on the eve of the judgment.**

## The Scroll, the Lamb, and Three Windows on One Story

**We have already walked this era twice. Daniel 2 and 7 mapped it as kingdoms; Revelation's seven churches retraced it from inside the church. Now the Lamb takes a sealed scroll and opens it section by section — the same age, a third window. Before we read a single seal, fix what kind of book this is.**

- **Revelation is a SIGNIFIED book — symbols where it says symbols, plain where it turns plain**
  - "The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him, to shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass; and he sent and signified [it] by his angel unto his servant John" (Rev 1:1)
  - "Signified" — given in signs. So we read each seal as a sign UNTIL the text itself drops the signs and speaks literally. We do not decide that switch by preference; the text decides it for us (Study 0's rule: plain unless the text marks a figure).
  - [STR] This is the master-key to the whole chapter. Seals one through five are clearly symbolic — colored horses, balances, robed souls. Then at verse 12 the imagery changes character entirely: real earthquakes, a real sun, a real moon, real stars. The seam between verse 11 and verse 12 is where the sign-book turns into a literal almanac.

- **The seals run PARALLEL to the churches and the trumpets — one era, three vantage points**
  - The seven churches (Study 10) gave the church's INNER spiritual state, age by age. The seven seals give the OUTWARD providence — what God permitted to befall the church across the same ages. The seven trumpets (Study 12) will give the political and military judgments on the nations that touched her.
  - [→] Same river, three banks. They do not contradict; they corroborate. "Precept upon precept; line upon line" (Isa 28:10) — and three independent lines that land on the same history are three witnesses, not one guess.

- **Why a scroll opened seal by seal pictures successive eras**
  - An ancient scroll was sealed in sections; you could read no further until the next seal was broken. So the opening of seal after seal is the unrolling of history in sequence — the same forward march we saw in the metals of Daniel 2 and the fixed order of the seven church-letters (Rev 1:11).
  - [DYK🔎] The seals are not seven unrelated snapshots; they are one continuous document. That is why each seal picks up roughly where the last left off, the way Ephesus gave way to Smyrna, Smyrna to Pergamos, in Study 10.

## Self-Interpretation: Horses Are Providential Agencies

**A reader could ask, "Why read the four horses as agencies God sends across history? Isn't that imported?" No — the Bible interprets its own horses. Zechariah saw the same vision and an angel told him plainly what colored horses mean.**

- **Zechariah's colored horses ARE God's agencies sent into the earth — the prophet asks, the angel answers**
  - "In the first chariot [were] red horses; and in the second chariot black horses; And in the third chariot white horses; and in the fourth chariot grisled and bay horses." (Zech 6:2-3)
  - "Then I answered and said unto the angel that talked with me, What [are] these, my lord?" (Zech 6:4)
  - "And the angel answered and said unto me, These [are] the four spirits of the heavens, which go forth from standing before the Lord of all the earth." (Zech 6:5)
  - [STR] There is the Bible's own dictionary entry: colored horses going forth = spirits/agencies sent out from God's presence into the earth. Zechariah's horses even share the colors — red, black, white. The figure is fixed by Scripture, not by us.

- **So the four horses of Revelation 6 = four successive providential agencies riding through the church age**
  - "comparing spiritual things with spiritual" (1 Cor 2:13) — we let Zechariah unlock Revelation. Each horse is a stage of the church's outward condition, sent forth in turn from before the throne.
  - [→] Notice the safety in this. We are not free-associating on the colors; we are reading Revelation 6 with the lexicon Revelation's own Bible supplies. That is the difference between typology the text authorizes and numerology a man invents.

## The First Seal — The White Horse: The Pure, Conquering Gospel

**The Lamb breaks seal one. A white horse rides out — the church in apostolic purity, the gospel going forth victorious in its first century.**

- **A white horse, a bow, a crown, conquering**
  - "And I saw, and behold a white horse: and he that sat on him had a bow; and a crown was given unto him: and he went forth conquering, and to conquer." (Rev 6:2)
  - In Scripture white = purity and righteousness: the saints' "fine linen is the righteousness of saints" (Rev 19:8). The rider goes "conquering, and to conquer" — the unstoppable advance of the gospel in the first age, the church still pure, the word running and being glorified.
  - [→] Revelation's OWN white-horse rider is Christ Himself, "The Word of God," riding to conquer (Rev 19:11-13). So the first seal's white horse is the era when His word rode out clean and victorious — the apostolic church before the corruptions crept in. The color says it: this age was pure.

- **This is the providential face of Ephesus**
  - Study 10 showed Ephesus (apostolic, A.D. 31 onward) as a church of first works and first love. The first seal is the same age seen from outside: the gospel conquering across the Roman world. Inner state and outward providence agree — one era, two windows.

## The Second Seal — The Red Horse: Persecution and Blood

**Seal two. A red horse — the age of imperial persecution, when peace was taken from the church and the martyrs' blood ran.**

- **A red horse, peace taken, a great sword**
  - "And there went out another horse [that was] red: and [power] was given to him that sat thereon to take peace from the earth, and that they should kill one another: and there was given unto him a great sword." (Rev 6:4)
  - Red is the color of blood and slaughter. Peace is removed; a great sword is given. This is the era of the great pagan-Roman persecutions, when to confess Christ was to court death.
  - [→] This is the outward face of Smyrna. Smyrna was promised "tribulation ten days" (Rev 2:10) — by the year-day key (Study 9: "each day for a year," Num 14:34; Eze 4:6) ten symbolic days = ten years, the Diocletian persecution of A.D. 303-313, the last and fiercest. The red horse rides the same blood-soaked decades.

## The Third Seal — The Black Horse: Declension and Corruption

**Seal three. A black horse with a balance — the church grown dark, mingling truth and error, weighing and rationing the bread of life. The age of declension that set up the apostasy.**

- **A black horse, balances, measured grain, oil and wine spared**
  - "And I beheld, and lo a black horse; and he that sat on him had a pair of balances in his hand." (Rev 6:5)
  - "And I heard a voice in the midst of the four beasts say, A measure of wheat for a penny, and three measures of barley for a penny; and [see] thou hurt not the oil and the wine." (Rev 6:6)
  - Black is the color of darkness and spiritual famine (contrast the white of seal one). The balances ration grain at scarcity prices — the pure word measured out, scarce, mixed. This is the church of the third and fourth centuries sliding into error, the philosophy of Alexandria (the very allegorizing of Study 0) corrupting the simple gospel.
  - [DYK🔎] "Hurt not the oil and the wine." Oil and wine are Scripture's symbols of the Spirit and the gospel of Christ's blood. Even in the darkest declension a faithful remnant kept the oil and the wine untouched — God preserved a witness through the corruption. The famine was of the pure word; the genuine grace was not utterly extinguished.

- **The outward face of Pergamos**
  - Pergamos (Study 10) was the church "where Satan's seat is" — compromised, holding the doctrine of Balaam, drifting toward the throne of the world. The black horse is the same age from outside: the truth weighed out scarce while error multiplied.

## The Fourth Seal — The Pale Horse: Death, and the Papal Centuries

**Seal four. A pale, corpse-colored horse, and for the only time the rider is NAMED — Death, with the grave at his heels. The long centuries of papal supremacy, when the apostate power dealt death to the saints across a quarter of the earth.**

- **A pale horse named Death, with Hell following, killing four ways**
  - "And I looked, and behold a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him. And power was given unto them over the fourth part of the earth, to kill with sword, and with hunger, and with death, and with the beasts of the earth." (Rev 6:8)
  - "Pale" is the greenish hue of a corpse. This is the deadly age of the great apostasy — the 1260 years of the little horn (Dan 7:25, "wear out the saints... time and times and the dividing of time"), the papal centuries of inquisition, crusade, and martyrdom.
  - [DYK🔎] The four killers — "sword, and with hunger, and with death, and with the beasts of the earth" — are Scripture's own fixed list of God's "four sore judgments": "the sword, and the famine, and the noisome beast, and the pestilence" (Eze 14:21). John is not inventing a quartet; he is quoting Ezekiel's covenant-judgment formula. Once again the Bible supplies its own vocabulary.
  - [→] The four seals so far trace one descending arc the church-letters already taught: PURE (white/Ephesus) → PERSECUTED (red/Smyrna) → CORRUPTED (black/Pergamos) → DEADLY (pale/Thyatira and the 1260 years). Three windows, one identical history. When three independent prophecies map the same road, the map is not a coincidence.

## The Fifth Seal — The Souls Under the Altar: The Martyrs Cry "How Long?"

**Seal five. No horse now — instead, beneath the altar, the souls of the slain crying for justice. This is the cry of the centuries of blood: the martyrs of all the persecuting ages pleading for the judgment, and being told to wait a little longer.**

- **The souls under the altar, slain for the word, crying "how long?"**
  - "I saw under the altar the souls of them that were slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held: And they cried with a loud voice, saying, How long, O Lord, holy and true, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth?" (Rev 6:9-10)
  - "And white robes were given unto every one of them; and it was said unto them, that they should rest yet for a little season, until their fellowservants also and their brethren, that should be killed as they [were], should be fulfilled." (Rev 6:11)
  - These are the martyrs of the red and pale horses — the slain of the persecuting ages — represented as pleading for God to vindicate them. The answer: rest yet a little season; the full number is not complete; the judgment will come.

[Q] **"Doesn't 'the souls of them that were slain... cried' prove the dead are conscious — souls alive and speaking after death?"**
→ No — and the proof is in the Bible's own figure, not in our preference. Read it the way Scripture has already taught us to read. When righteous Abel was murdered, God said: **"the voice of thy brother's blood crieth unto me from the ground"** (Gen 4:10). Abel's blood was not literally conscious and talking; it was a figure — innocent blood crying out to God for justice. The martyrs "under the altar" cry the same way: their shed blood, like Abel's, pleads from the ground for vindication. The picture is deliberately drawn from the sanctuary, where the blood of the sacrifice was poured "at the bottom of the altar" — the slain saints are the sacrifices, their blood at the altar's base crying. Take it as conscious souls and you must explain how disembodied "souls" wear "white robes" (v. 11) and lie literally "under" a literal altar. The figure is plain once you read it as Scripture's own martyr-blood symbol.

- **And the rest of Scripture forbids the conscious-soul reading anyway**
  - "For the living know that they shall die: but the dead know not any thing... Also their love, and their hatred, and their envy, is now perished" (Eccl 9:5-6)
  - A symbolic book (Rev 1:1, "signified") gives us a symbolic martyr-cry; the plain didactic books tell us the dead "know not any thing." Never break Scripture against itself (John 10:35). The fifth seal does not overturn the state of the dead — it confirms how the Bible speaks of righteous blood. (We arm this fully in Study 27.)
  - [→] "White robes were given" = the verdict of vindication, righteousness imputed and acknowledged (Rev 19:8, "fine linen is the righteousness of saints"). The martyrs are declared righteous and told the judgment is coming — which is exactly where the sixth seal goes next.

## The Seam — Where the Signs Turn Literal

**Now watch the hinge of the whole chapter. Up to verse 11 we have been reading signs — horses, balances, robes. At verse 12 the Lamb opens the sixth seal and the language abruptly changes character. These are no longer colored symbols. They are events in the literal heavens, and Christ Himself, in the Gospels, told us they would be literal and told us WHEN.**

- **The sixth seal opens with literal cosmic signs in fixed order**
  - "And I beheld when he had opened the sixth seal, and, lo, there was a great earthquake; and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the moon became as blood; And the stars of heaven fell unto the earth, even as a fig tree casteth her untimely figs, when she is shaken of a mighty wind." (Rev 6:12-13)
  - "And the heaven departed as a scroll when it is rolled together; and every mountain and island were moved out of their places." (Rev 6:14)
  - Four things in order: (1) a great earthquake, (2) the sun blackened, (3) the moon as blood, (4) the stars falling.

- **Christ's OWN sign-list fixes these as literal AND dates them AFTER the 1260-year tribulation**
  - "Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken: And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven" (Matt 24:29-30)
  - "But in those days, after that tribulation, the sun shall be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, And the stars of heaven shall fall... And then shall they see the Son of man coming in the clouds" (Mark 13:24-26)
  - "And there shall be signs in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars... Men's hearts failing them for fear... for the powers of heaven shall be shaken" (Luke 21:25-26)
  - [STR] This is the master-key to the date. Jesus places these signs "**immediately after the tribulation**" — after the great papal persecution of the saints (the 1260 years, the deadly pale-horse age). He lists them in the SAME ORDER as Revelation 6: sun darkened, moon failing, stars falling, then His coming. The sixth seal is not poetry about the end of the world; it is Christ's own literal sign-calendar, placed at a specific point in history — just after the persecuting power's power to slay was broken in the mid-1700s.

- **The Old Testament foretold the very same blood-moon and rolled-up-heavens**
  - "The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and the terrible day of the LORD come" (Joel 2:31)
  - "And all the host of heaven shall be dissolved, and the heavens shall be rolled together as a scroll" (Isa 34:4)
  - Three witnesses across Testaments — Joel, Isaiah, Christ — describe the identical signs in the identical order Revelation 6 gives. The figure is fixed by Scripture itself: these are literal signs in the literal heavens, just before the day of the Lord.
  - [DYK🔎] On the day of Pentecost Peter quoted Joel's very prophecy — "The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before that great and notable day of the Lord come" (Acts 2:20) — as a still-future program of signs preceding the second advent. The apostles read it literally and unfulfilled. So did we, until history rang the bells.

## Document from History — When the Signs Fell

> **History's lane.** Everything above is proved from the Bible: the seals are providential agencies (Zech 6), the sixth-seal signs are literal and fall "after the tribulation" in fixed order (Matt 24; Mark 13; Luke 21; Joel 2; Isa 34). The MEANING is settled by Scripture alone. What history adds is only the receipt — the record that these literal signs actually fell, on schedule, in the order Christ named. This is the historicist proof-form: the Bible predicts X → the record shows X happened.

- **Why the signs fell when they did — the close of the 1260-year tribulation**
  - Christ dated the signs "immediately after the tribulation" (Matt 24:29). The 1260-year persecuting power's grip was broken as the eighteenth century moved on; the persecuting fury that had slain the saints across the papal centuries was spent by roughly the middle of the 1700s. Right at the close of that long tribulation — exactly where Jesus said to look — the first sign fell.

- **The great earthquake — Lisbon, November 1, 1755**
  - On the morning of November 1, 1755, the city of Lisbon, Portugal was destroyed by one of the most violent earthquakes in recorded history, felt across Europe and North Africa, tens of thousands killed in minutes. It is the most famous earthquake of the modern age and was widely received in its own time as a sign of divine judgment. The first item of the sixth seal — "a great earthquake" (Rev 6:12) — entered the record on schedule.

- **The Dark Day — May 19, 1780 (no eclipse can explain it)**
  - On May 19, 1780, an unnatural darkness fell over New England in the daytime. It was NOT a solar eclipse (the moon was nearly full, on the opposite side of the earth) and was never explained by any ordinary cause. Candles were lit at noon; fowls went to roost; the legislature of Connecticut, debating, was moved to adjourn under the impression the day of judgment had come. Contemporary records describe the sun as appearing "as sackcloth" — the very phrase of Revelation 6:12.
  - **The blood moon that same night.** When the moon rose that night it appeared the color of blood — exactly "the moon became as blood" (Rev 6:12) and "the moon into blood" (Joel 2:31; Acts 2:20). Sun darkened by day, moon as blood by night — the second and third signs, in order, on a single recorded date.

- **The falling stars — November 13, 1833 (the great Leonid meteor storm)**
  - In the predawn hours of November 13, 1833, the most spectacular meteor storm in recorded history fell over North America — observers estimated tens of thousands of meteors an hour, the whole sky raining fire from horizon to horizon for hours. It was seen across the entire United States. Witnesses described the stars falling exactly "as a fig tree casteth her untimely figs, when she is shaken of a mighty wind" (Rev 6:13) — the meteors streaming down like fruit shaken from a tree. The fourth sign, in order, on the record.
  - [→] Earthquake (1755) → sun darkened and moon as blood (1780) → stars falling (1833) — the four signs of the sixth seal, in the precise order Christ and Revelation give them, each entering the historical record at its appointed point in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, just after the close of the long tribulation. The MEANING was Scripture's; the DATES are history's signature on Scripture's prediction.

## The Sealing — Revelation 7 Falls Inside the Sixth Seal

**Between the falling stars (6:13) and the end of the world (6:14-17), the action pauses. Chapter 7 is a parenthesis dropped INTO the sixth seal: before the final winds of destruction are loosed, God seals His servants. That places the sealing work in our own era — after 1833, before the wrath. We live inside this verse.**

- **Four angels hold the winds until the servants are sealed**
  - "And after these things I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, that the wind should not blow on the earth, nor on the sea, nor on any tree." (Rev 7:1)
  - "And I saw another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God: and he cried with a loud voice to the four angels... Saying, Hurt not the earth, neither the sea, nor the trees, till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads." (Rev 7:2-3)
  - The "winds" of destruction (the final wrath of 6:14-17) are held back. The world's final ruin is restrained for one reason: God is sealing a people. This sealing belongs to the time AFTER the 1833 sign and BEFORE the day of wrath — which is to say, now.
  - [→] The sequence is the whole point: the sixth seal opened (1755-1833), the signs fell, and then the four winds are held while the sealing goes forward, and only AFTER the sealing comes the wrath (6:14-17). We are reading our own location on the prophetic clock: between verse 13 and verse 14. The signs are behind us; the wrath is ahead; the sealing is the work of the present hour. (The full sealed company — the 144,000 and the great multitude — is Study 29.)

- **The great multitude that comes out of this very tribulation**
  - "After this I beheld, and, lo, a great multitude, which no man could number... clothed with white robes" (Rev 7:9)
  - "These are they which came out of great tribulation, and have washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb." (Rev 7:14)
  - The same white robes the fifth-seal martyrs received (6:11) are washed white "in the blood of the Lamb" — the sealing reaches its harvest. The cry of the martyrs "how long?" is answered by a sealed multitude no man can number.

## The Sixth Seal Ends at the Second Coming

**The parenthesis closes and the sixth seal finishes its run — straight to the great day of wrath, the open face of the returning Christ. The signs that began at Lisbon end at the throne.**

- **The heavens depart, and the lost cry to be hidden from the Lamb**
  - "And the heaven departed as a scroll when it is rolled together; and every mountain and island were moved out of their places." (Rev 6:14)
  - "And the kings of the earth... hid themselves in the dens and in the rocks of the mountains; And said to the mountains and rocks, Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb: For the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?" (Rev 6:15-17)
  - This is the literal second advent — "the sign of the Son of man" Christ placed right after the same signs (Matt 24:30). The sixth seal runs from the cosmic signs of the 1700s-1800s to the very appearing of Christ. We stand inside it, between the signs already given and the wrath not yet poured.
  - [STR] "Who shall be able to stand?" That question is the door into the rest of the series. Revelation 7 just answered it: the SEALED shall stand. The whole burden of the studies ahead — the judgment (18), the three messages (19), the seal of God (24) — is to make sure that when the winds are loosed, you are among the sealed who can stand.

## Appeal

- The seventh seal — half an hour of silence in heaven — we leave for its place; but the sixth seal has already located US. The signs are not future to us; they are behind us. Lisbon shook in 1755. The sun went black over New England in 1780 and the moon rose as blood that night. The stars fell over America in 1833. God hung His alarm clock in the literal heavens, exactly where Christ said to look — "immediately after the tribulation" — and the world slept through the alarm.
- We are living between Revelation 6:13 and 6:14 — after the last sign, before the loosed winds, in the held-breath hour while God seals His servants in their foreheads. That is not a doctrine to admire; it is a position to take seriously. The next event the prophecy expects is the wrath of the Lamb.
- "The great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?" The Bible's own answer is the sealed — those whose robes are washed white in the blood of the Lamb. The martyrs under the altar were given white robes and told to wait; the great multitude came out of tribulation with the same robes washed clean. The seal is not a mark you earn by fear; it is the righteousness of Christ received, His character settled into you so the winds cannot move you.
- The alarm has already sounded. The question the chapter ends on is the only one that finally matters: when the heavens roll back like a scroll and the face on the throne is unveiled, will it be a face you have hidden FROM — or the face of the Lamb you have loved? Let Him seal you now, while the winds are still held.
