# Study 32: The Image Restored — The Millennium and the New Earth

**The trial that opened in Eden reaches its verdict. A literal thousand years opens every book before the universe until every intelligence — saved, lost, angel, and devil — confesses that God is just; then sin and its author are destroyed, not perpetuated; and a literal new earth restores Eden. The image of God, defined in Study 3 and broken in Study 2, is fully restored — His face seen, His name written. The Way Home is complete.**

- Revelation 21:1-5

## Two Resurrections, a Thousand Years Apart

**Before we can read the millennium, we must fix one thing the plain text states twice: there are TWO bodily resurrections, and they are one thousand years apart. This is the floor the whole chapter stands on — and it is the same conditionalist truth Study 27 established. The dead are asleep until a voice calls them out. No soul is anywhere being rewarded or punished now; everyone waits for his resurrection.**

- **the righteous rise first — at the Second Coming (Study 31)**
  - "Blessed and holy is he that hath part in the first resurrection: on such the second death hath no power, but they shall be priests of God and of Christ, and shall reign with him a thousand years" (Rev 20:6)
  - this first resurrection is the advent harvest of Study 31 — "the dead in Christ shall rise first" (1 Thess 4:16) — the only ones raised when Jesus comes
  - [→] the FIRST resurrection is a privilege, not a default. Only the blessed and holy are in it. The wicked are passed over for now.

- **the wicked rise last — after the thousand years**
  - "But the rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished. This is the first resurrection" (Rev 20:5)
  - "the rest of the dead" = the unsaved of all ages. They do not stir for a thousand years after the righteous rise.
  - Jesus named both companies plainly: "all that are in the graves shall hear his voice, And shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation" (John 5:28-29)
  - [STR] two resurrections, two companies, one thousand years between them. Revelation 20 simply puts a clock on what John 5 already said.

[Q] **"Don't most Christians teach one general resurrection and judgment at the same moment?"**
→ The plain text forbids it. "The rest of the dead lived not again UNTIL the thousand years were finished" (Rev 20:5) is meaningless unless one company is already living when the thousand years begin. Scripture is its own expositor (Study 0): John 5:29 names the two resurrections; Revelation 20 dates them a millennium apart. To collapse them into one is to break the text against itself (John 10:35). Hold both verses, and the picture is simple — righteous first, wicked last.

## The Millennium Is Literal — and the Earth Is the Prison

**The thousand years is not a golden age of earthly peace, nor a symbol for "a long time." It is a literal span with a literal geography: a depopulated, ruined earth on which Satan is chained by circumstance, with no one left to deceive — while the redeemed are in heaven opening the books. Read the chapter as written and every piece fits.**

- **Satan bound — the accuser chained to a wasteland**
  - "And I saw an angel come down from heaven, having the key of the bottomless pit and a great chain in his hand. And he laid hold on the dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil, and Satan, and bound him a thousand years" (Rev 20:1-2)
  - "And cast him into the bottomless pit, and shut him up, and set a seal upon him, that he should deceive the nations no more, till the thousand years should be fulfilled: and after that he must be loosed a little season" (Rev 20:3)
  - the "chain" is no literal iron link on a spirit-being — it is a chain of circumstances. His whole work is to "deceive the nations" (v.3); when there are no living nations to deceive, his occupation is gone. He is bound by having nothing left to do.

- **the "bottomless pit" = the unformed, empty earth — Scripture's own word**
  - the Greek behind "bottomless pit" is _abyssos_ — the very word the Greek Old Testament uses for the "deep" of Genesis 1:2: "the earth was without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep" (Gen 1:2)
  - Jeremiah was shown the earth AFTER the day of the Lord in exactly that creation-language, run in reverse: "I beheld the earth, and, lo, it was without form, and void; and the heavens, and they had no light" (Jer 4:23)
  - "I beheld, and, lo, there was no man, and all the birds of the heavens were fled" (Jer 4:25)
  - "I beheld, and, lo, the fruitful place was a wilderness, and all the cities thereof were broken down at the presence of the LORD, and by his fierce anger" (Jer 4:26)
  - [→] the same phrase that opens the Bible — "without form, and void" — describes the earth at the start of the millennium. Creation is undone back to chaos: no man, no light, no cities. The "bottomless pit" is not a hole somewhere else. It is THIS planet, emptied and dark, and that is Satan's cell.

[DYK🔎] The earthquake and the brightness of the Second Coming (Study 31) leave the surface a ruin: the living wicked are slain at the advent, the righteous are caught up, and the saved dead are raised — so when the dust settles, every righteous person has left the planet and every wicked person is dead. There is literally no living human left for Satan to tempt. That is the chain. For a thousand years the destroyer wanders a graveyard world with nothing to destroy.

- **the saints reign — in heaven, reviewing the records**
  - "And I saw thrones, and they sat upon them, and judgment was given unto them... and they lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years" (Rev 20:4)
  - "judgment was given unto them" — the redeemed are seated as a court. They do not overrule God; they examine His verdicts.
  - this is the work Paul promised: "Do ye not know that the saints shall judge the world?... Know ye not that we shall judge angels?" (1 Cor 6:2-3)
  - [→] this is the SECOND phase of the judgment. The investigative judgment of 1844 (Study 18) examined the cases of the professed people of God before the universe. Now, for a thousand years, the universe — through the redeemed — examines the cases of the LOST, and the fall of the angels, until every question is answered.

[ILL] A jury retires to deliberate. The judge opens every file — every piece of evidence, every transcript, every sealed record. Nothing is withheld. The jury takes as long as it needs. That is the millennium: God handing the redeemed a thousand years to examine every decision He ever made — and finding not one error. The 1844 judgment vindicated God's people before the universe. The millennium vindicates God before His people. He opens every book. He hides nothing. At the end of a thousand years, every saint will say: God was just.

## The Scapegoat Bears the Guilt — Type Fulfilled

**The Day of Atonement type Study 18 left unfinished now reaches its last figure. Two goats stood before the high priest: one slain for the people (Christ), and one — the live goat, the scapegoat — sent away alive into a desolate wilderness, bearing the confessed iniquities. Christ is the slain goat; the scapegoat is the ORIGINATOR of sin, on whom the responsibility he authored is finally rolled back.**

- **the live goat — sent into a land not inhabited**
  - "And Aaron shall lay both his hands upon the head of the live goat, and confess over him all the iniquities of the children of Israel, and all their transgressions in all their sins, putting them upon the head of the goat, and shall send him away by the hand of a fit man into the wilderness" (Lev 16:21)
  - "And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited: and he shall let go the goat in the wilderness" (Lev 16:22)
  - the scapegoat is NOT a sacrifice — its blood atones for no one. It is sent away to carry the guilt where it belongs, after the atonement by blood is already finished.

- **the antitype — Satan, bound on the desolate earth**
  - "a land not inhabited" — the millennial earth, emptied of every living human (Jer 4:25), is precisely a land not inhabited
  - the one fit to bear final responsibility for sin is the one who originated it: "How art thou fallen from heaven, O Lucifer" — the iniquity that began in him (echoing Study 1) is rolled back onto him
  - [→] the scapegoat of Leviticus 16 is now fulfilled. Sin is traced back to its author. Satan wanders the waste a thousand years bearing the guilt he originated — not to atone (he cannot), but to receive at last the full weight of the rebellion he started.

[Q] **"Does making Satan the scapegoat mean Satan helps atone for our sins?"**
→ No — and the type guards against it. Atonement is by BLOOD, and only the SLAIN goat is killed (Lev 16:9,15). The live goat's blood is never shed; it atones for nothing. It is sent away only AFTER reconciliation is complete (Lev 16:20). Christ alone bears our sins as a Saviour. Satan bears them only as the instigator who must finally own what he started, just as a murderer bears the guilt of a crime he incited others to commit. Our salvation rests wholly on the slain Lamb (Study 4); the scapegoat simply closes the books on the author of the evil.

## After the Thousand Years — The Verdict of the Universe

**When the millennium ends, the New Jerusalem comes down to earth, the wicked are raised, and the last act of the great controversy plays out — not as a contest God might lose, but as a demonstration that settles every charge forever. Mark this carefully: this final battle is AFTER the thousand years, and is NOT the battle of Armageddon, which fell at the Second Coming (Study 31). Armageddon gathers the LIVING nations BEFORE the millennium; this gathers the RESURRECTED wicked AFTER it.**

- **the Holy City descends — and the saints come with it**
  - "And I John saw the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down from God out of heaven, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband" (Rev 21:2)
  - "And his feet shall stand in that day upon the mount of Olives, which is before Jerusalem on the east, and the mount of Olives shall cleave in the midst thereof" (Zech 14:4)
  - Christ, His people, and the city return to the earth Satan has prowled alone — the wasteland becomes the throne-ground of the final assize.

- **the second resurrection — Satan has subjects again**
  - "And when the thousand years are expired, Satan shall be loosed out of his prison" (Rev 20:7)
  - he is "loosed" because the wicked dead are raised — "the rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished" (Rev 20:5) — and the deceiver suddenly has an innumerable army
  - "And shall go out to deceive the nations which are in the four quarters of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them together to battle: the number of whom is as the sand of the sea" (Rev 20:8)

- **the last siege — and fire from heaven**
  - "And they went up on the breadth of the earth, and compassed the camp of the saints about, and the beloved city: and fire came down from God out of heaven, and devoured them" (Rev 20:9)
  - [→] even after a thousand years bearing guilt on a ruined earth, Satan's character is unchanged. The moment he has subjects he marshals them against the city of God. This proves what the cosmic trial has always been about: sin is not a mistake to be fixed but a rebellion that must end. The accuser given a thousand years to reflect comes out unrepentant and reaches for the throne again.

[STR] **Two battles, never to be confused** — Armageddon (Rev 16:12-16, Study 31) musters the LIVING nations at the SIXTH plague, BEFORE the millennium, and is broken by Christ's appearing. Gog and Magog (Rev 20:7-9) musters the RESURRECTED wicked AFTER the thousand years, against the descended city, and is ended by fire. Same enemy, two distinct musters, a thousand years apart. Hold them apart and Revelation reads cleanly.

- **every knee bows — the verdict made unanimous**
  - "That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth; and that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father" (Phil 2:10-11)
  - before the fire falls, every being — saved and lost, angel and demon — acknowledges God's justice
  - the redeemed sing it freely: "Great and marvellous are thy works, Lord God Almighty; just and true are thy ways, thou King of saints" (Rev 15:3); "for thy judgments are made manifest" (Rev 15:4)
  - [→] this is the final vindication — not forced worship, but forced honesty. Satan himself bows. Every intelligence in the universe, having seen the open books, confesses: God was right. The two charges of Study 1 — "they only obey for reward," "the law cannot be kept" — are answered before the whole creation, and the verdict is unanimous.

## Sin Destroyed — Not Perpetuated

**Now the fire of Revelation 20:9 does its work. The lake of fire is the second death — the final, total, irreversible end of sin, sinners, and death itself. It is destruction, not endless torture; the wages of sin is DEATH, and that wage is paid in full (Rom 6:23). On this point especially the Bible must be its own expositor, because one phrase has been wrested for centuries.**

- **the great white throne — the books opened once more**
  - "And I saw a great white throne, and him that sat on it... and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works" (Rev 20:11-12)
  - "And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire" (Rev 20:15)
  - "And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death" (Rev 20:14)

- **the wage is death — ashes, not agony**
  - "For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the LORD of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch" (Mal 4:1)
  - "And ye shall tread down the wicked; for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet in the day that I shall do this, saith the LORD of hosts" (Mal 4:3)
  - "But the wicked shall perish... they shall consume; into smoke shall they consume away" (Ps 37:20)
  - "For yet a little while, and the wicked shall not be: yea, thou shalt diligently consider his place, and it shall not be" (Ps 37:10)

- **a clean and final end — never to rise again**
  - "What do ye imagine against the LORD? he will make an utter end: affliction shall not rise up the second time" (Nah 1:9)
  - even Satan is reduced to ashes and ceases to exist: "I will bring thee to ashes upon the earth in the sight of all them that behold thee... thou shalt be a terror, and never shalt thou be any more" (Eze 28:18-19)
  - [→] God does not perpetuate suffering — He ends it. Sin, sinners, the great deceiver, death itself — all consumed, "root and branch" (Mal 4:1), never to rise a second time (Nah 1:9). The universe is left clean. This is mercy and justice at once: the rebellion is over, and it stays over.

[Q] **"Doesn't 'tormented day and night for ever and ever' (Rev 20:10) prove eternal torment?"**
→ Not when Scripture defines its own "for ever." The very fire that devours the wicked is called "eternal fire" — yet Jude says Sodom and Gomorrha "are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire" (Jude 7), and Sodom is plainly not still burning. Isaiah says of the same destruction, "the smoke thereof shall go up for ever" (Isa 34:10) — describing a desolation, not a perpetual furnace. "For ever" in the Bible means "as long as there is something to consume" — until complete. Compare spiritual with spiritual (Study 0): Malachi says ashes (Mal 4:3), Nahum says it will not rise a second time (Nah 1:9), Ezekiel says of Satan himself "never shalt thou be any more" (Eze 28:19), and Obadiah says the wicked "shall be as though they had not been" (Obad 16). The lake of fire is the SECOND DEATH (Rev 20:14) — and death is the opposite of unending conscious life. The fire is eternal in its RESULTS, not its duration.

## The New Earth — Eden Restored, and Better

**The fire that ends sin also purges the earth, and out of it God brings forth the new creation. This is not the dissolving of matter into a disembodied "heaven somewhere else" — it is a literal new earth, the inheritance of the meek, where God Himself comes down to dwell with redeemed humanity forever. Acts called it "the times of restitution of all things" (Acts 3:21) — a giving-back of what Eden lost.**

- **"Behold, I make all things new" — a re-creation**
  - "And I saw a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the first earth were passed away; and there was no more sea" (Rev 21:1)
  - "And he that sat upon the throne said, Behold, I make all things new. And he said unto me, Write: for these words are true and faithful" (Rev 21:5)
  - "For, behold, I create new heavens and a new earth: and the former shall not be remembered, nor come into mind" (Isa 65:17)
  - "Nevertheless we, according to his promise, look for new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness" (2 Pet 3:13)
  - the same God who said "Let there be light" says "Behold, I make all things new." Not a repair — a re-creation.

- **God dwells with man — the sanctuary's whole purpose reached**
  - "Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them, and they shall be his people, and God himself shall be with them, and be their God" (Rev 21:3)
  - every sanctuary in Scripture aimed at this one sentence: God built the tabernacle so He could "dwell among them" (Ex 25:8, Study 5); now the type gives way to the reality — Himself with His people, no veil between.
  - [→] the tabernacle in the wilderness was a "dwelling place" that pointed forward. Garden, tabernacle, temple, Christ incarnate, the believer's body (Study 25) — every dwelling was temporary, pointing here. This is the permanent one.

- **no more curse — the literal life of the redeemed**
  - "And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away" (Rev 21:4)
  - "And there shall be no more curse: but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him" (Rev 22:3)
  - the curse of Genesis 3 — death, sorrow, thorns, sweat — is undone; creation itself made at peace: "The wolf and the lamb shall feed together, and the lion shall eat straw like the bullock... They shall not hurt nor destroy in all my holy mountain" (Isa 65:25)

- **the Sabbath kept — forever, by all flesh**
  - "For as the new heavens and the new earth, which I will make, shall remain before me, saith the LORD, so shall your seed and your name remain" (Isa 66:22)
  - "And it shall come to pass, that from one new moon to another, and from one sabbath to another, shall all flesh come to worship before me, saith the LORD" (Isa 66:23)
  - [→] the seventh-day Sabbath (Study 24) is not abolished in glory — it is kept by all flesh, world without end. The sign given at a sinless creation (Gen 2:2-3) is honored in the sinless new creation. The seal of God is worn into eternity.

- **the tree of life — access restored**
  - "In the midst of the street of it, and on either side of the river, was there the tree of life, which bare twelve manner of fruits, and yielded her fruit every month: and the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations" (Rev 22:2)
  - the tree once guarded by "a flaming sword which turned every way, to keep the way of the tree of life" (Gen 3:24) is now freely accessible
  - [→] the Way Home ends at the tree. The sword is removed. The way is open. Every door the bridge of Study 2 lost is reopened — and stays open.

[ILL] A mirror does not generate light. It does not strain to be bright. It simply faces the source — and reflects what shines upon it. Turn it away, and it goes dark — not because the light failed, but because the mirror moved. Across this whole series the redeemed have been mirrors learning to stop turning away. "We all, with open face beholding as in a glass the glory of the Lord, are changed into the same image from glory to glory" (2 Cor 3:18). On the new earth the mirror never turns again. The Light shines, and the image holds forever.

## They Shall See His Face — The Image Restored

**Here the entire series resolves into one line. The single thread running from Genesis 1 to Revelation 22 is the image of God in man — created, marred, and at last restored. The capstone verse states it without a figure: the redeemed see God's face, and His name is in their foreheads. Name = character = image (Study 3). The thing lost in Eden is given back.**

- **the name in the forehead = the image restored — a text-supported identity, not a number game**
  - "And they shall see his face; and his name shall be in their foreheads" (Rev 22:4)
  - Scripture itself ties "name" to character and sanctifying work: the Sabbath was given "to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them" (Eze 20:12) — the seal is God finishing His work in the believer
  - the redeemed company already wore it at the close: "a Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father's name written in their foreheads" (Rev 14:1, Study 29)
  - the Father's name written within is His character reproduced within — the literal restoration the whole Bible was driving toward, not a numerology of letters
  - [→] Moses asked, "shew me thy glory" (Ex 33:18), and was hidden in the rock to catch only the afterglow. Now they SEE His face. The name in the forehead IS the image of God restored. Genesis 1:26 — "Let us make man in our image" — is answered, at last, by Revelation 22:4.

- **the circle closes — image, broken image, restored image**
  - Gen 1:26-27 — man created in God's image
  - Gen 3:6-7 — the image shattered by sin (Study 2)
  - Rev 14:1 / Rev 22:4 — the image restored, His name in their foreheads
  - the entire biblical narrative fits inside this single arc — and it is finished

- **the sanctuary mapped the whole journey home**
  - outer court: sacrifice — the cost of restoration (the cross, Study 4)
  - holy place: intercession — the process of restoration (sanctification, Study 5)
  - most holy place: judgment, then glory — the completion of restoration (1844, then the redeemed standing where "no sin enters," made deathless: "this corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal must put on immortality," 1 Cor 15:53)
  - [→] the three apartments were always the three stages of the way home — justification, sanctification, glorification. On the new earth the last stage is reached: the redeemed stand in the unveiled presence of God and live. The sanctuary was never just a building. It was the map home, and the traveler has arrived.

- **the new Eden is the old Eden — but tested and proven**
  - Eden had innocence; the new earth has tested, proven, vindicated righteousness
  - "the former shall not be remembered, nor come into mind" (Isa 65:17) — no scar of the controversy remains
  - [→] the image restored is stronger than the image created — because it has been tried in the fire of a real cosmic trial and held. A universe that has SEEN where rebellion leads, and chosen God anyway, can never fall again. "Affliction shall not rise up the second time" (Nah 1:9).

[DYK🔎] The COL line that hung over the old version of this study — "When the character of Christ shall be perfectly reproduced in His people, then He will come to claim them as His own" (COL 69.1) — is a true and beautiful confirmation of what the Bible has already proved here: the harvest waits for ripe grain (Rev 14:15), the bride for her own readiness (Rev 19:7-8), the sealing for finished work (Rev 7:3). But the doctrine never rested on the quotation. It rests on the texts. Study 29 proved the character-readiness of the sealed company from Revelation itself; this study simply shows where that ripened image finally stands — face to face with God, His name written, in a world made new.

## Appeal — The Series Closes

- The trial that began in Eden has reached its verdict.
- Study 1 filed the charges. Study 2 showed the break and the bridge. Study 3 defined what salvation really is — the restoration of God's image. Study 4 showed why only Christ could do it. Study 5 unfolded the sanctuary as the map home. The prophetic studies traced God's hand through history on His own timeline, to 1844 and the open judgment. The three angels sounded; Babylon was unmasked; the call went out — "Come out of her, my people." The Sabbath was sealed as God's sign; the body claimed as His temple; the heart conquered like Canaan by the new covenant. The final crisis came, the sealed company was made ready, probation closed, the time of trouble was endured, and Christ came in glory.
- Now you have seen the very end — the thousand years of open books, the accuser silenced and then destroyed, every knee bowed, sin consumed root and branch, and "Behold, I make all things new."

- The whole of it answers two ancient charges. Satan said no one would serve God without a bribe; the redeemed serve Him after every bribe is stripped away. Satan said the law could not be kept; a restored people keep it, by Christ's character reproduced in them. The universe has watched both charges fall. God is vindicated. And His vindication is your homecoming.

- So the question of the whole series narrows to one: where will YOU stand when the books are opened?
- "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... and he that is righteous, let him be righteous still: and he that is holy, let him be holy still" (Rev 22:11) — every life will be fixed in the character it chose.
- Leave the accuser's jurisdiction now: "Come out of her, my people" (Rev 18:4).
- Wear the Judge's seal now, through sanctification: "I am the LORD that sanctify them" (Eze 20:12).
- Let Christ's character be reproduced — not by self-effort, but by beholding: "changed into the same image from glory to glory" (2 Cor 3:18).
- Answer both charges with your life: "keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (Rev 14:12).

- The Way Home is complete when you arrive. And you arrive when Christ sees His own image in you.
- The mirror faces the Light. The image is restored. The Father's name is written.
- The sanctuary doors are still open. The High Priest still ministers. The seal is still being placed.
- But not forever.

- Satan accused. Christ displayed. The universe watched. God was vindicated.
- The tree of life stands open. The sword is removed. The way is clear.
- "And they shall see his face; and his name shall be in their foreheads" (Rev 22:4).

- **The court is in session. Will you take the stand?**
