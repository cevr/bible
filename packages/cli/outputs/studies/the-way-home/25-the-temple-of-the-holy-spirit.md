# Study 25: The Temple of the Holy Spirit

**Scripture says it plainly: your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost, bought with a price, and not your own (1 Cor 6:19-20). That is the doctrine — and it is proved from the plainest New Testament temple texts, not from numbers or numerology. From it follows a sober application the last days press on us: the image of God is restored in the mind, the mind lives in a body, and how that body is treated bears on whether the mind stays clear enough to behold Christ and discern truth from lying wonders. Daniel "purposed in his heart" before his test. So must we.**

## Prep Reading

- 1 Corinthians 6:19-20
- 1 Corinthians 3:16-17

## The Doctrine: The Body Is a Temple

**Start with the load-bearing text. Before any application about food or drink, settle the plain declaration: the body is God's temple. Everything else in this study is the outworking of that one literal fact.**

- **Not a metaphor — a declaration**
  - "Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?" (1 Cor 3:16)
  - "If any man defile the temple of God, him shall God destroy; for the temple of God is holy, which temple ye are" (1 Cor 3:17)
  - The sanctuary in the Old Testament was where God dwelt among His people. Now: your body is where God dwells. The figure is the Bible's own — Paul names it.
  - [STR] This is the doctrine of the study, stated flatly by an apostle. It does not rest on a clever reading, a number, or an inference. It rests on Paul saying, in plain words, "the temple of God... which temple ye are."

- **Bought with a price**
  - "What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own? For ye are bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body, and in your spirit, which are God's" (1 Cor 6:19-20)
  - Two claims, both plain: you are not your own + glorify God in your body
  - The body is not neutral territory. It is purchased property. And the Owner has standards.

- **Study 5 carried this type — now it lands in you**
  - Study 5 mapped the sanctuary as the path of restoration: Christ the Ladder, the Gate, the House of God. The earthly tabernacle was a copy of the heavenly (Heb 8:5).
  - The same Spirit who filled the earthly temple now fills the believer (1 Cor 3:16). The body is the place God has chosen to indwell.
  - [→] So when we ask "does it matter what I do with my body?" the answer is not a health opinion. It is the same answer the sanctuary gave: the place God dwells is holy.

[Q] **"Isn't this legalism? Shouldn't grace cover what I eat?"**

> Grace does not make the body irrelevant — it makes it sacred. "Ye are bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body, and in your spirit, which are God's" (1 Cor 6:20). The body is not yours to abuse. It was purchased. And the purchase was not cheap. This is not legalism — it is stewardship of something that belongs to Someone else. Caring for the temple does not buy your salvation; it honors the One who bought you.

## Where Restoration Happens — and Why the Body Matters

**The doctrine stands on its own (1 Cor 3:16; 6:19-20). Now the application: because salvation restores God's image in the mind, and the mind lives in the body, the temple-text reaches into the dinner table. This is the chain from Study 3 — applied.**

- **A new heart — which is a new mind**
  - "A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you" (Eze 36:26)
  - "I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts" (Jer 31:33)
  - Study 3 established: salvation = restoration of God's image. That image is restored in the mind — the seat of character, decision, love.

- **Transformed by the renewing of your mind**
  - "Be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God" (Rom 12:2)
  - The word "transformed" is _metamorphoo_ — the same word used for Christ's transfiguration (Matt 17:2)
  - Transformation happens in the mind. The mind is where character lives. The mind is where beholding happens (2 Cor 3:18).
  - [→] If the mind is the seat of restoration, then anything that affects the mind affects the restoration. This is why the body matters.

- **The order is text-stated — body, then mind**
  - "I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service" (Rom 12:1)
  - "And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind" (Rom 12:2)
  - Paul connects them deliberately, in order: present your body (v.1) → the mind is renewed (v.2) → transformation follows. The sequence is not accidental — it is the verse's own order.
  - "Your reasonable service" — not extreme devotion. _Reasonable._ Caring for the temple is the minimum, not the maximum.

- **The body-mind link**
  - The mind does not float free of the body. It depends on it.
  - A fevered brain thinks poorly. A drugged mind cannot discern. A malnourished body produces a sluggish mind.
  - If the mind cannot think clearly, it cannot behold clearly. If it cannot behold, it cannot be changed.
  - [→] This is not about health tips. It is about whether the instrument of transformation — the mind — is functional enough to do its work. Satan knows this. It is why appetite was his first weapon.

## Appetite — Satan's First Weapon

- **The first temptation in Eden was appetite**
  - "And when the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was pleasant to the eyes, and a tree to be desired to make one wise, she took of the fruit thereof, and did eat" (Gen 3:6)
  - The first sin was not murder, theft, or idolatry. It was eating what God said not to eat.
  - "Good for food" — appetite. "Pleasant to the eyes" — desire. "Make one wise" — pride.
  - [→] Satan did not begin with doctrine. He began with the body. He knew: control the appetite and you control the person.

- **Christ's first temptation was appetite**
  - "And when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, he was afterward an hungred. And when the tempter came to him, he said, If thou be the Son of God, command that these stones be made bread" (Matt 4:2-3)
  - Christ met the same test Eve failed — appetite under pressure
  - He conquered where she fell. Not by willpower but by the word: "It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God" (Matt 4:4)
  - [→] The battle for the image begins at the body. Both Eden and the wilderness prove it. Satan's opening move — every time — is appetite.

- **Paul knew the danger**
  - "But I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection: lest that by any means, when I have preached to others, I myself should be a castaway" (1 Cor 9:27)
  - "And every man that striveth for the mastery is temperate in all things. Now they do it to obtain a corruptible crown; but we an incorruptible" (1 Cor 9:25)
  - Paul is not describing health advice. He is describing survival. He disciplines his body because the body can disqualify the preacher.
  - [→] If the apostle Paul considered his own body a threat to his salvation, on what basis would we consider ours irrelevant?

## God's Original Diet — The Creation Design

**Here the study moves from doctrine to wise stewardship. What follows is the Creator's original design and a counsel of health — the leading application of "your body is God's temple." It is not a new commandment whose breaking forfeits salvation; it is the wisdom of living near to the design of the One who made the body.**

- **Eden: plant-based by design**
  - "And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat" (Gen 1:29)
  - God designed the human body. He also gave its food. Fruits, grains, nuts, seeds — this was the original diet. No death required. No blood shed.
  - [→] Before sin, the diet was plant-based. That is the Creator's own original design. The wisdom of it is simply this: the nearer we live to the design of the One who made the body, the better the temple serves its purpose.

- **After sin: herbs added, labor required**
  - "Cursed is the ground for thy sake; in sorrow shalt thou eat of it all the days of thy life. Thorns also and thistles shall it bring forth to thee; and thou shalt eat the herb of the field" (Gen 3:17-18)
  - The ground is cursed. Getting food now involves labor and thorns. Vegetables (_herb of the field_) are added to the diet.
  - The diet expanded — but still no meat. From creation to the flood, humanity's recorded diet was plant-based.

- **After the flood: meat permitted — with limits**
  - "Every moving thing that liveth shall be meat for you; even as the green herb have I given you all things. But flesh with the life thereof, which is the blood thereof, shall ye not eat" (Gen 9:3-4)
  - The flood destroyed all vegetation. Meat was permitted as a concession after catastrophe — and even in the permission, God set a limit: no blood.
  - [→] Meat was allowed after a global catastrophe destroyed the food supply. The original design was never repealed — it was supplemented, with a restriction attached. Note carefully: God _permitted_ flesh here. So this is counsel and design, not a salvation test. The doctrine is the temple; the diet is the wise application of it.

- **Clean and unclean — before the law**
  - "Of every clean beast thou shalt take to thee by sevens, the male and his female: and of beasts that are not clean by two" (Gen 7:2)
  - Noah knew the difference between clean and unclean animals — long before Leviticus was written
  - This distinction predates Israel, predates the ceremonial law, predates the sanctuary
  - [→] Like the Sabbath, the clean/unclean distinction was built into creation knowledge, not invented at Sinai — a sign it belongs to the original design, not to the shadow nailed to the cross (Study 6).

[DYK🔎] After the flood, recorded human lifespan plummeted. Before the flood: Adam 930, Methuselah 969, Noah 950. After the flood: Shem 600, Eber 464, Peleg 239, Abraham 175, Moses 120. Multiple factors contributed — the post-flood environment and the change of diet among them. The correlation is striking, though it is offered as observation, not as a doctrine to bind the conscience: drifting from the original design accompanied the sharpest decline in human longevity in the biblical record.

## Daniel — The Test Case

**This is not a parable or an allegory. Daniel 1 is literal history — the record of real Hebrew captives at a real king's table in Babylon, c. 605 BC. The history is itself the evidence.**

- **Babylon's table vs. God's design**
  - "But Daniel purposed in his heart that he would not defile himself with the portion of the king's meat, nor with the wine which he drank" (Dan 1:8)
  - Daniel was a captive. He had no power. He had every reason to comply.
  - But he "purposed in his heart" — a mind-level decision about a body-level practice, made _before_ the pressure came
  - [→] The first act of faithfulness in the book of Daniel is a settled choice about food. Not a prayer first, not a theological debate first — a decision, purposed in advance, about the body.

- **The test**
  - "Prove thy servants, I beseech thee, ten days; and let them give us pulse to eat, and water to drink" (Dan 1:12)
  - Pulse = plant-based food. Water instead of wine.
  - "At the end of ten days their countenances appeared fairer and fatter in flesh than all the children which did eat the portion of the king's meat" (Dan 1:15)

- **The result — mind, not just body**
  - "As for these four children, God gave them knowledge and skill in all learning and wisdom: and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams" (Dan 1:17)
  - "He found them ten times better than all the magicians and astrologers that were in all his realm" (Dan 1:20)
  - The diet accompanied clearer minds. Clearer bodies → clearer minds → greater capacity for wisdom and revelation.
  - Daniel received the very visions that unlock the prophecies of Studies 7-9 and 14. A clouded mind would have been a poorer vessel for them.
  - [→] Stated cautiously: God gives revelation as He wills, and Scripture credits the wisdom to God's gift (Dan 1:17). But the text deliberately ties the clear body to the clear mind to the capacity for divine wisdom. The body-mind link is the chapter's own connection, not an overlay.

[DYK🔎] "Ten times better" (Dan 1:20) — this is a Hebrew idiom meaning "far better, beyond all comparison," not a literal multiplier and not a hidden reference to the number of the commandments. The same idiom appears in Genesis 31:7, where Laban "changed my wages ten times" — that is, repeatedly, again and again. To read "ten" here as a coded pointer to the Ten Commandments would be exactly the numerology the serpent's method invites and the Bible's plain-sense rule forbids (Study 0). The text says only this, and it is enough: Daniel kept his body clear, and his mind was clear, and the wisdom God gave him was beyond compare.

[ILL] A radio receives a signal — but only if the antenna is clean and the circuits are functional. Static in the hardware means static in the reception. Daniel kept his receiver clear. God sent the signal. The visions of Daniel 2, 7, 8, and 9 came through a mind that was not clouded by Babylon's table.

[STR] **Daniel 1 sets up Daniel 3.** The man who "purposed in his heart" about the king's _table_ (Dan 1:8) is the same circle of men who later refuse to bow to the king's _image_ (Dan 3). The small, private, settled-in-advance faithfulness about the body trained the public, life-or-death faithfulness in the crisis. Last-day readiness is built the same way: the mind that can stand before the image of the beast (Study 28) is the mind kept clear and the will kept settled long before the test arrives.

## The Days of Noah — A Last-Day Warning

- **Christ ties His return to Noah's time**
  - "But as the days of Noe were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be. For as in the days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark" (Matt 24:37-38)
  - "And knew not until the flood came, and took them all away; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" (Matt 24:39)
  - "Eating and drinking" — not merely existing, but indulging without discernment
  - Christ names this as a sign of the last days: consumption without thought, appetite without restraint, the day coming on them _unawares_

- **Take heed — surfeiting dulls the heart**
  - "And take heed to yourselves, lest at any time your hearts be overcharged with surfeiting, and drunkenness, and cares of this life, and so that day come upon you unawares" (Luke 21:34)
  - "Surfeiting" = excess in eating. Christ directly links overeating and drunkenness to being caught unaware at His return.
  - The body dulls the heart. The heart misses the signs. The day comes unawares.
  - [→] This is not a health lecture. It is an eschatological warning from Christ's own lips. What you consume bears on whether you are awake or asleep when He comes.

- **Whose god is their belly**
  - "Whose end is destruction, whose God is their belly, and whose glory is in their shame, who mind earthly things" (Phil 3:19)
  - Appetite as god — the belly ruling the mind, the body ruling the spirit
  - The inversion of God's order: Spirit → mind → body becomes belly → mind → everything else
  - [→] When appetite becomes master, the instrument of transformation is enslaved to the very thing it was meant to govern. The temple is occupied by the wrong tenant.

## Temperance — The Forgotten Virtue

- **It is a fruit of the Spirit and a link in Peter's chain**
  - "But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, temperance: against such there is no law" (Gal 5:22-23)
  - "And to knowledge temperance; and to temperance patience; and to patience godliness" (2 Pet 1:6)
  - Temperance is not an optional extra — it is named among the Spirit's own fruit and set in the chain of Christian growth. You cannot endure patiently if you cannot govern yourself.
  - The Greek is _egkrateia_ — self-mastery, self-control. Power over the appetites and passions, given by the Spirit (Gal 5:23), not white-knuckled willpower.

- **Wine and strong drink**
  - "Wine is a mocker, strong drink is raging: and whosoever is deceived thereby is not wise" (Prov 20:1)
  - "Be not among winebibbers; among riotous eaters of flesh: for the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty: and drowsiness shall clothe a man with rags" (Prov 23:20-21)
  - "Woe unto them that rise up early in the morning, that they may follow strong drink" (Isa 5:11)
  - Scripture does not treat strong drink as neutral. It treats it as a deceiver — something that impairs the very faculty God is trying to restore.
  - [→] If the mind is the seat of transformation, and drink clouds the mind, then it works directly against the plan of salvation — not because God is arbitrary, but because the mind cannot behold what it cannot clearly see.

- **The principle: anything that clouds the mind hinders the restoration**
  - Drugs, alcohol, overeating, stimulants — not a list of arbitrary rules but a single principle
  - Whatever impairs the mind impairs the beholding. Whatever impairs the beholding impairs the transformation. Whatever impairs the transformation delays the restoration.
  - [→] Temperance is the guardrail on the road of sanctification. The doctrine remains the temple (1 Cor 6:19); temperance is the Spirit's wisdom for keeping that temple clear.

## The Chain Runs Through the Body

- **Study 3's chain, completed:**
  - Image = Character = Law = Love = Knowing God = Life
  - The image is restored in the mind (Eze 36:26; Rom 12:2)
  - The mind depends on the body (1 Cor 9:27; Dan 1:17)
  - The body is the temple of the Spirit (1 Cor 6:19) — the doctrine that anchors it all
  - Defile the temple → cloud the mind → block the beholding → slow the restoration
  - [→] The health counsel is not the gospel, and keeping it does not earn salvation. But it is not an appendix either: it is the wise stewardship of the very temple where the gospel does its restoring work. The chain runs from the dinner table to the throne of God.

- **The two charges run through the body**
  - Charge B: "The law cannot be kept." Satan knows: cloud the mind and obedience grows harder — not because the law is flawed, but because the instrument of obedience is impaired. Every substance that dulls the mind is Charge B's ally.
  - Charge A: "They only obey for reward." Appetite is the original bribe — Eve saw that the tree was "good for food" (Gen 3:6). When appetite rules, self-interest rules, and Charge A finds a foothold.
  - [→] Satan attacked the body first in Eden and first in the wilderness. The care of the temple is not a side issue — it is the front line of the cosmic trial, where the mind is either kept clear for the witness or surrendered to the haze.

- **From Eden to Eden**
  - Eden: plant-based diet, no death, no disease, image of God intact (Gen 1:29)
  - New Earth: tree of life, leaves for healing, no more pain, image of God restored (Rev 22:1-2; Rev 21:4)
  - Between the two: God progressively restoring what was lost — including the body
  - [→] The original diet and the final restoration bookend the story. God's health design is not a cultural relic; it is the design of Eden, with a preview of Eden restored at the end of the river.

## Appeal

- The doctrine is plain and load-bearing: your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost, bought with a price, and you are not your own (1 Cor 6:19-20). Begin there.
- That image is restored in the mind — and the mind lives in a body. What you do with the body either aids the restoration or hinders it.
- God designed the body and gave its food, and the two were matched in Eden. The nearer we live to that design, the clearer the mind, the easier the beholding. This is wise stewardship, not a new law.
- Satan's first weapon was appetite — in Eden and in the wilderness. He knows the body-mind link.
- Daniel proved it in real history: a body kept clear, a mind kept clear, wisdom and visions beyond compare — and the man who purposed about the table stood firm before the image.
- Christ warned it: in the days of Noah, eating and drinking without thought marked those caught unawares.
- Paul practiced it: he kept his body under subjection lest he himself become a castaway.

- Your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit. It is purchased property. Treat it as such — not to earn His favor, but because it is His dwelling.
- "Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God" (1 Cor 10:31)
- Glory = character = image (Study 3). Eating and drinking to the glory of God means living so the temple stays clear enough for His image to be restored in you.
- The final crisis is won or lost in a mind that can still discern truth from lying wonders. Like Daniel, purpose in your heart now — before the test.

Now — how does God actually conquer the territory of the heart and form the very character the judgment displays and the final crisis tests? Israel faced Canaan with the same question: the land is promised, but it must be taken. How? Not by might, nor by self-effort, but by the new covenant — God's own work, received by faith.
