# Study 21: The Two Beasts — The Papacy and the United States Named

**Revelation 13 sets two beasts on the stage of the last days: a sea-beast that wears every feature of Daniel 7's little horn — wounded almost to death in 1798 — and a second, two-horned earth-beast that rises looking like a lamb and ends speaking like a dragon. The first the Bible names by its own marks: the papacy. The second the text describes by so many specifications that only one nation on earth fits the description — the very land founded on liberty, foretold to enforce a religious mark. This is where the Way Home stops being ancient history and becomes the reader's near future.**

## Two Beasts, One Dragon Behind Them

**Revelation 13 is the dragon's chapter. The serpent who could not destroy the Child (Study 20) now works through two visible powers — first a beast from the sea, then a beast from the earth. Before we identify either, fix who is behind both: the dragon, already named by the chapter before.**

- **The dragon was already identified in the previous vision — Satan, working through earthly Rome**
  - "And the great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world: he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him." (Rev 12:9)
  - Revelation 12 calls the dragon by name. We do not have to guess in chapter 13 — Scripture defined the symbol one chapter earlier (Isa 28:10, line upon line). The dragon is Satan, the unseen actor; the beasts are his visible agents.
  - [→] This is the cosmic trial (Study 1) reaching the last days. Same Satan, same two charges, now wearing two earthly faces.

- **The sea-beast receives its authority FROM the dragon — it is a delegated power, not a fourth actor**
  - "And the dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority." (Rev 13:2)
  - The beast is not Satan; it is what Satan empowers. This keeps the two distinct — exactly as Revelation 12 kept "the dragon" (Satan) apart from "pagan Rome" (his tool).

[STR] **The cast of Revelation 13:** the DRAGON (Satan, behind everything) → the SEA-BEAST (a religio-political power that receives the dragon's seat and authority) → the EARTH-BEAST (a second power that enforces the worship of the first). Identify each one from the Bible's own marks, in order, and the whole chapter opens like a lock.

## The Sea-Beast Is Daniel 7's Little Horn — The Same Power, Named Again

**The fastest way to identify the first beast is not to invent a meaning but to lay it beside a prophecy already explained. Daniel 7 named a power by its character, its deeds, and a calendar (Study 8). Revelation 13's sea-beast carries the SAME marks, point for point. When two prophecies describe the same power with the same fingerprints, Scripture is naming one thing twice — and Scripture cannot be broken.**

- **Both beasts rise out of the same place — the sea — and the body is a composite of Daniel's first three beasts**
  - "And I stood upon the sand of the sea, and saw a beast rise up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns, and upon his horns ten crowns, and upon his heads the name of blasphemy." (Rev 13:1)
  - "And the beast which I saw was like unto a leopard, and his feet were as the feet of a bear, and his mouth as the mouth of a lion" (Rev 13:2)
  - Daniel's beasts came up "from the sea" too (Dan 7:3): the lion (Babylon, Dan 7:4), the bear (Medo-Persia, Dan 7:5), the leopard (Greece, Dan 7:6), and the dreadful ten-horned fourth beast (Rome, Dan 7:7). Revelation gathers leopard + bear + lion + ten horns into ONE body. The text is back-referencing Daniel on purpose: this beast inherits everything the four empires were, the way the papacy inherited the seat of Rome.
  - [DYK🔎] Daniel saw the beasts in chronological order — lion, bear, leopard — looking _forward_ in time. John names them in reverse — leopard, bear, lion (Rev 13:2) — looking _backward_ from his own day. The same four empires, viewed from opposite ends of history. The Author is signing His own cross-reference.

- **The angel already defined the symbols, so we are not guessing**
  - "These great beasts, which are four, are four kings, which shall arise out of the earth." (Dan 7:17)
  - "The ten horns out of this kingdom are ten kings that shall arise: and another shall rise after them; and he shall be diverse from the first, and he shall subdue three kings." (Dan 7:24)
  - Beast = kingdom, horn = king — by Heaven's own dictionary (Study 8). We carry those definitions straight into Revelation 13.

[STR] **Six fingerprints — Daniel 7 and Revelation 13 describe ONE power**

- **(1) BLASPHEMY against God.** Daniel: "he shall speak great words against the most High" (Dan 7:25). Revelation: "upon his heads the name of blasphemy" (Rev 13:1); "he opened his mouth in blasphemy against God, to blaspheme his name, and his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven" (Rev 13:6). And Scripture defines what blasphemy IS — a man claiming what belongs to God alone: "who can forgive sins but God only?" (Mark 2:7); "thou, being a man, makest thyself God" (John 10:33).
- **(2) A GREAT MOUTH speaking great things.** Daniel: "a mouth speaking great things" (Dan 7:8). Revelation: "there was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies" (Rev 13:5). Identical phrase.
- **(3) WAR ON THE SAINTS.** Daniel: "shall wear out the saints of the most High" (Dan 7:25). Revelation: "it was given unto him to make war with the saints, and to overcome them" (Rev 13:7); and Revelation 17 will call the same woman "drunken with the blood of the saints" (Rev 17:6).
- **(4) IT RISES AS PAGAN ROME YIELDS — receiving Rome's own seat.** Daniel: the little horn comes up "among" the ten horns of the fourth (Roman) beast, after them (Dan 7:8, 24). Revelation: "the dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority" (Rev 13:2) — the same seat the pagan empire vacated.
- **(5) THE SAME 1260-YEAR CALENDAR.** Daniel: "they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time" (Dan 7:25). Revelation: "power was given unto him to continue forty and two months" (Rev 13:5). A "time, times, and half" = 3½ years = 42 months = 1260 days. The two prophecies hand us the exact same span by two different reckonings (proved next).
- **(6) CAPTIVITY AT THE END OF THE PERIOD.** Revelation: "He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity" (Rev 13:10) and "one of his heads as it were wounded to death" (Rev 13:3). The 1260 close with the power itself led captive — the deadly wound.

- **Six matched marks is not coincidence — it is identification**
  - [→] One coincidence proves nothing. Six independent fingerprints — blasphemy, a great mouth, war on the saints, rising in Rome's seat, the identical 1260-year clock, and captivity at its end — landing on a single power is identification, not guesswork. "The scripture cannot be broken" (John 10:35): two prophecies describing one thing must name one thing. Daniel and John are naming the papal phase of Rome.

[Q] **"But couldn't this just be pagan Rome, or Rome in general?"**
→ No — the marks rule that out. This beast _receives_ its seat from the dragon (pagan Rome's successor, not pagan Rome itself), it _blasphemes by claiming divine prerogatives_ (forgiving sins, sitting "in the temple of God... shewing himself that he is God," 2 Thess 2:4), and it runs on a _1260-year clock that begins after the ten divisions of Rome already exist_ (Dan 7:24). Pagan Rome was gone before the ten horns formed. Only the power that rose AMONG those divisions, in Rome's vacated seat, claiming God's own prerogatives, fits every line.

## The 42 Months Are 1260 Years — And the Wound Is Dated

**The sea-beast's reign is not left vague. The text gives a number, and Scripture gives the rule to read it. We proved the year-day key from God's own mouth in Study 9; here we simply apply it — and then watch history keep the appointment to the year.**

- **Forty-two months is the same period Daniel gave — and the year-day key converts it to 1260 years**
  - "power was given unto him to continue forty and two months" (Rev 13:5)
  - 42 months × 30 days (a prophetic month) = 1260 prophetic days. By the rule God twice stated — "each day for a year" (Num 14:34); "I have appointed thee each day for a year" (Eze 4:6) — 1260 days = 1260 years.
  - Daniel's "time, times, and the dividing of time" (Dan 7:25) is the same 1260 by a different path (3½ × 360 = 1260). "At the mouth of two or three witnesses shall the matter be established" (Deut 19:15) — and Revelation 12 (1260 days), Daniel 7 (time/times/half), and Revelation 13 (42 months) are three witnesses to ONE span.
  - [→] The Bible converts its own units. We are not importing a meaning; we are reading the number the way the same Book taught us to read it.

- **The period ends in a "deadly wound" — and the text says it is captivity by the sword**
  - "And I saw one of his heads as it were wounded to death; and his deadly wound was healed: and all the world wondered after the beast." (Rev 13:3)
  - "He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints." (Rev 13:10)
  - The power that _led_ others into captivity is, at the close of its 1260 years, itself _led_ captive. That is a specific, datable kind of fall — not a vague decline. The Bible has just told us WHAT to look for at the end of the period: a captivity.

### Document from history — the deadly wound, on schedule

> _This is the fulfillment lane. The MEANING (1260 years, ending in captivity) is proved above from the Bible. The DATE is read off the historical record — the Bible predicted, history recorded._

- The 1260 years of papal supremacy ran from **A.D. 538** (when Justinian's decree took effect and the last of the three opposing Arian kingdoms — the Ostrogoths — was driven from Rome, establishing the bishop of Rome's civil dominance; documented in Study 8) to **A.D. 1798**.
- On **February 10–20, 1798**, the French general **Louis-Alexandre Berthier**, under the Directory, marched on Rome, proclaimed a republic, and took **Pope Pius VI** prisoner. The pope was carried off into exile and died a captive at **Valence, France, on August 29, 1799** — the supreme pontiff "led into captivity" exactly as Revelation 13:10 framed the end of the period. The historian and the encyclopedia record the date; the Bible recorded the _event-type_ 1700 years in advance.
- 538 + 1260 = 1798. The wound fell at the very year the prophecy's clock ran out.

- **And the wound was foretold to HEAL — "all the world wondered after the beast"**
  - "And his deadly wound was healed: and all the world wondered after the beast." (Rev 13:3)
  - The prophecy did not stop at the wound; it predicted recovery. Historically, a new pope (**Pius VII**) was elected **March 14, 1800**, and the papal power began its long recovery. Revelation 17 says the same thing in other words: the beast "was, and is not, and yet is" (Rev 17:8) — _was_ (538-1798), _is not_ (the wound), _yet is_ (the healing). One power, one wound, one revival, two chapters agreeing.

[DYK🔎] The same 1798 that wounded the beast also _opened_ the time of the end (Dan 12:4) and stands as the close-date of the 1290 years (Study 15). One year, struck by several independent prophetic clocks at once — paganism's 1290, the papacy's 1260, the time-of-the-end door — all converging. A coincidence cannot be engineered across that many lines reckoned from that many different starting points.

## 666 — The Number of a Man, Not Free Numerology

**Here the chapter offers its own built-in identification test — and it is crucial to read it exactly as the verse frames it. The Bible does NOT invite us to play number games with random words. It hands us one precise instruction.**

- **The text limits the search before we begin: it is a MAN'S number, and it is a NAME or TITLE**
  - "Here is wisdom. Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast: for it is the number of a man; and his number is Six hundred threescore and six." (Rev 13:18)
  - The verse sets three guard-rails: (1) it is "the number of a man" — a man's title, not an abstract code; (2) it belongs to the beast already identified by six marks — we are not fishing for a fresh candidate; (3) it is to be _counted_, which in the ancient world where letters doubled as numerals (Latin and Greek both) means summing the numeric value of a name.
  - [STR] This is the discipline that separates legitimate Bible numbering from numerology. We do not start with "what adds to 666?" — anything can, given enough tries. We start with the power Scripture has ALREADY named, and we test whether its official Latin title sums to 666.

- **The title the beast claims for itself, in Latin numerals, sums to exactly 666**
  - A historic Latin title applied to the office is **VICARIVS FILII DEI** — "Vicar of the Son of God." Counting only the Latin numeral-letters (V, I, C, L, D — Latin has no separate U; V serves):
    - VICARIVS → V(5) + I(1) + C(100) + I(1) + V(5) = **112**
    - FILII → I(1) + L(50) + I(1) + I(1) = **53**
    - DEI → D(500) + I(1) = **501**
    - 112 + 53 + 501 = **666**
  - The number lands on the very claim that makes the beast the beast — a man styling himself the stand-in for the Son of God on earth (the blasphemy of mark #1, Mark 2:7; John 10:33). The number is not a separate proof bolted on; it confirms the identity the six marks already established.

[Q] **"Isn't this just cherry-picking? Other names add to 666 too."**
→ That objection would land if 666 were our _first_ and _only_ evidence. It is not. The beast is identified by SIX independent textual marks before we ever count a number (blasphemy, great mouth, war on saints, Rome's seat, the 1260 years, the captivity). The number is the _seventh_ witness, and it falls on the official Latin TITLE of the very office the six marks already exposed — a man "making himself God" (John 10:33). The verse itself forbids free numerology by fixing the search to "the number of a man" who is the beast already in view. We are reading the test the way the text wrote it.

## The Second Beast — Nine Specifications That Name One Nation

**Now the camera turns. A SECOND beast appears — and the text describes it with such precision that, laid against the historical map, only one power on earth can occupy the description. The Bible does not say "United States." But it gives nine textual specifications, and we follow them like coordinates to a single point. This is the textual proof the next study (the Final Crisis) leans on.**

- **(1) It rises out of the EARTH, not the sea — and the Bible defines the difference**
  - "And I beheld another beast coming up out of the earth" (Rev 13:11)
  - The first beast rose from "the sea" (Rev 13:1). Scripture defines that symbol in this very book: "The waters which thou sawest... are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues" (Rev 17:15). A sea-power rises out of the crowded, populated, war-torn Old World. But this beast rises from the EARTH — the opposite of waters: a sparsely settled new territory, not amid the clamoring nations but apart from them.
  - [→] Heaven's own dictionary points away from Europe, Asia, Africa — the crowded "sea" of nations — to a fresh, unpeopled "earth." A new continent.

- **(2) It arises right as the first beast falls — c. 1798**
  - The earth-beast appears at the close of the sea-beast's 1260 years, just as the deadly wound is dealt (the order of the chapter: vv. 1-10 the sea-beast and its wound; v. 11 the second beast "coming up" at that point). A power coming into prominence on the world stage _as the papacy goes into captivity in 1798_.

- **(3) It is YOUNG — a lamb, not a battle-scarred old empire**
  - "and he had two horns like a lamb" (Rev 13:11)
  - The other beasts were full-grown predators — lion, bear, leopard, dreadful iron-toothed monster. This one is a _lamb_: new, young, fresh on the scene. Not an ancient empire but a recent rise.

- **(4) It rises PEACEFULLY — no conquered crowns**
  - Note what is absent: the lamb-beast has no crowns on its horns (contrast Rev 13:1, "upon his horns ten crowns"). It does not climb to power by tearing crowns from other kings (as the little horn "plucked up" three, Dan 7:8). It rises quietly, not by overthrowing established monarchies but on open ground.

- **(5) It has TWO HORNS LIKE A LAMB — youthful, gentle, Christ-like in profession**
  - "two horns like a lamb" (Rev 13:11)
  - A horn is a power (Dan 7:24). Two lamb-like horns = two powers that are _innocent and Christ-like in appearance_ — a nation whose founding principles look like the Lamb's own: gentleness, liberty, the absence of kingly oppression. The two horns are described below (specs 6 and 7) by their content.

- **(6) Republican — no crowns means power resides in the PEOPLE**
  - The crownless horns mark a power with no king, no pope ruling its civil life — a _republic_, where authority rises from the people rather than descending from a throne. This is the first lamb-like horn: civil liberty without a monarch.

- **(7) Protestant — its religion looks like the Lamb's, until it is "caused" to change**
  - The earth-beast only enforces the FIRST beast's worship when it later "causeth" it (Rev 13:12) — meaning its native religion is NOT that of the first beast. It begins outside Rome's system: a Protestant character. This is the second lamb-like horn: religious liberty, a faith professing to follow the Lamb. The lamb-like _profession_ is real; the dragon-like _future_ is the tragedy.

- **(8) "He spake as a dragon" — the lamb-mouthed nation enacts coercion**
  - "and he spake as a dragon" (Rev 13:11)
  - A nation "speaks" through its legislatures and its courts — that is how a government utters its will. A beast that LOOKS like a lamb but SPEAKS like a dragon is a power founded on liberty (lamb-horns) that comes to _enact_ the very religious coercion the dragon and the first beast practiced (dragon-voice). The contradiction is the whole point: the land of freedom, foretold to legislate compulsion in religion.

- **(9) It makes an IMAGE to the first beast and enforces its mark**
  - "saying to them that dwell on the earth, that they should make an image to the beast, which had the wound by a sword, and did live." (Rev 13:14)
  - An "image" is a likeness — a copy of the first beast's defining feature. The first beast's defining sin was _church wielding the state's sword to compel worship_ (war on the saints, Dan 7:25; Rev 13:7). An _image_ of it is therefore an apostate church using THIS nation's civil power to compel worship — a new church-state union, made in the New World in the likeness of the Old. (How that image is formed, and what the mark is, the Final Crisis study unpacks.)

[STR] **Nine coordinates, one point on the map:** a power that (1) rises from open _earth_ not crowded _seas_, (2) comes up _c. 1798_ as the papacy falls, (3) is _young_ (a lamb), (4) rises _peaceably_ (no conquered crowns), (5) is _Christ-like in profession_ (lamb-horns), (6) is _republican_ (no king), (7) is _Protestant_ (not Rome's religion natively), (8) but _"speaks as a dragon"_ (legislates religious coercion), and (9) builds an _image_ to the papacy and enforces a _mark_. No power in history but one satisfies the whole list at the right time. The Bible drew the portrait; only one nation steps into the frame.

### Document from history — the only candidate that fits

> _Fulfillment lane again. The MEANING of every symbol above is proved from Scripture; here we simply note the single power that occupies the description on the historical map. The Bible predicted the portrait; history supplies the sitter._

- **A new land, not the crowded Old World:** the **United States of America** arose on a sparsely settled continent — "earth," not the "sea" of the teeming Old World nations.
- **Rising as the papacy fell:** the U.S. declared independence in **1776**, secured it by treaty in **1783**, and by **1798** — the very year the first beast received its deadly wound — was an established, rising republic on the world stage.
- **Young, peaceful, crownless:** it grew not by tearing crowns from established kings but on largely open ground — a _lamb_ among the predator-empires.
- **The two lamb-like horns, written into its founding law:** the U.S. Constitution guarantees every state "a Republican Form of Government" (Article IV, Section 4) — civil liberty without a king (horn one); and the First Amendment forbids any establishment of religion, while Article VI bars any religious test for office — religious liberty, a Protestant separation of church and state (horn two). Two horns like a lamb, named in the founding documents.
- **The "dragon-voice" still ahead:** a republic founded on freedom, foretold one day to "speak as a dragon" — to legislate religious compulsion. (The shape of that crisis is Study 28.)

- **And the same nation is the cradle of the false prophet's wonders**
  - The earth-beast "doeth great wonders, so that he maketh fire come down from heaven" and "deceiveth them that dwell on the earth by the means of those miracles" (Rev 13:13-14). Modern Spiritualism — table-rappings claiming to be the dead returned, the seedbed of the lying wonders — began at **Hydesville, New York, in March 1848**, in this very nation. The "earth" that produced the lamb-beast also produced the wonder-working deception. (The state of the dead and these wonders are treated fully in Study 27.)

## The Second Beast Is the "False Prophet"

**Revelation does not leave the earth-beast unnamed in its later chapters. The same wonder-working power that prepares men to worship the image is given a title by the Book itself — and that title tells us its method.**

- **Scripture re-labels the earth-beast "the false prophet" — by its function**
  - "And the beast was taken, and with him the false prophet that wrought miracles before him, with which he deceived them that had received the mark of the beast, and them that worshipped his image." (Rev 19:20)
  - The earth-beast is the one that "wrought miracles" before the first beast and made men receive "the mark" and worship "his image" (Rev 13:12-16). Revelation 19:20 describes that exact actor and calls it "the false prophet." Same deeds, same name — Scripture's own cross-naming (compare also the three frog-spirits "out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet," Rev 16:13 — the unholy trinity: dragon, sea-beast, earth-beast/false prophet).
  - [→] A false prophet works by _miracles_, not arguments. That is the warning: this power's strength is not logic but wonders — "great signs and wonders; insomuch that, if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect" (Matt 24:24). The defense is not cleverness; it is the written Word (Isa 8:20).

[Q] **"How can the United States be a 'beast' in prophecy? It is a Christian nation founded on the Bible."**
→ Precisely — that is what makes it a _lamb_. The prophecy is not slandering the nation's founding; it _honors_ it as Christ-like in profession (two horns like a lamb). The tragedy the verse foretells is a _change_: the lamb that comes to "speak as a dragon," the republic founded on liberty that is "caused" to enforce a religious mark. Scripture does not deny the nation's good beginning; it warns where it is foretold to end. A reader who loves the principles of liberty has every reason to take this warning most seriously of all — because it is liberty that is foretold to be surrendered.

## Why This Matters — The Stage Is Set for the Final Test

**With both beasts named, the geography of the last conflict is fixed. Revelation 13 closes by showing the two powers cooperating to force a single choice on the whole world.**

- **The image, the mark, and the boycott — the machinery of the final test**
  - "And he had power to give life unto the image of the beast, that the image of the beast should both speak, and cause that as many as would not worship the image of the beast should be killed." (Rev 13:15)
  - "And he causeth all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads: And that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark, or the name of the beast, or the number of his name." (Rev 13:16-17)
  - The earth-beast gives the _image_ (the new church-state union) coercive life, and enforces the _mark_ by economic boycott and death-decree. The whole world is pressed toward one choice. (The mark itself, and the seal set over against it, are Studies 24 and 28.)

- **Over against the mark stands the remnant — the answer to both charges**
  - "And the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." (Rev 12:17)
  - "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." (Rev 14:12)
  - The whole drama of Revelation 13 narrows, at last, to two companies: those who worship the beast and its image, and those who "keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." That second company is Heaven's living answer to Satan's two ancient charges (Study 1) — proof that the law CAN be kept, and that God's people serve Him for love, not under threat of a buy-or-sell boycott.
  - [→] This is why the chapter matters to _you_. It is not a museum of dead empires. It is the map of the test the last generation will face — and it asks now whose mark you intend to bear.

[ILL] Picture two passports presented at one border. One bears "the mark of the beast" — the badge of submission to a counterfeit authority that compels worship. The other bears the seal of the Lamb — those who "keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." At the final crossing, no third document is accepted. Revelation 13 is the announcement, printed two thousand years early, that this border is coming. The Way Home runs straight through it.
