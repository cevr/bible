## The Rewrite Thesis

Rewrite the 17-study "The Way Home" narrative series into a **zero-to-100 historicist, literal, Bible-only** study chain that recreates the chain of truth the SDA pioneers established — but **proved from the Bible itself**, inside the existing Way Home narrative frame (cosmic trial → Satan's two charges → broken bridge → exile in Babylon → the call home → restoration).

### The Four Non-Negotiable Rules

1. **BIBLE-ONLY PROOF.** Every doctrine is established from Scripture by the Bible's own method: plain literal sense; Scripture its own expositor; precept upon precept (Isa 28:10); compare spiritual with spiritual (1 Cor 2:13); never break Scripture against itself (John 10:35); year-day key from the Bible (Num 14:34; Eze 4:6). **Do NOT prove doctrine by citing pioneers or Ellen White.** Their words may stand only as a _confirming devotional echo AFTER_ the Bible proof — never as the ground. The reader must verify every doctrine with an open Bible alone.

2. **HISTORY IS THE ONE EXCEPTION — FULFILLMENT ONLY, NEVER DOCTRINE.** The historicist proof-form is always: _Bible predicts X → history records X happened on schedule._ So when a prophecy's _fulfillment_ is a datable event, that event is documented from the historical record (and may cite a historian/encyclopedia/pioneer who recorded the **date**). The meaning is still proved from the Bible. History appears ONLY in the "Document from history" lane of each study.

3. **TYPOLOGY YES, SPIRITUALIZING NO.** Typology (a literal thing the text itself authorizes as a figure of another literal thing — Jacob's wrestling → the literal time of trouble, Jer 30:7; sanctuary → Christ's literal heavenly ministry, Heb 8; Passover → the literal cross, 1 Cor 5:7) **stays**. Spiritualizing (asserting a _doctrine_ on a non-literal/inner/allegorical reading instead of the plain literal sense) is the **error to remove**. The test: _does a doctrine REST on a non-literal reading?_ If yes → defect; establish the literal sense first, demote any devotional application to clearly secondary.

4. **KEEP THE NARRATIVE FRAME.** Keep the Way Home story, the whiteboard format and markers (`[→]` insight, `[DYK🔎]` did-you-know, `[Q]` objection+answer, `[ILL]` illustration, `[STR]` structural/key point, `[TANGENT]`), Prep Reading lists, a Central Verse, an Appeal, and a `[→ Study N]` transition. Devotional appeals are welcome — they may not carry doctrine.

### Doctrinal Line: MATURE SDA (Uriah Smith _DAR_ + settled Ellen White positions)

On contested pioneer points follow the settled denominational position: the **"daily" = paganism**; seals/trumpets/churches per the historicist _DAR_ scheme; the investigative judgment beginning **1844**. Use the Millerite movement for the time-prophecies and 1844 history, corrected where the pioneers later refined — but even these are proved from the Bible, not asserted on Smith's/White's authority.

- "Not under the law" (Rom 6:14) = freed from the curse, not the standard.
- Plants the literal weekly Sabbath inside the ark (for Study 24) — far enough to defeat Col 2:16.
  **Proof texts:** Ex 31:18; 25:16; Deut 31:24-26; 1 Kgs 8:9; Rom 7:12; 3:31; 6:14; 8:4; Col 2:14-17; Heb 10:1-10; 9:10; 7:12; Eph 2:15; Matt 5:17-19; Isa 42:21; Jer 31:31-33; James 2:10-12; Lev 23:37-38.
