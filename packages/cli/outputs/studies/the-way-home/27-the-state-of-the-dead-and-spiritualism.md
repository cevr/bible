# Study 27: The State of the Dead and the Last Deception — Spiritualism

**The dead are not in heaven and not in torment — they are asleep, literally unconscious, knowing nothing, until the trumpet wakes them. This is not a small or morbid point: the FIRST lie ever told — "ye shall not surely die" — is the very lie on which Satan builds his last and greatest deception, spiritualism. He could not personate your dead mother, summon a "saint," or counterfeit Christ's return if the dead were silent in the grave. So the truth about death is not gloom; it is the one bulwark that disarms the lying wonders gathering the world to Armageddon. Learn it now, with an open Bible, while it is still easy to test.**

## The First Lie Is Still in the Field

**We open Study 27 where Study 0 opened the whole series — at the serpent's mouth in Eden. There the first shot was fired at the WORD ("hath God said?"); here we trace the first false DOCTRINE he planted, and we will find it has never left the field. It is, in fact, the doctrine on which his final deception stands.**

- **God's plain word at the tree: death is the wage of sin**
  - "But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it: for in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die" (Gen 2:17 — God's own sentence)
  - "For dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return" (Gen 3:19) — the penalty God named is a return to the dust, not a transfer to another life.
  - [STR] God said the wages of sin is DEATH. He did not say the wages of sin is relocation. Hold that plain sentence; the whole study turns on whether we keep it.

- **The serpent's flat contradiction — the first false doctrine**
  - "And the serpent said unto the woman, Ye shall not surely die" (Gen 3:4)
  - Set the two sentences side by side. God: "thou shalt surely die." Satan: "Ye shall not surely die." There is no third option — one of them is lying.
  - [→] Every form of the doctrine that man does not really die — that something in him survives, conscious, the moment the body falls — is a paraphrase of Genesis 3:4. The wording changes; the lie is identical.

[STR] **The lie has a logic, and the logic is the key to the whole deception.** IF something in man cannot die — an immortal soul that lives on, awake, after the body — THEN the dead are still alive somewhere; THEN they can be reached, summoned, and spoken with; THEN whatever speaks must be that dead person. Pull the first domino ("ye shall not surely die") and the whole row of spiritualism stands up. Refuse the first domino and the whole row falls. This is why we must settle the state of the dead before we can be safe in the final crisis.

[DYK🔎] The doctrine that man is born with a deathless, conscious soul is not native to the Hebrew Scriptures — it came into the church through pagan, Platonic philosophy, the same Alexandrian channel Study 0 traced. Plato taught the soul was immortal and the body its prison; the early apostasy "embellished" Scripture with that pagan import, exactly as it allegorized the plain word. The first lie of Eden married Greek philosophy and was christened — but it is still Genesis 3:4 underneath.

## What Man Is — Dust Plus Breath Equals a Living Soul

**Before we ask what happens at death, the Bible tells us plainly what man IS. The immortal-soul error assumes man is a deathless soul temporarily housed in a body. Genesis says the opposite, in the plainest creation arithmetic.**

- **Man does not HAVE a soul — man IS a soul, made of two parts**
  - "And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul" (Gen 2:7)
  - Read the equation exactly as written: dust of the ground + breath of life = a living soul. The text does not say God put a soul INTO the body; it says the body plus the breath BECAME a soul.
  - [→] So "soul" in the Bible is the whole LIVING PERSON, not a separable ghost. Take the breath away and you do not release a soul — you UN-make the living soul, and man returns to dust.

- **"Soul" simply means a living being — the Bible uses it of animals too**
  - The very word rendered "living soul" of man (Gen 2:7) is the same Hebrew word (_nephesh_) the KJV renders "living creature" of the animals: "the moving creature that hath life... every living creature that moveth" (Gen 1:20-21,24). A "soul" is what God's creatures ARE, not an immortal spark they secretly contain.
  - "And it came to pass, as her soul was in departing, (for she died)" (Gen 35:18) — Rachel's "soul departing" is simply her life/breath ceasing; the verse itself defines it: "for she died."

- **The soul is mortal — Scripture says so flatly**
  - "The soul that sinneth, it shall die" (Eze 18:20)
  - That single sentence ends the debate. If the soul can die, the soul is not immortal. The Bible never once calls the soul immortal or undying — not one time. That phrase belongs to philosophy, not to Scripture.

[Q] **"But Ecclesiastes 12:7 says the spirit returns to God — isn't that the conscious soul going home?"**
→ Read what "spirit" means by Scripture's own definition. "Then shall the dust return to the earth as it was: and the spirit shall return unto God who gave it" (Eccl 12:7). This is creation in reverse: at creation God gave the dust the BREATH of life (Gen 2:7); at death He takes that breath back. The "spirit" (_ruach_, also "breath," cf. Ps 146:4 "his breath goeth forth") is the life-spark, the breath of life — not a conscious entity. Two things go their way at death: the dust goes back to the ground, and the life-principle goes back to the God who lent it. What you do NOT have is a third thing — a conscious YOU — floating off. The person is the dust PLUS the breath together. Separate them and the living soul ceases, exactly as a candle's flame ceases when you separate the wax from the fire — the flame does not "go somewhere," it stops.

[STR] **This is the whole foundation, and it is one verse wide:** Genesis 2:7. Dust + breath = a living soul. Subtract the breath and you do not get a surviving soul; you get dust again (Gen 3:19) plus the breath returned to God (Eccl 12:7). Man is mortal by make. Immortality is not something he owns — it is something he must be GIVEN. Keep that, and every text that follows simply fills in the picture.

## Immortality Belongs to God Alone — and Comes Only Through Christ

**Here is the hinge that turns the doctrine from gloom into gospel. Man is mortal — so where does eternal life come from? Not from inside us. It is GOD'S possession, His gift, and it is handed to us through one channel only: Jesus Christ, and at one moment only: the resurrection.**

- **Only God is naturally immortal**
  - "Who only hath immortality, dwelling in the light which no man can approach unto; whom no man hath seen, nor can see" (1 Tim 6:16)
  - "Only." Read it slowly. If God ONLY hath immortality, then no man is born with it. The deathless soul of philosophy is, by this verse, a possession that belongs to God alone.

- **Eternal life is a GIFT, not a natural property — and the wage of sin is still death**
  - "For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord" (Rom 6:23)
  - Death is what we have EARNED; eternal life is what is GIVEN. If we already possessed an undying soul, eternal life could not be a gift — you cannot be given what you already own.
  - We are told to SEEK for it: "To them who by patient continuance in well doing seek for glory and honour and immortality, eternal life" (Rom 2:7). You do not seek what you already have.

- **Christ brought immortality to light — it was in the dark until the gospel**
  - "Who hath abolished death, and hath brought life and immortality to light through the gospel" (2 Tim 1:10)
  - Immortality is something Christ BROUGHT to light. It was not glowing in every human breast all along; it is His to give, opened by His own resurrection.

- **And it is bestowed at the resurrection — not at death**
  - "Behold, I shew you a mystery; We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed, In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump: for the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed. For this corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal must put on immortality" (1 Cor 15:51-53)
  - Mark the WHEN: "at the last trump." This MORTAL "must put on" immortality — meaning it does not have it yet — and it puts it on at the resurrection, not at the funeral.

[→] Lay the chain end to end and the gospel rings: man is mortal (Gen 2:7; Eze 18:20) → only God has immortality (1 Tim 6:16) → it is Christ's gift (Rom 6:23; 2 Tim 1:10) → handed over at the resurrection (1 Cor 15:53). Every link removes the dead from the reach of the séance and puts our whole hope on the empty tomb and the coming trumpet.

[DYK🔎] God closed Eden's gate for this very reason. "Lest he put forth his hand, and take also of the tree of life, and eat, and live for ever" — "therefore the LORD God sent him forth" (Gen 3:22-23). God BARRED fallen man from the tree of life precisely so that sinful man would NOT live forever. The natural-immortality doctrine asks us to believe the very thing God shut the gate to prevent: that fallen man lives forever anyway, by nature, with no tree of life needed. Eden's flaming sword (Gen 3:24) is a standing rebuke to Genesis 3:4.

## Death Is a Sleep — Conscious of Nothing, Until the Resurrection

**Now the central question: where are the dead, and what do they know? Scripture's answer is gathered line upon line, and it is consistent from Job to Revelation — the dead are ASLEEP, conscious of nothing, awaiting one event: the resurrection.**

- **The dead know nothing at all — the plainest statement in the Bible on the subject**
  - "For the living know that they shall die: but the dead know not any thing, neither have they any more a reward; for the memory of them is forgotten. Also their love, and their hatred, and their envy, is now perished" (Eccl 9:5-6)
  - "the dead know not any thing." Not "the dead know a little"; not "the dead know in a different way." Nothing. Their very emotions — love, hatred, envy — have "perished." A conscious soul in heaven or torment knows a great deal; the dead "know not any thing."

- **No work, no thought, no wisdom, no remembering of God in the grave**
  - "Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest" (Eccl 9:10)
  - "His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish" (Ps 146:4) — the moment the breath leaves, the THOUGHTS perish. A thinking, conscious soul has not perished its thoughts; it is thinking harder than ever.
  - "For in death there is no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks?" (Ps 6:5)
  - "The dead praise not the LORD, neither any that go down into silence" (Ps 115:17) — "silence," not song in glory.

- **The dead do not praise God; the LIVING do**
  - "For the grave cannot praise thee, death cannot celebrate thee... The living, the living, he shall praise thee" (Isa 38:18-19)
  - Job, dying, knows nothing of his own children's fortunes: "His sons come to honour, and he knoweth it not; and they are brought low, but he perceiveth it not of them" (Job 14:21). The dead are not watching over us from above; they perceive nothing.

- **Even DAVID, "a man after God's own heart," is not in heaven — he is in his grave**
  - "Men and brethren, let me freely speak unto you of the patriarch David, that he is both dead and buried, and his sepulchre is with us unto this day" (Acts 2:29)
  - "For David is not ascended into the heavens" (Acts 2:34)
  - Peter preached this at Pentecost, full of the Spirit. If anyone earned an immediate flight to glory it was David — yet Peter says plainly he is dead, buried, and "not ascended into the heavens." That alone settles it.

[STR] **Scripture's chosen word for death is SLEEP — and it means a real, unconscious sleep.** "So man lieth down, and riseth not: till the heavens be no more, they shall not awake, nor be raised out of their sleep" (Job 14:12). David, Daniel's righteous, the dead in Christ — all "sleep" (Dan 12:2; 1 Thess 4:13). A sleeper is alive but unconscious of the passing time; he wakes thinking no time has passed at all. So it will be: to the one who dies, the next conscious instant is the resurrection morning — however many centuries the world counts in between.

### Christ's Own Definition: "Our Friend Lazarus Sleepeth"

**The clearest teacher on death is Christ Himself, and He chose the deathbed of His friend to define it. Watch Him correct the disciples' confusion — and notice He calls Lazarus BACK, never down.**

- **Christ calls death "sleep" — and then says it plainly so none can mistake Him**
  - "Our friend Lazarus sleepeth; but I go, that I may awake him out of sleep" (John 11:11)
  - "Howbeit Jesus spake of his death: but they thought that he had spoken of taking of rest in sleep. Then said Jesus unto them plainly, Lazarus is dead" (John 11:13-14)
  - Christ deliberately equates death with sleep, then makes the equation unmistakable: "Lazarus is dead" = Lazarus "sleepeth." This is Christ's own author-marked definition of what death is — a sleep.

- **He raised him by calling him FORTH — back from the grave, not down from bliss**
  - "And when he thus had spoken, he cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come forth. And he that was dead came forth, bound hand and foot with graveclothes" (John 11:43-44)
  - [→] Lazarus had been dead four days. If his conscious soul were in heaven, then Christ's "miracle" was to drag a happy man OUT of paradise and BACK into a sick world — and Lazarus never said one word about where he had been. The text knows nothing of a soul recalled from glory; it shows a man called UP from the grave, asleep, knowing nothing of the four days, woken by the Voice.

[Q] **"Didn't Jesus tell the thief, 'Today shalt thou be with me in paradise' (Luke 23:43)? That sounds like the dead go straight to heaven."**
→ Two things, both from the text. First, Christ Himself did NOT go to paradise that day — three days later, risen, He told Mary: "Touch me not; for I am not yet ascended to my Father" (John 20:17). If Jesus had not ascended by Sunday, He was not in heaven's paradise with the thief on Friday. Second, the original has no punctuation; the comma's placement is the translator's choice. Read it so it agrees with John 20:17 and Christ's own "Lazarus sleepeth": "Verily I say unto thee today, Thou shalt be with me in paradise" — the EMPHASIS ("I tell you today, on this dark day when I seem defeated") fits the scene, and it does not break Christ against Himself. The thief's hope is the same as ours: not a flight that afternoon, but a place in the kingdom at the resurrection — exactly what he asked for: "Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom" (Luke 23:42). The rule of Study 0 governs: we do not let one ambiguous verse break the plain, repeated testimony.

### The One Hope Is the Resurrection — Not a Soul's Flight

**If the dead are asleep, then the hope of the gospel is not that the soul escapes the body — it is that the WHOLE PERSON is raised. This is precisely how the apostles comfort the grieving: not "your loved one is already with the Lord," but "the dead in Christ shall rise."**

- **Paul's comfort for the bereaved is RESURRECTION, not a soul already in heaven**
  - "But I would not have you to be ignorant, brethren, concerning them which are asleep, that ye sorrow not, even as others which have no hope... them also which sleep in Jesus will God bring with him" (1 Thess 4:13-14)
  - "For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout... and the dead in Christ shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air" (1 Thess 4:16-17)
  - [STR] Notice what Paul does NOT say. He does not console them with "your dead are already happy in heaven." He points them entirely to the SECOND COMING and the resurrection. If the dead were already with the Lord, the resurrection would be a demotion, and this comfort would make no sense. The whole consolation hangs on the dead being asleep, awaiting the trump.

- **The dead wait IN THE GRAVES for the Voice — all of them, both kinds**
  - "Marvel not at this: for the hour is coming, in the which all that are in the graves shall hear his voice, And shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation" (John 5:28-29)
  - The good and the evil alike are "in the graves" until "the hour" — not in heaven, not in hell, but in the graves, awaiting His voice.
  - "And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt" (Dan 12:2)

- **Without the resurrection the dead are simply PERISHED — Paul's own logic**
  - "And if Christ be not raised, your faith is vain; ye are yet in your sins. Then they also which are fallen asleep in Christ are perished" (1 Cor 15:17-18)
  - [→] Read that and the immortal-soul theory collapses on Paul's own argument. IF the dead in Christ were already conscious in heaven, then even with no resurrection they would be perfectly fine — yet Paul says without the resurrection they are "perished," lost. The dead have NO life apart from the resurrection. Their entire hope is the trumpet. That is why Satan must counterfeit it.

[ILL] Stephen, the first martyr, full of the Holy Ghost, with heaven open before his eyes — how does Scripture describe his death? "And when he had said this, he fell asleep" (Acts 7:60). The Spirit-filled writer of Acts, recording the death of a man who had just SEEN Jesus standing in glory, calls it falling asleep. If ever a soul flew straight to glory it was Stephen's — yet the Bible's word is "asleep." What the Bible calls sleep, let no man call a séance.

**Central Verse:** "For the living know that they shall die: but the dead know not any thing, neither have they any more a reward; for the memory of them is forgotten" (Ecclesiastes 9:5)

## So Who Is Speaking in the Séance? — Spiritualism Unmasked

**Now the doctrine becomes armor. If the dead "know not any thing," are silent, asleep in the grave until the resurrection — then whatever appears in a séance, whatever "spirit" speaks with your father's voice, whatever radiant being claims to be a departed saint, CANNOT be the dead. The Bible has already told us who it is, and what it is for.**

- **The dead cannot come back — Scripture is flat about it**
  - "As the cloud is consumed and vanisheth away: so he that goeth down to the grave shall come up no more. He shall return no more to his house, neither shall his place know him any more" (Job 7:9-10)
  - "no more." The dead do not revisit, do not appear, do not communicate. So a "spirit of the departed" is, by definition, an impostor — the question is only WHO is behind the mask.

- **God FORBADE consulting the dead — because it is consulting the ENEMY**
  - "Regard not them that have familiar spirits, neither seek after wizards, to be defiled by them: I am the LORD your God" (Lev 19:31)
  - "A man also or woman that hath a familiar spirit, or that is a wizard, shall surely be put to death" (Lev 20:27)
  - "There shall not be found among you... a consulter with familiar spirits, or a wizard, or a necromancer. For all that do these things are an abomination unto the LORD" (Deut 18:11-12)
  - [STR] God does not legislate a death penalty against contacting NOTHING. He outlaws it so severely because something real is reached — but it is not the beloved dead; it is "an abomination," a demonic counterfeit. The medium does not contact the departed; she contacts the deceiver.

- **The accuser of Eden wears costumes — even an angel of light**
  - "And no marvel; for Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light" (2 Cor 11:14)
  - The same being who took the serpent's form in Eden takes the form of a glorious angel, a departed loved one, a "Mary," a "saint" — even, at the last, a counterfeit Christ. He has had millennia to study your dead and can mimic the voice, the face, the favorite phrase.

- **Lying wonders are the agreed end-time strategy — Scripture names it three times over**
  - "For there shall arise false Christs, and false prophets, and shall shew great signs and wonders; insomuch that, if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect" (Matt 24:24)
  - "Even him, whose coming is after the working of Satan with all power and signs and lying wonders, And with all deceivableness of unrighteousness in them that perish; because they received not the love of the truth" (2 Thess 2:9-10)
  - "Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils" (1 Tim 4:1) — "doctrines of DEVILS," taught by "seducing SPIRITS." The voices in the séance are teaching a curriculum, and its author is named.

[→] String it together and spiritualism is exposed before it speaks: the dead know nothing and cannot return (Eccl 9:5; Job 7:9) → therefore no spirit is the departed → Satan masquerades (2 Cor 11:14) and his agents are "seducing spirits" teaching "doctrines of devils" (1 Tim 4:1) → working "lying wonders" to deceive even the elect (Matt 24:24; 2 Thess 2:9-10). The "ghost" is the accuser of Eden, still running Genesis 3:4. He told the first lie about death; he uses the SAME lie to stage the last deception.

### Endor: The Oldest Impersonation on Record

**The Bible gives us the textbook case — King Saul, the witch, and the "Samuel" that came up. The SDA pioneers read it exactly as the doctrine demands, and the text supports them at every joint: this was no return of the prophet, but the oldest recorded impersonation of the dead.**

- **The setting: God had STOPPED answering Saul, so Saul broke God's own law**
  - "And when Saul enquired of the LORD, the LORD answered him not, neither by dreams, nor by Urim, nor by prophets" (1 Sam 28:6)
  - So Saul did the forbidden thing: "Seek me a woman that hath a familiar spirit... Behold, there is a woman that hath a familiar spirit at Endor" (1 Sam 28:7)
  - [STR] Mark the contradiction the doctrine exposes: God had REFUSED to speak to Saul by any lawful means — yet at a witch's table, through a "familiar spirit" God had condemned to death, suddenly "Samuel" appears and speaks freely. Would the God who shut every righteous door open an unlawful one He had cursed? The Voice at Endor is not God's prophet; it is the very "familiar spirit" the law named (Lev 20:27).

- **The "Samuel" preached Satan's gospel, not God's**
  - The apparition told Saul: "to morrow shalt thou and thy sons be with me" (1 Sam 28:19). With ME — in the realm of the dead, conscious, together. That is the immortal-soul doctrine in the apparition's own mouth: it preaches that the dead live on, awake, "with" each other. The real Samuel would never preach Genesis 3:4.

- **Scripture's own VERDICT on the event: Saul consulted a "familiar spirit," and it killed him**
  - "So Saul died for his transgression which he committed against the LORD... and ALSO for asking counsel of one that had a familiar spirit, to enquire of it; And enquired not of the LORD" (1 Chr 10:13-14)
  - [→] This is the inspired commentary, and it is decisive. The Bible does NOT say "Saul died for consulting the prophet Samuel." It says he died "for asking counsel of one that had a FAMILIAR SPIRIT" — and "enquired NOT of the LORD." Heaven files the whole transaction under demonic consultation, not under a genuine visit from the dead. Endor is the Bible's own demonstration that what comes up at the medium's call is a familiar spirit wearing the dead man's mantle.

[Q] **"But verse 14 says 'Saul perceived that it was Samuel.' Doesn't the text itself call it Samuel?"**
→ The text reports what SAUL perceived and what the medium said — and then Scripture gives its own ruling against both. This is how Scripture often narrates a deception: it tells you what the deceived person believed, then exposes it. Saul, terrified and cut off from God, "perceived" through a witch's familiar spirit exactly what the spirit meant him to perceive — a convincing likeness. But 1 Chronicles 10:13 — the Holy Spirit's own summary — calls it "a familiar spirit," not Samuel. And the doctrine we have already proved from a dozen plain texts forbids the alternative: Samuel was dead and "knew not any thing" (Eccl 9:5), could "come up no more" (Job 7:9), and was not God's to summon at a forbidden altar. A convincing impersonation that says "thou shalt be with me" is precisely what 2 Corinthians 11:14 predicts. We let the clear texts govern the one hard scene — the rule of Study 0 — and the hard scene resolves into the very first séance and the very first impersonation of the dead.

From that obscure cottage the movement exploded across America and then the world: séances, mediums, "spirit communications," organized into a religion claiming millions within a generation. Whatever the rappings were, mark the prophetic geography and timing: a lying-wonders movement, claiming contact with the departed, breaking out in the UNITED STATES at the very moment the Advent message about the judgment was going forth (1844 had just passed). The two-horned beast of Revelation 13 — the United States (Study 28) — is the power Scripture says "doeth great wonders... and deceiveth them that dwell on the earth by the means of those miracles" (Rev 13:13-14). Spiritualism is that deceiving-wonder, sprung up in that land, on that clock.

[DYK🔎] **The doctrine's pagan pedigree — how the lie entered Christendom.** The natural-immortality teaching that makes spiritualism possible did not come from the Hebrew prophets; it was imported from Greek (Platonic) philosophy through the same Alexandrian school that gave the church the allegorical method (Study 0). Plato's deathless, conscious soul was baptized into Christian dress in the early centuries of the apostasy, and the festival of the dead, prayers to departed "saints," purgatory, and the cult of relics all grew from that one pagan root. So spiritualism is not a new religion — it is the FIRST lie (Gen 3:4), dressed first in a Greek toga, then in a Roman cassock, and now in a séance veil. One lie, three costumes, one author.

[→] Here is the historicist receipt: the Bible PREDICTED a final flood of lying wonders, working through a power rising in the New World, deceiving "them that dwell on the earth" (Rev 13:13-14; 16:14; Matt 24:24). History RECORDS that a wonder-working movement claiming to speak with the dead broke out in the United States in 1848 and swept the earth. The prediction is the Bible's; the fulfillment is on the record. The meaning — that these "spirits of devils, working miracles" (Rev 16:14) are demons, not the dead — stands on the doctrine we proved from Scripture first.

## Appeal

- The first lie ever told was about death — "ye shall not surely die" — and it is still the lie on which Satan builds his last deception. Refuse it now, with an open Bible, and you cannot be deceived later.
- Your dead are not in torment, and they are not watching you from above. They are asleep — at perfect rest, knowing nothing, safe in God's memory, awaiting His voice. This is not a colder hope; it is a cleaner one. No loved one is suffering tonight, and none can be reached, used, or counterfeited to lead you astray.
- Our hope is not a soul's escape but a body's resurrection — "the dead in Christ shall rise" at the shout of the descending Lord (1 Thess 4:16). We do not climb up to them in death; He comes down and wakes them all at once. Set your whole hope on that morning.
- When the lying wonders come — and they are already in the field — you have one defense, and it is enough: not the brilliance of the vision, but the plain written Word. "To the law and to the testimony." Test every spirit there.
- Will you let the dead sleep in peace, and stake everything on the resurrection — taking God at His plain word, while the test is still easy to apply?
