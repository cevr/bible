## The Commandments of God and the Faith of Jesus

> The three angels' messages are the everlasting gospel in judgment-hour form, and they end by describing the people they produce — a patient company keeping BOTH the commandments of God AND the faith of Jesus: law and gospel held together, ripened to stand.

**The everlasting gospel preached in the hour of judgment — Rev. 14:6-7; Rom. 1:16-17:**

- _Rev. 14:6._ "And I saw another angel fly in the midst of heaven, having the everlasting gospel to preach unto them that dwell on the earth, and to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people" — not a new gospel but the one eternal good news, borne worldwide in the last hour; whatever the three messages are, they are gospel first.
- _Rev. 14:7._ "Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come: and worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters" — the gospel preached in a judgment-hour setting: fear God, give Him glory, worship the Creator — present truth because the hour of His judgment IS come.
- _17LtMs, Lt 183, 1902, par. 4._ White: "The subject of the first angel's message is the everlasting gospel" — the gospel is the substance the judgment-hour messages carry; take the gospel out and no message remains.
- _Rom. 1:16._ "For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth" — the everlasting gospel of _Rev. 14:6_ is POWER, not mere information; it actually saves the believer it finds.
- _Rom. 1:17._ "For therein is the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith: as it is written, The just shall live by faith." — the gospel reveals a righteousness of God received by faith; the message of Revelation 14 carries righteousness by faith (see "Righteousness by Faith — The Third Angel's Message in Verity").

**The judgment-hour call is the whole duty of man — Eccl. 12:13-14; Dan. 7:10:**

- _Eccl. 12:13._ "Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter: Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man" — the identical twofold call the first and third angels sound (_Rev. 14:7, 12_); the message is not a novelty but the conclusion of the whole matter.
- _Eccl. 12:14._ "For God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether it be good, or whether it be evil" — Solomon's reason is the angel's reason: the duty is pressed because the judgment comes (_Rev. 14:7_).
- _Dan. 7:10._ "thousand thousands ministered unto him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him: the judgment was set, and the books were opened" — the heavenly courtroom whose hour has come (_Rev. 14:7_); the open books make the message urgent, for a people must be ripened who can stand there (see "The Time of Trouble — Standing Without a Mediator").

**The third angel — two worships sorted, and a people described — Rev. 14:9, 12:**

- _Rev. 14:9._ "If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark in his forehead, or in his hand" — the third angel's warning against false worship stands over against the true worship of v. 7; two loyalties are being sorted out in the judgment hour.
  - **the third angel's message** = the everlasting gospel (_Rev. 14:6_) in its judgment-hour form: righteousness by faith producing a commandment-keeping people — not law apart from gospel, for the message ends by describing the people it makes, who keep BOTH "the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (_Rev. 14:9-12_; _Rom. 1:16-17_; _Eccl. 12:13-14_; _Dan. 7:10_).
- _1SM 372.2._ White: "Several have written to me, inquiring if the message of justification by faith is the third angel's message, and I have answered, 'It is the third angel's message, in verity.'" — the keystone, her 1890 answer: Rev. 14:9-12 is gospel, the very righteousness by faith carried from "Righteousness by Faith — The Third Angel's Message in Verity."
- _Rev. 14:12._ "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." — the three messages END in a people: patient, commandment-keeping, holding the faith of Jesus — law and gospel together, neither alone.
  - **the faith of Jesus** = not merely faith IN Jesus (though it includes trust in His ability to save fully) but the faith OF Jesus — the very faith by which Christ Himself overcame, now living in the saints (_Rev. 3:21_, "even as I also overcame"; _Heb. 12:2_, "the author and finisher of our faith"; _Gal. 2:20_, "the faith of the Son of God"; _1 John 5:4_) — the gospel half of the message that makes the law half possible.
- _CIHS 167.1._ White: "The result of an acceptance of these messages is given in the word: 'Here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus.' In order to be prepared for the judgment, it is necessary that men should keep the law of God. That law will be the standard of character in the judgment" — the messages are measured by their result: a people prepared to stand.
- _TMR 127.1._ Smith: the message "is given in the period of the 'patience of the saints'... And this period of the saints' patience is marked by a most important fact, viz., the keeping of the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus" — the pioneer reading: the third message is identified by the people it produces.
- _AJB 301.1._ Bates: "the third giving a most solemn warning against false worship, and presenting the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus" — the historical anchor: from the late 1840s the earliest Sabbatarian Adventists read the third message so; this is the doctrinal foundation of the movement.

**The faith OF Jesus — the faith by which He overcame — Rev. 3:21; Heb. 12:2; Gal. 2:20:**

- _Rev. 3:21._ "To him that overcometh will I grant to sit with me in my throne, even as I also overcame, and am set down with my Father in his throne" — "even as I ALSO overcame": Christ overcame by faith in the flesh He took (see "The Second Adam — The Nature Christ Took"), and grants the overcomer the same faith and the same seat (see "Complete Victory — Overcoming as He Overcame").
- _Heb. 12:2._ "Looking unto Jesus the author and finisher of our faith; who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross" — Jesus is not merely the object of faith but its author and finisher; He exercised faith Himself, enduring the cross, and that same faith He originates and perfects in the saints.
- _Gal. 2:20._ "the life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself for me" — the faith OF the Son of God living in the believer: Christ's own faith reproduced in the saint, the very mechanism by which the crucified-with-Christ life is lived out in the flesh.
- _3SM 172.3._ White: "What constitutes the faith of Jesus, that belongs to the third angel's message? Jesus becoming our sin-bearer that He might become our sin-pardoning Saviour... He came to our world and took our sins that we might take His righteousness. And faith in the ability of Christ to save us amply and fully and entirely is the faith of Jesus." — EGW defines the symbol this section owns: trust in Christ's FULL ability to save, grounded in the great exchange.
- _TMR 134.2._ Smith: "This term is used in distinction from the commandments of God... it is spoken of as being kept in the same manner that the commandments of God are kept" — Smith's own point reaches only this far: the faith of Jesus is the gospel half, distinct from yet kept alongside the law half. The overcoming-faith sense developed above is not Smith's reading but the Bible spine's own (_Rev. 3:21_; _Heb. 12:2_; _Gal. 2:20_) — a Scripture-grounded sense wide enough to hold both witnesses: the faith EGW names, which trusts Christ's ability to save fully, and the gospel Smith names, kept as the commandments are kept.

**That faith works by love and produces the keeping — 1 John 5:3-4; John 14:15:**

- _1 John 5:3._ "For this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous" — commandment-keeping is the fruit of love, not a burden set against the gospel; this is HOW they of _Rev. 14:12_ keep.
- _1 John 5:4._ "whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith" — the victory IS faith: the overcoming faith of _Rev. 3:21_ is the faith of _Rev. 14:12_, producing obedience in the born-again (see "Born of Water and of the Spirit — How He Lived, How We Begin").
- _John 14:15._ "If ye love me, keep my commandments." — Christ's own order: love first, obedience following; the faith of Jesus working by love yields the commandment-keeping of _Rev. 14:12_.

**The same twofold mark — the remnant the dragon wars against — Rev. 12:17; 19:10:**

- _Rev. 12:17._ "And the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — the same twofold mark identifies the remnant; the people of _Rev. 14:12_ are the object of the dragon's final war.
- _Rev. 19:10._ "the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy" — Scripture defines its own term: the second mark of the remnant is self-interpreted within the book.
- _TMR 114.1._ Smith: "The preaching of the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus. This is the cause of the conflict between the dragon and the last fragment of the church... It is because the saints are keeping all the commandments of God that the dragon makes war upon them" — _Rev. 14:12_ and _Rev. 12:17_ name one people; the keeping provokes the war.

**The book closes as chapter 14 described — Rev. 22:14:**

- _Rev. 22:14._ "Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city." — Revelation's last benediction falls on the very people the three messages produce; the commandment-keeping, faith-of-Jesus company inherits the city (see "The Harvest — A People in Whom Christ Is Vindicated").

**DEFINITION — THE COMMANDMENTS OF GOD AND THE FAITH OF JESUS =** the third angel's message is the everlasting gospel (_Rev. 14:6_; _17LtMs, Lt 183, 1902_) preached in the hour of God's judgment (_Rev. 14:7_; _Dan. 7:10_; _Eccl. 12:13-14_) — the power of God unto salvation, revealing the righteousness of God by faith (_Rom. 1:16-17_) — righteousness by faith, "the third angel's message, in verity" (_1SM 372.2_; see "Righteousness by Faith — The Third Angel's Message in Verity"). It is not law apart from gospel, for the messages END by describing the people they produce: "here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (_Rev. 14:12_; _CIHS 167.1_; _TMR 127.1_) — the same twofold-marked remnant the dragon wars against (_Rev. 12:17_; _TMR 114.1_), whose second mark Scripture self-interprets as the spirit of prophecy (_Rev. 19:10_). The faith of Jesus is the faith BY which He Himself overcame (_Rev. 3:21_), which He authors and finishes (_Heb. 12:2_), which lives in the believer as His own life of faith reproduced (_Gal. 2:20_), and which trusts His ability "to save us amply and fully and entirely" (_3SM 172.3_) — the gospel half kept alongside the law half (_TMR 134.2_). That faith is itself "the victory that overcometh the world" (_1 John 5:4_), working by love to keep commandments that are not grievous (_1 John 5:3_; _John 14:15_). Law and gospel are therefore not rivals in the message but its two marks on one people: the commandments show what the faith of Jesus produces, and the faith of Jesus supplies what the commandments demand. The judgment-hour setting makes this present truth — the message must ripen a people who can stand when the books are opened (_CIHS 167.1_; see "The Time of Trouble — Standing Without a Mediator"), and the book closes as chapter 14 described: "they that do his commandments" enter through the gates into the city (_Rev. 22:14_; see "The Harvest — A People in Whom Christ Is Vindicated").

**Symbols defined here:**

- **the third angel's message** = the everlasting gospel in its judgment-hour form: righteousness by faith producing a commandment-keeping people — never law apart from gospel (_Rev. 14:6-7_; _Rev. 14:9-12_; _Rom. 1:16-17_; _1SM 372.2_; _AJB 301.1_).
- **the faith of Jesus** = the very faith by which Christ overcame, authored and finished by Him, now living in the saints and producing commandment-keeping; trust in His ability to save amply, fully, and entirely (_Rev. 14:12_; _Rev. 3:21_; _Heb. 12:2_; _Gal. 2:20_; _1 John 5:3-4_; _3SM 172.3_; _TMR 134.2_).

**Symbols carried:** righteousness by faith — imputed and imparted, here named as the message itself (see "Righteousness by Faith — The Third Angel's Message in Verity"); the overcomer — the same victory, by the same faith, "even as I also overcame" (see "Complete Victory — Overcoming as He Overcame"); the second Adam / sinful flesh — the flesh in which that overcoming faith was first lived (see "The Second Adam — The Nature Christ Took"); sin as the consented transgression of the law the saints here keep (see "What Sin Is — Transgression, Not Inheritance").

**For discussion:**

1. _Rev. 14:12_ names both "the commandments of God" and "the faith of Jesus." Which half do you naturally emphasize — and what would it look like this week to hold them as one message, where faith supplies what the law demands (_1 John 5:3-4_)?
2. If the faith of Jesus is the very faith by which He overcame (_Rev. 3:21_; _Gal. 2:20_), what changes when temptation comes — trying harder, or trusting fuller in "the ability of Christ to save us amply and fully and entirely" (_3SM 172.3_)?
3. "The hour of his judgment is come" (_Rev. 14:7_; _Dan. 7:10_) — the courtroom is already in session. How does that change what "present truth" means for your obedience and your witness today?
