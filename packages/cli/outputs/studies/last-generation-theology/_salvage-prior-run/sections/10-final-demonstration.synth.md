## The Harvest — A People in Whom Christ Is Vindicated

> The whole chain ends where the parable said it would: when the character of Christ is perfectly reproduced in His people the grain is ripe, the sickle falls, and in a faultless remnant the accuser is answered and God is vindicated before the watching universe.

**The harvest law — the ripeness of the grain sets the hour of the sickle — Mark 4:26-29; Rev. 14:15; James 5:7-8:**

- _Mark 4:26-27._ "So is the kingdom of God, as if a man should cast seed into the ground; And should sleep, and rise night and day, and the seed should spring and grow up, he knoweth not how" — the kingdom works as a crop works: sown, growing unseen, a fixed law moving toward a harvest.
- _Mark 4:28._ "For the earth bringeth forth fruit of herself; first the blade, then the ear, after that the full corn in the ear" — growth is ordered and gradual, and its goal is not blade or ear but FULL corn: maturity.
- _Mark 4:29._ "But when the fruit is brought forth, immediately he putteth in the sickle, because the harvest is come" — the ripeness of the fruit, not a date on a calendar, brings the sickle; the moment the crop is ready, the reaping is IMMEDIATE.
  - **the harvest / the ripe grain** = the ripened likeness of Christ reproduced in His people — character matured from blade to full corn — which is itself the signal for the sickle; when the fruit is fully brought forth, Christ immediately reaps, so the ripeness of the crop sets the hour of His coming (_Mark 4:28-29_; _Rev. 14:15_, "the harvest of the earth is ripe"; _James 5:7-8_, the husbandman waiting for "the precious fruit of the earth"; _Rev. 19:7-8_, the wife made ready).
- _Rev. 14:15._ "Thrust in thy sickle, and reap: for the time is come for thee to reap; for the harvest of the earth is ripe" — heaven's own reading of _Mark 4:29_ enacted at the end of the world: the time to reap IS the ripeness of the harvest; Christ comes when the crop is ready.
- _James 5:7._ "Behold, the husbandman waiteth for the precious fruit of the earth, and hath long patience for it, until he receive the early and latter rain" — the Husbandman Himself waits with long patience — not for time to pass but for grain to ripen under the two rains.
- _James 5:8._ "Be ye also patient; stablish your hearts: for the coming of the Lord draweth nigh" — the coming draws nigh exactly as the fruit ripens; the waiting saints and the waiting Lord wait for the same thing.
- _COL 69.1._ White: "Christ is waiting with longing desire for the manifestation of Himself in His church. When the character of Christ shall be perfectly reproduced in His people, then He will come to claim them as His own." — the keystone, written directly on _Mark 4:29_: the ripe fruit IS the reproduced character of Christ; He tarries for the harvest of His own likeness, then comes.
- _DA 671.2._ White: "Christ has given His Spirit as a divine power to overcome all hereditary and cultivated tendencies to evil, and to impress His own character upon His church." — how the grain ripens: the Spirit overcomes every hereditary and cultivated tendency to evil and stamps Christ's character on the church — the birth of "Born of Water and of the Spirit — How He Lived, How We Begin" grown to full corn.

**The sealed firstfruits — the 144,000 stand with the Lamb — Rev. 7:2-4; 14:1-5; Zeph. 3:13:**

- _Rev. 7:2-3._ "Hurt not the earth, neither the sea, nor the trees, till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads" — the winds of strife are held back until the servants of God are sealed; the sealing must finish before the end can break.
- _Rev. 7:4._ "And I heard the number of them which were sealed: and there were sealed an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel" — the sealed company numbered and named.
  - **the 144,000 / the remnant** = the last generation — the sealed servants of God, redeemed from among men as firstfruits, who follow the Lamb whithersoever He goeth and in whose mouth is found no guile: without fault before the throne, the remnant of Israel that shall not do iniquity nor speak lies; in them the gospel's full power is demonstrated (_Rev. 7:2-4_; _Rev. 14:1-5_; _Zeph. 3:13_; _Eph. 5:27_; _Jude 1:24_).
- _LDE 219.4._ White: "Just as soon as the people of God are sealed in their foreheads—it is not any seal or mark that can be seen, but a settling into the truth, both intellectually and spiritually, so they cannot be moved—just as soon as God's people are sealed and prepared for the shaking, it will come." — the seal defined: a settling into truth so fixed the sealed cannot be moved — the inward reality behind the faultless standing.
- _Rev. 14:1._ "a Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father's name written in their foreheads" — the sealed of _Rev. 7_ seen standing with the Lamb; the seal is the FATHER'S NAME — His character — written in the mind.
- _Rev. 14:3._ "they sung as it were a new song before the throne... and no man could learn that song but the hundred and forty and four thousand, which were redeemed from the earth" — a song of experience no other company can learn; their history is unrepeatable.
- _Rev. 14:4._ "These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth. These were redeemed from among men, being the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb" — FIRSTFRUITS: harvest language again — the first ripe sheaf of the field, proof that the harvest law has run its full course in human flesh.
- _Rev. 14:5._ "And in their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault before the throne of God" — the crowning trait of the last generation: guileless mouths, faultless standing — and not before men, but before the THRONE.
- _Zeph. 3:13._ "The remnant of Israel shall not do iniquity, nor speak lies; neither shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth: for they shall feed and lie down, and none shall make them afraid" — the Old Testament twin of _Rev. 14:5_: the remnant shall NOT DO iniquity, and no deceit is in their mouth — salvation from sin demonstrated in a people (see "What Salvation Really Is — Saved From Sin").
- _SSP 250.1._ Haskell: "In their mouth was found no guile; for the soul temple had been so thoroughly cleansed before leaving earth that the human mouth became a channel for the words of God.... This will be the mind of those who are sealed. They are without fault; because Christ's righteousness covers them as a garment.... They overcame by the blood of the Lamb." — three of the section's own terms in one witness — no guile, without fault, overcame by the blood — with _Zeph. 3:13_ in its printed margin; the faultlessness is Christ's righteousness as a garment, not human merit.

**Faultless — kept and presented by His power, not their merit — Jude 1:24; Eph. 5:27; Rev. 19:7-8:**

- _Jude 1:24._ "Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy" — the keeping and the presenting are both HIS act: "faultless" (_Rev. 14:5_) is a doxology to His ability, not a boast of theirs.
- _Eph. 5:27._ "That he might present it to himself a glorious church, not having spot, or wrinkle, or any such thing; but that it should be holy and without blemish" — HE presents it to HIMSELF: the spotless church is Christ's own workmanship, the end for which He gave Himself.
- _Rev. 19:7._ "the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready" — the readiness is real: the ripeness is actual, not a legal fiction; the harvest and the wedding are one event under two figures.
- _Rev. 19:8._ "And to her was granted that she should be arrayed in fine linen, clean and white: for the fine linen is the righteousness of saints" — "made herself ready," yet the arraying "was GRANTED": the righteousness of saints is a granted robe — righteousness by faith to the very end (see "Righteousness by Faith — The Third Angel's Message in Verity").
- _1SM 366.1._ White: "God requires the entire surrender of the heart, before justification can take place; and in order for man to retain justification, there must be continual obedience, through active, living faith that works by love and purifies the soul." — the granted robe and the actual obedience are not rivals: the harvest of a guileless people is justification by faith retained and ripened, not a work replacing it.

**The vindication — the accuser answered before the watching universe — Eph. 3:10; Rev. 12:10-11:**

- _Eph. 3:10._ "To the intent that now unto the principalities and powers in heavenly places might be known by the church the manifold wisdom of God" — the demonstration has an audience: the unfallen universe learns the wisdom of God BY THE CHURCH.
- _16MR 115.1._ White: "In heaven Satan had declared that the sin of Adam revealed that human beings could not keep the law of God.... Christ came to unmask the deceiver.... The Majesty of heaven would undertake the cause of man, and with the same facilities that man may obtain, stand the test and proving of God as man must stand it." — the charge on trial: that the law cannot be kept in human flesh; Christ answered it "with the same facilities that man may obtain" (see "The Second Adam — The Nature Christ Took").
- _Rev. 12:10._ "Now is come salvation... for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night" — courtroom language: Satan is the ACCUSER, and the day-and-night accusation is silenced not by argument but by evidence.
- _Rev. 12:11._ "And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death" — the evidence named: THEY overcame HIM, by the blood and by testimony; the vindication of God is worked out in overcomers.
- _DA 671.3._ White: "The very image of God is to be reproduced in humanity. The honor of God, the honor of Christ, is involved in the perfection of the character of His people." — the stake named plainly: God's own honor rides on the perfected character of His people — the manifold wisdom of _Eph. 3:10_ shown in the last generation.

**"Even as I also overcame" — the whole chain ripens here — Rev. 3:21:**

- _Rev. 3:21._ "To him that overcometh will I grant to sit with me in my throne, even as I also overcame, and am set down with my Father in his throne" — the last promise to the last church: the overcomer's victory is of one kind with Christ's own — same nature, same power, same throne.
- _2SP 39.3._ White: "It was in the order of God that Christ should take upon himself the form and nature of fallen man, that he might be made perfect through suffering, and himself endure the strength of Satan's fierce temptations..." — the ground of "even as I": He overcame in the nature of fallen man, the very flesh in which the accuser said the law could not be kept (see "The Second Adam — The Nature Christ Took").
- _CWCP 84.1._ Jones: "Perfection, perfection of character, is the Christian goal—perfection attained in human flesh in this world. Christ attained it in human flesh in this world and thus made and consecrated a way by which, in Him, every believer can attain it." — the pioneer spine: the harvest is attainable because the Forerunner already ripened in this soil (see "Complete Victory — Overcoming as He Overcame").
- _GC 425.1._ White: "Those who are living upon the earth when the intercession of Christ shall cease in the sanctuary above are to stand in the sight of a holy God without a mediator. Their robes must be spotless, their characters must be purified from sin by the blood of sprinkling.... This work is more clearly presented in the messages of Revelation 14." — the ripe grain and the close of intercession joined — and joined by the witness herself to Revelation 14 (see "The Time of Trouble — Standing Without a Mediator").
- _GC 614.1._ White: "When He leaves the sanctuary, darkness covers the inhabitants of the earth. In that fearful time the righteous must live in the sight of a holy God without an intercessor." — the setting of the final demonstration: the sealed stand faultless while no mediator pleads — the harvest standing ripe in the field after the rain has ceased.
- _COL 415.5._ White: "The last rays of merciful light, the last message of mercy to be given to the world, is a revelation of His character of love. The children of God are to manifest His glory. In their own life and character they are to reveal what the grace of God has done for them." — the message and the demonstration are one thing: the third angel's message ends as a people in whom the character it preached is visible (see "The Commandments of God and the Faith of Jesus").

**DEFINITION — THE HARVEST — A PEOPLE IN WHOM CHRIST IS VINDICATED =** the end of the whole chain is a crop. The kingdom grows as grain grows — first the blade, then the ear, after that the full corn — and "when the fruit is brought forth, immediately he putteth in the sickle, because the harvest is come" (_Mark 4:26-29_): the ripeness of the grain, not an arbitrary date, sets the hour of the sickle (_Rev. 14:15_), and the Husbandman waits with long patience through early and latter rain for "the precious fruit of the earth" (_James 5:7-8_). The ripe fruit is the character of Christ perfectly reproduced in His people (_COL 69.1_), wrought by the Spirit overcoming every hereditary and cultivated tendency to evil (_DA 671.2_). The reaped sheaf is the 144,000 — sealed in their foreheads before the winds break (_Rev. 7:2-4_), settled into the truth so they cannot be moved (_LDE 219.4_), bearing the Father's name, following the Lamb whithersoever He goeth, firstfruits unto God, "and in their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault before the throne of God" (_Rev. 14:1-5_) — the remnant that "shall not do iniquity" (_Zeph. 3:13_). Their faultlessness is His act, not their merit: He is "able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless" (_Jude 1:24_); He presents to Himself a church without spot or wrinkle (_Eph. 5:27_); the bride's readiness is a GRANTED arraying in the righteousness of saints (_Rev. 19:7-8_), Christ's righteousness covering them as a garment (_SSP 250.1_), justification by faith retained through living obedience (_1SM 366.1_). And the demonstration has an audience: by the church the manifold wisdom of God is made known to principalities and powers (_Eph. 3:10_); Satan's ancient charge — that the law cannot be kept in human flesh (_16MR 115.1_) — is answered when a people overcome the accuser "by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony" (_Rev. 12:10-11_), overcoming "even as I also overcame" (_Rev. 3:21_): in the same fallen nature Christ took (_2SP 39.3_), by the way He consecrated in human flesh (_CWCP 84.1_), standing at last without a mediator in spotless robes (_GC 425.1_; _GC 614.1_). Here every thread of the handbook ripens — the living soul in whom the ripening happens ("Dust and Breath — What Man Is"), sin as transgression ("What Sin Is — Transgression, Not Inheritance"), the nature Christ took ("The Second Adam — The Nature Christ Took"), the new birth ("Born of Water and of the Spirit — How He Lived, How We Begin"), salvation from sin ("What Salvation Really Is — Saved From Sin"), the granted robe ("Righteousness by Faith — The Third Angel's Message in Verity"), the overcomer ("Complete Victory — Overcoming as He Overcame"), the judgment-hour message ("The Commandments of God and the Faith of Jesus"), the standing without an intercessor ("The Time of Trouble — Standing Without a Mediator") — for the honor of God is involved in the perfection of the character of His people (_DA 671.3_), and the last message of mercy is His character of love made visible in them (_COL 415.5_). When that fruit is brought forth, immediately He puts in the sickle.

**Symbols defined here:**

- **the harvest / the ripe grain** = the ripened likeness of Christ reproduced in His people — character matured from blade to full corn — which is itself the signal for the sickle; the ripeness of the crop, not an arbitrary date, sets the hour of His coming (_Mark 4:26-29_; _Rev. 14:15_; _James 5:7-8_; _Rev. 19:7-8_; _COL 69.1_).
- **the 144,000 / the remnant** = the last generation — the sealed servants of God, redeemed from among men as firstfruits, following the Lamb whithersoever He goeth, without guile and without fault before the throne, the remnant of Israel that shall not do iniquity nor speak lies; in them the gospel's power is demonstrated and God is vindicated (_Rev. 14:1-5_; _Rev. 7:2-4_; _Zeph. 3:13_; _Eph. 5:27_; _Jude 1:24_; _LDE 219.4_).

**Symbols carried:** the living soul — the whole man in whom this ripening happens (see "Dust and Breath — What Man Is"); sin as transgression, the thing this people has ceased to do (see "What Sin Is — Transgression, Not Inheritance"); the fallen nature Christ took, the flesh in which the vindication is won (see "The Second Adam — The Nature Christ Took"); the new birth by the Spirit, the planting of the grain that ripens here (see "Born of Water and of the Spirit — How He Lived, How We Begin"); salvation as salvation FROM sin, here demonstrated in a people (see "What Salvation Really Is — Saved From Sin"); the granted robe of righteousness by faith (see "Righteousness by Faith — The Third Angel's Message in Verity"); overcoming "even as I also overcame" (see "Complete Victory — Overcoming as He Overcame"); the commandment-keeping, faith-of-Jesus people the message produces (see "The Commandments of God and the Faith of Jesus"); the standing without a mediator, the setting of the final demonstration (see "The Time of Trouble — Standing Without a Mediator").

**For discussion:**

1. _Mark 4:29_ and _Rev. 14:15_ both hang the sickle on one condition — "the harvest of the earth is ripe" — and _COL 69.1_ names the ripeness: the character of Christ "perfectly reproduced in His people." If the crop's maturity, not the calendar, sets the hour, what does the Husbandman's "long patience" (_James 5:7-8_) say about why He tarries — and about what our surrender today has to do with His coming?
2. The 144,000 are "without fault before the throne of God" (_Rev. 14:5_), yet Jude says it is HE who "is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless" (_Jude 1:24_), and the bride's fine linen is "granted" her (_Rev. 19:8_). How does holding both — real sinlessness, and all of it His gift worn "as a garment" (_SSP 250.1_) — keep the call to perfection from collapsing into either presumption or despair?
3. _Eph. 3:10_ says the watching "principalities and powers" learn the manifold wisdom of God "by the church," and _Rev. 12:10-11_ says the accuser is cast down by a people who "overcame him by the blood of the Lamb." When you resist temptation this week, whose reputation is actually on trial (_DA 671.3_) — and how does fighting for God's vindication rather than your own record change the way you fight?
