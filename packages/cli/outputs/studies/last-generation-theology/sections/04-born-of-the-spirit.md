## Born of Water and of the Spirit — How He Lived, How We Begin

> Christ began human life conceived of the Holy Ghost and lived it in unbroken dependence on the indwelling Father — and what He was from His first birth, we become by the second: born of water and of the Spirit, cleansing joined to power, a new creature partaking of the divine nature.

**Conceived of the Holy Ghost — the life begins in the Spirit — Matt. 1:20; Luke 1:35:**

- _Matt. 1:20._ "fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost" — His human life begins IN the Spirit; before a word is spoken or a work done, the Son enters our flesh already inside the Spirit-filled, God-surrendered existence — He never knew any other.
- _Luke 1:35._ "The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God" — mark the two agents of His birth: the Holy Ghost, and the power of the Highest; this exact vocabulary returns upon the church (_Acts 1:8_).
- _1SM 226.2._ White: "In His humanity He was a partaker of the divine nature. In His incarnation He gained in a new sense the title of the Son of God" — in OUR nature (see "The Second Adam — The Nature Christ Took") He was Himself "a partaker of the divine nature" — the very phrase afterward written over the believer (_2 Pet. 1:4_).
- _MWM 302.1._ Millerite conference report (Memoirs of William Miller): the first advent "took place in the days of Herod, when He was conceived of the Holy Ghost, born of the Virgin Mary" — a settled first-advent fact: the Spirit-filled life this section traces starts at His conception.

**From childhood the Father's will was His life — Luke 2:49; Ps. 40:8:**

- _Luke 2:49._ "How is it that ye sought me? wist ye not that I must be about my Father's business?" — twelve years old, and the Father's will is already the "must" of His existence; the surrendered life did not begin at Jordan — it was His from the first.
- _Ps. 40:8._ "I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart" — the Messianic key: the law WITHIN the heart, obedience by delight and not constraint — the very thing the new birth writes in us (_Eze. 36:26-27_).

**How He lived — the Son can do nothing of Himself — John 5:19, 30; 14:10; Heb. 5:8-9; John 6:57; Gal. 2:20:**

- _John 5:19._ "The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do" — the astonishing disclaimer: in the flesh He took, Christ renounces independent power; He lived as man must live — by dependence.
- _John 5:30._ "I can of mine own self do nothing... I seek not mine own will, but the will of the Father which hath sent me" — the confession restated: not His own will but the Father's; the surrendered will is the mechanism of the sinless life.
- _John 14:10._ "the words that I speak unto you I speak not of myself: but the Father that dwelleth in me, he doeth the works" — the works were the Father's, wrought through an indwelt, surrendered man — the very arrangement offered to us.
- _DA 208.2._ White: "So utterly was Christ emptied of self that He made no plans for Himself. He accepted God's plans for Him... So should we depend upon God, that our lives may be the simple outworking of His will" — His "can do nothing of Himself" means total surrender and total dependence — and the sentence ends on us: "so should we."
- _SC 75.2._ White: "The Saviour has said of Himself, 'The Son can do nothing of Himself;' 'the Father that dwelleth in Me, He doeth the works.'... We shall work as He worked; we shall manifest the same spirit" — _John 5:19_ and _14:10_ read as one truth and handed straight to the believer.
- _DA 363.1._ White: "As a man He supplicated the throne of God till His humanity was charged with a heavenly current that should connect humanity with divinity... His experience is to be ours" — the channel named: AS A MAN He drew from God; power came by dependence, prayed for and received — and the paragraph closes by handing that experience over.
- _Heb. 5:8-9._ "Though he were a Son, yet learned he obedience by the things which he suffered; and being made perfect, he became the author of eternal salvation unto all them that obey him" — obedience LEARNED, in suffering flesh: the sinless life was a lived acquisition, not an exercised advantage — and its fruit is salvation "unto all them that obey him."
- _CHR 27.3._ Waggoner: "not only counted as a sinner but actually taking upon Himself sinful nature... 'in all points tempted like as we are, yet without sin'" — the nature was ours (see "The Second Adam — The Nature Christ Took"); the sinlessness was in never consenting — for sin is in the consent, not the flesh (see "What Sin Is — Transgression, Not Inheritance").
- _DA 117.2._ White: "our Saviour took humanity, with all its liabilities. He took the nature of man, with the possibility of yielding to temptation. We have nothing to bear which He has not endured" — therefore His victory was won by no advantage withheld from us; what He used, we may use.
- _John 6:57._ "As the living Father hath sent me, and I live by the Father: so he that eateth me, even he shall live by me" — Christ names His own life-principle and hands it over in the same breath: the dependence He lived by becomes the dependence we live by.
- _Gal. 2:20._ "the life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God" — the mechanism made explicit: the indwelt, surrendered life is lived BY FAITH — and not a faith we manufacture, but "the faith of the Son of God," His own faith imparted.

**What He was by birth, we become by the new birth — John 3:3-7:**

- _John 3:3._ "Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God" — not reform, not resolve: a BIRTH; nothing less can even see the kingdom.
- _John 3:4._ "How can a man be born when he is old? can he enter the second time into his mother's womb, and be born?" — Nicodemus's bewilderment measures the demand: the change required is as total as beginning life over.
- _John 3:5._ "Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God" — the charter of the second birth: water AND Spirit, together, or no entrance.
  - **the new birth (born again / born of water and of the Spirit)** = the Spirit re-creating the whole man — "an entire change of the entire man" (_TDG 48.4_), a new heart and new spirit given (_Eze. 36:26-27_), making a "new creature" (_2 Cor. 5:17_) able to see and enter the kingdom — the second birth that puts a man where Christ stood from His first: inside the Spirit-filled, God-surrendered life (_John 3:5-7_; _Titus 3:5_; _2 Pet. 1:4_).
- _John 3:6._ "That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit" — the flesh reproduces only itself (see "What Sin Is — Transgression, Not Inheritance"); only a second parentage produces a second kind of man.
- _John 3:7._ "Marvel not that I said unto thee, Ye must be born again" — His "must" was the Father's business (_Luke 2:49_); ours is the birth that brings us into it.
- _DA 171.5._ White: "Nicodemus knew that Christ here referred to water baptism and the renewing of the heart by the Spirit of God" — water = baptism; Spirit = the renewed heart; the two are one requirement, not two.
- _TDG 48.4._ White: "Transformation of heart means an entire change of the entire man... it is an inward work, and yet, it is seen, because it works outward from within" — the new birth is total, and it shows.

**The washing — the laver of regeneration — cleansing joined to power — Titus 3:5; Eph. 5:26; Eze. 36:25-27:**

- _Titus 3:5._ "Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us, by the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost" — the two clauses are one act: washing (cleansing) joined to renewing (power), and neither is by works.
  - **the washing / the laver of regeneration (regeneration by water, word, and Spirit)** = the cleansing of regeneration in which washing and power are inseparable — the clean water (_Eze. 36:25_), the word (_Eph. 5:26_), and the renewing Spirit (_Titus 3:5_) are one act, which both removes the filthiness AND puts the Spirit within to cause the cleansed one to walk in God's statutes (_Eze. 36:27_; _Zech. 4:6_).
- _Eph. 5:26._ "That he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word" — the washing water is the word: the cleansing is wrought by water and word and Spirit together (_John 3:5_; _Titus 3:5_).
- _Eze. 36:25._ "Then will I sprinkle clean water upon you, and ye shall be clean: from all your filthiness, and from all your idols, will I cleanse you" — the OT washing-promise: God does the sprinkling and God does the cleansing.
- _Eze. 36:26._ "A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh" — the washing is joined to a transplant: stony heart out, heart of flesh in — the new birth in prophecy.
- _Eze. 36:27._ "And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them" — cleansing (v. 25) is joined to power (v. 27): the indwelling Spirit CAUSES the walk — the delight-obedience Christ had from birth (_Ps. 40:8_) now promised to us.
- _SD 105.3._ White: "All true reformation begins with soul-cleansing. It is by the washing of regeneration and the renewing of the mind through the power of the Holy Spirit, that a change is wrought in the life" — washing and power never separated; a cleansing without the Spirit's renewing is no regeneration.

**Not by might — the power named and offered — Zech. 4:6; Acts 1:8:**

- _Zech. 4:6._ "Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts" — the power-source of the whole re-creation named once for all: human might is expressly ruled out — the Spirit-not-flesh principle by which Christ lived and by which we are re-made.
- _COL 66.1._ White: "As the plant receives the sunshine, the dew, and the rain, we are to open our hearts to the Holy Spirit. The work is to be done 'not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit, saith the Lord of hosts'" — after the birth, growth runs by the same law: receiving, not achieving.
- _Acts 1:8._ "ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me" — hear _Luke 1:35_ again: the Holy Ghost "come upon," the power of the Highest — the overshadowing of His birth becomes the endowment of ours.

**The result — a new creature, partaker of the divine nature — 2 Cor. 5:17; 2 Pet. 1:4:**

- _2 Cor. 5:17._ "Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new" — the finished result: not the old man improved but a new creation, living from the same source Christ lived from.
- _2 Pet. 1:4._ "that by these ye might be partakers of the divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust" — the very phrase _1SM 226.2_ used of Christ's humanity is now written over the believer: what He was, we are made.
- _CWCP 43.4._ Jones: "God sending His own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh... condemned sin in the flesh: that the righteousness of the law might be fulfilled in us, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit... for to make in Himself of twain [God and man] one new man" — the purpose of the incarnation lands here: righteousness fulfilled IN US who walk after the Spirit; the "one new man" is the new creature of _2 Cor. 5:17_ — the ground of "Complete Victory — Overcoming as He Overcame."

**DEFINITION — BORN OF WATER AND OF THE SPIRIT — HOW HE LIVED, HOW WE BEGIN =** Christ's human life began in the Spirit — conceived of the Holy Ghost (_Matt. 1:20_; _MWM 302.1_), "that holy thing" made by the Holy Ghost and the power of the Highest (_Luke 1:35_), Himself in His humanity "a partaker of the divine nature" (_1SM 226.2_) — and it was lived the whole way in surrendered dependence: the Father's business from childhood (_Luke 2:49_), the law within the heart (_Ps. 40:8_), "the Son can do nothing of himself" (_John 5:19, 30_), "the Father that dwelleth in me, he doeth the works" (_John 14:10_; _DA 208.2_; _SC 75.2_; _DA 363.1_), obedience learned in suffering flesh (_Heb. 5:8-9_). He overcame by no power unavailable to us (_DA 117.2_; _CHR 27.3_), and He hands the arrangement over intact: "I live by the Father: so he that eateth me, even he shall live by me" (_John 6:57_) — a life lived thereafter "by the faith of the Son of God" (_Gal. 2:20_). What He was from His first birth we become by the second: born of water and of the Spirit (_John 3:3-7_) — the washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Ghost (_Titus 3:5_), the washing of water by the word (_Eph. 5:26_), the sprinkled clean water, the given heart, and the indwelling Spirit that causes the walk (_Eze. 36:25-27_) — an entire change of the entire man (_TDG 48.4_), cleansing joined to power, not by might nor by power but by His Spirit (_Zech. 4:6_), the Holy Ghost coming upon us as upon Mary (_Luke 1:35_ → _Acts 1:8_). The result is a new creature (_2 Cor. 5:17_), a partaker of the divine nature (_2 Pet. 1:4_; _CWCP 43.4_) — a man begun where Christ began, living from the source Christ lived from, so that overcoming as He overcame (see "Complete Victory — Overcoming as He Overcame") is not imitation from without but the outworking of the same indwelt life.

**Symbols defined here:**

- **the new birth (born again / born of water and of the Spirit)** = the Spirit re-creating the whole man — an entire change of the entire man, a new heart and new spirit given, so that the man becomes a new creature able to see and enter the kingdom; the second birth that puts a man where Christ stood from His first birth, inside the Spirit-filled, God-surrendered life (_John 3:3-7_; _2 Cor. 5:17_; _Eze. 36:26-27_; _Titus 3:5_; _2 Pet. 1:4_; _TDG 48.4_).
- **the washing / the laver of regeneration (regeneration by water, word, and Spirit)** = the cleansing of regeneration in which washing and power are joined and inseparable — water sprinkled clean, the word that sanctifies, and the renewing of the Holy Ghost are one act, removing filthiness AND supplying the indwelling Spirit that causes the cleansed one to walk in God's statutes; not by might or works, but by the Spirit (_Titus 3:5_; _Eph. 5:26_; _Eze. 36:25-27_; _Zech. 4:6_; _SD 105.3_).

**Symbols carried:** the second Adam and sinful flesh — the nature He lived this life in (see "The Second Adam — The Nature Christ Took"); sin as consented transgression and the flesh / fallen nature that reproduces only itself (see "What Sin Is — Transgression, Not Inheritance"); the living soul — the new birth re-creates the WHOLE man because man is one undivided whole (see "Dust and Breath — What Man Is").

**For discussion:**

1. Christ said, "The Son can do nothing of himself" (_John 5:19_) and "I live by the Father" (_John 6:57_). Where in this week's decisions are you still living "of your own self" — and what would it look like, concretely, to live those hours by dependence as He did?
2. Ezekiel 36:25-27 joins the cleansing to the Spirit who "causes" obedience, and Titus 3:5 joins the washing to the renewing. Which half have you been attempting alone — cleansing without power, or power without cleansing — and what does receiving both as ONE act change about how you pray?
3. Jesus was born into the Spirit-filled life; we must be born into it (_John 3:7_). If Acts 1:8 is the believer's birthright and not merely the apostles' history, what would you begin asking God for differently tomorrow morning?
