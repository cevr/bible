## The Seal or the Mark — the Distinction Made Visible

> The war on appointed worship that opened at Cain's altar terminates at the Sabbath: the two classes reach their final, universal, visible form — all the world marked for the beast's self-appointed worship, or sealed in the forehead with the sign of Him that made heaven and earth — and judgment makes the distinction permanent.

**The war on worship goes universal — Rev. 13:3-4:**

- _Rev. 13:3._ "his deadly wound was healed: and all the world wondered after the beast" — the power whose fourth-century church-world union supplanted the Creator's appointed day (the union traced in "The Church in the Wilderness — the Mountains Keep the Faith"; _GC_ chs. 3-4) recovers its dominion, and the wondering is now worldwide. The two classes are about to become visible on a world scale.
- _Rev. 13:4._ "they worshipped the dragon which gave power unto the beast: and they worshipped the beast, saying, Who is like unto the beast? who is able to make war with him?" — the text names the issue itself: WORSHIP. The serpent's seed does not end in mere irreligion but in a rival worship, dragon-empowered, universal — the enmity of the two seeds (see "The Enmity — Two Seeds from One Fall") fought out at the last altar.

**The mark enforced — and the siege of buying and selling — Rev. 13:16-17:**

- _Rev. 13:16._ "he causeth all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads" — the rival worship is ENFORCED on every class of men; the badge of allegiance is set where choice lives (the forehead) or where conduct complies (the hand).
  - **the mark of the beast** = allegiance to the self-appointed worship-system enforced in place of the Creator's appointed worship — the way of Cain full grown, the substitute offering (_Gen. 4:3-5_) become world law; received in forehead or hand (_Rev. 13:16_), set opposite "the commandments of God" (_Rev. 14:9-12_), and enforced by economic siege (_Rev. 13:17_). Defined in full at the close.
- _Rev. 13:17._ "that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark, or the name of the beast, or the number of his name" — the city-system's final weapon against the separate class: the confederated commerce of Babel turned into a siege engine (see "Babel — Congregating for Convenience"; see "The Two Lineages — the City and the Name"). Cain's congregation no longer merely excludes the separate; it starves them. The pilgrims (see "Abraham and Lot — the Tent and the Plain") are pressed toward conformity.
- _CS 59.3._ White: "The time is coming when we cannot sell at any price. The decree will soon go forth prohibiting men to buy or sell of any man save he that hath the mark of the beast" — confirms _Rev. 13:17_ as a literal, coming decree against the class that will not conform.

**The first angel answers — worship Him that MADE — Rev. 14:6-7; Exo. 20:8-11; Gen. 2:1-3:**

- _Rev. 14:7._ "Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come: and worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters" — heaven's counter-summons: worship the CREATOR. The phrase is lifted from the fourth commandment; the angel answers the beast's worship by pointing all the world back to the Creator's own memorial.
- _Exo. 20:8, 10._ "Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy... the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God" — the commandment the first angel echoes; the day is the LORD'S, not man's to appoint.
- _Exo. 20:11._ "For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is... wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it" — the exact ground _Rev. 14:7_ stands on: "made heaven, and earth, and the sea." To worship Him that made is to keep the memorial He appointed for the making.
- _Gen. 2:3._ "And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work which God created and made" — the Sabbath instituted in Eden, before any fall or any nation — one of the three institutions of Eden the serpent's seed has warred on from the beginning (see "The Days of Noah — the Institutions of Eden Under Attack"). The end-time battle over worship is a fight over the original, appointed day.
- _CCh 334.6._ White: "The Sabbath question is to be the issue in the great final conflict in which all the world will act a part... Each sabbath institution bears the name of its author, an ineffaceable mark that shows the authority of each" — each day carries its author's authority: the Creator's appointed Sabbath against the self-chosen day — Abel's appointed offering against Cain's substitute, on a world scale (see "Cain and Abel — the Two Worships").

**God names the Sabbath His SIGN — Exo. 31:13-17; Eze. 20:12, 20:**

- _Exo. 31:13._ "Verily my sabbaths ye shall keep: for it is a sign between me and you throughout your generations; that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you" — God Himself names the Sabbath His SIGN: the mark of the God who sanctifies, set between Him and His people.
- _Exo. 31:17._ "It is a sign... for ever: for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, and on the seventh day he rested, and was refreshed" — the sign is "for ever," and its content is creation — the same credential the first angel proclaims (_Rev. 14:7_).
- _Eze. 20:12._ "I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them" — the sign identifies WHOSE people they are, and that God — not they themselves — sanctifies them.
- _Eze. 20:20._ "And hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign between me and you, that ye may know that I am the LORD your God" — the sign marks those who acknowledge the Creator's authority against every rival: the self-appointed system carries the authority of its own maker.

**The sign set in the forehead is the seal — Rev. 7:2-3; 14:1; Eze. 9:4-6:**

- _Rev. 7:2-3._ "having the seal of the living God... Hurt not the earth, neither the sea, nor the trees, till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads" — the judgment winds are held back until the servants of God are sealed in their FOREHEADS. A sign set in the forehead is a seal; the Sabbath-sign of _Exo. 31_ and _Eze. 20_ is here fixed on the separate class before the plagues fall.
  - **the seal of God** = the Creator's sign of ownership and sanctification set in the forehead — the seventh-day Sabbath kept by faith. Scripture calls the Sabbath God's "sign" that He is the LORD who made heaven and earth and who sanctifies His people (_Exo. 31:13-17_; _Eze. 20:12, 20_); the sign in the forehead is the seal (_Rev. 7:2-3_), and the sealed bear the Father's name (_Rev. 14:1_). Defined in full at the close.
- _Rev. 14:1._ "a Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father's name written in their foreheads" — the sealed bear the Father's NAME — His character and authority — the exact opposite of the beast's name and mark (_Rev. 13:17_).
- _Eze. 9:4._ "set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations that be done in the midst thereof" — the Old Testament type of the sealing: its mark — the seal-type — falls on the MOURNERS over abomination, not on the comfortable majority of the professed people.
- _Eze. 9:6._ "but come not near any man upon whom is the mark; and begin at my sanctuary" — the slaying spares only the marked, and it begins at the sanctuary: the distinction is drawn inside the professed church first, then made permanent by judgment — the flood-pattern of the two classes repeated (see "As in the Days of Noah — the Last Mingling").
- _SSP 132.2._ Haskell: "this knowledge of God, is the seal placed in the foreheads of the chosen generation... this, Jehovah has placed in His Sabbath... The seal placed in the forehead by the angel, cannot be read by man; only God and heavenly beings can read it. For this reason, no civil law can enforce Sabbath keeping. Man may keep the form one day in seven, but only a knowledge of God can give the seal in the forehead" — the pioneer exposition binding _Rev. 7_ to the Sabbath-sign of _Exo. 31_ and _Eze. 20_: the seal is a knowledge of God, and precisely because it is inward, no decree can counterfeit it — only the mark can be enforced by law.

**The third angel names the two classes — Rev. 14:9-12:**

- _Rev. 14:9-10._ "If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark in his forehead, or in his hand, The same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God" — the third angel's warning defines the mark by its object: WORSHIP of the beast and his image — allegiance to the self-appointed system enforced in place of the Creator's appointed worship. The way of Cain (see "Cain and Abel — the Two Worships") stands full grown: a substitute worship, now persecuting the true.
- _Rev. 14:12._ "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" — the sealed class named against the mark-bearers: commandment-keepers (the Sabbath memorial included) holding the faith of Jesus — Abel's line in its final, visible form.
- _TMR 112.1._ Andrews: "the worship of the beast and his image, and the reception of his mark, is the opposite of keeping the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus. And we have seen that one of the commandments of God has been superseded by an institution of the Romish apostasy" — the pioneer statement of the antithesis: the mark is the supplanting of the Sabbath commandment itself.
- _GC 605.2._ White: "The Sabbath will be the great test of loyalty... While one class, by accepting the sign of submission to earthly powers, receive the mark of the beast, the other, choosing the token of allegiance to divine authority, receive the seal of God" — the master statement: the Sabbath is the final test that draws the visible line between the two classes.
- _8T 117.4._ White: "The sign, or seal, of God is revealed in the observance of the seventh-day Sabbath, the Lord's memorial of creation.... The mark of the beast is the opposite of this—the observance of the first day of the week. This mark distinguishes those who acknowledge the supremacy of the papal authority from those who acknowledge the authority of God" (compiled in _LDE 224.3_) — seal = the Creator's memorial; mark = its opposite: the badge distinguishing the two allegiances.
- _Ev 235.2._ White: "When you obey the decree that commands you to cease from labor on Sunday and worship God, while you know that there is not a word in the Bible showing Sunday to be other than a common working day, you consent to receive the mark of the beast, and refuse the seal of God" — the mark is received KNOWINGLY: informed submission to the enforced substitute over the Creator's appointed day.
- _SR 383.3._ White: "In the issue of the great contest, two distinct, opposite classes are developed. One class 'worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark'... The other class, in marked contrast to the world, 'keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus'" — the two-classes thesis of the whole study stated in its final-conflict form.

**The door shuts — the distinction fixed forever — Rev. 22:11:**

- _Rev. 22:11._ "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... and he that is holy, let him be holy still" — probation closes and the two classes are fixed forever. As at the flood the LORD shut the door and judgment fixed the separation the mingled world had blurred (see "As in the Days of Noah — the Last Mingling"), so the distinction made visible by seal and mark is made permanent by judgment. After this word there is no third class and no crossing over.

**DEFINITION — THE SEAL OR THE MARK — THE DISTINCTION MADE VISIBLE =** the final, universal form of the two classes, drawn at the point of appointed worship. The healed beast carries the war on worship to all the world (_Rev. 13:3-4_) and enforces a mark — allegiance to a self-appointed worship-system, the way of Cain full grown — on forehead and hand, with an economic siege against all who refuse (_Rev. 13:16-17_). Heaven answers by the first angel's summons to "worship him that made heaven, and earth" (_Rev. 14:7_), the very language of the fourth commandment (_Exo. 20:8-11_), pointing to the memorial planted in Eden before any fall or nation (_Gen. 2:1-3_). God has already named that memorial His SIGN — the sign that He is the LORD who made all and who sanctifies His people (_Exo. 31:13-17_; _Eze. 20:12, 20_) — and a sign set in the forehead is a seal: the seal of the living God fixed on His servants before the winds are loosed (_Rev. 7:2-3_), the Father's name written within (_Rev. 14:1_), typed by the mark set on the men that sigh and cry over abomination before the slaying begins at the sanctuary (_Eze. 9:4-6_). The third angel names the two companies plainly: mark-bearers who worship the beast, and saints who "keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (_Rev. 14:9-12_; _TMR 112.1_) — and the testimony confirms what the verses built: "The Sabbath will be the great test of loyalty," one class receiving the mark of the beast, the other the seal of God (_GC 605.2_; _8T 117.4_; _Ev 235.2_), a knowledge of God no civil law can enforce (_SSP 132.2_). Then the door shuts: "he that is holy, let him be holy still" (_Rev. 22:11_) — the distinction, made visible at last, is made permanent by judgment. Two altars, two names in the forehead, two classes — and after the sealing, no third.

**Symbols defined here:**

- **the seal of God** = the Creator's sign of ownership and sanctification set in the forehead — the seventh-day Sabbath kept by faith; God's own "sign" that He is the LORD who made heaven and earth and who sanctifies His people, marking the separate class before the judgment falls (_Rev. 7:2-3_; _Rev. 14:1_; _Exo. 31:13-17_; _Exo. 20:8-11_; _Gen. 2:1-3_; _Eze. 20:12, 20_; _Eze. 9:4_).
- **the mark of the beast** = allegiance to the self-appointed worship-system enforced in place of the Creator's appointed worship — the way of Cain grown to world scale; received in forehead or hand, set opposite "the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus," and enforced by the decree that "no man might buy or sell" (_Rev. 13:16-17_; _Rev. 14:9-11_; _Rev. 14:12_; _Rev. 13:3-4_; _Rev. 14:7_).

**Symbols carried:** the way of Cain and the two offerings — the self-chosen worship against the appointed lamb, here full grown into the mark (see "Cain and Abel — the Two Worships"); the enmity and the two seeds, now in their last, visible array (see "The Enmity — Two Seeds from One Fall"); the institutions of Eden — the Sabbath of _Gen. 2:1-3_, the last of the three under attack (see "The Days of Noah — the Institutions of Eden Under Attack"); the city-system whose confederated commerce becomes the buy-or-sell siege (see "Babel — Congregating for Convenience"; see "The Two Lineages — the City and the Name"); pilgrims and strangers, the class the siege is aimed at (see "Abraham and Lot — the Tent and the Plain"); the days of the Son of man, the heedless age in which the sealing quietly closes and whose mingling judgment ends (see "As in the Days of Noah — the Last Mingling"); the church-world union that supplanted the appointed day (see "The Church in the Wilderness — the Mountains Keep the Faith"). The summons that gathers the sealed out of Babylon before the plagues is the study's close (see "Come Out, My People — the Pilgrim Call").

**For discussion:**

1. The seal and the mark are both set in the FOREHEAD (_Rev. 7:3_; _Rev. 13:16_) — but only the mark may also go in the HAND (_Rev. 13:16_). What does that difference teach about the two allegiances — and why can "no civil law can enforce Sabbath keeping" (_SSP 132.2_) while a decree CAN produce compliance of the hand, knowingly given (_Ev 235.2_)?
2. Ezekiel's type puts its mark — the seal-type — on "the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations" (_Eze. 9:4_), and the slaying "begin[s] at my sanctuary" (_Eze. 9:6_). If the sealing distinguishes mourners from the comfortable INSIDE the professed people of God, what am I sighing and crying over now — and what am I comfortable with?
3. The mark is received by those who obey the decree "while you know that there is not a word in the Bible" behind it (_Ev 235.2_), and the siege says "no man might buy or sell" without it (_Rev. 13:17_). What would it cost you, concretely — income, standing, security — to stand with them "that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (_Rev. 14:12_), and what are you doing now so that the cost is already counted before _Rev. 22:11_ fixes the classes forever?
