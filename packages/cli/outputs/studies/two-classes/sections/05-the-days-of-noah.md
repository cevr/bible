## The Days of Noah — the Institutions of Eden Under Attack

> Eden gave three gifts before sin — marriage, the appointed diet, and the Sabbath — and by Noah's day the serpent's seed had struck the first two openly and corrupted worship at its root; the flood found a world wholly absorbed in the lawful, and one household that did all God commanded.

**The three gifts before sin — the institutions of Eden — Gen. 2:24; 1:29; 3:18; 2:1-3:**

- _Gen. 2:24._ "Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh" — the FIRST institution of Eden: marriage, spoken before sin; one man, one woman, one flesh. This is the marriage law the serpent's seed will attack.
  - **the institutions of Eden** = the three gifts God established before sin, each rooted in Eden: MARRIAGE (_Gen. 2:24_, one man and one woman made one flesh), the APPOINTED DIET (_Gen. 1:29_, the seed-bearing herbs, fruits, and grains given for meat), and the SABBATH (_Gen. 2:1-3_, the seventh day blessed and sanctified). All three predate the fall; all three are assaulted by the serpent's seed running down through Cain's line — polygamy strikes marriage (_Gen. 4:19_; _Gen. 6:2_), indulged appetite strikes the diet (_Matt. 24:38_; _Luke 17:27_), and self-appointed worship strikes the appointed worship (_Gen. 4:3-5_; see "Cain and Abel — the Two Worships"), the last war meeting the Sabbath at the end (Rev. 13-14). Defined in full at the close.
- _Gen. 1:29._ "Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat" — the SECOND gift, touching appetite: the original diet. Fruits, grains, herbs, the seed-bearing bounty; flesh is conspicuously absent from the grant.
  - **the original diet** = the _Gen. 1:29_ grant — "every herb bearing seed... and every tree... yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat" — fruits, grains, and (after the fall) the herb of the field (_Gen. 3:18_). Flesh was NOT granted in Eden and was not granted until after the flood (_Gen. 9:3_); therefore through the whole antediluvian age the vegetable grant still stood, and the days-of-Noah eating and drinking was appetite indulged against, not within, God's appointed diet. Defined in full at the close.
- _Gen. 3:18._ "Thorns also and thistles shall it bring forth to thee; and thou shalt eat the herb of the field" — after the fall the diet is enlarged to "the herb of the field" (vegetables), but still no flesh; the vegetable grant of _Gen. 1:29_ continues even under the curse.
- _Gen. 2:1-3._ "And on the seventh day God ended his work which he had made; and he rested on the seventh day... And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it" — the THIRD institution: the Sabbath, blessed and sanctified before sin — God's own memorial of creation, the appointed rest and the appointed worship.
- _AH 340.4._ White: "He referred them to the blessed days of Eden when God pronounced all things 'very good.' Then marriage and the Sabbath had their origin, twin institutions for the glory of God in the benefit of humanity" — names two of the three outright: marriage and the Sabbath, "twin institutions," born in Eden before sin, corroborating _Gen. 2:24_ and _Gen. 2:1-3_.
- _Ed 250.2._ White: "The Sabbath and the family were alike instituted in Eden, and in God's purpose they are indissolubly linked together. On this day more than on any other, it is possible for us to live the life of Eden" — Sabbath and family "indissolubly linked," both Eden institutions; to keep them is to live the life of Eden.
- _CD 81.2._ White: "Grains, fruits, nuts, and vegetables constitute the diet chosen for us by our Creator" — the diet confirmed: the Creator's chosen table is the _Gen. 1:29_ grant, flesh absent — the last of the three gifts to receive its witness.

**The first blow — marriage struck in Cain's line, then in Seth's — Gen. 4:19; 6:2; Luke 3:38; Deut. 14:1:**

- _Gen. 4:19._ "And Lamech took unto him two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the other Zillah" — the serpent's seed strikes marriage first: Lamech of Cain's line is Scripture's first polygamist, breaking the one-flesh law of _Gen. 2:24_. The attack begins in the city-building line (see "The Two Lineages — the City and the Name").
- _Gen. 6:2._ "That the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives of all which they chose" — the attack crosses into the covenant line: marriage debased into appetite, choosing by the eye, "all which they chose." The two classes merge (the mingling itself is walked in "The Mingling — How the Peculiar People Lost Their Peculiarity").
- _Luke 3:38._ "Which was the son of Enos, which was the son of Seth, which was the son of Adam, which was the son of God" — Scripture's own key to _Gen. 6:2_: the "sons of God" are the line of Seth, traced through Enos to Adam who "was the son of God." The Bible defines its own figure — the covenant line, not angels.
- _Deut. 14:1._ "Ye are the children of the LORD your God" — the idiom confirmed by usage: God's covenant people are His sons; "sons of God" names the faithful line, sealing the Sethite reading of _Gen. 6:2_.
- _PP 81.2._ White: "For some time the two classes remained separate... So long as this separation continued, they maintained the worship of God in its purity. But in the lapse of time they ventured, little by little, to mingle... Mingling with the depraved, they became like them in spirit and in deeds; the restrictions of the seventh commandment were disregarded, 'and they took them wives of all which they chose'" — the confirming witness names "the two classes," quotes _Gen. 6:2_ as the covenant line's fall through intermarriage, and states outright that the seventh commandment was disregarded: Lamech's plurality now the practice of the professed people of God, the first institution debased and the distinction dissolved with it.

**The second blow — appetite made the horizon of life — Matt. 24:38-39; Luke 17:27; Gen. 9:3:**

- _Matt. 24:38._ "For as in the days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark" — Christ names the mark of the heedless class: not scandalous crime but lawful things — eating, drinking, marrying — made the whole of life; appetite made the horizon.
  - **eating and drinking** = Christ's mark of the heedless class before the flood: lawful appetite made the whole of life, so that the ordinary swallowed the eternal and men "knew not" until judgment fell (_Matt. 24:38-39_; _Luke 17:27_) — the sign of a world that has erased the distinction between the holy and the common. Defined in full at the close.
- _Luke 17:27._ "They did eat, they drank, they married wives, they were given in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark, and the flood came, and destroyed them all" — Luke piles the verbs: eat, drank, married — the tempo of a life wholly given to the permitted, right up to the shut door. The same class Christ warns will be present at His coming (see "As in the Days of Noah — the Last Mingling").
- _Gen. 9:3._ "Every moving thing that liveth shall be meat for you; even as the green herb have I given you all things" — the proof-text of the whole appetite argument: only AFTER the flood is flesh granted. Through the entire antediluvian age the _Gen. 1:29_ grant still stood; the world "did eat, they drank" in violation of, not permission for, the original diet.
- _3SM 289.1._ White: "The flesh of dead animals was not the original food for man. Man was permitted to eat it after the Flood because all vegetation had been destroyed" — confirms the grant-boundary exactly as _Gen. 1:29_ against _Gen. 9:3_: flesh was no part of the Eden table, and the permission is post-flood.
- _DA 636.2._ White: "The crisis is stealing gradually upon us... Men are still eating and drinking, planting and building, marrying, and giving in marriage... yet probation's hour is fast closing" — the heedless-class pattern of _Matt. 24:38_ carried to the end: the same absorption in the lawful while the door swings shut.

**The third blow — appointed worship: the way of Cain runs to the end:**

- The war on the third institution was already open when Cain brought his self-appointed offering and God refused it (_Gen. 4:3-5_ — the ground walked in "Cain and Abel — the Two Worships"). Scripture records no antediluvian Sabbath-breaking, and none is claimed here; what it records is the principle — worship on man's terms against worship on God's terms — and that war does not close with the flood. It runs down the whole controversy and meets the Sabbath of _Gen. 2:1-3_ at the end, where the appointed rest becomes the visible line between the two classes (Rev. 13-14; see "The Seal or the Mark — the Distinction Made Visible").

**The fruit of the threefold assault — one apostate mass — Gen. 6:5:**

- _Gen. 6:5._ "And GOD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually" — the fruit of the threefold assault: with marriage debased, appetite enthroned, and worship self-appointed, the whole earth becomes one apostate mass — save one household.

**Noah — the distinction kept, and remade by God's own hand — 2 Pet. 2:5; Gen. 6:3; Heb. 11:7; Gen. 6:22; Matt. 24:39; Gen. 7:16:**

- _2 Pet. 2:5._ "And spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly" — the one preserved witness: "a preacher of righteousness," the peculiar line that would not be absorbed. Eighth person: the remnant few against "the world of the ungodly."
- _Gen. 6:3._ "My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also is flesh: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years" — Scripture dates the window itself: 120 years of striving Spirit before the judgment, the measured probation of the mingled world.
- _4T 308.3._ White: "Noah preached to the people of his time that God would give them one hundred and twenty years in which to repent of their sins and find refuge in the ark, but they refused the gracious invitation" — the _Gen. 6:3_ window preached: 120 years of warning refused, so the world's "knew not" (_Matt. 24:39_) was willful; they had Noah's preaching and rejected it.
- _Heb. 11:7._ "By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith" — the ark itself, built by faith, "condemned the world": Noah's obedience redrew the line the world had blurred, and his righteousness is the righteousness of faith.
- _Gen. 6:22._ "Thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded him, so did he" — the obedience that condemned the world stated plainly: ALL that God commanded. The peculiar people are marked by keeping the commandments the mingled world neglected.
- _Matt. 24:39._ "And knew not until the flood came, and took them all away; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" — the mingled world's ignorance is willful ignorance; the judgment remakes the distinction they had erased. Christ fixes the pattern on the last day.
- _Gen. 7:16._ "And they that went in, went in male and female of all flesh, as God had commanded him: and the LORD shut him in" — "the LORD shut him in": the distinction the mingling dissolved is remade by God's own hand at the judgment. Probation closes by divine act, not human choice — the shut-in faithful, the shut-out world.
- _EP 55.4._ White: "Noah and his household were within the ark, 'and the Lord shut him in.' The massive door, impossible for those within to close, was slowly swung to its place by unseen hands. Noah was shut in and the rejecters of God's mercy were shut out" — confirms _Gen. 7:16_ as the close of probation dividing the two classes: one door, two sides, God's hand on it.
- _EW 284.1._ White: "When the rain descended and the flood came, Noah and his family had entered the ark, and God had shut them in... God would not suffer the wicked to destroy those who... would not bow to the decree of the beast or receive his mark" — the inspired bridge to the end: the shut-in distinction of Noah's day is the very deliverance of those who refuse the beast's decree — the last mingling and the last separation (see "As in the Days of Noah — the Last Mingling"; "The Seal or the Mark — the Distinction Made Visible").

**DEFINITION — THE DAYS OF NOAH — THE INSTITUTIONS OF EDEN UNDER ATTACK =** before sin God gave three gifts, the institutions of Eden: marriage — one man, one woman, one flesh (_Gen. 2:24_); the appointed diet — the seed-bearing herbs, fruits, and grains, flesh absent (_Gen. 1:29_; enlarged to the herb of the field after the fall, _Gen. 3:18_); and the Sabbath — the seventh day blessed and sanctified (_Gen. 2:1-3_) — marriage and the Sabbath "twin institutions" born in Eden (_AH 340.4_), Sabbath and family "indissolubly linked" (_Ed 250.2_), the Creator's chosen diet confirmed (_CD 81.2_). By Noah's day the serpent's seed had struck them in order: marriage by Lamech's polygamy in Cain's line (_Gen. 4:19_) and then by the covenant line itself — the sons of God, the line of Seth (_Luke 3:38_; _Deut. 14:1_), taking "wives of all which they chose" while "the restrictions of the seventh commandment were disregarded" (_Gen. 6:2_; _PP 81.2_); appetite by eating and drinking made the whole of life (_Matt. 24:38_; _Luke 17:27_) — and since flesh was not granted until after the flood (_Gen. 9:3_; _3SM 289.1_), the antediluvian table itself ran against the still-standing grant of Eden; and appointed worship by the way of Cain's self-chosen offering (_Gen. 4:3-5_) — worship on man's terms against worship on God's terms — a war that meets the Sabbath only at the end (Rev. 13-14). The fruit was total: "every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually" (_Gen. 6:5_) — save Noah, "a preacher of righteousness" (_2 Pet. 2:5_) through the 120-year window God Himself had set (_Gen. 6:3_; _4T 308.3_), whose ark-building faith "condemned the world" (_Heb. 11:7_) because he did "according to all that God commanded him" (_Gen. 6:22_). Then the distinction the mingling had erased was remade by God's own hand: "the LORD shut him in" (_Gen. 7:16_; _EP 55.4_) while the world that "knew not" was taken away (_Matt. 24:39_) — and Christ stakes the last age on the same pattern, when those who will not bow to the beast's decree are shut in as Noah was (_EW 284.1_): "so shall also the coming of the Son of man be."

**Symbols defined here:**

- **the institutions of Eden** = the three gifts God established before sin: MARRIAGE (one man and one woman made one flesh), the APPOINTED DIET (the seed-bearing bounty given for meat), and the SABBATH (the seventh day blessed and sanctified) — all three predating the fall, all three assaulted by the serpent's seed: polygamy against marriage, indulgence against the diet, self-appointed worship against the appointed, the last war ending at the Sabbath (_Gen. 2:24_; _Gen. 1:29_; _Gen. 2:1-3_; _Gen. 4:19_; _Gen. 6:2_).
- **the original diet** = the _Gen. 1:29_ grant — fruits, grains, herbs, and after the fall the herb of the field — with flesh not granted until after the flood, so that the vegetable grant stood through the whole antediluvian age and the days-of-Noah appetite ran against, not within, God's appointment (_Gen. 1:29_; _Gen. 3:18_; _Gen. 9:3_).
- **eating and drinking** = Christ's mark of the heedless class before the flood: lawful appetite — eating, drinking, marrying — made the whole of life, so that the ordinary swallowed the eternal and men "knew not" until judgment fell; not gross crime but total absorption in the permitted, the sign of a world that has erased the distinction between the holy and the common (_Matt. 24:38-39_; _Luke 17:27_).

**Symbols carried:** the sons of God, the daughters of men, and the mingling — the alliance by which the peculiar people lost their peculiarity (see "The Mingling — How the Peculiar People Lost Their Peculiarity"); Cain's offering and the way of Cain — the first war on appointed worship, whose antediluvian record is the altar, not the Sabbath (see "Cain and Abel — the Two Worships"); the city of Cain and Lamech's polygamy — the civilization that carried the attack (see "The Two Lineages — the City and the Name"); the two seeds and the God-put enmity the mingling dissolved (see "The Enmity — Two Seeds from One Fall"). Forward: Christ's days-of-Noah pattern for the last generation (see "As in the Days of Noah — the Last Mingling") and the worship war's terminus at seal versus mark (see "The Seal or the Mark — the Distinction Made Visible").

**For discussion:**

1. Every count against the antediluvian world in _Matt. 24:38_ is a LAWFUL thing — "eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage" — yet "the flood came, and took them all away." How does the permitted become the destroyer, and where in your own week has a lawful thing quietly become the whole of life?
2. Flesh was not granted until after the flood (_Gen. 9:3_), so the antediluvians' indulgence ran against a standing grant of God (_Gen. 1:29_). Where do we treat God's later concessions as if they erased His original appointments — in appetite, in marriage, in rest — and what would returning toward "the life of Eden" (_Ed 250.2_) cost this week?
3. Noah "condemned the world" not by argument but by obedience — an ark built to "all that God commanded him" (_Heb. 11:7_; _Gen. 6:22_) — and then "the LORD shut him in" (_Gen. 7:16_). If the distinction between the two classes is finally remade by God's hand and not ours, what is our part now, before the door — and what was the world's part during Noah's 120 years of warning?
