## The Two Lineages — the City and the Name

> The two worships become two civilizations: Cain's line goes out from the Presence and builds a city; Seth's line stays and calls upon the name of the LORD — and from Genesis 4 to Revelation the war of the two classes runs on between the city and the Name.

**From the altar to the city — the worship-war becomes a civilization — Gen. 4:3, 5, 16-17:**

- _Gen. 4:3._ "Cain brought of the fruit of the ground an offering unto the LORD" — the self-appointed offering: worship on man's own terms, the fruit of the cursed ground and his own labor, refusing the blood (the first false worship, defined in "Cain and Abel — the Two Worships"). The city-line's religion begins at this altar.
- _Gen. 4:5._ "But unto Cain and to his offering he had not respect. And Cain was very wroth, and his countenance fell" — God's non-respect for the self-willed offering ignites the wrath that becomes murder (v. 8) and then a civilization (v. 17). The two lineages divide first at the altar, then spread into two peoples.
- _COL 152.1._ White: "The Pharisee and the publican represent two great classes... Their first two representatives are found in the first two children that were born into the world" — the perennial two classes of worshipers are rooted in the two offerings; what Genesis 4 now traces is those two worships hardening into two lineages.
- _Gen. 4:16._ "And Cain went out from the presence of the LORD, and dwelt in the land of Nod, on the east of Eden" — the city-line begins with a departure. Everything Cain builds afterward is built by a fugitive from the face of God; he settles away from the Presence, and the geography of the two classes is born here.
- _Gen. 4:17._ "he builded a city, and called the name of the city, after the name of his son, Enoch" — the first city in Scripture: built by the first murderer, self-named and self-perpetuating. Cain, cut off from the Name, makes a name.
  - **the city of Cain** = the serpent-seed's congregation — fortified convenience built by the fugitive from God's presence: man staying put in the cursed earth, distinguished in arts, industry, and violence-as-culture while God is forgotten; the civilization whose spirit prevails in the eating-drinking-marrying world Christ names as the type of the last age (_Gen. 4:16-17_; _Gen. 4:21-23_; _Matt. 24:38_; _PP 81.1_).
- _PP 81.1._ White: "He had gone out from the presence of the Lord, cast away the promise of the restored Eden, to seek his possessions and enjoyment in the earth under the curse of sin, thus standing at the head of that great class of men who worship the god of this world" — Cain heads a CLASS, not merely a family: the worshipers of the god of this world, seeking their portion in the earth under the curse.

**The city's culture — the home broken, the arts advancing, violence sung — Gen. 4:19-24; Gen. 2:24; Matt. 19:4-8:**

- _Gen. 4:19._ "And Lamech took unto him two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the other Zillah" — the first polygamist, fifth in Cain's line. The serpent-seed's assault on the FIRST institution of Eden — marriage — is now culture, not lapse.
- _Gen. 2:24._ "Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh" — the Eden charter Lamech breaks: one man, one wife, one flesh. The war of the two lineages runs through the home, not merely the altar.
- _Matt. 19:4._ "he which made them at the beginning made them male and female" — Christ roots marriage in creation; the Cainite innovation is a departure from the order the Maker fixed.
- _Matt. 19:6._ "What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder" — Lamech's two wives put asunder what God joined: the city-line remaking the home to suit itself.
- _Matt. 19:8._ "from the beginning it was not so" — Christ's rule against every corruption of marriage back to Eden; the Cainite polygamy is judged by the original, unbroken pattern.
- _Gen. 4:21._ "he was the father of all such as handle the harp and organ" — Jubal: music and culture flourish inside the city of Cain — refinement without repentance, arts advancing while God is forgotten.
- _Gen. 4:22._ "Tubalcain, an instructer of every artificer in brass and iron" — metallurgy and industry, the technological genius of the Cainite city. The line godless in worship is distinguished in earthly and material progress.
- _PP 81.1._ White: "In that which pertains to mere earthly and material progress, his descendants became distinguished... To the crime of murder, in which Cain had led the way, Lamech, the fifth in descent, added polygamy" — the two marks of the city named together: material brilliance, and the broken home.
- _Gen. 4:23._ "for I have slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt" — Lamech's sword-song: violence set to poetry, boasted openly to his two wives. Cain's murder has become the city's culture; bloodshed is now sung, not hidden.
- _Gen. 4:24._ "If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamech seventy and sevenfold" — God's protective word over Cain twisted into a boast of self-vengeance. The city-line's religion is self-serving even where it names God.

**The Name — the faithful seed re-established — Gen. 4:25-26; Luke 3:38:**

- _Gen. 4:25._ "For God, said she, hath appointed me another seed instead of Abel, whom Cain slew" — the faithful seed re-established in Seth: "another seed" in the room of the martyr. The two lineages are now formally set — the line of the murderer and the line of the appointed seed.
- _Luke 3:38._ "Which was the son of Enos, which was the son of Seth, which was the son of Adam, which was the son of God" — the genealogy of Christ runs through Enos and Seth to "the son of God": the Sethite stream is the covenant line, the lineage of the promised Seed of "The Enmity — Two Seeds from One Fall."
- _Gen. 4:26._ "then began men to call upon the name of the LORD" — the dividing act of the faithful seed. Cain named a city after his son; Seth's line calls on the Name of the LORD. Not a wall but a confessed allegiance marks off the faithful.
  - **calling on the name of the LORD** = the dividing act of the faithful seed — open, avowed worship: a public profession of loyalty to God that marks off the Sethite line from the city; its living crown is Enoch, who walked with God in the corrupt age and preached judgment to the very city of Cain (_Gen. 4:26_; _Gen. 5:24_; _Jude 1:14-15_).
- _PP 80.2._ White: "The faithful had worshiped God before; but as men increased, the distinction between the two classes became more marked. There was an open profession of loyalty to God on the part of one, as there was of contempt and disobedience on the part of the other" — the definition confirmed: what began at Enos was not worship itself but MARKED worship, the open profession that makes the two classes visible.

**The crown of the calling line — Enoch, the seventh from Adam — Gen. 5:22, 24; Jude 1:14-15:**

- _Gen. 5:22._ "And Enoch walked with God after he begat Methuselah three hundred years, and begat sons and daughters" — the walk was not a hermit's trance but the whole tenor of a working life: husband, father, citizen, three hundred years of communion in the heart of a corrupt age.
- _PP 85.1._ White: "Enoch's walk with God was not in a trance or vision, but in all the duties of his daily life... as a husband and father, a friend, a citizen, he was the steadfast, unwavering servant of the Lord" — calling on the Name embodied: open, avowed faithfulness lived out in the ordinary offices of life.
- _GW 52.1._ White: "In the midst of a life of active labor, Enoch steadfastly maintained his communion with God... he would withdraw, to spend a season in solitude" — Enoch's rhythm of withdrawal and return is the personal form of the Sethite separation: apart to commune, back to labor.
- _GW 51.2._ White: "The infinite, unfathomable love of God through Christ, became the subject of his meditations day and night. With all the fervor of his soul he sought to reveal that love to the people among whom he dwelt" — the inner spring of the walk: communion kept apart, then poured out toward the people — separation that exists for testimony, not escape.
- _Gen. 5:24._ "And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him" — the crown of the Sethite stream: the man who held the upward current while the world flowed the other way is translated. Proof that the calling-on-the-Name line can be kept pure in the midst of the city's age.
- _Jude 1:14._ "And Enoch also, the seventh from Adam, prophesied of these, saying, Behold, the Lord cometh with ten thousands of his saints" — the New Testament fixes Enoch as the seventh from Adam and the first advent preacher: the faithful line's witness is judgment-preaching from the beginning.
- _MWV2 17.3._ Miller: "Jude tells us that Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied, saying, Behold the Lord cometh, with ten thousands of his saints" — the pioneer of the advent movement stands Enoch at the head of his own line of evidence: the second-advent hope is as old as the seventh from Adam.
- _Jude 1:15._ "To execute judgment upon all, and to convince all that are ungodly among them" — Enoch's message was a warning of judgment on the ungodly city-line: the faithful seed does not merely withdraw; it testifies against the civilization it separates from.
- _PP 86.1._ White: "His labors were not restricted to the Sethites. In the land where Cain had sought to flee from the divine Presence, the prophet of God made known the wonderful scenes that had passed before his vision" — separation is not silence: the mountain-line carried the warning down into the very land of Cain's flight.

**Mountains and plains — the geography of the two classes — Gen. 4:16; 6:1-2:**

- **mountains and plains** = the geography of the two classes: the Cainites, spreading from their first settlement away from the Presence, dispersed over the plains and valleys; the Sethites, to escape their contaminating influence, withdrew to the mountains — God's people dwelling apart, the serpent's seed congregating for convenience. This is the geography of SEPARATION, not of appointed worship — worship is "in spirit and in truth" wherever the faithful are (_John 4:21-24_); the mountain is where the peculiar character is kept, not where God is uniquely found. Scripture gives the pattern its own voice in the angel's word to Lot, "Escape to the mountain, lest thou be consumed" (_Gen. 19:17_; taken up in "Abraham and Lot — the Tent and the Plain") (_Gen. 4:16_; _Gen. 6:1_; _Gen. 6:2_; _Gen. 19:17_).
- _PP 81.2._ White: "For some time the two classes remained separate. The race of Cain... dispersed over the plains and valleys where the children of Seth had dwelt; and the latter, in order to escape from their contaminating influence, withdrew to the mountains, and there made their home. So long as this separation continued, they maintained the worship of God in its purity" — THE ANCHOR: "the two classes" named outright, the mountain/plain geography given exactly, and the doctrinal weight stated — separation preserved pure worship.
- _Gen. 6:1._ "when men began to multiply on the face of the earth, and daughters were born unto them" — the setting for the fatal breakdown of the separation the mountains had kept.
- _Gen. 6:2._ "the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives of all which they chose" — the sons of God = the covenant line of Seth (_Luke 3:38_), not angels; the descent from the mountains to marry the city dissolves the peculiar character of the faithful (walked in full in "The Mingling — How the Peculiar People Lost Their Peculiarity").
- _Gen. 6:5._ "every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually" — the moral fruit of the merged lineages: when the two classes mingle, the Cainite spirit prevails, and the whole earth is condemned.
- _GC 65.3._ White: "Behind the lofty bulwarks of the mountains—in all ages the refuge of the persecuted and oppressed—the Waldenses found a hiding place. Here the light of truth was kept burning amid the darkness of the Middle Ages" — the geography recurs through history: the mountains keep the faith while the plains keep the world (traced in "The Church in the Wilderness — the Mountains Keep the Faith").

**The end-time antitype — the city civilization named for the last days — Matt. 24:38:**

- _Matt. 24:38._ "For as in the days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark" — Christ names the world the Cainite city-spirit conquered as the type of the last age. Note what damns it: not open atheism but appetite and self-indulgent normalcy — eating, drinking, marrying — the city of Cain's spirit running at full culture until the door shuts.
- _DA 633.1._ White: "Christ does not here bring to view a temporal millennium... He tells us that as it was in Noah's day, so will it be when the Son of man comes again" — the two-lineages pattern is prophetically appointed, not merely historical: the last generation replays the city and the Name.
- _TTAM 6.1._ J. White: "In the third angel's message... but two classes are brought to view. One is oppressive, and persecutes the saints, and has the mark of the beast... The other class is oppressed and driven... and are sealed with the seal of the living God by keeping 'the commandments of God'" — the pioneer witness confirms independently that at the end only two classes exist: the marked and the sealed. That this final division is the antitype of Genesis 4's two lineages is this handbook's argument, drawn out in "The Seal or the Mark — the Distinction Made Visible."

**DEFINITION — THE TWO LINEAGES — THE CITY AND THE NAME =** the two worships of the first altar (_Gen. 4:3-5_) harden into two civilizations. Cain, having "gone out from the presence of the LORD" (_Gen. 4:16_), "builded a city" (_Gen. 4:17_) — the first in Scripture, built by a fugitive and named for his son — and his line fills it with the marks of the class: the home broken by Lamech's polygamy against the one-flesh charter of Eden (_Gen. 4:19_; _Gen. 2:24_; _Matt. 19:4-8_), the arts and industry of Jubal and Tubalcain advancing while God is forgotten (_Gen. 4:21-22_; _PP 81.1_), and violence sung as culture in Lamech's sword-song (_Gen. 4:23-24_). Over against the city stands the Name: the faithful seed re-established in Seth, "another seed instead of Abel" (_Gen. 4:25_), the covenant line of the genealogy of Christ (_Luke 3:38_), marked off not by a wall but by open, avowed worship — "then began men to call upon the name of the LORD" (_Gen. 4:26_; _PP 80.2_) — and crowned in Enoch, the seventh from Adam, who "walked with God" through all the duties of a working life (_Gen. 5:22, 24_; _PP 85.1_) and carried the first advent-and-judgment sermon into the very land of Cain's flight (_Jude 1:14-15_; _PP 86.1_; _MWV2 17.3_). The two classes take a geography: Cainites over the plains and valleys, Sethites withdrawn to the mountains, and "so long as this separation continued, they maintained the worship of God in its purity" (_PP 81.2_) — until the sons of God came down to marry the daughters of men and the merged earth grew "only evil continually" (_Gen. 6:1-2, 5_). Christ appoints this whole picture to the last days: "as in the days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage" (_Matt. 24:38_; _DA 633.1_), and the pioneer witness confirms that at the end "but two classes are brought to view," the marked and the sealed (_TTAM 6.1_). Every soul stands in the city or calls on the Name; there is no third lineage.

**Symbols defined here:**

- **the city of Cain** = the serpent-seed's congregation — fortified convenience built by the fugitive from God's presence; man staying put in the cursed earth, distinguished in arts, industry, and violence-as-culture while God is forgotten; the civilization whose spirit prevails in the eating-drinking-marrying world Christ names as the type of the last age (_Gen. 4:16-17_; _Gen. 4:21-23_; _Matt. 24:38_; _PP 81.1_).
- **calling on the name of the LORD** = the dividing act of the faithful seed — open, avowed worship, a public profession of loyalty that marks off the Sethite line from the city; crowned in Enoch, who walked with God in the corrupt age and preached judgment to the city of Cain (_Gen. 4:26_; _Gen. 5:24_; _Jude 1:14-15_; _PP 80.2_).
- **mountains and plains** = the geography of the two classes — the serpent's seed congregating over the plains and valleys for convenience, the faithful withdrawing to the mountains to keep their peculiar character and their worship pure; the geography of separation, not of appointed worship, given its biblical voice in "Escape to the mountain, lest thou be consumed" (_Gen. 4:16_; _Gen. 6:1-2_; _Gen. 19:17_; _PP 81.2_; _GC 65.3_).

**For discussion:**

1. The two lineages divide first at the altar — Cain's offering of his own labor against the blood (_Gen. 4:3-5_) — and only afterward become two civilizations. Where in your own worship do you bring God "the fruit of the ground," terms of your own appointing, rather than what He has appointed — and why does self-willed worship, not open atheism, head the city-line?
2. Cain, cut off from the Presence, "builded a city, and called the name of the city, after the name of his son" (_Gen. 4:17_) — he lost the Name and made a name — while Seth's line "began... to call upon the name of the LORD" (_Gen. 4:26_) as "an open profession of loyalty" (_PP 80.2_). Where in your own life are you building a name — security, reputation, accumulation — in place of openly calling on the Name?
3. The city of Cain was brilliant: harp and organ, brass and iron (_Gen. 4:21-22_) — "distinguished" in "mere earthly and material progress" (_PP 81.1_) — and Christ says the last days will look exactly like it: "eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage" (_Matt. 24:38_). If what damned the city-line was not open atheism but self-indulgent normalcy, how do we tell whether our own work, art, and comfort are Sethite labor or Cainite culture?
