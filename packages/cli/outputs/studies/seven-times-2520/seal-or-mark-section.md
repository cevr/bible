## The Seal or the Mark — the Final Marked Contrast

> At the end there are only two companies and two marks set in the same place — the forehead: the seal of the living God, which is His Sabbath sign and the settled, faultless character of the commandment-keeping remnant; and the mark of the beast, forced conformity to a false worship, enforced through buying and selling. To receive the one is to refuse the other, and the whole come-out-of-the-cities call is the preparation to stand sealed.

**The first company — the sealed bear the Father's name — Rev. 14:1, 4-5:**

- _Rev. 14:1._ "And I looked, and, lo, a Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father's name written in their foreheads" — the sealed remnant bear the Father's NAME in the very place the mark is counterfeited; the seal is set where the mark is forged.
  - **the seal of God** = the Sabbath sign and the settled, sanctified character of the commandment-keeping, faith-of-Jesus remnant — set in the forehead, the mind and allegiance (_Rev. 7:2-3_, sealed "in their foreheads"; _Rev. 14:1_, "his Father's name written in their foreheads"; _Eze. 20:12_, His sabbaths "a sign... that I am the LORD that sanctify them"; _Exo. 31:13_; _Rev. 14:12_, "they that keep the commandments of God"). Defined in full at the close.
- _Rev. 14:4._ "These are they which were not defiled with women; for they are virgins. These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth. These were redeemed from among men, being the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb" — the sealed company is defined by total separation and unreserved following: "follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth" — the obedience that leaves the city's defiling stream.
- _Rev. 14:5._ "And in their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault before the throne of God" — the seal produces settled, faultless character; the righteousness by faith of the earlier sections finished in them. The seal's inward reality is character, not a visible mark.
- _3AM 90.2._ White: "Just as soon as the people of God are sealed in their foreheads—it is not any seal or mark that can be seen, but a settling into the truth, both intellectually and spiritually, so they cannot be moved" — the seal defined as settled, immovable character, corroborating "without fault" (_Rev. 14:5_) as the seal's inward substance.

**The seal of the living God — set in the forehead — Rev. 7:2-3:**

- _Rev. 7:2._ "And I saw another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God: and he cried with a loud voice to the four angels, to whom it was given to hurt the earth and the sea" — the seal of the LIVING God is held back until His servants are sealed; the judgment of the four winds waits on the sealing.
- _Rev. 7:3._ "Hurt not the earth, neither the sea, nor the trees, till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads" — the seal is set "in their foreheads," the same locus as the mark (_Rev. 13:16_): the whole contest is over what is written in the forehead — the mind and allegiance.

**Scripture's own definition of the seal — the Sabbath, the sign of sanctifying — Eze. 20:12, 20; Exo. 31:13:**

- _Eze. 20:12._ "Moreover also I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them" — God's own definition: His sabbaths are "a sign between me and them" that He is the LORD that sanctifies. Sealing IS sanctifying, and the Sabbath is its sign.
- _Eze. 20:20._ "And hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign between me and you, that ye may know that I am the LORD your God" — the Sabbath is the sign of knowing the true God — the very knowledge the mark's false worship counterfeits. The Sabbath is the seal.
- _Exo. 31:13._ "Verily my sabbaths ye shall keep: for it is a sign between me and you throughout your generations; that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you" — the Sabbath named a perpetual SIGN of sanctification: Scripture's own basis for equating sign and seal.
- _THB 44.2._ Smith: "Sign and seal are synonymous terms. See Romans 4:11... 'a seal of the righteousness of the faith.' The Sabbath is a sign between God and his people; hence it seals his law as genuine... Take this fourth commandment from the ten, and the seal of the living God is gone" — pioneer confirmation defining the seal from Scripture: the Sabbath sign is the seal of the righteousness of faith (_Rom. 4:11_); remove the fourth commandment and the seal is gone.
- _DD 49.2._ White: "Too late they see that the Sabbath of the fourth commandment is the seal of the living God. Too late they see the true nature of their spurious Sabbath and the sandy foundation upon which they have been building" — the seal is the Sabbath of the fourth commandment, set over against the "spurious Sabbath" that constitutes the mark.

**The second company — those who receive the mark — Rev. 14:9-11:**

- _Rev. 14:9._ "If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark in his forehead, or in his hand" — the second company: those who worship the beast and receive the mark — placed in forehead OR hand, the very seat of the seal (_Rev. 7:3_).
  - **the mark of the beast** = forced conformity to the beast's worship — its counterfeit Sabbath, the false day — received in the forehead or hand, the very places God sets His seal and bound His law. It is enforced through commerce, and its wage is the unmingled wrath of God and "no rest," the antithesis of the Sabbath rest of the seal (_Rev. 13:16-17_; _Rev. 14:9-11_; _Deut. 6:8_, the law bound "upon thine hand... between thine eyes"). Defined in full at the close.
- _Rev. 14:10._ "The same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation; and he shall be tormented with fire and brimstone in the presence of the holy angels, and in the presence of the Lamb" — the mark's wage is the UNMINGLED wrath of God; the eternal stakes that make the city/country choice a salvation issue.
- _Rev. 14:11._ "And the smoke of their torment ascendeth up for ever and ever: and they have no rest day nor night, who worship the beast and his image, and whosoever receiveth the mark of his name" — the marked "have no rest" — the exact opposite of the Sabbath REST that is the seal. The contrast is rest-keepers versus the restless.

**The mark forced — and its lever is commerce — Rev. 13:16-17:**

- _Rev. 13:16._ "And he causeth all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads" — the mark is forced upon all, and set in the hand or forehead, the two places God sets His seal (_Rev. 7:3_) and bound His law (_Deut. 6:8_): a deliberate counterfeit.
- _Rev. 13:17._ "And that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark, or the name of the beast, or the number of his name" — the mark's leverage is commerce: buying and selling is the lever. To be free of dependence on the buy-and-sell system is to be free to refuse the mark.
  - **buying and selling** = the world's commercial dependence — the very lever by which the mark is enforced ("no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark"). It is the life of Sodom that Christ names in the day of His revealing, and the merchandise of doomed Babylon; the separated, self-provisioning country life breaks that dependence, freeing the soul to refuse the mark (_Rev. 13:17_; _Luke 17:28-30_; _Rev. 18:4, 11_). Defined in full at the close.
- _3AM 94.3._ White: "When you obey the decree that commands you to cease from labor on Sunday and worship God, while you know that there is not a word in the Bible showing Sunday to be other than a common working-day, you consent to receive the mark of the beast, and refuse the seal of God... But the seal of the living God is placed upon those who conscientiously keep the Sabbath of the Lord" — the marked-contrast itself: receiving the mark is refusing the seal, and the seal rests on Sabbath-keepers. The two are mutually exclusive choices.

**Two companies only — the marked versus the sealed — Rev. 14:12:**

- _Rev. 14:12._ "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" — the sealed company defined precisely: commandment-keeping plus the faith of Jesus — the law and the gospel, the righteousness by faith of the earlier sections made the substance of the seal.
- _TTAM 6.1._ Andrews: "In the third angel's message... but two classes are brought to view. One is oppressive, and persecutes the saints, and has the mark of the beast, and worships the beast and his image... The other class is oppressed and driven... they get 'the victory over the beast, and his image, and over his MARK' and are sealed with the seal of the living God by keeping 'the commandments of God'" — pioneer confirmation of the two-companies-only contrast: the marked versus the sealed who gain victory over the mark by keeping the commandments — exactly the Revelation 13-14 frame.
- _3AM 113.4._ White: "Justification by faith is the third angel's message in verity—The Lord in His great mercy sent a most precious message to His people through Elders Waggoner and Jones... It presented justification through faith in the Surety; it invited the people to receive the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience to all the commandments of God" — names the 1888 message and ties the seal of the commandment-keeping remnant back to the righteousness by faith of the earlier sections: the seal IS that righteousness settled.

**The come-out — buying and selling, Sodom, and Babylon — Luke 17:28-30; Rev. 18:4:**

- _Luke 17:28-30._ "Likewise also as it was in the days of Lot; they did eat, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they builded; But the same day that Lot went out of Sodom it rained fire and brimstone from heaven, and destroyed them all. Even thus shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed" — Christ Himself ties "they bought, they sold" to the day of His revealing: the buy-and-sell city life of Sodom is the very condition He warns His people to come OUT of before the end.
- _Rev. 18:4._ "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues" — the end-time call is literal separation from the doomed commercial city (Babylon, whose merchants weep, _Rev. 18:11_) — the same come-out that frees from the mark.
- _AH 141.4._ White: "Again and again the Lord has instructed that our people are to take their families away from the cities, into the country, where they can raise their own provisions; for in the future the problem of buying and selling will be a very serious one" — names the buy-and-sell crisis of _Rev. 13:17_ and prescribes the country-living provision ("raise their own provisions") as the preparation to stand free of the mark.
- _CL 21.1._ White: "The Protestant world have set up an idol Sabbath in the place where God's Sabbath should be, and they are treading in the footsteps of the Papacy. For this reason I see the necessity of the people of God moving out of the cities into retired country [places,] where they may cultivate the land and raise their own produce... I see the necessity of making haste to get all things ready for the crisis" — joins the counterfeit-Sabbath/mark crisis directly to the command to leave the cities and self-provision: the section's whole thesis in one line.

_History anchor:_ Ellen White dates the lingering of the sealing/closing work to the church's post-1844 and post-1888 unbelief (_GC 458.1_, "unbelief separated them from God... others were raised up to proclaim the message"), naming the 1888 Waggoner-Jones message of justification by faith as "the third angel's message in verity" (_3AM 113.4_; cf. _TM 91-92_, Letter 57, 1895). The sealing work has lingered as ancient Israel wandered, because of unbelief.

**DEFINITION — THE SEAL OR THE MARK =** the final, marked contrast of Revelation 13-14: two companies only, two marks set in the same place — the forehead (and the hand). The **seal of God** is the Sabbath sign and the settled, sanctified, faultless character of the commandment-keeping, faith-of-Jesus remnant — for God's own word names His sabbaths "a sign between me and them, that I am the LORD that sanctify them" (_Eze. 20:12, 20_; _Exo. 31:13_), the angel seals God's servants "in their foreheads" (_Rev. 7:2-3_), the sealed bear "his Father's name" there (_Rev. 14:1_), keep "the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (_Rev. 14:12_), and are "without fault" (_Rev. 14:5_) — a settling into the truth so they cannot be moved (_3AM 90.2_). The **mark of the beast** is its deliberate counterfeit: forced conformity to a false worship and its spurious Sabbath, received "in his forehead, or in his hand" (_Rev. 14:9_; _Rev. 13:16_) — the very places God sets His seal and bound His law (_Deut. 6:8_) — enforced through commerce, "that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark" (_Rev. 13:17_), its wage the unmingled wrath of God (_Rev. 14:10_) and "no rest day nor night" (_Rev. 14:11_), the antithesis of the Sabbath rest of the seal. The two are mutually exclusive: to receive the mark is to refuse the seal (_3AM 94.3_), and only the marked or the sealed remain (_TTAM 6.1_). The lever of the mark is **buying and selling** — the buy-and-sell life of Sodom Christ names "in the day when the Son of man is revealed" (_Luke 17:28-30_), the merchandise of doomed Babylon (_Rev. 18:11_) — so the call is to "Come out of her, my people" (_Rev. 18:4_), to take families "into the country, where they can raise their own provisions" (_AH 141.4_; _CL 21.1_): the come-out-of-the-cities, self-provisioning country life is the preparation to stand sealed and refuse the mark.

**Symbols defined here:**

- **the seal of God** = the Sabbath sign and the settled, sanctified, faultless character of the commandment-keeping, faith-of-Jesus remnant, set in the forehead — the mind and allegiance (_Eze. 20:12, 20_; _Exo. 31:13_; _Rev. 7:2-3_; _Rev. 14:1, 5, 12_; _Deut. 6:8_; _THB 44.2_; _DD 49.2_; _3AM 90.2_).
- **the mark of the beast** = forced conformity to the beast's counterfeit worship and false Sabbath, received in the forehead or hand, enforced through commerce, its wage the unmingled wrath of God and "no rest" — the antithesis of the Sabbath rest of the seal (_Rev. 13:16-17_; _Rev. 14:9-11_; _Deut. 6:8_).
- **buying and selling** = the world's commercial dependence, the lever by which the mark is enforced; the buy-and-sell life of Sodom and merchandise of Babylon the come-out call breaks (_Rev. 13:17_; _Luke 17:28-30_; _Rev. 18:4, 11_).

**Symbols carried:** the "righteousness by faith" — "commandment-keeping plus the faith of Jesus" (_Rev. 14:12_) — that is the inward substance of the seal is the very thread established in "Righteousness by Faith" and named "the third angel's message in verity" (_3AM 113.4_); the literal separation that frees from the mark is the come-out-of-the-cities, country-living obedience carried by "Come Out of Her, My People — the Country-Living Call."

**For discussion:**

1. The seal and the mark are set in the same place — the forehead — and Scripture says the seal "is not any seal or mark that can be seen, but a settling into the truth" (_3AM 90.2_; _Rev. 14:5_). If the seal is character and not a visible badge, how is it being formed in us now, before the testing decree comes (_Rev. 7:3_; _Rev. 14:12_)?
2. The mark's whole leverage is "that no man might buy or sell" (_Rev. 13:17_), and Christ ties "they bought, they sold" to "the day when the Son of man is revealed" (_Luke 17:28-30_). What practical step toward self-provisioning country life — "raise their own provisions" (_AH 141.4_) — frees the soul to refuse the mark, and what holds us in the buy-and-sell dependence today?
3. Ellen White says receiving the mark is to "refuse the seal of God," and the two cannot be held together (_3AM 94.3_). Since the Sabbath is the sign "that I am the LORD that sanctify them" (_Eze. 20:12_), how does keeping the true Sabbath answer the mark's counterfeit worship — and why is the choice ultimately about whom we know and worship, not merely which day we rest?
