# 1. The Year-Day Principle

> _In symbolic prophecy a day stands for a year. This is not a human convention imposed upon the text, but a rule Scripture supplies of itself (Num. 14:34; Eze. 4:6), and the pioneers — Miller, Litch, and Smith — made it the foundation of all prophetic reckoning. Every great time-line hangs upon it: the 1260 days of papal supremacy, the 2300 days to the cleansing of the sanctuary, the 70 weeks cut off for the Jews, and the 1290 and 1335. The seventy weeks (490 years) ran out to the very year in the death and rejection of Messiah, and so stand as the divine seal that the rule is true. (DAR 141, 191-192; GC 324, 326-328.)_

### The rule given in Scripture itself

- **Numbers 14:34** — "After the number of the days in which ye searched the land, even forty days, **each day for a year**, shall ye bear your iniquities, even forty years." God converts forty days of searching into forty years of wandering — the year-day measure stated in so many words.

  > "Figures sometimes have two or more different significations, as day is used in a figurative sense to represent three different periods of time." — MWV1 21.

  > "Following his rule of making Scripture its own interpreter, Miller learned that a day in symbolic prophecy represents a year (Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:6); he saw that the period of 2300 prophetic days, or literal years, would extend far beyond the close of the Jewish dispensation…" — GC 324.

- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "Thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: **I have appointed thee each day for a year**." A second independent witness; the prophet lies a day for every year of Judah's guilt. Two texts establish the rule "in the mouth of two witnesses."

  > "Figures always have a figurative meaning, and are used much in prophecy, to represent future things, times and events… Day, meaning year." — MWV1 21.

  > "The rule given us in the Bible is, that when a day is used as a symbol, it stands for a year. Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 14:34… As each day stands for a year, we have twelve hundred and sixty years for the continuation of the supremacy of this horn." — DAR 141-142.

- **Ezekiel 4:5** — "I have laid upon thee the years of their iniquity, according to the number of the days, three hundred and ninety days." The 390 days for Israel are by God's own word 390 years — the principle applied a third time in the same passage.

  > "The rule given us in the Bible is, that when a day is used as a symbol, it stands for a year. Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 14:34." — DAR 141.

- **Numbers 14:33** — "Your children shall wander in the wilderness forty years." The forty years of the sentence answer day-for-day to the forty days of unbelief, fixing the proportion God Himself uses.

  > "Following his rule of making Scripture its own interpreter, Miller learned that a day in symbolic prophecy represents a year (Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:6)…" — GC 324.

### The rule confirmed by the pioneers' own method

- **Miller's Rule X** — Miller's tenth rule of interpretation lists the figures of prophecy and their meanings: "mountains, meaning governments; beasts, meaning kingdoms; waters, meaning people . . . **Day, meaning year**," citing Ezekiel 4:6 as the proof. Symbols are explained by Scripture, and the day is one of them.

  > "Figures always have a figurative meaning, and are used much in prophecy, to represent future things, times and events; such as mountains, meaning governments; beasts, meaning kingdoms. Waters, meaning people… Day, meaning year." — MWV1 21.

- **Litch — "the Holy Ghost hath defined it"** — Litch answers the objection "how long is a time?" by letting Scripture interpret Scripture: Daniel's "time, times, and dividing of time" (Dan. 7:25), John's "forty-two months" (Rev. 13:5), and the same span reduced to "1260 days" (Rev. 11-12) are all the same period — "divine time, each day being the representative . . . of a full solar year."

  > "But we are asked, 'how do you know how long a time is, and how much is meant by times?' I answer, the Holy Ghost has defined it… The forty-two months are reduced, Revelation 11th and 12th chapters, to days; 1260 days… I call the time, then, neither Jewish time nor Chaldee time, but divine time; each day being the representative, either of a day of twenty four hours, or, if used figuratively, of a full solar year." — PREX1 92.

- **Smith on the rule** — "The rule given us in the Bible is, that when a day is used as a symbol, it stands for a year. Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 14:34." Smith grounds the whole computation of Daniel 7 on these two texts.

  > "We must now consider that we are in the midst of symbolic prophecy; hence in this measurement the time is not literal, but symbolic also… The rule given us in the Bible is, that when a day is used as a symbol, it stands for a year. Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 14:34." — DAR 141.

- **The day = a thousand years has its own place** — "One day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day" (2 Pet. 3:8; cf. Ps. 90:4). The day stands for differing periods according to its use; in symbolic prophecy the measure is the year.

  > "Figures sometimes have two or more different significations, as day is used in a figurative sense to represent three different periods of time." — MWV1 21.

### The seven-year "week" — Scripture's own unit

- **Genesis 29:27** — "Fulfil her week, and we will give thee this also for the service which thou shalt serve with me **yet seven other years**." A "week" in the prophetic sense is seven years; Jacob's two weeks were fourteen years. This is the very key to Daniel's "seventy weeks."

  > "The word here translated 'determined' literally signifies 'cut off.' Seventy weeks, representing 490 years, are declared by the angel to be cut off, as specially pertaining to the Jews." — GC 326.

- **Genesis 29:28** — "Jacob did so, and fulfilled her week" — the week of years actually served out, establishing that a "week" can stand for seven literal years.

  > "Seventy weeks, representing 490 years, are declared by the angel to be cut off, as specially pertaining to the Jews." — GC 326.

- **Leviticus 25:8** — "Thou shalt number seven sabbaths of years . . . seven times seven years; and the space of the seven sabbaths of years shall be unto thee **forty and nine years**." Forty-nine years are reckoned as "seven sabbaths of years" — a week of weeks of years, the same arithmetic that turns 70 weeks into 490 years.

  > "The word here translated 'determined' literally signifies 'cut off.' Seventy weeks, representing 490 years, are declared by the angel to be cut off, as specially pertaining to the Jews." — GC 326-328.

- **Leviticus 25:9** — The jubilee trumpet sounds "in the day of atonement" of "the seventh month" — the autumn type that, with the seventh-month reckoning, fixes the close of the 2300 days in the autumn of 1844.

  > "Miller and his associates at first believed that the 2300 days would terminate in the spring of 1844, whereas the prophecy points to the autumn of that year." — GC 328.

  > "In the parable of Matthew 25 the time of waiting and slumber is followed by the coming of the bridegroom. This was in accordance with the arguments just presented, both from prophecy and from the types." — GC 400.

### The seventy weeks — the principle vindicated to the very year

- **Daniel 9:24** — "**Seventy weeks are determined** upon thy people and upon thy holy city, to finish the transgression . . . and to anoint the most Holy." The word "determined" (Heb. _nehhtak_) properly signifies **"cut off"** — and there is but one period from which 70 weeks could be cut off: the 2300 days of Daniel 8:14.

  > "If this view of the subject is correct, we should naturally expect, when the angel completed his explanation of the vision, that he would commence with the very point which had been omitted; namely, the time… he commences upon the very point there omitted, and says, 'Seventy weeks are determined upon thy people and upon thy holy city.'" — DAR 191-192.

  > "The word here translated 'determined' literally signifies 'cut off.'… As the 2300 days was the only period of time mentioned in chapter 8, it must be the period from which the seventy weeks were cut off; the seventy weeks must therefore be a part of the 2300 days, and the two periods must begin together." — GC 326.

- **Daniel 9:25** — "From the going forth of the commandment to restore and to build Jerusalem unto the Messiah the Prince shall be seven weeks, and threescore and two weeks." Sixty-nine weeks = 483 years from the decree of Artaxerxes (457 B.C.) reach to A.D. 27 and the anointing of Christ at His baptism.

  > "After citing Daniel's attention back to the former vision in the most direct and emphatic manner, and assuring him that he had now come forth to give him understanding in the matter, he commences upon the very point there omitted." — DAR 191.

  > "In its completest form it was issued by Artaxerxes, king of Persia, 457 B.C.… 'From the going forth of the commandment to restore and to build Jerusalem unto the Messiah the Prince shall be seven weeks, and threescore and two weeks'—namely, sixty-nine weeks, or 483 years… From this date, 483 years extend to the autumn of A.D. 27." — GC 326-327.

- **Daniel 9:26** — "After threescore and two weeks shall Messiah be cut off, but not for himself." In the midst of the seventieth week, A.D. 31, Messiah was crucified — exactly where 483 years from 457 B.C. place it.

  > "'In the midst of the week He shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease.' In A.D. 31, three and a half years after His baptism, our Lord was crucified. With the great sacrifice offered upon Calvary, ended that system of offerings which for four thousand years had pointed forward to the Lamb of God." — GC 327-328.

- **Daniel 9:27** — "He shall confirm the covenant with many for one week: and in the midst of the week he shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease." The final week (A.D. 27-34) and the cessation of the sacrifices at the cross fall to the year, proving the year-day measure.

  > "The 'week' here brought to view is the last one of the seventy; it is the last seven years of the period allotted especially to the Jews. During this time, extending from A.D. 27 to A.D. 34, Christ, at first in person and afterward by His disciples, extended the gospel invitation especially to the Jews." — GC 327-328.

- **"Cut off" — the textual demonstration** — "The word here rendered determined signifies 'cut off;' and there is no period from which the seventy weeks could be cut off but the 2300 days." The 490 years are a part of the 2300; both begin together at 457 B.C.

  > "The word here rendered determined signifies 'cut off;' and there is no period from which the seventy weeks could be cut off but the 2300 days of the previous vision. How direct and natural, then, is the connection… he says, 'Seventy weeks are cut off.' Cut off from what? — The 2300 days, most assuredly." — DAR 192.

- **The seventy weeks ran out in A.D. 34** — "The seventy weeks, or 490 years, especially allotted to the Jews, ended . . . in A.D. 34," when the nation sealed its rejection of the gospel. The whole 490-year span fulfilled to the year is the divine seal upon day-for-a-year.

  > "The seventy weeks, or 490 years, especially allotted to the Jews, ended, as we have seen, in A.D. 34. At that time, through the action of the Jewish Sanhedrin, the nation sealed its rejection of the gospel by the martyrdom of Stephen and the persecution of the followers of Christ." — GC 328.

  > "The seventy weeks were evidently fulfilled in the year A.D. 33, beginning 457 years B.C., at the going forth of the commandment to Ezra to restore the law and the people to Jerusalem." — MWV1 43.

### The great time-periods that hang upon it

- **Daniel 8:14** — "Unto **two thousand and three hundred days**; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." Reckoned as years from 457 B.C., the 2300 reach to 1844. The 490 years (A.D. 34) being cut off from the 2300 leave 1810 years, which from A.D. 34 extend to 1844.

  > "The prophecy which seemed most clearly to reveal the time of the second advent was that of Daniel 8:14: 'Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed.'" — GC 324.

  > "The seventy weeks—490 days—having been cut off from the 2300, there were 1810 days remaining… From A.D. 34, 1810 years extend to 1844. Consequently the 2300 days of Daniel 8:14 terminate in 1844." — GC 326-328.

- **Daniel 7:25** — "And they shall be given into his hand until **a time and times and the dividing of time**." Three and a half prophetic years = 1260 days = 1260 years of papal supremacy, 538 to 1798.

  > "We thus have three years and a half for the continuance of this power… The ordinary Jewish year… contained three hundred and sixty days. Three years and a half contained twelve hundred and sixty days. As each day stands for a year, we have twelve hundred and sixty years for the continuation of the supremacy of this horn… For 538 + 1260 = 1798." — DAR 141.

  > "Daniel has given the time of his power, 'a time, times, and dividing of a time.' John has given it, forty-two months. In forty-two months there are three and a half years. The forty-two months are reduced, Revelation 11th and 12th chapters, to days; 1260 days." — PREX1 92.

- **Revelation 13:5** — "Power was given unto him to continue **forty and two months**." Forty-two months of thirty days = 1260 days = 1260 years — the same span as Daniel's "time, times, and a half," proving the two prophets describe one power.

  > "Power was given to the little horn to continue for a time, times, and the dividing of time, or 1260 years. Daniel 7:25. To this beast also power was given for forty-two months, or 1260 years. Revelation 13:5." — DAR 524.

  > "Daniel has given the time of his power, 'a time, times, and dividing of a time.' John has given it, forty-two months. In forty-two months there are three and a half years. The forty-two months are reduced, Revelation 11th and 12th chapters, to days; 1260 days." — PREX1 92.

- **Revelation 12:6** — "That they should feed her there **a thousand two hundred and threescore days**." John states the very number — 1260 — leaving no room to doubt the length of the period.

  > "To this beast also power was given for forty-two months, or 1260 years. Revelation 13:5." — DAR 524.

  > "The forty-two months are reduced, Revelation 11th and 12th chapters, to days; 1260 days. We are not, therefore, left to vague conjecture as to the import of the time, for the Holy Ghost has defined it." — PREX1 92.

- **Revelation 12:14** — "Where she is nourished for **a time, and times, and half a time**." The identical period of Daniel 7:25, set beside the plain "1260 days" of verse 6, so that John interprets himself.

  > "A time, as we have seen from chapter 4:23, is one year; two times, the least that could be denoted by the plural, two years, and the dividing of time, or half a time… half a year… We thus have three years and a half for the continuance of this power." — DAR 141.

  > "Daniel has given the time of his power, 'a time, times, and dividing of a time.' John has given it, forty-two months… The forty-two months are reduced, Revelation 11th and 12th chapters, to days; 1260 days." — PREX1 92.

- **Revelation 11:2-3** — The holy city trodden "forty and two months," and the two witnesses prophesy "a thousand two hundred and threescore days." Months and days reduced to one figure, 1260 — Scripture's own conversion that "the Holy Ghost hath defined."

  > "The forty-two months are reduced, Revelation 11th and 12th chapters, to days; 1260 days. We are not, therefore, left to vague conjecture as to the import of the time, for the Holy Ghost has defined it." — PREX1 92.

- **Daniel 12:7** — "It shall be for **a time, times, and an half**." The same 1260-year period for the scattering of the holy people, closing in 1798.

  > "We thus have three years and a half for the continuance of this power… As each day stands for a year, we have twelve hundred and sixty years for the continuation of the supremacy of this horn." — DAR 141.

  > "After describing the terrible career of the little horn, and stating that the saints should be given into his hand for 1260 years, bringing us down to 1798…" — DAR 314.

- **Daniel 12:11-12** — "From the time that the daily shall be taken away . . . a thousand two hundred and **ninety days**," and "blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and **five and thirty days**." The 1290 (508-1798) and 1335 (508-1843/44) — both reckoned as years on the same rule.

  > "Still another prophetic period is here introduced, denoting 1335 years… The only clue we have to the solution of this question, is the fact that it is spoken of in immediate connection with the 1290 years, which commenced, as shown above, in 508… From this point they would extend to 1843; for 1335 added to 508 make 1843." — DAR 314-316.
