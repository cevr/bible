# 9. The Seven Seals — the Providential History of the Church

> _Where the seven churches trace the inward spiritual state of God's people, the seven seals unroll the outward providence of the gospel and the church through the self-same Christian era — from apostolic purity to the great day of wrath. As the Lamb breaks each seal, John beholds in succession a white horse (gospel conquest), a red horse (persecution), a black horse (declension under Constantine), a pale horse (papal death), the souls of the martyrs, the literal signs of the sixth seal (the 1755 earthquake, the 1780 dark day, the 1833 falling stars), and at last silence in heaven when the King appears. The seven, being the number of completeness, embrace the whole history of the church from the opening of the Christian era to the coming of Christ. (DAR 402-434; GC 304-308, 333-334.)_

### The Lamb opens the book — a complete sevenfold history

- **Revelation 6:1** — "And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seals... one of the four beasts saying, Come and see." The Lamb who alone was worthy to take the book now breaks its seals one by one; John is summoned to behold the scenes of each.

  > "HAVING taken the book, the Lamb proceeds at once to open the seals; and the attention of the apostle is called to the scenes that transpire under each seal." — DAR 402.

- **Revelation 5:9** — Christ was found worthy "to take the book, and to open the seals thereof." The opening of the seals is the unrolling of providential history that none but the slain Lamb could disclose.

  > "The view which to us seems most consistent is that the book contained a record of events which were to transpire; and when the seals were broken, and the record was brought to light, the scenes were presented before John... by a representation of what was described in the book being made to pass before his mind in living characters." — DAR 402.

- **Seven = completeness** — The number seven denotes in Scripture completeness and perfection; the seven seals therefore embrace the whole of a certain class of events, reaching down to the close of probationary time. The seals give the religious history of the church; the trumpets, running contemporaneously, give the political commotions of the nations.

  > "The number seven has already been noticed as denoting in the Scriptures completeness and perfection. The seven seals therefore embrace the whole of a certain class of events, reaching down to the close of probationary time.... The seals denote events of a religious character, and contain the history of the church from the opening of the Christian era to the coming of Christ." — DAR 402.2.

### First seal — the WHITE HORSE: apostolic purity and gospel conquest (A.D. 31–c.100)

- **Revelation 6:2** — "Behold a white horse: and he that sat on him had a bow; and a crown was given unto him: and he went forth conquering, and to conquer." The whiteness denotes the purity of the gospel in the apostolic age; the rider goes forth in triumph carrying the truth to all the world.

  > "And I saw, and behold a white horse; and he that sat on him had a bow; and a crown was given unto him: and he went forth conquering, and to conquer." — DAR 402.

- **Mark 16:15** — "Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature." The commission that set the white horse riding forth conquering.

  > "And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seals... and behold a white horse;... and he went forth conquering, and to conquer." — DAR 402.

- **Colossians 1:23** — The gospel "was preached to every creature which is under heaven" — the conquest of the first seal in the apostolic generation.

  > "John saw the characters which made up the various scenes, not fixed and motionless upon canvas, but living and moving, and engaging actively in the parts assigned them." — DAR 402.

- **Revelation 19:11** — A later "white horse," whose rider is "Faithful and True," confirms that the white horse is the symbol of pure, victorious righteousness.

  > "And behold a white horse; and he that sat on him had a bow; and a crown was given unto him: and he went forth conquering, and to conquer." — DAR 402.

### Second seal — the RED HORSE: persecution and bloodshed (c.100–c.323)

- **Revelation 6:4** — "There went out another horse that was red: and power was given to him that sat thereon to take peace from the earth, and that they should kill one another: and there was given unto him a great sword." The change of color is significant: if white is gospel purity, red is the bloodshed of the great pagan persecutions that followed.

  > "If the whiteness of the first horse denoted the purity of the gospel in the period which that symbol covers, the redness of the second horse would signify that in this period that original purity began to be corrupted.... Errors began to arise. Worldliness came in. The ecclesiastical power sought the alliance of the secular. Troubles and commotions were the result." — DAR 404.

- **Matthew 10:34** — "I came not to send peace, but a sword." The truth, faithfully held, drew out the sword of the persecutor; peace was taken from the earth.

  > "This state of things answers well to the declaration of the prophet that power was given to him that sat on the horse 'to take peace from the earth, and that they should kill one another: and there was given unto him a great sword.'" — DAR 404.

### Third seal — the BLACK HORSE: declension under Constantine (c.323–c.538)

- **Revelation 6:5** — "Lo a black horse; and he that sat on him had a pair of balances in his hand." Blackness marks the moral darkening of the church as paganism was incorporated into Christianity, the period in which the false system that resulted in the papacy rapidly developed.

  > "It is seen by this how paganism was incorporated into Christianity, and how, during this period, the false system which resulted in the establishment of the papacy, rapidly rounded out to its full outlines, and ripened into all its deplorable perfection of strength and stature." — DAR 407.

- **Revelation 6:6** — "A measure of wheat for a penny... and see thou hurt not the oil and the wine." The balances denote the union of religious and civil power in one person, beginning with Constantine; the scarcity-price denotes a church grasping after worldly goods, while the warning to spare "the oil and the wine" guards the graces of the Spirit, faith and love.

  > "The balances denoted that religion and civil power would be united in the person who would administer the executive power in the government.... The measures of wheat and barley for a penny denote that the members of the church would be eagerly engaged after worldly goods.... The Oil and Wine... 'denote the graces of the Spirit, faith and love.'" — DAR 407.

- **Constantine's union of church and state** — "It represents a secular period, or union of church and state. Constantine aided the clergy, and put them under obligations to him... called the Council of Nicaea." The prosperity Constantine procured for the church bred the corruptions that finally terminated in the apostasy.

  > "It represents a secular period, or union of church and state. Constantine aided the clergy, and put them under obligations to him. He legislated for the church, called the Council of Nicaea, and was most prominent in that Council.... Mosheim says of this period, 'There was continual war and trouble.'" — DAR 404.3.

  > "The discerning reader will easily perceive what detriment the church received from the peace and prosperity procured by Constantine, and from the imprudent methods employed to allure the different nations to embrace the gospel." — DAR 406.

### Fourth seal — the PALE HORSE: papal death and persecution (538–1798)

- **Revelation 6:8** — "Behold a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him. And power was given unto them over the fourth part of the earth, to kill with sword, and with hunger, and with death." The pale (livid, corpse-like) color is unnatural — fit emblem of the death-dealing dominion of the papacy over its territory.

  > "The colors of the white, red, and black horses... are natural; but a pale color is unnatural.... The rider on this horse is named Death; and Hell (ᾅδης, the grave) follows with him.... It must refer to the time in which the papacy bore its unrebuked, unrestrained, and persecuting rule, commencing about A. D. 538, and extending to the time when the Reformers commenced their work." — DAR 408.

- **"Power was given unto him... namely, the papacy"** — The margin reads "him," the power personified by Death on the pale horse, the papacy; the "fourth part of the earth" is the territory over which Rome held jurisdiction, where it slew with sword, hunger, death, and the beasts of the earth.

  > "'And power was given unto them' — him, says the margin; that is, the power personified by Death on the pale horse; namely, the papacy. By the fourth part of the earth is doubtless meant the territory over which this power had jurisdiction; while the words sword, hunger, death... and beasts of the earth, are figures denoting the means by which it has put to death its martyrs." — DAR 408.3.

### Fifth seal — the SOULS UNDER THE ALTAR: the martyrs cry "How long?"

- **Revelation 6:9** — "I saw under the altar the souls of them that were slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held." The martyrs of the foregoing seals, slain for their fidelity, are figuratively seen beneath the altar — their blood, like Abel's, crying from the ground; this is no proof of conscious souls in death but symbolic, as Abel's blood "crieth."

  > "This representation is popularly regarded as a strong proof of the doctrine of the disembodied and conscious state of the dead.... This view of the passage is inadmissible, for several reasons: (1) The popular view places these souls in heaven; but the altar of sacrifice on which they were slain, and beneath which they were seen, cannot be there." — DAR 410.

  > "The word souls may be regarded as here meaning simply the martyrs, those who had been slain.... Lying there, victims of papal bloodthirstiness and oppression, they cried to God for vengeance, in the same manner that Abel's blood cried to him from the ground. Genesis 4:10." — DAR 412.

- **Revelation 6:10** — "How long, O Lord, holy and true, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth?" The cry for vindication against the persecuting power.

  > "These souls pray that their blood may be avenged, — an article which the immaterial soul, as popularly understood, is not supposed to possess." — DAR 412.

- **Revelation 6:11** — "White robes were given unto every one of them; and it was said unto them, that they should rest yet for a little season." The white robes are the partial answer — the vindication of their misrepresented characters — until the full number of martyrs should be completed.

  > "The White Robes. — These were given as a partial answer to their cry, 'How long, O Lord, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood?'... They had gone down to the grave in the most ignominious manner. Their lives had been misrepresented, their reputations tarnished, their names defamed." — DAR 412.2.

### Sixth seal — the LITERAL SIGNS in earth and heaven

- **Revelation 6:12** — "And I beheld when he had opened the sixth seal, and, lo, there was a great earthquake; and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the moon became as blood." Here the language turns from the highly figurative to the strictly literal: a great earthquake, the darkened sun, the bloodied moon.

  > "Between the fifth and sixth seals there seems to be a sudden and entire change in the language, from the highly figurative to the strictly literal.... By no principle of interpretation can the language of the preceding seals be made to be literal, nor can the language of this any more easily be made to be figurative." — DAR 414.2-3.

- **Revelation 6:13** — "And the stars of heaven fell unto the earth, even as a fig tree casteth her untimely figs, when she is shaken of a mighty wind." The falling stars of the sixth seal — the meteoric shower.

  > "And the Stars of Heaven Fell. — The voice of history still is, Fulfilled!... We refer to the great meteoric shower of Nov. 13, 1833." — DAR 422.7.

- **THE GREAT EARTHQUAKE — Lisbon, Nov. 1, 1755** — The first sign, the great earthquake, was fulfilled in the Lisbon earthquake of November 1, 1755, which "extended over a tract of at least 4,000,000 square miles," pervading the greater part of Europe, Africa, and America — no recorded earthquake combining so great an extent with such violence.

  > "The Great Earthquake. — The first event under this seal... is a great earthquake. As the more probable fulfillment of this prediction, we refer to the great earthquake of Nov. 1, 1755, known as the earthquake of Lisbon.... 'The great earthquake of Nov. 1, 1755, extended over a tract of at least 4,000,000 square miles.... It pervaded the greater portion of Europe, Africa, and America.'" — DAR 414.3-418.

  > "In fulfillment of this prophecy there occurred, in the year 1755, the most terrible earthquake that has ever been recorded. Though commonly known as the earthquake of Lisbon, it extended to the greater part of Europe, Africa, and America.... It pervaded an extent of not less than four million square miles." — GC 304-305.

- **THE DARKENED SUN — May 19, 1780** — "The sun became black as sackcloth of hair" was fulfilled in the Dark Day of May 19, 1780, "a most unaccountable darkening of the whole visible heavens and atmosphere in New England," not caused by any eclipse, and standing alone as the most mysterious phenomenon of its kind.

  > "The Darkening of the Sun. — Following the earthquake, it is announced that 'the sun became black as sackcloth of hair.'... This portion of the prediction has also been fulfilled.... the wonderful darkening of the sun, May 19, 1780.... 'Almost, if not altogether alone, as the most mysterious and yet unexplained phenomenon of its kind in nature's diversified range of events... stands the dark day of May 19th, 1780, — a most unaccountable darkening of the whole visible heavens and atmosphere in New England.'" — DAR 418.3-420.

  > "Twenty-five years later appeared the next sign mentioned in the prophecy—the darkening of the sun and moon.... 'Almost, if not altogether alone, as the most mysterious and as yet unexplained phenomenon of its kind, ... stands the dark day of May 19, 1780,—a most unaccountable darkening of the whole visible heavens and atmosphere in New England.'" — GC 306-308.

- **THE MOON AS BLOOD — night of May 19, 1780** — That same night the darkness was "as gross as has ever been observed," and whenever the full moon (which had fulled the day before, proving no eclipse) did appear, it had the appearance of blood — exactly as the prophecy foretold.

  > "'The darkness of the following evening was probably as gross as has ever been observed since the Almighty fiat gave birth to light.'... 'The darkness was as uncommon in the night as it was in the day, as the moon had fulled the day before.' This statement respecting the phase of the moon proves the impossibility of an eclipse.... And whenever on this memorable night the moon did appear... it had, according to this prophecy, the appearance of blood." — DAR 422.1-6.

- **THE FALLING STARS — Nov. 13, 1833** — "And the stars of heaven fell" was fulfilled in the great meteoric shower of November 13, 1833 — "the most universal and wonderful which has ever been recorded, the whole firmament, over all the United States, being then, for hours, in fiery commotion." On each occasion the people instinctively cried, "The Judgment has come."

  > "'Extensive and magnificent showers of shooting stars have been known to occur at various places in modern times; but the most universal and wonderful which has ever been recorded, is that of the 13th of November, 1833, the whole firmament, over all the United States, being then, for hours, in fiery commotion.'... They were each instinctively associated... with the great day of which they were the forerunners. And on each occasion the cry was raised, 'The Judgment has come; the world's at an end.'" — DAR 422.7-426.

  > "This prophecy received a striking and impressive fulfillment in the great meteoric shower of November 13, 1833. That was the most extensive and wonderful display of falling stars which has ever been recorded; 'the whole firmament, over all the United States, being then, for hours, in fiery commotion!'" — GC 333-334.

- **Matthew 24:29** — "Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken." Christ's own sign-list matches the sixth seal exactly and fixes its time — "after the tribulation," i.e., after the 1260-year papal persecution had begun to abate.

  > "Our Lord has not only told us that such an event should occur as a sign of his coming, but he has told us also when it should occur. 'Immediately after the tribulation of those days,' says Matthew.... The days are the days of papal supremacy, the 1260 years, from 538 to 1798; the tribulation is the oppression of Christians by the Catholic power till restrained by the work of the Reformation. The tribulation may be said to have ceased about the middle of the eighteenth century." — DAR 428.3-430.

  > "Prophecy not only foretells the manner and object of Christ's coming, but presents tokens by which men are to know when it is near.... 'The sun shall be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars of heaven shall fall, and the powers that are in heaven shall be shaken. And then shall they see the Son of man coming in the clouds with great power and glory.' Mark 13:24-26." — GC 304.

  > "This prophecy received a striking and impressive fulfillment in the great meteoric shower of November 13, 1833. That was the most extensive and wonderful display of falling stars which has ever been recorded." — GC 333.

- **Joel 2:31** — "The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and the terrible day of the LORD come." The Old-Testament prophet foretold the very signs of 1780; the eyewitness descriptions of the Dark Day are "but an echo of the words of the Lord, recorded by the prophet Joel."

  > "The description of this event, as given by eyewitnesses, is but an echo of the words of the Lord, recorded by the prophet Joel, twenty-five hundred years previous to their fulfillment: 'The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and terrible day of the Lord come.' Joel 2:31." — GC 308.1.

- **Luke 21:25-26** — "There shall be signs in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars... the powers of heaven shall be shaken." The parallel discourse confirms these as the appointed tokens that the King is near, even at the doors.

  > "Prophecy not only foretells the manner and object of Christ's coming, but presents tokens by which men are to know when it is near. Said Jesus: 'There shall be signs in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars.' Luke 21:25." — GC 304.

### The sixth seal reaches to the great day of wrath

- **Revelation 6:14** — "And the heaven departed as a scroll when it is rolled together; and every mountain and island were moved out of their places." Having beheld the past signs fulfilled, we now stand between verses 13 and 14, looking to the still-future rolling-up of the heavens at the voice of God.

  > "And the Heaven Departed as a Scroll. — In this event our minds are turned to the future. From looking at the past, and beholding the word of God fulfilled, we are now called to look at events in the future.... Here is our position, unmistakably defined. We stand between the 13th and 14th verses of this chapter. We wait for the heavens to depart as a scroll when it is rolled together." — DAR 432.2-3.

- **Isaiah 34:4** — "The heavens shall be rolled together as a scroll: and all their host shall fall down, as the leaf falleth off from the vine." The prophets describe the same closing scene of the sixth seal — the departing heaven.

  > "It is the voice of God, as he speaks in terrible majesty from his throne in heaven, that causes this fearful commotion in earth and sky.... He is to speak again, and not only the earth will shake, but the heavens also.... Isaiah (24: 19, 20) says: 'The earth is utterly broken down, the earth is clean dissolved, the earth is moved exceedingly.'" — DAR 432.3-4.

- **Revelation 6:15-16** — Kings, great men, and bondmen alike hide "in the dens and in the rocks of the mountains," crying, "Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb." The largest and last prayer-meeting ever held — addressed not to God but to the rocks, in despair.

  > "Many now affect to despise the institution of prayer; but at one time or another all men will pray. Those who will not now pray to God in penitence, will then pray to the rocks and mountains in despair; and this will be the largest prayer-meeting ever held." — DAR 434.1-2.

- **Revelation 6:17** — "For the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?" The sixth seal closes by ushering in the great day of wrath, just short of Christ's personal appearing, which is reserved for the next seal.

  > "The involuntary language of their anguished hearts is, 'The great day of his wrath is come, and who shall be able to stand?'" — DAR 434.

  > "That seal does not bring us to the second advent, although it embraces events that transpire in close connection therewith.... But the seal stops just short of that event. The personal appearing of Christ must therefore be allotted to the next seal." — DAR 452.4.

### Revelation 7 — the sealing falls within the sixth seal (parenthesis)

- **Revelation 7:1** — "I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, that the wind should not blow." The four winds — strife, war, and commotion — are restrained while the work of God goes forward; chapter 7 is thrown in parenthetically between the sixth and seventh seals.

  > "The sixth chapter closed with the events of the sixth seal, and the seventh seal is not mentioned until we reach the opening of chapter 8. The whole of chapter 7 is therefore thrown in here parenthetically. Why is it thus thrown in at this point? — Evidently for the purpose of stating additional particulars concerning the sixth seal." — DAR 435.2.

  > "The seventh chapter stands parenthetically between the sixth and seventh seals, from which it appears that the sealing work of that chapter belongs to the sixth seal." — DAR 452.3.

- **Revelation 7:2** — "I saw another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God." The sealing message arises in the closing scenes of the sixth seal — the very era in which we now live.

  > "It comes in, therefore, between the 13th and 14th verses of Revelation 6; but there, as already shown, is just where we now stand. Hence the first part of Revelation 7 relates to a work the accomplishment of which may be looked for at the present time." — DAR 435.

- **Revelation 7:3** — "Hurt not the earth... till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads." The winds of destruction are held until God's people are sealed; this sealing work belongs to the sixth seal, just before the great day of wrath.

  > "The seventh chapter stands parenthetically between the sixth and seventh seals, from which it appears that the sealing work of that chapter belongs to the sixth seal." — DAR 452.3.

- **Revelation 14:1** — The 144,000 stand "on the mount Sion... having his Father's name written in their foreheads" — the sealed company of Revelation 7, the fruit of the sealing held back by the four angels.

  > "It must transpire before the departing of the heavens as a scroll; for after that event there is no place for such a work as this. And it must take place subsequently to the signs in the sun, moon, and stars; for these signs have been fulfilled, and such a work has not yet been accomplished." — DAR 435.

### Seventh seal — SILENCE IN HEAVEN: the King appears

- **Revelation 8:1** — "And when he had opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven about the space of half an hour." The series is here resumed and concluded: the sixth seal stopped just short of the second advent, so the personal appearing of Christ is allotted to the seventh. When the Lord descends with all the holy angels — when the heavenly harpers leave the courts above to come down with their Lord — there is silence in heaven.

  > "The series of seven seals is here resumed and concluded.... But the seal stops just short of that event. The personal appearing of Christ must therefore be allotted to the next seal.... And when all the heavenly harpers leave the courts above to come down with their divine Lord, as he descends to gather the fruit of his redeeming work, will there not be silence in heaven?" — DAR 452.2-4.

- **Matthew 25:31** — "When the Son of man shall come in his glory, and all the holy angels with him." The emptying of heaven's courts at the advent is the cause of the half-hour silence of the seventh seal.

  > "But when the Lord appears, he comes with all the holy angels with him. Matthew 25:31. And when all the heavenly harpers leave the courts above to come down with their divine Lord... will there not be silence in heaven?" — DAR 452.4.

### The seals view the era as PROVIDENCE, not spiritual state

- **Zechariah 6:1-8** — The four chariots with red, black, white, and "grisled and bay" horses — "the four spirits of the heavens, which go forth from standing before the Lord of all the earth" — show that horses of differing color are a Scriptural symbol of successive providential agencies sent forth over the earth.

  > "Perhaps the first noticeable feature in these symbols is the contrast in the color of the horses. This is doubtless designed to be significant." — DAR 404.

- **Seals vs. churches** — The seven churches (Rev. 2–3) describe the inward spiritual condition of God's people; the seven seals describe the outward providence — the church's fortunes and the world's history — over the very same Christian centuries. The trumpets, again, run contemporaneously but trace political war among the nations.

  > "The trumpets denote a series of events which transpire contemporaneously with the events of the seals, but of an entirely different character. A trumpet is a symbol of war; hence the trumpets denote great political commotions to take place among the nations during the gospel age. The seals denote events of a religious character, and contain the history of the church from the opening of the Christian era to the coming of Christ." — DAR 402.2.
