# 21. The Law and the Sabbath in Prophecy

> _The last great controversy is fought over the law of God, and over its heart, the Sabbath. When the temple in heaven is opened, the ark — the receptacle of the ten commandments — is seen (Rev. 11:19), and the law is shown unchanged. The dragon makes war on the remnant who "keep the commandments of God" (Rev. 12:17; 14:12); the little horn "thought to change times and laws" (Dan. 7:25) — and the only law it dared touch was the fourth commandment, tearing out the Sabbath and erecting Sunday in its place. The Sabbath is the seal of God and the memorial of creation, restored in the first angel's call to "worship him that made heaven, and earth" (Rev. 14:7; Isa. 58). The commandment-keepers stand at the close of probation. (DAR 140-141, 627-628; GC 433-454, 640; EW 32-33.)_

### The ark in the heavenly temple holds the law

- **Revelation 11:19** — "The temple of God was opened in heaven, and there was seen in his temple the ark of his testament." The ark, by definition, is the receptacle of the law; the heavenly sanctuary contains the great original of which Sinai's tables were a copy. This opening marks the beginning of the judgment, when attention is drawn back to God's law. _(GC 433.1-2.)_
- **Hebrews 9:4** — The earthly ark held "the tables of the covenant" — the ten commandments. The ark's value was solely as the place of the law; so the opening of the heavenly ark sets the Decalogue before the church. _(GC 433.2.)_
- **Hebrews 8:5** — The earthly priests "serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things." The pattern was shown Moses in the mount; the law in the heavenly ark is "the great original," its earthly transcript unerring. _(GC 434.1.)_
- **Revelation 15:5** — "The temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven was opened." The sanctuary is called the place of "the testimony" — another name for the law of God — confirming the law stands at the center of heaven's worship. _(GC 433.1.)_
- **Early Writings (EW 32-33)** — In vision the ark was seen, the tables opened, "the ten commandments written... with the finger of God," and "the fourth, the Sabbath commandment, shone above them all... a halo of glory was all around it." The Sabbath was not nailed to the cross; "the pope had changed it... for he was to change times and laws." _(EW 32.3-33.1.)_

### The controversy is over the commandments

- **Revelation 12:17** — "The dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." The last church is marked by commandment-keeping; this is the very point of Satan's final assault. _(DAR 516.3.)_
- **Revelation 14:12** — "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." The fruit of the threefold message is a commandment-keeping company — distinguished, of necessity, by the seventh-day Sabbath. _(DAR 627.3; GC 453.3.)_
- **Revelation 22:14** — "Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city." Obedience to the commandments is the condition of entrance to the city of God — the law's authority reaches to the very end. _(Cf. DAR 627.3.)_
- **Ecclesiastes 12:13** — "Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man." The whole of human duty is bound up in the commandments; the controversy turns on whether men will obey or transgress them. _(Cf. GC 452.2.)_
- **John 14:15** — "If ye love me, keep my commandments." Christ makes obedience the test of love; the remnant's commandment-keeping is no legalism but loyalty to the Lawgiver. _(Cf. GC 453.3.)_

### The little horn thought to change the law — and touched the Sabbath

- **Daniel 7:25** — The little horn "shall... think to change times and laws." These are not human laws (which it could change at will) but the laws of the Most High — which it could only _think_ to change, not truly change. The papacy expunged the second commandment, divided the tenth, and tore the Sabbath from the fourth, erecting Sunday in its place. _(DAR 140.3-141.1.)_
- **Daniel 7:8** — The little horn rose "among" the ten horns with "a mouth speaking great things." Identified as the papacy (538-1798), this is the power that presumed to alter the Decalogue — the "great words against the most High." _(DAR 140.2-3.)_
- **Daniel 7:25 (with 2 Thess. 2)** — Daniel's little horn and Paul's "man of sin" are complements: "Only by changing God's law could the papacy exalt itself above God." The change of the Sabbath is precisely how that self-exaltation was accomplished. _(GC 446.1.)_
- **Isaiah 24:5** — "They have transgressed the laws, changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant." Scripture foretells a transgression that _changes_ an ordinance — fulfilled in the substitution of Sunday for the Sabbath of the everlasting covenant. _(Cf. GC 446.2.)_
- **The Catholic catechism** — Compared with the Bible, the catechisms drop the second commandment, split the tenth, and rewrite the fourth to enjoin Sunday — Rome openly claiming the change was "made by the church," not by God. _(DAR 554.1-2.)_

### The law of God is perpetual and unchangeable

- **Matthew 5:17-18** — "Think not that I am come to destroy the law... Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law." Christ did not abolish but established the law; its smallest stroke stands while heaven and earth endure. _(GC 434.1.)_
- **Psalm 111:7-8** — "All his commandments are sure. They stand fast for ever and ever." The commandments are not temporary ordinances but eternal verities — incapable of being changed by any earthly power. _(Cf. GC 434.1.)_
- **Psalm 89:34** — "My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips." God Himself will not alter His word; the claim that He transferred the Sabbath's sanctity to another day contradicts His own declaration. _(EW 33.1.)_
- **Psalm 119:142** — "Thy law is the truth." The law is identified with truth itself; to change it is to corrupt the truth — the very work charged to the little horn. _(Cf. GC 434.1.)_
- **James 2:10-12** — "Whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is guilty of all." The Decalogue is an indivisible unity; to break the fourth commandment is to break the whole — so men "shall be judged by the law of liberty." _(Cf. EW 33.1.)_
- **Romans 3:31** — "Do we then make void the law through faith? God forbid: yea, we establish the law." The gospel does not annul the law but upholds it; faith and obedience stand together. _(Waggoner / Jones, the law and the gospel.)_
- **Romans 7:7** — "I had not known sin, but by the law." The law remains the standard that defines sin; were it abolished, sin itself would be undefinable. _(Cf. GC 467-468.)_

### The Sabbath: memorial of creation and seal of God

- **Genesis 2:1-3** — "God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work." Instituted at creation, before sin and before Israel, the Sabbath is the world's first memorial and belongs to all mankind. _(GC 453.1.)_
- **Exodus 20:8-11** — "Remember the sabbath day... the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God... For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth... and rested the seventh day." The fourth commandment alone names and titles the Lawgiver as Creator — making it the seal of His law. _(GC 434.2; 452.1.)_
- **Mark 2:27-28** — "The sabbath was made for man... therefore the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath." Made for man at creation, the Sabbath is Christ's own institution; He is its Lord, not its abolisher. _(Cf. GC 446.3.)_
- **Luke 23:56** — The women "rested the sabbath day according to the commandment." Even across the crucifixion the Sabbath stood unchanged — Christ resting in the tomb on the seventh day, His followers honoring it "according to the commandment." _(Cf. EW 33.1.)_
- **Ezekiel 20:12** — "I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them." The Sabbath is God's appointed sign of sanctification — a sign that identifies His people. _(Bates, THB 44.1.)_
- **Ezekiel 20:20** — "Hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign between me and you, that ye may know that I am the LORD your God." Repeated for emphasis: the Sabbath is the token by which God's worshipers know Him from all others. _(Bates, THB 44.1-2.)_
- **Isaiah 8:16** — "Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples." The seal of God's law is found in the fourth commandment — the only precept bearing the name, title, and dominion of the Lawgiver, the Creator. _(GC 452.1.)_
- **Sign and seal are one** — "Sign and seal are synonymous terms" (Rom. 4:11). The Sabbath being the sign between God and His people, it seals His law as genuine; "take this fourth commandment from the ten, and the seal of the living God is gone." _(Bates, THB 44.2.)_

### The Sabbath restored in the last message — the final test

- **Revelation 14:7** — "Worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters." The first angel's call to worship the Creator is in the very language of the fourth commandment — a summons back to the Sabbath as the judgment hour strikes. _(GC 437-438; DAR 585.3.)_
- **Isaiah 58:12-14** — "Thou shalt be called, The repairer of the breach... if thou turn away thy foot from the sabbath... and call the sabbath a delight." A last-day people repair the breach made in God's law by restoring the trodden-down Sabbath — the very work of the third angel's message. _(GC 452.4-453.)_
- **Ezekiel 22:26** — "They have... hid their eyes from my sabbaths, and I am profaned among them." The priests' sin was profaning the holy and hiding their eyes from the Sabbath; the last message exposes and reverses that apostasy. _(Cf. Isa. 58:13.)_
- **Isaiah 66:22-23** — "As the new heavens and the new earth... so shall your seed and your name remain. And it shall come to pass... from one sabbath to another, shall all flesh come to worship before me." The Sabbath outlasts this world and is kept in the earth made new — the final proof of its perpetuity. _(Cf. GC 453.1.)_
- **The line of demarcation** — The third angel's message brings its adherents to the seventh day, the only mark that distinguishes them; "no plainer line of demarcation could be drawn between two classes" — Sabbath-keepers and Sunday-keepers. _(DAR 627.3-628.1.)_
- **Two seals, two marks** — God's seal is His Sabbath; the beast's mark is its rival sabbath, Sunday. "Christendom will at last be divided into just two classes" — those sealed with the seal of the living God and those who receive the mark. _(DAR 628.2.)_
- **The seal of the living God** — "Too late they see that the Sabbath of the fourth commandment is the seal of the living God." In the final crisis the Sabbath becomes the great test of loyalty, distinguishing the worshipers of God from the worshipers of the beast. _(GC 640.1; DAR 627-628.)_
- **Worship Christ, keep His commandments** — "If thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments" (Matt. 19:17). The Lord who is worshiped as Creator in the first message is the Lord who points men to obedience as the way of life — the law and the gospel meeting in the Sabbath. _(Cf. GC 453.3.)_
