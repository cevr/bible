# 26. The Close of Probation and the Time of Jacob's Trouble

> _When the investigative judgment ends, every case is forever decided — "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... he that is holy, let him be holy still." Christ lays down the censer, ceases His intercession, and Michael stands up; the temple fills with smoke and no man can enter till the seven last plagues are poured out. God's people then pass through the time of Jacob's trouble — without a Mediator, yet hidden under the shadow of the Almighty, standing through the death decree, and delivered at last by the voice of God. (GC 490; GC 613-634; GC 635-652; EW 279-285; DAR 293-318.)_

### Probation Closes — Every Case Decided

- **Revelation 22:11** — "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... he that is holy, let him be holy still." When the work of the investigative judgment closes, the destiny of all is fixed for life or death; mercy no longer pleads. This is the sentence Christ utters as probation ends, a short time before His appearing.

  > "When the work of the investigative judgment closes, the destiny of all will have been decided for life or death. Probation is ended a short time before the appearing of the Lord in the clouds of heaven." — GC 490.2.

  > "When the third angel's message closes, mercy no longer pleads for the guilty inhabitants of the earth.... Then Jesus ceases His intercession in the sanctuary above.... Every case has been decided for life or death." — GC 613.2.

- **Revelation 22:12** — "Behold, I come quickly; and my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be." Character is fixed before He comes; the reward is already apportioned because probation has closed.

  > "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... and he that is holy, let him be holy still. And, behold, I come quickly; and My reward is with Me, to give every man according as his work shall be." — GC 490.2.

- **Daniel 12:1** — "At that time shall Michael stand up... and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was." Michael, the Archangel — Christ Himself — rises from His mediatorial throne; His "standing up" marks the close of His priestly intercession and ushers in the unequalled time of trouble.

  > "And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people: and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time." — DAR 293.1.

  > "Who, then, is Michael? and what is his standing up? — Michael is called, in Jude 9, the archangel.... But the archangel is Michael; hence also Michael is the Son of God. The expression of Daniel, 'the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people,' is alone sufficient to identify the one here spoken of as the Saviour of men." — DAR 294.1.

  > "'At that time shall Michael stand up, the great Prince which standeth for the children of thy people: and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time.' Daniel 12:1." — GC 613.1.

- **Daniel 12:10** — "Many shall be purified, and made white, and tried; but the wicked shall do wickedly... but the wise shall understand." The decision is moral and irreversible: the righteous are purified and sealed, the wicked are confirmed in evil, before probation shuts.

  > "Here are the events, to come, as we believe, in the following order: ... (4) The standing up of Michael, or the beginning of the reign of Christ, and his coming in the clouds of heaven." — DAR 293.2.

  > "Every case had been decided for life or death.... Christ had received His kingdom, having made the atonement for His people and blotted out their sins. The subjects of the kingdom were made up." — EW 280.1.

### The Censer Laid Down — No More Intercession

- **Hebrews 9:24** — Christ "now to appear in the presence of God for us." His present work is intercession in the heavenly sanctuary; when that ministry ends, the saints must stand without an Advocate.

  > "Then Jesus ceases His intercession in the sanctuary above. He lifts His hands and with a loud voice says, 'It is done;' ... Every case has been decided for life or death." — GC 613.2.

  > "While Jesus had been standing between God and guilty man, a restraint was upon the people; but when He stepped out from between man and the Father, the restraint was removed.... In that fearful time, after the close of Jesus' mediation, the saints were living in the sight of a holy God without an intercessor." — EW 280.2.

- **Revelation 8:3-5** — The angel offers incense "with the prayers of all saints" — then "took the censer, and filled it with fire of the altar, and cast it into the earth." The casting down of the censer pictures the close of intercession; voices, thunders, and an earthquake follow, the prelude to the plagues.

  > "Then I saw Jesus, who had been ministering before the ark containing the ten commandments, throw down the censer. He raised His hands, and with a loud voice said, 'It is done.'" — EW 279.2.

  > "As Jesus moved out of the most holy place, I heard the tinkling of the bells upon His garment; and as He left, a cloud of darkness covered the inhabitants of the earth. There was then no mediator between guilty man and an offended God." — EW 280.2.

- **Revelation 15:8** — "The temple was filled with smoke... and no man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues... were fulfilled." Once mercy's door shuts, no intercession can be made in the sanctuary; the saints must live "without an intercessor."

  > "When He leaves the sanctuary, darkness covers the inhabitants of the earth. In that fearful time the righteous must live in the sight of a holy God without an intercessor.... The wicked have passed the boundary of their probation." — GC 614.1.

  > "It was impossible for the plagues to be poured out while Jesus officiated in the sanctuary; but as His work there is finished, and His intercession closes, there is nothing to stay the wrath of God." — EW 280.2.

### The Plagues Fall — Wrath Without Mixture

- **Revelation 16:1-2** — "Go your ways, and pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth." The first vial brings "a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast." Probation being past, the plagues fall on those who chose the beast over God's seal.

  > "Many of the wicked were greatly enraged as they suffered the effects of the plagues. It was a scene of fearful agony.... Loud, wailing cries were heard in every direction, 'It was you who kept me from receiving the truth which would have saved me from this awful hour.'" — EW 282.1.

  > "Christ has made the atonement for His people and blotted out their sins.... Every case has been decided for life or death." — GC 613.2.

- **Revelation 16:8-11** — The fourth vial scorches men with fire; the fifth fills the beast's kingdom with darkness; yet they "blasphemed the name of God... and they repented not." The plagues are unmixed wrath — no mercy is mingled, because probation has closed and no repentance follows.

  > "The people turned upon their ministers with bitter hate and reproached them, saying, 'You have not warned us....' But I saw that the ministers did not escape the wrath of God. Their suffering was tenfold greater than that of their people." — EW 282.1.

  > "The wicked have passed the boundary of their probation; the Spirit of God, persistently resisted, has been at last withdrawn. Unsheltered by divine grace, they have no protection from the wicked one." — GC 614.1.

- **Revelation 14:9-10** — Whoever worships the beast "shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation." The third angel's warning defines who receives the plagues: the worshipers of the beast and his image.

  > "When the third angel's message closes, mercy no longer pleads for the guilty inhabitants of the earth.... the final test has been brought upon the world, and all who have proved themselves loyal to the divine precepts have received 'the seal of the living God.'" — GC 613.2.

  > "While Jesus had been ministering in the sanctuary, the judgment had been going on for the righteous dead, and then for the righteous living.... The subjects of the kingdom were made up." — EW 280.1.

### The Time of Jacob's Trouble

- **Jeremiah 30:5-7** — "A voice of trembling, of fear, and not of peace... all faces are turned into paleness... it is even the time of Jacob's trouble; but he shall be saved out of it." The prophet names this final crisis of God's people — anguish unequalled, yet ending in deliverance.

  > "The people of God will then be plunged into those scenes of affliction and distress described by the prophet as the time of Jacob's trouble.... 'it is even the time of Jacob's trouble; but he shall be saved out of it.' Jeremiah 30:5-7." — GC 616.1.

- **Genesis 32:24-28** — Jacob wrestles alone till break of day: "I will not let thee go, except thou bless me." His night of agony, pleading the covenant while in deadly peril from Esau, typifies the saints wrestling for deliverance in the time of trouble.

  > "Jacob's night of anguish, when he wrestled in prayer for deliverance from the hand of Esau (Genesis 32:24-30), represents the experience of God's people in the time of trouble.... His only hope was in the mercy of God; his only defense must be prayer." — GC 616.2.

- **Daniel 12:1** — "Such as never was since there was a nation" — the trouble is unparalleled, falling after Michael stands up; yet "thy people shall be delivered, every one... found written in the book." The same verse that opens the trouble guarantees the deliverance.

  > "And at that time shall Michael stand up... and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time: and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book." — DAR 293.1.

  > "'... and at that time thy people shall be delivered, everyone that shall be found written in the book.' Daniel 12:1." — GC 613.1.

- **Revelation 13:15** — The image of the beast causes "that as many as would not worship the image... should be killed." The death decree against the commandment-keepers is the sharpest edge of Jacob's trouble.

  > "A decree will finally be issued against those who hallow the Sabbath of the fourth commandment, denouncing them as deserving of the severest punishment and giving the people liberty, after a certain time, to put them to death." — GC 615.2.

  > "Though a general decree has fixed the time when commandment keepers may be put to death, their enemies will in some cases anticipate the decree, and before the time specified, will endeavor to take their lives. But none can pass the mighty guardians stationed about every faithful soul." — GC 631.1.

### Famine of the Word — The Wicked's Despair

- **Amos 8:11-12** — "A famine... not of bread... but of hearing the words of the LORD: and they shall... run to and fro to seek the word of the LORD, and shall not find it." When probation closes, the Spirit is withdrawn from the impenitent; they seek and cannot find.

  > "The wicked have passed the boundary of their probation; the Spirit of God, persistently resisted, has been at last withdrawn. Unsheltered by divine grace, they have no protection from the wicked one." — GC 614.1.

  > "This argument will appear conclusive; and a decree will finally be issued against those who hallow the Sabbath of the fourth commandment... giving the people liberty, after a certain time, to put them to death." — GC 615.2.

- **Ezekiel 7:19** — "Their silver and their gold shall not be able to deliver them in the day of the wrath of the LORD... because it is the stumblingblock of their iniquity." Riches are worthless when the plagues fall; nothing can shield the lost in that day.

  > "God's long-suffering has ended. The world has rejected His mercy, despised His love, and trampled upon His law.... Unsheltered by divine grace, they have no protection from the wicked one." — GC 614.1.

- **Proverbs 11:4** — "Riches profit not in the day of wrath: but righteousness delivereth from death." Only the imputed righteousness of Christ, received before probation closed, avails in the time of trouble.

  > "Christ had received His kingdom, having made the atonement for His people and blotted out their sins.... And the kingdom, and the greatness of the kingdom under the whole heaven, was given to Jesus and the heirs of salvation." — EW 280.1.

### God's People Hidden and Kept

- **Psalm 91:1-11** — "He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty... There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling." Though the plagues rage and "a thousand shall fall at thy side," the sealed are sheltered by God Himself.

  > "When He leaves the sanctuary, darkness covers the inhabitants of the earth.... Satan will then plunge the inhabitants of the earth into one great, final trouble." — GC 614.1.

  > "I saw the saints leaving the cities and villages, and associating together in companies, and living in the most solitary places. Angels provided them food and water, while the wicked were suffering from hunger and thirst." — EW 282.2.

- **Psalm 91:12-16** — "He shall give his angels charge over thee... I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him." Angels provide food and water for the saints in their solitary retreats while the wicked perish of hunger and thirst.

  > "Angels provided them food and water, while the wicked were suffering from hunger and thirst.... But in this hour of trial the saints were calm and composed, trusting in God and leaning upon His promise that a way of escape would be made for them." — EW 282.2.

  > "The people of God—some in prison cells, some hidden in solitary retreats in the forests and the mountains—still plead for divine protection.... It is now, in the hour of utmost extremity, that the God of Israel will interpose for the deliverance of His chosen." — GC 635.2.

- **Psalm 27:5** — "In the time of trouble he shall hide me in his pavilion... he shall set me up upon a rock." God's hiding of His people through the plagues is a settled covenant promise.

  > "The people of God—some in prison cells, some hidden in solitary retreats in the forests and the mountains—still plead for divine protection.... It is now, in the hour of utmost extremity, that the God of Israel will interpose for the deliverance of His chosen." — GC 635.2.

- **Isaiah 26:20-21** — "Come, my people, enter thou into thy chambers... hide thyself... until the indignation be overpast. For, behold, the LORD cometh out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth." The saints are sheltered while wrath is poured out; then the Lord comes forth to judge.

  > "The people of God... still plead for divine protection, while in every quarter companies of armed men, urged on by hosts of evil angels, are preparing for the work of death. It is now, in the hour of utmost extremity, that the God of Israel will interpose for the deliverance of His chosen." — GC 635-636.

- **Zephaniah 2:3** — "Seek ye the LORD, all ye meek of the earth... it may be ye shall be hid in the day of the LORD'S anger." The call to seek righteousness and meekness now is the only preparation for being hid then.

  > "While Jesus had been ministering in the sanctuary, the judgment had been going on for the righteous dead, and then for the righteous living.... The marriage of the Lamb was consummated." — EW 280.1.

### The Death Decree and the Cry Day and Night

- **Revelation 13:15** — The image speaks and decrees death for the loyal. The "general decree" fixes a time when commandment-keepers may be slain — the supreme test of Jacob's trouble.

  > "Though a general decree has fixed the time when commandment keepers may be put to death, their enemies will in some cases anticipate the decree.... But none can pass the mighty guardians stationed about every faithful soul." — GC 631.1.

  > "When the protection of human laws shall be withdrawn from those who honor the law of God, there will be, in different lands, a simultaneous movement for their destruction.... It will be determined to strike in one night a decisive blow." — GC 635.1.

- **Luke 18:7-8** — "Shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him... I tell you that he will avenge them speedily." The saints cry to God under the death decree; He delays long but delivers suddenly. The closing question — "shall he find faith on the earth?" — measures the faith demanded of those who endure.

  > "Some are assailed in their flight from the cities and villages; but the swords raised against them break and fall powerless as a straw. Others are defended by angels in the form of men of war." — GC 631.1.

  > "The people of God... still plead for divine protection.... It is now, in the hour of utmost extremity, that the God of Israel will interpose for the deliverance of His chosen." — GC 635.2.

### The Deliverance — The Voice of God

- **Isaiah 26:21** — "The LORD cometh out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth." At the appointed hour of the death decree, God interposes; the deliverance of His people is the destruction of their enemies.

  > "When the protection of human laws shall be withdrawn from those who honor the law of God, there will be, in different lands, a simultaneous movement for their destruction. As the time appointed in the decree draws near, the people will conspire to root out the hated sect." — GC 635.1.

  > "By the people of God a voice, clear and melodious, is heard, saying, 'Look up,' and lifting their eyes to the heavens, they behold the bow of promise.... 'They come! they come! holy, harmless, and undefiled.'" — GC 636.1.

- **Revelation 16:17-18** — The seventh vial brings "a great voice out of the temple of heaven, from the throne, saying, It is done," with "a great earthquake, such as was not since men were upon the earth." At that voice God declares the day and hour of Jesus' coming, delivers the everlasting covenant to His people, and the captives are loosed.

  > "Again a voice, musical and triumphant, is heard, saying: 'They come! they come! holy, harmless, and undefiled. They have kept the word of My patience; they shall walk among the angels;' and the pale, quivering lips of those who have held fast their faith utter a shout of victory." — GC 636.1.

  > "The voice of God is heard from heaven, declaring the day and hour of Jesus' coming, and delivering the everlasting covenant to His people. Like peals of loudest thunder His words roll through the earth." — GC 640.2.

- **Daniel 12:1** — "At that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book." The trouble ends as it began — at Michael's word; the same Prince who closed probation now delivers His own.

  > "And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people... and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book." — DAR 293.1.

  > "It is now, in the hour of utmost extremity, that the God of Israel will interpose for the deliverance of His chosen.... 'And the Lord shall cause His glorious voice to be heard, and shall show the lighting down of His arm.'" — GC 635.

- **Jeremiah 30:7** — "But he shall be saved out of it." The promise that closes the Jacob's-trouble prophecy is deliverance; the night of anguish breaks into the day of redemption.

  > "The people of God will then be plunged into those scenes of affliction and distress described by the prophet as the time of Jacob's trouble.... 'it is even the time of Jacob's trouble; but he shall be saved out of it.' Jeremiah 30:5-7." — GC 616.1.

  > "And when the blessing is pronounced on those who have honored God by keeping His Sabbath holy, there is a mighty shout of victory." — GC 640.2.
