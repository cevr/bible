# 7. The Daily, and the 1290 and 1335 Days

> _The deeper time-lines of Daniel 11 and 12 hang upon one disputed word — "the daily" (Heb. tamid, the continual). It is not the Jewish sacrifice, but the continual desolating power, paganism, which had to be taken away to make room for the papal "abomination that maketh desolate." That removal was accomplished in A.D. 508; from it run the 1290 years to 1798 (508 + 1290) and the 1335 years to 1843/44 (508 + 1335). The "time of the end" opens in 1798 with the wounding of the papacy, and the blessing on him "that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days" fell upon the advent believers of 1843-44. (DAR 154-157, 254-258, 313-316; GC 356, 439.)_

### "The Daily" — the continual abomination is paganism, not sacrifice

- **Daniel 8:11** — The little horn "magnified himself even to the prince of the host, and by him the daily was taken away." The word "sacrifice" is supplied by the translators; the act belongs to Rome in both phases, pagan and papal.

  > "This little horn must be understood to symbolize Rome in its entire history, including its two phases, pagan and papal. These two phases are elsewhere spoken of as the 'daily' (sacrifice is a supplied word) and the 'transgression of desolation;' the daily (desolation) signifying the pagan form, and the transgression of desolation, the papal.… 'By him' (the papal form) 'the daily' (the pagan form) 'was taken away.' Pagan Rome was remodeled into papal Rome." — DAR 154.

- **Daniel 8:12** — "An host was given him against the daily by reason of transgression." The barbarians who overran Rome were themselves converted to the Catholic faith and became "the instruments of the dethronement of their former religion," perpetuating the empire in another form.

  > "The barbarians that subverted the Roman empire in the changes, attritions, and transformations of those times, became converts to the Catholic faith, and the instruments of the dethronement of their former religion. Though conquering Rome politically, they were themselves vanquished religiously by the theology of Rome, and became the perpetrators of the same empire in another phase." — DAR 155.

- **Daniel 8:13** — "How long shall be the vision concerning the daily, and the transgression of desolation?" Two desolating powers are set side by side — the daily (paganism) and the transgression of desolation (the papacy) — and the question asks how long both shall tread down the sanctuary.

  > "These two verses close the vision proper of chapter 8; and they introduce the one remaining point which of all others would naturally be of the most absorbing interest to the prophet and to all the church; namely, the time the desolating powers previously brought to view were to continue. How long shall they continue their course of oppression against God's people, and of blasphemy against high Heaven?" — DAR 156.

  > "Then we have two desolating powers, which for a long period oppress, or desolate the church.… By the 'continuance of desolation,' or the perpetual desolation, we must understand that paganism, through all its long history, is meant; and by 'the transgression of desolation' is meant the papacy." — DAR 157.

- **The word itself** — "Daily" (tamid) occurs in the Old Testament about one hundred and two times, "and is, in the great majority of instances, rendered continual or continually. The idea of sacrifice does not attach to the word at all." Hence "the continual" = paganism, the continual desolating power.

  > "The word here rendered daily occurs in the Old Testament, according to the Hebrew Concordance, one hundred and two times, and is, in the great majority of instances, rendered continual or continually. The idea of sacrifice does not attach to the word at all. Nor is there any word in the text which signifies sacrifice; that is wholly a supplied word." — DAR 157.

### The taking away of the daily — A.D. 508

- **Daniel 11:31** — "And arms shall stand on his part, and they shall pollute the sanctuary of strength, and shall take away the daily, and they shall place the abomination that maketh desolate." The taking away of paganism is the historical key to both the 1290 and 1335 days.

  > "The power of the empire was committed to the carrying on of the work before mentioned. 'And they shall pollute the sanctuary of strength,' or Rome.… it would signify the removal of the seat of empire from Rome to Constantinople, which contributed its measure of influence to the downfall of Rome. The passage would then be parallel to Daniel 8:11 and Revelation 13:2." — DAR 254.

- **Conversion of Clovis, 496** — Until the conversion of Clovis, king of France, "the French and other nations of Western Rome were pagan; but subsequently to that event, the efforts to convert idolaters to Romanism were crowned with great success." This prepared the crisis of 508.

  > "As we approach the year A. D. 508, we behold a grand crisis ripening between Catholicism and the pagan influences still existing in the empire. Up to the time of the conversion of Clovis, king of France, A. D. 496, the French and other nations of Western Rome were pagan; but subsequently to that event, the efforts to convert idolaters to Romanism were crowned with great success." — DAR 255.

- **508 — paganism subdued** — "From the time when these successes were fully accomplished, namely, 508, the papacy was triumphant so far as paganism was concerned." In that very year a Christian king (Arthur) was crowned in Britain, and at Constantinople the Catholic party for the first time "waged a successful war against both the civil authority of the empire and the church of the East."

  > "From the time when these successes were fully accomplished, namely, 508, the papacy was triumphant so far as paganism was concerned.… In England, Arthur, the first Christian king, founded the Christian worship on the ruins of the pagan.… he was elected monarch of Britain in 508." — DAR 256.

  > "Let it be marked that in this year, 508, paganism had so far declined, and Catholicism had so far relatively increased in strength, that the Catholic Church for the first time waged a successful war against both the civil authority of the empire and the church of the East, which had for the most part embraced the Monophysite doctrine. The extermination of 65,000 heretics was the result." — DAR 257.

- **The mark of the year** — "In 508 the mine is sprung beneath the throne of the Eastern empire." Paganism, as a controlling power, was broken; the way lay open for the papal abomination to be set up thirty years later, in 538.

  > "In 508 the mine is sprung beneath the throne of the Eastern empire. The result of the confusion and strife it occasions is the humiliation of its rightful lord. Now the question is, At what time was paganism so far suppressed as to make room for its substitute and successor, the papal abomination?… Is there any other date for its being 'placed,' or 'set up,' in the room of paganism but 508?" — DAR 258.

### The 1290 days (years) — 508 to 1798

- **Daniel 12:11** — "And from the time that the daily shall be taken away, and the abomination that maketh desolate set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days." The margin reads "to set up the abomination," fixing the start at the removal of paganism.

  > "The margin reads, 'To set up the abomination,' etc. With this reading the text would stand thus: 'And from the time that the daily sacrifice shall be taken away to set up [or in order to set up] the abomination that maketh desolate, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days.'" — DAR 313.

- **The reckoning** — "The daily has already been shown to be... the daily or continual abomination, that is, paganism.... For the historical events showing how this was accomplished in 508, see on chapter 11:31.... Counting back, then, 1290 years from 1798, we have the year 508, where it has been shown that paganism was taken away, thirty years before the setting up of the papacy." 508 + 1290 = 1798.

  > "The daily has already been shown to be, not the daily sacrifice of the Jews, but the daily or continual abomination, that is, paganism. (See on chapter 8:13.) This had to be taken away to prepare the way for the papacy.… Counting back, then, 1290 years from 1798, we have the year 508, where it has been shown that paganism was taken away, thirty years before the setting up of the papacy." — DAR 313.

- **Both periods end together** — "The two periods, therefore, the 1290 and the 1260 days, terminate together in 1798, the one beginning in 538, and the other in 508, thirty years previous."

  > "The two periods, therefore, the 1290 and the 1260 days, terminate together in 1798, the one beginning in 538, and the other in 508, thirty years previous." — DAR 313-314.

- **Daniel 12:7** — The man clothed in linen sware "that it shall be for a time, times, and an half." This is the 1260 years of papal supremacy (538-1798); "this period... began with the supremacy of the papacy, A.D. 538, and terminated in 1798." Its close fixes the inner edge of the time of the end.

  > "The forty and two months are the same as the 'time and times and the dividing of time,' three years and a half, or 1260 days, of Daniel 7—the time during which the papal power was to oppress God's people. This period, as stated in preceding chapters, began with the supremacy of the papacy, A.D. 538, and terminated in 1798. At that time the pope was made captive by the French army, the papal power received its deadly wound." — GC 439.

- **Miller's summary** — "His 1290 days, beginning when the ten kings... should be converted to the Christian faith, and the daily sacrifice abomination taken out of the way, viz., A.D. 508, which would end us in 1798, when the Pope lost his power to reign over the kings... by being deprived of his civil power by Bonaparte."

  > "By the numbers given in Daniel 12:11-13, his 1290 days, beginning when the ten kings, represented by the ten toes in Nebuchadnezzar's dream, and ten horns in Daniel's vision, should be converted to the Christian faith, and the daily sacrifice abomination taken out of the way, viz, A. D. 508, which would end us in 1798, when the Pope lost his power to reign over the kings and trample on the holy people, and the abomination of desolation ceased his civil reign, by being deprived of his civil power by Bonaparte." — MWV2 113.

### The 1335 days (years) — 508 to 1843, and the blessing on those who wait

- **Daniel 12:12** — "Blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days." This period begins from the same point as the 1290 — 508 — for it is "spoken of in immediate connection with the 1290 years, which commenced... in 508." 508 + 1335 = 1843.

  > "The only clue we have to the solution of this question, is the fact that it is spoken of in immediate connection with the 1290 years, which commenced, as shown above, in 508.… 'Blessed is he that waiteth and cometh to the 1335 days.' From what point? — From the same point, undoubtedly, as that from which the 1290 date; namely, 508.… From this point they would extend to 1843; for 1335 added to 508 make 1843." — DAR 314.

- **Miller's reckoning** — "I then showed you that the number 1335 days, beginning at the same time as the 1290 days, viz. A.D. 508, would end in 1843.... this brought us to the same year that Daniel's whole number, 2300, brought us." The forty-five years' difference between 1290 and 1335 spans 1798 to 1843.

  > "I then showed you that the number 1335 days, beginning at the same time as the 1290 days, viz. A. D. 508, would end in 1843, at the resurrection, for Daniel would stand in his lot at the end of these days. And you have undoubtedly notice that this brought us to the same year that Daniel's whole number, 2300, brought us, which is forty-five years, the difference between the two numbers, 1290 and 1335." — MWV2 113.

  > "Therefore take 1335 years from 1810 years, would leave 475 years; and he could reckon from the end of the 70 weeks, or 490 years, to the end of pagan Rome, would be 475, from thence to the time he should stand in his lot, would be 1335 years." — MWV2 100.

- **The blessing identified** — The thing promised at the close of the 1335 days "is a blessing unto those who wait and come to that time; that is, those who are then living" — the joy of receiving the advent truth. "If a new and glorious truth was a blessing in the days of Christ to those who received it, why was it not equally so in A.D. 1843?"

  > "The only thing promised at the end of the 1335 days is a blessing unto those who wait and come to that time; that is, those who are then living. What is this blessing? Looking at the year 1843, when these years expired, what do we behold? — We see a remarkable fulfillment of prophecy in the great proclamation of the second coming of Christ." — DAR 314-315.

  > "If a new and glorious truth was a blessing in the days of Christ to those who received it, why was it not equally so in A. D. 1843?" — DAR 315.

- **Marked by a prophetic period** — The advent movement was worthy to be set off by a time prophecy, "since our Lord, in verse 14 of his great prophecy of Matthew 24, makes a special announcement of this movement; and since it is still further set forth in Revelation 14:6, 7, under the symbol of an angel flying through mid-heaven."

  > "Why not, since the period in which it was to occur; namely, the time of the end, is introduced by a prophetic period; since our Lord, in verse 14 of his great prophecy of Matthew 24, makes a special announcement of this movement; and since it is still further set forth in Revelation 14:6, 7, under the symbol of an angel flying through mid-heaven with a special announcement of the everlasting gospel to the inhabitants of the earth? Surely the Bible gives great prominence to this movement." — DAR 315-316.

- **Daniel 12:13** — "But go thou thy way till the end be: for thou shalt rest, and stand in thy lot at the end of the days." The "days" here reach back to the 2300 (Dan 8:14), which "terminated in 1844, and brought us to the cleansing of the sanctuary." Daniel stands in his lot when his case is decided in the judgment, "in the person of his Advocate, our great High Priest."

  > "The 2300 days, as has been already shown, terminated in 1844, and brought us to the cleansing of the sanctuary. How did Daniel at that time stand in his lot? Answer: In the person of his Advocate, our great High Priest, as he presents the cases of the righteous for acceptance to his Father.… And when Daniel's case comes up for examination, he is found righteous, stands in his lot, is assigned a place in the heavenly Canaan." — DAR 316.

### Matthew 24:15 — Christ's own seal on Daniel's "abomination"

- **Matthew 24:15** — "When ye therefore shall see the abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet, stand in the holy place... let him understand." Christ Himself points His church back to Daniel's prophecy of the desolating powers, vouching for the prophetic time-lines and bidding the reader understand.

  > "Daniel, in the ninth chapter, speaks of desolations and abominations, plural. More than one abomination, therefore, treads down the church; that is, so far as the church is concerned, both paganism and the papacy are abominations. But as distinguished from each other, the language is restricted, and one is the 'daily' desolation, and the other is pre-eminently the transgression or 'abomination' of desolation." — DAR 255.

  > "Unless they are to be reckoned from this point, it is impossible to locate them, and they must be excepted from the prophecy of Daniel when we apply to it the words of Christ, 'Whoso readeth, let him understand.' Matthew 24:15." — DAR 314.

### "The time of the end" — opened in 1798

- **Daniel 12:4** — "Shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end: many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased." The book of Daniel was sealed only "till the time of the end," after which it would be opened and understood.

  > "'Many running to and fro.' This is another important and evident sign of the end. Daniel 12:4, 'But thou, O Daniel, shut up; the words and seal the book, even to the time of the end. Many shall run to and fro.'… All must acknowledge, that this text is remarkably fulfilled in this day." — MWV2 289.

- **The end of the 1290 fixes the date** — Because the 1290 closes the long oppression of "the holy people" in 1798, that year opens "the time of the end." From it the book of Daniel was unsealed.

  > "Counting back, then, 1290 years from 1798, we have the year 508, where it has been shown that paganism was taken away, thirty years before the setting up of the papacy.… The two periods, therefore, the 1290 and the 1260 days, terminate together in 1798, the one beginning in 538, and the other in 508, thirty years previous." — DAR 313-314.

  > "This period, as stated in preceding chapters, began with the supremacy of the papacy, A.D. 538, and terminated in 1798. At that time the pope was made captive by the French army, the papal power received its deadly wound." — GC 439.

- **GC: 1798 unsealing** — "Since 1798 the book of Daniel has been unsealed, knowledge of the prophecies has increased, and many have proclaimed the solemn message of the judgment near." Martin Luther had placed the judgment three centuries in his future; only after 1798 did the advent message go forth.

  > "The Reformers did not proclaim it. Martin Luther placed the judgment about three hundred years in the future from his day. But since 1798 the book of Daniel has been unsealed, knowledge of the prophecies has increased, and many have proclaimed the solemn message of the judgment near." — GC 356.

- **The two signs** — "Many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased." Miller marks both as evident signs of the end, "literally true; in this day of invention and improvement, knowledge increase[s]," fulfilled in the Bible, tract, and Sabbath-school societies and the worldwide advent awakening.

  > "If it means missionaries of the cross, no man can dispute the fulfillment. See the heralds of salvation crossing and re-crossing on every part of the habitable globe.… No man, unless he is wilfully ignorant, can deny that this sign is not actually and literally fulfilled." — MWV2 289.

  > "'Even to the time of the end many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased.' View this in any point you please, whether theological or scientifical, it is literally true; in this day of invention and improvement, knowledge increase. What of the fifty different moral societies, which have become general in the Christian world? Is there no increase of knowledge in our Bible societies, Sabbath schools, tract societies, temperance societies…?" — MWV2 290.

- **Daniel 12:9-10** — "The words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end.... none of the wicked shall understand; but the wise shall understand." The seal is not that the prophecy cannot be known, but that its full meaning waited for the time of the end, when the wise — the advent believers — would understand it.

  > "The time of the end was the time in which the Spirit of God was to break the seal from off this book; and consequently this was the time during which the wise should understand, while the wicked, lost to all sense of the value of eternal truth, with hearts callous and hardened in sin, would grow continually more wicked and more blind." — DAR 311-312.

  > "the words are sealed, that is, made sure, unalterable, will stand until every word has its fulfillment… not, as some suppose, that Daniel's prophecy is sealed, closed up, out of sight, and cannot be understood.… 'But the wise shall understand,' says the angel. What shall the wise understand? They shall understand the vision." — MWV2 100.

### Daniel 12:1 — Michael standing up closes the line

- **Daniel 12:1** — "And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince... and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was." "At that time" reaches back to the planting of the king of the north — the events following the time of the end. Michael is "the archangel" (Jude 9), "our Lord Jesus Christ."

  > "A DEFINITE time is introduced in this verse… 'At that time.' What time? — The time to which we are brought by the closing verse of the preceding chapter, — the time when the king of the north shall plant the tabernacles of his palace in the glorious holy mountain.… And when this event takes place, he is to come to his end; and then, according to this verse, we look for the standing up of Michael, the great prince." — DAR 293.

  > "Who, then, is Michael? and what is his standing up? — Michael is called, in Jude 9, the archangel. This means the chief angel, or the head over the angels.… The voice of the Son of God is the voice of the archangel; the archangel, then, is the Son of God. But the archangel is Michael; hence also Michael is the Son of God." — DAR 294.

- **The standing up = taking the kingdom** — As "there shall stand up yet three kings in Persia" means to reign, so Michael's standing up is His taking His own kingdom: "the work of mercy is done, and the probation of our race is ended." The deliverance of "every one that shall be found written in the book" closes the chain begun with the taking away of the daily.

  > "The key to the interpretation of this expression is furnished us in verses 2 and 3 of chapter 11: 'There shall stand up yet three kings in Persia;' 'A mighty king shall stand up, that shall reign with great dominion.'… They signify to take the kingdom, to reign.… At that time, Michael shall stand up, shall take the kingdom, shall commence his reign.… The work of mercy is done, and the probation of our race is ended." — DAR 295.

- **Daniel 12:2-3** — "Many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake... and they that be wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament." The same "wise" who understand the unsealed book at the time of the end (12:10) are raised and glorified at its end — the reward of those who waited and came to the 1335 days.

  > "From this point they would extend to 1843; for 1335 added to 508 make 1843. Commencing in the spring of the former year, they ended in the spring of the latter." — DAR 314.

  > "would not Daniel's mind naturally turn to the principal and longest period, the 2300 days, rather than to any of its subdivisions? If this is so, the 2300 are the days intended." — DAR 316.

### The day-year principle that measures these periods

- **Numbers 14:34** — "After the number of the days... each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities." The divinely-given rule: a prophetic day stands for a literal year.

  > "We have here a new prophetic period introduced; namely, 1290 prophetic days, which would denote the same number of literal years." — DAR 313.

- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year." The same key, by which 1290 days = 1290 years and 1335 days = 1335 years.

  > "Still another prophetic period is here introduced, denoting 1335 years.… From that point there shall be, says the prophet, 1290 days. And the very next sentence reads, 'Blessed is he that waiteth and cometh to the 1335 days.'" — DAR 314.
