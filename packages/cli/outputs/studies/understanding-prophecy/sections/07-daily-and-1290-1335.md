# 7. The Daily, and the 1290 and 1335 Days

> _The deeper time-lines of Daniel 11 and 12 hang upon one disputed word — "the daily" (Heb. tamid, the continual). It is not the Jewish sacrifice, but the continual desolating power, paganism, which had to be taken away to make room for the papal "abomination that maketh desolate." That removal was accomplished in A.D. 508; from it run the 1290 years to 1798 (508 + 1290) and the 1335 years to 1843/44 (508 + 1335). The "time of the end" opens in 1798 with the wounding of the papacy, and the blessing on him "that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days" fell upon the advent believers of 1843-44. (DAR 154-157, 254-258, 313-316; GC 356, 439.)_

### "The Daily" — the continual abomination is paganism, not sacrifice

- **Daniel 8:11** — The little horn "magnified himself even to the prince of the host, and by him the daily was taken away." The word "sacrifice" is supplied by the translators; the act belongs to Rome in both phases, pagan and papal. _(DAR 154; the little horn "symbolize[s] Rome in its entire history, including its two phases, pagan and papal" — the "daily" the pagan, the "transgression of desolation" the papal.)_
- **Daniel 8:12** — "An host was given him against the daily by reason of transgression." The barbarians who overran Rome were themselves converted to the Catholic faith and became "the instruments of the dethronement of their former religion," perpetuating the empire in another form. _(DAR 155.)_
- **Daniel 8:13** — "How long shall be the vision concerning the daily, and the transgression of desolation?" Two desolating powers are set side by side — the daily (paganism) and the transgression of desolation (the papacy) — and the question asks how long both shall tread down the sanctuary. _(DAR 156; DAR 157.)_
- **The word itself** — "Daily" (tamid) occurs in the Old Testament about one hundred and two times, "and is, in the great majority of instances, rendered continual or continually. The idea of sacrifice does not attach to the word at all." Hence "the continual" = paganism, the continual desolating power. _(DAR 157.)_

### The taking away of the daily — A.D. 508

- **Daniel 11:31** — "And arms shall stand on his part, and they shall pollute the sanctuary of strength, and shall take away the daily, and they shall place the abomination that maketh desolate." The taking away of paganism is the historical key to both the 1290 and 1335 days. _(DAR 254.)_
- **Conversion of Clovis, 496** — Until the conversion of Clovis, king of France, "the French and other nations of Western Rome were pagan; but subsequently to that event, the efforts to convert idolaters to Romanism were crowned with great success." This prepared the crisis of 508. _(DAR 255.)_
- **508 — paganism subdued** — "From the time when these successes were fully accomplished, namely, 508, the papacy was triumphant so far as paganism was concerned." In that very year a Christian king (Arthur) was crowned in Britain, and at Constantinople the Catholic party for the first time "waged a successful war against both the civil authority of the empire and the church of the East." _(DAR 256; DAR 257.)_
- **The mark of the year** — "In 508 the mine is sprung beneath the throne of the Eastern empire." Paganism, as a controlling power, was broken; the way lay open for the papal abomination to be set up thirty years later, in 538. _(DAR 258.)_

### The 1290 days (years) — 508 to 1798

- **Daniel 12:11** — "And from the time that the daily shall be taken away, and the abomination that maketh desolate set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days." The margin reads "to set up the abomination," fixing the start at the removal of paganism. _(DAR 313.)_
- **The reckoning** — "The daily has already been shown to be... the daily or continual abomination, that is, paganism.... For the historical events showing how this was accomplished in 508, see on chapter 11:31.... Counting back, then, 1290 years from 1798, we have the year 508, where it has been shown that paganism was taken away, thirty years before the setting up of the papacy." 508 + 1290 = 1798. _(DAR 313.)_
- **Both periods end together** — "The two periods, therefore, the 1290 and the 1260 days, terminate together in 1798, the one beginning in 538, and the other in 508, thirty years previous." _(DAR 313-314.)_
- **Daniel 12:7** — The man clothed in linen sware "that it shall be for a time, times, and an half." This is the 1260 years of papal supremacy (538-1798); "this period... began with the supremacy of the papacy, A.D. 538, and terminated in 1798." Its close fixes the inner edge of the time of the end. _(GC 439.)_
- **Miller's summary** — "His 1290 days, beginning when the ten kings... should be converted to the Christian faith, and the daily sacrifice abomination taken out of the way, viz., A.D. 508, which would end us in 1798, when the Pope lost his power to reign over the kings... by being deprived of his civil power by Bonaparte." _(MWV2 113.)_

### The 1335 days (years) — 508 to 1843, and the blessing on those who wait

- **Daniel 12:12** — "Blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days." This period begins from the same point as the 1290 — 508 — for it is "spoken of in immediate connection with the 1290 years, which commenced... in 508." 508 + 1335 = 1843. _(DAR 314.)_
- **Miller's reckoning** — "I then showed you that the number 1335 days, beginning at the same time as the 1290 days, viz. A.D. 508, would end in 1843.... this brought us to the same year that Daniel's whole number, 2300, brought us." The forty-five years' difference between 1290 and 1335 spans 1798 to 1843. _(MWV2 113; MWV2 100.)_
- **The blessing identified** — The thing promised at the close of the 1335 days "is a blessing unto those who wait and come to that time; that is, those who are then living" — the joy of receiving the advent truth. "If a new and glorious truth was a blessing in the days of Christ to those who received it, why was it not equally so in A.D. 1843?" _(DAR 314-315.)_
- **Marked by a prophetic period** — The advent movement was worthy to be set off by a time prophecy, "since our Lord, in verse 14 of his great prophecy of Matthew 24, makes a special announcement of this movement; and since it is still further set forth in Revelation 14:6, 7, under the symbol of an angel flying through mid-heaven." _(DAR 315-316.)_
- **Daniel 12:13** — "But go thou thy way till the end be: for thou shalt rest, and stand in thy lot at the end of the days." The "days" here reach back to the 2300 (Dan 8:14), which "terminated in 1844, and brought us to the cleansing of the sanctuary." Daniel stands in his lot when his case is decided in the judgment, "in the person of his Advocate, our great High Priest." _(DAR 316.)_

### Matthew 24:15 — Christ's own seal on Daniel's "abomination"

- **Matthew 24:15** — "When ye therefore shall see the abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet, stand in the holy place... let him understand." Christ Himself points His church back to Daniel's prophecy of the desolating powers, vouching for the prophetic time-lines and bidding the reader understand. _(DAR 255; cf. DAR 315-316, where Matt. 24:14 marks the advent proclamation of the 1335-day blessing.)_

### "The time of the end" — opened in 1798

- **Daniel 12:4** — "Shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end: many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased." The book of Daniel was sealed only "till the time of the end," after which it would be opened and understood. _(MWV2 289.)_
- **The end of the 1290 fixes the date** — Because the 1290 closes the long oppression of "the holy people" in 1798, that year opens "the time of the end." From it the book of Daniel was unsealed. _(DAR 313-314; GC 439.)_
- **GC: 1798 unsealing** — "Since 1798 the book of Daniel has been unsealed, knowledge of the prophecies has increased, and many have proclaimed the solemn message of the judgment near." Martin Luther had placed the judgment three centuries in his future; only after 1798 did the advent message go forth. _(GC 356.)_
- **The two signs** — "Many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased." Miller marks both as evident signs of the end, "literally true; in this day of invention and improvement, knowledge increase[s]," fulfilled in the Bible, tract, and Sabbath-school societies and the worldwide advent awakening. _(MWV2 289; MWV2 290.)_
- **Daniel 12:9-10** — "The words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end.... none of the wicked shall understand; but the wise shall understand." The seal is not that the prophecy cannot be known, but that its full meaning waited for the time of the end, when the wise — the advent believers — would understand it. _(DAR 311-312; MWV2 100.)_

### Daniel 12:1 — Michael standing up closes the line

- **Daniel 12:1** — "And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince... and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was." "At that time" reaches back to the planting of the king of the north — the events following the time of the end. Michael is "the archangel" (Jude 9), "our Lord Jesus Christ." _(DAR 293; DAR 294.)_
- **The standing up = taking the kingdom** — As "there shall stand up yet three kings in Persia" means to reign, so Michael's standing up is His taking His own kingdom: "the work of mercy is done, and the probation of our race is ended." The deliverance of "every one that shall be found written in the book" closes the chain begun with the taking away of the daily. _(DAR 295.)_
- **Daniel 12:2-3** — "Many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake... and they that be wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament." The same "wise" who understand the unsealed book at the time of the end (12:10) are raised and glorified at its end — the reward of those who waited and came to the 1335 days. _(DAR 314; DAR 316.)_

### The day-year principle that measures these periods

- **Numbers 14:34** — "After the number of the days... each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities." The divinely-given rule: a prophetic day stands for a literal year. _(DAR 313.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year." The same key, by which 1290 days = 1290 years and 1335 days = 1335 years. _(DAR 314.)_
