# 10. The Seven Trumpets — the Political Judgments on the Nations

> _Where the seven churches trace the inner life of the gospel church and the seven seals her outward fortunes, the seven trumpets sound the political and military judgments God brought upon the nations that had to do with His church. The first four overthrew Western Rome by the barbarian floods (Goths under Alaric, Vandals under Genseric, Huns under Attila, and the final extinction of the Western emperor); the fifth woe loosed the Saracens of Mohammed, the sixth the Ottoman Turks. The sixth trumpet's "hour, day, month, and year" measured 391 years and 15 days, running from July 27, 1449 to August 11, 1840 — the very day Litch had publicly fixed two years beforehand, openly vindicating the year-day rule and giving "a mighty impetus to the great Advent movement." (DAR 452-487; GC 334-335; PREX2 183-188.)_

### The trumpets as a distinct series — political judgments, not the seals

- **Revelation 8:1** — "When he had opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven about the space of half an hour." The seventh seal opens here; the trumpets are therefore a new and separate series, beginning where the seals close. The silence is the heavens emptied as Christ and all the angels descend.

  > "But when the Lord appears, he comes with all the holy angels with him. Matthew 25:31. And when all the heavenly harpers leave the courts above to come down with their divine Lord, as he descends to gather the fruit of his redeeming work, will there not be silence in heaven?" — DAR 452.

- **Revelation 8:2** — "And I saw the seven angels which stood before God; and to them were given seven trumpets." Seven angels, seven blasts — symbols of successive wars and overthrows by which God judged the nations touching His people, the political counterpart to the seals.

  > "This verse introduces a new and distinct series of events. In the seals we have had the history of the church during what is called the gospel dispensation. In the seven trumpets, now introduced, we have the principal political and warlike events which were to transpire during the same time." — DAR 453.

- **Revelation 8:3-5** — An angel with a golden censer offers "much incense, with the prayers of all saints" at the golden altar; then he fills the censer with "fire of the altar," casts it into the earth, and there follow "voices, and thunderings, and lightnings, and an earthquake." Intercession at the altar precedes the sounding — the judgments fall in answer to the saints' cause.

  > "The seven angels with their warlike trumpets had been introduced; terrible scenes were to transpire under their sounding; but before they commence, the people of God are pointed to the work of mediation in their behalf above, and their source of help and strength during this time…. they were to know that their great High Priest still ministered for them in the sanctuary in heaven, and that thither they could direct their prayers, and have them offered, with incense, to their Father in heaven." — DAR 454.

- **Revelation 8:6** — "And the seven angels which had the seven trumpets prepared themselves to sound." The signal that the historical series is about to open upon the Roman world.

  > "The subject of the seven trumpets is here resumed…. The seven angels prepare themselves to sound. Their sounding comes in as a complement to the prophecy of Daniel 2 and 7, commencing with the breaking up of the old Roman empire into its ten divisions, of which, in the first four trumpets, we have a description." — DAR 455.

### The first four trumpets — the barbarian floods that broke Western Rome

> _Uriah Smith follows Keith and Gibbon: "Little or nothing is left for the professed interpreter to do but to point to the pages of Gibbon." The first four trumpets are the four successive invasions that subverted the Western Empire, A.D. 395-493._

| Trumpet | Verse   | Symbol                            | Historical fulfilment                                | Leader / date                |
| ------- | ------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| First   | 8:7     | Hail, fire, blood on the earth    | Gothic invasions of the empire                       | Alaric, c. 395 onward        |
| Second  | 8:8-9   | Burning mountain into the sea     | Naval war on the maritime coasts; Africa & Italy     | Genseric the Vandal, 428-468 |
| Third   | 8:10-11 | Blazing star "Wormwood" on rivers | Desolating wars on the Alpine river-sources of Italy | Attila the Hun               |
| Fourth  | 8:12    | Sun, moon, stars darkened         | Extinction of the Western imperial line              | Odoacer / Theodoric, 476-493 |

- **Revelation 8:7** — "Hail and fire mingled with blood... cast upon the earth: and the third part of trees was burnt up." "Hail" = the northern origin of the invaders; "fire" = the burning of city and country; "blood" = the slaughter — the Gothic war under Alaric, "the first sore and heavy judgment which fell on Western Rome," from the close of the fourth century.

  > "The terrible effects of this Gothic invasion are represented as 'hail,' from the fact of the northern origin of the invaders; 'fire,' from the destruction by flame of both city and country; and 'blood,' from the terrible slaughter of the citizens of the empire by the bold and intrepid warriors." — DAR 455.

- **Revelation 8:8-9** — "As it were a great mountain burning with fire was cast into the sea: and the third part of the sea became blood... the third part of the ships were destroyed." A maritime warfare: Genseric the Vandal (A.D. 428-468), whose conquests "were, for the most part, naval," preyed on Roman commerce from Carthage and destroyed the fleets sent against him.

  > "His conquests were, for the most part, naval; and his triumphs were 'as it were a great mountain burning with fire, cast into the sea.' What figure would better, or even so well, illustrate the collision of navies, and the general havoc of war on the maritime coasts?… From the port of Carthage he repeatedly made piratical sallies, and preyed on the Roman commerce, and waged war with that empire." — DAR 458.

  > "A last and desperate attempt to dispossess Genseric of the sovereignty of the seas, was made in the year 468 by Leo, the emperor of the East…. After the failure of this great expedition, Genseric again became the tyrant of the sea." — DAR 459.

- **Revelation 8:10-11** — "There fell a great star from heaven, burning as it were a lamp... upon the third part of the rivers, and upon the fountains of waters... the name of the star is called Wormwood." A brilliant meteoric chieftain whose ravages fell on the river-sources of the Alps and Italy — Attila and his Huns, "the scourge of God."

  > "In the manner of his appearance, he strongly resembled a brilliant meteor flashing in the sky. He came from the East gathering his Huns, and poured them down… with the rapidity of a flashing meteor, suddenly on the empire…. As a matter of fact, the principal operations of Attila were on the regions of the Alps, and on the portions of the empire whence the rivers flow down into Italy." — DAR 461.

  > "These words… recall us for a moment to the character of Attila, to the misery of which he was the author or the instrument, and to the terror that was inspired by his name…. He styled himself, 'The Scourge of God.'" — DAR 462.

- **Revelation 8:12** — "The third part of the sun was smitten, and the third part of the moon, and the third part of the stars; so as the third part of them was darkened." The "sun" of Roman imperial power was extinguished when the Western line of emperors ended (476-493); the "moon and stars" — consulship and senate — lingered, then expired "star by star," till a Gothic king reigned over Rome.

  > "The imperial Roman power, of which either Rome or Constantinople had been jointly or singly the seat, whether in the West or the East, was no longer recognized in Italy, and the third part of the sun was smitten, till it emitted no longer the faintest rays. The power of the Caesars was unknown in Italy; and a Gothic king reigned over Rome…. yet the moon and the stars still shone, or glimmered, for a little longer in the Western empire." — DAR 464.

  > "'She saw her glories star by star expire,' till not even a single star remained, to glimmer on the vacant and dark night." — DAR 467.

- **Revelation 8:13** — "Woe, woe, woe, to the inhabiters of the earth by reason of the other voices of the trumpet of the three angels, which are yet to sound!" The remaining three trumpets are so much heavier than the barbarian floods that they are singled out as woes — the first incursions were "but as the preliminary drops of a shower before the torrent."

  > "Fearful as were the calamities brought upon the empire by the first incursions of these barbarians, they were comparatively light as contrasted with the calamities which were to follow. They were but as the preliminary drops of a shower before the torrent which was soon to fall upon the Roman world. The three remaining trumpets are overshadowed with a cloud of woe." — DAR 467.

### The fifth trumpet — first woe: the Saracens of Mohammed

> _"There is scarcely so uniform an agreement among interpreters... as respecting the application of the fifth and sixth trumpets, or the first and second woes, to the Saracens and Turks." (DAR 469, quoting Keith.)_

- **Revelation 9:1-2** — "A star fall from heaven unto the earth: and to him was given the key of the bottomless pit. And he opened the bottomless pit; and there arose a smoke... and the sun and the air were darkened." The fallen star = the overthrow and death of Chosroes, king of Persia (628), which opened the way for Mohammed; the "smoke" darkening sun and air = the false religion of Islam spreading its darkness over the Eastern empire. The "bottomless pit" (Gr. _abussos_) fits the trackless Arabian desert whence the Saracens swarmed.

  > "Chosroes, after his entire discomfiture and loss of empire, was murdered in the year 628; and the year 629 is marked by 'the conquest of Arabia,' and 'the first war of the Mohammedans against the Roman empire.'… The bottomless pit needed but a key to open it, and that key was the fall of Chosroes…. In this instance it may appropriately refer to the unknown wastes of the Arabian desert, from the borders of which issued the hordes of Saracens, like swarms of locusts." — DAR 472.

- **Revelation 9:3-4** — "There came out of the smoke locusts upon the earth... and it was commanded them that they should not hurt the grass... but only those men which have not the seal of God in their foreheads." The Saracen hosts, like locusts from the desert, were charged (under Abubeker) to spare the verdure — and were directed against an apostate church that "had not the seal of God," having torn away the Sabbath.

  > "The Roman empire declined, as it arose, by conquest; but the Saracens and the Turks were the instruments by which a false religion became the scourge of an apostate church; and hence, instead of the fifth and sixth trumpets, like the former, being designated by that name alone, they are called woes." — DAR 469.

- **Revelation 9:5-6** — They were "not to kill them, but that they should be tormented five months." Their commission was to harass, not destroy — torment, not conquest of the Greek empire — for a literal prophetic "five months."

  > "'And their power was to hurt men five months.' Thus far their commission extended, to torment by constant depredations, but not politically to kill them. 'Five months,' thirty days to a month, give us one hundred and fifty days; and these days, being symbolic, signify one hundred and fifty years. Commencing July 27, 1299, the one hundred and fifty years reach to 1449." — DAR 479.

- **Revelation 9:7-10** — "The shapes of the locusts were like unto horses prepared unto battle; and on their heads were as it were crowns like gold... hair as the hair of women... teeth as the teeth of lions... breastplates of iron... tails like unto scorpions." Smith applies each detail to the Arabs: the turbans ("crowns like gold"), the long uncut hair, the lion-like ferocity, the cuirasses ("breastplates of iron"), and the swift cavalry whose charge was "as the sound of chariots of many horses running to battle."

  > "The turbans of the Saracens, like unto a coronet, were their ornament and their boast…. The Arabs, unlike other men, had their hair as the hair of women, or uncut…. for, as denoting their ferocity and strength to devour, their teeth were as the teeth of lions…. 'The cuirass (or breastplate) was in use among the Arabs in the days of Mohammed.'… 'With a touch of the hand, the Arab horses darted away with the swiftness of the wind.'" — DAR 477.

- **Revelation 9:11** — "They had a king over them... whose name in the Hebrew tongue is Abaddon, but in the Greek tongue hath his name Apollyon" — that is, _Destroyer_, fittingly the spirit of the destroying Mohammedan power.

  > "In Hebrew, 'Abaddon,' the destroyer; in Greek, 'Apollyon,' one that exterminates, or destroys. Having two different names in two languages, it is evident that the character, rather than the name of the power, is intended to be represented. If so, as expressed in both languages, he is a destroyer. Such has always been the character of the Ottoman government." — DAR 479.

- **Revelation 9:12** — "One woe is past; and, behold, there come two woes more hereafter." The Saracen woe closes when the five months end and the work passes to the Turk.

  > "The first woe was to continue from the rise of Mohammedanism until the end of the five months. Then the first woe was to end, and the second to begin. And when the sixth angel sounded, it was commanded to take off the restraints which had been imposed on the nation, by which they were restricted to the work of tormenting men, and their commission was enlarged so as to permit them to slay the third part of men." — DAR 480.

### The sixth trumpet — second woe: the Ottoman Turks (391 years 15 days → Aug 11, 1840)

> _Here is the great public test of the historicist method: a date computed from "an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year" and fixed two years in advance — fulfilled to the very letter._

- **Revelation 9:13-14** — "The sixth angel sounded... Loose the four angels which are bound in the great river Euphrates." The "four angels" = the four chief Ottoman sultanies of Aleppo, Iconium, Damascus, and Bagdad, in the country of the Euphrates; previously restrained, now loosed.

  > "The Four Angels. — These were the four principal sultanies of which the Ottoman empire was composed, located in the country watered by the great river Euphrates. These sultanies were situated at Aleppo, Iconium, Damascus, and Bagdad. Previously they had been restrained; but God commanded, and they were loosed." — DAR 480.

- **Revelation 9:15** — "The four angels were loosed, which were prepared for an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year, for to slay the third part of men." By the year-day rule: a prophetic year = 360 years, a month = 30 years, a day = 1 year, and an hour = 15 days — totaling **391 years and 15 days**, now to slay (overthrow the Greek empire) rather than merely torment.

  > "And when the sixth angel sounded, it was commanded to take off the restraints which had been imposed on the nation… and their commission was enlarged so as to permit them to slay the third part of men." — DAR 480.

  > "The four angels were loosed for an hour, a day, a month, and a year, to slay the third part of men. This period amounts to three hundred and ninety-one years and fifteen days; during which Ottoman supremacy was to exist in Constantinople." — PREX2 183.1.

  > "The specified time for the continuance of Turkish or Mahometan supremacy over the Greeks, was an hour, day, month, and year. A prophetic year, three hundred and sixty days; a month, thirty days; one day; and an hour, or the twenty-fourth part of a day…. Three hundred and ninety-one years and fifteen days." — PREX2 188.2.

- **Day-for-a-year — the rule that fixes the date.** **Numbers 14:34** — "each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities." **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year." The same divine measure that turns 391 prophetic days into 391 historical years.

  > "And when the sixth angel sounded, it was commanded to take off the restraints which had been imposed on the nation… and their commission was enlarged so as to permit them to slay the third part of men. This command came from the four horns of the golden altar." — DAR 480.

  > "A prophetic year, three hundred and sixty days; a month, thirty days; one day; and an hour, or the twenty-fourth part of a day. Three hundred and sixty, the number of days in a prophetic year, divided by twenty-four, the number of hours in a day, gives us fifteen days. Three hundred and ninety-one years and fifteen days." — PREX2 188.

- **Revelation 9:16-19** — "The number of the army of the horsemen were two hundred thousand thousand... breastplates of fire, and of jacinth, and brimstone... and out of their mouths issued fire and smoke and brimstone." The vast Turkish cavalry, and "fire, smoke, and brimstone" = the gunpowder artillery by which Mahomet II battered down the walls of Constantinople in 1453 — Gibbon, "the unconscious commentator," attributing the city's fall to the Ottoman cannon.

  > "It was to 'the fire and the smoke and the sulphur,' to the artillery and firearms of Mahomet, that the killing of the third part of men, i. e., the capture of Constantinople, and by consequence the destruction of the Greek empire, was owing…. It well deserves remark, how Gibbon, always the unconscious commentator on the Apocalyptic prophecy, puts this new instrumentality of war into the foreground of his picture." — DAR 483.

- **The commencement and the close.** The Ottoman supremacy over the Greeks began when Constantine XIII (Deacozes/Palaeologus) accepted the throne only "by permission of Amurath, the Turkish sultan" — virtually saying, "I cannot reign unless you permit." The 150 years ending July 27, 1449, the 391 years and 15 days then began, and "the period would end Aug. 11, 1840."

  > "But he would not venture to ascend the throne without the consent of Amurath, the Turkish sultan. He therefore sent ambassadors to ask his consent, and obtained it before he presumed to call himself sovereign…. The authority and supremacy of the Turkish power was acknowledged when Constantine virtually said, 'I cannot reign unless you permit.'" — DAR 480.

  > "This supremacy of the Mohammedans over the Greeks was to continue, as already noticed, three hundred and ninety-one years and fifteen days. Commencing when the one hundred and fifty years ended, July 27, 1449, the period would end Aug. 11, 1840." — DAR 485.

- **Revelation 9:18** — "By these three was the third part of men killed" — the destruction of the Greek (Eastern) empire, capital fallen, "her religion trampled in the dust by the Moslem conquerors."

  > "how the Turks at length 'rushing through the breaches,' 'Constantinople was subdued, her empire subverted, and her religion trampled in the dust by the Moslem conquerors.'… For what is it but a comment on the words of our prophecy? 'By these three was the third part of men killed, by the fire, and by the smoke, and by the sulphur, which issued out of their mouths.'" — DAR 483.

- **The fulfilment — Aug 11, 1840.** Just as the supremacy began by a voluntary surrender, so it ended by one: embroiled with Mehemet Ali of Egypt, the sultan accepted the intervention of the four powers (England, Russia, Austria, Prussia), and on **August 11, 1840** the ultimatum was placed by Rifat Bey into the pasha's hands — the sultan's independence "GONE," the empire thenceforth existing "only by the sufferance of these Christian powers." "Thus was the prophecy fulfilled to the very letter."

  > "Judging from the manner of the commencement of the Ottoman supremacy… we should naturally conclude that the fall or departure of the Ottoman independence would be brought about in the same way; that at the end of the specified period, that is, on the 11th of August, 1840, the sultan would voluntarily surrender his independence into the hands of the Christian powers." — DAR 485.

  > "From the first publication of the calculation of this matter in 1838… the time set for the fulfillment of the prophecy — Aug. 11, 1840 — was watched by thousands with intense interest. And the exact accomplishment of the event predicted… gave a mighty impetus to the great Advent movement then beginning to attract the attention of the world." — DAR 487.

- **Litch's prediction, made in 1838.** Two years beforehand, Josiah Litch published this calculation; "only a few days previous to its accomplishment he wrote" that the 391 years 15 days "will end on the 11th of August, 1840, when the Ottoman power in Constantinople may be expected to be broken." At the very time specified, Turkey placed herself under the control of the Christian nations: "The event exactly fulfilled the prediction."

  > "Two years before, Josiah Litch, one of the leading ministers preaching the second advent, published an exposition of Revelation 9, predicting the fall of the Ottoman Empire…. and only a few days previous to its accomplishment he wrote: '…it will end on the 11th of August, 1840, when the Ottoman power in Constantinople may be expected to be broken. And this, I believe, will be found to be the case.'" — GC 334-335.

  > "At the very time specified, Turkey, through her ambassadors, accepted the protection of the allied powers of Europe, and thus placed herself under the control of Christian nations. The event exactly fulfilled the prediction. (See Appendix.) When it became known, multitudes were convinced of the correctness of the principles of prophetic interpretation adopted by Miller and his associates, and a wonderful impetus was given to the advent movement." — GC 335.

  > "But, although the four angels were thus loosed by the voluntary submission of the Greeks, yet another doom awaited the seat of empire. Amurath, the sultan to whom the submission of Deacozes was made… soon after died, and was succeeded in the empire, in 1451, by Mahomet II., who set his heart on Constantinople, and determined to make it a prey." — PREX2 183.

- **Revelation 9:20-21** — "The rest of the men which were not killed by these plagues yet repented not... that they should not worship devils, and idols of gold, and silver... Neither repented they of their murders, nor of their sorceries, nor of their fornication, nor of their thefts." The Saracen and Turkish scourges were sent upon apostate Christendom, yet the surviving Roman world learned no lesson — saint-worship, image-worship, and pretended miracles ("sorceries") continued.

  > "The worship of devils (demons, dead men deified) and of idols of gold, silver, brass, stone, and wood, may find a fulfillment in the saint worship and image worship of the Roman Catholic Church; while of murders, sorceries (pretended miracles through the agency of departed saints), fornications, and thefts, in countries where the Roman religion has prevailed, there has been no lack." — DAR 487.

### The seventh trumpet — third woe: the kingdom delivered to Christ

- **Revelation 11:14** — "The second woe is past; and, behold, the third woe cometh quickly." With Aug. 11, 1840, the second woe closes; the third woe (the seventh trumpet) follows quickly — the events of the last days.

  > "And the exact accomplishment of the event predicted, showing, as it did, the right application of the prophecy, gave a mighty impetus to the great Advent movement then beginning to attract the attention of the world." — DAR 487.

  > "At the very time specified, Turkey, through her ambassadors, accepted the protection of the allied powers of Europe… The event exactly fulfilled the prediction. (See Appendix.)… a wonderful impetus was given to the advent movement." — GC 335.

- **Revelation 11:15** — "The seventh angel sounded; and there were great voices in heaven, saying, The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ; and he shall reign for ever and ever." The final blast carries us past all earthly judgments to the kingdom given to Christ — the goal toward which the whole series of trumpets has pointed.

  > "The hordes of Saracens and Turks were let loose as a scourge and punishment upon apostate Christendom. Men suffered the punishment, but learned therefrom no lesson." — DAR 487.
