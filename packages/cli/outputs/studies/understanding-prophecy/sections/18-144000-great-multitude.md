# 18. The 144,000 and the Great Multitude

> _Revelation brings to view two companies among the redeemed. The 144,000 are the living saints sealed in the forehead and translated without seeing death — the firstfruits, who stand with the Lamb on Mount Zion bearing the Father's name, follow Him whithersoever He goeth, and sing a song of deliverance no other host can learn. The great multitude is the larger, uncountable throng of the redeemed of all ages, raised from the dead at the second advent, who came out of great tribulation and washed their robes white in the blood of the Lamb. The sealing of the 144,000 is the last work done for God's people before translation. (DAR 446-451; DAR 582-584; GC 648-649; EW 15-18.)_

### The Sealing of the 144,000 (Revelation 7:1-8)

- **Revelation 7:1-3** — Four angels hold the four winds till "we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads." The sealing of God's living people is the work that must be finished before the winds of strife are loosed.

  > "The Seal of the Living God. — This is the distinguishing characteristic of the ascending angel; he bears with him the seal of the living God… The nature of his work is evidently embraced in his having the seal of the living God." — DAR 437-446.

- **Revelation 7:4** — "I heard the number of them which were sealed: an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel." A definite number, drawn from the living, sealed in the last days.

  > "Those whom James here addresses are (1) Christians; for they are his brethren; (2) They are not the converts to Christianity from the Jews, the twelve tribes of his own day; for he addresses them in view of the coming of the Lord… He is thus addressing the last generation of Christians, the Christians of our own day; and he calls them the twelve tribes scattered abroad." — DAR 446.1-2.

- **Revelation 7:5-8** — Twelve thousand sealed "out of" each tribe — Dan and Ephraim omitted, Levi and Joseph put in their place. The enumeration differs from every other in Scripture; not all once written among these tribes endure to the end.

  > "But in the passage before us, Ephraim and Dan are omitted, and Levi and Joseph put in their places. The omission of Dan is accounted for by commentators on the ground that that tribe was the one chiefly addicted to idolatry… Twelve thousand were sealed 'out of' each of the twelve tribes, showing that not all who in the records of heaven had a place among these tribes when this sealing work commenced, stood the test, and were overcomers at last." — DAR 447-448.1.

- **Ezekiel 9:4** — "Set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations." The Old-Testament type of the sealing — a mark in the forehead distinguishing God's faithful from the apostate mass.

  > "The seal of God, as brought to view in our text, is to be applied to the servants of God. We are not, of course, to suppose that in this case it is some literal mark to be made in the flesh, but that it is some institution or observance having special reference to God, which will serve as a 'mark of distinction' between the worshipers of God and those who are not in truth his servants." — DAR 437-446.

- **Ephesians 4:30** — "Ye are sealed unto the day of redemption." The seal is the impress of the Holy Spirit, set on the character of those who are ready for translation.

  > "The seal of God, as brought to view in our text, is to be applied to the servants of God… some institution or observance having special reference to God, which will serve as a 'mark of distinction' between the worshipers of God and those who are not in truth his servants, though they may profess to follow him." — DAR 437.3.

- **Revelation 22:4** — "His name shall be in their foreheads." The Father's name is His seal; the commandment that bears His descriptive title — the Sabbath — is the seal of His law.

  > "They have the name of the Lamb's Father in their foreheads… we at once perceive that the Father regards his name as his seal. That commandment of the law which contains God's name is therefore the seal of the law. The Sabbath commandment is the only one which has this." — DAR 584.2.

### Spiritual Israel — Who the Sealed Tribes Are (Galatians 3:29)

- **Galatians 3:29** — "If ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise." The twelve tribes of Revelation 7 are not the literal Jewish nation but spiritual Israel — believers in Christ of every nation.

  > "Through faith in Christ the wild olive scions, the Gentiles, are grafted into the tame olive stock, and thus the twelve tribes are perpetuated… as belonging to these twelve tribes, will all the redeemed be reckoned, whether on earth they were Jews or Gentiles." — DAR 446.2-447.

- **Galatians 3:7-9** — "They which are of faith, the same are the children of Abraham... blessed with faithful Abraham." Faith, not lineage, makes one a true Israelite and heir of the sealing.

  > "Through faith in Christ the wild olive scions, the Gentiles, are grafted into the tame olive stock, and thus the twelve tribes are perpetuated. And here we find an explanation of the language of the same apostle: 'They are not all Israel which are of Israel,' and 'he is not a Jew which is one outwardly, ... but he is a Jew which is one inwardly.'" — DAR 446.2.

- **Romans 2:28-29** — "He is not a Jew which is one outwardly... but he is a Jew which is one inwardly." Uriah Smith cites this very text to prove the sealed twelve tribes are Christians of the last generation.

  > "And here we find an explanation of the language of the same apostle: 'They are not all Israel which are of Israel,' and 'he is not a Jew which is one outwardly, ... but he is a Jew which is one inwardly.' Romans 9:6-8; 2:28, 29." — DAR 447.

- **James 1:1** — James writes "to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad," yet addresses them "in view of the coming of the Lord" — therefore the last generation of Christians, called the twelve tribes.

  > "They are not the converts to Christianity from the Jews, the twelve tribes of his own day; for he addresses them in view of the coming of the Lord… He is thus addressing the last generation of Christians, the Christians of our own day; and he calls them the twelve tribes scattered abroad." — DAR 446.2-447.

- **Revelation 21:12** — The gates of the New Jerusalem — a New-Testament city — bear "the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel," and through them all the redeemed pass; so all the saved, Jew or Gentile, are reckoned among these tribes.

  > "So we find on the gates of the New Jerusalem — which is a New Testament or Christian, not a Jewish, city — the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel… And as through these gates, so inscribed, all the redeemed hosts will go in and out, so, as belonging to these twelve tribes, will all the redeemed be reckoned, whether on earth they were Jews or Gentiles." — DAR 447.

### The 144,000 on Mount Zion — Translated Without Death (Revelation 14:1-5)

- **Revelation 14:1** — "A Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father's name written in their foreheads." The very company hunted by the beast in chapter 13 stands victorious on the heavenly Zion.

  > "The 13th chapter closed with the people of God, a small and apparently weak and defenseless company, in deadly conflict with the mightiest powers of earth… Glance forward with the apostle to the very next scene in the program, and what do we behold? — The very same company standing on Mount Zion with the Lamb, — a victorious company." — DAR 582.2-583.

- **Revelation 14:2-3** — They sing "as it were a new song... and no man could learn that song but the hundred forty and four thousand." It is the song of Moses and the Lamb — the song of their own deliverance, which no other company can learn.

  > "They sing a new song which no other company is able to learn. In chapter 15:3, it is called the song of Moses and the song of the Lamb… Therefore the song of the 144,000, is the song of their deliverance. No others can join in it; for no other company will have had an experience like theirs." — DAR 584.3.

  > "And they sing 'a new song' before the throne, a song which no man can learn save the hundred and forty and four thousand. It is the song of Moses and the Lamb—a song of deliverance… for it is the song of their experience—an experience such as no other company have ever had." — GC 648-649.

- **Revelation 14:4** — "These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth. These were redeemed from among men, being the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb." "Redeemed from among men" — from among the living — means translation; the 144,000 are the living saints changed at the second coming.

  > "They are 'redeemed from among men' (verse 4), an expression which can be applicable only to those who are translated from among the living… A redemption from among men, from among the living, must mean a different thing, and can mean only one thing, and that is translation. Hence the 144,000 are the living saints, who will be translated at the second coming of Christ." — DAR 583.4.

  > "And the Lamb Shall Lead Them. — Another testimony concerning the same company, and applying at the same time, says, 'These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth.' Revelation 14:4." — DAR 451.1.

- **Revelation 14:5** — "In their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault before the throne of God." A character without falsehood, perfected through the final crisis.

  > "VERSE 1… And in their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault before the throne of God." — DAR 582.2.

  > "'In their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault' before God. 'Therefore are they before the throne of God, and serve Him day and night in His temple.'" — GC 648.3.

- **Revelation 3:10** — "Because thou hast kept the word of my patience, I also will keep thee from the hour of temptation, which shall come upon all the world." The promise to Philadelphia — the church state to which the 144,000 belong — kept through the last trial.

  > "They are the overcomers in the sixth or Philadelphian state of the church. (See Revelation 3:11, 12.)" — DAR 583.3.

- **Revelation 3:11-12** — "Hold that fast which thou hast, that no man take thy crown... I will write upon him the name of my God, and the name of the city of my God." The overcomers of Philadelphia receive the Father's name written in the forehead — the very mark of the 144,000.

  > "They are the overcomers in the sixth or Philadelphian state of the church. (See Revelation 3:11, 12.)" — DAR 583.3.

- **Daniel 12:1** — "There shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation... and at that time thy people shall be delivered." The 144,000 pass through this unequaled trouble, standing without a mediator through the seven last plagues, yet are delivered.

  > "They pass through the great time of trouble such as never was since there was a nation. Daniel 12:1… They stand without a mediator through the terrific scenes of the seven last plagues, those exhibitions of God's unmingled wrath in the earth… They pass through the severest time of trouble the world has ever known, although they are delivered out of it." — DAR 450.1.

  > "'These are they which came out of great tribulation;' they have passed through the time of trouble such as never was since there was a nation… they have stood without an intercessor through the final outpouring of God's judgments. But they have been delivered." — GC 648-649.

- **Revelation 14:12** — "Here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." Accused of legalism for refusing the mark, the 144,000 are shown to rest wholly on the merits of the Lamb's blood.

  > "And though the 144,000 are accused of rejecting Christ, and trusting to their own works for salvation, because they refuse to violate the commandments of God (Revelation 14:1, 12), in the great day that calumny will be wiped off. It will be seen that they have rested their hope of life on the merits of the shed blood of their divine Redeemer." — DAR 450.2.

### The Great Multitude — The Redeemed of All Ages (Revelation 7:9-17)

- **Revelation 7:9** — "A great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues, stood before the throne... clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands." The countless host of the saved of every age, raised at the second advent — proof the sealing is the last work before translation.

  > "This vast throng are undoubtedly the saved out of every nation, kindred, tribe, and tongue, raised from the dead at the second coming of Christ, showing that the sealing is the last work accomplished for the people of God prior to translation." — DAR 448.2-3.

- **Revelation 7:10-12** — They cry "Salvation to our God which sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb," and all the angels fall and worship. The redeemed ascribe their victory wholly to God and the Lamb.

  > "And cried with a loud voice, saying, Salvation to our God which sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb… And all the angels stood round about the throne, and about the elders and the four beasts, and fell before the throne on their faces, and worshiped God." — DAR 448.2.

- **Revelation 7:13-14** — The elder asks "What are these... and whence came they?" — and John's "Sir, thou knowest" implies he did not know. Smith holds that this question singles out a special company within the throng: the 144,000, so transformed John no longer recognizes them.

  > "But if a special company in this vast throng were referred to, distinguished by some special mark or position, then it might not be so evident who they were… We therefore incline to the view that attention is called to a special company by the questions which were proposed by one of the elders; and no company is brought to view to which special allusion would more naturally be made than to the company spoken of in the first part of the chapter; namely, the 144,000." — DAR 449.2-450.

- **Revelation 7:14** — "These... came out of great tribulation, and have washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb." True of all the redeemed, but emphatically of the 144,000, who pass through the great time of trouble.

  > "While it is true in some degree of all Christians that they must 'through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God,' it is true in a very emphatic sense of the 144,000. They pass through the great time of trouble such as never was since there was a nation… There is peculiar force in saying of these that they have washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb." — DAR 450.1-2.

  > "'These are they which came out of great tribulation;' they have passed through the time of trouble such as never was since there was a nation… But they have been delivered, for they have 'washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb.'" — GC 648-649.

- **Revelation 7:15-17** — "They shall hunger no more, neither thirst any more; neither shall the sun light on them, nor any heat... God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes." The reward answers to their suffering in the plagues, when the sun scorched men and the rivers turned to blood.

  > "May it not be that when the pastures, with all fruits and vegetation, are dried up… and the rivers and fountains are turned to blood… the saints who pass through that time will be brought occasionally to the extreme degrees of hunger and thirst? But the kingdom once gained, 'they shall hunger no more, neither thirst any more.'… We remember that the 144,000 live through the time when power is given unto the sun 'to scorch men with fire.'" — DAR 450.4-451.

### The Song and the Sea of Glass — The Victors over the Beast (Revelation 15:2-3)

- **Revelation 15:2** — "Them that had gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark... stand on the sea of glass, having the harps of God." The same 144,000, now standing victorious on the sea of glass before the throne.

  > "Upon the crystal sea before the throne… are gathered the company that have 'gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark, and over the number of his name.' With the Lamb upon Mount Zion, 'having the harps of God,' they stand, the hundred and forty and four thousand that were redeemed from among men." — GC 648.3-649.

- **Revelation 15:3** — "They sing the song of Moses... and the song of the Lamb." The song of deliverance — Moses' song was the song of his own experience and rescue (Exodus 15); so this is the song of the saints' deliverance, learnable by none but them.

  > "The song of Moses, as may be seen by reference to Exodus 15, was the song of his experience and deliverance. Therefore the song of the 144,000, is the song of their deliverance. No others can join in it; for no other company will have had an experience like theirs." — DAR 584.3.

  > "It is the song of Moses and the Lamb—a song of deliverance. None but the hundred and forty-four thousand can learn that song; for it is the song of their experience—an experience such as no other company have ever had." — GC 649.

### Their Eternal Reward — God Dwells with Them (Revelation 21:3-4)

- **Revelation 21:3** — "Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them, and they shall be his people." The final union of the redeemed with God — the consummation of the covenant.

  > "Friends long separated by death are united, nevermore to part, and with songs of gladness ascend together to the City of God." — GC 645.

  > "'Therefore are they before the throne of God, and serve Him day and night in His temple: and He that sitteth on the throne shall dwell among them.'" — GC 648-649.

- **Revelation 21:4** — "God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain." Smith joins this with Isaiah 25:8 — "He will swallow up death in victory" — as the promise fulfilled to the delivered saints.

  > "The phraseology of this promise to the 144,000 is also partially found in the following glowing prophecy from the pen of Isaiah: 'He will swallow up death in victory; and the Lord God will wipe away tears from off all faces… for the Lord hath spoken it.' Isaiah 25:8." — DAR 451.2.

- **1 Thessalonians 4:16-17** — "The dead in Christ shall rise first: then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them... to meet the Lord in the air." The resurrection of the great multitude and the translation of the living 144,000 in one event at Christ's coming.

  > "The living righteous are changed 'in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye.' At the voice of God they were glorified; now they are made immortal and with the risen saints are caught up to meet their Lord in the air." — GC 645.

  > "The graves opened, and the dead came up clothed with immortality. The 144,000 shouted, 'Alleluia!' as they recognized their friends who had been torn from them by death, and in the same moment we were changed and caught up together with them to meet the Lord in the air." — EW 16.1.

### Witness of the Spirit of Prophecy

- **The sealed company translated.** "The 144,000 were all sealed and perfectly united. On their foreheads was written, God, New Jerusalem, and a glorious star containing Jesus' new name." Sealed, they stand through the last crisis and are changed at the advent.

  > "The 144,000 were all sealed and perfectly united. On their foreheads was written, God, New Jerusalem, and a glorious star containing Jesus' new name… Then it was that the synagogue of Satan knew that God had loved us who could wash one another's feet and salute the brethren with a holy kiss, and they worshiped at our feet." — EW 15.1.

- **Caught up at the resurrection.** "The graves opened, and the dead came up clothed with immortality. The 144,000 shouted, 'Alleluia!'... and in the same moment we were changed and caught up together with them to meet the Lord in the air." The living translated join the risen dead.

  > "The graves opened, and the dead came up clothed with immortality. The 144,000 shouted, 'Alleluia!' as they recognized their friends who had been torn from them by death, and in the same moment we were changed and caught up together with them to meet the Lord in the air." — EW 16.1.

  > "The living righteous are changed 'in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye.' At the voice of God they were glorified; now they are made immortal and with the risen saints are caught up to meet their Lord in the air." — GC 645.1.

- **On the sea of glass in a perfect square.** "Here on the sea of glass the 144,000 stood in a perfect square... Jesus brought the crowns, and with His own right hand placed them on our heads. He gave us harps of gold and palms of victory."

  > "We all entered the cloud together, and were seven days ascending to the sea of glass, when Jesus brought the crowns, and with His own right hand placed them on our heads. He gave us harps of gold and palms of victory. Here on the sea of glass the 144,000 stood in a perfect square." — EW 16.2.

  > "The glittering ranks are drawn up in the form of a hollow square about their King… Upon the heads of the overcomers, Jesus with His own right hand places the crown of glory… In every hand are placed the victor's palm and the shining harp." — GC 645.3.

- **The deliverance at midnight.** "It was at midnight that God chose to deliver His people... there was a mighty shout of victory over the beast and over his image." The 144,000 delivered out of the time of trouble by the voice of God.

  > "It was at midnight that God chose to deliver His people… And when the never-ending blessing was pronounced on those who had honored God in keeping His Sabbath holy, there was a mighty shout of victory over the beast and over his image." — EW 285.

- **The final scene by the great pen.** Upon the sea of glass "are gathered the company that have gotten the victory over the beast... with the Lamb upon Mount Zion... the hundred and forty and four thousand that were redeemed from among men... having been translated from the earth, from among the living... the first fruits unto God and to the Lamb." The whole prophetic chain resolved in one tableau.

  > "Upon the crystal sea before the throne… are gathered the company that have 'gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark, and over the number of his name.' With the Lamb upon Mount Zion, 'having the harps of God,' they stand, the hundred and forty and four thousand that were redeemed from among men… These, having been translated from the earth, from among the living, are counted as 'the first fruits unto God and to the Lamb.'" — GC 648.3-649.
