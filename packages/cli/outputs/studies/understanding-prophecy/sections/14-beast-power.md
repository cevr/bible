# 14. The Beast Power of Revelation 13 — the Papacy

> _The first beast of Revelation 13 — the seven-headed, ten-horned leopard beast
> that rises from the sea — is the Papacy, the very same persecuting power John's
> own teacher Daniel saw as the little horn of chapter 7. Every mark of identity
> answers: it inherits the body of Daniel's lion, bear, and leopard kingdoms; the
> dragon (pagan Rome) hands it his seat and power; it blasphemes God, makes war on
> the saints, and reigns its forty-two months — 1260 years, 538 to 1798 — until its
> deadly wound at the captivity of the pope. Paul names the same power the "man of
> sin," and John shows it again as the scarlet woman of Revelation 17; the title
> graven on the papal mitre, VICARIVS FILII DEI, numbers the man — 666. (DAR
> 520-532, 580; GC 439, 446, 49.)_

### The beast rises from the sea — out of the nations

- **Revelation 13:1** — "I... saw a beast rise up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns, and upon his horns ten crowns, and upon his heads the name of blasphemy." The sea is the Bible symbol of "peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues" (Rev. 17:15); a beast rising from it denotes a power arising amid a thickly-peopled, war-tossed territory — Western Europe in the breakup of Rome.

  > "THE sea is a symbol of “peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues.” Revelation 17:15. A beast is the Bible symbol of an unrighteous nation, or power.... Whenever a beast is seen to come up out of the sea, it denotes that the power arises in a thickly populated territory; and if the winds are represented as blowing upon the sea, as in Daniel 7:2, 3, political commotion, civil strife, and revolution are indicated." — DAR 520.2.

  > "By the dragon of the previous chapter, and the beast first introduced in this, we have the Roman power as a whole brought to view in its two phases, pagan and papal; hence these two symbols have each the seven heads and ten horns." — DAR 521.1.

- **Revelation 13:2** — "The beast which I saw was like unto a leopard, and his feet were as the feet of a bear, and his mouth as the mouth of a lion: and the dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority." The body gathers up Daniel's beasts in reverse — leopard (Greece), bear (Medo-Persia), lion (Babylon) — for this last power inherits all that went before; and the dragon, pagan Rome, hands the new Rome his throne.

  > "We now come to the leopard beast of chapter 13. What does that symbolize? The answer still is, The Roman empire.... Ah! there has been a change in the religious character of the empire; and this beast symbolizes Rome in its professedly Christian form. And it is this change of religion, and this alone, which makes a change in the symbol necessary." — DAR 522-523.

- **Daniel 7:6** — "Another, like a leopard... the beast had also four heads; and dominion was given to it." Greece, Persia, and Babylon stand in Daniel's prior visions; Revelation 13 stitches their forms into one composite body — proof the same prophetic chain is being carried to its climax.

  > "The third kingdom, Grecia, is represented by this symbol.... The leopard had four heads, the rough goat four horns, the kingdom was to have four divisions; and thus it was." — DAR 116-117.

### The dragon gives his seat — pagan Rome to papal Rome

- **Revelation 12:3-4** — "A great red dragon, having seven heads and ten horns... and the dragon stood before the woman... for to devour her child as soon as it was born." The dragon is the Roman empire in its pagan phase — the power that, through Herod, sought the life of the infant Christ. Same seven heads, same ten horns: one Roman power in two religious phases.

  > "No formal answer to this question need be given to any one who has read how Herod, in a fiendish effort to destroy the infant Jesus, sent forth and slew all the children in Bethlehem, from two years old and under. But who was Herod? — A Roman governor.... It is not, therefore, without the most conclusive reason that the Roman empire is considered by Protestant commentators generally to be the power indicated by the great red dragon." — DAR 509-512.

  > "By the dragon of the previous chapter, and the beast first introduced in this, we have the Roman power as a whole brought to view in its two phases, pagan and papal; hence these two symbols have each the seven heads and ten horns." — DAR 521.1.

- **Revelation 13:2** (latter clause) — "The dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority." Pagan Rome was succeeded by papal Rome; the seat of empire (the city of Rome) and the authority passed from the Caesars to the popes. The change of symbol marks only the change of religion — Rome pagan becoming Rome professedly Christian.

  > "To this beast the dragon gives his seat, his power, and great authority. By what power was pagan Rome succeeded? We all know that it was by papal Rome.... But two phases of empire are here recognized; and in the prophecy, Rome is pagan until Rome is papal." — DAR 523.2.

- **Revelation 12:6** — "The woman fled into the wilderness... that they should feed her there a thousand two hundred and threescore days." The same 1260 of the beast's reign, seen from the church's side: while the beast rules, the true church is driven into the wilderness.

  > "And the church fled into the wilderness at the time the papacy was established, in 538, where it was nourished by the word of God and the ministration of angels during the long, dark, and bloody rule of that power, 1260 years." — DAR 514-515.

### Six points of identity — the little horn and the leopard beast are one power

> Uriah Smith draws the parallel out in six matched marks, and concludes that two
> symbols which "come upon the stage of action at the same time, occupy the same
> territory, maintain the same character, do the same work, exist the same length
> of time, and meet the same fate" must denote one identical power. (DAR 524-525.)

| #   | The little horn (Daniel 7)                                   | The leopard beast (Revelation 13)                      |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Speaks great words against the Most High — 7:25              | Opens his mouth in blasphemy against God — 13:6        |
| 2   | Makes war with the saints, prevails — 7:21                   | Makes war with the saints, overcomes — 13:7            |
| 3   | A mouth speaking great things — 7:8, 20                      | A mouth speaking great things and blasphemies — 13:5   |
| 4   | Rises as pagan Rome ceases                                   | Rises as the dragon (pagan Rome) gives his seat — 13:2 |
| 5   | Reigns a time, times, and dividing of time = 1260 yrs — 7:25 | Continues forty-two months = 1260 yrs — 13:5           |
| 6   | Dominion taken away at period's end — 7:26                   | Led into captivity at period's end — 13:10             |

- **Daniel 7:8** — "Another little horn, before whom there were three of the first horns plucked up by the roots: and... in this horn were eyes like the eyes of man, and a mouth speaking great things." A power rising among the ten kingdoms of fallen Rome, plucking up three (Heruli A.D. 493, Vandals 534, Ostrogoths 538): the Papacy. The eyes = the shrewdness of the papal hierarchy; the great mouth = the arrogant claims of the bishops of Rome.

  > "And “in this horn were eyes like the eyes of a man, and a mouth speaking great things,” — the eyes, a fit emblem of the shrewdness, penetration, cunning, and foresight of the papal hierarchy; and the mouth speaking great things, a fit symbol of the arrogant claims of the bishops of Rome." — DAR 119.1.

  > "The position is here confidently taken that the three powers, or horns, plucked up before the papacy, were the Heruli, the Vandals, and the Ostrogoths." — DAR 129.2.

  > "From the historical testimony above cited, we think it clearly established that the three horns plucked up were the powers named; viz., the Heruli in A. D. 493, the Vandals in 534, and the Ostrogoths in 538." — DAR 136.2.

- **Daniel 7:24-25** — "He shall be diverse from the first... and he shall subdue three kings. And he shall speak great words against the most High, and shall wear out the saints of the most High, and think to change times and laws: and they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time." The full charge-sheet of the Papacy: subduing three horns, blasphemy, persecution, the attempted change of God's law (the Sabbath), for 1260 years.

  > "Says Daniel, of the little horn, the papacy: “He shall think to change times and the law.” Daniel 7:25, R.V. And Paul styled the same power the “man of sin,” who was to exalt himself above God.... Only by changing God’s law could the papacy exalt itself above God." — GC 446.

  > "“And shall wear out the saints of the Most High.” Has the papacy done this?... All know that for long years the papal church has pursued its relentless work against the true followers of God.... Wars, crusades, massacres, inquisitions, and persecutions of all kinds, — these were their weapons of extinction." — DAR 137.

- **Daniel 7:21** — "The same horn made war with the saints, and prevailed against them." History answers exactly: Waldenses, Albigenses, the Inquisition, the Duke of Alva — Scott's and Barnes' histories reckon the slain in the millions.

  > "“No computation can reach the numbers who have been put to death, in different ways, on account of their maintaining the profession of the gospel, and opposing the corruptions of the Church of Rome. A million of poor Waldenses perished in France; nine hundred thousand orthodox Christians were slain in less than thirty years after the institution of the order of the Jesuits. The Duke of Alva boasted of having put to death in the Netherlands thirty-six thousand by the hand of the common executioner.... The Inquisition destroyed, by various tortures, one hundred and fifty thousand within thirty years.”" — DAR 137.

### Blasphemy — exalting itself in the place of God

- **Revelation 13:5-6** — "There was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies... and he opened his mouth in blasphemy against God, to blaspheme his name, and his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven." Blasphemy is the assuming of God's prerogatives: the power claims to forgive sins, turns men from the heavenly sanctuary to an earthly throne, and points to Rome as the eternal city.

  > "He blasphemes the tabernacle in heaven by turning the attention of his subjects to his own throne and palace instead of to the tabernacle of God; by turning their attention away from the city of God, Jerusalem above, and pointing them to Rome as the eternal city; and he blasphemes them that dwell in heaven by assuming to exercise the power of forgiving sins." — DAR 526.

- **2 Thessalonians 2:3-4** — "That man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition; who opposeth and exalteth himself above all that is called God... so that he as God sitteth in the temple of God, shewing himself that he is God." Paul foretold the very apostasy that produced the papal power — sitting in the temple (the church) and claiming the place of God. The "man of sin" is the little horn and the leopard beast under another name.

  > "The apostle Paul, in his second letter to the Thessalonians, foretold the great apostasy which would result in the establishment of the papal power.... Even at that early date he saw, creeping into the church, errors that would prepare the way for the development of the papacy." — GC 49.

  > "The “man of sin,” which is also styled “the mystery of iniquity,” “the son of perdition,” and “that wicked,” represents the papacy, which, as foretold in prophecy, was to maintain its supremacy for 1260 years. This period ended in 1798." — GC 356.

- **2 Thessalonians 2:7** — "For the mystery of iniquity doth already work." The leaven was at work even in Paul's day; "little by little, at first in stealth and silence," the customs of heathenism crept into the church until the papacy stood full-formed.

  > "Little by little, at first in stealth and silence, and then more openly as it increased in strength and gained control of the minds of men, “the mystery of iniquity” carried forward its deceptive and blasphemous work. Almost imperceptibly the customs of heathenism found their way into the Christian church." — GC 49.

- **2 Thessalonians 2:8** — "Then shall that Wicked be revealed, whom the Lord shall consume with the spirit of his mouth, and shall destroy with the brightness of his coming." The same power persists as a spiritual force to the very end, to be destroyed only at Christ's appearing — the close of the long controversy.

  > "...the papal; which as a spiritual power continues to the end (2 Thessalonians 2:8), and as a temporal power to the time when his dominion is taken away just before the end. Daniel 7:26." — DAR 525.3.

### Forty-two months — the 1260 years, 538 to 1798

- **Revelation 13:5** — "Power was given unto him to continue forty and two months." Forty-two months of thirty days = 1260 prophetic days; by the year-day principle, 1260 years. The identical period appears five times in Daniel and Revelation as "time, times, and a half," "1260 days," and "42 months."

  > "Power was given to the little horn to continue for a time, times, and the dividing of time, or 1260 years. Daniel 7:25. To this beast also power was given for forty-two months, or 1260 years. Revelation 13:5." — DAR 524.7.

  > "The forty and two months are the same as the “time and times and the dividing of time,” three years and a half, or 1260 days, of Daniel 7—the time during which the papal power was to oppress God’s people." — GC 439.

- **Numbers 14:34** — "After the number of the days in which ye searched the land, even forty days, each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities." The God-given key: in symbolic prophecy a day stands for a year. This unlocks the 1260, the 2300, and the seventy weeks alike.

  > "All Scripture language is to be taken literally, unless there exists some good reason for supposing it to be figurative; and all that is figurative is to be interpreted by that which is literal. That the language here used is symbolic, is evident from verse 17.... These beast are therefore symbols of four great kingdoms; and the circumstances under which they arose... are symbolic also." — DAR 113.

  > "Following his rule of making Scripture its own interpreter, Miller learned that a day in symbolic prophecy represents a year (Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:6); he saw that the period of 2300 prophetic days, or literal years, would extend far beyond the close of the Jewish dispensation." — GC 324.

- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "Thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: I have appointed thee each day for a year." A second witness to the day-for-a-year rule — established in the mouth of two prophets.

  > "Following his rule of making Scripture its own interpreter, Miller learned that a day in symbolic prophecy represents a year (Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:6); he saw that the period of 2300 prophetic days, or literal years, would extend far beyond the close of the Jewish dispensation." — GC 324.

- **Revelation 13:5; Daniel 7:25** — The 1260 years "began with the supremacy of the papacy, A.D. 538, and terminated in 1798." From the rooting-up of the third horn (the Ostrogoths) and the operation of Justinian's decree making the pope head of the churches, to the year the period ran out.

  > "This period, as stated in preceding chapters, began with the supremacy of the papacy, A.D. 538, and terminated in 1798. At that time the pope was made captive by the French army, the papal power received its deadly wound, and the prediction was fulfilled, “He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity.”" — GC 439.2.

### The deadly wound and its healing — 1798

- **Revelation 13:3** — "I saw one of his heads as it were wounded to death; and his deadly wound was healed: and all the world wondered after the beast." The wounded head is the papal head — for only after Rome turned "Christian" was there one head, the papal, to be smitten. The wound is the same event as the going-into-captivity.

  > "The head that was wounded to death was the papal head.... after this change, there was but one head, and that was the papal.... Thus it is placed beyond controversy that it was none other than the papal head that was wounded to death, and his deadly wound was healed. This wounding is the same as the going into captivity. Revelation 13:10." — DAR 525.3.

- **Revelation 13:10** — "He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints." The power that for 1260 years led the saints captive was itself led captive — fulfilled when Berthier, the French general, took the pope prisoner in 1798; Pius VI died in exile at Valence in 1799.

  > "It was inflicted when the pope was taken prisoner by Berthier, the French general, and the papal government was for a time abolished, in 1798. Stripped of his power, both civil and ecclesiastical, the captive pope, Pius VI, died in exile at Valence in France, Aug. 29, 1799." — DAR 525.3.

  > "By verse 10 we are again referred to the events of 1798, when that power that had for 1260 years led the saints of God into captivity, was led into captivity itself, as already noticed." — DAR 527.1.

  > "At that time the pope was made captive by the French army, the papal power received its deadly wound, and the prediction was fulfilled, “He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity.”" — GC 439.2.

- **Revelation 13:3** (latter clause) — "His deadly wound was healed." The wound healed with the election of a new pope, March 14, 1800, and the gradual recovery of papal influence — "all the world wondered after the beast," its prestige restored before the end.

  > "But the deadly wound was healed when the papacy was re-established, though with a diminution of its former power, by the election of a new pope, March 14, 1800." — DAR 525.3.

### Universal worship and the war on the saints

- **Revelation 13:4** — "They worshipped the dragon which gave power unto the beast: and they worshipped the beast, saying, Who is like unto the beast? who is able to make war with him?" The homage paganism once gave the dragon is transferred to the beast; its seeming invincibility draws the worship of the world.

  > "THE sea is a symbol of “peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues.”... A beast is the Bible symbol of an unrighteous nation, or power, representing sometimes the civil power alone, sometimes the ecclesiastical in connection with the civil." — DAR 520-522.

- **Revelation 13:7** — "It was given unto him to make war with the saints, and to overcome them: and power was given him over all kindreds, and tongues, and nations." A universal dominion and a universal persecution — exactly the reach and record of the medieval Papacy.

  > "The little horn made war with the saints, and prevailed against them. Daniel 7:21. This beast also (Revelation 13:7) makes war with the saints, and overcomes them." — DAR 524.3.

  > "“And shall wear out the saints of the Most High.”... All know that for long years the papal church has pursued its relentless work against the true followers of God.... Wars, crusades, massacres, inquisitions, and persecutions of all kinds, — these were their weapons of extinction." — DAR 137.

- **Revelation 13:8** — "All that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world." Only those written in the Lamb's book of life refuse the homage — the line is drawn between the worshippers of the beast and the people of God.

  > "Thus closes chapter 13, leaving the people of God with the powers of earth in deadly array against them, and the decrees of death and banishment from society out upon them, for their adherence to the truth.... This will be the “hour of temptation,” or trial, which is to come, as the closing test, upon all the world." — DAR 581.

- **Revelation 13:9-10** — "If any man have an ear, let him hear... Here is the patience and the faith of the saints." The same solemn call of Christ to the seven churches; under the beast's long oppression the saints are kept by patience and faith, not by the sword.

  > "By verse 10 we are again referred to the events of 1798, when that power that had for 1260 years led the saints of God into captivity, was led into captivity itself, as already noticed." — DAR 527.1.

### The number of the beast — 666

- **Revelation 13:18** — "Here is wisdom. Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast: for it is the number of a man; and his number is Six hundred threescore and six." The number is "the number of a man" — pointing not to a people (as _Lateinos_, a kingdom) but to the title of a single man.

  > "Here is wisdom. Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast: for it is the number of a man; and his number is Six hundred threescore and six." — DAR 579.4.

  > "The number, says the prophecy, is the number of a man; and if it is to be derived from a name or title, the natural conclusion would be that it must be the name or title of some particular man. But in this we have the name of a people, or kingdom, not of “a man,” as the prophecy says." — DAR 579.5.

- **Revelation 13:18 — VICARIVS FILII DEI** — The title the popes apply to themselves, "Vicegerent of the Son of God," graven on the papal mitre. Taking the Latin letters used as numerals and summing their values yields exactly 666: V(5) + I(1) + C(100) + I(1) + V(5) + I(1) + L(50) + I(1) + I(1) + D(500) + I(1) = 666. "Here we have indeed the number of a man, even the 'man of sin.'"

  > "The most plausible name we have ever seen suggested as containing the number of the beast, is the title which the pope applies to himself, and allows others to apply to him. That title is this: Vicarius Filii Dei, “Vicegerent of the Son of God.” Taking the letters out of this title which the Latins used as numerals, and giving them their numerical value, we have just 666." — DAR 580.1.

  > "Here we have indeed the number of a man, even the “man of sin;” and it is a little singular, perhaps providential, that he should select a title which shows the blasphemous character of the beast, and then cause it to be inscribed upon his miter, as if to brand himself with the number 666." — DAR 580.4.

### The same beast as Babylon the harlot — Revelation 17

- **Revelation 17:3-4** — "I saw a woman sit upon a scarlet coloured beast, full of names of blasphemy, having seven heads and ten horns... arrayed in purple and scarlet... having a golden cup in her hand full of abominations." The same seven heads and ten horns as the beast of chapter 13; the woman (a church) rides the beast (the civil power) — the apostate church wielding the state, decked in the very purple and scarlet of papal pomp.

  > "We here have the woman, the church, seated upon a scarlet-colored beast, the civil power, by which she is upheld, and which she controls and guides to her own ends, as a rider controls the animal upon which he is seated.... purple and scarlet are the chief colors in the robes of popes and cardinals." — DAR 657-662.

- **Revelation 17:5** — "Upon her forehead was a name written, MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS." The same "mystery of iniquity" Paul named (2 Thess. 2:7), here owned as the mother-church of all spiritual fornication.

  > "That this apostate woman, as presented in this chapter, is a symbol of the Roman Catholic Church, is generally believed by Protestants.... she is called the mother of harlots... which shows that there are other independent religious organizations that constitute the apostate daughters, and belong to the same great family." — DAR 657-660.

  > "Babylon is said to be “the mother of harlots.” By her daughters must be symbolized churches that cling to her doctrines and traditions, and follow her example of sacrificing the truth and the approval of God, in order to form an unlawful alliance with the world." — GC 382.

- **Revelation 17:6** — "I saw the woman drunken with the blood of the saints, and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus." The persecuting record of the beast of chapter 13 (13:7) re-stated of the woman — one and the same blood-guilty power.

  > "The secret of his wonder was just this: all the persecution he had witnessed had been from pagan Rome, the open enemy of Christ.... but when he looked forward, and saw a church professedly Christian persecuting the followers of the Lamb, and drunken with their blood, he could but wonder with great amazement." — DAR 660.

  > "“And shall wear out the saints of the Most High.”... All know that for long years the papal church has pursued its relentless work against the true followers of God." — DAR 137.

- **Revelation 17:18** — "The woman which thou sawest is that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth." The inspired identification: the woman is the city that ruled the kings of the earth — Rome, and in John's prophetic horizon, papal Rome reigning over the kingdoms of Christendom.

  > "In verse 15 we have a plain definition of the Scripture symbol of waters; they denote peoples, multitudes, nations, and tongues.... This chapter has, naturally, more especial reference to the old mother, or Catholic Babylon." — DAR 662.

- **Revelation 17:8** — "The beast that thou sawest was, and is not... and shall ascend out of the bottomless pit, and go into perdition." The beast's death-and-revival ("was, and is not, and yet is") echoes the deadly-wound-and-healing of 13:3 — the same wounded-and-restored Roman power.

  > "Thus Rome in its pagan form was a persecuting power in its relation to the people of God, during which time it constituted the beast that was;... and during a brief period, while this transition was going on, it lost its ferocious and persecuting character, and then it could be said of the beast that it was not. Time passed on, and it degenerated into popery, and again assumed its bloodthirsty and oppressive character, and then it constituted the beast that “yet is.”" — DAR 658-659.

### Summary — three texts that fix the identity

- **The body and the throne — Revelation 13:2.** "Like unto a leopard... the dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority." Pagan Rome's seat passing to papal Rome.
- **The period — Revelation 13:5 with Daniel 7:25.** "Forty and two months" = "a time and times and the dividing of time" = 1260 years, 538-1798.

  > "This period, as stated in preceding chapters, began with the supremacy of the papacy, A.D. 538, and terminated in 1798. At that time the pope was made captive by the French army, the papal power received its deadly wound." — GC 439.2.

- **The number — Revelation 13:18.** "Six hundred threescore and six" = VICARIVS FILII DEI, the number of a man, the man of sin.

  > "The most plausible name we have ever seen suggested as containing the number of the beast, is the title which the pope applies to himself.... That title is this: Vicarius Filii Dei, “Vicegerent of the Son of God.”... Taking the letters... which the Latins used as numerals, and giving them their numerical value, we have just 666." — DAR 580.
