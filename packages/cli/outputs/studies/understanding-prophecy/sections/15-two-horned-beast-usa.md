# 15. The Two-Horned Beast and the Image of the Beast — the United States

> _After the leopard-beast (the Papacy) goes into captivity in 1798, John sees "another beast coming up out of the earth" with two lamb-like horns — a youthful power, rising not by conquest but like a silent seed in a previously unoccupied land, republican in government (no crowns) and Protestant in religion. Of all the prophetic symbols only this one remains, and of all the nations only one fits every specification: the United States of America. But the lamb that should speak gently is foretold to "speak as a dragon" — making an image to the beast (a church-state union enforcing religious dogma, as Rome did) and forcing its mark by no-buy/no-sell penalties and a death decree. (DAR 527-578; GC 439-450; TTR 860-862.)_

### The third symbol — "another beast out of the earth" (Rev. 13:11)

- **Revelation 13:11** — "And I beheld another beast coming up out of the earth; and he had two horns like a lamb, and he spake as a dragon." This is the third great symbol in the prophetic chain. The dragon (pagan Rome) and the leopard-beast (papal Rome) each represented a great false-religious system; analogy requires this third beast to represent the only remaining controlling system — Protestantism — embodied in a nationality. _(DAR 527.3-528.1.)_
- **Daniel 7:2-3** — Daniel's four world-empires arose when "the four winds of the heaven strove upon the great sea." Sea = strife of peoples; so a beast rising from the **earth** by contrast denotes a new, sparsely-settled territory, not the crowded Old World. _(DAR 534.2; GC 439.3-440.1.)_
- **Revelation 17:15** — "The waters... are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues." The Bible's own dictionary: sea/waters = densely-peopled lands; therefore "out of the earth" points to the Western Hemisphere, previously unknown to civilization. _(DAR 534.2; GC 440.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:1** — The first (leopard) beast rose "up out of the sea." The deliberate contrast with verse 11's "out of the earth" marks the second beast as a power arising where no nation was overthrown to make room for it. _(DAR 534.2-535.1.)_

### Nine marks of identity — only the United States fits

- **Revelation 13:11 ("coming up")** — The Greek ἀναβαῖνον means "to grow or spring up as a plant"; the very figure secular historians chose unbidden — "like a silent seed we grew into empire," "emerging amid the silence of the earth." Smith lists nine specifications, all met by the U.S. and by no other nation. _(DAR 535.1-536.2; 540.1-540.10.)_
- **Revelation 13:11 (chronology, "another beast")** — John sees this beast "coming up" at the very moment the leopard-beast goes into captivity (vs. 3, 10) — that is, 1798, when the Papacy's deadly wound was inflicted. In 1798 only one young power in the New World was rising into prominence: the United States, independent since 1776, its independence sealed by the 1783 treaty. _(DAR 529.8-530.3; 537.1; GC 440.2.)_
- **Revelation 13:11 (age, "two horns like a lamb")** — Why "like a lamb"? Because the horns of a lamb are horns that have but just begun to grow — denoting a **youthful** power. In 1798 England, France, and Russia were old; only the New World yields a young, rising power. _(DAR 531.3-531.4; 537.2.)_
- **Revelation 13:11 (manner of rise)** — Lamb-like = innocent, gentle, harmless in appearance. Other nations arose by overthrowing predecessors with "aggressive wars and successful conquests"; the U.S. grew peacefully into vast "mighty regions" — "victoria sine clade" — adding nearly two million square miles in fifty years without dethroning any power. _(DAR 534.4-536.2; 540.4-540.5.)_

### The two horns — republicanism and Protestantism (civil and religious liberty)

- **Revelation 13:11 (no crowns)** — The two lamb-horns bear no crowns. A crown symbolizes a kingly or monarchical government; the absence of crowns marks a **republic**, where power is lodged in the hands of the people. Constitution Art. IV §4: "The United States shall guarantee to every State... a republican form of government." _(DAR 538.1-538.2.)_
- **Revelation 13:14 ("saying to them that dwell on the earth, that they should make an image")** — National action is referred to **the people** themselves ("saying to them"), not handed down by a monarch — proof the government is republican, the power vested in the populace. _(DAR 539.1; GC 442.3.)_
- **Revelation 13:12 ("causeth... them... to worship the first beast")** — In Catholic lands the people worship the beast voluntarily; here they render that worship only when **caused** (compelled) by the civil power — proof the nation's own religion is non-Catholic, i.e. **Protestant**. The two horns = republicanism + Protestantism, civil and religious liberty. Constitution: "No religious test shall ever be required... Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion." _(DAR 538.1; 539.2; GC 442.2.)_

### "He spake as a dragon" — the lamb's voice turns persecuting

- **Revelation 13:11 ("spake as a dragon")** — After all the lamb-like good features comes the painful clause: he "spake as a dragon." The dragon and the leopard-beast were both persecutors; this beast, speaking like them, shows himself a dragon at heart — destined likewise to persecute. The "speaking" of a nation is the action of its legislative and judicial authorities. _(DAR 539.3-541.1; GC 442.1-442.2.)_
- **Matthew 7:15** — A wolf in sheep's clothing: lamb-like in profession, dragon in deed. The contradiction between professed liberty and actual intolerance is the very note Revelation 13:11 sounds. _(GC 442.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:13-14 ("great wonders... fire from heaven... deceiveth")** — Miracle-working deception, originating in the U.S. (Spiritualism, begun at Hydesville, N.Y., 1848), prepares men to "make an image to the beast." This identifies the two-horned beast with the false prophet of Revelation 19:20, who "wrought miracles before the beast." _(DAR 542.3-545.1.)_
- **Revelation 19:20** — "The beast was taken, and with him the false prophet that wrought miracles before him, with which he deceived them that had received the mark of the beast, and them that worshipped his image." The false prophet IS the two-horned beast — the miracle-power that deceives men into the mark and image. _(DAR 542.4-543.2.)_

### The image of the beast — a church-state union like Rome's

- **Revelation 13:14-15 ("make an image to the beast... give life unto the image")** — To know the image we must know the beast: the Papacy was "a church clothed with civil power," an ecclesiastical body authorized to punish dissenters with confiscation, imprisonment, torture, and death (dating from Justinian's decree, A.D. 538). An IMAGE of it = another ecclesiastical establishment clothed with similar civil power. _(DAR 546.2-546.3; GC 443.1-443.2.)_
- **Revelation 13:14 ("they should make an image")** — How formed in a republic? When the leading Protestant churches, uniting on doctrines held in common, induce the state to enforce their decrees, "then Protestant America will have formed an image of the Roman hierarchy," and civil penalties on dissenters will inevitably follow. The two-horned beast gives this image "life" — legal power to act. _(DAR 546.4-547.1; GC 445.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:15 ("the image... should both speak, and cause that as many as would not worship the image... should be killed")** — The image "speaks" by legislative enactment; its sole stated penalty is death — the death decree against those who refuse its worship. _(DAR 549.1-549.4.)_
- **Daniel 3:5-6** — Nebuchadnezzar's golden image: "whoso falleth not down and worshippeth shall the same hour be cast into the midst of a burning fiery furnace." The ancient type of a state-enforced image-worship under death penalty — the same demand Revelation 13:15 foretells. _(Cf. DAR 549.2.)_

### The mark of the beast — Sunday vs. the Sabbath

- **Revelation 13:16-17 ("a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads; and that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark")** — The two-horned beast enforces the **first** beast's mark. The mark is found in the Papacy's special characteristic: the little horn that "thinks to change times and laws" (Dan. 7:25) — the one change actually made is the change of the Sabbath from the seventh day to Sunday. _(DAR 548.5-551.1; 557.3-558.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:16-17 (right hand / forehead)** — From the ancient custom (Bishop Newton, Prideaux): servants and devotees received their master's or deity's mark on hand or forehead. Symbolically, forehead = belief/conviction, hand = act of obedience to the rival law. _(DAR 550.2-551.1.)_
- **Daniel 2:43** — "They shall mingle themselves with the seed of men: but they shall not cleave one to another, even as iron is not mixed with clay." The iron-and-clay feet picture the attempted welding of church (clay) and state (iron) in the divided modern nations — a union that cannot truly cohere, the political condition out of which the image-movement springs. _(See the kingdom-sequence study on Dan. 2; cf. DAR 565-567 on church-state agitation.)_
- **Catholic testimony (Sabbath changed by Rome)** — Standard catechisms admit there is "no Scriptural authority" for Sunday: "the church... has substituted Sunday for Saturday," claiming "the very act of changing the Sabbath into Sunday" as the "mark" of her authority. To honor Sunday by law is thus to honor Rome's badge. _(DAR 555.1-557.2; TTR 860.1-860.2.)_

### Enforcement — buy/sell penalty and death decree

- **Revelation 13:17 ("no man might buy or sell")** — The penalty for refusing the mark is a forfeiture of social and economic privileges — a deprivation of the right to buy and sell, making the faithful "outlaws in the land." _(DAR 549.3-549.4.)_
- **Revelation 13:15 (the death decree)** — Beyond economic penalty, "as many as would not worship the image... should be killed." Smith notes this is the will and endeavor of the power — "he makes such an enactment, passes such a law, but is not able to execute it; for God interposes" (cf. Rev. 15:2; Ps. 91:9-10). _(DAR 577.3-578.3.)_
- **Sunday-law enforcement already begun** — Prophecy made concrete: under "Factory Law" pretexts Adventist printing offices were closed in Switzerland and London on Sunday grounds — early instalments of the predicted persecution, while the worship of the beast spreads worldwide though the "image" itself is found only in the U.S. _(DAR 577.1-577.2.)_

### The third angel's warning and the dividing line

- **Revelation 14:9-11** — "If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark... the same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God." God's most fearful threat in Scripture is leveled against this very worship — the merciful warning given just before the crisis. _(DAR 549.3-550.1; GC 445.2-445.3.)_
- **Revelation 14:12** — "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." Set in direct contrast to the beast-worshippers; keeping God's law (the Sabbath included) on one hand, and its violation on the other, draws the final line between the two companies. _(GC 445.3-446.1.)_
- **Revelation 16:13-14** — "Three unclean spirits like frogs... the spirits of devils, working miracles, go forth unto the kings of the earth... to gather them to the battle of that great day." Dragon (Spiritualism/paganism), beast (Papacy), false prophet (apostate Protestant America) unite in a threefold confederacy — the same satanic miracle-power of Revelation 13:13-14 marshalling the world for the last conflict. _(DAR 546.1; 543.2.)_
- **Revelation 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins." Against the church-state image and its mark, heaven's call is separation — the loud cry that gathers the faithful before the plagues fall. _(Cf. GC 445-446.)_

### Two republics — Rome and America (A.T. Jones)

- **Revelation 13:11-17 fulfilled in the Sunday movement** — Jones: "Whoever observes Sunday does recognize the authority of the papacy.... The enactment of Sunday laws does recognize the authority of the Catholic Church.... Just what there is in this movement, therefore, is the literal fulfillment of that prophecy in Revelation 13:11-17. It is the making of the image of the papal beast, and the enforcement of the worship of the beast and his image." _(TTR 860.2.)_
- **The lesson of the ages — separation of church and state** — Jones: paganism, Catholicism, and false Protestantism each demonstrated "that any connection between religion and the State is debasing to both"; civil and religious liberty stand only "when religion and the State are separate" — the very principle the two-horned beast, speaking as a dragon, abandons in forming the image. _(TTR 861.1-862.)_
