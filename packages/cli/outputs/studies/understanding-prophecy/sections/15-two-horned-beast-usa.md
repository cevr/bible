# 15. The Two-Horned Beast and the Image of the Beast — the United States

> _After the leopard-beast (the Papacy) goes into captivity in 1798, John sees "another beast coming up out of the earth" with two lamb-like horns — a youthful power, rising not by conquest but like a silent seed in a previously unoccupied land, republican in government (no crowns) and Protestant in religion. Of all the prophetic symbols only this one remains, and of all the nations only one fits every specification: the United States of America. But the lamb that should speak gently is foretold to "speak as a dragon" — making an image to the beast (a church-state union enforcing religious dogma, as Rome did) and forcing its mark by no-buy/no-sell penalties and a death decree. (DAR 527-578; GC 439-450; TTR 860-862.)_

### The third symbol — "another beast out of the earth" (Rev. 13:11)

- **Revelation 13:11** — "And I beheld another beast coming up out of the earth; and he had two horns like a lamb, and he spake as a dragon." This is the third great symbol in the prophetic chain. The dragon (pagan Rome) and the leopard-beast (papal Rome) each represented a great false-religious system; analogy requires this third beast to represent the only remaining controlling system — Protestantism — embodied in a nationality.

  > "These verses bring to view the third great symbol in the line of prophecy we are examining, usually denominated the two-horned beast.... The dragon, pagan Rome, and the leopard beast, papal Rome, present before us great organizations standing as the representatives of two great systems of false religion. Analogy would seem to require that the remaining symbol, the two-horned beast, have a similar application.... But the only remaining system which is exercising a controlling influence in the world to-day is Protestantism." — DAR 527.3.

  > "If, then, Protestantism is the religion to which we are to look, to what nationality, as the representative of that religion, does the prophecy have application?... A careful investigation has led to the conclusion that it does apply to Protestant America, or the government of the United States." — DAR 528.1.

- **Daniel 7:2-3** — Daniel's four world-empires arose when "the four winds of the heaven strove upon the great sea." Sea = strife of peoples; so a beast rising from the **earth** by contrast denotes a new, sparsely-settled territory, not the crowded Old World.

  > "Another consideration pointing to the locality of this power is drawn from the fact that John saw it arising from the earth. If the sea, from which the leopard beast arose (Revelation 13:1), denotes peoples, nations, and multitudes (Revelation 17:15), the earth would suggest, by contrast, a new and previously unoccupied territory." — DAR 534.2.

  > "But the beast with lamblike horns was seen 'coming up out of the earth.'... It could not, then, arise among the crowded and struggling nationalities of the Old World—that turbulent sea of 'peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues.' It must be sought in the Western Continent." — GC 439.3-440.1.

- **Revelation 17:15** — "The waters... are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues." The Bible's own dictionary: sea/waters = densely-peopled lands; therefore "out of the earth" points to the Western Hemisphere, previously unknown to civilization.

  > "If the sea, from which the leopard beast arose (Revelation 13:1), denotes peoples, nations, and multitudes (Revelation 17:15), the earth would suggest, by contrast, a new and previously unoccupied territory." — DAR 534.2.

  > "Instead of overthrowing other powers to establish itself, the nation thus represented must arise in territory previously unoccupied and grow up gradually and peacefully.... It must be sought in the Western Continent." — GC 440.1.

- **Revelation 13:1** — The first (leopard) beast rose "up out of the sea." The deliberate contrast with verse 11's "out of the earth" marks the second beast as a power arising where no nation was overthrown to make room for it.

  > "And this expression must have been designedly used to point out the contrast between the rise of this beast and that of other national prophetic symbols.... But no other nation was overturned in order to make room for the United States, and the struggle for its independence was already fifteen years in the past when it came into the field of prophecy. The prophet saw only peace." — DAR 534.2-535.1.

### Nine marks of identity — only the United States fits

- **Revelation 13:11 ("coming up")** — The Greek ἀναβαῖνον means "to grow or spring up as a plant"; the very figure secular historians chose unbidden — "like a silent seed we grew into empire," "emerging amid the silence of the earth." Smith lists nine specifications, all met by the U.S. and by no other nation.

  > "The word which is used in verse 11, to describe the manner in which this beast comes up, is very expressive. It is ἀναβαῖνον (anabainon), one of the prominent definitions of which is, 'To grow or spring up as a plant.' And it is a remarkable fact that this very figure has been chosen by political writers, without any reference to the prophecy, as the one conveying the best idea of the manner in which this government has arisen." — DAR 535.1.

  > "(1.) That the government symbolized by the two-horned beast must be some government distinct from the powers of the Old World, whether civil or ecclesiastical." — DAR 540.1.

- **Revelation 13:11 (chronology, "another beast")** — John sees this beast "coming up" at the very moment the leopard-beast goes into captivity (vs. 3, 10) — that is, 1798, when the Papacy's deadly wound was inflicted. In 1798 only one young power in the New World was rising into prominence: the United States, independent since 1776, its independence sealed by the 1783 treaty.

  > "It was at the time when this beast went into captivity, or was killed (politically) with the sword (verse 10)... that John saw the two-horned beast coming up. If the leopard beast, as we have conclusively proved, signifies the papacy, and the going into captivity met its fulfillment in the temporary overthrow of the popedom by the French in 1798, then we have the time definitely specified when we are to look for the rise of this power." — DAR 529.8.

  > "In 1783, the war of the Revolution closed with a treaty of peace with Great Britain, whereby the independence of the United States was acknowledged.... Thus are we brought to the year 1798, when this government is introduced into prophecy." — DAR 537.1.

  > "What nation of the New World was in 1798 rising into power, giving promise of strength and greatness, and attracting the attention of the world? The application of the symbol admits of no question.... it points unmistakably to the United States of America." — GC 440.2.

- **Revelation 13:11 (age, "two horns like a lamb")** — Why "like a lamb"? Because the horns of a lamb are horns that have but just begun to grow — denoting a **youthful** power. In 1798 England, France, and Russia were old; only the New World yields a young, rising power.

  > "Why does he add, 'like a lamb'? It must be for the purpose of denoting the character of this beast, showing that it is not only of a very innocent and harmless demeanor, ostensibly, but also that it is a very youthful power; for the horns of a lamb are horns that have but just begun to grow." — DAR 531.3.

  > "What notable power was at that time coming into prominence, but still in its youth? England was not, nor was France, nor Russia, nor any European power. For a young and rising power at that epoch, we are obliged to turn our eyes to the New World." — DAR 531.4.

- **Revelation 13:11 (manner of rise)** — Lamb-like = innocent, gentle, harmless in appearance. Other nations arose by overthrowing predecessors with "aggressive wars and successful conquests"; the U.S. grew peacefully into vast "mighty regions" — "victoria sine clade" — adding nearly two million square miles in fifty years without dethroning any power.

  > "New nations generally arise by overthrowing other nations, and taking their place. But no other nation was overturned in order to make room for the United States, and the struggle for its independence was already fifteen years in the past when it came into the field of prophecy. The prophet saw only peace." — DAR 534.4-535.1.

  > "(4.) That it must rise in a peaceful and quiet manner, not augmenting its power, as other nations have done, by aggressive wars and successful conquests. (5.) That its progress must be so rapid as to strike the beholder with as much wonder as would the perceptible growth of an animal before his eyes." — DAR 540.4-540.5.

### The two horns — republicanism and Protestantism (civil and religious liberty)

- **Revelation 13:11 (no crowns)** — The two lamb-horns bear no crowns. A crown symbolizes a kingly or monarchical government; the absence of crowns marks a **republic**, where power is lodged in the hands of the people. Constitution Art. IV §4: "The United States shall guarantee to every State... a republican form of government."

  > "Article IV, sec. 4 of the Constitution of the United States, reads: 'The United States shall guarantee to every State in this Union a republican form of government.'... These articles profess the amplest guarantee of civil and religious liberty, the entire and perpetual separation of church and state; and what better symbols of them could be given than 'two horns like a lamb'?" — DAR 538.1.

  > "The two-horned beast symbolizes a nation with a republican form of government. This is shown by the absence of crowns both upon its head and its horns. A crown is an appropriate symbol of a kingly or monarchical form of government; and the absence of crowns, as in this case, would suggest a government in which the power is not vested in any such ruling member, but is, per consequence, lodged in the hands of the people." — DAR 538.2.

- **Revelation 13:14 ("saying to them that dwell on the earth, that they should make an image")** — National action is referred to **the people** themselves ("saying to them"), not handed down by a monarch — proof the government is republican, the power vested in the populace.

  > "From verse 14 we learn that appeal is made to the people when any national action is to be performed: 'Saying to them that dwell on the earth, that they should make an image to the beast,' etc. Were the government a monarchy, national questions would hardly be submitted in this unqualified manner, to the people; and the fact that appeal is here made to the people shows that the form of the government is such that the power is vested in their hands." — DAR 539.1.

  > "'Saying to them that dwell on the earth, that they should make an image to the beast.' Here is clearly presented a form of government in which the legislative power rests with the people, a most striking evidence that the United States is the nation denoted in the prophecy." — GC 442.3.

- **Revelation 13:12 ("causeth... them... to worship the first beast")** — In Catholic lands the people worship the beast voluntarily; here they render that worship only when **caused** (compelled) by the civil power — proof the nation's own religion is non-Catholic, i.e. **Protestant**. The two horns = republicanism + Protestantism, civil and religious liberty. Constitution: "No religious test shall ever be required... Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion."

  > "These articles profess the amplest guarantee of civil and religious liberty, the entire and perpetual separation of church and state; and what better symbols of them could be given than 'two horns like a lamb'?" — DAR 538.1.

  > "But in all Catholic countries, the people voluntarily worship the beast, or obey the dictates of Catholicism, without being 'caused,' or compelled, to do so by the government. The fact that the people of this government do not render this worship till caused to do so by the civil power, shows that the religion they entertain is not Catholicism. As an almost inevitable consequence, it follows that it is Protestantism." — DAR 539.2.

  > "The Constitution provides that 'Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion, or prohibiting the free exercise thereof,' and that 'no religious test shall ever be required as a qualification to any office or public trust under the United States.' Only in flagrant violation of these safeguards to the nation's liberty, can any religious observance be enforced by civil authority." — GC 442.2.

### "He spake as a dragon" — the lamb's voice turns persecuting

- **Revelation 13:11 ("spake as a dragon")** — After all the lamb-like good features comes the painful clause: he "spake as a dragon." The dragon and the leopard-beast were both persecutors; this beast, speaking like them, shows himself a dragon at heart — destined likewise to persecute. The "speaking" of a nation is the action of its legislative and judicial authorities.

  > "9. The Dragon Voice. — After contemplating all the good features presented in this symbol, it is with pain we read that 'he spake as a dragon.'" — DAR 539.3.

  > "The 'speaking' of the nation is the action of its legislative and judicial authorities.... The prediction that it will speak 'as a dragon' and exercise 'all the power of the first beast' plainly foretells a development of the spirit of intolerance and persecution that was manifested by the nations represented by the dragon and the leopardlike beast." — GC 442.1.

- **Matthew 7:15** — A wolf in sheep's clothing: lamb-like in profession, dragon in deed. The contradiction between professed liberty and actual intolerance is the very note Revelation 13:11 sounds.

  > "The lamblike horns and dragon voice of the symbol point to a striking contradiction between the professions and the practice of the nation thus represented.... By such action it will give the lie to those liberal and peaceful principles which it has put forth as the foundation of its policy." — GC 442.1.

- **Revelation 13:13-14 ("great wonders... fire from heaven... deceiveth")** — Miracle-working deception, originating in the U.S. (Spiritualism, begun at Hydesville, N.Y., 1848), prepares men to "make an image to the beast." This identifies the two-horned beast with the false prophet of Revelation 19:20, who "wrought miracles before the beast."

  > "In that part of the prediction which sets forth the work of the two-horned beast, we read that 'he doeth great wonders, so that he maketh fire come down from heaven on the earth in the sight of men.' In this specification we have still further proof that the United States is the government represented by the two-horned beast." — DAR 542.3.

  > "Spiritualism answers to the prophecy in that it had its origin in the United States, thus connecting its wonders with the work of the two-horned beast. Commencing in Hydesville, N. Y., in the family of Mr. John D. Fox, in the latter part of March, 1848, it spread with incredible rapidity through all the world." — DAR 545.1.

- **Revelation 19:20** — "The beast was taken, and with him the false prophet that wrought miracles before him, with which he deceived them that had received the mark of the beast, and them that worshipped his image." The false prophet IS the two-horned beast — the miracle-power that deceives men into the mark and image.

  > "This identifies the two-horned beast with the false prophet of Revelation 19:20; for this false prophet is the power that works miracles before the beast, 'with which he deceived them that had received the mark of the beast, and them that worshiped his image,' — the identical work of the two-horned beast." — DAR 542.4.

  > "The earthly government with which it was to be especially connected is that represented by the two-horned beast, or false prophet. The agency lying back of the outward manifestations was to be Satanic, the spirit of devils." — DAR 543.2.

### The image of the beast — a church-state union like Rome's

- **Revelation 13:14-15 ("make an image to the beast... give life unto the image")** — To know the image we must know the beast: the Papacy was "a church clothed with civil power," an ecclesiastical body authorized to punish dissenters with confiscation, imprisonment, torture, and death (dating from Justinian's decree, A.D. 538). An IMAGE of it = another ecclesiastical establishment clothed with similar civil power.

  > "Closely associated with this working of miracles is the erection of an image to the beast.... The deception accomplished by the working of the miracles prepares the way for compliance with this demand for the formation of an image to the beast." — DAR 546.2.

  > "The papacy was a church clothed with civil power, — an ecclesiastical body having authority to punish all dissenters with confiscation of goods, imprisonment, torture, and death. What would be an image of the papacy? — Another ecclesiastical establishment clothed with similar power." — DAR 546.3.

  > "Then to learn what the image is like and how it is to be formed we must study the characteristics of the beast itself—the papacy." — GC 443.1.

  > "The result was the papacy, a church that controlled the power of the state and employed it to further her own ends, especially for the punishment of 'heresy.' In order for the United States to form an image of the beast, the religious power must so control the civil government that the authority of the state will also be employed by the church to accomplish her own ends." — GC 443.2.

- **Revelation 13:14 ("they should make an image")** — How formed in a republic? When the leading Protestant churches, uniting on doctrines held in common, induce the state to enforce their decrees, "then Protestant America will have formed an image of the Roman hierarchy," and civil penalties on dissenters will inevitably follow. The two-horned beast gives this image "life" — legal power to act.

  > "There are certain points which they hold in common, and which are sufficient to form a basis of co-operation. Chief among these may be mentioned the doctrine of the conscious state of the dead and the immortality of the soul... and also the doctrine that the first day of the week is the Christian Sabbath." — DAR 546.4.

  > "Let, now, an ecclesiastical organization be formed by these churches; let the government legalize such organization, and give it power... to enforce upon the people the dogmas which the different denominations can all adopt as the basis of union, and what do we have? — Just what the prophecy represents, — an image to the papal beast, endowed with life by the two-horned beast, to speak and act with power." — DAR 547.1.

  > "When the leading churches of the United States, uniting upon such points of doctrine as are held by them in common, shall influence the state to enforce their decrees and to sustain their institutions, then Protestant America will have formed an image of the Roman hierarchy, and the infliction of civil penalties upon dissenters will inevitably result." — GC 445.1.

- **Revelation 13:15 ("the image... should both speak, and cause that as many as would not worship the image... should be killed")** — The image "speaks" by legislative enactment; its sole stated penalty is death — the death decree against those who refuse its worship.

  > "The acts ascribed to the image are, speaking, and enforcing the worship of itself under the penalty of death; and this is the only enactment which the prophecy mentions as enforced under the death penalty." — DAR 549.2.

- **Daniel 3:5-6** — Nebuchadnezzar's golden image: "whoso falleth not down and worshippeth shall the same hour be cast into the midst of a burning fiery furnace." The ancient type of a state-enforced image-worship under death penalty — the same demand Revelation 13:15 foretells.

  > "The acts ascribed to the image are, speaking, and enforcing the worship of itself under the penalty of death; and this is the only enactment which the prophecy mentions as enforced under the death penalty." — DAR 549.2.

### The mark of the beast — Sunday vs. the Sabbath

- **Revelation 13:16-17 ("a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads; and that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark")** — The two-horned beast enforces the **first** beast's mark. The mark is found in the Papacy's special characteristic: the little horn that "thinks to change times and laws" (Dan. 7:25) — the one change actually made is the change of the Sabbath from the seventh day to Sunday.

  > "12. The Mark of the Beast. — The two-horned beast enforces upon its subjects the mark of the first beast." — DAR 548.5.

  > "We would therefore remind such persons that, according to the prophecy, the only change ever to be made in the law of God, was to be made by the little horn of Daniel 7, the man of sin of 2 Thessalonians 2; and the only change that has been made in it, is the change of the Sabbath." — DAR 557.3.

- **Revelation 13:16-17 (right hand / forehead)** — From the ancient custom (Bishop Newton, Prideaux): servants and devotees received their master's or deity's mark on hand or forehead. Symbolically, forehead = belief/conviction, hand = act of obedience to the rival law.

  > "We therefore now call attention to the very important inquiry, What constitutes the mark of the beast? The figure of a mark is borrowed from an ancient custom. Bishop Newton (Dissertations on the Prophecies, Vol. III, p. 241) says: —" — DAR 550.2.

  > "It would naturally be looked for in some of the special characteristics of the papal power. Daniel, describing that power under the symbol of a little horn, speaks of it as waging a special warfare against God, wearing out the saints of the Most High, and thinking to change times and laws." — DAR 551.1.

- **Daniel 2:43** — "They shall mingle themselves with the seed of men: but they shall not cleave one to another, even as iron is not mixed with clay." The iron-and-clay feet picture the attempted welding of church (clay) and state (iron) in the divided modern nations — a union that cannot truly cohere, the political condition out of which the image-movement springs.

  > "But to a union of church and state in the popular sense of the phrase, — a union, not of one church, but of all the churches recognized as orthodox, or evangelical... giving the churches the privilege of enforcing, by civil laws, institutions and usages of religion... to such a union, we say, they are not opposed. They are essentially and practically, despite their professions, open advocates of a union of church and state." — DAR 565.4.

- **Catholic testimony (Sabbath changed by Rome)** — Standard catechisms admit there is "no Scriptural authority" for Sunday: "the church... has substituted Sunday for Saturday," claiming "the very act of changing the Sabbath into Sunday" as the "mark" of her authority. To honor Sunday by law is thus to honor Rome's badge.

  > "'Ans. Had she not such power, she could not have done that in which all modern religionists agree with her — she could not have substituted the observance of Sunday, the first day of the week, for the observance of Saturday, the seventh day, a change for which there is no Scriptural authority.'... 'Ans. By the very act of changing the Sabbath into Sunday, which Protestants allow of.'" — DAR 556.

  > "Catholics plainly acknowledge that there is no Scriptural authority for the change they have made, but that it rests wholly upon the authority of the church; and they claim it as a token, or mark, of the authority of that church; the 'very act of changing the Sabbath into Sunday' being set forth as proof of its power in this respect." — DAR 557.2.

  > "The papacy substituted the Sunday institution for the Sabbath of the Lord, enforced its acceptance and observance upon all, and prohibited under a curse the keeping of the Sabbath of the Lord She did it, and justified herself in it, precisely as these now do." — TTR 860.1.

### Enforcement — buy/sell penalty and death decree

- **Revelation 13:17 ("no man might buy or sell")** — The penalty for refusing the mark is a forfeiture of social and economic privileges — a deprivation of the right to buy and sell, making the faithful "outlaws in the land."

  > "The penalty attached to a refusal to receive this mark is a forfeiture of all social privileges, a deprivation of the right to buy and sell. The mark is the mark of the papal beast." — DAR 549.3.

  > "...and if they refuse to do this, they lose the rights of citizenship, and become outlaws in the land; and they must do that which constitutes the worship of the image of the beast, or forfeit their lives." — DAR 549.4.

- **Revelation 13:15 (the death decree)** — Beyond economic penalty, "as many as would not worship the image... should be killed." Smith notes this is the will and endeavor of the power — "he makes such an enactment, passes such a law, but is not able to execute it; for God interposes" (cf. Rev. 15:2; Ps. 91:9-10).

  > "Some one may now say, As you expect this movement to carry, you must look for a period of religious persecution in the United States; nay, more, you must take the position that all the saints of God are to be put to death; for the image is to cause that all who will not worship it shall be killed." — DAR 577.3.

  > "He causes all to receive a mark, and all who will not worship the image to be killed; that is, he wills, purposes, and endeavors to do this. He makes such an enactment, passes such a law, but is not able to execute it; for God interposes in behalf of his people." — DAR 578.3.

- **Sunday-law enforcement already begun** — Prophecy made concrete: under "Factory Law" pretexts Adventist printing offices were closed in Switzerland and London on Sunday grounds — early instalments of the predicted persecution, while the worship of the beast spreads worldwide though the "image" itself is found only in the U.S.

  > "In Switzerland, by an iniquitous application of a so-called 'Factory Law,' in behalf of Sunday, the authorities have closed the large printing establishment of the Seventh-day Adventists.... This has been repeated in London, where the office of a Seventh-day Adventist paper, The Present Truth, has been closed by the English authorities, on the same grounds." — DAR 577.1.

  > "While, according to the prophecy, the 'image' can be looked for only in the United States, the worship of the beast will prevail in other countries also; for all the world is to wonder after the beast." — DAR 577.2.

### The third angel's warning and the dividing line

- **Revelation 14:9-11** — "If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark... the same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God." God's most fearful threat in Scripture is leveled against this very worship — the merciful warning given just before the crisis.

  > "On the other hand, God sends forth a message a little before the fearful crisis is upon us... declaring that all who do any of these things 'shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation.'... he who does comply, exposes himself to the most terrible threatenings of divine wrath to be found in the word of God." — DAR 549.4.

  > "The third angel's warning is: 'If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark in his forehead, or in his hand, the same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God.' 'The beast' mentioned in this message, whose worship is enforced by the two-horned beast, is the first, or leopardlike beast of Revelation 13—the papacy." — GC 445.2.

- **Revelation 14:12** — "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." Set in direct contrast to the beast-worshippers; keeping God's law (the Sabbath included) on one hand, and its violation on the other, draws the final line between the two companies.

  > "Since those who keep God's commandments are thus placed in contrast with those that worship the beast and his image and receive his mark, it follows that the keeping of God's law, on the one hand, and its violation, on the other, will make the distinction between the worshipers of God and the worshipers of the beast." — GC 445.3.

  > "The special characteristic of the beast, and therefore of his image, is the breaking of God's commandments. Says Daniel, of the little horn, the papacy: 'He shall think to change times and the law.'... Such an act of obedience to papal laws would be a mark of allegiance to the pope in the place of God." — GC 446.1.

- **Revelation 16:13-14** — "Three unclean spirits like frogs... the spirits of devils, working miracles, go forth unto the kings of the earth... to gather them to the battle of that great day." Dragon (Spiritualism/paganism), beast (Papacy), false prophet (apostate Protestant America) unite in a threefold confederacy — the same satanic miracle-power of Revelation 13:13-14 marshalling the world for the last conflict.

  > "It is thus preparing to fulfill Revelation 16:14, and gather the nations to the battle of the great day." — DAR 546.1.

  > "Thus we have a prophecy (and there are many others) setting forth the development, in the last days, of a wonderworking power, manifested to a startling and unprecedented degree in the interest of falsehood and error. The earthly government with which it was to be especially connected is that represented by the two-horned beast, or false prophet. The agency lying back of the outward manifestations was to be Satanic, the spirit of devils." — DAR 543.2.

- **Revelation 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins." Against the church-state image and its mark, heaven's call is separation — the loud cry that gathers the faithful before the plagues fall.

  > "When the leading churches of the United States, uniting upon such points of doctrine as are held by them in common, shall influence the state to enforce their decrees and to sustain their institutions, then Protestant America will have formed an image of the Roman hierarchy, and the infliction of civil penalties upon dissenters will inevitably result." — GC 445-446.

### Two republics — Rome and America (A.T. Jones)

- **Revelation 13:11-17 fulfilled in the Sunday movement** — Jones: "Whoever observes Sunday does recognize the authority of the papacy.... The enactment of Sunday laws does recognize the authority of the Catholic Church.... Just what there is in this movement, therefore, is the literal fulfillment of that prophecy in Revelation 13:11-17. It is the making of the image of the papal beast, and the enforcement of the worship of the beast and his image."

  > "Therefore, whoever observes Sunday does recognize the authority of the papacy, and does do homage to the Catholic Church. The enactment of Sunday laws does recognize the authority of the Catholic Church.... Just what there is in this movement, therefore, is the literal fulfillment of that prophecy in Revelation 13:11-17. It is the making of the image of the papal beast, and the enforcement of THE WORSHIP OF THE BEAST AND HIS IMAGE." — TTR 860.2.

- **The lesson of the ages — separation of church and state** — Jones: paganism, Catholicism, and false Protestantism each demonstrated "that any connection between religion and the State is debasing to both"; civil and religious liberty stand only "when religion and the State are separate" — the very principle the two-horned beast, speaking as a dragon, abandons in forming the image.

  > "Thus upon a test of ages, by paganism, Catholicism, and false Protestantism, there was demonstrated to the world that any connection between religion and the State is debasing to both; that if the religious power rises superior to the civil power, it is ruinous to the State; and that religious and civil rights are both secure, and religion and liberty go forward together only when religion and the State are separate." — TTR 861.1-862.
