# 19. The Woman Who Rides the Beast — Babylon

> _In prophecy a woman is the symbol of a church: a pure woman, the true church of Christ; a corrupt woman, an apostate church. The woman of Revelation 17 is the great apostasy — Papal Rome, "Babylon the Great, the Mother of Harlots" — arrayed in purple and scarlet, holding a golden cup of abominations, drunken with the blood of the saints, and seated upon a scarlet beast (the civil power) that she controls and guides "as a rider controls the animal upon which he is seated." Her seven heads are the seven mountains, or successive forms of government, of Rome; she is "that great city which reigneth over the kings of the earth." From her come the apostate daughters — fallen Protestantism — and against the whole family heaven cries, "Babylon is fallen... come out of her, my people." (DAR 657-663; GC 381-383.)_

### The Symbol-Key: A Woman = A Church (Pure or Corrupt)

- **2 Corinthians 11:2** — Paul has "espoused" the church "to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ." The faithful church is a chaste woman betrothed to the Lord — the standard against which the harlot is read.

  > "In the Bible the sacred and enduring character of the relation that exists between Christ and His church is represented by the union of marriage.… And Paul employs the same figure in the New Testament when he says: 'I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ.' 2 Corinthians 11:2." — GC 381.

- **Ephesians 5:23-27** — Christ is "the head of the church," who gave Himself to "present it to himself a glorious church, not having spot, or wrinkle... holy and without blemish." The marriage figure: Christ the husband, the pure church His bride.

  > "In Revelation 17 Babylon is represented as a woman—a figure which is used in the Bible as the symbol of a church, a virtuous woman representing a pure church, a vile woman an apostate church." — GC 381.

- **Jeremiah 31:32** — "I was an husband unto them, saith the LORD." God binds His people to Himself by covenant as a husband to a wife; apostasy is therefore adultery.

  > "The Lord has joined His people to Himself by a solemn covenant, He promising to be their God, and they pledging themselves to be His and His alone.… And, again: 'I am married unto you.' Jeremiah 3:14." — GC 381.

- **Revelation 12:1** — The true church is "a woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars" — pure, radiant, heavenly. The harlot of chapter 17 is her counterfeit.

  > "'A woman,' the true church. A corrupt woman is used to represent an apostate or corrupt church. Ezekiel 23:2-4; Revelation 17:3-6, 15, 18. By parity of reasoning, a pure woman, as in this instance, would represent the true church." — DAR 509.

- **Jeremiah 6:2** — The LORD "likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman." Scripture itself supplies the symbol: the people of God pictured as a woman.

  > "In Revelation 17 Babylon is represented as a woman—a figure which is used in the Bible as the symbol of a church, a virtuous woman representing a pure church, a vile woman an apostate church." — GC 381.

### A Corrupt Woman = An Apostate Church; Fornication = Spiritual Idolatry

- **Isaiah 1:21** — "How is the faithful city become an harlot! it was full of judgment; righteousness lodged in it; but now murderers." Departure from God turns the once-pure church into a harlot — the exact transformation pictured in Revelation 17.

  > "The unfaithfulness of the church to Christ in permitting her confidence and affection to be turned from Him, and allowing the love of worldly things to occupy the soul, is likened to the violation of the marriage vow.… 'But thou didst trust in thine own beauty, and playedst the harlot because of thy renown.'" — GC 381-382.

- **Jeremiah 3:6-9** — "Backsliding Israel" went up "upon every high mountain... and there hath played the harlot," and "treacherous" Judah "went and played the harlot also." Unfaithfulness to God is fornication; here are two sister-harlots — mother and daughter apostasy foreshadowed.

  > "'As a wife treacherously departeth from her husband, so have ye dealt treacherously with Me, O house of Israel, saith the Lord;' 'as a wife that committeth adultery, which taketh strangers instead of her husband!' Ezekiel 16:8, 13-15, 32; Jeremiah 3:20." — GC 381.

- **Ezekiel 23:2-4** — "Two women, the daughters of one mother... Aholah the elder, and Aholibah her sister," who "committed whoredoms" — Samaria and Jerusalem. The pattern of a mother-apostasy with harlot daughters; the very framework of Babylon "the mother of harlots."

  > "Babylon is said to be 'the mother of harlots.' By her daughters must be symbolized churches that cling to her doctrines and traditions, and follow her example of sacrificing the truth and the approval of God, in order to form an unlawful alliance with the world." — GC 382-383.

- **Hosea 2:2-5** — "Plead with your mother... for she is not my wife... for she said, I will go after my lovers." Spiritual harlotry = chasing other gods and the world's favor instead of the Lord.

  > "In the New Testament, language very similar is addressed to professed Christians who seek the friendship of the world above the favor of God. Says the apostle James: 'Ye adulterers and adulteresses, know ye not that the friendship of the world is enmity with God?'" — GC 382.

### The Vision of the Woman on the Beast (Revelation 17:1-6)

- **Revelation 17:1-2** — "The judgment of the great whore that sitteth upon many waters: with whom the kings of the earth have committed fornication, and the inhabitants of the earth have been made drunk with the wine of her fornication." The illicit union of church and state, and the world intoxicated with her false doctrines.

  > "Between this church and the kings of the earth there has been illicit connection, and with the wine of her fornication, or her false doctrines, the inhabitants of the earth have been made drunk." — DAR 657.

- **Revelation 17:3** — "I saw a woman sit upon a scarlet coloured beast, full of names of blasphemy, having seven heads and ten horns." The church (woman) seated upon and controlling the civil power (beast) — the prophecy "distinguishes between church and state." She rides and guides it "to her own ends."

  > "This prophecy is more definite than others applicable to the Roman power, in that it distinguishes between church and state. We here have the woman, the church, seated upon a scarlet-colored beast, the civil power, by which she is upheld, and which she controls and guides to her own ends, as a rider controls the animal upon which he is seated." — DAR 657.3.

- **Revelation 17:4** — "Arrayed in purple and scarlet colour, and decked with gold and precious stones and pearls, having a golden cup in her hand full of abominations." Purple and scarlet are "the chief colors in the robes of popes and cardinals"; the golden cup — symbol of pure doctrine — pours out only abominations.

  > "Purple and scarlet are the chief colors in the robes of popes and cardinals.… And from the golden cup in her hand, — symbol of purity of doctrine and profession, which should have contained only that which is unadulterated and pure, … — there came forth only abominations, and wine of her fornication." — DAR 658.1.

- **Revelation 17:5** — "Upon her forehead a name written, MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS AND ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH." Her own brand identifies her: not merely a harlot, but the mother — proof of apostate daughters belonging "to the same great family."

  > "This woman is explicitly called Babylon.… she is called the mother of harlots, as already noticed, which shows that there are other independent religious organizations that constitute the apostate daughters, and belong to the same great family." — DAR 658.2.

- **Revelation 17:6** — "I saw the woman drunken with the blood of the saints, and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus." John wondered because this persecutor was no longer pagan Rome but a power professing the name of Christ — the church drunk on the blood of the faithful.

  > "It was not strange that pagans should persecute Christ's followers; but when he looked forward, and saw a church professedly Christian persecuting the followers of the Lamb, and drunken with their blood, he could but wonder with great amazement." — DAR 658.3-4.

  > "And no other power could be so truly declared 'drunken with the blood of the saints' as that church which has so cruelly persecuted the followers of Christ." — GC 382.

### Seven Heads = Seven Mountains = Rome (Revelation 17:7-11)

- **Revelation 17:9** — "Here is the mind which hath wisdom. The seven heads are seven mountains, on which the woman sitteth." The seven heads are first explained as seven mountains, then as seven kings — "forms of government." The seat is Rome.

  > "The seven heads are explained to be, first, seven mountains, and then seven kings, or forms of government.… The seven forms of government that have existed in the Roman empire are usually enumerated as follows: (1) kingly; (2) consular; (3) decemvirate; (4) dictatorial; (5) triumvirate; (6) imperial; and (7) papal." — DAR 659.3.

- **Revelation 17:10** — "There are seven kings: five are fallen, and one is, and the other is not yet come." The heads are successive in time — not literal hills, which are not successive. So they denote the successive ruling forms of the Roman power.

  > "'Five are fallen,' says the angel, or passed away; 'one is;' the sixth was then reigning; another was to come, and continue for a short space.… Kings, consuls, decemvirs, dictators, and triumvirs had passed away in John's day. He was living under the imperial form." — DAR 659.3.

  > "The seven heads are evidently successive in order of time; for we read, 'Five are fallen, and one is, and the other is not yet come.' Revelation 17. But the seven hills on which Rome is built, are not successive, and it would be absurd to apply such language to them." — DAR 602.3-5.

- **Revelation 17:11** — "The beast that was, and is not, even he is the eighth, and is of the seven, and goeth into perdition." Rome viewed "in three phases" — the same persecuting power reappearing "in its bloody and blasphemous form," doomed to perdition.

  > "Thus Rome in its pagan form was a persecuting power in its relation to the people of God, during which time it constituted the beast that was.… Time passed on, and it degenerated into popery, and again assumed its bloodthirsty and oppressive character, and then it constituted the beast that 'yet is.'" — DAR 659.2-3.

- **Revelation 13:1** — The kindred beast rises "having seven heads and ten horns" with "the name of blasphemy" — the same Roman power, the seven heads marking it across history. The wounded head "cannot be a literal mountain; for it would be folly to speak of wounding a mountain to death."

  > "We are informed in chapter 13 that one of the seven heads was wounded to death. This head therefore cannot be a literal mountain; for it would be folly to speak of wounding a mountain to death." — DAR 602.3.

  > "The head that was wounded to death was the papal head.… whatever is said of the dragon applies to Rome only in its pagan form, and whatever is said of the leopard beast applies to Rome only in its professedly Christian form." — DAR 525.3.

- **Daniel 7:23** — "The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon earth... and shall devour the whole earth." Rome — the fourth world-kingdom — is the beast the harlot rides; the prophets agree on the empire that carries her.

  > "In the explanation in verse 23, the angel said, 'The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon the earth.' These beast are therefore symbols of four great kingdoms." — DAR 113.

### The Waters, the Horns, and the Great City (Revelation 17:12-18)

- **Revelation 17:12-13** — "The ten horns... are ten kings... these have one mind, and shall give their power and strength unto the beast." The kingdoms of Europe were once "unanimous in giving their support to the papacy."

  > "This language must refer to the past, when the kingdoms of Europe were unanimous in giving their support to the papacy, and upholding it in all its pretensions." — DAR 661.1-2.

- **Revelation 17:14** — "These shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings." The final battle — the title "King of kings" belongs to the second coming.

  > "Here we are carried into the future, to the time of the great and final battle; for at this time the Lamb has assumed the title of King of kings and Lord of lords, a title which he does not assume till his second coming." — DAR 661.3.

- **Revelation 17:15** — "The waters which thou sawest, where the whore sitteth, are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues." Scripture defines its own symbol: the "many waters" of verse 1 are the multitudes over whom Babylon reigns.

  > "In verse 15 we have a plain definition of the Scripture symbol of waters; they denote peoples, multitudes, nations, and tongues." — DAR 662.1.

- **Revelation 17:16** — "The ten horns... these shall hate the whore, and shall make her desolate and naked... and burn her with fire." The same civil powers that upheld her at last turn and destroy her — her judgment.

  > "The angel told John, while calling his attention to this subject, that he would show him the judgment of this great harlot. In verse 16 that judgment is specified." — DAR 662.1.

- **Revelation 17:18** — "The woman which thou sawest is that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth." The woman = the city = "the great Roman power" — papal Rome ruling over the kings of the earth.

  > "What city? (See chapter 17:18: 'And the woman which thou sawest is that great city which reigneth over the kings [kingdoms] of the earth.') That city is the papal Roman power." — DAR 504.2.

  > "'The seven heads are seven mountains,' and 'The woman is that great city;' and it will not be difficult to show that the mountains and the city are both used symbolically." — DAR 602.2.

### Babylon: Her Name, Her Cup, Her Fall (Revelation 14:8; 18:1-5)

- **Genesis 11:9** — "Therefore is the name of it called Babel; because the LORD did there confound the language." "Babylon" is derived from "Babel" and "signifies confusion" — the fit name for apostate, sect-divided religion.

  > "The term 'Babylon' is derived from 'Babel,' and signifies confusion. It is employed in Scripture to designate the various forms of false or apostate religion." — GC 381.

- **Jeremiah 51:7** — "Babylon hath been a golden cup in the LORD's hand, that made all the earth drunken: the nations have drunken of her wine." The golden cup and the intoxicating wine of Revelation 17 are drawn straight from ancient Babylon — the type of the spiritual harlot.

  > "And from the golden cup in her hand, — symbol of purity of doctrine and profession, which should have contained only that which is unadulterated and pure, … — there came forth only abominations, and wine of her fornication, fit symbol of her abominable doctrines, and still more abominable practices." — DAR 657-658.

- **Revelation 14:8** — "Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication." The second angel's message: a moral fall — the spreading of false doctrine to all nations.

  > "In Revelation 14 the first angel is followed by a second proclaiming: 'Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication.' Revelation 14:8." — GC 381.

  > "In chapter 14 we had a message announcing the fall of Babylon. Babylon is a term which embraces not only the Roman Catholic Church, but religious bodies which have sprung from her, bringing many of her errors and traditions along with them." — DAR 663.2.

- **Revelation 16:19** — "Great Babylon came in remembrance before God, to give unto her the cup of the wine of the fierceness of his wrath." The cup she filled for others is at last returned to her in judgment.

  > "In verse 19 of the preceding chapter, we were informed that 'great Babylon came in remembrance before God, to give unto her the cup of the wine of the fierceness of his wrath.' The prophet now takes up more particularly the subject of this great Babylon." — DAR 657.2.

- **Revelation 18:1-3** — "Another angel come down from heaven, having great power; and the earth was lightened with his glory... Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen, and is become the habitation of devils." The loud cry — the final, fuller proclamation of the fall, now embracing all apostate religion.

  > "SOME movement of mighty power is symbolized in these verses.… Babylon is a term which embraces not only the Roman Catholic Church, but religious bodies which have sprung from her, bringing many of her errors and traditions along with them." — DAR 663.1-3.

- **Revelation 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues." God still has His people within Babylon; they are called out before the plagues fall.

  > "The people of God are there after her fall, and are called out in order that they may not receive of her plagues; and in these plagues is embraced her literal destruction." — DAR 663.3.

  > "Many of the Protestant churches are following Rome's example of iniquitous connection with 'the kings of the earth'… And the term 'Babylon'—confusion—may be appropriately applied to these bodies." — GC 383.

- **Revelation 18:5** — "For her sins have reached unto heaven, and God hath remembered her iniquities." The cup of her guilt is full; judgment is sealed.

  > "As a consequence of her fall, she piles up an accumulation of sins even to the heavens, and becomes subject to the judgments of God, which can no longer be delayed." — DAR 663.

- **Revelation 18:24** — "In her was found the blood of prophets, and of saints, and of all that were slain upon the earth." The persecuting harlot, drunk with martyr-blood, is held to full account.

  > "All the persecution he had witnessed had been from pagan Rome, the open enemy of Christ.… but when he looked forward, and saw a church professedly Christian persecuting the followers of the Lamb, and drunken with their blood, he could but wonder with great amazement." — DAR 658.4.

- **Revelation 19:2** — "He hath judged the great whore, which did corrupt the earth with her fornication, and hath avenged the blood of his servants at her hand." Heaven's verdict and vindication of the saints she slew.

  > "The apostle here introduces the song of triumph which the redeemed saints strike up on victor harps, when they behold the complete destruction of that great system of opposition to God and his true worship comprehended in great Babylon." — DAR 680.

### The Mother and Her Daughters — Fallen Protestantism

- **Ezekiel 23:4** — "Aholah the elder, and Aholibah her sister... Samaria is Aholah, and Jerusalem Aholibah." The two-sister figure: a mother-apostasy reproduced in her offspring — the prototype of Babylon and her harlot daughters.

  > "Babylon is said to be 'the mother of harlots.' By her daughters must be symbolized churches that cling to her doctrines and traditions, and follow her example of sacrificing the truth and the approval of God, in order to form an unlawful alliance with the world." — GC 382-383.

- **Revelation 17:5** — Babylon is named "THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS." She is not Rome to the exclusion of all others; the title "mother" proves "there are other independent religious organizations that constitute the apostate daughters, and belong to the same great family."

  > "Is Rome, then, Babylon, to the exclusion of all other religious bodies? — No, from the fact that she is called the mother of harlots, as already noticed, which shows that there are other independent religious organizations that constitute the apostate daughters, and belong to the same great family." — DAR 658.2.

- **Revelation 14:8 with 18:2** — "Babylon is fallen, is fallen." By her daughters "must be symbolized churches that cling to her doctrines and traditions" — Protestant bodies "once pure" that "have become corrupt," forming "iniquitous connection with the kings of the earth" or seeking "the favor of the world."

  > "The message of Revelation 14, announcing the fall of Babylon must apply to religious bodies that were once pure and have become corrupt.… Many of the Protestant churches are following Rome's example of iniquitous connection with 'the kings of the earth'… and other denominations, by seeking the favor of the world." — GC 382-383.

  > "Babylon is a term which embraces not only the Roman Catholic Church, but religious bodies which have sprung from her, bringing many of her errors and traditions along with them." — DAR 663.2.

---

_Symbol-key summary: a woman = a church (pure: 2 Cor. 11:2; Rev. 12:1 — corrupt: Isa. 1:21; Jer. 3:6-9). Fornication = spiritual idolatry / unlawful church-state union (Rev. 17:2; Jas. 4:4). The scarlet beast = the civil power the church rides and controls (Rev. 17:3; DAR 657.3). Seven heads = seven mountains = successive forms of Roman government; the seat is Rome (Rev. 17:9-10; DAR 659.3). "That great city" = papal Rome reigning over the kings of the earth (Rev. 17:18; DAR 504.2). Babylon = Babel = confusion = apostate religion — mother (Catholic Rome) and daughters (fallen Protestantism) — whose fall is announced in Rev. 14:8 and proclaimed with power in Rev. 18:1-5, with God's people called out before the plagues. (DAR 657-663; GC 381-383.)_
