# 29. The Millennium and the Two Resurrections

> _The thousand years of Revelation 20 are bounded by two resurrections a thousand years apart. At the second coming the righteous dead rise (the first resurrection) and the living saints are translated — all to heaven; the wicked are slain by the brightness of His coming, and the earth is left a desolate, depopulated waste, with Satan "bound" by sheer circumstance, having no one left to tempt. The redeemed sit in judgment with Christ above for a thousand years. Then the wicked dead rise (the second resurrection), the Holy City descends, the deceived nations compass the camp of the saints, fire comes down from God — the second death — and the cleansing fire purifies the earth for the new creation, where the great controversy is ended forever. (DAR 687-728; GC 653-678; EW 51-54, 289-295.)_

### The Two Resurrections — a Thousand Years Apart

- **John 5:28-29** — "All that are in the graves shall hear his voice, and shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation." Two distinct resurrections to two distinct destinies — the textual key to Revelation 20. _(DAR 693.4; GC 544.)_
- **Revelation 20:5-6** — "The rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished. This is the first resurrection. Blessed and holy is he that hath part in the first resurrection." The righteous rise first; the wicked wait the thousand years — "no language could more plainly prove two resurrections." _(DAR 693.3-693.4.)_
- **Revelation 20:4** — "They lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years" — the souls of them beheaded for Jesus, who had not worshipped the beast nor received his mark. The first resurrection raises the faithful of all ages, including the martyrs against Rome. _(DAR 692.1-693.2.)_
- **1 Thessalonians 4:16-17** — "The Lord himself shall descend... and the dead in Christ shall rise first: then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds." The first resurrection and the translation of the living happen together at the advent — all caught up to meet the Lord. _(DAR 693; EW 52.2.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:51-54** — "We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed, in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump... the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed." The righteous dead and living together "put on immortality" — "death is swallowed up in victory." _(GC 644; EW 287.1.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:23** — "Christ the firstfruits; afterward they that are Christ's at his coming." Resurrection is "every man in his own order" — Christ first, His people at His coming, a definite sequence, not a general rising of all at once. _(GC 547.)_
- **Luke 14:14** — Thou "shalt be recompensed at the resurrection of the just." Christ Himself names a separate "resurrection of the just" — the first resurrection, the reward of the righteous. _(GC 549-550.)_
- **Job 19:25-26** — "I know that my redeemer liveth, and that he shall stand at the latter day upon the earth: and... in my flesh shall I see God." The patriarch's hope was a bodily resurrection at the latter day — the saints' confidence in the first resurrection. _(GC 299.)_

### The Wicked Slain at the Advent — the Earth Left Desolate

- **2 Thessalonians 2:8** — "That Wicked... whom the Lord shall consume with the spirit of his mouth, and shall destroy with the brightness of his coming." The living wicked are not converted but consumed at the appearing of Christ. _(GC 657.1.)_
- **Malachi 4:1** — "The day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble... it shall leave them neither root nor branch." The wicked are burned up root and branch — destroyed, not eternally tortured. _(GC 673.1; EW 295.1.)_
- **Jeremiah 25:33** — "The slain of the LORD shall be at that day from one end of the earth even unto the other end... they shall not be lamented, neither gathered, nor buried; they shall be dung upon the ground." The earth is left strewn with the unburied dead — no living wicked remain to bury them. _(GC 657; EW 289.3.)_
- **Isaiah 24:1** — "The LORD maketh the earth empty, and maketh it waste, and turneth it upside down, and scattereth abroad the inhabitants thereof." The earth is emptied of its inhabitants at the coming of the Lord. _(GC 657.1.)_
- **Isaiah 24:3-6** — "The land shall be utterly emptied, and utterly spoiled... the earth is defiled under the inhabitants thereof; because they have transgressed the laws, changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant... the inhabitants of the earth are burned, and few men left." The curse devours the earth because of the broken law — a depopulated globe. _(GC 657.1; DAR 695.)_
- **Jeremiah 4:23-26** — "I beheld the earth, and, lo, it was without form, and void; and the heavens, and they had no light... and, lo, there was no man, and all the birds of the heavens were fled... the fruitful place was a wilderness, and all the cities thereof were broken down." The millennial earth returns to a chaos like the beginning — "without form, and void," no man upon it. _(GC 658.2-658.3; DAR 690.3.)_
- **Jeremiah 4:27** — "The whole land shall be desolate; yet will I not make a full end." Desolate but not annihilated — reserved a thousand years, then renewed. _(GC 658.2; DAR 695.)_

### Satan Bound a Thousand Years — Bound by Circumstance

- **Revelation 20:1-3** — An angel binds "the dragon, that old serpent... and bound him a thousand years, and cast him into the bottomless pit... that he should deceive the nations no more." Satan is shut up in the abyss of a depopulated, ruined earth. _(DAR 687.1-690.1.)_
- **Revelation 20:7** — "When the thousand years are expired, Satan shall be loosed out of his prison." His "binding" is the removing of his subjects beyond his reach; his "loosing" is their being raised again so he can resume his work — bound and loosed by circumstance, not by literal chain. _(DAR 691.1-691.2.)_
- **Jeremiah 4:23** & **Genesis 1:2** — The "bottomless pit" (abyss) answers to the earth "without form, and void" — the very words of the creation chaos. Satan's prison is this dark, desolate, lifeless earth. _(GC 658.3; DAR 690.3.)_
- **Isaiah 24:21-22** — "The LORD shall punish... the kings of the earth upon the earth. And they shall be gathered together, as prisoners are gathered in the pit, and shall be shut up in the prison, and after many days shall they be visited." The wicked are shut in the prison of the grave and "visited" — raised — after the long interval. _(GC 658.2-659.)_
- **Isaiah 14:12-17** — "How art thou fallen from heaven, O Lucifer... Is this the man that made the earth to tremble... that made the world as a wilderness?" Satan, surveying the wreck of his rebellion, is left alone with his angels to behold the ruin he wrought. _(GC 660.1-660.2; DAR 691.)_
- **Jude 1:6** — The angels "he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day." Fallen angels are reserved in darkness for the judgment — Satan and his host left to "realize the effect of the curse." _(GC 660.1.)_

### The Saints Reign and Judge with Christ Above

- **Revelation 20:4** — "I saw thrones, and they sat upon them, and judgment was given unto them." The redeemed sit enthroned in heaven; their employment during the thousand years is to assign the wicked dead the punishment due their deeds. _(DAR 692.2; EW 52.2.)_
- **1 Corinthians 6:2-3** — "Do ye not know that the saints shall judge the world?... Know ye not that we shall judge angels?" The saints share Christ's judgment of the lost and of fallen angels — the very work of the millennial reign. _(DAR 692.2; GC 660.4.)_
- **Revelation 20:6** — "They shall be priests of God and of Christ, and shall reign with him a thousand years"; on such "the second death hath no power." The first-resurrection saints are forever safe — beyond the reach of the second death. _(DAR 693; GC 673.3.)_
- **Daniel 7:22** — "Judgment was given to the saints of the most High; and the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom." The prophecy of Daniel meets the vision of John: judgment given to the saints. _(GC 660.4.)_
- **Revelation 5:10** — "Hast made us unto our God kings and priests: and we shall reign on the earth." The redeemed reign as kings and priests — first in heaven through the thousand years, then on the renewed earth. _(EW 51.3; EW 290.3.)_
- **Psalm 149:5-9** — The saints have "the high praises of God... in their mouth, and a twoedged sword in their hand; to execute vengeance upon the heathen... to execute upon them the judgment written: this honour have all his saints." The honor of executing the written judgment belongs to all the saints. _(GC 660.4.)_

### The Second Resurrection, the Holy City, and the Second Death

- **Revelation 20:5** — "The rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished." At the close of the thousand years the wicked dead are raised — the second resurrection. _(DAR 694.1; EW 292.1.)_
- **Revelation 21:2** & **Revelation 21:10** — "The holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down from God out of heaven" — "that great city, the holy Jerusalem, descending out of heaven from God." The Holy City, with the saints within it, descends to the earth at the end of the thousand years. _(DAR 694.3; GC 662-663.)_
- **Revelation 20:8-9** — Satan loosed deceives the nations, "Gog and Magog, to gather them together to battle... and they went up on the breadth of the earth, and compassed the camp of the saints about, and the beloved city." The risen wicked, marshalled by Satan, surround the City — the last assault. _(DAR 694.2-694.3; GC 663.)_
- **Revelation 20:9** — "And fire came down from God out of heaven, and devoured them." The besieging host is consumed by fire from heaven — the execution of the judgment the saints had meted out. _(DAR 697.1; EW 294.1.)_
- **Revelation 20:11-12** — "I saw a great white throne... and I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened... and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works." The wicked stand at the great white throne to receive the sentence already recorded. _(DAR 697.2-697.3; GC 666.)_
- **Revelation 20:13-14** — "Death and hell delivered up the dead... and they were judged every man according to their works. And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death." The grave gives up the wicked, and death itself is destroyed — this is the second death. _(DAR 697.2; GC 668.2.)_
- **Revelation 20:15** — "Whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire." The lake of fire is the agency that closes God's controversy with sin — utter, final destruction, not endless torment. _(DAR 697.1; DAR 698.1-698.3.)_
- **Malachi 4:1-3** — "The day that cometh shall burn them up... and ye shall tread down the wicked; for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet." The wicked become ashes — reduced to nothing, root and branch, while the righteous tread the cleansed ground. _(GC 673.1; EW 295.1.)_
- **2 Peter 3:10** — "The day of the Lord will come as a thief... the earth also and the works that are therein shall be burned up." The same fire that consumes the wicked dissolves the elements and burns up the works of the earth. _(DAR 698.1-698.2; GC 674.1.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:26** — "The last enemy that shall be destroyed is death." Death and the grave, cast into the lake of fire, are abolished — the final enemy overthrown. _(GC 673.2-673.3.)_

### The New Heavens and the New Earth — the Controversy Ended

- **2 Peter 3:13** — "We, according to his promise, look for new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness." The same fire that purifies the earth fits it for the eternal home of the righteous. _(GC 674.1; DAR 702.3.)_
- **Revelation 21:1** — "I saw a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the first earth were passed away; and there was no more sea." The purifying fire sweeps away every trace of the curse; this very earth, renewed, becomes the inheritance of the saved. _(DAR 702.1-702.3; GC 674.1.)_
- **Revelation 21:3-4** — "The tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them... and God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain." God dwells with His people; pain, death, and sorrow are forever gone. _(GC 676.1; DAR 704.)_
- **Revelation 21:5** — "He that sat upon the throne said, Behold, I make all things new... Write: for these words are true and faithful." The Creator's own pledge of a wholly renewed creation, sworn faithful and true. _(GC 674-675.)_
- **Revelation 22:1-5** — "A pure river of water of life... the tree of life, which bare twelve manner of fruits... and there shall be no more curse... and they shall reign for ever and ever." Eden restored — the tree of life, no curse, no night, and the redeemed reigning forever. _(DAR 710-718; GC 675.1-676.4; EW 289.1.)_
- **Malachi 4:2** — "Unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings; and ye shall go forth, and grow up as calves of the stall." The same day that burns the wicked brings healing and gladness to the righteous. _(GC 675.)_
- **Revelation 5:13** — "Every creature... heard I saying, Blessing, and honour, and glory, and power, be unto him that sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb for ever and ever." The whole clean universe joins in one anthem — "the great controversy is ended. Sin and sinners are no more. The entire universe is clean." _(GC 678.2-678.3.)_
