# 13. The Woman Clothed in White (Revelation 12) — the True Church

> _In prophecy a pure woman is the symbol of the true church, and a corrupt woman of an apostate church (Eze. 23; Rev. 17). The woman of Revelation 12 is the church of all ages: clothed with the sun of the gospel, the moon of the Mosaic types beneath her feet, crowned with the twelve apostles. She brings forth the man child, Christ; flees into the wilderness for 1260 years (538-1798) while the papacy reigns; is preserved through the dark ages; and at the last the dragon makes war on the remnant of her seed who keep the commandments of God and have the testimony of Jesus. (DAR 509-519; GC 381-382, 433-439.)_

### The woman is the church — the bride espoused to Christ

- **Revelation 12:1** — "A woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars." A pure woman is the church; a corrupt woman (Rev. 17) an apostate church. By parity of reasoning the pure woman here is the true church.

  > "“A woman,” the true church. A corrupt woman is used to represent an apostate or corrupt church. Ezekiel 23:2-4; Revelation 17:3-6, 15, 18. By parity of reasoning, a pure woman, as in this instance, would represent the true church." — DAR 509.3.

- **2 Corinthians 11:2** — "I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ." The church is the betrothed virgin; Paul's figure fixes the symbol.

  > "In the Bible the sacred and enduring character of the relation that exists between Christ and His church is represented by the union of marriage… And Paul employs the same figure in the New Testament when he says: “I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ.” 2 Corinthians 11:2." — GC 381.2.

- **Jeremiah 6:2** — "I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman." God's own likeness for His people is a woman — Zion the church.

  > "“A woman,” the true church… By parity of reasoning, a pure woman, as in this instance, would represent the true church." — DAR 509.3.

- **Isaiah 54:5-6** — "Thy Maker is thine husband... the LORD hath called thee as a woman forsaken... a wife of youth." The covenant between God and His people is a marriage; the church is His wife.

  > "The Lord has joined His people to Himself by a solemn covenant, He promising to be their God, and they pledging themselves to be His and His alone… And, again: “I am married unto you.” Jeremiah 3:14." — GC 381.2.

- **Hosea 2:19-20** — "I will betroth thee unto me for ever... in righteousness, and in judgment... and thou shalt know the LORD." The marriage figure of Christ and His church, quoted to establish the woman-symbol.

  > "He declares: “I will betroth thee unto Me forever; yea, I will betroth thee unto Me in righteousness, and in judgment, and in loving-kindness, and in mercies.” Hosea 2:19." — GC 381.2.

- **Ephesians 5:25-27** — "Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it... that he might present it to himself a glorious church, not having spot, or wrinkle... holy and without blemish." The pure woman is the church Christ cleanses and presents spotless.

  > "In the Bible the sacred and enduring character of the relation that exists between Christ and His church is represented by the union of marriage. The Lord has joined His people to Himself by a solemn covenant, He promising to be their God, and they pledging themselves to be His and His alone." — GC 381.2.

- **Isaiah 62:5** — "As the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride, so shall thy God rejoice over thee." The relation of God to His church is that of bridegroom to bride.

  > "In the Bible the sacred and enduring character of the relation that exists between Christ and His church is represented by the union of marriage." — GC 381.2.

- **Galatians 4:26** — "Jerusalem which is above is free, which is the mother of us all." The woman, the church, is the mother who brings forth her children — and the man child.

  > "Verses 1 and 2 cover a period of time commencing just previous to the opening of the present dispensation, when the church was earnestly longing for and expecting the advent of the Messiah, and extending to the time of the full establishment of the gospel church with its crown of twelve apostles. Luke 2:25, 26, 38." — DAR 510.1.

- **Revelation 19:7-8** — "The marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready... arrayed in fine linen, clean and white: for the fine linen is the righteousness of saints." The woman's white raiment is the imputed righteousness of Christ — the same sun-clothing of Revelation 12.

  > "In the Bible the sacred and enduring character of the relation that exists between Christ and His church is represented by the union of marriage." — GC 381.2.

### Her clothing — sun, moon, and crown of stars

- **Revelation 12:1** (clause) — "clothed with the sun." The sun is the light and glory of the gospel dispensation; the church is clothed with the righteousness of the gospel just risen.

  > "“The sun,” the light and glory of the gospel dispensation." — DAR 509.4.

  > "The woman, the church, had the moon under her feet; that is, the Mosaic dispensation had just ended, and the woman was clothed with the light of the gospel sun, which had just risen." — DAR 510.2.

- **Malachi 4:2** — "The Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings." Christ Himself is the Sun whose light clothes the woman — the antitype and substance.

  > "“The moon,” the Mosaic dispensation. As the moon shines with a borrowed light derived from the sun, so the former dispensation shone with a light borrowed from the present. There they had the type and shadow; here we have the antitype and substance." — DAR 509.5.

  > "The Mosaic dispensation shone with a light borrowed from the Christian dispensation, just as the moon shines with light borrowed from the sun. How appropriate, therefore, to represent the former by the moon, and the latter by the sun." — DAR 510.2.

- **Revelation 12:1** (clause) — "the moon under her feet." The moon is the Mosaic dispensation: as the moon shines with borrowed light from the sun, so the former dispensation shone with light borrowed from the gospel — type and shadow beneath the antitype.

  > "“The moon,” the Mosaic dispensation. As the moon shines with a borrowed light derived from the sun, so the former dispensation shone with a light borrowed from the present. There they had the type and shadow; here we have the antitype and substance." — DAR 509.5.

  > "The woman, the church, had the moon under her feet; that is, the Mosaic dispensation had just ended, and the woman was clothed with the light of the gospel sun, which had just risen." — DAR 510.2.

- **Song of Solomon 6:10** — "Fair as the moon, clear as the sun, and terrible as an army with banners." The bride is described in exactly these heavenly symbols — sun and moon — that crown the woman of Revelation 12.

  > "“The sun,” the light and glory of the gospel dispensation. … “The moon,” the Mosaic dispensation. As the moon shines with a borrowed light derived from the sun, so the former dispensation shone with a light borrowed from the present." — DAR 509.4-5.

- **Psalms 89:36-37** — "His seed shall endure for ever... It shall be established for ever as the moon, and as a faithful witness in heaven." Sun and moon are the standing prophetic emblems of God's enduring covenant and church.

  > "No symbols more fitting and impressive could be found than are here employed. The Mosaic dispensation shone with a light borrowed from the Christian dispensation, just as the moon shines with light borrowed from the sun." — DAR 510.2.

- **Revelation 12:1** (clause) — "a crown of twelve stars." The crown of twelve stars is the twelve apostles, the foundation of the New Testament church.

  > "“A crown of twelve stars,” the twelve apostles." — DAR 509.6.

  > "By the figure of prolepsis, the church is represented as fully organized, with its twelve apostles, before the man child, Christ, appeared upon the scene." — DAR 510.2.

- **Revelation 22:16** — "I am the root and the offspring of David, and the bright and morning star." The stars are her teachers and leaders, and Christ the chief Star — light reflected from the apostolic foundation.

  > "“A crown of twelve stars,” the twelve apostles." — DAR 509.6.

### The man child and the dragon — first advent and pagan Rome

- **Revelation 12:2** — "She being with child cried, travailing in birth, and pained to be delivered." Verses 1-2 cover the time just before the gospel dispensation, when the church longed and watched for the Messiah (Luke 2:25, 26, 38).

  > "Verses 1 and 2 cover a period of time commencing just previous to the opening of the present dispensation, when the church was earnestly longing for and expecting the advent of the Messiah, and extending to the time of the full establishment of the gospel church with its crown of twelve apostles. Luke 2:25, 26, 38." — DAR 510.1.

- **Revelation 12:3** — "A great red dragon, having seven heads and ten horns, and seven crowns upon his heads." The dragon is pagan Rome — the red dragon was the principal standard of the Roman legions, painted red.

  > "“A great red dragon,” pagan Rome. (See under verses 4 and 5.)" — DAR 509.7.

  > "And it may be a fact worth mentioning that during the second, third, fourth, and fifth centuries of the Christian era, next to the eagle the dragon was the principal standard of the Roman legions; and that dragon was painted red, as though, in faithful response to the picture held up by the seer of Patmos, they would exclaim to the world, We are the nation which that picture represents." — DAR 511.3-512.1.

- **Revelation 12:4** — "His tail drew the third part of the stars of heaven... and the dragon stood before the woman... for to devour her child as soon as it was born." A third part of the Jewish rulers — the kings — were swept away by Rome; and Rome, in Herod, sought to slay the new-born Christ.

  > "The Jews had three classes of rulers, — kings, priests, and the Sanhedrim. A third of these, the kings, were taken away by the Roman power." — DAR 510.4.

  > "No formal answer to this question need be given to any one who has read how Herod, in a fiendish effort to destroy the infant Jesus, sent forth and slew all the children in Bethlehem, from two years old and under. But who was Herod? — A Roman governor. From Rome, Herod derived his power." — DAR 511.3-512.1.

- **Revelation 12:5** — "She brought forth a man child, who was to rule all nations with a rod of iron: and her child was caught up unto God, and to his throne." The man child is Christ alone — none other was caught up to God's throne (Ps. 2:7-9; Heb. 8:1; Rev. 3:21).

  > "The testimony concerning the “man child” which the dragon seeks to destroy, is applicable to only one being that has appeared in this world, and that is our Lord Jesus Christ. No other one has been caught up to God and his throne; but he has been thus exalted. Ephesians 1:20, 21; Hebrews 8:1; Revelation 3:21." — DAR 511.1-2.

- **Genesis 3:15** — "I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head." The first promise: the woman's Seed, Christ, who bruises the serpent — the war of Revelation 12 in germ.

  > "The dragon stood before the woman, to devour her child… The testimony concerning the “man child” which the dragon seeks to destroy, is applicable to only one being that has appeared in this world, and that is our Lord Jesus Christ." — DAR 511.1.

### War in heaven — Michael's victory at the first advent

- **Revelation 12:7-8** — "There was war in heaven: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon... And prevailed not; neither was their place found any more in heaven." Michael is Christ (Jude 9; 1 Thess. 4:16); the war is His personal contest with Satan at His first advent, in which Satan "prevailed not."

  > "And to what point are we carried back? — Evidently to the commencement of Christ’s ministry here upon earth. To prove that Michael is Christ, see Jude 9; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; John 5:28, 29; and that this was a special time of warfare between him and Satan need not be argued." — DAR 513.2.

  > "The war mentioned in the scripture now before us is between the Devil and Michael, Christ. The great effort of the former against the latter, personally, was during his mission here on earth; and Christ’s great personal victory over him was in that very contest." — DAR 515.1.

- **Revelation 12:9** — "The great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan... cast out into the earth." Here John names a second actor — Satan himself — distinct from pagan Rome; Satan is humiliated and cast down at the cross.

  > "Another symbol is here introduced, and John hastens to tell us what this symbol represents. It is the Devil and Satan. But this is not the same as the dragon of verses 3 and 4… It was because Rome, as a universal empire, was then the only possible general agent to carry out Satan’s will in the earth. But there is no occasion to confound the two symbols." — DAR 513.3-514.1.

- **Revelation 12:10** — "Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God... for the accuser of our brethren is cast down." The song is sung prospectively: Christ's victory made salvation forever sure.

  > "But this song was sung prospectively. These things were made sure. The great victory had been won by Christ which put the question of their establishment forever at rest." — DAR 516.1.

- **Revelation 12:11** — "They overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death." The faithful "brethren" overcome through all the ages by the Lamb's blood and their testimony — the martyr church.

  > "The prophet then glances rapidly over the working of Satan from that time to the end (verses 11, 12), during which time the faithful “brethren” overcome him by the blood of the Lamb and the word of their testimony, while his wrath increases as his time grows short." — DAR 516.2.

- **Revelation 12:12** — "Woe to the inhabiters of the earth and of the sea! for the devil is come down unto you, having great wrath, because he knoweth that he hath but a short time." Baffled and hopeless, Satan turns his rage upon the woman, the church.

  > "Henceforth the church (the woman) is the object of his malice, and he resorts to all those nefarious means against her that would naturally characterize a baffled and hopeless rage." — DAR 515.2.

  > "The prophet then glances rapidly over the working of Satan from that time to the end (verses 11, 12)… while his wrath increases as his time grows short." — DAR 516.2.

### The woman in the wilderness — 1260 years, 538-1798

- **Revelation 12:6** — "The woman fled into the wilderness... that they should feed her there a thousand two hundred and threescore days." The church fled when the papacy was established in 538, nourished by the word of God and the ministry of angels through 1260 years.

  > "And the church fled into the wilderness at the time the papacy was established, in 538, where it was nourished by the word of God and the ministration of angels during the long, dark, and bloody rule of that power, 1260 years." — DAR 512.2.

  > "The 1260 years of papal supremacy began in A.D. 538, and would therefore terminate in 1798." — GC 266.3.

- **Revelation 12:14** — "To the woman were given two wings of a great eagle, that she might fly into the wilderness... where she is nourished for a time, and times, and half a time." The eagles' wings are the haste and the help God gave her (Ex. 19:4); the "time, times, and half a time" equals the 1260 days.

  > "The eagles’ wings given her appropriately signify the haste with which the true church was obliged to provide for her own safety when the man of sin was installed in power, together with the assistance God provided her to this end. The like figure is used to describe God’s dealings with ancient Israel… “I bare you on eagles’ wings, and brought you unto myself.” Exodus 19:4." — DAR 517.2-3.

- **Daniel 7:25** — "They shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time." The identical phrase fixes the little horn's persecution and the woman's wilderness to one and the same 1260 years of papal supremacy.

  > "The mention of the period during which the woman is nourished in the wilderness as “a time and times and half a time,” the exact phraseology used in Daniel 7:25, furnishes a key for the explanation of the latter passage… making in all 1260 days; and this, being symbolic, signifies 1260 literal years." — DAR 517.3.

  > "The forty and two months are the same as the “time and times and the dividing of time,” three years and a half, or 1260 days, of Daniel 7—the time during which the papal power was to oppress God’s people. This period… began with the supremacy of the papacy, A.D. 538, and terminated in 1798." — GC 439.2.

- **Revelation 13:5** — "Power was given unto him to continue forty and two months." Forty-two months = 1260 days = the time the church suffered oppression from Rome, A.D. 538 to 1798.

  > "The periods here mentioned—“forty and two months,” and “a thousand two hundred and threescore days”—are the same, alike representing the time in which the church of Christ was to suffer oppression from Rome. The 1260 years of papal supremacy began in A.D. 538, and would therefore terminate in 1798." — GC 266.3.

  > "“Power was given unto him to continue forty and two months.” … The forty and two months are the same as the “time and times and the dividing of time,” three years and a half, or 1260 days, of Daniel 7—the time during which the papal power was to oppress God’s people." — GC 439.2.

- **Numbers 14:34** — "Each day for a year." The year-day principle: each prophetic day stands for a literal year, turning 1260 days into 1260 years.

  > "This shows that a “time” is one year, 360 days; two “times,” two years, or 720 days; and “half a time,” half a year, or 180 days, making in all 1260 days; and this, being symbolic, signifies 1260 literal years." — DAR 517.3.

- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year." The second witness to the year-day rule by which the wilderness period is computed.

  > "This shows that a “time” is one year, 360 days; two “times,” two years, or 720 days; and “half a time,” half a year, or 180 days, making in all 1260 days; and this, being symbolic, signifies 1260 literal years." — DAR 517.3.

### The wilderness state, the flood, and the earth's help

- **Revelation 12:13** — "When the dragon saw that he was cast unto the earth, he persecuted the woman which brought forth the man child." Satan, defeated at the cross, turns with tenfold fury on the true church.

  > "Here we are again carried back to the time when Satan became fully aware that he had utterly failed in all his attempts against the Lord of glory in his earthly mission; and seeing this, he turned with tenfold fury, as already noticed, upon the church which Christ had established." — DAR 517.1.

- **Revelation 12:14** (clause) — "into the wilderness, into her place." The wilderness is seclusion and concealment: the true church hidden in caves and the valleys of the Piedmont (the Waldenses), God watching over and nourishing her.

  > "This must denote a state of seclusion from the public gaze, and of concealment from her foes… the caves and the hidden recesses of the valleys of the Piedmont may be taken as representative places, where the truth of the gospel was sacredly cherished from the rage of its foes. Here God watched over his church, and by his providence protected and nourished her." — DAR 517.1.

  > "Behind the lofty bulwarks of the mountains—in all ages the refuge of the persecuted and oppressed—the Waldenses found a hiding place. Here the light of truth was kept burning amid the darkness of the Middle Ages. Here, for a thousand years, witnesses for the truth maintained the ancient faith." — GC 65.3.

- **Revelation 12:15** — "The serpent cast out of his mouth water as a flood after the woman, that he might cause her to be carried away of the flood." The flood is the deluge of papal persecution — fifty to a hundred million carried away — yet the days were shortened for the elect's sake (Matt. 24:22).

  > "Through it Satan could hurl a mighty flood of persecution against the church in every direction; and this he was not slow to do… From fifty to one hundred million were carried away by the flood; but the church was not entirely swallowed up; the days were shortened for the elect’s sake. Matthew 24:22." — DAR 518.1.

- **Revelation 12:16** — "The earth helped the woman, and the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed up the flood." The Reformation: God raised up Luther and his colaborers; Protestant soil in Germany, Switzerland, Holland, England, and Scandinavia absorbed the flood of papal fury.

  > "“The earth helped the woman” by opening its mouth and swallowing up the flood. The Reformation of the sixteenth century began its work. God raised up the noble Luther and his colaborers… And soon there was enough Protestant soil found in Switzerland, Germany, Holland, England, Norway, and Sweden, to swallow up the flood of papal fury, and rob it of its power to harm the church." — DAR 518.2-519.

### The remnant of her seed — the link to the last-day church

- **Revelation 12:17** — "The dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." The remnant is the last generation of the true church; the war is against those who keep all ten commandments — including the Sabbath — and hold the spirit of prophecy.

  > "Verse 17 brings to view another and a final outburst of his wrath, this time against the last generation of Christians to live on the earth… for the war of the dragon is directed against the remnant of the woman’s seed; that is, the remnant of the seed, or individuals, that constitute the true church; and no generation but the last can truthfully be represented by the remnant." — DAR 519.1-2.

  > "The line of prophecy in which these symbols are found begins with Revelation 12, with the dragon that sought to destroy Christ at His birth. The dragon is said to be Satan (Revelation 12:9); he it was that moved upon Herod to put the Saviour to death." — GC 438.

- **Revelation 14:12** — "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." The same commandment-keeping remnant, defined again under the third angel — the Sabbath reform of the last days.

  > "This remnant is characterized by their keeping of the commandments of God, and having the testimony of Jesus Christ. This points to a Sabbath reform to be accomplished in the last days… This is more particularly brought to view in the message of Revelation 14:9-12." — DAR 519.2.

  > "In Revelation 14, men are called upon to worship the Creator; and the prophecy brings to view a class that, as the result of the threefold message, are keeping the commandments of God. One of these commandments points directly to God as the Creator." — GC 437.

- **Daniel 7:25** (clause) — "think to change times and laws." The dragon's agent presumed to change God's law (the Sabbath); the remnant's loyalty to the unchanged commandments marks them out for the dragon's final wrath.

  > "Within the holy of holies, in the sanctuary in heaven, the divine law is sacredly enshrined—the law that was spoken by God Himself amid the thunders of Sinai and written with His own finger on the tables of stone." — GC 433.

  > "The special characteristic of the beast, and therefore of his image, is the breaking of God’s commandments. Says Daniel, of the little horn, the papacy: “He shall think to change times and the law.” Daniel 7:25, R.V… Only by changing God’s law could the papacy exalt itself above God." — GC 446.

> _Thus the line of prophecy that opened with the dragon seeking to destroy the new-born Christ runs unbroken to the remnant church: pagan Rome, papal Rome, and at the last a third power — the two-horned beast — all driven by the same dragon, all warring on the woman clothed in white, the commandment-keeping people of God. (DAR 519.3; GC 438.2.)_
