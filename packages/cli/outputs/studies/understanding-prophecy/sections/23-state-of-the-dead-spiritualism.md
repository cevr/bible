# 23. The State of the Dead and the Last Deception (Spiritualism)

> _The truth about death is the bulwark against the final deception. The dead sleep an unconscious sleep, knowing nothing, awaiting the call of the resurrection morning; there is no immortal soul that survives the body, for God alone hath immortality. This one truth shuts the door on spiritualism — Satan's masterpiece, by which he personates the dead, counterfeits departed friends, and works lying miracles to deceive the world. The first lie, "Ye shall not surely die," laid the foundation of spiritualism, and at the end binds Romanism, apostate Protestantism, and spiritualism into the threefold union of Revelation 16:13-14. "To the law and to the testimony" is the only test. (GC 531-562; GC 588; DAR 646-648; EW 262-263.)_

### The dead sleep — they know nothing

- **Ecclesiastes 9:5-6** — "The living know that they shall die: but the dead know not any thing." Their love, hatred, and envy have perished; they have no more portion under the sun. The plainest statement of Scripture flatly contradicts the doctrine of conscious survival.

  > "What say the Scriptures concerning these things? David declares that man is not conscious in death. … Solomon bears the same testimony: 'The living know that they shall die: but the dead know not anything.' 'Their love, and their hatred, and their envy, is now perished; neither have they any more a portion forever in anything that is done under the sun.'" — GC 545.

  > "The doctrine of natural immortality, first borrowed from the pagan philosophy, and in the darkness of the great apostasy incorporated into the Christian faith, has supplanted the truth, so plainly taught in Scripture, that 'the dead know not anything.'" — GC 551.

- **Ecclesiastes 9:10** — "There is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest." The grave is a place of silence and inactivity, not of conscious bliss or torment.

  > "'There is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest.' Ecclesiastes 9:5, 6, 10." — GC 545.

- **Psalm 146:3-4** — "His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish." David declares man is not conscious in death — the very text Sister White cites to overthrow the immortality error.

  > "David declares that man is not conscious in death. 'His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish.' Psalm 146:4." — GC 545.3.

- **Psalm 115:17** — "The dead praise not the LORD, neither any that go down into silence." The dead are silent; they are not in heaven praising God.

  > "The Bible clearly teaches that the dead do not go immediately to heaven. They are represented as sleeping until the resurrection. … They that go down to the grave are in silence. They know no more of anything that is done under the sun." — GC 549-550.

- **Psalm 6:5** — "In death there is no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks?" There is no remembrance, no consciousness, in death.

  > "In the very day when the silver cord is loosed and the golden bowl broken (Ecclesiastes 12:6), man's thoughts perish. They that go down to the grave are in silence. They know no more of anything that is done under the sun." — GC 549-550.

- **Job 14:21** — "His sons come to honour, and he knoweth it not... but he perceiveth it not of them." The dead do not know what passes among the living — therefore they cannot return to "minister" to them.

  > "The doctrine of man's consciousness in death, especially the belief that spirits of the dead return to minister to the living, has prepared the way for modern spiritualism. … The fallen angels who do his bidding appear as messengers from the spirit world." — GC 551-552.

- **Job 7:9-10** — "He that goeth down to the grave shall come up no more. He shall return no more to his house." The dead do not haunt the living or revisit their homes; any "spirit" claiming to do so is a deceiver.

  > "The Bible declares that the dead know not anything, that their thoughts have perished; they have no part in anything that is done under the sun; they know nothing of the joys or sorrows of those who were dearest to them on earth." — GC 556.

### Man is mortal — God alone hath immortality

- **Genesis 2:7** — "Man became a living soul." Man does not _have_ a soul; the union of body and breath _is_ the living soul. The soul is not a separate immortal entity.

  > "Immortality, promised to man on condition of obedience, had been forfeited by transgression. Adam could not transmit to his posterity that which he did not possess; and there could have been no hope for the fallen race had not God, by the sacrifice of His Son, brought immortality within their reach." — GC 533.

- **Ecclesiastes 12:7** — "The dust return to the earth as it was: and the spirit shall return unto God who gave it." At death the breath (spirit) of life returns to God; the man returns to dust — he does not float on as a conscious ghost.

  > "And only through Christ can immortality be obtained. … 'He that believeth on the Son hath everlasting life: and he that believeth not the Son shall not see life.' John 3:36." — GC 533.

- **1 Timothy 6:15-16** — God "only hath immortality." Immortality belongs to God alone; man does not possess it inherently.

  > "While 'death passed upon all men, for that all have sinned,' Christ 'hath brought life and immortality to light through the gospel.' Romans 5:12; 2 Timothy 1:10. And only through Christ can immortality be obtained." — GC 533.

  > "Upon the fundamental error of natural immortality rests the doctrine of consciousness in death—a doctrine, like eternal torment, opposed to the teachings of the Scriptures, to the dictates of reason, and to our feelings of humanity." — GC 545.2.

- **Genesis 3:24** — Cherubim and a flaming sword kept the way of the tree of life, that fallen man should not "live forever" in sin. Immortality, forfeited by transgression, is bestowed only through Christ.

  > "Had man after his fall been allowed free access to the tree of life, he would have lived forever, and thus sin would have been immortalized. But cherubim and a flaming sword kept 'the way of the tree of life' (Genesis 3:24)… Therefore there is not an immortal sinner." — GC 533.3.

### Death is a sleep — Christ taught it so

- **John 11:11-14** — "Our friend Lazarus sleepeth... Lazarus is dead." Christ called death a sleep; the disciples thought He spoke of rest in sleep, but He meant Lazarus had died. Death is sleep, not conscious life.

  > "They sleep; they are awakened by the trump of God to a glorious immortality. … As they are called forth from their deep slumber they begin to think just where they ceased. The last sensation was the pang of death; the last thought, that they were falling beneath the power of the grave." — GC 549-550.

- **John 11:43-44** — "Lazarus, come forth." Lazarus, four days dead, was called _back_ — not down from heaven nor up from torment. Had he been in bliss, Christ would have done him no kindness recalling him.

  > "Nowhere in the Sacred Scriptures is found the statement that the righteous go to their reward or the wicked to their punishment at death. … The Bible clearly teaches that the dead do not go immediately to heaven. They are represented as sleeping until the resurrection." — GC 549-550.

- **Job 14:12** — "Man lieth down, and riseth not: till the heavens be no more, they shall not awake, nor be raised out of their sleep." The dead sleep until the final waking — the resurrection, not death, restores them.

  > "Blessed rest for the weary righteous! Time, be it long or short, is but a moment to them. They sleep; they are awakened by the trump of God to a glorious immortality." — GC 549.

- **Acts 2:29** — "David... is both dead and buried, and his sepulchre is with us unto this day." Peter testifies that even David, a man after God's own heart, lay in his tomb.

  > "Nowhere in the Sacred Scriptures is found the statement that the righteous go to their reward or the wicked to their punishment at death. The patriarchs and prophets have left no such assurance. Christ and His apostles have given no hint of it." — GC 545-549.

- **Acts 2:34** — "David is not ascended into the heavens." The plainest apostolic word: the righteous dead have not yet gone to heaven; they await the resurrection.

  > "The Bible clearly teaches that the dead do not go immediately to heaven. They are represented as sleeping until the resurrection. 1 Thessalonians 4:14; Job 14:10-12." — GC 549-550.

### The hope of the sleeping saints — the resurrection

- **1 Thessalonians 4:13-17** — "Them which sleep in Jesus will God bring with him... the dead in Christ shall rise first." The sleeping saints are awakened at the descent of the Lord with a shout and the trump of God; only then are they caught up — proof they were not previously in heaven.

  > "They sleep; they are awakened by the trump of God to a glorious immortality. 'For the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible.…' 1 Corinthians 15:52-54." — GC 549-550.

- **1 Corinthians 15:51-54** — "We shall all be changed, in a moment... at the last trump... the dead shall be raised incorruptible." Mortals put on immortality _at the resurrection_, not at death — "then shall be brought to pass... Death is swallowed up in victory."

  > "'For the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible.… So when this corruptible shall have put on incorruption, and this mortal shall have put on immortality, then shall be brought to pass the saying that is written, Death is swallowed up in victory.'" — GC 549-550.

- **John 5:28-29** — "All that are in the graves shall hear his voice, and shall come forth." The dead are _in their graves_ awaiting His voice — the resurrection of life and the resurrection of damnation.

  > "The patriarchs and prophets have left no such assurance. Christ and His apostles have given no hint of it. The Bible clearly teaches that the dead do not go immediately to heaven. They are represented as sleeping until the resurrection." — GC 545-549.

- **Daniel 12:2** — "Many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake." The dead sleep in the dust until they awake — some to everlasting life, some to shame and contempt.

  > "The Bible clearly teaches that the dead do not go immediately to heaven. They are represented as sleeping until the resurrection. … They sleep; they are awakened by the trump of God to a glorious immortality." — GC 545-549.

- **Job 19:25-26** — "I know that my redeemer liveth... in my flesh shall I see God." Job's hope is not a disembodied soul but the redeemer standing at the latter day and flesh restored — resurrection hope.

  > "When they arise from the tomb, their first glad thought will be echoed in the triumphal shout: 'O death, where is thy sting? O grave, where is thy victory?'" — GC 549-550.

### The first lie — foundation of spiritualism

- **Genesis 3:4** — "Ye shall not surely die." The serpent's contradiction of God was "the first sermon ever preached upon the immortality of the soul," and the foundation of all spiritualism was laid in that assurance given to Eve in Eden.

  > "The only one who promised Adam life in disobedience was the great deceiver. And the declaration of the serpent to Eve in Eden—'Ye shall not surely die'—was the first sermon ever preached upon the immortality of the soul." — GC 533.2.

  > "Satan has long been preparing for his final effort to deceive the world. The foundation of his work was laid by the assurance given to Eve in Eden: 'Ye shall not surely die.' … Little by little he has prepared the way for his masterpiece of deception in the development of spiritualism." — GC 561.2.

- **1 Timothy 4:1** — "In the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils." Paul foretells a last-day apostasy under demon teaching — the very spiritualism now spreading.

  > "Furthermore, God has expressly forbidden all pretended communication with departed spirits. … But the 'familiar spirits,' as these visitants from other worlds were called, are declared by the Bible to be 'the spirits of devils.'" — GC 556.

  > "I saw that the saints must have a thorough understanding of present truth, which they will be obliged to maintain from the Scriptures. They must understand the state of the dead; for the spirits of devils will yet appear to them, professing to be beloved relatives or friends, who will declare to them unscriptural doctrines." — EW 262.2.

- **The Roman dunghill** — The immortality of the soul is a false doctrine Rome borrowed from paganism and incorporated into Christendom; Martin Luther classed it among the "monstrous fables" of the Roman decretals.

  > "The theory of the immortality of the soul was one of those false doctrines that Rome, borrowing from paganism, incorporated into the religion of Christendom. Martin Luther classed it with the 'monstrous fables that form part of the Roman dunghill of decretals.'" — GC 549.2.

### Satan personates the dead — the masterpiece of deception

- **2 Corinthians 11:14** — "Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light." The arch-deceiver disguises himself; "Satan is converted, after the modern order of things," appearing as an angel of light through spiritualism.

  > "As spiritualism more closely imitates the nominal Christianity of the day, it has greater power to deceive and ensnare. Satan himself is converted, after the modern order of things. He will appear in the character of an angel of light." — GC 588.2.

  > "The rapping delusion was presented before me, and I saw that Satan has power to bring before us the appearance of forms purporting to be our relatives or friends who sleep in Jesus. … All this is to deceive the world and ensnare them into the belief of this delusion." — EW 262.1.

- **Matthew 24:24** — "False Christs, and false prophets... shall shew great signs and wonders; insomuch that, if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect." Satan's spirits personating "beloved relatives or friends" will work miracles to sustain their pretensions.

  > "These persons overlook the testimony of the Scriptures concerning the wonders wrought by Satan and his agents. … 'The coming of the Lord is to be preceded by the working of Satan with all power and signs and lying wonders.' … Men are deceived by the miracles which Satan's agents have power to do, not which they pretend to do." — GC 553.2.

  > "Many will be confronted by the spirits of devils personating beloved relatives or friends and declaring the most dangerous heresies. These visitants will appeal to our tenderest sympathies and will work miracles to sustain their pretensions." — GC 560.1.

- **Revelation 13:13-14** — The two-horned beast "doeth great wonders... and deceiveth them that dwell on the earth by the means of those miracles." Lying wonders, not truth, are the engine of the final deception.

  > "But this prophecy is not fulfilled in the great advancement in knowledge… for the wonders to which the prophet had reference are evidently wrought for the purpose of deceiving the people, as we read in verse 14: 'And deceiveth them that dwell on the earth by the means of those miracles which he had power to do in the sight of the beast.'" — DAR 542.

  > "Many endeavor to account for spiritual manifestations by attributing them wholly to fraud and sleight of hand on the part of the medium. … there have been, also, marked exhibitions of supernatural power. … was the direct work of evil angels, who thus introduced one of the most successful of soul-destroying delusions." — GC 553.

- **Counterfeit of the dead** — "He has power to bring before men the appearance of their departed friends. The counterfeit is perfect; the familiar look, the words, the tone, are reproduced with marvelous distinctness." Knowing the dead are _asleep and unconscious_ is the only safeguard.

  > "He has power to bring before men the appearance of their departed friends. The counterfeit is perfect; the familiar look, the words, the tone, are reproduced with marvelous distinctness. … they give ear 'to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils.'" — GC 552.1.

  > "I saw that Satan has power to bring before us the appearance of forms purporting to be our relatives or friends who sleep in Jesus. It will be made to appear as if these friends were actually present… and the same tone of voice that they had while living will fall upon the ear." — EW 262.1.

### "Regard not them that have familiar spirits"

- **Leviticus 19:31** — "Regard not them that have familiar spirits, neither seek after wizards." God expressly forbids all pretended communion with the dead — these "familiar spirits" are agents of Satan.

  > "Furthermore, God has expressly forbidden all pretended communication with departed spirits. … the 'familiar spirits' … are declared by the Bible to be 'the spirits of devils.' … The work of dealing with familiar spirits was pronounced an abomination to the Lord, and was solemnly forbidden under penalty of death. Leviticus 19:31; 20:27." — GC 556.2.

- **Isaiah 8:19** — "Should not a people seek unto their God? for the living to the dead?" To consult the dead on behalf of the living is folly and rebellion; the living should seek the living God.

  > "But none need be deceived by the lying claims of spiritualism. God has given the world sufficient light to enable them to discover the snare. As already shown, the theory which forms the very foundation of spiritualism is at war with the plainest statements of Scripture." — GC 556.

- **Isaiah 8:20** — "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them." This is THE test. Every spirit, vision, and wonder is to be measured by the written Word; what contradicts the law of God is darkness.

  > "The Holy Scriptures are to be accepted as an authoritative, infallible revelation of His will. They are the standard of character, the revealer of doctrines, and the test of experience." — GC vii.

  > "'To the law and to the testimony.' While conflicting doctrines and theories abound, the law of God is the one unerring rule by which all opinions, doctrines, and theories are to be tested. Says the prophet: 'If they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.'" — GC 452.2.

### The threefold union and the lying wonders of the last days

- **Revelation 16:13** — "Three unclean spirits like frogs come out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet." Spiritualism (dragon), the Papacy (beast), and apostate Protestantism (false prophet) unite in one threefold confederacy against God's people.

  > "The sources from which these spirits issue, denote that they will work among three great religious divisions of mankind, represented by the dragon, the beast, and the false prophet, or Paganism, Catholicism, and Protestantism." — DAR 650.2.

  > "The Protestants of the United States will be foremost in stretching their hands across the gulf to grasp the hand of spiritualism; they will reach over the abyss to clasp hands with the Roman power; and under the influence of this threefold union, this country will follow in the steps of Rome in trampling on the rights of conscience." — GC 588.

- **Revelation 16:14** — "They are the spirits of devils, working miracles... to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty." These working-miracle demons go forth to the kings of the earth; the supernatural fires this last alliance.

  > "But it is one province of these spirits of devils to deceive, for they go forth working miracles, and thereby deceive the kings of the earth, that they should believe a lie." — DAR 650.1.

  > "Says the prophet: 'I saw three unclean spirits like frogs; ... they are the spirits of devils, working miracles, which go forth unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty.' … Except those who are kept by the power of God, through faith in His word, the whole world will be swept into the ranks of this delusion." — GC 561.

- **Two errors bind the union** — "Through the two great errors, the immortality of the soul and Sunday sacredness, Satan will bring the people under his deceptions." The former lays the foundation of spiritualism; the latter creates the bond of sympathy with Rome.

  > "Through the two great errors, the immortality of the soul and Sunday sacredness, Satan will bring the people under his deceptions. While the former lays the foundation of spiritualism, the latter creates a bond of sympathy with Rome." — GC 588.1.

- **Romanism + Protestantism + spiritualism** — Papists and Protestants and spiritualists alike will receive the form of godliness without the power, and Satan determines to sweep all into the ranks of spiritualism — the grand uniting of the three.

  > "Satan determines to unite them in one body and thus strengthen his cause by sweeping all into the ranks of spiritualism. … Papists, Protestants, and worldlings will alike accept the form of godliness without the power, and they will see in this union a grand movement for the conversion of the world." — GC 588.3.

- **Revelation 18:23** — "By thy sorceries were all nations deceived." Babylon's fall is bound up with her sorceries — the spiritualistic enchantments by which she deceives the nations at the end.

  > "Little by little he has prepared the way for his masterpiece of deception in the development of spiritualism. … Except those who are kept by the power of God, through faith in His word, the whole world will be swept into the ranks of this delusion. The people are fast being lulled to a fatal security." — GC 561.

  > "And I saw three unclean spirits like frogs come out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet. … For they are the spirits of devils, working miracles, which go forth unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world." — DAR 646.

### The conflict just before us

- **Revelation 1:18** — "I am he that liveth, and was dead... and have the keys of hell and of death." Christ alone holds the keys of death and the grave; resurrection is His prerogative, not the dead's natural estate — our anchor against every counterfeit.

  > "They sleep; they are awakened by the trump of God to a glorious immortality. 'For the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible.…' … When they arise from the tomb, their first glad thought will be echoed in the triumphal shout: 'O death, where is thy sting? O grave, where is thy victory?'" — GC 549-550.

- **The saints must know the truth of the dead** — "The saints must have a thorough understanding of present truth... They must understand the state of the dead; for the spirits of devils will yet appear to them, professing to be beloved relatives or friends." This delusion will be met face to face; only the prepared will stand.

  > "I saw that the saints must have a thorough understanding of present truth, which they will be obliged to maintain from the Scriptures. They must understand the state of the dead… The people of God must be prepared to withstand these spirits with the Bible truth that the dead know not anything, and that they who thus appear are the spirits of devils. … This delusion will spread, and we shall have to contend with it face to face." — EW 262.2-262.3.

- **The train of cars** — The rapidity of the delusion was shown as "a train of cars going with the speed of lightning... the whole world was on board," with Satan as the stately conductor; only a little company on the narrow path, "firmly united by the truth," escapes.

  > "I saw the rapidity with which this delusion was spreading. A train of cars was shown me, going with the speed of lightning. … It seemed that the whole world was on board. Then he showed me the conductor, a fair, stately person… He said, 'It is Satan. He is the conductor, in the form of an angel of light.'" — EW 263.1-263.2.

  > "I saw a little company traveling a narrow pathway. All seemed to be firmly united by the truth. This little company looked careworn, as if they had passed through severe trials and conflicts. … causing them to look triumphant as if their victories were nearly won." — EW 263.1-263.2.

- **Stand on the Word alone** — "All whose faith is not firmly established upon the word of God will be deceived and overcome." Against the lying wonders of the last conflict, the Bible — and the Bible only — is the safeguard of God's people.

  > "Just before us is 'the hour of temptation, which shall come upon all the world, to try them that dwell upon the earth.' … All whose faith is not firmly established upon the word of God will be deceived and overcome." — GC 560.2.

  > "Except those who are kept by the power of God, through faith in His word, the whole world will be swept into the ranks of this delusion. The people are fast being lulled to a fatal security, to be awakened only by the outpouring of the wrath of God." — GC 561.
