# 28. The Second Coming of Christ in Glory

> The blessed hope is no secret rapture and no spiritual sentiment, but the personal, literal, visible, and audible return of the same Jesus who ascended. He comes in the clouds with all the holy angels; every eye shall see Him; the dead in Christ rise incorruptible, the living righteous are changed and caught up; and the wicked are slain by the brightness of His coming. This one glorious advent ends the great controversy and gathers the redeemed home. (DAR 684-686; GC 640-645; EW 15-18, 286-295.)

### He comes in person — the same Jesus, in like manner

- **Acts 1:9-11** — A cloud received Him out of their sight, and the two angels declared, "This same Jesus... shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go." As truly, bodily, and visibly as He went up, so He returns — no spiritualizing allowed. _(GC 640-645.)_
- **John 14:1-3** — "I will come again, and receive you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also." The hope of the church is a literal return to gather His own to the Father's house. _(GC 640.)_
- **Hebrews 9:28** — "Unto them that look for him shall he appear the second time without sin unto salvation." His first coming was to bear sin; the second, to save them that wait. _(MWV2 19.)_
- **Titus 2:11-14** — Believers are "looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ." The appearing is "glorious" — not invisible, not secret. _(GC 640-645.)_
- **Job 19:25-27** — "I know that my redeemer liveth... in my flesh shall I see God... mine eyes shall behold, and not another." The oldest hope of the patriarchs is a personal, eyes-shall-see-Him advent. _(GC 644.)_

### Every eye shall see Him — visible and audible, not secret

- **Revelation 1:7** — "Behold, he cometh with clouds; and every eye shall see him, and they also which pierced him: and all kindreds of the earth shall wail because of him." Universal sight refutes every secret-coming theory. _(GC 641-643.)_
- **Matthew 24:27** — "As the lightning cometh out of the east, and shineth even unto the west; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be." Open and flashing across the whole sky — the opposite of a hidden, in-the-desert appearing. _(GC 645.)_
- **Matthew 24:30** — "They shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory," and all the tribes of earth mourn. _(GC 641-643.)_
- **Mark 13:26** — "Then shall they see the Son of man coming in the clouds with great power and glory." _(GC 641.)_
- **Luke 21:27-28** — "Then shall they see the Son of man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.... lift up your heads; for your redemption draweth nigh." For the righteous it is deliverance, not dread. _(GC 640-642.)_
- **Daniel 7:13-14** — "One like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven," receiving an everlasting dominion that shall not pass away — the same cloud-coming Christ described to the high priest. _(DAR 113; GC 641.)_

### With all the holy angels and the glory of the Father

- **Matthew 25:31** — "When the Son of man shall come in his glory, and all the holy angels with him, then shall he sit upon the throne of his glory." _(GC 641-642.)_
- **Matthew 16:27** — "The Son of man shall come in the glory of his Father with his angels; and then he shall reward every man according to his works." _(EW 288.)_
- **Matthew 24:31** — "He shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds." The trumpet and the angelic gathering are public, not silent. _(EW 16; GC 645.)_
- **Mark 13:27** — He sends His angels to "gather together his elect from the four winds, from the uttermost part of the earth to the uttermost part of heaven." _(EW 16.)_
- **Jude 1:14** — Enoch prophesied, "Behold, the Lord cometh with ten thousands of his saints." The myriads attend the King in His triumph. _(GC 641.)_
- **Revelation 19:11-16** — Heaven opened, the white-horse Warrior called Faithful and True, crowned with many crowns, "KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS," the armies of heaven following Him. Christ comes this time as conquering King, His mediatorial work closed and kingly attire on. _(DAR 684-685.)_

### The resurrection of the righteous and the translation of the living

- **1 Thessalonians 4:15-18** — "The Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in Christ shall rise first," then the living are "caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air." The shout, voice, and trump make it audible; the catching-up is the only "rapture" Scripture knows — at the visible coming. _(EW 16; GC 644-645.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:51-54** — "We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed, in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump.... the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed." Mortality puts on immortality; death is swallowed up in victory. _(MWV2 19, 29.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:22-23** — "Every man in his own order—Christ the firstfruit, afterwards they that are Christ's at his coming." Christ comes to raise and gather His saints to Him in the air. _(MWV2 19.)_
- **Philippians 3:20-21** — We "look for the Saviour," who "shall change our vile body, that it may be fashioned like unto his glorious body." Translation conforms the living saint to Christ's glorified form. _(EW 16-17.)_
- **Colossians 3:4** — "When Christ, who is our life, shall appear, then shall ye also appear with him in glory." _(EW 288-289.)_
- **1 John 3:2** — "When he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is." _(EW 16-17.)_
- **Isaiah 25:9** — "Lo, this is our God; we have waited for him, and he will save us... we will be glad and rejoice in his salvation." The waiting people break into triumph at His appearing. _(GC 640-642.)_

### The wicked slain by the brightness of His coming

- **2 Thessalonians 1:7-10** — The Lord Jesus is "revealed from heaven with his mighty angels, in flaming fire taking vengeance on them that know not God," who are "punished with everlasting destruction from the presence of the Lord," while He comes "to be glorified in his saints." The same coming that rewards the just destroys the impenitent. _(DAR 685-686; GC 641-642.)_
- **Revelation 6:14-17** — The heaven departs as a scroll, every mountain and island moved; kings, great men, and bondmen alike hide in the rocks crying, "Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb: for the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?" The lost dread the very face the saints long to see. _(GC 642.)_
- **Revelation 19:17-21** — The beast and false prophet (the two-horned beast of chapter 13) are taken and cast into the lake of fire, and the remnant "slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse, which sword proceeded out of his mouth" — the word of His mouth. The persecuting powers meet their end at the advent. _(DAR 685-686.)_
- **2 Thessalonians 2:8** — That Wicked one "the Lord shall consume with the spirit of his mouth, and shall destroy with the brightness of his coming." The brightness itself slays. _(DAR 686.)_
- **2 Peter 3:10** — "The day of the Lord will come as a thief in the night; in the which the heavens shall pass away with a great noise, and the elements shall melt with fervent heat." The advent shakes the very frame of nature. _(GC 641-642.)_

### The signs that herald it and the cloud that announces it

- **Matthew 24:29-31** — "Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven," then "the sign of the Son of man" appears — the small cloud growing into the glory-cloud of the returning King. _(EW 15; GC 640.)_
- **Revelation 14:14** — "A white cloud, and upon the cloud one sat like unto the Son of man, having on his head a golden crown" — the reaper come to gather the harvest of the earth. _(DAR 582; GC 641.)_
- **Zechariah 14:4-5** — "His feet shall stand in that day upon the mount of Olives... and the LORD my God shall come, and all the saints with thee." The descent is to the very place of His ascension. _(EW 17-18.)_
- **Matthew 24:36-37** — "Of that day and hour knoweth no man... but as the days of Noe were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" — sudden upon a careless world, yet sure as the flood. _(MWV2 19.)_
- **Revelation 22:12** — "Behold, I come quickly; and my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be." He brings the reward with Him — the saints are not glorified until He appears. _(EW 288.)_
