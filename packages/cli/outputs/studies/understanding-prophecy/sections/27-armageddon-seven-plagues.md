# 27. Armageddon and the Seven Last Plagues

> _When the High Priest lays off His mediatorial garments and probation closes, the seven golden vials of God's unmingled wrath — no longer mixed with mercy — are emptied upon the kingdom of the beast: sores, sea to blood, rivers to blood, scorching sun, darkness on the beast's throne, the Euphrates dried for the way of the kings of the east, and the great voice, "It is done." The sixth vial, through the three frog-spirits, gathers all the powers of earth to the battle of that great day — Armageddon — the final muster of the dragon, beast, and false prophet against God and His people, broken at last by the appearing of the King of kings on the white horse. The plagues fall only on those who bear the mark of the beast; the saints, sheltered under the Most High, behold but are not touched. (DAR 638-656; GC 627-657; EW 289-290.)_

### The Sanctuary Scene — Probation Closed, Wrath Unmingled

- **Revelation 15:1** — "Seven angels having the seven last plagues; for in them is filled up the wrath of God." This chapter is "but an introduction to the most terrific judgments of the Almighty," carrying us forward to a new series of events after the close of mercy. _(DAR 638; DAR 638.1.)_
- **Revelation 15:5-6** — The temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven is opened, and the seven angels come out "clothed in pure and white linen." The sanctuary ministry is the backdrop: when the work of mercy is past, the plague-angels go forth. _(DAR 638.)_
- **Revelation 15:7** — One of the four beasts gives the angels "seven golden vials full of the wrath of God, who liveth for ever and ever." These vials are Heaven's "unmingled wrath," the fullness of the cup for the last generation of the wicked. _(DAR 641.1; GC 627.)_
- **Revelation 15:8** — The temple is filled with smoke "and no man was able to enter... till the seven plagues... were fulfilled." No intercession remains; Christ has ceased His mediation, and probation has closed before a single vial falls. _(DAR 641.3; EW 52.1.)_
- **Revelation 14:9-11** — Whoever worships the beast and his image "shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation." The identical mark warned against in the third message marks out the victims of the plagues — proof they fall after probation. _(DAR 641.3; GC 627.)_
- **Revelation 22:11-12** — "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... he that is holy, let him be holy still." The irrevocable decree that fixes every destiny is spoken before the plagues fall; then Christ comes "quickly" with His reward. _(GC 613-614.)_

> "When Christ ceases His intercession in the sanctuary, the unmingled wrath threatened against those who worship the beast and his image and receive his mark... will be poured out." _(GC 627.3.)_ "The seven last plagues will be poured out after Jesus leaves the sanctuary." _(EW 52.1.)_

### The First Five Vials — Poured on the Kingdom of the Beast

- **Revelation 16:1-2** — The first vial: "a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image." The very first plague is aimed precisely at the mark-bearers — the visible identification of who is smitten. _(DAR 641.2-641.3; GC 627-628.)_
- **Revelation 16:3** — The second vial: the sea "became as the blood of a dead man: and every living soul died in the sea." A literal, terrible judgment, answering to the turning of Egypt's waters to blood. _(DAR 644; GC 628.)_
- **Revelation 16:4-7** — The third vial: rivers and fountains "became blood," and the angel of the waters declares it just: "they have shed the blood of saints and prophets, and thou hast given them blood to drink; for they are worthy." Retribution measured to the persecutors. _(DAR 645.)_
- **Revelation 16:8-9** — The fourth vial: the sun given "power... to scorch men with fire," and men were scorched with great heat, yet "blasphemed the name of God... and they repented not." Drought and famine as Joel describes; still no repentance. _(DAR 647.4; GC 628.1.)_
- **Revelation 16:10-11** — The fifth vial: poured "upon the seat of the beast" — the papacy — "and his kingdom was full of darkness; and they gnawed their tongues for pain." Note that those smitten by the first plague are still alive here: the plagues do not at once destroy all their victims. _(DAR 646.1; GC 628.2.)_

### The Sixth Vial — Euphrates Dried, the Kings of the East, the Gathering to Armageddon

- **Revelation 16:12** — The sixth vial dries "the great river Euphrates... that the way of the kings of the east might be prepared." As the literal Euphrates (and the unmentioned Tigris) is dried under the fourth vial, the Euphrates here symbolizes "the power holding possession of the territory watered by that river" — the Ottoman, or Turkish, empire (cf. Rev. 9:14). _(DAR 647.1; DAR 648.2-648.3.)_
- **Revelation 16:12 (the way prepared)** — The consumption of the Turkish power opens the road to "the battle of the great day of God Almighty... near Jerusalem," for the Turks hold Palestine. The drying up removes the obstacle to the final muster. _(DAR 648.6.)_
- **Revelation 16:13** — "Three unclean spirits like frogs come out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet." These issue from "three great religious divisions of mankind... Paganism, Catholicism, and Protestantism." _(DAR 650.2.)_
- **Revelation 16:14** — "The spirits of devils, working miracles, which go forth unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty." Spiritualism is the fitting agency, deceiving the nations into so unequal a warfare — against the Lord of hosts Himself. _(DAR 649.2; DAR 650.1; GC 561.)_
- **Revelation 16:15** — "Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments." The warning shows probation has closed and the danger now is apostasy through the working of these lying spirits. _(DAR 650.3.)_
- **Revelation 16:16** — "He gathered them together into a place called in the Hebrew tongue Armageddon." The Greek pronoun refers back to the kings of verse 14 (so Wesley, Wakefield, the Syriac, and Sawyer render it), and the hills of Megiddo overlooking Esdraelon name the historic field of decisive battle. _(DAR 651.1-651.6; DAR 652.1-652.6.)_
- **Revelation 17:12-14** — The ten kings "have one mind, and shall give their power and strength unto the beast"; then "these shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings." Armageddon is the confederated powers of earth versus the Lamb — and the Lamb prevails. _(DAR 657-662.)_

### The Seventh Vial — "It Is Done" — and the Voice of God

- **Revelation 16:17** — The seventh vial poured "into the air," and "there came a great voice out of the temple of heaven, from the throne, saying, It is done." All is finished: the cup of human guilt is full, the last soul has chosen, the number of the saved is complete, the books are closed. _(DAR 653.1; DAR 656.1.)_
- **Revelation 16:18** — "Voices, and thunders, and lightnings; and there was a great earthquake, such as was not since men were upon the earth." It is at midnight, by this voice, that God delivers His people; the firmament opens and shuts and the mountains shake. _(DAR 652.7; GC 636.2-636.3.)_
- **Revelation 16:19** — "The great city was divided into three parts... and great Babylon came in remembrance before God." The great city — the false and apostate religion of the world — is rent into Paganism, Catholicism, and apostate Protestantism, and Babylon drinks the cup of the fierceness of God's wrath. _(DAR 653.4.)_
- **Revelation 16:20** — "Every island fled away, and the mountains were not found." The convulsion of the seventh plague tears the very face of the earth. _(GC 637.)_
- **Revelation 16:21** — "A great hail out of heaven, every stone about the weight of a talent" — about fifty-seven pounds each — and still "men blasphemed God." This is the bitter dregs of the seventh vial, the last instrument of wrath upon the impenitent. _(DAR 653.5; DAR 654.1.)_
- **Joel 3:16** — "The Lord also shall roar out of Zion, and utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the heavens and the earth shall shake: but the Lord will be the hope of his people." The voice heard above all the voices of the seventh plague is the voice of God — terror to the wicked, refuge to the saints. _(DAR 653.3.)_

### The Battle Joined — and Broken by the Coming of Christ

- **Joel 3:9-12** — "Prepare war, wake up the mighty men... Beat your plowshares into swords... Assemble yourselves... let the heathen be wakened, and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat: for there will I sit to judge all the heathen." The world's powers are summoned to the battle — but the field is God's tribunal, not their victory. _(DAR 648.6; GC 656.)_
- **Joel 3:13-14** — "Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe... Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision: for the day of the Lord is near in the valley of decision." The gathering is a harvest of judgment on a ripened world. _(GC 656.)_
- **Zephaniah 3:8** — "Wait ye upon me, saith the Lord, until the day that I rise up to the prey: for my determination is to gather the nations... to pour upon them mine indignation... for all the earth shall be devoured with the fire of my jealousy." God Himself gathers the nations — for judgment, not for their triumph. _(DAR 648.6.)_
- **Jeremiah 25:31-33** — "The Lord hath a controversy with the nations... and the slain of the Lord shall be at that day from one end of the earth even unto the other." For six thousand years the controversy has run; now the Lord pleads with all flesh by the sword. _(GC 656.1.)_
- **Isaiah 26:21** — "The Lord cometh out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity: the earth also shall disclose her blood." The Judge leaves His place; the long-hidden blood of the saints is requited. _(GC 656.3.)_
- **Zechariah 14:12** — "Their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, and their eyes shall consume away in their holes, and their tongue shall consume away in their mouth." The plague upon all the people that have fought against Jerusalem — the consuming of the assembled enemy. _(GC 656.3.)_
- **Revelation 19:11-13** — "Heaven opened, and behold a white horse; and he that sat upon him was called Faithful and True... clothed with a vesture dipped in blood... his name is called The Word of God." Christ Himself, no longer Priest but Conqueror, rides forth to end Armageddon. _(DAR 680-681.)_
- **Revelation 19:14-16** — His armies follow on white horses; out of His mouth "goeth a sharp sword, that with it he should smite the nations," and on His vesture is written "KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS." The same title by which He overcame in chapter 17:14. _(DAR 681; Rev. 17:14.)_
- **Revelation 19:17-18** — The angel in the sun calls the fowls to "the supper of the great God," to eat "the flesh of kings, and... captains, and... mighty men." The mustered armies of Armageddon become the slain feast of the field. _(DAR 682.)_
- **Revelation 19:19-21** — "The beast, and the kings of the earth, and their armies, gathered together to make war against him" — the same confederacy of the sixth vial — are broken: "the beast was taken, and with him the false prophet... cast alive into a lake of fire," and "the remnant were slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse." Armageddon ends not in battle but in the brightness of His coming. _(DAR 682-683.)_
- **Revelation 6:15-17** — "The kings of the earth, and the great men... hid themselves... and said to the mountains and rocks, Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb: for the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?" The mark-bearers' terror at the King's appearing. _(DAR 442.)_
- **Revelation 11:18** — "The nations were angry, and thy wrath is come... and shouldest destroy them which destroy the earth." The seven last plagues fill up the wrath of this present generation. _(DAR 506.3.)_

### The Saints Sheltered — Plagues Fall Only on the Mark-Bearers

- **Daniel 12:1** — "At that time shall Michael stand up... and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was... and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book." Michael standing up = the close of probation; the plagues are the time of trouble, yet the sealed are delivered. _(DAR 305; GC 613.)_
- **Psalm 91:7-10** — "A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee... There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling." The express promise of the saints' immunity while the overflowing scourge passes over. _(DAR 656.2-656.3.)_
- **Isaiah 26:20** — "Come, my people, enter thou into thy chambers... hide thyself as it were for a little moment, until the indignation be overpast." The hiding of God's people during the outpouring of wrath. _(GC 635-636.)_
- **Revelation 15:2** — They "that had gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark" stand on the sea of glass, singing "the song of Moses... and the song of the Lamb." The victors are seen safe before the vials are even poured — a deliverance anticipated. _(DAR 639.1; DAR 640.1.)_
- **Joel 3:16 (latter clause)** — "But the Lord will be the hope of his people, and the strength of the children of Israel." The same voice that shakes heaven and earth is the refuge of the redeemed. _(DAR 653.3; GC 636.)_

> "It is at midnight that God manifests His power for the deliverance of His people." _(GC 636.2.)_ When the plagues end, "the wrath of God in the seven last plagues had been visited upon the inhabitants of the earth," the wicked are destroyed, and "the earth looked like a desolate wilderness." _(EW 289.3; EW 290.1.)_
