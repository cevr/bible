# 27. Armageddon and the Seven Last Plagues

> _When the High Priest lays off His mediatorial garments and probation closes, the seven golden vials of God's unmingled wrath — no longer mixed with mercy — are emptied upon the kingdom of the beast: sores, sea to blood, rivers to blood, scorching sun, darkness on the beast's throne, the Euphrates dried for the way of the kings of the east, and the great voice, "It is done." The sixth vial, through the three frog-spirits, gathers all the powers of earth to the battle of that great day — Armageddon — the final muster of the dragon, beast, and false prophet against God and His people, broken at last by the appearing of the King of kings on the white horse. The plagues fall only on those who bear the mark of the beast; the saints, sheltered under the Most High, behold but are not touched. (DAR 638-656; GC 627-657; EW 289-290.)_

### The Sanctuary Scene — Probation Closed, Wrath Unmingled

- **Revelation 15:1** — "Seven angels having the seven last plagues; for in them is filled up the wrath of God." This chapter is "but an introduction to the most terrific judgments of the Almighty," carrying us forward to a new series of events after the close of mercy.

  > "The whole chapter is but an introduction to the most terrific judgments of the Almighty that ever have been, or are to be, visited upon this earth in its present state; namely, the seven last plagues. The most that we here behold is a solemn preparation for the outpouring of these unmixed vials." — DAR 638.

  > "This chapter introduces the seven last plagues, a manifestation of Heaven's unmingled wrath, and the fullness of its measure, for the last generation of the wicked. The work of mercy is then forever past." — DAR 638.1.

- **Revelation 15:5-6** — The temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven is opened, and the seven angels come out "clothed in pure and white linen." The sanctuary ministry is the backdrop: when the work of mercy is past, the plague-angels go forth.

  > "Verse 5 shows that these plagues fall after the close of the ministration in the sanctuary; for the temple is opened before they are poured out. They are given in charge to seven angels, and these angels are clothed in linen pure and white, a fit emblem of the purity of God's righteousness and justice in the infliction of these judgments." — DAR 638.

- **Revelation 15:7** — One of the four beasts gives the angels "seven golden vials full of the wrath of God, who liveth for ever and ever." These vials are Heaven's "unmingled wrath," the fullness of the cup for the last generation of the wicked.

  > "This chapter gives a description of the seven vials of the unmingled wrath of God, and the effects that follow as they are poured upon the earth." — DAR 641.1.

  > "The nation with which He bears long, and which He will not smite until it has filled up the measure of its iniquity in God's account, will finally drink the cup of wrath unmixed with mercy." — GC 627.

- **Revelation 15:8** — The temple is filled with smoke "and no man was able to enter... till the seven plagues... were fulfilled." No intercession remains; Christ has ceased His mediation, and probation has closed before a single vial falls.

  > "The Chronology of the Plagues... is conclusive proof that these judgments are not poured out till after this angel closes his work, and that the very class who hear his warning, and reject it, are the ones to receive the first drops from the overflowing vials of God's indignation." — DAR 641.3.

  > "I was shown that the seven last plagues will be poured out after Jesus leaves the sanctuary." — EW 52.1.

- **Revelation 14:9-11** — Whoever worships the beast and his image "shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation." The identical mark warned against in the third message marks out the victims of the plagues — proof they fall after probation.

  > "It is poured out upon those who have the mark of the beast, and who worship his image, — the identical work against which the third angel warns us." — DAR 641.3.

  > "When Christ ceases His intercession in the sanctuary, the unmingled wrath threatened against those who worship the beast and his image and receive his mark (Revelation 14:9, 10), will be poured out." — GC 627.

- **Revelation 22:11-12** — "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... he that is holy, let him be holy still." The irrevocable decree that fixes every destiny is spoken before the plagues fall; then Christ comes "quickly" with His reward.

  > "Then Jesus ceases His intercession in the sanctuary above. He lifts His hands and with a loud voice says, 'It is done;' and all the angelic host lay off their crowns as He makes the solemn announcement: 'He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... and he that is holy, let him be holy still.' Revelation 22:11. Every case has been decided for life or death." — GC 613-614.

> "When Christ ceases His intercession in the sanctuary, the unmingled wrath threatened against those who worship the beast and his image and receive his mark... will be poured out." _(GC 627.3.)_ "The seven last plagues will be poured out after Jesus leaves the sanctuary." _(EW 52.1.)_

### The First Five Vials — Poured on the Kingdom of the Beast

- **Revelation 16:1-2** — The first vial: "a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image." The very first plague is aimed precisely at the mark-bearers — the visible identification of who is smitten.

  > "The first went, and poured out his vial upon the earth; and there fell a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshiped his image." — DAR 641.2.

  > "Says the revelator, in describing those terrific scourges: 'There fell a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshiped his image.'... Terrible as these inflictions are, God's justice stands fully vindicated." — GC 627-628.

- **Revelation 16:3** — The second vial: the sea "became as the blood of a dead man: and every living soul died in the sea." A literal, terrible judgment, answering to the turning of Egypt's waters to blood.

  > "These plagues are almost identical with those which God inflicted upon the Egyptians as he was about to deliver his people from the yoke of bondage, the literality of which is seldom, if ever, called in question.... A more infectious and deadly substance can scarcely be conceived of than the blood of a dead man." — DAR 644.

  > "The sea 'became as the blood of a dead man: and every living soul died in the sea.'... Terrible as these inflictions are, God's justice stands fully vindicated." — GC 628.

- **Revelation 16:4-7** — The third vial: rivers and fountains "became blood," and the angel of the waters declares it just: "they have shed the blood of saints and prophets, and thou hast given them blood to drink; for they are worthy." Retribution measured to the persecutors.

  > "Such is the description of the terrible retribution for the 'blood of saints' shed by violent hands, which will be given to those who have done, or wish to do, such deeds.... the justice of God will stand vindicated, and his judgments approved. Even the angels are heard exclaiming, Thou art righteous, O Lord, because thou hast judged thus." — DAR 645.

- **Revelation 16:8-9** — The fourth vial: the sun given "power... to scorch men with fire," and men were scorched with great heat, yet "blasphemed the name of God... and they repented not." Drought and famine as Joel describes; still no repentance.

  > "Power is given unto the sun, and he pours upon them a flood of liquid fire, and they are scorched with great heat. Here, as the record runs, their woe first seeks utterance in fearful blasphemy." — DAR 645.4.

  > "In the plague that follows, power is given to the sun 'to scorch men with fire. And men were scorched with great heat.' Verses 8, 9. The prophets thus describe the condition of the earth at this fearful time: 'The land mourneth; ... because the harvest of the field is perished.'" — GC 628.1.

- **Revelation 16:10-11** — The fifth vial: poured "upon the seat of the beast" — the papacy — "and his kingdom was full of darkness; and they gnawed their tongues for pain." Note that those smitten by the first plague are still alive here: the plagues do not at once destroy all their victims.

  > "The plagues do not at once destroy all their victims; for some who were at first smitten with sores, we find still living under the fifth vial, and gnawing their tongues for pain.... It is poured upon the seat of the beast, the papacy." — DAR 646.1.

  > "These plagues are not universal, or the inhabitants of the earth would be wholly cut off. Yet they will be the most awful scourges that have ever been known to mortals." — GC 628.2.

### The Sixth Vial — Euphrates Dried, the Kings of the East, the Gathering to Armageddon

- **Revelation 16:12** — The sixth vial dries "the great river Euphrates... that the way of the kings of the east might be prepared." As the literal Euphrates (and the unmentioned Tigris) is dried under the fourth vial, the Euphrates here symbolizes "the power holding possession of the territory watered by that river" — the Ottoman, or Turkish, empire (cf. Rev. 9:14).

  > "What is the great River Euphrates, upon which this vial is poured out?... One view is that it is the literal River Euphrates in Asia; another is that it is a symbol of the nation occupying the territory through which that river flows. The latter opinion is preferable." — DAR 647.1.

  > "It must be understood figuratively as symbolizing the power holding possession of the territory watered by that river, which is the Ottoman, or Turkish, empire." — DAR 648.2.

  > "In this latter text [Revelation 9:14], all must concede that the Euphrates symbolizes the Turkish power; and being the first and only other occurrence of the word in the Revelation, it may well be considered as governing its use in this book." — DAR 648.3.

- **Revelation 16:12 (the way prepared)** — The consumption of the Turkish power opens the road to "the battle of the great day of God Almighty... near Jerusalem," for the Turks hold Palestine. The drying up removes the obstacle to the final muster.

  > "Where is the battle to be fought? — Near Jerusalem.... But Jerusalem is in the hands of the Turks; they hold possession of the land of Palestine and the sacred sepulchers.... when they shall withdraw their support, and leave it to itself, as they will do under the sixth plague, that symbolic river will be wholly dried up; Turkey will be no more, and the way will be all open for the nations to make their last grand rally to the Holy Land." — DAR 648.6.

- **Revelation 16:13** — "Three unclean spirits like frogs come out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet." These issue from "three great religious divisions of mankind... Paganism, Catholicism, and Protestantism."

  > "The sources from which these spirits issue, denote that they will work among three great religious divisions of mankind, represented by the dragon, the beast, and the false prophet, or Paganism, Catholicism, and Protestantism." — DAR 650.2.

- **Revelation 16:14** — "The spirits of devils, working miracles, which go forth unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty." Spiritualism is the fitting agency, deceiving the nations into so unequal a warfare — against the Lord of hosts Himself.

  > "The agency now already abroad in the world, known as modern Spiritualism, is every way a fitting means to be employed in this work.... what fitter instruments could be employed to gather them to so rash and hopeless an enterprise?" — DAR 649.2.

  > "To many it may seem incredible that the nations should be willing to engage in such an unequal warfare as to go up to battle against the Lord of Hosts; but it is one province of these spirits of devils to deceive, for they go forth working miracles, and thereby deceive the kings of the earth, that they should believe a lie." — DAR 650.1.

  > "Little by little he has prepared the way for his masterpiece of deception in the development of spiritualism.... Except those who are kept by the power of God, through faith in His word, the whole world will be swept into the ranks of this delusion." — GC 561.

- **Revelation 16:15** — "Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments." The warning shows probation has closed and the danger now is apostasy through the working of these lying spirits.

  > "Probation must have closed, and Christ have left his mediatorial position, before the plagues begin to fall.... It will be noticed that this warning is spoken in connection with the working of the spirits." — DAR 650.3.

- **Revelation 16:16** — "He gathered them together into a place called in the Hebrew tongue Armageddon." The Greek pronoun refers back to the kings of verse 14 (so Wesley, Wakefield, the Syriac, and Sawyer render it), and the hills of Megiddo overlooking Esdraelon name the historic field of decisive battle.

  > "If the word them refers to the kings of verse 14, it is certain that no good agency would be made use of to gather them; and if the spirits are referred to by the word he, why is it in the singular number?" — DAR 651.1.

  > "The Syriac Testament reads: 'And they collected them together in a place called in Hebrew Armageddon.'" — DAR 651.6.

  > "Sawyer's translation renders it: 'And they assembled them in the place called in Hebrew Armageddon.'" — DAR 652.1.

  > "The hills of Megiddo, overlooking the plain of Esdraelon, was the place where Barak and Deborah destroyed Sisera's army, and where Josiah was routed by the Egyptian king Pharaoh-Necho." — DAR 652.6.

- **Revelation 17:12-14** — The ten kings "have one mind, and shall give their power and strength unto the beast"; then "these shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings." Armageddon is the confederated powers of earth versus the Lamb — and the Lamb prevails.

  > "These make war with the Lamb. Verse 14. Here we are carried into the future, to the time of the great and final battle; for at this time the Lamb has assumed the title of King of kings and Lord of lords, a title which he does not assume till his second coming." — DAR 661.3.

### The Seventh Vial — "It Is Done" — and the Voice of God

- **Revelation 16:17** — The seventh vial poured "into the air," and "there came a great voice out of the temple of heaven, from the throne, saying, It is done." All is finished: the cup of human guilt is full, the last soul has chosen, the number of the saved is complete, the books are closed.

  > "This plague will envelop equally the habitable globe. It will be universal. The very air will be deadly." — DAR 653.1.

  > "'There came a great voice out of the temple of heaven from the throne, saying, It is done!' Thus all is finished. The cup of human guilt has been filled up. The last soul has availed itself of the plan of salvation. The books are closed. The number of the saved is completed." — DAR 656.1.

- **Revelation 16:18** — "Voices, and thunders, and lightnings; and there was a great earthquake, such as was not since men were upon the earth." It is at midnight, by this voice, that God delivers His people; the firmament opens and shuts and the mountains shake.

  > "And there were voices, and thunders, and lightnings; and there was a great earthquake, such as was not since men were upon the earth, so mighty an earthquake, and so great." — DAR 652.7.

  > "It is at midnight that God manifests His power for the deliverance of His people.... whence comes the voice of God like the sound of many waters, saying: 'It is done.'... That voice shakes the heavens and the earth.... The firmament appears to open and shut.... The mountains shake like a reed in the wind." — GC 636.2-636.3.

- **Revelation 16:19** — "The great city was divided into three parts... and great Babylon came in remembrance before God." The great city — the false and apostate religion of the world — is rent into Paganism, Catholicism, and apostate Protestantism, and Babylon drinks the cup of the fierceness of God's wrath.

  > "The great city is divided into three parts; that is, the three grand divisions of the false and apostate religion of the world (the great city), Paganism, Catholicism, and relapsed Protestantism, seem to be set apart each to receive its appropriate doom.... and great Babylon comes in remembrance before God." — DAR 653.4.

- **Revelation 16:20** — "Every island fled away, and the mountains were not found." The convulsion of the seventh plague tears the very face of the earth.

  > "The whole earth heaves and swells like the waves of the sea. Its surface is breaking up. Its very foundations seem to be giving way. Mountain chains are sinking. Inhabited islands disappear." — GC 637.

- **Revelation 16:21** — "A great hail out of heaven, every stone about the weight of a talent" — about fifty-seven pounds each — and still "men blasphemed God." This is the bitter dregs of the seventh vial, the last instrument of wrath upon the impenitent.

  > "'And there fell upon men a great hail out of heaven.' This is the last instrumentality used in the infliction of punishment upon the wicked, — the bitter dregs of the seventh vial." — DAR 653.5.

  > "'Every stone about the weight of a talent.' A talent, according to various authorities, as a weight, is about fifty-seven pounds avoirdupois.... But mankind, at this time, will have no shelter.... Again the wicked give vent to their woe in blasphemy; for the plague of the hail is 'exceeding great.'" — DAR 654.1.

- **Joel 3:16** — "The Lord also shall roar out of Zion, and utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the heavens and the earth shall shake: but the Lord will be the hope of his people." The voice heard above all the voices of the seventh plague is the voice of God — terror to the wicked, refuge to the saints.

  > "'There were voices.' Above all will be heard the voice of God. 'The Lord also shall roar out of Zion, and utter his voice from Jerusalem... but the Lord will be the hope of his people, and the strength of the children of Israel.' Joel 3:16.... This will cause the great earthquake, such as was not since men were upon the earth." — DAR 653.3.

### The Battle Joined — and Broken by the Coming of Christ

- **Joel 3:9-12** — "Prepare war, wake up the mighty men... Beat your plowshares into swords... Assemble yourselves... let the heathen be wakened, and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat: for there will I sit to judge all the heathen." The world's powers are summoned to the battle — but the field is God's tribunal, not their victory.

  > "The kings of the East, the nationalities, powers, and kingdoms lying east of Palestine, will act a conspicuous part in the matter; for Joel says in reference to this scene, 'Let the heathen be wakened, and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat.'" — DAR 648.6.

  > "Now all have made their decisions; the wicked have fully united with Satan in his warfare against God. The time has come for God to vindicate the authority of His downtrodden law. Now the controversy is not alone with Satan, but with men." — GC 656.

- **Joel 3:13-14** — "Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe... Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision: for the day of the Lord is near in the valley of decision." The gathering is a harvest of judgment on a ripened world.

  > "'A noise shall come even to the ends of the earth; for the Lord hath a controversy with the nations, He will plead with all flesh; He will give them that are wicked to the sword.' Jeremiah 25:31. For six thousand years the great controversy has been in progress.... Now all have made their decisions." — GC 656.

- **Zephaniah 3:8** — "Wait ye upon me, saith the Lord, until the day that I rise up to the prey: for my determination is to gather the nations... to pour upon them mine indignation... for all the earth shall be devoured with the fire of my jealousy." God Himself gathers the nations — for judgment, not for their triumph.

  > "For what is the way of these kings to be prepared? Answer: To come up to the battle of the great day of God Almighty. Where is the battle to be fought? — Near Jerusalem. (Joel and Zephaniah.)" — DAR 648.6.

- **Jeremiah 25:31-33** — "The Lord hath a controversy with the nations... and the slain of the Lord shall be at that day from one end of the earth even unto the other." For six thousand years the controversy has run; now the Lord pleads with all flesh by the sword.

  > "'The Lord hath a controversy with the nations, He will plead with all flesh; He will give them that are wicked to the sword.' Jeremiah 25:31. For six thousand years the great controversy has been in progress... The time has come for God to vindicate the authority of His downtrodden law." — GC 656.1.

- **Isaiah 26:21** — "The Lord cometh out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity: the earth also shall disclose her blood." The Judge leaves His place; the long-hidden blood of the saints is requited.

  > "'The Lord cometh out of His place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity: the earth also shall disclose her blood, and shall no more cover her slain.' Isaiah 26:21." — GC 656.3.

- **Zechariah 14:12** — "Their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, and their eyes shall consume away in their holes, and their tongue shall consume away in their mouth." The plague upon all the people that have fought against Jerusalem — the consuming of the assembled enemy.

  > "'And this shall be the plague wherewith the Lord will smite all the people that have fought against Jerusalem; Their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, and their eyes shall consume away in their holes, and their tongue shall consume away in their mouth.'... In the mad strife of their own fierce passions, and by the awful outpouring of God's unmingled wrath, fall the wicked inhabitants of the earth." — GC 656.3.

- **Revelation 19:11-13** — "Heaven opened, and behold a white horse; and he that sat upon him was called Faithful and True... clothed with a vesture dipped in blood... his name is called The Word of God." Christ Himself, no longer Priest but Conqueror, rides forth to end Armageddon.

  > "With verse 11 a new scene is introduced. We are here carried back to the second coming of Christ, this time under the symbol of a warrior riding forth to battle. Why is he represented thus? — Because he is going forth to war, — to meet 'the kings of the earth and their armies,' and this would be the only proper character in which to represent him on such a mission. His vesture is dipped in blood." — DAR 684.

- **Revelation 19:14-16** — His armies follow on white horses; out of His mouth "goeth a sharp sword, that with it he should smite the nations," and on His vesture is written "KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS." The same title by which He overcame in chapter 17:14.

  > "Christ has at this time closed his mediatorial work, and laid off his priestly robes for kingly attire; for he has on his vesture and on his thigh a name written, King of kings and Lord of lords. This is in harmony with the character in which he here appears." — DAR 685.

- **Revelation 19:17-18** — The angel in the sun calls the fowls to "the supper of the great God," to eat "the flesh of kings, and... captains, and... mighty men." The mustered armies of Armageddon become the slain feast of the field.

  > "The angel standing in the sun, and issuing his call from thence to the fowls of heaven to come to the supper of the great God, denotes that this proclamation will go wherever the sun's rays fall upon this earth.... Thus, while the saints are partaking of the marriage supper of the Lamb, the wicked in their own persons furnish a great supper for the fowls of heaven." — DAR 685.

- **Revelation 19:19-21** — "The beast, and the kings of the earth, and their armies, gathered together to make war against him" — the same confederacy of the sixth vial — are broken: "the beast was taken, and with him the false prophet... cast alive into a lake of fire," and "the remnant were slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse." Armageddon ends not in battle but in the brightness of His coming.

  > "The fact that these are cast alive into the lake of fire, shows that these powers will not pass away and be succeeded by others, but be living powers at the second advent of Christ." — DAR 685.

  > "These are slain by the sword of Him that sits upon the horse, which sword proceeds out of his mouth. This sword is doubtless what is spoken of elsewhere as 'the spirit of his mouth' and 'the breath of his lips,' with which the Lord shall slay the wicked at his appearing and kingdom." — DAR 686.

- **Revelation 6:15-17** — "The kings of the earth, and the great men... hid themselves... and said to the mountains and rocks, Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb: for the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?" The mark-bearers' terror at the King's appearing.

  > "The day which they thought never would come, has at last taken them as in a snare; and the involuntary language of their anguished hearts is, 'The great day of his wrath is come, and who shall be able to stand?'... Those who will not now pray to God in penitence, will then pray to the rocks and mountains in despair." — DAR 434.

- **Revelation 11:18** — "The nations were angry, and thy wrath is come... and shouldest destroy them which destroy the earth." The seven last plagues fill up the wrath of this present generation.

  > "'And Thy Wrath Is Come.' — The wrath of God for the present generation is filled up in the seven last plagues (chapter 15:1), which consequently must here be referred to, and which are soon to be poured out upon the earth." — DAR 506.3.

### The Saints Sheltered — Plagues Fall Only on the Mark-Bearers

- **Daniel 12:1** — "At that time shall Michael stand up... and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was... and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book." Michael standing up = the close of probation; the plagues are the time of trouble, yet the sealed are delivered.

  > "His priestly robes are laid aside for royal vesture. The work of mercy is done, and the probation of our race is ended.... And from that time on... the nations are broken as with a rod of iron, and dashed in pieces like a potter's vessel, by a time of trouble such as never was, a series of judgments unparalleled in the world's history." — DAR 295.3.

  > "When the third angel's message closes, mercy no longer pleads for the guilty inhabitants of the earth.... all who have proved themselves loyal to the divine precepts have received 'the seal of the living God.' Then Jesus ceases His intercession in the sanctuary above." — GC 613.

- **Psalm 91:7-10** — "A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee... There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling." The express promise of the saints' immunity while the overflowing scourge passes over.

  > "But what is the condition of the saints while the 'overflowing scourge' is passing over? They are the special subjects of God's protection, without whose notice not a sparrow falls to the ground. Many are the promises which come crowding in to afford them comfort, summarily contained in the beautiful and expressive language of the 91st psalm." — DAR 656.2.

  > "'A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee.... there shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling.' Psalms 91:2-10." — DAR 656.3.

- **Isaiah 26:20** — "Come, my people, enter thou into thy chambers... hide thyself as it were for a little moment, until the indignation be overpast." The hiding of God's people during the outpouring of wrath.

  > "It is now, in the hour of utmost extremity, that the God of Israel will interpose for the deliverance of His chosen.... a dense blackness, deeper than the darkness of the night, falls upon the earth. Then a rainbow, shining with the glory from the throne of God, spans the heavens and seems to encircle each praying company." — GC 635-636.

- **Revelation 15:2** — They "that had gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark" stand on the sea of glass, singing "the song of Moses... and the song of the Lamb." The victors are seen safe before the vials are even poured — a deliverance anticipated.

  > "The prophet is permitted to anticipate a little in verses 2-4, and behold them as victors upon the sea of glass as it were mingled with fire, or sparkling and refulgent with the glory of God, singing the song of Moses and the Lamb.... we have here indubitable proof... that the saints are taken to heaven to receive a portion of their reward." — DAR 639.1.

  > "The song the victors sing, the song of Moses and the Lamb, given here in epitome in these words: 'Great and marvelous are thy works, Lord God Almighty; just and true are thy ways, thou King of saints,' is a song of infinite grandeur." — DAR 640.1.

- **Joel 3:16 (latter clause)** — "But the Lord will be the hope of his people, and the strength of the children of Israel." The same voice that shakes heaven and earth is the refuge of the redeemed.

  > "'There were voices.' Above all will be heard the voice of God.... 'but the Lord will be the hope of his people, and the strength of the children of Israel.' Joel 3:16." — DAR 653.3.

  > "By the people of God a voice, clear and melodious, is heard, saying, 'Look up,' and lifting their eyes to the heavens, they behold the bow of promise.... 'They come! they come! holy, harmless, and undefiled. They have kept the word of My patience; they shall walk among the angels.'" — GC 636.

> "It is at midnight that God manifests His power for the deliverance of His people." _(GC 636.2.)_ When the plagues end, "the wrath of God in the seven last plagues had been visited upon the inhabitants of the earth," the wicked are destroyed, and "the earth looked like a desolate wilderness." _(EW 289.3; EW 290.1.)_
