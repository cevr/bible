# 16. The Mark of the Beast

> _The mark of the beast is the badge of submission to the beast's authority in the very point where he exalted himself against God — His law and His holy day. The little horn "thought to change times and laws"; the change of the seventh-day Sabbath to Sunday is therefore the sign of his assumed authority, and its acceptance, after the truth is known and Sunday is enforced, is the mark. Over against it stands the seal of God — the fourth commandment, the only precept bearing the Lawgiver's name, title, and dominion. No man has yet received the mark; it is the final test, when Christendom will be divided into just two classes. (DAR 624, 628-630; GC 446-449, 604-605.)_

### The power that thought to change God's law

- **Daniel 7:25** — The little horn shall "speak great words against the most High, and wear out the saints," and "think to change times and laws." The papacy's one attempt to alter the decalogue — striking out the second commandment and changing the fourth from the seventh day to the first — exactly fulfills this; only by changing God's law could it exalt itself above God.

  > "Says Daniel, of the little horn, the papacy: 'He shall think to change times and the law.'… Only by changing God's law could the papacy exalt itself above God; whoever should understandingly keep the law as thus changed would be giving supreme honor to that power by which the change was made. Such an act of obedience to papal laws would be a mark of allegiance to the pope in the place of God." — GC 446.

  > "The 'mark of the beast' is that institution which this power has set up as proof of its authority to legislate for the church… It consists in a change of the law of God, by which the signature of royalty is taken from the law, — the seventh-day Sabbath, the great memorial of Jehovah's creative work, is torn from its place in the decalogue, and a false and counterfeit Sabbath, the first day of the week, is set up in its stead." — DAR 624.

- **Revelation 13:16-17** — The two-horned beast "causeth all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads," so "that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark, or the name of the beast." The mark is enforced by the United States in homage to the papacy.

  > "Of all the symbols mentioned, one alone, the two-horned beast of Revelation 13, is left; and of all the countries of the earth respecting which any reason exists why they should be mentioned in prophecy, one alone, the United States government, remains." — DAR 533-534.

  > "The two-horned beast enforces the mark of the beast; that is, he establishes by law the observance of the first day of the week, or Sunday Sabbath." — DAR 624.

  > "The statement that the beast with two horns 'causeth the earth and them which dwell therein to worship the first beast' indicates that the authority of this nation is to be exercised in enforcing some observance which shall be an act of homage to the papacy." — GC 442.

- **Revelation 13:18** — "Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast: for it is the number of a man; and his number is Six hundred threescore and six." The number marks the beast as a man-power exalting human authority over God's.

  > "The 'mark of the beast' is that institution which this power has set up as proof of its authority to legislate for the church, and command the consciences of men under sin. It consists in a change of the law of God, by which the signature of royalty is taken from the law." — DAR 624.

### What constitutes the mark — Sunday for the Sabbath

- **Revelation 14:9-11** — The third angel warns "with a loud voice, If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark," he "shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God." The most fearful threatening in Scripture is leveled at this one act of homage.

  > "And now another proclamation is sent forth, announcing in solemn tones that if any man shall do this, he shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God… This is as severe a threatening as God can make to be inflicted in this life, and it is the last." — DAR 620.

  > "The most fearful threatening ever addressed to mortals is contained in the third angel's message. That must be a terrible sin which calls down the wrath of God unmingled with mercy." — GC 449.

- **Revelation 14:12** — Over against the mark stands the remnant: "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." The opposite of receiving the mark is keeping the very commandment the beast changed.

  > "The message, presenting as its chief burden a warning against receiving the mark of the beast, will of course bring its adherents to discard that practice which constitutes the mark, and to adopt the opposite. It does lead them to discard the observance of the first day of the week, and adopt that of the seventh day." — DAR 627-628.

  > "While one class, by accepting the sign of submission to earthly powers, receive the mark of the beast, the other, choosing the token of allegiance to divine authority, receive the seal of God." — GC 605.

- **Daniel 7:25 with Exodus 20:8-11** — The only "time" and "law" the papacy claims to have changed is the fourth commandment, moving the Sabbath of the LORD to the first day. Roman writers cite "the very act of changing the Sabbath into Sunday" as "the sign, or mark, of the authority of the Roman Church."

  > "As the sign of the authority of the Catholic Church, papist writers cite 'the very act of changing the Sabbath into Sunday, which Protestants allow of'… What then is the change of the Sabbath, but the sign, or mark, of the authority of the Roman Church—'the mark of the beast'?" — GC 446-448.

- **Romans 6:16** — "To whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are to whom ye obey." Keeping Sunday in deference to the church's authority is yielding obedience to that power in the place of God.

  > "The two-horned beast enforces the mark of the beast; that is, he establishes by law the observance of the first day of the week, or Sunday Sabbath." — DAR 624.

  > "So this movement explains how this beast-power has brought into Christendom certain institutions which antagonize the requirements of the Most High, and shows that if we yield to these, we worship this power. 'Know ye not,' says Paul, 'that to whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are to whom ye obey?' Romans 6:16." — DAR 626.

- **The mark defined** — "The mark of the beast is that institution which this power has set up as proof of its authority... It consists in a change of the law of God, by which the seventh-day Sabbath... is torn from its place in the decalogue, and a false and counterfeit Sabbath, the first day of the week, is set up in its stead."

  > "The 'mark of the beast' is that institution which this power has set up as proof of its authority to legislate for the church, and command the consciences of men under sin. It consists in a change of the law of God, by which the signature of royalty is taken from the law, — the seventh-day Sabbath, the great memorial of Jehovah's creative work, is torn from its place in the decalogue, and a false and counterfeit Sabbath, the first day of the week, is set up in its stead." — DAR 624.

### The Sabbath is the seal of God — His name, title, and dominion

- **Exodus 31:13** — "Verily my sabbaths ye shall keep: for it is a sign between me and you... that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you." God Himself names the Sabbath His sign; sign, seal, token, and mark are synonymous in Scripture usage.

  > "We have seen above that in Scripture usage, sign, seal, token, and mark are synonymous terms. Now, the Lord expressly says that the Sabbath is a sign between him and his people. 'Verily my Sabbaths ye shall keep; for it is a sign between me and you throughout your generations; that ye may know that I am the Lord that doth sanctify you.' Exodus 31:13." — DAR 440.

  > "The keeping of the true Sabbath, in obedience to God's law, is an evidence of loyalty to the Creator." — GC 605.

- **Exodus 31:17** — "It is a sign between me and the children of Israel for ever: for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth... and on the seventh day he rested." The sign rests on creation — the Sabbath identifies the LORD as Creator, the only ground of His right to rule.

  > "On my part it is the seal of my authority, the sign that I have the right to command obedience; on your part it is a token that you take me to be your God." — DAR 440-441.

- **Ezekiel 20:12** — "I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them." The same divine seal is repeated by the prophet.

  > "Here the Lord told his people that the very object of their keeping the Sabbath, that is, observing the fourth commandment, was that they might know that he was the true God." — DAR 440.

- **Ezekiel 20:20** — "Hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign between me and you, that ye may know that I am the LORD your God." The Sabbath is the appointed token by which God's people know and avow their God.

  > "The same fact is again stated by the prophet Ezekiel 20:12, 20. Here the Lord told his people that the very object of their keeping the Sabbath, that is, observing the fourth commandment, was that they might know that he was the true God." — DAR 440.

- **Exodus 20:8-11** — The fourth commandment alone contains the name ("the LORD thy God"), His title and creative work ("the LORD made heaven and earth"), and His dominion ("heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is"). A seal must carry the name, office, and territory of the lawgiver — the fourth commandment alone supplies all three, and so is the seal of the law.

  > "As we examine this commandment, which contains the declaration, 'For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is,' etc., we see at once that we are reading the requirements of Him who created all things… Now the author of this law has declared who he is, the extent of his dominion, and his right to rule… Thus with the fourth commandment in its place, this wonderful document, the decalogue… has a signature; it has that which renders it intelligible and authentic; it has a seal. But without the fourth commandment, it lacks all these things." — DAR 438-440.

- **Genesis 2:1-3** — "God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested." The Sabbath was sanctified at creation, before sin, and so is primary, immutable, and eternal — not a ceremonial shadow that ended at the cross.

  > "The laws and institutions which existed before man's fall were primary in their nature… In other words, they were, in the very nature of things, immutable and eternal… These [ceremonial and typical laws], and these only, were abolished at the cross. The Sabbath law was a primary law, and therefore immutable and eternal." — DAR 630.

- **A seal authenticates a law** — "A seal is used to render valid or authentic any enactments... the name must always be used." Passing through the decalogue, only the fourth commandment shows _who_ the Lawgiver is and _by what right_ He commands — it is the seal of God.

  > "A seal is used to render valid or authentic any enactments, or laws, which a person or power may promulgate… Even with a literal seal, the name must always be used… affix the signature of royalty, showing who it is that demands obedience, and his right to demand it." — DAR 438-439.

### The seal of God in the forehead — the contrast company

- **Revelation 7:2-3** — "Another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God... Hurt not the earth... till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads." The sealing is the binding of the Sabbath truth upon God's people before the winds of trouble are loosed.

  > "By the scenes introduced in the verses before us, namely, the four winds apparently about to blow, bringing war and trouble upon the land, and this work restrained till the servants of God should be sealed… we conclude that the seal of God, here placed upon his servants, is some distinguishing mark, or religious characteristic, through which they will be exempted from the judgments of God that fall on the wicked around them." — DAR 442.

  > "He who reads every heart and tries every motive will leave none who desire a knowledge of the truth, to be deceived as to the issues of the controversy… Everyone is to have sufficient light to make his decision intelligently." — GC 605.

- **Revelation 7:4** — "There were sealed an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel" — the same company developed by the third angel's message.

  > "He is thus addressing the last generation of Christians, the Christians of our own day; and he calls them the twelve tribes scattered abroad… so, as belonging to these twelve tribes, will all the redeemed be reckoned, whether on earth they were Jews or Gentiles." — DAR 446.

- **Revelation 14:1** — The 144,000 stand with the Lamb "having his Father's name written in their foreheads." The seal in the forehead (Rev 7:3) is the Father's name (Rev 14:1) — and the name is found in the fourth commandment.

  > "Again, the 144,000, who in the chapter before us are said to be sealed with the seal of God in their foreheads, are again brought to view in Revelation 14:1, where they are said to have the Father's name written in their foreheads." — DAR 439.

- **Ezekiel 9:4** — "Set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations." The slaughter is stayed for those who bear God's distinguishing mark — the type of the final sealing.

  > "We are reminded… of the mark made by the man with a writer's ink-horn (Ezekiel 9) upon all those who were to be spared by the men with the slaughtering weapons who followed after; and we conclude that the seal of God, here placed upon his servants, is some distinguishing mark, or religious characteristic, through which they will be exempted from the judgments of God." — DAR 442.

- **Isaiah 8:16** — "Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples." The closing work seals the law — and the seal of the law is the Sabbath.

  > "Having now ascertained that the seal of God is his holy Sabbath, having his name, we are prepared to proceed with the application." — DAR 442.

- **Revelation 22:14** — "Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city." Only the commandment-keepers — those bearing the seal, not the mark — enter the city.

  > "It brings out a company of whom it can be said, 'Here are they that keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus.'… No plainer line of demarcation could be drawn between two classes." — DAR 627.

  > "The keeping of the true Sabbath, in obedience to God's law, is an evidence of loyalty to the Creator. While one class, by accepting the sign of submission to earthly powers, receive the mark of the beast, the other, choosing the token of allegiance to divine authority, receive the seal of God." — GC 605.

### Seal versus mark — the two opposing signs

|            | **Seal of God**                       | **Mark of the beast**                     |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Sign       | The seventh-day Sabbath               | The first-day (Sunday) sabbath            |
| Authority  | The Creator, by His own law           | The papacy, by changing God's law         |
| Placed     | "In their foreheads" (Rev 7:3)        | In forehead or hand (Rev 13:16)           |
| Located in | The fourth commandment (Ex 20:8-11)   | The change of that commandment (Dan 7:25) |
| Result     | Right to the tree of life (Rev 22:14) | The wine of God's wrath (Rev 14:10)       |

- **The two classes** — "Christendom will at last be divided into just two classes... those who are sealed with the seal of the living God... and those who are sealed with the seal of the beast." One keeps the seventh day, the other the first — opposite extremities of the week.

  > "Christendom will at last be divided into just two classes; to wit, those who are sealed with the seal of the living God, that is, have his mark, or keep his Sabbath; and those who are sealed with the seal of the beast, that is, have his mark, or keep his Sabbath. One is the seventh day; the other is just as far removed from it as possible, even to the other extremity of the week, namely, the first day." — DAR 628.

  > "In the issue of the contest all Christendom will be divided into two great classes—those who keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus, and those who worship the beast and his image and receive his mark." — GC 450.

- **Revelation 12:17** with **Revelation 14:12** — The dragon makes war on "the remnant... which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." The mark divides the world; the commandment-keeping remnant is the company that refuses it.

  > "It brings out a company of whom it can be said, 'Here are they that keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus.' In the very heart of Christendom this work is done; and those who receive the message are rendered peculiar by their practice in reference to the commandments of God." — DAR 627.

### The mark is not yet received — it is the final test

- **Revelation 14:9-10** — "But not one is made to suffer the wrath of God until the truth has been brought home to his mind and conscience, and has been rejected." The mark cannot be received in ignorance; everyone is given sufficient light to decide intelligently.

  > "But not one is made to suffer the wrath of God until the truth has been brought home to his mind and conscience, and has been rejected… The decree is not to be urged upon the people blindly. Everyone is to have sufficient light to make his decision intelligently." — GC 605.

- **Isaiah 58:12-14** — The remnant are "the repairer of the breach, the restorer of paths to dwell in," who "turn away thy foot from the sabbath." The breach is the gap left in God's law by the change of the Sabbath; the last message repairs it.

  > "This is in harmony with the argument on the seal of God… It was there shown that sign, seal, mark, and token are synonymous terms, and that God takes his Sabbath to be his sign, mark, or seal, in reference to his people." — DAR 628.

- **The final test** — "The Sabbath will be the great test of loyalty, for it is the point of truth especially controverted." Only when Sunday observance is enforced by law, and the truth of the Sabbath is set before all, will the line be drawn: one class receiving the mark of the beast, the other the seal of God.

  > "The Sabbath will be the great test of loyalty, for it is the point of truth especially controverted. When the final test shall be brought to bear upon men, then the line of distinction will be drawn between those who serve God and those who serve Him not." — GC 605.

  > "The third message utters a solemn protest against all this evil. It exposes the work of the beast, shows the nature of its opposition to the law of God, warns the people against compliance with its demands, and points out to all the way of truth." — DAR 624-625.

- **The decisive choice** — "Whoever shall trample upon God's law to obey a human enactment receives the mark of the beast; he accepts the sign of allegiance to the power which he chooses to obey instead of God." The mark is finally received in the act of choosing man's enforced sabbath over God's.

  > "With the issue thus clearly brought before him, whoever shall trample upon God's law to obey a human enactment receives the mark of the beast; he accepts the sign of allegiance to the power which he chooses to obey instead of God." — GC 604.

  > "It is shown that the mark of the beast is an institution which has been arrayed in Christian garb, and insidiously introduced into the Christian church in such a way as to nullify the authority of Jehovah and enthrone that of the beast. Stripped of all disguises, it is simply setting up a counterfeit Sabbath of its own on the first day of the week, in place of the Sabbath of the Lord on the seventh day." — DAR 626.

- **James 2:10-12** — "Whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is guilty of all." To break the fourth commandment for the sake of the beast's institution is to break the whole law — there is no neutral ground in the final test.

  > "The Sabbath is a part of that law which our Lord openly declared that he came not to destroy. On the other hand, he most solemnly affirmed that it should endure in every jot and tittle while the earth should continue. Matthew 5:17-20." — DAR 630.
