# 16. The Mark of the Beast

> _The mark of the beast is the badge of submission to the beast's authority in the very point where he exalted himself against God — His law and His holy day. The little horn "thought to change times and laws"; the change of the seventh-day Sabbath to Sunday is therefore the sign of his assumed authority, and its acceptance, after the truth is known and Sunday is enforced, is the mark. Over against it stands the seal of God — the fourth commandment, the only precept bearing the Lawgiver's name, title, and dominion. No man has yet received the mark; it is the final test, when Christendom will be divided into just two classes. (DAR 624, 628-630; GC 446-449, 604-605.)_

### The power that thought to change God's law

- **Daniel 7:25** — The little horn shall "speak great words against the most High, and wear out the saints," and "think to change times and laws." The papacy's one attempt to alter the decalogue — striking out the second commandment and changing the fourth from the seventh day to the first — exactly fulfills this; only by changing God's law could it exalt itself above God. _(GC 446; DAR 624.)_
- **Revelation 13:16-17** — The two-horned beast "causeth all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads," so "that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark, or the name of the beast." The mark is enforced by the United States in homage to the papacy. _(DAR 533-534, 624; GC 442.)_
- **Revelation 13:18** — "Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast: for it is the number of a man; and his number is Six hundred threescore and six." The number marks the beast as a man-power exalting human authority over God's. _(DAR 624.)_

### What constitutes the mark — Sunday for the Sabbath

- **Revelation 14:9-11** — The third angel warns "with a loud voice, If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark," he "shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God." The most fearful threatening in Scripture is leveled at this one act of homage. _(DAR 620, 624; GC 449.)_
- **Revelation 14:12** — Over against the mark stands the remnant: "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." The opposite of receiving the mark is keeping the very commandment the beast changed. _(DAR 627-628; GC 605.)_
- **Daniel 7:25 with Exodus 20:8-11** — The only "time" and "law" the papacy claims to have changed is the fourth commandment, moving the Sabbath of the LORD to the first day. Roman writers cite "the very act of changing the Sabbath into Sunday" as "the sign, or mark, of the authority of the Roman Church." _(GC 446-448.)_
- **Romans 6:16** — "To whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are to whom ye obey." Keeping Sunday in deference to the church's authority is yielding obedience to that power in the place of God. _(DAR 624, 626.)_
- **The mark defined** — "The mark of the beast is that institution which this power has set up as proof of its authority... It consists in a change of the law of God, by which the seventh-day Sabbath... is torn from its place in the decalogue, and a false and counterfeit Sabbath, the first day of the week, is set up in its stead." _(DAR 624.)_

### The Sabbath is the seal of God — His name, title, and dominion

- **Exodus 31:13** — "Verily my sabbaths ye shall keep: for it is a sign between me and you... that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you." God Himself names the Sabbath His sign; sign, seal, token, and mark are synonymous in Scripture usage. _(DAR 440; GC 605.)_
- **Exodus 31:17** — "It is a sign between me and the children of Israel for ever: for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth... and on the seventh day he rested." The sign rests on creation — the Sabbath identifies the LORD as Creator, the only ground of His right to rule. _(DAR 440-441.)_
- **Ezekiel 20:12** — "I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them." The same divine seal is repeated by the prophet. _(DAR 440.)_
- **Ezekiel 20:20** — "Hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign between me and you, that ye may know that I am the LORD your God." The Sabbath is the appointed token by which God's people know and avow their God. _(DAR 440.)_
- **Exodus 20:8-11** — The fourth commandment alone contains the name ("the LORD thy God"), His title and creative work ("the LORD made heaven and earth"), and His dominion ("heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is"). A seal must carry the name, office, and territory of the lawgiver — the fourth commandment alone supplies all three, and so is the seal of the law. _(DAR 438-440.)_
- **Genesis 2:1-3** — "God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested." The Sabbath was sanctified at creation, before sin, and so is primary, immutable, and eternal — not a ceremonial shadow that ended at the cross. _(DAR 630.)_
- **A seal authenticates a law** — "A seal is used to render valid or authentic any enactments... the name must always be used." Passing through the decalogue, only the fourth commandment shows _who_ the Lawgiver is and _by what right_ He commands — it is the seal of God. _(DAR 438-439.)_

### The seal of God in the forehead — the contrast company

- **Revelation 7:2-3** — "Another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God... Hurt not the earth... till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads." The sealing is the binding of the Sabbath truth upon God's people before the winds of trouble are loosed. _(DAR 442; GC 605.)_
- **Revelation 7:4** — "There were sealed an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel" — the same company developed by the third angel's message. _(DAR 446.)_
- **Revelation 14:1** — The 144,000 stand with the Lamb "having his Father's name written in their foreheads." The seal in the forehead (Rev 7:3) is the Father's name (Rev 14:1) — and the name is found in the fourth commandment. _(DAR 439.)_
- **Ezekiel 9:4** — "Set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations." The slaughter is stayed for those who bear God's distinguishing mark — the type of the final sealing. _(DAR 442.)_
- **Isaiah 8:16** — "Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples." The closing work seals the law — and the seal of the law is the Sabbath. _(DAR 442.)_
- **Revelation 22:14** — "Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city." Only the commandment-keepers — those bearing the seal, not the mark — enter the city. _(DAR 627; GC 605.)_

### Seal versus mark — the two opposing signs

|            | **Seal of God**                       | **Mark of the beast**                     |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Sign       | The seventh-day Sabbath               | The first-day (Sunday) sabbath            |
| Authority  | The Creator, by His own law           | The papacy, by changing God's law         |
| Placed     | "In their foreheads" (Rev 7:3)        | In forehead or hand (Rev 13:16)           |
| Located in | The fourth commandment (Ex 20:8-11)   | The change of that commandment (Dan 7:25) |
| Result     | Right to the tree of life (Rev 22:14) | The wine of God's wrath (Rev 14:10)       |

- **The two classes** — "Christendom will at last be divided into just two classes... those who are sealed with the seal of the living God... and those who are sealed with the seal of the beast." One keeps the seventh day, the other the first — opposite extremities of the week. _(DAR 628; GC 450.)_
- **Revelation 12:17** with **Revelation 14:12** — The dragon makes war on "the remnant... which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." The mark divides the world; the commandment-keeping remnant is the company that refuses it. _(DAR 627.)_

### The mark is not yet received — it is the final test

- **Revelation 14:9-10** — "But not one is made to suffer the wrath of God until the truth has been brought home to his mind and conscience, and has been rejected." The mark cannot be received in ignorance; everyone is given sufficient light to decide intelligently. _(GC 605.)_
- **Isaiah 58:12-14** — The remnant are "the repairer of the breach, the restorer of paths to dwell in," who "turn away thy foot from the sabbath." The breach is the gap left in God's law by the change of the Sabbath; the last message repairs it. _(DAR 628.)_
- **The final test** — "The Sabbath will be the great test of loyalty, for it is the point of truth especially controverted." Only when Sunday observance is enforced by law, and the truth of the Sabbath is set before all, will the line be drawn: one class receiving the mark of the beast, the other the seal of God. _(GC 605; DAR 624-625.)_
- **The decisive choice** — "Whoever shall trample upon God's law to obey a human enactment receives the mark of the beast; he accepts the sign of allegiance to the power which he chooses to obey instead of God." The mark is finally received in the act of choosing man's enforced sabbath over God's. _(GC 604; DAR 626.)_
- **James 2:10-12** — "Whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is guilty of all." To break the fourth commandment for the sake of the beast's institution is to break the whole law — there is no neutral ground in the final test. _(DAR 630.)_
