# 6. Daniel 9 — The Seventy Weeks Unlock the 2300 Days (The Keystone)

> _This is the keystone of the whole historicist arch. In Daniel 8 the angel Gabriel
> explained ram, he-goat, and little horn, but left Daniel fainting and the 2300 days
> unexplained ("none understood it"). In chapter 9 Gabriel returns to "give skill and
> understanding" and finishes the unfinished vision. Seventy weeks — 490 years — are
> "cut off" (determined) from the 2300, dating from the decree to restore Jerusalem
> (Artaxerxes, 457 B.C.). Those 490 years measure off the Messiah to the day: 69 weeks
> (483 yrs) to His anointing at baptism (A.D. 27); cut off in the midst of the last week
> (A.D. 31 crucifixion); the 70 weeks closing A.D. 34 when the gospel turns to the
> Gentiles. Subtract 490 from 2300 and 1810 years remain past A.D. 34 — reaching the
> autumn of 1844. Locate the seventy weeks and the 2300 days are surely located.
> (DAR 192, 206-207; GC 326-328, 410.)_

### Gabriel returns to finish the unexplained vision of Daniel 8

- **Daniel 8:14** — "Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." The longest time-prophecy in Scripture — but in chapter 8 it is left without a starting date. _(DAR 192; GC 326.)_
- **Daniel 8:26-27** — The vision of the evening and morning "is true," yet shut up "for many days"; Daniel "fainted, and was sick," and "none understood it." The 2300-day number was given but its application was withheld — the loose end chapter 9 ties. _(DAR 211; GC 325-326.)_
- **Daniel 9:1-2** — Daniel, "in the first year of Darius," understood "by books" Jeremiah's seventy years of captivity were ending — and so prayed for his people, mistaking those literal seventy years for the indignation. _(PREX1 130.)_
- **Daniel 9:21** — "Even the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the beginning" — the very same angel of chapter 8 — was "caused to fly swiftly" to Daniel "about the time of the evening oblation." The same messenger comes back to finish the same vision. _(DAR 191; GC 326.)_
- **Daniel 9:22-23** — Gabriel: "I am now come forth to give thee skill and understanding... therefore understand the matter, and consider the vision." He sends Daniel back to "the vision" — the only vision left unexplained, that of the 2300 days. _(GC 326.)_

### "Seventy weeks are cut off" — the seventy weeks are part of the 2300 days

- **Daniel 9:24** — "Seventy weeks are determined upon thy people and upon thy holy city" — 70 weeks of years = 490 years, to finish transgression, make an end of sins, make reconciliation, bring in everlasting righteousness, seal up vision and prophecy, and anoint the most Holy. _(DAR 196; GC 326.)_
- **The Hebrew "determined" = "cut off."** The word _nehhtak_ (Dan. 9:24) "properly" means "to cut off" — Gesenius, Stockius, the Septuagint, Theodotion, and the Vulgate all carry the sense of cutting. The 490 years are cut off — but from what? _(DAR 192.)_
- **There is only one period to cut from.** "There is no period from which the seventy weeks could be cut off but the 2300 days of the previous vision." The 70 weeks are the first 490 years of the 2300; therefore the two periods begin together. _(DAR 192; GC 326.)_
- **Numbers 14:34** — "each day for a year" — the wilderness sentence, God's own rule that a prophetic day stands for a literal year. _(DAR 197, 207.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year." The second witness to the year-day principle that turns 70 weeks (490 days) into 490 years and 2300 days into 2300 years. _(DAR 197, 207.)_

### The starting date: the decree to restore and build Jerusalem, 457 B.C.

- **Daniel 9:25** — Reckon "from the going forth of the commandment to restore and to build Jerusalem unto the Messiah the Prince" — seven weeks and threescore and two weeks (69 weeks); "the street shall be built again, and the wall, even in troublous times." The decree, not the captivity's end, is the anchor. _(DAR 196; GC 326.)_
- **Four candidate decrees — only one fits.** Cyrus (B.C. 536, Ezra 1) and Darius (B.C. 519, Ezra 6) concerned only the temple; Nehemiah's grant (B.C. 444, Neh. 2) was merely verbal and too late. Only Artaxerxes' decree to Ezra (B.C. 457, Ezra 7) restored the full civil and ecclesiastical state. _(DAR 197-200.)_
- **Ezra 7:11-26** — The full decree of Artaxerxes to Ezra: silver and gold, temple service, and power to "set magistrates and judges... whether it be unto death, or to banishment" — a restoration of the whole Jewish polity, the only grant ample enough to "restore and build Jerusalem." _(DAR 198; GC 326.)_
- **Ezra 6:14** — The Jews built "according to the commandment of Cyrus, and Darius, and Artaxerxes" — three kings, but "the commandment" (singular). The three decrees are reckoned as one whole, perfected and "gone forth" only in the grant to Ezra. _(DAR 206; GC 326-327.)_
- **Nehemiah 2:1-8** — Nehemiah's request "in the twentieth year of Artaxerxes" (B.C. 444) was only spoken permission with travel-letters; he found Jerusalem's work already underway (Neh. 2:16) under Ezra's decree, finishing in 52 days — far short of the prophecy's seven weeks (49 years) for the building. Hence not the starting point. _(DAR 199-200.)_
- **The 457 B.C. date is fixed by astronomy.** The canon of Ptolemy, "demonstrated by the concurrent agreement of more than twenty eclipses," places the seventh of Artaxerxes at B.C. 457 — a date that cannot be moved without unsettling all chronology. _(DAR 205.)_

### The 69 weeks (483 years): to Messiah the Prince — A.D. 27 anointing

- **Daniel 9:25** — "unto the Messiah the Prince shall be seven weeks, and threescore and two weeks" — 69 weeks = 483 years. From the autumn of 457 B.C., 483 years reach to the autumn of A.D. 27. _(DAR 201; GC 327.)_
- **"Messiah" means "the Anointed One."** At A.D. 27 Christ was anointed — not crowned with oil but with the Spirit, at His baptism, exactly where the 483 years end. _(GC 327.)_
- **Matthew 3:16-17** — At His baptism "the Spirit of God" descended "like a dove" and the Father's voice declared, "This is my beloved Son." The visible anointing of the Messiah the Prince. _(DAR 201.)_
- **Acts 10:38** — "God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power" — Peter naming the baptism as the anointing that made Him Messiah (the Anointed). _(GC 327.)_
- **Luke 4:18-19** — "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel... to preach the acceptable year of the Lord." Christ Himself claims the anointing the 69 weeks foretold. _(GC 327.)_
- **Mark 1:14-15** — Straightway after His baptism Jesus came "preaching the gospel of the kingdom of God... The time is fulfilled." The only prophetic time then closing was the 69 weeks to Messiah the Prince — and the Messiah announces its fulfilment with His own lips. _(DAR 201-202; GC 327.)_
- **Luke 3:1** — "In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar" John's ministry — and soon Christ's baptism — is dated; the 15th of Tiberius corresponds to A.D. 27, the very year the 483 years expire. (The seeming clash with "about thirty years" is resolved by the four-year error in the Dionysian era — Christ was born about B.C. 4.) _(DAR 201, footnote.)_
- **Galatians 4:4** — "When the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son" — the "fulness of the time" is no vague phrase but the precise terminus of the prophetic 69 weeks. _(GC 327.)_

### The midst of the week: Messiah cut off — A.D. 31 crucifixion

- **Daniel 9:26** — "And after threescore and two weeks shall Messiah be cut off, but not for himself" — the Anointed One slain, His death substitutionary ("not for himself"), and the city and sanctuary doomed to "the prince that shall come" (Rome). _(DAR 202-203, 211.)_
- **Daniel 9:27** — "He shall confirm the covenant with many for one week" (the last seven years, A.D. 27-34); "and in the midst of the week he shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease." The middle of the 70th week — three and a half years after A.D. 27 — falls in the spring of A.D. 31. _(DAR 202-203; GC 327-328.)_
- **Matthew 27:50-51** — When Jesus "yielded up the ghost... the veil of the temple was rent in twain from the top to the bottom." Heaven itself caused the sacrifice and oblation to cease at the cross; type met antitype, the shadow-service ended. _(DAR 202; GC 328.)_
- **A.D. 31 fixed by His four Passovers.** Christ kept four Passovers (John 2:13; 5:1; 6:4; 13:1), giving a ministry of three and a half years from autumn A.D. 27 to spring A.D. 31 — "exactly... the midst of the seventieth week." Thirteen ancient authorities locate the crucifixion in the spring of A.D. 31. _(DAR 203-204.)_

### The 70 weeks close: A.D. 34 — the gospel turns to the Gentiles

- **Daniel 9:24** — The 70 weeks were "determined upon thy people" — allotted to the Jews as the special people of God; when the 490 years run out, that special standing ends. Going forward from the cross (A.D. 31) three and a half years reaches the autumn of A.D. 34. _(DAR 204-205; GC 328.)_
- **Acts 1:8** — "Ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem... and unto the uttermost part of the earth." The widening of the gospel beyond the Jews waits on the close of their seventy weeks. _(GC 328.)_
- **Acts 3:25** — "Ye are the children of the prophets, and of the covenant" — within the 70th week (A.D. 27-34) the offer of the covenant goes "to you first," to the Jews; only at A.D. 34 does it pass to all nations. _(GC 327-328.)_
- **The martyrdom of Stephen marks A.D. 34.** The Sanhedrin "sealed its rejection of the gospel by the martyrdom of Stephen and the persecution of the followers of Christ" (Acts 9:1-18); the scattered disciples "went everywhere preaching," and the apostles turned to the Gentiles — exactly the event due when the Jews' allotted period expired. _(DAR 204-205; GC 328.)_

### The keystone arithmetic: 490 from 2300 → 1844

| Event                                             | Reference                 | Date                 |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Decree to Ezra — "going forth of the commandment" | Ezra 7; Dan. 9:25         | autumn 457 B.C.      |
| 7 weeks (49 yrs): street & wall rebuilt           | Dan. 9:25                 | B.C. 408             |
| 69 weeks (483 yrs): Messiah anointed at baptism   | Dan. 9:25; Mark 1:15      | autumn A.D. 27       |
| Midst of 70th week: Messiah cut off (crucified)   | Dan. 9:26-27; Matt. 27:51 | spring A.D. 31       |
| 70 weeks (490 yrs) end: gospel to the Gentiles    | Dan. 9:24; Acts 9         | autumn A.D. 34       |
| Remainder of the 2300: 2300 − 490 = 1810 yrs      | Dan. 8:14                 | A.D. 34 + 1810       |
| 2300 days end: "the sanctuary shall be cleansed"  | Dan. 8:14                 | autumn **A.D. 1844** |

- **2300 − 490 = 1810.** "The seventy weeks are but the first 490 years of the 2300. Take 490 from 2300, and there remain 1810." The 490 ended in the autumn of A.D. 34. _(DAR 206-207; GC 328.)_
- **A.D. 34 + 1810 = autumn 1844.** "If to this date we now add the remaining 1810 years... we have the autumn of A.D. 1844. Thus speedily and surely do we find the termination of the 2300 days, when once the seventy weeks have been located." _(DAR 206-207; GC 328, 410.)_
- **The same chain in The Great Controversy.** "The seventy weeks — 490 days — having been cut off from the 2300, there were 1810 days remaining... From A.D. 34, 1810 years extend to 1844. Consequently the 2300 days of Daniel 8:14 terminate in 1844." _(GC 328, 410.)_
- **Miller's witness (1840s).** "The seventy weeks were evidently fulfilled in the year A.D. 33, beginning 457 years B.C., at the going forth of the commandment to Ezra to restore the law and the people to Jerusalem" — Gabriel was "so particular in defining the beginning and the end" precisely because definite time was meant. _(MWV1 43.)_
- **Litch's witness.** Daniel's prayer over Jeremiah's seventy years is "the true connecting link between the vision of the ram and goat of the 8th chapter and the prophecy of seventy weeks"; Gabriel corrects Daniel — not seventy years of captivity, but seventy sevens "cut off" for the people. _(PREX1 130.)_
- **Why it must be the autumn.** It takes 457 full years B.C. and 1843 full years A.D. to make 2300; since Ezra reached Jerusalem only in the fifth month and the work began in the seventh (autumn) of 457, the whole period runs to the seventh month — autumn, Jewish time — of 1844. _(DAR 207.)_
- **The whole arch stands or falls here.** As the pioneers said: "If the connection between the seventy weeks of Daniel 9 and the 2300 days of Daniel 8 does not exist, the whole system is shaken to its foundation; if it does exist, as we suppose, the system must stand." Every specification of Daniel 9 was "unquestionably fulfilled at the time appointed" — fixing 457 B.C. and, with it, 1844 beyond reasonable doubt. _(DAR 195; GC 410.)_
