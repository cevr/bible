# Understanding Prophecy — The Full Chain

_In the manner of Stephen N. Haskell's Bible Handbook: short, pointed studies that gather the texts on a line of truth and let Scripture speak for itself. Each link is a chain of verses with a brief word of explanation and the pioneer or Ellen G. White page where the argument is drawn out at length — so the reader may take the Bible in hand and prove every point._

This handbook walks the COMPLETE interlocking historicist argument of the Seventh-day Adventist pioneers. It begins with the year-day principle and Miller's rules of interpretation; passes through the four parallel outlines of Daniel — chapters 2, 7, 8, and 9 — which re-walk the same history at deepening resolution until the 2300 days are unlocked by the seventy weeks; enters the Revelation with its churches, seals, trumpets, and the bittersweet little book; rises to the 1844 sanctuary message and the investigative judgment; and closes with the law and the Sabbath, the two covenants, the loud cry, the close of probation, Armageddon, the second coming, and the millennium. Every study is so framed that the reader can take the Bible in hand and prove each link, following the chain from the year-day rule to the cleansing of the sanctuary to the kingdom of glory.

**The historicist method.** A symbolic prophetic day stands for a literal year (Num. 14:34; Eze. 4:6). Scripture is its own expositor — every word has its bearing, and the meaning of one passage is gathered from all the rest. The Bible defines its own symbols: a beast is a kingdom (Dan. 7:17, 23), waters are peoples (Rev. 17:15), a woman is a church (2 Cor. 11:2; Jer. 6:2), a day is a year. Prophecy is history written beforehand, fulfilled to the letter, and given to be understood.

> "The Bible Handbook is not an exhaustive study; but contains suggestive texts on important lines of thought." — S. N. Haskell

# Part I — Foundation

# 1. The Year-Day Principle

> _In symbolic prophecy a day stands for a year. This is not a human convention imposed upon the text, but a rule Scripture supplies of itself (Num. 14:34; Eze. 4:6), and the pioneers — Miller, Litch, and Smith — made it the foundation of all prophetic reckoning. Every great time-line hangs upon it: the 1260 days of papal supremacy, the 2300 days to the cleansing of the sanctuary, the 70 weeks cut off for the Jews, and the 1290 and 1335. The seventy weeks (490 years) ran out to the very year in the death and rejection of Messiah, and so stand as the divine seal that the rule is true. (DAR 141, 191-192; GC 324, 326-328.)_

### The rule given in Scripture itself

- **Numbers 14:34** — "After the number of the days in which ye searched the land, even forty days, **each day for a year**, shall ye bear your iniquities, even forty years." God converts forty days of searching into forty years of wandering — the year-day measure stated in so many words. _(MWV1 21; GC 324.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "Thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: **I have appointed thee each day for a year**." A second independent witness; the prophet lies a day for every year of Judah's guilt. Two texts establish the rule "in the mouth of two witnesses." _(MWV1 21; DAR 141-142.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:5** — "I have laid upon thee the years of their iniquity, according to the number of the days, three hundred and ninety days." The 390 days for Israel are by God's own word 390 years — the principle applied a third time in the same passage. _(DAR 141.)_
- **Numbers 14:33** — "Your children shall wander in the wilderness forty years." The forty years of the sentence answer day-for-day to the forty days of unbelief, fixing the proportion God Himself uses. _(GC 324.)_

### The rule confirmed by the pioneers' own method

- **Miller's Rule X** — Miller's tenth rule of interpretation lists the figures of prophecy and their meanings: "mountains, meaning governments; beasts, meaning kingdoms; waters, meaning people . . . **Day, meaning year**," citing Ezekiel 4:6 as the proof. Symbols are explained by Scripture, and the day is one of them. _(MWV1 21.)_
- **Litch — "the Holy Ghost hath defined it"** — Litch answers the objection "how long is a time?" by letting Scripture interpret Scripture: Daniel's "time, times, and dividing of time" (Dan. 7:25), John's "forty-two months" (Rev. 13:5), and the same span reduced to "1260 days" (Rev. 11-12) are all the same period — "divine time, each day being the representative . . . of a full solar year." _(PREX1 92.)_
- **Smith on the rule** — "The rule given us in the Bible is, that when a day is used as a symbol, it stands for a year. Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 14:34." Smith grounds the whole computation of Daniel 7 on these two texts. _(DAR 141.)_
- **The day = a thousand years has its own place** — "One day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day" (2 Pet. 3:8; cf. Ps. 90:4). The day stands for differing periods according to its use; in symbolic prophecy the measure is the year. _(MWV1 21.)_

### The seven-year "week" — Scripture's own unit

- **Genesis 29:27** — "Fulfil her week, and we will give thee this also for the service which thou shalt serve with me **yet seven other years**." A "week" in the prophetic sense is seven years; Jacob's two weeks were fourteen years. This is the very key to Daniel's "seventy weeks." _(GC 326.)_
- **Genesis 29:28** — "Jacob did so, and fulfilled her week" — the week of years actually served out, establishing that a "week" can stand for seven literal years. _(GC 326.)_
- **Leviticus 25:8** — "Thou shalt number seven sabbaths of years . . . seven times seven years; and the space of the seven sabbaths of years shall be unto thee **forty and nine years**." Forty-nine years are reckoned as "seven sabbaths of years" — a week of weeks of years, the same arithmetic that turns 70 weeks into 490 years. _(GC 326-328.)_
- **Leviticus 25:9** — The jubilee trumpet sounds "in the day of atonement" of "the seventh month" — the autumn type that, with the seventh-month reckoning, fixes the close of the 2300 days in the autumn of 1844. _(GC 328, 400.)_

### The seventy weeks — the principle vindicated to the very year

- **Daniel 9:24** — "**Seventy weeks are determined** upon thy people and upon thy holy city, to finish the transgression . . . and to anoint the most Holy." The word "determined" (Heb. _nehhtak_) properly signifies **"cut off"** — and there is but one period from which 70 weeks could be cut off: the 2300 days of Daniel 8:14. _(DAR 191-192; GC 326.)_
- **Daniel 9:25** — "From the going forth of the commandment to restore and to build Jerusalem unto the Messiah the Prince shall be seven weeks, and threescore and two weeks." Sixty-nine weeks = 483 years from the decree of Artaxerxes (457 B.C.) reach to A.D. 27 and the anointing of Christ at His baptism. _(DAR 191; GC 326-327.)_
- **Daniel 9:26** — "After threescore and two weeks shall Messiah be cut off, but not for himself." In the midst of the seventieth week, A.D. 31, Messiah was crucified — exactly where 483 years from 457 B.C. place it. _(GC 327-328.)_
- **Daniel 9:27** — "He shall confirm the covenant with many for one week: and in the midst of the week he shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease." The final week (A.D. 27-34) and the cessation of the sacrifices at the cross fall to the year, proving the year-day measure. _(GC 327-328.)_
- **"Cut off" — the textual demonstration** — "The word here rendered determined signifies 'cut off;' and there is no period from which the seventy weeks could be cut off but the 2300 days." The 490 years are a part of the 2300; both begin together at 457 B.C. _(DAR 192.)_
- **The seventy weeks ran out in A.D. 34** — "The seventy weeks, or 490 years, especially allotted to the Jews, ended . . . in A.D. 34," when the nation sealed its rejection of the gospel. The whole 490-year span fulfilled to the year is the divine seal upon day-for-a-year. _(GC 328; MWV1 43.)_

### The great time-periods that hang upon it

- **Daniel 8:14** — "Unto **two thousand and three hundred days**; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." Reckoned as years from 457 B.C., the 2300 reach to 1844. The 490 years (A.D. 34) being cut off from the 2300 leave 1810 years, which from A.D. 34 extend to 1844. _(GC 324, 326-328.)_
- **Daniel 7:25** — "And they shall be given into his hand until **a time and times and the dividing of time**." Three and a half prophetic years = 1260 days = 1260 years of papal supremacy, 538 to 1798. _(DAR 141; PREX1 92.)_
- **Revelation 13:5** — "Power was given unto him to continue **forty and two months**." Forty-two months of thirty days = 1260 days = 1260 years — the same span as Daniel's "time, times, and a half," proving the two prophets describe one power. _(DAR 524; PREX1 92.)_
- **Revelation 12:6** — "That they should feed her there **a thousand two hundred and threescore days**." John states the very number — 1260 — leaving no room to doubt the length of the period. _(DAR 524; PREX1 92.)_
- **Revelation 12:14** — "Where she is nourished for **a time, and times, and half a time**." The identical period of Daniel 7:25, set beside the plain "1260 days" of verse 6, so that John interprets himself. _(DAR 141; PREX1 92.)_
- **Revelation 11:2-3** — The holy city trodden "forty and two months," and the two witnesses prophesy "a thousand two hundred and threescore days." Months and days reduced to one figure, 1260 — Scripture's own conversion that "the Holy Ghost hath defined." _(PREX1 92.)_
- **Daniel 12:7** — "It shall be for **a time, times, and an half**." The same 1260-year period for the scattering of the holy people, closing in 1798. _(DAR 141, 314.)_
- **Daniel 12:11-12** — "From the time that the daily shall be taken away . . . a thousand two hundred and **ninety days**," and "blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and **five and thirty days**." The 1290 (508-1798) and 1335 (508-1843/44) — both reckoned as years on the same rule. _(DAR 314-316.)_

### The proportion summarized

| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------- |
| 70 weeks (Dan. 9:24-27)                                                 | 490 years  | 457 B.C. → A.D. 34   |

_The seventy weeks, fulfilled to the year in the first advent, are the proof; the rest of the prophetic time stands or falls with them. (DAR 192; GC 328.)_

# 2. Miller's Rules of Interpretation

> _The historicist position is that prophecy is not a sealed riddle for the few, but a "more sure word" lighted for the whole church. Scripture is its own expositor: every word has its bearing, the Bible defines its own symbols, the prophetic day stands for a literal year, and prophecy is history written beforehand. William Miller, laying aside every commentary and comparing Scripture with Scripture, recovered the very method by which Daniel and the Revelation are unlocked — the hermeneutic that founds this whole chain. (SLWM 48-51; GC 320-324; GC 343-344.)_

### The sure word: prophecy is given to be understood

- **2 Peter 1:19** — "We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place." Prophecy is a lamp for the present darkness, not an obscurity — heeding it is commended. _(SLWM 49.1, cited under Miller's rule 4; GC 324.2.)_
- **2 Peter 1:20** — "No prophecy of the scripture is of any private interpretation." No text may be wrested to a man's private notion; it must be read in harmony with all Scripture. The foundation text against guessing. _(SLWM 49.1-49.2.)_
- **2 Peter 1:21** — "Holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost." Because one Spirit authored the whole, the whole interprets itself; Miller held the chronological portions "as much a portion of the word of God" as any other. _(GC 324.2.)_
- **Revelation 1:1-3** — Christ gave the Revelation "to shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass," and pronounces blessed "he that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy." A book meant to be read and understood, with a blessing attached. _(GC 320.2.)_
- **Daniel 12:4** — "Shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end: many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased." Sealed only until the time of the end — then the very book invites study and its knowledge increases. _(GC 356-357; SLWM 48.1.)_
- **Daniel 12:9-10** — "The words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end... the wise shall understand." Understanding is promised to the wise in the last days; the prophecy is not forever locked. _(GC 356.)_

### Scripture is its own expositor — compare Scripture with Scripture

- **Isaiah 28:9-10** — "Whom shall he teach knowledge?... precept upon precept; line upon line; here a little, and there a little." Doctrine is built by gathering every passage on a subject and laying line beside line — Miller's working method. _(SLWM 49.1, cited under rule 4.)_
- **Isaiah 28:13** — "But the word of the LORD was unto them precept upon precept... that they might go, and fall backward." The same line-upon-line method that builds up the believing also exposes the careless who will not gather the whole. _(SLWM 49.1.)_
- **1 Corinthians 2:13** — "Comparing spiritual things with spiritual." The Spirit's own rule: explain spiritual things by other spiritual things — Scripture by Scripture, not by man's wisdom. _(SLWM 49.2, cited under rule 5.)_
- **Isaiah 34:16** — "Seek ye out of the book of the LORD, and read: no one of these shall fail, none shall want her mate." Every prophecy has its "mate" elsewhere in the book; search them out and the matched testimony is complete. _(GC 324.3-325.)_
- **John 5:39** — "Search the scriptures... they are they which testify of me." The command to search, with Christ as the unifying subject of all Scripture. _(SLWM 50.1.)_
- **Luke 24:27** — "Beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself." Christ Himself taught by surveying the whole of Scripture — the pattern of comparing text with text. _(SLWM 49.1, cited under rule 4.)_
- **Proverbs 1:5-6** — "A man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels: to understand a proverb, and the interpretation... the words of the wise, and their dark sayings." The dark sayings of prophecy are meant to be understood by the diligent. _(SLWM 48.1.)_

### Every word necessary; nothing hidden from faith

- **2 Timothy 3:16-17** — "All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine... that the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works." Therefore the prophetic and chronological portions are profitable and not to be skipped. Miller leaned on this directly. _(SLWM 48.3, rule 2; GC 324.2.)_
- **Deuteronomy 29:29** — "The secret things belong unto the LORD our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us." What God has revealed is ours to study — including the appointed times. _(GC 324.1; SLWM 48.4, rule 3.)_
- **Amos 3:7** — "Surely the Lord GOD will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets." God acts only after disclosing His purpose; prophecy is His advance notice to His servants. _(GC 324.1.)_
- **James 1:5** — "If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God... and it shall be given him." The prerequisite to understanding is asking in faith, not human cleverness. _(SLWM 48.4, rule 3.)_

### Prophecy is history written beforehand

- **Isaiah 46:9-10** — "I am God, and there is none like me... declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done." God's signature is foretelling the future — so fulfilled prophecy becomes the criterion for reading what is still future. _(GC 343.3-344.)_
- **Daniel 2:28** — "There is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets, and maketh known to the king Nebuchadnezzar what shall be in the latter days." History, from Babylon to the kingdom of God, was sketched in outline before it happened. _(SLWM 48.1.)_
- **Daniel 2:44** — "In the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed... it shall stand for ever." The chart of empires ends in a fixed, literal event — proof that prophecy is history beforehand and is to be literally fulfilled. _(SLWM 50.4, rule 11; GC 321.3.)_
- **Habakkuk 2:2-3** — "Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables... for the vision is yet for an appointed time... though it tarry, wait for it." Prophecy runs to an appointed time and is to be made plain — Miller's warrant that fulfillment, not lacking even one word, marks the true event. _(SLWM 50.5, rule 13.)_

### The Holy Spirit, not man's wisdom, the true guide

- **John 16:13** — "When he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth... and he will shew you things to come." The same Spirit that authored prophecy guides into its meaning; the interpreter's dependence is on Him, not on a sectarian creed. _(SLWM 49.2, rule 5.)_
- **1 Corinthians 2:13** — "Not in the words which man's wisdom teacheth, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth." (See above.) The express warrant against leaning on human expositors who "guess at its meaning." _(SLWM 49.2, rule 5.)_

### The Bible defines its own symbols

The decisive rule: where a figure is used, trace the word through the Bible until you find it explained, then substitute the explanation. Miller's own proof texts:

| Symbol   | Bible meaning             | Defining text                                                                                                                       |
| -------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mountain | government / kingdom      | **Daniel 2:35, 44** — the stone becomes "a great mountain, and filled the whole earth"; "the God of heaven shall set up a kingdom." |
| Beast    | kingdom                   | **Daniel 7:17, 23** — "These great beasts... are four kings"; "The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon earth."            |
| Waters   | peoples, nations, tongues | **Revelation 17:15** — "The waters which thou sawest... are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues."                     |
| Woman    | a church / city (Zion)    | **Revelation 12:1; Jeremiah 6:2** — "a woman clothed with the sun"; "I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely... woman."     |
| Day      | a year                    | **Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 14:34** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year"; "each day for a year."                                |

_(SLWM 49.5-50.1, rules 8-9, 12; GC 324.3.)_

- **Daniel 7:17, 23** — Heaven's own dictionary: the four beasts "are four kings," the fourth beast "the fourth kingdom upon earth." When the Bible defines its symbol, the guessing stops. _(SLWM 49.5, rule 8.)_
- **Revelation 17:15** — Waters defined as "peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues." The interpreter does not invent the meaning; he reads it off the page. _(SLWM 49.5, rule 8.)_
- **Daniel 2:35, 44** — A mountain that "filled the whole earth" is interpreted in the same chapter as a kingdom God sets up. Symbol and definition stand together. _(SLWM 49.5, rule 8.)_

### Day for a year — the key to the time-prophecies

- **Numbers 14:34** — "After the number of the days... forty days, each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities." The first statement of the year-day measure. _(GC 324.3; SLWM 49.5.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year." The principle repeated and made a rule — so the 2300 days, the 1260, the 70 weeks, the 1290 and 1335 are years. _(GC 324.3; SLWM 49.5, rule 8.)_
- **Daniel 8:14** — (Applying the rule) "Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." Read by the year-day measure, the 2300 days reach far beyond the Jewish age — the prophecy that "most clearly" revealed the time. _(GC 324.3-325.)_

### When the literal stands, take it literally

- **Daniel 2:44** — A plainly literal kingdom "which shall never be destroyed." Where the words make good sense and do no violence to nature, they are to be understood literally. _(SLWM 50.4, rule 11.)_
- **Matthew 24:15** — "When ye therefore shall see the abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet... (whoso readeth, let him understand)." Christ sends the reader back to Daniel and commands him to understand — endorsing the study of the prophet by the same plain method. _(GC 234-235; SLWM 50.1.)_

### Miller's fourteen rules, in brief

Miller's own published rules of interpretation, each grounded on Scripture:

1. **Every word must have its proper bearing** on the subject. _(Matt. 5:18.)_
2. **All Scripture is necessary** and may be understood by diligent study. _(2 Tim. 3:15-17.)_
3. **Nothing revealed will be hid** from those who ask in faith. _(Deut. 29:29; James 1:5-6.)_
4. **Bring all Scriptures together** on a subject; if the theory holds without contradiction, you cannot be in error. _(Isa. 28; Luke 24:27; 2 Peter 1:19-20.)_
5. **Scripture must be its own expositor**, being a rule of itself. _(1 Cor. 2:12-16.)_
6. **God reveals things to come by figures and parables**, the same thing oftentimes given again and again; combine them in one. _(Hab. 2:2; Dan. 2, 7, 8.)_
7. **Visions are always mentioned as such.** _(2 Cor. 12:1.)_
8. **Figures always have a figurative meaning** — mountains = governments; beasts = kingdoms; waters = peoples; day = year. _(Dan. 2:35,44; 7:8,17; Rev. 17:1,15; Eze. 4:6.)_
9. **Parables illustrate** and are explained the same way as figures. _(Mark 4:13.)_
10. **A figure may have two or more significations** — "day" is indefinite, a day-for-a-year, or a day-for-a-thousand-years; the right reading harmonizes with the Bible. _(Eccl. 7:14; Eze. 4:6; 2 Peter 3:8.)_
11. **If a word makes good sense as it stands, take it literally**; if not, figuratively. _(Rev. 12:1-2; 17:3-7.)_
12. **Trace a figure through the Bible**; when you find it explained, substitute the explanation. _(Miller's method, GC 320.1.)_
13. **A prophecy is fulfilled when every word is literally met**; if one word lacks fulfillment, look for another event. _(Ps. 22:5; Rev. 17:17.)_
14. **The most important rule is faith** — a faith willing to sacrifice the dearest object on earth rather than disbelieve God's word.

_(SLWM 48.2-51.1; GC 320.1-320.2.)_

# Part II — The Outlines of Daniel

# 3. Daniel 2 — The Great Image: Kingdoms Throughout Time

> _In the second year of his reign, the heathen monarch of Babylon dreamed a dream that God had Daniel read back to him and interpret: a great metal man whose head of gold, breast of silver, belly of brass, legs of iron, and feet of iron-and-clay outline the whole course of world empire from Babylon to the end. God Himself names the rule of each metal — a metal is a kingdom — and the prophecy closes when a stone "cut out without hands" smites the image on its feet, grinds the kingdoms to chaff, and becomes a mountain that fills the whole earth. That stone is the everlasting kingdom of Christ. This is not a lesson about Nebuchadnezzar's pride; it is the master-outline of history, from gold to glory. (DAR 42-77; PK 547-548; GC 341.)_

### God's own rule of interpretation — a metal is a kingdom

- **Daniel 2:28** — "There is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets, and maketh known to the king Nebuchadnezzar what shall be in the latter days." The dream is no riddle of arrogance but a divine outline of "what shall be" — prophecy, given to the captive's God, not the king's wise men. _(DAR 42-43.)_
- **Daniel 2:31-33** — A great image: head of fine gold, breast and arms of silver, belly and thighs of brass, legs of iron, feet part iron and part clay. Descending metals of descending worth picture the descending course of "earthly pomp and glory." _(DAR 41-42.)_
- **Daniel 2:38** — "Thou art this head of gold." God then says "after thee shall arise another kingdom" — proving each metal is not a man but a successive kingdom; the king is put for his kingdom. _(DAR 52; GEP 15.)_
- **Daniel 2:39-40** — "After thee shall arise another kingdom inferior to thee, and another third kingdom of brass... and the fourth kingdom shall be strong as iron." Four metals, four kingdoms, named by God in order — the interpretation is not left to man. _(DAR 56; MWV2 43.)_
- **Daniel 7:17** — Scripture's own key: "These great beasts, which are four, are four kings" — i.e., kingdoms (cf. Dan. 7:23). The parallel vision proves the symbols of Daniel 2 are kingdoms, not abstractions. _(DAR 62.)_
- **Daniel 7:23** — "The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon earth." The four beasts of Daniel 7 run exactly parallel to the four metals of Daniel 2, fixing the count and order beyond dispute. _(DAR 62.)_

### The named kingdoms, in order, with metal and date

| Head of **gold**             | Babylon                     | 606–538 B.C.        | Dan. 2:38        |

- **Daniel 2:38** — **Babylon, the head of gold.** "Thou art this head of gold" — the golden kingdom of a golden age, founded by Nimrod, that took God's people captive and so entered the field of prophecy. _(DAR 43, 46.)_
- **Daniel 2:39a** — **Medo-Persia, the silver breast and arms.** "Another kingdom inferior to thee" — inferior not in power or extent but in wealth and magnificence. The two arms answer to the two-fold Medo-Persian power. _(DAR 52-53.)_
- **Daniel 5:28** — "Thy kingdom is divided, and given to the Medes and Persians." The handwriting on Belshazzar's wall fixes the silver kingdom by name the very night Babylon fell. _(DAR 50.)_
- **Daniel 5:30-31** — "In that night was Belshazzar the king of the Chaldeans slain. And Darius the Median took the kingdom" — the gold passes to the silver, B.C. 538, exactly as the image foretold. _(DAR 50-51.)_
- **Daniel 8:20** — "The ram which thou sawest having two horns are the kings of Media and Persia" — Scripture itself names the second kingdom, confirming the silver. _(DAR 52; GEP 34.)_
- **Daniel 2:39b** — **Greece, the brass.** "A third kingdom of brass, which shall bear rule over all the earth" — fulfilled when Alexander, one to twenty against Persia at Arbela (B.C. 331), became absolute lord of the East. _(DAR 54.)_
- **Daniel 8:21** — "The rough goat is the king of Grecia: and the great horn... is the first king" — God names the brass kingdom and its first king, Alexander. _(DAR 54; GEP 34.)_
- **Daniel 8:22** — "Four kingdoms shall stand up out of the nation, but not in his power" — Alexander's empire divided among his four generals, still the brass kingdom, not yet the iron. _(DAR 56.)_
- **Daniel 2:40** — **Imperial Rome, the iron.** "The fourth kingdom shall be strong as iron... it shall break in pieces and bruise." Rome's iron monarchy successively broke the images of gold, silver, and brass and "filled the world." _(DAR 56-57.)_
- **Luke 2:1** — "There went out a decree from Caesar Augustus, that all the world should be taxed." The iron kingdom is no guess: it is the Rome of the Gospels, ruling the world at Christ's birth. _(DAR 57.)_

### The ten toes — divided Rome that never reunites (the iron-and-clay church-state lesson)

- **Daniel 2:41** — "The kingdom shall be divided; but there shall be in it of the strength of the iron." The iron does not vanish — Rome breaks into pieces that still carry Rome's iron in them. _(DAR 57.)_
- **Daniel 2:42** — "As the toes of the feet were part of iron, and part of clay, so the kingdom shall be partly strong, and partly broken." The ten toes are the ten kingdoms into which Western Rome divided, A.D. 351–476 — the same as the ten horns of Daniel 7. _(DAR 57-58, 62-63.)_
- **Daniel 2:43** — "They shall mingle themselves with the seed of men: but they shall not cleave one to another, even as iron is not mixed with clay." Where war failed, the kings tried marriage and diplomacy to reunite Europe into one empire — and failed. From Canute to Napoleon, every attempt has broken on this word: _they shall not cleave_. _(DAR 67-68.)_
- **Daniel 7:17, 23** — The fourth beast and its ten horns confirm the toes: ten contemporaneous kingdoms, never again consolidated under one universal monarch. No fifth world-empire of man will ever arise. _(DAR 62, 66.)_

### The stone cut without hands — the kingdom of God set up in the days of these kings

- **Daniel 2:34-35** — "A stone was cut out without hands, which smote the image upon his feet... and brake them to pieces... and the stone... became a great mountain, and filled the whole earth." The smiting is violent and immediate; the kingdoms become chaff blown away, "no place... found for them." This is no peaceful spread of the gospel at the first advent — it is the sudden end of human empire. _(DAR 61, 70.)_
- **Daniel 2:44** — "In the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed... it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand for ever." The fifth kingdom is set up not at Christ's first advent but after Rome's tenfold division — "in the days of these kings," contemporaneous with the toes still standing. _(DAR 68-70.)_
- **Daniel 2:45** — "The stone was cut out of the mountain without hands... the great God hath made known to the king what shall come to pass... and the dream is certain, and the interpretation thereof sure." "Without hands" — no human agency; the Lord does it by His own divine power. The chain of prophecy is sealed as certain. _(DAR 70.)_
- **Revelation 11:15** — "The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ; and he shall reign for ever and ever." Revelation echoes Daniel 2:44 — the stone-kingdom is Christ's everlasting reign that succeeds, not coexists with, the empires of men. _(DAR 68; GC 341.)_
- **Isaiah 28:16** — "Behold, I lay in Zion for a foundation a stone, a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation." The stone of Daniel 2 is the same Stone of prophecy — Christ Himself, the sure foundation of His kingdom.
- **Psalm 118:22** — "The stone which the builders refused is become the head stone of the corner." The rejected Stone becomes the Head — Christ, despised at His first coming, supreme at His second.
- **Luke 20:17-18** — "The stone which the builders rejected... Whosoever shall fall upon that stone shall be broken; but on whomsoever it shall fall, it will grind him to powder." Christ applies the Stone to Himself; on the kingdoms of earth it falls and grinds them to chaff, exactly as Daniel 2:35 declares.

### The certainty and the lesson

- **Daniel 2:29-30** — "He that revealeth secrets maketh known to thee what shall come to pass... this secret is not revealed to me for any wisdom that I have." The outline is God's, not Daniel's — therefore "the dream is certain, and the interpretation thereof sure" (v. 45). _(DAR 42.)_
- **Daniel 2:36-37** — "We will tell the interpretation... Thou, O king, art a king of kings: for the God of heaven hath given thee a kingdom." God who gives kingdoms also takes them away; the rise and fall of these empires is His doing, not chance. _(PK 547-548.)_
- **Summary of the chain** — From the head of gold to the stone, four universal empires only — Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Rome — then division, then the kingdom of God; no fifth world-empire of man, and no reunion of the toes. The whole of history laid open in eight verses, "from a given period to the end of this world." _(MWV2 43; LJL 30-31; GEP 15, 34-35; PK 548.)_

# 4. Daniel 7 — The Four Beasts, the Little Horn, and the Judgment

> _Daniel's night-vision repeats the image of chapter 2 at higher resolution: four
> wild beasts rise from the wind-tossed sea — lion (Babylon), bear (Medo-Persia),
> leopard (Greece), and a dreadful nameless beast (Rome) with ten horns. Among them
> a little horn rises, plucks up three, blasphemes the Most High, wears out the
> saints, thinks to change times and laws, and reigns 1260 years (538-1798). Then
> the scene shifts upward: the Ancient of Days takes the throne of judgment, the
> books are opened, and one like the Son of man is brought before Him to receive the
> kingdom. The same Papacy is met again in Daniel 8, 2 Thessalonians 2, and
> Revelation 13. (DAR 113-144; GC 439, 446.)_

### The four winds, the great sea, the four beasts

- **Daniel 7:2-3** — "The four winds of the heaven strove upon the great sea... and four great beasts came up from the sea, diverse one from another." Winds = strife and war (Jer. 49:36-37); the sea = "peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues" (Rev. 17:15). Out of the political turmoil of nations the four world-empires arise. _(DAR 113-114.)_
- **Daniel 7:17** — "These great beasts, which are four, are four kings, which shall arise out of the earth." The angel's own key: each beast is a kingdom — the same four metals of Daniel 2 under living symbols. _(DAR 113-114.)_
- **Daniel 7:18** — "But the saints of the most High shall take the kingdom... for ever, even for ever and ever." The vision's goal is not another earthly empire but the everlasting kingdom of the saints — the fifth kingdom of Daniel 2's stone. _(DAR 125.)_

### Beast one — the lion: Babylon

- **Daniel 7:4** — "The first was like a lion, and had eagle's wings... the wings thereof were plucked... and a man's heart was given to it." The lion with eagle's wings = Babylon, the head of gold; the wings denote the rapidity of Nebuchadnezzar's conquests, and their plucking and the "man's heart" picture the kingdom's decline and Nebuchadnezzar's humbling (Dan. 4). _(DAR 115.1.)_

### Beast two — the bear: Medo-Persia

- **Daniel 7:5** — "Another beast, a second, like to a bear, and it raised up itself on one side, and it had three ribs in the mouth of it... Arise, devour much flesh." The bear = Medo-Persia; it raised itself "on one side" because the Persian division came up last but rose higher — the same fact as the higher horn of the ram (Dan. 8:3). The three ribs = Babylon, Lydia, and Egypt, ground down by this power (B.C. 538-331). _(DAR 115.3.)_

### Beast three — the leopard: Greece

- **Daniel 7:6** — "Another, like a leopard, which had upon the back of it four wings of a fowl; the beast had also four heads; and dominion was given to it." The four-winged leopard = Grecia; the wings denote the lightning speed of Alexander's marches, and the four heads the fourfold division of his empire among his generals after his death — exactly fulfilled and no more, for "the prophecy permitted no more." _(DAR 116-117.)_

### Beast four — dreadful and terrible: Rome, with ten horns

- **Daniel 7:7** — "A fourth beast, dreadful and terrible, and strong exceedingly; and it had great iron teeth: it devoured and brake in pieces... and it had ten horns." No beast in nature could symbolize it; the iron teeth answer to the iron legs of Daniel 2. This is Rome — diverse from all before it, treading down the whole earth. _(DAR 117.5-118.1; DAR 64.)_
- **Daniel 7:23** — "The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon earth... shall devour the whole earth, and shall tread it down, and break it in pieces." The angel names it plainly as the fourth and last of the world-empires — Rome. _(DAR 126.)_
- **Daniel 7:24** — "The ten horns out of this kingdom are ten kings that shall arise." The ten horns = the ten kingdoms into which Western Rome was broken between A.D. 351 and 483 (the same as the ten toes of Dan. 2): Alemanni, Franks, Burgundians, Suevi, Vandals, Visigoths, Anglo-Saxons, Heruli, Lombards, and Ostrogoths. _(DAR 125.2; DAR 126.)_
- **Daniel 2:40** — "The fourth kingdom shall be strong as iron... shall it break in pieces and bruise." The cross-link: the iron of the image and the iron-toothed beast are one and the same Rome. _(DAR 64.)_

### The little horn — the Papacy

- **Daniel 7:8** — "Among them another little horn, before whom there were three of the first horns plucked up by the roots: and... in this horn were eyes like the eyes of man, and a mouth speaking great things." The little horn rises among the ten — a power within Rome, not outside it: the Papacy. The eyes = the shrewdness and foresight of the papal hierarchy; the mouth speaking great things = the arrogant claims of the bishops of Rome. _(DAR 118.3-119.1.)_
- **Daniel 7:20** — "That horn... whose look was more stout than his fellows." Though "little" at first, it grew until it overtopped the kingdoms around it — a power both ecclesiastical and political, diverse from the secular horns. _(DAR 118.4; DAR 126.3.)_
- **Daniel 7:24 (b)** — "He shall be diverse from the first, and he shall subdue three kings." The three horns plucked up to make room for the Papacy were the three Arian powers that stood in its way: the **Heruli (A.D. 493), the Vandals (534), and the Ostrogoths (538).** _(DAR 129.2; DAR 136.2.)_
- **Daniel 7:8** (the three plucked) — "before whom there were three of the first horns plucked up by the roots." The clearing of the Ostrogoths from Rome in 538 left the bishop of Rome supreme — the very year the 1260 years begin. _(DAR 136.2; DAR 141.1.)_

### The marks of the little horn — blasphemy, persecution, changing the law

- **Daniel 7:25** — "He shall speak great words against the most High, and shall wear out the saints of the most High, and think to change times and laws: and they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time." Four marks: (1) blasphemy against God, (2) persecution of the saints, (3) presuming to change God's times and law, (4) a reign of 1260 years. _(DAR 138-141.)_
- **Daniel 7:21** — "The same horn made war with the saints, and prevailed against them." The persecuting power: fifty million martyrs — the lowest historian's estimate — answer that this war was indeed waged, from the Waldenses to the Inquisition. _(DAR 125.3; DAR 140.1.)_
- **Daniel 7:25 (c)** — "think to change times and laws." Not human laws (which it could change), but the laws of the Most High, which it could only _think_ to change: it expunged the second commandment, divided the tenth, and tore the Sabbath of Jehovah from the fourth commandment, erecting Sunday in its place. _(DAR 140.3; GC 446.1-2.)_
- **2 Thessalonians 2:3-4** — "That man of sin... the son of perdition; who opposeth and exalteth himself above all that is called God... so that he as God sitteth in the temple of God." Paul's "man of sin" is the same little horn — a power within the professed church, exalting itself above God. _(GC 446.1.)_
- **2 Thessalonians 2:7-8** — "The mystery of iniquity doth already work: only he who now letteth will let, until he be taken out of the way." The "let" (hindrance) was pagan imperial Rome; when it was "taken out of the way," the man of sin was revealed — and at last the Lord "shall consume" him "with the brightness of his coming." _(GC 439.)_
- **Daniel 8:9** — "Out of one of them came forth a little horn, which waxed exceeding great, toward the south, and toward the east, and toward the pleasant land." The little horn of Daniel 8, rising after Greece's four divisions and growing exceeding great, is the same Rome/Papacy that "magnified himself even to the prince of the host" (8:11). _(DAR 145+.)_
- **Revelation 13:1-2** — "A beast rise up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns... like unto a leopard, and his feet were as the feet of a bear, and his mouth as the mouth of a lion: and the dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority." John's beast combines all of Daniel's beasts in one — the Papacy, heir to the power, seat, and authority of pagan Rome. _(GC 439.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:5-7** — "There was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies; and power... to continue forty and two months... And it was given unto him to make war with the saints, and to overcome them." The same blasphemy, the same war on the saints, the same prophetic period as Daniel 7:25. _(GC 439.1-2.)_

### The 1260 years — 538 to 1798

- **Daniel 7:25 (d)** — "a time and times and the dividing of time" = 3½ prophetic years = 1260 prophetic days. _(DAR 141.1.)_
- **Numbers 14:34** — "each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities." The day-year principle, stated plainly. _(DAR 141.1.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year." The second witness to the day-for-a-year rule of prophetic time. _(DAR 141.1.)_
- **Revelation 12:6** — "the woman fled into the wilderness... that they should feed her there a thousand two hundred and threescore days." The same 1260 of the church in the wilderness under papal supremacy. _(DAR 141.)_
- **Revelation 12:14** — "a time, and times, and half a time" — the identical phrase as Daniel 7:25, fixing the three expressions (1260 days = 42 months = a time, times, and half a time) as one period. _(DAR 141.1.)_
- **Revelation 11:2-3** — "forty and two months... a thousand two hundred and threescore days." Forty-two months of thirty days = 1260 days — Scripture's own arithmetic equating the periods. _(DAR 141.1.)_
- **Daniel 7:25 fulfilled** — Justinian's edict (533) made the bishop of Rome head of all the churches, but took effect only when the Ostrogoths were driven from Rome in **538**. Counting forward: **538 + 1260 = 1798**, when Berthier with a French army entered Rome, proclaimed a republic, and took the pope prisoner — the deadly wound. _(DAR 141.1; GC 439.2.)_

### The judgment scene — the Ancient of Days and the books

- **Daniel 7:9-10** — "The thrones were cast down [set up], and the Ancient of days did sit, whose garment was white as snow... the judgment was set, and the books were opened." The Ancient of Days = God the Father, a personal Being, taking the throne of judgment; "thrones were cast down" should read _set up_. The opened books bring the investigative judgment to view. _(DAR 119.2-4; DAR 120.1.)_
- **Daniel 7:10 (b)** — "thousand thousands ministered unto him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him." The heavenly court convenes — a scene that, by Daniel 8:14, is "even now transpiring in the sanctuary above." _(DAR 121.1.)_
- **Daniel 8:14** — "Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." The judgment of Daniel 7 is the cleansing of the heavenly sanctuary, which opened in 1844 at the close of the 2300 days. _(DAR 121.1.)_
- **Daniel 7:11-12** — "I beheld even till the beast was slain, and his body destroyed, and given to the burning flame. As concerning the rest of the beasts, they had their dominion taken away; yet their lives were prolonged for a season." The fourth beast persists, blaspheming, until destroyed by fire at the end — unlike the earlier beasts, whose peoples lived on after their dominion passed. _(DAR 121.2-5.)_
- **Daniel 7:13-14** — "One like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him. And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom." This is **not** the second advent to earth, for it is a coming _to_ the Ancient of Days in the heavenly temple. The Son of man receives His kingdom _before_ His return — at the close of His priestly work, the marriage of the Lamb. _(DAR 122.2-3.)_
- **Matthew 22:11-13** — "the king came in to see the guests" — the inspection of the wedding company for the wedding garment pictures the investigative judgment that examines every professed believer's record. _(Parallel; cf. DAR 122.)_
- **Revelation 14:7** — "Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come." The first angel's message announces that the very judgment of Daniel 7:9-10 has begun — the message of 1844. _(GC 439; DAR 121.1.)_
- **Acts 1:11** — "this same Jesus... shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go." The coming of Daniel 7:13 (to the Father) and the coming of Acts 1:11 (to the earth) are distinct events — the first inaugurates the judgment, the second executes it. _(DAR 122.3.)_

### The verdict — the saints possess the kingdom

- **Daniel 7:22** — "Until the Ancient of days came, and judgment was given to the saints of the most High; and the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom." Three events in order: the Ancient of Days comes to judgment, the verdict is rendered in the saints' favor, and they receive the everlasting kingdom. _(DAR 125.4.)_
- **Daniel 7:26** — "But the judgment shall sit, and they shall take away his dominion, to consume and to destroy it unto the end." The same judgment that vindicates the saints strips the little horn of its dominion and consumes it forever. _(DAR 121.2-5.)_
- **Daniel 7:27** — "the kingdom... under the whole heaven, shall be given to the people of the saints of the most High, whose kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions shall serve and obey him." The vision closes where Daniel 2 closed — with the eternal kingdom of God filling the earth, after every beast and horn has passed. _(DAR 125.)_

# 5. Daniel 8 — The Ram, the He-Goat, the Little Horn, and the Sanctuary

> _Daniel 8 retraces the same march of empires as chapters 2 and 7, but begins one
> kingdom later — dropping Babylon (already falling) and opening with Medo-Persia. Here
> the angel Gabriel names the powers outright: the ram is Media and Persia, the he-goat
> is Grecia, its great horn the first king. The "little horn" that follows is Rome in both
> its phases, pagan and papal — never Antiochus — and "the daily" together with "the
> transgression of desolation" fills the long 2300 days that reach to the cleansing of the
> sanctuary in 1844. The angel's own interpretation fixes the meaning beyond dispute.
> (DAR 145-182; TTHDS 1-2; GC 328.)_

### The setting and the day-for-a-year key

- **Daniel 8:1-2** — In Belshazzar's third year Daniel sees himself "at Shushan in the palace... by the river of Ulai." Shushan was the metropolis of Elam, then held by Babylon, where the king had a royal palace; soon it passed to the Medes and Persians — the very empire about to be unveiled. _(DAR 146.)_
- **Numbers 14:34** — "each day for a year" — the wilderness sentence makes a day stand for a year, the rule by which the 2300 days are read as 2300 years. _(DAR 178; GC 328.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year" — the second witness establishing the year-day principle for all the time-prophecies of Daniel and Revelation. _(DAR 178.)_

### The ram — Medo-Persia (named by the angel)

- **Daniel 8:3-4** — A ram with two unequal horns, "the higher came up last," pushing westward, northward, and southward, doing "according to his will." The two horns are the two nationalities of the empire; the higher-and-later horn is Persia, which rose after Media and overshadowed it. _(DAR 146; DAR 180.)_
- **Daniel 8:20** — "The ram which thou sawest having two horns are the kings of Media and Persia." The angel names it plainly; no conjecture is permitted. As the disciples said, "now speakest thou plainly, and speakest no proverb." _(DAR 180.)_

### The he-goat — Grecia, and its notable horn (Alexander)

- **Daniel 8:5** — "an he goat came from the west... and touched not the ground: and the goat had a notable horn between his eyes." Grecia lay west of Persia; the goat that scarcely touches the ground pictures the marvelous swiftness of the Macedonian conquest — the same speed shown by the four wings of the leopard in chapter 7. _(DAR 147-148.)_
- **Daniel 7:6** — The third beast, "like a leopard, which had upon the back of it four wings of a fowl... and four heads" — the parallel symbol of Greece: swift (four wings) and four-fold after Alexander (four heads), confirming the goat. _(DAR 148.)_
- **Daniel 8:6-7** — The goat runs at the ram "in the fury of his power," breaks his two horns, casts him down, and stamps upon him — the overthrow of Persia by Alexander at the Granicus, Issus, and Arbela (B.C. 331), with none to deliver. _(DAR 148-149.)_
- **Daniel 8:21** — "the rough goat is the king of Grecia: and the great horn that is between his eyes is the first king." That first king was Alexander the Great. _(DAR 148; DAR 180.)_
- **Daniel 2:39** — "another third kingdom of brass, which shall bear rule over all the earth" — the same Grecia of the image, here the goat that swept "on the face of the whole earth." _(DAR 153.)_

### The four horns — the division of Greece

- **Daniel 8:8** — "the great horn was broken; and for it came up four notable ones toward the four winds of heaven." Alexander fell in the prime of his strength; his empire shattered and consolidated into four divisions. _(DAR 149-150.)_
- **Daniel 8:22** — "four kingdoms shall stand up out of the nation, but not in his power." The fourfold division — none equal to the original kingdom; the angel's own commentary on the four horns. _(DAR 180-181.)_

### The little horn — Rome, not Antiochus

- **Daniel 8:9** — "out of one of them came forth a little horn, which waxed exceeding great, toward the south, and toward the east, and toward the pleasant land." This power "exceeding great" must surpass both Persia ("great") and Grecia ("very great"); Antiochus Epiphanes — one of twenty-six Syrian kings, who paid tribute to Rome and was evacuated from Egypt at Rome's command — cannot fit. The power that exceeds them all is Rome. _(DAR 151-153.)_

# 6. Daniel 9 — The Seventy Weeks Unlock the 2300 Days (The Keystone)

> _This is the keystone of the whole historicist arch. In Daniel 8 the angel Gabriel
> explained ram, he-goat, and little horn, but left Daniel fainting and the 2300 days

> understanding" and finishes the unfinished vision. Seventy weeks — 490 years — are
> "cut off" (determined) from the 2300, dating from the decree to restore Jerusalem
> (Artaxerxes, 457 B.C.). Those 490 years measure off the Messiah to the day: 69 weeks
> (483 yrs) to His anointing at baptism (A.D. 27); cut off in the midst of the last week
> (A.D. 31 crucifixion); the 70 weeks closing A.D. 34 when the gospel turns to the

> autumn of 1844. Locate the seventy weeks and the 2300 days are surely located.

### Gabriel returns to finish the unexplained vision of Daniel 8

- **Daniel 8:14** — "Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." The longest time-prophecy in Scripture — but in chapter 8 it is left without a starting date. _(DAR 192; GC 326.)_
- **Daniel 8:26-27** — The vision of the evening and morning "is true," yet shut up "for many days"; Daniel "fainted, and was sick," and "none understood it." The 2300-day number was given but its application was withheld — the loose end chapter 9 ties. _(DAR 211; GC 325-326.)_
- **Daniel 9:1-2** — Daniel, "in the first year of Darius," understood "by books" Jeremiah's seventy years of captivity were ending — and so prayed for his people, mistaking those literal seventy years for the indignation. _(PREX1 130.)_
- **Daniel 9:21** — "Even the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the beginning" — the very same angel of chapter 8 — was "caused to fly swiftly" to Daniel "about the time of the evening oblation." The same messenger comes back to finish the same vision. _(DAR 191; GC 326.)_
- **Daniel 9:22-23** — Gabriel: "I am now come forth to give thee skill and understanding... therefore understand the matter, and consider the vision." He sends Daniel back to "the vision" — the only vision left unexplained, that of the 2300 days. _(GC 326.)_

### "Seventy weeks are cut off" — the seventy weeks are part of the 2300 days

- **Daniel 9:24** — "Seventy weeks are determined upon thy people and upon thy holy city" — 70 weeks of years = 490 years, to finish transgression, make an end of sins, make reconciliation, bring in everlasting righteousness, seal up vision and prophecy, and anoint the most Holy. _(DAR 196; GC 326.)_
- **The Hebrew "determined" = "cut off."** The word _nehhtak_ (Dan. 9:24) "properly" means "to cut off" — Gesenius, Stockius, the Septuagint, Theodotion, and the Vulgate all carry the sense of cutting. The 490 years are cut off — but from what? _(DAR 192.)_
- **There is only one period to cut from.** "There is no period from which the seventy weeks could be cut off but the 2300 days of the previous vision." The 70 weeks are the first 490 years of the 2300; therefore the two periods begin together. _(DAR 192; GC 326.)_
- **Numbers 14:34** — "each day for a year" — the wilderness sentence, God's own rule that a prophetic day stands for a literal year. _(DAR 197, 207.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year." The second witness to the year-day principle that turns 70 weeks (490 days) into 490 years and 2300 days into 2300 years. _(DAR 197, 207.)_

### The starting date: the decree to restore and build Jerusalem, 457 B.C.

- **Daniel 9:25** — Reckon "from the going forth of the commandment to restore and to build Jerusalem unto the Messiah the Prince" — seven weeks and threescore and two weeks (69 weeks); "the street shall be built again, and the wall, even in troublous times." The decree, not the captivity's end, is the anchor. _(DAR 196; GC 326.)_
- **Four candidate decrees — only one fits.** Cyrus (B.C. 536, Ezra 1) and Darius (B.C. 519, Ezra 6) concerned only the temple; Nehemiah's grant (B.C. 444, Neh. 2) was merely verbal and too late. Only Artaxerxes' decree to Ezra (B.C. 457, Ezra 7) restored the full civil and ecclesiastical state. _(DAR 197-200.)_
- **Ezra 7:11-26** — The full decree of Artaxerxes to Ezra: silver and gold, temple service, and power to "set magistrates and judges... whether it be unto death, or to banishment" — a restoration of the whole Jewish polity, the only grant ample enough to "restore and build Jerusalem." _(DAR 198; GC 326.)_
- **Ezra 6:14** — The Jews built "according to the commandment of Cyrus, and Darius, and Artaxerxes" — three kings, but "the commandment" (singular). The three decrees are reckoned as one whole, perfected and "gone forth" only in the grant to Ezra. _(DAR 206; GC 326-327.)_
- **Nehemiah 2:1-8** — Nehemiah's request "in the twentieth year of Artaxerxes" (B.C. 444) was only spoken permission with travel-letters; he found Jerusalem's work already underway (Neh. 2:16) under Ezra's decree, finishing in 52 days — far short of the prophecy's seven weeks (49 years) for the building. Hence not the starting point. _(DAR 199-200.)_
- **The 457 B.C. date is fixed by astronomy.** The canon of Ptolemy, "demonstrated by the concurrent agreement of more than twenty eclipses," places the seventh of Artaxerxes at B.C. 457 — a date that cannot be moved without unsettling all chronology. _(DAR 205.)_

### The 69 weeks (483 years): to Messiah the Prince — A.D. 27 anointing

- **Daniel 9:25** — "unto the Messiah the Prince shall be seven weeks, and threescore and two weeks" — 69 weeks = 483 years. From the autumn of 457 B.C., 483 years reach to the autumn of A.D. 27. _(DAR 201; GC 327.)_
- **"Messiah" means "the Anointed One."** At A.D. 27 Christ was anointed — not crowned with oil but with the Spirit, at His baptism, exactly where the 483 years end. _(GC 327.)_
- **Matthew 3:16-17** — At His baptism "the Spirit of God" descended "like a dove" and the Father's voice declared, "This is my beloved Son." The visible anointing of the Messiah the Prince. _(DAR 201.)_
- **Acts 10:38** — "God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power" — Peter naming the baptism as the anointing that made Him Messiah (the Anointed). _(GC 327.)_
- **Luke 4:18-19** — "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel... to preach the acceptable year of the Lord." Christ Himself claims the anointing the 69 weeks foretold. _(GC 327.)_
- **Mark 1:14-15** — Straightway after His baptism Jesus came "preaching the gospel of the kingdom of God... The time is fulfilled." The only prophetic time then closing was the 69 weeks to Messiah the Prince — and the Messiah announces its fulfilment with His own lips. _(DAR 201-202; GC 327.)_
- **Luke 3:1** — "In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar" John's ministry — and soon Christ's baptism — is dated; the 15th of Tiberius corresponds to A.D. 27, the very year the 483 years expire. (The seeming clash with "about thirty years" is resolved by the four-year error in the Dionysian era — Christ was born about B.C. 4.) _(DAR 201, footnote.)_
- **Galatians 4:4** — "When the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son" — the "fulness of the time" is no vague phrase but the precise terminus of the prophetic 69 weeks. _(GC 327.)_

### The midst of the week: Messiah cut off — A.D. 31 crucifixion

- **Daniel 9:26** — "And after threescore and two weeks shall Messiah be cut off, but not for himself" — the Anointed One slain, His death substitutionary ("not for himself"), and the city and sanctuary doomed to "the prince that shall come" (Rome). _(DAR 202-203, 211.)_
- **Daniel 9:27** — "He shall confirm the covenant with many for one week" (the last seven years, A.D. 27-34); "and in the midst of the week he shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease." - **A.D. 31 fixed by His four Passovers.** Christ kept four Passovers (John 2:13; 5:1; 6:4; 13:1), giving a ministry of three and a half years from autumn A.D. 27 to spring A.D. 31 — "exactly... the midst of the seventieth week." Thirteen ancient authorities locate the crucifixion in the spring of A.D. 31. _(DAR 203-204.)_

### The 70 weeks close: A.D. 34 — the gospel turns to the Gentiles

- **Daniel 9:24** — The 70 weeks were "determined upon thy people" — allotted to the Jews as the special people of God; when the 490 years run out, that special standing ends. Going forward from the cross (A.D. 31) three and a half years reaches the autumn of A.D. 34. _(DAR 204-205; GC 328.)_

# 7. The Daily, and the 1290 and 1335 Days

> _The deeper time-lines of Daniel 11 and 12 hang upon one disputed word — "the daily" (Heb. tamid, the continual). It is not the Jewish sacrifice, but the continual desolating power, paganism, which had to be taken away to make room for the papal "abomination that maketh desolate." That removal was accomplished in A.D. 508; from it run the 1290 years to 1798 (508 + 1290) and the 1335 years to 1843/44 (508 + 1335). The "time of the end" opens in 1798 with the wounding of the papacy, and the blessing on him "that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days" fell upon the advent believers of 1843-44. (DAR 154-157, 254-258, 313-316; GC 356, 439.)_

### "The Daily" — the continual abomination is paganism, not sacrifice

- **Daniel 8:11** — The little horn "magnified himself even to the prince of the host, and by him the daily was taken away." The word "sacrifice" is supplied by the translators; the act belongs to Rome in both phases, pagan and papal. _(DAR 154; the little horn "symbolize[s] Rome in its entire history, including its two phases, pagan and papal" — the "daily" the pagan, the "transgression of desolation" the papal.)_
- **Daniel 8:12** — "An host was given him against the daily by reason of transgression." The barbarians who overran Rome were themselves converted to the Catholic faith and became "the instruments of the dethronement of their former religion," perpetuating the empire in another form. _(DAR 155.)_
- **Daniel 8:13** — "How long shall be the vision concerning the daily, and the transgression of desolation?" Two desolating powers are set side by side — the daily (paganism) and the transgression of desolation (the papacy) — and the question asks how long both shall tread down the sanctuary. _(DAR 156; DAR 157.)_
- **The word itself** — "Daily" (tamid) occurs in the Old Testament about one hundred and two times, "and is, in the great majority of instances, rendered continual or continually. The idea of sacrifice does not attach to the word at all." Hence "the continual" = paganism, the continual desolating power. _(DAR 157.)_

### The taking away of the daily — A.D. 508

- **Daniel 11:31** — "And arms shall stand on his part, and they shall pollute the sanctuary of strength, and shall take away the daily, and they shall place the abomination that maketh desolate." The taking away of paganism is the historical key to both the 1290 and 1335 days. _(DAR 254.)_
- **Conversion of Clovis, 496** — Until the conversion of Clovis, king of France, "the French and other nations of Western Rome were pagan; but subsequently to that event, the efforts to convert idolaters to Romanism were crowned with great success." This prepared the crisis of 508. _(DAR 255.)_
- **508 — paganism subdued** — "From the time when these successes were fully accomplished, namely, 508, the papacy was triumphant so far as paganism was concerned." In that very year a Christian king (Arthur) was crowned in Britain, and at Constantinople the Catholic party for the first time "waged a successful war against both the civil authority of the empire and the church of the East." _(DAR 256; DAR 257.)_
- **The mark of the year** — "In 508 the mine is sprung beneath the throne of the Eastern empire." Paganism, as a controlling power, was broken; the way lay open for the papal abomination to be set up thirty years later, in 538. _(DAR 258.)_

### The 1290 days (years) — 508 to 1798

- **Daniel 12:11** — "And from the time that the daily shall be taken away, and the abomination that maketh desolate set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days." The margin reads "to set up the abomination," fixing the start at the removal of paganism. _(DAR 313.)_
- **The reckoning** — "The daily has already been shown to be... the daily or continual abomination, that is, paganism.... For the historical events showing how this was accomplished in 508, see on chapter 11:31.... Counting back, then, 1290 years from 1798, we have the year 508, where it has been shown that paganism was taken away, thirty years before the setting up of the papacy." 508 + 1290 = 1798. _(DAR 313.)_
- **Both periods end together** — "The two periods, therefore, the 1290 and the 1260 days, terminate together in 1798, the one beginning in 538, and the other in 508, thirty years previous." _(DAR 313-314.)_
- **Daniel 12:7** — The man clothed in linen sware "that it shall be for a time, times, and an half." This is the 1260 years of papal supremacy (538-1798); "this period... began with the supremacy of the papacy, A.D. 538, and terminated in 1798." Its close fixes the inner edge of the time of the end. _(GC 439.)_
- **Miller's summary** — "His 1290 days, beginning when the ten kings... should be converted to the Christian faith, and the daily sacrifice abomination taken out of the way, viz., A.D. 508, which would end us in 1798, when the Pope lost his power to reign over the kings... by being deprived of his civil power by Bonaparte." _(MWV2 113.)_

The same "wise" who understand the unsealed book at the time of the end (12:10) are raised and glorified at its end — the reward of those who waited and came to the 1335 days. _(DAR 314; DAR 316.)_

# Part III — The Revelation

# 8. The Seven Churches of Revelation 1-3

> _The messages to the seven churches of Asia are not seven local letters merely, but a continuous prophecy: seven successive periods of the Christian church, running from apostolic Ephesus to the judgment-hour Laodicea. Each name, in its very meaning, fits the spiritual condition of its age; together they span the whole gospel dispensation, the number seven denoting fulness and completeness. This is no novel view — Bishop Newton, More, and Vitringa held it before us. (DAR 328-329, 345-347; GC 381-382.)_

### The seal of the seven: stars and candlesticks defined

- **Revelation 1:11** — "What thou seest, write in a book, and send it unto the seven churches which are in Asia; unto Ephesus, and unto Smyrna, and unto Pergamos, and unto Thyatira, and unto Sardis, and unto Philadelphia, and unto Laodicea." The seven are named in fixed order; that order is the order of history, apostolic to advent. _(DAR 345.3-347.1.)_
- **Revelation 1:20** — "The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches: and the seven candlesticks which thou sawest are the seven churches." Scripture defines its own symbols: the lampstand is the church; the star is its messenger. _(DAR 328.3-329.1.)_
- **Revelation 1:20** (the stars) — The seven stars held in Christ's right hand "are the angels of the seven churches," and an angel of a church is its minister; held in His right hand, they denote the upholding, guidance, and protection given to all true ministers of the whole gospel age. _(DAR 328.3.)_
- **Revelation 1:20** (the candlesticks) — John saw only seven lampstands, "and seven is a number denoting fulness and completeness"; so the whole gospel church, in seven divisions or periods, is symbolized — the Son of man standing in the midst to take cognizance of all His churches through the dispensation. _(DAR 329.1.)_
- **Revelation 1:11; Revelation 1:20** (why prophetic) — "Many contend... that the seven epistles are prophetical of so many successive periods, or states, of the church, from the beginning to the conclusion of all" — the view is "neither new nor local." _(DAR 345.3.)_

### 1. Ephesus — apostolic purity (A.D. 31-100)

- **Revelation 2:1** — "These things saith he that holdeth the seven stars in his right hand, who walketh in the midst of the seven golden candlesticks." Christ opens to the first church as the One who upholds the ministry and watches over all the lampstands. _(DAR 347.2.)_
- **Revelation 2:2-3** — "I know thy works, and thy labour, and thy patience... thou hast tried them which say they are apostles, and are not, and hast found them liars." The early Christians had the doctrine of Christ in purity, the gifts of the Spirit, and tried and exposed false apostles. The name Ephesus, "desirable," well describes the church in its first state. _(DAR 347.1.)_
- **Revelation 2:4** — "Nevertheless I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love." Even the apostolic age cooled toward its close; the decline begins. _(DAR 347.1; 348.)_
- **Revelation 2:5** — "Repent... or else I will come unto thee quickly, and will remove thy candlestick out of his place." The threat to remove the lampstand confirms the candlestick is the church-period itself.

### 2. Smyrna — the persecuted church (A.D. 100-313)

- **Revelation 2:8** — "These things saith the first and the last, which was dead, and is alive." The risen Christ presents Himself to a dying, persecuted people as the One who Himself conquered death. _(DAR 354.1.)_
- **Revelation 2:9** — "I know thy works, and tribulation, and poverty, (but thou art rich)." Smyrna signifies "myrrh," a fit name for the church passing through the fiery furnace of persecution and proving herself a "sweet-smelling savor" unto God. _(DAR 354.1.)_
- **Revelation 2:10** — "Ye shall have tribulation ten days: be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee a crown of life." The "ten days" are prophetic: ten years (day-for-a-year), fulfilled in the last and bloodiest of the ten persecutions, under Diocletian, A.D. 302-312. _(DAR 352.3-353; Num. 14:34; Eze. 4:6.)_
- **Revelation 2:11** — "He that overcometh shall not be hurt of the second death." To the martyr church, the promise that the worst the persecutor can do reaches only the first death.

### 3. Pergamos — worldly exaltation (A.D. 313-538)

- **Revelation 2:12** — "These things saith he which hath the sharp sword with two edges." To a church tempted to compromise, Christ comes as the One whose word divides truth from error. _(DAR 354.5.)_
- **Revelation 2:13** — "I know thy works, and where thou dwellest, even where Satan's seat is." Pergamos signifies "height, elevation" — the period from Constantine's professed conversion (A.D. 323) to the establishment of the papacy (A.D. 538), when the church was exalted into worldly favor. _(DAR 354.5.)_
- **Revelation 2:14-15** — "Them that hold the doctrine of Balaam... so hast thou also them that hold the doctrine of the Nicolaitans." Worldly policy, pride, and popularity bred false doctrine — the working of the "mystery of iniquity" that ripened into the papal man of sin. _(DAR 354.5.)_
- **Revelation 2:16-17** — "Repent; or else I will come unto thee quickly, and will fight against them with the sword of my mouth.... To him that overcometh will I give to eat of the hidden manna." Christ's remedy is the sword of His mouth — His word — against corrupting doctrine.

### 4. Thyatira — papal supremacy and the long tribulation (538-1798)

- **Revelation 2:18** — "These things saith the Son of God, who hath his eyes like unto a flame of fire, and his feet are like fine brass." Searching eyes for the longest and darkest period of the church. _(DAR 359.1.)_
- **Revelation 2:19** — "I know thy works, and charity, and service, and faith, and thy patience... and the last to be more than the first." Thyatira ("sweet savor of labor," "sacrifice of contrition") is the one church commended for spiritual _improvement_ — believers grew in grace amid the great tribulation such as never was. _(DAR 359.1; Matt. 24:21.)_
- **Revelation 2:20-23** — "Thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants." Jezebel pictures the apostate, persecuting power tolerated within Christendom during the 1260 years of papal triumph (538-1798). _(DAR 359.1.)_
- **Revelation 2:24-25** — "Unto the rest in Thyatira... which have not known the depths of Satan... that which ye have already hold fast till I come." A faithful remnant is addressed through the whole supremacy; "till I come" carries this church across the centuries.
- **Revelation 2:26-28** — "He that overcometh, and keepeth my works unto the end, to him will I give power over the nations.... And I will give him the morning star." The overcomers of the dark ages are promised dominion and Christ Himself, the Morning Star.

### 5. Sardis — the reformed churches grown formal (1798-1833)

- **Revelation 3:1** — "These things saith he that hath the seven Spirits of God, and the seven stars; I know thy works, that thou hast a name that thou livest, and art dead." Sardis ("that which remains," "song of joy") is the reformed churches — having a name to live, but sunk into formality. _(DAR 363.1-363.3.)_
- **Revelation 3:2-3** — "Be watchful, and strengthen the things which remain, that are ready to die.... If therefore thou shalt not watch, I will come on thee as a thief." A call to the Protestant bodies, from the era after the papal deadly wound (1798) to the great Advent awakening, to revive before probation closes. _(DAR 363.3.)_
- **Revelation 3:4-5** — "Thou hast a few names even in Sardis which have not defiled their garments; and they shall walk with me in white... I will not blot out his name out of the book of life." A faithful few remain undefiled; their names stand in the book of life unblotted.
- **Revelation 3:6** — "He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches." The same closing summons binds every period into one prophetic series.

### 6. Philadelphia — the Advent movement, the open door (1833-1844)

- **Revelation 3:7** — "These things saith he that is holy, he that is true, he that hath the key of David, he that openeth, and no man shutteth; and shutteth, and no man openeth." Christ holds the key — the opening of the most holy place of the heavenly sanctuary in 1844. _(DAR 366.3; 368.)_
- **Revelation 3:8** — "I have set before thee an open door, and no man can shut it: for thou hast a little strength, and hast kept my word." Philadelphia signifies "brotherly love," the position and spirit of those who received the Advent message up to the autumn of 1844, who laid aside party names and selfishness in one consecrated movement. _(DAR 366.3.)_
- **Revelation 3:9-10** — "I will make them of the synagogue of Satan... to come and worship before thy feet.... I also will keep thee from the hour of temptation, which shall come upon all the world." A promise of vindication and of preservation through the trial coming on the whole earth. _(DAR 366.3-367.)_
- **Revelation 3:11-12** — "Behold, I come quickly: hold that fast which thou hast.... Him that overcometh will I make a pillar in the temple of my God." To the Advent believers, the imminent coming and a fixed, permanent place in God's temple — "he shall go no more out."

### 7. Laodicea — the judgment-hour church (1844-advent)

- **Revelation 3:14** — "These things saith the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the beginning of the creation of God." The last church meets Christ as the faithful and true Witness — fitting, for this is the period of the investigative Judgment. _(DAR 371.1.)_
- **Revelation 3:15-16** — "Thou art neither cold nor hot.... because thou art lukewarm... I will spue thee out of my mouth." Laodicea signifies "the judging of the people," or "a just people"; it is the last stage of the church, under the third angel's message, while the great day of atonement is transpiring. _(DAR 371.1; Rev. 14:9-14.)_
- **Revelation 3:17** — "Thou sayest, I am rich, and increased with goods, and have need of nothing; and knowest not that thou art wretched, and miserable, and poor, and blind, and naked." The self-deceived, self-satisfied condition of the church living in the judgment hour. _(DAR 371.)_
- **Revelation 3:18** — "I counsel thee to buy of me gold tried in the fire... and white raiment... and anoint thine eyes with eyesalve, that thou mayest see." The threefold remedy: tried faith and love, Christ's righteousness, and spiritual discernment. _(DAR 371-372.)_
- **Revelation 3:19-20** — "As many as I love, I rebuke and chasten: be zealous therefore, and repent. Behold, I stand at the door, and knock." Even the lukewarm are loved; Christ still pleads at the door of the last church. _(DAR 372.)_
- **Revelation 3:21-22** — "To him that overcometh will I grant to sit with me in my throne.... He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches." The series closes as it ran — the overcomer enthroned with Christ, and the Spirit's call sounding to every age at once.

### The church as a woman — symbol confirmed

- **Revelation 1:20 with Revelation 12; Revelation 17** — A woman is the Bible symbol of a church: "a virtuous woman representing a pure church, a vile woman an apostate church." The lampstand-church of Revelation 1-3 is the same pure woman who, fallen, becomes Babylon. _(GC 381.1.)_
- **2 Corinthians 11:2** — "I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ." The marriage figure binds Christ to His church through all seven periods; her unfaithfulness is spiritual adultery. _(GC 381.2-382.)_

# 9. The Seven Seals — the Providential History of the Church

> _Where the seven churches trace the inward spiritual state of God's people, the seven seals unroll the outward providence of the gospel and the church through the self-same Christian era — from apostolic purity to the great day of wrath. As the Lamb breaks each seal, John beholds in succession a white horse (gospel conquest), a red horse (persecution), a black horse (declension under Constantine), a pale horse (papal death), the souls of the martyrs, the literal signs of the sixth seal (the 1755 earthquake, the 1780 dark day, the 1833 falling stars), and at last silence in heaven when the King appears. The seven, being the number of completeness, embrace the whole history of the church from the opening of the Christian era to the coming of Christ. (DAR 402-434; GC 304-308, 333-334.)_

### The Lamb opens the book — a complete sevenfold history

- **Revelation 6:1** — "And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seals... one of the four beasts saying, Come and see." The Lamb who alone was worthy to take the book now breaks its seals one by one; John is summoned to behold the scenes of each. _(DAR 402.)_
- **Revelation 5:9** — Christ was found worthy "to take the book, and to open the seals thereof." The opening of the seals is the unrolling of providential history that none but the slain Lamb could disclose. _(DAR 402.)_
- **Seven = completeness** — The number seven denotes in Scripture completeness and perfection; the seven seals therefore embrace the whole of a certain class of events, reaching down to the close of probationary time. The seals give the religious history of the church; the trumpets, running contemporaneously, give the political commotions of the nations. _(DAR 402.2.)_

### First seal — the WHITE HORSE: apostolic purity and gospel conquest (A.D. 31–c.100)

- **Revelation 6:2** — "Behold a white horse: and he that sat on him had a bow; and a crown was given unto him: and he went forth conquering, and to conquer." The whiteness denotes the purity of the gospel in the apostolic age; the rider goes forth in triumph carrying the truth to all the world. _(DAR 402.)_
- **Mark 16:15** — "Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature." The commission that set the white horse riding forth conquering. _(DAR 402.)_
- **Colossians 1:23** — The gospel "was preached to every creature which is under heaven" — the conquest of the first seal in the apostolic generation. _(DAR 402.)_
- **Revelation 19:11** — A later "white horse," whose rider is "Faithful and True," confirms that the white horse is the symbol of pure, victorious righteousness. _(DAR 402.)_

### Second seal — the RED HORSE: persecution and bloodshed (c.100–c.323)

- **Revelation 6:4** — "There went out another horse that was red: and power was given to him that sat thereon to take peace from the earth, and that they should kill one another: and there was given unto him a great sword." The change of color is significant: if white is gospel purity, red is the bloodshed of the great pagan persecutions that followed. _(DAR 404.)_
- **Matthew 10:34** — "I came not to send peace, but a sword." The truth, faithfully held, drew out the sword of the persecutor; peace was taken from the earth. _(DAR 404.)_

### Third seal — the BLACK HORSE: declension under Constantine (c.323–c.538)

- **Revelation 6:5** — "Lo a black horse; and he that sat on him had a pair of balances in his hand." Blackness marks the moral darkening of the church as paganism was incorporated into Christianity, the period in which the false system that resulted in the papacy rapidly developed. _(DAR 407.)_
- **Revelation 6:6** — "A measure of wheat for a penny... and see thou hurt not the oil and the wine." The balances denote the union of religious and civil power in one person, beginning with Constantine; the scarcity-price denotes a church grasping after worldly goods, while the warning to spare "the oil and the wine" guards the graces of the Spirit, faith and love. _(DAR 407.)_
- **Constantine's union of church and state** — "It represents a secular period, or union of church and state. Constantine aided the clergy, and put them under obligations to him... called the Council of Nicaea." The prosperity Constantine procured for the church bred the corruptions that finally terminated in the apostasy. _(DAR 404.3; 406.)_

### Fourth seal — the PALE HORSE: papal death and persecution (538–1798)

- **Revelation 6:8** — "Behold a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him. And power was given unto them over the fourth part of the earth, to kill with sword, and with hunger, and with death." The pale (livid, corpse-like) color is unnatural — fit emblem of the death-dealing dominion of the papacy over its territory. _(DAR 408.)_
- **"Power was given unto him... namely, the papacy"** — The margin reads "him," the power personified by Death on the pale horse, the papacy; the "fourth part of the earth" is the territory over which Rome held jurisdiction, where it slew with sword, hunger, death, and the beasts of the earth. _(DAR 408.3.)_

### Fifth seal — the SOULS UNDER THE ALTAR: the martyrs cry "How long?"

- **Revelation 6:9** — "I saw under the altar the souls of them that were slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held." The martyrs of the foregoing seals, slain for their fidelity, are figuratively seen beneath the altar — their blood, like Abel's, crying from the ground; this is no proof of conscious souls in death but symbolic, as Abel's blood "crieth." _(DAR 410-412.)_
- **Revelation 6:10** — "How long, O Lord, holy and true, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth?" The cry for vindication against the persecuting power. _(DAR 412.)_
- **Revelation 6:11** — "White robes were given unto every one of them; and it was said unto them, that they should rest yet for a little season." The white robes are the partial answer — the vindication of their misrepresented characters — until the full number of martyrs should be completed. _(DAR 412.2.)_

### Sixth seal — the LITERAL SIGNS in earth and heaven

- **Revelation 6:12** — "And I beheld when he had opened the sixth seal, and, lo, there was a great earthquake; and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the moon became as blood." Here the language turns from the highly figurative to the strictly literal: a great earthquake, the darkened sun, the bloodied moon. _(DAR 414.2-3.)_
- **Revelation 6:13** — "And the stars of heaven fell unto the earth, even as a fig tree casteth her untimely figs, when she is shaken of a mighty wind." The falling stars of the sixth seal — the meteoric shower. _(DAR 422.7.)_
- **THE GREAT EARTHQUAKE — Lisbon, Nov. 1, 1755** — The first sign, the great earthquake, was fulfilled in the Lisbon earthquake of November 1, 1755, which "extended over a tract of at least 4,000,000 square miles," pervading the greater part of Europe, Africa, and America — no recorded earthquake combining so great an extent with such violence. _(DAR 414.3-418; GC 304-305.)_
- **THE DARKENED SUN — May 19, 1780** — "The sun became black as sackcloth of hair" was fulfilled in the Dark Day of May 19, 1780, "a most unaccountable darkening of the whole visible heavens and atmosphere in New England," not caused by any eclipse, and standing alone as the most mysterious phenomenon of its kind. _(DAR 418.3-420; GC 306-308.)_
- **THE MOON AS BLOOD — night of May 19, 1780** — That same night the darkness was "as gross as has ever been observed," and whenever the full moon (which had fulled the day before, proving no eclipse) did appear, it had the appearance of blood — exactly as the prophecy foretold. _(DAR 422.1-6.)_
- **THE FALLING STARS — Nov. 13, 1833** — "And the stars of heaven fell" was fulfilled in the great meteoric shower of November 13, 1833 — "the most universal and wonderful which has ever been recorded, the whole firmament, over all the United States, being then, for hours, in fiery commotion." On each occasion the people instinctively cried, "The Judgment has come." _(DAR 422.7-426; GC 333-334.)_
- **Matthew 24:29** — "Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken." Christ's own sign-list matches the sixth seal exactly and fixes its time — "after the tribulation," i.e., after the 1260-year papal persecution had begun to abate. _(DAR 428.3-430; GC 304, 333.)_
- **Joel 2:31** — "The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and the terrible day of the LORD come." The Old-Testament prophet foretold the very signs of 1780; the eyewitness descriptions of the Dark Day are "but an echo of the words of the Lord, recorded by the prophet Joel." _(GC 308.1.)_
- **Luke 21:25-26** — "There shall be signs in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars... the powers of heaven shall be shaken." The parallel discourse confirms these as the appointed tokens that the King is near, even at the doors. _(GC 304.)_

### The sixth seal reaches to the great day of wrath

- **Revelation 6:14** — "And the heaven departed as a scroll when it is rolled together; and every mountain and island were moved out of their places." Having beheld the past signs fulfilled, we now stand between verses 13 and 14, looking to the still-future rolling-up of the heavens at the voice of God. _(DAR 432.2-3.)_
- **Isaiah 34:4** — "The heavens shall be rolled together as a scroll: and all their host shall fall down, as the leaf falleth off from the vine." The prophets describe the same closing scene of the sixth seal — the departing heaven. _(DAR 432.3-4.)_
- **Revelation 6:15-16** — Kings, great men, and bondmen alike hide "in the dens and in the rocks of the mountains," crying, "Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb." The largest and last prayer-meeting ever held — addressed not to God but to the rocks, in despair. _(DAR 434.1-2.)_
- **Revelation 6:17** — "For the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?" The sixth seal closes by ushering in the great day of wrath, just short of Christ's personal appearing, which is reserved for the next seal. _(DAR 434; 452.4.)_

### Revelation 7 — the sealing falls within the sixth seal (parenthesis)

- **Revelation 7:1** — "I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, that the wind should not blow." The four winds — strife, war, and commotion — are restrained while the work of God goes forward; chapter 7 is thrown in parenthetically between the sixth and seventh seals. _(DAR 435.2; 452.3.)_
- **Revelation 7:2** — "I saw another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God." The sealing message arises in the closing scenes of the sixth seal — the very era in which we now live. _(DAR 435.)_
- **Revelation 7:3** — "Hurt not the earth... till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads." The winds of destruction are held until God's people are sealed; this sealing work belongs to the sixth seal, just before the great day of wrath. _(DAR 452.3.)_
- **Revelation 14:1** — The 144,000 stand "on the mount Sion... having his Father's name written in their foreheads" — the sealed company of Revelation 7, the fruit of the sealing held back by the four angels. _(DAR 435.)_

### Seventh seal — SILENCE IN HEAVEN: the King appears

- **Revelation 8:1** — "And when he had opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven about the space of half an hour." The series is here resumed and concluded: the sixth seal stopped just short of the second advent, so the personal appearing of Christ is allotted to the seventh. When the Lord descends with all the holy angels — when the heavenly harpers leave the courts above to come down with their Lord — there is silence in heaven. _(DAR 452.2-4.)_
- **Matthew 25:31** — "When the Son of man shall come in his glory, and all the holy angels with him." The emptying of heaven's courts at the advent is the cause of the half-hour silence of the seventh seal. _(DAR 452.4.)_

### The seals view the era as PROVIDENCE, not spiritual state

- **Zechariah 6:1-8** — The four chariots with red, black, white, and "grisled and bay" horses — "the four spirits of the heavens, which go forth from standing before the Lord of all the earth" — show that horses of differing color are a Scriptural symbol of successive providential agencies sent forth over the earth. _(DAR 404.)_
- **Seals vs. churches** — The seven churches (Rev. 2–3) describe the inward spiritual condition of God's people; the seven seals describe the outward providence — the church's fortunes and the world's history — over the very same Christian centuries. The trumpets, again, run contemporaneously but trace political war among the nations. _(DAR 402.2.)_

# 10. The Seven Trumpets — the Political Judgments on the Nations

> _Where the seven churches trace the inner life of the gospel church and the seven seals her outward fortunes, the seven trumpets sound the political and military judgments God brought upon the nations that had to do with His church. The first four overthrew Western Rome by the barbarian floods (Goths under Alaric, Vandals under Genseric, Huns under Attila, and the final extinction of the Western emperor); the fifth woe loosed the Saracens of Mohammed, the sixth the Ottoman Turks. The sixth trumpet's "hour, day, month, and year" measured 391 years and 15 days, running from July 27, 1449 to August 11, 1840 — the very day Litch had publicly fixed two years beforehand, openly vindicating the year-day rule and giving "a mighty impetus to the great Advent movement." (DAR 452-487; GC 334-335; PREX2 183-188.)_

### The trumpets as a distinct series — political judgments, not the seals

- **Revelation 8:1** — "When he had opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven about the space of half an hour." The seventh seal opens here; the trumpets are therefore a new and separate series, beginning where the seals close. The silence is the heavens emptied as Christ and all the angels descend. _(DAR 452.)_
- **Revelation 8:2** — "And I saw the seven angels which stood before God; and to them were given seven trumpets." Seven angels, seven blasts — symbols of successive wars and overthrows by which God judged the nations touching His people, the political counterpart to the seals. _(DAR 452-455.)_
- **Revelation 8:3-5** — An angel with a golden censer offers "much incense, with the prayers of all saints" at the golden altar; then he fills the censer with "fire of the altar," casts it into the earth, and there follow "voices, and thunderings, and lightnings, and an earthquake." Intercession at the altar precedes the sounding — the judgments fall in answer to the saints' cause. _(DAR 452-455.)_
- **Revelation 8:6** — "And the seven angels which had the seven trumpets prepared themselves to sound." The signal that the historical series is about to open upon the Roman world. _(DAR 455.)_

### The first four trumpets — the barbarian floods that broke Western Rome

> _Uriah Smith follows Keith and Gibbon: "Little or nothing is left for the professed interpreter to do but to point to the pages of Gibbon." The first four trumpets are the four successive invasions that subverted the Western Empire, A.D. 395-493._

| Trumpet | Verse   | Symbol                            | Historical fulfilment                                | Leader / date                |
| ------- | ------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| First   | 8:7     | Hail, fire, blood on the earth    | Gothic invasions of the empire                       | Alaric, c. 395 onward        |
| Second  | 8:8-9   | Burning mountain into the sea     | Naval war on the maritime coasts; Africa & Italy     | Genseric the Vandal, 428-468 |
| Third   | 8:10-11 | Blazing star "Wormwood" on rivers | Desolating wars on the Alpine river-sources of Italy | Attila the Hun               |

- **Revelation 8:7** — "Hail and fire mingled with blood... cast upon the earth: and the third part of trees was burnt up." "Hail" = the northern origin of the invaders; "fire" = the burning of city and country; "blood" = the slaughter — the Gothic war under Alaric, "the first sore and heavy judgment which fell on Western Rome," from the close of the fourth century. _(DAR 455.)_
- **Revelation 8:8-9** — "As it were a great mountain burning with fire was cast into the sea: and the third part of the sea became blood... the third part of the ships were destroyed." A maritime warfare: Genseric the Vandal (A.D. 428-468), whose conquests "were, for the most part, naval," preyed on Roman commerce from Carthage and destroyed the fleets sent against him. _(DAR 458-459.)_
- **Revelation 8:10-11** — "There fell a great star from heaven, burning as it were a lamp... upon the third part of the rivers, and upon the fountains of waters... the name of the star is called Wormwood." A brilliant meteoric chieftain whose ravages fell on the river-sources of the Alps and Italy — Attila and his Huns, "the scourge of God." _(DAR 461-462.)_
- **Revelation 8:12** — "The third part of the sun was smitten, and the third part of the moon, and the third part of the stars; so as the third part of them was darkened." The "sun" of Roman imperial power was extinguished when the Western line of emperors ended (476-493); the "moon and stars" — consulship and senate — lingered, then expired "star by star," till a Gothic king reigned over Rome. _(DAR 464-467.)_
- **Revelation 8:13** — "Woe, woe, woe, to the inhabiters of the earth by reason of the other voices of the trumpet of the three angels, which are yet to sound!" The remaining three trumpets are so much heavier than the barbarian floods that they are singled out as woes — the first incursions were "but as the preliminary drops of a shower before the torrent." _(DAR 467-469.)_

### The fifth trumpet — first woe: the Saracens of Mohammed

> _"There is scarcely so uniform an agreement among interpreters... as respecting the application of the fifth and sixth trumpets, or the first and second woes, to the Saracens and Turks." (DAR 469, quoting Keith.)_

- **Revelation 9:1-2** — "A star fall from heaven unto the earth: and to him was given the key of the bottomless pit. And he opened the bottomless pit; and there arose a smoke... and the sun and the air were darkened." The fallen star = the overthrow and death of Chosroes, king of Persia (628), which opened the way for Mohammed; the "smoke" darkening sun and air = the false religion of Islam spreading its darkness over the Eastern empire. The "bottomless pit" (Gr. _abussos_) fits the trackless Arabian desert whence the Saracens swarmed. _(DAR 469-472.)_
- **Revelation 9:3-4** — "There came out of the smoke locusts upon the earth... and it was commanded them that they should not hurt the grass... but only those men which have not the seal of God in their foreheads." The Saracen hosts, like locusts from the desert, were charged (under Abubeker) to spare the verdure — and were directed against an apostate church that "had not the seal of God," having torn away the Sabbath. _(DAR 472-476.)_
- **Revelation 9:5-6** — They were "not to kill them, but that they should be tormented five months." Their commission was to harass, not destroy — torment, not conquest of the Greek empire — for a literal prophetic "five months." _(DAR 477-480.)_
- **Revelation 9:7-10** — "The shapes of the locusts were like unto horses prepared unto battle; and on their heads were as it were crowns like gold... hair as the hair of women... teeth as the teeth of lions... breastplates of iron... tails like unto scorpions." Smith applies each detail to the Arabs: the turbans ("crowns like gold"), the long uncut hair, the lion-like ferocity, the cuirasses ("breastplates of iron"), and the swift cavalry whose charge was "as the sound of chariots of many horses running to battle." _(DAR 477-479.)_
- **Revelation 9:11** — "They had a king over them... whose name in the Hebrew tongue is Abaddon, but in the Greek tongue hath his name Apollyon" — that is, _Destroyer_, fittingly the spirit of the destroying Mohammedan power. _(DAR 479-480.)_
- **Revelation 9:12** — "One woe is past; and, behold, there come two woes more hereafter." The Saracen woe closes when the five months end and the work passes to the Turk. _(DAR 480.)_

### The sixth trumpet — second woe: the Ottoman Turks (391 years 15 days → Aug 11, 1840)

> _Here is the great public test of the historicist method: a date computed from "an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year" and fixed two years in advance — fulfilled to the very letter._

- **Revelation 9:13-14** — "The sixth angel sounded... Loose the four angels which are bound in the great river Euphrates." The "four angels" = the four chief Ottoman sultanies of Aleppo, Iconium, Damascus, and Bagdad, in the country of the Euphrates; previously restrained, now loosed. _(DAR 480.)_
- **Revelation 9:15** — "The four angels were loosed, which were prepared for an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year, for to slay the third part of men." By the year-day rule: a prophetic year = 360 years, a month = 30 years, a day = 1 year, and an hour = 15 days — totaling **391 years and 15 days**, now to slay (overthrow the Greek empire) rather than merely torment. _(DAR 480; PREX2 183.1, 188.2.)_
- **Day-for-a-year — the rule that fixes the date.** **Numbers 14:34** — "each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities." **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year." The same divine measure that turns 391 prophetic days into 391 historical years. _(DAR 480; PREX2 188.)_
- **Revelation 9:16-19** — "The number of the army of the horsemen were two hundred thousand thousand... breastplates of fire, and of jacinth, and brimstone... and out of their mouths issued fire and smoke and brimstone." The vast Turkish cavalry, and "fire, smoke, and brimstone" = the gunpowder artillery by which Mahomet II battered down the walls of Constantinople in 1453 — Gibbon, "the unconscious commentator," attributing the city's fall to the Ottoman cannon. _(DAR 483-484.)_
- **The commencement and the close.** The Ottoman supremacy over the Greeks began when Constantine XIII (Deacozes/Palaeologus) accepted the throne only "by permission of Amurath, the Turkish sultan" — virtually saying, "I cannot reign unless you permit." The 150 years ending July 27, 1449, the 391 years and 15 days then began, and "the period would end Aug. 11, 1840." _(DAR 480, 485.)_
- **Revelation 9:18** — "By these three was the third part of men killed" — the destruction of the Greek (Eastern) empire, capital fallen, "her religion trampled in the dust by the Moslem conquerors." _(DAR 483-484.)_
- **The fulfilment — Aug 11, 1840.** Just as the supremacy began by a voluntary surrender, so it ended by one: embroiled with Mehemet Ali of Egypt, the sultan accepted the intervention of the four powers (England, Russia, Austria, Prussia), and on **August 11, 1840** the ultimatum was placed by Rifat Bey into the pasha's hands — the sultan's independence "GONE," the empire thenceforth existing "only by the sufferance of these Christian powers." "Thus was the prophecy fulfilled to the very letter." _(DAR 485-487.)_
- **Litch's prediction, made in 1838.** Two years beforehand, Josiah Litch published this calculation; "only a few days previous to its accomplishment he wrote" that the 391 years 15 days "will end on the 11th of August, 1840, when the Ottoman power in Constantinople may be expected to be broken." At the very time specified, Turkey placed herself under the control of the Christian nations: "The event exactly fulfilled the prediction." _(GC 334-335; PREX2 183-188.)_
- **Revelation 9:20-21** — "The rest of the men which were not killed by these plagues yet repented not... that they should not worship devils, and idols of gold, and silver... Neither repented they of their murders, nor of their sorceries, nor of their fornication, nor of their thefts." The Saracen and Turkish scourges were sent upon apostate Christendom, yet the surviving Roman world learned no lesson — saint-worship, image-worship, and pretended miracles ("sorceries") continued. _(DAR 487.)_

### The seventh trumpet — third woe: the kingdom delivered to Christ

- **Revelation 11:14** — "The second woe is past; and, behold, the third woe cometh quickly." With Aug. 11, 1840, the second woe closes; the third woe (the seventh trumpet) follows quickly — the events of the last days. _(DAR 480, 487; GC 334-335.)_
- **Revelation 11:15** — "The seventh angel sounded; and there were great voices in heaven, saying, The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ; and he shall reign for ever and ever." The final blast carries us past all earthly judgments to the kingdom given to Christ — the goal toward which the whole series of trumpets has pointed. _(DAR 487.)_

### Why this trumpet mattered to the Advent movement

- **The year-day rule, publicly vindicated.** That "the time set for the fulfillment... — Aug. 11, 1840 — was watched by thousands with intense interest," and that "the exact accomplishment of the event predicted... gave a mighty impetus to the great Advent movement," proved the soundness of the prophetic method by which the same students reckoned the 2300 days to 1844. _(DAR 487; GC 334-335.)_
- **One method, two dates.** The day-for-a-year principle (Num. 14:34; Eze. 4:6) that closed the sixth trumpet on Aug 11, 1840 is the same principle by which Daniel's 2300 evening-mornings reach to 1844 — so the Ottoman fulfilment, openly verified by history, stands as God's seal upon the chronology of the cleansing of the sanctuary. _(GC 334-335; DAR 485-487.)_

# 11. Revelation 10 — The Little Book and the Bittersweet Disappointment

> _The mighty angel of Revelation 10 comes down with a little book OPEN in his hand — the prophecy of Daniel, sealed "to the time of the end" (Dan. 12:4, 9) and now at last unsealed. Setting one foot on the sea and one on the land, he lifts his hand and swears that "time shall be no longer" — that no prophetic period reaches beyond the close of the 2300 days in 1844. The book is honey-sweet in the mouth (the blessed hope of Christ's near coming) but bitter in the belly (the great disappointment when, at the appointed time, the Lord did not come). To the disappointed the angel says, "Thou must prophesy again" — the renewed proclamation of the third angel that follows. (DAR 488-496; GC 374, 404, 423; SSP 187-190.)_

### The mighty angel with the OPEN book (Daniel unsealed)

- **Revelation 10:1-2** — "Another mighty angel come down from heaven, clothed with a cloud: and a rainbow upon his head, and his face as the sun... and he had in his hand a little book OPEN." A book once shut is now spread open at the time of the end — the symbol of the prophecy of Daniel made plain. _(DAR 488.1, 488.4.)_
- **Daniel 12:4** — "Shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end: many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased." Daniel's book was sealed only UNTIL the time of the end; therefore at the time of the end it must be opened — and there is no record of its opening save here in Revelation 10. _(DAR 488.4-489.1.)_
- **Daniel 12:9** — "Go thy way, Daniel: for the words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end." The same sealed book, the same appointed unsealing — the little book in the angel's hand is Daniel's prophecy laid open. _(DAR 488.4; DAR 309.4.)_
- **Daniel 8:26** — "Shut thou up the vision; for it shall be for many days." The 2300-day vision was likewise sealed for "many days," to be understood only when the long period drew near. _(DAR 488.4.)_
- **Daniel 8:17** — "At the time of the end shall be the vision." The vision belongs to the time of the end; only then would the book be opened and the chronology of the angel's message be understood. _(DAR 309.4.)_

### The angel's posture, voice, and the sealed thunders

- **Revelation 10:2-3** — He set his right foot on the sea and his left on the earth, and "cried with a loud voice, as when a lion roareth." A world-wide proclamation — sea and land — uttered with the loud voice of a great message. _(DAR 490.1.)_
- **Revelation 10:3-4** — "Seven thunders uttered their voices... Seal up those things which the seven thunders uttered, and write them not." What the thunders spoke John was forbidden to write; we leave them where John left them — sealed, unwritten, and to us unknown. _(DAR 491.2.)_

### "Time shall be no longer" — no prophetic period beyond 1844

- **Revelation 10:5-6** — The angel "lifted up his hand to heaven, and sware by him that liveth for ever and ever... that there should be time no longer." Not that time itself should cease — the very next verse speaks of days yet to come — but that no more PROPHETIC TIME should be measured; the longest prophetic period reaches only to 1844. _(DAR 492.2.)_
- **Revelation 10:7** — "But in the days of the voice of the seventh angel... the mystery of God should be finished, as he hath declared to his servants the prophets." The seventh trumpet begins to sound when prophetic time is at an end (1844); the "mystery of God" is the gospel, to be finished in these closing days. _(DAR 493.2-494.1; SSP 187.1.)_
- **Daniel 8:14** — "Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." This longest of the prophetic lines, the 2300 years, terminating in the autumn of 1844, is the very "time" beyond which no prophetic period extends. _(SSP 187.1; CET 50.1.)_
- **Numbers 14:34** — "Each day for a year." The day-for-a-year rule (with Eze. 4:6) is the key that turns the 2300 days into 2300 years, reaching from 457 B.C. to A.D. 1844. _(DAR 192; CET 50.1.)_
- **Amos 3:7** — "Surely the Lord GOD will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets." The mystery to be finished is the same the prophets foretold — God hides nothing from His servants. _(DAR 493.4-494.1.)_

### One angel, one message — Revelation 10 = the first angel of Revelation 14

- **Revelation 14:6-7** — "Another angel... having the everlasting gospel to preach... Saying with a loud voice, Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come." Both angels have a special message, both cry with a loud voice, both proclaim time and judgment — the angel of Revelation 10 and the first angel of Revelation 14 are the same advent proclamation. _(DAR 490.1; SSP 187.1.)_
- **Revelation 11:18** — "The time of the dead, that they should be judged... reward unto thy servants the prophets, and to the saints." The seventh trumpet brings in the judgment hour announced by the angel — the same investigative work begun in 1844. _(DAR 493.3.)_

### Eat the book — sweet in the mouth (the type of the prophets)

- **Revelation 10:8-9** — "Go and take the little book which is open... Take it, and eat it up; and it shall make thy belly bitter, but it shall be in thy mouth sweet as honey." John, standing as the representative of the church, is bidden to eat — to study the open prophecy until its meaning is comprehended. _(DAR 495.2; SSP 188.1.)_
- **Ezekiel 2:8-10** — "Open thy mouth, and eat that I give thee... a roll of a book... written within and without... lamentations, and mourning, and woe." The prophet's parallel: he too must eat a book before he proclaims. _(SSP 188.1.)_
- **Ezekiel 3:1-3** — "Eat this roll, and go speak... and it was in my mouth as honey for sweetness." Ezekiel ate the same sweet roll before his commission — the exact figure renewed in Revelation 10. _(SSP 188.1.)_
- **Jeremiah 15:16** — "Thy words were found, and I did eat them; and thy word was unto me the joy and rejoicing of mine heart." The honey-like sweetness of the advent truth was Jeremiah's joy in eating God's words — the same experience set under the same figure. _(DAR 495.3; SSP 188.1.)_

### Bitter in the belly — the great disappointment of 1844

- **Revelation 10:10** — "I took the little book... and ate it up; and it was in my mouth sweet as honey: and as soon as I had eaten it, my belly was bitter." The blessed hope was honey to the taste; the passing of the appointed time turned it to wormwood — the disappointment, not the truth, was bitter. _(DAR 495.3; SSP 189.1.)_
- **Habakkuk 2:3** — "The vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry." The "tarrying time" was foreseen; when the spring of 1844 passed, it was found the vision had not failed — the decree of 457 B.C. carried the period to the autumn. _(CET 50.1-50.3; GSAM 153.2.)_
- **Habakkuk 2:1** — "I will stand upon my watch, and set me upon the tower, and will watch to see what he will say... when I am reproved." God provided beforehand for the disappointed watchers through Habakkuk's tower — patient waiting through the apparent delay. _(GSAM 153.2.)_
- **Daniel 8:27** — "I Daniel fainted, and was sick certain days... and I was astonished at the vision, but none understood it." Even the prophet was sickened and dismayed at the vision he could not yet comprehend — a foretaste of the bitterness of those who ate the book in 1844. _(DAR 495.3.)_
- **Luke 24:21** — "We trusted that it had been he which should have redeemed Israel." The disciples' bitter disappointment at the cross was the very type of 1844 — "We looked for Him to save us, but He has not come." _(GC 404.2; SSP 189.1.)_
- **Acts 1:6-7** — "Lord, wilt thou at this time restore again the kingdom to Israel?... It is not for you to know the times or the seasons." As the first disciples mistook the EVENT while God's purpose stood, so the advent believers mistook the event of 1844, not the time. _(GC 404.2; DAR 495.3.)_

### The sanctuary — the key that explained the bitterness

- **Daniel 8:14** — "Then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." When the cloud of disappointment was pierced, the truth of the heavenly sanctuary was discovered: the event of 1844 was not the second advent but the opening of the investigative judgment — Christ's entrance within the veil. _(GC 423.1; SSP 187.2-188.)_
- **Revelation 11:18** — "The time of the dead, that they should be judged." The sanctuary truth showed that the hour of judgment, not the end of the world, had come — the mistake was in the event, never in the time. _(GC 423.1; DAR 493.3.)_

### "Thou must prophesy again" — the post-disappointment commission

- **Revelation 10:11** — "And he said unto me, Thou must prophesy again before many peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings." After the first and second messages ceased as leading proclamations, a renewed message was foretold — the third angel's message, now in process of fulfilment. _(DAR 496.2; SSP 190.1.)_
- **Revelation 14:6-7** — "The everlasting gospel... to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people." The renewed proclamation goes again to the same world-wide audience — peoples, nations, tongues, kings — swelling at last into the loud cry. _(SSP 190.2.)_
- **Habakkuk 2:3** — "It will surely come, it will not tarry." The vision that seemed to tarry will yet speak and not lie; the disappointed are commanded to wait and to prophesy again, for the promise stands sure. _(GSAM 153.2-153.3; CET 50.3.)_

# 12. The 1843 & 1850 Charts, the Tarrying Time, and the True Midnight Cry

> _Habakkuk 2 is the hub of the whole Advent experience: "Write the vision, make it plain upon tables" was fulfilled in the prophetic chart of Fitch and Hale (1842-43) and its corrected successor (1850) — the vision made plain on tables, afterward endorsed as "directed by the hand of the Lord." "Though it tarry, wait for it" foretold the tarrying time that followed the spring-1844 disappointment; and the parable of the ten virgins maps the first cry, the slumber, and the TRUE midnight cry — Samuel Snow's seventh-month movement, "Behold, the bridegroom cometh," fixing the tenth day of the seventh month, Oct. 22, 1844, as the cleansing of the sanctuary. The disappointment was resolved when it was seen that the Bridegroom came, not to earth, but to the Ancient of Days (Dan. 7:13) to receive His kingdom. (GC 392-408, 426-432; EW 74, 232-238; TRMC, Aug. 22, 1844.)_

### The vision made plain upon tables — the prophetic chart

- **Habakkuk 2:1** — "I will stand upon my watch, and set me upon the tower, and will watch to see what he will say unto me." The watchman on the tower is the believer who studies prophecy and waits for its word; Bates titled his tract on this very pathway "Second Advent Way Marks and High Heaps," urging the people to "stand continually upon the watch tower in the day time." _(BP2 42, 46; GC 392.)_
- **Habakkuk 2:2** — "Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it." As early as 1842 this command suggested to Charles Fitch the preparation of a prophetic chart to illustrate Daniel and the Revelation; its publication was regarded as a literal fulfillment of Habakkuk, the vision "made plain upon tables." _(GC 392.)_
- **Habakkuk 2:2** — The chart of 1843 (Fitch & Hale) was afterward declared inspired in its providential use: "I have seen that the 1843 chart was directed by the hand of the Lord, and that it should not be altered; that the figures were as He wanted them; that His hand was over and hid a mistake in some of the figures, so that none could see it, until His hand was removed." The corrected 1850 chart then displayed the figures rightly. _(EW 74.)_
- **Daniel 8:14** — "Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." This was the central line of the chart — the great prophetic period whose terminus the tables were "made plain" to proclaim. _(EW 74; GC 398-399.)_

### The day-for-a-year rule the chart was built upon

- **Numbers 14:34** — "Each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities" — the inspired key that a prophetic day stands for a literal year, the rule by which the 2300 days were reckoned as 2300 years on the chart. _(GC 324; DAR 192.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year" — the second witness to the year-day principle, doubly establishing the measure of the prophetic periods. _(GC 324; DAR 193.)_

### Though it tarry, wait for it — the tarrying time

- **Habakkuk 2:3** — "For the vision is yet for an appointed time... though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry." No one noticed before the disappointment that an apparent delay — a tarrying time — stood in the same prophecy; after the spring of 1844 this scripture became very significant. _(GC 392.)_
- **Habakkuk 2:3** — The disappointed but faithful ones, searching again, "discovered a tarrying time — 'Though it tarry, wait for it.' In their love for Christ's immediate coming, they had overlooked the tarrying of the vision, which was calculated to manifest the true waiting ones." The hand of the Lord was now removed from the figures, and the periods were seen to reach to 1844, not 1843. _(EW 236.)_
- **Habakkuk 2:4** — "The just shall live by his faith." The believers held fast through the dark interval by faith, not by sight, exactly as the prophecy that foretold the tarrying also prescribed the remedy. _(GC 392.)_
- **Hebrews 10:36-37** — "Ye have need of patience... For yet a little while, and he that shall come will come, and will not tarry." The apostle's own echo of Habakkuk — patience through delay, then certain coming — strengthened the waiting Advent band. _(GC 392-393.)_
- **Hebrews 10:38** — "Now the just shall live by faith: but if any man draw back, my soul shall have no pleasure in him." The tarrying separated those who lived by faith from those who drew back when the Lord did not come at the expected hour. _(GC 404.)_

### The two cries — the parable of the ten virgins

- **Matthew 25:1** — "Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto ten virgins, which took their lamps, and went forth to meet the bridegroom." The lamps are the Bible; the going forth is the widespread reformation under the first angel's message proclaiming Christ's soon coming. _(GC 393-394.)_
- **Matthew 25:2-4** — The wise took oil in their vessels; the foolish took none. The wise had the grace of the Spirit and a personal faith that disappointment could not overthrow; the foolish had only a theory of the truth and good emotions. _(GC 394.)_
- **Matthew 25:5** — "While the bridegroom tarried, they all slumbered and slept." The tarrying of the bridegroom is the passing of the spring-1844 time of expectation — the disappointment and seeming delay — during which all, wise and foolish alike, slumbered. _(GC 394.)_
- **Matthew 25:6** — "And at midnight there was a cry made, Behold, the bridegroom cometh; go ye out to meet him." In the summer of 1844, midway between the spring expectation and the autumn fulfilment, the message was proclaimed in the very words of Scripture — this is the TRUE midnight cry, the seventh-month movement. _(GC 398; EW 238.)_
- **Matthew 25:7** — "Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps." The midnight cry roused the whole waiting body; like a tidal wave it swept the land, fanaticism vanishing before it "like early frost before the rising sun." _(GC 400.)_
- **Matthew 25:10** — "They that were ready went in with him to the marriage: and the door was shut." Those who, by the testimony of Scripture, followed Christ by faith into the most holy place "went in" to the marriage; this took place in heaven while the saints were yet on earth. _(GC 427.)_
- **Matthew 25:13** — "Watch therefore, for ye know neither the day nor the hour wherein the Son of man cometh." The watching command frames the whole parable — and the disappointment proved that the date of the event, not the spiritual watch, was what God had appointed to test His people. _(GC 393, 405.)_

### The seventh-month movement — Snow's argument from the types

- **Leviticus 23:27** — "On the tenth day of this seventh month there shall be a day of atonement... ye shall afflict your souls." Snow reasoned from the Mosaic types that, as the spring feasts (Passover, firstfruits, Pentecost) had met their antitype to the very day at Christ's first advent, so the autumn type — the Day of Atonement on the tenth of the seventh month — must meet its antitype in the cleansing of the sanctuary that very year. _(TRMC, Aug. 22, 1844, p. 4; GC 399.)_
- **Leviticus 16:29-30** — "In the seventh month, on the tenth day... ye shall afflict your souls... for on that day shall the priest make an atonement for you, to cleanse you." The high priest entered the most holy place once a year on this day to cleanse the sanctuary — the exact type of the antitypical cleansing of Daniel 8:14, which Snow placed on the tenth day of the seventh month, 1844. _(TRMC, p. 4; GC 399; DAR 222.)_
- **Leviticus 16:33-34** — "He shall make an atonement for the holy sanctuary... And this shall be an everlasting statute... to make an atonement for the children of Israel for all their sins once a year." The yearly, fixed-date character of the type fixed the antitype's date: "the tenth day of the seventh Jewish month... which in the year 1844 fell upon the twenty-second of October." _(GC 399.)_
- **Leviticus 23:29** — "Whatsoever soul it be that shall not be afflicted in that same day, he shall be cut off from among his people." The solemnity of afflicting the soul on that day underscored, for the movement, the searching, heart-humbling character of the autumn cry. _(GC 400; EW 238.)_
- **Leviticus 25:9** — "Then shalt thou cause the trumpet of the jubile to sound on the tenth day of the seventh month, in the day of atonement." Snow joined the jubilee trumpet to the Day of Atonement: as the jubilee proclaimed liberty and redemption to all the land on the tenth of the seventh month, so the antitypical jubilee would bring "the glorious deliverance of the people of God." _(TRMC, Aug. 22, 1844, p. 4.)_

### The seventh-month date — Oct. 22, 1844

- **Daniel 8:14** — "Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." The discovery that the decree of Artaxerxes (the starting point) went into effect in the autumn of 457 B.C. — not at the year's beginning — moved the terminus of the 2300 years to the autumn of 1844, harmonizing prophecy with the type. _(GC 398.)_
- **Leviticus 23:32** — "In the ninth day of the month at even... from even unto even, shall ye celebrate your sabbath." The Day of Atonement was reckoned by the actual Karaite (not Rabbinic) new moon, which placed the tenth day of the seventh month in 1844 on October 22 — the precise date of the true midnight cry. _(GC 399-400.)_
- **Hebrews 10:37** — "He that shall come will come, and will not tarry." After the long uncertainty, the seventh-month cry came with the force of a definite, scripturally-fixed point of time, and "the conclusion seemed irresistible." _(GC 399-400.)_

### Why the time CAN be known — answering "no man knoweth"

- **Amos 3:7** — "Surely the Lord GOD will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets." God hides nothing of His great purposes from His waiting people; the prophetic periods were given to be understood, not concealed. _(GC 324; TRMC, p. 1.)_
- **Matthew 24:36** — "Of that day and hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels... but my Father only." Snow showed that since this declares the same of the Son as of angels and men, it cannot mean the time is forever unknowable; rather, "none but God the Father maketh known" the time — and through His word He does make it known. _(TRMC, Aug. 22, 1844, p. 1.)_
- **Daniel 12:10** — "None of the wicked shall understand; but the wise shall understand." The wise were promised understanding of the very prophetic time the careless could not see — the warrant of the whole movement. _(TRMC, p. 1.)_

### The disappointment resolved — the Bridegroom came to the Ancient of Days

- **Daniel 7:13** — "One like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him." This coming — to the Ancient of Days in heaven, NOT to the earth — is the same event as the bridegroom going in to the marriage in the parable. The disappointment lay not in the date but in mistaking the event. _(GC 426.)_
- **Daniel 7:14** — "There was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom... his dominion is an everlasting dominion." The marriage of Matthew 25 is Christ's reception of His kingdom from the Ancient of Days; "the bridegroom came... not to the earth, as the people expected, but to the Ancient of Days in heaven, to the marriage, the reception of His kingdom." _(GC 426-427.)_
- **Daniel 8:14** — The true event of Oct. 22, 1844, was the beginning of the cleansing of the heavenly sanctuary — Christ's entrance into the most holy place — which Daniel 8:14, Daniel 7:13, and Matthew 25 all describe as one. The TIME was right; only the EVENT had been misread. _(GC 426; DAR 222.)_
- **Habakkuk 2:20** — "But the LORD is in his holy temple: let all the earth keep silence before him." The answer to the disappointment was that the Lord had entered the holy temple above to begin His closing ministry — exactly the prophet's own conclusion to the vision of the appointed time. _(GC 426-428.)_

### The everlasting gospel that drove the chart and the cry

- **Revelation 14:6** — "I saw another angel fly in the midst of heaven, having the everlasting gospel to preach unto them that dwell on the earth." The chart, the tarrying, and the midnight cry were all the outworking of this first angel's worldwide proclamation. _(EW 232; DAR 582.)_
- **Revelation 14:7** — "Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come." Miller and the pioneers "sounded far and near the cry, 'Fear God, and give glory to Him; for the hour of His judgment is come'" — the judgment-hour message that the cleansing of the sanctuary, begun Oct. 22, 1844, fulfilled. _(EW 232; GC 355.)_

# 13. The Woman Clothed in White (Revelation 12) — the True Church

> _In prophecy a pure woman is the symbol of the true church, and a corrupt woman of an apostate church (Eze. 23; Rev. 17). The woman of Revelation 12 is the church of all ages: clothed with the sun of the gospel, the moon of the Mosaic types beneath her feet, crowned with the twelve apostles. She brings forth the man child, Christ; flees into the wilderness for 1260 years (538-1798) while the papacy reigns; is preserved through the dark ages; and at the last the dragon makes war on the remnant of her seed who keep the commandments of God and have the testimony of Jesus. (DAR 509-519; GC 381-382, 433-439.)_

### The woman is the church — the bride espoused to Christ

- **Revelation 12:1** — "A woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars." A pure woman is the church; a corrupt woman (Rev. 17) an apostate church. By parity of reasoning the pure woman here is the true church. _(DAR 509.3.)_
- **2 Corinthians 11:2** — "I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ." The church is the betrothed virgin; Paul's figure fixes the symbol. _(GC 381.2.)_
- **Jeremiah 6:2** — "I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman." God's own likeness for His people is a woman — Zion the church. _(DAR 509.3.)_
- **Isaiah 54:5-6** — "Thy Maker is thine husband... the LORD hath called thee as a woman forsaken... a wife of youth." The covenant between God and His people is a marriage; the church is His wife. _(GC 381.2.)_
- **Hosea 2:19-20** — "I will betroth thee unto me for ever... in righteousness, and in judgment... and thou shalt know the LORD." The marriage figure of Christ and His church, quoted to establish the woman-symbol. _(GC 381.2.)_
- **Ephesians 5:25-27** — "Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it... that he might present it to himself a glorious church, not having spot, or wrinkle... holy and without blemish." The pure woman is the church Christ cleanses and presents spotless. _(GC 381.2.)_
- **Isaiah 62:5** — "As the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride, so shall thy God rejoice over thee." The relation of God to His church is that of bridegroom to bride. _(GC 381.2.)_
- **Galatians 4:26** — "Jerusalem which is above is free, which is the mother of us all." The woman, the church, is the mother who brings forth her children — and the man child. _(DAR 510.1.)_
- **Revelation 19:7-8** — "The marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready... arrayed in fine linen, clean and white: for the fine linen is the righteousness of saints." The woman's white raiment is the imputed righteousness of Christ — the same sun-clothing of Revelation 12. _(GC 381.2.)_

### Her clothing — sun, moon, and crown of stars

- **Revelation 12:1** (clause) — "clothed with the sun." The sun is the light and glory of the gospel dispensation; the church is clothed with the righteousness of the gospel just risen. _(DAR 509.4; 510.2.)_
- **Malachi 4:2** — "The Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings." Christ Himself is the Sun whose light clothes the woman — the antitype and substance. _(DAR 509.5; 510.2.)_
- **Revelation 12:1** (clause) — "the moon under her feet." The moon is the Mosaic dispensation: as the moon shines with borrowed light from the sun, so the former dispensation shone with light borrowed from the gospel — type and shadow beneath the antitype. _(DAR 509.5; 510.2.)_
- **Song of Solomon 6:10** — "Fair as the moon, clear as the sun, and terrible as an army with banners." The bride is described in exactly these heavenly symbols — sun and moon — that crown the woman of Revelation 12. _(DAR 509.4-5.)_
- **Psalms 89:36-37** — "His seed shall endure for ever... It shall be established for ever as the moon, and as a faithful witness in heaven." Sun and moon are the standing prophetic emblems of God's enduring covenant and church. _(DAR 510.2.)_
- **Revelation 12:1** (clause) — "a crown of twelve stars." The crown of twelve stars is the twelve apostles, the foundation of the New Testament church. _(DAR 509.6; 510.2.)_
- **Revelation 22:16** — "I am the root and the offspring of David, and the bright and morning star." The stars are her teachers and leaders, and Christ the chief Star — light reflected from the apostolic foundation. _(DAR 509.6.)_

### The man child and the dragon — first advent and pagan Rome

- **Revelation 12:2** — "She being with child cried, travailing in birth, and pained to be delivered." Verses 1-2 cover the time just before the gospel dispensation, when the church longed and watched for the Messiah (Luke 2:25, 26, 38). _(DAR 510.1.)_
- **Revelation 12:3** — "A great red dragon, having seven heads and ten horns, and seven crowns upon his heads." The dragon is pagan Rome — the red dragon was the principal standard of the Roman legions, painted red. _(DAR 509.7; 511.3-512.1.)_
- **Revelation 12:4** — "His tail drew the third part of the stars of heaven... and the dragon stood before the woman... for to devour her child as soon as it was born." A third part of the Jewish rulers — the kings — were swept away by Rome; and Rome, in Herod, sought to slay the new-born Christ. _(DAR 510.4; 511.3-512.1.)_
- **Revelation 12:5** — "She brought forth a man child, who was to rule all nations with a rod of iron: and her child was caught up unto God, and to his throne." The man child is Christ alone — none other was caught up to God's throne (Ps. 2:7-9; Heb. 8:1; Rev. 3:21). _(DAR 511.1-2.)_
- **Genesis 3:15** — "I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head." The first promise: the woman's Seed, Christ, who bruises the serpent — the war of Revelation 12 in germ. _(DAR 511.1.)_

### War in heaven — Michael's victory at the first advent

- **Revelation 12:7-8** — "There was war in heaven: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon... And prevailed not; neither was their place found any more in heaven." Michael is Christ (Jude 9; 1 Thess. 4:16); the war is His personal contest with Satan at His first advent, in which Satan "prevailed not." _(DAR 513.2; 515.1.)_
- **Revelation 12:9** — "The great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan... cast out into the earth." Here John names a second actor — Satan himself — distinct from pagan Rome; Satan is humiliated and cast down at the cross. _(DAR 513.3-514.1.)_
- **Revelation 12:10** — "Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God... for the accuser of our brethren is cast down." The song is sung prospectively: Christ's victory made salvation forever sure. _(DAR 516.1.)_
- **Revelation 12:11** — "They overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death." The faithful "brethren" overcome through all the ages by the Lamb's blood and their testimony — the martyr church. _(DAR 516.2.)_
- **Revelation 12:12** — "Woe to the inhabiters of the earth and of the sea! for the devil is come down unto you, having great wrath, because he knoweth that he hath but a short time." Baffled and hopeless, Satan turns his rage upon the woman, the church. _(DAR 515.2; 516.2.)_

### The woman in the wilderness — 1260 years, 538-1798

- **Revelation 12:6** — "The woman fled into the wilderness... that they should feed her there a thousand two hundred and threescore days." The church fled when the papacy was established in 538, nourished by the word of God and the ministry of angels through 1260 years. _(DAR 512.2; GC 266.3.)_
- **Revelation 12:14** — "To the woman were given two wings of a great eagle, that she might fly into the wilderness... where she is nourished for a time, and times, and half a time." The eagles' wings are the haste and the help God gave her (Ex. 19:4); the "time, times, and half a time" equals the 1260 days. _(DAR 517.2-3.)_
- **Daniel 7:25** — "They shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time." The identical phrase fixes the little horn's persecution and the woman's wilderness to one and the same 1260 years of papal supremacy. _(DAR 517.3; GC 439.2.)_
- **Revelation 13:5** — "Power was given unto him to continue forty and two months." Forty-two months = 1260 days = the time the church suffered oppression from Rome, A.D. 538 to 1798. _(GC 266.3; 439.2.)_
- **Numbers 14:34** — "Each day for a year." The year-day principle: each prophetic day stands for a literal year, turning 1260 days into 1260 years. _(DAR 517.3.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year." The second witness to the year-day rule by which the wilderness period is computed. _(DAR 517.3.)_

### The wilderness state, the flood, and the earth's help

- **Revelation 12:13** — "When the dragon saw that he was cast unto the earth, he persecuted the woman which brought forth the man child." Satan, defeated at the cross, turns with tenfold fury on the true church. _(DAR 517.1.)_
- **Revelation 12:14** (clause) — "into the wilderness, into her place." The wilderness is seclusion and concealment: the true church hidden in caves and the valleys of the Piedmont (the Waldenses), God watching over and nourishing her. _(DAR 517.1; GC 65.3.)_
- **Revelation 12:15** — "The serpent cast out of his mouth water as a flood after the woman, that he might cause her to be carried away of the flood." The flood is the deluge of papal persecution — fifty to a hundred million carried away — yet the days were shortened for the elect's sake (Matt. 24:22). _(DAR 518.1.)_
- **Revelation 12:16** — "The earth helped the woman, and the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed up the flood." The Reformation: God raised up Luther and his colaborers; Protestant soil in Germany, Switzerland, Holland, England, and Scandinavia absorbed the flood of papal fury. _(DAR 518.2-519.)_

### The remnant of her seed — the link to the last-day church

- **Revelation 12:17** — "The dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." The remnant is the last generation of the true church; the war is against those who keep all ten commandments — including the Sabbath — and hold the spirit of prophecy. _(DAR 519.1-2; GC 438.)_
- **Revelation 14:12** — "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." The same commandment-keeping remnant, defined again under the third angel — the Sabbath reform of the last days. _(DAR 519.2; GC 437.)_
- **Daniel 7:25** (clause) — "think to change times and laws." The dragon's agent presumed to change God's law (the Sabbath); the remnant's loyalty to the unchanged commandments marks them out for the dragon's final wrath. _(GC 433; 446.)_

> _Thus the line of prophecy that opened with the dragon seeking to destroy the new-born Christ runs unbroken to the remnant church: pagan Rome, papal Rome, and at the last a third power — the two-horned beast — all driven by the same dragon, all warring on the woman clothed in white, the commandment-keeping people of God. (DAR 519.3; GC 438.2.)_

# 14. The Beast Power of Revelation 13 — the Papacy

> _The first beast of Revelation 13 — the seven-headed, ten-horned leopard beast
> that rises from the sea — is the Papacy, the very same persecuting power John's
> own teacher Daniel saw as the little horn of chapter 7. Every mark of identity
> answers: it inherits the body of Daniel's lion, bear, and leopard kingdoms; the
> dragon (pagan Rome) hands it his seat and power; it blasphemes God, makes war on
> the saints, and reigns its forty-two months — 1260 years, 538 to 1798 — until its
> deadly wound at the captivity of the pope. Paul names the same power the "man of
> sin," and John shows it again as the scarlet woman of Revelation 17; the title
> graven on the papal mitre, VICARIVS FILII DEI, numbers the man — 666. (DAR
> 520-532, 580; GC 439, 446, 49.)_

### The beast rises from the sea — out of the nations

- **Revelation 13:1** — "I... saw a beast rise up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns, and upon his horns ten crowns, and upon his heads the name of blasphemy." The sea is the Bible symbol of "peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues" (Rev. 17:15); a beast rising from it denotes a power arising amid a thickly-peopled, war-tossed territory — Western Europe in the breakup of Rome. _(DAR 520.2; DAR 521.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:2** — "The beast which I saw was like unto a leopard, and his feet were as the feet of a bear, and his mouth as the mouth of a lion: and the dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority." The body gathers up Daniel's beasts in reverse — leopard (Greece), bear (Medo-Persia), lion (Babylon) — for this last power inherits all that went before; and the dragon, pagan Rome, hands the new Rome his throne. _(DAR 522-523.)_
- **Daniel 7:6** — "Another, like a leopard... the beast had also four heads; and dominion was given to it." Greece, Persia, and Babylon stand in Daniel's prior visions; Revelation 13 stitches their forms into one composite body — proof the same prophetic chain is being carried to its climax. _(DAR 116-117.)_

### The dragon gives his seat — pagan Rome to papal Rome

- **Revelation 12:3-4** — "A great red dragon, having seven heads and ten horns... and the dragon stood before the woman... for to devour her child as soon as it was born." The dragon is the Roman empire in its pagan phase — the power that, through Herod, sought the life of the infant Christ. Same seven heads, same ten horns: one Roman power in two religious phases. _(DAR 509-512; DAR 521.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:2** (latter clause) — "The dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority." Pagan Rome was succeeded by papal Rome; the seat of empire (the city of Rome) and the authority passed from the Caesars to the popes. The change of symbol marks only the change of religion — Rome pagan becoming Rome professedly Christian. _(DAR 523.2.)_
- **Revelation 12:6** — "The woman fled into the wilderness... that they should feed her there a thousand two hundred and threescore days." The same 1260 of the beast's reign, seen from the church's side: while the beast rules, the true church is driven into the wilderness. _(DAR 514-515.)_

### Blasphemy — exalting itself in the place of God

- **Revelation 13:5-6** — "There was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies... and he opened his mouth in blasphemy against God, to blaspheme his name, and his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven." Blasphemy is the assuming of God's prerogatives: the power claims to forgive sins, turns men from the heavenly sanctuary to an earthly throne, and points to Rome as the eternal city. _(DAR 526.)_
- **2 Thessalonians 2:3-4** — "That man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition; who opposeth and exalteth himself above all that is called God... so that he as God sitteth in the temple of God, shewing himself that he is God." Paul foretold the very apostasy that produced the papal power — sitting in the temple (the church) and claiming the place of God. The "man of sin" is the little horn and the leopard beast under another name. _(GC 49; GC 356.)_
- **2 Thessalonians 2:7** — "For the mystery of iniquity doth already work." The leaven was at work even in Paul's day; "little by little, at first in stealth and silence," the customs of heathenism crept into the church until the papacy stood full-formed. _(GC 49.)_
- **2 Thessalonians 2:8** — "Then shall that Wicked be revealed, whom the Lord shall consume with the spirit of his mouth, and shall destroy with the brightness of his coming." The same power persists as a spiritual force to the very end, to be destroyed only at Christ's appearing — the close of the long controversy. _(DAR 525.3.)_

### Forty-two months — the 1260 years, 538 to 1798

- **Revelation 13:5** — "Power was given unto him to continue forty and two months." Forty-two months of thirty days = 1260 prophetic days; by the year-day principle, 1260 years. The identical period appears five times in Daniel and Revelation as "time, times, and a half," "1260 days," and "42 months." _(DAR 524.7; GC 439.)_
- **Numbers 14:34** — "After the number of the days in which ye searched the land, even forty days, each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities." The God-given key: in symbolic prophecy a day stands for a year. This unlocks the 1260, the 2300, and the seventy weeks alike. _(DAR 113; GC 324.)_
- **Ezekiel 4:6** — "Thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: I have appointed thee each day for a year." A second witness to the day-for-a-year rule — established in the mouth of two prophets. _(GC 324.)_
- **Revelation 13:5; Daniel 7:25** — The 1260 years "began with the supremacy of the papacy, A.D. 538, and terminated in 1798." From the rooting-up of the third horn (the Ostrogoths) and the operation of Justinian's decree making the pope head of the churches, to the year the period ran out. _(GC 439.2.)_

### The deadly wound and its healing — 1798

- **Revelation 13:3** — "I saw one of his heads as it were wounded to death; and his deadly wound was healed: and all the world wondered after the beast." The wounded head is the papal head — for only after Rome turned "Christian" was there one head, the papal, to be smitten. The wound is the same event as the going-into-captivity. _(DAR 525.3.)_
- **Revelation 13:10** — "He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints." The power that for 1260 years led the saints captive was itself led captive — fulfilled when Berthier, the French general, took the pope prisoner in 1798; Pius VI died in exile at Valence in 1799. _(DAR 525.3; DAR 527.1; GC 439.2.)_
- **Revelation 13:3** (latter clause) — "His deadly wound was healed." The wound healed with the election of a new pope, March 14, 1800, and the gradual recovery of papal influence — "all the world wondered after the beast," its prestige restored before the end. _(DAR 525.3.)_

### Universal worship and the war on the saints

- **Revelation 13:4** — "They worshipped the dragon which gave power unto the beast: and they worshipped the beast, saying, Who is like unto the beast? who is able to make war with him?" The homage paganism once gave the dragon is transferred to the beast; its seeming invincibility draws the worship of the world. _(DAR 520-522.)_
- **Revelation 13:7** — "It was given unto him to make war with the saints, and to overcome them: and power was given him over all kindreds, and tongues, and nations." A universal dominion and a universal persecution — exactly the reach and record of the medieval Papacy. _(DAR 524.3; DAR 137.)_
- **Revelation 13:8** — "All that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world." Only those written in the Lamb's book of life refuse the homage — the line is drawn between the worshippers of the beast and the people of God. _(DAR 581.)_
- **Revelation 13:9-10** — "If any man have an ear, let him hear... Here is the patience and the faith of the saints." The same solemn call of Christ to the seven churches; under the beast's long oppression the saints are kept by patience and faith, not by the sword. _(DAR 527.1.)_

### The number of the beast — 666

- **Revelation 13:18** — "Here is wisdom. Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast: for it is the number of a man; and his number is Six hundred threescore and six." The number is "the number of a man" — pointing not to a people (as _Lateinos_, a kingdom) but to the title of a single man. _(DAR 579.4-5.)_
- **Revelation 13:18 — VICARIVS FILII DEI** — The title the popes apply to themselves, "Vicegerent of the Son of God," graven on the papal mitre. Taking the Latin letters used as numerals and summing their values yields exactly 666: V(5) + I(1) + C(100) + I(1) + V(5) + I(1) + L(50) + I(1) + I(1) + D(500) + I(1) = 666. "Here we have indeed the number of a man, even the 'man of sin.'" _(DAR 580.1; DAR 580.4.)_

### The same beast as Babylon the harlot — Revelation 17

# 15. The Two-Horned Beast and the Image of the Beast — the United States

> _After the leopard-beast (the Papacy) goes into captivity in 1798, John sees "another beast coming up out of the earth" with two lamb-like horns — a youthful power, rising not by conquest but like a silent seed in a previously unoccupied land, republican in government (no crowns) and Protestant in religion. Of all the prophetic symbols only this one remains, and of all the nations only one fits every specification: the United States of America. But the lamb that should speak gently is foretold to "speak as a dragon" — making an image to the beast (a church-state union enforcing religious dogma, as Rome did) and forcing its mark by no-buy/no-sell penalties and a death decree. (DAR 527-578; GC 439-450; TTR 860-862.)_

### The third symbol — "another beast out of the earth" (Rev. 13:11)

- **Revelation 13:11** — "And I beheld another beast coming up out of the earth; and he had two horns like a lamb, and he spake as a dragon." This is the third great symbol in the prophetic chain. The dragon (pagan Rome) and the leopard-beast (papal Rome) each represented a great false-religious system; analogy requires this third beast to represent the only remaining controlling system — Protestantism — embodied in a nationality. _(DAR 527.3-528.1.)_
- **Daniel 7:2-3** — Daniel's four world-empires arose when "the four winds of the heaven strove upon the great sea." Sea = strife of peoples; so a beast rising from the **earth** by contrast denotes a new, sparsely-settled territory, not the crowded Old World. _(DAR 534.2; GC 439.3-440.1.)_
- **Revelation 17:15** — "The waters... are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues." The Bible's own dictionary: sea/waters = densely-peopled lands; therefore "out of the earth" points to the Western Hemisphere, previously unknown to civilization. _(DAR 534.2; GC 440.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:1** — The first (leopard) beast rose "up out of the sea." The deliberate contrast with verse 11's "out of the earth" marks the second beast as a power arising where no nation was overthrown to make room for it. _(DAR 534.2-535.1.)_

### Nine marks of identity — only the United States fits

- **Revelation 13:11 ("coming up")** — The Greek ἀναβαῖνον means "to grow or spring up as a plant"; the very figure secular historians chose unbidden — "like a silent seed we grew into empire," "emerging amid the silence of the earth." Smith lists nine specifications, all met by the U.S. and by no other nation. _(DAR 535.1-536.2; 540.1-540.10.)_
- **Revelation 13:11 (chronology, "another beast")** — John sees this beast "coming up" at the very moment the leopard-beast goes into captivity (vs. 3, 10) — that is, 1798, when the Papacy's deadly wound was inflicted. In 1798 only one young power in the New World was rising into prominence: the United States, independent since 1776, its independence sealed by the 1783 treaty. _(DAR 529.8-530.3; 537.1; GC 440.2.)_
- **Revelation 13:11 (age, "two horns like a lamb")** — Why "like a lamb"? Because the horns of a lamb are horns that have but just begun to grow — denoting a **youthful** power. In 1798 England, France, and Russia were old; only the New World yields a young, rising power. _(DAR 531.3-531.4; 537.2.)_
- **Revelation 13:11 (manner of rise)** — Lamb-like = innocent, gentle, harmless in appearance. Other nations arose by overthrowing predecessors with "aggressive wars and successful conquests"; the U.S. grew peacefully into vast "mighty regions" — "victoria sine clade" — adding nearly two million square miles in fifty years without dethroning any power. _(DAR 534.4-536.2; 540.4-540.5.)_

### The two horns — republicanism and Protestantism (civil and religious liberty)

- **Revelation 13:11 (no crowns)** — The two lamb-horns bear no crowns. A crown symbolizes a kingly or monarchical government; the absence of crowns marks a **republic**, where power is lodged in the hands of the people. Constitution Art. IV §4: "The United States shall guarantee to every State... a republican form of government." _(DAR 538.1-538.2.)_
- **Revelation 13:14 ("saying to them that dwell on the earth, that they should make an image")** — National action is referred to **the people** themselves ("saying to them"), not handed down by a monarch — proof the government is republican, the power vested in the populace. _(DAR 539.1; GC 442.3.)_
- **Revelation 13:12 ("causeth... them... to worship the first beast")** — In Catholic lands the people worship the beast voluntarily; here they render that worship only when **caused** (compelled) by the civil power — proof the nation's own religion is non-Catholic, i.e. **Protestant**. The two horns = republicanism + Protestantism, civil and religious liberty. Constitution: "No religious test shall ever be required... Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion." _(DAR 538.1; 539.2; GC 442.2.)_

### "He spake as a dragon" — the lamb's voice turns persecuting

- **Revelation 13:11 ("spake as a dragon")** — After all the lamb-like good features comes the painful clause: he "spake as a dragon." The dragon and the leopard-beast were both persecutors; this beast, speaking like them, shows himself a dragon at heart — destined likewise to persecute. The "speaking" of a nation is the action of its legislative and judicial authorities. _(DAR 539.3-541.1; GC 442.1-442.2.)_
- **Matthew 7:15** — A wolf in sheep's clothing: lamb-like in profession, dragon in deed. The contradiction between professed liberty and actual intolerance is the very note Revelation 13:11 sounds. _(GC 442.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:13-14 ("great wonders... fire from heaven... deceiveth")** — Miracle-working deception, originating in the U.S. (Spiritualism, begun at Hydesville, N.Y., 1848), prepares men to "make an image to the beast." The false prophet IS the two-horned beast — the miracle-power that deceives men into the mark and image. _(DAR 542.4-543.2.)_

### The image of the beast — a church-state union like Rome's

# 16. The Mark of the Beast

> _The mark of the beast is the badge of submission to the beast's authority in the very point where he exalted himself against God — His law and His holy day. The little horn "thought to change times and laws"; the change of the seventh-day Sabbath to Sunday is therefore the sign of his assumed authority, and its acceptance, after the truth is known and Sunday is enforced, is the mark. Over against it stands the seal of God — the fourth commandment, the only precept bearing the Lawgiver's name, title, and dominion. No man has yet received the mark; it is the final test, when Christendom will be divided into just two classes. (DAR 624, 628-630; GC 446-449, 604-605.)_

### The power that thought to change God's law

- **Daniel 7:25** — The little horn shall "speak great words against the most High, and wear out the saints," and "think to change times and laws." The papacy's one attempt to alter the decalogue — striking out the second commandment and changing the fourth from the seventh day to the first — exactly fulfills this; only by changing God's law could it exalt itself above God. _(GC 446; DAR 624.)_
- **Revelation 13:16-17** — The two-horned beast "causeth all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads," so "that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark, or the name of the beast." The mark is enforced by the United States in homage to the papacy. _(DAR 533-534, 624; GC 442.)_
- **Revelation 13:18** — "Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast: for it is the number of a man; and his number is Six hundred threescore and six." The number marks the beast as a man-power exalting human authority over God's. _(DAR 624.)_

### What constitutes the mark — Sunday for the Sabbath

- **Revelation 14:9-11** — The third angel warns "with a loud voice, If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark," he "shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God." The most fearful threatening in Scripture is leveled at this one act of homage. _(DAR 620, 624; GC 449.)_
- **Revelation 14:12** — Over against the mark stands the remnant: "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." The opposite of receiving the mark is keeping the very commandment the beast changed. _(DAR 627-628; GC 605.)_
- **Daniel 7:25 with Exodus 20:8-11** — The only "time" and "law" the papacy claims to have changed is the fourth commandment, moving the Sabbath of the LORD to the first day. Roman writers cite "the very act of changing the Sabbath into Sunday" as "the sign, or mark, of the authority of the Roman Church." _(GC 446-448.)_
- **Romans 6:16** — "To whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are to whom ye obey." Keeping Sunday in deference to the church's authority is yielding obedience to that power in the place of God. _(DAR 624, 626.)_
- **The mark defined** — "The mark of the beast is that institution which this power has set up as proof of its authority... It consists in a change of the law of God, by which the seventh-day Sabbath... is torn from its place in the decalogue, and a false and counterfeit Sabbath, the first day of the week, is set up in its stead." _(DAR 624.)_

### The Sabbath is the seal of God — His name, title, and dominion

- **Exodus 31:13** — "Verily my sabbaths ye shall keep: for it is a sign between me and you... that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you." God Himself names the Sabbath His sign; sign, seal, token, and mark are synonymous in Scripture usage. _(DAR 440; GC 605.)_
- **Exodus 31:17** — "It is a sign between me and the children of Israel for ever: for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth... and on the seventh day he rested." The sign rests on creation — the Sabbath identifies the LORD as Creator, the only ground of His right to rule. _(DAR 440-441.)_
- **Ezekiel 20:12** — "I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them." The same divine seal is repeated by the prophet. _(DAR 440.)_
- **Ezekiel 20:20** — "Hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign between me and you, that ye may know that I am the LORD your God." The Sabbath is the appointed token by which God's people know and avow their God. _(DAR 440.)_
- **Exodus 20:8-11** — The fourth commandment alone contains the name ("the LORD thy God"), His title and creative work ("the LORD made heaven and earth"), and His dominion ("heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is"). A seal must carry the name, office, and territory of the lawgiver — the fourth commandment alone supplies all three, and so is the seal of the law. _(DAR 438-440.)_
- **Genesis 2:1-3** — "God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested." The Sabbath was sanctified at creation, before sin, and so is primary, immutable, and eternal — not a ceremonial shadow that ended at the cross. _(DAR 630.)_
- **A seal authenticates a law** — "A seal is used to render valid or authentic any enactments... the name must always be used." Passing through the decalogue, only the fourth commandment shows _who_ the Lawgiver is and _by what right_ He commands — it is the seal of God. _(DAR 438-439.)_

### The seal of God in the forehead — the contrast company

- **Revelation 7:2-3** — "Another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God... Hurt not the earth... till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads." The sealing is the binding of the Sabbath truth upon God's people before the winds of trouble are loosed. _(DAR 442; GC 605.)_
- **Revelation 7:4** — "There were sealed an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel" — the same company developed by the third angel's message. _(DAR 446.)_

- **Ezekiel 9:4** — "Set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations." The slaughter is stayed for those who bear God's distinguishing mark — the type of the final sealing. _(DAR 442.)_

- **Revelation 22:14** — "Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city." Only the commandment-keepers — those bearing the seal, not the mark — enter the city. _(DAR 627; GC 605.)_

- **Ezek. 20:12** — "I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them." The Sabbath is the appointed sign of the Creator-God; it is the very point the first angel revives. _(GC 437.)_
- **Acts 17:24-31** — "God that made the world and all things therein... now commandeth all men every where to repent: because he hath appointed a day, in the which he will judge the world." Paul joins the same two truths the first angel joins: the Creator and the coming judgment. _(DAR 586.)_
- **Psa. 96:5** — "All the gods of the nations are idols: but the LORD made the heavens." The Maker alone is to be worshipped; the first message restores the worship of the true Creator-God in an idolatrous world. _(GC 437.)_

### The Second Angel — "Babylon Is Fallen"

- **Rev. 14:8** — "Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication." The apostate church, fallen from her first purity, makes the nations drunk with false doctrine; first preached in the summer of 1844. _(DAR 600, 607-608; GC 381, 389.)_
- **Rev. 17:1-6** — "Babylon the Great, the Mother of Harlots... drunken with the blood of the saints." Babylon is the great harlot riding the beast — the union of apostate religion with the world; her "daughters" are the fallen Protestant churches. _(DAR 607; GC 381-382.)_
- **Isa. 21:9** — "Babylon is fallen, is fallen; and all the graven images of her gods he hath broken." The prophetic phrase the second angel borrows; the doom of literal Babylon foreshadows the moral fall of mystic Babylon. _(DAR 600.)_
- **Rev. 18:1-3** — "Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen, and is become the habitation of devils... For all nations have drunk of the wine of the wrath of her fornication." The fuller "loud cry" repetition of the second angel's message under the mighty angel whose glory lightens the earth. _(DAR 607; GC 603.)_
- **Rev. 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues." Because Babylon is fallen, God calls His honest-hearted people out of her before the plagues fall. _(DAR 590; GC 390, 603.)_
- **Rev. 18:5** — "For her sins have reached unto heaven, and God hath remembered her iniquities." The measure of her cup is full; her judgment is certain — the reason the call to separate is so urgent. _(GC 390.)_
- **2 Cor. 6:17** — "Come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord... and I will receive you." The standing principle behind the second angel's call: God's people must not remain in fellowship with a fallen communion. _(GC 390.)_

### The Third Angel — The Mark Warning and the Commandment-Keeping Remnant

- **Rev. 14:9-10** — "If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark... the same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation." The most fearful threatening ever addressed to mortals; the only sin that calls down wrath unmingled with mercy. _(DAR 619; GC 449.)_
- **Rev. 14:11** — "And the smoke of their torment ascendeth up for ever and ever: and they have no rest day nor night, who worship the beast and his image." The third angel's solemn warning against receiving the mark; the final, irrevocable separation of the lost. _(DAR 619-620; GC 449.)_
- **Rev. 13:15-17** — "...cause that as many as would not worship the image of the beast should be killed... receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads." The mark the third angel warns against is the enforced worship of the beast through his image — apostate Protestantism (the two-horned beast / United States) compelling allegiance to Rome. _(DAR 619; GC 445.)_
- **Dan. 7:25** — "He shall... think to change times and laws." The little horn (the Papacy) presumed to change God's law, transferring the Sabbath to Sunday; the third message restores the trampled commandment, and Sunday-keeping enforced becomes the mark. _(DAR 630; GC 446-447.)_
- **Rev. 14:12** — "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." Over against the worshippers of the beast stand the remnant — keeping all ten commandments (including the Sabbath) and holding the faith of Jesus. _(DAR 626; GC 445.)_
- **Rev. 12:17** — "The dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." The same commandment-keeping remnant; the third angel's people are the object of the dragon's last wrath. _(DAR 626; GC 445.)_
- **Rev. 22:14** — "Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city." The closing benediction of the Bible matches the third angel: only the commandment-keepers enter the city. _(DAR 626.)_
- **Rev. 14:1-5** — "A Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father's name written in their foreheads... without fault before the throne of God." The fruit of the threefold message: the sealed, victorious company that stands with the Lamb — the firstfruits redeemed from the earth. _(DAR 582-585; GC 648-649.)_

### A Blessing on the Dead Who Die in the Lord

- **Rev. 14:13** — "Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth... that they may rest from their labours; and their works do follow them." A special benediction pronounced "from henceforth" — from the time the third message begins; those who die in this last truth are blessed. _(DAR 590.)_

### The Harvest of the Earth — The Second Coming

- **Rev. 14:14** — "And I looked, and behold a white cloud, and upon the cloud one sat like unto the Son of man, having on his head a golden crown, and in his hand a sharp sickle." Immediately after the threefold message comes Christ on the cloud — crowned, with the sickle of harvest; the messages are the last warning before His appearing. _(DAR 635; GC 311, 453.)_
- **Rev. 14:15-16** — "Thrust in thy sickle, and reap: for the time is come for thee to reap; for the harvest of the earth is ripe... and the earth was reaped." The ingathering of the righteous (the wheat) at the second advent. _(DAR 635.)_
- **Matt. 13:39** — "The harvest is the end of the world; and the reapers are the angels." Christ's own key: the harvest of Rev. 14 is the end of the world. _(DAR 635.)_
- **Rev. 14:17-18** — "Another angel came out from the altar... Thrust in thy sharp sickle, and gather the clusters of the vine of the earth; for her grapes are fully ripe." The second, terrible harvest — the gathering of the wicked for destruction. _(DAR 635-636.)_
- **Rev. 14:19-20** — "The angel thrust in his sickle... and cast it into the great winepress of the wrath of God... blood came out of the winepress, even unto the horse bridles, by the space of a thousand and six hundred furlongs." The treading of the winepress: the slaying of the lost when Christ appears in vengeance. _(DAR 636; GC 657.)_
- **Joel 3:13** — "Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe... for the press is full, the fats overflow; for their wickedness is great." The Old Testament source of the winepress imagery — the day of the Lord's vengeance. _(DAR 636.)_
- **Rev. 19:11-15** — "He was clothed with a vesture dipped in blood... and he treadeth the winepress of the fierceness and wrath of Almighty God." The Faithful and True on the white horse treads the same winepress; the harvest of Rev. 14 and the advent of Rev. 19 are one event — the close of the three angels' work. _(DAR 636; GC 641-642.)_

# 18. The 144,000 and the Great Multitude

> _Revelation brings to view two companies among the redeemed. The 144,000 are the living saints sealed in the forehead and translated without seeing death — the firstfruits, who stand with the Lamb on Mount Zion bearing the Father's name, follow Him whithersoever He goeth, and sing a song of deliverance no other host can learn. The great multitude is the larger, uncountable throng of the redeemed of all ages, raised from the dead at the second advent, who came out of great tribulation and washed their robes white in the blood of the Lamb. The sealing of the 144,000 is the last work done for God's people before translation. (DAR 446-451; DAR 582-584; GC 648-649; EW 15-18.)_

### The Sealing of the 144,000 (Revelation 7:1-8)

- **Revelation 7:1-3** — Four angels hold the four winds till "we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads." The sealing of God's living people is the work that must be finished before the winds of strife are loosed. _(DAR 437-446.)_
- **Revelation 7:4** — "I heard the number of them which were sealed: an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel." A definite number, drawn from the living, sealed in the last days. _(DAR 446.1-2.)_
- **Revelation 7:5-8** — Twelve thousand sealed "out of" each tribe — Dan and Ephraim omitted, Levi and Joseph put in their place. The enumeration differs from every other in Scripture; not all once written among these tribes endure to the end. _(DAR 447-448.1.)_
- **Ezekiel 9:4** — "Set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations." The Old-Testament type of the sealing — a mark in the forehead distinguishing God's faithful from the apostate mass. _(DAR 437-446.)_
- **Ephesians 4:30** — "Ye are sealed unto the day of redemption." The seal is the impress of the Holy Spirit, set on the character of those who are ready for translation. _(DAR 446.)_
- **Revelation 22:4** — "His name shall be in their foreheads." The Father's name is His seal; the commandment that bears His descriptive title — the Sabbath — is the seal of His law. _(DAR 584.2.)_

### Spiritual Israel — Who the Sealed Tribes Are (Galatians 3:29)

- **Galatians 3:29** — "If ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise." The twelve tribes of Revelation 7 are not the literal Jewish nation but spiritual Israel — believers in Christ of every nation. _(DAR 446.2-447.)_
- **Galatians 3:7-9** — "They which are of faith, the same are the children of Abraham... blessed with faithful Abraham." Faith, not lineage, makes one a true Israelite and heir of the sealing. _(DAR 446.2.)_
- **Romans 2:28-29** — "He is not a Jew which is one outwardly... but he is a Jew which is one inwardly." Uriah Smith cites this very text to prove the sealed twelve tribes are Christians of the last generation. _(DAR 447.)_
- **James 1:1** — James writes "to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad," yet addresses them "in view of the coming of the Lord" — therefore the last generation of Christians, called the twelve tribes. _(DAR 446.2-447.)_
- **Revelation 21:12** — The gates of the New Jerusalem — a New-Testament city — bear "the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel," and through them all the redeemed pass; so all the saved, Jew or Gentile, are reckoned among these tribes. _(DAR 447.)_

### The 144,000 on Mount Zion — Translated Without Death (Revelation 14:1-5)

- **Revelation 14:1** — "A Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father's name written in their foreheads." The very company hunted by the beast in chapter 13 stands victorious on the heavenly Zion. _(DAR 582.2-583.)_
- **Revelation 14:2-3** — They sing "as it were a new song... and no man could learn that song but the hundred forty and four thousand." It is the song of Moses and the Lamb — the song of their own deliverance, which no other company can learn. _(DAR 584.3; GC 648-649.)_
- **Revelation 14:4** — "These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth. These were redeemed from among men, being the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb." "Redeemed from among men" — from among the living — means translation; the 144,000 are the living saints changed at the second coming. _(DAR 583.4; DAR 451.1.)_
- **Revelation 14:5** — "In their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault before the throne of God." A character without falsehood, perfected through the final crisis. _(DAR 582.2; GC 648.3.)_
- **Revelation 3:10** — "Because thou hast kept the word of my patience, I also will keep thee from the hour of temptation, which shall come upon all the world." The promise to Philadelphia — the church state to which the 144,000 belong — kept through the last trial. _(DAR 583.3.)_
- **Revelation 3:11-12** — "Hold that fast which thou hast, that no man take thy crown... I will write upon him the name of my God, and the name of the city of my God." The overcomers of Philadelphia receive the Father's name written in the forehead — the very mark of the 144,000. _(DAR 583.3.)_
- **Daniel 12:1** — "There shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation... and at that time thy people shall be delivered." The 144,000 pass through this unequaled trouble, standing without a mediator through the seven last plagues, yet are delivered. _(DAR 450.1; GC 648-649.)_
- **Revelation 14:12** — "Here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." Accused of legalism for refusing the mark, the 144,000 are shown to rest wholly on the merits of the Lamb's blood. _(DAR 450.2.)_

### The Great Multitude — The Redeemed of All Ages (Revelation 7:9-17)

- **Revelation 7:9** — "A great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues, stood before the throne... clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands." The countless host of the saved of every age, raised at the second advent — proof the sealing is the last work before translation. _(DAR 448.2-3.)_
- **Revelation 7:10-12** — They cry "Salvation to our God which sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb," and all the angels fall and worship. The redeemed ascribe their victory wholly to God and the Lamb. _(DAR 448.2.)_
- **Revelation 7:13-14** — The elder asks "What are these... and whence came they?" — and John's "Sir, thou knowest" implies he did not know. Smith holds that this question singles out a special company within the throng: the 144,000, so transformed John no longer recognizes them. _(DAR 449.2-450.)_
- **Revelation 7:14** — "These... came out of great tribulation, and have washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb." True of all the redeemed, but emphatically of the 144,000, who pass through the great time of trouble. _(DAR 450.1-2; GC 648-649.)_
- **Revelation 7:15-17** — "They shall hunger no more, neither thirst any more; neither shall the sun light on them, nor any heat... God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes." The reward answers to their suffering in the plagues, when the sun scorched men and the rivers turned to blood. _(DAR 450.4-451.)_

### The Song and the Sea of Glass — The Victors over the Beast (Revelation 15:2-3)

- **Revelation 15:2** — "Them that had gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark... stand on the sea of glass, having the harps of God." The same 144,000, now standing victorious on the sea of glass before the throne. _(GC 648.3-649.)_
- **Revelation 15:3** — "They sing the song of Moses... and the song of the Lamb." The song of deliverance — Moses' song was the song of his own experience and rescue (Exodus 15); so this is the song of the saints' deliverance, learnable by none but them. _(DAR 584.3; GC 649.)_

### Their Eternal Reward — God Dwells with Them (Revelation 21:3-4)

- **Revelation 21:3** — "Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them, and they shall be his people." The final union of the redeemed with God — the consummation of the covenant. _(GC 645; GC 648-649.)_
- **Revelation 21:4** — "God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain." Smith joins this with Isaiah 25:8 — "He will swallow up death in victory" — as the promise fulfilled to the delivered saints. _(DAR 451.2.)_
- **1 Thessalonians 4:16-17** — "The dead in Christ shall rise first: then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them... to meet the Lord in the air." The resurrection of the great multitude and the translation of the living 144,000 in one event at Christ's coming. _(GC 645; EW 16.1.)_

### Witness of the Spirit of Prophecy

- **The sealed company translated.** "The 144,000 were all sealed and perfectly united. On their foreheads was written, God, New Jerusalem, and a glorious star containing Jesus' new name." Sealed, they stand through the last crisis and are changed at the advent. _(EW 15.1.)_
- **Caught up at the resurrection.** "The graves opened, and the dead came up clothed with immortality. The 144,000 shouted, 'Alleluia!'... and in the same moment we were changed and caught up together with them to meet the Lord in the air." The living translated join the risen dead. _(EW 16.1; GC 645.1.)_
- **On the sea of glass in a perfect square.** "Here on the sea of glass the 144,000 stood in a perfect square... Jesus brought the crowns, and with His own right hand placed them on our heads. He gave us harps of gold and palms of victory." _(EW 16.2; GC 645.3.)_
- **The deliverance at midnight.** "It was at midnight that God chose to deliver His people... there was a mighty shout of victory over the beast and over his image." The 144,000 delivered out of the time of trouble by the voice of God. _(EW 285.)_
- **The final scene by the great pen.** Upon the sea of glass "are gathered the company that have gotten the victory over the beast... with the Lamb upon Mount Zion... the hundred and forty and four thousand that were redeemed from among men... having been translated from the earth, from among the living... the first fruits unto God and to the Lamb." The whole prophetic chain resolved in one tableau. _(GC 648.3-649.)_

# 19. The Woman Who Rides the Beast — Babylon

> _In prophecy a woman is the symbol of a church: a pure woman, the true church of Christ; a corrupt woman, an apostate church. The woman of Revelation 17 is the great apostasy — Papal Rome, "Babylon the Great, the Mother of Harlots" — arrayed in purple and scarlet, holding a golden cup of abominations, drunken with the blood of the saints, and seated upon a scarlet beast (the civil power) that she controls and guides "as a rider controls the animal upon which he is seated." Her seven heads are the seven mountains, or successive forms of government, of Rome; she is "that great city which reigneth over the kings of the earth." From her come the apostate daughters — fallen Protestantism — and against the whole family heaven cries, "Babylon is fallen... come out of her, my people." (DAR 657-663; GC 381-383.)_

### The Symbol-Key: A Woman = A Church (Pure or Corrupt)

- **2 Corinthians 11:2** — Paul has "espoused" the church "to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ." The faithful church is a chaste woman betrothed to the Lord — the standard against which the harlot is read. _(GC 381.)_
- **Ephesians 5:23-27** — Christ is "the head of the church," who gave Himself to "present it to himself a glorious church, not having spot, or wrinkle... holy and without blemish." The marriage figure: Christ the husband, the pure church His bride. _(GC 381.)_
- **Jeremiah 31:32** — "I was an husband unto them, saith the LORD." God binds His people to Himself by covenant as a husband to a wife; apostasy is therefore adultery. _(GC 381.)_
- **Revelation 12:1** — The true church is "a woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars" — pure, radiant, heavenly. The harlot of chapter 17 is her counterfeit. _(DAR 509.)_
- **Jeremiah 6:2** — The LORD "likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman." Scripture itself supplies the symbol: the people of God pictured as a woman. _(GC 381.)_

### A Corrupt Woman = An Apostate Church; Fornication = Spiritual Idolatry

- **Isaiah 1:21** — "How is the faithful city become an harlot! it was full of judgment; righteousness lodged in it; but now murderers." Departure from God turns the once-pure church into a harlot — the exact transformation pictured in Revelation 17. _(GC 381-382.)_
- **Jeremiah 3:6-9** — "Backsliding Israel" went up "upon every high mountain... and there hath played the harlot," and "treacherous" Judah "went and played the harlot also." Unfaithfulness to God is fornication; here are two sister-harlots — mother and daughter apostasy foreshadowed. _(GC 381.)_
- **Ezekiel 23:2-4** — "Two women, the daughters of one mother... Aholah the elder, and Aholibah her sister," who "committed whoredoms" — Samaria and Jerusalem. The pattern of a mother-apostasy with harlot daughters; the very framework of Babylon "the mother of harlots." _(GC 382-383.)_
- **Hosea 2:2-5** — "Plead with your mother... for she is not my wife... for she said, I will go after my lovers." Spiritual harlotry = chasing other gods and the world's favor instead of the Lord. _(GC 381-382.)_

### The Vision of the Woman on the Beast (Revelation 17:1-6)

- **Revelation 17:1-2** — "The judgment of the great whore that sitteth upon many waters: with whom the kings of the earth have committed fornication, and the inhabitants of the earth have been made drunk with the wine of her fornication." The illicit union of church and state, and the world intoxicated with her false doctrines. _(DAR 657.)_
- **Revelation 17:3** — "I saw a woman sit upon a scarlet coloured beast, full of names of blasphemy, having seven heads and ten horns." The church (woman) seated upon and controlling the civil power (beast) — the prophecy "distinguishes between church and state." She rides and guides it "to her own ends." _(DAR 657.3.)_
- **Revelation 17:4** — "Arrayed in purple and scarlet colour, and decked with gold and precious stones and pearls, having a golden cup in her hand full of abominations." Purple and scarlet are "the chief colors in the robes of popes and cardinals"; the golden cup — symbol of pure doctrine — pours out only abominations. _(DAR 658.1.)_
- **Revelation 17:5** — "Upon her forehead a name written, MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS AND ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH." Her own brand identifies her: not merely a harlot, but the mother — proof of apostate daughters belonging "to the same great family." _(DAR 658.2.)_
- **Revelation 17:6** — "I saw the woman drunken with the blood of the saints, and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus." John wondered because this persecutor was no longer pagan Rome but a power professing the name of Christ — the church drunk on the blood of the faithful. _(DAR 658.3-4; GC 382.)_

### Seven Heads = Seven Mountains = Rome (Revelation 17:7-11)

- **Revelation 17:9** — "Here is the mind which hath wisdom. The seven heads are seven mountains, on which the woman sitteth." The seven heads are first explained as seven mountains, then as seven kings — "forms of government." The seat is Rome. _(DAR 659.3.)_
- **Revelation 17:10** — "There are seven kings: five are fallen, and one is, and the other is not yet come." The heads are successive in time — not literal hills, which are not successive. So they denote the successive ruling forms of the Roman power. _(DAR 659.3; DAR 602.3-5.)_
- **Revelation 17:11** — "The beast that was, and is not, even he is the eighth, and is of the seven, and goeth into perdition." Rome viewed "in three phases" — the same persecuting power reappearing "in its bloody and blasphemous form," doomed to perdition. _(DAR 659.2-3.)_
- **Revelation 13:1** — The kindred beast rises "having seven heads and ten horns" with "the name of blasphemy" — the same Roman power, the seven heads marking it across history. The wounded head "cannot be a literal mountain; for it would be folly to speak of wounding a mountain to death." _(DAR 602.3; DAR 525.3.)_
- **Daniel 7:23** — "The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon earth... and shall devour the whole earth." Rome — the fourth world-kingdom — is the beast the harlot rides; the prophets agree on the empire that carries her. _(DAR 113.)_

### The Waters, the Horns, and the Great City (Revelation 17:12-18)

- **Revelation 17:12-13** — "The ten horns... are ten kings... these have one mind, and shall give their power and strength unto the beast." The kingdoms of Europe were once "unanimous in giving their support to the papacy." _(DAR 661.1-2.)_
- **Revelation 17:14** — "These shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings." The final battle — the title "King of kings" belongs to the second coming. _(DAR 661.3.)_
- **Revelation 17:15** — "The waters which thou sawest, where the whore sitteth, are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues." Scripture defines its own symbol: the "many waters" of verse 1 are the multitudes over whom Babylon reigns. _(DAR 662.1.)_
- **Revelation 17:16** — "The ten horns... these shall hate the whore, and shall make her desolate and naked... and burn her with fire." The same civil powers that upheld her at last turn and destroy her — her judgment. _(DAR 662.1.)_
- **Revelation 17:18** — "The woman which thou sawest is that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth." The woman = the city = "the great Roman power" — papal Rome ruling over the kings of the earth. _(DAR 504.2; DAR 602.1.)_

### Babylon: Her Name, Her Cup, Her Fall (Revelation 14:8; 18:1-5)

- **Genesis 11:9** — "Therefore is the name of it called Babel; because the LORD did there confound the language." "Babylon" is derived from "Babel" and "signifies confusion" — the fit name for apostate, sect-divided religion. _(GC 381.)_
- **Jeremiah 51:7** — "Babylon hath been a golden cup in the LORD's hand, that made all the earth drunken: the nations have drunken of her wine." The golden cup and the intoxicating wine of Revelation 17 are drawn straight from ancient Babylon — the type of the spiritual harlot. _(DAR 657-658.)_
- **Revelation 14:8** — "Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication." The second angel's message: a moral fall — the spreading of false doctrine to all nations. _(GC 381; DAR 663.2.)_
- **Revelation 16:19** — "Great Babylon came in remembrance before God, to give unto her the cup of the wine of the fierceness of his wrath." The cup she filled for others is at last returned to her in judgment. _(DAR 657.2.)_
- **Revelation 18:1-3** — "Another angel come down from heaven, having great power; and the earth was lightened with his glory... Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen, and is become the habitation of devils." The loud cry — the final, fuller proclamation of the fall, now embracing all apostate religion. _(DAR 663.1-3.)_
- **Revelation 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues." God still has His people within Babylon; they are called out before the plagues fall. _(DAR 663.3; GC 383.)_
- **Revelation 18:5** — "For her sins have reached unto heaven, and God hath remembered her iniquities." The cup of her guilt is full; judgment is sealed. _(DAR 663.)_
- **Revelation 18:24** — "In her was found the blood of prophets, and of saints, and of all that were slain upon the earth." The persecuting harlot, drunk with martyr-blood, is held to full account. _(DAR 658.4.)_
- **Revelation 19:2** — "He hath judged the great whore, which did corrupt the earth with her fornication, and hath avenged the blood of his servants at her hand." Heaven's verdict and vindication of the saints she slew. _(DAR 680.)_

### The Mother and Her Daughters — Fallen Protestantism

- **Ezekiel 23:4** — "Aholah the elder, and Aholibah her sister... Samaria is Aholah, and Jerusalem Aholibah." The two-sister figure: a mother-apostasy reproduced in her offspring — the prototype of Babylon and her harlot daughters. _(GC 382-383.)_
- **Revelation 17:5** — Babylon is named "THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS." She is not Rome to the exclusion of all others; the title "mother" proves "there are other independent religious organizations that constitute the apostate daughters, and belong to the same great family." _(DAR 658.2.)_
- **Revelation 14:8 with 18:2** — "Babylon is fallen, is fallen." By her daughters "must be symbolized churches that cling to her doctrines and traditions" — Protestant bodies "once pure" that "have become corrupt," forming "iniquitous connection with the kings of the earth" or seeking "the favor of the world." _(GC 382-383; DAR 663.2.)_

# Part IV — Sanctuary & Consummation

# 20. The Sanctuary, the Cleansing, and the Investigative Judgment (1844)

> _The disappointment of 1844 was not the failure of prophecy but the failure of an interpretation. The 2300 days ended exactly on time, but the sanctuary to be cleansed was never the earth — it was the true tabernacle in heaven, of which Moses' was the pattern (Heb. 8-9). As the earthly high priest cleansed the sanctuary on the tenth day of the seventh month from the recorded sins of Israel (Lev. 16), so at the close of the 2300 days our High Priest passed into the most holy place to begin the antitypical Day of Atonement — the investigative judgment. This corrected the two errors of the Adventists: not the earth but the heavenly sanctuary; not the second advent but the opening of the judgment. (DAR 145-157; GC 409-432, 479-491; EW 250-253.)_

### The True Sanctuary Is in Heaven — the Earthly Was Its Pattern

- **Exodus 25:8-9** — "Let them make me a sanctuary; that I may dwell among them... according to all that I shew thee, after the pattern." The earthly tabernacle was built to a heavenly pattern shown Moses in the mount; therefore the original it copied exists above. _(GC 412-415; STTHD 197.)_
- **Hebrews 8:1-2** — We have "an high priest... a minister of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man." Christ ministers in the genuine sanctuary in heaven, not a building made with hands. _(GC 413-414; STTHD 197.1.)_
- **Hebrews 8:5** — The earthly priests "serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things," Moses being charged to "make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount." The shadow proves the substance; the type proves the antitype. _(GC 414-415; TTHDS 15.2.)_
- **Hebrews 9:1-5** — The first covenant had "a worldly sanctuary" — the candlestick, table, and shewbread in the first apartment ("the sanctuary"); within the second veil "the Holiest of all," the ark and the mercyseat. Scripture describes the two apartments to teach the structure of the heavenly. _(GC 411-412; EW 251.3-252.1.)_
- **Hebrews 9:11-12** — "Christ being come an high priest... by a greater and more perfect tabernacle, not made with hands... by his own blood he entered in once into the holy place, having obtained eternal redemption for us." The greater tabernacle is the heavenly sanctuary where Christ now officiates. _(GC 414; STTHD 197.1.)_
- **Hebrews 9:24** — "Christ is not entered into the holy places made with hands... but into heaven itself, now to appear in the presence of God for us." The place of His ministry is heaven itself. _(GC 414.)_

### The Two-Apartment Priestly Ministry

- **Hebrews 9:6-7** — The priests went "always into the first tabernacle," but "into the second went the high priest alone once every year, not without blood... for the errors of the people." Two apartments, two phases of ministry: the continual, and the yearly cleansing. _(GC 418-420; BP3 130.1-130.4.)_
- **Hebrews 9:8** — The Holy Ghost signified "that the way into the holiest of all was not yet made manifest, while as the first tabernacle was yet standing." The most holy ministry opens only after the first-apartment work closes — the very transition of 1844. _(GC 420; EW 251.2.)_
- **Hebrews 4:14-16** — "We have a great high priest, that is passed into the heavens, Jesus the Son of God"; therefore "come boldly unto the throne of grace." Christ's heavenly priesthood is the believer's living access. _(GC 489.)_
- **Hebrews 7:25** — He "is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them." The first-apartment ministry of intercession ran for eighteen centuries. _(GC 421.2.)_
- **Hebrews 6:19-20** — Our hope "entereth into that within the veil; whither the forerunner is for us entered, even Jesus, made an high priest for ever." Christ as forerunner went within the veil — quoted by GC to fix the place of His ministration. _(GC 421.1.)_
- **Hebrews 10:19-22** — We have "boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus... through the veil, that is to say, his flesh." The blood that opens the holiest is His own. _(GC 489; TTHDS 21.3.)_

### The Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16) Is the Type of the Cleansing

- **Leviticus 16:2** — Aaron must "come not at all times into the holy place within the vail before the mercy seat... that he die not." The most holy place was entered on one day only — the type of one closing work. _(BP3 130.3; TTHDS 21.1.)_
- **Leviticus 16:15-16** — The high priest brought the goat's blood "within the vail... and sprinkle it upon the mercy seat," making "an atonement for the holy place, because of the uncleanness of the children of Israel... in all their sins." The sanctuary was cleansed of the recorded sins borne into it through the year. _(BP3 130.3; STTHD 249.1; GC 418-419.)_
- **Leviticus 16:18-19** — He sprinkled the blood "seven times, and cleanse it, and hallow it from the uncleanness of the children of Israel." The act is expressly called a cleansing — accomplished by blood, not by fire. _(BP3 130.4; STTHD 249.1.)_
- **Leviticus 16:29-30** — "In the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month, ye shall afflict your souls... For on that day shall the priest make an atonement for you, to cleanse you, that ye may be clean from all your sins." A fixed day of soul-searching and final cleansing for Israel. _(BP3 130.1-130.3; TTHDS 21.1.)_
- **Leviticus 16:33-34** — "He shall make an atonement for the holy sanctuary... for all the people... to make an atonement for the children of Israel for all their sins once a year." The closing service of the yearly round — the last work of the priest. _(STTHD 249.1; GC 352.2.)_
- **Leviticus 23:27-29** — "On the tenth day of this seventh month there shall be a day of atonement... ye shall afflict your souls"; whoever "shall not be afflicted in that same day... shall be cut off." Probation closed for any who neglected the day — the type of the close of probation in the judgment. _(GC 400, 430; BP3 130.4.)_

### What the Heavenly Cleansing Removes — Sin from the Record

- **Hebrews 9:22-23** — "Almost all things are by the law purged with blood... It was therefore necessary that the patterns of things in the heavens should be purified with these; but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than these." Both sanctuaries are cleansed; the heavenly with Christ's blood. _(GC 417.2-417.3; TTHDS 21.3.)_
- **Hebrews 9:26** — "Now once in the end of the world hath he appeared to put away sin by the sacrifice of himself." The putting-away of sin is the work — the heavenly sanctuary holds the recorded sins to be blotted out. _(GC 421.3; STTHD 249.)_
- **Hebrews 9:27-28** — "As it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment: so Christ was once offered... and unto them that look for him shall he appear the second time without sin unto salvation." Judgment precedes the second appearing; the cleansing comes before the coming. _(GC 421.3-422.1; GC 352.2.)_
- **Acts 3:19-21** — "Repent... that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come"; the heaven must receive Christ "until the times of restitution." The blotting out of sins is a future, definite work tied to Christ's return — not the moment of pardon. _(GC 485-486.)_
- **Malachi 3:1-3** — "The Lord, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple"; He sits "as a refiner and purifier of silver... that they may offer unto the Lord an offering in righteousness." The Lord's coming to His temple is His entry into the most holy to purify His people — the same event as Daniel 8:14. _(GC 424-426; GC 426.1.)_

### Daniel 8:14 — the 2300 Days End, the Sanctuary Is Cleansed (1844)

- **Daniel 8:13** — "How long shall be the vision concerning the daily... to give both the sanctuary and the host to be trodden under foot?" The question that frames the answer: how long until the sanctuary is vindicated. _(DAR 154-157; STTHD 197.)_
- **Daniel 8:14** — "Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed." On the year-day principle (Num. 14:34; Eze. 4:6) the 2300 years, dated from 457 B.C., reach to A.D. 1844; and since no earthly sanctuary then stood, the sanctuary cleansed is the heavenly. _(DAR 145-157, 192; GC 326-329, 417.1; STTHD 249.2.)_
- **Daniel 9:24** — "Seventy weeks are determined" — cut off — upon thy people; the 490 years are the first allotment of the 2300, dating both periods from 457 B.C. and fixing 1844 as the terminus. _(DAR 192; GC 326-327, 410.)_
- **Daniel 7:13-14** — "One like the Son of man came... to the Ancient of days... and there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom." Christ's approach to the Ancient of Days at the judgment is the same event as His coming to the temple in Daniel 8 and Malachi 3. _(GC 426.1; DAR 144.)_

### Daniel 7:9-10 and Revelation 11:19 — the Judgment Scene Opened in the Most Holy

- **Daniel 7:9-10** — "The Ancient of days did sit... the judgment was set, and the books were opened." The investigative judgment is a courtroom scene in heaven, its verdict drawn from the books of record. _(GC 479-480; DAR 144.)_
- **Revelation 11:19** — "The temple of God was opened in heaven, and there was seen in his temple the ark of his testament." The opening of the most holy place — where the ark and the law are — depicts the very transition of 1844, when the inner apartment ministry began. _(GC 433; EW 251.3-252.2; DAR 503.)_
- **Revelation 14:7** — "Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come... worship him that made heaven, and earth." The first angel's message announces the opening of the investigative judgment in 1844 — the right message at the right time. _(GC 352.2, 424-425; DAR 583.)_

### The Two Errors Corrected — and the Doctrine the Disappointment Uncovered

- **Daniel 8:14 (the sanctuary)** — Miller, holding the "popular view," made the earth the sanctuary and its cleansing the purification of the earth by fire at the advent; his error was "accepting the popular view as to what constitutes the sanctuary." Error one: not the earth, but the heavenly sanctuary. _(GC 352.1; STTHD 19.3, 21.1.)_
- **Daniel 8:14 (the event)** — The cleansing was "the closing work of the atonement — a removal or putting away of sin," involving "a work of investigation, a work of judgment," immediately preceding the advent — not the advent itself. Error two: not the second coming, but the opening of the judgment. _(GC 352.2; GC 421.2-422.1.)_
- **Revelation 3:7-8 (EW)** — Christ "had shut the door of the holy place... and had opened the door into the most holy, which He opened in 1844, where the ark is." The shut door and the open door are the change of apartments at the end of the 2300 days. _(EW 42.2-43.1.)_
- **The vindication of the disappointed** — "Man has erred; but there has been no failure on the part of God... man erroneously believed the earth to be the sanctuary." Jesus "has risen up and shut the door of the holy place... and has opened a door into the most holy place and entered in to cleanse the sanctuary." The prophecy stood; the interpretation was mended. _(EW 250.1-251.1.)_
- **The center of present truth** — "The subject of the sanctuary and the investigative judgment should be clearly understood by the people of God... The sanctuary in heaven is the very center of Christ's work in behalf of men." This is the doctrine the disappointment uncovered — the key that opened the whole prophetic structure. _(GC 488.2-488.3.)_

# 21. The Law and the Sabbath in Prophecy

> _The last great controversy is fought over the law of God, and over its heart, the Sabbath. When the temple in heaven is opened, the ark — the receptacle of the ten commandments — is seen (Rev. 11:19), and the law is shown unchanged. The dragon makes war on the remnant who "keep the commandments of God" (Rev. 12:17; 14:12); the little horn "thought to change times and laws" (Dan. 7:25) — and the only law it dared touch was the fourth commandment, tearing out the Sabbath and erecting Sunday in its place. The Sabbath is the seal of God and the memorial of creation, restored in the first angel's call to "worship him that made heaven, and earth" (Rev. 14:7; Isa. 58). The commandment-keepers stand at the close of probation. (DAR 140-141, 627-628; GC 433-454, 640; EW 32-33.)_

### The ark in the heavenly temple holds the law

- **Revelation 11:19** — "The temple of God was opened in heaven, and there was seen in his temple the ark of his testament." The ark, by definition, is the receptacle of the law; the heavenly sanctuary contains the great original of which Sinai's tables were a copy. This opening marks the beginning of the judgment, when attention is drawn back to God's law. _(GC 433.1-2.)_
- **Hebrews 9:4** — The earthly ark held "the tables of the covenant" — the ten commandments. The ark's value was solely as the place of the law; so the opening of the heavenly ark sets the Decalogue before the church. _(GC 433.2.)_
- **Hebrews 8:5** — The earthly priests "serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things." The pattern was shown Moses in the mount; the law in the heavenly ark is "the great original," its earthly transcript unerring. _(GC 434.1.)_
- **Revelation 15:5** — "The temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven was opened." The sanctuary is called the place of "the testimony" — another name for the law of God — confirming the law stands at the center of heaven's worship. _(GC 433.1.)_
- **Early Writings (EW 32-33)** — In vision the ark was seen, the tables opened, "the ten commandments written... with the finger of God," and "the fourth, the Sabbath commandment, shone above them all... a halo of glory was all around it." The Sabbath was not nailed to the cross; "the pope had changed it... for he was to change times and laws." _(EW 32.3-33.1.)_

### The controversy is over the commandments

- **Revelation 12:17** — "The dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." The last church is marked by commandment-keeping; this is the very point of Satan's final assault. _(DAR 516.3.)_
- **Revelation 14:12** — "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." The fruit of the threefold message is a commandment-keeping company — distinguished, of necessity, by the seventh-day Sabbath. _(DAR 627.3; GC 453.3.)_
- **Revelation 22:14** — "Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city." Obedience to the commandments is the condition of entrance to the city of God — the law's authority reaches to the very end. _(Cf. DAR 627.3.)_
- **Ecclesiastes 12:13** — "Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man." The whole of human duty is bound up in the commandments; the controversy turns on whether men will obey or transgress them. _(Cf. GC 452.2.)_
- **John 14:15** — "If ye love me, keep my commandments." Christ makes obedience the test of love; the remnant's commandment-keeping is no legalism but loyalty to the Lawgiver. _(Cf. GC 453.3.)_

### The little horn thought to change the law — and touched the Sabbath

- **Daniel 7:25** — The little horn "shall... think to change times and laws." These are not human laws (which it could change at will) but the laws of the Most High — which it could only _think_ to change, not truly change. The papacy expunged the second commandment, divided the tenth, and tore the Sabbath from the fourth, erecting Sunday in its place. _(DAR 140.3-141.1.)_
- **Daniel 7:8** — The little horn rose "among" the ten horns with "a mouth speaking great things." Identified as the papacy (538-1798), this is the power that presumed to alter the Decalogue — the "great words against the most High." _(DAR 140.2-3.)_
- **Daniel 7:25 (with 2 Thess. 2)** — Daniel's little horn and Paul's "man of sin" are complements: "Only by changing God's law could the papacy exalt itself above God." The change of the Sabbath is precisely how that self-exaltation was accomplished. _(GC 446.1.)_
- **Isaiah 24:5** — "They have transgressed the laws, changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant." Scripture foretells a transgression that _changes_ an ordinance — fulfilled in the substitution of Sunday for the Sabbath of the everlasting covenant. _(Cf. GC 446.2.)_
- **The Catholic catechism** — Compared with the Bible, the catechisms drop the second commandment, split the tenth, and rewrite the fourth to enjoin Sunday — Rome openly claiming the change was "made by the church," not by God. _(DAR 554.1-2.)_

### The law of God is perpetual and unchangeable

- **Matthew 5:17-18** — "Think not that I am come to destroy the law... Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law." Christ did not abolish but established the law; its smallest stroke stands while heaven and earth endure. _(GC 434.1.)_
- **Psalm 111:7-8** — "All his commandments are sure. They stand fast for ever and ever." The commandments are not temporary ordinances but eternal verities — incapable of being changed by any earthly power. _(Cf. GC 434.1.)_
- **Psalm 89:34** — "My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips." God Himself will not alter His word; the claim that He transferred the Sabbath's sanctity to another day contradicts His own declaration. _(EW 33.1.)_
- **Psalm 119:142** — "Thy law is the truth." The law is identified with truth itself; to change it is to corrupt the truth — the very work charged to the little horn. _(Cf. GC 434.1.)_
- **James 2:10-12** — "Whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is guilty of all." The Decalogue is an indivisible unity; to break the fourth commandment is to break the whole — so men "shall be judged by the law of liberty." _(Cf. EW 33.1.)_
- **Romans 3:31** — "Do we then make void the law through faith? God forbid: yea, we establish the law." The gospel does not annul the law but upholds it; faith and obedience stand together. _(Waggoner / Jones, the law and the gospel.)_
- **Romans 7:7** — "I had not known sin, but by the law." The law remains the standard that defines sin; were it abolished, sin itself would be undefinable. _(Cf. GC 467-468.)_

### The Sabbath: memorial of creation and seal of God

- **Genesis 2:1-3** — "God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work." Instituted at creation, before sin and before Israel, the Sabbath is the world's first memorial and belongs to all mankind. _(GC 453.1.)_
- **Exodus 20:8-11** — "Remember the sabbath day... the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God... For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth... and rested the seventh day." The fourth commandment alone names and titles the Lawgiver as Creator — making it the seal of His law. _(GC 434.2; 452.1.)_
- **Mark 2:27-28** — "The sabbath was made for man... therefore the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath." Made for man at creation, the Sabbath is Christ's own institution; He is its Lord, not its abolisher. _(Cf. GC 446.3.)_
- **Luke 23:56** — The women "rested the sabbath day according to the commandment." Even across the crucifixion the Sabbath stood unchanged — Christ resting in the tomb on the seventh day, His followers honoring it "according to the commandment." _(Cf. EW 33.1.)_
- **Ezekiel 20:12** — "I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them." The Sabbath is God's appointed sign of sanctification — a sign that identifies His people. _(Bates, THB 44.1.)_
- **Ezekiel 20:20** — "Hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign between me and you, that ye may know that I am the LORD your God." Repeated for emphasis: the Sabbath is the token by which God's worshipers know Him from all others. _(Bates, THB 44.1-2.)_
- **Isaiah 8:16** — "Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples." The seal of God's law is found in the fourth commandment — the only precept bearing the name, title, and dominion of the Lawgiver, the Creator. _(GC 452.1.)_
- **Sign and seal are one** — "Sign and seal are synonymous terms" (Rom. 4:11). The Sabbath being the sign between God and His people, it seals His law as genuine; "take this fourth commandment from the ten, and the seal of the living God is gone." _(Bates, THB 44.2.)_

### The Sabbath restored in the last message — the final test

- **Revelation 14:7** — "Worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters." The first angel's call to worship the Creator is in the very language of the fourth commandment — a summons back to the Sabbath as the judgment hour strikes. _(GC 437-438; DAR 585.3.)_
- **Isaiah 58:12-14** — "Thou shalt be called, The repairer of the breach... if thou turn away thy foot from the sabbath... and call the sabbath a delight." A last-day people repair the breach made in God's law by restoring the trodden-down Sabbath — the very work of the third angel's message. _(GC 452.4-453.)_
- **Ezekiel 22:26** — "They have... hid their eyes from my sabbaths, and I am profaned among them." The priests' sin was profaning the holy and hiding their eyes from the Sabbath; the last message exposes and reverses that apostasy. _(Cf. Isa. 58:13.)_
- **Isaiah 66:22-23** — "As the new heavens and the new earth... so shall your seed and your name remain. And it shall come to pass... from one sabbath to another, shall all flesh come to worship before me." The Sabbath outlasts this world and is kept in the earth made new — the final proof of its perpetuity. _(Cf. GC 453.1.)_
- **The line of demarcation** — The third angel's message brings its adherents to the seventh day, the only mark that distinguishes them; "no plainer line of demarcation could be drawn between two classes" — Sabbath-keepers and Sunday-keepers. _(DAR 627.3-628.1.)_
- **Two seals, two marks** — God's seal is His Sabbath; the beast's mark is its rival sabbath, Sunday. "Christendom will at last be divided into just two classes" — those sealed with the seal of the living God and those who receive the mark. _(DAR 628.2.)_
- **The seal of the living God** — "Too late they see that the Sabbath of the fourth commandment is the seal of the living God." In the final crisis the Sabbath becomes the great test of loyalty, distinguishing the worshipers of God from the worshipers of the beast. _(GC 640.1; DAR 627-628.)_
- **Worship Christ, keep His commandments** — "If thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments" (Matt. 19:17). The Lord who is worshiped as Creator in the first message is the Lord who points men to obedience as the way of life — the law and the gospel meeting in the Sabbath. _(Cf. GC 453.3.)_

# 22. The Two Covenants — the Everlasting Gospel in Prophecy

> _The two covenants are not two periods of time but two conditions of the heart. The "old" is man's own promise — "all this we will do" — self-confidence that ends in bondage (Hagar, Sinai). The "new" is God's promise, His law written on the heart by faith (Sarah, the Jerusalem above, liberty). One everlasting covenant runs from Eden through Abraham, sealed in the blood of Christ, and this righteousness by faith is the very "everlasting gospel" the first angel proclaims (Rev. 14:6) — the faith by which the 144,000 and the remnant live. (EVCO 6, 83-84; GTI 184; GC 467-478.)_

### One Everlasting Covenant — from Eden, confirmed in Christ

- **Genesis 3:15** — "I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed." The first preaching of the gospel: the moment man sinned and lost his inheritance, restoration was promised through the woman's Seed. The everlasting covenant begins here, not at Sinai. _(EVCO 38.)_
- **Genesis 17:7** — "I will establish my covenant... for an everlasting covenant, to be a God unto thee, and to thy seed after thee." One covenant, called everlasting, sealed with Abraham — the same one "through the blood of the everlasting covenant" by which men are perfected. _(EVCO 83.)_
- **Hebrews 13:20** — "the blood of the everlasting covenant." There is but one everlasting covenant, and it stands in Christ's blood — the unbroken line that runs through the whole Bible. _(EVCO 6, 83-84.)_
- **Galatians 3:8** — "the scripture... preached before the gospel unto Abraham, saying, In thee shall all nations be blessed." The covenant to Abraham was the everlasting gospel itself — gospel and covenant are one. _(EVCO 84; GTI 132.)_

### The Old Covenant — the people's own promise (self-righteousness, bondage)

- **Galatians 4:22-25** — "These are the two covenants; the one from the mount Sinai, which gendereth to bondage, which is Agar." Hagar, the bondmaid, answers to "Jerusalem which now is, and is in bondage with her children." The old covenant is symbolized by the slave-woman: she can bring forth none but slaves. _(GTI 184, 187.)_
- **Exodus 19:8** — "All that the LORD hath spoken we will do." The fatal error: the people made themselves the surety. Instead of "let it be done unto us according to Thy will," they assumed the responsibility of working the works of God in their own strength. _(EVCO 332.)_
- **Romans 10:3** — they "going about to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted themselves unto the righteousness of God." Their promises "were good for nothing" — this self-confidence is the essence of the old covenant. _(EVCO 332.)_
- **Hebrews 8:7** — "For if that first covenant had been faultless, then should no place have been sought for the second." The fault was not in God's holy law but in the people who promised in their own strength (v. 8, "finding fault with them"). _(EVCO 323, 326.)_
- **Galatians 4:24** — "which gendereth to bondage." The law spoken from Sinai, however holy, "can not beget freemen" any more than Hagar could bear a free child — the moment it is leaned on for righteousness, it brings bondage. _(GTI 184; EVCO 344.)_

### The New Covenant — God's promise, the law written on the heart (liberty)

- **Jeremiah 31:31-33** — "I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people." The new covenant is not a new law but the old law in a new place — God Himself does the writing. _(EVCO 326; GC 467-468.)_
- **Hebrews 8:8-10** — "I will make a new covenant... I will put my laws into their mind, and write them in their hearts." Both covenants are made "with the house of Israel" and founded upon promises; the new is better because its promises are God's, not man's. _(EVCO 326.)_
- **Hebrews 8:6** — Christ is "the mediator of a better covenant, which was established upon better promises." The superiority lies wholly in the Promiser: God cannot fail. _(EVCO 323, 326.)_
- **Hebrews 8:12-13** — "I will be merciful to their unrighteousness... their sins... will I remember no more." Forgiveness, not human resolve, is the foundation of the new covenant. _(EVCO 326; GC 477.)_
- **Hebrews 10:16-17** — "I will put my laws into their hearts... And their sins and iniquities will I remember no more." The same promise restated: heart-obedience plus full pardon. _(GC 467-468.)_
- **Ezekiel 36:26-27** — "A new heart also will I give you... And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes." The mechanism of the new covenant: God supplies the heart that keeps His law. _(EVCO 344; GC 468.)_
- **Galatians 4:26-28** — "Jerusalem which is above is free, which is the mother of us all... we, brethren, as Isaac was, are the children of promise." Sarah, the freewoman, is the new covenant; her children are free, born "by promise." _(GTI 184, 187.)_
- **2 Corinthians 3:6** — "the letter killeth, but the spirit giveth life." Ministers of "the new testament" deal not in the letter (the old covenant's dead works) but in the life-giving Spirit. _(GC 467-468.)_

_(Gal. 4:22-31; EVCO 323-326, 332, 344; GTI 184-187.)_

### Justified by Faith, Not by the Law — Abraham the Pattern

- **Genesis 15:6** — "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." Abraham was justified by faith before circumcision, before Sinai — the pattern for every believer. _(EVCO 84; WOR 77.)_
- **Romans 4:3-5** — "to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness." Righteousness is reckoned, not earned — the heart of the new covenant. _(WOR 77; GC 472.)_
- **Romans 4:13-16** — "the promise... was not to Abraham, or to his seed, through the law, but through the righteousness of faith... Therefore it is of faith, that it might be by grace; to the end the promise might be sure to all the seed." If the inheritance were by law, faith is void; it is by grace so the promise cannot fail. _(WOR 77-78; EVCO 84.)_
- **Galatians 3:6-9** — "they which be of faith are blessed with faithful Abraham." Those of faith — Jew or Gentile — are the true children of Abraham and heirs of the same gospel-promise. _(GTI 132; EVCO 84.)_

### The Covenant Is in the Seed — Christ, and a Testament in His Blood

- **Galatians 3:16** — "to Abraham and his seed were the promises made... not... seeds, as of many; but as of one... which is Christ." The whole covenant centers in one Seed; all the promises are Yea and Amen in Him. _(GTI 132; EVCO 84.)_
- **Galatians 3:17-18** — "the covenant, that was confirmed before of God in Christ, the law, which was four hundred and thirty years after, cannot disannul." The law given at Sinai could not annul or replace the earlier covenant of promise — proof the everlasting covenant antedates and outranks Sinai. _(GTI 132; EVCO 83-84.)_
- **Genesis 22:18** — "in thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed." The promise reaches every nation through the Seed — the same scope as the everlasting gospel of Revelation 14:6. _(EVCO 84.)_
- **Hebrews 9:15-17** — "he is the mediator of the new testament, that by means of death... they which are called might receive the promise of eternal inheritance... For where a testament is, there must also... be the death of the testator." The new covenant becomes "of force" only by Christ's death — it is sealed in His blood. _(EVCO 83-84; Heb. 13:20.)_
- **Hebrews 9:11-14** — Christ "by his own blood... entered in once into the holy place, having obtained eternal redemption." The blood of the everlasting covenant ministered in the heavenly sanctuary — the gospel foundation under the whole prophetic chain. _(EVCO 83-84.)_

### The Law Established by Faith, Not Made Void

- **Galatians 3:10-13** — "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us." The curse falls only on those under the old covenant's "works of the law"; Christ bears it so the blessing of Abraham reaches the Gentiles. _(GTI 184; GC 467-468.)_
- **Galatians 3:19, 24** — the law "was added because of transgressions, till the seed should come"; it is "our schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ, that we might be justified by faith." The law drives the sinner to the promise — it never was a rival way of salvation. _(EVCO 344; GC 467.)_
- **Galatians 3:21** — "Is the law then against the promises of God? God forbid." Law and gospel never conflict; the law cannot give life, so life must come by the promise. _(EVCO 344.)_
- **Romans 3:31** — "Do we then make void the law through faith? God forbid: yea, we establish the law." Faith does not abolish the law but writes it in the heart — the new covenant keeps the very commandments of God. _(GC 468; Rev. 14:12.)_
- **Galatians 3:26-29** — "ye are all the children of God by faith... if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise." All barriers fall in Christ; faith makes us heirs of the everlasting covenant. _(GTI 187; EVCO 84.)_

### The Everlasting Gospel — the Faith of the Remnant

- **Revelation 14:6** — "another angel... having the everlasting gospel to preach unto them that dwell on the earth." The first angel proclaims the same everlasting gospel preached to Abraham — righteousness by faith in the everlasting covenant — to every nation before the end. _(EVCO 84; GC 467-478.)_
- **Revelation 14:12** — "Here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." The remnant unites the law written in the heart (new covenant) with the faith of Jesus — the living proof that faith establishes the law. _(GC 468, 477; EVCO 344.)_
- **Hebrews 8:10 with Revelation 22:14** — the law written within (new covenant) produces those who "do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life." The covenant promise reaches its consummation in the restored Eden inheritance promised to Abraham. _(EVCO 83-84; GC 477.)_
- **Galatians 4:30-31** — "Cast out the bondwoman and her son... we are not children of the bondwoman, but of the free." The final separation: those who live by their own promise (Babylon's bondage) are cast out; the free children of the promise stand. This is the experience of righteousness by faith that prepares the remnant for translation. _(GTI 187; GC 472-478.)_

# 23. The State of the Dead and the Last Deception (Spiritualism)

> _The truth about death is the bulwark against the final deception. The dead sleep an unconscious sleep, knowing nothing, awaiting the call of the resurrection morning; there is no immortal soul that survives the body, for God alone hath immortality. This one truth shuts the door on spiritualism — Satan's masterpiece, by which he personates the dead, counterfeits departed friends, and works lying miracles to deceive the world. The first lie, "Ye shall not surely die," laid the foundation of spiritualism, and at the end binds Romanism, apostate Protestantism, and spiritualism into the threefold union of Revelation 16:13-14. "To the law and to the testimony" is the only test. (GC 531-562; GC 588; DAR 646-648; EW 262-263.)_

### The dead sleep — they know nothing

- **Ecclesiastes 9:5-6** — "The living know that they shall die: but the dead know not any thing." Their love, hatred, and envy have perished; they have no more portion under the sun. The plainest statement of Scripture flatly contradicts the doctrine of conscious survival. _(GC 545; GC 551.)_
- **Ecclesiastes 9:10** — "There is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest." The grave is a place of silence and inactivity, not of conscious bliss or torment. _(GC 545.)_
- **Psalm 146:3-4** — "His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish." David declares man is not conscious in death — the very text Sister White cites to overthrow the immortality error. _(GC 545.3.)_
- **Psalm 115:17** — "The dead praise not the LORD, neither any that go down into silence." The dead are silent; they are not in heaven praising God. _(GC 549-550.)_
- **Psalm 6:5** — "In death there is no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks?" There is no remembrance, no consciousness, in death. _(GC 549-550.)_
- **Job 14:21** — "His sons come to honour, and he knoweth it not... but he perceiveth it not of them." The dead do not know what passes among the living — therefore they cannot return to "minister" to them. _(GC 551-552.)_
- **Job 7:9-10** — "He that goeth down to the grave shall come up no more. He shall return no more to his house." The dead do not haunt the living or revisit their homes; any "spirit" claiming to do so is a deceiver. _(GC 556.)_

### Man is mortal — God alone hath immortality

- **Genesis 2:7** — "Man became a living soul." Man does not _have_ a soul; the union of body and breath _is_ the living soul. The soul is not a separate immortal entity. _(GC 533.)_
- **Ecclesiastes 12:7** — "The dust return to the earth as it was: and the spirit shall return unto God who gave it." At death the breath (spirit) of life returns to God; the man returns to dust — he does not float on as a conscious ghost. _(GC 533.)_
- **1 Timothy 6:15-16** — God "only hath immortality." Immortality belongs to God alone; man does not possess it inherently. _(GC 533; GC 545.2.)_
- **Genesis 3:24** — Cherubim and a flaming sword kept the way of the tree of life, that fallen man should not "live forever" in sin. Immortality, forfeited by transgression, is bestowed only through Christ. _(GC 533.3.)_

### Death is a sleep — Christ taught it so

- **John 11:11-14** — "Our friend Lazarus sleepeth... Lazarus is dead." Christ called death a sleep; the disciples thought He spoke of rest in sleep, but He meant Lazarus had died. Death is sleep, not conscious life. _(GC 549-550.)_
- **John 11:43-44** — "Lazarus, come forth." Lazarus, four days dead, was called _back_ — not down from heaven nor up from torment. Had he been in bliss, Christ would have done him no kindness recalling him. _(GC 549-550.)_
- **Job 14:12** — "Man lieth down, and riseth not: till the heavens be no more, they shall not awake, nor be raised out of their sleep." The dead sleep until the final waking — the resurrection, not death, restores them. _(GC 549.)_
- **Acts 2:29** — "David... is both dead and buried, and his sepulchre is with us unto this day." Peter testifies that even David, a man after God's own heart, lay in his tomb. _(GC 545-549.)_
- **Acts 2:34** — "David is not ascended into the heavens." The plainest apostolic word: the righteous dead have not yet gone to heaven; they await the resurrection. _(GC 549-550.)_

### The hope of the sleeping saints — the resurrection

- **1 Thessalonians 4:13-17** — "Them which sleep in Jesus will God bring with him... the dead in Christ shall rise first." The sleeping saints are awakened at the descent of the Lord with a shout and the trump of God; only then are they caught up — proof they were not previously in heaven. _(GC 549-550.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:51-54** — "We shall all be changed, in a moment... at the last trump... the dead shall be raised incorruptible." Mortals put on immortality _at the resurrection_, not at death — "then shall be brought to pass... Death is swallowed up in victory." _(GC 549-550.)_
- **John 5:28-29** — "All that are in the graves shall hear his voice, and shall come forth." The dead are _in their graves_ awaiting His voice — the resurrection of life and the resurrection of damnation. _(GC 545-549.)_
- **Daniel 12:2** — "Many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake." The dead sleep in the dust until they awake — some to everlasting life, some to shame and contempt. _(GC 545-549.)_
- **Job 19:25-26** — "I know that my redeemer liveth... in my flesh shall I see God." Job's hope is not a disembodied soul but the redeemer standing at the latter day and flesh restored — resurrection hope. _(GC 549-550.)_

### The first lie — foundation of spiritualism

- **Genesis 3:4** — "Ye shall not surely die." The serpent's contradiction of God was "the first sermon ever preached upon the immortality of the soul," and the foundation of all spiritualism was laid in that assurance given to Eve in Eden. _(GC 533.2; GC 561.2.)_
- **1 Timothy 4:1** — "In the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils." Paul foretells a last-day apostasy under demon teaching — the very spiritualism now spreading. _(GC 556; EW 262.2.)_
- **The Roman dunghill** — The immortality of the soul is a false doctrine Rome borrowed from paganism and incorporated into Christendom; Martin Luther classed it among the "monstrous fables" of the Roman decretals. _(GC 549.2.)_

### Satan personates the dead — the masterpiece of deception

- **2 Corinthians 11:14** — "Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light." The arch-deceiver disguises himself; "Satan is converted, after the modern order of things," appearing as an angel of light through spiritualism. _(GC 588.2; EW 262.1.)_
- **Matthew 24:24** — "False Christs, and false prophets... shall shew great signs and wonders; insomuch that, if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect." Satan's spirits personating "beloved relatives or friends" will work miracles to sustain their pretensions. _(GC 553.2; GC 560.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:13-14** — The two-horned beast "doeth great wonders... and deceiveth them that dwell on the earth by the means of those miracles." Lying wonders, not truth, are the engine of the final deception. _(DAR 542; GC 553.)_
- **Counterfeit of the dead** — "He has power to bring before men the appearance of their departed friends. The counterfeit is perfect; the familiar look, the words, the tone, are reproduced with marvelous distinctness." Knowing the dead are _asleep and unconscious_ is the only safeguard. _(GC 552.1; EW 262.1.)_

### "Regard not them that have familiar spirits"

- **Leviticus 19:31** — "Regard not them that have familiar spirits, neither seek after wizards." God expressly forbids all pretended communion with the dead — these "familiar spirits" are agents of Satan. _(GC 556.2.)_
- **Isaiah 8:19** — "Should not a people seek unto their God? for the living to the dead?" To consult the dead on behalf of the living is folly and rebellion; the living should seek the living God. _(GC 556.)_
- **Isaiah 8:20** — "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them." This is THE test. Every spirit, vision, and wonder is to be measured by the written Word; what contradicts the law of God is darkness. _(GC vii; GC 452.2.)_

### The threefold union and the lying wonders of the last days

- **Revelation 16:13** — "Three unclean spirits like frogs come out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet." Spiritualism (dragon), the Papacy (beast), and apostate Protestantism (false prophet) unite in one threefold confederacy against God's people. _(DAR 646.3; GC 588.)_
- **Revelation 16:14** — "They are the spirits of devils, working miracles... to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty." These working-miracle demons go forth to the kings of the earth; the supernatural fires this last alliance. _(DAR 646.3-648; GC 561.)_
- **Two errors bind the union** — "Through the two great errors, the immortality of the soul and Sunday sacredness, Satan will bring the people under his deceptions." The former lays the foundation of spiritualism; the latter creates the bond of sympathy with Rome. _(GC 588.1.)_
- **Romanism + Protestantism + spiritualism** — Papists and Protestants and spiritualists alike will receive the form of godliness without the power, and Satan determines to sweep all into the ranks of spiritualism — the grand uniting of the three. _(GC 588.3.)_
- **Revelation 18:23** — "By thy sorceries were all nations deceived." Babylon's fall is bound up with her sorceries — the spiritualistic enchantments by which she deceives the nations at the end. _(GC 561; DAR 646.)_

### The conflict just before us

- **Revelation 1:18** — "I am he that liveth, and was dead... and have the keys of hell and of death." Christ alone holds the keys of death and the grave; resurrection is His prerogative, not the dead's natural estate — our anchor against every counterfeit. _(GC 549-550.)_
- **The saints must know the truth of the dead** — "The saints must have a thorough understanding of present truth... They must understand the state of the dead; for the spirits of devils will yet appear to them, professing to be beloved relatives or friends." This delusion will be met face to face; only the prepared will stand. _(EW 262.2-262.3.)_
- **The train of cars** — The rapidity of the delusion was shown as "a train of cars going with the speed of lightning... the whole world was on board," with Satan as the stately conductor; only a little company on the narrow path, "firmly united by the truth," escapes. _(EW 263.1-263.2.)_
- **Stand on the Word alone** — "All whose faith is not firmly established upon the word of God will be deceived and overcome." Against the lying wonders of the last conflict, the Bible — and the Bible only — is the safeguard of God's people. _(GC 560.2; GC 561.)_

# 24. Comparing the Sevens — Churches, Seals, Trumpets, Plagues

> _The book of Revelation runs four "sevens" over the very same stretch of
> history, each viewing it from a different watch-tower. The seven_ **churches**
> _give the church's inward, religious state; the seven_ **seals** _her outward
> providential fortunes; the seven_ **trumpets** _the political and warlike
> judgments on the nations among whom she dwells; and the seven_ **plagues**
> _the final, unmingled wrath poured out on the wicked AFTER probation has
> forever closed. Three of the lines run together through the gospel
> dispensation; the fourth begins only when mercy has ceased to plead. (DAR
> 345, 402, 452, 638-642; GC 627-629.)_

### The four lines at a glance

| Series             | Standpoint           | Subject viewed                     | Reaches                                    | Key text          |
| ------------------ | -------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| **Seven Churches** | Religious            | Spiritual state of the church      | Ephesus (apostolic) → Laodicea (last days) | Rev. 1:11, 20     |
| **Seven Seals**    | Providential         | Outward fortunes of the church     | First century → second coming              | Rev. 6:1-2, 12-14 |
| **Seven Trumpets** | Political / military | Wars and judgments on the nations  | Rome's fall → 1840 → end                   | Rev. 8:2; 9:15    |
| **Seven Plagues**  | Final wrath          | The wicked, after probation closes | Wholly future, "without mixture"           | Rev. 15:1; 16:1-2 |

### Series 1 — The Seven CHURCHES: the religious standpoint

- **Revelation 1:11** — "What thou seest, write in a book, and send it unto the seven churches... Ephesus... Smyrna... Pergamos... Thyatira... Sardis... Philadelphia... Laodicea." Seven literal Asian congregations chosen because their names and conditions sketch seven successive states of the whole church to the end. _(DAR 345.1; 346.3.)_
- **Revelation 1:20** — "The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches: and the seven candlesticks... are the seven churches." The candlestick is the church; the star (angel) is her ministry — Scripture interprets its own symbols. _(DAR 345.1; 347.2.)_
- **Revelation 2:1-5** — Ephesus, "the apostolic age," had received the doctrine of Christ in its purity, yet "left thy first love." This is the church's _inner_ condition, not her outward battles. _(DAR 347.1, 347.3.)_
- **Revelation 1:19** — "Write the things which thou hast seen, and the things which are, and the things which shall be hereafter." The threefold division that frames the whole prophecy as successive periods, not a single moment. _(DAR 346.3.)_

> **Bullet — the Churches' standpoint.** The seven churches give the spiritual,
> religious history of the church — her love, zeal, doctrine, and decline —
> from apostolic Ephesus to lukewarm Laodicea. Many expositors (Vitringa,
> Matthew Henry) held that the Spirit here "delineated seven different states
> of the Christian church, which would appear in succession, extending to the
> coming of our Lord." _(DAR 346.3; 347.1.)_

### Series 2 — The Seven SEALS: the providential standpoint

- **Revelation 6:1-2** — The Lamb opens the first seal; "behold a white horse... he went forth conquering, and to conquer." The white horse is "a fit emblem of the triumphs of the gospel in the first century," its whiteness the purity of faith — the church's outward fortunes, not her trumpet-wars. _(DAR 402.1; 403.1.)_
- **Revelation 6:8** — The pale horse: "his name that sat on him was Death... to kill with sword, and with hunger, and with death." The fortunes of the church darken through the centuries of decline and persecution. _(DAR 402-413.)_
- **Revelation 6:9-11** — The fifth seal: souls "under the altar... slain for the word of God... How long, O Lord, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood?" The era of papal martyrdom; white robes given, and a command to "rest yet for a little season" — the church's persecuted providential lot. _(DAR 402; 422-423.)_
- **Revelation 6:12-14** — The sixth seal: "a great earthquake; and the sun became black... the moon became as blood... the stars of heaven fell." The Lisbon earthquake (1755), the Dark Day (1780), the falling stars (1833) — public providences marking the approach of the end. We "stand between the 13th and 14th verses." _(DAR 402; 432.2.)_
- **Revelation 8:1** — The seventh seal: "silence in heaven about the space of half an hour." When heaven empties at Christ's coming; "the world's dream of carnal security" is broken. The seals run from the first century to the second advent. _(DAR 432.4; 433.1.)_

> **Bullet — the Seals' standpoint.** The seven seals embrace the outward,
> providential history of the same church — her conquests, her decline, her
> martyrdoms, and the signs preceding her Lord — "reaching down to the close of
> probationary time." Where the churches show the heart, the seals show the
> fortunes. _(DAR 402.2; 403.1.)_

### Series 3 — The Seven TRUMPETS: the political standpoint

- **Revelation 8:2** — "I saw the seven angels which stood before God; and to them were given seven trumpets." A new and distinct series. "In the seals we have had the history of the church... In the seven trumpets... we have the principal political and warlike events which were to transpire during the same time." _(DAR 453.2-453.3.)_
- **Revelation 8:7** — The first trumpet: "hail and fire mingled with blood... the third part of trees was burnt up." The Gothic invasions that broke Western Rome — judgments on the _nations_, not on the church's spiritual state. _(DAR 453-462.)_
- **Revelation 9:1, 5, 10** — The fifth trumpet (first woe): a fallen star, the bottomless pit, and power "to hurt men five months." The 150 years (a prophetic five months) of Ottoman tormenting — day-for-a-year (Num. 14:34; Eze. 4:6). _(DAR 472-480.)_
- **Revelation 9:15** — The sixth trumpet: "the four angels were loosed, which were prepared for an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year, for to slay the third part of men." The four sultanies on the Euphrates (Aleppo, Iconium, Damascus, Bagdad) loosed to overthrow the Eastern empire. _(DAR 480.2-480.3.)_
- **Revelation 9:15 (the time computed)** — An hour + day + month + year = 391 years and 15 days. Beginning July 27, 1449 (when Constantine reigned only by the Turk's permission), the period "would end Aug. 11, 1840." _(DAR 481.1; 485.1.)_
- **Revelation 9:18-21** — "By these three was the third part of men killed, by the fire, and by the smoke, and by the brimstone." The gunpowder and cannon by which Constantinople fell (1453) and Ottoman supremacy was finally broken — "yet repented not." _(DAR 484.2; 487.3.)_

> **The 1840 test (sixth trumpet).** Josiah Litch, "in 1838, two years before
> the predicted event," published from Revelation 9 that Ottoman independence
> would depart in "A.D. 1840, sometime in the month of August." On the
> **eleventh day of August, 1840**, the sultan's ultimatum passed into foreign
> hands — "the matter would be forever beyond the control" of the Turk — and
> Mohammedan independence in Constantinople departed on the very day. "The
> exact accomplishment... gave a mighty impetus to the great Advent movement."
> _(DAR 485.2; 486.2; 487.1; GC 334.4.)_

> **Bullet — the Trumpets' standpoint.** The seven trumpets give the political
> and military judgments on the nations among whom the church dwells — Rome's
> fall under the four winds of barbarian invasion, the Saracen and Ottoman
> woes, ending in the verifiable date of August 11, 1840. They are God's
> scourges on apostate Christendom, not the spiritual record of the churches.
> _(DAR 453.3; 487.4.)_

### Series 4 — The Seven PLAGUES: the final, unmingled wrath

- **Revelation 15:1** — "Seven angels having the seven last plagues; for in them is filled up the wrath of God." Called the **last** plagues because in them God's wrath is "filled up" — "a manifestation of Heaven's unmingled wrath... for the last generation of the wicked. The work of mercy is then forever past." _(DAR 638.1; 641.1.)_
- **Revelation 15:8** — "The temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God... and no man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues... were fulfilled." No mediation during the plagues — the door of the sanctuary is shut; probation is over before the first vial falls. _(DAR 641; GC 627.3.)_
- **Revelation 16:1-2** — "Go your ways, and pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth. And the first... poured out his vial... and there fell a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast." The plague falls on the very class warned by the third angel — proof it follows the close of his work. _(DAR 641.2-641.3.)_
- **Revelation 14:9-10** — "If any man worship the beast... The same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out **without mixture** into the cup of his indignation." The plagues are this very wine — unmixed with the mercy that has tempered every prior judgment. _(DAR 642.3; GC 627.3.)_
- **Revelation 16:10-12** — The fifth vial fills the beast's kingdom "with darkness," the sixth dries up "the great river Euphrates" — yet men "blasphemed... and repented not." Because mercy has withdrawn, the plagues harden rather than convert. _(DAR 641-650.)_
- **Revelation 16:17** — The seventh vial poured "into the air; and there came a great voice out of the temple of heaven, from the throne, saying, It is done." The wrath finished; the voice of God shaking earth and heaven (cf. Rev. 6:14). _(DAR 432.3.)_
- **Revelation 22:11** — "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... and he that is holy, let him be holy still." The decree that fixes every destiny — the close of probation — spoken just before the plagues; after it, no man's character can change and mercy no longer pleads. _(GC 613.2; cf. GC 627.3.)_

> **Bullet — the Plagues' standpoint.** The seven last plagues stand alone:
> they fall only on the wicked, only on those who have the mark of the beast,
> and only **after probation has closed** — "when Christ ceases His
> intercession in the sanctuary." "All the judgments upon men, prior to the
> close of probation, have been mingled with mercy... but in the final
> judgment, wrath is poured out unmixed with mercy." This is what makes the
> plagues unique among the four sevens. _(GC 627.3; 628.2; DAR 642.3.)_

### Why they are FOUR lines, not one

- **Revelation 4:1** — "Come up hither, and I will shew thee things which must be hereafter." Each new vision (1:11 churches, 6:1 seals, 8:2 trumpets, 15:1 plagues) restarts the prophet's view, sweeping the same ground from a fresh standpoint rather than continuing where the last left off. _(DAR 384; 453.3.)_
- **Revelation 11:18** — "The nations were angry, and thy wrath is come, and the time of the dead, that they should be judged." The seventh trumpet, like the seventh seal and seventh plague, lands at one and the same terminus — the judgment and reward — proving the lines are parallel, not consecutive. _(DAR 504-505.)_
- **Daniel 12:1; Revelation 22:11** — "Michael shall stand up" the moment the third angel's message closes and "mercy no longer pleads"; the unjust are sealed unjust, the holy sealed holy. This single hinge separates the first three sevens (running _through_ probation) from the fourth (poured out _after_ it). _(GC 613.2; 627.3.)_

> **Summary distinguishing all four.** Churches = the church's _religious_ life
> (her spiritual state), Seals = her _providential_ life (her outward
> fortunes), Trumpets = the _political_ judgments on the nations around her —
> all three running together through the gospel dispensation; while Plagues =
> the _final wrath_ on the wicked, alone among the four because it falls only
> after probation closes, "without mixture" of mercy, on those who bear the
> mark of the beast. _(DAR 453.3; 641.3; 642.3; GC 627.3-628.2.)_

# 25. Babylon Fallen, the Loud Cry, and "Come Out of Her"

> _The second angel's announcement of Babylon's fall (Rev. 14:8) is to be repeated at the close, swelling into the loud cry of Revelation 18: another angel comes down from heaven, "having great power," lightens the whole earth with his glory, and cries, "Babylon the great is fallen," adding the call, "Come out of her, my people." This is the final warning, the latter rain, the close of the gospel work — the swelling of the third angel's message before probation ends. Babylon is mother and daughters together: apostate Rome and the fallen Protestant churches that cling to her doctrines. (DAR 663-673; GC 603-612, 390; EW 277-279.)_

### Babylon Identified — Mother and Daughters

- **Revelation 17:5** — "Upon her forehead was a name written, MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS AND ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH." She is a mother, therefore she has daughters — churches that cling to her doctrines and traditions. _(GC 382; DAR 663-664.)_
- **Revelation 17:1-2** — The great whore "sitteth upon many waters," and "the kings of the earth have committed fornication with her, and the inhabitants of the earth have been made drunk with the wine of her fornication." False doctrine (wine) intoxicating the nations through union of church and state. _(DAR 663-664.)_
- **Revelation 14:8** — "Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication." The second angel's message, first preached in the summer of 1844 against the churches that rejected the advent truth. _(GC 381, 389; DAR 663.)_

### The Fall Is Moral, Not Yet Literal

- **Revelation 18:2** — "Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen, and is become the habitation of devils, and the hold of every foul spirit, and a cage of every unclean and hateful bird." The fall is moral — a fall by apostasy — for God's people are still in her after this fall and must be called out. _(DAR 663; GC 604.)_
- **Revelation 18:3** — "All nations have drunk of the wine of the wrath of her fornication, and the kings of the earth have committed fornication with her, and the merchants of the earth are waxed rich through the abundance of her delicacies." Worldliness, pride, and union with the kings of the earth complete the picture of apostasy. _(DAR 663-665.)_
- **Revelation 17:5 / 18:24** — "In her was found the blood of prophets, and of saints, and of all that were slain upon the earth." The accumulated guilt of persecution mounts up against her. _(DAR 671-672.)_
- **Revelation 16:13-14** — Three unclean spirits like frogs go out of the dragon, beast, and false prophet — "spirits of devils, working miracles" — gathering the nations. Spiritualism is the agency by which Babylon becomes "the habitation of devils." _(DAR 667.)_

### Another Angel — The Earth Lightened with His Glory

- **Revelation 18:1** — "And after these things I saw another angel come down from heaven, having great power; and the earth was lightened with his glory." This new agency unites with and gives power to the third angel's message; no such description of a message to man is elsewhere in the Bible. _(GC 603-604; DAR 672; EW 277.)_
- **Revelation 18:2** — "And he cried mightily with a strong voice, saying, Babylon the great is fallen." The second angel's message is repeated, with added mention of the corruptions entering the churches since 1844 — this is the loud cry. _(GC 603; EW 277.)_
- **Revelation 14:9-10** — The third angel "followed them, saying with a loud voice, If any man worship the beast and his image... the same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God." The angel of Rev. 18 unites with this third message; together they form "the final warning to be given to the inhabitants of the earth." _(GC 604, 611.)_

### "Come Out of Her, My People"

- **Revelation 18:4** — "And I heard another voice from heaven, saying, Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues." A fifth celestial agency; the divine call to separate from fallen Babylon before the plagues fall. _(DAR 671-672; GC 390, 604.)_
- **Revelation 18:5** — "For her sins have reached unto heaven, and God hath remembered her iniquities." She has filled up the measure of her guilt; destruction can no longer be delayed. _(GC 604; DAR 671.)_
- **Jeremiah 51:6** — "Flee out of the midst of Babylon, and deliver every man his soul: be not cut off in her iniquity; for this is the time of the LORD'S vengeance." The Old Testament call out of literal Babylon prefigures the gospel call out of spiritual Babylon. _(DAR 666, 671.)_
- **Jeremiah 51:7** — "Babylon hath been a golden cup in the LORD'S hand, that made all the earth drunken... therefore the nations are mad." The cup of false doctrine; the same figure John uses of mystery Babylon. _(DAR 663-664.)_
- **Jeremiah 51:8-9** — "Babylon is suddenly fallen and destroyed... We would have healed Babylon, but she is not healed: forsake her." She might have been healed by receiving the truth, but rejected it; therefore "forsake her." _(DAR 666.)_
- **Jeremiah 51:45** — "My people, go ye out of the midst of her, and deliver ye every man his soul from the fierce anger of the LORD." The personal, individual nature of the call — each one must escape. _(DAR 671.)_
- **Jeremiah 50:8** — "Remove out of the midst of Babylon, and go forth out of the land of the Chaldeans, and be as the he goats before the flocks." God's people lead out, first and decisively. _(DAR 671.)_
- **Isaiah 52:11** — "Depart ye, depart ye, go ye out from thence, touch no unclean thing... be ye clean, that bear the vessels of the LORD." Separation joined to purity — the called-out people must be clean. _(GC 390.)_
- **2 Corinthians 6:17** — "Come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you." Paul applies the Babylon call to the church: separation is the condition of acceptance. _(GC 390; DAR 671.)_
- **Ezekiel 9:4** — A mark is set "upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations" — these are the ones who heed the call and are sealed before the judgments fall. _(DAR 672.)_

### The Loud Cry — Swelling of the Third Message

- **Revelation 18:1 (its power)** — The angel "lightens the earth with his glory"; this is a "work of world-wide extent and unwonted power," exceeding even the advent movement of 1840-44. _(GC 611; DAR 672.)_
- **Revelation 18:4 (joining the third message)** — As the midnight cry joined the second angel's message in 1844, so this message joins the third, swelling it to a loud cry that "will close with power and strength far exceeding the midnight cry." _(EW 277-278.)_
- **Revelation 13:13 / Revelation 16:14** — Satan counterfeits with "lying wonders, even bringing down fire from heaven," while genuine "miracles will be wrought, the sick will be healed," dividing the inhabitants of the earth. _(GC 612.)_

### The Latter Rain — Power for the Final Harvest

- **Joel 2:23** — "Be glad then, ye children of Zion... for he hath given you the former rain moderately, and he will cause to come down for you the rain, the former rain, and the latter rain in the first month." The latter rain ripens the harvest as the loud cry goes forth. _(GC 611.)_
- **Hosea 6:3** — "Then shall we know, if we follow on to know the LORD... and he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter and former rain unto the earth." The outpouring of the Spirit at the close, paralleling Pentecost. _(GC 611.)_
- **Zechariah 10:1** — "Ask ye of the LORD rain in the time of the latter rain." The latter rain is to be sought — the called-out people pray for the Spirit's outpouring. _(GC 611, by parallel.)_
- **James 5:7** — "The husbandman waiteth for the precious fruit of the earth, and hath long patience for it, until he receive the early and latter rain." The harvest is not gathered until the latter rain has done its work. _(GC 611, by parallel.)_
- **Acts 3:19** — "Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord." The "times of refreshing" are the latter rain at the close of the gospel. _(GC 612.)_

### Babylon's Final Destruction — After the Call

- **Revelation 18:6-7** — "Reward her even as she rewarded you, and double unto her double... for she saith in her heart, I sit a queen, and am no widow, and shall see no sorrow." Her self-exaltation and security precede her overthrow. _(DAR 671.)_
- **Revelation 18:8** — "Therefore shall her plagues come in one day, death, and mourning, and famine; and she shall be utterly burned with fire: for strong is the Lord God who judgeth her." The literal destruction follows the moral fall, after the people of God are called out. _(DAR 663, 671.)_
- **Revelation 18:9-11** — The kings and merchants of the earth "shall bewail her, and lament for her, when they shall see the smoke of her burning," crying, "in one hour is thy judgment come." Those who shared her sins share her doom. _(DAR 671.)_
- **Revelation 18:21** — "A mighty angel took up a stone like a great millstone, and cast it into the sea, saying, Thus with violence shall that great city Babylon be thrown down, and shall be found no more at all." Proud Babylon sinks like a millstone — total and final. _(DAR 672-673.)_
- **Revelation 18:20** — "Rejoice over her, thou heaven, and ye holy apostles and prophets; for God hath avenged you on her." Heaven vindicates the saints whose blood Babylon shed. _(DAR 671-672.)_

# 26. The Close of Probation and the Time of Jacob's Trouble

> _When the investigative judgment ends, every case is forever decided — "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... he that is holy, let him be holy still." Christ lays down the censer, ceases His intercession, and Michael stands up; the temple fills with smoke and no man can enter till the seven last plagues are poured out. God's people then pass through the time of Jacob's trouble — without a Mediator, yet hidden under the shadow of the Almighty, standing through the death decree, and delivered at last by the voice of God. (GC 490; GC 613-634; GC 635-652; EW 279-285; DAR 293-318.)_

### Probation Closes — Every Case Decided

- **Revelation 22:11** — "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... he that is holy, let him be holy still." When the work of the investigative judgment closes, the destiny of all is fixed for life or death; mercy no longer pleads. This is the sentence Christ utters as probation ends, a short time before His appearing. _(GC 490.2; GC 613.2.)_
- **Revelation 22:12** — "Behold, I come quickly; and my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be." Character is fixed before He comes; the reward is already apportioned because probation has closed. _(GC 490.2.)_
- **Daniel 12:1** — "At that time shall Michael stand up... and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was." Michael, the Archangel — Christ Himself — rises from His mediatorial throne; His "standing up" marks the close of His priestly intercession and ushers in the unequalled time of trouble. _(DAR 293.1; DAR 294.1; GC 613.1.)_
- **Daniel 12:10** — "Many shall be purified, and made white, and tried; but the wicked shall do wickedly... but the wise shall understand." The decision is moral and irreversible: the righteous are purified and sealed, the wicked are confirmed in evil, before probation shuts. _(DAR 293-318; EW 280.1.)_

### The Censer Laid Down — No More Intercession

- **Hebrews 9:24** — Christ "now to appear in the presence of God for us." His present work is intercession in the heavenly sanctuary; when that ministry ends, the saints must stand without an Advocate. _(GC 613.2; EW 280.2.)_
- **Revelation 8:3-5** — The angel offers incense "with the prayers of all saints" — then "took the censer, and filled it with fire of the altar, and cast it into the earth." The casting down of the censer pictures the close of intercession; voices, thunders, and an earthquake follow, the prelude to the plagues. _(EW 279.2; EW 280.2.)_
- **Revelation 15:8** — "The temple was filled with smoke... and no man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues... were fulfilled." Once mercy's door shuts, no intercession can be made in the sanctuary; the saints must live "without an intercessor." _(GC 614.1; EW 280.2.)_

### The Plagues Fall — Wrath Without Mixture

- **Revelation 16:1-2** — "Go your ways, and pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth." The first vial brings "a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast." Probation being past, the plagues fall on those who chose the beast over God's seal. _(EW 282.1; GC 613.2.)_
- **Revelation 16:8-11** — The fourth vial scorches men with fire; the fifth fills the beast's kingdom with darkness; yet they "blasphemed the name of God... and they repented not." The plagues are unmixed wrath — no mercy is mingled, because probation has closed and no repentance follows. _(EW 282.1; GC 614.1.)_
- **Revelation 14:9-10** — Whoever worships the beast "shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation." The third angel's warning defines who receives the plagues: the worshipers of the beast and his image. _(GC 613.2; EW 280.1.)_

### The Time of Jacob's Trouble

- **Jeremiah 30:5-7** — "A voice of trembling, of fear, and not of peace... all faces are turned into paleness... it is even the time of Jacob's trouble; but he shall be saved out of it." The prophet names this final crisis of God's people — anguish unequalled, yet ending in deliverance. _(GC 616.1.)_
- **Genesis 32:24-28** — Jacob wrestles alone till break of day: "I will not let thee go, except thou bless me." His night of agony, pleading the covenant while in deadly peril from Esau, typifies the saints wrestling for deliverance in the time of trouble. _(GC 616.2.)_
- **Daniel 12:1** — "Such as never was since there was a nation" — the trouble is unparalleled, falling after Michael stands up; yet "thy people shall be delivered, every one... found written in the book." The same verse that opens the trouble guarantees the deliverance. _(DAR 293.1; GC 613.1.)_
- **Revelation 13:15** — The image of the beast causes "that as many as would not worship the image... should be killed." The death decree against the commandment-keepers is the sharpest edge of Jacob's trouble. _(GC 615.2; GC 631.1.)_

### Famine of the Word — The Wicked's Despair

- **Amos 8:11-12** — "A famine... not of bread... but of hearing the words of the LORD: and they shall... run to and fro to seek the word of the LORD, and shall not find it." When probation closes, the Spirit is withdrawn from the impenitent; they seek and cannot find. _(GC 614.1; GC 615.2.)_
- **Ezekiel 7:19** — "Their silver and their gold shall not be able to deliver them in the day of the wrath of the LORD... because it is the stumblingblock of their iniquity." Riches are worthless when the plagues fall; nothing can shield the lost in that day. _(GC 614.1.)_
- **Proverbs 11:4** — "Riches profit not in the day of wrath: but righteousness delivereth from death." Only the imputed righteousness of Christ, received before probation closed, avails in the time of trouble. _(EW 280.1.)_

### God's People Hidden and Kept

- **Psalm 91:1-11** — "He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty... There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling." Though the plagues rage and "a thousand shall fall at thy side," the sealed are sheltered by God Himself. _(GC 614.1; EW 282.2.)_
- **Psalm 91:12-16** — "He shall give his angels charge over thee... I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him." Angels provide food and water for the saints in their solitary retreats while the wicked perish of hunger and thirst. _(EW 282.2; GC 635.2.)_
- **Psalm 27:5** — "In the time of trouble he shall hide me in his pavilion... he shall set me up upon a rock." God's hiding of His people through the plagues is a settled covenant promise. _(GC 635.2.)_
- **Isaiah 26:20-21** — "Come, my people, enter thou into thy chambers... hide thyself... until the indignation be overpast. For, behold, the LORD cometh out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth." The saints are sheltered while wrath is poured out; then the Lord comes forth to judge. _(GC 635-636.)_
- **Zephaniah 2:3** — "Seek ye the LORD, all ye meek of the earth... it may be ye shall be hid in the day of the LORD'S anger." The call to seek righteousness and meekness now is the only preparation for being hid then. _(EW 280.1.)_

### The Death Decree and the Cry Day and Night

- **Revelation 13:15** — The image speaks and decrees death for the loyal. The "general decree" fixes a time when commandment-keepers may be slain — the supreme test of Jacob's trouble. _(GC 631.1; GC 635.1.)_
- **Luke 18:7-8** — "Shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him... I tell you that he will avenge them speedily." The saints cry to God under the death decree; He delays long but delivers suddenly. The closing question — "shall he find faith on the earth?" — measures the faith demanded of those who endure. _(GC 631.1; GC 635.2.)_

### The Deliverance — The Voice of God

- **Isaiah 26:21** — "The LORD cometh out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth." At the appointed hour of the death decree, God interposes; the deliverance of His people is the destruction of their enemies. _(GC 635.1; GC 636.1.)_
- **Revelation 16:17-18** — The seventh vial brings "a great voice out of the temple of heaven, from the throne, saying, It is done," with "a great earthquake, such as was not since men were upon the earth." At that voice God declares the day and hour of Jesus' coming, delivers the everlasting covenant to His people, and the captives are loosed. _(GC 636.1; GC 640.2.)_
- **Daniel 12:1** — "At that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book." The trouble ends as it began — at Michael's word; the same Prince who closed probation now delivers His own. _(DAR 293.1; GC 635.)_
- **Jeremiah 30:7** — "But he shall be saved out of it." The promise that closes the Jacob's-trouble prophecy is deliverance; the night of anguish breaks into the day of redemption. _(GC 616.1; GC 640.2.)_

# 27. Armageddon and the Seven Last Plagues

> _When the High Priest lays off His mediatorial garments and probation closes, the seven golden vials of God's unmingled wrath — no longer mixed with mercy — are emptied upon the kingdom of the beast: sores, sea to blood, rivers to blood, scorching sun, darkness on the beast's throne, the Euphrates dried for the way of the kings of the east, and the great voice, "It is done." The sixth vial, through the three frog-spirits, gathers all the powers of earth to the battle of that great day — Armageddon — the final muster of the dragon, beast, and false prophet against God and His people, broken at last by the appearing of the King of kings on the white horse. The plagues fall only on those who bear the mark of the beast; the saints, sheltered under the Most High, behold but are not touched. (DAR 638-656; GC 627-657; EW 289-290.)_

### The Sanctuary Scene — Probation Closed, Wrath Unmingled

- **Revelation 15:1** — "Seven angels having the seven last plagues; for in them is filled up the wrath of God." This chapter is "but an introduction to the most terrific judgments of the Almighty," carrying us forward to a new series of events after the close of mercy. _(DAR 638; DAR 638.1.)_
- **Revelation 15:5-6** — The temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven is opened, and the seven angels come out "clothed in pure and white linen." The sanctuary ministry is the backdrop: when the work of mercy is past, the plague-angels go forth. _(DAR 638.)_
- **Revelation 15:7** — One of the four beasts gives the angels "seven golden vials full of the wrath of God, who liveth for ever and ever." These vials are Heaven's "unmingled wrath," the fullness of the cup for the last generation of the wicked. _(DAR 641.1; GC 627.)_
- **Revelation 15:8** — The temple is filled with smoke "and no man was able to enter... till the seven plagues... were fulfilled." No intercession remains; Christ has ceased His mediation, and probation has closed before a single vial falls. _(DAR 641.3; EW 52.1.)_
- **Revelation 14:9-11** — Whoever worships the beast and his image "shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation." The identical mark warned against in the third message marks out the victims of the plagues — proof they fall after probation. _(DAR 641.3; GC 627.)_
- **Revelation 22:11-12** — "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... he that is holy, let him be holy still." The irrevocable decree that fixes every destiny is spoken before the plagues fall; then Christ comes "quickly" with His reward. _(GC 613-614.)_

> "When Christ ceases His intercession in the sanctuary, the unmingled wrath threatened against those who worship the beast and his image and receive his mark... will be poured out." _(GC 627.3.)_ "The seven last plagues will be poured out after Jesus leaves the sanctuary." _(EW 52.1.)_

### The First Five Vials — Poured on the Kingdom of the Beast

- **Revelation 16:1-2** — The first vial: "a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image." The very first plague is aimed precisely at the mark-bearers — the visible identification of who is smitten. _(DAR 641.2-641.3; GC 627-628.)_
- **Revelation 16:3** — The second vial: the sea "became as the blood of a dead man: and every living soul died in the sea." A literal, terrible judgment, answering to the turning of Egypt's waters to blood. _(DAR 644; GC 628.)_
- **Revelation 16:4-7** — The third vial: rivers and fountains "became blood," and the angel of the waters declares it just: "they have shed the blood of saints and prophets, and thou hast given them blood to drink; for they are worthy." Retribution measured to the persecutors. _(DAR 645.)_
- **Revelation 16:8-9** — The fourth vial: the sun given "power... to scorch men with fire," and men were scorched with great heat, yet "blasphemed the name of God... and they repented not." Drought and famine as Joel describes; still no repentance. _(DAR 647.4; GC 628.1.)_
- **Revelation 16:10-11** — The fifth vial: poured "upon the seat of the beast" — the papacy — "and his kingdom was full of darkness; and they gnawed their tongues for pain." Note that those smitten by the first plague are still alive here: the plagues do not at once destroy all their victims. _(DAR 646.1; GC 628.2.)_

### The Sixth Vial — Euphrates Dried, the Kings of the East, the Gathering to Armageddon

- **Revelation 16:12** — The sixth vial dries "the great river Euphrates... that the way of the kings of the east might be prepared." As the literal Euphrates (and the unmentioned Tigris) is dried under the fourth vial, the Euphrates here symbolizes "the power holding possession of the territory watered by that river" — the Ottoman, or Turkish, empire (cf. Rev. 9:14). _(DAR 647.1; DAR 648.2-648.3.)_
- **Revelation 16:12 (the way prepared)** — The consumption of the Turkish power opens the road to "the battle of the great day of God Almighty... near Jerusalem," for the Turks hold Palestine. The drying up removes the obstacle to the final muster. _(DAR 648.6.)_
- **Revelation 16:13** — "Three unclean spirits like frogs come out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet." These issue from "three great religious divisions of mankind... Paganism, Catholicism, and Protestantism." _(DAR 650.2.)_
- **Revelation 16:14** — "The spirits of devils, working miracles, which go forth unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty." Spiritualism is the fitting agency, deceiving the nations into so unequal a warfare — against the Lord of hosts Himself. _(DAR 649.2; DAR 650.1; GC 561.)_
- **Revelation 16:15** — "Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments." The warning shows probation has closed and the danger now is apostasy through the working of these lying spirits. _(DAR 650.3.)_
- **Revelation 16:16** — "He gathered them together into a place called in the Hebrew tongue Armageddon." The Greek pronoun refers back to the kings of verse 14 (so Wesley, Wakefield, the Syriac, and Sawyer render it), and the hills of Megiddo overlooking Esdraelon name the historic field of decisive battle. _(DAR 651.1-651.6; DAR 652.1-652.6.)_
- **Revelation 17:12-14** — The ten kings "have one mind, and shall give their power and strength unto the beast"; then "these shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings." Armageddon is the confederated powers of earth versus the Lamb — and the Lamb prevails. _(DAR 657-662.)_

### The Seventh Vial — "It Is Done" — and the Voice of God

- **Revelation 16:17** — The seventh vial poured "into the air," and "there came a great voice out of the temple of heaven, from the throne, saying, It is done." All is finished: the cup of human guilt is full, the last soul has chosen, the number of the saved is complete, the books are closed. _(DAR 653.1; DAR 656.1.)_
- **Revelation 16:18** — "Voices, and thunders, and lightnings; and there was a great earthquake, such as was not since men were upon the earth." It is at midnight, by this voice, that God delivers His people; the firmament opens and shuts and the mountains shake. _(DAR 652.7; GC 636.2-636.3.)_
- **Revelation 16:19** — "The great city was divided into three parts... and great Babylon came in remembrance before God." The great city — the false and apostate religion of the world — is rent into Paganism, Catholicism, and apostate Protestantism, and Babylon drinks the cup of the fierceness of God's wrath. _(DAR 653.4.)_
- **Revelation 16:20** — "Every island fled away, and the mountains were not found." The convulsion of the seventh plague tears the very face of the earth. _(GC 637.)_
- **Revelation 16:21** — "A great hail out of heaven, every stone about the weight of a talent" — about fifty-seven pounds each — and still "men blasphemed God." This is the bitter dregs of the seventh vial, the last instrument of wrath upon the impenitent. _(DAR 653.5; DAR 654.1.)_
- **Joel 3:16** — "The Lord also shall roar out of Zion, and utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the heavens and the earth shall shake: but the Lord will be the hope of his people." The voice heard above all the voices of the seventh plague is the voice of God — terror to the wicked, refuge to the saints. _(DAR 653.3.)_

### The Battle Joined — and Broken by the Coming of Christ

- **Joel 3:9-12** — "Prepare war, wake up the mighty men... Beat your plowshares into swords... Assemble yourselves... let the heathen be wakened, and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat: for there will I sit to judge all the heathen." The world's powers are summoned to the battle — but the field is God's tribunal, not their victory. _(DAR 648.6; GC 656.)_
- **Joel 3:13-14** — "Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe... Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision: for the day of the Lord is near in the valley of decision." The gathering is a harvest of judgment on a ripened world. _(GC 656.)_
- **Zephaniah 3:8** — "Wait ye upon me, saith the Lord, until the day that I rise up to the prey: for my determination is to gather the nations... to pour upon them mine indignation... for all the earth shall be devoured with the fire of my jealousy." God Himself gathers the nations — for judgment, not for their triumph. _(DAR 648.6.)_
- **Jeremiah 25:31-33** — "The Lord hath a controversy with the nations... and the slain of the Lord shall be at that day from one end of the earth even unto the other." For six thousand years the controversy has run; now the Lord pleads with all flesh by the sword. _(GC 656.1.)_
- **Isaiah 26:21** — "The Lord cometh out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity: the earth also shall disclose her blood." The Judge leaves His place; the long-hidden blood of the saints is requited. _(GC 656.3.)_
- **Zechariah 14:12** — "Their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, and their eyes shall consume away in their holes, and their tongue shall consume away in their mouth." The plague upon all the people that have fought against Jerusalem — the consuming of the assembled enemy. _(GC 656.3.)_
- **Revelation 19:11-13** — "Heaven opened, and behold a white horse; and he that sat upon him was called Faithful and True... clothed with a vesture dipped in blood... his name is called The Word of God." Christ Himself, no longer Priest but Conqueror, rides forth to end Armageddon. _(DAR 680-681.)_
- **Revelation 19:14-16** — His armies follow on white horses; out of His mouth "goeth a sharp sword, that with it he should smite the nations," and on His vesture is written "KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS." The same title by which He overcame in chapter 17:14. _(DAR 681; Rev. 17:14.)_
- **Revelation 19:17-18** — The angel in the sun calls the fowls to "the supper of the great God," to eat "the flesh of kings, and... captains, and... mighty men." The mustered armies of Armageddon become the slain feast of the field. _(DAR 682.)_
- **Revelation 19:19-21** — "The beast, and the kings of the earth, and their armies, gathered together to make war against him" — the same confederacy of the sixth vial — are broken: "the beast was taken, and with him the false prophet... cast alive into a lake of fire," and "the remnant were slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse." Armageddon ends not in battle but in the brightness of His coming. _(DAR 682-683.)_
- **Revelation 6:15-17** — "The kings of the earth, and the great men... hid themselves... and said to the mountains and rocks, Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb: for the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?" The mark-bearers' terror at the King's appearing. _(DAR 442.)_
- **Revelation 11:18** — "The nations were angry, and thy wrath is come... and shouldest destroy them which destroy the earth." The seven last plagues fill up the wrath of this present generation. _(DAR 506.3.)_

### The Saints Sheltered — Plagues Fall Only on the Mark-Bearers

- **Daniel 12:1** — "At that time shall Michael stand up... and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was... and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book." Michael standing up = the close of probation; the plagues are the time of trouble, yet the sealed are delivered. _(DAR 305; GC 613.)_
- **Psalm 91:7-10** — "A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee... There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling." The express promise of the saints' immunity while the overflowing scourge passes over. _(DAR 656.2-656.3.)_
- **Isaiah 26:20** — "Come, my people, enter thou into thy chambers... hide thyself as it were for a little moment, until the indignation be overpast." The hiding of God's people during the outpouring of wrath. _(GC 635-636.)_
- **Revelation 15:2** — They "that had gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark" stand on the sea of glass, singing "the song of Moses... and the song of the Lamb." The victors are seen safe before the vials are even poured — a deliverance anticipated. _(DAR 639.1; DAR 640.1.)_
- **Joel 3:16 (latter clause)** — "But the Lord will be the hope of his people, and the strength of the children of Israel." The same voice that shakes heaven and earth is the refuge of the redeemed. _(DAR 653.3; GC 636.)_

> "It is at midnight that God manifests His power for the deliverance of His people." _(GC 636.2.)_ When the plagues end, "the wrath of God in the seven last plagues had been visited upon the inhabitants of the earth," the wicked are destroyed, and "the earth looked like a desolate wilderness." _(EW 289.3; EW 290.1.)_

# 28. The Second Coming of Christ in Glory

> The blessed hope is no secret rapture and no spiritual sentiment, but the personal, literal, visible, and audible return of the same Jesus who ascended. He comes in the clouds with all the holy angels; every eye shall see Him; the dead in Christ rise incorruptible, the living righteous are changed and caught up; and the wicked are slain by the brightness of His coming. This one glorious advent ends the great controversy and gathers the redeemed home. (DAR 684-686; GC 640-645; EW 15-18, 286-295.)

### He comes in person — the same Jesus, in like manner

- **Acts 1:9-11** — A cloud received Him out of their sight, and the two angels declared, "This same Jesus... shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go." As truly, bodily, and visibly as He went up, so He returns — no spiritualizing allowed. _(GC 640-645.)_
- **John 14:1-3** — "I will come again, and receive you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also." The hope of the church is a literal return to gather His own to the Father's house. _(GC 640.)_
- **Hebrews 9:28** — "Unto them that look for him shall he appear the second time without sin unto salvation." His first coming was to bear sin; the second, to save them that wait. _(MWV2 19.)_
- **Titus 2:11-14** — Believers are "looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ." The appearing is "glorious" — not invisible, not secret. _(GC 640-645.)_
- **Job 19:25-27** — "I know that my redeemer liveth... in my flesh shall I see God... mine eyes shall behold, and not another." The oldest hope of the patriarchs is a personal, eyes-shall-see-Him advent. _(GC 644.)_

### Every eye shall see Him — visible and audible, not secret

- **Revelation 1:7** — "Behold, he cometh with clouds; and every eye shall see him, and they also which pierced him: and all kindreds of the earth shall wail because of him." Universal sight refutes every secret-coming theory. _(GC 641-643.)_
- **Matthew 24:27** — "As the lightning cometh out of the east, and shineth even unto the west; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be." Open and flashing across the whole sky — the opposite of a hidden, in-the-desert appearing. _(GC 645.)_
- **Matthew 24:30** — "They shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory," and all the tribes of earth mourn. _(GC 641-643.)_
- **Mark 13:26** — "Then shall they see the Son of man coming in the clouds with great power and glory." _(GC 641.)_
- **Luke 21:27-28** — "Then shall they see the Son of man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.... lift up your heads; for your redemption draweth nigh." For the righteous it is deliverance, not dread. _(GC 640-642.)_
- **Daniel 7:13-14** — "One like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven," receiving an everlasting dominion that shall not pass away — the same cloud-coming Christ described to the high priest. _(DAR 113; GC 641.)_

### With all the holy angels and the glory of the Father

- **Matthew 25:31** — "When the Son of man shall come in his glory, and all the holy angels with him, then shall he sit upon the throne of his glory." _(GC 641-642.)_
- **Matthew 16:27** — "The Son of man shall come in the glory of his Father with his angels; and then he shall reward every man according to his works." _(EW 288.)_
- **Matthew 24:31** — "He shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds." The trumpet and the angelic gathering are public, not silent. _(EW 16; GC 645.)_
- **Mark 13:27** — He sends His angels to "gather together his elect from the four winds, from the uttermost part of the earth to the uttermost part of heaven." _(EW 16.)_
- **Jude 1:14** — Enoch prophesied, "Behold, the Lord cometh with ten thousands of his saints." The myriads attend the King in His triumph. _(GC 641.)_
- **Revelation 19:11-16** — Heaven opened, the white-horse Warrior called Faithful and True, crowned with many crowns, "KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS," the armies of heaven following Him. Christ comes this time as conquering King, His mediatorial work closed and kingly attire on. _(DAR 684-685.)_

### The resurrection of the righteous and the translation of the living

- **1 Thessalonians 4:15-18** — "The Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in Christ shall rise first," then the living are "caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air." The shout, voice, and trump make it audible; the catching-up is the only "rapture" Scripture knows — at the visible coming. _(EW 16; GC 644-645.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:51-54** — "We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed, in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump.... the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed." Mortality puts on immortality; death is swallowed up in victory. _(MWV2 19, 29.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:22-23** — "Every man in his own order—Christ the firstfruit, afterwards they that are Christ's at his coming." Christ comes to raise and gather His saints to Him in the air. _(MWV2 19.)_
- **Philippians 3:20-21** — We "look for the Saviour," who "shall change our vile body, that it may be fashioned like unto his glorious body." Translation conforms the living saint to Christ's glorified form. _(EW 16-17.)_
- **Colossians 3:4** — "When Christ, who is our life, shall appear, then shall ye also appear with him in glory." _(EW 288-289.)_
- **1 John 3:2** — "When he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is." _(EW 16-17.)_
- **Isaiah 25:9** — "Lo, this is our God; we have waited for him, and he will save us... we will be glad and rejoice in his salvation." The waiting people break into triumph at His appearing. _(GC 640-642.)_

### The wicked slain by the brightness of His coming

- **2 Thessalonians 1:7-10** — The Lord Jesus is "revealed from heaven with his mighty angels, in flaming fire taking vengeance on them that know not God," who are "punished with everlasting destruction from the presence of the Lord," while He comes "to be glorified in his saints." The same coming that rewards the just destroys the impenitent. _(DAR 685-686; GC 641-642.)_
- **Revelation 6:14-17** — The heaven departs as a scroll, every mountain and island moved; kings, great men, and bondmen alike hide in the rocks crying, "Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb: for the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?" The lost dread the very face the saints long to see. _(GC 642.)_
- **Revelation 19:17-21** — The beast and false prophet (the two-horned beast of chapter 13) are taken and cast into the lake of fire, and the remnant "slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse, which sword proceeded out of his mouth" — the word of His mouth. The persecuting powers meet their end at the advent. _(DAR 685-686.)_
- **2 Thessalonians 2:8** — That Wicked one "the Lord shall consume with the spirit of his mouth, and shall destroy with the brightness of his coming." The brightness itself slays. _(DAR 686.)_
- **2 Peter 3:10** — "The day of the Lord will come as a thief in the night; in the which the heavens shall pass away with a great noise, and the elements shall melt with fervent heat." The advent shakes the very frame of nature. _(GC 641-642.)_

### The signs that herald it and the cloud that announces it

- **Matthew 24:29-31** — "Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven," then "the sign of the Son of man" appears — the small cloud growing into the glory-cloud of the returning King. _(EW 15; GC 640.)_
- **Revelation 14:14** — "A white cloud, and upon the cloud one sat like unto the Son of man, having on his head a golden crown" — the reaper come to gather the harvest of the earth. _(DAR 582; GC 641.)_
- **Zechariah 14:4-5** — "His feet shall stand in that day upon the mount of Olives... and the LORD my God shall come, and all the saints with thee." The descent is to the very place of His ascension. _(EW 17-18.)_
- **Matthew 24:36-37** — "Of that day and hour knoweth no man... but as the days of Noe were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" — sudden upon a careless world, yet sure as the flood. _(MWV2 19.)_
- **Revelation 22:12** — "Behold, I come quickly; and my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be." He brings the reward with Him — the saints are not glorified until He appears. _(EW 288.)_

# 29. The Millennium and the Two Resurrections

> _The thousand years of Revelation 20 are bounded by two resurrections a thousand years apart. At the second coming the righteous dead rise (the first resurrection) and the living saints are translated — all to heaven; the wicked are slain by the brightness of His coming, and the earth is left a desolate, depopulated waste, with Satan "bound" by sheer circumstance, having no one left to tempt. The redeemed sit in judgment with Christ above for a thousand years. Then the wicked dead rise (the second resurrection), the Holy City descends, the deceived nations compass the camp of the saints, fire comes down from God — the second death — and the cleansing fire purifies the earth for the new creation, where the great controversy is ended forever. (DAR 687-728; GC 653-678; EW 51-54, 289-295.)_

### The Two Resurrections — a Thousand Years Apart

- **John 5:28-29** — "All that are in the graves shall hear his voice, and shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation." Two distinct resurrections to two distinct destinies — the textual key to Revelation 20. _(DAR 693.4; GC 544.)_
- **Revelation 20:5-6** — "The rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished. This is the first resurrection. Blessed and holy is he that hath part in the first resurrection." The righteous rise first; the wicked wait the thousand years — "no language could more plainly prove two resurrections." _(DAR 693.3-693.4.)_
- **Revelation 20:4** — "They lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years" — the souls of them beheaded for Jesus, who had not worshipped the beast nor received his mark. The first resurrection raises the faithful of all ages, including the martyrs against Rome. _(DAR 692.1-693.2.)_
- **1 Thessalonians 4:16-17** — "The Lord himself shall descend... and the dead in Christ shall rise first: then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds." The first resurrection and the translation of the living happen together at the advent — all caught up to meet the Lord. _(DAR 693; EW 52.2.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:51-54** — "We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed, in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump... the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed." The righteous dead and living together "put on immortality" — "death is swallowed up in victory." _(GC 644; EW 287.1.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:23** — "Christ the firstfruits; afterward they that are Christ's at his coming." Resurrection is "every man in his own order" — Christ first, His people at His coming, a definite sequence, not a general rising of all at once. _(GC 547.)_
- **Luke 14:14** — Thou "shalt be recompensed at the resurrection of the just." Christ Himself names a separate "resurrection of the just" — the first resurrection, the reward of the righteous. _(GC 549-550.)_
- **Job 19:25-26** — "I know that my redeemer liveth, and that he shall stand at the latter day upon the earth: and... in my flesh shall I see God." The patriarch's hope was a bodily resurrection at the latter day — the saints' confidence in the first resurrection. _(GC 299.)_

### The Wicked Slain at the Advent — the Earth Left Desolate

- **2 Thessalonians 2:8** — "That Wicked... whom the Lord shall consume with the spirit of his mouth, and shall destroy with the brightness of his coming." The living wicked are not converted but consumed at the appearing of Christ. _(GC 657.1.)_
- **Malachi 4:1** — "The day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble... it shall leave them neither root nor branch." The wicked are burned up root and branch — destroyed, not eternally tortured. _(GC 673.1; EW 295.1.)_
- **Jeremiah 25:33** — "The slain of the LORD shall be at that day from one end of the earth even unto the other end... they shall not be lamented, neither gathered, nor buried; they shall be dung upon the ground." The earth is left strewn with the unburied dead — no living wicked remain to bury them. _(GC 657; EW 289.3.)_
- **Isaiah 24:1** — "The LORD maketh the earth empty, and maketh it waste, and turneth it upside down, and scattereth abroad the inhabitants thereof." The earth is emptied of its inhabitants at the coming of the Lord. _(GC 657.1.)_
- **Isaiah 24:3-6** — "The land shall be utterly emptied, and utterly spoiled... the earth is defiled under the inhabitants thereof; because they have transgressed the laws, changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant... the inhabitants of the earth are burned, and few men left." The curse devours the earth because of the broken law — a depopulated globe. _(GC 657.1; DAR 695.)_
- **Jeremiah 4:23-26** — "I beheld the earth, and, lo, it was without form, and void; and the heavens, and they had no light... and, lo, there was no man, and all the birds of the heavens were fled... the fruitful place was a wilderness, and all the cities thereof were broken down." The millennial earth returns to a chaos like the beginning — "without form, and void," no man upon it. _(GC 658.2-658.3; DAR 690.3.)_
- **Jeremiah 4:27** — "The whole land shall be desolate; yet will I not make a full end." Desolate but not annihilated — reserved a thousand years, then renewed. _(GC 658.2; DAR 695.)_

### Satan Bound a Thousand Years — Bound by Circumstance

- **Revelation 20:1-3** — An angel binds "the dragon, that old serpent... and bound him a thousand years, and cast him into the bottomless pit... that he should deceive the nations no more." Satan is shut up in the abyss of a depopulated, ruined earth. _(DAR 687.1-690.1.)_
- **Revelation 20:7** — "When the thousand years are expired, Satan shall be loosed out of his prison." His "binding" is the removing of his subjects beyond his reach; his "loosing" is their being raised again so he can resume his work — bound and loosed by circumstance, not by literal chain. _(DAR 691.1-691.2.)_
- **Jeremiah 4:23** & **Genesis 1:2** — The "bottomless pit" (abyss) answers to the earth "without form, and void" — the very words of the creation chaos. Satan's prison is this dark, desolate, lifeless earth. _(GC 658.3; DAR 690.3.)_
- **Isaiah 24:21-22** — "The LORD shall punish... the kings of the earth upon the earth. And they shall be gathered together, as prisoners are gathered in the pit, and shall be shut up in the prison, and after many days shall they be visited." The wicked are shut in the prison of the grave and "visited" — raised — after the long interval. _(GC 658.2-659.)_
- **Isaiah 14:12-17** — "How art thou fallen from heaven, O Lucifer... Is this the man that made the earth to tremble... that made the world as a wilderness?" Satan, surveying the wreck of his rebellion, is left alone with his angels to behold the ruin he wrought. _(GC 660.1-660.2; DAR 691.)_
- **Jude 1:6** — The angels "he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day." Fallen angels are reserved in darkness for the judgment — Satan and his host left to "realize the effect of the curse." _(GC 660.1.)_

### The Saints Reign and Judge with Christ Above

- **Revelation 20:4** — "I saw thrones, and they sat upon them, and judgment was given unto them." The redeemed sit enthroned in heaven; their employment during the thousand years is to assign the wicked dead the punishment due their deeds. _(DAR 692.2; EW 52.2.)_
- **1 Corinthians 6:2-3** — "Do ye not know that the saints shall judge the world?... Know ye not that we shall judge angels?" The saints share Christ's judgment of the lost and of fallen angels — the very work of the millennial reign. _(DAR 692.2; GC 660.4.)_
- **Revelation 20:6** — "They shall be priests of God and of Christ, and shall reign with him a thousand years"; on such "the second death hath no power." The first-resurrection saints are forever safe — beyond the reach of the second death. _(DAR 693; GC 673.3.)_
- **Daniel 7:22** — "Judgment was given to the saints of the most High; and the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom." The prophecy of Daniel meets the vision of John: judgment given to the saints. _(GC 660.4.)_
- **Revelation 5:10** — "Hast made us unto our God kings and priests: and we shall reign on the earth." The redeemed reign as kings and priests — first in heaven through the thousand years, then on the renewed earth. _(EW 51.3; EW 290.3.)_
- **Psalm 149:5-9** — The saints have "the high praises of God... in their mouth, and a twoedged sword in their hand; to execute vengeance upon the heathen... to execute upon them the judgment written: this honour have all his saints." The honor of executing the written judgment belongs to all the saints. _(GC 660.4.)_

### The Second Resurrection, the Holy City, and the Second Death

- **Revelation 20:5** — "The rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished." At the close of the thousand years the wicked dead are raised — the second resurrection. _(DAR 694.1; EW 292.1.)_
- **Revelation 21:2** & **Revelation 21:10** — "The holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down from God out of heaven" — "that great city, the holy Jerusalem, descending out of heaven from God." The Holy City, with the saints within it, descends to the earth at the end of the thousand years. _(DAR 694.3; GC 662-663.)_
- **Revelation 20:8-9** — Satan loosed deceives the nations, "Gog and Magog, to gather them together to battle... and they went up on the breadth of the earth, and compassed the camp of the saints about, and the beloved city." The risen wicked, marshalled by Satan, surround the City — the last assault. _(DAR 694.2-694.3; GC 663.)_
- **Revelation 20:9** — "And fire came down from God out of heaven, and devoured them." The besieging host is consumed by fire from heaven — the execution of the judgment the saints had meted out. _(DAR 697.1; EW 294.1.)_
- **Revelation 20:11-12** — "I saw a great white throne... and I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened... and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works." The wicked stand at the great white throne to receive the sentence already recorded. _(DAR 697.2-697.3; GC 666.)_
- **Revelation 20:13-14** — "Death and hell delivered up the dead... and they were judged every man according to their works. And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death." The grave gives up the wicked, and death itself is destroyed — this is the second death. _(DAR 697.2; GC 668.2.)_
- **Revelation 20:15** — "Whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire." The lake of fire is the agency that closes God's controversy with sin — utter, final destruction, not endless torment. _(DAR 697.1; DAR 698.1-698.3.)_
- **Malachi 4:1-3** — "The day that cometh shall burn them up... and ye shall tread down the wicked; for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet." The wicked become ashes — reduced to nothing, root and branch, while the righteous tread the cleansed ground. _(GC 673.1; EW 295.1.)_
- **2 Peter 3:10** — "The day of the Lord will come as a thief... the earth also and the works that are therein shall be burned up." The same fire that consumes the wicked dissolves the elements and burns up the works of the earth. _(DAR 698.1-698.2; GC 674.1.)_
- **1 Corinthians 15:26** — "The last enemy that shall be destroyed is death." Death and the grave, cast into the lake of fire, are abolished — the final enemy overthrown. _(GC 673.2-673.3.)_

### The New Heavens and the New Earth — the Controversy Ended

- **2 Peter 3:13** — "We, according to his promise, look for new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness." The same fire that purifies the earth fits it for the eternal home of the righteous. _(GC 674.1; DAR 702.3.)_
- **Revelation 21:1** — "I saw a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the first earth were passed away; and there was no more sea." The purifying fire sweeps away every trace of the curse; this very earth, renewed, becomes the inheritance of the saved. _(DAR 702.1-702.3; GC 674.1.)_
- **Revelation 21:3-4** — "The tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them... and God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain." God dwells with His people; pain, death, and sorrow are forever gone. _(GC 676.1; DAR 704.)_
- **Revelation 21:5** — "He that sat upon the throne said, Behold, I make all things new... Write: for these words are true and faithful." The Creator's own pledge of a wholly renewed creation, sworn faithful and true. _(GC 674-675.)_
- **Revelation 22:1-5** — "A pure river of water of life... the tree of life, which bare twelve manner of fruits... and there shall be no more curse... and they shall reign for ever and ever." Eden restored — the tree of life, no curse, no night, and the redeemed reigning forever. _(DAR 710-718; GC 675.1-676.4; EW 289.1.)_
- **Malachi 4:2** — "Unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings; and ye shall go forth, and grow up as calves of the stall." The same day that burns the wicked brings healing and gladness to the righteous. _(GC 675.)_
- **Revelation 5:13** — "Every creature... heard I saying, Blessing, and honour, and glory, and power, be unto him that sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb for ever and ever." The whole clean universe joins in one anthem — "the great controversy is ended. Sin and sinners are no more. The entire universe is clean." _(GC 678.2-678.3.)_
