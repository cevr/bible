• The inquiries therefore naturally follow: What is meant by the
term Babylon? what is its fall? and how is it fulfilled? As to the
etymology of the word, we learn something from the marginal
readings of Genesis 10:10 and 11:9. The beginning of Nimrod's
kingdom was Babel, or Babylon; and the place was so called
because God there confounded the language of the builders of
the tower: and the word means confusion. The word is here
used figuratively to designate the great symbolic city of the book
of Revelation, probably with special reference to the
signification of the term, and the circumstances from which it
originated. It applies to something on which, as specifying its
chief characteristic, may be written the word "confusion.
