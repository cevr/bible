kingdom was Babel, or Babylon; and the place
was so called

the tower; and the word means confusion. The word is here
used figuratively to designate the great symbolic city of the book
of Revelation, probably with special reference to the
signification of the term, and the circumstances from which it
originated. It applies to something on which, as specifying its
chief characteristic, may be written the word "confusion.
