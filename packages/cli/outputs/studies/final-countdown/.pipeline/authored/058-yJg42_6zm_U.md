## Has the Judgment of the Living Begun? Part 2 — Week 13

### The Parable Repeated and the Fourth Angel's Message

> "I am often referred to the parable of the ten virgins... This parable has been and will be fulfilled to the very letter, for it has a special application to this time." (RH Aug. 19, 1890, par. 3)

The fourth angel's message repeats the former messages and the parable of the ten virgins. As the Midnight Cry awoke the virgins the first time — proven by the Eastern question — so a final cry will give power to the last message.

- **Rev. 18:1-4** — Another angel comes down, the earth lightened with his glory; he cries mightily, "Babylon is fallen," and calls God's people out so they receive not her plagues. This is the fourth angel's message and the final sealing call.
- **Rev. 7:1-3** — Four angels hold the four winds; the angel ascending from the east bears the Seal of the living God and cries to hurt not the earth till the servants of God are sealed in their foreheads.

### The Four Winds, the Dry Bones, and Prophesying

The loud cry is a command to prophesy to the four winds, putting flesh back on Israel's dead bones. The shaking is the straight testimony called forth by the True Witness.

- **Ezek. 37:8-10** — Prophesy to the wind: "Come from the four winds, O breath, and breathe upon these slain"; the breath enters and they stand an exceeding great army — the 144,000.
- **Micah 3:1-12** — The false prophets who "cry, Peace" and tear the flesh from the bones; only true prophecy about the four winds can restore Israel's flesh and skin.
- **Isa. 27:2-8** — Jacob takes root, blossoms, and fills the world with fruit; God "stayeth his rough wind in the day of the east wind" — the held-back wind during the harvest and latter rain.
- **Hosea 8:7** — "They have sown the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind" — the rough wind is the whirlwind, coming from all four directions with destruction.

### The East Wind, the Locusts, and the People of the East

The whirlwind, the four winds, the east wind, and the locust are the same power. Wind is the Bible symbol of strife, conquest, and war by which nations rise.

- **Ex. 10:13** — Moses stretches his rod and the Lord brings an east wind that brings the locusts — the east wind brings the locust.
- **Judg. 6:5, 6; 7:2, 12; 8:24** — The Midianites, Amalekites, and children of the East lay like grasshoppers; their gold earrings mark them Ishmaelites — the people of the East are the locust.
- **Rev. 9:3, 7** — Locusts hurt only the men without the Seal of God; shaped like war horses, with crowns of gold, faces of men, and teeth as the teeth of lions.
- **Ex. 10:19** — A mighty strong west wind takes away the locust and casts it into the Red Sea; the west wind removes what the east wind brought. The locusts came because Pharaoh made an anti-Sabbath law.

### The Whirlwind of Wrath — Wind as War

- **Job 1:19** — A great wind from the wilderness smites the four corners of the house and slays the young men.
- **Isa. 17:13** — The nations rush like many waters but are chased as chaff of the mountains before the wind, like a rolling thing before the whirlwind — the chaff blown away after the sifting.
- **Isa. 41:16** — "Thou shalt fan them, and the wind shall carry them away, and the whirlwind shall scatter them" — the great time of trouble.
- **Ezek. 13:11** — The untempered mortar falls; an overflowing shower (the latter rain), great hailstones, and a stormy wind rend it.
- **Ezek. 13:13** — The Lord rends it with a stormy wind in His fury, an overflowing shower in His anger, and great hailstones to consume it.
- **Jer. 4:1-13** — In the time of judgment God's fury comes forth like fire; "blow ye the trumpet," the lion comes from his thicket, the destroyer of the Gentiles is on his way, and a full wind from the four corners comes as clouds and a whirlwind with horses swifter than eagles.

> "Angels are holding the four winds, represented as an angry horse seeking to break loose, and rush over the face of the whole earth, bearing destruction and death in its path." (3SM 409.1)

### Joel's Locust and the Day of the Lord

- **Joel 1:4-6** — The palmerworm, locust, cankerworm, and caterpillar are four types of the same locust nation, strong and without number, "whose teeth are the teeth of a lion."
- **Joel 1:15-20** — "The day of the Lord is at hand"; a destruction from the Almighty; fire devours the pastures (people) and the trees of the field, and the rivers of waters are dried up — the locusts devour as a plague on anti-Sabbath law.
- **Joel 2** — Blow the trumpet in Zion, sound an alarm of war; the day of the Lord, a day of darkness, clouds, and thick darkness; a great people whose appearance is as horses, running like the noise of chariots, set in battle array — the four winds warring on the whole world.

### The King of the North Like a Whirlwind

- **Dan. 11:40** — At the time of the end the king of the north comes against him "like a whirlwind, with chariots, and with horsemen, and with many ships" — the same power of Revelation 9, the people of the East, the Ottoman power.

### The Plagues Are the Wrath of God Poured in War

- **Rev. 16:1, 2** — The seven angels pour out the vials of the wrath of God; the first falls on those who have the mark of the beast. The plagues begin when probation closes, Michael stands, and the four winds blow.
- **Rev. 14:9** — The third angel's loud cry: whoever receives the mark drinks of the wine of the wrath of God — fire, brimstone, and smoke.
- **Rev. 9:18** — The fire, smoke, and brimstone "which issued out of their mouths" — the wrath of God comes from the mouth of the horse-like locust, the Ottoman power.
- **Rev. 9:20** — The men not killed by "these plagues" repented not — the woes and trumpets are called plagues; Revelation 8 and 9 are the first plagues (wars), so the seven last plagues are also wars. (GC on the Eastern question)

### The Last Crisis — 9/11 and the Falling Towers

> "The great final crisis is just before us, when the destiny of every human being will be forever decided. A great work is to be done in setting before men the saving truths of the Gospel. To present these truths is the work of the third angel's message." (Australasian Union Conference Record, June 1, 1900, par. 4)

> "We are living in the time of the end... Plagues and judgments are already falling upon the despisers of the grace of God. The calamities by land and sea, the unsettled state of society, the alarms of war, are portentous... the final movements will be rapid ones." (9T 11)

The night-season vision of the fireproof New York buildings rising story after story, consumed as if made of pitch, with the fire engines unable to stay the destruction, describes the world just before Christ's coming. (9T 11-16)

- **James 5** — "Ye have heaped treasure together for the last days... ye have nourished your hearts, as in a day of slaughter; ye have condemned and killed the just" — the robbers and extortioners amassing riches at the last crisis. (9T 11-16, quoting James 5)
- **Ezek. 9:1-4** — Before the slaughter, the men with destroying (slaughter) weapons draw near; the man clothed with linen with the writer's inkhorn sets a mark on the foreheads of those who sigh and cry for the abominations — the sealing precedes the loosing of the winds.
- **Heb. 12:27** — Only that which cannot be shaken shall remain.

> "The world is stirred with the spirit of war. The prophecy of the eleventh chapter of Daniel has nearly reached its complete fulfillment. Soon the scenes of trouble spoken of in the prophecies will take place." (9T 11-16)

- **Dan. 11:40-45** — The king of the north comes like a whirlwind, plants the tabernacles of his palace between the seas and the glorious holy mountain, "yet he shall come to his end, and none shall help him." (See DAR, 1897 ed., on vv. 40, 45)
- **Joel 1:15-18, 12** — Quoted in 9T 11-16: the day of the Lord, the garners desolate, the cattle perplexed for lack of pasture — the Twin Towers connected to Joel's locust and the day of the Lord.
- **Jer. 4:19, 20** — Quoted in 9T 11-16: "the sound of the trumpet, the alarm of war. Destruction upon destruction is cried; for the whole land is spoiled."
- **Jer. 30:7** — Quoted in 9T 11-16: "that day is great... it is even the time of Jacob's trouble: but he shall be saved out of it."
- **Rev. 13:16; 15:2** — Quoted in 9T 11-16: in the conflict all Christendom is divided into two classes — those who keep the commandments in the faith of Jesus, and those who worship the beast and receive his mark.

> "Now comes the word that I have declared that New York is to be swept away by a tidal wave. This I have never said... 'Then the words of Revelation 18:1-3 will be fulfilled.' The whole of the eighteenth chapter of Revelation is a warning of what is coming on the earth." (LS 411.5)

- **Rev. 18:1-3** — Fulfilled when the towers fall: "Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen" — two towers fell; the World Trade Center, a one-world system, ties to the merchants of the earth. The falling towers signaled that the sealing message and the judgment of the living had begun.

### The Latter Rain Before the Day of Slaughter

- **Isa. 30:23-25** — In the time of the latter rain, the grain winnowed with shovel and fan, "rivers and streams of waters in the day of the great slaughter, when the towers fall" — the sealing and the falling towers placed together in Scripture.
- **Dan. 12:1** — When Sunday law becomes universal, Michael stands up, the time of trouble such as never was begins, and the four winds start to blow as angry horses let loose.
- **Ezek. 37:9, 10** — "Prophesy unto the wind... Come from the four winds, O breath" — the breath enters and they stand an exceeding great army.

### The Blind Man and the Tower in Siloam

- **John 9** — The man born blind, anointed with clay and sent to wash in Siloam, pictures Laodicea — born blind, fifth-generation Adventists as blind as if never Adventist, who need the eyesalve from the True Witness.
- **John 9:11** — Receiving sight, the first place he sees is Siloam — a pointed parallel to Testimonies vol. 9, p. 11.
- **Luke 13:1-5** — The tower in Siloam fell and slew eighteen; not because they were sinners above others, but as a warning: "except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish" — 9/11 was a sprinkling of the plagues, a warning to repent and receive the Seal of God.

### Come Out of the Cities — The Country-Living Ark

The locust came because of an anti-Sabbath law; the plagues fall on those who disobey God's law. There is no longer an intercessor to stop the locust — they will not stop until the whole world is on fire.

- **Rev. 18** — "Come out of her, my people" — Babylon was a city; the call is to leave the cities, go to the country and the mountains, and prepare the ark of country living before the storm. More cities will be destroyed before probation closes.

### Questions Answered — The Third Woe, the Whirlwind, and Literal Interpretation

> "Winds are a symbol of strife. The four winds of heaven striving upon the great sea represent the terrible scenes of conquest and revolution by which kingdoms have attained a power." (GC 439.3)

- **Dan. 7** — The four winds and the rising of Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, and Rome through the wars that let those kingdoms arise; the strife has been held in check, but the coming winds are without mercy.
- **Rev. 17** — The waters represent peoples, nations, and tongues.
- **Rev. 13:11-14** — The two-horned beast (Protestant America) does great wonders and "maketh fire come down from heaven... in the sight of men"; because it already says he works miracles, the fire cannot be spiritualized — it is literal fire by literal miracle, powered by Satan.

> "Those who look for miracles as a sign of divine guidance are in grave danger of deception... the enemy will work through his agents who have departed from the faith, and they will seemingly work miracles, even to the bringing down of fire out of heaven in the sight of men." (3SM 408.4; cf. 22LtMs, Lt 410, 1907)

> "Men are deceived by the miracles which Satan's agents have power to do, not which they pretend to do." (GC 553.2)

- **Ezek. 38; 39** — Gog and Magog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal, with horses, horsemen, and armor; Sheba and Dedan and the merchants — Ishmaelites, one of the four locusts. The fire sent on Magog causes the heathen to acknowledge that the Lord is God.
- **Dan. 11:2** — "I will show thee the truth": three kings stand up in Persia — "king" is a literal, interpretive word and cannot be spiritualized into a "spiritual king of the north."
- **Dan. 7:16, 17** — The angel gives the interpretation: the beasts (symbols) are four kings (literal). Daniel 11 interprets Daniel 8, beginning with Persia, and must be read literally — king of the north and south meaning north and south of Jerusalem. Use Miller's rules of literal interpretation.
- **Dan. 8** — Daniel 11 interprets Daniel 8: Persia first, the great horn broken and four horns to the four winds (four points of the compass), and the first mention of the north.

### The Timeline of the End

- **Dan. 11:40** — 1798, the time of the end; the papacy received its deadly wound Feb. 15, 1798, and was already wounded, so the warring power of verse 40 is Turkey, the king of the north, not the papacy. (See DAR, 1897 ed.)
- **Dan. 11:45 (Part A)** — Turkey goes to Jerusalem (the East Wind) and plants his palace; this is not the third woe.
- **Dan. 11:45 (Part B)** — Turkey removed from Jerusalem by the West Wind (America); enraged and pushed back, with the Euphrates dried up, Turkey is no more — this loosing is what begins the third woe.
- **Rev. 9** — The locusts referred to as the Euphrates (the people of Turkey); the Euphrates dried up means Turkey is entirely removed.
- **Dan. 12:1** — "At that time" — when Turkey is removed from Jerusalem and the sealing is ended, Michael stands up, probation closes, the four winds blow, and the seven last plagues fall. The third woe is the seven last plagues. (1850 chart: "Third woe cometh quickly — The seven last plagues")
- **Rev. 10:6, 7; 11:15** — In the days of the voice of the seventh angel, when he begins to sound, the mystery of God is finished; the seventh trumpet holds seven last plagues in the one woe (seven within the seventh, as Jericho was compassed seven times on the seventh day).
