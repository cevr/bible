## The Battle of Armageddon — Week 14

_Judgment Day Ten-Week Study_

### Removing the Platform — Spiritualizing the Prophecies

> "The enemy of souls has sought to bring in the supposition that a great reformation was to take place among Seventh-day Adventists, and that this reformation would consist in giving up the doctrines which stand as the pillars of our faith." — Selected Messages vol. 1, p. 204.

- **Mark 12:24** — Learned men who teach that Scripture has a mystical, secret, spiritual meaning are false teachers; Jesus told such a class, "Ye know not the Scriptures, neither the power of God." Darkness Before Dawn 38.4
- **Rev. 16:16** — "He gathered them together into a place called in the Hebrew tongue Armageddon"; the study insists this final battle is literal, not spiritualized.

### The Four Winds Held Until the Sealing

- **Rev. 16:16** — The anchor text of the study: a real, literal last battle, not a battle for the mind.
- **Rev. 7:1-3** — Four angels hold the four winds that they should not blow on earth, sea, or tree, till the servants of God are sealed in their foreheads; an angel from the east cries with a loud voice.
- **Ezek. 37:1-10** — Prophesying to the wind, "Come from the four winds, O breath," puts flesh and breath back on the slain bones, raising an exceeding great army; true prophecy revives what false teachers have torn apart.
- **6T 14** — "There are wars and rumors of wars... As yet the four winds are held until the servants of God shall be sealed in their foreheads. Then the powers of earth will marshal their forces for the last great battle" — proof the last battle is a literal war. 6T 14
- **3SM 409.1** — Angels hold the four winds, "represented as an angry horse seeking to break loose, and rush over the face of the whole earth, bearing destruction and death in its path." 3SM 409.1

### The East Wind Before the Whirlwind

- **Isa. 27:2-8** — In the harvest, the day of slaughter; God "stayeth His rough wind in the day of the east wind" — an east wind comes first while the four winds are still held back.
- **Jer. 4:1-13** — "The destroyer of the Gentiles is on his way"; he comes up as clouds, his chariots as a whirlwind, his horses swifter than eagles — the same angry-horse power of the four winds.
- **Ex. 10:13** — The Lord brought an east wind that carried the locusts upon Egypt; wind represents war, so an east wind brings a war with the locust power (Turkey) before the four winds are loosed.
- **Ex. 10:19** — A mighty west wind removed the locusts into the Red Sea, but only out of Egypt, not from the earth — picturing a war from the West that drives the locusts back for a time.

### The Trumpets, the Woes, and the Whirlwind

- **Rev. 8:13** — An angel flying through heaven cries "Woe, woe, woe" for the three remaining trumpets yet to sound; each woe is connected to the Turk and the Ottoman power. GC 334.4
- **Rev. 9:18** — Fire, smoke, and brimstone issue out of the mouths of the locust-like horses; the first two woes delivered this in mercy on the papacy.
- **Rev. 9:20** — The rest of the men "repented not"; the second woe ended August 11, 1840, but the third woe has not yet come.

### Fire, Brimstone, and Smoke — The Wrath of God

- **Rev. 14:9, 10** — Whoever worships the beast and his image shall drink the wine of the wrath of God, poured without mixture, and be tormented with fire and brimstone.
- **Rev. 16:1, 2** — The vials of the wrath of God are poured out; the first falls as a grievous sore on those with the mark of the beast and who worship his image.
- **Rev. 16:14** — The spirits of devils work miracles, going forth to the kings of the earth to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty.
- **Rev. 18:8** — "Therefore shall her plagues come in one day" — the plagues fall within one prophetic day, a literal year.
- **Rev. 9:18** — The same fire, brimstone, and smoke of the first two woes is the wine of God's wrath in the last plagues; one and the same power delivers the final third woe.

### Joel and the Day of the Lord

- **Joel 1:15, 19, 20** — "The day of the Lord is at hand"; fire devours the pastures, the rivers of waters are dried up — Joel parallels Revelation 9 and 16.
- **Rev. 17:15** — Waters represent peoples, multitudes, and nations.
- **Joel 2:1-11** — Blow the trumpet, sound an alarm; a day of darkness and gloominess, a great and strong people whose appearance is as horses and horsemen, like the noise of chariots leaping on the mountains — the identical angry-horse power of the four winds.
- **Joel 2:13, 16** — "Rend your heart, and not your garments... let the bridegroom go forth of his chamber" — repentance and the garments tied to the day of the Lord.
- **Rev. 16:15, 16** — "Blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments, lest he walk naked"; placed at the sixth plague, Laodicea discovers only then whether they kept their garments — having spiritualized the prophecies, they are found naked.

### Turkey, Daniel 11, and the Close of Probation

- **Dan. 11:40** — At the time of the end the king of the north comes against the king of the south "like a whirlwind, with chariots, and with horsemen, and with many ships"; partly fulfilled in 1798, but the whirlwind was still held back.
- **Dan. 11:45** — He plants the tabernacles of his palace between the seas and the glorious holy mountain (Jerusalem), "yet he shall come to his end, and none shall help him" — Turkey set up at Jerusalem, then removed.
- **Dan. 12:1** — "At that time shall Michael stand up" — when the Turk is removed from Jerusalem, Michael stands up, probation closes, and the time of trouble such as never was begins.
- **Rev. 13:17** — The national Sunday law and "no buy, no sell" trigger the need to grow your own food; Turkey moves in retaliation after the Sunday law persecutes God's people.

### The Timeline of the End

> Ellen White's 1850 chart shows the second woe past (1840) and "Third woe cometh quickly — the seven last plagues," identifying the third woe as the seven last plagues. The chart is built on Dan. 2, 7, 8, and 9 and on Rev. 11:15-19.

- **Rev. 9:20** — The unrepentant remain; with the third woe the whirlwind of horses and locusts is fully unleashed in a series of final battles.
- **Rev. 16:16** — The seven last plagues are seven wars within the seventh trumpet — like Jericho's seven days with seven circuits on the seventh (Josh. 6), a seven within the seventh.
- **Dan. 12:1** — Michael standing up marks Jacob's trouble; God's people flee to the wilderness and mountains.

### The Great Battle of Armageddon

> "The nations of the world are eager for conflict; but they are held in check by the angels... they are to be kept under control till the time shall come for the great battle of Armageddon." — Letters and Manuscripts vol. 15, Letter 79, 1900, par. 34 (also Maranatha 257).

- **Rev. 16:12** — The sixth angel pours his vial on the great river Euphrates, drying it up — the consumption of the Ottoman power, Turkey no more.
- **Rev. 16:14** — The spirits of devils gather the kings of the earth to the battle of the great day of God Almighty; demons impersonating the dead will work miracles to enforce the Sunday law.
- **Rev. 16:16** — Falling under the sixth vial, Armageddon comes after probation closes; there is then no battle of the mind — the righteous are righteous still, the filthy filthy still.
- **Rev. 17** — Under the death decree the ten kings reign with the papacy one hour (fifteen days), and God's people flee fifteen days into the wilderness.

> The Eastern question pioneers confirm the literal battle at Jerusalem: E. J. Waggoner — "The events which will follow the removal of the Turkish capital to Jerusalem (Daniel 11:45) are narrated in Revelation 16:12-21. The battle of Armageddon is one of them" (Present Truth, Sept. 10, 1896, and Apr. 1, 1897). James White — when the four allied powers withdraw under the sixth plague, "that symbolic river will be clean dried up. Turkey will be no more, and the way will be all open for the nations to rush to the holy land" (Review and Herald, Dec. 2, 1862). A. T. Jones — "The last verses of Daniel 11 relate to Turkey, which, as king of the north, with its center at Constantinople" (Review and Herald, Oct. 2, 1900). The 1904 Sabbath-School Quarterly and the 1909 Review (vol. 86) declared this universal war "would terminate in the battle of Armageddon, the end of the world, and the coming of our Lord."

- **Joel 3:2** (valley of Jehoshaphat) — "Let the heathen be wakened, and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat"; all nations gathered to the final conflict outside Jerusalem.

### The Eastern Question and the Western Question

> A. T. Jones — "The last plagues, the preparing of the way of the kings of the East, and the gathering of these kings to the world-war, and the world-battle of Armageddon, is the final meaning and the only culmination of the Eastern Question" (Medical Missionary, vol. 15, Aug. 21, 1906).

- **Ezek. 37** — The valley of dry bones is the remnant of the seed of Israel in our day; the message of the four winds puts flesh back on the bones and garments on Laodicea. LM vol. 13, MS 189, 1898, par. 9
- **1 Cor. 10:11** — These things happened as ensamples "written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come"; Joel and Jeremiah's day-of-the-Lord prophecies find their complete fulfillment only with us, not in a past local fulfillment.

### Out of the Cities — Preparing for the Plagues

> "It will then be time to leave the large cities, preparatory to leaving the smaller ones for retired homes in secluded places among the mountains... we should be preparing to move to a better country, even a heavenly." — Country Living 32 (5T 464-465, 1885).

- **Rev. 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people"; Babylon was a city, so the call to come out of the cities is the call to flee the plagues that fall as wars and destruction on the cities.
- **Rev. 13:17** — The "no buy, no sell" is one of the progressive stages of the Sunday law; the death decree is the fifth and final stage, after probation closes.

### The 144,000 and the Garments (Questions Answered)

- **Rev. 7:1-3** — The servants sealed in their foreheads are the 144,000 who give the loud cry and the fourth angel's message.
- **Phil. 1:10; Phil. 2:16; 2 Thess. 2:2** — The "day of Christ" is the same as the day of the Lord; Christ is the Lord, standing up from the most holy place and coming with the judgments.
- **Rev. 16:15** — Keeping the garments is not proof of a spiritual-only war; it describes God's people clothed in the righteousness of Christ, living without a mediator through the literal plagues.
- **1SAT 141.2** — "If we place ourselves in a position that we will not recognize the light God sends... then we are in danger of sinning against the Holy Ghost" — rejecting the truth of the four winds and the foundations grieves the Spirit. 1SAT 141.2

### The Beast, the Mark, and Two Witnesses (Questions Answered)

- **Rev. 13:1-10** — The first beast with seven heads and ten horns reigns forty-two months — 1,260 years — and is the papacy that changed times and laws.
- **Rev. 13:11** — The second beast with lamb-like horns is America (republicanism and Protestantism), which will give its power to the first beast and enforce worship.
- **Rev. 17** — A woman with a golden cup in her hand; the Vatican displays a statue of a woman with a golden cup and a serpent, standing on the Bible — Babylon openly identifying herself.
- **Rev. 16** — The three parts of Babylon: the beast (papacy), the false prophet (American Protestantism), and the dragon.

> Catholic sources confirm Sunday as the mark of authority: the Convert's Catechism (p. 50) — "We observe Sunday instead of Saturday because the Catholic Church... transferred the solemnity from Saturday to Sunday"; the Catholic Record (London, Ontario, Sept. 1, 1923) — "Sunday is our mark of authority"; and Cardinal Gibbons' counselor C. F. Thomas — "the act is a mark of her ecclesiastical power and authority."

- **Rev. 11** (two witnesses) — The two witnesses are the Old and New Testaments, the same as the two olive trees.

### God Protects His People (Questions Answered)

- **Gen. 7** (Noah's ark) — As the ark went through the flood when the door was shut (the close of probation), so God's people remain on their country properties through the plagues, into the sixth plague, before fleeing.
- **Rev. 17:15** — Waters represent people; the tumultuous flood pictures the world wars and destruction surrounding God's protected properties.
- **Rev. 16** — Some plagues fall on the seas, poisoning them and casting up the coastlines; flee to the mountains, away from the seas, fault lines, and cities.
- **1 Kings 17** (Elijah fed by ravens) — During the fifteen-day flight of Jacob's trouble, when even the gardens are cut off, God feeds His people by angels, ravens, and manna.
