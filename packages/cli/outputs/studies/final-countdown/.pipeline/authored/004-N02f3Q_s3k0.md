## Revealing the Mark of the Beast — The Final Countdown, Study 3

### Introducing the Mark of the Beast

> "Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked." God tells us things ahead so we can be aware and act on them — prophecy is not merely head knowledge.

- **Ps. 91:8** — A promise to God's people: He shows us the end of the wicked ahead of time so we change how we live, not just what we know.
- **Dan. 12:4** — Daniel is the book _sealed_ until the time of the end; the two prophetic books are inseparable and interpret each other.
- **Rev. 10:2** — Revelation is the _book open_ — the little book in the angel's hand, opened to be understood with Daniel.
- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — He causeth all to receive a mark in their right hand or forehead, so that no man may buy or sell without it; this is the central warning to understand.

### The Seal of the King — Foundation of the Mark of God

> We do not study the counterfeit to learn the truth; we study the true, and then we spot the counterfeit instantly. So we begin with the mark of God.

- **Gen. 41:40-42** — Pharaoh takes off his ring and puts it on Joseph's hand; the ring signifies that Joseph now acts in the name of the king.
- **Esther 3:10-12** — The king gives Haman his ring; whatever Haman writes is sealed with the king's ring and stands as if the king himself decreed it.
- **Esther 8:8** — What is written in the king's name and sealed with the king's ring no man can reverse; the seal denotes ownership and authority — what is done is done.

### God's Mark Is His Law and His Sabbath

- **Deut. 6:6-8** — God's words are bound for a sign upon the hand and as frontlets between the eyes — His sign is in what you do and what you think.
- **Ex. 13:9** — The sign upon the hand and the memorial between the eyes is "that the Lord's law may be in thy mouth"; the sign is the Lord's law.
- **Ezek. 20:20** — "Hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign between me and you" — the Hebrew _oth_ (H226) means a distinguishing mark; God's mark is the Sabbath.
- **Ex. 20:8-11** — Remember the Sabbath day: the only commandment that says "remember" is the one the world forgot; it is rooted in creation, not given only to the Jews, and it sets God's people visibly apart.

### The Sealing — Old Testament and Last Day

- **Ezek. 9:1-4** — Amid the slaughter, the man with the writer's inkhorn sets a mark on the foreheads of those who sigh and cry for the abominations; those marked are spared.
- **Rev. 7:1-4** — At the end of time, the angel with the seal of the living God seals the servants of God in their foreheads; this is the same Sabbath sign, placed on the 144,000.

### Identifying the Beast and Its Counterfeit Mark

- **Rev. 13:1-3** — The leopard-like beast from the sea with seven heads and ten horns, healed of its deadly wound — established last study as the papal system.
- **Dan. 7:25** — This power speaks great words against the Most High, wears out the saints, and thinks "to change times and laws" — God's times and God's law, namely the Sabbath.
- **Rev. 17:3-5** — A woman (a church) arrayed in purple and scarlet, decked with gold and precious stones, with a golden cup of abominations, named Mystery Babylon, the mother of harlots — the same blasphemous power riding the beast.

> The papacy testifies of itself: "Sunday is our mark of authority... The church is above the Bible, and this transference of Sabbath observance is proof of that fact." (Catholic Record, Sept. 1, 1923). "What warrant have you for keeping Sunday preferably to the ancient Sabbath which was Saturday? — We have for it the authority of the Catholic Church." (Catholic Christian Instructed, ch. 23, p. 272). "Protestants do not realize that by observance of the Sunday, they accept the authority of the spokesperson of the Church, the Pope." (Our Sunday Visitor, Feb. 5, 1950). The Lateran inscription names her "the Mother and the Head of all churches of the city and the world."

### Who Has the Mark of the Beast?

> The mark is not yet received — the testing time has not yet come.

- **Ev 234.2** — No one has yet received the mark of the beast; there are true Christians in every church, and none are condemned until the decree enforcing the counterfeit Sabbath goes forth and the line is clearly drawn.
- **Rev. 13:16** — He causeth all — small and great, rich and poor — to receive a mark in their right hand or forehead; the law is aimed at the whole world, not only God's people.
- **Rev. 14:9, 10** — The third angel warns that whoever worships the beast and his image and receives his mark — in forehead (belief) or hand (compliance) — shall drink the wine of the wrath of God.
- **Rev. 14:12** — Here is the patience of the saints: they keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus — the differentiating factor stated right in Revelation.
- **Rev. 13:11, 14, 16, 17** — A second beast with two lamb-like horns speaks as a dragon and makes an image to the first beast, enforcing the mark and the no-buy-no-sell decree.
- **GC 605** — Keeping the false Sabbath in compliance with the law of the state is an avowal of allegiance to a power opposed to God; keeping the true Sabbath is loyalty to the Creator. One class receives the mark of the beast, the other the seal of God.

### A Warning to Heed and a Call to Prepare

> When God sends warnings proclaimed by holy angels flying in the midst of heaven, He requires every reasoning person to heed the message and to study the prophecies to learn what the mark of the beast is and how to avoid it. (Darkness Before Dawn p. 37; cf. GC 594.)

- **12MR 219.4** — When power is allied with wickedness it serves satanic agencies; the Protestant world has set up an idol Sabbath in the place of God's Sabbath and treads in the footsteps of the papacy — for this reason God's people are to move out of the cities into the country to cultivate the land and make haste to get ready for the crisis.
- **1 Cor. 10:11** — All these things happened to them for examples and are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come; the Bible's stories teach us how to prepare for what is coming.

### Questions Answered

- **Ezek. 20:20** — Why "sabbaths" (plural)? Because God's law as a whole is part of the sign, and Israel kept additional Sabbath rests besides the weekly one.
- **Ex. 31:16, 17** — The seventh-day Sabbath is a perpetual covenant and sign forever, rooted in the six days of creation — the sign to which all the other Sabbath rests pointed.
- **1 Cor. 10:11** — On "no buy, no sell" and property: as Noah poured everything into the ark by faith, God's people invest in obedience and trust Him to guard their property; the "own nothing" new world order comes after Sunday law, not before, so prepare the land now.
