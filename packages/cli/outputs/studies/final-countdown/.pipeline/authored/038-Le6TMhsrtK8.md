## The Turk in the Bible — Week 4

### The Final Warning and the Personal Duty to Give It

> "Go and tell the world of their danger." This study traces how the first angel's message rose, and our duty to give it as the experience of the pioneers is repeated to the very letter.

- **Rev. 14:6-12** — The Three Angels' Messages are the final warning and last call to the world, given just before and surrounding the close of probation.
- **Matt. 24:48-51** — The evil servant who says his Lord delays His coming and joins the drunken is cut asunder; a warning against presuming the Advent is far off.
- **Ezek. 33:8,9** — Miller felt his burden: if the watchman does not warn the wicked, the wicked dies in his iniquity but his blood is required at the watchman's hand. MWM 92.3
- **Matt. 24** (great image, Dan. 2) — Short reformative literature should move readers from the health message to the second coming. CW 126.1

### William Miller's Rules and His Conclusion

> Those proclaiming the third angel's message search the Scriptures upon the same plan Father Miller adopted in "Views of the Prophecies and Prophetic Chronology."

- **Dan. 8; Dan. 9** — Applying Miller's literal, day-for-year rules to the prophetic periods, all the prophecies terminate together about A.D. 1843. MWM 76.1
- **Rev. 14:7** — This 1843 terminus marks the start of the first angel's message, "the hour of his judgment is come"; the study holds 1843, not 1844, for this point. MWM 76.1

### Miller's Struggle to Go and Tell It to the World

- **Ezek. 33:8,9** — Convinced of a personal duty respecting the nearness of the Advent, Miller heard ringing in his ears, "Go and tell the world of their danger." MWM 92.2, MWM 92.3
- **MWM 92.4** — He struggled nine years longer, presenting Christ's coming only to those circumstance threw in his way, resisting the call to go out as a public teacher at age fifty.
- **MWM 97.2** — One Saturday after breakfast the impression came with more force than ever, "Go and tell it to the world."
- **MWM 97.3** — Overwhelmed, Miller settled into his chair saying, "I can't go, Lord," and "Why not?" seemed to be the response.
- **MWM 97.3** — He covenanted that if he should ever receive an invitation to speak publicly he would go and tell what the Bible says of the Lord's coming, expecting never to be called upon.

### God Holds Miller to His Covenant

- **MWM 98.1** — In about half an hour, before he had left the room, a son of Mr. Guilford of Dresden came asking him to come and speak to the people on the subject of the Lord's coming.
- **MWM 98.1** — Angry at the covenant he had made, Miller rebelled and retired to a grove; impressed with "Will you make a covenant with God, and break it so soon?" he finally submitted and went to Dresden.

### Miller's First Public Labors and the Spreading Message

- **MWM 98.2** — About the first Sabbath in August 1833 Miller delivered his first public lecture on the second Advent; a revival followed in which, of thirteen families, all but two persons were hopefully converted.
- **MWM 99.1** — Elder Fuller of Poultney, Vermont, sent for him to lecture, with similar effect; the straight truth as God gives it has its effect.
- **MWM 144.3** — On Miller's visit to Boston in the winter of 1840, Brother Himes issued the first number of the Signs of the Times on March 20, 1840, an anchor date for understanding present prophecy.

### 1840 — The Advent Conference and the Ottoman Supremacy

- **Dan. (prophetic periods)** — Those looking with intense anxiety for the passing of the Ottoman Supremacy saw the time had come for a body of people to proclaim the message down to the ending of Daniel's vision. Joseph Bates Autobiography 259.5 (AJB 259.5)
- **Joseph Bates Autobiography 259.5** — A call for a Second Advent Conference at Boston came about the time the Ottoman empire lost its supremacy, weeks before news of its fall reached America; from there the work continued until the message ended in autumn 1844.
- **Second Advent Way Marks and High Heaps 50.1** — October 14, 1840, the first Second Advent Conference convened in Boston and sent out its first circular; the flying angel began to move from the same quarter of the globe where, sixty years before, the sun was darkened.
- **Matt. 24; Rev. (signs)** — These heavenly signs—the darkened sun, the moon refusing her light, and the falling of the stars (1833, the year of Miller's first lecture)—mark the nearness of the second coming. Gen. 1:14

- **5T 707** — When separated from those of like faith and compelled to stand alone, many are surprised how confused their ideas are; there has been a departure from the living God, putting human in place of divine wisdom.
- **21MR 436.6** — The testing time is right upon us; we must build upon the Rock, present the eternal principles of truth, and show what the Word declares is to take place; the God who gave Daniel instruction will confirm His servants' testimony.
- **21MR 437.1** — All the messages given from 1840 to 1844 are to be made forcible now, for many have lost their bearings; the messages are to go to all the churches.

### Revelation 9 — The Fall of the Ottoman Empire as Fulfilled Prophecy

> "In the year 1840 another remarkable fulfillment of prophecy excited widespread interest." GC 334-335

- **Rev. 9** — Two years before, Josiah Litch published an exposition of Revelation 9 predicting the fall of the Ottoman Empire, calculating it would be overthrown in A.D. 1840, in August. GC 334
- **Rev. 9** — Litch's calculation: allowing the first period of 150 years fulfilled before Deacozes ascended the throne by permission of the Turks, the 391 years and 15 days would end August 11, 1840, when the Ottoman power in Constantinople would be broken. GC 334-335 (Josiah Litch, Signs of the Times, Aug. 1, 1840)
- **Rev. 9** — When it became known, multitudes were convinced of the correctness of the prophetic principles adopted by Miller and his associates, and a wonderful impetus was given to the Advent movement; from 1840 to 1844 the work rapidly extended. GC 335

### The Seven Trumpets and the Three Woes

- **Rev. 8:2** — The seven angels stood before God and were given seven trumpets; in Scripture a trumpet is a loud warning of God's approach and an alarm to war.
- **Rev. 8:13** — An angel flying through the midst of heaven cried with a loud voice, "Woe, woe, woe," each woe belonging to one of the three remaining trumpets—the fifth, sixth, and seventh.
- **Rev. 14:6** — The angel flying in the midst of heaven with the everlasting gospel is the same angel of Revelation 8:13; the three woes are connected to and bolster the Three Angels' Messages.
- **Rev. 14:7** — Saying with a loud voice, "the hour of his judgment is come"; the loud-voiced angel in the midst of heaven links the trumpets to the first angel's message.
- **Rev. 10:7** — When the seventh angel begins to sound, the mystery of God shall be finished; understanding the earlier trumpets fixes our place in this prophetic line.
- **Rev. 10:6** — The seventh angel declares "that there should be time no longer," the close of the prophetic time periods.

### Revelation 9 — The Fifth Trumpet and the First Woe

- **Rev. 9:1-4** — The fifth angel sounded, a star fell, the bottomless pit was opened, and locusts came out of the smoke with scorpion power; they were to hurt only those without the seal of God in their foreheads.
- **Judges 6:5,6** — The Midianites came as grasshoppers for multitude and impoverished Israel; the locust symbol identifies a people of the East.
- **Judges 7:12** — The Midianites, the Amalekites, and the children of the East lay in the valley like grasshoppers for multitude—adding the eastern peoples to the locust symbol.
- **Judges 8:24** — The children of the East had golden earrings "because they were Ishmaelites"; the locusts of Revelation 9 represent the Ishmaelites and people of the East—the Turkish Ottoman power.
- **Rev. 9:10-12** — Their power was to hurt men five months; their king is Abaddon (Hebrew) / Apollyon (Greek); one woe is past, and two woes follow.

### The Prophetic Time Calculation

- **Rev. 13:5** — The beast continued forty and two months; 42 × 30 days = 1260 days, and by the day-for-year principle, 1260 years—the pattern for converting prophetic months.
- **Ezek. 4:6** — The day-for-year principle: each prophetic day equals a literal year.
- **Gen. 7:11; 8:3,4** — Noah's flood account dates the months so that thirty days are reckoned to a biblical month.
- **Rev. 9:15** — The four angels were prepared for an hour, a day, a month, and a year to slay the third part of men; the army of horsemen numbered two hundred thousand thousand, with breastplates of fire and heads as lions.
- **Rev. 9 (five months)** — Five months × 30 days = 150 days, by day-for-year = 150 years; the first period beginning July 27, 1299, when Othman founded the Turkish Ottoman Empire.
- **Rev. 9:15 (hour, day, month, year)** — An hour = 15 days, a day = 1 year, a month = 30 years, a year = 360 years; together 391 years and 15 days, ending August 11, 1840, when the Turk ruled only by permission of the Christian powers. GC 334-335

### Joel and the Locust — A Final Look

> The Book of Joel has everything to do with the time we are living in now.

- **Joel 1:4** — The palmerworm, locust, cankerworm, and caterpillar are stages of the same insect—the locust—again the children of the East, the Ishmaelites, the Ottoman Turkish powers.
- **Joel 1:20; Joel 2:1** — Blow the trumpet in Zion and sound an alarm, for the day of the Lord cometh; the same trumpet-alarm symbolism of Revelation 9.
- **Joel 2:3,4** — A day of darkness, fire devouring before them, their appearance as horses and horsemen—the same symbols used in Revelation 9 to describe this power.

### The Repeated Experience and the Coming Fulfillment

- **Rev. 14** — This is a message for the entire world, not only for Seventh-day Adventists; the everlasting gospel is to be preached to all.
- **Rev. 18** — A fourth angel comes down with a message; just as the Eastern question gave impetus to the first angel, something will again give power to the fourth angel's message.
- **Rev. 9 (unfulfilled portion)** — A next part of the Ottoman prophecy not fulfilled in 1840 is still to be fulfilled; events now happening with Turkey point to its accomplishment.
