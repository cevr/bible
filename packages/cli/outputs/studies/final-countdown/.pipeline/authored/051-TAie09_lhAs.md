### Daniel 11 Is Literal — A Crash Course

> "Daniel 11 doesn't deal with a kingdom based on religion but on geographic location." (on-screen note)

- **Dan. 7:16** — Daniel asks "the truth of all this," and the angel makes known "the interpretation" — truth equals interpretation.
- **Dan. 7:17** — "These great beasts, which are four, are four kings" — the beast is the symbol, but "kings" is the literal interpretation; truth = interpretation = literal = kings.
- **Dan. 11:2** — "Now will I shew thee the truth… three kings in Persia": because "kings" is never a symbol, Daniel 11 must be read literally, not spiritualized.
- **Dan. 11:3** — A mighty king (Alexander the Great) stands up and rules with great dominion.
- **Dan. 11:4** — His kingdom is broken and divided toward the four winds — Greece literally split among four generals (Ptolemy/south, Seleucus/east, Lysimachus/north toward Turkey, Cassander/west), narrowing to the King of the North and King of the South relative to Jerusalem.
- **Dan. 11:40–45** — Whoever rules the northern geographical area of the old Grecian Empire is "the King of the North"; Turkey now occupies that location, so the King of the North of verses 40–45 is Turkey.

### The Sequence of Final Events

- **Dan. 11:45** — Turkey goes to Jerusalem and comes to its end before probation closes; this happens after a Sunday law begins.
- **Dan. 12:1** — At that time — when Turkey is removed from Jerusalem — Michael stands up, and a time of trouble such as never was begins as the plagues fall.
- **Rev. 16:12** — The kings of the earth first annihilate Turkey (the Euphrates dried up), clearing the way for the kings of the East.
- **Rev. 16:16** — The wicked gather to Armageddon in the sixth plague, contending over Jerusalem, but God stops the battle at the very end — this is the literal close of the Eastern question.
