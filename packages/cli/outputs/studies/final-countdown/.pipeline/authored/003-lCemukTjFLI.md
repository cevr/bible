## Who is The Beast of Revelation 13? — Study 2

### Why We Must Study the Beast

> "When God sends to men warnings so important that they are represented as proclaimed by holy angels flying in the midst of heaven, He requires every person endowed with reasoning powers to heed the message."

- **Ps. 91:8** — Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked; God's people watch the judgments fall but do not receive them. To stand on the right side, we must study and stay in the word.
- **Dan. 12:4** — Daniel was told to shut up the words and seal the book until the time of the end; Daniel is the book sealed.
- **Rev. 10:2** — The angel held a little book open; Revelation is the book opened, and it interprets Daniel. The two support and unlock each other.
- **Rev. 1:1** — This is the revelation of Jesus Christ, given to be revealed, not kept sealed.
- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — He causeth all, small and great, rich and poor, to receive a mark in their right hand or forehead, and no man may buy or sell without it. To know the mark, we must first know who the beast is. _Darkness Before Dawn p. 37: "The fearful judgments denounced against the worship of the beast and his image should lead all to a diligent study of the prophecies to learn what the mark of the beast is, and how they are to avoid receiving it."_
- **2 Tim. 4:3** — The time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine; that time has fully come, and the multitudes turn away to fables.

### The Beast Described in Revelation 13

> A composite beast — leopard body, bear's feet, lion's mouth, seven heads, ten horns — cannot exist in nature, so it must be understood figuratively (Miller's Rule 11).

- **Rev. 13:1** — A beast rose up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns, ten crowns, and upon his heads the name of blasphemy.
- **Rev. 13:2** — The beast was like a leopard, with feet of a bear and mouth of a lion; the dragon gave him his power, seat, and great authority.
- **Rev. 13:3** — One of his heads was wounded to death, and his deadly wound was healed, and all the world wondered after the beast.
- **Rev. 13:4** — They worshipped the dragon and the beast, saying, Who is like unto the beast? who is able to make war with him? — proof it is a worshipped, religious entity.
- **Rev. 13:5** — A mouth was given him speaking great things and blasphemies, and power to continue forty and two months — a time prophecy.
- **Rev. 13:6** — He opened his mouth in blasphemy against God, His name, His tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven.
- **Rev. 13:7** — It was given him to make war with the saints and to overcome them, with power over all kindreds, tongues, and nations.
- **Rev. 13:8** — All that dwell on the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world.
- **Rev. 13:10** — He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity; here is the patience and faith of the saints.

### A Beast Is a Kingdom — Daniel Identifies It

- **Dan. 7:2-7** — Four great beasts rose from the sea: a lion, a bear, a leopard, and a dreadful fourth beast with great iron teeth and ten horns — the same animal symbols gathered into the one beast of Revelation 13.
- **Dan. 7:16, 17** — Daniel asked the truth of the vision and was given the interpretation; in Bible prophecy the interpretation is the literal truth.
- **Dan. 7:17** — These great beasts, which are four, are four kings which shall arise out of the earth.
- **Dan. 7:23** — The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon earth; therefore a beast represents a kingdom, and Revelation 13 describes a real kingdom doing real deeds.
- **Jer. 50:17** — Israel is scattered; the lion that devoured him is Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon — so the lion is Babylon, the first of the world kingdoms.
- **Dan. 2** — The same succession is given in the image: head of gold, breast and arms of silver, following Babylon, Medo-Persia, Grecia, then pagan Rome. Rome's iron is why the fourth beast has iron teeth.

### The Little Horn — Papal Rome

> Historically the power that took the throne after pagan Rome was papal Rome — the papacy.

- **Dan. 7:8** — Among the ten horns came up a little horn, before whom three of the first horns were plucked up by the roots; it had eyes like a man and a mouth speaking great things.
- **Dan. 7:21** — The same horn made war with the saints and prevailed against them.
- **Dan. 7:24** — The ten horns are ten kings (regions) of the Roman Empire — the ten Germanic tribes into which Rome divided.
- **Dan. 7:25** — He shall speak great words against the Most High, wear out the saints, think to change times and laws, and they shall be given into his hand until a time, times, and the dividing of time. _Hobbes, Leviathan: "the Papacy is no other than the ghost of the deceased Roman empire, sitting crowned upon the grave thereof." American Catholic Quarterly Review (April 1911): the Romans "asked him to rule them... commenced the temporal sovereignty of the popes."_

### The Three Horns Uprooted

- **Dan. 7:8, 24** — Three of the first horns were plucked up as the little horn rose; the Heruli (A.D. 493), the Vandals (A.D. 534), and the Ostrogoths (A.D. 538) were the three destroyed, clearing the way for the papacy.

### Testing the Beast — Blasphemy

- **John 10:33** — The Jews charged Jesus with blasphemy because, being a man, He made Himself God; so to claim to be God is blasphemy.
- **Rev. 13:5, 6** — The beast's mouth speaks blasphemies against God. _Lucius Ferraris: "The Pope... is not a mere man, but as it were God, and the vicar of God... king of kings." St. Alphonsus Liguori: "the priest has the power... of changing them from the slaves of Satan into children of God. And God Himself is obliged to abide by the judgment of His priests." Joseph Deharbe: "The priest does really and truly forgive the sins."_ — these claims fulfill the mark of blasphemy.

### Testing the Beast — Deadly Wound, World Wonder, Worship

- **Rev. 13:3** — The deadly wound: Pope Pius VI was taken captive by General Berthier on February 15, 1798, and died in prison — the head wounded to death exactly as prophesied.
- **Rev. 13:3** — All the world wondered: for over a thousand years the papacy was the foremost world kingdom, and the pope is still among the most followed leaders on earth.
- **Rev. 13:4, 8** — Worshipped and religious: the beast is worshipped, confirming it is a religious system.

### Testing the Beast — Reign of 42 Months (1260 Years)

- **Dan. 7:25** — Power given for a time, times, and the dividing of time.
- **Rev. 13:5** — Power given to continue forty and two months — the same period, worded differently, establishing the matter by two witnesses.
- **Ezek. 4:6** — A day for a year; so 42 months = 1260 days = 1260 years (a time = 360, times = 720, half a time = 180). The papal rule began in 538 (the year the last horn was uprooted) and ended with the deadly wound in 1798 — exactly 1260 years.

### Testing the Beast — Persecution of the Saints

- **Rev. 13:7; Dan. 7:21, 25** — The beast makes war with the saints and wears them out. _W.E.H. Lecky: "The Church of Rome has shed more innocent blood than any other institution that has ever existed among mankind." The Western Watchman defended the Inquisition: "We never thought it needed a defense."_ — an estimated hundred million perished for refusing papal doctrines.

### Testing the Beast — Changing God's Times and Law

- **Dan. 7:25** — The little horn would think to change times and laws. _Catholic Christian Instructed (chap. 23, p. 272): the warrant for keeping Sunday is "the authority of the Catholic Church." Catholic Record (Sept. 1, 1923): "Sunday is our mark of authority... The church is above the bible, and this transference of Sabbath observance is proof of that fact."_
- **Ex. 20:1-17** — The Ten Commandments: the Catholic catechism omits the second (against graven images), because of the idols in its temples, and splits the tenth in two to keep the count — changing God's law as prophesied. _The Convert's Catechism of Catholic Doctrine, p. 48._
- **Rev. 22:18, 19** — A warning not to take from the words of God lest one's name be removed from the book of life — why receiving this beast's mark is so grave.

### The Mark, the Plagues, and the Call Out

- **Rev. 13:16** — He causeth all to receive a mark in their right hand or forehead; knowing the beast makes the mark identifiable.
- **Rev. 14:9** — If any man worship the beast and his image and receive his mark, the same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God.
- **Rev. 18:4** — Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins and receive not of her plagues — those marked share the beast's punishment.

### The Second Beast — Enforcer to Come

- **Rev. 13:11** — Another beast came up out of the earth, with two horns like a lamb — a new kingdom rising about 1798, when the first beast received its deadly wound.
- **Rev. 13:17** — No man may buy or sell save he that has the mark, the name, or the number of the beast; this second power will enforce the no-buy, no-sell mark. Who it is, and what the mark is, is the subject of the next study.

### Questions Answered — The Number, the Heads, and the Final Kingdom

- **Rev. 13:18** — Here is wisdom: the number of the beast is the number of a man, six hundred threescore and six (666); the papal title _Vicarius Filii Dei_ totals 666 in Roman numerals. _See Uriah Smith, Daniel and Revelation, on Revelation 13._
- **Lev. 11:2, 3** — On the 613 laws: the sanitary and dietary laws (clean and unclean meats) still stand for health, though the ceremonial sacrificial laws were fulfilled at the cross.
- **Dan. 8:8** — On beasts versus horns: the he-goat's great horn was broken and four notable ones came up — a horn springs out of the body of a kingdom.
- **Dan. 8:20, 21** — The ram's two horns are the kings of Media and Persia (one kingdom, two kings); the rough goat is Grecia, its great horn the first king (Alexander) — so the beast is the main kingdom and the horns are kings over its regions.
- **Dan. 7:13, 14** — On Islam and Daniel 7:13: the Son of Man comes with the clouds to the Ancient of Days and receives an everlasting dominion — this is Jesus receiving the final kingdom, set in the judgment scene of Dan. 7:9-12.
- **Dan. 7:9-12** — The Ancient of Days (God the Father) sits, thrones are set, the judgment is set and the books are opened; the beast is slain and given to the burning flame.
- **Rev. 17:13, 14** — Ten kings of one mind give their power to the beast (the papacy) and make war with the Lamb, but the Lamb overcomes them, for He is Lord of lords and King of kings.
- **Rev. 17:15** — The waters where the woman sits are peoples, multitudes, nations, and tongues.
- **Rev. 17:16, 17** — The ten horns shall hate the whore, make her desolate, and burn her with fire, for God put it in their hearts to fulfill His will; when the words of God are fulfilled, the Lamb receives His kingdom and fills the whole earth.
