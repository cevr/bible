## 144,000: Who Are They? — Study 17 (The Final Countdown)

### The Sealing And The Four Winds

> "If it is connected to this 144,000 in this chapter, do you not think there is some connection between the 144,000 and these four winds?"

- **Rev. 7:1-4** — Four angels hold the four winds of the earth until the servants of God are sealed in their foreheads; the number sealed is 144,000 of all the tribes of the children of Israel.
- **Rev. 17:15** — The "sea" in this prophecy is people; so the winds are held back from blowing on the peoples of the earth.
- **Rev. 7:2-4** — Another angel ascends from the east with the seal of the living God, crying to the four angels not to hurt the earth till the sealing is complete; once the sealing is over, probation shuts and the winds blow.
- **GC 439** — Winds are a symbol of strife; the four winds striving upon the great sea represent the terrible scenes of conquest and revolution by which kingdoms have attained to power.
- **3SM 409.1** — The angels hold the four winds, represented as an angry horse seeking to break loose and rush over the whole earth, bearing destruction and death in its path.
- **Dan. 12:1** — Michael stands up and a time of trouble such as never was follows; at that time God's people who are written in the book are delivered — the same scene that follows the sealing.

### The Mark Of The Beast And The Final Test

- **Rev. 13** — The whole question circles back to the mark of the beast: the exaltation by human authority of Sunday in place of the Bible Sabbath. 7T 141
- **7T 141** — The substitution of the laws of men for the law of God, exalting Sunday in place of the Sabbath, is the last act in the drama; when it becomes universal, God will reveal Himself.
- **15MR 15** — The image of the beast is formed before probation closes, for it is the great test by which the eternal destiny of God's people is decided; this is the test they must pass before they are sealed.
- **15MR 15** — All who keep God's law and refuse a spurious sabbath receive the seal of the living God; those who accept the Sunday sabbath receive the mark of the beast.
- **RH Jan 30, 1900** — There can be only two classes: each is stamped either with the seal of the living God or with the mark of the beast.

### Israel Of Today And The Sinfulness Of Sin

- **9T 164** — To be purified, Seventh-day Adventists must have the Holy Spirit; "the Israel of today" who humble themselves are God's modern Israel, not literal Jews removed as a people after the stoning of Stephen.
- **3T 266** — The true people of God view sin in its real, sinful character and deal faithfully with sin, especially in the closing work of the church, in the sealing time of the 144,000 who are to stand without fault before the throne of God.

### Not All Who Profess Will Be Sealed

> "Not all who profess to keep the Sabbath will be sealed."

- **CET 189** — Many who teach the truth to others will not receive the seal; they had the light of truth and knew every point of the faith, but had not corresponding works and did not command their households after them.
- **Matt. 8:29 / Mark 5:7** — Even the devils confessed Jesus as the Son of God; knowing without obedient works saves no one — actions must align with words.
- **Isa. 58:1** — The 144,000 cry aloud and spare not, giving the loud cry to friends, family, and workplace, warning of the coming no-buy-no-sell mark of the beast.

### The Sealing Is A Settling Into The Truth

- **CET 189** — None receive the seal while a spot or stain remains; we must cleanse the soul-temple, then the latter rain falls as the early rain fell at Pentecost.
- **Rev. 3:17** — We are too easily satisfied, feeling rich and increased with goods, not knowing we are wretched, miserable, poor, blind, and naked — the Laodicean state.
- **Mar 200.2** — The sealing is not a visible mark but a settling into the truth, both intellectually and spiritually, so the people of God cannot be moved.
- **CET 177** — The number of this company had lessened; the careless and indifferent who would not plead and agonize were shaken out and left behind in darkness, their places filled by others taking hold of the truth.

### The Latter Rain And The Loud Cry

- **CET 177** — Those clothed with the armor speak the truth with great power; many bound by relatives are set free, and an angel says the change is "the latter rain, the refreshing from the presence of the Lord, the loud cry of the third angel."
- **Rev. 18:1** — Another angel comes down from heaven with great power, and the earth is lightened with his glory — the source of that great power.
- **Deut. 32:2** — Doctrine drops as the rain; the latter rain is the final outpouring of the message/doctrine that must first be received before it can be given to the world.
- **Rev. 14:9** — The third angel warns with a loud voice that whoever worships the beast and receives his mark shall drink the wine of the wrath of God — this warning is the loud cry of the third angel.
- **UL 218** — Self is the most difficult thing to manage; lay self, temptations, ideas, and feelings at the foot of the cross, then the soul is ready for divine instruction.
- **Jude 24** — He is able to keep you from falling and present you faultless before His glory with exceeding joy.

### The Number Is Literal

- **Rev. 7:4** — "I heard the number of them which were sealed" — Scripture records a literal number, not a title.
- **Judg. 7:7** — Gideon ended with 300 men, a literal number though small.
- **Dan. 7** — The lion's one head, the bear's leadership, the leopard's four heads, and the terrible beast's ten horns with one plucking up others are all literal counts, not spiritualized numbers.
- **EW 15** — The living saints, 144,000 in number, knew and understood the voice while the wicked thought it thunder; they were all sealed and perfectly united, with God, New Jerusalem, and Jesus' new name on their foreheads.
- **CET 13** — "Eight in number" children all converted — Ellen White's plain literal use of a number.
- **CET 20** — "Twelve in number" went down to be baptized — again a literal number, confirming the Bible's consistent literal usage.

### The Largest Body Of United Righteous

- **Gen. 7** — Only eight souls entered the ark out of the world's millions; the 144,000 is by far a larger remnant.
- **1 Kings 19:18 / Rom. 11:4** — In Elijah's day 7,000 had not bowed to Baal; the faithful remnant grows larger across history, and 144,000 is the greatest body of 100% united righteous ever.
- **Rev. 7:4** — The 144,000 are an army; as only men were numbered in Israel, women and children are counted on top of this, as the 5,000 fed were men "beside women and children."

### The Vision Of The Straight And Narrow Path

- **EW 14** — The Advent people travel a path cast high above the world, with a bright light behind them at its beginning which the angel says is the midnight cry; this light shines all along the path so they do not stumble.
- **EW 14** — Those who keep their eyes fixed on Jesus are safe, but those who rashly deny the light behind them lose it, are left in darkness, and fall off the path; the 144,000 are those who do not deny the midnight-cry light.
- **EW 284** — God will not suffer the wicked to slay those expecting translation who refuse the decree and mark of the beast; those who mocked the saints' going up will behold their glorious deliverance.

### Redeemed From Among Men — Translated Alive

- **Rev. 14:1, 4-5** — The 144,000 stand with the Lamb on Mount Zion, His Father's name in their foreheads; not defiled with women, following the Lamb wherever He goes, redeemed from among men, with no guile in their mouths and without fault before the throne.
- **DAR 583.4** — "Redeemed from among men" can apply only to those translated from among the living; the 144,000 are the living saints who will be translated at the second coming of Christ.
- **Phil. 3:11** — Paul labored to attain a resurrection from among the dead — the hope of those who sleep in Jesus — contrasted with the 144,000's redemption from among the living.
- **Heb. 11:5** — By faith Enoch was translated that he should not see death, having the testimony that he pleased God. EP 49.4
- **EP 49.4** — Enoch's godly character represents the holiness required of those redeemed from the earth at Christ's advent; as Enoch was translated, so the living righteous, the 144,000, will be translated before the earth's destruction by fire.
- **1 Cor. 15:51, 52; 1 Thess. 4:16-18** — The promise that the living righteous will be changed and caught up — the translation of the 144,000.
- **Isa. 60:5** — The harvest the 144,000 reap: the abundance of the sea and the forces of the Gentiles converted and gathered in as the great multitude.

### Questions Answered: 144,000 And The Great Multitude

- **Rev. 7:4** — The 144,000 are of the tribes of Israel — modern Adventism, the priests who know the truth and give the loud cry; judgment begins at the house of God.
- **Rev. 7:9** — The great multitude is of all nations, kindreds, peoples, and tongues — a separate, distinct group with different clothing, those coming in through the loud cry.
- **Rev. 14:4** — Not defiled with "women" means not defiled with the doctrines of the other churches, Babylon; the 144,000 have no guile and are not joined to Babylon.
- **Rev. 18:1-4** — The 144,000 give the fourth angel's message — the third angel given with greater power — calling "Come out of her, my people," that they partake not of Babylon's sins.

### Questions Answered: The Secret Rapture

> "The righteous go through the time of trouble; they are not taken away."

- **Luke 17:35-37** — Two are in the field, one taken and one left; when asked "Where, Lord?" Jesus answers "Wheresoever the body is, thither will the eagles be gathered" — the taken are taken away in judgment, not raptured.
- **Matt. 7:24-27** — The house on the sand was taken away by the flood; being "taken" is destruction, not deliverance.
- **Gen. 7** — In Noah's day it was the people outside the ark who were taken away in judgment, while Noah was left safe.
- **Dan. 12:1** — In the time of trouble such as never was, God's people are not removed but pass through it; the righteous are those left, while the wicked are taken in judgment.
- **Ps. 91:7** — A thousand shall fall at thy side and ten thousand at thy right hand, but it shall not come nigh thee — the righteous stand through the time of trouble, not taken away.
