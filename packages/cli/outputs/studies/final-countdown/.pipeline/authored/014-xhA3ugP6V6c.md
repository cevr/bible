## Connecting the Dots - Sunday Law Is Already Here! — Study 13

### The Warning to Flee Jerusalem — A Type for the Last Days

> "This entire chapter is a warning to those who shall live in the last scenes of this earth's history." — _Christ Triumphant_ 353.3.

- **Matt. 24:1, 2** — Jesus foretells that not one stone of the temple will be left upon another; a literal prophecy of Jerusalem's destruction in AD 70.
- **Matt. 24:3** — The disciples ask privately for the sign of His coming and of the end of the world, joining the fall of Jerusalem to the final events.
- **Matt. 24:15-19** — When the abomination of desolation (the Roman armies) stands in the holy place, those in Judea must flee to the mountains without turning back. CTr 353.3.
- **Luke 21:20, 21** — "When ye shall see Jerusalem compassed with armies," flee to the mountains; this was the promised sign the waiting Christians watched for. GC 30.2.
- **Matt. 24** — Christ's prophetic words reached beyond AD 70 to the final conflagration when the Lord arises to punish the world for its iniquity. CTr 353.3.

### The Promised Sign and the Christians' Escape

> "Not one Christian perished in the destruction of Jerusalem." — _Great Controversy_ 30.2.

- **Luke 21:20, 21** — After Cestius surrounded the city he unexpectedly abandoned the siege, giving God's people their opportunity to obey the Savior's warning. GC 30.2.
- **Luke 21:20, 21** — God's merciful providence overruled events so that neither Jew nor Roman should hinder the flight of the Christians; the Jews pursued the retreating army while the believers escaped. GC 30.2.
- **Matt. 24:16-20** — When the siege was resumed by Titus terrible calamities fell upon Jerusalem, but every obedient Christian had already fled. GC 30.2.
- **Luke 21:21** — Josephus records 1.1 million perished, the majority Jews trapped during Passover; the warning to obey and leave is deadly serious. _(Wikipedia, Siege of Jerusalem, 70 CE.)_

### The Day of the Lord Comes as a Thief

> "For yourselves know perfectly that the day of the Lord so cometh as a thief in the night." — 1 Thess. 5:2.

- **1 Thess. 5:1, 2** — Paul says the times and seasons are so plain he need not write; the day of the Lord arrives unseen, like a thief, upon the unwatchful.
- **Rev. 16:14** — The kings of the earth are gathered to "the battle of the great day of God Almighty" — the day of God's plagues.
- **Rev. 18:8** — Her plagues come in one day, death and mourning and famine, and she is utterly burned with fire; this is the day of the Lord.
- **Joel 2:1, 2** — Blow the trumpet in Zion and sound an alarm, for the day of the Lord is at hand — a day of darkness, gloominess, and thick darkness.
- **1 Thess. 5:2, 3** — The day of the Lord is the close of probation; when they say "peace and safety," then sudden destruction comes and they shall not escape.
- **1 Thess. 5:4-8** — Believers are not in darkness that the day should overtake them as a thief; they must watch and be sober, putting on faith, love, and the hope of salvation.
- **1 Thess. 5:9** — God hath not appointed us to wrath but to obtain salvation through Jesus Christ; He does not want us to perish in the plagues poured on the cities.

### Unconscious of the Close of Probation

- **1 Thess. 5:3** — The wicked will be planting, building, eating, and drinking, unconscious that the final irrevocable decision has been pronounced in the sanctuary above. Mar 264.2.
- **Gen. 7:16** — As Noah entered the ark and the door was shut unknown to the world, so probation will close before men realize it; once shut it is closed forever.

### Not One in Twenty Ready — The Solemn Call to Obey

> "It is a solemn statement that I make to the church, that not one in twenty whose names are registered upon the church books are prepared to close their earthly history." — _Christian Service_ 41.1.

- **Matt. 24:16** — As the obedient Christians proved their faith by physically fleeing Jerusalem, so readiness requires action, not merely profession. ChS 41.1.
- **Luke 21:21** — The faithful who fled were few against Jerusalem's millions; look only to Jesus and obey, not to the unprepared majority. ChS 41.1.

### The Decree Enforcing the Papal Sabbath — Our Signal

> "As the siege of Jerusalem by the Roman armies was the signal for flight to the Judean Christians, so the assumption of power on the part of our nation, in the decree enforcing the papal Sabbath, will be a warning to us." — _Country Living_ 32 (1885).

- **Luke 21:20** — The Roman siege was the signal to flee; the national decree enforcing Sunday is the parallel signal to leave the large cities for retired homes in secluded places among the mountains. CL 32.1.
- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — A time is coming when none may buy or sell; therefore God's people must move to the country to raise their own provisions. RH July 5, 1906, par. 10.
- **Rev. 18:8** — "Out of the cities; out of the cities!" — the earthquakes and floods will come, and we are not to establish ourselves in the wicked cities. RH July 5, 1906, par. 10.
- **Lt 90, 1897** — The Protestant world has set up an idol Sabbath and treads in the footsteps of the papacy; hence the necessity of moving out of the cities to cultivate the land. CL 21.1.

### The First Siege — The Sunday Law Has Already Come

> "The time is not far distant..." (1885) became "Out of the cities!" (1906) — the warning sign fell between those dates.

- **Rev. 10:6** — There should be "time no longer"; we cannot measure our siege as Jerusalem's was measured, so we must make haste while probation lingers.
- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — The National Sunday-Rest Bill (Senate Bill No. 2983, introduced by Senator H. W. Blair, May 21, 1888) was the first siege and the out-of-the-cities call. _(National Sunday-Rest Bill, 1888.)_
- **RH Dec. 18, 1888** — We are not to presume on prophecy and let calamity come; we must work earnestly to delay as long as possible the threatened calamity and lift the standard of religious liberty.

### Preparing the Ark — Grow Your Own Food

> "Though the dwelling may be small, yet there should be land in connection with it that may be cultivated." — _Adventist Home_ 139.4 (RH, 1903).

- **Gen. 6:14-22** — As Noah labored 120 years to build and provision the ark, so the country home must be a true ark, not a tiny dinghy, prepared with seeds, food, and shelter for years.
- **Rev. 13:17** — Because none may buy or sell, the family must raise produce, flax, and herbs — not merely survive on potatoes — to be healthy for the final medical-missionary work. CL 21.1.
- **Heb. 11:7** — Noah's obedience was counted righteousness; the same obedient action of leaving the cities is required of God's people now.

### Martyrs, the 144,000, and the Final Loud Cry

- **Deut. 33:25** — "As thy days, so shall thy strength be" — those who flee, are imprisoned, or are martyred _prior to the last closing conflict_ are promised strength. _(Letters and Manuscripts, vol. 6, Ms 6, 1889, par. 40.)_
- **Rev. 14:1-5** — The 144,000 are living saints who must be alive to see Jesus come and to give the loud cry, so they do not perish in the great destruction; type of Noah's family and the three Hebrews.
- **Dan. 3** — The three worthies who refused to bow to the image are a type of those who will face the image of the beast; none of them died.
- **Dan. 11:45; 12:1** — Daniel 11:45 is the sign immediately before Michael stands up; since probation has not closed, verse 45 cannot yet be fulfilled.
- **Dan. 12:1** — "At that time shall Michael stand up" marks the close of probation and the beginning of the time of trouble such as never was.

### Bread and Water Sure — The Time of Trouble vs. Sunday Law

> Mar 181.2 is misapplied to Sunday-law preparation; it belongs to the later flight into the wilderness.

- **Dan. 12:1** — The time of trouble follows the close of probation, years after Sunday law; only then does the counsel against laying up provision apply. Mar 181.2.
- **Isa. 33:2** — "O Lord, be gracious unto us... be thou our arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble" — the time when the plagues are falling.
- **Isa. 33:16** — "He shall dwell on high... bread shall be given him; his waters shall be sure" — God sustains the fleeing saints in the wilderness, when planning to hide food becomes contrary to principle. Mar 181.2.

### How Far From the City — Counsel on the Country Home

- **Rev. 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and receive not of her plagues" — the heart of the fourth angel's message and the final call out of the cities.
- **Rev. 18:1** — Another angel comes down with great power, lighting the earth with his glory and crying mightily; the final loud cry that gathers the great multitude into the ark.
