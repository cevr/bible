## The 3 Stages of the Tribulation — The Final Countdown, Study 24

### Two Different Tribulations Distinguished

> A lot of people talk about the tribulation, but few really understand it. We need to dissect what the Bible actually says — because what's in the Bible is the most important thing.

- **Dan. 12:1** — When Michael (Jesus) stands up, He rises from His intercession; the final time of trouble follows, "such as never was since there was a nation." This is the trouble for the wicked, not for God's people.
- **Matt. 24:21** — The "great tribulation, such as was not since the beginning of the world" is a separate event — the long persecution of God's people, not the final time of trouble.
- **Matt. 24:22** — "Except those days should be shortened, there should no flesh be saved: but for the elect's sake those days shall be shortened" — God cut short the era of persecution.
- **Dan. 7** — The persecuting power reigns for a prophetic 1260 years (3½ prophetic years), the era when pagan and papal Rome killed Christians.
- **Rev. 12; Rev. 13** — The same 1260-year persecution; Revelation 13 frames it as the 42 months of the beast's authority.
- **Matt. 24:29** — "Immediately after the tribulation of those days" the sun is darkened, the moon withholds her light, and the stars fall — proof this tribulation is past, marked by literal heavenly signs.

### The Heavenly Signs That Closed the Persecution

- **Rev. 6:12, 13** — At the sixth seal: a great earthquake, the sun black as sackcloth, the moon as blood, and the stars of heaven falling like untimely figs — the same signs Jesus foretold, marking the end of the persecution era.
- **Rev. 6:12** — The great earthquake fulfilled in the Lisbon earthquake of November 1, 1755 (All Saints' Day), felt across Europe and North Africa, killing about 50,000 — persecution waned thereafter, before 1798.
- **Rev. 6:12; Matt. 24:29** — The sun darkened on May 19, 1780 (the New England Dark Day): unexplained midday darkness lasting into the night, with the moon appearing as blood that night.
- **Rev. 6:13; Matt. 24:29** — The stars fell November 12, 1833, with over 100,000 shooting stars per hour, witnessed across North America. _GC 266.4: "The persecution of the church did not continue throughout the entire period of the 1260 years... Through the influence of the Reformation the persecution was brought to an end prior to 1798."_

### Stage One — No Buy, No Sell and Judgments Begin to Fall

> The first stage brings the "no buy, no sell" against the righteous and destruction of cities upon the wicked. Probation is still open; these judgments are sent to wake people up and call them to repentance, as the plagues did in Egypt.

- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — All, small and great, are caused to receive a mark, so that no man may buy or sell except he have the mark, name, or number of the beast.
- **Rev. 13:1, 2** — A beast rises out of the sea, representing the Roman papacy.
- **Dan. 7:16, 17** — A beast in prophecy is a kingdom — the Bible interprets its own symbols.
- **Rev. 13:11** — The second beast, rising out of the earth, is the United States of America.
- **EW 85.2** — "At the commencement of the time of trouble, we were filled with the Holy Ghost as we went forth and proclaimed the Sabbath more fully" — the mark concerns God's time and law, i.e. enforced Sunday worship against the seventh-day Sabbath.
- **EW 85.3** — "The commencement of that time of trouble... does not refer to the time when the plagues shall begin to be poured out, but to a short period just before they are poured out, while Christ is in the sanctuary" — probation is still open, the loud cry is given.

### National Ruin Follows National Apostasy

- **Isa. 1:4** — A sinful nation, laden with iniquity, has forsaken the Lord and provoked the Holy One of Israel — describing America joining church and state.
- **Isa. 1:7** — "Your country is desolate, your cities are burned with fire" — the consequence of the national apostasy.
- **Jer. 6:19** — God brings evil upon this people, "because they have not hearkened unto my words, nor to my law, but rejected it" — judgment for breaking the Sabbath nationally.
- **Luke 21:10, 11** — "Nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom," with great earthquakes, famines, pestilences, and fearful signs — a sign of the first stage of the time of trouble.
- **Hos. 8:7** — "They have sown the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind" — wind is war in Scripture; having sown war, the nation reaps a world war from every direction.
- **Rev. 10** — In the closing scenes "time shall be no longer"; no time prophecy fixes the length of this no-buy-no-sell period. _4SP 410.2: "When Protestant churches shall seek the support of the secular power... then will there be a national apostasy which will end only in national ruin."_

### Destruction of the Cities — Come Out of Them

> Sodom and Gomorrah stand as God's example to the wicked cities of today that make a law against His law.

- **Luke 17:29** — "The same day that Lot went out of Sodom it rained fire and brimstone from heaven, and destroyed them all" — a pattern of the coming destruction; God's people must be out of the cities.
- **2 Pet. 2:6** — Sodom and Gomorrah turned to ashes were made "an ensample unto those that after should live ungodly."
- **1 Cor. 10:11** — "All these things happened unto them for ensamples... written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come" — these histories warn us of the end.
- **CL 8.3** — "The time is near when large cities will be swept away, and all should be warned of these coming judgments."
- **CL 7.5** — "Calamities will come — calamities most awful, most unexpected; and these destructions will follow one after another" — repentant cities may be spared a time, but the deceived are left to calamity that their senses may be awakened.

### Stage Two — Close of Probation and the Seven Last Plagues

> The second stage: probation closes, the time of trouble of Daniel 12:1 begins, and the plagues fall. This trouble is for the wicked — God's wrath is not on His people.

- **Dan. 12:1** — Michael (Jesus) stands up from His intercession; the time of trouble such as never was begins, "and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book."
- **Rom. 8:34** — Christ "maketh intercession for us" — sitting equals interceding; when He stands, that intercession ends.
- **Rev. 15:1** — Seven angels have the seven last plagues, "for in them is filled up the wrath of God" — God's wrath is not upon His people.
- **Rev. 16:1, 2** — The vials of God's wrath are poured upon the earth; the first plague falls on the men who have the mark of the beast and worship his image.
- **Joel 2:1** — "Blow ye the trumpet in Zion, and sound an alarm... for the day of the Lord cometh" — the watchman's trumpet signals war.
- **Joel 2:2** — "A day of darkness and of gloominess, a day of clouds and of thick darkness."
- **Joel 2:30, 31** — Wonders in heaven and earth — blood, fire, pillars of smoke from the destroyed cities, the sun darkened and the moon turned to blood before the great and terrible day of the Lord — a third witness to these signs.

### God's People Protected Through the Plagues

- **Dan. 12:1; CET 187.4** — "When this time of trouble comes, every case is decided; there is no longer probation, no longer mercy for the impenitent. The seal of the living God is upon His people."
- **Ps. 91:5, 6** — Be not afraid of the terror by night, the arrow by day, the pestilence in darkness, nor the destruction at noonday.
- **Ps. 91:7** — "A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee."
- **Ps. 91:8** — "Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked" — the righteous merely witness the plagues.
- **Ps. 91:9, 10** — Because the Lord is our habitation, "there shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling."
- **Ps. 37:39** — "The salvation of the righteous is of the LORD: he is their strength in the time of trouble."
- **Job 38:23** — The hail is "reserved against the time of trouble, against the day of battle and war."
- **Rev. 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues."
- **Rev. 18:6** — God rewards her double according to her works — the persecution she dealt is returned with interest.
- **Rev. 18:11** — "The merchants of the earth shall weep and mourn over her; for no man buyeth their merchandise any more" — her own no-buy-no-sell, doubled back upon her.

### Stage Three — The Time of Jacob's Trouble

> The final stage is the time of Jacob's trouble. A death decree goes forth against God's people, but the Bible assures us they shall be saved out of it.

- **Jer. 30:5-7** — A voice of trembling and fear; men's faces turn pale, "for that day is great, so that none is like it: it is even the time of Jacob's trouble, but he shall be saved out of it."
- **CET 100.3** — The four angels hold the four winds until Jesus' work in the sanctuary is done; then the seven last plagues come. A decree goes forth to slay the saints, who cry day and night for deliverance — and the 144,000 are delivered by the voice of God. _(See Gen. 32.)_
- **GC 648.3** — The righteous "have endured the anguish of the time of Jacob's trouble; they have stood without an intercessor... But they have been delivered, for they have washed their robes... In their mouth was found no guile; for they are without fault before God."
- **Isa. 33:2** — "Lord, be gracious unto us; we have waited for thee: be thou their arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble."
- **Isa. 33:16** — "He shall dwell on high: his place of defence shall be the munitions of rocks: bread shall be given him; his waters shall be sure" — God's people flee to the mountain caves, provided for as Elijah was.
- **1 Kings 18; 1 Kings 19** — Elijah, with a death decree from Jezebel for prophesying against Baal's prophets, was hidden and fed by ravens and angels — an example of God's care in Jacob's trouble.
- **1 Cor. 10:11** — These histories were written as examples "for those who live at the end of the world."
- **Ps. 27:5** — "For in the time of trouble he shall hide me in his pavilion... he shall set me up upon a rock."

### The Final Trouble and the Second Coming

- **GC 614.1** — "When He leaves the sanctuary, darkness covers the inhabitants of the earth. In that fearful time the righteous must live in the sight of a holy God without an intercessor... Satan will then plunge the inhabitants of the earth into one great, final trouble... The whole world will be involved in ruin more terrible than that which came upon Jerusalem of old."
- **Rev. 22:11** — "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still... and he that is righteous, let him be righteous still" — every destiny is fixed at the close of probation.
- **Rev. 22:12** — "Behold, I come quickly; and my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be" — Jesus' second coming ends the whole tribulation.
