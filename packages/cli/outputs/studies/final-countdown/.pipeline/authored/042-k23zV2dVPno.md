## The Fallen Daughters of Babylon — Week 6

### The Second Angel's Message

> "And there followed another angel, saying, Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication."

- **Rev. 14:8** — The second angel proclaims the fall of Babylon, the message this study traces to its first historical giving.
- **Rev. 18:2** — The same cry, "Babylon the Great is fallen," repeated in the fourth angel's message that must yet be given.
- **Rev. 18:4** — "Come out of her my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins and that ye receive not of her plagues" — the call addressed to God's people still inside fallen Babylon.
- **Matt. 24:48-51** — The evil servant who says "My lord delayeth his coming" and eats and drinks with the drunken; Christ's warning to those who reject the nearness of His coming.

### Who and What Is Babylon

- **Gen. 10:10; 11:9** — From the marginal readings, Nimrod's kingdom began at Babel, so called because God confounded the language of the builders; the word means confusion. DAR 601.1
- **Rev. 17:5** — Her name on her forehead, "Mystery, Babylon the Great, the Mother of Harlots," reveals her family connections — a mother implies daughters. DAR 601.3
- **Rev. 17** — The woman of chapter 17 is a church; a vile woman represents an impure or apostate church, and Babylon is herself a harlot and the mother of daughters like herself. DAR 603.4
- **Rev. 12** — The woman is a symbol of a church; the pure woman of chapter 12 establishes the symbol that a chaste woman stands for a pure church. DAR 603.4
- **Rev. 18:24** — Babylon must be composed of many bodies; in her is found the blood of prophets and saints, showing she takes in the entire apostate church of the earth. DAR 603.4

> Babylon is the apostate religious world in general, the papal church in particular, or the city of Rome; but it is not confined to the Romish Church, which is only a prominent part of great Babylon. DAR 601.2; DAR 601.3

### Babylon's Three Divisions and the Daughters

- **Rev. 16:13** — Three unclean spirits like frogs come out of the mouth of the dragon, the beast, and the false prophet — the three parts of Babylon packaged together.
- **Rev. 13:1, 2** — The beast with seven heads and ten horns is the papacy (the beast division), already fallen centuries before. DAR 604.1
- **Dan. 2** — The great image of the prophecy used to trace the succession of kingdoms; the chart was built on this and the visions of Daniel and John.
- **Rev. 13:11** — The two-horned beast (lamb-like, speaking as a dragon) is the false prophet, identified with the daughters — Protestant America. DAR 604.1
- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — The mark of the beast: none may buy or sell save he that has the mark, the name, or the number of the beast.

> Paganism is the dragon, the great Romish apostasy is the beast, and the daughters — descendants from that church, including the two-horned beast and the great body of the Protestant churches — are the third division of great Babylon. DAR 604.1

### The Fall Is a Moral Fall

- **Rev. 18:4** — Because the voice still calls "Come out of her, my people" after the fall, the fall cannot be a literal overthrow but a moral one; she still exists to sin and her plagues are still future. DAR 607.2; DAR 612.1
- **Rev. 14:6, 7** — The first angel preaches the everlasting gospel with a loud voice, "Fear God and give glory to him, for the hour of his judgment is come."
- **Rev. 14:8** — Because the judgment-hour message was rejected, the second angel announces the moral fall of the churches; the result of rejecting present truth could not have been different. DAR 612.2

> About fifty thousand severed their connection with the denominations, raising the cry "Babylon is fallen" and "Come out of her, my people." DAR 612.1

### The Churches Testify to Their Own Fall

- **Rev. 14:8** — The churches' own writers verbalized the very words of the second angel's message about their own condition.

> The Christian Palladium, May 15, 1844: lukewarmness, division, anarchy, and desolation are distressing the borders of Zion. DAR 613.1

> The Religious Telescope, 1844: "Has God forgotten to be gracious? or is the door of mercy closed?" — unknowingly describing the shut door of the holy place. DAR 613.2

> Even the Philadelphia Sun, Nov. 11, 1844, published a call to a week of special prayer for the outpouring of the Holy Spirit — yet the Spirit leads into the truth they had rejected. DAR 613.3

> Professor Finney of the Oberlin Evangelist, February 1844: the Protestant churches were apathetic or hostile to nearly all moral reforms, with spiritual apathy almost all-pervading and fearfully deep. DAR 613.4

> The Watchman and Reflector: unparalleled church dissension among the Baptists, dating back to the very midst of the awakening. DAR 614.3

### The Laodicean Crossover and the Original Faith

- **Rev. 2; Rev. 3** — The seven churches in order: Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamos, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, Laodicea — the framework for dating the church eras.
- **Rev. 3:4** — "Thou hast a few names even in Sardis which have not defiled their garments" — those few were called out of the Sardis (nominal) church in 1844. By rejecting God's message of time, the nominal churches completed their fall. (S.S. Snow and B. Matthias, 1845)
- **Rev. 2; Rev. 3** — The Philadelphia church (Brotherly Love) was made up of those who came out of Sardis in 1844 under the cry "Babylon is fallen"; the Laodicean church is the next and last stage. (Hiram Edson)
- **Rev. 3:14-20** — Laodicea is lukewarm, saying "I am rich, and increased with goods, and have need of nothing," not knowing she is wretched, miserable, poor, blind, and naked; counseled to buy gold, white raiment, and eyesalve.
- **Hab. 2:1-4** — "Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it"; the vision will not lie though it tarry. The 1843 prophetic chart fulfilled this command. 4SP 241.2

> "God is not giving us a new message. We are to proclaim the message that in 1843 and 1844 brought us out of the other churches." RH Jan. 19, 1905, par. 22

### Rejecting the First Angel Brought the Fall

- **Ev 197.2** — The vision Christ presented to John — the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus — is to be proclaimed to all nations; the churches represented by Babylon fell to become a persecuting power with horns like a lamb but speaking like a dragon.
- **4SP 230.1** — The churches that refused the first angel's message rejected light sent in mercy to arouse them from worldliness and backsliding and to keep them separate from the world.
- **4SP 231.1** — The first angel's message was given to separate the church of Christ from the corrupting influence of the world, but the multitude chose worldly wisdom over the heart-searching message of truth.
- **4SP 231.3** — As the antediluvians who rejected Noah's words found the Spirit ceasing to strive, so when men despise God's warnings His Spirit ceases to arouse conviction; when the Spirit ceases to impress truth, all hearing and all preaching is vain.
- **4SP 232.1** — When the churches spurned God's counsel by rejecting the Advent message, the Lord rejected them; the second angel proclaimed Babylon fallen, and in the summer of 1844 about fifty thousand withdrew.
- **4SP 232.2** — The message of Babylon's fall must apply to a body once pure now corrupt — fitting not the Romish Church but the Protestant churches, divided into innumerable conflicting creeds where God is not, the work of man and Satan.
- **GC 380.2** — In refusing the first angel's warning, they rejected the means of restoration and turned to the friendship of the world — the cause of the worldliness, backsliding, and spiritual death in the churches in 1844.

### Charles Fitch and the Cry in 1843

- **Rev. 14:8** — The message "Babylon is fallen" began to be introduced in 1843, requiring a literal historical fulfillment; the first message ("the hour of his judgment is come") came in 1837, so the second had to follow. (Second Advent Way Marks and High Heaps 64.3)
- **Rev. 18:1-4** — "Come out of her, my people" — the sermon by Charles Fitch in 1843 that gave this specific cry; he was the messenger God sent forth with the second angel's message. (Way Marks and High Heaps 64.3)

> The August 11, 1840 fall of the Ottoman Empire (calculated from Rev. 9) gave power to the judgment-hour message and proved Miller's rules of interpretation; "the Bible and history doth agree."

### The Midnight Cry and the 144,000

- **Christian Experience and Teachings 50, 51** — Hope centered on the coming of the Lord in 1844; the second angel's message was first proclaimed by the servants of God that summer, and in connection with it the midnight cry, "Behold, the Bridegroom cometh," aroused thousands from city to city.
- **Rev. 16:15** — "Behold, I come as a thief; blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments" — the watchman who keeps the garments of Christ's righteousness lest he walk naked into Armageddon.
- **Rev. 7; Rev. 14** — The 144,000 are found in both chapters; they are the sealed people, and Revelation 14 opens with them before flowing into their message, the three angels' messages of 1844.

> The Seventh-day Adventist Church is not Babylon — "if you are teaching that the Seventh-day Adventist Church is Babylon, you are wrong. God has not given you any such message to bear." Satan inspires false theories to divert souls from the true issue.
