## No Buy. No Sell. No Escape. — Study 19

### The Mark and the Seal Are the Same Act

> The end-time conflict is about worship. A mark is set in the forehead on both sides — the question is whose mark.

- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — A power causes all, small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in their right hand or forehead; and no man may buy or sell without the mark or name of the beast.
- **Ezek. 9:4** — The Lord sets a mark on the foreheads of those who sigh and cry for the abominations; the word for "mark" is the same word as "seal."
- **Rev. 7:2-8** — Another angel ascends with the seal of the living God, sealing the servants of God in their foreheads — the same forehead sealing as Ezekiel 9.

### The Sun-Worship Abomination Behind Sunday

- **Ezek. 8:12** — The ancients of Israel do abominations in the dark in the chambers of their imagery, saying the Lord sees them not.
- **Ezek. 8:13, 14** — A greater abomination follows: women sit weeping for Tammuz, a pagan sun-deity, at the gate of the Lord's house.
- **Ezek. 8:15, 16** — The greatest abomination: twenty-five men in the inner court, backs to the temple, faces to the east, worshipping the sun — picturing the end-time church facing the sun. Sun-worship passed from paganism (Sol Invictus) into the church under Constantine in A.D. 321 as Dies Solis, the day of the sun, the origin of Sunday.

### The Beast and Its Mark of Authority

- **Rev. 13:1** — The beast rises from the sea with seven heads and ten horns, full of the name of blasphemy — its attributes (deadly wound, worshipped, reigns 42 months, persecutes the saints, removes three kingdoms, thinks to change God's time and law) identify the papacy.
- **Dan. 7:16, 17** — A "beast" in prophecy means a kingdom; so the mark of the beast is the mark of this kingdom-power.
- Identifying the beast as the papacy of 538-1798, we ask the power directly what its mark is. Rome answers: "Sunday is our mark of authority... The church is above the Bible, and this transference of sabbath observance is proof of that fact." (Catholic Record, Sept. 1, 1923); the change from Sabbath to Sunday "was her act... a mark of her ecclesiastical power and authority." (C. F. Thomas, Nov. 11, 1895)
- Rome further confesses you may read the Bible from Genesis to Revelation and find no line authorizing the sanctification of Sunday — the Scriptures enforce Saturday, the day she never sanctifies. (Cardinal Gibbons, _Faith of Our Fathers_)

### Sunday Enforced — The Image and Mark of the Beast

- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — Because America causes all to receive the mark and forbids buying and selling, this is the moment the mark of the beast becomes enforced.
- The change of the Sabbath into Sunday is the sign or mark of the authority of the Roman Church — "the mark of the beast." GC 448.1
- Enforcing Sunday-keeping by Protestant churches is an enforcement of the worship of the papacy; in enforcing a religious duty by secular power the churches form an image to the beast, so Sunday enforcement in the United States is an enforcement of the worship of the beast and his image. GC 448.3

### A Conflict Over Worship

- **Rev. 14:6, 7** — The first angel's everlasting gospel calls every nation to fear God, give Him glory, and worship Him that made heaven and earth — worship of the Creator, who set apart the seventh day.
- **Rev. 14:9-11** — The third angel warns that whoever worships the beast and his image and receives his mark drinks the wine of the wrath of God; the mark is a matter of worship, not a barcode, tattoo, or chip.
- **Rev. 14:12** — Here are the saints who keep the commandments of God — all of them, not nine — and the faith of Jesus.

### Two-Horned Beast: Lamb-Like, Speaking as a Dragon

- **Rev. 13:11** — Another beast comes up out of the earth with two lamb-like horns but speaks as a dragon — Protestant America, Christian in appearance yet persecuting in voice.
- **Rev. 13:12** — He exercises all the power of the first beast and causes the earth to worship the first beast whose deadly wound was healed.
- The lamb-like horns and dragon voice point to a contradiction between the nation's professions and practice; its "speaking" is the action of its legislative and judicial authorities, enforcing some observance that is an act of homage to the papacy. GC 442.1
- The present push of "Christian nationalism" — openly calling for Sabbath laws on Sunday and privileging Sunday-keeping Christianity while classing seventh-day keepers as not Christian — is this prophecy unfolding before our eyes.

### The Mark Becomes the Mark Only When Enforced

> "We therefore now call attention to the very important inquiry, What constitutes the mark of the beast?" — Uriah Smith

- **Dan. 7:25** — The little horn (papal power) wages war against God, wears out the saints, and thinks to change times and laws — which must be the laws of the Most High, for only His law touches time (the fourth commandment). DAR 551.1
- The mark and worship of the beast are enforced by the two-horned beast; receiving the mark is a specific act it causes to be done. The third angel's message is sent mercifully in advance to prepare the people for the coming danger. DAR 560.1
- A person receives the mark only by adopting the change knowing it to be the work of the beast and accepting it on that power's authority, in opposition to the requirements of God; thus past Reformers and present sincere Sunday-keepers do not now bear the mark. DAR 560.1

### Two Classes — No Third Option

- **Rev. 15:2, 3** — In the final contest all Christendom divides into two classes only: those who keep the commandments and faith of Jesus, and those who worship the beast and receive his mark; the victors stand on the sea of glass singing the song of Moses and the Lamb. GC 450.1
- **CET 189.1** — Not all who profess to keep the Sabbath will be sealed; many who teach the truth knew the Master's will and every point of faith but had not corresponding works — refusing the seal of God means receiving the mark, with no neutral ground.

### National Apostasy and the Coming No-Buy, No-Sell

- A time is coming when the law of God is made void in our land; the rulers of our nation will by legislative enactments enforce the Sunday law, bringing God's people into great peril, and national apostasy will be followed by national ruin. RH Dec. 18, 1888
- Those keeping God's commandments must bestir themselves and work earnestly to delay as long as possible the threatened calamity; when a Protestant government sacrifices the principles of its Constitution we may plead, "It is time for thee, Lord, to work, for they have made void thy law." RH Dec. 18, 1888

### Out of the Cities — Building the Antitypical Ark

> "In harmony with the light given me, I am urging the people to come out from the great centers of population."

- The Lord urges His people out of the great centers of population; those who remain in the cities unnecessarily do so at the peril of their soul's salvation — not merely martyrdom, but salvation. CL 9.4
- Again and again God instructs His people to take their families away from the cities into the country to raise their own provisions, "for in the future the problem of buying and selling will be a very serious one." CL 9.5
- **Gen. 19** — As Lot was commanded out of Sodom into the mountains and forbidden to flee to the plains, so we must take literally the call to secluded places among the mountains, not spiritualize it away.
- **Jer. / Isa.** — God's people are pictured in the mountains, where the rocks become their munition and defense in the coming second siege.

### Acting in Faith on the Warning

- **Gen. 6-7 (Noah)** — Noah was counted righteous because he saw the warning and built the ark; we build an antitypical ark by leaving the city and preparing, and God's angels guard the ark from those who try to tear it apart.
- **Gen. 41 (Joseph)** — Forewarned of seven years of famine, Joseph did not ask what to do but took action and prepared — our pattern for buying-and-selling prophecy.
- **Matt. 24 (A.D. 70)** — When Jesus warned to flee Jerusalem into the mountains, every believing Christian obeyed though it made no sense; that is our call now.
- The time has come, as God opens the way, for families to move out of the cities; parents should secure a place as their means allow, with land to be cultivated, and separated families may be united in such places — a promise, not a threat. CL 24.2
- Self-sufficiency requires the three pillars — land, water, and wood (about one acre to feed one person for a year; about six cords of wood from six acres of forest to heat a home) — and a return to primitive godliness, knowing the physical Bible without dependence on technology.
