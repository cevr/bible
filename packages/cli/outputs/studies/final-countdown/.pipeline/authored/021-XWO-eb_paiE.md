## Alert! — Now's Your Chance to Prepare! — Study 20

### The Siege of Jerusalem as Our Pattern

> "When Christ referred to the destruction of Jerusalem, His prophetic words reached beyond that event to the final conflagration." — Christ Triumphant 353.

- **Luke 21:20, 21** — When the disciples saw Jerusalem compassed with armies, they were to flee to the mountains and the dwellers in Judea were to depart out; Christ's sign demanded immediate action. CTr 353.2
- **Luke 21** (whole chapter) — Christ warned His disciples of the destruction of Jerusalem and the temple, an event foretold by Daniel; the entire chapter is a warning to those who live in the last scenes of earth's history. CTr 353.3
- **Luke 21:20, 21** — When the idolatrous Roman standards were set up in the holy ground outside the walls, the followers of Christ were to find safety in flight; the signal must be immediately obeyed without a moment's hesitation, lest they share the general destruction. GC 25.4

### God Makes a Way of Escape

- **Luke 21:20, 21** — After the Romans under Cestius surrounded the city they unexpectedly abandoned the siege when attack seemed certain; the Roman general withdrew without apparent reason because God's merciful providence was directing events for the good of His people. GC 30.2
- **Luke 21:20, 21** — The promised sign had been given; events were so overruled that neither Jews nor Romans hindered the flight, and the Christians escaped unmolested to Pella beyond Jordan. GC 30.2
- **Luke 21:20, 21** — Not one Christian perished in the destruction of Jerusalem, though Josephus records that over 1.1 million people died in the siege. GC 30.2
- **Gen. 7** — As God gave Noah just enough time to finish the ark, so He parts the Red Sea and the Jordan for those willing to go when He says go; the believers who heeded the sign did not hesitate, and God made a way.

### The Warning to Leave the Cities

> "The time is not far distant, when, like the early disciples, we shall be forced to seek a refuge in desolate and solitary places." — Country Living, 1885.

- **Luke 21:20, 21** — As the siege of Jerusalem was the signal for flight to the Judean Christians, so the assumption of power by our nation in a decree enforcing the papal Sabbath will be our warning to leave the large cities for retired homes in secluded places among the mountains. CL 32.1 (1885)
- **Rev. 13:14, 15** — When the leading churches of the United States unite to influence the state to enforce their decrees, Protestant America will have formed an image of the Roman hierarchy, and civil penalties upon dissenters will result; this was the sign to get out of the city. GC 445.1
- **Rev. 13:11-17** — The image to the beast is the form of apostate Protestantism developed when the churches seek the aid of the civil power to enforce their dogmas. GC 445.2
- **Rev. 13** — The Protestant world has set up an idol sabbath where God's Sabbath should be and is treading in the footsteps of the papacy; for this reason God's people must move out of the cities into retired country places where they may cultivate the land and raise their own produce. CL 21.1 (1897)

### The First Siege Has Already Come

- **Dan. 9** (siege of Jerusalem foretold) — AD 66 the first siege of Jerusalem warned the believers to flee to the country and mountains; the AD 70 second siege came with no mercy for those who did not heed.
- **Rev. 13** — The National Sunday-Rest Bill (Blair Bill, Senate Bill 2904, 1888) was the first antitypical siege; the message to flee to the country and secure homes and land has already been given.
- **Ex. 20:8-11** — When the United States government incorporated the fourth commandment in its legislation and deliberately changed its Sabbath to Sunday, it asserted its independence of God in the very likeness of the papacy. _(A.T. Jones, Review and Herald, 6/26/1900)_
- **Ex. 20:8-11** — From 1888 until 1892 the whole National Reform combination tried to get Congress to decide the Sabbath question by law and fix it to Sunday as the American sabbath; the American Sabbath Union, organized in 1888, labored through 1890 for a national Sunday law. _(A.T. Jones, American Sentinel, 3/5/1891)_

### Now Is the Time to Prepare

- **Rev. 13** — Since the first siege is past, we are not waiting for any further sign before entering preparation mode; the next event is the final siege at Sunday law.
- **Ex. 20:8-11** — Get out of the cities as soon as possible and purchase a little piece of land where you can have a garden. CL 17.3 (1903)
- **Ex. 20:8-11** — The time has come when, as God opens the way, families should move out of the cities into the country; the parents should get as suitable a place as their means will allow, with land that may be cultivated. CL 24.2 (1903)

### Judgments Are Coming on the Cities

- **Hag. 2:6, 7** — When the Lord arises to shake terribly the earth He will not cease until His work in punishment is done; the destruction will begin in certain places, will be sudden, and but few will escape.
- **Isa. 26:21** — The wrath of God is preparing to come upon all the cities, not all at once but one after another; if punishment in one city does not bring others to repentance, their time will come too. (probation is still open while repentance is possible)
- **Gen. 19:15-17** — As Lot was commanded to flee Sodom, God's people are told to leave the cities so they are not consumed in the judgments meant for the wicked; to be destroyed in a judgment for the wicked is to be counted with the wicked.
- **Gen. 7:1, 23** — As Noah outside the ark would have perished, so safety is found only inside the antitypical ark of a prepared country home.

### Economic Distress and Civil War

- **James 5:1-4** — In India, China, Russia, and the cities of America thousands are dying of starvation while the moneyed men control the market, buying low and selling high; this means starvation to the poorer classes and will result in a civil war.
- **Dan. 12:1** — There will be a time of trouble such as never was and economic distress like never before; as in the Dust Bowl of the Dirty Thirties, the city dwellers starved while those with farms, root cellars, hand pumps, and draft animals were fed.
- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — Before the full no-buy, no-sell decree, years of starvation may drive society to brand dissenters "useless eaters" and cut them wholly off from food, water, heat, and power.

### Preparing the Antitypical Ark

> "Everything has to be in the ark. ... The only access to things we have is in our antitypical ark, in our property, in our mountain home."

- **Gen. 6:14-22** — As Noah had access only to what was within the ark during the flood, our self-sufficient country home must supply everything; this preparation is more serious now than the farming generation of the 1890s, when nearly half the labor force were farmers, ever faced.
- **3 John 1:2** — Because the health message is the right arm of the gospel, the prepared garden must grow not only food but herbs for natural remedies — peppermint, oregano, cayenne, mullein, raspberry leaf.
- **Gen. 1:29** — Survival requires growing the complete package: carbohydrates (wheat, potatoes, corn, squash), fats (sunflower, pumpkin seed, flaxseed, soybean, brassica oils), and proteins (peas, beans, lentils, soybeans). About one acre per person is needed for a year's food.

### Faith, Patience, and Total Surrender

- **1 Cor. 10:13** — God will not suffer us to be tempted above what we are able, but with the temptation will make a way to escape; He does not remove us from the valley of the shadow of death but blesses those who have heeded the warning and reached the ark.
- **Ps. 46:10** — "Be still, and know that I am God"; as Moses spent 40 years shepherding before God said "You're ready," His people must work fervently yet wait patiently, without panic, anxiety, or worry, which spring from a lack of faith.
- **Rev. 18:1-4** — The majority of God's people are still in Babylon, but the fourth angel's loud-cry message calls them to come out of the cities and join those already prepared in the ark.
- **Gen. 22:9-12** — God stopped Abraham only when the knife was descending and his will was fully surrendered; this is how willing we must be to sacrifice everything for the truth and for obedience.
