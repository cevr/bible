## Adventists Are Not Christians — The Final Countdown, Study 18

> What is the image of the beast, and when are we to see it formed? On our timeline of final events the image must be set up before the Sunday law, the mark of the beast, and the no-buy-no-sell — and it is forming right now, under our nose.

### The Second Beast and the Image It Builds

- **Rev. 13:11** — A second beast rises out of the earth with two horns like a lamb but speaks as a dragon: a young, Christian-looking world power that will yet speak with the voice of persecution.
- **Rev. 13:12** — He exercises all the power of the first beast (the papal beast that ruled 538–1798) and causes the earth to worship that beast whose deadly wound was healed.
- **Rev. 13:13** — He does great wonders, even making fire come down from heaven in the sight of men, deceiving the world by real miracles.
- **Rev. 13:14** — By those miracles he tells men to make an image to the beast which had the wound by a sword and did live — there is the image of the beast.
- **Rev. 13:15** — He gives life to the image so that it both speaks and decrees that as many as will not worship it should be killed.

### A Beast Is a Kingdom; an Image Is a Likeness

- **Dan. 7:17, 23** — The four great beasts are four kings; the fourth beast is a fourth kingdom — confirming that in prophecy a beast represents a kingdom.
- **Gen. 1:26** — God made man "in our image, after our likeness"; an image is a likeness, a thing made to look like the original.
- **Ex. 20:4** — "Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness" — an image is a likeness, so an image to the beast must look like the beast: America will come to resemble the papacy of the Dark Ages.

### What Constitutes the Papacy — and Its Image

> To understand the image of the papacy, we must first understand the papacy itself.

- **Rev. 13:14** — The command to make an image to the beast sends us back to define the beast: the papacy was a church clothed with civil power, an ecclesiastical body with authority to punish all dissenters by confiscation, imprisonment, torture, and death. DAR 546.3 (Uriah Smith, Thoughts on Daniel and the Revelation)
- **Rev. 13:14** — An image of the papacy would be another ecclesiastical body clothed with similar power: let the Protestant churches be clothed with power to define and punish heresy under the penalties of civil law, and we have an exact representation of the papacy in its supremacy. DAR 546.3
- **Rev. 13:14** — The left's opposition to a religious amendment is the very stimulus that drives its friends to secure it; no neutral ground is left, and the government must become either nominally wholly Christian or in reality wholly secular. (Uriah Smith, The Marvel of Nations, 1897, p.229)

### When Is the Image Formed? Church United to State

- **Rev. 13:15** — When the leading churches of the United States, uniting on points of doctrine held in common, influence the state to enforce their decrees and sustain their institutions, then Protestant America will have formed an image of the Roman hierarchy, and civil penalties on dissenters will result. GC 445.1
- **Rev. 13:14** — The image to the beast represents that form of apostate Protestantism developed when the Protestant churches seek the aid of the civil power to enforce their dogmas. GC 445.2
- **Rev. 13:14** — Sunday-reform bodies such as the American Sabbath Union and the Woman's Christian Temperance Union swung into line, the latter adding to its temperance aim "the enthroning of Christ in American politics" to secure a theocracy and better observance of Sunday — papacy over again, called in prophecy an image of the beast. DAR 571.4
- **Rev. 13:14** — National Reformers (Christian nationalists) urge that the organized church itself officially speak "the voice of God in the halls of Congress," for no association is clothed with that authority but the church is divine. DAR 571.5-6 (F. M. Foster, Christian Statesman, October 1892)

### Speaking As a Dragon Today

> "You have no idea the dragon you have awakened." — this is biblical language; the dragon is being awakened to speak, to legislate, and to put the image into effect.

- **Rom. 13** — At the Charlie Kirk memorial the call went out for rulers to "wield the sword for the terror of evil men," appealing to Paul's godly government — but the "evil" are the heretics of differing opinion, and under a Sunday law that terror falls on those who do not worship on Sunday.
- **Rev. 13:15** — Voices now openly say the magistrate may seize the property of "apostate" churches and give it to "the faithful," calling jail or eviction the moderate, soft position — no longer freedom of religion but church-state structure, papacy all over again.

### A Christian Sunday, Never the Seventh Day

- **Rev. 13:15** — Christian-nationalist advocates affirm that Sabbath laws would close businesses "on Sundays," explicitly privileging the Christian holy day — Sunday, not Saturday — as the law of the land. (The word "Sabbath" has been held in U.S. law since the early 1900s to mean Sunday, the first day.)
- **Matt. 7:22-23** — Many will plead, "I prophesied in Thy name," yet hear, "I never knew you; depart from me, ye that work iniquity" — keeping or claiming the Sabbath, loving Jesus, or reading the Bible is not the criterion when one is opposed to the third angel's message.

> Asked which churches may vote in a Christian nation, the advocate answered: Catholics, Orthodox, Baptists, Anglicans, Presbyterians — yes; Mormons, Jehovah's Witnesses, and Seventh-day Adventists — no, "they're a cult." Adventists deceive themselves to think they can walk in agreement with an ideology directly opposed to their message warning against the mark of the beast.

### The Protestant Spirit Already Manifested

- **Rev. 13** — Rome destroyed multitudes she adjudged heretics, and the Protestant church has shown the same spirit: the burning of Michael Servetus at Geneva under Calvin, the oppression of dissenters by the Church of England, the hanging of Quakers and whipping of Baptists by the Puritan fathers — proof that men of strong religious prejudice cannot forbear to coerce, a state of things we now look for under the closing prophecy of chapter 13. DAR 605.1
- **Rev. 13** — Whenever the church has obtained secular power she has used it to punish dissent; Protestant churches that allied with worldly powers persecuted nonconformist ministers with fine, imprisonment, torture, and martyrdom in the 16th and 17th centuries. GC 443.3

### Miracles, the Counterfeit Revival, and the False Fire

- **Rev. 13:14** — Closely tied to the working of miracles is the erection of an image to the beast; the deception wrought by the miracles prepares men to comply with the demand to make the image. DAR 546.2
- **5T 451.1** — When Protestantism reaches across the gulf to grasp the hand of Rome and clasp hands with spiritualism, and the country repudiates every principle of its Constitution as a Protestant and republican government, then we may know the end is near.
- **GC 464.1** — Before God's final judgments there will be among His people such a revival of primitive godliness as has not been seen since apostolic times, and many will separate from the worldly churches to accept the truths proclaimed for the second coming.
- **GC 464.1** — Satan will forestall the genuine revival with a counterfeit, making it appear in deceived churches that God's blessing is poured out, with great seeming religious interest, while the work is of another spirit.
- **2 Thess. 2:9, 10** — The coming of the Lord is preceded by the working of Satan with all power and signs and lying wonders; before the second advent there will be like manifestations of satanic power. GC 553.2
- **Rev. 13:13, 14** — John foretells real miracle-working power — fire down from heaven — and "no mere impostures are here foretold": men are deceived by the miracles Satan's agents truly have power to do, not which they only pretend. GC 553.2
- **Matt. 24:24** — Those who look to miracles as a sign of divine guidance are in grave danger; the enemy works through his agents who have departed from the faith — ex-Adventist evangelists who will seemingly bring fire down from heaven by "lying wonders" to deceive, if possible, the very elect. 19MR 372.2

### The Mark and the Need of an Ark

- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — He causes all, small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in the right hand or forehead, so that no man may buy or sell save he that has the mark, the name, or the number of the beast — the end goal of the image of the beast.
- **CL 9.5** — Again and again the Lord has instructed our people to take their families from the cities into the country where they can raise their own provisions, for the problem of buying and selling will be very serious; we should now get out of the cities into rural districts, free from the interference of enemies. (Letter 5, 1904)

### Preparing the Modern Ark: Land, Water, Wood

> The three pillars of a country property are land, water, and wood — how much, and how to get it.

- **CL 9.5** — Like Noah, who could not buy or sell in the ark and so prepared it to sustain all its life, we need a country home ready to sustain us and those who join us through the loud cry; one acre of garden per person, with separate land for wood and pasture, plus livestock, food preservation, water systems, natural remedies, and education for the no-buy-no-sell years.

### Q&A — The Sunday Law and the Death Decree

- **Rev. 10** — We live in "time no longer"; there are no time prophecies left to tell how long until the Sunday law, only the certainty that it will be pushed by the churches upon the state, and that growing our own food will take years.
- **Matt. 24:20** — The great tribulation of which Jesus spoke was the Dark Ages of 538–1798, not the final beheading some fear; the death decree comes only at the sixth plague.
- **Dan. 12:1** — When the death decree comes, God's people will be delivered; the faithful who are sealed and have not taken the mark are saved and will flee into the wilderness, fed and protected, until deliverance comes.
