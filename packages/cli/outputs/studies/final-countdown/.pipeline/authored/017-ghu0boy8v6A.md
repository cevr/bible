## The Final Warning Has Begun — Are You Ready for the Close of Probation? — Study 16

### The Closing Scenes of the Atonement

> "Solemn are the scenes connected with the closing work of the atonement." The judgment of the living is now upon us, and there are real events — scenes — attending the close of probation.

- **Mark 13:33** — Watch, lest probation pass while you are unaware; only the watching servant will not be overtaken. CIHS 188.1
- **Rev. 3:3** — To the one who refuses to watch, Christ comes as a thief; the watchful know what to look for and are not surprised. CIHS 188.1
- The events connected with the close of probation and the work of preparation for the time of trouble have been _clearly_ presented; any version of these events newer than that original presentation is error. DD 36.3

### The Four Winds and the Angry Horse

- **Rev. 7:1-3** — Four angels hold the four winds from the four corners of the earth until the servants of God are sealed in their foreheads; the winds and the sealing are linked.
- The four winds are represented as "an angry horse seeking to break loose, and rush over the face of the whole earth, bearing destruction and death in its path." 3SM 409.1
- **Jer. 4:7, 11-13** — The destroyer of the Gentiles comes; a full wind (north, south, east, and west) brings desolation, his chariots as a whirlwind and his horses swifter than eagles — this is past the time of cleansing.

### The Locust Army of Joel

- **Joel 1:4-6** — The palmerworm, locust, cankerworm, and caterpillar are four stages of one creature; this devouring locust is "a nation" with the teeth of a lion.
- **Joel 2:1-5** — The day of the Lord brings darkness, fire, and devastation; the locusts have "the appearance of horses," running like horsemen with the noise of chariots set in battle array.
- **Dan. 7:16, 17** — A beast represents a kingdom; the locust, being a beast, likewise represents a nation.
- **Rev. 16:14** — The day of the Lord is "the battle of that great day of God Almighty."
- **Rev. 18:8** — Her plagues come in one day — death, mourning, famine, and fire; this is the day of the Lord, the day of the plagues.

### The Locusts Identified as Turkey

- **Rev. 9:2-4** — Smoke from the bottomless pit darkens the sun; locusts come forth and are commanded to hurt only the men who lack the seal of God — the same sealing language as Revelation 7.
- **Rev. 9:7-9** — The locusts are shaped like horses prepared for battle, with teeth as the teeth of lions (as in Joel and Jeremiah) and wings sounding like chariots and many horses running to battle.
- **Judges 6:5, 6; 7:12; 8:24** — The locust-like multitudes are identified as the descendants of Ishmael and the people of the east.
- The 1840 fulfillment was Josiah Litch's exposition of Revelation 9, predicting the fall of the Ottoman Empire; at the time specified, Turkey accepted the protection of the allied powers — the event exactly fulfilled the prediction. GC 334, 335
- These horsemen, chariots, full winds, and whirlwinds are all "the same players" — the worldwide war from Turkey held back by the angels.

### Revelation 18 and the Fall of the Towers

> "Then the words of Revelation 18:1-3 will be fulfilled" when the great buildings of New York are thrown down. LS 411.5

- **Rev. 18:1-3** — Another angel lightens the earth with his glory, crying "Babylon is fallen, is fallen"; verse 3 names the merchants of the earth, identifying the World Trade Center where the merchants of the earth combined.
- **John 9:7** — The blind man must wash in the pool of Siloam ("Sent") to come seeing; Laodicea, thinking she sees, must wash to receive her sight.
- **Luke 13:4, 5** — Those slain when the tower in Siloam fell were no worse sinners than others; their fall was a warning to repent — a picture of the towers falling as a sign of coming judgment.
- **Rev. 18:4, 11** — "Come out of her, my people" and the merchants weeping that no man buys their merchandise belong to a later event than 9/11 — the full outpouring of the plagues, not the 2001 sign.

### Searching Jerusalem and the Sealing

- **Zeph. 1:9-12** — A great crashing and a cry connected with the merchants precede the time when God searches Jerusalem with candles — the sealing work.
- **Ezek. 9** — God's mark is set on the foreheads of those who sigh and cry for the abominations; six men with slaughter weapons follow — the day of slaughter comes right after the sealing.
- **Isa. 27:2, 3, 6-9** — God waters His vineyard night and day with the latter rain (the doctrine), causing Israel to take root, blossom, and fill the world with fruit; He stays His rough wind in the day of the east wind, after which the iniquity of Jacob is purged.

### The Latter Rain and the Whirlwind

- **Joel 2:23** — God gives the former rain moderately, then pours the latter rain on roots grown deep enough to absorb it — the truth coming to prepared soil.
- **Hosea 8:7** — They have sown the wind (war) and shall reap the whirlwind (the four winds); the stalk yields no meal and the stranger swallows it up — the meaning of 9/11.
- **Prov. 1:27** — Destruction comes as a whirlwind; the destruction of the wicked in the plagues is that whirlwind.
- **Isa. 30:25** — Rivers and streams of waters come upon every high mountain "in the day of the great slaughter, when the towers fall" — the latter rain (original doctrine) returning since 2001.

### Turkey as a National Guidepost

> "These angels now hold the winds of strife, waiting for the church of God to prepare for his coming."

- The sealing angel goes through Jerusalem (the church) to seal the faithful, and while this work goes forward Turkey stands as a national guidepost, that men may know what is going on in the sanctuary above. SDP 248
- **Dan. 11:45** — "No man knows when Turkey will take its departure from Europe, but when that move is made, earth's history will be short"; the events at the close of probation are the original, clearly-presented teaching, not the spiritualized interpretation. SDP 248
- **Dan. 12:1** — At that time (the end of Dan. 11:45) Michael stands up and there is a time of trouble such as never was — this is the close of probation, the event clearly presented. SDP 248
- The fate of Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, and Rome is recorded for today's nations; while the world watches Turkey, let the servant of God watch his great High Priest, whose ministry for sin is almost over. SDP 248

### The Spurious Loud Cry and the True

- **Acts 3:19** — "Repent ye therefore... that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come" — the latter rain now falling; do not mistake it for something else.
- Satan stirs up minds to give false interpretations of Scripture — a spurious loud cry — so the real message may not have its effect; this is one of the greatest evidences that the true loud cry will soon be heard. 3SM 410.1
- If a message causes no shaking among the conservative Laodiceans, it is because it is not the true loud cry, not the latter rain.

### Question: Are the Locust Armies of Joel God's Remnant?

- **Joel 2:11** — God calls the locust host "his army" because they do His bidding (the destruction of the plagues), not because they are His holy people; the heathen who sting like scorpions are never God's description of His people.
- **Rev. 17:16** — The ten horns hate the harlot and burn her, for God put it in their hearts to fulfill His will; doing God's will does not make them God's people.
- **Joel 3:11** — "Assemble yourselves... all ye heathen" — confirming this devouring army is the heathen, not the remnant.

### Question: Did 9/11 Begin the Third Woe?

- **Rev. 9 (Third Woe)** — No; the third woe falls _after_ the close of probation and is the same as the seven last plagues, administered through worldwide war. The 1850 Adventist chart reads "The third woe cometh quickly — the seven last plagues."
- **Dan. 11:45 / Dan. 12:1** — Turkey going to Jerusalem is the sign that probation will close imminently; when Turkey is removed from Jerusalem and Michael stands up, Turkey wages all-out war, beginning the third woe — the seven last plagues.
