## The Last Warning — Study 25

### The Seven Last Plagues and the Heavenly Sanctuary

> The last message of warning must answer one question: warning for what? If we do not know what we are warning about, we cannot warn anyone.

- **Rev. 15:1, 5, 6, 8** — Seven angels have the seven last plagues that fill up the wrath of God; the heavenly temple is opened, then filled with smoke from God's glory, and no man can enter until the plagues are finished.
- **Ex. 40:33-35** — Moses finished the work, a cloud covered the tabernacle, the glory of the Lord filled it, and Moses could not enter — a type of the close of probation when the heavenly atonement is done.
- **2 Chron. 5:14** — The priests could not stand to minister because the cloud and glory of the Lord filled the house of God; the smoke displays who truly controls the world.

### The Third Angel's Message and the Wine of God's Wrath

- **Rev. 14:9-11** — The third angel cries with a loud voice: whoever worships the beast and receives his mark drinks the wine of the wrath of God — the seven last plagues — undiluted in the cup of His indignation.
- **Rev. 13:11-17** — The warning points back to the beast and its image, whose mark bars men from buying or selling; this is what the third angel warns against.
- **Jer. 25:15-16** — Take the wine cup of His fury and cause all nations to drink it; they drink, are moved, and are mad because of the sword God sends among them — the plagues enrage the wicked rather than bring repentance.
- **Rev. 18:6, 8, 11** — Babylon is rewarded double for her works in the same cup she filled; her plagues come in one day — death, mourning, famine, fire — and the merchants weep, for no man buys their merchandise anymore.
- **Rev. 13:17** — The beast decreed that men could not buy or sell; God answers that it will be doubly worse for those who enforced it — they reap what they sowed unless they repent first.

### The Watchman's Solemn Duty

- **Ezek. 33:1-3** — When God brings the sword upon a land, the people set a watchman; when he sees the sword come he must blow the trumpet and warn the people.
- **Ezek. 33:4-5** — Whoever hears the trumpet and takes no warning bears his own blood; he that takes warning delivers his soul.
- **Ezek. 33:6** — If the watchman sees the sword and blows not the trumpet, the people's blood will be required at the watchman's hand — the responsibility is deadly serious on every side.
- **Ezek. 33:7** — The son of man is set a watchman to the house of Israel, to hear the word at God's mouth and warn the people; everyone who knows the truth shares this obligation.

> "In a special sense Seventh-day Adventists have been set in the world as watchmen and light bearers. To them has been entrusted the last warning for a perishing world... the proclamation of the first, second, and third angels' messages. There is no other work of so great importance. They are to allow nothing else to absorb their attention." 9T 19.1

### Gideon's Three Hundred — The Few Who Will Not Be Afraid

- **Judges 7:3** — "Whosoever is fearful and afraid, let him return"; 22,000 of 32,000 departed, leaving 10,000 — a warning that fear will thin God's army.
- **Judges 7:6-7** — Only 300 lapped water with their hand to their mouth, ready and watching; by these few the Lord said He would save and deliver the Midianites.
- **Judges 7:12** — The Midianites and Amalekites lay in the valley like grasshoppers for multitude, their camels as the sand of the sea — a picture tied to Revelation 9 and Joel. (See GC 334.4)
- **Judges 7:20** — The three companies blew the trumpets, broke the pitchers, held the lamps, and cried, "The sword of the Lord" — a type of the watchmen giving the last warning.
- **1 Cor. 10:11** — These things are written for examples unto the ends of the world; Gideon's 300 typify the select 144,000 group that gives the last warning and is not afraid.

### Blow the Trumpet — The Day of the Lord

- **Joel 2:1-5** — Blow the trumpet in Zion and sound an alarm, for the day of the Lord comes — a day of darkness, gloominess, clouds and thick darkness; this is not the second coming but the day of the plagues (Rev. 16).
- **Joel 2:13-15** — Rend your heart and not your garments and turn to the Lord, for He is gracious and merciful; sanctify a fast and call a solemn assembly — the work of the day of atonement, repenting before probation closes.
- **Amos 3:3, 6-7** — Can two walk together except they be agreed? When a trumpet is blown in the city the people are afraid; surely the Lord reveals His secret to His servants the prophets before the evil comes.
- **Isa. 58:1** — Cry aloud, spare not, lift up thy voice like a trumpet, and show the people their transgression — the watchman who sees clearly must proclaim loudly.
- **1 Cor. 14:8** — If the trumpet gives an uncertain sound, who shall prepare for the battle? A message that says "we cannot know prophecy" leaves the people unready for the war that is coming.

### The Ten Virgins and the Midnight Cry Repeated

- **Matt. 25:4, 6, 7** — The wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps; at midnight the cry was made, "Behold, the bridegroom cometh," and the virgins arose and trimmed their lamps.
- **Dan. 12:1** — The message burning in the virgins' lamps is the time of trouble such as never was — the very warning seen throughout Scripture.

> "When the third angel's message is preached as it should be, power attends its proclamation... I am often referred to the parable of the ten virgins... This parable has been and will be fulfilled to the very letter, for it has a special application to this time." Review and Herald, Aug. 19, 1890, par. 3

- **Dan. 2** — The Advent preachers of 1840-44 illustrated the rise and fall of the four universal kingdoms with a carved image of Nebuchadnezzar's statue, giving the message with power because people could see it; the midnight cry will be repeated in the loud cry "to the very letter." (The Advent Herald, May 29, 1844, p. 135 — Bro. Fitch on Daniel 2, 7, and 8)
- **Dan. 7** — The same prophetic beasts are to be set before the cities in tangible displays, so people can see in person the kingdoms and the warning of the judgments to come.
