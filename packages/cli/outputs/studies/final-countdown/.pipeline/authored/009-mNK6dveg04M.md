## Fear What's Coming? — Study 8

_The Final Countdown — End-Time Events Timeline live study series, with MacKenzie Drebit (Amazing Discoveries)._

### Fear Comes From the Unknown — God Gives a Sound Mind

> The fear of the final events comes because people are in the dark and don't know what's coming. God's answer is to shift us from the unknown to the known — to understand the time so we are not caught unaware like a thief in the night.

- **2 Tim. 1:7** — God has not given us the spirit of fear, but of power, love, and a sound mind; that sound mind is the frontal lobe, the seat of reason where we receive the seal of God or the mark of the beast. Fear puts us in a reactive, panicked state; faith and a sound mind keep us steady when everything collapses.

### The Events Connected With the Close of Probation Are Clearly Presented

> "The events connected with the close of probation and the work of preparation for the time of trouble are clearly presented." Most have no more understanding of these truths than if they had never been revealed.

- **Rev. 3:15** — Christ wishes we were cold or hot, not lukewarm; the cold person realizes how spiritually poor he is and is driven to change, while the lukewarm is complacent and feels no need.
- **Rev. 3:17** — Laodicea thinks she is rich and in need of nothing, not knowing she is wretched, miserable, poor, blind, and naked — which is exactly why she has no idea what events lead to the close of probation.
- _EGW: "The events connected with the close of probation… are clearly presented. But multitudes have no more understanding of these important truths than if they had never been revealed. Satan watches to catch away every impression that would make them wise unto salvation, and the time of trouble will find them unready." Mar 160._

### Nothing to Fear But Forgetting How God Led Us

- _EGW: "We have nothing to fear for the future, except as we shall forget the way the Lord has led us, and His teaching in our past history." CET 204.1 (Christian Experience and Teachings 204.1)._ We must not despise the old paths and our pioneer history.
- _EGW: "But if men refuse to search for the truth as for hid treasure, if the mind is pleased with the theories of error, the soul will remain in darkness… Error never sanctifies. It can do no good. And how full of darkness is the soul that receives error as truth." Ms 4, 1898._ Only truth sanctifies; a false understanding of the events produces a false growth of character.

### The Revelation Is a Book We Need to Study

> "As we near the close of this world's history, the prophecies relating to the last days especially demand our study." The last book of the New Testament is full of truth we need to understand.

- **Rev. 1:1** — The revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave to show His servants things which must shortly come to pass — prophecy is from Jesus, the road map of where the pitfalls lie.
- **Rev. 1:2** — John bore record of the word of God and the testimony of Jesus Christ and all things that he saw.
- **Rev. 1:3** — Blessed is he that readeth and they that keep the things written; prophecy is not merely intellectual but practical — it demands action and changes the character.
- _EGW: "As we near the close of this world's history, the prophecies relating to the last days especially demand our study… But Christ through His servant John has here declared what shall be in the last days." COL 133.2._

### The Prudent Foresee and Prepare; the Sluggard Sleeps

> A prudent man foresees the evil and hides himself. Fear comes from not preparing; by preparing we become ready.

- **Prov. 22:3** — A prudent man foreseeth the evil and hideth himself, but the simple pass on and are punished; the prudent makes provision while the simple presume.
- **Prov. 6:6-8** — Go to the ant, thou sluggard; she provideth her meat in the summer and gathereth her food in the harvest — we must store up for the winter of final events while it is still the preparation time.
- **1 Cor. 10:11** — All these things happened unto them for examples and are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come; the ant's harvest applies directly to us.
- **Prov. 6:9-11** — How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? Poverty comes as an armed robber upon the unprepared — if you don't prepare for the winter, you will starve.
- **Prov. 18:15** — The heart of the prudent getteth knowledge and the ear of the wise seeketh knowledge; intellectual fear is removed by acquiring the knowledge we need.

### Three Areas of Preparation: Spiritual, Mental, Physical

> Spiritual fear is alleviated by obedience; mental fear by acquiring knowledge; and both are sealed by physical, practical preparation.

- **Rom. 8:28** — All things work together for good to them that love God; whatever happens, ask what God wants you to learn and how to grow, for past experiences prepare us for what is coming.
- **Phil. 4:7** — The peace of God which passeth all understanding shall keep your hearts and minds; this peace comes because God tells us ahead of time and nothing surprises us.
- **John 14:27, 29** — Peace I leave with you; let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid; "now I have told you before it come to pass" — God tells us beforehand precisely so we need not fear.

### A Calm Walk With Jesus Through Conflict

- _EGW: "Christ calls us to walk with him in this world in the path of humble, trustful obedience, which will secure a pure, holy, happy life. Which will we choose — liberty in Christ, or bondage and tyranny in the service of Satan?" RH Aug. 2, 1881, par. 7._
- _EGW: "We need not be alarmed if this path of liberty is laid through conflicts and sufferings… The peace which passeth knowledge will cost us battles with the powers of darkness, struggles severe against selfishness and inward sins." RH Aug. 2, 1881, par. 8._ Peace is won by fighting the good fight.

### Perfect Love Casts Out Fear

> When love for souls and for Christ propels us, fear is no longer even an option.

- **1 John 4:18** — There is no fear in love, but perfect love casteth out fear, because fear hath torment; he that feareth is not made perfect in love.
- **1 John 4:17** — Herein is our love made perfect, that we may have boldness in the day of judgment; we can be bold in the time of trouble when our love is perfected and our fear cast out.

### Noah's Obedience — The Pattern for Final Preparation

> God said, "Build a boat" — not "I will make you float." He says, "Prepare for the final events and I will protect you in your obedience," not in a magical bubble of disobedience. Noah put all that he had into the ark.

- _EGW: "The 'time of trouble such as never was' is soon to open upon us; and we shall need an experience which we do not now possess, and which many are too indolent to obtain… In that time of trial, every soul must stand for himself before God. Though Noah, Daniel, and Job were in the land… they shall but deliver their own souls by their righteousness." GC 622.4 (Ezek. 14:20)._ That righteousness was obedience.

### The Sequence: No Buy, No Sell to the Time of Trouble

> On the whiteboard timeline: independent stages of fines, imprisonment, and inducements (bribery) lead into the full no-buy-no-sell period, then the close of probation, then the seven last plagues — the time of trouble. We are here, in the preparation time.

- _EGW: The out-of-the-cities / country-living message began in 1888, and many have lived and died since without preparing; we are mercifully still in the preparation time and must get out of the cities before the destruction of the cities. (Country Living principle.)_

### The Sealing and Judgment of the Living

> Revelation 18:1-3 began the sealing / judgment of the living, identifying the 144,000 who give the loud cry. The sealing is "a settling into the truth, both intellectually and spiritually" — which is why understanding the true final events is part of being sealed.

- **Rev. 18:1-3** — The beginning point of the sealing and the judgment of the living.
- **Rev. 7:1, 3** — Four angels hold the four winds until the servants of God are sealed in their foreheads; only when the sealing is complete do the four winds blow.
- **Rev. 14** — The 144,000 and their message connected to the sealing.
- **Isa. 30:22-25** — Among the passages confirming the sealing and judgment of the living.
- **Ezek. 37** — The four winds, connected to the four winds of Revelation 7.
- **Jer. 4:1-13** — Read with the four-winds passages as part of the sealing sequence.
- _EGW: "Just as soon as the people of God are sealed in their foreheads — it is not any seal or mark that can be seen, but a settling into the truth, both intellectually and spiritually, so they cannot be moved — just as soon as God's people are sealed and prepared for the shaking, it will come. Indeed, it has begun already." 4BC 1161 (LDE 219)._
- _EGW: "Everyone is to have sufficient light to make his decision intelligently." GC 605 (LDE 217.4)._

### The Image of the Beast — the Great Test Before Probation Closes

- _EGW: "The Lord has shown me clearly that the image of the beast will be formed before probation closes; for it is to be the great test for the people of God, by which their eternal destiny will be decided." 2SM 81 (LDE 227.3); the great test is shown to be Sunday-law enforcement._
- **Rev. 13:11-17** — The two-horned beast makes an image to the beast portrayed in Revelation 13:1-10; to learn what the image is, study the characteristics of the beast itself — the papacy.
- **Rev. 13:17** — The "no buy, no sell" that captivated the speaker's study for seventeen years; the mark-of-the-beast test must take place to complete the sealing process.

- **Rev. 15, 16** — The chapters describing when probation's time is closed and the plagues poured out.
- **Rev. 14:9-12** — The third angel's warning against the mark, marking that time of decision.

### Time No Longer — Why No Time Can Be Calculated Now

> We make no time predictions from Noah or Esther; we are in the day of atonement, prophetically still "October 22, 1844," until probation closes. The Bible gives no time prophecy between 1844 and the close of probation.

- **Rev. 10:6** — Time no longer — meaning prophetic time ended at the great disappointment of October 22, 1844; once probation closes, time resumes and the plagues fall in measured periods (one day, one hour).

### The Pioneer Charts — What Was Clearly Presented

> The 1843 chart was voted by the conference (1842) to lay out the prophecies of Daniel and Revelation; the 1850 "Ellen White chart" was made by vision (with Otis Nichols) and shows the image of the beast, the ten kings of Revelation 17, and the seven last plagues as the third woe. This is the light from behind — the true midnight cry.

- **Dan. 2** — The pioneers fully grasped the prophetic line of kingdoms underlying the charts.
- **Rev. 11** — Featured on the 1850 chart in connection with the final events.
- **Rev. 17** — The ten kings who give their power to the beast, shown on the chart in connection with the image of the beast.

### Q&A — Two Times of Trouble, Jacob's Trouble, and the Death Decree

> "Little" and "big" time of trouble denote intensity, not length; the intensity rises all the way to the second coming.

- **Dan. 12:1** — The second, full time of trouble; its first phase is the plagues falling (trouble for the wicked) and its second phase is Jacob's trouble (trouble for God's people).
- **Ezek. 4:6** — A day for a year; an hour is a twenty-fourth of a day, so the "one hour" of judgment equals fifteen days of prophetic time — the span of Jacob's trouble.
- _EGW: "Jacob and Esau represent two classes… Jacob's distress when he learned that Esau was marching against him with four hundred men represents the trouble of the righteous as the decree goes forth to put them to death." 3SG 131.1._
- _EGW: "Soon we heard the voice of God like many waters, which gave us the day and hour of Jesus' coming. The living saints, 144,000 in number, knew and understood the voice…" LS 65.1._
- _EGW: "Soon I heard the voice of God, which shook the heavens and the earth… their captivity was turned." EW 272.3._ The death decree and Jacob's trouble fall at the very end, the final hour of trial.
