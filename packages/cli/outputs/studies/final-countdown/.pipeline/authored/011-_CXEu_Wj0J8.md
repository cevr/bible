## I Investigated Ellen G. White's "Country Living" Book — Study 10

### The Image of the Beast and the Mark

> "If we do not want to receive the mark of the beast or worship the image of the beast... and if you don't take that, you're not going to be able to buy or sell." The whole crisis turns on the difference between the image and the mark.

- **Rev. 13:14** — A power on earth deceives men and tells them to make an image to the beast that was wounded by a sword and lived. The image is the punch line of the chapter.
- **Rev. 13:15** — Life is given to the image so that it both speaks and decrees that all who refuse to worship it should be killed.
- **Rev. 13:16** — All — small and great, rich and poor, free and bond — are caused to receive a mark in the right hand or forehead. Not some, but all.
- **Rev. 13:17** — No man may buy or sell except he have the mark, the name of the beast, or the number of his name. This is why preparation to raise your own produce is urgent.
- **Dan. 7:16, 17** — The angel explains that the beasts are kings or kingdoms; therefore the image is made to a kingdom, not to a person.

### The Out-of-the-Cities Line of Events

> The little booklet _Country Living_ (compiled 1946, after Ellen White's death) gathers her counsel out of chronological order. Read each quote by its original date and the seeming contradictions resolve.

- **Luke 21:20, 21** — As the siege of Jerusalem by Roman armies was the signal for the Judean Christians to flee, so a decree enforcing the papal Sabbath will be the warning to us. CL 32.1; 5T 464.3 (1885)
- **Gen. 19:15-17** — Had Lot stayed in Sodom when destruction came, he could not have been saved; the call to flee is not optional. CL 31.4
- **Gen. 7** — Had Noah not entered the ark, he would not have been saved; obedience must be immediate. The final movements will be rapid ones.

### "Out of the Cities" — The Message Intensifies

> The 1885 statement says "the time is not far distant." By 1906 it is an urgent imperative. Something happened between those dates that changed the emphasis.

- **CL 32.1** — "The time is not far distant... we shall be forced to seek a refuge in desolate and solitary places." 5T 464.3 (1885)
- **CL 31.4** — "Out of the cities; out of the cities! — this is the message the Lord has been giving me." Life Sketches 409, 410; Review and Herald July 5, 1906, par. 7
- **CL 31.4 (context)** — At Loma Linda an awful scene of buildings shaken down passed before her; for years the great cities have been warned of divine judgments for long-continued disobedience. RH July 5, 1906
- **CL 24.2** — "The time has come, when, as God opens the way, families should move out of the cities." It has already come, even if you do not know the sign. Manuscript 50, 1903

### The First Sign Has Already Come

> The warning was tied to a Sunday law. If "the time has come," the first such siege must already be past — and it is.

- **CL 21.1** — "The Protestant world have set up an idol Sabbath... For this reason I see the necessity of the people of God moving out of the cities." Letter 90, 1897
- **John 19:6, 7** — The Sunday idol is set up as was the image; men placing themselves where God should be echo Pilate's accusers who said, "We have a law, and by our law he ought to die." 12LtMs Letter 90, 1897, par. 15
- **Dan. 3** — As Nebuchadnezzar's advisers ensnared the Hebrew captives by a decree that every knee bow to the image, so men strive today to turn God's people from their allegiance; but the persecutors are destroyed by the recoil of their own actions.

### National Reform and the Threatened Sunday Law

> "Petitions signed by thousands have been sent in, asking that the civil and religious interest be not blended." Clergymen professing to serve God uttered blasphemies and cared no more for the Bible than infidels.

- **2 Thess. 2:3, 4** — When men lay aside a plain "Thus saith the Lord" and accept human enactments, they reveal the attributes of the great apostasy.
- **Blair National Sunday-Rest Bill (1888)** — Senate Bill No. 2983, introduced by Senator H. W. Blair, May 21, 1888, to secure the first day of the week as a day of rest and religious worship — the first siege, just as the Roman armies came and withdrew. The Lonely Years vol. 3, p. 395
- **GC 605.3** — Those who present the third angel's message have been called alarmists, their predictions of church-state persecution called groundless; but as Sunday legislation is agitated the message now produces an effect it could not before.
- **CL 11.1** — Erelong there will be such strife and confusion in the cities that those who wish to leave will not be able — and not be able and be lost. We must prepare before the crisis.

### The World's Fair of 1893 and the Forming Image

> Uriah Smith records that in carrying the point to close the Fair on Sunday, Congress did the very thing the Constitution forbids.

- **Ex. 20:8-11** — A senator called for a Bible and had the fourth commandment read; grave statesmen then voted that the day enjoined as the Sabbath is Sunday. DAR 573.4 (Uriah Smith, 1897)
- **DAR 574 (Uriah Smith, 1897)** — This legislating on a religious question broke down the barrier against church-state union; the amendment clergy boast they now have Congress in their hands. "How far away, then, is that image?"
- **GC 445** — When the leading churches of the United States influence the state to enforce their decrees and sustain their institutions, then Protestant America will have formed an image of the Roman hierarchy, and civil penalties on dissenters will result.
- **GC 443** — Wherever the church has obtained secular power she has punished dissent; the Church of England's long persecution of nonconformists in the 16th and 17th centuries is the pattern.
- **2 Thess. 2:3** — "There shall come a falling away, and that man of sin be revealed." Apostasy in the church prepares the way for the image to the beast. GC 443

### The Prophecy Must Be Fulfilled

> Skeptics said an indifferent, liberal America could never enforce religious law. The answer of faith: the prophecy must be fulfilled.

- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — To receive the mark in the forehead is to assent in mind to the institution that constitutes it; to receive it in the hand is to signify allegiance by outward act. DAR 579 (Uriah Smith)
- **Rev. 13:11-14** — The very liberal opposition becomes the stimulus that secures the religious amendment; an irrepressible conflict is precipitated and the government must become nominally Christian or wholly secular. The Marvel of Nations 230, 235

### Sealing Time of the 144,000

> The closing work for the church is the sealing of the 144,000 — living saints sealed during the Sunday-law crisis.

- **Rev. 7:4** — The 144,000 are of the tribes of Israel — God's commandment-keeping people who give the final loud cry and are sealed before Jesus comes.
- **Rev. 14:1** — They are a literal number who stand without fault; nothing in a literal 144,000 breaks natural law, so by Miller's rules it is read literally.
- **3T 266.2** — In the closing work for the church, in the sealing time of the 144,000 who stand without fault before the throne, God's people view sin in its real character and deal plainly with it.
- **Hab. 2:2** — God showed Ellen White the need of "getting out a chart" so the truth made plain upon tables would draw souls; the vision came at Otis Nichols' house, who then published it. 15MR 210.3; Letter 26, 1850
- **CL 30** — The cities are to be worked from outposts — not by God's people living in them, but by visiting them to warn of what is coming. Letter 182, 1902

### Truth Is the Plain Interpretation — Against Spiritualizing

> "Truth equals interpretation." When the angel gives the plain interpretation, no symbol remains to be spiritualized away.

- **Dan. 7:16, 17** — Daniel asks "the truth" and is given the interpretation: the symbolic beasts are literal kings. Symbol resolves into plain truth.
- **Dan. 11:2** — "Now will I show thee the truth" — the plain, unsymbolized interpretation; therefore "three kings" means literal kings.
- **GC 598.3** — The truths most plainly revealed have been clouded by learned men who teach the Scriptures have a mystical, secret meaning not in the language; such men Jesus called false teachers who "know not the Scriptures."
- **GC 360.1** — Wolff condemned the phantomizing system that reads "Jews" as Gentiles and "Jerusalem" as the church — the same spiritualizing now applied to Daniel 11.
- **AA 474.2** — The follower of Christ will meet spiritualistic interpretations of Scripture but is not to accept them.
- **1SM 196.3** — Such fanciful, spiritualistic interpretations undermine the foundations of our faith. (Cf. 5BC 1089.1 on the omega of apostasy: a mystical meaning on plain words makes vital truths of little significance.)

### Daniel 8 — Literal Directions, Literal Kingdoms

> The directions and numbers in Daniel are literal, not spiritual. Read by Miller's rules, the prophecy becomes simple.

- **Dan. 8:3, 4** — The ram with two horns pushes west, north, and south, so it is located in the east — Persia, which is modern Iran.
- **Dan. 8:20** — The angel states the ram's two horns are the kings of Media and Persia; no assumption needed.
- **Dan. 8:5-7** — The he-goat comes from the west — Greece — and breaks the ram's horns, as Alexander conquered Persia.
- **Dan. 8:8** — When the great horn is broken, four notable horns come up toward the four winds: Thrace, Syria, Egypt, and Macedonia (the western horn).
- **Dan. 8:9** — Out of one of them (Macedonia, the west) comes the little horn, Rome — therefore Rome arises in the west and cannot be the king of the north.

### Daniel 11 and the King of the North

> "Now will I show thee the truth" — Daniel 11 is the unsymbolized full story of Daniel 8: Persia, Greece, and its four divisions, read by literal geography.

- **Dan. 11:2** — Three kings stand up in Persia, then a fourth richer than all stirs against Greece — plainly named kings, no symbols.
- **Dan. 11:3, 4** — A mighty king (Alexander) rules, then his kingdom is broken and divided toward the four winds — identical to Daniel 8.
- **Dan. 11:40** — At the time of the end the king of the south pushes and the king of the north comes like a whirlwind with chariots, horsemen, and many ships — a literal, realistic war that breaks no natural law.
- **Dan. 12:4** — The time of the end is when Daniel's book is unsealed — after the 1260-year period.
- **Dan. 12:7** — "A time, times, and a half" = 1260 prophetic days. Ezek. 4:6
- **Ezek. 4:6** — "A day for a year" makes the period 1260 years — 538 to 1798, the papal reign.

### The 1798 Fulfillment

> The papacy received its deadly wound February 15, 1798, so it was already out of the way; the king-of-the-north war of verse 40 must be another power.

- **Dan. 11:40** — In 1798 Turkey (the Ottoman Empire, then holding Syria and Lebanon north of Israel) was the king of the north; Egypt the king of the south; the war between Egypt, France, and Turkey fulfilled the chariots, horsemen, and many ships. Bible Readings for the Home Circle (1888) p. 391-393
- **Jer. 4:5-13** — The same invader is the lion, the trumpet of alarm, the destroyer of the Gentiles, the full wind (war) coming as clouds with chariots as a whirlwind and horses swifter than eagles — laying the cities waste.

### The Locust Power of Revelation 9

> The fifth and sixth trumpets describe the same king of the north under the symbol of locusts — the Ottoman power.

- **Rev. 9:2, 3** — Smoke rises from the bottomless pit and locusts come upon the earth — the fifth trumpet, the first woe.
- **Rev. 9:7** — The locusts are like horses prepared for battle with the faces of men (horsemen) and the teeth of lions.
- **Rev. 9:17** — Fire-breathing locust-horses with heads of lions, issuing fire, smoke, and brimstone — breaking natural law, hence a symbol of a nation. GC 334.4 identifies the Ottoman/Turkish power.

### The Locusts Identified — Children of the East

> Scripture interprets its own symbol: the locust is a nation, and that nation is the Muslim descendants of Ishmael.

- **Judges 6:4, 5** — The Midianites come up as grasshoppers (locusts) without number and destroy the increase of the land unto Gaza.
- **Judges 7:12** — The Midianites, Amalekites, and "all the children of the east" lay along the valley like grasshoppers for multitude.
- **Judges 8:24** — Gideon's foes wore golden (crescent) earrings "because they were Ishmaelites" — the locusts are the descendants of Ishmael.
- **Joel 1:4, 6** — The four stages of locust devour the land, "for a nation is come up upon my land" with teeth of a lion — the locust is a kingdom.
- **Joel 1:15, 17, 19** — The day of the Lord is at hand as destruction from the Almighty; barns broken, fields wasted, fire devouring the pastures.
- **Joel 2:1-4** — Blow the trumpet, an alarm of war; a day of darkness, fire before and behind, an army appearing as horses and horsemen.
- **Joel 3:9-12** — Prepare war, beat plowshares into swords; after the harvest the heathen gather to the valley of Jehoshaphat for judgment — the battle scene.

### Daniel 11:44, 45 — Tidings Out of the East and North

> "But tidings out of the east and out of the north shall trouble him." We are watching these very tidings — Iran's war and Russia's involvement — stir the Turk.

- **Dan. 11:44** — Tidings (news) out of the east (Persia/Iran) and the north (Russia) trouble the king of the north (Turkey), who goes forth with great fury to destroy.
- **Dan. 11:45** — He plants his palace between the seas in the glorious holy mountain (Jerusalem) — a literal location — yet comes to his end, and none helps him.
- **Dan. 12:1** — The next event after verse 45 is the close of probation, the standing up of Michael, and the time of trouble such as never was.
- **19LtMs Letter 103, 1904, par. 17** — "The world is stirred with the spirit of war... The prophecy of the eleventh of Daniel has nearly reached its complete fulfillment," and much of that history will be repeated.

### The East Wind, the West Wind, and the Four Winds

> The winds are war; the holding and loosing of them marks the close of probation.

- **Isa. 27:7, 8** — Just before the day of slaughter He stays His rough wind in the day of the east wind — a war in the east blowing before the four winds.
- **Ezek. 9:2, 4** — Six men come from the north with slaughter weapons, but first a mark is set; the sealing precedes the slaughter.
- **Ex. 10:13, 19** — The east wind brings the locust upon Egypt, and a mighty west wind removes it — type of the east wind (war in Iran) bringing the Turk to Jerusalem and a west wind (war from America) removing him.
- **Rev. 7:1** — Four angels hold the four winds of the earth until the servants of God are sealed in their foreheads.
- **6T 14** — The four winds are held until the sealing is finished; then the powers of earth marshal for the last great battle.
- **15LtMs Letter 79** — When the restraining power is removed, deadly instruments of warfare will be invented and the nations unite under satanic agencies for the battle of Armageddon.
- **Ezek. 37** — The dry bones come together at prophesying, but live only when the prophet prophesies to the four winds; preaching the four-winds message awakens God's sleeping people.

- **Rev. 16:16** — The nations are gathered to a place called Armageddon — a literal war, not to be spiritualized away.
- **GC 334** — In 1840 Josiah Litch's exposition of Revelation 9 predicted the fall of the Ottoman Empire to the very day, August 11, 1840 — a fulfillment that excited wide interest.
- **4T 279.1** — Elder Uriah Smith spoke upon the Eastern Question to deep interest; the people listened with earnest attention.

- **Dan. 12:1** — Waggoner: the king of the north comes to his end in Palestine, then Michael stands up and there is a time of trouble such as never was. Present Truth, April 1, 1897
- **Ev 577** — Daniel and the Revelation should have attention as never before; we may say less of the Roman power and more of what the prophets have written, keeping the human agent out of sight.
