## Country Living Q&A Part 2: Your Questions Answered — Study 22

### Selling Houses and Lands: "Time of Trouble" vs. Now

> A popular but dangerous idea teaches that we should sell our country properties right before Sunday law. The Bible and the Spirit of Prophecy never taught a mass sale of lands; the "time of trouble" texts speak of a later moment.

- **Dan. 12:1** — When Michael stands up, probation has closed; the "time of trouble such as never was" is the period of the seven last plagues, not the Sunday-law crisis. This is when houses and lands are of no use, for the saints must flee before infuriated mobs. (CCh 281; EW 56)
- **Matt. 19:16-22** — The rich young ruler went away sorrowful, loving his possessions more than the souls he could have saved; soon the overflowing scourge sweeps such possessions away. (EW 49.1)
- **Isa.** — In that time of flight the rocks shall be the saints' munitions; we cannot plan to flee but must be ready to leave property at the angels' direction.
- **EW 56** — It is God's will that the saints cut loose from every encumbrance before the time of trouble and lay their property on the altar; He will teach them when to dispose of it so they are free, with no clogs to weigh them down. A country property is not an "encumbrance." (EW 56)
- **EW 57** — God has not required all His people to dispose of property at the same time; He teaches each, in a time of need, when to sell and how much to sell. (EW 57.1)

### Raising Your Own Provisions: Why We Move to the Country

> The whole premise is a coming time when no one can buy or sell. We move now to build the "ark," not to abandon it.

- **Rev. 13:17** — No man may buy or sell except he that has the mark of the beast; this warning is why we are to raise our own veggies and provisions and live in the country.
- **CL 9.5** — Again and again the Lord has instructed our people to take their families out of the cities into the country where they can raise their own provisions, for the problem of buying and selling will be very serious; get out where you are free from the interference of enemies. (CL 9.5)
- **Heb. 11:7** — By faith Noah, warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear and prepared an ark; so country living is righteousness by faith — we believe and obey before we see the danger.
- **Gen. 41** — Pharaoh's dream of seven fat and seven lean cows carried no command, yet Joseph moved with fear and prepared; the same principle governs our preparation for the coming time of trouble.
- **1 Chron. 12:32** — The children of Issachar had understanding of the times, to know what Israel ought to do; God's people are to understand prophecy so they know what to do now.

### Will Only the Men Be Sealed? (Women, Children, and the 144,000)

> A viewer feared Ezekiel 9 means only marked men survive. The counting of "men" always represents whole households.

- **Ezek. 9:1-6** — The man with the writer's inkhorn sets a mark on the foreheads of those who sigh and cry for the abominations; the destroyers slay all who lack the mark. The mark is set on the faithful, household and all.
- **Ex. 12:37** — About 600,000 men went up out of Egypt "beside children"; the men were counted, but women and children went with them as part of the household.
- **Matt. 14:21** — About 5,000 men ate "beside women and children" — the women and children still ate; the men are simply counted as heads.
- **Rev. 14:1-5** — The 144,000 are an army numbered by its men, yet women and children are reckoned with them; a woman without a faithful husband is still counted, for Christ becomes the head of her household.

### Faith for the Elderly and the Able-Bodied

> No one need fear they cannot make it to the end because of age, infirmity, or circumstance. The duty differs by ability.

- **(Lot)** — As God pulled Lot out of Sodom, He will deliver the elderly and helpless when the cities are to be destroyed if no provision has been made for them; until then, rest in His care.
- **Prov. 29:18 (vision)** — Where there is no vision the people perish; conviction from the Word drove the move and pushed us through every obstacle.
- **Heb. 11:7 (Noah)** — Noah's ark held not only himself but his family and the animals; the able-bodied must build large enough to make a way for those who cannot move themselves.
- **Country Living** — Excuses about children's education are answered: no city education is good enough; if forced to choose between book knowledge and practical country knowledge, "lose the books." (CL)
- **Gen. 41 (Joseph)** — Joseph, country-educated in the Jewish economy, was raised to the highest place in Egypt; God can elevate the faithful without passing them through the world's Babylonian systems.

### Practical Provisions: Water, Food, Fuel, and Seed

> "Provisions" means everything you depend on daily — and most of it comes from off the property. The land must supply heat, water, food, light, seed, fuel, fertilizer, and remedies.

- **Gen. 1:29** — God designed food to grow in the soil; we reject hydroponics as unsustainable and unnatural and grow in healthy soil for greater nutrition. ("of this you may freely eat")
- **(Water)** — Seek a clean spring, well, or rainwater; gravity, sand, and charcoal filters, boiling, or a wood-stove distiller purify water, but arsenic cannot be boiled out, so test your source.
- **(Land needed)** — One acre per person feeds a family (about 1.5 acres rounded down), and one acre regrows one cord of wood per year against a four-to-six-cord annual need, so five acres is too small; see "You Must Watch This Before Sunday Law."
- **(Animal power)** — Without fuel, no tractor can plow enough ground by hand, so oxen or trained animals ("Samson") must do the work, requiring hay, water, and grazing land.
- **(Plant-based fats)** — Grow flax for omega-3, sunflower, pumpkin seeds, brassica oilseeds, and where climate allows olives, walnuts, or hazelnuts; protein comes from dried beans, soy, and broad-bean tofu.
- **(Diet and the Day of Atonement)** — We are in the antitypical day of atonement; animal products excite the animal passions, so a plant-based diet best keeps the mind open to God's voice.

### Inviting Others and Self-Supporting Work in the Country

> Who is safe to bring, and how God's people can stay faithful and self-supporting as ordinary jobs close.

- **Matt. 7:16-20** — By their fruits you shall know them; we cannot read hearts, so invite prayerfully, set clear agreements, and trust God to shut or open the door.
- **(Shake off the dust)** — As Jesus taught, when truth is no longer received, shake the dust from your feet; share the warning with unbelieving family but never compromise truth to hold them.
- **(Forsake family for Christ)** — He that forsakes father, mother, brother, or sister for Christ's name is blessed; unbelieving family must not hold us back from obeying God, yet each must pray when and how to separate.
- **(Home businesses)** — Handyman and carpentry work, snow plowing, market-garden produce, a medical-missionary clinic offering treatments, and excavation are self-supporting trades for the country.

### Time, Tribulation, and the Time of Trouble

> Two distinct events are often confused: the past tribulation of the Dark Ages and the future time of trouble.

- **Matt. 24:21-22, 29** — The "great tribulation" is the 1260 years of papal persecution (538–1798); except those days were shortened no flesh would be saved, for the righteous would have been extinguished. Immediately after, the sun was darkened and the stars fell.
- **Dan. 12:1** — The "time of trouble such as never was" is a future time of worldwide war and the loosing of the four winds, bound up with the day of the Lord and the seven last plagues — a separate event from the tribulation, which ended in 1798.
- **(No date-setting)** — Since 1844 there is no prophetic time to the close of probation; we cannot say the period of no buying and selling will be three-and-a-half or seven years, so we plan to raise provisions year after year.
- **(Move now)** — The out-of-the-cities message has sounded since 1888; the next events are judgments on the cities, so God's people must be out now. The best time to start was twenty years ago; the second best is today.

### Walking by Faith Through Every Step

> Country living is a continual faith walk; each obstacle is a test that changes the character.

- **John 10:27** — "My sheep know My voice"; the great lesson is to learn to hear and follow the Lord's voice now, against the day the 144,000 must hear it under trial.
- **(Build like Noah)** — Noah built for 120 years; we keep building and praying, taking each step the Lord shows, knowing a five-acre starting place is a stepping stone, not the final destination.
- **(Claim the promises)** — Gather the roses and lilies among the thorns; renew the commitment daily — "Lord, today I choose to have faith when I see no possibility" — and move forward listening for His voice.
