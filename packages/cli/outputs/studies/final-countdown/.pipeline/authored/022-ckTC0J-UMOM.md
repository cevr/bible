## Country Living Q&A — The Final Countdown, Study 21

### Warned Beforehand, Called to Prepare

> God does not tell us things merely as a tidbit of information; He tells us beforehand so that we will do something about it. Preparation must come before the crisis, not in it.

- **Rev. 13:17** — Because no man will be able to buy or sell, God's people must learn now how to grow food and provide for themselves without fuel, power, or commerce.
- **Gen. 41** — Pharaoh's dream of the seven fat and seven lean cows warned of famine ahead; the seven years of plenty were given for Joseph to prepare. Simple foreknowledge moved him to act.
- **Rev. 13** — As in Joseph's day, the Lord has told us ahead of time what is coming; acting on that warning is both a privilege and a requirement.
- **Gen. 6** — Noah was told a flood was coming, and building the ark filled his whole vision; it took 120 years and he had to travel to gather wood and seed. Preparation is no excuse for compromise.

### Reaping and Threshing Without Machinery

- **Rev. 13:17** — The no-buy, no-sell time demands skill in harvesting grain by hand: a scythe with a cradle lays the heads in one direction, or a hand sickle cuts and binds sheaves to dry in the field.
- **Gen. 41** — Grain is the easiest crop to grow and store; work the ground, broadcast or seed the rows, and leave it untended until harvest, just as the patriarchs laid up grain against famine.

### When to Stop Growing — Fleeing the Country Home

> The time to flee is not ours to decide. God sends His angels; they show us the way, and He feeds us. There is nothing for us to plan or carry.

- **Rev. 18:8** — Her plagues come "in one day" — prophetically one year — so once the plagues fall it is contrary to the Bible to lay up food or plant a garden for the next season.
- **Matt. 24:17, 18** — When the sign comes to flee, we are not even to return to the house for a coat; we simply go, depending wholly on God. _(EW 56.2 — it is contrary to the Bible to make provision for our temporal wants in the time of trouble; food laid up would be taken by violent hands.)_
- **Isa. 33:16** — In flight to the mountains, "his place of defense shall be the munitions of rocks... bread shall be given him; his waters shall be sure" — to prepare otherwise is to disbelieve this promise.
- **Matt. 5** — Jesus drew the crowds out of the cities into the mountains, as in the Sermon on the Mount, to a place where they could think clearly and hear God; God's people are always called to the mountains.

### The Science of the Soil — Learning to Garden

> The city grows lazy people; the discipline learned in labor builds the very stamina needed to search the word and develop Christian character.

- **AH 142.3** — "No one can succeed in agriculture or gardening without attention to the laws involved. The special needs of every variety of plant must be studied... compliance with the laws governing each is the condition of success."

### Faith for the Foreign and Landless

- **Gen. 12** — Abraham was called to move countries; where immigration or finances seem to bar the way, claim the promises, pray as never before, and move forward prayerfully, letting God open and close the doors.

### Holding Property Until the End

> Country property is protected by angels while we remain to grow our food and preach the loud cry; that protection is removed only when we flee.

- **EW 56.2** — The misused saying "houses and lands will be of no use in the time of trouble" speaks of the fleeing time alone, not of selling our country homes now; the weight of evidence forbids abandoning the very preparation God commands.
