## The Judgment has Already Started and No One Noticed — Study 15

### Plagues and Judgments Are Already Falling

> "We are living in the time of the end. The fast-fulfilling signs of the times declare that the coming of Christ is near at hand." — 9T 11.

- **Rev. 16** — When we hear "plagues" we think of the seven last plagues poured out after probation closes; yet Ellen White says plagues and judgments are "already falling," so there must be plagues before the seven last plagues. 9T 11.
- **Rev. 9:20** — "The rest of the men which were not killed by these plagues" — Revelation 9 continues the seven trumpets of Revelation 8, and these trumpet judgments are themselves called plagues, proving plagues have already fallen.
- **Rev. 8** — The seven trumpets are seven alarms of war; a trumpet is an alarm of war, and these were literal wars in history, so wars and plagues can be used synonymously. GC 334.
- The calamities by land and sea, the unsettled state of society, and the alarms of war are portentous, forecasting approaching events of the greatest magnitude; the agencies of evil are consolidating for the last great crisis, and the final movements will be rapid ones. 9T 11.

### Nine-Eleven, the Day of the Lord, and Daniel 11

> "On one occasion, when in New York City, I was in the night season called upon to behold buildings rising story after story toward heaven... warranted to be fireproof." — 9T 12.

- The "scene that next passed before me was an alarm of fire" — the lofty, supposedly fireproof buildings were pronounced perfectly safe, yet were consumed as if made of pitch, and the fire engines could do nothing to stay the destruction. 9T 13.
- **Dan. 11** — "The world is stirred with the spirit of war. The prophecy of the eleventh chapter of Daniel has nearly reached its complete fulfillment." This vision of burning New York buildings was 9/11, tied directly to war, oil, and the Middle East. 9T 14.
- **Joel 1:15-18, 12** — "Alas for the day! for the day of the Lord is at hand"; the vine is dried up and joy is withered from the sons of men, the very passage Ellen White quotes to identify the day of the Lord. 9T 15.
- **Rev. 16:14** — The spirits of devils go forth to gather the kings of the earth "to the battle of that great day of God Almighty"; the day of God, the day of the Lord, is the day of the plagues.
- **Jer. 4:19, 20** — "I am pained at my very heart... because thou hast heard, O my soul, the sound of the trumpet, the alarm of war. Destruction upon destruction is cried; for the whole land is spoiled." 9T 15.
- **Jer. 4:7, 11-13** — The destroyer of the Gentiles comes to lay the cities waste; a dry wind and a full wind, clouds and chariots like a whirlwind, horses swifter than eagles, and a woe pronounced — note the whirlwind, horses, chariots, and woe.

### The Four Winds and the Sealing of the Living

- **Rev. 7:1-3** — Four angels stand on the four corners of the earth holding the four winds that they should not blow on the earth, the sea, or any tree, until the servants of God are sealed in their foreheads.
- **Rev. 7:2** — Another angel ascends from the east having the seal of the living God and cries with a loud voice; the loud voice signals urgency, for the winds were about to blow and God's people were not yet sealed.
- **Dan. 7:2** — The winds strove upon the great sea; winds are a symbol of war, blowing every time a nation changes power, so holding the four winds is restraining strife and commotion among rising nations.

### Early Writings: "Hold! Hold! Hold! Hold!"

> "I saw four angels who had a work to do on the earth, and were on their way to accomplish it." — EW 38.

- Jesus is clothed with priestly garments, still in the most holy place of the heavenly sanctuary, which means probation is open and people can still repent; yet His people were not ready for the four winds, which only blow after probation closes. EW 38.
- He gazed in pity on the remnant and cried, "My blood, Father, My blood, My blood, My blood!" — four pleadings of His blood for the four winds. EW 38.
- An angel with a commission from Jesus flew swiftly to the four angels, waving something up and down and crying with a loud voice, "Hold! Hold! Hold! Hold! until the servants of God are sealed in their foreheads" — a four-fold "Hold" for the four winds. EW 38.
- The four angels had power from God to hold the four winds and were about to let them go; but while their hands were loosening and the winds were about to blow, the merciful eye of Jesus gazed on the unsealed remnant and bid them hold until His servants were sealed with the seal of the living God. EW 38.

### The Judgment Passing to the Living

> "Solemn are the scenes connected with the closing work of the atonement. Momentous are the interests involved therein." — CIHS 188.1.

- The judgment is now passing in the sanctuary above, and for many years this work has been in progress; soon — none know how soon — it will pass to the cases of the living, and our lives are to come up in review. CIHS 188.1.
- **Rev. 10:6** — There is no prophetic time from 1844 until the close of probation, so we do not know the day, yet we are told it will "soon" pass to the cases of the living.
- **Rev. 3:3** — "Watch and pray, for ye know not when the time is. If therefore thou shalt not watch, I will come on thee as a thief" — only those who are not watching have Him come as a thief. CIHS 188.1.
- **6T 130** — "Now, when the great work of judging the living is about to begin, shall we allow unsanctified ambition to take possession of the heart and lead us to neglect the education required to meet the needs in this day of peril?"

### Ezekiel 9: The Mark Before the Slaughter

- **Ezek. 9:1-3** — A loud voice calls every man with his destroying weapon near, and six men come with slaughter weapons, one clothed in linen with a writer's inkhorn — the same loud voice and slaughter that the four winds bring.
- **Ezek. 9:4-6** — Go through Jerusalem and set a mark upon the foreheads before the slaughter; slay utterly old and young, "but come not near any man upon whom is the mark; and begin at my sanctuary" — the sealing of living people protects them from the slaughter weapon, exactly as in Revelation 7.

### The Seal Is a Settling Into the Truth

- **Mar 200.2** — "Just as soon as the people of God are sealed in their foreheads — it is not any seal or mark that can be seen, but a settling into the truth, both intellectually and spiritually, so they cannot be moved." You cannot be settled in something you are unsure of; the sealed must know, study, and not be deceived about the final events.
- The judgments of God are now upon the land, "to give us warning, that we may know what is coming" — the shaking has begun already, and the 9/11 plague-judgment-war stirring is a warning of what is coming. Mar 200.2.

### The Latter Rain Is Doctrine

> "Ask ye of the Lord rain in the time of the latter rain." — Zech. 10:1.

- **Zech. 10:1, 2** — Ask for rain in the time of the latter rain; "for the idols have spoken vanity, and the diviners have seen a lie, and have told false dreams; they comfort in vain" — a command, not a question, proving we can know the time of the latter rain.
- **Deut. 32:2** — "My doctrine shall drop as the rain, my speech shall distil as the dew... and as the showers upon the grass" — the rain and the showers are doctrine, specifically the correct doctrine of the final events; the latter rain is the sealing message.
- **Ezek. 37:7-10** — Prophesying to the wind to "come from the four winds" is what woke the dry bones; preaching the prophecy of the four winds and the judgment of the living, with its noise and shaking, is what raises the army.

### Woe to the Idol Shepherds

- **Zech. 11:17** — "Woe to the idol shepherd that leaveth the flock" — not the "idle" lazy pastor, but the idol (I-D-O-L) shepherd; his arm is dried up and his right eye darkened, so he cannot see prophecy or have vision.
- **Isa. 30:22, 23** — First you must defile and cast away your graven images of silver and molten images of gold — your pet ideas and false doctrines — and only then will He give the rain of thy seed; you cannot receive new doctrine while still clinging to the false.
- **Hosea 13:2** — They have made molten images "according to their own understanding" — the idols are their own private understanding of prophecy, leaving them dead bones fed false vision and false peace.
- **Micah 3:2, 5, 6** — The prophets who make God's people err "bite with their teeth, and cry, Peace"; therefore night and darkness shall be upon them and they shall have no vision — false prophecy leaves men with no light.

### Be Not in Darkness — You Know the Times

- **1 Thess. 5:1-2** — "Of the times and the seasons, brethren, ye have no need that I write unto you. For yourselves know perfectly that the day of the Lord so cometh as a thief in the night" — the church knew the prophecies, times, and seasons.
- **1 Thess. 5:3-4** — "When they shall say, Peace and safety, then sudden destruction cometh upon them... But ye, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief" — only the wicked are surprised by the day of the Lord.
- **Rev. 18:8** — The day that comes as a thief is not the loud, glorious Second Coming but the day of the Lord, the close of probation, the day of the plagues.

### When the Plagues Fall, Few Will Know

- **Rev. 16:15** — "Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed is he that watcheth and keepeth his garments" — brought up only in the sixth plague, this is when the deceived finally realize they were false prophets, dry bones, naked and miserable, poor and blind.
- **GC 334.4** — The falling of the Ottoman power on August 11, 1840 was an exact fulfillment of prophecy; God takes care that prophecy and history agree, for if more than one event could perfectly fulfill it there would be no absolute truth — denying a perfect fulfillment by attributing it to Satan is a dangerous deception.
- There is to be in the churches a wonderful manifestation of the power of God, but it will not move those who have not humbled themselves; they will see only something they think dangerous, something which will arouse their fears, and they will brace themselves to resist and oppose the work, asking, "Should we not know the Spirit of God, when we have been in the work so many years?" LDE 209.3 (Review and Herald Extra, Dec. 23, 1890).

### Questions Answered

- **Dan. 11:41** — On who escapes out of the hand of the king — Edom, Moab, and the chief of the children of Ammon — consult Uriah Smith's _Daniel and Revelation_ (the 1897 edition that Ellen White referred to), at his treatment of Daniel 11:41.
- **Rev. 6** — The four horsemen of the apocalypse (white, red, black, and pale horse) are the seven seals of Revelation 6; study Uriah Smith's _Daniel and Revelation_ (1897) on Revelation 6.
- **Rev. 7:1** — The four winds are sent by God, not Satan; they are God's angels and God's plagues, and it was God who said "Hold," for the four winds bring His judgments, destruction, and death upon the earth.
