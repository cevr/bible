## The Criteria for a Country Property — The Final Countdown, Study 11

### The Warning: An Ark for the Coming Destruction

> "We are living in the very last days of this earth's history. The destruction of these cities is a warning to us to prepare for what is coming upon the earth." — Christ Triumphant 56

- **CTr 56** — God gave direction that an ark was to be built for those who desired to be saved from the coming destruction; in the same way He commands us to build our antitypical ark for the coming time of trouble. CTr 56
- **CTr 56** — When God warns us of judgments meant for the wicked, He does not want us to partake of them; if we do, we are counted with the wicked, so we must not be swept away in the modern flood that is coming. CTr 56
- **CTr 56** — He was about to clothe Himself with vengeance and execute His judgment against the transgressors of His law, so we need to be in our ark before the plagues and the time of trouble fall. CTr 56

### Out of the Cities into the Country

- **Letter 5, 1904, par. 3** — Again and again the Lord has instructed that His people take their families away from the cities into the country where they can raise their own provisions, for the problem of buying and selling will be a very serious one.
- **Letter 5, 1904, par. 3** — Get out of the cities into rural districts where the houses are not crowded closely together and where you will be free from the interference of enemies.
- **2SM 356** — He wants us to live where we can have elbow room; if we place ourselves under objectionable influences, we cannot expect God to work a miracle to undo the results of our wrong course. 2SM 356
- **2SM 356** — Get out of the cities as soon as possible and purchase a little piece of land where you can have a garden and your children can learn lessons of simplicity and purity. 2SM 356
- **CL 21** — Move out of the cities into retired country places where you can cultivate the land and raise your own produce, making haste to get all things ready for the crisis. CL 21
- **MM 310** — To parents in the cities the Lord sends the warning cry: gather your children away from those who practice evil and get out of the cities as fast as possible. MM 310
- **MM 310** — Parents can secure small homes in the country with land for cultivation, orchards, vegetables, and small fruits to take the place of flesh meat; God will help His people to find such homes outside the cities. MM 310
- **CL 29** — The Lord desires His people to move into the country, settle on the land, raise their own fruit and vegetables, and bring their children into direct contact with the works of God in nature. CL 29

### Secluded Homes Among the Mountains

- **CL 32** — It will be time to leave the large cities, preparatory to leaving the smaller ones, for retired homes in secluded places among the mountains — homes in the mountains, not caves. CL 32
- **Gen. 19:12-14** — The angels commanded Lot to bring his family out of Sodom because God had sent them to destroy the city; the command was to leave, not to wait for a miracle bubble.
- **Gen. 19:15-17** — "Escape for thy life; look not behind thee, neither stay thou in all the plain; escape to the mountain, lest thou be consumed" — the mountains are where God can protect you.
- **Gen. 19:18-20** — Lot compromised and begged to flee to the little city of Zoar instead of the mountain; his hesitation and debating with God cost him his family.

### Ellen White's Own Garden — What "A Little Piece of Land" Meant

> "Father has excellent health. He has worked hard on the place here... I have been gathering up shrubs and flowers until we have quite a garden."

- **CL 29** — Her personal garden held over an acre of strawberries, raspberries, more than an acre of potatoes, several acres of corn, fifty hard maples, peach and pear trees, and pie plant — more than six acres, which she called "quite a garden." CL 29
- **MS / Paradise Valley account** — The Whites bought adjoining parcels rather than a single small lot; borrowing money to secure land is not against counsel when you have a plan to pay it back quickly, for the property ought not pass into the hands of worldlings.
- **MS (Elmshaven account)** — Ellen White refused to sell small lots or let families crowd in on every spare piece of uncultivated land, showing the property must keep space enough for an uncultivated buffer area.

### How Far From the Cities — "Miles Away"

- **CET 217.1 (1874)** — Oakland, San Francisco, Sacramento, and Woodland were named as "large cities" — yet Sacramento was only 17,000-18,000 and San Francisco 149,000; we must be far from cities like these, not only from New York or Los Angeles. CET 217.1
- **PM 186.4** — The workers connected with the publishing house must be closely guarded, so that young men and women are not placed where they will be ensnared by Satan. PM 186.4
- **FE 492.3** — As the years pass and the city grows, it becomes necessary to move the work to a more rural place; if the cities spread, His people must move farther out, again and again. FE 492.3
- **FE 492.3** — God's people should obtain homes where they can live without requiring high wages — land and houses are far cheaper in the country than in the city. FE 492.3
- **LDE 95.2** — The call is for our people to locate miles away from the large cities; one look at San Francisco shows the necessity of getting out. LDE 95.2
- **PM 186.3** — Of New York, "any place within thirty miles of that city would be too near" — at a wagon pace of 3.5 mph that is 8.5 hours away, and still too close. PM 186.3
- **LDE 107.5** — A school site eight miles from the small town of St. Helena (about 2.3 hours by wagon) was pronounced superior and free from city temptations; the working minimum is at least two hours from a large city. LDE 107.5

### The Size and Substance of the Ark — Land, Water, Wood

- **CL 21** — Noah's ark was not tiny; it was large enough for all the animals and provisioned to last 370 days and beyond — the pattern for a property sized to survive the no-buy-no-sell crisis, getting all things ready beforehand. CL 21
- **CL 21** — Get all things ready for the crisis: a self-sufficient homestead needs garden, greenhouse, livestock, logging, food preservation, water storage, natural remedies, and education — about one acre of land produces a year of food for one person, so plan a five-acre fenced garden, ten acres of wood, ten of pasture, and a minimum of forty-plus acres total. CL 21

### The Saints Are Protected in Their Country Homes

- **EW 34** — In the time of trouble the saints fled from the cities and villages to their homes in the country; the wicked who pursued them with the sword found it broke and fell as powerless as straw. EW 34
- **Rev. 14:9-12** — The third angel's message warns people not to receive the mark of the beast and its plagues; warning the cities of what is coming is the very reason God's people visit them.
- **Rev. 18:1-4** — The fourth angel's message — "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins" — is the final cry to leave the cities; we warn the cities by visiting from outposts, not by living in them.
- **Ev 377** — In every city where the truth is proclaimed churches are to be raised up, and meeting houses may be purchased; but the people of God travel in to work the cities rather than settling in them, and these outposts will grow fewer as the end nears. Ev 377
- **CL 30** — The cities are to be worked from outposts; they are to be warned not by God's people living in them but by visiting them to warn of what is coming upon the earth. CL 30
