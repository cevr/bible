## Be Ready For Their Next Move — Study 4

_Series: The Final Countdown_

### Their Next Move: The No-Buy-No-Sell

> Effective immediately, individuals found to be persistently undermining national unity and safety will face economic and civil restrictions. The enemy's plan is to sanction God's people out of the economy — and history shows it is no fantasy.

- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — The beast power causes all, small and great, rich and poor, to receive a mark; and no man may buy or sell unless he has the mark, name, or number of the beast. This is their next move against God's people.
- **Rev. 17:15** — The sea from which the first beast rose represents peoples, multitudes, nations, and tongues — establishing the symbolic key for reading Revelation 13.
- **Rev. 13:11** — Another beast comes up out of the earth; the earth being the opposite of the sea, an unpopulated region — identifying the United States as the next world power in prophecy.
- **Rev. 13:14** — This power deceives the earth by miracles and says they should make an image to the beast — America makes an identity built to look exactly like the papacy of the dark ages.

### The Third Angel: The Mark Brings the Wrath of God

- **Rev. 14:9, 10** — The third angel cries with a loud voice: whoever worships the beast and his image and receives his mark shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God. Those with the mark receive the wine/wrath of God.

### The Fourth Angel: Come Out of Her

- **Rev. 18:1-4** — A mighty angel lightens the earth with glory and cries that Babylon is fallen; then a voice says, "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues." Come out of false doctrine, do not receive the mark, and do not receive the plagues.

### The Close of Probation Anchors the Timeline

- **Rev. 15:1** — Seven angels have the seven last plagues, for in them is filled up the wrath of God — proving the wine of the wrath of God is the seven last plagues.
- **Rev. 15:5** — After this the temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven was opened; an opened, empty sanctuary means the ministry is finished — this is the close of probation, occurring just before the plagues fall.
- **Rev. 16:1** — A great voice out of the temple commands the seven angels to pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth, confirming probation closes immediately before the plagues begin.

### The Time of Trouble and the Deliverance of God's People

- **Dan. 12:1** — Michael stands up (leaving the most holy place, so probation is closed), beginning a time of trouble such as never was; yet at that time God's people shall be delivered.
- **Isa. 33:1, 2** — Woe to the spoiler; "O Lord, be gracious unto us... be thou their arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble" — God's salvation is given in the time of trouble.
- **Isa. 33:16** — He shall dwell on high, his place of defense the munitions of rocks; "bread shall be given him; his waters shall be sure" — God's people are delivered in the mountains, where their bread and water is made sure only during the time of trouble, after probation closes.

### Probation Is Still Open — So We Have a Work to Do

> The no-buy-no-sell happens before the close of probation. If we can still "come out" and still decide whether the mark goes on hand or forehead, then probation is open, the plagues are not yet falling, and our bread and water is not sure without our own diligent input.

- **Rev. 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people" requires a decision; the ability to decide and to choose against the mark proves probation is still open during the no-buy-no-sell period.

### Joseph and Noah: Faith That Prepares

- **Gen. 41:25** — Joseph tells Pharaoh that the dream is one: God has shown what He is about to do — seven years of plenty followed by seven years of famine. This was a prophecy of coming crisis.
- **Gen. 41:35** — They gather and lay up food in the good years as a store against the famine, so the land perished not — they did not starve because they prepared.
- **Rev. 1:1, 3** — The revelation of Jesus Christ; "blessed is he that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy, and keep those things which are written therein: for the time is at hand." To keep the prophecy means to act on it — belief causes action.
- **Heb. 11:7** — By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not yet seen, moved with fear and prepared an ark to the saving of his house, condemning the world. Faith without works is dead; Noah without works would have died.
- **Matt. 24** — Jesus foretells floods and the destruction of cities, underscoring the obedience of moving to the safety of the country before the crisis.

### The Counsel to Move to the Country

> "Educate our people to get out of the cities into the country, where they can obtain a small piece of land and make a home for themselves and their children." AH 373.2

- **Gen. 41:35** — As Joseph educated the people to grow and store food for a specific coming crisis, other "Josephs" must now educate God's people how to prepare for the no-buy-no-sell. 12MR 219.4 — "I see the necessity of the people of God moving out of the cities into retired country places where they may cultivate the land and raise their own produce... I see the necessity of making haste to get all things ready for the crisis."

### Three Pillars of Preparation: Land, Water, Wood

- **Heb. 11:7** — As Noah prepared the ark over 120 years, God's people must secure land (to grow food, build, and store), water (for drinking, garden, cleaning, hydrotherapy, and fire prevention), and wood (for heating, cooking, and building) — the three pillars of a country property, prepared with haste and stamina until God opens the way.
