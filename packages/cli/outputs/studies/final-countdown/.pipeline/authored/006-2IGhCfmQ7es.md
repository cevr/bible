## Surviving Without Buying or Selling: How Much Land Do You Really Need? — Study 5

### Defining the Sequence: When Is the Time of Trouble?

> "The Lord has shown me repeatedly that it is contrary to the Bible to make any provision for our temporal wants in the time of trouble." — Early Writings 56.2. The whole study turns on rightly defining _when_ the time of trouble begins, so this quote is read in its proper context.

- **Rev. 15:5** — The heavenly temple is opened, meaning Jesus has finished His mediation; this marks the close of probation, just before the plagues of Revelation 16. EW 56.2
- **Dan. 12:1** — Michael (Christ) stands up from ministering in the most holy place; His standing up closes probation and triggers the time of trouble such as never was. EW 56.3
- **Rev. 13:16** — The mark of the beast is enforced upon all, small and great, rich and poor, in the right hand or forehead; this event is still future and comes before the close of probation.
- **Rev. 13:17** — No man may buy or sell except he that has the mark; refusing it means all earthly support is cut off — the "no buy, no sell."
- **Rev. 14:9, 10** — Whoever worships the beast and receives his mark drinks the wine of God's wrath; the choice is the seal of God or the mark of the beast.
- **Rev. 18:1-4** — The fourth angel lightens the earth with glory and calls "Come out of her, my people"; that probation is still open during this final call proves the no-buy-no-sell precedes the close of probation.

### Bread and Water Sure: A Promise for the Time of Trouble

> Houses and lands will be of no use to the saints in the time of trouble, for they will then have to flee before infuriated mobs. — Early Writings 56.3.

- **Isa. 33:2** — A chapter explicitly about "the time of trouble," fixing the setting after probation has closed.
- **Isa. 33:16** — In that time of trouble the faithful dwell in munitions of rocks; bread shall be given him and his waters shall be sure — the "bread and water sure" applies only while fleeing, after the plagues have begun, not during the no-buy-no-sell.
- **Isa. 33:1-16** — The full passage shows the divinely supplied bread and water belongs to the fleeing saints in the time of trouble; until that line is crossed, God expects us to raise our own provisions. EW 56.2

### Out of the Cities: A Message Long Neglected

> The out-of-the-cities call has been on the table since 1888, but Laodicea slept and the warning was lost. We are now near the end and unready.

- **Heb. 11:7** — By faith Noah, warned of things not yet seen, prepared an ark to the saving of his house and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith; he believed the prophecy and acted on it.
- **Adventist Home 373.2** — "Educate our people to get out of the cities into the country, where they can obtain a small piece of land and make a home for themselves and their children." AH 373.2
- **Manuscript Releases vol. 12, 219.4** — "I see the necessity of the people of God moving out of the cities into retired country places where they may cultivate the land and raise their own produce... making haste to get all things ready for the crisis." 12MR 219.4
- **Adventist Home 139.6** — "God will help His people to find such homes outside of the cities" — a promise that He will provide the land and the means, as He helped Noah, Joseph, and Abraham. AH 139.6

### The Three Pillars of a Country Property

> The question is not _whether_ to prepare but _how_; and the only opinions that matter come from those who have actually done the thing — survived year to year off the land.

- **Heb. 11:7** — As Noah's ark was the divinely-appointed refuge with all provisions aboard, so the country property — land, water, and wood — is the antitypical ark for which we put in all we have.
- **Dan. 1** (Daniel and his three friends ate "pulse") — A diet of lentils and plant proteins left them fairer and fatter in flesh, the model for the high-protein, plant-based eating needed to function and grow strong during the no-buy-no-sell.

### Trusting God While Doing All in Our Power

> God warns ahead so we need not panic; faith acts first, then God opens the way.

- **PP 290.1** — At the Red Sea the people advanced into the water by faith before God divided it; "they did all that was in their power to do, and then the mighty One of Israel divided the sea." If we do everything in our power, He will make the way to the promised land. PP 290.1

### The Stages Leading to No Buy, No Sell

> Judgment begins at the house of God: those who know better are held accountable first, and at every stage of pressure people drop off to one side or the other.

- **1 Pet. 4:17** — Judgment begins at the house of God; God's people who know the truth are tested first, choosing the seal of God or the mark of the beast at each step.
- **Testimonies vol. 9, 97** — "The time of God's destructive judgments is the time of mercy for those who have had no opportunity to learn what is truth... His hand is still stretched out to save, while the door is closed to those who would not enter." 9T 97.2
- **Rev. 7** — The 144,000 receive the seal of God and proclaim the loud cry; through their message the great multitude comes out of every nation, refuses the mark, and joins the antitypical ark.

### Time of Trouble vs. The Tribulation

> "Pray ye that your flight be not in the winter... for then shall be great tribulation, such as was not since the beginning of the world." The "great tribulation" of Matthew 24 is not the end-time time of trouble.

- **Matt. 24:20-22** — The "great tribulation, such as was not," cut short "for the elect's sake," refers to the 1260-year papal persecution (538-1798), not the future time of trouble.
- **Matt. 24:29** — "Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened" — the heavenly signs follow the shortened persecution (persecution easing by 1755), confirming the tribulation is past, anchored by the 1798 end of papal rule.

> Historical markers built on Dan. 2, 7, 8, 9: 538 to 1798 = the 1260 years of papal supremacy; the persecution shortened so that "no flesh" should perish, easing about 1755; the Lisbon earthquake of 1755 fulfilled the darkening, followed by the falling of the stars in 1833 — signs of Christ's coming after the tribulation.

### How Long? No Time Prophecy Remains

> Some ask whether the no-buy-no-sell lasts three and a half years. Scripture forbids fixing any such period.

- **Rev. 10:6** — The angel swears that "there should be time no longer" until probation closes; since the Sunday law and no-buy-no-sell fall inside probationary time, there is no remaining day-for-a-year nor any literal time prophecy to measure it.
- **Ezek. 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year" — the day-for-a-year principle, which no longer applies to events after probation's close, so we cannot calculate the duration of the no-buy-no-sell.

> "All we know is that we will have to raise our own provisions for the time of trouble... the time of buying and selling will be very serious." Because growing food takes months and years, the no-buy-no-sell must last long enough to prove obedience and weed out all who would take the mark — likely years, not weeks.

### Proving Obedience: Selling All to Follow

> Christ went into the wilderness and the mountains every day; to follow Him we must be where we can do likewise.

- **Mark 10:21, 22** (the rich young ruler) — Told to sell all he had and follow, he went away sad; the test now is whether we will sell all and invest in the antitypical country home to follow God.
- **Dan. 12:1** — "At that time thy people shall be delivered" — God's people make it through; their bread and water are sure in the time of trouble, refuting the idea of mass martyrdom and proving the call is to prepare, not to die in the city.

### No Death Decree Until After Probation Closes

> The death decree does not fall during the Sunday law or no-buy-no-sell; it comes only after the close of probation, and even then the faithful do not die.

- **Rev. 13:17** — During the no-buy-no-sell the pressure is economic, not lethal; one either starves, takes the mark, or has obeyed God and prepared — there is no death decree yet.
- **Dan. 12:1** — When the death decree finally goes forth after probation closes, "thy people shall be delivered" — no faithful one dies from that point onward, as Israel was not lost in the Red Sea.

### A Total Economic Boycott — Not a Currency Problem

> The no-buy-no-sell is not about which money is accepted; it is a complete cutting-off from all commerce, like the boycotts of old.

- **Testimonies vol. 5, 152.2** — "The time is coming when we cannot sell at any price. The decree will soon go forth prohibiting men to buy or sell of any man save him that hath the mark of the beast." No bartering, no alternate currency, no Bitcoin will avail — it is a total economic boycott. 5T 152.2

### One-Year Duration of the Time of Trouble

> The no-buy-no-sell has no fixed length, but the time of trouble after probation closes is measured in Scripture.

- **Rev. 10:6** — "Time no longer" applies only until probation closes; afterward the plagues fall in a defined period.
- **Rev. 18:8** — "Therefore shall her plagues come in one day" — the plagues are dated to "one day."
- **Ezek. 4:6** — Applying the day-for-a-year measure to the plagues' "one day" yields a time of trouble that lasts one year.
