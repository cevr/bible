## What Happens Next Will Shock You — The Final Countdown, Study 12

> An overview of the biblical prophetic timeline of the last days, walking from 1888 to the close of probation, showing that each waymark — out of the cities, the shaking, the sealing, the image of the beast, Sunday law, the loud cry, Satan personating Christ, the seven last plagues, and Armageddon — is grounded in scripture and the Spirit of Prophecy, not in private speculation.

### The First Siege of Sunday Law — 1888

> "We're going to show from the Bible and the Spirit of Prophecy that these aren't just my own ideas." The whole timeline opens in 1888, the year of the National Sunday Rest Bill.

- **Matt. 24** — As Christ warned, the first siege of Jerusalem by the Roman armies was the signal for the Judean Christians to flee; those who waited for the second siege were destroyed. This pattern is the model for the first siege of Sunday law in 1888. _(timeline waymark)_
- The National Sunday-Rest Bill, Senate Bill No. 2983, introduced in the first session of the Fiftieth Congress by Senator H. W. Blair, May 21, 1888 — the first siege, now withdrawn but already accomplished. _(supporting evidence)_
- Ellen White's 1885 warning that, like the early disciples, God's people will be forced to seek refuge, and the decree enforcing the papal Sabbath will be the signal to leave the large cities for retired homes among the mountains. 5T 464.3
- After 1888 she wrote that because the Protestant world had set up an idol Sabbath and was treading in the footsteps of the papacy, she saw the necessity of God's people moving out of the cities to cultivate the land and raise their own produce. CL 21.1

### Righteousness by Faith and the Out-of-the-Cities Message — 1888 Onward

- **Rev. 3** — God's people fell into the Laodicean state; righteousness by faith was rejected, deepening their lukewarmness.
- Righteousness by faith is faith that acts on what God says will happen; Noah was accounted righteous because he built the ark in faith on God's prophecy of the flood — a hearer who does nothing has no righteousness by faith.
- The out-of-the-cities message has been sounding since 1888 — longer than Noah had to build his ark — and runs all the way to the close of probation, calling two separate groups out at two separate times.

### 9/11 and the Buildings That Fall — 2001

> One of the most ominous dates in history; the place of the vision (New York City) is the place of the event.

- **Rev. 18:1-3** — Ellen White declared these verses would be fulfilled when the great buildings of New York are thrown down — a prophecy connected to 2001. LS 411.5
- **Isa. 30:25** — "When the towers fall," rivers and streams of waters break out upon every high mountain — the latter rain, coming as the towers fall.
- Vision of the night season in New York City: fireproof buildings rising story after story, consumed as if made of pitch, the fire engines unable to stay the destruction — a foreview of 9/11. 9T 11.1; 9T 12.3
- "What terrible scenes will take place when the Lord shall rise to shake terribly the earth!" — the whole of Revelation 18 is a warning of what is coming on the earth. LS 411.5

### The Fourth Angel's Message — 2001

- **Rev. 18:1-4** — Another angel comes down from heaven with great power, the earth lightened with his glory, crying "Babylon the great is fallen... Come out of her, my people." This fourth angel falls on the same line as the towers, 2001.
- The fourth angel's message is the beginning of the light whose glory fills the whole earth — it has begun, but is now being given again.

### The Shaking

- **Ezek. 37:7** — Ezekiel prophesied to the dry bones; there was a noise, and behold a shaking, and the bones came together — prophesying the final events causes the shaking.
- **Ezek. 38:19** — In the fire of God's wrath, there shall be a great shaking in the land of Israel; the shaking has commenced in the church and will only grow stronger.

### The Sealing of the 144,000 — Beginning 2001

- **Rev. 7:1-4** — Four angels hold the four winds; another angel ascends from the east with the seal of the living God, sealing the servants of God in their foreheads — and the number sealed is 144,000.
- **Rev. 7:3** — "I heard the number of them which were sealed" — it says the number, not the title, indicating a literal 144,000.
- The seal is not a visible mark but a settling into the truth, both intellectually and spiritually, so they cannot be moved; one cannot settle into the truth unless the truth has first been given. Mar 200.2
- As soon as God's people are sealed and prepared for the shaking, it will come — indeed it has begun, the judgments of God upon the land warning us of what is coming. Mar 200.2
- **1 Pet. 4:17** — Judgment must begin at the house of God; the sealing comes to God's people first.

### The Image of the Beast (Formed Before Probation Closes)

- **Rev. 13:14, 15** — The two-horned beast tells those on earth to make an image to the beast which had the wound by a sword and did live; he gives life to the image, that it should speak and decree death for non-worshipers.
- The image is to be formed by studying the characteristics of the beast itself — the papacy, a church that controlled the power of the state; for the United States to form an image, the religious power must so control the civil government that the church wields the authority of the state. GC 443
- The image of the beast is the church-state structure; it will be formed before probation closes, for it is the great test for the people of God by which their eternal destiny will be decided. 2SM 80.4; LDE 227.3

### National Sunday Law — The Mark of the Beast

> The mark of the beast is Sunday worship; the image of the beast comes first, then the buildup to Sunday law (the second siege).

- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — All, small and great, are caused to receive a mark in the right hand or forehead, so that no man may buy or sell save he that has the mark or the name of the beast.
- **Rev. 13:15** — The second beast (America, American Protestantism) gives "life" to a dead institution — resurrecting a church-state structure, not literal life to a person.
- When Sunday observance is enforced by law, those who reject God's sign and honor Rome's token will accept the sign of allegiance to Rome — the mark of the beast; no one has yet received it because it is not yet enforced by the state, and people must first be brought to choose. GC 449.1
- When the nation so abjures the principles of its government as to enact a Sunday law, Protestantism will join hands with popery, giving life to the tyranny long watching to spring again into active despotism. 5T 711.4

### The Loud Cry and Growing Your Own Food

- **Rev. 14:9, 10** — The third angel follows with a loud voice, warning that whoever worships the beast and his image and receives his mark shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, tormented with fire and brimstone.
- The honest, once bound by husbands and parents, eagerly lay hold of the truth, which becomes dearer than life; the change is the latter rain, the refreshing from the presence of the Lord, the loud cry of the third angel. CET 177.2
- **Rev. 14:14-16** — The Son of man thrusts in His sickle and the harvest of the earth is reaped — the harvesting message of the angels ministering in heaven.
- **Rev. 14:17-19** — Another angel with a sharp sickle gathers the clusters of the vine, for the grapes are fully ripe — the final gathering announced before probation closes.

### The Close of Probation

- **Dan. 11:45–12:1** — He plants the tabernacles of his palace in the glorious holy mountain, yet comes to his end; "and at that time" Michael stands up and a time of trouble begins — one unbroken sentence, the sign that probation has closed.
- When Michael stands up He leaves the most holy place; probation closes — the righteous remain righteous, the filthy filthy still.
- The events connected with the close of probation and the work of preparation for the time of trouble are clearly presented, yet multitudes understand them no better than if never revealed; Satan catches away every impression. DD 36.3

### Satan Personates Christ — At the Close of Probation

> "This quote alone should show it does not happen before Sunday law." Satan's appearing is the culmination, not the precursor.

- The wrath of Satan increases as his time grows short, and his work of deceit reaches its culmination in the time of trouble — after God's long-suffering has ended and the wicked have passed the boundary of their probation. TA 273.1
- As the crowning act in the great drama of deception, Satan himself will attempt to personate Christ — only the wicked welcome him, after the close of probation. TA 273.1

### Turkey Removed From Jerusalem — Daniel 11:45

- **Dan. 11:45** — He comes to his end with none to help him; the removal of the Turkish power from Jerusalem falls on the same line as the close of probation, triggering the time of trouble.

### The Time of Trouble

- **Dan. 12:1** — "There shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation" — this trouble begins when Michael stands up at the close of probation, triggered by the events connected with it.

### The Seven Last Plagues and the Third Woe

- **Rev. 15:1** — Seven angels having the seven last plagues, in which is filled up the wrath of God — falling after the close of probation, after a brief gap.
- **Rev. 16:1** — A great voice from the temple commands the seven angels to pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth.
- **Rev. 8:13** — The angel flying through heaven cries "Woe, woe, woe"; the third woe is the seven last plagues, connected to the first two woes.
- **Rev. 9** — The fifth, sixth, and seventh trumpets are the three woes; the first two woes were the Ottoman Empire, so the third woe is also the Ottoman power — a scourge on those who persecute God's people. GC 334.4
- In 1840 the predicted fall of the Ottoman Empire was a remarkable fulfillment of prophecy; Turkey, through her ambassadors, fulfilled it at the very time specified. GC 334.4

### The New World (American) Order

- **Rev. 17:12** — The ten horns are ten kings who have received no kingdom as yet, but receive power as kings one hour with the beast — the new world order, established late in the plagues.
- God holds back the world so His people can finish their work and protects their property until the time to flee — God, not Satan, is in control.

### Jacob's Trouble and the Death Decree — In the Sixth Plague

- **Rev. 13:15** — The image of the beast causes that as many as would not worship should be killed — the death decree.
- **Jer. 30:5-7** — "Alas! for that day is great... it is even the time of Jacob's trouble; but he shall be saved out of it" — once the death decree goes forward, none of God's people die.
- **Rev. 16:12** — The sixth angel pours his vial on the Euphrates and the water is dried up, signifying the Ottoman/Turkish power removed so the ten kings can reign. CET 100.3
- The four angels hold the four winds until Christ's work in the sanctuary is done, and then come the seven last plagues, which enrage the wicked against the righteous — placing the death decree late, in the sixth plague. CET 100.3
- **Rev. 17:12** — The ten kings reign one hour with the beast — one prophetic hour being fifteen literal days, the brief span of the death decree before God's deliverance comes.

### The Battle of Armageddon

- **Rev. 16:16** — He gathers them together into a place called in the Hebrew tongue Armageddon — the closing battle of the prophetic timeline.
