## October 22 — Real or Mythical? — Week 8

_Judgment Day Ten-Week Study_

### The Seventh Trumpet and the Mystery of God

> A message based on time: in the days of the seventh angel's voice, the mystery of God — the time prophecies given to the prophets — is finished, only days before the judgment of the dead begins.

- **Rev. 10:5, 6** — The angel standing on sea and earth swears that "there should be time no longer" — meaning prophetic time, not the end of the world. This sets up our first question: what is "time no longer"?
- **Rev. 10:7-9** — In the days of the seventh angel's voice the mystery of God is finished; the prophet eats the little book, sweet in the mouth but bitter in the belly — the disappointment that follows the message.
- **Dan. 12:4** — The little book is the sealed book of Daniel, shut up "till the time of the end."
- **Rev. 10:2** — The same little book is now seen open in the angel's hand, proving we have reached the time of the end.
- **Rev. 11:15, 18** — When the seventh angel sounded, the kingdoms became the Lord's and the "time of the dead, that they should be judged" began — so the seventh trumpet sounds shortly before the judgment of the dead.

### Judgment Takes Place in the Sanctuary

- **Isa. 16:5** — The throne is established in mercy "in the tabernacle of David"; the word tabernacle means sanctuary, so judgment and righteousness take place inside the sanctuary.
- **Heb. 8:5** — The earthly sanctuary served as the "example and shadow of heavenly things," a pattern of the real Sanctuary in heaven where Christ now ministers as High Priest.
- **Lev. 16:15-17** — The high priest brings the blood within the veil and makes an atonement for the holy place because of the uncleanness of Israel — the cleansing the judgment requires.
- **Ex. 30:10** — Aaron makes atonement upon the horns of the altar "once in a year" — the Day of Atonement comes around once a year.
- **Lev. 23:27** — That day is fixed: "the tenth day of this seventh month there shall be a day of atonement." Mark the 10th day of the seventh month — it is critical to October 22.

### Daniel 8:14 and Why We Must Prove the Date

> We have become so Laodicean we cannot even prove our most fundamental prophecy. This is a humbling message: many talk of the 2300 days yet cannot show how to reach October 22.

- **Dan. 8:14** — After 2300 days the sanctuary shall be cleansed — the foundation prophecy of the cleansing.
- **Eze. 4:6** — "Each day for a year": the 2300 days are 2300 years, reaching to the autumn of 1844 and the antitypical Day of Atonement.

### The Latter Rain Is the Doctrine — Don't Reject the Light Behind

> The latter rain may be falling on hearts all around us, but the blind will not discern or receive it.

- **Deut. 32:2** — "My doctrine shall drop as the rain... as the small rain upon the tender herb" — the latter rain is doctrine, the foundational truths of the fourth angel's message, not merely an emotional outpouring.
- _Supporting: "We shall not recognize the manifestations of the Holy Spirit in the latter rain. It may be falling on hearts all around us, but we shall not discern or receive it." TM 507.1_
- _Supporting: Those who have not humbled themselves "will see only something which in their blindness they think dangerous... and they will brace themselves to resist it... 'should we not know the Spirit of God, when we have been in the work so many years?'" Mar 219.2_

### Reckoning Biblical Time — New Moons and the Thirty-Day Month

- **Isa. 66:23** — "From one new moon to another, and from one sabbath to another" — a month is reckoned from new moon to new moon.
- **Gen. 7:11; 8:2-4** — The flood from the 17th day of the second month to the 17th day of the seventh month is 150 days over five months — 150 divided by 5 gives 30 days to a biblical month.
- **Gen. 1:14** — God set lights in the firmament "for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years" — the moon marks biblical time.

### Finding the First Month by the Barley Harvest

> To find the seventh month you must first find the first month, then simply count to seven.

- **Matt. 24:32** — The parable of the fig tree teaches us to tell the time of year by the growth of vegetation — "when his branch is yet tender, and putteth forth leaves."
- **Ex. 12:1, 2** — "This month shall be unto you the beginning of months... the first month of the year" — fixed at the time Israel left Egypt.
- **Ex. 9:28-32** — The hail plague struck "the flax and the barley... for the barley was in the ear," while wheat and rye were not grown up — the first month is when barley is ripe.
- **Ex. 23:15** — The Feast of Unleavened Bread is kept "in the month Abib," the first month, "for in it thou camest out from Egypt."
- **Lev. 23:5, 6, 9-11** — The Passover on the 14th, Unleavened Bread on the 15th of the first month, and the wave sheaf of firstfruits — all anchored to the ripe barley harvest.
- _Supporting: The Israel Agricultural Ministry's barley letter places the barley harvest in "the first and second week of April," so the first biblical month (Abib) is April._
- **Joel 2:23** — God gives "the former rain, and the latter rain in the first month" — the latter rain ripens this first-month harvest of barley.

### Counting to October 22 — The Trumpet of the First Day

- **Lev. 23:27** — Again: the Day of Atonement is the 10th day of the seventh month. With April as the first month, count April, May, June, July, August, September — October is the seventh month.
- **Lev. 23:24** — The first day of the seventh month is "a memorial of blowing of trumpets" — so ten days before the Day of Atonement a trumpet must blow.
- **Rev. 10:5-7** — Tying it together: the seventh angel's voice (the trumpet of the first day) sounds, and the mystery of God — the time prophecy — is finished only days before the judgment.
- **Rev. 8:2** — The seven angels stand before God and are given seven trumpets — the seventh angel of Revelation 10 and 11 is the seventh trumpet.
- _The Caraite Jews begin the year with the new moon in April (as the Bible teaches), while the Rabbinical mode wrongly begins it in March. If March were correct, the seventh month would not be October and October 22 could not stand. ("The True Midnight Cry," Aug. 22, 1844, p. 2.3)_

### The Historic Proclamation of October 22, 1844

> They could not proclaim the date until they saw the new moon with the eye — only then was the mystery solved.

- **Gen. 1:5** — "The evening and the morning were the first day" — the day begins at evening, so the new moon is reckoned from the start of the next day.
- **Rev. 10:5-7** — In October 1844 the astronomical new moon fell on the 11th, but the visible sliver appeared after sunset on the 12th — the biblical 13th, the first day of the seventh month. Counting ten days inclusively (13th through the 22nd) reaches October 22, the Day of Atonement, when the seventh trumpet began to sound and the mystery was finished.
- _Supporting (on-screen slide): "These fulfillments are all perfectly plain. The first message, based on time, has been given." (Review and Herald, July 22, 1852)_
- _Supporting: "This time, which the angel declares with a solemn oath... is of prophetic time, which would precede the advent of our Lord... After this period of time, reaching from 1842 to 1844, there can be no definite tracing of the prophetic time. The longest reckoning reaches to the autumn of 1844." CTr 344.5_

### The Dry Bones — Waking God's Sleeping People

> The poor, blind, and naked Laodiceans are the dry bones; the doctrine of the prophecies is the rain that wets them and brings them to life.

- **Eze. 37:1-10** — The valley of very dry bones is prophesied to twice — first bone comes to bone with a shaking, then breath comes from the four winds — and they live as "an exceeding great army," the 144,000 who accept the light from behind, the prophecies, the latter rain.
- **Deut. 32:2** — The rain that wets the dry bones is doctrine — the foundation that gives them life.

### Q&A — The 6,000 Years and the Three Woes

- **Rev. 10:6** — On the 6,000 years: the speaker affirms the earth's 6,000-year span but holds it has already ended, since "time no longer" forbids any prophetic time running past 1844; to extend it contradicts Revelation 10:6.
- **Rev. 8:13** — The three woes belong to the fifth, sixth, and seventh trumpets; the second woe (the Eastern question) is already past, and the third woe — still future — is connected to the four winds and the seven last plagues.
- **Rev. 14:6** — The "another angel" of Revelation 8:13 is the same angel flying in the midst of heaven who gives the three angels' messages, binding the trumpets to the three angels' messages and to the loud cry.
