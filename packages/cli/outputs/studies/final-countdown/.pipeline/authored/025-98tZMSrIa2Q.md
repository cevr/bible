## Are You Ready? The Loud Cry of the Third Angel — Study 23

### The Loud Cry Sounds Throughout Scripture

> The loud cry is not a single doctrine or a single experience — the term "a loud voice," "crying aloud," runs all through the Bible, and it must be fully understood in all its facets.

- **Rev. 14:7** — The first angel cries with a loud voice, "Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come" — the message is sounded loudly.
- **Rev. 14:9** — The third angel follows, "saying with a loud voice," warning against worshiping the beast, his image, and receiving his mark.
- **Rev. 14:15** — Another angel cries with a loud voice, "Thrust in thy sickle, and reap... for the harvest of the earth is ripe" — proving the loud cry is a harvest message, not a sowing message, and marking when it is finally given.
- **Rev. 18:1-4** — The fourth angel comes down with great power, lightening the earth with his glory (light is truth), and cries mightily with a strong voice, "Babylon the great is fallen"; verse 4 is the final plea, "Come out of her, my people," so as not to receive her plagues.
- **Ezek. 9:1, 4** — The same loud voice cries while a marking and sealing takes place in Jerusalem; God's mark is set only on the men "that sigh and that cry for all the abominations" done in the midst — they see the coming time of trouble and plead that God's people be made ready.
