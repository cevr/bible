## Decoding the 1843 Chart — Week 5

> "I saw that those who had no experience in the first and second angel's message must receive them from those who had an experience and followed down through the messages." We follow the three angels' messages in their order, gaining the experience God's people gained. _SG vol. 1, 75_

### Following the Messages in Their Order

- **Rev. 8:13** — An angel cries "Woe, woe, woe" to the inhabitants of the earth by reason of the three remaining trumpets; the last three trumpets are coupled with the three woes.
- **Rev. 9:15** — The four angels were loosed, prepared for "an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year"; this time-prophecy added up (391 years, 15 days) to the fall of the Ottoman Empire, August 11, 1840.
- **Rev. 9:20, 21** — These trumpet-plagues fell on a system that repented not of its idolatry, murders, sorceries, and fornications — the same power identified later in the book.
- **Rev. 17** — The woman riding the beast, full of blasphemy, is the papal power against which the trumpets served as a defense for God's people.
- **Rev. 14** — Three angels are messengers of the everlasting gospel; when the Ottoman prophecy was fulfilled, the flying messengers began "to make their message tell." When the principles of prophetic interpretation were vindicated, "from 1840 to 1844 the work rapidly extended." _GC 334–335_

### Write the Vision and Make It Plain

> Brethren Fitch and Hale presented the pictorial prophecies of Daniel and John painted on cloth; the conference voted unanimously to lithograph 300 of them — "the '43 charts." _Bates' Autobiography 262.1, 263.1_

- **Hab. 2:2** — "Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it" — fulfilled when the prophetic chart was prepared and published. _GC 391–392_
- **Hab. 2:1** — The watchman stands upon his tower and watches to see what God will say, and what he shall answer when reproved; the message carries a reproof that must humble us.
- **Hab. 2:3** — "The vision is yet for an appointed time... though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry" — pointing to the appointed time of 1844.
- **Hab. 2:4** — "The just shall live by his faith" — righteousness by faith is bound to believing the vision, even when it seems to tarry.

### The Chart Directed by the Lord

> "As early as 1842 the Spirit of God had moved upon Charles Fitch to devise the prophetic chart, which was generally regarded by Adventists as a fulfillment of the command given by the prophet Habakkuk." _SP vol. 4, 241.2_

- **Hab. 2:2** — The Bible was designed as a guide; matters concerning salvation "were not left involved in mystery... Said the Lord by the prophet Habakkuk, Write the vision, and make it plain." _Darkness Before Dawn 7.5_
- **Hab. 2:2** — "I saw that the truth should be made plain on tables... I saw that the old chart was directed by the Lord, and that not a peg of it should be altered without inspiration." The figures were as God wanted them, and His hand hid a mistake in some of the figures. _1EGWLM 243.2_
- **Hab. 2:2** — "I have seen that the 1843 chart was directed by the hand of the Lord, and that it should not be altered; that the figures were as He wanted them; that His hand was over and hid a mistake in some of the figures." One mistake — not license to cut away the chart's prophecies. _EW 74_

### The Foundation Laid in 1842, 1843, and 1844

> "Our faith in reference to the messages of the first, second, and third angels was correct. The great waymarks we have passed are immovable... These pillars of truth stand firm as the eternal hills." _Evangelism 223_

- **Rev. 14** — The first, second, and third angels' messages are the pillars of truth; God's people are now to fix their eyes on the heavenly sanctuary where our High Priest ministers in the judgment. _Evangelism 223_
- **Jer. 6:16** — "Stand ye in the ways... and ask for the old paths... But they said, We will not walk therein" — men today refuse the foundation laid at the beginning of the work. "That which was truth then, is truth today." _MS 31_

### The Tarrying Time and the Hidden Mistake

> "After the disappointment, this scripture appeared very significant... No one, however, then noticed that an apparent delay in the accomplishment of the vision — a tarrying time — is presented in the same prophecy." _GC 391–392_

- **Matt. 25:1-5** — The ten virgins took their lamps to meet the bridegroom; "while the bridegroom tarried, they all slumbered and slept" — the tarrying time foretold in the parable.
- **Rev. 10:9, 10** — The little book (Daniel) was eaten — sweet as honey in the mouth (the lovely prophecies proven), but bitter in the belly (the spring 1844 disappointment).
- **Rev. 10:11** — "Thou must prophesy again before many peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings" — prophesy again, not prophesy differently; the message itself was not changed.
- **Hab. 2:3** — "God designed to prove them. His hand covered a mistake in the reckoning of the prophetic periods"; the disappointment was a searching test of the true waiting ones. _EW 235.3_
- **Hab. 2:3** — When the Lord's hand was removed, the mistake was explained: the prophetic periods reached to 1844, and the same evidence that closed them in 1843 proved they would terminate in 1844 — "they discovered a tarrying time." _EW 236_
- **Dan. 8:14** — The mistake was in the starting point: had the decree not gone forth at the beginning of 457 B.C., the 2300 years would not close at the end of 1843, but reach to the fall of 1844. _Life Sketches 58_
- **Dan. 8:14** — "They were correct in their reckoning of the prophetic periods; prophetic time closed in 1844, and Jesus entered the most holy place to cleanse the sanctuary"; their error was thinking the sanctuary was the earth. _EW 243.2_

### Meet the Iceberg — Hold the Foundation

> "The warning has come: Nothing is to be allowed to come in that will disturb the foundation of the faith upon which we have been building ever since the message came in 1842, 1843, and 1844... It is to be as the Rock of Ages." _GC Bulletin_

- **Rev. 17** — The seductive theories that come to undermine the foundation flow from the harlot of Babylon riding the beast.
- **Hab. 2:2** — The instruction to "meet it" — the iceberg of seductive theories — was given in the ship vision; we drive straight into the error with truth, though it shakes the people. _1SM 205.3–206.3_
- **Rev. 14** — "I was shown three steps — the first, second, and third angels' messages... Woe to him who shall move a block or stir a pin of these messages"; the destiny of souls hangs on how they are received. _EW 258.3_
- **Rev. 14** — Individuals approach the immovable platform: some step on rejoicing, others find fault and call it laid wrong; but the faithful glorify God, for He was the Master Builder. _EW 258.3_
- **Hab. 2:2, 3** — The publication of the chart was the united testimony of the original Advent faith and a fulfillment of Habakkuk; "it was necessary that 1843 should be the first published time" so the vision should tarry, and the virgins slumber before the Midnight Cry. _RH Dec. 1850_
- **Hab. 2:3; Dan. 8:14** — The "seeming contradiction" — though it tarry, yet it will not tarry — is explained only by Second Advent history; the appointed time refers to the vision of the 2300 days that seemed to tarry by reaching into 1844. _RH Dec. 1850_
- **Heb. 10:36-39** — "For yet a little while, and he that shall come will come, and will not tarry. Now the just shall live by faith: but if any man draw back, my soul shall have no pleasure in him." _RH Dec. 1850_
- **Matt. 24:48** — In the tarrying, the evil servant says in his heart, "My lord delayeth his coming," and smites his fellow-servants — denying that the fulfillments are fulfillments at all. _RH Dec. 1850_
- **James 5:7-9** — "Be patient therefore, brethren, unto the coming of the Lord... establish your hearts: for the coming of the Lord draweth nigh... behold, the judge standeth before the door." _RH Dec. 1850_
- **Luke 12:35-37** — "Let your loins be girded about, and your lights burning... blessed are those servants, whom the lord when he cometh shall find watching" — to wait implies a passing by of the time. _RH Dec. 1850_
- **Dan. 8:14** — "If the vision did not speak in the autumn of 1844, then it never spoke, and never can speak"; all the evidence that pointed to 1843 proved the days would end in the fall of 1844. _RH Dec. 1850_

### Other Foundation Can No Man Lay

> "When the power of God testifies as to what is truth, that truth is to stand forever as the truth. No after suppositions contrary to the light God has given are to be entertained." _Christ in His Sanctuary 19.1_

- **1 Cor. 3:11** — "Let none seek to tear away the foundations of our faith... Men may suppose... that they can lay a stronger foundation than that which has been laid. But this is a great deception. Other foundation can no man lay than that which has been laid." _8T 297_
