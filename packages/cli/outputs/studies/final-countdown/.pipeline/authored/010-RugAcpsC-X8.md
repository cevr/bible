## How to Survive What's Coming — Study 9

_Series: The Final Countdown_

### The Wrath of God Upon the Cities

> We are living amid the perils of the last days. The wrath of God is preparing to come upon all the cities — not all at once but one after another. — _17LtMs, Ms 233, 1902, par. 4_

- **Rev. 14:9, 10** — In the third angel's message, whoever worships the beast and receives his mark drinks the wine of the wrath of God poured out without mixture.
- **Rev. 16** — The seven angels are told to pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth; this is the chapter of the seven last plagues, poured without mercy, unlike the ten plagues of Egypt which were still mixed with mercy.
- **Dan. 12:1** — The time of trouble such as never was; it does not happen overnight but builds through a succession of events.

### The Destruction of the Cities Is a Warning

- **Matt. 24** — Jesus told of the destruction coming on Jerusalem; the signs were not a call to do nothing but a command to take decided action and flee the city.
- **AH 146.3** — "So long as God gives me power... I shall continue to call upon parents to leave the cities and get homes in the country, where they can cultivate the soil." The terrible calamities recently visited on the land show the judgments of the Lord are falling, a warning to prepare for what is coming.
- **Zeph. 1:14-16** — The great day of the Lord is near, a day of wrath, trouble, distress, wasteness, desolation, darkness and gloom; a day of the trumpet and alarm against the fenced cities and high towers.
- **Rev. 18:8** — Her plagues come in one day; in prophecy a day is a year, so the plagues come within one prophetic year.
- **Ezek. 4:6; Num. 14:34** — The prophetic principle that a day represents a year.

### Sodom: An Ensample to Those Who Should Live Ungodly

- **2 Pet. 2:6** — God turned Sodom and Gomorrah into ashes, making them an example to those who afterward should live ungodly — meaning other cities at the end will likewise be turned to ashes.
- **1 Cor. 10:11** — All these things happened to them for examples and were written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come.

### Lot's Deliverance — and His Delay

- **Gen. 19:12-14** — The angels tell Lot to bring all he has out of Sodom, for the Lord has sent them to destroy it; but to his sons-in-law he seemed as one that mocked. To be inside a city marked for destruction is to be destroyed with it.
- **Gen. 19:15-17** — The angels lay hold of Lot's hand and his family's and set them outside the city: "Escape for thy life; look not behind thee, neither stay thou in all the plain; escape to the mountain." Not the city, not even the plain — the mountain.
- **Gen. 19:18-20** — Lot argues he cannot escape to the mountain "lest some evil take me," and pleads to flee to the little city of Zoar instead — the same excuses people make today about going only a little way out.
- **PP 159.3** — Lot's sons-in-law laughed at his "superstitious fears"; well enough off where they were, they could see no evidence of danger and could not believe beautiful Sodom would be destroyed.
- **PP 160.1** — Lot delayed; daily distressed yet with no true conception of the city's iniquity, he did not realize the necessity for God's judgments to check sin, and his family clung to Sodom.
- **PP 161.1** — Confused and terrified, Lot pleaded he might die; his faith had grown dim. He should have trusted himself wholly to the divine Messenger without doubt or question, but like so many others he endeavored to plan for himself, fleeing to Bela (Zoar), a city only a few miles off yet equally doomed.
- **PP 161.2** — Lot's wife looked back and became a monument of God's judgment; though her body was on the plain, her heart clung to Sodom and she perished with it because she resented that her possessions and children were left to ruin. Lot's hesitancy and delay led her to lightly regard the divine warning.

### Out of the Cities — Into the Mountains

> Who will be warned? We say again, Out of the cities. — _CL 14.4_

- **CL 14.4** — Do not consider it a great deprivation to go into the hills and mountains; seek the retirement where you can be alone with God to learn His will and way.
- **LDE 95.3** — The Lord calls for His people to locate away from the cities, for in such an hour as ye think not fire and brimstone will be rained from heaven upon these cities; proportionate to their sins will be their visitation. When one city is destroyed, do not regard it as a light affair and build again in that same destroyed city.
- **2SM 356.5** — "If we place ourselves under objectionable influences, can we expect God to work a miracle to undo the results of our wrong course?—No, indeed. Get out of the cities as soon as possible, and purchase a little piece of land" with a garden, where children can watch the flowers growing and learn lessons of simplicity and purity.
- **19MR 334.3** — Let no centers be made in the cities; make no more investments there. Fathers and mothers, gather your children into your own houses, away from those disregarding God's commandments — what shall it profit a man to gain the whole world and lose his own soul.
- **6T 195.1** — The experience of the Israelites was written for the last days; before the overflowing scourge comes, the Lord calls all Israelites indeed to prepare. "Get out of the large cities as fast as possible." Establish church schools and give children the word of God as the foundation of their education.
- **GC 622.4** — The time of trouble such as never was is soon to open; we need an experience we do not now possess and which many are too indolent to obtain. In that time every soul must stand for himself before God; though Noah, Daniel, and Job were in the land, they should deliver only their own souls by their righteousness (Ezek. 14:20).
- **CL 32.1** — "It will then be time to leave the large cities, preparatory to leaving the smaller ones for retired homes in secluded places among the mountains." City is crossed off — large or small; the place of safety is the mountains, read as literally written.

### The Seven Last Plagues Are the Effects of War

- **Rev. 16:10** — The fifth plague brings darkness on the seat of the beast; the meaning of this darkness is unlocked by comparison with Revelation 9.
- **Rev. 9:2** — Darkness rises out of the bottomless pit, a clue for understanding the darkness of Revelation 16:10. (See also Ellen White's 1850 chart on the woes.)
- **Rev. 7:1** — The four winds; in Scripture wind is a symbol of war.
- **Isa. 27:8** — A rough wind and an east wind, the same symbol of war.
- **Rev. 8; Rev. 9** — The seven trumpets; deadly instruments of war will be invented in connection with Armageddon and the plagues.
- **Rev. 9:20** — The trumpets are identified as plagues and as wars, defining the principle that the plagues are wars; the seven trumpets and the seven last plagues strike the exact same areas (darkness, the Euphrates, etc.).

### How Long, and How Far — The Death Decree and the Flight

- **Rev. 18:10** — Babylon's judgment comes "in one hour"; prophetically one hour is a twenty-fourth of a prophetic year, which equals fifteen days.
- **Rev. 17:12** — The ten kings receive power as kings "one hour" with the beast — the same fifteen days during which the ten kings reign with the papacy. (See the 1850 Ellen White chart, downloadable at JudgmentDayDocumentary.com.)
- **Isa. 33:2, 16** — In the time of trouble, when God's people flee into the mountains at the death decree, their bread shall be given and their water sure; this is when we do not store up food but are led by God's angels to the caves.

### Country-Living Q&A: Faith, Family, and the Plan

- **Gen. 7** _(Noah's ark)_ — Faith is not staying in the city expecting a magical bubble of protection; that is presumption. Righteousness was Noah's obedience in building the ark — and the ark was built large, to carry others, not himself alone.
- **Mark 8:36** — What shall it profit a man if he gain the whole world and lose his own soul; an unbelieving spouse is not a reason to disobey God's warning to move — go in faith and pray they follow.
- **Isa. 60** — The Gentiles coming to Zion are called camels and other creatures; the animals brought into the ark represent the great multitude who join the 144,000 (who built the ark) and come in before the door closes — so we prepare provisions not only for ourselves but for all who heed the message.
