## The First Angel's Message — Week 3 (Judgment Day Ten-Week Study)

### William Miller's Method of Bible Study

> "Those who are engaged in proclaiming the third angel's message are searching the Scriptures upon the same plan that Father Miller adopted... we shall all do well to heed the principles set forth." (Review and Herald, Nov. 25, 1884)

- **Rev. 10:9, 10** — Miller took the little book, ate it up: sweet as honey in the mouth, but bitter in the belly; the opened prophecies of Daniel are digested, then come toil and disappointment. The 1844 experience is to be repeated.
- **EW 232** — Angels of God accompanied Miller; a world in wickedness and a cold, worldly church led him to endure toil, privation, and suffering. So it is when one stands for truth: God's word is a sword that separates, so we must stand for truth because it is truth.
- **Gen. 1:1ff** — Miller commenced with Genesis and read verse by verse, proceeding no faster than the meaning unfolded, comparing scripture with scripture. Memoirs of William Miller 69.3.
- **GC 320** — He laid aside all prepossessions and let the Bible be its own interpreter, finding it a system of revealed truths so plain "the wayfaring man, though a fool, need not err therein."

### Rule I — Every Word Must Have Its Proper Bearing

- **Matt. 5:18** — Till heaven and earth pass, not one jot or tittle shall pass from the law; therefore every word, even the smallest, is important and must have its proper bearing on the subject. Miller's Works, Vol. 1, p. 20.

### Rule II — All Scripture Is Necessary and May Be Understood

- **2 Tim. 3:15-17** — All scripture is given by inspiration of God and is profitable for doctrine, reproof, correction, and instruction; it makes us wise unto salvation and thoroughly furnishes the man of God. Understanding comes by diligent study, not by trying harder.

### Rule III — Nothing Revealed Will Be Hid From Those Who Ask in Faith

- **Deut. 29:29** — The secret things belong to God, but the things revealed belong to us and our children; what is in the Bible has been revealed for us to understand.
- **Matt. 21:22** — Whatsoever we ask in prayer, believing, we shall receive; so we may ask God to teach us to understand the prophecies and He will.

### Rule IV — Bring All Scriptures on a Subject Together

- **Luke 24:27** — Beginning at Moses and all the prophets, Christ expounded the things concerning Himself in all the scriptures; doctrine is found by gathering every passage on the subject.
- **Luke 24:44, 45** — All things written in the law of Moses, the prophets, and the Psalms concerning Him must be fulfilled; then He opened their understanding. If a theory has no contradiction, it cannot be in error.

### Rule V — Scripture Must Be Its Own Expositor

- **1 Cor. 2:12-16** — We have received the Spirit of God, speaking not in man's wisdom but as the Holy Ghost teaches, comparing spiritual things with spiritual; the Bible, not a teacher's guess or creed, must be the rule of interpretation.

### Rule VI — The Same Things Are Revealed Again by Different Visions

- **Hosea 12:10** — God has multiplied visions and used similitudes by the prophets; because the same truth is shown more than once, we combine the visions to establish it by two or three witnesses.
- **Hab. 2:2** — Write the vision and make it plain upon tables; prophecy is not meant to be hidden or confusing but revealed and understood.

### Rule VII — Visions Are Always Mentioned as Such

- **Dan. 8:1, 2** — "A vision appeared unto me, even unto me Daniel"; when something is a vision it is named as such, so explanations that are not labeled visions are plain, not figurative.

### Rule VIII — Figures Always Have a Figurative Meaning

- **Rev. 17:1** — The great whore sits upon many waters; "waters" is the figure to be defined. Figures represent future things — mountains meaning governments (Dan. 2:35, 44), beasts meaning kingdoms (Dan. 7:8, 17), waters meaning people (Rev. 17:1, 15).
- **Rev. 17:15** — The angel plainly declares the waters are peoples, multitudes, nations, and tongues; we do not guess the meaning, the Bible interprets itself.

### Rule IX — Parables Are Explained the Same Way as Figures

- **Mark 4:13** — "Know ye not this parable? and how then will ye know all parables?" Understanding one parable, by the subject and the Bible, opens the rest.

### Rule X — Figures May Have More Than One Signification

- **Eccl. 7:14** — "Day" used in an indefinite sense, the first of three figurative meanings of a day.
- **Ezek. 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year"; the day-for-a-year principle, a definite signification of "day."
- **2 Pet. 3:8** — One day is with the Lord as a thousand years; the third signification — a day for a thousand years. Each must be set in its proper context to harmonize with the Bible.

### Rule XI — If a Word Makes Good Sense as It Stands, Take It Literally

- **Rev. 12:1, 2** — A woman clothed with the sun, the moon under her feet, a crown of twelve stars; this breaks natural law, so symbolism must be present and the figures interpreted to their literal meaning.
- **Rev. 17:3-7** — A woman sitting on a scarlet beast full of names of blasphemy, with seven heads and ten horns ("in the spirit," therefore a vision); breaking natural law, it requires figures — but where a word does no violence to the laws of nature, it must be understood literally, not spiritualized.

### Rule XII — Trace the Figure, Substitute the Explanation

- **Rev. 13:1, 2** — Trace a figure through the Bible and replace it with its explanation: beast for kingdom, lion with eagle's wings for Babylon, bear for Medo-Persia; if it makes good sense with no contradiction, you need look no further.

### Rule XIII — The True Historical Event Must Fulfill Every Word

- **Acts 3:18** — What God showed by the mouth of His prophets He has fulfilled; if every word of a prophecy is literally fulfilled, that history is the true event, for God takes care that history and prophecy agree.
- **Rev. 17:17** — God puts it in their hearts to fulfill His will; prophecy must match history exactly, and if one word lacks fulfillment, look for another event or await its future development.

### Rule XIV — The Most Important Rule: You Must Have Faith

- **Miller's Works, Vol. 1, p. 22** — A faith that requires a sacrifice and would give up the dearest object on earth; trusting that the prophecies, once rightly understood, will surely come to pass.
- **Matt. 10:29, 30** — He who notices the sparrow's fall and numbers the hairs of our head will guard the translation of His word, so that those who trust Him will not err, though they know no Hebrew or Greek.

### Miller's Chronology and the Conclusion of 1843

> "The commencement of the chain of truth was given to him, and he was led on to search for link after link, until he looked with wonder and admiration upon the Word of God." EW 229.1

- **Gen. 6:3** — The 120 years to the flood, a predicted event fulfilled within a given time.
- **Gen. 7:4** — The seven days before the flood, with forty days of predicted rain.
- **Gen. 15:13** — The four hundred years of the sojourn of Abraham's seed.
- **Gen. 40:12-20** — The three days of the butler's and baker's dreams.
- **Gen. 41:28-54** — The seven years of Pharaoh's plenty and famine.
- **Num. 14:34** — The forty years in the wilderness, reckoned a day for a year.
- **1 Kings 17:1** — The three and a half years of famine in Elijah's day.
- **Isa. 7:8** — The sixty-five years to the breaking of Ephraim.
- **Jer. 25:11** — The seventy years' captivity.
- **Dan. 4:13-16** — Nebuchadnezzar's seven times.
- **Dan. 9:24-27** — The seven weeks, threescore and two weeks, and one week — seventy weeks determined upon the Jews, dated from B.C. 457. Memoirs of William Miller 74.2.
- **Dan. 8:14** — The 2300 prophetic days marking the vision from the Persian to the end, all terminating together about A.D. 1843. Memoirs of William Miller 75.1.
- **Dan. 12:11** — The 1335 days reckoned from the taking away of the daily and setting up of the abomination, dated from the rise of the papal supremacy about A.D. 508. Memoirs of William Miller 76.1.

### The First Angel's Message Given in America

- **Rev. 14:6, 7** — "Fear God, and give glory to Him; for the hour of His judgment is come"; the everlasting gospel, not to be changed or said to be in error, had its most direct fulfillment in America and was carried to distant lands. GC 368.1.

### Questions Answered — A Literal Historical Event in Prophecy

- **Rev. 13:1** — A beast rising from the sea with seven heads and ten horns breaks natural law, so it is a figure: the beast is the papal kingdom.
- **Rev. 13:3** — One head wounded to death, the deadly wound healed; the literal historical event is 1798, when the pope was taken captive and died in prison.
- **Rev. 13:5** — Power given to continue forty and two months — 1260 years by the day-for-a-year rule — from A.D. 538 to 1798, a literal historical date proving the prophecy. An interpretation with no exact historical fulfillment is incorrect.

### Questions Answered — The 1290 and 1335 Days of Daniel 12

- **Dan. 12:10** — Many shall be purified and made white; none of the wicked shall understand, but the wise shall understand — a promise to those who seek.
- **Dan. 12:11** — From the taking away of the daily (the word "sacrifice" omitted as a man-added italic) and the setting up of the abomination, 1290 days; dated 508 to 1798.
- **Dan. 12:12** — Blessed is he that waiteth and cometh to the 1335 days; the patience of the saints, reaching from 508 to A.D. 1843.
- **Dan. 12:13** — Daniel shall rest and stand in his lot at the end of the days, proved correct.
- **Dan. 8:12** — The daily is first mentioned here, so understanding the 1290 and 1335 requires going back to Daniel 8; those who gave the judgment-hour cry held the correct view of the daily before 1844. EW 74.2.

### Questions Answered — The Papacy and the Three Players of the Last Days

> "We may have less to say in some lines in regard to the Roman power and the papacy... the human agent is to be kept out of sight, hid in Christ." Ev 577.

- **Rev. 13:11** — After the first beast's deadly wound, the two-horned beast — America — comes prominently on the scene as the present player; its mother, the papacy, "has had her day, and it is past," yet the wound is healed and she will rise again. Spalding-Magan Collection 1.4.
- **Rev. 16:13** — The three players uniting at the end: the dragon (Satan), the beast (the papacy of Revelation 13), and the false prophet (America) form a threefold union.
- **Rev. 17:10** — Seven kings, five fallen, one is, the other not yet come; following the rules and the context of the original scarlet beast, this cannot be stretched to the U.S. presidents from Reagan to Trump.
- **Dan. 9:25** — The 2300 days are anchored to the decree to restore and build Jerusalem; no date is named outright in the Bible, but God ties each prophecy to a literal historical event we can locate and count from.
