## The Man Who Survived the End of the World — Study 6

_Series: The Final Countdown_

### Noah's Story Is a Warning, Not a Passive Miracle

> Noah was trapped at sea for 370 days — but was that just a miracle, or the fruit of 120 years of warning and preparation? Jesus said we must learn the lessons of Noah's day for the end of the world.

- **Ps. 91:8** — Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked; God's protection is real, but it does not mean we stand in the firing line doing nothing.
- **Heb. 11:7** — By faith Noah, warned of God of things not yet seen, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith.
- **Matt. 24:37-39** — As the days of Noah were, so shall the coming of the Son of man be; the world ate, drank, and married, and knew not until the flood took them all away.

### How Long Noah Was in the Ark

- **Gen. 7:11, 12** — In the 600th year of Noah's life, the second month, the seventeenth day, the fountains of the deep broke up and rain fell forty days and forty nights.
- **Gen. 8:14-17** — In the second month, on the twenty-seventh day, the earth was dried and God told Noah to go forth from the ark; from the 17th to the 27th of a 360-day year is 370 days in the ark.

### God's Specific Commands to Build the Ark

> This was not happenstance. Noah was given a prophecy that in 120 years a flood would come, and exact directions for how to prepare.

- **Gen. 6:14** — Make thee an ark of gopher wood, with rooms, pitched within and without; God commanded the best quality, built to last, not a shoddy short-term job.
- **Gen. 6:15** — The length 300 cubits, breadth 50, height 30; the size was God's command, large enough to house roughly 1,400 kinds of land animals.
- **Gen. 6:18-22** — God established His covenant, commanded two of every sort and all manner of food gathered, and Noah did according to all that God commanded him.

### The Animals Represent Kinds of People

- **Acts 10:11, 12, 28** — Peter's vision of the sheet of four-footed beasts and fowls taught that God calls no man common or unclean; the creatures of the ark typify men of all nations.
- **Rev. 7:1-3** — Four angels hold the four winds while the sealing angel cries, "Hurt not the earth till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads."
- **Rev. 7:4** — There were sealed 144,000 of all the tribes of Israel — the people who were supposed to know prophecy and carry the message.
- **Rev. 7:9** — After this, a great multitude which no man could number, of all nations and kindreds and peoples and tongues — the "all kinds" of animals that respond to the message.
- **Rev. 14** — The 144,000 carry the three angels' messages, the special end-of-the-world warning to be given with a loud cry.
- **Rev. 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins and receive not of her plagues"; the warning to escape the judgments, as the flood was a judgment.

### The Gentiles Come to the Light

- **Isa. 60:1-3** — Arise, shine; though darkness covers the earth, the Lord's glory rises upon thee and the Gentiles come to thy light, and kings to the brightness of thy rising.
- **Isa. 60:4, 5** — All gather and come to thee; the abundance of the sea shall be converted unto thee, the forces of the Gentiles shall come — the great multitude responds though Israel struggles.
- **Isa. 60:6, 7** — The multitude of camels and dromedaries of Midian, all from Sheba, the flocks of Kedar — the children of the east, even the Muslim peoples, gathered in as prophecy is fulfilled.
- **Rev. 17:15** — The waters and sea represent peoples and multitudes and nations and tongues; the "abundance of the sea" converted is a great multitude of people.

### A Working Faith — Preach and Prepare Together

> _"He directed Noah to build an ark and at the same time preach to the inhabitants of the world that God would bring the flood waters upon the earth."_ — Signs of the Times, December 20, 1877. Every blow of the hammer was a sermon. _"Noah's faith was a working faith... that led him to act in accordance with the word of God."_ — ST April 18, 1895.

- **1 Cor. 10:11** — All these things happened unto them for examples, written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come; Noah's story is for you and me.

> _"The whole surface of the earth was changed at the flood... yet Noah preserved seed and took it with him in the ark."_ — Spiritual Gifts vol. 3, p. 76.2. Noah invested all he possessed in the ark. _"All that he possessed he invested in the ark."_ — From Eternity Past, p. 53.

### Our Antitypical Ark: The Country Property

> The flood prefigures the no-buy, no-sell crisis. We must perceive, prepare, and preach — just as Noah did for 120 years — building a self-sufficient ark before the storm. _"Again and again the Lord has instructed that our people are to take their families away from the cities into the country where they can raise their own provisions, for in the future the problem of buying and selling will be a very serious one."_ — Country Living, p. 9. _"The time is not far distant when we shall be forced to seek refuge in desolate and solitary places."_ — Country Living (last page).

- **Rev. 13:17** — No man might buy or sell save he that had the mark; no mark means no buy, no sell — the final test God's people will face, requiring preparation, not just a quiet flight.
- **Rev. 14:9** — The third angel cries with a loud voice that whoever worships the beast and receives his mark shall drink the wine of the wrath of God; we escape by preparing and heeding the warning.

### The End-Time Timeline: From Judgment to Deliverance

> 1844 began the message of judgment; 1888 was the first call out of the cities. 137 years later we should have been building, planting, and planning, but we are not. The same flow of events applies: warning, preparation, final warning, then the door shut.

- **Rev. 18:4** — The final loud cry — "Come out of her" — calls the great multitude into the ark before the close of probation and the seven last plagues.
- **Rev. 17:12, 13** — The ten horns are ten kings (regions of the new world order) which receive power as kings one hour with the beast and give their strength unto the beast.
- **Rev. 18:8** — Her plagues come in one day — a year by the prophetic day-for-a-year principle.
- **Rev. 18:10** — In one hour is thy judgment come; the new world order's one hour follows after Babylon's judgment.
- **Rev. 17:16** — The ten horns shall hate the whore, make her desolate and naked, eat her flesh, and burn her with fire — the judgment that comes at the very end.
- **Ezek. 4:6** — Each day appointed for a year — the prophetic key by which one hour equals fifteen days and one day equals a year.

### Fleeing to the Mountains When the Death Decree Comes

> After the close of probation and through the plagues, God's people remain on their self-sufficient property — the ark — until the new world order enforces the death decree. Only then do they flee into the mountains, trusting wholly on God.

- **Dan. 12:1** — Michael stands up and a time of trouble commences, such as never was; the death decree belongs to this final stage.
- **Isa. 33:2** — O Lord, be gracious unto us; be thou their arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble.
- **Isa. 33:16** — He shall dwell on high; his place of defense shall be the munitions of rocks; bread shall be given him, his waters shall be sure — God feeds the fleeing saints in the wilderness.

### Two Classes, One Choice

> _"Many at first appeared to receive the warning, yet they did not turn to God with true repentance... and were soon among the most defiant scoffers."_ — From Eternity Past, pp. 53, 54. _"In that time before the flood, as in this, there were two classes, the righteous and the wicked... God gave direction that an ark was to be built for those who desired to be saved from the coming destruction."_ — Christ Triumphant, p. 56. There is no third group — either the seal of God or the mark of the beast; we show we believe God by preparing, and in building the ark the character itself is transformed. _"Agriculture is the A B C of education."_ — Health Food Ministry, p. 39.
