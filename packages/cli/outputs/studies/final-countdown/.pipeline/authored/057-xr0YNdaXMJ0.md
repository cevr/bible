## Has the Judgment of the Living Begun? Part 1 — Week 12

### The Shaking: The Straight Testimony to Laodicea

> The truth of prophecy is the straight testimony that wakes God's people; some will not bear it, and that is what causes the shaking.

- **Deut. 32:2** — God's doctrine drops as the rain; the latter rain is not a strange outpouring but the doctrine itself, the prophetic truth of the end times.
- **Ezek. 37:1-10** — The valley of very dry bones must be shaken and breathed upon; God commands His servant to prophesy to the bones, and prophecy is what wakes the dead.
- **Ezek. 37:7-10** — As the prophet prophesied there was a noise and a shaking; he prophesies to the wind to come from the four winds, and breath enters the slain so they live as an exceeding great army.

> "I asked the meaning of the shaking... it would be caused by the straight testimony called forth by the counsel of the True Witness to the Laodiceans... Some will not bear this straight testimony. They will rise up against it." CET 176.1

### The Sealing: Holding the Four Winds

- **Rev. 7:1-4** — Four angels stand on the four corners of the earth holding the four winds until the servants of God are sealed in their foreheads; earth, sea, and trees represent peoples, and the angel from the East brings the light of truth.
- **Rev. 7:2** — The sealing angel ascends from the East, as the sun rises bringing light; light represents truth, and this message seals.
- **Rev. 7:3, 4** — The winds are held back until the 144,000 of the tribes of Israel are sealed in their foreheads.

### Ezekiel 9: The Mark and the Judgment Beginning at the House of God

- **Ezek. 9:1-3** — A loud cry calls the men with destroying weapons; one man among them is clothed in linen with a writer's inkhorn, like a pen by his side.
- **Ezek. 9:4** — The man with the inkhorn goes through the midst of Jerusalem (the final church movement) and sets a mark — a seal — upon the foreheads of those who sigh and cry for the abominations.
- **Ezek. 9:5, 6** — The slaughter begins at the sanctuary, at the ancient men before the house; God's people of position are judged first, then the laity, then the world.

### The Vineyard, the Fruit, and the Slaughter Wind

- **Isa. 27:2, 3** — God's people are a vineyard which He waters every moment — watered with the latter rain.
- **Isa. 27:6** — Jacob takes root, and Israel blossoms and buds and fills the face of the world with fruit; this is the time of harvest.
- **Isa. 27:7, 8** — The day of fruit is also the day of slaughter; in the time of the latter rain He debates with His people, staying His rough wind in the day of the east wind.
- **Hos. 8:7** — They have sown the wind and shall reap the whirlwind — the four winds from all directions bringing destruction in their path.
- **Rev. 7:1** — Back to the four angels holding the four winds: the winds would blow on God's people, so He must first seal His servants.

### What the Seal Is: A King's Signet on His People

- **Rev. 9:4** — The locusts are commanded not to hurt the grass, green things, or trees, but only those men who lack the seal of God — a past historical event, yet showing the seal protects God's people.
- **Gen. 41:40-42** — Pharaoh sets Joseph over all Egypt and gives him his ring; the ring carries the king's authority.
- **Esther 3:10-12** — The king gives Haman his ring; the decree is written in the king's name and sealed with the king's ring, so it carries the king's authority and cannot be questioned.
- **Esther 8:8** — The writing sealed with the king's ring may no man reverse; this is why the seal matters — it is given in the king's name and cannot be reversed.
- **Rev. 7** — The 144,000 have the seal of the living God and the Father's name written in their foreheads; they come proclaiming the loud cry in the Father's name, stamped with the seal of the King.

### The Sabbath: The Seal of the Living God

- **Ex. 31:13** — God's sabbaths are a sign between Him and His people, that they may know He is the Lord that sanctifies them; the same thing that woke the bones makes them know the Lord.
- **Rom. 4:15** — Where no law is, there is no transgression; Satan would remove the law to remove sin, but to remove the law he must remove its seal.
- **Ex. 20:8-11** — The fourth commandment contains the seal of God: His name (the Lord thy God), His territory (heaven and earth), and His title (Creator); remove the seventh day and you remove the seal.
- **Isa. 8:16** — Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples — the Sabbath is that seal of the law.
- **Rev. 14:12** — Here is the patience of the saints who keep the commandments of God (including the fourth, which contains the seal) and the faith of Jesus — the doctrine of the latter rain and the Three Angels' messages.

### The Seal Tested: The Mark of the Beast in Opposition

> The seal must be tested through observance or disregard of the Sabbath; the three Hebrews were sealed in the test on the plains of Dura, willing to die rather than bow.

- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — The beast causes all to receive a mark in the right hand or forehead, that no man might buy or sell without it — a counterfeit seal set in direct opposition to God's.
- **Rev. 13:11, 12** — Another beast (Protestant America) comes up out of the earth with two lamblike horns but speaks as a dragon, causing the world to worship the first beast whose deadly wound was healed; the mark therefore has to do with worship.
- **Dan. 7:25** — This power speaks great words against the Most High and thinks to change times and laws — the times and laws of God; the only commandment dealing with time is the fourth, so the mark is the transfer of the Sabbath from the seventh day to the first.
- **2SM 80.4** — "The Lord has shown me clearly that the image of the beast will be formed before probation closes; for it is to be the great test for the people of God, by which their eternal destiny will be decided." (the great test that decides eternal destiny, i.e. the close of probation)
- **Ev 234.2** — "No one has yet received the mark of the beast. The testing time has not yet come... None are condemned until they have had the light and have seen the obligation of the fourth commandment. But when the decree shall go forth enforcing the counterfeit sabbath, and the loud cry of the third angel shall warn men against the worship of the beast and his image, the line will be clearly drawn." (defines the loud cry as the warning against the mark)
- **Mar 164.4** — "This is the test that the people of God must have before they are sealed... All who prove their loyalty to God by observing His law, and refusing to accept a spurious sabbath... will receive the seal of the living God. Those who yield the truth... and accept the Sunday sabbath, will receive the mark of the beast."

### The Loud Cry: The Fourth Angel's Message

- **Rev. 18:1-4** — Another angel comes down with great power, and the earth is lightened with his glory; he cries "Babylon is fallen," and a voice says "Come out of her, my people," that ye be not partakers of her sins and receive not of her plagues — the final loud cry call.
- **Rev. 14:9, 10** — The third angel warns with a loud voice that whoever worships the beast and his image and receives his mark shall drink the wine of the wrath of God, poured out without mixture.
- **Rev. 16:2** — The first vial is poured upon the earth, and a grievous sore falls upon those who have the mark of the beast and worship his image; the plagues fall on those who take the mark, so the seal protects God's people from them.
- **Mar 200.2** — "Just as soon as the people of God are sealed in their foreheads — it is not any seal or mark that can be seen, but a settling into the truth, both intellectually and spiritually... it will come. Indeed, it has begun already; the judgments of God are now upon the land, to give us warning." (the sealing is a settling into the truth; the shaking has begun)

### Standing on the Platform: The 1842-1844 Foundation

> Nothing is to disturb the foundation of faith laid since the message came in 1842, 1843, and 1844; this truth is to be as the Rock of Ages.

- **Rev. 10** — Time is no longer; there are no more day-for-a-year time prophecies to predict these events — beware those who set times of three-and-a-half or seven years; we know the events, not the dates.
- **CIHS 188.1** — "Solemn are the scenes connected with the closing work of the atonement... The judgment is now passing in the sanctuary above... Soon — none know how soon — it will pass to the cases of the living." (Quotes Mark 13:33 and Rev. 3:3 — watch, lest He come as a thief.)

### Q&A: The 144,000 and the Great Multitude

- **Rev. 9:4** — The locusts hurt only those men without the seal; the grass, green things, and trees represent people, so the locusts devour people who lack the seal of God, not literal vegetation.
- **Rev. 17:15** — The waters represent peoples, multitudes, nations, and tongues — the Bible interprets its own symbols.
- **Rev. 7:9** — The great multitude no man can number, of all nations and tongues, stands before the throne with white robes and palms in their hands — distinct from the 144,000.
- **EW 39.3** — Enoch, translated alive and not an Israelite, bears a glorious palm on his right arm; like the great multitude with palms, a type of those who go to heaven without seeing death.
- **EW 16.2** — The 144,000 stand on the sea of glass clothed with a glorious white mantle (a priestly garment) from shoulders to feet — distinct from the great multitude's white robes and palms.
- **Rev. 7:9, 14** — The great multitude come out of great tribulation, alive through the plagues until Jesus appears, so they cannot be the risen dead; both they and the 144,000 are living when Jesus comes.
