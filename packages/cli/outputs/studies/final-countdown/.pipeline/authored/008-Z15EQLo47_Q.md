## The Secret to Skipping Death Revealed — Study 7

### Enoch Walked With God

> The secret to skipping death and surviving the end-time events is hidden in one of the Bible's shortest stories. Only two men have ever escaped death; a final living group will do the same.

- **Gen. 5:21-24** — Enoch lived 365 years, walked with God, "and he was not; for God took him" — the first man translated to heaven without seeing death. The first key: to be translated alive, you must walk with God.
- **Amos 3:3** — "Can two walk together, except they be agreed?" To walk with God, Enoch had to be in agreement with God; we likewise must agree with His will and ways.
- **2 Thess. 2:10** — Those who perish do so because they "received not the love of the truth" — walking with God requires a love of the truth itself, not of what is popular or what family, friends, or church say.

### Translated By Faith — That He Should Not See Death

- **Heb. 11:5** — "By faith Enoch was translated that he should not see death... he had this testimony, that he pleased God." The second key: we must please God to be translated. EP 49.4
- **Rev. 14:12** — "Here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." The same faith Enoch had is the faith of the final group, the 144,000.
- **EP 49.4 (From Eternity Past)** — Enoch's godly character "represents the state of holiness which must be attained by those who shall be 'redeemed from the earth' (Revelation 14:3) at Christ's second advent. Then, as before the Flood, iniquity will prevail."

### Enoch and the 144,000 — The Same Experience

> Enoch was taken alive; the 144,000 are redeemed alive. Both are Adventists (waiting for the advent), both live in the country, both preach judgment, both warn of the second coming.

- **Rev. 7:4** — The sealed number is 144,000 of all the tribes of the children of Israel — the special end-time group compared to Enoch.
- **Rev. 14:3** — The 144,000 "were redeemed from the earth" and sing a new song no man else could learn; like Enoch, they are taken alive.
- **EP 49.4 (From Eternity Past)** — "Like Enoch, God's people will seek for purity of heart... Like Enoch they will warn the world of the Lord's second coming and by their holy example will condemn the sins of the ungodly. As Enoch was translated to heaven, so the living righteous will be translated from the earth before its destruction by fire." See 1 Cor. 15:51, 52; 1 Thess. 4:16-18.

### Enoch the Prophet — A Judgment Message

> Just your holy example becomes a living rebuke; the ungodly feel judged even when you say nothing.

- **Jude 1:14-16** — Enoch, "the seventh from Adam," prophesied of the Lord coming "with ten thousands of his saints, to execute judgment upon all" — proof he understood the final judgment of the ungodly.
- **Jude 1:16** — The ungodly are "murmurers, complainers, walking after their own lusts... having men's persons in admiration because of advantage" — gaining advantage for selfish gain, the same spirit seen at the end.
- **CET 238.1 (Christian Experience and Teachings)** — "The prophetic gift is not limited to any one age... Enoch, the seventh from Adam, was a prophet. Looking down through the centuries, he saw with prophetic vision the coming of the Lord, and the execution of the final judgments upon the ungodly."

### The Three Angels' Messages — The Judgment Begun

- **Rev. 14:6, 7** — The first angel's message: "Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come" — the announcement that the judgment has begun.
- **Rev. 14:8** — The second angel: "Babylon is fallen."
- **Rev. 14:9, 10** — The third angel warns that whoever worships the beast and receives his mark "shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God" — the judgments fall on the wicked once the judgment process ends.

### The Fourth Angel — Light Lightening the Earth

> Light is truth, but specifically the true understanding of last-day prophecy. The lamp gives light only when filled with oil — the Holy Spirit, who leads into all truth and shows things to come.

- **Rev. 18:1-3** — Another angel comes down with great power and "the earth was lightened with his glory," crying "Babylon the great is fallen"; the merchants of the earth waxed rich through the abundance of her delicacies — the fourth angel's message, the loud cry.
- **John 16:13** — The Holy Spirit (the oil in the lamp) "will guide you into all truth... and he will shew you things to come" — a true understanding of prophecy.
- **LS 411.5 (Life Sketches)** — "Now comes the word that... New York is to be swept away by a tidal wave. This I have never said... when the Lord shall arise to shake terribly the earth, then the words of Revelation 18:1-3 will be fulfilled" — fulfilled when the great buildings fall (the Twin Towers, Sept. 11, 2001).

### The Day of Great Slaughter — When the Towers Fall

- **Isa. 30:23-25** — In the day the latter rain falls, "in the day of the great slaughter, when the towers fall," there shall be rivers and streams of waters upon every high hill — the towers falling marks the day of great slaughter.
- **Isa. 27:7** — Speaks of the slaughter, "the slaughter of them that are slain"; verse 8, "when it shooteth forth thou wilt debate with it" — a great debate on truth precedes the day of slaughter; the defenced cities shall be desolate.
- **Ezek. 9:1-6** — At the cry "with a loud voice," six men with slaughter weapons come, but the man with the writer's inkhorn first sets a mark on the foreheads of those that sigh and cry; begin at the sanctuary — God's sealing of His people before the day of slaughter, beginning with His own people.

### The Time of Trouble — God's People on Earth

> It is a deception that God's people will be taken away before the time of trouble; they will be here, sustained in the mountains.

- **Dan. 12:1** — The "time of trouble, such as never was" — God's people are on the earth during this time, not removed before it.
- **Isa. 33:1, 2** — A time of woe; "be thou their arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble" — Isaiah 33 names both the time of woe and the time of trouble.
- **Isa. 33:16** — "He shall dwell on high... bread shall be given him; his waters shall be sure" — in the time of trouble God's people are in the mountains and their bread and water are sure; before that time, our bread and water are not sure without our own effort.
- **Rev. 13:17** — The "no buy, no sell" — none can buy or sell food, water, power, clothing, or any necessity unless they worship as the beast dictates; this triggers the close of probation and the seven last plagues.

### Come Out of Her — The Loud Cry

- **Rev. 18:4** — "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues" — the fourth angel's message repeating the second and third: do not take the mark or receive the wine of the wrath of God.
- **Rev. 18:3** — "The merchants of the earth are waxed rich through the abundance of her delicacies" — connected to the merchants who met at the World Trade Center.

### Watching and Working — Obedience Like Enoch and Noah

> When men of God saw prophecy of future events, they took action. Be seen working — stand out like Noah building his ark, so the world asks "What is that?"

- **ChS 96.1 (Christian Service)** — "Those who are watching for the Lord, are purifying their souls by obedience to the truth. With vigilant watching they combine earnest working... They are declaring the truth that is now specially applicable. As Enoch, Noah, Abraham, and Moses each declared the truth for his time, so will Christ's servants now give the special warning for their generation."
- **COL 332.1 (Christ's Object Lessons)** — "Be ambitious, for the Master's glory, to cultivate every grace of character... This you may do; for Enoch pleased Him though living in a degenerate age. And there are Enochs in this our day."
- **CCh 312.4 (Counsels for the Church)** — Christ's followers "are to be channels of light... Enoch did not become polluted with the iniquities existing in his day; why need we in our day?"

### Leave the Cities — Country Living

> Country living is not just about saving our skin; it is a character development that cannot take place in the city.

- **Rev. 13:17** — Because of the coming "no buy, no sell," God's commandment-keeping people must prepare now to grow their own food and be self-sufficient. CL 30.4
- **CL 30.4 (Country Living)** — "As God's commandment-keeping people, we must leave the cities. As did Enoch, we must work in the cities but not dwell in them."
- **CL 13.2 (Country Living)** — Schools in the city wait to "attract and demoralize" children, and "the work of character building is tenfold harder" there — a tenfold easier advantage is found in the country.
- **CL 30.2 (Country Living)** — "The cities are to be worked from outposts... 'Shall not the cities be warned? Yes; not by God's people living in them, but by their visiting them, to warn them of what is coming upon the earth.'"
- **CL 30.5 (Country Living)** — As the voice of Lot was heard in Sodom, "yet Lot could have preserved his family from many evils, had he not made his home in this wicked, polluted city... Enoch walked with God, and yet he did not live in the midst of any city, polluted with every kind of violence and wickedness, as did Lot in Sodom."

### Follow the Lamb Whithersoever He Goeth

- **Rev. 14:1** — The Lamb stands on Mount Zion with the 144,000 "having his Father's name written in their foreheads" — His ownership, His seal, His character embodied in them.
- **Rev. 14:4** — They "were not defiled with women" (not polluted by false doctrine or a false church), "they are virgins... these are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth" — if Jesus went to the country to commune with God, His followers go where He goes.
- **Rev. 14:5** — "In their mouth was found no guile" — they do not speak like the world in any way; they are visibly, recognizably different.

- **Rev. 14:12** — The saints "keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus"; the seal of God is found in the fourth commandment — the seventh-day Sabbath.
- **Rev. 13** — The beast (the papacy) seeks to change "times and laws"; the only time and law changed is the Sabbath to Sunday, which is the mark of the beast. A progressive Sunday law leads to no buying or selling for those who refuse to worship on Sunday.

### Q&A — The 15 Days of Babylon's Fall

- **Rev. 18:10** — "In one hour is thy judgment come" — using the day-for-a-year principle (Ezek. 4:6), one hour is a twenty-fourth of a prophetic day (a year); 360 days divided by 24 is roughly 15 literal days.

### Q&A — The Seven Trumpets and the Seven Last Plagues

> The ten plagues of Egypt and the seven last plagues happen for the same reason; some Egyptian plagues struck God's people, but none of the last plagues touch God's people.

- **Ex. 10:13, 19** — The plague of locusts was brought by an east wind and removed by a west wind; since wind equals wars, this is typology of an eastern war and a western war connected to the woe and the locust beast.
- **Rev. 9:20, 21** — The seven trumpet plagues are the "first plagues" — local in scope.
- **Rev. 16** — The seven last plagues are identical in order to the trumpet plagues, but global — which is why they are called the "last" plagues, implying seven "first" plagues precede them.

### Q&A — Prophecy Revives the Dry Bones

- **Ezek. 37** — Israel's dry bones, stripped of skin, are revived only when Ezekiel prophesies to them; prophecy is what wakes God's people.
- **Micah 3** — The false teachers of prophecy "tear the skin off" the bones and remove the people's life — which is why God's people lack the passion for the truth and for getting ready for the last-day events.
