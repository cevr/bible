## Ellen White's Prophetic Charts — Week 9

### The Light From Behind

> The light is truth, which an angel said was the Midnight Cry. Those who denied the light behind them lost sight of the mark and of Jesus and stumbled off the path.

- **Rev. 14:6, 7** — The first angel's message centered the believers' attention on the Savior and directed the inhabitants of earth to the movement of the Son of God into the heavenly sanctuary. The Cross and Its Shadow 39-41.
- **Hab. 2:1-4** — "Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables." This prophecy is the basis of the prophetic charts; the vision will tarry but will surely speak and not lie. GC 391, 392; EW 14.1.

### The True Midnight Cry — Five Points of Proof

> These are the five points that brought the believers to October 22, 1844; they are the original light, the original faith, the original path.

- **Gen.** — The 6,000 years; the chronology from the beginning of Genesis, calculated as Miller did, is the first point of proof in The True Midnight Cry.
- **The 2520** — The "seven times of the Gentiles," the second point of proof, runs from the pagan rule of Babylon down nearly to our day.
- **Dan. 8:14** — The 2300 days, the most fundamental sanctuary prophecy of Adventism, the third point of proof.
- **Dan. 9:24-27** — The 70 weeks, the fourth point, reaching all the way to Jesus.
- **Heb.** — The types: the feasts, symbols, and examples of the sanctuary that teach us to understand the heavenly sanctuary; the fifth point of proof.

### The Great Disappointment Was Not a Failure of the Prophetic Periods

> God would not permit the great Advent movement to end in darkness and disappointment, nor leave His word involved in doubt and uncertainty.

- **Rev. 10** — The little book is sweet in the mouth but bitter in the belly; the bitter disappointment of October 22, 1844. CIHS 6, 7.
- **Rev. 11:15-18** — The Judgment of the Dead is connected with the sounding of the seventh trumpet, which began October 12.
- **Dan. 8:14** — The mistake was not in the reckoning of the prophetic periods but in the events expected; the sanctuary to be cleansed was the heavenly sanctuary, not the earth. GC 410.3; 10MR 269.1.

### The Heavenly Sanctuary Revealed

> Hiram Edson, stopped midway through a field, saw distinctly that the High Priest had entered the most holy place for the first time, not coming out to the earth.

- **Rev. 10** — Edson's mind was directed to the tenth chapter of Revelation, where he saw the vision had spoken and did not lie; the prophecy was fulfilled and the Judgment of the Dead began. CIHS 9.3.
- **Heb.** — The epistle to the Hebrews taught the antitypical fulfillment of the types and shadows; the believers, drawing near their Lord, were directed to the heavenly sanctuary. The Cross and Its Shadow 39-41.

### The Sanctuary, the Sabbath, and the Spirit of Prophecy United

> When light from the antitypical sanctuary service came to God's people, He gave them the Spirit of Prophecy again to reveal Christ's ministry in heaven.

- **Ex. 20:8-11** — Elder Bates urged the seventh-day Sabbath as the true Sabbath; the fourth commandment is the longest, defining the Living God who created all things. CET 85, 86.
- **Rev. 11:19** — In vision the temple of God was opened in heaven and the ark shown, with the fourth commandment in the very center of the ten precepts encircled by a soft halo of light. CET 85, 86.
- **Gen. 2:2, 3** — When the foundations of the earth were laid, the foundation of the Sabbath was also laid; had the true Sabbath been kept there would never have been an infidel or atheist. CET 85, 86.

### The Coming Image and Mark of the Beast

> The beast of Revelation 13 changed God's law regarding time, transferring the Sabbath from the seventh day to the first; an idolatrous sabbath will be set up.

- **Rev. 13:15** — Power is given to the image of the beast to speak and to cause that as many as would not worship the image should be killed.
- **Rev. 13:17** — No man may buy or sell save he that has the mark, or the name of the beast, or the number of his name; refusing the false sabbath bars buying and selling, so we should begin now to grow our own food.
- **Dan. 3** — Daniel's three friends faced an image set up to be worshiped, an idol like that of Revelation 13; God saved them as He will save the obedient.

### The Need for Another Prophetic Chart

> On returning to Brother Nichols', the Lord gave Ellen White a vision showing the truth must be made plain upon tables; it would cause many to decide for the truth by the three angels' messages.

- **Hab. 2:1-4** — As early as 1842 this prophecy had moved Charles Fitch to prepare a prophetic chart illustrating the visions of Daniel and Revelation — the 1843 chart, carrying the first two angels' messages. GC 391, 392; 4SP 241.2.
- **Hab. 2:2** — "Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it"; the publication of the chart was itself a fulfillment of this prophecy, and those who deny it leave the original faith. ARSH Dec. 1850, 13.6.

### The 1843 Chart and the Tarrying Time

> The hand of the Lord was removed from the figures and the mistake explained: the prophetic periods reached to 1844, not 1843.

- **Hab. 2:3** — "Though it tarry, wait for it"; the believers had overlooked the tarrying of the vision, and 1843 had to be the first published time so that the vision should tarry. EW 236.
- **Dan. 8:14** — B.C. 457 is the year from which to date the 2300 days; the 1843 date was necessary to create the tarrying time before the Midnight Cry aroused the virgin band. ARSH Dec. 1850, 13.5.

### The Truth Stands Forever as Truth

> When the power of God testifies as to what is truth, that truth is to stand forever; no after suppositions contrary to the light God has given are to be entertained.

- **Hab. 2:3** — "At the end it shall speak, and not lie"; if the chart is a fulfillment of this prophecy then what is on it is truth, and we must heed it. CIHS 19.1.

### The 1850 Chart — The Third Angel's Message Made Plain

> "God shewed me the necessity of getting out a chart. I saw it was needed." The vision was given October 23, 1850, and executed within a month in Boston. 15MR 210.3; James White letter to Bro. & Sis. Loveland.

- **Rev. 14:6-12** — The three angels' messages: the first two made plain on the 1843 chart and the third made plain on the 1850 chart, given by vision from God. 1EGWLM 242.4, 242.5.
- **Rev. 13:11-18** — In vision the two-horned beast had a dragon's mouth and its power in its head; this image of the papacy appears on the 1850 chart. 1EGWLM 244.
- **Rev. 17** — The mother of harlots was shown separate and distinct from her daughters; she has had her day, and her daughters, the Protestant sects, will exercise the power once manifested by the mother. 1EGWLM 244.

### The Charts Were Ordered by God — Touch Them Not

> "Means had been wasted in getting out charts" with uncouth, disgusting images; but God was in the publishment of the chart by Brother Nichols, and there was a prophecy of this chart in the Bible.

- **Hab. 2:2** — The truth should be made plain upon tables; the earth and its fullness are the Lord's, and necessary means should not be spared to make it plain. The 1843 and 1850 charts are the only two that fulfill Habakkuk 2. 1EGWLM 243.2.
- **Ezek. 37:1-10** — The valley of dry bones: prophesy upon these bones; the charts prophesied to God's people and the bones came together, and the prophesying to the four winds brings the sealing and a shaking that sifts the true from the false.
- **Rev. 7** — The four winds of Ezekiel's prophecy are tied to the sealing of Revelation 7; the breath enters the slain and they stand up an exceeding great army.

### Do Not Make the Spirit of Prophecy of None Effect

> "The very last deception of Satan will be to make of none effect the testimony of the Spirit of God" — done by those who claim to believe the prophet yet disregard her visions.

- **Rev. 12:17** — The dragon makes war with the remnant who keep the commandments of God and have the testimony of Jesus; to deny the chart is to make that testimony of none effect.
- **Rev. 19:10** — "The testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy"; the remnant has both the commandments and the Spirit of Prophecy, and must not make them of none effect.

### Question — Where Was Jesus Before 1844?

> The 144,000 follow the Lamb whithersoever He goeth; we should be able to follow Him into the holy place where He ministered from 1840 to 1844.

- **Rev. 14:4** — The 144,000 follow the Lamb wherever He goes; therefore we can follow Him into the holy place before He entered the most holy.
- **Rev. 8:2-6** — The angel stands at the golden altar in the holy place with the censer of incense; the seven trumpets show where and when Christ ministered, exactly like the yearly tabernacle service, the courtyard being the earth and the holy place in heaven.

### Question — The Eastern Question and Armageddon

> "The last plagues, the preparing of the way of the kings of the East, and the gathering of these kings to the world-battle of Armageddon, is the final meaning and the only culmination of the Eastern Question." A. T. Jones, Medical Missionary, Aug. 21, 1906.

- **Rev. 16** — The Eastern question — the involvement of the powers of the East and the Ottoman Empire (the woes), not Islam as a religion — culminates in the seven last plagues, the way of the kings of the East, and the battle of Armageddon; this is part of the third angel's message and is on Ellen White's chart.
