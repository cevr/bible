## The Shaking is Here — Week 11

### The Shaking and the Falling Away

> This bonus study moves from the opening of the judgment to its closing, and begins the conversation on the judgment of the living. The shaking is the start of that work.

- **2 Thess. 2:1-3** — Before the day of Christ there must come a shaking and a falling away first; let no man deceive you. The remnant are not prepared for what is coming; the cry is "Get ready, get ready, get ready," for the fierce anger of the Lord is soon to come, His wrath poured out unmixed with mercy. EW 119.1
- **Rev. 16:1** — God's seven angels pour out the vials of His wrath upon the earth — the fierce anger unmixed with mercy, the close of probation.

### The Straight Testimony to Laodicea

> The shaking is caused by the straight testimony called forth by the counsel of the True Witness.

- **Rev. 3:17** — Laodicea says "I am rich and increased with goods," not knowing she is wretched, miserable, poor, blind, and naked. CET 176.1
- The shaking results when some will not bear the straight testimony but rise up against it; the True Witness is Jesus, who calls it forth. CET 176.1
- The solemn testimony has been lightly esteemed, if not entirely disregarded; it must work deep repentance, and all who receive it will obey and be purified. CET 176.2

### The Harvest, the Wheat, and the Tares

- **Matt. 13:28-30** — Let both grow together until the harvest; the tares are bound and burned, the wheat gathered into the barn. The tares look like wheat, not like obvious weeds.
- **4T 306.3** — God's people trace down the roll of fast-fulfilling prophecy and purify their souls by obeying the truth, that they not be found without the wedding garment.
- **Rev. 16:15** — "Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments, lest he walk naked." Watching requires clean eyes; clothing requires the garment.

### The Mighty Shaking Has Commenced

> The mighty shaking has commenced and will go on. CET 107.3

- All will be shaken out who are not willing to take a bold, unyielding stand for the truth; it must be a freewill offering. CET 107.3
- **Hab. 2:2** — Write the vision and make it plain; the truth made plain. The lightnings of Sinai will not move those unmoved by the plain truths of the word.

### The Fourth Angel and the Light Despised

> There is to be in the churches a wonderful manifestation of the power of God, but it will not move those who have not humbled themselves. Mar 219.2

- **Rev. 18:1** — Another angel comes down having great power, and the earth is lightened with his glory — the straight truth from the True Witness given to Laodicea.
- In their blindness they see the light as something dangerous that arouses their fears, and brace themselves to resist it, opposing the work. Mar 219.2
- **TM 112.1** — When the shaking comes by the introduction of false theories, surface readers anchored nowhere are like shifting sand; Daniel and Revelation must be studied, as well as the other prophecies of the Old and New Testaments.

### Refusing the Light: The Babylon Parallel

- **DAR 612.2 (Uriah Smith)** — When the churches refused the second-advent message, loss of spirituality and a moral fall followed; rejecting truth forges the shackles of error. The same can happen in our church.
- **Way Marks and High Heaps p. 64** — When the message was first introduced in 1843, the churches treated it with scorn; some looked favorably until it was presented in clear scriptural light, then withdrew and declared it the doctrine of the devil.

### Ezekiel's Vision: Prophesying to the Dry Bones

- **Ezek. 37:1-3** — Ezekiel is set in a valley full of very dry bones and asked, "Son of man, can these bones live?"
- **Ezek. 37:4-7** — He is commanded to prophesy to the bones; as he prophesies there is a noise and a shaking, and the bones come together. The preaching of prophecy to Laodicea causes the shaking.
- **Ezek. 37:8-10** — He prophesies to the wind, and breath from the four winds enters the slain; they live and stand up an exceeding great army.
- **Rev. 14:6, 7** — Prophesying that gives the bones flesh, skin, and the knowledge that He is God echoes the first angel's message.

### The Latter Rain and the Harvest

- **Deut. 11:14** — God gives the first rain and the latter rain, that you may gather your corn, wine, and oil.
- **Jer. 5:24** — God gives both the former and latter rain in its season, reserving the appointed weeks of the harvest; the latter rain prepares for the harvest.
- **Joel 2:23** — Rejoice, children of Zion, for He gives the former rain and the latter rain in the first month — a prophecy for right now.
- **Joel 2:1-4** — Blow the trumpet in Zion; the day of the Lord is at hand, a day of darkness and gloominess.
- **Rev. 16:14, 15** — The spirits of devils gather the kings to the battle of the great day of God Almighty; again the warning about the garments.

### The Rain Is Doctrine — and the Foundation

> The rain is doctrine, the true prophecies of the end of the world coming from the True Witness.

- **Deut. 32:2** — "My doctrine shall drop as the rain, my speech shall distil as the dew" — the latter rain is the outpouring of true prophetic truth.
- **1 Tim. 4:1** — In the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits and doctrines of devils; spiritualizing the scripture is spiritualism.
- **Mar 28.3** — In the absence of persecution some appear sound, but in the crisis, when the law of God is made void, the church is sifted and many give heed to seducing spirits and doctrines of devils.
- **4T 89.2** — The refining process proves some to be dross; the shaking of God blows away multitudes like dry leaves, for they are not steadfast with God.
- **Mar 219.4** — There must be no neglect of the former rain; only those living up to the light they have will recognize the manifestation of the Holy Spirit in the latter rain.

### Everything That Can Be Shaken

- **Rev. 6:13** — The stars fall as a fig tree casts her untimely figs when shaken of a mighty wind — the wind aids the shaking.
- **Matt. 11:7** — "What went ye out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken with the wind?" The wind sifts out those who will not aid the fourth angel's message.
- **Heb. 12:27** — "Yet once more" signifies the removing of those things that are shaken, that the things which cannot be shaken may remain.

### The Image of the Beast (Q&A — The 1850 Chart)

> Q&A on the 1850 chart: how can the beast's number be 666 if the chart places that name on the lamb-like-horned beast?

- **Rev. 13:11** — A beast comes up out of the earth with two horns like a lamb but speaks as a dragon — America, exercising all the power of the first beast.
- **Rev. 13:14** — This beast deceives those on the earth and bids them make an image to the first beast which had the deadly wound. An image looks much like the original.
- **Rev. 17:5** — Upon her forehead is written "Mystery, Babylon the Great, the Mother of Harlots"; the lamb-horned beast (Protestant America) makes an image that bears the same number 666 because it acts exactly like the mother.
