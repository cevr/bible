## Ten-Week Judgment Day Bible Study — Week 1

> An introduction to the Three Angels' Messages of Revelation 14 — the "Judgment Day" message that began as a movement in the 1840s and is the final warning to go to all the world. This study lays the foundation: how important these messages are, why they must be given in their order, and why we must go back to the experience of those who first gave them.

### Contending for the Faith

- **Jude 1:3** — We are called to contend earnestly for the faith once delivered to the saints; these precious truths were given when the message first began, and we must return to them.

### The First Angel — The Hour of His Judgment Is Come

- **Rev. 14:6** — Another angel flies in the midst of heaven with the everlasting gospel, to be preached to every nation, kindred, tongue, and people; the message is unchanging good news for all the world.
- **Rev. 14:7** — Said with a loud voice: fear God and give glory to Him, for the hour of His judgment is come, and worship the Creator of heaven, earth, sea, and fountains of waters. This is Judgment Day — we must know when this hour opens and closes.

### The Second Angel — Babylon Is Fallen

- **Rev. 14:8** — Another angel announces that Babylon is fallen because she made all nations drink the wine of the wrath of her fornication; we must learn when this fall took place and what this wine is.

### The Third Angel — The Mark of the Beast

- **Rev. 14:9, 10** — A loud voice warns that whoever worships the beast and his image and receives his mark will drink the wine of the wrath of God, poured out without mixture into the cup of His indignation.
- **Rev. 14:11** — The smoke of their torment ascends forever; the worshipers of the beast and those who receive the mark of his name have no rest day nor night. We do not want to be in this group.
- **Rev. 13:17** — The third message reaches back one chapter: those without the mark of the beast will not be able to buy or sell — a warning given ahead so we can prepare.

### The Patient Saints and the 144,000

- **Rev. 14:12** — Here is the patience of the saints, who keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus; this is the character these messages produce.
- **Rev. 14:1** — The chapter opens with the 144,000 — a spotless people with no guile whose message to the world is these very Three Angels' Messages; to be part of this group we must understand the message.

### These Messages Are Essential to Salvation

> "We have a most important work to do — the work of proclaiming the third angel's message... All should understand the truths contained in the three messages; for they are essential to salvation." CW 175.3

- The Three Angels' Messages are not optional prophecy study; they are essential to salvation, so all — me, you, and the whole world — must understand them fully and be able to present them. CW 175.3
- The solemn messages given in their order in the Revelation are to occupy the first place in the minds of God's people; nothing else is to be allowed to engross our attention. 8T 302.1

### Given in Their Order — A Perfect Chain of Truth

> "Many who embraced the third message had not had an experience in the two former messages." EW 256.2

- Satan sought to overthrow those who skipped the first two messages, but the third angel was pointing them to the most holy place, and those with experience in the past messages pointed the way into the heavenly sanctuary; many saw the perfect chain of truth and gladly received them in their order. EW 256.2
- These messages are represented as an anchor to the people of God; those who understand and receive them will be kept from being swept away by the many delusions of Satan. EW 256.2
- A true witness must obtain an experience in the first, second, and third angels' messages; the angels symbolize the people of God, enlightened by the Spirit and sanctified through the truth, who proclaim the three messages in their order. LS 429.1
- Sanctification comes through the truth — not through self-effort or flogging ourselves, but through studying and obeying the word. LS 429.1

### The Last Message of Warning and Mercy

- **Rev. 18:1** — A fourth angel comes down from heaven with great power, and the earth is lightened with his glory; this last message of warning and mercy involves you and me. COL 79.3
- **Rev. 18:2-4** — Babylon the great is fallen and become the habitation of devils; the call "Come out of her, my people" warns us not to partake of her sins or receive her plagues — adding detail to the first three messages.
- The parable of the mustard seed reaches its triumphant fulfillment in this last generation: the last message of warning and mercy goes to every nation, kindred, and tongue (Rev. 14:6-14) to take out of them a people for His name (Acts 15:14; Rev. 18:1), and the earth is lightened with His glory. COL 79.3
- The last message of warning and mercy is to lighten the whole earth and reach all classes — rich and poor, high and low — that Christ's house may be filled. COL 228.3

### Receive the Experience From Those Who First Gave It

> "As Jesus was crucified, so I saw that these messages have been crucified." 1SG 75

- Those who had no experience in the first and second angels' messages must receive them from those who had the experience, following down through the messages; those who embrace only part of the truths connected with the third message must gladly embrace the first, second, and third as God gave them, or have no part nor lot in the matter. 1SG 75
- **Jer. 6:16** — Stand in the ways, ask for the old paths, and walk therein; but Israel said, "We will not walk therein." We must not refuse the old paths in favor of an "updated" or "clearer" gospel of our own devising.

### The Revelation of Jesus Christ — Prophecy Is a Verb

- **Rev. 1:1** — The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave to show His servants things which must shortly come to pass — that is, prophecy.
- **Rev. 1:2** — John bore record of the word of God and the testimony of Jesus Christ; if we want a relationship with God we must hear His testimony, found in the book of Revelation.
- **Rev. 1:3** — Blessed are those who read and hear the words of this prophecy and keep them, for the time is at hand; true prophecy is an action that must change our character and our conduct.

### Probation, the Time of Trouble, and Preparation

- **Dan. 12:1** — When Michael (Jesus) stands up from the most holy place, probation closes; a time of trouble such as never was begins, yet His people — everyone found written in the book — shall be delivered.
- The events connected with the close of probation and the work of preparation for the time of trouble are clearly presented, yet multitudes understand them no more than if they had never been revealed; Satan watches to catch away every saving impression, and the time of trouble will find them unready. _(Darkness Before Dawn, p. 36-38)_
- The time of trouble such as never was is soon to open upon us, and we shall need an experience which we do not now possess and which many are too indolent to obtain; the most vivid presentation cannot reach the magnitude of the ordeal, and every soul must stand for himself before God. GC 622
- **Ezek. 14:20** — Though Noah, Daniel, and Job were in the land, they would deliver only their own souls by their righteousness — a righteousness accounted to them through obedience, as Noah obediently prepared the ark for a coming event he was warned of. GC 622

### Led Into All Truth by the Spirit

- **John 16:13** — When the Spirit of truth is come He will guide us into all truth and show us things to come — but only as long as we remain humble and teachable. TM 112.2
- There is need of a much closer study of the word of God; especially should Daniel and the Revelation have attention as never before, calling attention to what the prophets and apostles wrote under inspiration. TM 112.2
- When Daniel and Revelation are better understood, believers will have an entirely different religious experience and be given glimpses of the open gates of heaven that impress the character we must develop to receive the reward of the pure in heart. TM 114.3
- **Rev. 12:6** — A sample of true Bible study: the woman who fled into the wilderness for 1,260 days — what the woman, the wilderness, and the time periods mean — must be something we can search out and explain for ourselves from the Bible.

### The Sealed Book to Be Opened

- **Dan. 12:4** — Daniel was told to shut up the words and seal the book until the time of the end, when many shall run to and fro and knowledge shall be increased; the question for the next study is what event unsealed this book at the time of the end so we could understand these prophecies and the first angel's "hour of his judgment is come."
