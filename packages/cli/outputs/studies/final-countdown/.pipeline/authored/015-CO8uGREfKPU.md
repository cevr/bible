## Are You the 1 in 20? — Study 14 (The Final Countdown)

### The Shaking and the Winnowing Floor

> "We are in the shaking time, the time when everything that can be shaken will be shaken." The next event on the timeline is a mighty shaking among God's people that decides who is ready for the events to come.

- **Matt. 3:12** — Christ holds the fan, thoroughly purges His threshing floor, gathers the wheat into the garner, and burns the chaff; the winnowing of grain is itself a shaking process.
- **6T 331.2** — "If we only knew what is before us we would not be so dilatory in the work of the Lord. We are in the shaking time, the time when everything that can be shaken will be shaken." The shaking has begun, though the final shaking comes in the crisis. _Testimonies vol. 6, 331.2_
- **3SM 385.3** — The great issue so near at hand will weed out those whom God has not appointed, leaving a pure, true, sanctified ministry prepared for the latter rain. _Selected Messages vol. 3, 385.3_
- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — The great issue is the mark of the beast and the no-buy, no-sell crisis, the final division of God's people from the world. An initial weeding out comes first, then rain and harvest, then a final shaking.

### The Cause: A Straight Testimony to Laodicea

- **EW 270.2** — The shaking is caused by the straight testimony called forth by the counsel of the True Witness to the Laodiceans; some will not bear it and rise up against it, and this is what causes the shaking among God's people. _Early Writings 270.2_
- **Rev. 3:14-17** — The message of the True Witness to Laodicea: lukewarm, thinking themselves rich and in need of nothing, yet wretched, miserable, poor, blind, and naked — blind to prophecy and ignorant of the final events.
- **6T 331.2** — The Lord will not excuse those who know the truth if they do not in word and deed obey His commands; if we make no effort to win souls we are held responsible for the work we might have done. The sealing is a settling into the truth, which cannot happen without first knowing the truth. _Testimonies vol. 6, 331.2_

### False Theories and a Spurious Loud Cry

- **TM 112.1** — When the shaking comes by the introduction of false theories, surface readers anchored nowhere slide like shifting sand into any position; Daniel and Revelation must be studied, along with the other prophecies of the Old and New Testaments. _Testimonies to Ministers 112.1_
- **Deut. 32:1, 2** — "My doctrine shall drop as the rain" — the latter rain is the true doctrine returning to the people; on the wrong foundation it looks like a storm of confusion, on the right foundation it ripens the fruit.
- **3SM 410.1** — God is raising up a class to give the loud cry; Satan's object is to get up new theories and a spurious loud cry that the real message may not have its effect when it comes — itself one of the greatest evidences the true loud cry will soon be heard. _Selected Messages vol. 3, 410.1_

### Serving Mammon: Not One in Twenty

> "It is a solemn statement that I make to the church, that not one in twenty are prepared to close their earthly history." Are you the one in twenty?

- **ChS 41.1** — Not one in twenty whose names are registered upon the church books are prepared to close their earthly history; they are professedly serving God but more earnestly serving mammon, a half-and-half work that constantly denies Christ. A worker can serve mammon by withholding truth to keep funding, friends, or membership. _Christian Service 41.1_
- **GC 608.2** — As the storm approaches, a large class who professed the third angel's message but were never sanctified by obedience abandon their position and join the opposition; gifted, pleasing evangelists employ their powers to deceive, become the most bitter enemies of their former brethren, and stir up the rulers when Sabbathkeepers are brought before the courts. _Great Controversy 608.2_

### Separation, Unity, and Departing from the Faith

- **6T 400.3** — As trials thicken, both separation and unity appear in the ranks; some now ready to take up weapons of warfare will prove they did not build on the rock, and those who had great light but did not improve it go out from us, not having received the love of the truth, departing from the faith. Departing the faith means leaving the truth — bright lights ceasing to emit light. _Testimonies vol. 6, 400.3_
- **CET 107.3** — The mighty shaking has commenced and will go on; all will be shaken out who are not willing to take a bold, unyielding stand for the truth and to sacrifice for God — a sacrifice that must be a freewill offering, for the plain truths of God's word, not thunders and lightnings of Sinai, are what move the honest. _Christian Experience and Teachings 107.3_

### The Latter Rain and the Valley of Dry Bones

- **CET 177.2** — Those clothed with the armor speak forth truth with great power; the bound are freed, the honest lay hold of truth dearer than life — and the angel explains the great change is the latter rain, the refreshing from the presence of the Lord, the loud cry of the third angel. _Christian Experience and Teachings 177.2_
- **Ezek. 37:1-10** — The valley is full of very dry bones (Israel, blind and dead); the prophet is commanded to prophesy, and as he prophesies there is a noise and a shaking and the bones come together — yet there is no breath until he prophesies to the four winds and the breath enters and they stand, an exceeding great army.
- **Rev. 7:1-3** — Four angels hold the four winds until the servants of God are sealed in their foreheads; the four winds of Ezekiel reappear here, tied directly to the sealing of the 144,000 — the sealing being a settling into the truth.

### The Prophets Who Slay the Bones

- **Micah 3:1-5** — The heads of Jacob and princes of Israel, who should know judgment, instead pluck the skin off God's people and break their bones; these are the prophets who make the people err, biting with their teeth and crying "Peace" — false teachers who left the people listless and unaware of the final events.

### Built Upon the Rock — What Cannot Be Shaken

- **Mar 219.4** — There must be no neglect of the former rain; only those living up to the light they have receive greater light, and unless we daily advance in the active Christian virtues we will not recognize the latter rain even as it falls all around us. _Maranatha 219.4_
- **Matt. 7:24-27** — The wise man who hears and does builds on the rock; when the rain, the floods, and the winds beat on the house it stands, but the house of the hearer-who-does-not falls, and great is its fall.
- **Heb. 12:25-27** — "Yet once more" signifies the removing of the things that are shaken, that the things which cannot be shaken may remain; to be shaken is to be removed, so we must be so settled into the truth we cannot be moved.
- **Amos 9:9** — God will sift the house of Israel among all nations as corn is sifted in a sieve, yet not the least grain shall fall to the earth — the striving will be shaken but not removed.
- **Isa. 30:28** — His breath, like an overflowing stream, reaches to the midst of the neck to sift the nations; the unpopular truth will rise to the neck, and only the firm will not be swept away and drowned.

### Q&A: Time, the Latter Rain, and the 1260 Years

- **Rev. 10:6** — "Time no longer" is prophetic time, suspended since October 22, 1844, during the day of atonement; therefore no date can be set for Christ's coming, and dates like 2027 or 2030 break the laws of prophecy. Prophetic time resumes at the close of probation.
- **Mar 219.2** — There is to be a wonderful manifestation of God's power, but it will not move those who have not humbled themselves; in their blindness they think it dangerous, brace to resist it, and persist in saying "I am rich, and increased with goods, and have need of nothing" — bracing against the latter rain. _Maranatha 219.2_
- **Rev. 12:6** — The woman in the wilderness 1,260 days; on the day-for-a-year principle this is 1,260 years, the period of papal supremacy.
- **Ezek. 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year" — the prophetic key that converts the 1,260 days into 1,260 years.
- **Rev. 13:5** — The beast continues forty-two months — the same 1,260 days/years of the antichrist (papal) power, fulfilled from A.D. 538 to 1798 when it received the deadly wound.
