## The True Midnight Cry — Week 7

_Series: Judgment Day Ten-Week Study. The Midnight Cry was the message "Behold, the Bridegroom cometh," given at the prophetic midnight of the Second Advent movement. To deny this light from behind is to lose sight of Jesus and fall from the path._

### The Second Angel and the Midnight Cry

> "In connection with the message, the midnight cry was given." The fall of Babylon and the Midnight Cry cannot be separated — the second angel's message carries the cry that gives it added power.

- **Rev. 3:4** — Babylon's churches had "a few names even in Sardis" who had not defiled their garments; it was time to call them out of the corrupt mass. JUBST June 19, 1845, p.117.2 (S.S. Snow and B. Matthias).
- **Rev. 18:2** — In 1843 God sent forth the cry, "Babylon is fallen, and is become the habitation of devils, and the hold of every foul spirit, and a cage of every unclean and hateful bird. Come out of her, my people." First given in 1843, fulfilled in the summer of 1844.
- **Rev. 14:8** — The second angel "cried, Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city"; first proclaimed in the summer of 1844, and many left the fallen churches. CET 50, 51.

### The First Disappointment — Spring of 1844

> Miller and his associates first looked to the spring of 1844, but the prophecy pointed to the autumn. Understanding Bible reckoning — that the Jewish year begins in the spring with the month Abib — resolves the apparent discrepancy.

- **Dan. 8:14** — The 2300 days terminated in the year 1844; the misapprehension that put the end in the spring brought disappointment, but did not weaken the argument. CIHS 86.2.
- **Ex. 12:1-6** — Scripture testimony was clear that every year commenced with the new moon in the spring, just fourteen days before the yearly Passover. The Autobiography of Elder Joseph Bates, p.293.4.
- **Ex. 13:3-4** — The same testimony fixed the 17th day of April, 1844, Roman time, as the close of the year 1843, Bible time; so the first disappointment of "1843" actually fell in the spring of 1844. The Autobiography of Elder Joseph Bates, p.293.4.

### The Tarrying Time

> Down to April 1844 the churches were open to the Advent message; after the spring disappointment, opposition arose, and the believers entered the tarrying time — a season of doubt prophesied to precede the cry.

- **Matt. 25:5** — "While the bridegroom tarried, they all slumbered and slept." The disappointment threw the waiting ones into doubt and uncertainty; the bridegroom "tarried" past the first point of expectation. DAR 593.2.
- **Hab. 2:1-4** — "Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables... though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry." The 1843 chart was the writing of this vision, and the tarrying time was foretold in it.
- **Christ in His Sanctuary p.121, 122** — Interwoven with the prophecies was instruction "specially adapted to their state of uncertainty," encouraging them to wait patiently; what was dark to their understanding would in due time be made plain.

### The Cry Made at Midnight

> "At midnight there was a cry made." Just such a cry as the parable foretold was suddenly and involuntarily raised, midway between the spring of 1844 and the autumn to which the 2300 days really extended.

- **Matt. 25:6** — "And at midnight there was a cry made, Behold, the bridegroom cometh; go ye out to meet him." The very phraseology was adopted involuntarily, in fulfillment of prophecy. DAR 593.2.
- **Matt. 25:7** — "Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps" — exactly what the believers did during the first disappointment and the tarrying time, making their light shine brighter.
- **Matt. 25:9-13** — The wise sent the foolish to "buy for yourselves"; the message must be bought anew. "Watch therefore, for ye know neither the day nor the hour."
- **Ezek. 4:6** — The day-for-a-year principle: the great prophetic period of 2300 days meant 2300 years. The cause of the sudden arousing was the discovery that this period extended to the autumn, not the spring, of 1844. DAR 593.2.

### The Light From Behind — Ellen White's First Vision

> "Near the close of the second angel's message I saw a great light from heaven." The Midnight Cry was a bright light set up behind the Advent people, lighting their feet so they would not stumble.

- **Early Writings p.237, 238** — As the churches refused the first angel's message they fell from God's favor; but the oppressed who accepted "Babylon is fallen" heard the voices of angels crying, "Behold, the Bridegroom cometh; go ye out to meet Him."
- **Christian Experience and Teachings p.57** — "It was not long after the passing of the time, in 1844, that my first vision was given me," while five women knelt in prayer at Portland.
- **Early Writings p.14.1** — On a straight and narrow path "cast up high above the world" the Advent people traveled to the city, with a bright light set behind them at the beginning of the path, which an angel said was the Midnight Cry; those who denied that light went out into perfect darkness and fell from the path.

### Snow's Midnight Cry at the Exeter Camp Meeting

> At the Exeter, New Hampshire camp meeting in the summer of 1844, S. S. Snow gave the path of his Midnight Cry message; the power of God swept the camp.

- **The Great Second Advent Movement p.522, 523** — On Sunday forenoon, while Joseph Bates preached, a man rode in at full speed, sat by the family of Elder John Couch, and whispered the cause of the disappointment and "the midnight cry that was now due."
- **The Great Second Advent Movement p.522, 523** — Sister Couch arose and said, "here is a man who has light on the midnight cry"; Bates replied, "then let him come up here on the platform, and give it to the people."
- **The Great Second Advent Movement p.522, 523** — The minister who walked into the stand was S. S. Snow; "as he uttered those words the mighty power of God swept over that camp." The Midnight Cry paper, edited by Brother Southard, "swept over the land with the velocity of a tornado... God was in it."
- **The Great Second Advent Movement p.522, 523** — Snow questioned the people: a day in prophecy is a year, a night is six months, midnight is three months, and they had been in the tarrying time just three months — "Then it is just the midnight now, and I am here with the midnight cry."
- **Dan. 8:14** — Snow showed that the decree went forth in the fall of 457 B.C., so the 2300 days would terminate Oct. 22, 1844, "on the tenth day of the seventh month," not in the spring as previously supposed. The Great Second Advent Movement p.522, 523.

### The 1843 Chart and the Original Faith

> The publication of the 1843 chart was itself a fulfillment of Habakkuk's "write the vision," showing the tarrying time and dating the 2300 days from 457 B.C.

- **Hab. 2:2, 3** — The chart published by J. V. Himes was "a fulfillment of Hab. 2"; to deny that the chart was a subject of prophecy is to leave the original faith, from which it follows that B.C. 457 was the year to date the 2300 days. Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, December 1850, p.13.6.

### Q&A — Fleeing to the Mountains

> Asked whether the move to the mountains is literal: by Miller's rules, if a thing does not break natural law, take it literally — and God's people have always found refuge in the mountains.

- **Rev. 13:16, 17** — "No man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark... of the beast." Refusing the mark and being unable to buy or sell breaks no natural law, so it is literal.
- **Isa. 33:16** — "He shall dwell on high: his place of defense shall be the munitions of rocks... bread shall be given him; his waters shall be sure." Literal mountains, literal bread and water, secure during the time of trouble.
- **Dan. 12:1** — "At that time shall Michael stand up... and there shall be a time of trouble such as never was"; the people written in the book are delivered and provided for after the close of probation.
- **Country Living (Testimonies for the Church)** — "The time is not far distant when, like the early disciples, we shall be forced to seek a refuge in desolate and solitary places... it will then be time to leave the large cities... for retired homes in secluded places among the mountains."

### Q&A — The King of the South and End-Time Typology

> By Miller's rules Daniel 11 is a literal prophecy; if the king of the north is literal, the king of the south must be literal too. The Old Testament histories are written for our admonition.

- **Dan. 11:40-45** — Read literally, the king of the south is literal Egypt, abundantly outlined in Uriah Smith's "Daniel and the Revelation." Spiritualizing the prophecy makes it dark; the literal method makes it plain (DAR Preface).
- **Ezek. 38, 39** — "Set thy face against Gog and the land of Magog"; in the latter years the people are gathered against the mountains of Israel, and "in that day there shall be a great shaking in the land of Israel."
- **1 Cor. 10:11** — "Now all these things happened unto them for ensamples... written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come"; the scattering of Israel typifies our time.
- **Luke 21:12** — "They shall lay their hands on you... being brought before kings and rulers for my name's sake" — why we must learn to defend our faith as watchmen.
- **Rev. 1:1** — "The revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him, to show unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass" — God reveals prophecy so we will be ready and not taken as a thief.

### Q&A — The Foundations of 1840 to 1844

> The messages given from 1840 to 1844 are the truth, laid as a foundation that is not to be disturbed.

- **Manuscript Releases vol. 21, p.437.1** — "All the messages given from 1840 to 1844 are to be made forcible now; for there are many who have lost their bearings. The messages are to go to all the churches."
- **General Conference Bulletin April 6, 1903, par. 35** — "Nothing is to be allowed to come in that will disturb the foundation of the faith upon which we have been building ever since the message came in 1842, 1843, and 1844... It is to be as the Rock of Ages." (Compiled in the 1842 chart vote that produced the 1843 chart.)
