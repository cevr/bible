## When Is the Time of the End? — Week 2

### The Three Angels' Messages Are Essential to Salvation

> When the books of Daniel and Revelation are better understood, believers will have an entirely different religious experience. This is the experience the 144,000 need in order to pass through the time of trouble.

- **Rev. 14** — The Three Angels' messages are the final warning given to the world; the whole study walks through this chapter and its messages.
- **Rev. 13:17** — In the coming crisis no man may buy or sell who lacks the mark or name of the beast; the 144,000 of the next chapter refuse that mark, so we must prepare for the no-buy-no-sell time.

  _EGW supports:_ "When the books of Daniel and Revelation are better understood, believers will have an entirely different religious experience." (TM 114.3) — "We have a most important work to do, the work of proclaiming the third angel's message... All should understand the truths contained in the three messages; for they are essential to salvation." (CW 175.3) — "Those who had no experience in the first and second angels' messages must receive them from those who had an experience... As Jesus was crucified, so I saw that these messages have been crucified... those who embrace but a part of the truths connected with the third message must gladly embrace the first, second and third messages as God has given them... or have no part nor lot in the matter." (1SG 75.1)

### The Book Sealed Until the Time of the End

- **Dan. 12:4** — Daniel is told to shut up the words and seal the book "even to the time of the end," when many shall run to and fro and knowledge shall be increased; the prophecies stay sealed until that time.
- **Dan. 12:5, 6** — Two stand on either bank of the river and ask the man clothed in linen, "How long shall it be to the end of these wonders?"
- **Dan. 12:7** — He swears by Him that liveth forever that it shall be "for a time, times, and an half," after which the scattering of the holy people will be finished; this is the prophetic time-period to be unlocked.
- **Dan. 12:8, 9** — Daniel hears but understands not; he is told the words are "closed up and sealed till the time of the end."
- **Dan. 12:10** — Many shall be purified and made white, but none of the wicked shall understand; "the wise shall understand" is a promise to all who ask in humility.

### A Time, Times, and Half a Time — the 1260 Years

- **Rev. 10:1, 2** — A mighty angel (Jesus) comes down from heaven, His feet as pillars of fire, holding in His hand "a little book open"; where Daniel's book was sealed, here at this point in history it is open.
- **Rev. 12:14** — The woman is given two eagle's wings to fly into the wilderness, nourished "for a time, and times, and half a time" — the same prophetic period.
- **Rev. 12:6** — The woman (the true church) flees into the wilderness and is fed there "a thousand two hundred and threescore days"; threescore is sixty, giving 1260.
- **Num. 14:34** — "Each day for a year" establishes the day-for-a-year principle, making the 1260 days into 1260 years.
- **Ezek. 4:6** — "I have appointed thee each day for a year" confirms the day-for-a-year rule that opens the prophetic periods.
- **Dan. 7:25** — The little-horn power speaks great words against the Most High, wears out the saints, and thinks to change times and laws, given into his hand "until a time and times and the dividing of time" — the papal reign of 538 to 1798, exactly 1260 years.

### 1798 — the Wound and the Unsealing

> After the 1260 years, the deadly wound falls on the papacy and the sealed book begins to be opened.

- **Dan. 7:25** — The 1260 years that began in 538 end in 1798, when Berthier took the pope captive (he died in prison), ending papal supremacy.
- **Rev. 13** — The same wounding of the beast appears here as the deadly wound; the "42 months," 1260 days, and three and a half years are all this one period, 538 to 1798.

  _Historical note:_ February 15, 1798 was also William Miller's 16th birthday and the year he obtained the Cruden's Concordance that would unlock the prophecies. "We were interested in seeing his old family Bible, which cost $18.50, and his quarto copy of Cruden's Concordance, which was originally purchased, in 1798, for $8. These two books were almost the only ones he looked at while preparing his lectures." (Memoirs of William Miller, p. 246.2)

### Let the Bible Be Its Own Interpreter — William Miller's Method

> God used the unsealing of the book through a man willing to let the Bible interpret itself.

- **Gen. (study begun in Genesis)** — Miller began with Genesis and read verse by verse, comparing scripture with scripture by aid of the marginal references and the concordance, until each obscure text "ceased to be a difficulty." (GC 320; Memoirs of William Miller, p. 69)
- **Rev. 22:18, 19** — Because one jot or tittle changed could cost one's name in the book of life, the study uses the King James (Textus Receptus) so that every word counts, especially in prophecy.

  _EGW / method notes:_ God led Miller to compare scripture with history and found all fulfilled prophecy "had been fulfilled literally"; the Bible is "a system of revealed truths, so clearly and simply given that the wayfaring man, though a fool, need not err therein." (Memoirs of William Miller, p. 69, 72, 75) — "God sent His angel to move upon the heart of a farmer who had not believed the Bible, to lead him to search the prophecies... The commencement of the chain of truth was given to him, and he was led on to search for link after link, until he looked with wonder and admiration upon the Word of God." (EW 229.1)

### How God Prepared the Man — Plattsburgh and the Year Without a Summer

- **(Deism abandoned)** — At the Battle of Plattsburgh (Sept. 11, 1814) Miller, a captain with 1500 regulars and about 4000 volunteers, saw 15,000 British defeated and concluded the victory was "the work of a mightier power than man," weakening his deistical principles. (Memoirs of William Miller, p. 48-53)
- **(Israel comparison)** — Miller compared the United States to the children of Israel "before whom God drove out the inhabitants of their land," seeing that God is involved in the affairs of nations, as Babylon, Persia, and Greece all happened just as the Bible foretold. (Memoirs of William Miller, p. 48-53)
- **(1816, the Year Without a Summer)** — The eruption of Mount Tambora dropped world temperatures, bringing hard frost every month and crop failures; unable to farm, Miller turned to his Bible and Concordance, the event God used to set him studying the prophecies.

### The Day-for-a-Year Principle and Methodical Study

- **Num. 14:34** — "Forty days, each day for a year" — Miller saw that prophetic days are extended over as many literal years (Memoirs of William Miller, p. 75.2).
- **Ezek. 4:6** (with Ezek. 4:4-6) — "I have appointed thee each day for a year"; Miller cited Num. 14:34 and Ezek. 4:4-6 together as the basis for the day-for-a-year rule.

  _EGW supports:_ "Observe system in the study of the Scriptures in your families... be sure that the soul is fed with the bread of life... It is impossible to estimate the good results of one hour or even half an hour each day devoted in a cheerful, social manner to the word of God. Make the Bible its own expositor, bringing together all that is said concerning a given subject at different times and under varied circumstances." (Review and Herald) — "Those who are engaged in proclaiming the third angel's message are searching the Scriptures upon the same plan that Father Miller adopted," from "Views of the Prophecies and Prophetic Chronology," giving the simple but important rules for Bible study, of which Rule 1 is "Every word must have its proper bearing on the subject presented in the Bible." (Review and Herald)

### The Three (and Fourth) Angels' Messages as Prophecy

> Read as prophecy: each angel is a symbol for a messenger who gives a message, and each message has a literal historical date of fulfillment.

- **Rev. 14:6-12** — The Three Angels' messages span these verses; read as prophecy that foretells when each message will be given to the world.
- **Rev. 14:6** — "I saw another angel fly in the midst of heaven, having the everlasting gospel to preach unto them that dwell on the earth"; the angel is a symbol for a messenger who proclaims "the hour of his judgment is come" to every nation, kindred, tongue, and people.
- **Rev. 13** — The first beast (rising from the sea, seven heads and ten horns) is papal Rome, ruling 42 months; the second beast represents America and brings the mark of the beast, a literal event God's people must prepare for.
- **Rev. 14 (the 144,000)** — Revelation 14 prophesies this group and the succession of messages; the messages come in order — first, second, third — the first given several years before the second, the second a year before the third, with a larger gap before the fourth angel.

  _Note:_ The study taught that the spiritual reign of Christ, a temporal millennium before the end of the world, and the Jews' return are "not sustained by the word of God," being contrary to Miller's literal rules of interpretation. (Memoirs of William Miller, p. 72)
