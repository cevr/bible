## The thesis (the circuit)

**Jesus gives us PEACE** (_John 14:27_) at the **GATE** — and the gate is
**FAITH** (_John 10:9_; _Rom 5:1-2_, "being justified by faith, we have peace").
That peace IS the abiding of the **HOLY SPIRIT** (the Comforter, _John 14:16-17,
26-27_ — peace and the Comforter are given in the same breath). The Spirit
opens the **WORD** (the Table of Shewbread — _John 16:13_, "he will guide you
into all truth"). The Word, received in **PRAYER** (the Altar of Incense —
_Luke 11:13_, "ask… the Holy Spirit"), draws down yet more of the Spirit. And
the Spirit-filled, Word-fed life breaks out in **WORKS** (the Candlestick —
_Matt 5:14-16_, "ye are the light of the world"). **Faith without works is
dead** (_James 2:26_) — so the circuit must complete.

One sentence: **Peace → Faith (gate) → the Spirit → the Word (table) → Prayer
(incense) → Works (lampstand) — and the light shines back out.**

## The four pieces (Holy Place furniture)

The Holy Place held three pieces of furniture, entered through one door
(_Heb 9:2_; _Exo 40:22-27_). This study reads the DOOR as a fourth station.

- **The Door / Gate = Faith.** Christ is the door (_John 10:9_); entering by
  faith we have peace (_Rom 5:1_). Owns: **peace, faith, justification-entry.**
- **The Table of Shewbread = the Word.** Twelve loaves, bread of His presence
  (_Exo 25:30_; _Lev 24:5-9_); man lives by every word (_Matt 4:4_); the Word
  is bread (_John 6:48-63_). Owns: **the Word, daily feeding, understanding.**
- **The Altar of Incense = Prayer / receiving the Spirit.** Incense = the
  prayers of saints (_Rev 8:3-4_; _Ps 141:2_); ask and the Spirit is given
  (_Luke 11:13_; _Matt 7:7_). Owns: **prayer, the asking, the Spirit received.**
- **The Candlestick / Lampstand = Good Works.** The lamp gives light (_Exo
  25:31-37_); ye are the light of the world (_Matt 5:14-16_); works as light.
  Owns: **works, the light shining out, witness.**

## The relations (the heart of the study — the user's ask)

The study's distinctive question is **what each piece is to the others**:

1. **Peace is the Spirit's gift, received by faith.** Jesus offers peace; the
   peace He gives is His own presence by the Comforter (_John 14:27_ + _14:16_).
2. **The Spirit gives understanding of the Word.** The same Spirit who is our
   peace opens the Word — without Him the Word is a closed table (_John 16:13_;
   _1 Cor 2:10-14_).
3. **Prayer is how the Spirit is asked for and received.** The incense rises;
   "ask and it shall be given" (_Luke 11:13_). Prayer feeds the Word and the
   Word feeds prayer.
4. **The Word is dead without works.** Faith without works is dead (_James
   2:26_); the light must shine (_Matt 5:16_). Works are the Word made visible.

Each piece both RECEIVES from the one before and FEEDS the one after — a
circuit, not a ladder.

## Sourcing rules (Miller's Rule 12 — Bible defines its own figures)

**Bible PRIMARY.** Every symbol is defined first from Scripture — the verse,
the chapter, or cross-canon. The furniture meanings are proven from the Law
(Exodus/Leviticus) and the NT fulfillment (Hebrews, John, James, Matthew).

**Pioneer/EGW SECONDARY only.** The cloud of witnesses (Waggoner, Jones,
Haskell, Stephen Haskell's _Cross and Its Shadow_ on the sanctuary, EGW esp.
_Christ's Object Lessons_, _Steps to Christ_, _Desire of Ages_, _Acts of the
Apostles_) corroborates — never the sole/primary proof. Machine-verify every
refcode against the local DB (`bible egw "REF"` / `bible egw search`) before
shipping. **Never invent refcodes.**
