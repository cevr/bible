# The Day of the LORD — Corpus Across the Prophets (KJV)

Research dossier for the Seven Trumpets study. Every passage below is FULL KJV text pulled
via `bible verse --json`. FTS sweeps run: "day of the LORD", "day of vengeance", "day of
wrath", "all nations", "all the heathen", "inhabitants of the earth", "end of the earth even
unto the other", "great and terrible day". Texts the assignment list missed but the sweeps
surfaced are included and marked **[FTS-surfaced]**: Jer 46:10; Lam 2:22; Zeph 1:7-8;
Isa 26:20-21; Isa 61:2; Joel 3:1-2; Hag 2:6-7; Jer 30:11; Ezek 38:19-23; 39:21; Ps 110:5-6;
Acts 2:19-20; Rev 14:18-20; Rev 19:15.

**Flag legend (after each passage):**

- **(a) Named?** — NAMED = the phrase "day of the LORD" (or LORD's-possessive: "day of the
  LORD'S wrath / anger / sacrifice / vengeance", NT "day of the Lord") appears in the text.
  UNNAMED = the phrase never appears, but the passage carries the day-of-the-LORD signature.
- **(b) Universal-scope markers** — "all nations", "all the heathen", "all the inhabitants
  of the earth", "from one end of the earth even unto the other", "the world", "all flesh",
  "all the earth", "kings of the earth / whole world".
- **(c) Trumpet/vial vocabulary** — trumpet/alarm, fire, blood, smoke, brimstone,
  darkness/darkened lights, earthquake/shaking, Euphrates, winepress/treading, whirlwind,
  towers, sword, hail, cup of wrath.

## I. NAMED "Day of the LORD" Texts

### Isaiah 2:10-21

> 2:10 ¶ Enter into the rock, and hide thee in the dust, for fear of the LORD, and for the glory of his majesty.
> 2:11 The lofty looks of man shall be humbled, and the haughtiness of men shall be bowed down, and the LORD alone shall be exalted in that day.
> 2:12 For the day of the LORD of hosts [shall be] upon every [one that is] proud and lofty, and upon every [one that is] lifted up; and he shall be brought low:
> 2:13 And upon all the cedars of Lebanon, [that are] high and lifted up, and upon all the oaks of Bashan,
> 2:14 And upon all the high mountains, and upon all the hills [that are] lifted up,
> 2:15 And upon every high tower, and upon every fenced wall,
> 2:16 And upon all the ships of Tarshish, and upon all pleasant pictures.
> 2:17 And the loftiness of man shall be bowed down, and the haughtiness of men shall be made low: and the LORD alone shall be exalted in that day.
> 2:18 And the idols he shall utterly abolish.
> 2:19 And they shall go into the holes of the rocks, and into the caves of the earth, for fear of the LORD, and for the glory of his majesty, when he ariseth to shake terribly the earth.
> 2:20 In that day a man shall cast his idols of silver, and his idols of gold, which they made [each one] for himself to worship, to the moles and to the bats;
> 2:21 To go into the clefts of the rocks, and into the tops of the ragged rocks, for fear of the LORD, and for the glory of his majesty, when he ariseth to shake terribly the earth.

- (a) **NAMED** — "the day of the LORD of hosts" (v.12).
- (b) Universal: "every one that is proud", "all the cedars… all the high mountains… every high tower… all the ships of Tarshish" — exhaustive every/all catalogue; no nation named, the target is mankind's loftiness as such.
- (c) **Towers** (v.15 "every high tower… every fenced wall"); **earthquake** ("shake terribly the earth" ×2, vv.19, 21); hide-in-the-rocks = Rev 6:15-16 sixth-seal language; cast away silver and gold (v.20) = Ezek 7:19.

### Isaiah 13:6-13

> 13:6 ¶ Howl ye; for the day of the LORD [is] at hand; it shall come as a destruction from the Almighty.
> 13:7 Therefore shall all hands be faint, and every man's heart shall melt:
> 13:8 And they shall be afraid: pangs and sorrows shall take hold of them; they shall be in pain as a woman that travaileth: they shall be amazed one at another; their faces [shall be as] flames.
> 13:9 Behold, the day of the LORD cometh, cruel both with wrath and fierce anger, to lay the land desolate: and he shall destroy the sinners thereof out of it.
> 13:10 For the stars of heaven and the constellations thereof shall not give their light: the sun shall be darkened in his going forth, and the moon shall not cause her light to shine.
> 13:11 And I will punish the world for [their] evil, and the wicked for their iniquity; and I will cause the arrogancy of the proud to cease, and will lay low the haughtiness of the terrible.
> 13:12 I will make a man more precious than fine gold; even a man than the golden wedge of Ophir.
> 13:13 Therefore I will shake the heavens, and the earth shall remove out of her place, in the wrath of the LORD of hosts, and in the day of his fierce anger.

- (a) **NAMED** — vv.6, 9 (burden of Babylon, ch. 13:1 — yet the scope explodes past Babylon).
- (b) Universal: "I will punish **the world**" (v.11); "all hands… every man's heart" (v.7).
- (c) **Darkened lights** (v.10 sun/moon/stars — the sixth-seal triad); **earthquake/shaking** (v.13 "shake the heavens… earth shall remove out of her place"); travail-pangs (v.8) = 1 Thess 5:3.

### Isaiah 34:1-10

> 34:1 ¶ Come near, ye nations, to hear; and hearken, ye people: let the earth hear, and all that is therein; the world, and all things that come forth of it.
> 34:2 For the indignation of the LORD [is] upon all nations, and [his] fury upon all their armies: he hath utterly destroyed them, he hath delivered them to the slaughter.
> 34:3 Their slain also shall be cast out, and their stink shall come up out of their carcases, and the mountains shall be melted with their blood.
> 34:4 And all the host of heaven shall be dissolved, and the heavens shall be rolled together as a scroll: and all their host shall fall down, as the leaf falleth off from the vine, and as a falling [fig] from the fig tree.
> 34:5 For my sword shall be bathed in heaven: behold, it shall come down upon Idumea, and upon the people of my curse, to judgment.
> 34:6 The sword of the LORD is filled with blood, it is made fat with fatness, [and] with the blood of lambs and goats, with the fat of the kidneys of rams: for the LORD hath a sacrifice in Bozrah, and a great slaughter in the land of Idumea.
> 34:7 And the unicorns shall come down with them, and the bullocks with the bulls; and their land shall be soaked with blood, and their dust made fat with fatness.
> 34:8 For [it is] the day of the LORD'S vengeance, [and] the year of recompences for the controversy of Zion.
> 34:9 ¶ And the streams thereof shall be turned into pitch, and the dust thereof into brimstone, and the land thereof shall become burning pitch.
> 34:10 It shall not be quenched night nor day; the smoke thereof shall go up for ever: from generation to generation it shall lie waste; none shall pass through it for ever and ever.

- (a) **NAMED** — "the day of the LORD'S vengeance" (v.8).
- (b) Universal: "**all nations**… all their armies" (v.2); "ye nations… the earth… the world" (v.1) — summoned as a totality, then "slaughter" (the day-of-slaughter word).
- (c) **Sword** (vv.5-6); **blood** (vv.3, 6, 7); **brimstone** + **fire** (v.9 "burning pitch"); **smoke** ascending for ever (v.10 = Rev 14:11; 19:3); rolled-up heavens + falling figs (v.4 = Rev 6:13-14, sixth seal verbatim).

### Jeremiah 46:10 — **[FTS-surfaced]** the day of the LORD at the EUPHRATES

> 46:10 For this [is] the day of the Lord GOD of hosts, a day of vengeance, that he may avenge him of his adversaries: and the sword shall devour, and it shall be satiate and made drunk with their blood: for the Lord GOD of hosts hath a sacrifice in the north country by the river Euphrates.

- (a) **NAMED** — "the day of the Lord GOD of hosts, a day of vengeance."
- (b) Local theatre (Egypt vs. Babylon at Carchemish) but the day-of-the-LORD/sacrifice formula is identical to Zeph 1:7-8 and Isa 34:6.
- (c) **EUPHRATES** — the only OT verse that puts "the day of the LORD" and "the river Euphrates" in the same sentence; **sword**; **blood**; sacrifice. Direct OT anchor for the sixth trumpet/sixth vial Euphrates theatre (Rev 9:14; 16:12).

### Lamentations 2:22 — **[FTS-surfaced]**

> 2:22 Thou hast called as in a solemn day my terrors round about, so that in the day of the LORD'S anger none escaped nor remained: those that I have swaddled and brought up hath mine enemy consumed.

- (a) **NAMED** — "the day of the LORD'S anger" (here already EXECUTED on Jerusalem, 586 BC — proof the day of the LORD has local installments before the universal finale).
- (b) "None escaped nor remained" — totality within its sphere.
- (c) Terrors round about; consuming enemy (sword implied from context, Lam 2:21).

### Ezekiel 7:19

> 7:19 They shall cast their silver in the streets, and their gold shall be removed: their silver and their gold shall not be able to deliver them in the day of the wrath of the LORD: they shall not satisfy their souls, neither fill their bowels: because it is the stumblingblock of their iniquity.

- (a) **NAMED** — "the day of the wrath of the LORD."
- (b) Chapter frame is "an end… upon the four corners of the land" (Ezek 7:2) — the END-formula later universalized.
- (c) Cast-away silver and gold = Isa 2:20; Zeph 1:18 verbatim parallel ("neither their silver nor their gold shall be able to deliver them").

### Ezekiel 13:5

> 13:5 Ye have not gone up into the gaps, neither made up the hedge for the house of Israel to stand in the battle in the day of the LORD.

- (a) **NAMED** — "the battle in the day of the LORD."
- (b) — (the failure of the false prophets is measured against the coming day).
- (c) **Battle** vocabulary — the day of the LORD is a battle one must "stand" in (= Rev 6:17 "who shall be able to stand?"; Rev 16:14 "the battle of that great day"); indictment of watchmen who give no alarm (cf. Joel 2:1, the trumpet duty).

### Ezekiel 30:2-3 — "the time of the heathen"

> 30:2 Son of man, prophesy and say, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Howl ye, Woe worth the day!
> 30:3 For the day [is] near, even the day of the LORD [is] near, a cloudy day; it shall be the time of the heathen.

- (a) **NAMED** — "the day of the LORD is near."
- (b) Universal: "**the time of the heathen**" — the day of the LORD defined as the season when the nations as a class are dealt with (Egypt is merely first in line, vv.4ff).
- (c) **Darkness** ("a cloudy day" = Joel 2:2; Zeph 1:15); "Howl ye" = Isa 13:6; Joel 1:15 onomatopoeia of the alarm.

### Joel 1:15

> 1:15 Alas for the day! for the day of the LORD [is] at hand, and as a destruction from the Almighty shall it come.

- (a) **NAMED**. Word-for-word twin of Isa 13:6 — Joel deliberately re-sounds Isaiah's Babylon alarm over Israel.
- (b) — (scope widens through ch. 2-3).
- (c) "Destruction from the Almighty" (Heb. _shod mi-Shaddai_).

### Joel 2:1-11 — the TRUMPET text of the corpus

> 2:1 ¶ Blow ye the trumpet in Zion, and sound an alarm in my holy mountain: let all the inhabitants of the land tremble: for the day of the LORD cometh, for [it is] nigh at hand;
> 2:2 A day of darkness and of gloominess, a day of clouds and of thick darkness, as the morning spread upon the mountains: a great people and a strong; there hath not been ever the like, neither shall be any more after it, [even] to the years of many generations.
> 2:3 A fire devoureth before them; and behind them a flame burneth: the land [is] as the garden of Eden before them, and behind them a desolate wilderness; yea, and nothing shall escape them.
> 2:4 The appearance of them [is] as the appearance of horses; and as horsemen, so shall they run.
> 2:5 Like the noise of chariots on the tops of mountains shall they leap, like the noise of a flame of fire that devoureth the stubble, as a strong people set in battle array.
> 2:6 Before their face the people shall be much pained: all faces shall gather blackness.
> 2:7 They shall run like mighty men; they shall climb the wall like men of war; and they shall march every one on his ways, and they shall not break their ranks:
> 2:8 Neither shall one thrust another; they shall walk every one in his path: and [when] they fall upon the sword, they shall not be wounded.
> 2:9 They shall run to and fro in the city; they shall run upon the wall, they shall climb up upon the houses; they shall enter in at the windows like a thief.
> 2:10 The earth shall quake before them; the heavens shall tremble: the sun and the moon shall be dark, and the stars shall withdraw their shining:
> 2:11 And the LORD shall utter his voice before his army: for his camp [is] very great: for [he is] strong that executeth his word: for the day of the LORD [is] great and very terrible; and who can abide it?

- (a) **NAMED** — vv.1, 11.
- (b) Universal in echo: "neither shall be any more after it" (v.2) = Dan 12:1 / Jer 30:7 / Matt 24:21 the-unrepeatable-day formula.
- (c) **TRUMPET + ALARM** (v.1 — the duty of warning IS blowing the trumpet because the day is at hand); **fire** (vv.3, 5); **darkness** (v.2); **darkened lights** (v.10 sun/moon/stars); **earthquake** (v.10); **horses/horsemen + chariots** (vv.4-5 = the 9th-trumpet locust-cavalry imagery of Rev 9:7-9 — "as the appearance of horses… noise of chariots"); thief at the windows (v.9 = 1 Thess 5:2); "who can abide it?" (v.11 = Rev 6:17; Nah 1:6; Mal 3:2).

### Joel 2:30-31 (with Acts 2:19-20, the NT republication)

> 2:31 The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and the terrible day of the LORD come.

> Acts 2:19 And I will shew wonders in heaven above, and signs in the earth beneath; blood, and fire, and vapour of smoke:
> Acts 2:20 The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before that great and notable day of the Lord come:

- (a) **NAMED** — "the great and the terrible day of the LORD"; Peter republishes it at Pentecost as "that great and notable day of the Lord" **[FTS-surfaced]**.

- (c) **Blood, fire, pillars of smoke** (= Rev 8:7-8 first/second trumpet matter; Rev 9:17-18 "fire and smoke and brimstone"); **darkened sun + blood moon** (= sixth seal, Rev 6:12 — the 1780/1833 signs of the Advent movement).

### Amos 5:18-20

> 5:18 Woe unto you that desire the day of the LORD! to what end [is] it for you? the day of the LORD [is] darkness, and not light.

- (a) **NAMED** — ×3. Earliest written occurrence of the phrase; Amos corrects a popular expectation, proving "the day of the LORD" was ALREADY a fixed term in Israel's vocabulary.

- (c) **Darkness** ×3 — no escape (the lion/bear/serpent chain).

### Zephaniah 2:2-3

> 2:2 Before the decree bring forth, [before] the day pass as the chaff, before the fierce anger of the LORD come upon you, before the day of the LORD'S anger come upon you.

- (b) Addressed to "all ye meek of **the earth**."
- (c) The "BEFORE the decree" structure — a probationary interval precedes the day (sealing time); "it may be ye shall be hid" = Isa 26:20; Ps 27:5; the hiding of Ezek 9's marked ones.

### Zechariah 14:1-3

> 14:1 ¶ Behold, the day of the LORD cometh, and thy spoil shall be divided in the midst of thee.

- (a) **NAMED**.
- (b) Universal: "I will gather **all nations**… to battle" — the day of the LORD = the gathering of all nations to one battle (= Rev 16:14, 16 Armageddon; Joel 3:2).
- (c) **Battle/sword**; gathering of the nations.

### Malachi 4:1, 5

> 4:1 ¶ For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the LORD of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.
> 4:5 Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the LORD:

- (a) **NAMED** — "the great and dreadful day of the LORD" (v.5; v.1 "the day" is the same day by context).
- (b) Universal: "**all** the proud, yea, and **all** that do wickedly" — moral classes, not nations; total ("neither root nor branch").
- (c) **Fire** (oven, stubble, burn up); Elijah-message BEFORE the day (v.5) — the warning ministry precedes the day, exactly as the trumpet precedes the battle.

### 1 Thessalonians 5:2-3

> 5:2 For yourselves know perfectly that the day of the Lord so cometh as a thief in the night.
> 5:3 For when they shall say, Peace and safety; then sudden destruction cometh upon them, as travail upon a woman with child; and they shall not escape.

- (b) "They… them" = the world-class at large vs. "ye, brethren" (v.4).

### 2 Peter 3:10

- (b) Universal absolutely: heavens + elements + earth + all works therein.
- (c) **Fire** (melt, burned up); great noise (= Jer 25:31 "a noise shall come even to the ends of the earth").

## II. UNNAMED-but-Universal Texts (the phrase never appears; the signature does)

### Isaiah 24:1-6, 17-23 — the whole-earth chapter

> 24:1 ¶ Behold, the LORD maketh the earth empty, and maketh it waste, and turneth it upside down, and scattereth abroad the inhabitants thereof.
> 24:2 And it shall be, as with the people, so with the priest; as with the servant, so with his master; as with the maid, so with her mistress; as with the buyer, so with the seller; as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the taker of usury, so with the giver of usury to him.
> 24:3 The land shall be utterly emptied, and utterly spoiled: for the LORD hath spoken this word.
> 24:4 The earth mourneth [and] fadeth away, the world languisheth [and] fadeth away, the haughty people of the earth do languish.
> 24:5 The earth also is defiled under the inhabitants thereof; because they have transgressed the laws, changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant.
> 24:6 Therefore hath the curse devoured the earth, and they that dwell therein are desolate: therefore the inhabitants of the earth are burned, and few men left.
>
> 24:17 Fear, and the pit, and the snare, [are] upon thee, O inhabitant of the earth.
> 24:18 And it shall come to pass, [that] he who fleeth from the noise of the fear shall fall into the pit; and he that cometh up out of the midst of the pit shall be taken in the snare: for the windows from on high are open, and the foundations of the earth do shake.
> 24:19 The earth is utterly broken down, the earth is clean dissolved, the earth is moved exceedingly.
> 24:20 The earth shall reel to and fro like a drunkard, and shall be removed like a cottage; and the transgression thereof shall be heavy upon it; and it shall fall, and not rise again.
> 24:21 And it shall come to pass in that day, [that] the LORD shall punish the host of the high ones [that are] on high, and the kings of the earth upon the earth.
> 24:22 And they shall be gathered together, [as] prisoners are gathered in the pit, and shall be shut up in the prison, and after many days shall they be visited.
> 24:23 Then the moon shall be confounded, and the sun ashamed, when the LORD of hosts shall reign in mount Zion, and in Jerusalem, and before his ancients gloriously.

- (a) **UNNAMED** — only "in that day" (v.21); the phrase never occurs in the chapter.
- (b) Universal: "the earth… the world… **the inhabitants of the earth**" (vv.1, 4, 6, 17); "**the kings of the earth** upon the earth" (v.21); all-ranks merism (v.2 priest/people, master/servant…); "few men left" (v.6).
- (c) **Fire** ("inhabitants of the earth are burned," v.6); **earthquake** (vv.18-20 — foundations shake, earth reels like a drunkard); **darkened lights** (v.23 moon confounded, sun ashamed); pit-prison + "after many days shall they be visited" (v.22 = the millennium-and-second-resurrection sequence of Rev 20); cause stated: "changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant" (v.5).

### Isaiah 26:20-21 — **[FTS-surfaced]** hide until the indignation be overpast

> 26:20 ¶ Come, my people, enter thou into thy chambers, and shut thy doors about thee: hide thyself as it were for a little moment, until the indignation be overpast.
> 26:21 For, behold, the LORD cometh out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity: the earth also shall disclose her blood, and shall no more cover her slain.

- (a) **UNNAMED** — "the indignation… the LORD cometh out of his place."
- (b) Universal: "**the inhabitants of the earth**"; "her slain" = "the slain of the LORD" (Jer 25:33; Isa 66:16).
- (c) **Blood**; the hiding-chamber for God's people while the day passes (= Zeph 2:3; Ps 91; Ezek 9 mark).

**Structure note (load-bearing for the study):**

1. vv.11-12 — a DATED time prophecy: **seventy years**, then Babylon punished. Prophetic time runs FIRST.
2. vv.15-29 — when the dated period closes, the **cup of fury goes to ALL NATIONS**: the catalogue starts at Jerusalem (v.18, "I begin to bring evil on the city which is called by my name," v.29 — judgment begins at the house of God, 1 Pet 4:17; Ezek 9:6) and widens to "**all the kingdoms of the world, which are upon the face of the earth**" (v.26), ending with Sheshach/Babylon drinking last.
3. vv.30-33 — the finale is pure day-of-the-LORD material — roar from on high, winepress shout, sword on **all the inhabitants of the earth**, whirlwind from the coasts of the earth, **slain of the LORD from one end of the earth even unto the other** — yet **the phrase "day of the LORD" never appears in the chapter**. The signature carries the identification, not the label.
4. vv.34-38 — the shepherds' "days of your slaughter" (v.34) — the day-of-slaughter terminus.

- (a) **UNNAMED** — strongest proof-case that the corpus is bigger than the phrase.
- (b) Universal: "**all the nations**" (vv.15, 17), "**all the kingdoms of the world**… upon the face of the earth" (v.26), "**all the inhabitants of the earth**" (vv.29, 30), "ends of the earth… **all flesh**" (v.31), "**from one end of the earth even unto the other**" (v.33).
- (c) **Cup of wrath/wine** (vv.15-17, 27-28 → Rev 14:8, 10; 16:19; 18:3); **sword** (vv.16, 27, 29, 31); **WINEPRESS** ("a shout, as they that tread the grapes," v.30); **whirlwind** (v.32); roar/great noise (vv.30-31 = Joel 3:16; 2 Pet 3:10); slaughter (v.34).

### Working conclusions for the study

1. **The phrase is a label, not the boundary.** Jer 25 — the most complete day-of-the-LORD scenario in the OT (cup to all nations, winepress shout, whirlwind, slain from end to end of the earth) — never uses the phrase. Identification rides on the SIGNATURE: universal scope + the fixed vocabulary kit.
2. **The signature kit is the trumpet/vial kit.** Trumpet and alarm (Joel 2:1; Zeph 1:16), fire/blood/smoke/brimstone (Joel 2:30; Isa 34:9; Ezek 38:22), darkened lights (Isa 13:10; Joel 2:31; 3:15; Isa 24:23), earthquake (Isa 2:19; 13:13; 24:18-20; Ezek 38:19-20; Hag 2:6), Euphrates (Jer 46:10 named-day text → Rev 9:14; 16:12), winepress (Isa 63:3; Joel 3:13; Jer 25:30 → Rev 14:19-20; 19:15), whirlwind (Jer 25:32; Isa 66:15), towers (Isa 2:15; Zeph 1:16), sword on all flesh (Isa 66:16; Jer 25:31), cup (Jer 25; Obad 16 → Rev 14:10; 16:19). Revelation's trumpets and vials are this corpus re-sounded in sequence.
3. **The pattern of Jer 25 is the pattern of the trumpets:** dated time prophecy runs out (seventy years // prophetic periods) → judgment begins at the house called by His name (v.29 // Ezek 9:6) → the cup goes to ALL nations → the unnamed day-of-the-LORD finale. Prophetic time first, then the universal day.
4. **Local installments prove, not exhaust, the day.** Babylon (Isa 13), Edom (Isa 34; Obad), Egypt (Ezek 30; Jer 46), Jerusalem (Lam 2:22; Zeph 1) each received "a day of the LORD" — and each text bursts its local frame ("punish the world," Isa 13:11; "all nations," Isa 34:2; "time of the heathen," Ezek 30:3; "all them that dwell in the land/earth," Zeph 1:18). The local day is the type; the universal day is the terminus.
5. **A probationary interval precedes the day** — "before the decree bring forth" (Zeph 2:2), Elijah sent "before" (Mal 4:5), the acceptable year proclaimed before the day of vengeance (Isa 61:2), hide "until the indignation be overpast" (Isa 26:20). The trumpet (warning) belongs to the interval; that is the loud-cry logic.
