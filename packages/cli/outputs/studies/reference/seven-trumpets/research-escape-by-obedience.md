# Escape by Obedience — the AD 70 / Babylon Pattern (day of slaughter = on God's apostate, unsealed people, begins at His house)

## Thesis (Cristian, 2026-06-09)

ONE judgment, described inward→outward by who is in view:

- **Day of slaughter** = the stroke on God's OWN professed-but-unsealed people (apostate Christendom), and it BEGINS AT HIS SANCTUARY (Ezek 9:6; 1 Pet 4:17). False watchmen fall first (GC 656.2).
- **Day of the LORD** = the same stroke as it reaches THE WORLD — all nations, universal sweep.
- **Sealing** = the protective obverse — the MARKING of God's true people (Ezek 9:4; Rev 7:3) — deciding who is exempt.
- Order is fixed: Jer 25:29 — "I begin to bring evil on the city which is called by my name... THEN a sword on all the inhabitants of the earth." Inner (house of God) → outer (all nations).
- ESCAPE IS BY OBEDIENCE to a present-truth warning, exactly as AD 70 (flee Judea) and the Babylonian siege (surrender and live). To be "taken"/slain proves disobedience, not misfortune. The country-living / come-out-of-the-cities call is the SAME message the Christians obeyed before Jerusalem fell, and Jeremiah preached before Babylon took the city.

## Jeremiah — "surrender and live" (the way of life and the way of death)

- **Jer 21:8-10** — "Behold, I set before you the way of life, and the way of death. He that abideth in this city shall die by the sword... but he that goeth out, and falleth to the Chaldeans... he shall live."
- **Jer 38:2** — "He that remaineth in this city shall die... but he that goeth forth to the Chaldeans shall live."
- **Jer 38:17-18, 20** — to Zedekiah: "If thou wilt assuredly go forth... then thy soul shall live, and this city shall not be burned... But if thou wilt not go forth... thou shalt not escape." v.20: "Obey, I beseech thee, the voice of the LORD... so it shall be well unto thee, and thy soul shall live."
- **Jer 27:12-13** — "Bring your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon, and serve him... and live. Why will ye die... by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence?"

## Matthew 24 / Luke 21 — flee Judea (already in the Fulfilled-Once section)

- Luke 21:20-21 — armies → flee to the mountains. The literal, datable sign (Roman standards, GC 25.4); obeying it saved lives.

## EGW — not one Christian perished

- **GC 30.2** (FULL): "Not one Christian perished in the destruction of Jerusalem. Christ had given His disciples warning, and all who believed His words watched for the promised sign... 'When ye shall see Jerusalem compassed with armies... then let them which are in Judea flee to the mountains...' Luke 21:20, 21. After the Romans under Cestius had surrounded the city, they unexpectedly abandoned the siege... God's merciful providence was directing events for the good of His own people. The promised sign had been given to the waiting Christians, and now an opportunity was offered for all who would, to obey the Saviour's warning... Without delay they fled to a place of safety—the city of Pella, in the land of Perea, beyond Jordan."
- **GC 30.1** (FULL — the AD70 day-of-slaughter HERALD, the four-winds cry against the temple): "For seven years a man continued to go up and down the streets of Jerusalem, declaring the woes that were to come upon the city... 'A voice from the east! a voice from the west! a voice from the four winds! a voice against Jerusalem and against the temple!... a voice against the whole people!'... 'Woe, woe to Jerusalem!' 'woe, woe to the inhabitants thereof!' His warning cry ceased not until he was slain in the siege he had foretold."

## Litch — pioneer witness

- **PREX1 176.2** (FULL): "When destruction was to come on Jerusalem, the Saviour took care to deliver those that trusted in him, and they were led to a place of safety before the dreadful siege began, and not a Christian perished there."

## How it threads into the study

- DAY OF SLAUGHTER section: add a closing sub-block "**Begins at the house of God — and the way of escape**": Ezek 9:6 / 1 Pet 4:17 / Jer 25:29 = inward→outward order; GC 656.2 false watchmen first; then the AD70/Babylon escape-by-obedience (Jer 21:8; GC 30.2; PREX1 176.2) — the slain = the disobedient who would not heed the warning.
- SEALING TIME section: frame sealing as the protective obverse — the mark of Ezek 9:4 IS obedience to present truth (sigh-and-cry = grieved at the church's apostasy = the obedient remnant); sealed = those who heed and flee; the seal already on them before the stroke (Rev 7:3 "till we have sealed").
- EQUATION: restate from "one event, four names" to "ONE judgment, three faces by audience": inner stroke on the apostate house (day of slaughter, begins at sanctuary) → outer stroke on the world (day of the LORD, all nations); sealing = the protective obverse that exempts the obedient. Escape = obedience to the warning (the loud cry), as AD70/Babylon.
