## A Time Is 360, Seven Times Is 2520 — the Reckoning

> Scripture defines its own clock: a "time" is 360 days, set side by side with the same span called 1260 days and 42 months; "seven times" is therefore twice the named papal half, 1260 + 1260 = 2520 — and the day-for-a-year key, stated twice by God, turns 2520 days into 2520 years.

**The wilderness span named three ways — one figure that defines a "time" — Rev. 12:6, 14; 11:2-3; 13:5:**

- _Rev. 12:6._ "And the woman fled into the wilderness, where she hath a place prepared of God, that they should feed her there a thousand two hundred and threescore days." — the papal-wilderness period stated as a plain day-count: 1260 days.
  - **a time** = a prophetic year of 360 days. Scripture equates "a time, and times, and half a time" (3.5 times) with "a thousand two hundred and threescore days" and "forty and two months" for the identical wilderness/papal span; 1260 / 3.5 = 360, so one time = 360 days — and carried day-for-a-year, one prophetic time = 360 years (_Rev. 12:6_; _Rev. 12:14_; _Rev. 11:2-3_; _Rev. 13:5_; _Dan. 7:25_). Defined in full at the close.
- _Rev. 12:14._ "where she is nourished for a time, and times, and half a time, from the face of the serpent." — the identical wilderness sojourn now named "a time, and times, and half a time" — 3.5 times. Set beside 12:6, 3.5 times = 1260 days, so ONE time = 360 days. The Bible defines its own figure.
- _Rev. 11:2-3._ "the holy city shall they tread under foot forty and two months. And I will give power unto my two witnesses, and they shall prophesy a thousand two hundred and threescore days, clothed in sackcloth." — the third name for the same span, in adjacent verses: "forty and two months" = "a thousand two hundred and threescore days." 1260 / 42 = 30-day months; 42 months / 3.5 = years of 12 months of 360 days. The "time" is fixed at 360.
- _Rev. 13:5._ "power was given unto him to continue forty and two months." — the papal beast's reign = 42 months, a fourth statement of the same 1260, confirming the 30-day month and the 360-day year by which a "time" is reckoned.

**The named papal half — 3.5 times — Dan. 7:25; 12:7:**

- _Dan. 7:25._ "they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time." — the named papal half: "a time and times and the dividing of time" = 3.5 times = 1260 (per _Rev. 12:6, 14_). This is the HALF; the whole "seven times" is twice this — 1260 + 1260 = 2520.
- _Dan. 12:7._ "it shall be for a time, times, and an half; and when he shall have accomplished to scatter the power of the holy people, all these things shall be finished." — "a time, times, and an half" (3.5 times = 1260) tied directly to scattering "the power of the holy people" — the very dispersion theme that the doubled period, 2520, measures in full.
- _PREX2 204.1._ Litch (confirmation, after Scripture has fixed the half): "The Gentile kings were to tread down the holy city forty-two months, or 1260 years; and the two witnesses were to be clothed in sackcloth 1260 days or years: the two periods making 2520 years, or seven prophetic times." — the whole as twice the named half, 1260 + 1260 = 2520, corroborating what _Dan. 7:25_ / _12:7_ with _Rev. 12:6_ already establish.

**The covenant "seven times" — the appointed scattering — Lev. 26:18, 21, 24, 28:**

- _Lev. 26:18._ "then I will punish you seven times more for your sins." — the covenant origin of "seven times" as the appointed period of scattering for unfaithful Israel — the source figure that, reckoned prophetically (7 x 360), yields 2520.
  - **2520** = "seven times" — 7 x 360 — the full length of the scattering of the holy people. Built two ways: (1) seven covenant "times" x 360 days = 2520, carried day-for-a-year to 2520 years; (2) the named papal half is 1260 ("a time, times, and an half"), and the whole is twice the half, 1260 + 1260 = 2520 (_Lev. 26:18, 21, 24, 28_; _Dan. 4:25_; _Dan. 7:25_; _Dan. 12:7_; _Num. 14:34_). Defined in full at the close.
- _Lev. 26:21._ "I will bring seven times more plagues upon you according to your sins." — "seven times" repeated in the same covenant chapter, marking it a fixed, defined chastisement-period, not a loose multiplier.
- _Lev. 26:24._ "and will punish you yet seven times for your sins." — the third occurrence of the covenant "seven times," establishing it as the formal measure of the dispersion.
- _Lev. 26:28._ "I, even I, will chastise you seven times for your sins." — the fourth covenant "seven times" — the repeated decreed period that becomes 2520 years when the day-for-a-year key is applied.

**"Seven times" as a measurable span in prophecy — Dan. 4:16, 25:**

- _Dan. 4:16._ "let seven times pass over him." — the sentence first announced: "seven times" as a literal, measurable span over Nebuchadnezzar — the same idiom the reckoning applies as 2520.
- _Dan. 4:25._ "and seven times shall pass over thee, till thou know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men." — "seven times" as a defined duration in prophecy (Nebuchadnezzar's banishment), supplying the prophetic vocabulary in which seven times = 7 x 360 = 2520.

**The day-for-a-year key — stated twice by God — Num. 14:34, 33; Eze. 4:6:**

- _Num. 14:34._ "After the number of the days in which ye searched the land, even forty days, each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities, even forty years." — God's own statement of the principle: forty days = forty years, "each day for a year." This converts 2520 prophetic days into 2520 years.
  - **the day-for-a-year principle** = one prophetic day stands for one literal year. God states it twice — "each day for a year" — the key that converts prophetic days into solar years and so makes 2520 days = 2520 years (_Num. 14:34_; _Num. 14:33_; _Eze. 4:6_). Defined in full at the close.
- _Num. 14:33._ "And your children shall wander in the wilderness forty years, and bear your whoredoms, until your carcases be wasted in the wilderness." — the "forty years" that the "forty days" stood for — Scripture demonstrating the day-to-year ratio in actual fulfilment, a scattering for unfaithfulness like the seven-times dispersion.
- _Eze. 4:6._ "thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: I have appointed thee each day for a year." — the second witness to the key — "I have appointed thee each day for a year" — establishing it "in the mouth of two witnesses" (_Num. 14:34_; _Eze. 4:6_), the hermeneutic that turns 2520 days into 2520 years.
- _PREX2 124.2._ Litch (confirmation, after Scripture has fixed both 360 and the day-year key): "Seven times; one time being 360 days, seven times would 2520 days. Each of these days represents a year, as in Daniel 7:25. 2520 years is the length of that dispersion." — the central reckoning stated bare: 360 x 7 = 2520 days, each day a year, hence 2520 years of dispersion.

**DEFINITION — A TIME IS 360, SEVEN TIMES IS 2520 =** Scripture supplies its own units. The same wilderness/papal span is named four ways in adjacent verses — "a thousand two hundred and threescore days," "forty and two months," "a time, and times, and half a time," and a "time and times and the dividing of time" (_Rev. 12:6, 14_; _Rev. 11:2-3_; _Rev. 13:5_; _Dan. 7:25_; _Dan. 12:7_). Setting 1260 days against 3.5 times fixes one "time" at 360 days; setting 1260 days against 42 months fixes the prophetic month at 30 days and the prophetic year at 360. "Seven times" is therefore reckoned two converging ways: 7 x 360 days = 2520 (the covenant chastisement of _Lev. 26:18, 21, 24, 28_, the measurable span of _Dan. 4:16, 25_), and twice the named papal half of 1260 — 1260 + 1260 = 2520 (_Dan. 7:25_; _Dan. 12:7_; with Litch, _PREX2 204.1_). God's twice-stated day-for-a-year key, "each day for a year" (_Num. 14:34_; _Num. 14:33_; _Eze. 4:6_), then converts the 2520 prophetic days into 2520 years — "the length of that dispersion" of "the power of the holy people" (_Dan. 12:7_; _PREX2 124.2_). The arithmetic alone is built here; its start and end dates belong to the owning section.

**Symbols defined here:**

- **a time** = a prophetic year of 360 days — fixed by equating 3.5 times with 1260 days and 42 months for the one wilderness/papal span; carried day-for-a-year, 360 years (_Rev. 12:6_; _Rev. 12:14_; _Rev. 11:2-3_; _Rev. 13:5_; _Dan. 7:25_).
- **the day-for-a-year principle** = one prophetic day stands for one literal year — "each day for a year," stated twice by God, the key that makes 2520 days = 2520 years (_Num. 14:34_; _Num. 14:33_; _Eze. 4:6_).
- **2520** = "seven times," 7 x 360 — the full scattering of the holy people; built as 7 covenant "times" x 360, and as twice the named papal half, 1260 + 1260 (_Lev. 26:18, 21, 24, 28_; _Dan. 4:16, 25_; _Dan. 7:25_; _Dan. 12:7_; _Num. 14:34_).

**Symbols carried:** the **seven times** as a dated 2520-year prophecy, and its termini (Manasseh's captivity, 677 BC, to AD 1844; the earlier captivity, ~723 BC, to 1798), belong to "The Seven Times — Daniel 4" as the owning section; the **2300 days** on which the 2520 converges belongs to "The Ram, the Goat, and the 2300 Days — Daniel 8."

**For discussion:**

1. The same span is called 1260 days, 42 months, and 3.5 "times" in adjoining verses (_Rev. 12:6, 14_; _Rev. 11:2-3_). How does letting Scripture interpose its own equivalences — rather than importing a 365-day year — guard us from forcing the prophecy to our calendar instead of God's?
2. God states the day-for-a-year key twice, "each day for a year" (_Num. 14:34_; _Eze. 4:6_), and both times in the setting of a scattering for unfaithfulness. Why does it matter that the principle that unlocks 2520 is established "in the mouth of two witnesses" rather than asserted once?
3. "Seven times" arrives by two independent paths — 7 x 360, and twice the named half of 1260 (_Lev. 26:18_; _Dan. 7:25_; _Dan. 12:7_). When two separate lines of Scripture land on the same number, what does that convergence teach us about how God means His prophecies to be proven?
