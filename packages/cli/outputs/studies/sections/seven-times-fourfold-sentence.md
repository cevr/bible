## Seven Times for Your Sins — the Fourfold Sentence

> Four times in one chapter the LORD names His measure on a covenant people — "seven times for your sins" — a corrective rod that Scripture's own usage shows is not a sevenfold blow but a measured span of time, executed as the scattering.

**The covenant premise — Lev. 26:14-17:**

- _Lev. 26:14-15._ "But if ye will not hearken unto me, and will not do all these commandments... but that ye break my covenant" — everything that follows is the LORD's measured answer to a covenant people, not random calamity.
  - **the curse is conditioned on breaking the covenant** (_Lev. 26:15_, "ye break my covenant"; _Lev. 26:25_ below names the sword that "avenge[s] the quarrel of my covenant" — a covenant penalty by definition).
- _Lev. 26:16-17._ "I will even appoint over you terror, consumption, and the burning ague... and ye shall be slain before your enemies: they that hate you shall reign over you" — the first stroke, before the formula begins; this is the baseline the fourfold "seven times" escalates from.

**First naming — the measure named — Lev. 26:18:**

- _Lev. 26:18._ "And if ye will not yet for all this hearken unto me, then I will punish you seven times more for your sins" — the measure named the first time.
  - **seven times** = the appointed measure of the covenant curse upon God's professed people — Scripture's own usage (_Dan. 4:16, 25_, below) shows it is a span of TIME that must "pass over" a subject under chastisement until he is humbled, not merely a sevenfold increase of intensity (_Lev. 26:18, 21, 24, 28_).
  - **if ye will not yet for all this hearken unto me** = the escalation hinge — repeated at each stage to mark a fresh refusal to repent that triggers the next, intensified measure (_Lev. 26:18, 21, 27_).

**Second naming — the cause fixed — Lev. 26:21:**

- _Lev. 26:21._ "And if ye walk contrary unto me, and will not hearken unto me; I will bring seven times more plagues upon you according to your sins" — "according to your sins" fixes it as judicial measure, not caprice; "will not hearken" states the cause.
- _MWV1 45.1._ Confirmation, placed after Scripture has carried the point — Miller: "Moses tells us the cause of their being scattered. Leviticus 26:21: 'And if ye walk contrary unto me, and will not hearken unto me.'" Cited only to locate the cause; the symbol is defined from Scripture above.

**Third naming — measure for measure — Lev. 26:23-25:**

- _Lev. 26:23-24._ "And if ye will not be reformed by me by these things... Then will I also walk contrary unto you, and will punish you yet seven times for your sins" — "yet seven times," the measure held in reserve, applied again; the reciprocity ("walk contrary... walk contrary") shows the LORD answering measure for measure.
- _Lev. 26:25._ "And I will bring a sword upon you, that shall avenge the quarrel of my covenant... and ye shall be delivered into the hand of the enemy" — names the instrument (sword, captivity) that carries out the seven-times sentence; explicitly a covenant penalty.

**Fourth and climactic naming — Lev. 26:27-28:**

- _Lev. 26:27-28._ "And if ye will not for all this hearken unto me... Then I will walk contrary unto you also in fury; and I, even I, will chastise you seven times for your sins" — the climactic stroke.
  - **chastise / I, even I** = corrective discipline with a definite term, guaranteed by the divine emphatic — the LORD personally fixing and executing the measure; "chastise" (not destroy) marks it as a corrective rod, not annihilation (_Lev. 26:28_; the instrument at _Lev. 26:25_).

**The sentence executed as the scattering — Lev. 26:33; Deut. 28:64:**

- _Lev. 26:33._ "And I will scatter you among the heathen, and will draw out a sword after you: and your land shall be desolate, and your cities waste" — the fourfold warning climaxes in scattering; the "seven times" is executed AS the scattering, the land lying desolate as long as they are in the enemies' land — and that is what fixes the period in TIME.
- _Deut. 28:64._ "And the LORD shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other" — Moses' second witness restates the same sentence: scattering is the terminal stroke of the broken covenant.

**Scripture's rule for reading a measured penalty — Num. 14:34; Eze. 4:6:**

- _Num. 14:34._ "After the number of the days in which ye searched the land, even forty days, each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities, even forty years" — Scripture's own rule: the LORD reckons a sentence of chastisement in symbolic time-units, "each day for a year."
- _Eze. 4:6._ "thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: I have appointed thee each day for a year" — the second canonical witness to the day-year principle, tied expressly to "the house of Judah" bearing iniquity — the same covenant-people-under-chastisement frame as Leviticus 26.

**"Seven times" defined by the Bible's own usage — Dan. 4:16, 25:**

- _Dan. 4:16._ "let seven times pass over him" — "seven times" is a duration that must "pass over" a subject, the same idiom that governs Leviticus 26 when read prophetically.
- _Dan. 4:25._ "and seven times shall pass over thee, till thou know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men" — the Bible's own use of "seven times": a measured period of humbling that must pass over a subject under chastisement until he learns the lesson — not a multiplier of intensity.

**The unit and the bound — Dan. 7:25; Dan. 12:7:**

- _Dan. 7:25._ "and they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time" — a "time" is a measured prophetic period; this establishes "time" as the unit out of which "seven times" is built.
  - **a time** = a measured prophetic period, the bounded term of a treading-down (_Dan. 7:25_; _Dan. 12:7_, where the same "time, times, and an half" is sworn to a definite end).
- _Dan. 12:7._ "it shall be for a time, times, and an half; and when he shall have accomplished to scatter the power of the holy people, all these things shall be finished" — the decisive bridge: the prophetic "time, times, and an half" is sworn to run UNTIL the scattering of "the power of the holy people" is accomplished, binding the Leviticus 26 scattering directly to a definite prophetic period.
- _MWV1 43.3._ Confirmation, placed after Scripture has carried the point — Miller: "It is evident that these 'seven times' were a succession of years, for their land was to lie desolate as long as they were in their enemies' land... These 'seven times' are not yet accomplished, for Daniel says, 'When he shall have accomplished to scatter the power of the holy people all these things shall be finished.'" Exactly the prophetic-time reading the verses themselves support.

**DEFINITION — SEVEN TIMES FOR YOUR SINS =** the appointed measure of the covenant curse upon God's professed people, named four times in one chapter (_Lev. 26:18, 21, 24, 28_) as the rod the LORD metes out for persistent unfaithfulness. Each naming is reached only after a fresh refusal to repent — "if ye will not yet for all this hearken" — so the measure ascends through four warnings and climaxes in the scattering (_Lev. 26:33_; _Deut. 28:64_), the land lying desolate as long as the people are in the enemies' land. Scripture's own usage shows "seven times" is a span of TIME that must "pass over" a subject under chastisement until he is humbled (_Dan. 4:16, 25_), not merely a sevenfold intensity. Read by the day-year rule (_Num. 14:34_; _Eze. 4:6_) and bounded like the "time, times, and an half" sworn to run until the scattering of the holy people is finished (_Dan. 12:7_), it is a definite period of chastisement upon God's people, executed as the scattering — the corrective rod of "I, even I," guaranteed by the LORD Himself.

- **seven times = the appointed measure (a definite period) of the covenant curse**, executed as the scattering (_Lev. 26:18, 21, 24, 28, 33_; _Dan. 4:16, 25_; _Dan. 12:7_).
- **the curse is conditioned on breaking the covenant** — measured judgment on a covenant people, not random calamity (_Lev. 26:14-15, 25_).
- **chastise / I, even I = corrective discipline with a definite term**, divinely fixed — not annihilation (_Lev. 26:28_, _25_).

**Symbols defined here:**

- **seven times = the appointed MEASURE of the covenant curse — a definite period of chastisement that "passes over" a people**, executed as the scattering (_Lev. 26:18, 21, 24, 28, 33_; _Dan. 4:16, 25_; _Num. 14:34_; _Eze. 4:6_; _Dan. 12:7_).
- **if ye will not yet for all this hearken unto me = the escalation hinge** that structures the four ascending warnings (_Lev. 26:18, 21, 27_).
- **chastise / I, even I = God's measured corrective rod**, personally fixed and executed (_Lev. 26:28_; the instrument _Lev. 26:25_).
- **a time = a measured prophetic period**, the unit out of which "seven times" is built (_Dan. 7:25_; _Dan. 12:7_).

**Symbols carried:** the day-year principle (_Num. 14:34_; _Eze. 4:6_) and the prophetic "time, times, and an half" (_Dan. 7:25_; _Dan. 12:7_) are carried here only to define "seven times" as measured time and to bridge to it; the full chronological reckoning of the 2520 years — its commencement at the captivity of Manasseh (B.C. 677) and its terminus — belongs to the section that works the dates, "Seven Times — the 2520 Years," and is honored there for consistency.

**For discussion:**

1. Leviticus 26 makes the curse conditional — "if ye will not hearken... if ye break my covenant" (vv. 14-15). How does seeing the scattering as the measured discipline of a covenant-keeping God, rather than blind misfortune, change the way we read national and personal calamity?
2. Four times the sentence rises only after a fresh refusal: "if ye will not yet for all this hearken" (vv. 18, 21, 23, 27). What does this fourfold patience reveal about how God deals with persistent sin in His people before the heaviest stroke falls?
3. The fourth stroke is "chastise" — correction with a term — not "destroy," and it is sealed by "I, even I" (v. 28). Where in your own life is the difference between God's chastening and His abandonment worth holding onto (compare _Heb. 12:6_, "whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth")?
