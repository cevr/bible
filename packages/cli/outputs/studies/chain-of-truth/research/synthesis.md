## 2. The ORDER, more specifically — how does one START?

**This is the question on which A and B appeared to diverge. They do not actually conflict; they answer two different "firsts." Resolved position:**

> **You START with the METHOD (the key), not with any text. The first _doctrinal/textual_ doorway you reach by that method is Daniel 8:14 (the 2300 days); but the first thing you must _compute_ — the load-bearing anchor without which Daniel 8:14 is unanchored — is the 70 weeks dated from the 457 BC decree (Ezra 7 / Artaxerxes).**

There are **three distinct "firsts,"** and the apparent A-vs-B tension dissolves once they are kept separate (both adversarial reviews independently insisted on exactly this distinction — B's review explicitly flagged that the bare headline "the chain BEGINS at 457 BC begs the question," because 457 BC is itself _selected by_ the 70-weeks reading, which is _selected by_ the method):

1. **METHOD-first (link zero — the genuine starting move).** Before a single doctrinal link, Miller laid the key. This is _not_ a text; it is the practice of comparing Scripture with Scripture. **This is the defensible answer to "how does one begin the chain?"** The casket allegory makes it canonical: the jewels are "beautifully arranged in their several places" _only because_ "**with this key Bro. Miller opened the casket**" (BMD 3.3). The key precedes the jewels. Strip out the method and the chain dissolves into private opinion.
   - Receipt: _"I determined to lay aside all my prepossessions, to thoroughly compare Scripture with Scripture… I commenced with Genesis, and read verse by verse… and was fully satisfied that it is its own interpreter."_ (**WMAD 6.1**)
   - Decisive negative receipt: Miller _did not_ begin from an advent prophecy — _"I commenced their study with no expectation of finding the time of the Savior's coming"_ (**WMAD 12.1**). The advent doctrine was the **result** of the method, not its starting point. This single quote settles the question against any "Second-Advent-first" or "Daniel-2-first" reading of the _true_ start.

2. **DOCTRINAL/TEXTUAL doorway (the first prophetic text the method delivers).** When a prophecy must be named as the entrance to the _time-chain_, it is **Daniel 8:14** — _"The prophecy which seemed most clearly to reveal the time of the second advent was that of Daniel 8:14"_ (**GC 324.3**). The 2300 days is the longest line and the real subject of the chain.

3. **COMPUTATIONAL anchor (where the reckoning actually begins).** Daniel 8:14 **floats** — it has no fixed start. Miller explicitly rules out the rival start-dates: _"When did the 2300 years begin? Did it begin with Nebuchadnezzar's dream? No… did it begin when the angel Gabriel came…? No… Let us begin it where the angel told us, from the going forth of the decree… 457 years before Christ; take 457 from 2300, and it will leave A.D. 1843"_ (**MWV2 54.2**). The 70 weeks exist precisely to supply this: Gabriel gave them _"for the express purpose of showing the beginning of the vision"_ (**MWV1 46.2**). The carriers codify it as a rule: _"when the date of the seventy weeks is established, the key to unlock and understand the reckoning of the days is in our hand"_ (**S23D 19.2**; cf. **SOTB 4.1**).

**Where does Daniel 2 sit?** Not as the time-doorway. Daniel 2 is the **structural spine** — it legitimizes reading Daniel 8's little horn as Rome (because Dan 2, 7, 8 are "but one prophecy," **MWV1 188.2**), but **Miller's computation never touches a single number from Daniel 2.** It is foundational, not a time-link. So: Daniel 2 = spine (structural), Daniel 8:14 = subject/doorway (the line being dated), Daniel 9 / 457 BC = anchor (the start of the reckoning). Three roles, not three competing "starts."

**Resolved decisive answer:** One begins with the **method**. The first _text_ is **Daniel 8:14**. The first _computation_ is **the 70 weeks at 457 BC**. Anyone who says "the chain starts at the Second Advent" has confused the **terminus/motive** with the start; anyone who says "it starts at Daniel 2" has confused the **spine** with the doorway; anyone who says "it starts at 457 BC" without first naming the method has begged the question (457 BC is _selected by_ the 70-weeks reading, which is _selected by_ the method).

## 3. The chain in order (a teacher could follow this)

0. **Lay the KEY first.** Set aside all preconceptions and commentaries. Resolve to _compare Scripture with Scripture_ and let the Bible be its own interpreter. Begin at Genesis 1 and read verse by verse, using a concordance to define every figure from Scripture itself. _(WMAD 6.1; GC 320.1; BMD 3.3)_
1. **Adopt year-for-a-day.** Establish from Num 14:34 and Ezek 4:6 that a prophetic "day" = a literal year — the rule that turns prophetic periods into datable years. _(GC 324.3; MWV1 53.1–53.2)_
2. **Establish that Scripture is a harmonious, literal-as-makes-good-sense system** whose figures are all defined within it. _(WMAD 6.1; MWV1 33.1)_
3. **Fix the goal: the literal, personal, premillennial Second Advent** — not a temporal millennium, not the world's conversion. This is what every prophetic period is measuring _toward_. _(WMAD 6.2; GC 321.1; GC 321.3)_
4. **Lay the spine: Daniel 2, 7, 8 are ONE prophecy** — four kingdoms (Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Rome), Rome in its last state, the little horn of Dan 8 = Rome (not Antiochus). _(WMAD 9.1; MWV1 188.2; MWV1 42.3)_
5. **Name the longest line: Daniel 8:14, the 2300 days** = 2300 years reaching to the Advent — but note it has _no fixed start yet_ (it cannot begin at Nebuchadnezzar's dream or Gabriel's visit). _(MWV1 46.2; MWV2 54.2; GC 324.3)_
6. **Drop the anchor: the 70 weeks (Dan 9:24–27)**, cut off from the 2300 days, dated from **Artaxerxes' decree to Ezra (Ezra 7), 457 BC** — given by Gabriel to "seal (make sure) the vision" and show its beginning; the 490 years run to A.D. 33 at the cross. _(MWV1 46.2; MWV1 43.2; MWV2 68.2; MWV1 245.2/246.5; S23D 19.2; SOTB 4.1)_
7. **Compute: 457 BC + 2300 = A.D. 1843** (or 490 yrs to A.D. 33 + 1810 remaining = 1843). The spine, the longest line, and the anchor lock together. _(MWV2 54.2; MWV1 46.2)_
8. **Name the terminal event: the cleansing of the sanctuary (Dan 8:14)** — for Miller, the earth purified by fire at the Advent, closing back to step 3. _(GC 324.3; MWV1 181.2)_ _(Pioneers later re-identified the sanctuary as heavenly — see caveats.)_
9. **Bring in the converging witnesses:** the "seven times" / 2520 (Lev 26; 677 BC → 1843) and the "six thousand years," independent lines confirming the same 1843. _(TSAM 65.3; WMAD 11.2; MWV1 46.1)_

## 4. Caveats / contested points surfaced by the reviewers

1. **Discovery-order vs. logical-order (the biggest wrinkle, raised by BOTH researchers and confirmed by BOTH reviews).** In Miller's _autobiographical_ account, the **"seven times" / 2520 convinced him FIRST**, and the 2300 days came as _confirmation_: _"I was satisfied that the seven times terminated in 1843. Then I came to the 2300 days; they brought me to the same conclusion"_ (**TSAM 65.3**, verified verbatim by both reviews). Yet in his finished lectures and in GC 324.3, the **2300 days / Daniel 8:14 is the doorway** and the 2520 is demoted to a converging witness. _The "first link" therefore differs depending on whether you mean Miller's personal sequence of conviction (2520-first) or the expository/logical chain he taught (Dan 8:14 doorway, 457 BC anchor)._ This document answers the **logical/expository** question, as the task framed it, and discloses the discovery-order datum honestly rather than burying it.

2. **The reconstructed order is NOT Miller's published lecture order.** Review-of-B notes that Miller's own MWV1 exposition (a) **opens on Daniel 2's image**, not the Advent (the Advent is his _conclusion_, drawn _from_ the prophecies), and (b) argues the **70 weeks FIRST** (MWV1 42.3, 43.2) as the established definite time, _then_ carries the 2300 days by them — i.e., Miller's rhetoric runs **anchor-then-subject**, while our didactic chain runs **subject-then-anchor** (longest line, then its anchor). And (c) Miller lists the "seven times" _before_ the 2300 days (MWV1 43.3, item 1 of "prophecies remaining"), whereas we place it last as off-spine. These are defensible logical-dependency reconstructions, but **a teacher should not claim Miller himself laid the Advent before the spine, or the subject before the anchor.**

3. **"Spine" (Dan 2) vs. "anchor" (Dan 8 + 9) are different roles.** Daniel 2 is the structural backbone (the metallic image / stone), but the **datable machinery lives entirely in Dan 8 (2300 days) + Dan 9 (70 weeks)** — Miller's computation never uses a Daniel 2 number. Keep the two roles distinct; do not call Daniel 2 the "starting point."

4. **The 457 BC decree was asserted more confidently than Miller derived it.** Review-of-A flagged that A's chain never cited the **Artaxerxes-Longimanus / Ezra 7 grounding (MWV1 245.2, 246.5)** — the actual _why-457_ receipt — and that A's `MWV1 47.1` was off-target. Review-of-B added that the carriers (DAR 201.1; MWM 192.2) had to **defend 457 BC against rival decrees** (Cyrus, Darius, the 408 BC completion); the bare "457 BC" hides a sub-argument. **Receipt for the anchor must include the named decree (Ezra 7 / Artaxerxes, MWV1 43.2 + 245.2/246.5), not just "the best chronologers."**

5. **The 70 weeks prove the day-year rule (a load-bearing loop both chains under-credited).** PSC 47.3 states the 70-weeks vision supplies **both** the _length_ of a prophetic day **and** the _start_ of the reckoning. The 490 literal years fulfilled as 490 day-years is what **empirically validates** year-for-a-day (link 1). So link 1 and link 6 are not independent — the anchor also _proves the rule the whole chain rests on_.

6. **The sanctuary = the earth is Miller's own, and was later corrected.** Miller took Daniel 8:14's sanctuary as the _earth_, cleansed by fire at the Advent (GC 324.3; MWV1 181.2). The pioneers (Smith/DAR, Andrews) re-identified it as the **heavenly** sanctuary — indeed DAR 193.3, already in the corpus, embeds the heavenly reading ("the most holy of the heavenly sanctuary"). **This changes the _event named at the terminus_ (link 8), not the _order_ of the chain.** Fuller decree+sanctuary argument (CIHS 83.1/84.1; LJHCS) was out of scope and not fully traced.

7. **The "daily" (Dan 8:13) / A.D. 508 start for the 1335 days is the softest joint.** It rests on Miller's reading of "the daily" = Paganism (TSAM 66.2–3), was later contested among Adventists, and is sourced only to Miller's own reasoning. It sits in the _converging-witnesses_ tier, not the load-bearing spine.

8. **Chronology depends on then-current historical sources, in tension with "the Bible alone."** Miller uses **457 BC** (Artaxerxes, for the 70 weeks/2300) but **677 BC** (Manasseh's captivity, for the 2520) — multiple external anchor-dates from "the best chronologers" (WMAD 11.2). The exact **1843-vs-1844** reckoning (BC/AD zero-year adjustment; spring-1844 vs autumn-1844 "tarrying") is downstream of the chain's logic and only lightly sampled (GC 328.3; DAR 207.3); not fully reconstructed here.

9. **Two minor quote-integrity fixes (do not propagate the errors):** (a) Rule V verbatim is **MWM 70.6**; the MWV1 form spans **20.10–21.1** and reads "is my rule, **not** the Bible" (no "and"). (b) The BMD 3.3 bracketed gloss should quote the source exactly: **"The 'key attached' was his manner of interpreting the prophetic Word…"** (c) "I laid by all commentaries…" is **MWV1 11.1**, not page 12.

### Bottom line

- **How you begin:** with the **method** (compare Scripture with Scripture; the Bible its own interpreter), reading from Genesis forward — link zero, the key that opens the casket (WMAD 6.1; WMAD 12.1; BMD 3.3).
- **First text reached:** **Daniel 8:14**, the 2300 days (GC 324.3).
- **Load-bearing anchor that makes the chain compute:** the **70 weeks at 457 BC** (Ezra 7 / Artaxerxes), which alone fixes the start of the otherwise-floating 2300 days (MWV2 54.2; MWV1 46.2; S23D 19.2; SOTB 4.1).
- **Spine that licenses the cross-linking:** Daniel 2/7/8 as one prophecy (MWV1 188.2).
- **Everything else** (the 2520, the millennium of years, the 1335) is a **converging witness**, not the anchor — and Miller's _personal_ discovery order (2520-first) must not be confused with the _logical_ chain he taught.
