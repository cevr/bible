## The Holy Spirit — Who He Is, a Divine Person

> The Comforter Christ promised is not an influence, an energy, or an emanation, but a Person — "another Comforter" of His own kind, the Spirit of truth who teaches, convicts, intercedes, grieves, and wills; the Third Person of the Godhead, coordinate with the Father and the Son in the one baptismal name.

**Christ promises another Comforter — a Person who indwells — John 14:16-17, 26:**

- _John 14:16-17._ "I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever; Even the Spirit of truth; whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him; for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you" — Christ does not send a force but "another Comforter," given to abide for ever; the Spirit is "him," not "it," is known, and indwells — the grammar of a Person.
  - **another Comforter (Parakletos)** = a helper and advocate of the same kind as Christ — "another," given to abide for ever, establishing the Spirit as a Person on par with the personal Christ He replaces (_John 14:16_; _John 14:26_; _John 16:7_). (The KJV "another Comforter" underlies the Greek _allos parakletos_, "another of the same kind"; the Greek note is interpretive and is not in the KJV text itself.) Defined in full at the close.
  - **the Spirit of truth** = the personal Spirit whose office is to define and maintain the truth — guiding believers into all truth, hearing and speaking what He receives, not speaking of Himself, and glorifying Christ (_John 14:17_; _John 15:26_; _John 16:13-14_). Defined in full at the close.
- _John 14:26._ "the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance" — the Comforter is named the Holy Ghost, and "he" teaches and recalls: acts of intelligence and memory, the work of a mind, not a blind influence.
  - **the Holy Spirit / the Comforter** = the Third divine Person of the Godhead — "another Comforter" of the same kind as Christ, sent by the Father and Son to abide, teach, convict, and indwell believers as Christ's successor on earth; a Person, not an influence (_John 14:16-17_; _John 14:26_; _Matt. 28:19_; _Acts 5:3-4_; _1 Cor. 2:11_). Defined in full at the close.
- _DA 671.1._ White: "The Comforter is called 'the Spirit of truth.' His work is to define and maintain the truth. He first dwells in the heart as the Spirit of truth, and thus He becomes the Comforter" — the office is truth, and through that office He comforts; the symbol defined exactly as John 14-16 defines it.

**Sent by the Son, proceeding from the Father — He testifies and convicts — John 15:26; 16:7-8:**

- _John 15:26._ "when the Comforter is come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, which proceedeth from the Father, he shall testify of me" — proceeding from the Father, sent by the Son, yet "he" personally testifies: distinct from both, acting as a Person.
- _John 16:7._ "It is expedient for you that I go away: for if I go not away, the Comforter will not come unto you; but if I depart, I will send him unto you" — Christ's bodily presence is exchanged for the Spirit's; a Person succeeds a Person, "him" for Him.
- _John 16:8._ "when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment" — to reprove and convict the conscience is the work of a Person with discernment and will.
- _DA 669.2._ White: "The Holy Spirit is Christ's representative, but divested of the personality of humanity, and independent thereof" — the Spirit is Christ's personal representative, "independent" of Christ's humanity, the distinct successor of _John 16:7_ — not Christ's mere influence diffused.

**The hallmark personhood text — He wills, hears, speaks, foretells, glorifies — John 16:13-14:**

- _John 16:13._ "when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth: for he shall not speak of himself; but whatsoever he shall hear, that shall he speak: and he will shew you things to come" — "he shall not speak of himself" is the signature of a Person with a will who defers to Another; He guides, hears, speaks, and foretells.
- _John 16:14._ "He shall glorify me: for he shall receive of mine, and shall shew it unto you" — He glorifies Christ — a deliberate, deferential act of one who could speak of Himself but chooses to exalt Another.
- _DA 671.3._ White: "Of the Spirit Jesus said, 'He shall glorify Me.' ... so the Spirit was to glorify Christ by revealing His grace to the world" — confirms _John 16:14_: the Spirit personally glorifies Christ, the act of a Person who "shall not speak of himself."

**He speaks, calls, forbids, and directs — the Acts confirm the Person — Acts 13:2; 16:6-7:**

- _Acts 13:2._ "the Holy Ghost said, Separate me Barnabas and Saul for the work whereunto I have called them" — the Spirit speaks in the first person — "Separate me ... I have called" — claiming workers as His own and commissioning them: speech, ownership, and calling, all acts of a Person.
- _Acts 16:6._ "were forbidden of the Holy Ghost to preach the word in Asia" — the Spirit forbids; an itinerary is governed by His decision.
- _Acts 16:7._ "they assayed to go into Bithynia: but the Spirit suffered them not" — "suffered them not" — the apostles' steps redirected by personal authority, not by a vague impression.

**To lie to Him is to lie to God — He is both Person and Deity — Acts 5:3-4:**

- _Acts 5:3._ "Ananias, why hath Satan filled thine heart to lie to the Holy Ghost, and to keep back part of the price of the land?" — one can lie _to_ the Holy Ghost; only a Person can be lied to.
- _Acts 5:4._ "thou hast not lied unto men, but unto God" — Peter equates lying to the Holy Ghost with lying to God: the Spirit is at once a Person (one can lie to Him) and divine (He is God).

**He can be grieved, He intercedes, He has a mind — Eph. 4:30; Rom. 8:26-27:**

- _Eph. 4:30._ "grieve not the holy Spirit of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption" — only a Person can be grieved; the capacity for grief over sin reveals the Spirit's personal affection and will, not the indifference of a force.
- _Rom. 8:26._ "the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities ... the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered" — He helps and intercedes, the work of a Person who pleads on our behalf.
- _Rom. 8:27._ "he that searcheth the hearts knoweth what is the mind of the Spirit, because he maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God" — the Spirit has a "mind," and intercedes according to God's will — a Person who knows, pleads, and is in accord with God.
- _Ev 616.5._ White: "We need to realize that the Holy Spirit, who is as much a person as God is a person, is walking through these grounds" — the personhood stated flat: "as much a person as God is a person," confirming the grief of _Eph. 4:30_ and the will of _John 16:13_.

**He searches the deep things of God — He distributes as He wills — 1 Cor. 2:10-11; 12:11:**

- _1 Cor. 2:10._ "God hath revealed them unto us by his Spirit: for the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God" — the Spirit searches even the depths of God, the act of an omniscient Person.
- _1 Cor. 2:11._ "what man knoweth the things of a man, save the spirit of man which is in him? even so the things of God knoweth no man, but the Spirit of God" — as a man's own spirit alone knows the man, so the Spirit alone knows the mind of God: this establishes His deity, not merely His personhood.
- _1 Cor. 12:11._ "all these worketh that one and the selfsame Spirit, dividing to every man severally as he will" — He distributes the gifts "as he will": sovereign personal volition, the act of a divine Person, not a distributed energy parceling itself out.
- _Ev 617.1._ White: "The Holy Spirit has a personality, else He could not bear witness to our spirits and with our spirits that we are the children of God. He must also be a divine person, else He could not search out the secrets which lie hidden in the mind of God" — couples personhood with deity and rests the case on _1 Cor. 2:11_, the section's exact argument.

**The third in the one name — the Third Person of the Godhead — Matt. 28:19:**

- _Matt. 28:19._ "baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost" — the Holy Ghost stands coordinate with the Father and the Son in the one baptismal "name," placed within the Godhead as the third divine Person.
- _DA 671.2._ White: "Sin could be resisted and overcome only through the mighty agency of the Third Person of the Godhead, who would come with no modified energy, but in the fullness of divine power" — the locus classicus naming the Spirit "the Third Person of the Godhead," acting "in the fullness of divine power."
- _Ev 616.4._ White: "The eternal heavenly dignitaries—God, and Christ, and the Holy Spirit—arming them [the disciples] with more than mortal energy ... would advance with them to the work and convince the world of sin" — the Spirit named among the three "eternal heavenly dignitaries," confirming both _Matt. 28:19_ and the convicting work of _John 16:8_.
- _Ev 617.2._ White: "The prince of the power of evil can only be held in check by the power of God in the third person of the Godhead, the Holy Spirit" — a second independent statement placing the Spirit as "the third person of the Godhead," wielding the power of God.
- _Ev 617.3._ White: "We are to co-operate with the three highest powers in heaven,—the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost,—and these powers will work through us, making us workers together with God" — the Spirit ranked among "the three highest powers in heaven," the triune frame of _Matt. 28:19_.

**A note on the development of this truth.** Several early Adventist pioneers (among them James White, Uriah Smith, and J. N. Andrews) at times held semi-Arian or anti-Trinitarian views, regarding the Spirit as Christ's impersonal influence or emanation rather than a distinct divine Person. The section above sets out the developed and settled position, clarified chiefly through Ellen White's later statements clustering 1897-1906 (_DA_ 1898, "the Third Person of the Godhead"; _Ev_ 616-617) — not a flattened pioneer consensus. The witness cloud here is therefore EGW by verification: no anti-Trinitarian pioneer statement is cited as a witness because the Scripture and the mature testimony settle the doctrine, and the development is named honestly rather than erased.

**DEFINITION — THE HOLY SPIRIT, WHO HE IS, A DIVINE PERSON =** the Comforter Christ promised is "another Comforter" (_John 14:16_) — one of the very same kind as the personal Christ He succeeds — not an influence, energy, or emanation, but a Person. The Scripture proves His personhood by the acts only a Person can perform: He is called "him" and "he," is known, indwells, teaches, recalls (_John 14:17, 26_); testifies, convicts, and is sent (_John 15:26_; _16:7-8_); will not speak of Himself, but guides, hears, speaks, foretells, and glorifies Christ (_John 16:13-14_); speaks in the first person, calls, claims, forbids, and directs (_Acts 13:2_; _16:6-7_); can be lied to (_Acts 5:3_) and grieved (_Eph. 4:30_); helps, intercedes, and has a "mind" (_Rom. 8:26-27_); and distributes gifts "as he will" (_1 Cor. 12:11_). The same Scripture proves His deity: to lie to Him is to lie to God (_Acts 5:4_); He searches "the deep things of God" and alone knows God's mind as a man's own spirit knows the man (_1 Cor. 2:10-11_); and He stands third in the one baptismal name with the Father and the Son (_Matt. 28:19_). Hence He is the Third Person of the Godhead, coming "in the fullness of divine power" (_DA 671.2_), "as much a person as God is a person" (_Ev 616.5_), "a divine person" who searches the hidden things of God (_Ev 617.1_) — Christ's own representative on earth, independent of His humanity (_DA 669.2_), and the agent through whom the lost image of God is restored.

**Symbols defined here:**

- **the Holy Spirit / the Comforter** = the Third divine Person of the Godhead — "another Comforter" of the same kind as Christ, sent by the Father and Son to abide, teach, convict, and indwell believers as Christ's successor on earth; a Person, not an influence (_John 14:16-17, 26_; _Matt. 28:19_; _Acts 5:3-4_; _1 Cor. 2:11_; _DA 671.2_; _Ev 616.5_).
- **the Spirit of truth** = the personal Spirit whose office is to define and maintain the truth — guiding believers into all truth, hearing and speaking what He receives, not speaking of Himself, and glorifying Christ, proceeding from the Father (_John 14:17_; _John 15:26_; _John 16:13-14_; _DA 671.1_).
- **another Comforter (Parakletos)** = a helper and advocate of the same kind as Christ — "another" (of the same sort), given to abide for ever, establishing the Spirit as a Person on par with the personal Christ He replaces (_John 14:16, 26_; _John 16:7_).

**Symbols carried:** the **Godhead** — the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, three Persons in one name — is owned by "One God in Three Persons — Distinct, Divine, Eternal" (the frame of _Matt. 28:19_); the personal **Christ** whom this Spirit represents and glorifies is owned by "The Son — Self-Existent, Without Beginning"; the convicting, sanctifying, and image-restoring **office** of this Spirit is carried forward into "The Three Persons and Our Salvation — Why It Matters" (_COL 96.1_: "In all who will submit themselves to the Holy Spirit ... the lost image of God is to be restored in humanity").
