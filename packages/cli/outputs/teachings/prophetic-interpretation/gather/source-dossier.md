# Source Dossier for Heritage and Hope Ministries Letter

## Purpose

This file separates evidence from inference.

The main question is simple.

Does Daniel 11 give a textual reason to change the kings of the north and south from real powers into philosophies after the cross?

## Findings

1. Heritage and Hope supports historicism in its trumpet video.
2. The trumpet video rejects philosophies as the meaning of the trumpets.
3. The Daniel 11 video uses a different rule.
4. It says that all things change from literal to spiritual after the cross.
5. The Daniel 11 video uses this rule to make the king of the south atheism.
6. It makes the king of the north the papacy.
7. Louis F. Were used the same general rule in 1949.
8. Miller's rules do not contain this rule.
9. Daniel 11 does not state this rule.
10. Scripture shows that some covenant terms have a wider Christian use.
11. Scripture does not make all post-cross prophecy spiritual.
12. James White held a literal Rome view.
13. His view did not make the king a philosophy.
14. Uriah Smith, E. J. Waggoner, A. T. Jones, and S. N. Haskell held a literal Turkey view.
15. Smith read Daniel 11:35 as reaching the start of the time of the end in 1798.
16. Smith and Haskell identified the power in verses 36-39 as Revolutionary France.
17. Smith read verse 40 as a conflict between France, Egypt, and Ottoman Turkey.
18. The Battle of the Pyramids was a conflict between France and the Egyptian Mamelukes.
19. Ottoman Turkey declared war against France later in 1798.
20. Ellen White did not identify the final king of the north by name.
21. Daniel 12:1 places Michael's standing after the final king comes to his end.
22. Ellen White identifies Michael's standing with the end of Christ's sanctuary intercession.
23. Romanism and apostate Protestantism pursue the death decree after probation closes.
24. The fifth plague strikes the seat and kingdom of the beast.
25. The mature pioneers placed the plagues and literal Armageddon under the seventh trumpet.

## Scripture evidence

### Daniel 11 gives a plain historical explanation

- _Dan 11:2._ "And now will I shew thee the truth" - the angel gives the explanation in plain historical language.
- _Dan 11:2._ "three kings in Persia" - the kings are real rulers.
- _Dan 11:4._ "divided toward the four winds of heaven" - the directions control the later north and south titles.
- _Dan 11:5._ "the king of the south" - the title starts as a real ruler in a real place.
- _Dan 11:8._ "carry captives into Egypt" - the chapter identifies the southern territory.
- _Dan 11:22._ "also the prince of the covenant" - the video places the cross in this verse.
- _Dan 11:23-35._ The chapter continues with leagues, provinces, armies, ships, land, sword, flame, captivity, and spoil after the cross.
- _Dan 11:40._ "with chariots, and with horsemen, and with many ships" - the verse describes power, motion, and war.
- _Dan 11:41-43._ The text names the glorious land, Edom, Moab, Ammon, Egypt, Libya, and Ethiopia.
- _Dan 11:44-45._ The text gives directions, seas, a mountain, a palace, and the end of the king.
- _Dan 12:1._ "at that time" - the end of Daniel 11 connects to the standing of Michael.

The chapter does not announce a change of method after verse 22, verse 30, verse 35, or verse 39.

The cross cannot be a universal literal-to-spiritual rule.

- _Luke 21:20-24._ Christ predicts literal armies, literal Jerusalem, literal Judea, literal flight, literal swords, and literal captivity after the cross.
- _Acts 1:11._ Christ will return "in like manner" as the disciples saw Him leave.
- _Heb 8:1-2._ Christ ministers in "the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man."
- _Rev 11:19._ John sees the heavenly temple and the ark after the cross.
- _1 Cor 15:20-23._ The resurrection and the coming of Christ remain real future events.

The New Testament does give covenant terms a Christian use.

- _Rom 2:28-29._ A true Jew has circumcision of the heart.
- _Gal 3:26-29._ Those who belong to Christ are Abraham's seed.

This covenant truth does not make every king, nation, army, ship, place, and direction a philosophy.

## Miller's method

Ellen White printed the first five rules in RH November 25, 1884, paragraphs 23-24.

- Rule 1: Every word must have its proper bearing.
- Rule 4: Bring all the texts together. Form no theory with a contradiction.
- Rule 5: Scripture must be its own expositor.
- Rule 11: Use the literal sense when it makes good sense and does not violate nature.
- Rule 12: Let Scripture define a figure.
- Rule 13: Require every word of a prophecy to meet its historical event.

Daniel 11:40-45 makes good sense as real conflict.

The text does not call its kings philosophies.

Revelation 11:8 marks a figure with the words "spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt."

Daniel 11 does not mark the king of the south in this way.

The Bible does not define the king of the north as Catholicism or the king of the south as atheism in Daniel 11.

## The event sequence

1. _Dan 11:44-45._ The king goes forth to destroy many. He then comes to his end.
2. _Dan 12:1._ "At that time" Michael stands up. The unmatched time of trouble begins.
3. [SOP GC 613.1-2] Ellen White quotes Daniel 12:1. She then says that Jesus ends His intercession and every case is decided.
4. [SOP EW 36.1] When the High Priest finishes His sanctuary work, He stands up. The plagues then begin.
5. [SOP GC 615.2] Romanism and apostate Protestantism pursue the death decree during the time of trouble.
6. [SOP EW 36.2] The plagues enrage the wicked. A decree then goes forth to kill the saints.
7. _Rev 16:10-11._ The fifth plague falls on the seat of the beast. His kingdom becomes full of darkness.
8. _Rev 16:12-16._ The sixth plague gathers the kings of the earth to Armageddon.
9. _Rev 16:17-21._ The seventh plague brings the words "It is done." Babylon and the cities of the nations fall.

This sequence creates a test for the papal king-of-the-north view.

If Daniel 11:45 predicts the end of the papacy before Michael stands, Romanism cannot lead in the later death decree.

The same papal kingdom cannot then remain as the object of the fifth plague.

The objection does not prove the Turkey view by itself.

It does require the papal view to explain its chronology without changing the plain order of the passages.

[PIONEER Uriah Smith, TBI 230.1] The fifth plague is inflicted especially on the papacy. Rome is the seat. Its subjects form the kingdom.

[PIONEER Uriah Smith, STTHD 303.1] The fifth vial falls on old Rome. The whole Catholic world is filled with darkness.

## Ellen White evidence

### Plain meaning

[SOP GC 598.3] "The language of the Bible should be explained according to its obvious meaning, unless a symbol or figure is employed."

[SOP GC 173.2] Scripture "must be its own interpreter" and must not support a prior theory.

[SOP RH November 25, 1884, pars. 23-24] Third angel workers used the same plan as William Miller.

### Daniel 11

[SOP 9T 14.2] Daniel 11 had nearly reached its complete fulfillment in a world stirred by war.

[SOP 13MR 394.1] Much history in Daniel 11 will be repeated. Ellen White then quotes verses 30-36.

These texts show the importance and historical action of Daniel 11.

They do not name the final king of the north.

### The alpha and spiritualization

[SOP 1SM 197.4] The alpha was present. The omega would have a startling nature.

[SOP 1SM 200.1] *Living Temple* contained "the alpha of deadly heresies."

[SOP 1SM 203.2] The book misapplied Scripture in its teaching about the presence and personality of God.

[SOP SpM 328.3] The theories did not stand when Scripture was read in context and in simplicity.

[SOP MM 87.4] False theories "spiritualize the doctrines of present truth" until substance and shadow lose their difference.

The alpha evidence establishes a method problem.

It does not identify Louis Were, LeRoy Froom, Desmond Ford, or a present ministry as the omega.

Any link to these later writers is an inference.

The letter must state this limit.

## Pioneer evidence

### Daniel 11:35-40: France, Egypt, and Turkey

The pioneer interpretation follows a continuous historical line.

1. [PIONEER Uriah Smith, DAR 264.2; 489.1] Verse 35 reaches the end of papal supremacy in 1798. This date starts the time of the end.
2. [PIONEER Uriah Smith, DAR 265.1] Verses 36-39 describe a wilful and atheistic power. Smith identifies this power as Revolutionary France.
3. [PIONEER S. N. Haskell, SDP 241.1] Haskell also identifies France as the state which publicly denied God.
4. [PIONEER Uriah Smith, DAR 273.3] Verse 40 returns to the territorial titles. Egypt is the king of the south. Ottoman Turkey holds the old northern territory.
5. [PIONEER Uriah Smith, DAR 275.1] France invaded Egypt in 1798. France defeated the Egyptian Mamelukes at the Battle of the Pyramids on July 23.
6. [PIONEER Uriah Smith, DAR 275.2] The Ottoman sultan declared war against France on September 2, 1798. Smith identifies Turkey as the king of the north, Egypt as the king of the south, and France as "him."

This view gives verse 40 three distinct actors.

- France is the power described in verses 36-39.
- Egypt is the king of the south.
- Ottoman Turkey is the king of the north.

Atheism describes the character of Revolutionary France.

It does not replace Egypt as the king of the south.

The Battle of the Pyramids was not a war between Turkey and France.

It was a battle between France and the Egyptian Mamelukes.

Turkey declared war against France later in the same year.

### James White: literal Rome

James White argued that Daniel 2, 7, 8, and 11 cover the same history.

He concluded that Rome remains the last power in Daniel 11.

He also warned against firm claims about unfulfilled details.

His view was literal historicism.

He did not change Rome into a philosophy or a mindset.

He expected a concrete Roman power and a real fulfillment.

In his 1877 editorial, he asked if the seat of the beast would move to the United States instead of the Turkish government moving to Palestine.

His expected action was literal occupation in real geography.

### The literal Turkey line

[PIONEER Review and Herald, December 12, 1854, p. 134.18] The writer says that the power that rules Syria is the king of the north. The writer then identifies the Turkish dynasty.

[PIONEER Uriah Smith, DAR 273.3] Smith keeps the titles tied to the original northern and southern territories. He identifies Egypt and Turkey.

[PIONEER Uriah Smith, DAR 277.1] Smith says that the loss of the territorial rule leaves no principle to guide the interpretation.

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, PTUK April 1, 1897, p. 195.4] Waggoner identifies the old northern territory with the Turkish Empire.

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, PTUK April 1, 1897, p. 195.7] Waggoner identifies Turkey as the king of the north in verse 40.

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, ARSH October 2, 1900, p. 632.11] Jones says that the last verses of Daniel 11 refer to Turkey as the king of the north.

[PIONEER S. N. Haskell, SDP 248.1-2] Haskell calls Turkey a national guidepost that stands near the close of Christ's sanctuary work.

[SOP 4T 279.1] Ellen White records that Uriah Smith spoke on the Eastern Question to an attentive audience.

[SOP 4LtMs, Lt 55, 1884, pars. 6-7] Ellen White reports Smith's Eastern Question sermon with approval of his clarity and solemn appeal.

These Ellen White reports are relevant context.

They are not an inspired statement that every detail of Smith's interpretation is correct.

### The seventh trumpet, the plagues, and Armageddon

The mature pioneer position made the seventh trumpet a prophetic period.

[PIONEER James White, SSTR 67.5-6] The seventh angel is not the resurrection trumpet. Its sounding occupies prophetic days.

[PIONEER Uriah Smith, DAR 493.2-3] The seventh trumpet begins in 1844. The mystery of God finishes during its sounding.

[PIONEER J. N. Andrews, TMR 116.2] The plagues fall under the seventh angel. They form the third woe.

[PIONEER S. N. Haskell, BHB 128.2] Armageddon is the final battle that closes earthly scenes.

[PIONEER S. N. Haskell, BHB 128.6] Haskell places Armageddon under the sixth plague.

[PIONEER Uriah Smith, DAR 653.2] Smith places the gathering under the sixth vial and the battle under the seventh.

The pioneers did not agree on every detail.

William Miller held an earlier view of the seventh trumpet and the vials.

The mature Seventh-day Adventist expositors agreed on the main sequence.

The seventh trumpet contains the close of mercy, the literal plagues, the literal gathering to Armageddon, and the final overthrow of the nations.

They did not make Armageddon a philosophy or a mindset.

## Louis F. Were evidence

Louis F. Were published *The King of the North at Jerusalem* in 1949.

He identifies the papacy as a spiritual king of the north.

He states that "when passing over into the Christian era there is an automatic transition from literal to spiritual Babylon."

He extends this rule to Jerusalem, Israel, Rome, the lands, the enemies, and the compass directions.

He says that Daniel 11 changes from literal Rome to spiritual Rome at verses 30-31.

He says that verses 31-45 must describe spiritual Rome and spiritual conflict.

The Daniel 11 video uses the same type of rule at [20:20-20:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vcEs2hnH9CY&t=1220s).

The evidence shows a close method match.

It does not prove that the speakers took the rule directly from Were.

## Main questions for the ministry

1. Where does Daniel 11 state that its kings change into philosophies?
2. Why does the cross change every term from literal to spiritual?
3. Why does this rule not apply to the literal events in verses 23-35?
4. Why does a covenant change for God's people also change Egypt, ships, armies, directions, and territories?
5. Where does Scripture define the king of the south as atheism?
6. Where does Scripture define the king of the north as the papacy in verses 40-45?
7. How does the Daniel 11 method agree with the trumpet statement that the trumpets are not philosophies?
8. How does "no more a literal sanctuary" agree with Hebrews 8:1-2 and Revelation 11:19?
9. How does the view answer James White's literal Rome argument?
10. How does the view answer the literal Turkey line of Smith, Waggoner, Jones, and Haskell?
11. If the papal king ends before Michael stands, how does Romanism pursue the death decree after probation closes?
12. How does the papal kingdom remain to receive the fifth plague?
13. How does the view preserve the pioneer sequence of the seventh trumpet, the plagues, and literal Armageddon?
14. How does the view account for the three distinct actors in Daniel 11:40?
15. How does it account for the Battle of the Pyramids and Turkey's later declaration of war against France in 1798?

## Evidence limits

- The YouTube transcripts are automatic captions.
- Ellen White does not name the final king of the north.
- The pioneer record is not fully uniform.
- James White held a literal Rome view.
- Several later leading pioneer writers held a literal Turkey view.
- The evidence does not support the strict phrase "all pioneers" for every detail.
- William Miller differed from the mature SDA view of the seventh trumpet and the vials.
- Haskell placed Armageddon under the sixth plague.
- Smith placed the gathering under the sixth plague and the battle under the seventh.
- Revelation 16:10 says that the fifth plague strikes the beast's seat and darkens his kingdom.
- The verse does not state that the papacy ceases to exist at that point.
- The evidence does not prove direct dependence on Louis Were.
- The evidence does not identify any later writer as the omega of apostasy.
- The common-root conclusion is a method inference.
