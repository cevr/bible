# Reading 102: Origin, History, and Destiny of Satan

**The Bible gives sin a biography: a birthplace beside the throne, one career, and a fixed execution date. The cherub who covered the mercy seat chose to climb, and every combat since has dropped him one rung lower — heaven, earth, the cross, the pit, ashes. God did not destroy him at the first sin because the universe had to see the rebellion mature and condemn itself; the cross rang his death knell; the fire finishes the sentence. The fire was prepared for the devil — no man enters it except holding his hand.**

---

# Act I — Origin: The Cherub Who Covered

## Sin Did Not Start Here

- **before Eden, heaven** — the first sinners were angels
  - "God spared not the angels that sinned, but cast them down to hell, and delivered them into chains of darkness, to be reserved unto judgment" (2 Pet 2:4)
  - "the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day" (Jude 6)
- **held, not yet judged** — "reserved" twice: they await a verdict already certain
- [→] man did not invent sin. Man imported it. To trace the intruder, go up.

[DYK🔎] "hell" in 2 Pet 2:4 = _tartaroo_ — used once in all Scripture; not the lake of fire, but a condition of restraint and darkness until judgment. The chains are not iron — they are a shrunken sphere (see Act V: each fall shrinks it further).

---

## The Anointed Cherub That Covereth

Where did he start? Higher than any created being ever stood.

- "Thou sealest up the sum, **full of wisdom, and perfect in beauty**" (Eze 28:12)
- "Thou hast been in Eden the garden of God; every precious stone was thy covering" (Eze 28:13)
- "Thou art the **anointed cherub that covereth**; and I have set thee so: thou wast upon the holy mountain of God; thou hast walked up and down in the midst of the stones of fire" (Eze 28:14)
- "Thou wast **perfect in thy ways** from the day that thou wast created, till iniquity was found in thee" (Eze 28:15)

### "That covereth" — sanctuary geography

The title is a station. The sanctuary shows the post:

- "And thou shalt make two cherubims of gold... in the two ends of the mercy seat" (Ex 25:18) — "on the one end... on the other end" (Ex 25:19)
- "the cherubims shall stretch forth their wings on high, **covering the mercy seat** with their wings" (Ex 25:20)
- "thou shalt put the mercy seat above upon the ark; and in the ark thou shalt put the **testimony**" (Ex 25:21)
- "there I will meet with thee, and I will commune with thee **from above the mercy seat, from between the two cherubims**" (Ex 25:22)
- earthly = copy of heavenly: "the example and shadow of heavenly things... the **pattern** shewed to thee in the mount" (Heb 8:5)
- "the Holiest of all... the ark of the covenant... and over it the **cherubims of glory** shadowing the mercyseat" (Heb 9:3-5)
- "The LORD reigneth... he **sitteth between the cherubims**" (Ps 99:1)
- [→] Lucifer's post: wing outstretched over the law, under the glory, beside the throne. **The being who stood nearest the law hated it first.** Rebellion did not break in from outside; it hatched at the mercy seat.

[SOP] "Lucifer in heaven, before his rebellion, was a high and exalted angel, next in honor to God's dear Son." — SR 13.1

[PIONEER: A. T. Jones] "It was one of these bright cherubim, 'full of wisdom and perfect in beauty,' who stood close to the throne of God with outstretched, covering wings covering the mercy-seat... it was one of these exalted ones who forgot his place as creature, and aspired to be equal with God the Creator." — ECE 572.4

[TANGENT] Rev 11:19 — "the temple of God was opened in heaven, and there was seen in his temple the ark of his testament." The ark is still there. The law he rebelled against still lies under the mercy seat — and his post beside it stands vacant.

## Iniquity Was Found — The Five "I Wills"

- **not made a devil — made perfect** — "perfect in thy ways... **till** iniquity was found in thee" (Eze 28:15)
  - "found" — it appeared where nothing explained it. God did not put it there.
- **the inward climb**:
  - "Thine heart was **lifted up because of thy beauty**, thou hast corrupted thy wisdom by reason of thy brightness" (Eze 28:17)
  - "How art thou fallen from heaven, **O Lucifer, son of the morning!**" (Isa 14:12)
  - "For thou hast said in thine heart, **I will** ascend into heaven, **I will** exalt my throne above the stars of God: **I will** sit also upon the mount of the congregation, in the sides of the north: **I will** ascend above the heights of the clouds; **I will** be like the most High" (Isa 14:13-14)
- **the outward spread** — "By the multitude of thy **merchandise**... thou hast sinned: therefore I will cast thee as profane out of the mountain of God: and I will destroy thee, O covering cherub" (Eze 28:16)
  - merchandise: trafficking — peddling disaffection angel to angel

[SOP] "Little by little, Lucifer came to indulge a desire for self-exaltation." — GC 494.1

[SOP] "Lucifer was envious and jealous of Jesus Christ." — SR 14.1

[DYK🔎] Lucifer = "light bearer"; "son of the morning" = day star. Five "I wills" against one "I have set thee so" (Eze 28:14). Sin's whole grammar is the first person future.

- [→] sin has no origin story that explains it. The Bible refuses to give one:

[SOP] "Sin is an intruder, for whose presence no reason can be given. It is mysterious, unaccountable; to excuse it is to defend it." — GC 492.2

[Q] **"If God knew Lucifer would fall, why create him — why allow sin at all?"**
→ Because the alternative was a universe that obeys without loving. "God desires from all His creatures the service of love... He takes no pleasure in a forced allegiance, and to all He grants freedom of will, that they may render Him voluntary service" (GC 493.2). Love requires freedom; freedom includes the possibility of this. The cross (Rom 5:8) and the clean end (Nah 1:9) prove the risk was not taken lightly — nor taken twice.

---

## Heaven's Long Patience

- God's first response to the first sin: not a thunderbolt — an embassy.

[SOP] "In great mercy, according to His divine character, God bore long with Lucifer. The spirit of discontent and disaffection had never before been known in heaven. It was a new element, strange, mysterious, unaccountable." — PP 39.1

- [→] mercy exhausted persuasion before justice drew the sword. Hold that pattern — it repeats at the end of the millennium, when the whole universe watches the last appeal refused.

# Act II — The War: Cast Out

## War in Heaven

- "And there appeared another wonder in heaven; and behold a **great red dragon**" (Rev 12:3)
- "his tail drew the **third part of the stars** of heaven, and did cast them to the earth" (Rev 12:4)
  - stars = angels by the Bible's own key: "the seven stars are the **angels** of the seven churches" (Rev 1:20)
- "And there was **war in heaven**: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon; and the dragon fought and his angels, and **prevailed not**; neither was their place found any more in heaven" (Rev 12:7-8)
- "the great dragon was **cast out**, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world: he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him" (Rev 12:9)
- Christ the eyewitness: "**I beheld Satan as lightning fall from heaven**" (Luke 10:18)

[SOP] "Then there was war in heaven. The Son of God, the Prince of heaven, and His loyal angels engaged in conflict with the archrebel and those who united with him. The Son of God and true, loyal angels prevailed; and Satan and his sympathizers were expelled from heaven." — SR 19.1

[PIONEER: U. Smith] "To prove that Michael is Christ, see Jude 9; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; John 5:28, 29; and that this was a special time of warfare between him and Satan need not be argued." — DAR 513.2

[DYK🔎] Michael = "Who is like God?" — a name that is itself the answer to "I will be like the most High" (Isa 14:14). The war's two battle cries are the same sentence: one as ambition, one as a question with only one answer.

### The casting down comes in installments

Smith reads Rev 12:7-9 at the first advent; the first expulsion came before Eden (2 Pet 2:4). No contradiction — the "casting down" is a staircase, and he never climbs back up:

- **From heaven's councils** → to earth (first rebellion; 2 Pet 2:4)
- **From the heavenly courts as accuser** → shut out at the cross (Rev 12:10; John 12:31)
- **From the nations** → bound to a dead planet (Rev 20:1-3)
- **From existence** → ashes (Eze 28:18)

[PIONEER: U. Smith] "On each successive occasion, we behold a regularly increasing limitation of his power. He falls a degree lower in every succeeding combat." — DAR 515.1

[→] read his history as a descent with four landings. Every act that follows stands on one of these steps.

---

## Why Not Destroy Him Then?

[Q] **"Why didn't God annihilate Satan at the first sin and spare the universe six thousand years of misery?"**
→ Because an executed accuser leaves his accusations alive.

[SOP] "Had he been immediately blotted from existence, they would have served God from fear rather than from love. The influence of the deceiver would not have been fully destroyed, nor would the spirit of rebellion have been utterly eradicated. Evil must be permitted to come to maturity." — GC 498.3

[SOP] "Therefore God permitted him to demonstrate the nature of his claims, to show the working out of his proposed changes in the divine law. His own work must condemn him... The whole universe must see the deceiver unmasked." — PP 42.2

[ILL] A judge who shoots the defendant mid-trial ends the case but convinces no one — least of all the jury. God let the defendant publish his whole case, run his experiment on a world, and write his own verdict in blood and graves. When the sentence finally falls, every juror in the universe signs it.

[Q] **"Couldn't he have repented and been taken back?"**
→ He wished it — the wish was not repentance. "But no; heaven must not be placed in jeopardy... for sin originated with him, and the seeds of rebellion were within him" (EW 146.1). Sorrow for consequences is not surrender of the "I will."

---

## His Names Are His Record

One verse carries the rap sheet — "that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan, which **deceiveth the whole world**" (Rev 12:9):

- **Dragon** — open ferocity: the persecutor
- **Old serpent** — Eden's disguise: the deceiver (Gen 3:1)
- **Devil** (_diabolos_) — the slanderer: "the **accuser of our brethren**... which accused them before our God day and night" (Rev 12:10)
- **Satan** — the adversary: opponent of God and His people
- [→] four names, one career: deceive, accuse, oppose, devour. And note the fire's address label:
  - "Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, **prepared for the devil and his angels**" (Matt 25:41)
  - prepared for him — never for you. Men enter only by refusing to let go of him.

# Act III — History: One Long War on Earth

## Eden — The First Lie

- "Now the **serpent** was more subtil than any beast of the field... And he said unto the woman, **Yea, hath God said**, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden?" (Gen 3:1)
- the woman's rail: "We may eat of the fruit of the trees of the garden: but of the fruit of the tree which is in the midst of the garden, God hath said, Ye shall not eat of it, neither shall ye touch it, lest ye die" (Gen 3:2-3)
- the lie: "**Ye shall not surely die**: for God doth know that in the day ye eat thereof, then your eyes shall be opened, and ye shall be as gods, knowing good and evil" (Gen 3:4-5)
- the fall: "she took of the fruit thereof, and did eat, and gave also unto her husband with her; and he did eat" (Gen 3:6)

[SOP] "In order to accomplish his work unperceived, Satan chose to employ as his medium the serpent—a disguise well adapted for his purpose of deception." — PP 53.4

- **mark the lie** — "ye shall not surely die" = the natural immortality of the soul. His first doctrine on earth is his last one too (Act IV: spiritualism stands on it).
- **the sentence carried a promise**:
  - "Because thou hast done this, thou art cursed" (Gen 3:14)
  - "I will put **enmity** between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; **it shall bruise thy head**, and thou shalt bruise his heel" (Gen 3:15)
  - [→] war declared in Eden; the Head-bruising scheduled. The rest of the Bible is Gen 3:15 unfolding.

### Christ's own biography of him

- "He that committeth sin is of the devil; for the devil **sinneth from the beginning**. For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that he might **destroy the works of the devil**" (1 John 3:8)
- "Ye are of your father the devil... He was a **murderer from the beginning**, and abode not in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a **liar, and the father of it**" (John 8:44)
- "the beginning" = earth's dawn: "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth" (Gen 1:1) — from mankind's first chapter he has been at this
- [→] two trades, one tradesman: lies that lead to death, death blamed on God. Every grave preaches his gospel; that is why Christ calls him murderer, not merely deceiver.

---

## The Accuser at Court

He files charges in heaven's court — Scripture shows him at the bar:

- "the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and **Satan came also among them**... From going to and fro in the earth" (Job 1:6-7)
- "Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the LORD, and **Satan standing at his right hand to resist him**. And the LORD said unto Satan, The LORD rebuke thee, O Satan... is not this a **brand plucked out of the fire**?" (Zech 3:1-2)
- "the accuser of our brethren... accused them before our God **day and night**" (Rev 12:10)

[SOP] "Never is one received into the family of God without exciting the determined resistance of the enemy." — PK 585.2

- **the answer to every charge** — not your record, His:
  - "There is therefore now **no condemnation** to them which are in Christ Jesus" (Rom 8:1)
  - "Who shall lay any thing to the charge of God's elect? It is God that justifieth" (Rom 8:33)
  - "Who is he that condemneth? It is Christ that died, yea rather, that is risen again, who is even at the right hand of God, who also **maketh intercession** for us" (Rom 8:34)
- [→] two figures at the right hand: Satan at Joshua's, to resist (Zech 3:1); Christ at God's, to intercede (Rom 8:34). Every soul lives between those two right hands.

---

## The Wilderness — The Second Adam

- "And immediately the Spirit driveth him into the wilderness. And he was there in the wilderness forty days, **tempted of Satan**; and was with the wild beasts; and the angels ministered unto him" (Mark 1:12-13)
- Matthew gives the three assaults (Matt 4:1-11):
  - **appetite** — "command that these stones be made bread" (Matt 4:3) → "It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by **every word** that proceedeth out of the mouth of God" (Matt 4:4)
  - **presumption** — "cast thyself down: for it is written..." (Matt 4:5-6) → "It is written again, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God" (Matt 4:7)
  - **the world** — "All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt fall down and worship me" (Matt 4:8-9) → "Get thee hence, Satan... Thou shalt **worship the Lord thy God, and him only** shalt thou serve" (Matt 4:10)
  - "Then the devil **leaveth him**, and, behold, angels came and ministered unto him" (Matt 4:11)

[SOP] "Just where the ruin began, the work of our redemption must begin. As by the indulgence of appetite Adam fell, so by the denial of appetite Christ must overcome." — DA 117.3

- **first Adam**: a garden, every need met — fell at one fruit
- **second Adam**: a wilderness, forty days fasted — stood on "It is written" three times
- [→] Satan's sword is a doubt; Christ's sword is a text. He never argued, never explained, never performed — He quoted.

[SOP] "There was in Him nothing that responded to Satan's sophistry. He did not consent to sin. Not even by a thought did He yield to temptation. So it may be with us." — DA 123.3

- and the victory is transferable:
  - "we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities; but was **in all points tempted like as we are, yet without sin**" (Heb 4:15)
  - "I can do **all things** through Christ which strengtheneth me" (Phil 4:13)

## The Cross — His Death Knell

- "**Now** is the judgment of this world: **now** shall the prince of this world be **cast out**" (John 12:31)
- "the prince of this world cometh, and **hath nothing in me**" (John 14:30)
- "that through death he might **destroy him that had the power of death**, that is, the devil" (Heb 2:14)
- "having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a **shew of them openly**, triumphing over them in it" (Col 2:15)
- Gen 3:15 lands: the heel was bruised at Calvary; the Head-wound is fatal and cannot be dressed

[SOP] "Satan saw that his disguise was torn away... He had revealed himself as a murderer. By shedding the blood of the Son of God, he had uprooted himself from the sympathies of the heavenly beings." — DA 761.2

[SOP] "In the Saviour's expiring cry, 'It is finished,' the death knell of Satan was rung. The great controversy which had been so long in progress was then decided, and the final eradication of evil was made certain." — GC 503.3

- [→] the war was **decided** at the cross; it is not yet **concluded**. A.D. 31 is the verdict; the millennium's end is the execution. Between the two, the condemned works "because he knoweth that he hath but a **short time**" (Rev 12:12).

### What the cross answered — the charges and the exhibits

Satan's three accusations against the government of God, each answered in evidence:

- charge: *God is arbitrary, unloving* → exhibit: "God so loved the world, that he **gave** his only begotten Son" (John 3:16); "while we were **yet sinners**, Christ died for us" (Rom 5:8)
- charge: *the law is unjust, a yoke* → exhibit: "the law is **holy**, and the commandment holy, and just, and good" (Rom 7:12); "the law of the LORD is **perfect, converting the soul**" (Ps 19:7)
- charge: *obedience is impossible for creatures* → exhibit: "I can do all things **through Christ** which strengtheneth me" (Phil 4:13); a last-day people who "keep the commandments of God, and the **faith of Jesus**" (Rev 14:12)
- [→] the remnant is the closing exhibit. That is why the dragon hates them specifically.

## War on the Remnant

- his defeat published in heaven: "**Now is come salvation**... for the accuser of our brethren is cast down" (Rev 12:10)
- the saints' war manual, one verse: "they **overcame him** by the **blood of the Lamb**, and by the **word of their testimony**; and they loved not their lives unto the death" (Rev 12:11)
- "Woe to the inhabiters of the earth... for the devil is come down unto you, having **great wrath**, because he knoweth that he hath but a **short time**" (Rev 12:12)
- "when the dragon saw that he was cast unto the earth, he **persecuted the woman**" (Rev 12:13) — the church, hunted through the ages
- "the dragon was **wroth with the woman**, and went to make war with the **remnant of her seed**, which keep the **commandments of God**, and have the **testimony of Jesus Christ**" (Rev 12:17)
  - the testimony identified: "the testimony of Jesus is the **spirit of prophecy**" (Rev 19:10)
- [→] the last battlefield is marked with two flags: the commandments (including the Sabbath he has buried) and the prophetic gift (which he counterfeits). Find those two marks, find the war's front line.

# Act IV — The Last Deceptions

## An Angel of Light

- "no marvel; for **Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light**" (2 Cor 11:14)
- his end-time arsenal is miracle, not argument:
  - "he doeth great wonders, so that he maketh **fire come down from heaven** on the earth in the sight of men, and **deceiveth them** that dwell on the earth by the means of those **miracles**" (Rev 13:13-14)
  - "they are the **spirits of devils, working miracles**, which go forth unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty" (Rev 16:14) — issuing from dragon, beast, false prophet (Rev 16:13)
  - "false Christs, and false prophets... shall shew great signs and wonders; insomuch that, **if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect**" (Matt 24:24)
- [→] a miracle proves power, never truth. Fire from heaven is on **both** résumés (1 Kings 18 / Rev 13:13).

[SOP] "Through the two great errors, the immortality of the soul and Sunday sacredness, Satan will bring the people under his deceptions. While the former lays the foundation of spiritualism, the latter creates a bond of sympathy with Rome." — GC 588.1

[SOP] "The foundation of his work was laid by the assurance given to Eve in Eden: 'Ye shall not surely die.'... Little by little he has prepared the way for his masterpiece of deception in the development of spiritualism." — GC 561.2

- **first lie = last lie.** Eden's "ye shall not surely die" (Gen 3:4) matures into séance, spirit guide, and the ghost of a "departed loved one" preaching heresies at your bedside.

[SOP] "We must be prepared to withstand them with the Bible truth that the dead know not anything and that they who thus appear are the spirits of devils." — GC 560.1

[Q] **"Why would God allow deception that strong?"**
→ He doesn't send it first — it follows a choice. "Because they **received not the love of the truth**, that they might be saved. And for this cause God shall send them **strong delusion**, that they should believe a lie: that they all might be damned who believed not the truth, but had pleasure in unrighteousness" (2 Thess 2:10-12). Refused light becomes darkness by natural law; the delusion is the harvest of the refusal. And the Lord ends it: "then shall that Wicked be revealed, whom the Lord shall **consume with the spirit of his mouth**" (2 Thess 2:8), his coming "after the working of Satan with all power and signs and **lying wonders**" (2 Thess 2:9).

---

## The Crowning Act

[SOP] "As the crowning act in the great drama of deception, Satan himself will personate Christ... a majestic being of dazzling brightness, resembling the description of the Son of God given by John in the Revelation." — GC 624.2

- healing, blessing, a gentle voice, "heavenly" truths mixed in — the disguise of Eden perfected
- **how the remnant is not taken**, two tests:
  1. **doctrine** — "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is **no light in them**" (Isa 8:20) — the false christ blesses Sunday and the beast's worshipers; the Book convicts him
  2. **manner** — "Satan is not permitted to counterfeit the **manner** of Christ's advent" (GC 625.2): every eye, sky-wide, trumpet-loud (Matt 24:27) — anything local, quiet, or walking is not Him

[SOP] "None but those who have fortified the mind with the truths of the Bible will stand through the last great conflict." — GC 593.2

- **standing orders while the lion prowls**:
  - "Be **sober**, be **vigilant**; because your adversary the devil, as a **roaring lion**, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour: whom **resist stedfast in the faith**" (1 Pet 5:8-9)
  - "**Submit** yourselves therefore to God. **Resist** the devil, and he will **flee** from you. **Draw nigh** to God, and he will draw nigh to you" (James 4:7-8)
  - [→] submit first, resist second — resistance without surrender to God is Peter swinging a sword before Peter denying. The order of James 4:7 is the whole science of victory.

# Act V — Destiny: The Pit, the Fire, the Ashes

## Bound a Thousand Years

- "And I saw an angel come down from heaven, having the key of the **bottomless pit** and a great chain in his hand. And he laid hold on the dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil, and Satan, and **bound him a thousand years**, and cast him into the bottomless pit, and shut him up, and set a seal upon him, that he should **deceive the nations no more**, till the thousand years should be fulfilled" (Rev 20:1-3)

### The pit is this earth, desolate

[PIONEER: U. Smith] "The word there rendered deep is the same word that is here rendered bottomless pit; so that passage might have been translated, 'Darkness was upon the face of the abyss, or bottomless pit.'" — DAR 690.3 (on Gen 1:2)

- _abussos_: the earth's own word for itself in chaos — at the Second Coming the saints ascend, the wicked fall (2 Thess 2:8), and the planet reverts toward Gen 1:2
- **the chain is circumstance**: a deceiver with no one to deceive

[SOP] "It is in this sense that he is bound: there are none remaining, upon whom he can exercise his power. He is wholly cut off from the work of deception and ruin which for so many centuries has been his sole delight." — GC 659.1

[PIONEER: U. Smith] "Thus is he bound, being condemned throughout this period to a state of hopeless inactivity." — DAR 691.1

[SOP] "Here is to be the home of Satan with his evil angels for a thousand years... to wander up and down over the broken surface of the earth and see the effects of his rebellion." — EW 290.1

[DYK🔎] the sanctuary type sealed this destination in advance: on the Day of Atonement the high priest "shall lay both his hands upon the head of the live goat, and confess over him all the iniquities of the children of Israel... And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities **unto a land not inhabited**" (Lev 16:21-22). "As the priest, in removing the sins from the sanctuary, confessed them upon the head of the scapegoat, so Christ will place all these sins upon Satan, the originator and instigator of sin" (GC 485.3). The originator carries what he originated — to an uninhabited land. The millennium is the antitype's wilderness.

[TANGENT] a thousand years of enforced audit: the mind that invented sin, chained to the wreckage sin made, with nothing left to do but review it (SR 24.3 gives the preview: "Satan now beheld the terrible results of his rebellion").

---

## Loosed — The Last Campaign

- "when the thousand years are expired, **Satan shall be loosed** out of his prison" (Rev 20:7)
  - what looses him: the second resurrection — subjects again
- "and shall go out to **deceive the nations**... Gog and Magog, to gather them together to battle: the number of whom is as the sand of the sea" (Rev 20:8)
- "they went up on the breadth of the earth, and **compassed the camp of the saints about, and the beloved city**" (Rev 20:9)

[SOP] "As the wicked dead are raised and he sees the vast multitudes upon his side, his hopes revive, and he determines not to yield the great controversy." — GC 663.1

- [→] his last act is his first act — marshal a host against the throne. Six thousand years, and the "I will" has not learned one thing. That fixedness **is** the case for his sentence.

### The sentence confessed before it falls

- every case reviewed before the universe; then, from the archrebel himself:

[SOP] "His accusations against the mercy and justice of God are now silenced. The reproach which he has endeavored to cast upon Jehovah rests wholly upon himself. And now Satan bows down and confesses the justice of his sentence." — GC 670.2

- [→] the trial God refused to shortcut in Act II closes with the defendant conceding the verdict. Then — only then — the fire.

---

## Ashes — An Utter End

- "and **fire came down from God out of heaven, and devoured them**" (Rev 20:9)
- "the devil that deceived them was cast into the **lake of fire**... and shall be tormented day and night for ever and ever" (Rev 20:10)

[Q] **"Doesn't 'for ever and ever' mean eternal torment?"**
→ Bible "for ever": as long as a thing lasts (Jonah's "for ever" in the fish = three days). Watch the outcome-words Scripture stacks on this fire — they all end:
- "the wicked shall **perish**... they shall **consume**; into **smoke** shall they consume away" (Ps 37:20)
