## 1. What the Pioneers Actually Denied — John 17:3

> They denied one thing: that the Father, Son, and Spirit are one indivisible
> substance. They never denied that the Son is God.

The charge under review is not one charge but a bundle. Separate it, and most
of it falls apart before an answer is needed.

**The denial was narrow and it was named — three persons in one essence:**

- _Jn 17:3._ "And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent." — the Son is distinguished from "the only true God," by the Son's own mouth
- _1 Cor 8:6._ "But to us there is but one God, the Father, of whom are all things... and one Lord Jesus Christ, by whom are all things" — one God AND one Lord; Paul does not collapse them
- _Jn 14:28._ "my Father is greater than I" — a text the pioneers refused to explain away

[PIONEER J. White, LIFIN 343.2] "Neither are the Father and the Son parts of the 'three-one God.' They are two distinct beings, yet one in the design and accomplishment of redemption" — the whole objection in one sentence

[PIONEER J. H. Waggoner, AERS 164.3] "They take the denial of a trinity to be equivalent to a denial of the divinity of Christ" — the exact confusion ADL makes

[PIONEER J. H. Waggoner, AERS 164.3] "Were that the case, we should cling to the doctrine of a trinity as tenaciously as any can; but it is not the case" — they would hold it if it cost nothing

[WB]: ONE CIRCLE "3 persons, 1 substance" → crossed out. TWO CIRCLES "Father" + "Son," a line between labeled "one in design." Only the first is denied.

[Q] So what precisely did they reject?
→ The *metaphysical* claim (one essence, three persons) — AERS 164.2; never the *dignity* claim (Christ is God) — AERS 164.3

[→] Denying the essence formula is not denying the Son's Godhood — and the pioneers said so in the same breath.

## 2. "They Denied Christ's Divinity" — Col 2:9

> The charge dies on contact with the primary sources. The pioneers asserted
> Christ's full divinity in the plainest words available to them.

**The claim is that anti-trinitarianism reduces Christ. Hear them:**

- _Col 2:9._ "For in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily." — the pioneers' own proof-text, quoted by Smith and Waggoner alike
- _Jn 1:1._ "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."
- _Heb 1:8._ "But unto the Son he saith, Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever" — the Father addresses the Son as God

[PIONEER Hull, ARSH November 10, 1859, page 193.8] "Do you believe in the divinity of Christ? Most unquestionably we do" — asked and answered, 1859, in the denomination's own paper

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, CHR 14.1] "since there is none good but God, and Christ is good, it follows that Christ is God" — 1890

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, CHR 15.3] "This is most absolute and unequivocal testimony to the fact that Christ possesses by nature all the attributes of Divinity"

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, GCDB February 5, 1893, page 147.4] "Christ the righteousness of God, Christ the life of God; Christ is God! That is the message that now we are to give to the world"

[PIONEER U. Smith, LUJ 3.2] "So we are asked by some to look unto Jesus as only a man... not the divine Son of the Eternal Father and one with the Father in essential perfection" — Smith names this as the *enemy's* work, 1898

| The charge | The record |
| --- | --- |
| Denied Christ is God | "Christ is God!" — Jones 1893 |
| Reduced His divinity | "all the fulness of the Godhead" — Smith 1898 |
| Made Him a mere man | "Most unquestionably we do" — Hull 1859 |
| Withheld worship | "let all... worship the Son" — CHR 24.1 |

[Q] But weren't these late statements, after they had been corrected?
→ Hull's is 1859 — before Ellen White wrote a line on the subject; the position is original, not repaired

[→] ADL argues against a position no named pioneer ever held.

## 3. "They Made Christ a Created Being" — Rev 3:14

> Uriah Smith held that view in 1865 and repudiated it in print. Waggoner
> refuted it at length. The mature pioneer position is *begotten, not created.*

**Rev 3:14 is the whole case for the charge — and the pioneers answered it:**

- _Rev 3:14._ "These things saith the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the beginning of the creation of God;" — the alleged proof
- _Col 1:16._ "for by him were all things created" — if He made all things, He is not among them
- _Micah 5:2._ "whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting"
- _Jn 8:42._ "I proceeded forth and came from God" — proceeded, not produced
- _Jn 5:26._ "as the Father hath life in himself; so hath he given to the Son to have life in himself" — life inhering, not borrowed

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, CHR 21.2] "He is begotten, not created. As to when He was begotten, it is not for us to inquire" — the formula exactly

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, CHR 21.1] "if He created everything that was ever created and existed before all created things, it is evident that He Himself is not among created things"

[PIONEER U. Smith, LUJ 10.1] "by some divine impulse or process, not creation, known only to Omniscience, and possible only to Omnipotence, the Son of God appeared" — Smith, 1898, explicitly excluding creation

[PIONEER U. Smith, LUJ 12.1] "thus degrading him to the level of a created being" — Smith names the created-being reading as a degradation, 1898

[PIONEER U. Smith, LUJ 12.1] "present him, not as the 'beginning,' but as the beginner, of the creation of God" — arche means headship, not first item made

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, CHR 22.1] "He is of the very substance and nature of God and possesses by birth all the attributes of God" — substance language, from an anti-trinitarian

[Q] Smith *did* write in 1865 that Christ was created. Doesn't that prove the charge?
→ It proves one man revised one error — and the revision is the pioneer position, LUJ 10.1; ADL quotes the discarded draft, not the settled teaching

[→] "Begotten, not created" is the Nicene phrase itself — the pioneers kept the distinction that Arius denied.

## 4. "Ellen White Was Always a Trinitarian" — Heb 1:3

> She was Methodist by upbringing and never taught the Methodist article. Her
> published language is the pioneers' language, not the creed's.

**ADL's argument is biographical: she was Methodist, therefore trinitarian.
Her writings are the test, not her childhood pew:**

- _Heb 1:3._ "Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person" — *his person*: the Father has a person of which the Son is the image
- _Heb 1:8._ "Thy throne, O God" — the Father speaking *to* another

[SOP MR760 9.5] "theories that would remove the pillars of our faith concerning the sanctuary or concerning the personality of God or of Christ, are working as blind men" — 1905; *personality* is the pillar

[SOP SR 13.1] "Lucifer in heaven, before his rebellion, was a high and exalted angel, next in honor to God's dear Son" — the chapter ADL reads as demoting Christ in fact ranks Him above all creation

[PIONEER J. White, PERGO 3.1] "Deny their personality, and there is not a distinct idea in these quotations from Daniel" — two personages, or the vision is incoherent

The Methodist Article I she is said to have held reads that God is "without
body or parts." Compare:

[PIONEER J. White, PERGO 7.4] "What is God? He is material, organized intelligence, possessing both body and parts. Man is in his image." — the direct contradiction of the Methodist article

| Methodist Article I | Ellen White / pioneers |
| --- | --- |
| Without body or parts | Body and parts — PERGO 7.4 |
| One substance, three persons | Two distinct beings — LIFIN 343.2 |
| Impassible deity | Deity suffered — AERS 167.5 |

[Q] But she called the Holy Spirit "the third person of the Godhead."
→ She also wrote that the Spirit's nature "is a mystery" men "cannot explain" — AA 52.1; she affirms personality, declines the essence formula

[→] If she was a trinitarian, she never once wrote the sentence that would make her one.

## 5. "Ellen White Changed Her View" — Heb 13:8

> The claim requires a contradiction. ADL's own panel could not produce one —
> they produced a *circumstance*, and then read a change into it.

**The disputed passage is Spirit of Prophecy vol. 1, ch. 1 — the conferring
of honor. Read what she says it means:**

- _Heb 13:8._ "Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever."
- _Phil 2:9._ "Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him" — Scripture itself has a post-cross exaltation of One who was already God; exaltation language does not imply prior lack

[SOP PP 38.1] "There had been no change in the position or authority of Christ... But this had been the same from the beginning" — her own gloss on her own passage

[SOP 5BC 1126.4] "Christ was God essentially, and in the highest sense. He was with God from all eternity" — 1906

[SOP DA 530.3] "In Christ is life, original, unborrowed, underived" — 1898

The structure is the same in Scripture: a public declaration of a standing
position, occasioned by a challenge.

- chain: Mt 3:17 (this is my beloved Son) → Mt 17:5 (said again at transfiguration) → Phil 2:9 (highly exalted after the cross) → Jn 17:5 (the glory I *had* with thee)

[Q] Doesn't "devolved" and "conferred" prove a new status?
→ PP 38.1 answers in her own words: the *statement* was new, the *position* "had been the same from the beginning"

[Q] Then why did ADL's panel say she was inconsistent?
→ They read a narrative occasion as an ontological change — and skipped the sentence where she denies exactly that

[→] Her defenders in that debate quoted PP 38.1 correctly — and then drew the opposite conclusion from it than the pioneers did.

## 6. "Progressive Truth Means the Pioneers Were Corrected" — Prov 4:18

> Progressive light runs forward out of error, not backward into it. The
> movement's direction was *away* from the creeds, on every other point.

**Test the claim by its own rule:**

- _Prov 4:18._ "But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day."
- _Dan 12:4._ "knowledge shall be increased" — the ADL panel's own text

The pioneers came out of trinitarian churches — Bates a Christian Connexion
man from a Congregational background, Loughborough from the Methodists,
E. J. Waggoner and Canright from Baptist stock. On the Sabbath, the state of
the dead, the sanctuary, and the law, light moved them *out* of what those
churches taught. On the Godhead the claim is that light moved them *back in*.

| Doctrine | Direction of the light |
| --- | --- |
| Sabbath | Out of the creeds |
| State of the dead | Out of the creeds |
| Sanctuary / 1844 | Out of the creeds |
| Law and gospel | Out of the creeds |
| Godhead (ADL's claim) | Back into the creeds |

[SOP MR760 9.5] "Those who seek to remove the old landmarks are not holding fast" — the warning names the personality of God as a landmark

[SOP CW 30.1] "there was much talk about standing by the old landmarks. But there was evidence they knew not what the old landmarks were" — the caution cuts both ways; define the landmark from her list, not from sentiment

[RQ] If the Spirit led them correctly out of Babylon on five doctrines, on what principle did He lead them back on the sixth?

[Q] But Prescott and Jones later used trinitarian-sounding language.
→ Jones in 1907 called the Spirit "a person" while Jones in 1893 preached "Christ is God!" — the divinity was never the variable; the essence formula was

[→] Progressive truth is an argument the pioneers used *against* the creeds; it cannot be turned to restore them without reversing the rule.

## 7. "Two Beings Is Polytheism" — 1 Cor 8:6

> Scripture itself names two and calls the count one. The charge indicts Paul
> before it indicts the pioneers.

**Paul's own arithmetic:**

- _1 Cor 8:6._ "But to us there is but one God, the Father, of whom are all things, and we in him; and one Lord Jesus Christ, by whom are all things, and we by him." — two named, monotheism asserted in the same verse
- _Jn 10:30._ "I and my Father are one." — the unity Christ claims
- _Jn 17:11._ "that they may be one, as we are" — He defines His own oneness by the disciples' oneness

[PIONEER J. White, LIFIN 343.2] "This prayer did not contemplate one disciple with twelve heads, but twelve disciples, made one in object and effort" — Christ's own definition of the oneness

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, CHR 19.1] "Let no one imagine that we would exalt Christ at the expense of the Father... for their interests are one"

The unity of the Godhead is unity of nature, will, and glory under one
source — the Father. Deny the source and you get three unoriginated deities;
that is the tritheism the pioneers actually warned about.

| Position | One God because... |
| --- | --- |
| Pioneer | One source: the Father — 1 Cor 8:6 |
| Creedal | One substance shared three ways |
| Tritheism | (no unifying principle) |

[Q] But you worship the Son — isn't that a second object of worship?
→ Yes, and Scripture commands it — Heb 1:6; CHR 24.1: "let all, with the angels in heaven, worship the Son"

[→] The pioneers' monotheism is Paul's monotheism: one God the Father, one Lord Jesus Christ, and no apology for the second clause.

## 8. "The Word 'Trinity' Appears in Haskell and Wilcox" — Jn 10:30

> A word is not an argument. ADL's own panel conceded that James White's
> attack was aimed at "a very specific kind of Trinity" — then refused the
> same courtesy to Haskell.

**The panel's method applied consistently:**

When James White wrote against "the three-one God," ADL said: he meant a
particular caricature, not the real doctrine. When Haskell used the word
"Trinity" approvingly, ADL said: the word means what it means. Both cannot
stand.

[PIONEER J. White, ARSH November 5, 1861, page 184.4] "There is a sense in which they are one, but not one person, as claimed by Trinitarians" — he names the point of denial precisely: *not one person*

[PIONEER J. White, LIFIN 343.2] "They are two distinct beings" — the mature statement, unretracted

James White's later softening, correctly quoted by ADL, says Adventists hold
Christ's divinity "so nearly with the Trinitarians that we apprehend no trial
here." Read it whole: he is saying the *divinity* was never in dispute —
which is this study's thesis, from the mouth of the man ADL cites against it.

[Q] Wilcox's 1913 statement says "the divine Trinity" while Ellen White lived.
→ It was an editorial in a paper, not a vote of the church; the Fundamental Principles — the actual denominational statement — say "there is one God, a personal, spiritual being" and "there is one Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the eternal Father" (FPSDA 1.2, 1.3)

[Q] Doesn't 1 John 5:7 settle it?
→ It is absent from every Greek manuscript before the 16th century — the pioneers argued from texts they could defend

[→] Cite the word if you like; the pioneers asked what the word was made to carry, and refused only the load.

## 9. The Cost of the Trade — Phil 2:8

> The pioneers' objection was never abstract. They held that the essence
> formula empties the cross — because a God who cannot die did not die.

**This is the argument ADL never engaged:**

- _Phil 2:8._ "he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross."
- _Acts 20:28._ "the church of God, which he hath purchased with his own blood."
- _1 Cor 15:17._ "if Christ be not raised, your faith is vain"

[PIONEER J. H. Waggoner, AERS 164] "DOCTRINE OF A TRINITY SUBVERSIVE OF THE ATONEMENT" — the chapter title states the burden

[PIONEER J. H. Waggoner, AERS 165.1] "if the manhood only died, the sacrifice was only human. And so far as the vicarious death of Christ is concerned, this is Socinianism"

[PIONEER Hull, ARSH November 17, 1859, page 201.12] "Then it was only the humanity that died and we have only a human sacrifice"

The standard trinitarian writers Waggoner quotes say it plainly: "as God, he
could not die" (Buck); "Trinitarians do not hold to the sufferings or death of
divinity" (Mattison). Waggoner's reply is that this makes the offering human
and the atonement bankrupt.

| If deity cannot die | The pioneers' answer |
| --- | --- |
| Only the man died | Then the offering was human — AERS 165.1 |
| Divinity withdrew at the cross | Then Christ lived after God left — ARSH 1859 p201.12 |
| Two natures, one suffers | "we have only a human sacrifice" |

[SOP DA 530.3] "In Christ is life, original, unborrowed, underived" — the life that was laid down was His own, not on loan

[Q] So anti-trinitarianism *raises* the value of the cross?
→ That was precisely the claim — AERS 164.3: they would hold the trinity "as tenaciously as any" if it did not cost them "the dignity of the sacrifice"

[→] ADL argued that the pioneers diminished Christ; the pioneers argued they were the ones defending what His death was worth.

## 10. Appeal

The question is not whether men in 1859 were infallible. They were not, and
they said so. The question is whether the charge is true — and the charge is
that to deny three persons in one substance is to deny that Jesus is God.

Every pioneer named in those three videos answered that charge in print,
before it was made:

- Hull, 1859: "Do you believe in the divinity of Christ? Most unquestionably we do."
- J. H. Waggoner, 1884: "we firmly believe in the divinity of Christ."
- E. J. Waggoner, 1890: "it follows that Christ is God."
- Jones, 1893: "Christ is God!"
- Smith, 1898: "the fulness of the Godhead bodily."

And Ellen White, across sixty years, never wrote a line that unsaid any of it.

- _Jn 5:23._ "That all men should honour the Son, even as they honour the Father."
- _Heb 1:6._ "And let all the angels of God worship him."

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, CHR 24.1] "let all, with the angels in heaven, worship the Son, having no fear that they are worshiping and serving the creature instead of the Creator"

**Do not accept a charge against the fathers of this movement that their own
books refute on the page.** Read them. Then decide.

- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them."
