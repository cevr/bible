# Why We Need Christ: Human Nature, Sin, and Salvation

**We cannot earn pardon or create a new heart; Christ saves us by grace and gives us power to obey.**

KJV Bible study with Ellen White and E. J. Waggoner. Scripture establishes the teaching. The historical witnesses explain their reading.

## MAP

1. Made for life with God — Genesis 2:7
2. The fall and our present condition — Romans 5:12
3. We need a new heart — John 3:3-7
4. The law shows our need — Romans 3:19-24

6. Religious effort cannot purchase acceptance — Luke 18:9-14

## 1. Made for life with God — Genesis 2:7

> God made human beings good and dependent on Him for life.

- _Genesis 2:7._ “And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.” — God gives life; man becomes a living soul.
- _Genesis 1:27._ “So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.” — Human beings bear God’s image.
- _Genesis 1:31._ “And God saw every thing that he had made, and, behold, it was very good. And the evening and the morning were the sixth day.” — Sin was not part of God’s good creation.
- _Genesis 2:16-17._ Read this passage — Life requires trust in God’s word.

---

## 2. The fall and our present condition — Romans 5:12

> Sin brought death into the world. Our need includes a fallen heart and our own acts of sin.

- _Romans 5:12._ “Wherefore, as by one man sin entered into the world, and death by sin; and so death passed upon all men, for that all have sinned:” — Adam’s fall brought sin and death into human history.
- _Genesis 3:6-10._ Read this passage — Distrust leads to disobedience, shame, and hiding.
- _Genesis 3:17-19._ Read this passage — The curse affects work, the earth, and the body.
- _Romans 3:10-12._ Read this passage — No one can claim an independent righteousness.
- _Romans 3:23._ “For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;” — All have sinned.
- _Ezekiel 18:20._ “The soul that sinneth, it shall die. The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, neither shall the father bear the iniquity of the son: the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked shall be upon him.” — Personal guilt must not be confused with another person’s sin.

[SOP SC 62.2] “we cannot make ourselves righteous.” — Our nature needs Christ.

[Q] Does sharing Adam’s fallen condition mean I committed Adam’s act?
→ Romans 5:12 describes the reach of sin and death; Ezekiel 18:20 distinguishes each person’s guilt.

## 3. We need a new heart — John 3:3-7

> Education and discipline cannot give a fallen heart the spiritual life it lacks.

- _John 3:3-7._ “Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God. Nicodemus saith unto him, How can a man be born when he is old? can he enter the second time into his mother's womb, and be born? Jesus answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God. That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit. Marvel not that I said unto thee, Ye must be born again.” — Jesus calls for a new birth from the Spirit.
- _Jeremiah 17:9._ “The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked: who can know it?” — Our own heart can deceive us.
- _Romans 8:7-8._ Read this passage — The carnal mind cannot submit to God by itself.
- _Jeremiah 13:23._ “Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard his spots? then may ye also do good, that are accustomed to do evil.” — Habitual evil cannot cure itself.
- _Ezekiel 36:26-27._ Read this passage — God gives a new heart and power to obey.

[SOP SC 18.1] “Our hearts are evil, and we cannot change them.” — Human methods cannot regenerate.

---

## 4. The law shows our need — Romans 3:19-24

> The law exposes guilt. Christ provides the righteousness that we cannot supply.

- _Romans 3:19-24._ “Now we know that what things soever the law saith, it saith to them who are under the law: that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may become guilty before God. Therefore by the deeds of the law there shall no flesh be justified in his sight: for by the law is the knowledge of sin. But now the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets; Even the righteousness of God which is by faith of Jesus Christ unto all and upon all them that believe: for there is no difference: For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God; Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus:” — The law reveals sin; justification comes freely through Christ.
- _Isaiah 59:1-2._ Read this passage — Sin separates us from God.
- _Romans 6:23._ “For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.” — Death is sin’s wage; eternal life is God’s gift.
- _Isaiah 64:6._ “But we are all as an unclean thing, and all our righteousnesses are as filthy rags; and we all do fade as a leaf; and our iniquities, like the wind, have taken us away.” — Our own righteousness cannot cleanse our guilt.
- _Galatians 3:21-24._ Read this passage — The law cannot give life; it directs us to Christ.

## 5. Cain and Abel: two approaches to worship — Hebrews 11:4

> Both brothers brought an offering. Abel approached God by faith; Cain relied on his own merits.

- _Hebrews 11:4._ “By faith Abel offered unto God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain, by which he obtained witness that he was righteous, God testifying of his gifts: and by it he being dead yet speaketh.” — Scripture identifies faith as the basis of Abel’s accepted offering.
- _Genesis 4:1-10._ Read this passage — Read the whole account: offerings, warning, anger, and murder.
- _Hebrews 9:22._ “And almost all things are by the law purged with blood; and without shedding of blood is no remission.” — The biblical sacrificial pattern connects pardon with shed blood.
- _1 John 3:12._ “Not as Cain, who was of that wicked one, and slew his brother. And wherefore slew he him? Because his own works were evil, and his brother's righteous.” — Cain’s conduct reveals the character of his religion.
- _Jude 1:11._ “Woe unto them! for they have gone in the way of Cain, and ran greedily after the error of Balaam for reward, and perished in the gainsaying of Core.” — The way of Cain remains a warning.

[SOP PP 72.1] “The essential part, the recognition of the need of a Redeemer, was left out.” — Cain omitted the Redeemer.

| Cain | Abel |
|---|---|
| Both sinners | Both sinners |
| Brought an offering | Brought an offering |
| Trusted own merits | Trusted promised sacrifice |
| Partial obedience | Faith and obedience |

[SOP PP 72.5] “Cain and Abel represent two classes that will exist in the world till the close of time.” — Two ways of approaching God.

[Aside] Genesis records the offerings; Hebrews identifies Abel’s faith. PP 72.3 explains his sacrifice as faith in Christ’s coming atonement.

[Q] Was Cain rejected without a choice?
→ Genesis 4:6–7 records God’s warning and invitation; PP 72.4 rejects an arbitrary choice of one brother over the other.

## 6. Religious effort cannot purchase acceptance — Luke 18:9-14

> A person can worship, give, and work while still trusting in self.

- _Luke 18:9-14._ “And he spake this parable unto certain which trusted in themselves that they were righteous, and despised others: Two men went up into the temple to pray; the one a Pharisee, and the other a publican. The Pharisee stood and prayed thus with himself, God, I thank thee, that I am not as other men are, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, or even as this publican. I fast twice in the week, I give tithes of all that I possess. And the publican, standing afar off, would not lift up so much as his eyes unto heaven, but smote upon his breast, saying, God be merciful to me a sinner. I tell you, this man went down to his house justified rather than the other: for every one that exalteth himself shall be abased; and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted.” — The man who asks for mercy goes home justified.
- _Romans 10:2-4._ Read this passage — Religious zeal can seek to establish its own righteousness.
- _Galatians 2:16._ “Knowing that a man is not justified by the works of the law, but by the faith of Jesus Christ, even we have believed in Jesus Christ, that we might be justified by the faith of Christ, and not by the works of the law: for by the works of the law shall no flesh be justified.” — Works of the law cannot justify the sinner.

[SOP PP 73.1] “Humanity has no power to regenerate itself.” — Development cannot replace redemption.

[RQ] Am I asking Christ for mercy, or presenting my religious record as a claim upon God?

## 7. Sin, sickness, and suffering — John 9:1-3

> Suffering belongs to a world damaged by sin. A sick person must not be judged guilty from the illness alone.

- _John 9:1-3._ “And as Jesus passed by, he saw a man which was blind from his birth. And his disciples asked him, saying, Master, who did sin, this man, or his parents, that he was born blind? Jesus answered, Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents: but that the works of God should be made manifest in him.” — Jesus rejects the proposed blame for this man’s blindness.
- _Romans 8:20-23._ Read this passage — Creation groans; even Spirit-filled believers await bodily redemption.
- _Luke 13:1-5._ Read this passage — Greater suffering does not prove greater guilt.
- _Luke 13:16._ “And ought not this woman, being a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan hath bound, lo, these eighteen years, be loosed from this bond on the sabbath day?” — Jesus identifies Satan’s oppression in this case.
- _Acts 10:38._ “How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil; for God was with him.” — Christ brings relief from the devil’s oppression.
- _John 5:14._ “Afterward Jesus findeth him in the temple, and said unto him, Behold, thou art made whole: sin no more, lest a worse thing come unto thee.” — Some cases include a warning about further sin.
- _James 5:14-16._ Read this passage — Pray for the sick; the text says “if” they have committed sins.
- _Revelation 21:4._ “And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away.” — God’s final restoration removes death and pain.

[SOP DA 471.1] “It is true that all suffering results from the transgression of God’s law, but this truth had become perverted.” — A true principle was misused.

[Q] Is every illness punishment for the sufferer’s own sin?
→ John 9:1–3 rejects that conclusion; Romans 8:23 includes faithful believers among those who suffer.

[Aside] The origin of suffering in sin is a theological claim. These verses do not identify the immediate cause of every illness.

## 8. Christ meets our need — Romans 5:6-8

> Christ came to save us while we lacked strength and were still sinners.

- _Romans 5:6-8._ “For when we were yet without strength, in due time Christ died for the ungodly. For scarcely for a righteous man will one die: yet peradventure for a good man some would even dare to die. But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.” — God’s love and Christ’s sacrifice precede our improvement.
- _Isaiah 53:4-6._ Read this passage — Our sins and griefs point us to the suffering Saviour.
- _2 Corinthians 5:21._ “For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.” — Our righteousness is in the sinless Christ.
- _Acts 4:12._ “Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.” — Salvation is found in Christ alone.
- _Mark 2:5-12._ Read this passage — Christ has authority both to forgive and to heal.

[SOP SC 62.2] “Christ’s character stands in place of your character, and you are accepted before God just as if you had not sinned.” — Acceptance rests on Christ.

[Q] Must every believer receive full bodily healing now?
→ Romans 8:23 places complete bodily redemption ahead; Revelation 21:4 promises the final end of pain.

---

## 9. Salvation is a gift — Ephesians 2:8-10

> God saves by grace through faith. Good works belong to the life He creates in us.

- _Ephesians 2:8-10._ “For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast. For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.” — Grace saves; the new creation produces good works.
- _Titus 3:3-7._ Read this passage — Mercy and the Spirit’s renewal save us.
- _Romans 4:3-5._ Read this passage — A gift of righteousness is not a wage for labour.

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, CHR 69.1] “God does not adopt us as His children because we are good, but in order that He may make us good.” — Grace creates the changed life.

[WB] Grace → Faith → New life → Good works

## 10. Come to Christ now — John 6:37

> Do not wait until you can offer a better record. Come to Christ for pardon and cleansing.

- _John 6:37._ “All that the Father giveth me shall come to me; and him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out.” — Christ promises to receive the person who comes.
- _Acts 5:31._ “Him hath God exalted with his right hand to be a Prince and a Saviour, for to give repentance to Israel, and forgiveness of sins.” — Christ gives repentance as well as forgiveness.
- _1 John 1:9._ “If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.” — Confess sin; God promises pardon and cleansing.
- _Romans 5:1._ “Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ:” — Faith in Christ brings peace with God.

---

## 11. The place of effort and obedience — Philippians 2:12-13

> Human effort cannot earn salvation. God’s grace moves us to willing, active obedience.

- _Philippians 2:12-13._ “Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling. For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure.” — Read both verses: God works in us to will and to do.
- _John 15:4-5._ Read this passage — The branch bears fruit through continued union with Christ.
- _Romans 8:1-4._ Read this passage — God does what the law could not do through weak flesh.
- _Titus 2:11-14._ Read this passage — Saving grace teaches us to reject sin and do good.
- _James 2:17-18._ Read this passage — Living faith becomes visible in works.
- _1 Corinthians 15:10._ “But by the grace of God I am what I am: and his grace which was bestowed upon me was not in vain; but I laboured more abundantly than they all: yet not I, but the grace of God which was with me.” — Paul labours, yet credits God’s grace.

[SOP SC 61.1] “We do not earn salvation by our obedience; for salvation is the free gift of God, to be received by faith.” — Obedience does not buy salvation.

[SOP PP 73.2] “True faith, which relies wholly upon Christ, will be manifested by obedience to all the requirements of God.” — Faith changes conduct.

| Earning salvation | Living by faith |
|---|---|
| Self supplies merit | Christ supplies righteousness |
| Works purchase acceptance | Grace produces obedience |
| Trust in performance | Depend on Christ |

[Q] Does “effort cannot save” mean “do nothing”?
→ Philippians 2:12–13 joins our response with God’s work; 1 Corinthians 15:10 shows labour through grace.

---

## 12. Appeal: receive His rest — Matthew 11:28-30

> Bring your guilt, weakness, and need to Christ. Receive His pardon and learn to walk with Him.

- _Matthew 11:28-30._ “Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.” — The Saviour gives rest and teaches a new way of life.

[RQ] Will you stop using your own works as the basis of acceptance with God?

[RQ] Will you receive Christ’s mercy and yield your will to His work in you?

Come to Him now. Confess the sin you know. Trust His promise. Ask Him for the new heart and strength that He has promised.
