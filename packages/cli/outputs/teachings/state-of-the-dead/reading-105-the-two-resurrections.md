## 1. In Adam All Die — 1 Cor 15:22

> Death is not natural and not partial: one man opened the door and every man walks through it. The remedy must be as wide as the ruin.

[WB]: ADAM → sin → death → ALL | CHRIST → resurrection → ALL

- _1 Cor 15:22._ "For as in Adam all die, even so in Christ shall all be made alive" — the anchor: two heads, two "alls"; every grave Adam filled, Christ empties
- _Rom 5:12._ "by one man sin entered into the world, and death by sin; and so death passed upon all men, for that all have sinned" — the pedigree: sin first, death its wage
- chain: Gen 2:7 (dust + breath = a living soul) → Gen 3:19 (dust thou art, unto dust return) → Eccl 12:7 (dust to earth, breath to God) → Eccl 3:20 (all go unto one place)
- _1 Tim 6:16._ "Who only hath immortality" — God alone holds it; man never had it to keep

[SOP GC 533.1] "Adam could not transmit to his posterity that which he did not possess" — no child of Adam is born immortal

[→] if all die in Adam, life must come from outside Adam — a second Man (§4)

---

## 2. The Grave: Silence — Eccl 9:10

> The dead are not elsewhere, awake; they are in the grave, silent. No work, no thought, no praise — which is why everything hangs on a resurrection.

- _Eccl 9:10._ "Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest" — the anchor: four "no's," and the address is "the grave, whither thou goest"
- _Eccl 9:5-6._ "the dead know not any thing... their love, and their hatred, and their envy, is now perished" — mind and affections both stopped
- _Ps 146:4._ "His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish" — "that very day": no interval of consciousness
- _Ps 146:2._ "I will sing praises unto my God while I have any being" — David dates his praise: while he has being
- _Ps 6:5._ "in death there is no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks?" — not even the thought of God
- chain: Ps 115:17 (dead praise not, go down into silence) → Isa 38:18-19 (the grave cannot praise; "the living, the living") → Acts 2:29, 34 (David dead, buried, "not ascended into the heavens")

[SOP GC 546.2] "The fact that David remains in the grave until the resurrection proves that the righteous do not go to heaven at death." — Peter's Pentecost argument

[→] a silent grave makes the resurrection the only door to any future life — take it away and nothing is left (§5)

## 3. Death a Sleep — John 11:11

> Christ's own word for death is sleep: unconscious, temporary, ended by a voice. Four days or four thousand years — the sleeper feels neither.

[WB]: sleep = no knowledge of time + certain waking | death ——(a moment to them)—— His voice

- _John 11:11._ "Our friend Lazarus sleepeth; but I go, that I may awake him out of sleep" — the anchor: His chosen word, and His chosen errand
- _John 11:14._ "Then said Jesus unto them plainly, Lazarus is dead" — the figure explained by its Author (Rule 12): sleep = death, no third state
- chain: Job 14:12 (lieth down, riseth not till the heavens be no more) → Ps 13:3 (the sleep of death) → Dan 12:2 (sleep in the dust) → 1 Thess 4:13 (them which are asleep) → 1 Cor 15:51 (we shall not all sleep)
- _Job 14:14-15._ "all the days of my appointed time will I wait, till my change come. Thou shalt call, and I will answer thee" — waiting in the grave (Job 17:13), for a call
- _John 11:24._ "I know that he shall rise again in the resurrection at the last day" — Martha's creed: not "he is in heaven," but "he shall rise"

[SOP DA 527.3] "Christ represents death as a sleep to His believing children." — the Saviour's own figure

[SOP GC 549.3] "Blessed rest for the weary righteous! Time, be it long or short, is but a moment to them." — the comfort in the doctrine

[SOP GC 549.3] "As they are called forth from their deep slumber they begin to think just where they ceased." — last thought the grave, next thought the shout

[SOP GC 549.2] Luther, quoted: "when they are awaked, they shall seem to have slept scarce one minute" — the Reformer held the same

[Aside]: Lazarus came back with no report of four days in glory — he had none to give

## 4. Ransomed From the Grave — Hos 13:14

> God has spoken to the grave by name and promised to destroy it. The ransom is a Person: by man came death, by Man the resurrection.

- _Hos 13:14._ "I will ransom them from the power of the grave; I will redeem them from death: O death, I will be thy plagues; O grave, I will be thy destruction" — the anchor: a promise addressed to the prison itself
- _1 Cor 15:21._ "For since by man came death, by man came also the resurrection of the dead" — the ransom had to be paid in Adam's own coin: a man
- _John 3:16._ "whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life" — the alternatives are perish or life — not life in bliss or life in misery
- _Rev 1:18._ "I am he that liveth, and was dead; and, behold, I am alive for evermore, Amen; and have the keys of hell and of death" — the Ransomer holds the keys

**The Old Testament line — a bodily resurrection, plainly promised:**

- chain: Job 19:25-27 (in my flesh shall I see God) → Ps 16:10 with Acts 2:31 (soul not left in hell = Christ's resurrection) → Ps 49:15 (redeem my soul from the power of the grave) → Isa 26:19 (thy dead men shall live) → Eze 37:12-13 (I will open your graves) → Dan 12:2
- _Jer 31:16._ "they shall come again from the land of the enemy" — spoken to Rachel's slain children (Matt 2:17-18): the enemy's land is the grave

[PIONEER J. H. Waggoner, AERS 282.4] "Death is the enemy (1 Corinthians 15:26) from whose land they will be brought." — Bethlehem's mothers get their children back

[Q] isn't Eze 37 only a spiritual revival?
→ v. 12-13 is God's own explanation of the vision — "I will open your graves"; the explanation is literal — Eze 37:12-13; Rule 12

[PIONEER J. H. Waggoner, AERS 283.1] "the words quoted above are from the Lord's explanation of the vision; and no one should presume to explain the Lord's explanation" — take it as it reads

## 5. If the Dead Rise Not — 1 Cor 15:16-18

> Paul stakes everything on the resurrection: without it the sleeping saints are perished. That sentence is impossible if they are already in heaven.

- _1 Cor 15:16-18._ "For if the dead rise not, then is not Christ raised: And if Christ be not raised, your faith is vain; ye are yet in your sins. Then they also which are fallen asleep in Christ are perished" — the anchor: no resurrection = perished
- _1 Cor 15:19._ "If in this life only we have hope in Christ, we are of all men most miserable" — no hope between this life and the rising
- _Luke 14:14._ "thou shalt be recompensed at the resurrection of the just" — the pay-day is dated: not at death
- chain: 2 Tim 4:8 (crown "laid up... at that day") → Heb 11:39-40 (received not the promise; not perfect without us) → Rev 22:12 (my reward is with me) → John 6:39-40 (raise him up at the last day)

[SOP GC 546.3] "No resurrection would be necessary." — if the righteous went straight to heaven for four thousand years

[SOP GC 547.1] Tyndale, quoted: "then the preaching of the resurrection of the flesh were a thing in vain" — the martyr saw the same consequence

[Q] don't the righteous go to their reward at death?
→ reward is "at the resurrection of the just" — Luke 14:14; David "is not ascended" — Acts 2:34; judgment has an appointed day — Acts 17:31

[→] Eden's lie "ye shall not surely die" (Gen 3:4) makes the resurrection useless — that is why the pulpit went quiet on it (§6)

## 6. The Denial: Sadducees Old and New — Luke 20:27

> One party denied the resurrection outright; another makes it needless. Christ answers both from Moses: the dead live "unto him" because He will raise them.

| the error                  | what it does to the doctrine          |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Sadducee — "no resurrection" | denies it (Luke 20:27; Acts 23:8)   |
| Hymenaeus — "past already" | spiritualizes it (2 Tim 2:17-18)      |
| immortal soul — "in glory now" | makes it needless (Gen 3:4)       |
| Athens — mocked            | finds it incredible (Acts 17:32)      |

- _Luke 20:27._ "Then came to him certain of the Sadducees, which deny that there is any resurrection" — the anchor
- _Matt 22:29._ "Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God" — the two roots of every denial
- _Luke 20:37-38._ "Now that the dead are raised, even Moses shewed at the bush... he is not a God of the dead, but of the living: for all live unto him" — His proof-text is offered for "that the dead are raised"
- _Acts 26:8._ "Why should it be thought a thing incredible with you, that God should raise the dead?" — the Maker of Gen 2:7 can do it twice

[Q] "God of the living" — so Abraham is alive now?
→ Christ cites it to prove a resurrection, not a present life — Luke 20:37; if Abraham lived on, the text would prove nothing to a Sadducee

[SOP DA 606.1] "God counts the things that are not as though they were." — how the dead "live unto Him"

[SOP GC 547.2] "the hope of immortal blessedness at death has led to a widespread neglect of the Bible doctrine of the resurrection" — the modern Sadduceeism

## 7. The Seed — 1 Cor 15:36

> Nature preaches the resurrection every spring: the seed must die, and what rises is the same kind — with a better body.

[WB]: seed ↓ dies → ↑ same kind, new body

- _1 Cor 15:36._ "Thou fool, that which thou sowest is not quickened, except it die" — the anchor: death is not the obstacle to the harvest; it is the road
- _John 12:24._ "Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit" — spoken first of Himself: one Seed buried, a harvest of the risen
- _1 Cor 15:37-38._ "thou sowest not that body that shall be, but bare grain... But God giveth it a body as it hath pleased him, and to every seed his own body" — identity kept ("his own"), body new
- _1 Cor 15:35._ "How are the dead raised up? and with what body do they come?" — the two questions; §9 answers the first, §13 the second

[→] wheat raises wheat — the Seed that fell at Calvary decides what the harvest looks like (§8, §13)

## 8. Firstfruits: The Pledge — 1 Cor 15:20, 23

> The resurrection has an order, and its first sheaf is already waved. Christ's empty tomb is the sample and the security of the harvest.

[WB]: every man in his own order — CHRIST (firstfruits) → HIS, at His coming → the end

- _1 Cor 15:20._ "But now is Christ risen from the dead, and become the firstfruits of them that slept" — the anchor
- _1 Cor 15:23._ "every man in his own order: Christ the firstfruits; afterward they that are Christ's at his coming" — ranks, with dates
- _Lev 23:10-11._ "ye shall bring a sheaf of the firstfruits of your harvest... on the morrow after the sabbath the priest shall wave it" — the type: first sheaf, first day, before any sickle
- _Matt 27:52-53._ "many bodies of the saints which slept arose, And came out of the graves after his resurrection" — the sheaf was a handful, not one stalk
- _Eph 4:8._ "When he ascended up on high, he led captivity captive" — they went up with Him

[SOP DA 785.4] "His resurrection is the type and pledge of the resurrection of all the righteous dead." — the primary witness

[SOP DA 786.1] "As Christ arose, He brought from the grave a multitude of captives." — the wave sheaf had companions

| raised               | to what                         | ref              |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Nain, Jairus, Lazarus | mortal life — died again       | John 11:44       |
| saints with Christ   | immortal — ascended             | Matt 27:52-53    |
| all His, at His coming | immortal — the harvest        | 1 Cor 15:23      |

[SOP DA 786.2] "But these were not clothed with immortality. After they were raised, they were still subject to death." — Lazarus is a sign, not the firstfruits

- chain: John 14:19 (because I live, ye shall live also) → Rom 8:11 (He that raised Christ shall quicken your mortal bodies) → Phil 3:10-11 (the power of his resurrection)

## 9. Whose Voice? — John 5:28-29

> The dead are not raised by a law of nature but by a voice — the voice of the Son, who has life in Himself.

- _John 5:28-29._ "Marvel not at this: for the hour is coming, in the which all that are in the graves shall hear his voice, And shall come forth" — the anchor: "all," "in the graves," "his voice"
- _John 5:26._ "as the Father hath life in himself; so hath he given to the Son to have life in himself" — why His voice can do it
- _John 11:25._ "I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live" — the doctrine is a Person
- _John 11:43-44._ "he cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come forth. And he that was dead came forth" — the sample: a name called, a grave obeying
- chain: 1 Thess 4:16 (voice of the archangel) → Job 14:15 (thou shalt call, and I will answer) → Isa 26:19 (awake and sing, ye that dwell in dust)
- where they hear it from: "in the graves" — not called down from heaven, called up from the dust

[SOP DA 530.3] "In Christ is life, original, unborrowed, underived." — the ground of the whole hope

[SOP DA 787.2] "That voice will penetrate the graves and unbar the tombs, and the dead in Christ shall arise." — the cry of the cross was the rehearsal

[PIONEER J. H. Waggoner, AERS 285.2] "The voice of the archangel (the Son of God, compare John 5:27-29), and the trump of God will be heard." — pioneer: archangel's voice = the Son's

## 10. Two Classes, Two Resurrections — Acts 24:15

> Every grave opens, but not at once and not to one end. Two classes, two names, two times.

[WB]: 1st RES. (just) |———— 1000 years ————| 2nd RES. (unjust) → 2nd death

- _Acts 24:15._ "there shall be a resurrection of the dead, both of the just and unjust" — the anchor: two classes
- _John 5:29._ "they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation" — Christ's two names for them
- _Dan 12:2._ "some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt" — the OT already divides
- _Rev 20:5-6._ "But the rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished. This is the first resurrection" — a "first" demands a second; "the rest" demands a remainder

|              | first                         | second                          |
| ------------ | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| who          | just; "dead in Christ"        | unjust; "rest of the dead"      |
| when         | His coming (1 Thess 4:16)     | 1000 yrs after (Rev 20:5)       |
| name         | "of life" (John 5:29)         | "of damnation"                  |
| body         | incorruptible (1 Cor 15:52)   | marks of the curse (EW 292.1)   |
| end          | "die no more" (Luke 20:36)    | second death (Rev 20:14)        |

[PIONEER U. Smith, DAR 298.1] "The general resurrection is not a mixed resurrection." — the primary witness

[PIONEER U. Smith, DAR 693.4] "no language could more plainly prove two resurrections" — on Rev 20:5

[Q] John 5:28 says one "hour" — so one general resurrection?
→ "the hour... now is" of John 5:25 has already run for centuries — an "hour" is a period; the gap inside it is stated — Rev 20:5

## 11. The First: At His Coming — 1 Thess 4:16

> The resurrection of the just is fastened to one event: the Lord Himself descending. No coming, no rising; and the living gain no head start on the sleepers.

- _1 Thess 4:16-17._ "For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in Christ shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds" — the anchor: shout, voice, trump; dead first, living "together with them"
- _1 Thess 4:15._ "we which are alive... shall not prevent them which are asleep" — prevent = go before: no saint arrives early
- _1 Thess 4:13-14._ "that ye sorrow not, even as others which have no hope" — the comfort offered is the resurrection, not a present heaven
- _1 Cor 15:51-52._ "We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed, In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump" — same trump, two groups, one change
- chain: 1 Cor 15:23 (they that are Christ's at his coming) → Matt 24:31 (angels gather the elect, great sound of a trumpet) → Col 3:4 (when Christ our life shall appear) → Isa 25:9 (Lo, this is our God; we have waited for him)

[SOP GC 644.2] "He looks upon the graves of the righteous, then, raising His hands to heaven, He cries: 'Awake, awake, awake, ye that sleep in the dust, and arise!'" — the scene

[SOP GC 645.1] "Little children are borne by holy angels to their mothers' arms." — "comfort one another with these words" (1 Thess 4:18)

[PIONEER J. H. Waggoner, AERS 284.4] "if he should never come there would then be no resurrection of the dead" — the two doctrines stand or fall together

[→] advent and resurrection are one hope — a theology that sends saints to heaven at death has no need of either

## 12. The Special Resurrection — Dan 12:2

> Before the general rising of the just, "many" — not all — awake, of both kinds: those who died under the third angel's message, and those who pierced Him.

- _Dan 12:2._ "And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt" — the anchor: "many of," a partial and a mixed awaking
- _Rev 1:7._ "every eye shall see him, and they also which pierced him" — His murderers, dead for ages, must be raised to see it
- _Matt 26:64._ "Hereafter shall ye see the Son of man sitting on the right hand of power, and coming in the clouds of heaven" — promised to Caiaphas by name of office
- _Rev 14:13._ "Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth" — a special blessing on the third angel's dead

[SOP GC 637.1] "All who have died in the faith of the third angel's message come forth from the tomb glorified" — the righteous part

[SOP GC 637.1] "those that mocked and derided Christ's dying agonies, and the most violent opposers of His truth and His people, are raised to behold Him in His glory" — the wicked part

[Q] does Dan 12:2 put wicked in the first resurrection?
→ no — the first is "blessed and holy" only (Rev 20:6); a mixed "many" fits neither the first nor the second — a limited rising at God's voice, before He appears, per U. Smith, DAR 298.2-299.1; those wicked fall again at His brightness — GC 657.1

## 13. The Body That Shall Be — 1 Cor 15:42-44

> Same person, new body. Four things sown, four things raised — and the pattern is Christ's own glorious body.

| sown          | raised         |
| ------------- | -------------- |
| corruption    | incorruption   |
| dishonour     | glory          |
| weakness      | power          |
| natural body  | spiritual body |

- _1 Cor 15:42-44._ "So also is the resurrection of the dead. It is sown in corruption; it is raised in incorruption: It is sown in dishonour; it is raised in glory: it is sown in weakness; it is raised in power: It is sown a natural body; it is raised a spiritual body" — the anchor: "it... it" — one subject through all eight clauses
- _Phil 3:20-21._ "we look for the Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ: Who shall change our vile body, that it may be fashioned like unto his glorious body" — the pattern
- _Ps 17:15._ "I shall be satisfied, when I awake, with thy likeness" — David's expectation: awake, and like Him
- chain: 1 Cor 15:49 (bear the image of the heavenly) → 1 John 3:2 (we shall be like him) → 1 Cor 15:53 (this mortal must put on immortality) → 2 Tim 1:10 (immortality brought to light through the gospel)
- immortality is "put on" at the last trump — not possessed from birth (1 Cor 15:53-54; Rom 6:23 "the gift of God")

[SOP GC 644.3] "All come forth from their graves the same in stature as when they entered the tomb." — identity kept

[SOP GC 644.3] "All blemishes and deformities are left in the grave." — the curse is not raised with them

[Q] "spiritual body" — a ghost?
→ a body still: "in my flesh shall I see God" — Job 19:26; fashioned like His risen body — Phil 3:21; spiritual = quickened by the Spirit — Rom 8:11

## 14. Neither Can They Die Any More — Luke 20:36

> The first resurrection ends the possibility of death. The shout of the risen is a taunt flung back at the grave.

- _Luke 20:36._ "Neither can they die any more: for they are equal unto the angels; and are the children of God, being the children of the resurrection" — the anchor: not "shall not" but "cannot"
- _1 Cor 15:54-55._ "Death is swallowed up in victory. O death, where is thy sting? O grave, where is thy victory?" — Hos 13:14 and Isa 25:8 cashed at the same moment
- _1 Cor 15:56-57._ "The sting of death is sin... But thanks be to God, which giveth us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ" — sting drawn at the cross, victory given at the trump
- _Rev 20:6._ "Blessed and holy is he that hath part in the first resurrection: on such the second death hath no power, but they shall be priests of God and of Christ, and shall reign with him a thousand years" — their standing and their work
- _Rev 20:4._ "I saw thrones, and they sat upon them, and judgment was given unto them... and they lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years" — the millennium is spent judging, in heaven
- _Rev 2:11._ "He that overcometh shall not be hurt of the second death" — the promise, made in advance

[SOP EW 287.1] "Friends whom death had separated were united, never more to part." — what "cannot die any more" means at a graveside

[→] the first death is a sleep all men sleep; the only death worth fearing is the one the first resurrection puts out of reach

## 15. The Rest of the Dead — Rev 20:5

> A thousand years later the second voice. The wicked come up what they went down: same bodies, same enmity — raised not for another chance, but for sentence.

- _Rev 20:5._ "But the rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished" — the anchor: "lived not" — no consciousness in between; "until" — they do live again
- _Rev 20:13._ "the sea gave up the dead which were in it; and death and hell delivered up the dead which were in them" — every holding-place surrenders
- _Rev 20:12._ "I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened... and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works" — raised to stand and hear
- _Isa 24:22._ "shut up in the prison, and after many days shall they be visited" — the OT note of the same interval
- _Rev 20:7-8._ "Satan shall be loosed... to gather them together to battle: the number of whom is as the sand of the sea" — what looses him is the return of his subjects

[SOP GC 662.2] "As the wicked went into their graves, so they come forth with the same enmity to Christ and the same spirit of rebellion." — the primary witness: the grave converts no one

[SOP GC 662.1] "The righteous were clothed with immortal youth and beauty. The wicked bear the traces of disease and death." — the contrast visible at a glance

[Q] why raise the wicked at all?
→ for "the execution of the judgment written" — GC 661.2; every knee bows, every tongue confesses — Rom 14:11; Phil 2:10-11; sentence is heard, understood, owned

[Q] is it a second chance?
→ "They are to have no new probation" — GC 662.2; appointed once to die, "after this the judgment" — Heb 9:27; their first act is a siege — Rev 20:9

## 16. The Second Death — Rev 21:8

> The second resurrection ends in a second death — and from that one there is no waking. Not misery without end: an end.

- _Rev 20:9._ "they went up on the breadth of the earth, and compassed the camp of the saints about, and the beloved city: and fire came down from God out of heaven, and devoured them" — the anchor: fire from God, and the verb is "devoured"
- _Rev 21:8._ "But the fearful, and unbelieving, and the abominable, and murderers, and whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, shall have their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is the second death" — the roll; it opens with "the fearful, and unbelieving"
- _Rev 20:14-15._ "This is the second death. And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire" — Scripture names the lake: a death
- chain: Rom 6:23 (wages of sin is death) → Mal 4:1, 3 (burn them up; neither root nor branch; ashes) → Ps 37:10 (the wicked shall not be) → Obad 16 (as though they had not been) → Isa 28:21 (his strange act)
- _John 3:36._ "he that believeth not the Son shall not see life" — the lost do not live forever anywhere

| first death            | second death                 |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| all men (1 Cor 15:22)  | the impenitent (Rev 21:8)    |
| a sleep (John 11:11)   | destruction (Rev 20:9)       |
| ended by a voice       | no resurrection after        |

[SOP GC 544.2] "He deprives him of the existence which his transgressions have forfeited" — the penalty defined

[SOP GC 673.1] "In the cleansing flames the wicked are at last destroyed, root and branch" — Mal 4:1 fulfilled to the word

[PIONEER U. Smith, DAR 693.4] "On such as have part in the first resurrection, the second death will have no power." — the only shelter from it

## 17. The Last Enemy — 1 Cor 15:26

> Death is an enemy, never a friend or a doorway — and it is the last one standing. When the lake closes over it, the controversy is over.

- _1 Cor 15:26._ "The last enemy that shall be destroyed is death" — the anchor: enemy; last; destroyed
- _Rev 20:14._ "And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire" — death and the grave themselves die; Hos 13:14 kept to the letter
- _1 Cor 15:24-25._ "Then cometh the end... For he must reign, till he hath put all enemies under his feet" — the order of §8 finishes here: Christ, His people, the end
- _Isa 25:8._ "He will swallow up death in victory; and the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from off all faces" — the promise Paul quotes
- _Rev 21:4._ "there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away" — the last funeral has been held

## 18. Appeal

- two resurrections, and every one here will be in one of them — John 5:29 leaves no third
- which one is not decided at the trump; it is decided before you sleep — "as the wicked went into their graves, so they come forth" (GC 662.2)
- _Phil 3:10-11._ "That I may know him, and the power of his resurrection... If by any means I might attain unto the resurrection of the dead" — Paul's one ambition: to have part in the first
- _Rev 20:6._ "Blessed and holy is he that hath part in the first resurrection: on such the second death hath no power" — the blessing offered tonight
- at your graveside, or a loved one's: "sorrow not, even as others which have no hope" (1 Thess 4:13) — He has the keys (Rev 1:18)
- _John 11:25-26._ "I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live: And whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die. Believest thou this?"
