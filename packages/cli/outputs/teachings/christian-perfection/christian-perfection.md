# Christian Perfection

**God commands perfection and gives the power: by His Word and His Spirit we can stop sinning now, perfect at every stage of the path, and the one failure is a life that does not grow.**

## 1. Two Questions, One Answer — Matt 5:48

> Does God expect us to be perfect? Does He expect us to stop sinning? Yes. The standard is His own character, and the command is the promise.

[WB]: BE PERFECT → as the Father → His character → by Him → now

- _Matt 5:48._ "Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect" — the standard is not the church, not the neighbour: the Father
- _Gen 17:1._ "I am the Almighty God; walk before me, and be thou perfect" — the command comes wrapped in the name of the One who can do it
- chain: Heb 6:1 (go on unto perfection) → Col 1:28 (present every man perfect) → Eph 4:13 (unto a perfect man) → Eph 1:4 (chosen to be holy and without blame) — Jones's chain, LOF_ATJ 136.1 to 141.3

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 136.1] "Then it is not for us to say that perfection is not expected of us. It is expected of us." — the chapter's first move

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 137.2] "God is character, and it is perfection of character as His is, that He has set for you and me" — not His power, His character

[SOP COL 315.1] "God requires perfection of His children. His law is a transcript of His own character, and it is the standard of all character." — the law is the measure

[SOP DA 311.5] "He bids us by faith in Him attain to the glory of the character of God." — bids, by faith

[Q] does God really expect perfection, or is it an ideal?
→ "Be ye therefore perfect" is a command, and the "therefore" rests on the Father's own character — Matt 5:48; to call it impossible is to expect failure — LOF_ATJ 136.1

[→] the standard is settled; the only question left is the way — LOF_ATJ 136.2

## 2. Stop Sinning? God Forbid — Rom 6:1-2

> He does not save us in sin but from it. Every verse of Romans 6 intends that we be kept from sinning.

- _Rom 6:1-2._ "Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound? God forbid. How shall we, that are dead to sin, live any longer therein?" — grace is not a licence; it is a death to sin
- _Rom 6:6._ "our old man is crucified with him, that the body of sin might be destroyed, that henceforth we should not serve sin" — crucified, destroyed, free: three steps
- _1 John 2:1._ "My little children, these things write I unto you, that ye sin not" — the purpose of the letter, stated
- _1 John 3:9._ "Whosoever is born of God doth not commit sin; for his seed remaineth in him" — the seed is why: new birth bears its own fruit
- _1 John 3:8._ "For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that he might destroy the works of the devil" — not manage them: destroy them

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 144.4] "Then doesn't He intend that you and I shall be kept from sinning? And when we know that He intends it then we can confidently expect it." — expectation is the door

[SOP DA 668.3] "When we know God as it is our privilege to know Him, our life will be a life of continual obedience." — knowing Him, not straining

[RQ] Do you expect to sin this week? What would change if you expected to be kept?

[→] God forbids sin and intends to keep us from it; what He forbids He supplies the power to refuse — §3

## 3. The Means: the Seed and the Spirit — 1 Pet 1:23; Ezek 36:26-27

> God does not command and walk away. He gives two gifts: His Word, the seed that carries His life, and His Spirit, the new spirit that makes us walk in His ways.

[WB]: WORD (seed) + SPIRIT → new heart → walks in His statutes

**The seed: the Word carries the life it commands — 1 Pet 1:23:**

- _1 Pet 1:23._ "Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever" — born of a seed that cannot fail
- _Luke 8:11._ "Now the parable is this: The seed is the word of God" — Christ names the seed
- _2 Pet 1:3-4._ "his divine power hath given unto us all things that pertain unto life and godliness" — "exceeding great and precious promises," that we "might be partakers of the divine nature" — all things, already given

[SOP COL 38.1] "In every command and in every promise of the word of God is the power, the very life of God, by which the command may be fulfilled" — the command carries its power

[SOP Ed 126.4] "Every command is a promise; accepted by the will, received into the soul, it brings with it the life of the Infinite One." — receive the command as a promise

**The Spirit: a new spirit put within — Ezek 36:26-27:**

- _Ezek 36:26-27._ "A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you" — "I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes" — "I will" five times; "cause you to walk" is His work
- _Phil 2:13._ "For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure" — the willing and the doing, both His

[SOP DA 671.2] "Christ has given His Spirit as a divine power to overcome all hereditary and cultivated tendencies to evil, and to impress His own character upon His church." — power over inherited and cultivated sin

[Q] isn't this salvation by works?
→ no: perfection is His gift, never our product — Eph 2:8-10 (His workmanship); LOF_ATJ 139.2; SC 69.1

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 139.2] "Then let us forever abandon all idea that perfection is anything that we are to work out." — the doing is His

[SOP SC 69.1] "They have trusted in Christ for the forgiveness of sin, but now they seek by their own efforts to live aright. But every such effort must fail." — strain is the wrong engine

[→] the seed gives the life and the Spirit gives the power; next, when the power works — now

## 4. Power Over the Flesh, Now — Rom 8:2-4

> The Spirit puts the flesh under, and He does it now. Under the reign of grace it is easier to do right than wrong.

[WB]: SIN REIGNED: wrong easy | GRACE REIGNS: right easy

- _Rom 8:2._ "For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free from the law of sin and death" — one law overrules another
- _Rom 8:3-4._ "God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh: That the righteousness of the law might be fulfilled in us" — in us, not only for us
- _Rom 8:13._ "if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body, ye shall live" — through the Spirit: the flesh is put to death, not negotiated with
- _Gal 5:16._ "Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh" — a promise with "shall not"
- _1 Cor 9:27._ "I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection" — Paul's own word: subjection
- _Rom 5:21._ "as sin hath reigned unto death, even so might grace reign through righteousness" — the same reign, turned the other way

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 143.3] "When grace reigns, it is easier to do right than it is to do wrong." — the primary witness for "now"

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 145.3] "When is it that He presents us faultless before the presence of His glory? Now." — the congregation answered it

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 142.5] "He is as able just now as He was then or as He will ever be." — no waiting period

[SOP COL 333.1] "All His biddings are enablings." — the command and the power arrive together

[Q] what about Romans 7, "the good that I would I do not"?
→ Rom 7 is the man who wants to obey and cannot; his cry ends in "I thank God through Jesus Christ" (7:24-25) and 8:2 "hath made me free" — Rom 7 is the cry, Rom 8 the answer

[PIONEER Waggoner, WOR 121.5] "But conviction is not conversion, although a very necessary step to that condition." — Rom 7 is a doorway, not a dwelling

[→] the power is real and present; the next question is what "perfect" looks like on the first day — §5

## 5. Perfect in Its Sphere — Mark 4:28

> The sun at dawn is the perfect sun; the blade is a perfect plant; a baby is a perfect baby. God measures each stage by what He made it to be at that stage.

[WB]: sun at dawn · the blade · a baby → perfect at each point

- _Mark 4:28._ "For the earth bringeth forth fruit of herself; first the blade, then the ear, after that the full corn in the ear" — three stages, one plant, the same life
- _Prov 4:18._ "the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day" — the light grows; it is light the whole way
- _Luke 2:40._ "And the child grew, and waxed strong in spirit, filled with wisdom" — Christ Himself grew
- _Luke 2:52._ "And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man" — growth with no sin in it

[SOP COL 65.2] "At every stage of development our life may be perfect; yet if God's purpose for us is fulfilled, there will be continual advancement." — the anchor: perfect now, more later

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 151.5] "According to the rate of its progress it is as perfect at that point as it will be when its course is finished, at the point of maturity." — the blade argument

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 150.2] "And while growing all the time, He was perfect all the time." — Christ is the pattern of it

[SOP CT 141.1] "But at each stage of His development He was perfect, with the simple, natural grace of a sinless life." — the child Jesus

[PIONEER Waggoner, PTUK March 1, 1894, page 144.7] "Complete, yet growing in grace and knowledge; perfect all the time. The plant is perfect at every stage of its growth." — the same reading, 1894

| figure     | early stage    | still perfect? | the failure       |
| ---------- | -------------- | -------------- | ----------------- |
| the sun    | dawn           | yes            | never rises       |
| the wheat  | the blade      | yes            | no ear, no corn   |
| the child  | cannot run yet | yes            | never grows up    |

[Aside] a baby who cannot run: no one calls it a faulty baby; Jones's congregation answered "Perfect" — LOF_ATJ 151.7

[Q] "nobody's perfect"?
→ perfect in his sphere, not in God's greatness — 8T 64.1; the ultimate is not the only measure — LOF_ATJ 150.2

[SOP 8T 64.1] "As God is perfect in His sphere, so man may be perfect in his sphere." — the sphere is the key word

[→] perfect at the stage, and shown only what the stage can bear — §6

## 6. Shown As We Can Bear It — John 16:12

> God shows us our sinfulness step by step. Seen all at once, it would crush us into discouragement. So He counts us perfect at our stage, and each new sight of sin is a sign of growth, not of failure.

[WB]: MORE LIGHT → MORE SIN SEEN → MORE NEED OF HIM → MORE GROWTH

- _John 16:12._ "I have yet many things to say unto you, but ye cannot bear them now" — Christ holds back what we cannot yet carry
- _Mark 4:33._ "with many such parables spake he the word unto them, as they were able to hear it" — the measure is the hearer's strength
- _Deut 7:22._ "the LORD thy God will put out those nations before thee by little and little: thou mayest not consume them at once" — the land was taken by little and little, lest the wild beasts overrun it; so is the heart
- _Heb 5:14._ "But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age" — strong meat is truth given before the hearer can bear it, not a higher class of doctrine

[SOP Ev 57.3] "little by little, as they were able to bear it, He unfolded to them the great truths of the kingdom" — Christ's own method with truth, and with the truth about us

[SOP SC 64.2] "The closer you come to Jesus, the more faulty you will appear in your own eyes; for your vision will be clearer" — a growing sight of sin is evidence of nearness

[Q] I see more sin in me now than when I began; am I going backward?
→ no: the light is brighter, not the heart darker; seeing more of our need is how we grow — SC 64.2; Prov 4:18

[→] perfect at the stage is not finished at the goal — Paul holds both in one paragraph, §7

## 7. "Not Already Perfect" and "As Many as Be Perfect" — Phil 3:12-15

> Paul uses the word both ways in four verses: not yet perfect at the goal, and perfect in the way. Both are true of every growing Christian.

- _Phil 3:12._ "Not as though I had already attained, either were already perfect: but I follow after" — the goal not yet reached
- _Phil 3:14._ "I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus" — the pressing is the growing
- _Phil 3:15._ "Let us therefore, as many as be perfect, be thus minded" — the same men, called perfect, three verses later

| Phil 3:12                  | Phil 3:15                    |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| "not already perfect"      | "as many as be perfect"      |
| the full corn (the goal)   | the blade (the stage)        |
| "I press toward the mark"  | "be thus minded"             |

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 151.9] "though nearer to ultimate perfection, it is no more perfect, as it stands now, than it was the moment that it peeped through the ground." — nearer, not more

[SOP AA 560.3] "Sanctification is not the work of a moment, an hour, a day, but of a lifetime." — the stage is now; the goal is a lifetime

[SOP SL 10.1] "There is no such thing as instantaneous sanctification. True sanctification is a daily work, continuing as long as life shall last." — no shortcut to noon

[Q] if Paul was "not already perfect," how can anyone be perfect?
→ Phil 3:12 speaks of the goal and 3:15 of the stage; Paul claims neither sinlessness nor defeat — AA 561.1

[SOP AA 561.1] "None of the apostles and prophets ever claimed to be without sin." — the holiest claimed it least

[→] if every stage can be perfect, the test is not "have I arrived?" but "am I growing?" — §8

## 8. The Real Test: Is It Growing? — Luke 13:6-9

> God is not offended by a blade. He is grieved by a tree that bears no fruit after three years, a sun that never climbs, a baby that never grows up.

[WB]: three years → no fruit → cut it down? → one more year

- _Luke 13:6-7._ "A certain man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard; and he came and sought fruit thereon, and found none" — "these three years I come seeking fruit on this fig tree, and find none: cut it down; why cumbereth it the ground?" — not a young tree; an old one with leaves only
- _Luke 13:8-9._ "Lord, let it alone this year also, till I shall dig about it, and dung it: And if it bear fruit, well" — the Gardener pleads and works
- _Heb 5:12._ "when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have need that one teach you again" — a milestone missed
- _1 Cor 13:11._ "when I became a man, I put away childish things" — childhood is perfect for a child, a failure for a man
- _2 Pet 1:5-8._ "add to your faith virtue; and to virtue knowledge" — "they make you that ye shall neither be barren nor unfruitful" — the milestones, named

[SOP COL 65.2] "The plant must either grow or die." — no third option

[SOP AA 530.1] "the ladder of Christian progress, every step of which represents advancement in the knowledge of God, and in the climbing of which there is to be no standstill" — milestones are rungs

[SOP COL 67.1] "The object of the Christian life is fruit bearing—the reproduction of Christ's character in the believer, that it may be reproduced in others." — fruit is His character, shared

[SOP COL 217.2] "The pitying Saviour is saying concerning you, Spare it this year also, till I shall dig about it and dress it." — this year is mercy

[SOP COL 218.3] "The heart that does not respond to divine agencies becomes hardened until it is no longer susceptible to the influence of the Holy Spirit." — the danger is not slowness but refusal

[RQ] What milestone has God passed you through this year? Where has the growth stopped?

[→] growth by His life guards against two errors: claiming arrival and excusing a standstill — §9

## 9. Two Ditches — 1 John 1:8; 1 John 2:1

> Yes to victory over sin now, by the Spirit and the Word. No to perfectionism that claims sinlessness or measures others. John holds both.

- _1 John 1:8-9._ "If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves" — "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness" — the boast is the sin; the cure is cleansing from all
- _1 John 2:1._ "these things write I unto you, that ye sin not. And if any man sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous" — the aim and the safety net in one verse
- _Job 9:20._ "if I say, I am perfect, it shall also prove me perverse" — Jones reads this text, LOF_ATJ 138.4

| ditch: "I am sinless"     | the path of the just       | ditch: "nobody's perfect" |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| claims arrival            | perfect at the stage       | expects to sin            |
| measures others           | grows by His life          | excuses the standstill    |
| no need felt              | humble, and kept           | no victory sought         |

[SOP SL 7.2] "Those who are really seeking to perfect Christian character will never indulge the thought that they are sinless." — the closer, the humbler

[SOP 3SM 354.2] "You never have heard me say I am sinless." — Ellen White of herself

[SOP SL 9.1] "Those who are truly sanctified will not set up their own opinion as a standard of right and wrong." — no measuring others

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 138.1] "It is settled, then, that mine is not the standard." — not my estimate of me, not my estimate of you

[Q] 1 John 1:8 says we have sin; doesn't that cancel this?
→ 1:8 forbids the claim; 1:9 promises cleansing "from all unrighteousness"; 2:1 states the aim, "that ye sin not" — GC 473.1

[SOP GC 473.1] "the claim to be without sin is, in itself, evidence that he who makes this claim is far from holy." — the claim refutes itself

[Q] then am I lost when I fall?
→ no: "if any man sin, we have an advocate" — 1 John 2:1; fall, confess, rise, keep growing — SC 64.1

[SOP SC 64.1] "Even if we are overcome by the enemy, we are not cast off, not forsaken and rejected of God." — a fall is not a verdict

[→] victory is the aim, humility the climate, the Advocate the safety — and the whole work is His — §10

## 10. Appeal — Jude 1:24

> He is able to keep you from falling now, and to finish what He began. Yield to the seed, walk in the Spirit, and keep growing.

- _Jude 1:24._ "Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy" — able to keep, able to present: Jones's "Now"
- _Phil 1:6._ "he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ" — the Finisher is the Starter
- _Mark 4:29._ "But when the fruit is brought forth, immediately he putteth in the sickle, because the harvest is come" — the harvest waits on the fruit

[SOP COL 69.1] "When the character of Christ shall be perfectly reproduced in His people, then He will come to claim them as His own." — the full corn, and the harvest

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 144.8] "Would I rather be crucified and destroyed than to sin?" — the one question to settle today

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 153.2] "Then be of good cheer. Let it be Christ first, last, and all the time." — the chapter's last word

- decision: settle that perfection is expected, take the Word as seed, ask for the Spirit, and expect to be kept today
- decision: where you fall, confess and rise; where you have stopped, let the Gardener dig this year
- _2 Pet 3:18._ "But grow in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ" — the path of the just: more and more unto the perfect day
