# Sabbath School — Q4 W11 (2026)

## Prophetic Manifestation in Crucial Moments

_Week of December 5–11 · Read: 2 Pet. 2:5; Judg. 4:4–16; 1 Kings 18:21, 38, 39; Isa. 59:1–4; Neh. 9:30; Rev. 1:1–3_

**Memory Verse:** — _Amos 3:7._ "Surely the Lord GOD will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets."

**At every crisis God has sent a prophet with the same message: believe God, and He will count you righteous. The last crisis has a prophetic voice too, sent to lift up Christ our righteousness so that a people can stand in the judgment.**

## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — The Secret Told Before the Crisis — Amos 3:7

> God never strikes without warning first. Each warning comes with a way of escape, and the way is always faith in the word He sends.

[WB]: CRISIS → PROPHET → "BELIEVE" → RIGHTEOUS → DELIVERED

- _Amos 3:7._ "Surely the Lord GOD will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets." — no judgment comes unannounced; warning comes before wrath
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the prophet's message and the believer's response, in one verse
- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — the first and last thing every prophet asks for
- chain: Hos 12:13 (by a prophet Israel was preserved) → 1 Pet 1:10-11 (Spirit of Christ in the prophets) → Acts 10:43 (all the prophets witness remission) → Rom 3:21 (righteousness witnessed by the prophets)

[SOP DA 234.3] "God has given these things to us, and His blessing will attend the reverent, prayerful study of the prophetic scriptures." — the secret is meant to be read

[SOP LHU 332.3] "Satan is our destroyer, but Christ is our restorer. We must put faith into constant exercise, and trust in God, whatever our feelings may be." — 2 Chr 20:20 applied

[Q] Does each prophet bring a different way of salvation?
→ No. "witnessed by the law and the prophets" — Rom 3:21; the gospel was "preached before" to Abraham — Gal 3:8

[→] Each day this week is a crisis, a prophet, and the same call: believe God.

## 2. Sunday — Noah: Preacher of the Righteousness by Faith — Heb 11:7

> Noah preached a flood, but he also preached righteousness, and he received that righteousness himself by believing the warning before he could see it.

- _Heb 11:7._ "By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith." — the warning came first, then faith, then the ark, and the inheritance was righteousness
- _2 Pet 2:5._ "saved Noah the eighth [person], a preacher of righteousness" — he preached what he had received
- _Gen 6:8._ "But Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD." — grace is named before his walk (6:9)
- _Gen 6:5._ "every imagination of the thoughts of his heart [was] only evil continually" — this was the crisis
- chain: 1 Pet 3:20 (longsuffering waited; eight souls) → Matt 24:37 (as the days of Noe) → Gen 9:13 (the bow: a covenant kept)

[SOP PP 95.1] "He gave the world an example of believing just what God says." — faith takes God at His word

[SOP PP 92.2] "Those who would believe the message, and would prepare for that event by repentance and reformation, should find pardon and be saved." — the door was open to anyone who believed

[PIONEER Waggoner, EVCO 349.2] "“Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness,” and the same is affirmed of Enoch and Noah and all the patriarchs and prophets." — one gospel before the Flood and after it

[Q] Was Noah saved by building the ark?
→ He was saved by faith, and the building showed it: "his works testified of his sincerity" — PP 95.1; "God alone could preserve His servants upon the tempestuous waters" — PP 92.3

[→] Noah's day is ours (Matt 24:37). The same warning goes out, and the same righteousness by faith is offered with it.

## 3. Monday — Deborah: Victory by Believing the Word — Judg 4:14

> The prophetess did not fight Sisera. She gave Barak God's word. The battle turned on whether Israel would act on a promise it could not yet see.

- _Judg 4:14._ "Up; for this [is] the day in which the LORD hath delivered Sisera into thine hand: is not the LORD gone out before thee?" — "hath delivered": the promise speaks as if the battle were already won
- _Judg 4:8._ "If thou wilt go with me, then I will go" — faith leaning on a human prop
- _Judg 4:15._ "And the LORD discomfited Sisera, and all [his] chariots" — nine hundred iron chariots (4:13), and God alone defeated them
- _Judg 5:2._ "Praise ye the LORD for the avenging of Israel, when the people willingly offered themselves." — faith works by love; nobody was drafted
- chain: Judg 2:16 (judges raised up) → Judg 5:31 (they that love him as the sun) → Zech 4:6 (not by might)

| Israel's assets   | What won           |
| ----------------- | ------------------ |
| 10,000 unarmed    | "the LORD gone out" |
| Barak's skill     | the word believed  |
| Deborah's counsel | God alone          |

[SOP RC 329.6] "God alone could have discomfited the enemy, and the victory could be ascribed to Him alone." — the righteousness that saved Israel was God's own

[SOP RC 329.4] "He accepted the message from Deborah as the word of God, but he had little confidence in Israel" — he believed God and doubted the people

[Q] Barak's faith was weak. Did it count?
→ Barak is named in the roll of faith (Heb 11:32): "through faith subdued kingdoms, wrought righteousness" — Heb 11:33; "out of weakness were made strong" — Heb 11:34

[→] Faith does not depend on how strong the believer is. It depends on what God has said.

## 4. Tuesday — Elijah: No More Halting — 1 Kgs 18:21

> Carmel was a call to decide. The fire fell on the sacrifice, and the people fell on their faces. The last Elijah message presses the same choice, and the people who stand are clothed in Christ's righteousness.

- _1 Kgs 18:21._ "How long halt ye between two opinions? if the LORD [be] God, follow him: but if Baal, [then] follow him. And the people answered him not a word." — halting between two is unbelief, and silence is no answer
- _1 Kgs 18:18._ "ye have forsaken the commandments of the LORD" — the drought came from broken law, not from the prophet
- _1 Kgs 18:36._ "at [the time of] the offering of the [evening] sacrifice" — Elijah prayed at the hour of the sacrifice
- _1 Kgs 18:38._ "Then the fire of the LORD fell, and consumed the burnt sacrifice" — the fire fell on the offering, not on the guilty people
- _1 Kgs 18:39._ "The LORD, he [is] the God; the LORD, he [is] the God." — repentance follows once God has shown Himself
- _Mal 4:5._ "Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the LORD" — an Elijah message comes before the last day

[SOP PK 153.1] "At last Israel is aroused, undeceived, penitent." — this is what a prophet's message does

[SOP PK 188.1] "Those who have assumed the ornaments of the sanctuary, but are not clothed with Christ’s righteousness, will then appear in the shame of their own nakedness." — in the final test, profession without Christ's righteousness fails

[SOP 1888 534.6] "Somebody is to come in the spirit and power of Elijah, and when he appears, men may say, “You are too earnest" — written in 1890, defending the 1888 message

[→] Rev 3:18 offers "white raiment." Today the halting is between our own righteousness and His.

## 5. Wednesday — Hope in Exile: Sin Separates, the Redeemer Comes — Isa 59:1-2, 20

> God's hand never grew short. Sin built the wall. While the people were in exile, the prophets named the cure: God's own arm and God's own righteousness.

- _Isa 59:1._ "Behold, the LORD'S hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear:" — the problem is not on God's side
- _Isa 59:2._ "But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid [his] face from you, that he will not hear." — sin is what separates
- _Neh 9:30._ "Yet many years didst thou forbear them, and testifiedst against them by thy spirit in thy prophets: yet would they not give ear" — years of patience; the Spirit spoke through the prophets
- _Isa 59:16._ "therefore his arm brought salvation unto him; and his righteousness, it sustained him." — no man could intercede, so God brought His own righteousness
- _Isa 59:20._ "And the Redeemer shall come to Zion, and unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob" — the exile prophets end with a Redeemer
- chain: 2 Chr 36:15-16 (rising up betimes; mocked) → Jer 25:4 (ye have not hearkened) → Jer 23:6 (THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS) → Isa 61:10 (robe of righteousness)

[SOP SS 170.3] "because the sinner refuses the abundant provisions of grace, God is unable to deliver from sin." — exile is what refused grace looks like

[SOP AG 138.4] "Christ has satisfied the demands of the law for every repentant, believing sinner." — Isa 59:16 fulfilled at the cross

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 55.2] "the law has not a particle of righteousness to bestow upon any man" — the problem

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 61.1] "God puts His righteousness upon the believer. He covers him with it, so that his sin no more appears." — the answer: Isa 61:10

[Q] If God is so patient, why did the exile happen at all?
→ He waited "till [there was] no remedy" — 2 Chr 36:16; He did not leave them without hope: "Redeemer shall come" — Isa 59:20

## 6. Thursday — A Prophet for the Time of Waiting — Rev 1:1-3

> John wrote down the Revelation of Jesus Christ. A blessing rests on whoever reads it, and its last message is Christ formed in His people before He comes.

- _Rev 1:1._ "The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him, to shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass" — Christ is its subject and its source
- _Rev 1:2._ "Who bare record of the word of God, and of the testimony of Jesus Christ" — a witness, not an author
- _Rev 1:3._ "Blessed [is] he that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy, and keep those things which are written therein: for the time [is] at hand." — a blessing for reading it, hearing it, and keeping it
- _Rev 1:9._ "companion in tribulation, and in the kingdom and patience of Jesus Christ" — waiting in patience, as Rev 14:12 shows
- _Rev 14:12._ "here [are] they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." — commandments kept, together with the faith of Jesus
- chain: Rev 1:7 (He cometh with clouds) → Rev 10:7 (mystery finished as declared to the prophets) → Col 1:27 (Christ in you, the hope of glory)

[PIONEER Smith, DAR 323.3] "Jesus Christ is the Revelator, not John." — the title belongs to Christ

[PIONEER Smith, DAR 324.1] "A revelation is something revealed, something clearly made known, not something hidden and concealed." — it is plain, not mystical

[SOP 3SM 168.2] "The faith of Jesus has been overlooked and treated in an indifferent, careless manner. It has not occupied the prominent position in which it was revealed to John." — Rev 14:12 is half-preached

[PIONEER Jones, CWCP 124.1] "this is the finishing of the mystery of God, which is Christ in you the hope of glory." — Rev 10:7 joined to Col 1:27

[Q] Isn't Revelation a sealed book?
→ Dan 12:4 sealed it "to the time of the end"; Rev 1:3 blesses those who read it; "the prophecies relating to the last days especially demand our study" — LHU 378.2

## 7. Friday — 1844 and 1888: The Gift in the Last Crisis — Rev 19:10

> God gave the remnant the testimony of Jesus at two turning points. In 1844 it said "eyes on Jesus." In 1888 it said "receive His righteousness." Both times the gift pointed to Christ.

- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — the last church has a prophetic gift
- _Rev 19:10._ "for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — the gift is defined in Scripture
- _Rev 18:1._ "and the earth was lightened with his glory." — the loud cry that follows

| Crisis                   | Message the gift gave                 |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Disappointment (1844)    | eyes fixed on Jesus — EW 14.1          |
| Law vs. gospel (1888)    | justification through faith — TM 91.2  |
| Loud cry (now)           | third angel "in verity" — 1SM 372.2    |

[SOP EW 14.1] "If they kept their eyes fixed on Jesus, who was just before them, leading them to the city, they were safe." — the first vision was about looking to Jesus

[SOP 3AM 113.4] "It presented justification through faith in the Surety; it invited the people to receive the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience to all the commandments of God." — the 1888 message described

[SOP TM 91.2] "This is the message that God commanded to be given to the world. It is the third angel’s message, which is to be proclaimed with a loud voice" — this is the loud cry

[SOP 1SM 372.2] "It is the third angel’s message, in verity." — justification by faith

[PIONEER Loughborough, PGGC 40.3] "not to give a new revelation to take the place of the Scriptures, but simplifying and making clear the truths taught therein" — what the gift is for

[Q] Does the gift add to the Bible?
→ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word" — Isa 8:20; the gift does not add, it makes plain — PGGC 40.3

- [TANGENT] Luke 24:27 (all the prophets: things concerning Himself) → 1 Pet 1:11 (Spirit of Christ testified) → Rev 19:10 (testimony of Jesus) → TM 92.1 (the uplifted Saviour, the Lamb slain)

## 8. Appeal

- Every crisis this week was answered the same way: God spoke through a prophet, someone believed, and that person was counted righteous
- Noah believed before he saw, Barak went down on the strength of a word, and Israel cried "The LORD, he is the God"
- Our crisis is the judgment hour. The prophetic word holds out the same robe: Christ's righteousness, received by faith, which shows itself in obedience to all His commandments
- Stop halting between two opinions. Believe what God has said about your sin and about His Son's righteousness
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper."
- _Isa 61:10._ "for he hath clothed me with the garments of salvation, he hath covered me with the robe of righteousness" — decide today to put it on
