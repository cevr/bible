# Sabbath School — Q4 W13 (2026)

## The Word That Counts the Believer Righteous

_Quarterly: Blessings From the Prophetic Word · December 19–25 · Read: 1 Cor. 2:2; John 1:1–5; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; 2 Tim. 4:1–5; Isa. 40:1–5; John 17:20–23; 1 Cor. 1:10–13_

**Memory Verse:** — _2 Tim 3:16-17._ "All scripture [is] given by inspiration of God, and [is] profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness: That the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works."

**Theme: Every word God sends by His prophets points to Christ our righteousness, and whoever believes that word is counted righteous, comforted, and made one with God's people.**

1. God speaks; believing Him is righteousness — Gen 15:6 _(quarterly: Sabbath)_

## Open

- **Say:** This quarter we studied the gift of prophecy. Today one question: what is the blessing the prophetic word was sent to give?
- _2 Tim 3:16-17._ "All scripture [is] given by inspiration of God, and [is] profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness" — the word's aim is righteousness
- [RQ] According to Paul, what is Scripture instruction *in*?
  → "instruction in righteousness" — and only then "throughly furnished unto all good works"

---

## 1. God Speaks; Believing Him Is Righteousness — Gen 15:6

- **Say:** God never asked for blind faith. He speaks, and the one who believes what He says is counted righteous.
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — two beliefs, one faith: the prophet's word is God's word
- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — the word heard by faith = righteousness counted
- _Rom 4:3._ "Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness." — Paul's proof text for justification
- [RQ] What did Abraham do to be counted righteous?
  → "he believed in the LORD" — nothing more; God counted it (Gen 15:6)
- [RQ] What does Jehoshaphat promise to those who believe the prophets?
  → "so shall ye prosper" — the blessing rides on believing (2 Chr 20:20)

[WB]: GOD SPEAKS → I BELIEVE → COUNTED RIGHTEOUS

[PIONEER Waggoner, EVCO 67.3] "When God spoke the promise, Abraham said “Amen,” or, in other words, he built upon God, taking His word as a sure foundation." — believing = building on the word

- **Bridge:** If believing God's word is righteousness, the next question is what that word is about.

---

## 2. Every Prophet Speaks of Christ — Luke 24:27

- **Say:** The prophetic word has one subject. Christ is the Word behind every prophet, and the gift exists to show Him.
- _Luke 24:25._ "O fools, and slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken" — the disciples' problem was unbelief, not lack of data
- _Luke 24:27._ "And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself." — Christ the theme of every book
- _2 Tim 3:15._ "able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus" — the verse before the memory verse: Scripture's job is faith in Christ
- _Rev 19:10._ "for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy" — the gift's content is Jesus
- chain: John 1:1 (the Word was God) → 1 Cor 2:2 (Christ, and him crucified) → Gal 6:14 (glory in the cross)
- [RQ] On the Emmaus road, what did Jesus expound from Moses and all the prophets?
  → "the things concerning himself" (Luke 24:27)
- [RQ] What does the spirit of prophecy testify of?
  → Jesus: "the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy" (Rev 19:10)

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 44.1] "He is the One through whom the Divine will and the Divine power are made known to men." — why Christ is "the Word"

- **Bridge:** A word that exalts Christ will be counterfeited, and the counterfeit will look like the truth.

---

## 3. "It Is Written" Exposes the Counterfeit Gospel — Isa 8:20

- **Say:** Satan's first move was to doubt God's word; his last is to offer fables that leave people in their sins. The test is the written word.
- _Gen 3:1._ "Yea, hath God said" — the first attack is on believing God
- _2 Tim 4:3-4._ "For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine; but after their own lusts shall they heap to themselves teachers, having itching ears; And they shall turn away [their] ears from the truth, and shall be turned unto fables." — the end-time market for fables
- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, [it is] because [there is] no light in them." — law + testimony, the remnant's two marks
- _Matt 4:4._ "It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God." — Christ's only weapon
- [RQ] Why do people heap up teachers in the last days?
  → "after their own lusts" — they want teachers who leave sin undisturbed (2 Tim 4:3)
- [RQ] What is the test of every message?
  → "To the law and to the testimony" — if not according to this word, "no light in them" (Isa 8:20)

| God said (Gen 2:17)     | Serpent said (Gen 3:4)    |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------- |
| "thou shalt surely die" | "Ye shall not surely die" |

[PIONEER Jones, EMTF 21.4] "The people choose pleasing fables, which do not disturb them in their sins, rather than the reproving, searching declarations of the word of God." — fables shelter sin

- **Bridge:** The true word does disturb us in our sins, but only to bring the comfort no fable can give.

## 4. The Comfort Is a Verdict: Pardoned — Isa 40:1-2

- **Say:** The prophet's comfort is not a mood. It is a verdict, "her iniquity is pardoned," and the voice in the wilderness carries it to prepare the way of the Lord.
- _Isa 40:1-2._ "Comfort ye, comfort ye my people, saith your God. Speak ye comfortably to Jerusalem, and cry unto her, that her warfare is accomplished, that her iniquity is pardoned" — comfort = pardon announced
- _Isa 40:3._ "Prepare ye the way of the LORD, make straight in the desert a highway for our God." — the comforted become heralds
- _Isa 40:9._ "say unto the cities of Judah, Behold your God!" — the comfort is a Person shown
- [RQ] What two things does God tell the prophet to cry to Jerusalem?
  → "her warfare is accomplished" and "her iniquity is pardoned" (Isa 40:2)
- [RQ] Who is the comfort, in the end?
  → God Himself: "Behold your God!" (Isa 40:9)

[PIONEER Waggoner, GCDB March 2, 1897, page 210.4] "The message is that the warfare in every particular has been accomplished, has been fought, and won, absolutely. That is a thing for us to believe." — Isa 40:2 = finished victory, received by faith

- **Bridge:** A pardoned people cannot stay divided, because they were all pardoned at the same place.

## 5. One at the Same Cross — John 17:20-21

- **Say:** Unity is not organized; it comes when every believer draws near the same cross. Christ prayed for it, and He prayed for those who believe through the prophetic word.
- _John 17:20-21._ "Neither pray I for these alone, but for them also which shall believe on me through their word; That they all may be one; as thou, Father, [art] in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that thou hast sent me." — unity starts with faith in the apostles' word
- _1 Cor 1:13._ "Is Christ divided? was Paul crucified for you?" — party spirit forgets who was crucified
- chain: 1 Cor 1:10 (no divisions among you) → Eph 4:13 (unity of the faith) → Col 1:27 (Christ in you)
- [RQ] For whom did Jesus pray in John 17:20?
  → "them also which shall believe on me through their word" — that includes us
- [RQ] What question does Paul use to end the divisions at Corinth?
  → "Is Christ divided? was Paul crucified for you?" — only one was crucified, so there is one people (1 Cor 1:13)

[SOP 7BC 937.8] "We cannot draw nigh to God, and come to the same cross, without our hearts being blended together in perfect unity" — unity = shared justification

- **Bridge:** All that remains is whether we will taste the word ourselves.

## Decision

- **Say:** The prophets' message never changed: believe God, and it is counted for righteousness. God offers His righteousness in His word; He asks us to take Him at His word.
- _Ps 34:8._ "O taste and see that the LORD [is] good: blessed [is] the man [that] trusteth in him." — tasting = trusting
- _Rom 1:17._ "For therein is the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith: as it is written, The just shall live by faith." — the righteousness is revealed, then received
- [RQ] Who is blessed, according to the psalm?
  → "the man [that] trusteth in him" — the blessing is received by trust, not earned (Ps 34:8)

[SOP 7BC 984.6] "the loud cry of the third angel has already begun in the revelation of the righteousness of Christ, the sin-pardoning Redeemer." — the final blessing of the prophetic word
