## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — 2 Chr 20:20

> The prophets never asked for blind faith; they asked for faith in a God who speaks. Believing His word is how righteousness has always come (Gen 15:6).

- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — two beliefs, one faith: the prophet's word is God's word
- _2 Pet 1:19._ "We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts" — surer than the mount; the day star rises *in hearts*
- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — the first prophetic word heard by faith = righteousness counted
- chain: Rom 4:3 (Abraham believed God) → Rom 10:17 (faith by hearing the word) → Heb 11:6 (without faith impossible) → Hab 2:4 (just live by faith)

[PIONEER Waggoner, EVCO 67.3] "When God spoke the promise, Abraham said “Amen,” or, in other words, he built upon God, taking His word as a sure foundation." — believing = building on the word

[SOP Mar 17.4] "Whoever is with singleness of purpose seeking to do God’s will, earnestly heeding the light already given, will receive greater light" — light given to the believing

[WB]: GOD SPEAKS → PROPHET → I BELIEVE → COUNTED RIGHTEOUS

[→] every day this week is one blessing of that word: the word that justifies the one who believes it

## 2. Sunday — Prophecy Exalts Christ — 1 Cor 2:2

> The testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy: the gift exists to say "Behold the Lamb," and the remnant who have it also keep "the faith of Jesus."

**Paul's whole ministry — one Person, one event — 1 Cor 2:2; Gal 6:14:**

- _1 Cor 2:2._ "For I determined not to know any thing among you, save Jesus Christ, and him crucified." — the prophet-apostle's single subject
- _Gal 6:14._ "God forbid that I should glory, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ" — glory moved off self onto the cross

[SOP 5BC 1133.1] "When the sinner reaches the cross, and looks up to the One who died to save him, he may rejoice with fullness of joy; for his sins are pardoned." — what "Christ crucified" delivers

**Christ is the Word behind every prophet — John 1:1-5:**

- _John 1:1-5._ "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. The same was in the beginning with God. All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made. In him was life; and the life was the light of men. And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not." — the Creator is the Speaker
- chain: 1 Pet 1:10-11 (Spirit of Christ in prophets) → Acts 10:43 (all prophets witness) → Rom 3:21-22 (righteousness witnessed by the prophets)

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 44.1] "He is the One through whom the Divine will and the Divine power are made known to men." — why Christ is "the Word"

[SOP PP 366.3] "It is the voice of Christ that speaks to us through the Old Testament." — every prophet = Christ speaking

**The remnant's marks — Rev 12:17; 19:10; 14:12:**

| Rev 12:17 / 19:10      | Rev 14:12              |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- |
| keep the commandments  | keep the commandments  |
| testimony of Jesus     | faith of Jesus         |
| = spirit of prophecy   | = Christ our righteousness |

- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ" — the gift marks the last church
- _Rev 19:10._ "for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy" — the gift's content is Jesus

[SOP 12MR 193.4] "He came to our world and took our sins that we might take His righteousness." — what "the faith of Jesus" is

[SOP 3SP 182.1] "we see by faith through their eyes, and hear through their ears; and our faith with theirs grasps the evidence given." — lesson's quote: faith rests on prophetic testimony

[Q] Is the prophetic gift mainly about predicting events?
→ Its testimony is "of Jesus" — Rev 19:10; predictions serve faith in Him; Luke 24:27

## 3. Monday — Prophecy Affirms the Bible — 2 Tim 3:16-17

> Scripture is God-breathed "instruction in righteousness" that makes wise "unto salvation through faith"; the later prophetic gift is a lesser light pointing back to it.

- _2 Tim 3:16-17._ "All scripture [is] given by inspiration of God, and [is] profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness: That the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works." — the goal is righteousness, then works
- _2 Tim 3:15._ "able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus" — the verse just before: Scripture's job is faith in Christ
- _2 Pet 1:21._ "holy men of God spake [as they were] moved by the Holy Ghost" — origin: not human will

[SOP Ed 126.4] "Every command is a promise; accepted by the will, received into the soul, it brings with it the life of the Infinite One." — how the word instructs in righteousness

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 75.3] "when God speaks in His word, it is the same as though He spoke with an audible voice, and your faith is the evidence that you hear and believe." — inspiration means God speaking now

**Emmaus: Scripture before sight — Luke 24:25-27:**

- _Luke 24:25._ "O fools, and slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken" — the disciples' problem was unbelief, not lack of data
- _Luke 24:27._ "And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself." — Christ the theme of every book
- chain: Luke 24:44 (law, prophets, psalms — of Me) → John 5:39 (they testify of Me) → John 7:38 (believeth on me, as the scripture hath said)

[SOP DA 796.4] "Upon these their faith must be established. Christ performed no miracle to convince them, but it was His first work to explain the Scriptures." — why the Word came before recognition

## 4. Tuesday — Prophecy Is God's Truth Detector — 2 Tim 4:1-5

> Satan's oldest lie doubts God's word (Gen 3:1-5); his last one counterfeits the gospel. The detector is "It is written" — and the truth he most fears is righteousness by faith.

**The serpent's method: question the word, contradict the word — Gen 2:16-17; 3:1-5:**

| God said (Gen 2:17)       | Serpent said (Gen 3:4-5)   |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| "thou shalt surely die"   | "Ye shall not surely die"  |
| obey and live             | eat and be as gods         |

- _Gen 3:1._ "Yea, hath God said" — the first attack is on believing God
- _2 Chr 36:16._ "But they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused his prophets, until the wrath of the LORD arose against his people, till [there was] no remedy." — rejecting the prophets = no remedy
- _Matt 4:4._ "It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God." — Christ's only weapon

**Paul's charge — 2 Tim 4:1-5:**

- _2 Tim 4:2-4._ "Preach the word; be instant in season, out of season; reprove, rebuke, exhort with all longsuffering and doctrine. For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine; but after their own lusts shall they heap to themselves teachers, having itching ears; And they shall turn away [their] ears from the truth, and shall be turned unto fables." — the end-time market for fables
- chain: Isa 30:10 (speak smooth things) → 2 Thess 2:10 (received not the love of the truth) → 2 Thess 2:11 (strong delusion) → John 8:32 (truth makes free)

[PIONEER Jones, EMTF 21.4] "The people choose pleasing fables, which do not disturb them in their sins, rather than the reproving, searching declarations of the word of God." — fables shelter sin

[PIONEER James White, PRWO 3.5] "In the last days the plain truth of the word will be proclaimed." — the same prophecy guarantees a counter-witness

**The test, and the truth Satan most resists — Isa 8:20:**

- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, [it is] because [there is] no light in them." — law + testimony, the remnant's two marks

[SOP GC 593.1] "So closely will the counterfeit resemble the true that it will be impossible to distinguish between them except by the Holy Scriptures." — only the word discerns

[SOP GW 161.1] "The thought that the righteousness of Christ is imputed to us, not because of any merit on our part, but as a free gift from God, is a precious thought." — the truth under attack

[SOP GW 161.1] "The enemy of God and man is not willing that this truth should be clearly presented" — why the counterfeit targets the gospel

[Q] Isn't "truth detector" just checking doctrines like Sabbath and state of the dead?
→ Those matter (Rev 14:12 commandments), but the deepest counterfeit is self-righteousness; the test is "faith of Jesus" too — GW 161.1; Phil 3:9

[→] the remnant is detected by commandments *and* faith of Jesus: law plus Christ's righteousness

## 6. Thursday — Prophecy Unites the Body in Christ — John 17:20-23

> Unity is not organized; it is the fruit of every believer drawing near the same cross. The gifts were given "till we all come in the unity of the faith" — the full image of Christ in His people.

- _John 17:20-23._ "Neither pray I for these alone, but for them also which shall believe on me through their word; That they all may be one; as thou, Father, [art] in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that thou hast sent me. And the glory which thou gavest me I have given them; that they may be one, even as we are one: I in them, and thou in me, that they may be made perfect in one; and that the world may know that thou hast sent me, and hast loved them, as thou hast loved me." — "believe on me through their word": unity starts with faith in the prophetic word
- _1 Cor 1:13._ "Is Christ divided? was Paul crucified for you?" — party spirit forgets who was crucified
- _1 Cor 1:10._ "that ye all speak the same thing, and [that] there be no divisions among you" — same mind, same judgment

[SOP 7BC 937.8] "We cannot draw nigh to God, and come to the same cross, without our hearts being blended together in perfect unity" — unity = shared justification

[SOP 9T 194.2] "nothing short of the miraculous power of God can bring human beings with their different temperaments together in harmonious action" — unity is evidence (John 17:21)

**Gifts, not creeds — Eph 4:11-15:**

| Creeds                 | Gifts (Eph 4:11-13)        |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| man-made boundary      | God-set in the church      |
| sects multiply         | unity of the faith         |
| fixed at a point       | grow up into Christ        |

- _Eph 4:13._ "Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ" — unity's measure is Christ's fulness
- chain: Eph 2:20 (apostles, prophets, Christ cornerstone) → Col 1:27 (Christ in you, hope of glory) → Acts 16:31 (Believe on the Lord Jesus)

[PIONEER James White, PSG 5.1] "He [Paul] then presents the gifts as the means by which God designed to secure the unity of the church." — pioneer reading of Eph 4

[PIONEER James White, PSG 22.1] "claim as ours the Bible, and the whole Bible, gifts and all." — not gifts instead of Bible

[PIONEER Loughborough, PGGC 99.2] "One of the fruits of true gifts mentioned in the letter to the Ephesian church is the gathering of a people into the “unity of the faith.”" — the test applied to the advent movement

[PIONEER Jones, CWCP 124.1] "this developing in each believer the complete image of Jesus—this is the finishing of the mystery of God, which is Christ in you the hope of glory." — Eph 4:13 tied to the cleansing of the sanctuary

[Q] How can a church this diverse be one?
→ Not by uniformity but by the same cross — 7BC 937.8; John 17:23 "I in them"; Acts 6:1 shows division begins when the cross fades

[→] the "perfect man" of Eph 4:13 is a people finished in Christ — ready for the close of the sanctuary work

## 8. Appeal

- the prophets' message never changed: believe God — and it is counted for righteousness (Gen 15:6; 2 Chr 20:20)
- the gift of prophecy in the last church exists for one end: to lift up Christ our righteousness until "the earth was lightened with his glory" (Rev 18:1)

[SOP 7BC 984.6] "the loud cry of the third angel has already begun in the revelation of the righteousness of Christ, the sin-pardoning Redeemer." — the final blessing of the prophetic word

[SOP 3AM 115.1] "Christ and His righteousness are to be so blended with the third angel’s message that the whole world may be lightened with His glory." — present truth, 1888 in verity

- decide now: not to admire the prophets, but to believe the God who speaks through them — receive His righteousness, keep His commandments by His faith, stand in the judgment
- _Rom 1:16-17._ "For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek. For therein is the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith: as it is written, The just shall live by faith."
