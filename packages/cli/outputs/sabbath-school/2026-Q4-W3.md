# Sabbath School — Q4 W3 (2026)

## The Prophets' One Message: The Lord Our Righteousness

_Quarterly: Old Testament Prophets · October 10–16 · Read: James 5:17, 18; 1 Kings 19:1–16; 1 Kings 22:1–14; Exod. 15:20; 2 Kings 22:11–14; Jer. 14:1–15_

**Memory Verse:** — _2 Chr 20:20._ "And they rose early in the morning, and went forth into the wilderness of Tekoa: and as they went forth, Jehoshaphat stood and said, Hear me, O Judah, and ye inhabitants of Jerusalem; Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper."

**Theme: Every true prophet, weak as we are, said one thing — confess your sin and take THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS — and to believe His prophets is to believe that.**

## The road

1. Weak men, a strong word: righteousness is "in the LORD" — Jas 5:17 _(quarterly: Sun, Fri)_
2. "What the LORD saith": the true prophet points to Christ — 1 Kgs 22:14 _(quarterly: Mon)_
3. The law wounds, the Lord pardons — 2 Sam 12:13 _(quarterly: Tue, Wed)_
4. The false prophet: peace without righteousness — Jer 14:13 _(quarterly: Thu)_
5. Decision

## Open

- **Say:** Judah had no might and no plan. God sent one word through a prophet, and the king said: believe it. What did every prophet say, and why believe it?
- _2 Chr 20:12._ "for we have no might against this great company that cometh against us; neither know we what to do: but our eyes [are] upon thee" — the starting point of justification: confessed helplessness
- _2 Chr 20:15._ "for the battle [is] not yours, but God's" — the prophet's word: God fights for those who cannot
- [RQ] What two things does Jehoshaphat tell Judah to believe?
  → "the LORD your God" and "his prophets" (v.20) — and the prophet's word was "the battle is not yours, but God's" (v.15)

---

## 1. Weak Men, a Strong Word — Jas 5:17

- **Say:** The prophets were not better men than we are. Elijah's power was God's word; when he looked at himself, he fell apart.
- _Jas 5:17._ "Elias was a man subject to like passions as we are, and he prayed earnestly that it might not rain: and it rained not on the earth by the space of three years and six months." — the power was in the prayer of faith, not the man
- _1 Kgs 18:36._ "I have done all these things at thy word" — Carmel's secret: the word, not the prophet
- _1 Kgs 19:4._ "It is enough; now, O LORD, take away my life; for I [am] not better than my fathers" — he had been measuring himself
- _Isa 45:24._ "Surely, shall [one] say, in the LORD have I righteousness and strength" — the cure for Horeb
- chain: 2 Cor 12:9 (strength perfect in weakness) → 2 Cor 4:7 (treasure in earthen vessels)
- [RQ] On Carmel Elijah acted by what? Under the juniper, what was he measuring?
  → "at thy word" (18:36); then himself — "I am not better than my fathers" (19:4)
- [RQ] Where does Isaiah say righteousness and strength are found?
  → "in the LORD" (Isa 45:24) — never in the prophet, never in us

| Carmel (1 Kgs 18) | Horeb (1 Kgs 19)       |
| ----------------- | ---------------------- |
| "at thy word"     | "I only am left"       |
| eyes on God       | eyes on Jezebel / self |

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, PTUK July 4, 1895, page 418.5] "being a man of like passions with us, we also may be like him in faith." — Jas 5:17 is a promise, not an excuse

- **Bridge:** If the prophet's power was God's word, how do we tell God's word from man's?

## 2. "What the LORD Saith" — 1 Kgs 22:14

- **Say:** Micaiah stood alone against four hundred. The true prophet says only what God says — and what God says always points to Christ.
- _1 Kgs 22:13._ "the words of the prophets [declare] good unto the king with one mouth: let thy word, I pray thee, be like the word of one of them" — the pressure of 400 voices
- _1 Kgs 22:14._ "And Micaiah said, [As] the LORD liveth, what the LORD saith unto me, that will I speak." — the whole job description
- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, [it is] because [there is] no light in them" — Scripture judges the prophet
- _Acts 10:43._ "To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins" — every prophet's subject is the same Person
- [RQ] What did the messenger want Micaiah's word to be like, and what did he answer?
  → "like the word of one of them" (v.13); "what the LORD saith unto me, that will I speak" (v.14)
- [RQ] Of whom do all the prophets witness, and what do they offer?
  → of Christ: "through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins" (Acts 10:43)

[PIONEER J. N. Loughborough, SGL 32.2] "in the true prophet it is Christ first, last, and all the time." — the decisive test

- **Bridge:** And what God says to a sinner begins with a wound — four words from Nathan.

## 3. The Law Wounds, the Lord Pardons — 2 Sam 12:13

- **Say:** Prophets called kings back under God's rule. Nathan held up the mirror to David; Huldah sent God's word to Josiah. Each time, confession met pardon.
- _2 Sam 12:7._ "And Nathan said to David, Thou [art] the man." — the prophet holds up the mirror
- _2 Sam 12:13._ "And David said unto Nathan, I have sinned against the LORD. And Nathan said unto David, The LORD also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die." — justification in one sentence
- _Ps 32:2._ "Blessed [is] the man unto whom the LORD imputeth not iniquity" — David's own definition of righteousness by faith (Rom 4:6-8)
- _2 Kgs 22:11._ "And it came to pass, when the king had heard the words of the book of the law, that he rent his clothes." — the law does its work: conviction
- _2 Kgs 22:19._ "Because thine heart was tender, and thou hast humbled thyself before the LORD" — and "I also have heard [thee], saith the LORD": mercy to the contrite
- [RQ] How much time passes between David's "I have sinned" and Nathan's "put away thy sin"?
  → none — both stand in one verse (12:13)
- [RQ] Why did the LORD hear Josiah?
  → "thine heart was tender, and thou hast humbled thyself" (22:19) — contrition, not merit

[WB]: LAW → "THOU ART THE MAN" → "I HAVE SINNED" → "PUT AWAY"

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, PTUK August 31, 1893, page 337.8] "As soon as the sin is really confessed it is forgiven, and the soul is cleansed from it." — Nathan's answer came "instantly"

- **Bridge:** The false prophet skips the wound — he sells peace with no confession at all.

## 4. Peace Without Righteousness — Jer 14:13

- **Say:** The false prophet's signature is "assured peace" over unconfessed sin. Jeremiah's answer is a Name: THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS.
- _Jer 14:13._ "behold, the prophets say unto them, Ye shall not see the sword, neither shall ye have famine; but I will give you assured peace in this place." — the counterfeit gospel
- _Jer 6:14._ "They have healed also the hurt [of the daughter] of my people slightly, saying, Peace, peace; when [there is] no peace." — a cheap cure
- _Jer 23:22._ "But if they had stood in my counsel, and had caused my people to hear my words, then they should have turned them from their evil way" — real gospel saves FROM sin (Matt 1:21)
- _Jer 23:6._ "In his days Judah shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell safely: and this [is] his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS." — Jeremiah's answer to the false "peace," in the same chapter
- [RQ] What did the false prophets promise, and what would true ones have done?
  → "assured peace in this place" (14:13); "turned them from their evil way" (23:22)
- [RQ] What Name does Jeremiah give for the true peace?
  → "THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS" (23:6) — Israel "shall dwell safely" under Him

| False prophet               | True prophet                |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| "assured peace" (Jer 14:13) | "turned them" (Jer 23:22)   |
| leaves people in sin        | THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS  |

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, PTUK July 6, 1893, page 211.10] "Peace and righteousness, therefore are synonymous." — no righteousness, no peace: the false prophet's lie exposed

- **Bridge:** So "believe his prophets" today means one thing: take the peace that comes with His righteousness.

## Decision

- **Say:** Every true prophet, weak as we are, said one thing: confess, and receive THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS. That message is the loud cry. Believe it now.
- _Jer 14:20._ "We acknowledge, O LORD, our wickedness, [and] the iniquity of our fathers: for we have sinned against thee." — the confession the false prophets never asked for
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the decision
- [RQ] What establishes Judah — its might, or something else?
  → "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established" (v.20) — they had "no might" (v.12)

[SOP 7BC 984.6] "the loud cry of the third angel has already begun in the revelation of the righteousness of Christ, the sin-pardoning Redeemer." — believing His prophets now = receiving this

## If asked

- [Q] If the prophets sinned, why trust them?
  → Jas 5:17; 1 Kgs 22:14 — the trust is in "what the LORD saith," not in the man; Rom 11:5-6: God's work rests on grace
- [Q] Isn't fulfilled prediction enough to prove a prophet?
  → Not alone — Deut 13:1-3 lets a sign come to pass and still tests the message; Isa 8:20 + 1 Jn 4:2: the word must agree with the law and exalt Christ.
- [Q] Isn't any message of peace and assurance "false prophet" talk?
  → No — the false peace skips repentance (Jer 6:14); the true peace comes through a righteousness received (Jer 23:6; Rom 5:1) that turns from sin (Jer 23:22).
