## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — Believe God — 2 Chr 20:20

> Jehoshaphat's battle cry is the whole gospel in one verse: faith in God = establishment; faith in His word through the prophets = prosperity. The prophets never preached anything but "believe God."

[WB]: NO MIGHT (v.12) → EYES ON THEE → PROPHET: "battle is God's" → BELIEVE → SING → VICTORY

- _2 Chr 20:12._ "for we have no might against this great company that cometh against us; neither know we what to do: but our eyes [are] upon thee" — the starting point of justification: confessed helplessness
- _2 Chr 20:15._ "for the battle [is] not yours, but God's" — the prophet's word: God fights for those who cannot
- _2 Chr 20:17._ "stand ye [still], and see the salvation of the LORD with you" — salvation is received, not achieved
- _2 Chr 20:22._ "And when they began to sing and to praise, the LORD set ambushments" — faith praises before the victory is seen
- chain: Isa 7:9 (not believe → not established) → Gen 15:6 (believed → counted righteous) → Hab 2:4 (just live by faith) → Heb 11:6 (without faith impossible)

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, SITI February 25, 1889, page 104.61] "none of them [the gifts of the Spirit] has been used for this purpose more than the gift of prophecy." — "established" (2 Chr 20:20) is the gift's work

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, CHR 82.2] "The promise of God was considered as good as the actual accomplishment." — the definition of faith

**What the prophets preached — Christ our righteousness — Acts 10:43:**

- _Acts 10:43._ "To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins" — every prophet's subject is the same Person
- _Rom 3:21._ "the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets" — the prophets testify to righteousness by faith
- chain: 1 Pet 1:11 (Spirit of Christ in them) → Eph 2:20 (apostles + prophets, one foundation) → Rev 19:10 (testimony of Jesus = spirit of prophecy)

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, WOR 59.1] "The prophets preached the same Gospel that the apostles did." — one gospel, both testaments

[SOP CT 462.3] "Christ as manifested to the patriarchs, as symbolized in the sacrificial service, as portrayed in the law, and as revealed by the prophets is the riches of the Old Testament." — the prophets' treasure is Christ

[Q] "Believe his prophets" — isn't that faith in a human being?
→ No — faith in God's word through them; Jehoshaphat believed "Thus saith the LORD" (v.15), and Rev 19:10 says the prophet's testimony is Jesus' own.

[→] the whole week asks one question of every prophet: did he lead the people to believe God?

## 2. Sunday — The Humanity of Prophets — Jas 5:17

> Elijah was "a man subject to like passions as we are" — the fire on Carmel was God's, not his. When he looked at himself he despaired; righteousness and strength were only ever "in the LORD."

- _Jas 5:17._ "Elias was a man subject to like passions as we are, and he prayed earnestly that it might not rain: and it rained not on the earth by the space of three years and six months." — the power was in the prayer of faith, not the man
- _Jas 5:16._ "The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much" — "righteous" = one who believes God (Gen 15:6)
- _1 Kgs 18:36._ "I have done all these things at thy word" — Carmel's secret: the word, not the prophet

[SOP PK 156.3] "He did not wait for the full evidence that God had heard him, but was willing to venture all on the slightest token of divine favor." — faith takes God at His word

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, PTUK July 4, 1895, page 418.5] "being a man of like passions with us, we also may be like him in faith." — Jas 5:17 is a promise, not an excuse

**Horeb: the prophet with his eyes on himself — 1 Kgs 19:4, 10:**

- _1 Kgs 19:4._ "It is enough; now, O LORD, take away my life; for I [am] not better than my fathers" — he had been measuring himself
- _1 Kgs 19:10._ "I have been very jealous for the LORD God of hosts" — zeal recounted; "I, [even] I only, am left" — self at the center

| Carmel (1 Kgs 18)       | Horeb (1 Kgs 19)       |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------- |
| "at thy word"           | "I only am left"       |
| eyes on God             | eyes on Jezebel / self |
| fire from heaven        | juniper tree           |

[SOP 1888 488.3] "Did he see by faith the promises of God? Did he recount his faithfulness in every past emergency?" — the question for every discouraged soul

[SOP PK 174.1] "in a moment of weariness allowed the fear of death to overcome his faith in God." — the failure was unbelief

**God's answer: grace, not works — 1 Kgs 19:12, 18:**

- _1 Kgs 19:12._ "and after the fire a still small voice" — God is not in the display; He speaks His word
- _1 Kgs 19:18._ "Yet I have left [me] seven thousand in Israel, all the knees which have not bowed unto Baal" — God's work never depended on Elijah
- _Rom 11:5-6._ "Even so then at this present time also there is a remnant according to the election of grace. And if by grace, then [is it] no more of works" — Paul reads Elijah's 7000 as righteousness by grace
- _Isa 45:24._ "Surely, shall [one] say, in the LORD have I righteousness and strength" — the cure for Horeb

[SOP PK 175.1] "In his own strength man is strengthless; but in the might of God he may be strong to overcome evil and to help others to overcome." — EGW quotes Isa 45:24 here

[SOP PK 168.4] "He now knew that a quiet trust, a firm reliance on God, would ever find for him a present help in time of need." — lesson learned

[RQ] When you feel like a failure, whose righteousness are you measuring?

[→] God's question "What doest thou here?" (1 Kgs 19:9) is a recommission — Elijah is sent again (v.15-16), still a sinner, still used

## 3. Monday — Identifying True Prophets — 1 Kgs 22:14

> The true prophet says only what God says, however unpopular — and what God says, always, is Christ. The test is not charisma or majority but "Thus saith the LORD."

- _1 Kgs 22:14._ "And Micaiah said, [As] the LORD liveth, what the LORD saith unto me, that will I speak." — the whole job description
- _1 Kgs 22:13._ "the words of the prophets [declare] good unto the king with one mouth: let thy word, I pray thee, be like the word of one of them" — the pressure of 400 voices
- _1 Kgs 22:8._ "but I hate him; for he doth not prophesy good concerning me, but evil" — Ahab's charge is Micaiah's credential
- _1 Kgs 22:28._ "If thou return at all in peace, the LORD hath not spoken by me" — the Deut 18:22 test, staked publicly

| Micaiah             | The 400                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------- |
| one voice           | "one mouth"            |
| "what the LORD saith" | flatter the king  |
| hated, imprisoned   | the king's favor       |
| fulfilled (v.37)    | lying spirit (v.22)    |

[SOP PK 196.1] "The words of the prophet should have been enough to show the kings that their project was not favored by Heaven, but neither ruler felt inclined to heed the warning." — unbelief, not ignorance

**The tests God gave — Deut 18:18-22; Isa 8:20; 1 Jn 4:2:**

- _Deut 18:18._ "and will put my words in his mouth; and he shall speak unto them all that I shall command him" — the Prophet like Moses = Christ; every true prophet shares His mark
- _1 Sam 9:6._ "a man of God, and [he is] an honourable man; all that he saith cometh surely to pass" — Samuel's life and word matched
- _2 Pet 1:21._ "holy men of God spake [as they were] moved by the Holy Ghost" — origin is God, not man
- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, [it is] because [there is] no light in them" — Scripture judges the prophet
- _1 Jn 4:2._ "Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God" — the Christ-test

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, SITI July 15, 1886, page 423.7] "To confess that the Anointed Saviour of sinners is come in the flesh is a test that Spiritualism will not bear." — 1 Jn 4:2 = confessing a Saviour from sin

[PIONEER J. N. Loughborough, SGL 32.2] "in the true prophet it is Christ first, last, and all the time." — the decisive test

[Q] Isn't fulfilled prediction enough to prove a prophet?
→ Not alone — Deut 13:1-3 lets a sign come to pass and still tests the message; Isa 8:20 + 1 Jn 4:2: the word must agree with the law and exalt Christ.

[→] Micaiah stood with "what the LORD saith" against the majority — the remnant's position in every age (Rev 14:12)

## 4. Tuesday — Functions of Prophets — 2 Sam 12:13

> Prophets anointed and rebuked kings and priests for one purpose: to bring the nation back under God's rule and God's righteousness — conviction by the law, pardon by faith.

[WB]: PROPHET → law as mirror → "I have sinned" → "The LORD hath put away thy sin"

**Kings — Israel wanted a king to fight for them — 1 Sam 8:7, 20:**

- _1 Sam 8:7._ "for they have not rejected thee, but they have rejected me, that I should not reign over them" — the root sin: refusing God's rule
- _1 Sam 8:20._ "That we also may be like all the nations; and that our king may judge us, and go out before us, and fight our battles" — righteousness by human arm

| 1 Sam 8:20               | 2 Chr 20:15                  |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| "fight our battles"      | "the battle is not yours"    |
| king like the nations    | "believe his prophets"       |
| self-reliance            | faith                        |

- _2 Chr 24:20._ "Why transgress ye the commandments of the LORD, that ye cannot prosper?" — the prophet's reverse of 2 Chr 20:20: unbelief shows as transgression

[SOP PK 170.2] "The law of God, the divine standard of righteousness, is declared to be of no effect." — Elijah's age = ours

**Nathan and David — the law convicts, faith receives pardon — 2 Sam 12:7, 13:**

- _2 Sam 12:7._ "And Nathan said to David, Thou [art] the man." — the prophet holds up the mirror
- _2 Sam 12:13._ "And David said unto Nathan, I have sinned against the LORD. And Nathan said unto David, The LORD also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die." — justification in one sentence
- _Ps 32:2._ "Blessed [is] the man unto whom the LORD imputeth not iniquity" — David's own definition of righteousness by faith (Rom 4:6-8)
- _Ps 51:10._ "Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me." — pardon brings a new heart

[SOP 2BC 1023.3] "The conviction of his guilt was the saving of his soul. He saw himself in another light, as the Lord saw him" — the law's work

[SOP 3BC 1146.3] "pardon comes through faith that the sin, confessed and repented of, is borne by the great Sin-bearer." — the gospel's work

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, PTUK August 31, 1893, page 337.8] "As soon as the sin is really confessed it is forgiven, and the soul is cleansed from it." — Nathan's answer came "instantly"

**Priests — the prophet anoints, and points past them — Lev 8:12:**

- _Lev 8:12._ "And he poured of the anointing oil upon Aaron's head, and anointed him, to sanctify him." — Moses the prophet consecrates the priesthood
- chain: Mal 2:7 (priest's lips keep knowledge) → 1 Sam 2:35 (a faithful priest raised up) → Zech 6:13 (a priest upon His throne) — the prophets point to Christ, Priest-King in the sanctuary above

[→] every function — anointing, rebuke, teaching — served one end: a people under God's rule, pardoned and made new by faith

## 5. Wednesday — Women Prophets — 2 Kgs 22:19

> God spoke through Miriam, Deborah, and Huldah — sinners like the men — and each pointed Israel to the same place: God's salvation, God's word, a humbled heart.

| Prophetess | Ref       | Her message              |
| ---------- | --------- | ------------------------ |
| Miriam     | Exod 15   | "he hath triumphed"      |
| Deborah    | Judg 4    | "is not the LORD gone out" |
| Huldah     | 2 Kgs 22  | "thine heart was tender" |

- _Exod 15:21._ "And Miriam answered them, Sing ye to the LORD, for he hath triumphed gloriously; the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea." — "Miriam the prophetess" (v.20): her song credits God alone
- _Exod 15:2._ "The LORD [is] my strength and song, and he is become my salvation" — salvation is a Person, not an effort
- _Mic 6:4._ "and I sent before thee Moses, Aaron, and Miriam" — God names her a sent one
- _Num 12:2._ "Hath the LORD indeed spoken only by Moses? hath he not spoken also by us?" — and she sinned; the gift is no proof of personal righteousness

[SOP AA 561.1] "have claimed no righteousness of their own, but have trusted wholly in the righteousness of Christ." — the prophets' own standing

- _Judg 4:4._ "And Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lapidoth, she judged Israel at that time." — prophet and judge
- _Judg 4:14._ "this [is] the day in which the LORD hath delivered Sisera into thine hand: is not the LORD gone out before thee?" — past tense: God's promise counted as done

[SOP 2BC 1002.5] "He accepted the message from Deborah as the word of God, but he had little confidence in Israel" — Barak's half-faith

**Huldah — the law read, the heart broken, the sinner heard — 2 Kgs 22:11-19:**

- _2 Kgs 22:11._ "And it came to pass, when the king had heard the words of the book of the law, that he rent his clothes." — the law does its work: conviction
- _2 Kgs 22:14._ "went unto Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum" — the priest and the king's first men go to her for God's word; "(now she dwelt in Jerusalem in the college;)"
- _2 Kgs 22:19._ "Because thine heart was tender, and thou hast humbled thyself before the LORD" — and "I also have heard [thee], saith the LORD": mercy to the contrite

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, PBE 82.1] "the college, or school, in Jerusalem, in which dwelt Huldah the prophetess, was a school of the prophets" — Huldah taught

[SOP PK 399.2] "But because the king had humbled his heart before God, the Lord would acknowledge his promptness in seeking forgiveness and mercy." — pardon, not merit

- chain: Neh 6:14 (Noadiah — false prophetess too) → Joel 2:28 (daughters shall prophesy) → Luke 2:38 (Anna spake of Him to all who looked for redemption)

[PIONEER James White, LIFIN 324.5] "no prophecy in the Old Testament pointing to the opening of the Christian age as the time for spiritual gifts to be removed from the people of God" — Joel 2:28 still runs

[→] Anna's message (Luke 2:38) is every prophetess's: she spoke "of him" to those looking for redemption

## 6. Thursday — False Prophets — Jer 14:13-14

> The false prophet's signature is peace without righteousness — "assured peace" with no repentance, no turning from sin, no Lord our Righteousness. The true prophet brings both the wound and the cure.

- _Jer 14:13._ "behold, the prophets say unto them, Ye shall not see the sword, neither shall ye have famine; but I will give you assured peace in this place." — the counterfeit gospel
- _Jer 14:14._ "Then the LORD said unto me, The prophets prophesy lies in my name: I sent them not, neither have I commanded them, neither spake unto them: they prophesy unto you a false vision and divination, and a thing of nought, and the deceit of their heart." — source: their own heart
- _Jer 23:17._ "They say still unto them that despise me, The LORD hath said, Ye shall have peace" — peace pronounced over unrepentant sin
- _Jer 6:14._ "They have healed also the hurt [of the daughter] of my people slightly, saying, Peace, peace; when [there is] no peace." — a cheap cure

[SOP 3SG 21.3] "False prophets speak smooth things, prophesy lies, and cry, Peace and safety, which has ever been pleasing to unconsecrated professors" — EGW cites Jer 14:14 here

[PIONEER J. H. Waggoner, RDAC 25.1] "Crying, Peace, when danger is impending, has been the characteristic of false prophets in all ages, and is to be a prominent part of the delusion of the last days." — 1 Thess 5:3

| False prophet              | True prophet                  |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| "assured peace" (Jer 14:13) | "we have sinned" (Jer 14:20) |
| own heart (Jer 23:16)      | God's counsel (Jer 23:22)     |
| leaves people in sin       | "turned them from their evil way" |
| peace without Christ       | THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS    |

**The true prophet's peace — Jer 23:5-6, 22:**

- _Jer 23:22._ "But if they had stood in my counsel, and had caused my people to hear my words, then they should have turned them from their evil way" — real gospel saves FROM sin (Matt 1:21)
- _Jer 23:6._ "In his days Judah shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell safely: and this [is] his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS." — Jeremiah's answer to the false "peace," in the same chapter

[SOP DA 578.7] "Jeremiah will tell you, The Branch of David, “the Lord our Righteousness.”" — Jeremiah's witness to Christ

[SOP TMK 211.2] "How shall we distinguish God’s true servants from the false prophets who Christ said should arise to deceive many? There is only one test of character—the law of Jehovah." — law as test

[PIONEER E. J. Waggoner, PTUK July 6, 1893, page 211.10] "Peace and righteousness, therefore are synonymous." — no righteousness, no peace: the false prophet's lie exposed

- _Rev 14:12._ "here [are] they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" — commandments AND faith: the remnant's mark

[Q] Isn't any message of peace and assurance "false prophet" talk?
→ No — the false peace skips repentance (Jer 6:14); the true peace comes through a righteousness received (Jer 23:6; Rom 5:1) that turns from sin (Jer 23:22).

[→] Jer 14:20 "We acknowledge, O LORD, our wickedness" — the confession the false prophets never asked for, and the door to the true peace

## 7. Friday — Further Thought — 2 Cor 12:9

> God's messengers, Ellen White included, were never chosen for their strength; the gift rests on the weak who believe "My grace is sufficient" — and the message God sent through it was Christ our righteousness.

- _2 Cor 12:9._ "My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness." — the same word the angel gave a 17-year-old
- chain: Jer 1:6 ("I cannot speak") → 1 Cor 1:27 (God chose the weak) → 2 Cor 4:7 (treasure in earthen vessels)

[SOP 2SG 36.1] "When I had the assurance that all was right between me and God, then my confidence was strong." — her courage was justification

[SOP 2SG 38.1] "I then committed myself fully to God, to go at his bidding." — surrender, not ability

[TANGENT] Gen 15:6 (Abraham believed) → 2 Chr 20:20 (believe His prophets) → Hab 2:4 (just by faith) → Rom 1:17 (from faith to faith) → Rev 14:12 (faith of Jesus) — the prophets' one message, start to finish

## 8. Appeal

- The prophets were not better than their fathers (1 Kgs 19:4) — neither are we; their righteousness and ours is "in the LORD" (Isa 45:24)
- Every true prophet says what Micaiah said — "what the LORD saith" — and what the LORD says is THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS (Jer 23:6)
- Refuse the false peace (Jer 6:14); confess with Josiah and David (2 Kgs 22:19; 2 Sam 12:13) — and hear "The LORD also hath put away thy sin"
- Believe the prophets this week: take God's promise "as good as the actual accomplishment" (CHR 82.2) and begin to sing before the battle

[SOP 7BC 984.6] "the loud cry of the third angel has already begun in the revelation of the righteousness of Christ, the sin-pardoning Redeemer." — believing His prophets now = receiving this

[SOP 1SM 363.3] "through faith in Christ he may claim the righteousness of the Son of God as all-sufficient." — claim it today

- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the decision
