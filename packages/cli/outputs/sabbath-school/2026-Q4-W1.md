# Sabbath School — Q4 W1 (2026)

## The Word That Made You Will Clothe You

_Quarterly: The Creator Speaks · September 26 – October 2 · Read: Gen. 1:27, 28; Gen. 3:1–8; Luke 15:11–32; Gen. 3:14–24; Exod. 19:16–22; John 10:27–30_

**Memory Verse:** — _Heb 1:1-2._ "God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by [his] Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds;"

**Theme: Sin began when man doubted what God said, so righteousness comes back when man believes what God says; the one who believes is clothed with the robe God made.**

## The road

1. Made by the word, to live by the word — Gen 1:27-28 _(quarterly: Sun)_
2. The word doubted, the fig leaves sewn — Gen 3:1-8 _(quarterly: Mon)_

6. Decision

## Open

- **Say:** God has never stopped speaking. Today's one question: what happens to the man who believes what He says?
- _Heb 1:1-2._ "God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by [his] Son" — one Speaker in every age
- [RQ] Who is the Son through whom God now speaks?
  → The Creator: "by whom also he made the worlds" (Heb 1:2). The voice that made us is the voice that saves us

---

## 1. Made by the Word, to Live by the Word — Gen 1:27-28

- **Say:** Man was made by God's word, and his life hung on trusting God's word.
- _Gen 1:27._ "So God created man in his [own] image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them." — the image is a character to reflect
- _Gen 1:28._ "And God blessed them, and God said unto them" — the first thing man heard was a blessing
- _Gen 2:16-17._ "Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat: But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it" — life hung on trusting one word
- _Gen 3:8._ "the voice of the LORD God walking in the garden in the cool of the day" — in Eden, God's voice was close by
- [RQ] What was the first thing God did after He made man?
  → He spoke to him and blessed him: "God blessed them, and God said unto them" (Gen 1:28)
- [RQ] What did Adam have to do to keep the life God gave?
  → Believe one word: "thou shalt not eat of it" (Gen 2:17)

[SOP 6T 350.1] "The power that created all things is the power that re-creates the soul in His own likeness." — creation and redemption, one power

- **Bridge:** If life hung on believing the word, what happened when the word was doubted?

## 2. The Word Doubted, the Fig Leaves Sewn — Gen 3:1-8

- **Say:** Sin began as unbelief of God's word. Then man made his own covering. Fig leaves were the first self-righteousness.

| God said            | Serpent said           |
| ------------------- | ---------------------- |
| "surely die" (2:17) | "not surely die" (3:4) |
| "freely eat" (2:16) | "hath God said" (3:1)  |

- _Gen 3:1._ "Yea, hath God said" — the first attack was on the word
- _Gen 3:4._ "And the serpent said unto the woman, Ye shall not surely die:" — a flat contradiction of 2:17
- _Gen 3:7._ "and they sewed fig leaves together, and made themselves aprons." — man's first religion: cover yourself
- _Gen 3:8._ "and Adam and his wife hid themselves from the presence of the LORD God amongst the trees of the garden." — the voice they once loved now drove them into the trees
- [RQ] Where did the fall begin, at the tree or before it?
  → Before it, with doubt cast on the word: "Yea, hath God said" (Gen 3:1)
- [RQ] What did they do about their nakedness?
  → They covered themselves: "sewed fig leaves together, and made themselves aprons" (Gen 3:7)

[SOP PP 55.2] "She disbelieved the words of God, and this was what led to her fall." — the root of sin is unbelief, so the root of righteousness is faith

- **Bridge:** Man hid. Who moved next?

## 3. "Where Art Thou?": God Comes Looking — Gen 3:9; Luke 15:20

- **Say:** Man ran, and God came looking. Salvation starts with God, who seeks, calls, and clothes the one who comes back.
- _Gen 3:9._ "And the LORD God called unto Adam, and said unto him, Where [art] thou?" — an invitation to confess
- _Gen 3:10._ "I heard thy voice in the garden, and I was afraid, because I [was] naked; and I hid myself." — guilt hears love as a threat
- _Gen 3:12._ "The woman whom thou gavest [to be] with me, she gave me of the tree, and I did eat." — Adam justified himself by blaming
- _Luke 15:20._ "But when he was yet a great way off, his father saw him, and had compassion, and ran, and fell on his neck, and kissed him." — the Father runs first
- _Luke 15:22._ "Bring forth the best robe, and put [it] on him" — clothed before he is cleaned up

| Adam (Gen 3:12) | Prodigal (Luke 15:21) |
| --------------- | --------------------- |
| hides           | arises, goes          |
| "thou gavest"   | "I have sinned"       |

- [RQ] Who took the first step after the fall?
  → God: "the LORD God called unto Adam" (Gen 3:9)
- [RQ] What did the father put on the son who came home?
  → "the best robe" (Luke 15:22), not the son's own clothes

[SOP COL 203.2] "No opportunity is given him to ask a servant’s place. He is a son" — received as a son, not hired

- **Bridge:** The prodigal got a robe. What robe did Adam get?

## 4. The Covering God Made — Gen 3:15, 21

- **Say:** Before Adam left Eden, God promised the Seed and put enmity against sin in him, and He clothed him with a covering He made Himself.
- _Gen 3:15._ "And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel." — "I will put": the hatred of sin is God's gift
- _Gen 3:21._ "Unto Adam also and to his wife did the LORD God make coats of skins, and clothed them." — a victim died; God made the covering
- _Isa 61:10._ "he hath clothed me with the garments of salvation, he hath covered me with the robe of righteousness" — the coat of skins fulfilled

| Fig leaves (3:7) | Coats of skins (3:21) |
| ---------------- | --------------------- |
| man-made         | God-made              |
| nothing died     | a victim died         |
| works            | received by faith     |

- [RQ] Who made the coats of skins, and who put them on?
  → "the LORD God make coats of skins, and clothed them" (Gen 3:21). God made it and God put it on
- [RQ] What does that covering stand for?
  → "the robe of righteousness" (Isa 61:10)

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 61.1] "God puts His righteousness upon the believer. He covers him with it, so that his sin no more appears." — the robe is Christ's righteousness

- **Bridge:** That promise came through the Seed. When the Seed came, what did He say?

## 5. The Son, God's Last Word: Believe Him — Heb 1:3; John 14:9

- **Say:** The Son who made the worlds came and purged our sins. He shows the Father as He really is, and the one who believes Him is counted righteous.
- _Heb 1:3._ "when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high" — the Creator purged our sins Himself
- _John 14:9._ "he that hath seen me hath seen the Father" — the Son is God's clearest word
- _John 10:27-28._ "My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me: And I give unto them eternal life" — they hear, and life is given
- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — the whole message in one line
- [RQ] What does God count to the man who believes Him?
  → Righteousness: "he counted it to him for righteousness" (Gen 15:6)
- [RQ] What did the sheep do to get eternal life?
  → They heard His voice; "I give unto them eternal life" (John 10:27-28)

[WB]: hear → believe → counted righteous → clothed

[SOP LS 39.1] "My views of the Father were changed." — Ellen Harmon was pardoned by faith before she was a prophet (Fri)

- **Bridge:** The Creator is still speaking. What will you do with His word?

## Decision

- **Say:** Stop sewing fig leaves. Believe what God says, and take the robe He made.
- _Heb 12:25._ "See that ye refuse not him that speaketh." — He is speaking today
- _Rom 10:17._ "So then faith [cometh] by hearing, and hearing by the word of God." — faith comes from the word He speaks
- [RQ] Where does the faith to believe Him come from?
  → From hearing His word (Rom 10:17), not from our effort

[SOP 1SM 363.1] "If you would stand through the time of trouble, you must know Christ, and appropriate the gift of His righteousness, which He imputes to the repentant sinner."

## If asked

- [Q] I'm not hiding, I'm doing my best. Isn't that enough?
  → That is the fig-leaf apron. Isa 64:6; only a covering God makes will cover (Gen 3:21; Rev 3:18)
- [Q] Why did Sinai thunder if God wants nearness?
  → Exod 19:21: the people had to keep back. Unbelief made the distance; God wanted the law written in hearts (Jer 31:33)
- [Q] Why a prophet if we have the Bible?
  → The gift never replaces the Word; it leads back to it (Isa 8:20; 2 Tim 3:16). Its theme is Christ (Rev 19:10)
