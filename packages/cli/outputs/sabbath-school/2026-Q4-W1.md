# Sabbath School — Q4 W1 (2026)

## The Creator Speaks

_Week of September 26 – October 2 · Read: Gen. 1:27, 28; Gen. 3:1–8; Luke 15:11–32; Gen. 3:14–24; Exod. 19:16–22; John 10:27–30_

**Memory Verse:** — _Heb 1:1-2._ "God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by [his] Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds;"

**The Creator whose word made the worlds has never stopped speaking: in Eden, by the prophets, and at last by His Son, every word He speaks asks man to believe Him, and the one who believes is clothed with His righteousness.**

## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — One Speaker, One Demand — Heb 1:1-2

> The same God spoke through every prophet and then through His Son. His word always asks one thing: believe Me. God counts that faith for righteousness.

[WB]: GOD SPEAKS → prophets → Son → "believe" → counted righteous

- _Heb 1:1-2._ "God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by [his] Son" — the Speaker never changes. The Son who speaks is the Creator, "by whom also he made the worlds"
- _Heb 1:3._ "when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high" — the Word who made the worlds also purged our sins. Creation and justification come from one voice
- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — the whole message of the prophets in one line
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the prophetic gift exists to produce faith
- chain: Ps 33:9 (He spake, it was done) → Rom 4:17 (calleth things which be not) → Rom 4:5 (justifieth the ungodly) → Rom 3:21 (witnessed by the prophets)

[PIONEER Waggoner, GCDB February 15, 1897, page 23.3] "It is no new thing for God to speak, for he spake in times past to the fathers and prophets, and he now speaks unto us by Christ." — one Speaker in both dispensations

[PIONEER Waggoner, SFKK 7.2] "the full meaning conveyed by the Hebrew is that Abraham built upon God." — Gen 15:6 and 2 Chr 20:20 are the same act: building on His word

[SOP 1SM 367.1] "Christ’s righteousness is accepted in place of man’s failure, and God receives, pardons, justifies, the repentant, believing soul, treats him as though he were righteous" — what "counted" means

[→] The week's line: Eden's voice (Sun), the lie believed (Mon), God's call (Tue), the promise (Wed), the Son (Thu), the gift today (Fri)

## 2. Sunday — Eden: Made by the Word, to Live by the Word — Gen 1:27-28

> God made man in His image and spoke with him face to face. The word that made man is the same word that remakes him, and that is righteousness by faith.

- _Gen 1:27._ "So God created man in his [own] image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them." — the image is a character to reflect, not a statue
- _Gen 1:28._ "And God blessed them, and God said unto them" — the first thing man ever heard was a blessing spoken over him
- _Gen 2:16-17._ "Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat: But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it" — life hung on trusting one word
- _Gen 2:3._ "And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it" — a set time for communion, and the sign of the One who sanctifies
- _Gen 3:8._ "the voice of the LORD God walking in the garden in the cool of the day" — this was normal in Eden: God's voice, close by

[SOP CT 186.1] "To Adam and Eve in their Eden home, nature was full of the knowledge of God, teeming with divine instruction." — creation was a classroom

[PIONEER Jones, GCDB March 2, 1893, page 452.7] "the Sabbath of the Lord is just as certainly the sign of the creative power of God manifested in Jesus Christ through his word, in the salvation of my soul" — Gen 2:3: the word that made man is the word that justifies him

[SOP 6T 350.1] "The power that created all things is the power that re-creates the soul in His own likeness." — the Sabbath shows Him as Creator and Sanctifier

[RQ] Where does nature still speak of God, and where do you need His Word to read it rightly?

[→] Creation by the word works the same way as justification by the word: Ps 33:9 + Rom 4:17

## 3. Monday — Unbelief, Then Fig Leaves — Gen 3:1-8

> Sin began as unbelief of God's word. Shame came next, and then man sewed his own covering. Fig leaves were the first self-righteousness.

| God said                  | Serpent said           |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| "surely die" (2:17)       | "not surely die" (3:4) |
| "freely eat" (2:16)       | "hath God said" (3:1)  |
| man in His image (1:27)   | "as gods" (3:5)        |

- _Gen 3:1._ "Yea, hath God said" — the first attack was doubt cast on the word
- _Gen 3:7._ "and they sewed fig leaves together, and made themselves aprons." — man's first religion: cover yourself
- _Gen 3:8._ "And they heard the voice of the LORD God walking in the garden in the cool of the day: and Adam and his wife hid themselves from the presence of the LORD God amongst the trees of the garden." — the voice they once loved now drove them into the trees
- chain: Jer 23:24 (can any hide?) → Heb 4:13 (naked and opened) → Isa 64:6 (righteousnesses as filthy rags) → Rev 3:18 (white raiment, shame not appear)

[SOP PP 55.2] "She disbelieved the words of God, and this was what led to her fall." — the root of sin is unbelief, so the root of righteousness is faith

[PIONEER Jones, GCDB February 13, 1893, page 258.2] "If they had received those words, had retained those words, and the thoughts of God in those words, whose mind would they have retained? God’s." — the fall was trading God's word for Satan's

[SOP COL 311.1] "They have worn the garments of their own devising, by works of their own they have tried to cover their sins, and make themselves acceptable with God." — fig leaves = works-righteousness

[Q] I'm not hiding, I'm doing my best. Isn't that enough?
→ That is the fig-leaf apron. Isa 64:6; only a covering God makes will cover (Gen 3:21; Rev 3:18)

[→] The cure for unbelief is not more effort. It is hearing God's word again: Rom 10:17

## 4. Tuesday — "Where Art Thou?" — Gen 3:9; Luke 15:20

> Man ran, and God came looking. Salvation starts with God: He seeks, He calls, and He clothes the one who comes back confessing.

**God takes the first step — Gen 3:9-12:**

- _Gen 3:9._ "And the LORD God called unto Adam, and said unto him, Where [art] thou?" — God was not asking for information. It was an invitation to confess
- _Gen 3:10._ "I heard thy voice in the garden, and I was afraid, because I [was] naked; and I hid myself." — guilt hears love as a threat
- _Gen 3:12._ "The woman whom thou gavest [to be] with me, she gave me of the tree, and I did eat." — Adam justified himself by blaming others, even God

[SOP PP 57.4] "In their innocence and holiness they had joyfully welcomed the approach of their Creator; but now they fled in terror" — God did not change. They did

**The Father runs — Luke 15:**

| Adam (Gen 3:12)      | Prodigal (Luke 15:21) |
| -------------------- | --------------------- |
| hides                | arises, goes          |
| "thou gavest"        | "I have sinned"       |
| self-justified       | "no more worthy"      |

- _Luke 15:20._ "But when he was yet a great way off, his father saw him, and had compassion, and ran, and fell on his neck, and kissed him." — the Father runs before the son has said a word
- _Luke 15:22._ "Bring forth the best robe, and put [it] on him" — the father clothes him before he is cleaned up
- _Rom 7:24-25._ "O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death? I thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord." — the cry that ends hiding, and the answer
- chain: Luke 19:10 (seek and save) → Rom 5:8 (while yet sinners) → Luke 22:61 (the Lord turned, looked) → 1 John 1:9 (confess, cleanse)

[SOP COL 203.2] "No opportunity is given him to ask a servant’s place. He is a son" — he is received as a son, not hired as a servant

[PIONEER Waggoner, WOR 122.2] "It holds us to a sense of the dreadfulness of our bondage until in anguish we cry out" — "it" is the law, which drives us to the Deliverer

[→] "Where art thou?" is still the gospel's first question. Answer it with confession, not blame

## 5. Wednesday — Expelled, Yet Promised — Gen 3:15, 21

> Sin shut the gate and ended face-to-face speech. But before Adam left Eden, Christ preached him the gospel, put enmity against sin in him, and clothed him with a covering God made.

**The gospel at the gate — Gen 3:15-24:**

- _Gen 3:15._ "And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel." — "I will put": God gives the hatred of sin. It is grace, not willpower
- _Gen 3:21._ "Unto Adam also and to his wife did the LORD God make coats of skins, and clothed them." — something had to die so they could be clothed; God made the covering
- _Gen 3:24._ "Cherubims, and a flaming sword which turned every way, to keep the way of the tree of life." — the way is guarded, not destroyed
- _Isa 59:2._ "But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid [his] face from you, that he will not hear." — the separation is sin's work, not God's wish

[SOP 1BC 1084.7] "The gospel was first preached to Adam by Christ." — the first prophecy was gospel

[SOP GC 506.2] "It is the grace that Christ implants in the soul which creates in man enmity against Satan." — Gen 3:15 is righteousness by faith in seed form

| Fig leaves (3:7) | Coats of skins (3:21) |
| ---------------- | --------------------- |
| man-made         | God-made              |
| nothing died     | a victim died         |
| works            | received by faith     |

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 61.1] "God puts His righteousness upon the believer. He covers him with it, so that his sin no more appears." — Isa 61:10 is the coat of skins fulfilled

[SOP PP 67.2] "the atonement of Christ alone could span the abyss and make possible the communication of blessing or salvation from heaven to earth." — Christ is the only line still open

- chain: John 1:51 (ladder = Son of man) → Exod 25:22 (commune from between the cherubims) → Rev 22:14 (right to the tree of life)

[Q] Why did Sinai thunder if God wants nearness?
→ Exod 19:21: the people had to keep back. Unbelief made the distance; God wanted the law written in hearts (Jer 31:33)

[PIONEER Waggoner, GTI 137.2] "God would have put His law into their hearts, even as He put it into Abraham’s heart, if they had believed" — the terror of Sinai was the result of unbelief

[→] The cherubim that once barred the way later stood over the mercy seat where God speaks (Exod 25:22). The way back is through the blood

## 6. Thursday — God's Spokespeople — Num 12:6; John 14:9

> After the Fall God speaks through creation, prophets, Scripture, and most fully His Son. All of them point the same way: to Christ, believed.

| Channel   | Text        | Shows                  |
| --------- | ----------- | ---------------------- |
| Creation  | Rom 1:20    | power and Godhead      |
| Prophets  | Num 12:6    | vision and dream       |
| Scripture | 2 Tim 3:16  | instruction in righteousness |
| Son       | John 14:9   | the Father seen        |

- _Num 12:6._ "Hear now my words: If there be a prophet among you, [I] the LORD will make myself known unto him in a vision, [and] will speak unto him in a dream." — the prophet's channel, set by God's own word
- _2 Tim 3:15._ "able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus." — Scripture's goal is faith in Christ, then "instruction in righteousness" (v. 16)
- _John 14:9._ "he that hath seen me hath seen the Father" — the Son is God's clearest word
- _John 10:27-28._ "My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me: And I give unto them eternal life" — they hear, they follow, and life is given, not earned
- _Acts 10:43._ "To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins." — every prophet preached the same sermon
- chain: 1 Pet 1:11 (Spirit of Christ in them) → Luke 24:27 (all the prophets: Himself) → Rom 3:21 (righteousness witnessed by the prophets) → John 5:39 (they testify of me)

[SOP PP 366.3] "It is the voice of Christ that speaks to us through the Old Testament." — the Speaker behind every prophet

[PIONEER Jones, GCDB March 7, 1893, page 496.10] "the prophets of God will stand in that place, and witness to that man that he has it." — Rom 3:21: wherever righteousness by faith is, the prophets testify to it

[SOP 5T 738.4] "Christ came to reveal God to the world as a God of love" — the Son corrects every dark picture of the Father

[Q] Isn't nature enough to know God?
→ Nature shows His power (Rom 1:20), but it is "now marred and defiled by sin" (CT 186.1). Pardon comes only through the word preached (Rom 10:17; Acts 10:43)

[RQ] What does it change for you that the Father looks exactly like Jesus (John 14:9)?

## 7. Friday — Further Thought: The Gift Today — Rev 19:10

> The same Speaker raised up a prophet in the remnant. Her first message was the one she first received herself: a pardoned sinner's faith in a tender Father.

- _Rev 12:17._ "And the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — the remnant has two marks
- _Rev 19:10._ "for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — the inspired definition
- _Rev 14:12._ "here [are] they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." — the gift serves this people

**Ellen Harmon: justified first — LS 39:**

[SOP LS 39.1] "Faith now took possession of my heart." — she was pardoned by faith before she was a prophet

[SOP LS 39.1] "My views of the Father were changed." — the gift began with seeing the Father as He is (John 14:9)

**The gift points to the Word and to Christ our righteousness:**

[SOP EW 78.1] "not for a new rule of faith, but for the comfort of His people, and to correct those who err from Bible truth." — the purpose of the gift

[PIONEER James White, AWLF 13.4] "True visions are given to lead us to God, and his written word" — 1847: the pioneers' test

[SOP 3AM 113.4] "It presented justification through faith in the Surety; it invited the people to receive the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience to all the commandments of God." — the gift endorsed 1888

[SOP 1SM 372.2] "It is the third angel’s message, in verity." — justification by faith is present truth

[PIONEER Jones, GCDB February 7, 1893, page 183.6] "when they [at Minneapolis] were rejecting the loud cry, “the teaching of righteousness,” and then the Spirit of the Lord, by his prophet, stood there and told us what they were doing" — 1893: the gift stood with the 1888 message

[Q] Why a prophet if we have the Bible?
→ The gift never replaces the Word; it leads back to it (Isa 8:20; 2 Tim 3:16). Its theme is Christ (Rev 19:10)

## 8. Appeal — Heb 12:25

- The Creator who walked in Eden is still calling, "Where art thou?" (Gen 3:9). Come out from the trees
- Stop sewing fig leaves. Receive the covering God made: _Isa 61:10._ "he hath clothed me with the garments of salvation, he hath covered me with the robe of righteousness"
- Believe Him, and believe His prophets: _2 Chr 20:20._ "believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper."
- The face-to-face communion lost in Eden is promised back: "they shall see his face" (Rev 22:4)

[PIONEER Jones, CWCP 124.1] "this developing in each believer the complete image of Jesus—this is the finishing of the mystery of God, which is Christ in you the hope of glory." — the image of Eden restored is the finish of the sanctuary work

[SOP 1SM 363.1] "If you would stand through the time of trouble, you must know Christ, and appropriate the gift of His righteousness, which He imputes to the repentant sinner." — this is what the gift is for

- _Heb 12:25._ "See that ye refuse not him that speaketh." — He is speaking today
