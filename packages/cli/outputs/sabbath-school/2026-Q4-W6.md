## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — Rom 15:4

> Scripture was not written to satisfy curiosity. It was written for our learning and our hope, and the hope is Christ, the righteousness that Abraham received by believing.

[WB]: WRITTEN → LEARNING → PATIENCE → HOPE (Christ)

- _Rom 15:4._ "For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope." — "whatsoever" covers Moses, the songs and the prophets, all written for us
- _Deut 31:19._ "Now therefore write ye this song for you, and teach it the children of Israel: put it in their mouths, that this song may be a witness for me" — God wrote a song (Deut 32) and Moses copied it down
- _Rom 4:23._ "Now it was not written for his sake alone, that it was imputed to him;" — Abraham's justification was written down for us
- _Rom 4:24._ "But for us also, to whom it shall be imputed, if we believe on him that raised up Jesus our Lord from the dead;" — the same righteousness is given to us on the same terms
- chain: Gen 15:6 (believed → counted righteous) → Rom 3:21 (righteousness "witnessed by the law and the prophets") → 1 Cor 10:11 (written for us, at the end of the world) → 2 Chr 20:20 (believe His prophets)

[PIONEER Waggoner, GOSC 7.2] "We may find comfort and hope in the Scriptures, because Christ is in them." — the source of the hope in Rom 15:4

[SOP PP 366.3] "It is the voice of Christ that speaks to us through the Old Testament." — the prophets' pen, Christ's voice

[PIONEER Waggoner, WOR 80.2] "This shows most plainly that the promise of God to Abraham is as wide as the gospel of Christ." — Rom 4:23 is meant for us too

[→] The prophetic writings exist to make believers. Each day below shows a different way they do it.

## 2. Sunday — The Call to Write — Ex 17:14

> The first thing God told a prophet to write down was a victory won by trusting God, not by Israel's own strength. Later God had Jeremiah write so that He could forgive.

**The first written memorial: victory by trust — Ex 17:**

- _Ex 17:14._ "And the LORD said unto Moses, Write this [for] a memorial in a book, and rehearse [it] in the ears of Joshua: for I will utterly put out the remembrance of Amalek from under heaven." — the first command to write records the LORD's battle, not Joshua's
- _Ex 17:11._ "when Moses held up his hand, that Israel prevailed: and when he let down his hand, Amalek prevailed." — when they looked up they won; when they relied on themselves they lost
- _Ex 17:15._ "And Moses built an altar, and called the name of it Jehovahnissi:" — the LORD is the banner, and the altar gets the credit

[SOP PP 299.1] "while they made Him their trust, He would fight for them and subdue their enemies" — righteousness by faith before Sinai

**The roll written so that God could forgive — Jer 36; Hab 2:**

- _Jer 36:3._ "that they may return every man from his evil way; that I may forgive their iniquity and their sin." — God wanted the roll written so He could pardon
- _Hab 2:2._ "Write the vision, and make [it] plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it." — written so that anyone can read it and act on it
- _Hab 2:4._ "Behold, his soul [which] is lifted up is not upright in him: but the just shall live by his faith." — the written vision ends in faith

[SOP PK 432.2] "the Lord commanded Jeremiah to commit to writing the messages He desired to bear to those for whose salvation His heart of pity was continually yearning" — He wrote out of pity

[SOP GC 392.2] "The publication of this chart was regarded as a fulfillment of the command given by Habakkuk." — Fitch's 1842 chart; the Advent people obeyed Hab 2:2

[Q] We don't have the original manuscripts. Can we trust the Bible?
→ The promise is that the words are kept, not the parchment: Ps 12:6-7 "thou shalt preserve them"; Isa 40:8; 1 Pet 1:25. When Jehoiakim burned the roll, God had it rewritten (Jer 36:28).

## 3. Monday — Literary Genres and Styles — Isa 13:1

> Oracle, song, covenant lawsuit, letter: the forms differ, but every one of them carries Christ's voice calling people away from their own works to the Fountain.

- _Isa 13:1._ "The burden of Babylon, which Isaiah the son of Amoz did see." — an oracle the prophet saw, then carried as a burden
- chain: Isa 13:19 (literal Babylon overthrown) → Rev 14:8 (Babylon is fallen) → Rev 18:4 ("Come out of her, my people") — the same burden reaches the end of time
- _Isa 17:7._ "At that day shall a man look to his Maker, and his eyes shall have respect to the Holy One of Israel." — parallel lines: Maker = Holy One; the eyes turn away from self and toward Him
- _Jer 2:13._ "For my people have committed two evils; they have forsaken me the fountain of living waters, [and] hewed them out cisterns, broken cisterns, that can hold no water." — the covenant lawsuit's charge: they built their own cisterns, which is self-righteousness
- chain: Jer 2:5 ("What iniquity have your fathers found in me") → Jer 2:9 ("I will yet plead with you") — the Judge argues His case in order to win them back

[SOP GC 478.2] "It is by beholding that we become changed." — the cure for the broken cisterns

| Form | Text | The point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oracle | Isa 13:1 | Come out |
| Song | Deut 31:19 | Remember Him |
| Lawsuit | Jer 2:13 | Return to the Fountain |
| Letter | 1 Thess 2:13 | Believe; the word works |

- _1 Cor 7:10._ "And unto the married I command, [yet] not I, but the Lord" — Paul says honestly when he is quoting the Lord; compare 7:12 "speak I, not the Lord"
- _1 Cor 7:40._ "and I think also that I have the Spirit of God." — his own counsel is also given under the Spirit
- _1 Thess 2:13._ "ye received [it] not [as] the word of men, but as it is in truth, the word of God, which effectually worketh also in you that believe." — whatever the genre, the word works in those who believe it

[PIONEER James White, COTSN 14.3] "The Spirit of Christ inspired the prophets of the former dispensations." — one Author behind every genre

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 104.3] "You are not to work to do the word of God: the word of God is to work in you to cause you to do." — 1 Thess 2:13 is righteousness by faith

[Q] What is wrong with stressing the human element over the divine?
→ Then the word is received "as the word of men," and it cannot work in you (1 Thess 2:13; Heb 4:2, "not being mixed with faith").

## 4. Tuesday — Literary Assistants — Jer 36:18

> Baruch and Tertius held the pen, but the word was the LORD's. A king burned it and God had it written again, and the epistle a scribe wrote down is the Bible's fullest statement of righteousness by faith.

- _Jer 36:17._ "Tell us now, How didst thou write all these words at his mouth?" — the princes asked where the words came from
- _Jer 36:18._ "Then Baruch answered them, He pronounced all these words unto me with his mouth, and I wrote [them] with ink in the book." — the prophet dictated and the scribe wrote
- _Jer 36:4._ "Baruch wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the LORD" — three steps: the LORD, then Jeremiah, then Baruch, and the word is still the LORD's

[SOP 1SM 21.1] "The writers of the Bible were God’s penmen, not His pen." — the man is inspired, not his pen
[SOP 1SM 21.2] "thus the utterances of the man are the word of God." — the human channel is still God's word

**The roll the fire could not destroy:**

- _Jer 36:23._ "he cut it with the penknife, and cast [it] into the fire that [was] on the hearth" — the king burned the offer of forgiveness (36:3)
- _Jer 36:32._ "and there were added besides unto them many like words." — rejecting the word only brought more of it
- [SOP PK 436.2] "the words were still living in the heart of Jeremiah, “as a burning fire,”" — Jer 20:9

[SOP PK 435.2] "But the Hand that humbles to the dust, rebukes sin, and puts pride and ambition to shame, is the Hand that lifts up the penitent, stricken one." — the reproof is meant to lead to grace

**Tertius's pen, Paul's gospel — Rom 16:22:**

- _Rom 16:22._ "I Tertius, who wrote [this] epistle, salute you in the Lord." — a scribe wrote Romans
- _Rom 1:17._ "For therein is the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith: as it is written, The just shall live by faith." — and Romans is the epistle of righteousness by faith (quoting Hab 2:4)
- _Rom 16:26._ "made known to all nations for the obedience of faith:" — the letter closes with faith that obeys
- chain: Col 4:18 ("by the hand of me Paul") → 2 Thess 3:17 ("the token in every epistle") — Paul's own signature authenticated the letter

[PIONEER Waggoner, WOR 19.3] "The Gospel reveals the righteous law of God, because the Gospel has the law in itself." — the faith Romans teaches keeps the commandments

| Spoke | Wrote | Whose word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jeremiah | Baruch | "words of the LORD" |
| Paul | Tertius | "gospel of Christ" |

[Q] Did the scribes change the message?
→ Jer 36:4 "all the words of the LORD"; Rom 16:25 "according to my gospel"; the signature confirmed it (2 Thess 3:17).

## 5. Wednesday — Literary Sources — Luke 1:4

> The Spirit led inspired writers to search, select and arrange material, and the result is certainty: the certainty of salvation by the remission of sins, and of Enoch's faith that pleased God.

- _Luke 1:3._ "having had perfect understanding of all things from the very first, to write unto thee in order" — careful research, guided by the Spirit
- _Luke 1:4._ "That thou mightest know the certainty of those things, wherein thou hast been instructed." — the aim was assurance, not speculation
- _Luke 1:77._ "To give knowledge of salvation unto his people by the remission of their sins," — the certainty Luke records is forgiveness
- chain: Eccl 12:10 ("written [was] upright, [even] words of truth") → 1 Kgs 11:41 (book of the acts of Solomon) → Acts 17:28 (Paul quotes "your own poets") — the Spirit uses a source without endorsing the rest of it

[SOP YRP 228.2] "I have endeavored to select and group together events in the history of the church in such a manner as to trace the unfolding of the great testing truths" — the same method in her own work

**Enoch's prophecy: the judgment, and the faith that meets it — Jude 14-15:**

- _Jude 14._ "And Enoch also, the seventh from Adam, prophesied of these, saying, Behold, the Lord cometh with ten thousands of his saints," — the Advent hope was preached before the Flood
- _Jude 15._ "To execute judgment upon all, and to convince all that are ungodly among them of all their ungodly deeds" — the judgment was announced in the seventh generation from Adam
- _Heb 11:5._ "By faith Enoch was translated that he should not see death" — Enoch's walk was by faith
- _Heb 11:6._ "But without faith [it is] impossible to please [him]" — "pleased God" therefore means he believed

[PIONEER Waggoner, LBFCG 5.2] "Without faith the best deeds that a man can do will come infinitely short of the perfect righteousness of God, which is the only standard." — the lesson of Enoch

[SOP CCh 90.2] "Abel was a believer in Christ, and was as verily saved by His power as was Peter or Paul." — one gospel in every age
[SOP TMK 348.3] "The godly character of this prophet is to represent the state of holiness to which the people of God must attain who expect to be translated to heaven" — a type of the last generation

[Q] Does using sources weaken inspiration?
→ No. Luke 1:3 says "perfect understanding"; Eccl 12:10 says "words of truth"; 2 Pet 1:21 says they were "moved by the Holy Ghost." Selecting sources was itself part of the inspired work.

## 6. Thursday — Divine Message and Human Imperfections — 2 Cor 4:7

> The treasure is the light of God's glory in the face of Jesus. The vessel is weak so that the power is plainly God's, and that is the whole of righteousness by faith: Christ in you.

[WB]: EARTHEN VESSEL → TREASURE (Christ) → POWER "of God, not of us"

- _2 Cor 4:7._ "But we have this treasure in earthen vessels, that the excellency of the power may be of God, and not of us." — God chose weak vessels on purpose
- _2 Cor 4:6._ "to [give] the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ." — this is what the treasure is
- _2 Cor 4:5._ "For we preach not ourselves, but Christ Jesus the Lord" — the vessel points to Christ, never to itself
- chain: 2 Cor 12:9 (strength made perfect in weakness) → Gal 2:20 ("not I, but Christ liveth in me") → Col 1:27 ("Christ in you, the hope of glory")

| Vessel | Treasure |
| --- | --- |
| earthen | Christ's glory |
| weakness | power of God |
| "not of us" | "of God" |

[SOP 5T 747.1] "The union of the divine and the human, manifest in Christ, exists also in the Bible." — the written Word follows the pattern of the incarnate Word

[PIONEER Jones, CWCP 124.1] "this developing in each believer the complete image of Jesus—this is the finishing of the mystery of God, which is Christ in you the hope of glory." — what the treasure is for
[PIONEER Jones, CWCP 124.1] "This consummation is accomplished in the cleansing of the sanctuary" — Rev 10:7 and Dan 8:14 meet here

## 7. Friday — Further Thought: Ellen G. White's Writings — Rev 19:10

> The remnant has the testimony of Jesus. That gift does not add to the Bible. It leads people back to it and lifts up the message God sent in 1888: Christ our righteousness, shown in obedience to all the commandments.

- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — two marks of the remnant
- _Rev 19:10._ "worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — the gift is Jesus' own testimony; worship goes to God, not the messenger
- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, [it is] because [there is] no light in them." — every gift is tested by the word
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — believing the prophets is believing God

[SOP GCB May 31, 1909, par. 8] "But one night the angel of the Lord came to my bedside and said to me, “You must write out the things that I give you.”" — her commission to write, like Moses and Jeremiah (Ex 17:14; Jer 36:2)

[SOP 3SM 30.4] "the Lord has given a lesser light to lead men and women to the greater light." — the gift points to the Bible

## 8. Appeal

- Every word the prophets wrote was "written for our learning" (Rom 15:4), and it was written to bring you to believe God.
- The word does what it says. Take it as the word of God and let it work in you (1 Thess 2:13; Isa 55:11).
- Today, receive Christ's righteousness as Abraham did, by believing (Rom 4:23-24), and walk with God as Enoch did, ready for the judgment (Jude 14; Heb 11:5).

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 15.1] "Faith is the expecting the word of God to do what it says and the depending upon that word to do what it says." — the definition of faith

[SOP PK 387.2] "We must cherish and cultivate the faith of which prophets and apostles have testified—the faith that lays hold on the promises of God" — the faith the whole week has pointed to

- _Rom 15:13._ "Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, through the power of the Holy Ghost."
