## The Word That Works

_Quarterly: The Prophetic Writings · October 31–November 6 · Read: Exod. 17:14; Isa. 13:1; Jer. 36:17, 18; Rom. 16:22; Luke 1:1–4; Jude 14, 15; 2 Cor. 4:7_

## Open

- **Say:** Why did God have His prophets write at all? This morning the Scriptures answer that themselves.
- _Rom 15:4._ "For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope." — "whatsoever" covers Moses, the songs and the prophets
- [RQ] What did God mean the Scriptures to give us?
  → Hope: "that we ... might have hope" (Rom 15:4)

[PIONEER Waggoner, GOSC 7.2] "We may find comfort and hope in the Scriptures, because Christ is in them." — the source of the hope

## 1. Written so God could save — Ex 17:14

- **Say:** The first thing God told a prophet to write was a victory won by trusting Him. Later He had Jeremiah write so that He could forgive.
- _Ex 17:14._ "And the LORD said unto Moses, Write this [for] a memorial in a book, and rehearse [it] in the ears of Joshua: for I will utterly put out the remembrance of Amalek from under heaven." — the first command to write records the LORD's battle
- _Ex 17:11._ "when Moses held up his hand, that Israel prevailed: and when he let down his hand, Amalek prevailed." — they won while they looked up
- _Jer 36:3._ "that they may return every man from his evil way; that I may forgive their iniquity and their sin." — the roll was written so He could pardon
- [RQ] Whose victory did Moses write down?
  → The LORD's: "I will utterly put out the remembrance of Amalek" (Ex 17:14); Israel prevailed only while Moses' hands were up (17:11)
- [RQ] Why did God want Jeremiah's roll written?
  → "that I may forgive their iniquity and their sin" (Jer 36:3)

[SOP PK 432.2] "the Lord commanded Jeremiah to commit to writing the messages He desired to bear to those for whose salvation His heart of pity was continually yearning" — He wrote out of pity

- **Bridge:** But a scribe's hand wrote that roll. Whose word is it, then?

## 2. The pen is man's, the word is the LORD's — Jer 36:18

- **Say:** Baruch and Tertius held the pen; the word stayed the LORD's. A king burned it, and God only gave more.
- _Jer 36:18._ "Then Baruch answered them, He pronounced all these words unto me with his mouth, and I wrote [them] with ink in the book." — the prophet spoke, the scribe wrote
- _Jer 36:4._ "Baruch wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the LORD" — the LORD, then Jeremiah, then Baruch, and still the LORD's word
- _Jer 36:32._ "and there were added besides unto them many like words." — burning the word brought more of it
- _Rom 16:22._ "I Tertius, who wrote [this] epistle, salute you in the Lord." — a scribe wrote Romans
- _Rom 1:17._ "For therein is the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith: as it is written, The just shall live by faith." — and Romans is the epistle of righteousness by faith
- _Luke 1:4._ "That thou mightest know the certainty of those things, wherein thou hast been instructed." — Luke's research was for assurance
- [RQ] Where did the words Baruch wrote come from?
  → "He pronounced all these words unto me with his mouth" (Jer 36:18): "all the words of the LORD" (36:4)
- [RQ] What did the king's fire accomplish?
  → Nothing against the word: "there were added besides unto them many like words" (Jer 36:32)

[SOP 1SM 21.1] "The writers of the Bible were God’s penmen, not His pen." — the man is inspired, not his pen

- **Bridge:** If it is God's word, how must it be received?

## 3. Received as God's word, it works — 1 Thess 2:13

- **Say:** Oracle, song, lawsuit, letter: every form carries one voice calling us from our own cisterns to the Fountain. The word works only in those who believe it.
- _Jer 2:13._ "For my people have committed two evils; they have forsaken me the fountain of living waters, [and] hewed them out cisterns, broken cisterns, that can hold no water." — the covenant lawsuit's charge is self-righteousness
- _1 Thess 2:13._ "ye received [it] not [as] the word of men, but as it is in truth, the word of God, which effectually worketh also in you that believe." — whatever the genre, the word works in believers
- _Heb 4:2._ "For unto us was the gospel preached, as well as unto them: but the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard [it]." — unbelief leaves the word idle
- [RQ] What were Israel's two evils?
  → They "forsaken me the fountain of living waters" and "hewed them out cisterns" that "can hold no water" (Jer 2:13)
- [RQ] In whom does the word do its work?
  → "in you that believe" (1 Thess 2:13); it "did not profit them, not being mixed with faith" (Heb 4:2)

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 104.3] "You are not to work to do the word of God: the word of God is to work in you to cause you to do." — 1 Thess 2:13 is righteousness by faith

- **Bridge:** Then why put so living a word in such weak men?

## 4. The treasure is Christ in earthen vessels — 2 Cor 4:7

- **Say:** The treasure is God's glory in the face of Jesus. The vessel is weak so that the power is plainly God's: Christ in you.
- _2 Cor 4:6._ "to [give] the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ." — this is the treasure
- _2 Cor 4:7._ "But we have this treasure in earthen vessels, that the excellency of the power may be of God, and not of us." — weak vessels chosen on purpose
- _2 Cor 4:5._ "For we preach not ourselves, but Christ Jesus the Lord" — the vessel points away from itself
- [RQ] What is the treasure?
  → "the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ" (2 Cor 4:6)
- [RQ] Why does God keep it in earthen vessels?
  → "that the excellency of the power may be of God, and not of us" (2 Cor 4:7)

[WB]: EARTHEN VESSEL → TREASURE (Christ) → POWER "of God, not of us"

[PIONEER Jones, CWCP 124.1] "this developing in each believer the complete image of Jesus—this is the finishing of the mystery of God, which is Christ in you the hope of glory." — what the treasure is for

- **Bridge:** God put the same treasure in an earthen vessel in our own century. Where does that gift point?

## 5. The lesser light lifts up Christ our righteousness — Rev 19:10

- **Say:** The remnant has the testimony of Jesus. The gift adds no new Bible; it leads back to the Book and lifted up the message of 1888.
- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — two marks of the remnant
- _Rev 19:10._ "worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — Jesus' own testimony; worship goes to God
- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, [it is] because [there is] no light in them." — every gift is tested by the word
- [RQ] What two marks does the remnant carry?
  → They "keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ" (Rev 12:17)
- [RQ] What is the testimony of Jesus?
  → "the spirit of prophecy" (Rev 19:10), tested "To the law and to the testimony" (Isa 8:20)

[SOP TM 91.2] "it invited the people to receive the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience to all the commandments of God." — the message the gift endorsed

- **Bridge:** Every page, old and new, was written for one end: that you believe.

## Decision

- **Say:** The word that counted Abraham righteous was written for you. Believe it, and it will do in you what it says.
- _Rom 4:23._ "Now it was not written for his sake alone, that it was imputed to him;" — Abraham's justification was written down for us
- _Rom 4:24._ "But for us also, to whom it shall be imputed, if we believe on him that raised up Jesus our Lord from the dead;" — the same righteousness on the same terms
- _Heb 11:6._ "But without faith [it is] impossible to please [him]: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and [that] he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him." — Enoch pleased God by believing
- [RQ] On what terms is righteousness counted to us?
  → "if we believe on him that raised up Jesus our Lord from the dead" (Rom 4:24)

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 15.1] "Faith is the expecting the word of God to do what it says and the depending upon that word to do what it says." — the faith the week has pointed to

- _Rom 15:13._ "Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, through the power of the Holy Ghost."

## If asked

- [Q] We don't have the original manuscripts. Can we trust the Bible?
  → The promise keeps the words, not the parchment: Ps 12:6-7; Isa 40:8; 1 Pet 1:25; when the roll burned, God had it rewritten (Jer 36:28).
- [Q] Do scribes and sources weaken inspiration?
  → No: "all the words of the LORD" (Jer 36:4); Luke wrote with "perfect understanding" (Luke 1:3); they were "moved by the Holy Ghost" (2 Pet 1:21).
- [Q] Is Ellen White a second Bible, or infallible?
  → No: her writings bring no new light (5T 665.1), she never claimed infallibility (1SM 37.4), and Isa 8:20 tests every gift.
