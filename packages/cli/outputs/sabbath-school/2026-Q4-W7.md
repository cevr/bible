## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — Faith Comes by Hearing — 2 Chr 20:20

> The prophets were not asking for a hearing for themselves; they were asking Israel to believe God. Hearing the word is how faith, and so righteousness, arrives.

[WB]: GOD SPEAKS → PROPHET → HEAR → BELIEVE → RIGHTEOUSNESS

- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — two "believes," one faith: trusting the Lord means trusting what He sends
- _Rom 10:17._ "So then faith [cometh] by hearing, and hearing by the word of God." — no word heard, no faith
- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — the pattern for every week: God speaks, man believes, God counts righteousness
- chain: Heb 11:33 (through faith wrought righteousness) → Heb 11:36-37 (mocked, stoned, sawn asunder) → Heb 11:38 (world not worthy)
- _Acts 10:43._ "To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins." — every prophet points to the same Person

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 22.8] "Righteousness by faith is righteousness that comes by the word of God." — so refusing the word God sends is refusing righteousness itself
[PIONEER Jones, GNT 153.2] "whenever any word of God, any message of the word of God, is rejected, faith itself is rejected" — the week's key

[Q] Is rejecting a prophet really a faith issue, or only a matter of opinion?
→ Rom 10:17: faith comes only by the word; refuse the word and you refuse faith. GNT 153.2: "it is impossible to retain faith while rejecting that by which alone faith comes"

[→] Sunday: the first great refusal, at the border of Canaan.

## 2. Sunday — Anger at God's Messenger — Num 14:11

> Kadesh was not a failure of nerve; it was unbelief. Israel heard the promise and would not mix it with faith, so the land of rest stayed shut.

**Two reports, one land — Num 13:26-33:**

- _Num 13:30._ "And Caleb stilled the people before Moses, and said, Let us go up at once, and possess it; for we are well able to overcome it." — Caleb counted on the promise, not on the odds
- _Num 13:31._ "We be not able to go up against the people; for they [are] stronger than we." — the ten counted giants and left God out
- _Num 14:4._ "Let us make a captain, and let us return into Egypt." — rejecting God's messenger means picking a different leader
- _Num 14:11._ "how long will it be ere they believe me, for all the signs which I have shewed among them?" — God names the sin plainly: they will not believe

| Ten spies             | Caleb and Joshua       |
| --------------------- | ---------------------- |
| looked at giants      | looked at the promise  |
| "we be not able"      | "well able"            |
| leavened with unbelief | "followed me fully"   |
| fell in wilderness    | entered the land       |

- _Num 14:24._ "my servant Caleb, because he had another spirit with him, and hath followed me fully" — faith that works: believing became following
- _Heb 3:19._ "So we see that they could not enter in because of unbelief." — the inspired verdict on Kadesh
- _Heb 4:2._ "the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard [it]." — they had the gospel and did not believe it

[SOP PP 388.1] "They left God out of the question, and acted as though they must depend solely on the power of arms." — trusting self instead of God
[SOP PP 389.1] "when men yield their hearts to unbelief they place themselves under the control of Satan, and none can tell to what lengths he will lead them." — where unbelief leads
[SOP CC 123.3] "He had believed God’s promise that He would put His people in possession of Canaan, and in this he had followed the Lord fully." — Caleb's righteousness was by faith
[PIONEER Jones, STSG 60.3] "Israel would not believe, and so could not enter in." — Jones says it in eight words

[Q] Weren't they afraid for good reason? The walls were real.
→ Num 14:9 "the LORD [is] with us: fear them not"; faith never denies the facts, it weighs them against the word. "let none repeat the sin of the unfaithful spies" (CTr 123.5)

[SOP CC 107.5] "It is when the unbelieving cast contempt upon the Word of God that the faithful Calebs are called for." — the remnant's calling

## 3. Monday — Destroying the Message: Part 1 — Jer 36:23

> The roll Jehoiakim burned was God's offer of forgiveness. A king can burn the paper, but he cannot burn the word, and refusing the gospel only leaves him in his sin.

**Love wrote the roll — Jer 36:2-3:**

- _Jer 36:3._ "that they may return every man from his evil way; that I may forgive their iniquity and their sin." — what the roll was for: pardon
- _Jer 36:7._ "It may be they will present their supplication before the LORD, and will return every one from his evil way" — God's "it may be" is His longing for them

**The penknife — Jer 36:20-26:**

- _Jer 36:23._ "when Jehudi had read three or four leaves, he cut it with the penknife, and cast [it] into the fire that [was] on the hearth, until all the roll was consumed in the fire" — he did not even let it be read through
- _Jer 36:24._ "Yet they were not afraid, nor rent their garments" — compare Num 14:6: Joshua and Caleb did tear their clothes
- _Jer 36:25._ "Elnathan and Delaiah and Gemariah had made intercession to the king that he would not burn the roll: but he would not hear them." — three men pleaded and the king still would not hear

**The word outlives the fire — Jer 36:27-32:**

- _Jer 36:28._ "Take thee again another roll, and write in it all the former words" — the message comes back
- _Jer 36:32._ "and there were added besides unto them many like words." — the second roll was longer than the first
- _Isa 40:8._ "the word of our God shall stand for ever." — the message does not depend on whether people accept it

[SOP PK 436.2] "the words were still living in the heart of Jeremiah, “as a burning fire,”" — the word survives
[SOP 4T 180.1] "some in these days have even gone so far as to burn the written words of rebuke and warning, as did the wicked king of Israel" — Jehoiakim's sin repeated today

- _Jer 23:6._ "and this [is] his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS." — the prophet whose roll was burned is the one who gave Christ this name

[SOP DA 578.7] "Jeremiah will tell you, The Branch of David, “the Lord our Righteousness.”" — Jeremiah's gospel

[→] Burning the roll did not get rid of Jehoiakim's sin. It got rid of the pardon written in it.

## 4. Tuesday — Destroying the Message: Part 2 — 2 Chr 36:15-16

> God kept sending prophets because He had compassion. When people refuse the last offer of mercy, there is no remedy left, because the remedy is the message itself.

- _2 Chr 36:15._ "And the LORD God of their fathers sent to them by his messengers, rising up betimes, and sending; because he had compassion on his people, and on his dwelling place:" — God kept sending because He loved them
- _2 Chr 36:16._ "But they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused his prophets, until the wrath of the LORD arose against his people, till [there was] no remedy." — mock, despise, misuse, then no remedy
- _2 Chr 36:12._ "humbled not himself before Jeremiah the prophet [speaking] from the mouth of the LORD." — refusing the prophet was refusing God's mouth
- _2 Kgs 17:14._ "hardened their necks, like to the neck of their fathers, that did not believe in the LORD their God." — at the root of rejecting the prophets: they did not believe
- chain: Jer 25:4 (rising early, sending) → 2 Pet 3:9 (not willing any perish) → Matt 23:37 ("and ye would not")

[SOP GC 19.1] "When remonstrance, entreaty, and rebuke had failed, He sent to them the best gift of heaven; nay, He poured out all heaven in that one Gift." — God's last message was Christ Himself
[SOP PK 437.2] "The spirit of opposition to reproof, that led to the persecution and imprisonment of Jeremiah, exists today." — the danger is present now

**1888 — the same pattern in our own history:**

[SOP TM 91.2] "The Lord in His great mercy sent a most precious message to His people through Elders Waggoner and Jones." — God sent messengers to us too
[SOP 1888 636.2] "the brethren have known and have seen the fruits, and yet unbelief has come right in." — said about Minneapolis
[PIONEER Jones, GCDB February 23, 1893, page 359.8] "This is a testimony that had been despised, rejected, and criticised for two years" — Jones at the 1893 Conference: 2 Chr 36:16's "despised his words" in our own history

[Q] Would God ever stop sending?
→ Never willingly (2 Chr 36:15; 2 Pet 3:9). "No remedy" comes when people have refused the only remedy there is (Acts 4:12 cf. GC 19.1)

## 5. Wednesday — Hypocrisy — Matt 23:29-31

> The Pharisees honored dead prophets and killed living ones. Their own righteousness could not bear a message that brought God's righteousness by faith.

- _Matt 23:29-30._ "ye build the tombs of the prophets, and garnish the sepulchres of the righteous, And say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets." — they admired the prophets only once they were dead
- _Matt 23:31._ "Wherefore ye be witnesses unto yourselves, that ye are the children of them which killed the prophets." — their own words convict them
- _Matt 23:35._ "That upon you may come all the righteous blood shed upon the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel" — Abel "by faith" was "witness that he was righteous" (Heb 11:4); the first martyr was a man of faith
- _Acts 7:52._ "Which of the prophets have not your fathers persecuted? and they have slain them which shewed before of the coming of the Just One" — the prophets were killed for announcing the Just One, Christ our righteousness

**Why they could not hear — Rom 10:3:**

- _Rom 10:3._ "For they being ignorant of God's righteousness, and going about to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted themselves unto the righteousness of God." — pride in their own righteousness kept them from hearing
- _Phil 3:9._ "not having mine own righteousness, which is of the law, but that which is through the faith of Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith" — what they refused to receive

| own righteousness          | righteousness of God      |

[SOP DA 618.2] "But when obedience to God requires self-denial and humiliation, these very persons stifle their convictions, and refuse obedience." — how the tomb-builder thinks
[SOP YRP 352.4] "They would not receive the light and knowledge He brought, because it did not come in the way they had expected it to come." — written in 1889, the same year as the debate over the message

→ Honoring them is the very thing Matt 23:29-30 warns about. The test is whether we obey when the message humbles us (DA 618.2)

## 6. Thursday — For Our Admonition — 1 Cor 10:11

> Israel's story was written for us. They had the same Christ and the same gospel and faced the same test, and the only time God has fixed for us to hear is "To day."

- _1 Cor 10:11._ "Now all these things happened unto them for ensamples: and they are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come." — the final generation is the one this warning was written for
- _1 Cor 10:4._ "for they drank of that spiritual Rock that followed them: and that Rock was Christ." — Israel's gospel was Christ
- chain: 1 Cor 10:6 (lust) → 10:7 (idolatry) → 10:9 (tempt Christ) → 10:10 (murmur), every one a fruit of unbelief (Heb 3:19)
- _1 Cor 10:12._ "Wherefore let him that thinketh he standeth take heed lest he fall." — the warning
- _1 Cor 10:13._ "God [is] faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able" — the promise that follows it
- _Rom 15:4._ "whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope." — the record is there to give us hope

## 8. Appeal

- Every rejected prophet in this lesson was rejected for one reason: people would not believe God (Num 14:11; 2 Kgs 17:14). The cure is to believe God, and the faith to do it comes by hearing (Rom 10:17)
- Jehoiakim burned an offer of pardon (Jer 36:3). The Pharisees built tombs to protect their own righteousness (Rom 10:3). Don't do what they did: take the righteousness of God by faith (Phil 3:9)
- The message God sent to His people was the uplifted Saviour, and it is the third angel's message (TM 91.2; 1SM 372.2). Hear it this week and don't argue it away
- [RQ] What message from God are you holding a penknife to right now?

- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the promise
