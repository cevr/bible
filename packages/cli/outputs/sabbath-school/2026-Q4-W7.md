# Sabbath School — Q4 W7 (2026)

## Faith Comes by Hearing

_Quarterly: Now Hear This! · Nov 7–13 · Read: Num 13:26–30; Jer 36:20–26; 2 Chr 36:15, 16; Matt 23:30–35; 1 Cor 10:6–12_

**Memory Verse:** — _1 Cor 10:11._ "Now all these things happened unto them for ensamples: and they are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come."

**Theme: To refuse the message God sends is to refuse the faith by which alone Christ's righteousness comes, so today, hear and believe.**

1. Unbelief at the border — Num 14:11 _(quarterly: Sun)_

## Open

- **Say:** The prophets never asked Israel to believe them; they asked Israel to believe God. So what is really refused when a message from God is refused?
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — two "believes," one faith: trusting the Lord means trusting what He sends
- _Rom 10:17._ "So then faith [cometh] by hearing, and hearing by the word of God." — no word heard, no faith
- [RQ] Where does faith come from?
  → From hearing the word of God (Rom 10:17). Refuse the word, and faith has nothing to come from.

[WB]: GOD SPEAKS → PROPHET → HEAR → BELIEVE → RIGHTEOUSNESS

## 1. Unbelief at the Border — Num 14:11

- **Say:** Kadesh was not a failure of nerve. Israel heard the promise and would not believe it, so the land of rest stayed shut.
- _Num 13:30._ "And Caleb stilled the people before Moses, and said, Let us go up at once, and possess it; for we are well able to overcome it." — Caleb counted on the promise, not on the odds
- _Num 13:31._ "We be not able to go up against the people; for they [are] stronger than we." — the ten counted giants and left God out
- _Num 14:11._ "how long will it be ere they believe me, for all the signs which I have shewed among them?" — God names the sin plainly: they will not believe
- _Heb 4:2._ "the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard [it]." — they had the gospel and did not believe it

| Ten spies         | Caleb and Joshua      |
| ----------------- | --------------------- |
| looked at giants  | looked at the promise |
| "we be not able"  | "well able"           |
| fell in wilderness | entered the land     |

- [RQ] What does God Himself call Israel's sin at Kadesh?
  → Unbelief: "how long will it be ere they believe me" (Num 14:11).
- [RQ] Why did the word preached not help them?
  → It was "not being mixed with faith in them that heard" (Heb 4:2).

[PIONEER Jones, STSG 60.3] "Israel would not believe, and so could not enter in." — Jones says it in eight words

- **Bridge:** At Kadesh Israel refused a promise. At Jerusalem a king took a knife to a pardon.

## 2. The Penknife Cannot Burn the Pardon — Jer 36:3

- **Say:** The roll Jehoiakim burned was God's offer of forgiveness. He could burn the paper; he could not burn the word, and he kept his sin.
- _Jer 36:3._ "that they may return every man from his evil way; that I may forgive their iniquity and their sin." — what the roll was for: pardon
- _Jer 36:23._ "when Jehudi had read three or four leaves, he cut it with the penknife, and cast [it] into the fire that [was] on the hearth, until all the roll was consumed in the fire" — he did not even let it be read through
- _Jer 36:28._ "Take thee again another roll, and write in it all the former words" — the message comes back
- _Jer 23:6._ "and this [is] his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS." — the prophet whose roll was burned is the one who gave Christ this name
- [RQ] What was written on the roll the king burned?
  → An offer of pardon: "that I may forgive their iniquity and their sin" (Jer 36:3).
- [RQ] Did the fire get rid of the word?
  → No. God said, "Take thee again another roll, and write in it all the former words" (Jer 36:28).

[SOP DA 578.7] "Jeremiah will tell you, The Branch of David, “the Lord our Righteousness.”" — Jeremiah's gospel

- **Bridge:** Jehoiakim was one king. The next text shows a whole nation doing it, and a God who kept on sending.

## 3. God Keeps Sending, Even to Us — 2 Chr 36:15-16

- **Say:** God sent prophet after prophet because He had compassion. "No remedy" came only when the people had refused the remedy itself, and He has sent messengers to us too.
- _2 Chr 36:15._ "And the LORD God of their fathers sent to them by his messengers, rising up betimes, and sending; because he had compassion on his people, and on his dwelling place:" — God kept sending because He loved them
- _2 Chr 36:16._ "But they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused his prophets, until the wrath of the LORD arose against his people, till [there was] no remedy." — mock, despise, misuse, then no remedy
- _Rev 19:10._ "for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — the gift in the remnant exists to testify of Jesus
- [RQ] Why did God keep sending messengers?
  → "because he had compassion on his people" (2 Chr 36:15).
- [RQ] When was there "no remedy"?
  → When they "mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words" (2 Chr 36:16). The message was the remedy.

[SOP TM 91.2] "The Lord in His great mercy sent a most precious message to His people through Elders Waggoner and Jones." — God sent messengers to us too

- **Bridge:** Those who despised that message were not unbelievers outside the church. They were the most religious people in it.

## 4. Tomb-Builders Keep Their Own Righteousness — Rom 10:3

- **Say:** The Pharisees honored dead prophets and killed living ones. Their own righteousness could not bear a message that offered God's righteousness by faith.
- _Matt 23:29-30._ "ye build the tombs of the prophets, and garnish the sepulchres of the righteous, And say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets." — they admired the prophets only once they were dead
- _Matt 23:31._ "Wherefore ye be witnesses unto yourselves, that ye are the children of them which killed the prophets." — their own words convict them
- _Rom 10:3._ "For they being ignorant of God's righteousness, and going about to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted themselves unto the righteousness of God." — pride in their own righteousness kept them from hearing

| Tomb-builder         | Believer             |
| -------------------- | -------------------- |
| honors dead prophets | heeds living word    |
| "we would not have"  | "Lord, is it I?"     |
| own righteousness    | righteousness of God |

- [RQ] What did the Pharisees say about the prophets their fathers killed?
  → "If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers" (Matt 23:30).
- [RQ] Why could they not hear the message?
  → They were "going about to establish their own righteousness," and would not submit to God's (Rom 10:3).

[SOP YRP 352.4] "You may close the door to light as effectually as the Pharisees closed the door to Christ when He taught among them." — the tomb-builder's sin is open to us

- **Bridge:** Paul says these stories were written down for one generation in particular.

## 5. Written for Us: "To Day" — 1 Cor 10:11

- **Say:** Israel had the same Christ and the same gospel, and faced the same test. Their story was written for us, and the only time God gives us to hear is "To day."
- _1 Cor 10:4._ "for they drank of that spiritual Rock that followed them: and that Rock was Christ." — Israel's gospel was Christ
- _1 Cor 10:11._ "Now all these things happened unto them for ensamples: and they are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come." — the final generation is the one this warning was written for
- _1 Cor 10:12._ "Wherefore let him that thinketh he standeth take heed lest he fall." — the warning
- _Heb 3:15._ "While it is said, To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts, as in the provocation." — Kadesh repeated for us
- [RQ] Who was the Rock Israel drank from?
  → Christ (1 Cor 10:4). They had the same Saviour we have.
- [RQ] When does God ask us to hear His voice?
  → "To day" (Heb 3:15), not after we have argued it out.

[PIONEER Jones, STSG 65.3] "And still, “to-day,” it remains; for “some must enter therein.”" — God's rest is still open

- **Bridge:** The rest is still open. All God asks is the one thing Kadesh refused: believe Him.

## Decision

- **Say:** God offers His own righteousness, carried in the word He sends. He asks only that we hear it and believe Him, and He counts that faith for righteousness.
- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — God speaks, man believes, God counts righteousness
- _Phil 3:9._ "not having mine own righteousness, which is of the law, but that which is through the faith of Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith" — what the tomb-builders refused, offered to us
- [RQ] What did Abraham do that God counted for righteousness?
  → "he believed in the LORD" (Gen 15:6). Not effort: belief of the word God spoke.

[SOP 1SM 372.2] "It is the third angel’s message, in verity." — justification by faith is our message

## If asked

- [Q] Is rejecting a prophet really a faith issue, or only a matter of opinion?
  → Rom 10:17: faith comes only by the word, so refusing the word refuses faith (GNT 153.2).
- [Q] Weren't the spies afraid for good reason? The walls were real.
  → Num 14:9 "the LORD [is] with us: fear them not." Faith does not deny the facts; it weighs them against the promise.
- [Q] What if I have honest doubts about the gift of prophecy?
  → God gives "evidence sufficient to balance our faith" (4T 230.1); test it by its fruit (Matt 7:20) and by whether it lifts up Jesus (Rev 19:10).
