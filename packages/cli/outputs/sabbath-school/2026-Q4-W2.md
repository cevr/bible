# Sabbath School — Q4 W2 (2026)

## The Coal Before the Commission

_Quarterly: The Call of a Prophet · October 3–9 · Read: Gen. 15:6; Gal. 3:6–9; 1 Kings 18:19–39; Isaiah 53; Dan. 2:46–48; Mark 1:2–5; Matt. 11:1–6_

**Memory Verse:** — _Isa 6:8._ "Also I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Then said I, Here [am] I; send me."

**Theme: God sends no one He has not first cleansed: every prophet was a sinner counted righteous by faith in the Lamb, and was sent to call us to that same faith.**

## The road

1. Undone, then cleansed, then sent — Isa 6:5-8 _(quarterly: Sabbath, Tue)_
2. The coal came from the Lamb's altar — Isa 53:5-6, 11 _(quarterly: Tue)_
3. Believed, and counted righteous — Gen 15:6 _(quarterly: Sun, Wed)_
4. The fire falls on the sacrifice — 1 Kgs 18:36-38 _(quarterly: Mon)_
5. Behold the Lamb: the message for the last days — John 1:29 _(quarterly: Thu, Fri)_
6. Decision

## Open

- **Say:** Isaiah volunteered: "Here am I; send me." What made a man who had just cried "undone" ready to say that?
- _Isa 6:8._ "Also I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Then said I, Here [am] I; send me." — the call comes after something has happened to him
- [RQ] Whose voice is asking, and who is invited to answer?
  → "I heard the voice of the Lord": He asks "who will go for us?" and anyone He has cleansed may answer

## 1. Undone, Then Cleansed, Then Sent — Isa 6:5-8

- **Say:** A prophet is called in the order of the gospel. He sees the King, he is undone, he is cleansed by the altar, and only then is he sent.
- _Isa 6:1._ "I saw also the Lord sitting upon a throne, high and lifted up, and his train filled the temple." — the vision starts with the King
- _Isa 6:5._ "Woe [is] me! for I am undone; because I [am] a man of unclean lips" — the prophet's first discovery is his own sin
- _Isa 6:6._ "Then flew one of the seraphims unto me, having a live coal in his hand, [which] he had taken with the tongs from off the altar" — the cure comes from the altar, not from Isaiah
- _Isa 6:7._ "Lo, this hath touched thy lips; and thine iniquity is taken away, and thy sin purged." — he is cleansed by a gift; he does nothing to earn it
- [RQ] What did Isaiah do to remove his iniquity?
  → Nothing. The seraph brought the coal "from off the altar," and God said "thine iniquity is taken away" (v6-7)
- [RQ] When did the call "Whom shall I send?" come?
  → After "thy sin purged" (v7). The coal comes before the commission (v8)

[WB]: SEEN (v1) → UNDONE (v5) → COAL (v7) → SENT (v8)

[SOP 4BC 1139.3] "The vision given to Isaiah represents the condition of God’s people in the last days." — Isaiah 6 is our hour

- **Bridge:** The coal came from an altar. Isaiah tells us whose sacrifice lay on it.

## 2. The Coal Came from the Lamb's Altar — Isa 53:5-6, 11

- **Say:** The prophet who was cleansed preached the One who cleansed him. In Isaiah 53 the Servant takes our sin, and we receive His righteousness.
- _Isa 53:5._ "But he [was] wounded for our transgressions, [he was] bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace [was] upon him; and with his stripes we are healed." — substitution, stated plainly
- _Isa 53:6._ "the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all" — all of us, so it includes me
- _Isa 53:11._ "by his knowledge shall my righteous servant justify many; for he shall bear their iniquities." — Isaiah uses the word "justify"
- _Isa 53:1._ "Who hath believed our report?" — the chapter asks for faith
- [RQ] Where did our iniquity go?
  → "the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all" (v6)
- [RQ] What does the righteous Servant do for the many whose sins He bears?
  → He shall "justify many" (v11)

| Laid on Him | Given to us |
| --- | --- |
| "our transgressions" | "we are healed" |
| "iniquity of us all" | "justify many" |

[PIONEER Jones, CWCP 41.2] "All our sins which we have actually committed were laid upon Him, were imputed to Him, so that His righteousness may be laid upon us, may be imputed to us." — 1888 reading of Isa 53:6

- **Bridge:** "Who hath believed our report?" The first prophet God named shows what believing does.

## 3. Believed, and Counted Righteous — Gen 15:6

- **Say:** Abraham, whom God calls a prophet, was counted righteous for believing God's promise. Daniel was kept for the same reason. Faith, not merit, made them God's men.
- _Gen 20:7._ "for he [is] a prophet, and he shall pray for thee" — God's own title for Abraham
- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — the first "counted ... for righteousness" in the Bible
- _Gal 3:8._ "the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham" — Abraham heard the gospel of Christ
- _Gal 3:9._ "So then they which be of faith are blessed with faithful Abraham." — his blessing is open to every believer
- _Dan 6:23._ "So Daniel was taken up out of the den, and no manner of hurt was found upon him, because he believed in his God." — Scripture gives faith as the reason he was kept
- _Dan 9:18._ "for we do not present our supplications before thee for our righteousnesses, but for thy great mercies." — the faithful seer brings no righteousness of his own
- [RQ] What did God count to Abraham for righteousness?
  → "he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness" (Gen 15:6). His faith
- [RQ] Who may share Abraham's blessing?
  → "they which be of faith" (Gal 3:9)

[PIONEER Waggoner, GTI 106.4] "No Gentile can hope to be saved in any other way or by any other gospel than that by which Abraham was saved." — one gospel in every age

- **Bridge:** Elijah stood before a nation that wanted to earn God's answer. Carmel puts both ways on the table.

## 4. The Fire Falls on the Sacrifice — 1 Kgs 18:36-38

- **Say:** Carmel was a contest between two ways of being accepted. Baal's prophets bled for an answer; Elijah rested on the evening sacrifice and on God's word.
- _1 Kgs 18:21._ "How long halt ye between two opinions? if the LORD [be] God, follow him: but if Baal, [then] follow him." — the Elijah message is a call to decide
- _1 Kgs 18:36._ "LORD God of Abraham, Isaac, and of Israel, let it be known this day that thou [art] God in Israel, and [that] I [am] thy servant, and [that] I have done all these things at thy word." — he acted on the word alone
- _1 Kgs 18:37._ "thou hast turned their heart back again" — God turns the heart
- _1 Kgs 18:38._ "Then the fire of the LORD fell, and consumed the burnt sacrifice" — the fire fell on the offering
- _Mal 4:5-6._ "Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the LORD" — an Elijah message before the second advent
- [RQ] Where did the fire fall?
  → On "the burnt sacrifice" (v38), not on the prophets who "cut themselves" (v28)
- [RQ] Who turns the people's heart back?
  → God: "thou hast turned their heart back again" (v37)

| Baal's altar | The LORD's altar |
| --- | --- |
| "cut themselves" (v28) | the sacrifice bled |
| "no voice" (v26) | "the fire of the LORD fell" |
| human effort | "at thy word" (v36) |

- **Bridge:** Malachi promised Elijah again. Jesus said who came in that spirit, and what he preached.

## 5. Behold the Lamb: The Message for the Last Days — John 1:29

- **Say:** John prepared a people by pointing away from himself to the Lamb. Before the second advent the same work is done again, and its message is Christ's righteousness.
- _Mark 1:3._ "The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight." — Isa 40:3 fulfilled
- _Mark 1:4._ "John did baptize in the wilderness, and preach the baptism of repentance for the remission of sins." — what he preached: remission of sins
- _John 1:29._ "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world." — the center of his message
- _John 3:30._ "He must increase, but I [must] decrease." — the prophet makes himself small
- _Rev 19:10._ "for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — the gift in the remnant testifies of Jesus
- [RQ] To whom did John point the people?
  → "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world" (John 1:29)
- [RQ] What is the spirit of prophecy?
  → "the testimony of Jesus" (Rev 19:10). The gift always points to Him

[SOP 6T 19.1] "The message of Christ’s righteousness is to sound from one end of the earth to the other to prepare the way of the Lord." — what "prepare the way" means now

- **Bridge:** Every prophet this week was cleansed before he was sent. The call now comes to us in the same order.

## Decision

- **Say:** God offers the coal from the altar: Christ's righteousness counted to us by faith. He asks us to believe Him, and then to go.
- _Acts 10:43._ "To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins." — every prophet's one message
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the gift exists to produce faith
- _Isa 6:8._ "Here [am] I; send me." — the answer of a cleansed sinner
- [RQ] What must I do before I can say "Here am I"?
  → Believe. "whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins" (Acts 10:43); the coal is received, not earned

[SOP 4BC 1139.3] "The bow of promise is above the throne, and the work done for Isaiah will be performed in them." — the coal is promised to us

## If asked

- [Q] Was Abraham saved by obeying?
  → No. Counted righteous in Gen 15:6, before circumcision (Rom 4:11); his obedience (Gen 26:5; Heb 11:8) was faith's fruit
- [Q] Is repentance something I must produce before God accepts me?
  → No. "thou hast turned their heart back again" (1 Kgs 18:37); Christ is exalted "to give repentance" (Acts 5:31)
- [Q] Does the prophetic gift add to the Bible?
  → No. Isa 8:20 is the test; the gift is "the testimony of Jesus" (Rev 19:10) and points back to the Word
