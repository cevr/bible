## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — Seen, Cleansed, Sent — Isa 6:1-8

> A prophet is called in the order of the gospel: he sees the King, is undone, is cleansed by the altar, and only then is sent.

[WB]: SEEN (v1) → UNDONE (v5) → COAL (v7) → SENT (v8)

- _Isa 6:5._ "Woe [is] me! for I am undone; because I [am] a man of unclean lips" — the prophet's first discovery is his own sin
- _Isa 6:7._ "Lo, this hath touched thy lips; and thine iniquity is taken away, and thy sin purged." — he is cleansed by a gift; he does nothing to earn it
- _Isa 6:8._ "Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Then said I, Here [am] I; send me." — he is sent only after he is forgiven
- _John 12:41._ "These things said Esaias, when he saw his glory, and spake of him." — the King on the throne was Christ
- chain: Amos 3:7 (secrets to the prophets) → Hos 12:13 (Israel saved by a prophet) → 1 Pet 1:10-11 (Spirit of Christ in them) → Acts 10:43 (all the prophets: remission through His name)

[SOP PP 366.3] "It is the voice of Christ that speaks to us through the Old Testament." — every prophet speaks for Christ

[SOP 4BC 1139.3] "The vision given to Isaiah represents the condition of God’s people in the last days." — Isaiah 6 describes us in the Most Holy Place hour

[SOP 4BC 1139.3] "The bow of promise is above the throne, and the work done for Isaiah will be performed in them." — the coal is promised to us

- _Rev 11:19._ "there was seen in his temple the ark of his testament" — 1844 onward: the law in the ark and the mercy seat above it

[→] The quarterly asks who the prophets were; Isa 6 shows they were first forgiven sinners. The live coal comes before the commission.

## 2. Sunday — Abraham: The Gospel Preached to a Prophet — Gen 15:6

> Abraham, whom God Himself calls a prophet, was declared righteous for believing God's promise of Christ. That same gospel is the only one there is.

- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — the first "counted ... for righteousness" in the Bible
- _Gen 15:1._ "the word of the LORD came unto Abram in a vision" — a prophet's call: God's word comes, the man believes it
- _Gen 20:7._ "for he [is] a prophet, and he shall pray for thee" — God's own title for Abraham
- _Gal 3:8._ "the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham" — Abraham heard the gospel of Christ
- _Gal 3:9._ "So then they which be of faith are blessed with faithful Abraham." — his blessing is open to every believer
- chain: Rom 4:5 (believeth on Him that justifieth the ungodly) → Rom 4:11 (seal of the righteousness of the faith) → John 8:56 (rejoiced to see my day) → Gen 22:8 (God will provide himself a lamb)

[SOP PP 370.3] "So Abraham understood it (see Galatians 3:8, 16), and he trusted in Christ for the forgiveness of sins. It was this faith that was accounted unto him for righteousness." — his faith was faith in Christ

[PIONEER Waggoner, GTI 106.4] "No Gentile can hope to be saved in any other way or by any other gospel than that by which Abraham was saved." — one gospel in every age

**Root and fruit, both said of the same man:**

| Root (Gen 15:6) | Fruit (Gen 26:5) |
| --- | --- |
| "believed in the LORD" | "obeyed my voice" |
| counted righteous | kept "my commandments" |
| faith received | faith "wrought with his works" (Jas 2:22) |

- _Heb 11:8._ "By faith Abraham, when he was called to go out into a place which he should after receive for an inheritance, obeyed" — the obedience came out of faith

[Q] Was Abraham saved by obeying?
→ No — counted righteous in Gen 15, before circumcision (Rom 4:11) and before Moriah (Gen 22); obedience is faith's fruit

[SOP PP 153.4] "genuine faith will be manifest in obedience" — fruit, not root

[PIONEER Waggoner, GTI 104.1] "his faith “wrought righteousness.”" — faith does the work

[→] Rev 12:17 remnant = Abraham's children (Gal 3:29). They believe God and therefore keep His commandments.

## 3. Monday — Elijah: The Fire Falls on the Sacrifice — 1 Kgs 18:36-39

> Carmel was a contest between two ways of being accepted: Baal's prophets bled for an answer, and Elijah rested on the evening sacrifice and on God's word.

- _1 Kgs 18:21._ "How long halt ye between two opinions? if the LORD [be] God, follow him: but if Baal, [then] follow him." — the Elijah message is a call to decide
- _1 Kgs 18:36._ "LORD God of Abraham, Isaac, and of Israel, let it be known this day that thou [art] God in Israel, and [that] I [am] thy servant, and [that] I have done all these things at thy word." — he acted on the word alone
- _1 Kgs 18:37._ "thou hast turned their heart back again" — God turns the heart
- _1 Kgs 18:38._ "Then the fire of the LORD fell, and consumed the burnt sacrifice" — the fire fell on the offering, at "the offering of the [evening] sacrifice" (v36)

**Two altars:**

| Baal's altar | The LORD's altar |
| --- | --- |
| "cut themselves" (v28) | the sacrifice bled instead |
| "leaped upon the altar" (v26) | "at thy word" (v36) |
| "no voice" (v26) | "the fire of the LORD fell" |
| human effort | faith in the offering |

[SOP PK 121.2] "It was only by the exercise of strong faith in the unfailing power of God’s word that Elijah delivered his message." — the prophet lived by faith

- _Mal 4:5-6._ "Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the LORD" — an Elijah message before the second advent
- chain: 1 Kgs 17:1 ("before whom I stand") → 1 Kgs 18:18 (forsaken the commandments) → Jas 5:17 ("subject to like passions as we are") → Luke 1:17 (spirit and power of Elias)

[SOP 3T 62.1] "Those who are to prepare the way for the second coming of Christ are represented by faithful Elijah" — this is our work

[PIONEER Jones, GCDB February 7, 1893, page 181.7] "Do we not stand as did he [Elijah]? Then do we not need to have the faith that he had?" — 1893: Carmel faith is our need

[Q] Is repentance something I must produce first ("turn their heart back")?
→ No — "thou hast turned their heart back again" (1 Kgs 18:37); Acts 5:31 (exalted to give repentance)

[SOP 1SM 391.1] "Repentance is no less the gift of God than are pardon and justification" — the gift, not the price

[→] The last-day Elijah message is this same gospel: turn from Baal (self-made righteousness) to the LORD's sacrifice.

## 4. Tuesday — Isaiah: The Righteous Servant Justifies Many — Isa 53:5-6, 11

> Isaiah 53 is the gospel prophet's report: the Servant takes our sin and we receive His righteousness. The prophet's question is whether we will believe the report.

- _Isa 53:5._ "But he [was] wounded for our transgressions, [he was] bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace [was] upon him; and with his stripes we are healed." — substitution, stated plainly
- _Isa 53:6._ "the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all" — all of us, so it includes me
- _Isa 53:11._ "by his knowledge shall my righteous servant justify many; for he shall bear their iniquities." — Isaiah uses the word "justify"
- _Isa 53:1._ "Who hath believed our report?" — the chapter opens by asking for faith
- chain: Isa 53:7 (lamb to the slaughter) → Isa 53:12 (made intercession) → 2 Cor 5:21 (made the righteousness of God in him) → Jer 23:6 (THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS)

[SOP DA 25.2] "Christ was treated as we deserve, that we might be treated as He deserves." — the exchange in one line

[SOP DA 25.2] "He was condemned for our sins, in which He had no share, that we might be justified by His righteousness, in which we had no share." — Isa 53:11 explained

[PIONEER Jones, CWCP 41.2] "All our sins which we have actually committed were laid upon Him, were imputed to Him, so that His righteousness may be laid upon us, may be imputed to us." — 1888 reading of Isa 53:6

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 27.3] "He was made to be sin in order that we might be made righteousness." — 2 Cor 5:21 = Isa 53

- _Isa 61:10._ "he hath covered me with the robe of righteousness" — the result: the sinner is clothed

[RQ] Read Isa 53 again and say "for me" after each "our" and "us." What changes?

[→] The coal on Isaiah's lips (Isa 6:7) was taken from the altar of Isa 53. Isaiah preached the Christ who had cleansed him.

## 5. Wednesday — Daniel: "Because He Believed in His God" — Dan 6:23

> Daniel was a faithful seer because he trusted God, not himself. His prophecies point to the Messiah who brings in "everlasting righteousness" and to the judgment.

- _Dan 6:23._ "So Daniel was taken up out of the den, and no manner of hurt was found upon him, because he believed in his God." — Scripture gives faith as the reason he was kept
- _Dan 9:18._ "for we do not present our supplications before thee for our righteousnesses, but for thy great mercies." — the faithful seer brings no righteousness of his own

[SOP PK 555.1] "Though Daniel had long been in the service of God, and had been spoken of by heaven as “greatly beloved,” yet he now appeared before God as a sinner" — the godliest man prays as a sinner

**The pagan kings saw it:**

- chain: Dan 2:47 ("a God of gods, and a Lord of kings, and a revealer of secrets") → Dan 4:18 ("the spirit of the holy gods") → Dan 5:14 ("excellent wisdom") → Dan 6:26 ("the living God, and stedfast for ever")

**The prophecy is sure:**

- _Dan 2:45._ "the dream [is] certain, and the interpretation thereof sure." — four kingdoms fulfilled, so the fifth is certain
- _Dan 9:24._ "to make reconciliation for iniquity, and to bring in everlasting righteousness" — the 70 weeks are about Messiah's righteousness

[PIONEER Smith, DAR 193.3] "Everlasting righteousness was to be brought in; the righteousness which our Lord manifested in his sinless life." — the righteousness faith receives

[PIONEER Smith, DAR 193.3] "If the events of this period are accurately fulfilled, the prophecy is of God, and will all be accomplished" — 70 weeks fulfilled, so the 2300 days (Dan 8:14) hold

[Q] Is the prophetic gift mainly about predicting history?
→ The history proves it true (Dan 2:45), but its subject is Christ: Dan 7:13-14 (Son of man), Dan 9:24 (everlasting righteousness), Dan 8:14 (sanctuary cleansed)

[→] Dan 2:44: the stone is coming. Daniel's kind of faith is the only safe ground in the judgment hour.

## 6. Thursday — John: "Behold the Lamb" — Mark 1:2-5

> John prepared a people by pointing away from himself to the Lamb. A John-like message must be given again before the second advent, and it is the message of Christ's righteousness.

- _Mark 1:3._ "The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight." — Isa 40:3 fulfilled
- _Mark 1:4._ "John did baptize in the wilderness, and preach the baptism of repentance for the remission of sins." — what he preached: remission of sins
- _John 1:29._ "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world." — the center of his message
- _John 3:30._ "He must increase, but I [must] decrease." — the prophet makes himself small
- chain: Mal 4:5 (Elijah sent) → Luke 1:17 ("make ready a people prepared") → Matt 11:14 ("this is Elias") → Matt 11:11 (none greater)

[SOP DA 179.4] "Looking in faith to the Redeemer, John had risen to the height of self-abnegation." — faith empties the self

[PIONEER Waggoner, GCDB February 17, 1899, page 13.6] "John the Baptist was not a man simply who had a work to be finished when he died, but he was a voice, the embodiment of the word of God." — the voice (Mark 1:3) outlives the man

[SOP 8T 332.3] "In this age, just prior to the second coming of Christ in the clouds of heaven, such a work as that of John is to be done." — John's work is repeated today

[SOP 6T 19.1] "The message of Christ’s righteousness is to sound from one end of the earth to the other to prepare the way of the Lord." — what "prepare the way" means now

[Q] Why did John doubt in prison ("Art thou he that should come?")?
→ Jesus answered with Scripture and His works (Matt 11:4-5; Isa 61:1), not with deliverance

[SOP DA 224.2] "Jesus did not interpose to deliver His servant. He knew that John would bear the test." — faith tested, not abandoned

- _Matt 11:6._ "And blessed is [he], whosoever shall not be offended in me." — faith that holds on without sight

[SOP LHU 231.5] "Lift Him up, the Man of Calvary. Let humanity stand back, that all may behold Him in whom their hopes of eternal life are centered." — the forerunner's whole method

## 7. Friday — Further Thought: The Gift in the Remnant — Rev 19:10

> After 1844 God called a seventeen-year-old to keep the Advent people's eyes on Jesus. He was going into the Most Holy Place, and the gift's highest work was to lift up Christ our righteousness.

- _Rev 19:10._ "for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — the gift testifies of Jesus
- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — a mark of the remnant
- _Rev 14:12._ "here [are] they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." — the third angel's people
- chain: Dan 8:14 (sanctuary cleansed) → Rev 11:19 (ark seen) → Heb 11:6 (must believe that He is) → 2 Chr 20:20 (believe His prophets)

[SOP EW 14.1] "If they kept their eyes fixed on Jesus, who was just before them, leading them to the city, they were safe." — the first vision (Dec 1844), about the Advent people

[SOP EW 55.1] "Those who rose up with Jesus would send up their faith to Him in the holiest, and pray, “My Father, give us Thy Spirit.”" — faith follows Christ into the Most Holy Place

[PIONEER James White, AWLF 13.4] "True visions are given to lead us to God, and his written word" — the gift serves the Bible and does not replace it

**What the gift said about 1888:**

[SOP TM 91.2] "The Lord in His great mercy sent a most precious message to His people through Elders Waggoner and Jones." — the prophet endorsed the message

[SOP TM 91.2] "it invited the people to receive the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience to all the commandments of God." — the message itself

## 8. Appeal — "Here Am I; Send Me" — 2 Chr 20:20

- Every prophet this week was a believer before he was a messenger: Abraham believed, Elijah acted "at thy word," Isaiah was cleansed, Daniel "believed in his God," and John beheld the Lamb
- The call comes in the same order to us: see the King, confess "undone," receive the coal (Christ's righteousness counted and imparted), then go
- Believing God's prophets means believing what they all said: "To him give all the prophets witness" (Acts 10:43)
- Decision: will you stop halting between two opinions (1 Kgs 18:21) and receive Christ our righteousness as your only plea in the judgment hour (Dan 9:18)?
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper."
- _Isa 6:8._ "Here [am] I; send me."
- Closing hymn: SDAH 359, "Hark! the Voice of Jesus Calling" ("Here am I, O Lord, send me")
