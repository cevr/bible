# Sabbath School — Q4 W10 (2026)

## Prophetic Authority and Relevance

_Week of November 28–December 4 · Read: Exod. 4:10–16; Luke 8:22–25; John 3:1–10; Acts 16:25–34; 1 Cor. 5:1–5; Luke 7:28_

**Memory Verse:** — _Hebrews 4:12._ "For the word of God [is] quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and [is] a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart."

**A prophet's authority is Christ's own word in a human mouth, and its one demand is "believe" — the word that cuts the heart open (Heb 4:12) is the same word that counts righteous the one who believes it (Gen 15:6; 2 Chr 20:20).**

## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — The Word and Faith — Heb 4:2, 12

> A prophet's word carries God's own authority, but it profits nobody unless it is believed.
> The prophetic gift has always existed to produce faith.

[WB]: WORD → (mixed with) FAITH → REST · WORD → (unmixed) → wilderness

- _Heb 4:12._ "For the word of God [is] quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword" — the authority is in the word itself, not in the messenger
- _Heb 4:2._ "the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard [it]" — Israel had the prophet Moses and still died; the word was not the problem
- _Heb 4:3._ "For we which have believed do enter into rest" — the same word, believed, brings rest
- chain: 2 Chr 20:20 (believe God, believe His prophets) → Gen 15:6 (believed, counted righteous) → Rom 10:17 (faith comes by hearing the word) → Heb 11:6 (without faith, impossible)

[PIONEER Waggoner, EVCO 419.4] "We are in the world in precisely the same situation that ancient Israel was, with the same promises, the same prospects, the same enemies, the same dangers." — Heb 4 is about us

[PIONEER Jones, ARSH October 20, 1896, page 669.6] "From these scriptures it is plain that we are expected to look upon the word of God only as self-fulfilling. The word of God is self-fulfilling." — Heb 4:12: the power is in the word

[Q] Is "believe" enough, without obedience?
→ The faith the prophets asked for obeys — TM 91.2: righteousness "made manifest in obedience to all the commandments"; Gal 5:6

[SOP TM 91.2] "it invited the people to receive the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience to all the commandments of God." — the 1888 message in one sentence

[→] the week's question is not "Do prophets have authority?" but "Will I believe what they say?"

## 2. Sunday — Mediating God's Authority — Exod 4:10-16

> Moses had the best education in Egypt and still fled. In Midian he learned to trust God alone.
> A prophet is made by faith in God's "I will be with thy mouth," not by skill.

- _Exod 2:14._ "Who made thee a prince and a judge over us?" — Moses tried to deliver Israel by his own hand at forty, and it failed
- _Exod 4:12._ "Now therefore go, and I will be with thy mouth, and teach thee what thou shalt say." — the authority is God's, and Moses only carries it
- _Exod 4:16._ "he shall be to thee instead of a mouth, and thou shalt be to him instead of God" — with 7:1 ("Aaron thy brother shall be thy prophet"), the Bible defines a prophet: the mouth of another

| Moses' objection             | God's answer                         |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| "Who [am] I" (3:11)         | "Certainly I will be with thee" (3:12) |
| "What [is] his name?" (3:13) | "I AM hath sent me" (3:14)           |
| "I [am] not eloquent" (4:10) | "Who hath made man's mouth?" (4:11)  |
| "send … whom thou wilt" (4:13) | "I will be with thy mouth" (4:15)  |

[SOP PP 255.1] "The divine command given to Moses found him self-distrustful, slow of speech, and timid." — God did not choose a man who trusted himself

[SOP PP 255.1] "But having once accepted the work, he entered upon it with his whole heart, putting all his trust in the Lord." — trust turned the excuse into a calling

**Moses' authority rested on faith in Christ — Heb 11:24-27:**

- _Heb 11:26._ "Esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures in Egypt" — Moses' choice was a choice for Christ
- _Heb 11:27._ "he endured, as seeing him who is invisible" — what kept the prophet going

[SOP 5T 652.1] "He saw Jesus as his Saviour, and he believed that the Saviour’s merits would be imputed to him. This faith was to Moses no guesswork; it was a reality." — the first prophet was justified by faith

[PIONEER Smith, TBI 317.3] "In many respects Moses and Christ were unlike; but as prophets they were alike." — Deut 18:18: "put my words in his mouth"

## 3. Monday — The Authority of Jesus — Matt 21:23-25

> Asked where His authority came from, Jesus pointed to John the Baptist: "Why did ye not then believe him?"
> Rejecting a prophet's authority is always unbelief. Jesus used His authority to forgive and to judge.

- _Matt 21:23._ "By what authority doest thou these things? and who gave thee this authority?" — the rulers' question
- _Matt 21:25._ "If we shall say, From heaven; he will say unto us, Why did ye not then believe him?" — they could not answer, because the real issue was unbelief
- _Matt 7:29._ "he taught them as [one] having authority, and not as the scribes" — He did not lean on the rabbis' quotations

**What His authority does — refs:**

- _Mark 2:10._ "the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins" — His authority justifies sinners
- _John 5:24._ "He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation" — hear, believe, and you are not condemned
- _John 5:27._ "And hath given him authority to execute judgment also" — the Judge is the Saviour
- chain: John 17:2 (power over all flesh, to give eternal life) → Mark 1:27 (unclean spirits obey) → Luke 9:1 (He gives that authority to the Twelve) → Rev 19:10 (His testimony is the spirit of prophecy)

[SOP TMK 38.2] "His authority was as the authority of God." — not borrowed, as a prophet's is

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 8.2] "To Christ is committed the highest prerogative, that of judging." — this is the 1888 Christ, who is also the Judge

**The storm — Luke 8:22-25:**

- _Luke 8:25._ "And he said unto them, Where is your faith?" — He calms the storm and then asks about their faith

[SOP DA 336.1] "It was in faith—faith in God’s love and care—that Jesus rested, and the power of that word which stilled the storm was the power of God." — even Jesus lived by faith in the Father's word

[Q] If Christ has all authority, why do we need prophets at all?
→ Every true prophet carries Christ's own testimony — Rev 19:10; 1 Pet 1:11 ("the Spirit of Christ which was in them")

[PIONEER Prescott, CAS 9.2] "So it is Christ, it is Christ all the way through, Christ the agent in creation, Christ the spokesman for God, Christ whose Spirit inspired the prophets." — one Voice

[→] reject the prophet and you reject the Christ who speaks through him — Luke 16:31

## 5. Wednesday — Organizational Authority — Hos 12:13

> God led and kept His people through prophets. The first thing the prophet set in order was a lamb and its blood.
> Church order rests on faith in the blood, and Christ Himself is the Head.

- _Hos 12:13._ "And by a prophet the LORD brought Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet was he preserved." — the prophet led the deliverance and then kept the people
- _Exod 12:3._ "they shall take to them every man a lamb" — the first instruction given to the newly organized nation
- _Exod 12:13._ "when I see the blood, I will pass over you" — they were safe because of the blood, not because of their worth

[SOP SJ 18.4] "By His blood, through faith, we are redeemed." — what the Passover always meant

**Paul's discipline uses the same type — 1 Cor 5:1-8:**

- _1 Cor 5:5._ "To deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved" — the discipline aims at saving the man
- _1 Cor 5:7._ "Purge out therefore the old leaven, that ye may be a new lump, as ye are unleavened. For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us" — church order is built on the Lamb

**In a crisis the prophet's word was "believe" — 2 Chr 20:**

- _2 Chr 20:15._ "the battle [is] not yours, but God's" — Jahaziel, a Levite singer, given the Spirit in a crisis
- _2 Chr 20:17._ "stand ye [still], and see the salvation of the LORD" — salvation is God's work; Judah only had to stand
- chain: Judg 4:6-8 (Barak half-believes) → Deut 1:13 (wise men appointed) → Acts 6:3-7 (deacons; "obedient to the faith") → Titus 1:5 (elders) → Acts 13:2 (the Holy Ghost appoints)

[SOP PK 198.4] "Not by disciplined armies and fenced cities, but by a living faith in the God of Israel, could he hope to gain the victory" — Jehoshaphat

[SOP DG 37.4] "He accepted the message from Deborah as the word of God, but he had little confidence in Israel" — accepted the word but did not fully trust it, so the honor went to a woman

[SOP AA 28.2] "Christ’s name was to be their watchword, their badge of distinction, their bond of union, the authority for their course of action, and the source of their success." — the church's authority

[PIONEER Jones, PBE 90.1] "Thus Jesus Christ Himself, by the Spirit of prophecy, was the real Head of the schools of the prophets." — the gift keeps Christ as Head

[Q] Why church order if Christ is the Head?
→ He governs through order — 1 Cor 7:10 ("not I, but the Lord") vs 7:12 ("speak I, not the Lord"); Paul marks which is a command

[→] Israel was organized around the lamb, and so is the church — 1 Cor 5:7

## 6. Thursday — Canonical and Noncanonical Prophets — 2 Sam 12:13

> Nathan, Gad, Ahijah, Iddo, and John the Baptist wrote no book of the Bible, yet each spoke with full authority.
> Nathan told a guilty king that God had put away his sin, and John pointed to the Lamb.

- _1 Chr 29:29._ "the book of Nathan the prophet, and in the book of Gad the seer" — their books are lost, yet they were prophets
- _2 Chr 9:29._ "the prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite, and in the visions of Iddo the seer" — prophets outside the canon
- _1 Kgs 11:31._ "Take thee ten pieces: for thus saith the LORD" — Ahijah's authority was "thus saith the LORD", and it was fulfilled

**Nathan — 2 Sam 12:7-13:**

- _2 Sam 12:7._ "Thou [art] the man." — a prophet who wrote no Scripture convicts a king
- _2 Sam 12:13._ "I have sinned against the LORD. And Nathan said unto David, The LORD also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die." — a prophet with no book announces justification
- _Rom 4:6-8._ "David also describeth the blessedness of the man, unto whom God imputeth righteousness without works" — Paul draws justification by faith from this very story

[SOP 3BC 1146.3] "The blessing comes because of pardon; pardon comes through faith that the sin, confessed and repented of, is borne by the great Sin-bearer." — Nathan's message

[PIONEER Waggoner, GTI 123.1] "Christ bore the curse, in order that the blessing might come to us." — the blessing David describes

**John — the greatest prophet wrote no book — Luke 7:28:**

- _Luke 7:28._ "there is not a greater prophet than John the Baptist: but he that is least in the kingdom of God is greater than he" — his greatness was in what he pointed to
- _John 1:29._ "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world." — his whole message
- _Luke 7:29-30._ "And all the people that heard [him], and the publicans, justified God, being baptized with the baptism of John. But the Pharisees and lawyers rejected the counsel of God against themselves, being not baptized of him." — people's response to the prophet showed whether they believed

[SOP DA 220.1] "to John it was given to behold Him, to hear the testimony from heaven to His Messiahship, and to present Him to Israel as the Sent of God." — the prophet's whole job

[Q] Does a later prophet add to the Bible?
→ No. Isa 8:20 tests every prophet, and 3SM 29.2: "He has not given any additional light to take the place of his Word."

- chain: Joel 2:28 (sons and daughters shall prophesy) → Eph 4:11-13 (prophets "Till we all come … unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ") → Col 1:27 (Christ in you) → Mal 4:5-6 / Luke 1:17 ("a people prepared for the Lord")

[SOP 4BC 1184.6] "God calls for men who will prepare a people to stand in the great day of the Lord." — the Elijah work today

[→] the canon decides where a prophet's writing goes, but its authority comes from inspiration

## 7. Friday — Further Thought — Speaking With Authority — Rev 12:17

> The last church has two marks: the commandments, and the testimony of Jesus.
> Rev 14:12 restates them as the commandments and the faith of Jesus. The gift comes with the message of Christ our righteousness.

- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ" — the last church
- _Rev 19:10._ "the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy" — the Bible defines the term itself (Miller's Rule 5)
- _Rev 14:12._ "here [are] they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" — the same people, described by their faith

## 8. Appeal

- Every prophet this week said the same thing: "believe." Moses, Jahaziel, Nathan, John, and the remnant's gift all point to Christ our righteousness
- The word that has cut you this week was meant to heal you. Do not hide from it as Jehoiakim did; tear your heart open as Josiah did and look to the Lamb
- Christ, who holds all authority, is your High Priest in the heavenly sanctuary right now. Come boldly to Him
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper."
- _Heb 4:14._ "Seeing then that we have a great high priest, that is passed into the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast [our] profession."
- _Heb 3:7-8._ "To day if ye will hear his voice, Harden not your hearts" — decide today

[SOP UL 337.6] "Believe, oh, believe the Word of the Lord, and walk by faith, not by sight." — the week's last word
