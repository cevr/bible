# Sabbath School — Q4 W10 (2026)

## Believe His Prophets: The Word That Cuts Is the Word That Saves

_Quarterly: Prophetic Authority and Relevance · November 28–December 4 · Read: Exod. 4:10–16; Luke 8:22–25; John 3:1–10; Acts 16:25–34; 1 Cor. 5:1–5; Luke 7:28_

**Memory Verse:** — _Hebrews 4:12._ "For the word of God [is] quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and [is] a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart."

**Theme: Every true prophet speaks Christ's own word, and that word asks one thing of us — believe it — because the word that cuts the heart open is the word that counts the believer righteous.**

## Open

- **Say:** Israel had a prophet, and still died in the wilderness. This week asks why the prophet's word did them no good, and what makes it do us good.
- _Heb 4:12._ "For the word of God [is] quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword" — the authority is in the word itself, not in the messenger
- _Heb 4:2._ "the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard [it]" — Israel had Moses and still fell; the word was not the problem
- _Heb 4:3._ "For we which have believed do enter into rest" — the same word, believed, brings rest
- [RQ] Why did the word preached not profit Israel?
  → "not being mixed with faith in them that heard [it]" — Heb 4:2

---

## 1. A Prophet Is a Mouth God Fills — Exod 4:12

- **Say:** Moses failed by his own hand at forty. God made him a prophet only when he trusted the One who would be with his mouth.
- _Exod 2:14._ "Who made thee a prince and a judge over us?" — Moses' first attempt, in his own strength, failed
- _Exod 4:12._ "Now therefore go, and I will be with thy mouth, and teach thee what thou shalt say." — the authority is God's; Moses only carries it
- _Exod 4:16._ "he shall be to thee instead of a mouth, and thou shalt be to him instead of God" — the Bible's own definition of a prophet: the mouth of another
- _Heb 11:26._ "Esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures in Egypt" — Moses' choice was a choice for Christ
- [RQ] Who supplies what the prophet says?
  → God: "I will be with thy mouth, and teach thee what thou shalt say" — Exod 4:12
- [RQ] What did Moses value above Egypt's treasures?
  → "the reproach of Christ" — Heb 11:26

[SOP 5T 652.1] "He saw Jesus as his Saviour, and he believed that the Saviour’s merits would be imputed to him." — the first prophet was justified by faith

- **Bridge:** If God fills the prophet's mouth, whose voice is it? The rulers asked Jesus that very question.

---

## 2. The Voice in Every Prophet Is Christ's — Matt 21:25

- **Say:** Asked for His authority, Jesus pointed to John the Baptist. Rejecting a prophet is never a question of credentials; it is unbelief toward Christ, who speaks through them all.
- _Matt 21:23._ "By what authority doest thou these things? and who gave thee this authority?" — the rulers' question
- _Matt 21:25._ "If we shall say, From heaven; he will say unto us, Why did ye not then believe him?" — the real issue was unbelief
- _Mark 2:10._ "the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins" — His authority justifies sinners
- _Rev 19:10._ "the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy" — the Bible defines the term itself
- [RQ] What did Jesus say the rulers' real problem was?
  → "Why did ye not then believe him?" — Matt 21:25
- [RQ] Whose testimony is the spirit of prophecy?
  → "the testimony of Jesus" — Rev 19:10

[PIONEER Prescott, CAS 9.2] "So it is Christ, it is Christ all the way through, Christ the agent in creation, Christ the spokesman for God, Christ whose Spirit inspired the prophets." — one Voice

- **Bridge:** Christ's word through the prophets does two things to a hearer: it cuts, and it saves.

---

## 3. The Word That Cuts Is the Word That Saves — Acts 16:30-31

- **Say:** The written word either breaks a heart or gets cut up and burned. Its cut is meant to heal: it shows the sinner his need, then says "believe."

| Jehoiakim — Jer 36:23-24   | Josiah — 2 Kgs 22:11-13 |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| "cut it with the penknife" | "he rent his clothes"   |
| "were not afraid"          | "great [is] the wrath"  |
| burns the word             | "enquire of the LORD"   |

- _John 3:3._ "Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God." — said to a ruler who kept the law
- _John 3:14-15._ "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up: That whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life." — the answer to "How can these things be?" is look and live
- _Acts 16:30-31._ "Sirs, what must I do to be saved? And they said, Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house." — the jailer gets the same answer as the ruler
- [RQ] What must a man do with the lifted-up Son of man?
  → Believe in Him: "whosoever believeth in him should not perish" — John 3:15
- [RQ] What did Paul answer to "what must I do to be saved?"
  → "Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved" — Acts 16:31

[PIONEER Jones, ARSH December 27, 1898, page 832.1] "FAITH is the expecting the word of God to do what it says, and the depending upon that word to do what it says." — the jailer's "believe," defined

- **Bridge:** Even prophets who never wrote a book of the Bible carried this same word of pardon.

## 4. Prophets With No Book Still Preach the Lamb — 2 Sam 12:13

- **Say:** Nathan and John the Baptist wrote no book of Scripture, yet each spoke with full authority, and each announced the same thing: pardon through the Sin-bearer.
- _2 Sam 12:7._ "Thou [art] the man." — a prophet who wrote no Scripture convicts a king
- _2 Sam 12:13._ "I have sinned against the LORD. And Nathan said unto David, The LORD also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die." — a prophet with no book announces justification
- _Rom 4:6-8._ "David also describeth the blessedness of the man, unto whom God imputeth righteousness without works" — Paul draws justification by faith from this very story
- _Luke 7:28._ "there is not a greater prophet than John the Baptist: but he that is least in the kingdom of God is greater than he" — the greatest prophet wrote no book
- _John 1:29._ "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world." — his whole message
- [RQ] What did Nathan say the moment David confessed?
  → "The LORD also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die." — 2 Sam 12:13
- [RQ] Where did the greatest prophet point?
  → "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world." — John 1:29

[SOP 3BC 1146.3] "The blessing comes because of pardon; pardon comes through faith that the sin, confessed and repented of, is borne by the great Sin-bearer." — Nathan's message

- **Bridge:** God promised the last church this same gift, joined to the same message.

## 5. The Remnant: The Commandments and the Faith of Jesus — Rev 14:12

- **Say:** By a prophet God brought Israel out and kept them. The last church has the same gift, and Rev 14:12 shows what it produces: a people who keep the commandments by the faith of Jesus.
- _Hos 12:13._ "And by a prophet the LORD brought Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet was he preserved." — the prophet led the deliverance and then kept the people
- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ" — the last church
- _Rev 14:12._ "here [are] they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" — the same people, described by their faith

| Rev 12:17            | Rev 14:12              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------- |
| commandments of God  | commandments of God    |
| testimony of Jesus   | faith of Jesus         |
| = spirit of prophecy | = third angel's people |

- [RQ] Rev 14:12 keeps "the commandments of God." What stands where Rev 12:17 had "the testimony of Jesus"?
  → "the faith of Jesus" — Rev 14:12
- [RQ] What did God do for Israel by a prophet, besides bringing them out?
  → "by a prophet was he preserved" — Hos 12:13

[SOP TM 91.2] "it invited the people to receive the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience to all the commandments of God." — the third angel's message in verity

- **Bridge:** The gift was given to make a people who believe God. So the last question is whether we will believe.

## Decision

- **Say:** The word has cut you this week so that it can heal you. Do not burn it like Jehoiakim; rend your heart like Josiah, and believe the Christ who speaks in every prophet. He is your High Priest now.
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the prophet's one demand
- _Heb 4:14._ "Seeing then that we have a great high priest, that is passed into the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast [our] profession." — the cut leads to the throne of grace
- _Heb 3:7-8._ "To day if ye will hear his voice, Harden not your hearts" — decide today
- [RQ] What does God ask of you today, when you hear His voice?
  → Believe, and "Harden not your hearts" — 2 Chr 20:20; Heb 3:7-8

[SOP UL 337.6] "Believe, oh, believe the Word of the Lord, and walk by faith, not by sight." — the week's last word

## If asked

- [Q] If Christ has all authority, why do we need prophets at all?
  → Every true prophet carries Christ's own testimony — Rev 19:10; 1 Pet 1:11 ("the Spirit of Christ which was in them")
- [Q] Does a later prophet add to the Bible?
  → No. Isa 8:20 tests every prophet, and 3SM 29.2: "He has not given any additional light to take the place of his Word."
- [Q] Is "believe" enough, without obedience?
  → The faith the prophets asked for obeys — Rev 14:12 (commandments + faith of Jesus); Gal 5:6; TM 91.2
