# Sabbath School — Q4 W8 (2026)

## Christ, the Key to All the Scriptures

_Quarterly: Interpreting Prophetic Writings · November 14–20 · Read: Matt. 5:17–48; 1 Cor. 16:20; Matt. 4:1–11; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 24:25–27, 44–49_

**Memory Verse:** — _Luke 24:27._ "And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself."

**Theme: Scripture is its own interpreter, and read by faith every line of it opens one thing: Christ our righteousness.**

## Open

- **Say:** Two disciples had the right Bible and a broken heart. What did they lack, and what did Christ give them?
- _Luke 24:27._ "And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself." — every part of Scripture, one subject
- [RQ] According to this verse, what is "all the scriptures" about?
  → "the things concerning himself": Christ

---

## 1. Slow of Heart to Believe — Luke 24:25-27

- **Say:** The Emmaus pair weren't short on texts. They were short on faith, so they read the crown into the prophets and left out the cross.
- _Luke 24:21._ "But we trusted that it had been he which should have redeemed Israel" — they read the crown into the text and left out the cross
- _Luke 24:25._ "O fools, and slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken:" — the diagnosis is unbelief, not ignorance (Miller's Rule 14)
- _Luke 24:32._ "Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way, and while he opened to us the scriptures?" — Christ from Scripture heals the heart
- [RQ] What did Jesus say was wrong with them?
  → "slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken" (24:25)
- [RQ] What did He do to cure it?
  → He "opened to us the scriptures" (24:32), not a miracle, but the Word

[SOP DA 796.4] "Christ performed no miracle to convince them, but it was His first work to explain the Scriptures." — the risen Lord taught Bible study first

- **Bridge:** If Christ expounded Moses and the prophets, what did He say Moses and the prophets were for?

## 2. The Law and the Prophets Witness His Righteousness — Matt 5:17-18; Rom 3:21

- **Say:** Christ didn't replace the Old Testament; He filled it up. And the thing the law and the prophets witness to is the righteousness of God, given by faith.
- _Matt 5:17._ "Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil." — "fulfil" = fill up the measure, not cancel
- _Matt 5:18._ "one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled." — Miller's proof text for Rule 1
- _Rom 3:21._ "the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets" — the prophets' theme IS righteousness by faith
- _Rom 3:22._ "Even the righteousness of God [which is] by faith of Jesus Christ unto all and upon all them that believe" — given, not earned
- [RQ] Did Jesus come to destroy the law or the prophets?
  → "I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil" (Matt 5:17)
- [RQ] What do the law and the prophets witness to?
  → "the righteousness of God," which is "by faith of Jesus Christ" (Rom 3:21-22)

[SOP 2SM 104.2] "The Old Testament is the gospel in figures and symbols. The New Testament is the substance. One is as essential as the other." — one gospel, two Testaments

- **Bridge:** One enemy reads Scripture to a different end. Watch how he handles it.

## 3. "It Is Written Again" — Matt 4:6-7

- **Say:** Satan quotes Scripture by cutting it. Christ answers by setting Scripture beside Scripture, and He overcomes as a man by faith in the Word alone.
- _Matt 4:6._ "for it is written, He shall give his angels charge concerning thee" — Ps 91:11 cut short: "to keep thee in all thy ways" left out
- _Matt 4:7._ "Jesus said unto him, It is written again, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God." — Scripture set beside Scripture
- _Matt 4:4._ "It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God." — every word, not half a verse
- [RQ] How did Jesus answer a verse pulled out of context?
  → "It is written again" (4:7): another Scripture
- [RQ] What does He say a man lives by?
  → "every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God" (4:4)

[WB]: Satan vs Christ

- **Bridge:** Christ handled the Word by faith. Now He hands the Word to a lawyer and asks how he reads it.

## 4. "How Readest Thou?" — Luke 10:26-28; Matt 5:48

- **Say:** The lawyer read the law correctly and couldn't keep it. "This do, and live" drives the sinner to the One who did it, and "be perfect" turns out to be a promise of the Father's love.
- _Luke 10:26._ "He said unto him, What is written in the law? how readest thou?" — Christ sends him back to the text
- _Luke 10:28._ "And he said unto him, Thou hast answered right: this do, and thou shalt live." — the right answer, and an impossible demand for the flesh (Rom 8:3)
- _Luke 10:29._ "But he, willing to justify himself" — the wrong reaction to the law: self-justification, not faith
- _Matt 5:48._ "Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect." — "therefore" points back to 5:45, sun on the evil and the good
- _Luke 6:36._ "Be ye therefore merciful, as your Father also is merciful." — the parallel shows "perfect" means love like the Father's (Rule 4)
- _Rom 8:4._ "That the righteousness of the law might be fulfilled in us, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit." — fulfilled in us, not by us
- [RQ] What did the lawyer do with the law's answer?
  → "willing to justify himself" (Luke 10:29), he tried to make himself right
- [RQ] How does the parallel in Luke explain "perfect"?
  → "merciful, as your Father also is merciful" (Luke 6:36)

[SOP MB 76.2] "In every command or injunction that God gives there is a promise, the most positive, underlying the command." — every command is a promise

- **Bridge:** Christ didn't only explain righteousness. The prophets dated when He would bring it in.

## 5. Everlasting Righteousness, on Time — Mark 1:15; Dan 9:24

- **Say:** Christ opened His ministry with a time prophecy. The seventy weeks were given "to bring in everlasting righteousness," and they key the 2300 days to the most holy place.
- _Mark 1:15._ "And saying, The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe the gospel." — "the time" = Dan 9's 490 years
- _Dan 9:24._ "Seventy weeks are determined upon thy people and upon thy holy city, to finish the transgression, and to make an end of sins, and to make reconciliation for iniquity, and to bring in everlasting righteousness, and to seal up the vision and prophecy, and to anoint the most Holy." — righteousness by faith dated in prophecy
- [RQ] What did Jesus say was fulfilled when He began to preach?
  → "The time is fulfilled" (Mark 1:15)
- [RQ] What were the seventy weeks to bring in?
  → "everlasting righteousness" (Dan 9:24)

[WB]: 457 BC → AD 27 (baptism) → AD 31 (cross) → AD 34 → 1844 (most holy)

[PIONEER Smith, DAR 193.3] "Everlasting righteousness was to be brought in; the righteousness which our Lord manifested in his sinless life." — the robe He wove

- **Bridge:** The same Word that dates 1844 dates "everlasting righteousness." Believe the prophecy, and you must believe the promise.

## Decision

- **Say:** Christ is the key to every page. He offers the righteousness the whole Bible witnesses to; He asks us to believe it, not to earn it.
- _Luke 24:45._ "Then opened he their understanding, that they might understand the scriptures," — ask Him for this
- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — the prophets' message has always been "believe God"
- _Rev 14:12._ "Here is the patience of the saints: here [are] they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." — the people the prophetic word produces
- [RQ] What did God count to Abraham for righteousness?
  → that "he believed in the LORD" (Gen 15:6)

[SOP 1SM 372.2] "Several have written to me, inquiring if the message of justification by faith is the third angel’s message, and I have answered, “It is the third angel’s message, in verity.”" — present truth
