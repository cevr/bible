## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — The Key That Unlocks Disappointment — Luke 24:25-27

> The Emmaus pair had the right texts and the wrong event. Their problem wasn't learning; it was unbelief of "all that the prophets have spoken". The cure was Christ, expounded from Scripture.

- _Luke 24:25._ "O fools, and slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken:" — the diagnosis is unbelief, not ignorance (Miller's Rule 14)
- _Luke 24:21._ "But we trusted that it had been he which should have redeemed Israel" — they read the crown into the text and left out the cross
- chain: Luke 24:27 (Moses + all the prophets) → Luke 24:32 (hearts burn) → Luke 24:45 (understanding opened)

[SOP DA 796.4] "Christ performed no miracle to convince them, but it was His first work to explain the Scriptures." — the risen Lord taught Bible study first

[WB]: Emmaus | 1844 — the same pattern twice

| | Emmaus (AD 31) | Advent people (1844) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expected | throne, Rome broken | Christ to earth |
| Text right? | yes, the prophets | yes, Dan 8:14 |
| Event wrong | crown before cross | earth as sanctuary |
| Key | Christ from Scripture | Christ in most holy place |

[SOP GC 423.1] "Though, like the first disciples, they themselves had failed to understand the message which they bore, yet it had been in every respect correct." — the time held; the event was corrected

[SOP GC 354.1] "the danger of accepting the theories and interpretations of men, instead of making the Bible its own interpreter." — what the disappointment taught

[PIONEER Miller, MWV1 22.8] "The most important rule of all is, that you must have faith." — Rule 14: the method begins with believing God

[Q] They were wrong in 1844, so why trust the method?
→ the reckoning held and only the event was misread (GC 424.1); the cure was more Scripture, not less (Rev 10:9-11, Dan 8:14)

[→] GC 594.1: the disciples' blindness is our danger now, because the prophecies of the close of probation are just as plain

## 2. Sunday — The Bible, Its Own Interpreter — Matt 5:17-18

> Christ didn't replace Moses; He expounded him. He "magnified the law" by opening its heart, the same law the new covenant writes on the believer's heart by faith.

- _Matt 5:17._ "Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil." — "fulfil" = fill up the measure, not cancel
- _Matt 5:18._ "one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled." — Miller's proof text for Rule 1
- chain: Isa 42:21 (magnify the law) → Matt 5:21-22 (kill → anger) → Matt 5:27-28 (adultery → heart) → Jer 31:33 (law in inward parts) → Heb 8:10 (same promise, NT)
- _Rom 3:21._ "the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets" — the prophets' theme IS righteousness by faith

[PIONEER Miller, MWV1 20.2] "Every word must have its proper bearing on the subject presented in the Bible." — Rule 1, proved by Matt 5:18 (MWV1 20.3)

[PIONEER Miller, MWV1 20.10] "Scripture must be its own expositor, since it is a rule of itself." — Rule 5

[PIONEER Miller, MWV1 20.10] "If I depend on a teacher to expound it to me, and he should guess at its meaning" — then his guess becomes my rule

[SOP FE 187.2] "The Bible is its own expositor. One passage will prove to be a key that will unlock other passages" — the method in one line

[SOP MB 48.3] "to fill up the measure of the law’s requirement, to give an example of perfect conformity to the will of God." — Christ's "fulfil"

[SOP 2SM 104.2] "The Old Testament is the gospel in figures and symbols. The New Testament is the substance. One is as essential as the other." — one gospel, two Testaments

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 61.1] "God puts His righteousness upon the believer. He covers him with it, so that his sin no more appears." — what the law and prophets witness to (Rom 3:21)

[Q] Don't we need scholars to tell us what it means?
→ Rule 5: Scripture expounds Scripture (Isa 28:10; 2 Pet 1:20); "The entrance of thy words giveth light" (Ps 119:130; GC 320.1)

[→] Dan 2 interprets Dan 2 (2:28, 2:45), and the whole Bible works the same way

## 3. Monday — Let the History Fulfill Every Word — Isa 65:17-20; Isa 45:13

> Historical setting isn't a separate key. Bring all the scriptures together (Rule 4), then test the event by every word (Rule 13). Where Israel's history fails a word, look for another event.

- _Isa 65:20._ "There shall be no more thence an infant of days, nor an old man that hath not filled his days: for the child shall die an hundred years old; but the sinner [being] an hundred years old shall be accursed." — long life is plain sense, not a symbol (Rule 11)
- chain: Isa 65:19 (no more weeping) → Isa 25:8 (death swallowed up) → Isa 26:19 (dead arise) → Rev 21:4 ("no more death")
- _Jer 18:10._ "If it do evil in my sight, that it obey not my voice, then I will repent of the good, wherewith I said I would benefit them." — the promises to the nation were conditional (18:9)

[WB]: Rule 13 test on Isa 65:20

| Word | Restored Israel | Final new earth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| death at 100 | fits | ✗ Rev 21:4 |
| sinner accursed | fits | ✗ no sinner |
| new heavens | foretaste | full (Rev 21:1) |

[PIONEER Miller, MWV1 22.3] "If it makes good sense as it stands, and does no violence to the simple laws of nature, then it must be understood literally" — Rule 11

[PIONEER Miller, MWV1 22.6] "But if one word lacks a fulfilment, then you must look for another event, or wait its future development." — Rule 13 settles Isa 65:20

[SOP PK 704.1] "These promises were conditional on obedience." — Israel could have had Isa 65:20 and forfeited it

[SOP PK 713.1] "That which God purposed to do for the world through Israel, the chosen nation, He will finally accomplish through His church on earth today." — the promise passes to faith's Israel

**Cyrus: every word fulfilled, and it carries a promise of righteousness:**

- _Isa 45:13._ "I have raised him up in righteousness, and I will direct all his ways: he shall build my city, and he shall let go my captives" — named a century early, fulfilled to the letter (2 Chr 36:22-23)
- _Isa 45:25._ "In the LORD shall all the seed of Israel be justified, and shall glory." — the same chapter promises justification (45:24 "in the LORD have I righteousness")
- 1 Cor 16:20 "an holy kiss" → 1 Pet 5:14 "a kiss of charity": Scripture names the principle, and the principle is love (Rom 13:10)

[SOP PK 552.1] "the Jews had abundant evidence of the literal fulfillment of Isaiah’s prophecy concerning the sudden overthrow of their oppressors." — kept history guarantees the kept promise

## 4. Tuesday — "It Is Written Again" — Matt 4:5-7

> Satan quotes Scripture by cutting it, both in Eden and in the wilderness. Christ answers with "It is written again," which is Rule 4 in action, and He wins as a man by faith in a "Thus saith the Lord".

- _Gen 3:1._ "Yea, hath God said, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden?" — he stretches the one prohibition over every tree
- _Gen 3:4._ "Ye shall not surely die:" — then he flatly reverses Gen 2:17
- _Matt 4:6._ "for it is written, He shall give his angels charge concerning thee" — Ps 91:11 cut short: "to keep thee in all thy ways" left out
- _Matt 4:7._ "Jesus said unto him, It is written again, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God." — Scripture set beside Scripture

| | Satan's method | Christ's method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text | half a verse | "written again" |
| Promise | excuses sin | path of duty |
| Root | presumption | faith |

[SOP Mar 132.4] "some will seize upon passages of Scripture separated from the context, perhaps quoting half of a single verse as proving their point" — still in use

[SOP DA 120.1] "a firm reliance upon a “Thus saith the Lord,” was a sign that could not be controverted." — how Christ overcame

[SOP DA 126.1] "Faith claims God’s promises, and brings forth fruit in obedience. Presumption also claims the promises, but uses them as Satan did, to excuse transgression." — faith vs cheap grace

[PIONEER Jones, ARSH December 27, 1898, page 832.1] "FAITH is the expecting the word of God to do what it says, and the depending upon that word to do what it says." — "It is written" is faith: Christ leaned on the word alone

[SOP GC 451.2] "These words apply in the Christian age, as shown by the context" — reading in context keeps the Sabbath

## 5. Wednesday — "How Readest Thou?" — Luke 10:25-28; Matt 5:48

> The lawyer read the law correctly and couldn't keep it. "This do and live" sends the sinner to the One who did it. "Be perfect" is explained by its parallel as the Father's love, and that love is given, not achieved.

- _Luke 10:26._ "He said unto him, What is written in the law? how readest thou?" — Christ sends him back to the text
- _Luke 10:28._ "And he said unto him, Thou hast answered right: this do, and thou shalt live." — the right answer, and an impossible demand for the flesh (Rom 8:3)
- _Luke 10:29._ "But he, willing to justify himself" — the wrong reaction to the law: self-justification, not faith

[SOP DA 498.2] "The lawyer found himself a lawbreaker." — the law did its work (Rom 3:20-22)

**Parallel defines figure, Rule 4:**

- _Matt 5:48._ "Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect." — "therefore" points back to 5:45, sun on the evil and the good
- _Luke 6:36._ "Be ye therefore merciful, as your Father also is merciful." — the parallel shows "perfect" means love like the Father's
- chain: Matt 5:44 (love enemies) → Rom 13:10 (love fulfils law) → Rom 8:4 (righteousness of law "fulfilled in us")

[SOP MB 76.1] "because He has stooped to lift you up, therefore, said Jesus, you may become like Him in character" — perfection rests on grace

[SOP MB 76.2] "In every command or injunction that God gives there is a promise, the most positive, underlying the command." — every command is a promise

[PIONEER Jones, CWCP 84.2] "the High Priesthood and ministry of Christ in the true sanctuary is the only way by which any soul can attain this true goal" — perfection comes through the most holy place

[Q] Isn't "be ye perfect" impossible?
→ for the flesh, yes (Rom 8:3); "fulfilled in us" by the Spirit (Rom 8:4); Christ did it in our flesh (CWCP 84.1)

[→] "How readest thou?" ends where Luke 24 ends, at Christ, who "fulfils" (Matt 5:17)

## 6. Thursday — The Time Is Fulfilled — Mark 1:15; Dan 9:24

> Christ preached as a historicist. His gospel opened with a time prophecy, the seventy weeks, whose purpose was to "bring in everlasting righteousness" and to key the 2300 days to the most holy place.

- _Mark 1:15._ "And saying, The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe the gospel." — "the time" = Dan 9's 490 years
- _Dan 9:24._ "Seventy weeks are determined upon thy people and upon thy holy city, to finish the transgression, and to make an end of sins, and to make reconciliation for iniquity, and to bring in everlasting righteousness, and to seal up the vision and prophecy, and to anoint the most Holy." — righteousness by faith dated in prophecy

[SOP DA 233.1] "Thus the gospel message, as given by the Saviour Himself, was based on the prophecies." — Christ preached prophecy as gospel

[WB]: 457 BC → AD 27 (baptism) → AD 31 (cross) → AD 34 → 1844 (most holy)

[PIONEER Miller, MWV2 62.2] "evidently showing, that by Christ’s obedience unto death, he brought in everlasting righteousness." — Miller preached RBF from Dan 9

[PIONEER Smith, DAR 193.3] "Everlasting righteousness was to be brought in; the righteousness which our Lord manifested in his sinless life." — the robe He wove

[PIONEER Smith, DAR 193.3] "Thus the events of the seventy weeks furnish a key to the whole vision." — Dan 9 dates Dan 8:14

**Christ and Philip expound the same way:**

- _Luke 4:21._ "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears." — Isa 61:1-2 applied to Himself
- _Acts 8:35._ "Then Philip opened his mouth, and began at the same scripture, and preached unto him Jesus." — Isa 53 read plainly means Christ
- _Luke 24:47._ "repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name among all nations" — what exposition is for

[SOP DA 237.3] "Jesus stood before the people as a living expositor of the prophecies concerning Himself." — the model preacher

- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, [it is] because [there is] no light in them." — the test applies to every prophet, EGW included
- chain: Rev 12:17 (commandments + testimony) → Rev 19:10 (testimony = spirit of prophecy) → 2 Chr 20:20 ("believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper")
