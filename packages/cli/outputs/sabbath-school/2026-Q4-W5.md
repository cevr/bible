## Open

- **Say:** This week God speaks in many ways: by a Son, by a breath, by a voice, by a pen, by a sign. What is He saying every time?
- _Rev 1:1._ "The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him, to shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass" — given to be _shown_, not hidden
- [RQ] Whose revelation is the last book of the Bible?
  → "The Revelation of Jesus Christ" (Rev 1:1): Christ is the One revealing and the One revealed

## 1. God's Last Word Is a Son Who Purged Our Sins — Heb 1:1-3

- **Say:** Nature shows God's power and leaves us guilty. God's fullest word is a Person, and the first thing said of His work is that He purged our sins.
- _Heb 1:1, 2._ "God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by [his] Son" — many ways, one Speaker
- _Heb 1:3._ "upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins" — the Creator-Word is the Sin-purger
- _Rom 1:20._ "his eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse" — nature leaves man guilty, not saved
- _Rom 1:17._ "therein is the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith" — only the gospel reveals righteousness
- [RQ] What does nature leave a man, and where is the righteousness of God revealed?
  → "without excuse" (Rom 1:20); righteousness is revealed in the gospel, "from faith to faith" (Rom 1:17)
- [RQ] What had the Son done when He sat down?
  → "by himself purged our sins" (Heb 1:3), by the same word that upholds all things

[WB]: NATURE → without excuse · SON → purged our sins

[PIONEER Waggoner, GCDB March 11, 1891, page 75.13] "[Christ,] who upholds all things by the word of his power, can by that same word create in me a clean heart" — the creating Word recreates

- **Bridge:** If the Son is God's word, how did that word come to us on a page?

## 2. Breathed to Make Us Wise unto Salvation — 2 Tim 3:15-17

- **Say:** Scripture is God-breathed for one purpose: to make a sinner wise unto salvation through faith in Christ, and then furnish him for good works.
- _2 Tim 3:15._ "the holy scriptures, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus." — the goal: faith in Christ
- _2 Tim 3:16._ "All scripture [is] given by inspiration of God, and [is] profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness" — all, not parts
- _2 Tim 3:17._ "That the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works." — faith that works
- [RQ] What are the Scriptures able to make you, and by what means?
  → "wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus" (v. 15)
- [RQ] Does "instruction in righteousness" come before faith or after it?
  → after: it furnishes "the man of God" (v. 17), one already made wise "through faith" (v. 15)

[PIONEER Waggoner, PTUK October 24, 1895, page 674.3] "Whoever reads the words of the apostles and prophets as the Word of God, finds Christ." — the point of inspiration

- **Bridge:** Some prophets never wrote a line. Did their spoken word carry the same power?

## 3. A Spoken Word That Forgives on the Spot — 2 Sam 12:13

- **Say:** Prophets who never wrote spoke with the same authority, and their burden was "believe God." One spoken sentence from God justified David where he stood.
- _2 Pet 1:21._ "holy men of God spake [as they were] moved by the Holy Ghost." — oral or written, one Source
- _Heb 11:7._ "By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith." — no book, full authority
- _2 Sam 12:7._ "Thou [art] the man." — oral rebuke
- _2 Sam 12:13._ "I have sinned against the LORD. And Nathan said unto David, The LORD also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die." — justified by a spoken word
- [RQ] What did Noah become heir of by believing the warning?
  → "the righteousness which is by faith" (Heb 11:7)
- [RQ] When David confessed, how long did he wait for pardon?
  → not at all: "The LORD also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die" (2 Sam 12:13), on the spot

[PIONEER Jones, ARSH November 10, 1896, page 717.6] "Will you be made righteous by it [the word of God] now? Will you live by it now? This is justification by faith. This is righteousness by faith." — 1888 voice

- **Bridge:** A spoken word reaches one room. God had His word written so later generations could believe it too.

## 4. Written So That We Might Believe and Live — Hab 2:2-4

- **Say:** God put the word in writing so that people not yet born could believe and live. The vision He told Habakkuk to write is one sentence: the just shall live by faith.
- _Exod 17:14._ "Write this [for] a memorial in a book, and rehearse [it] in the ears of Joshua" — first command to write; the victory kept for the next generation
- _Hab 2:2._ "Write the vision, and make [it] plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it." — the Millerite chart text (GC 392.2)
- _Hab 2:4._ "the just shall live by his faith." — the content of the vision
- _John 20:31._ "But these are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing ye might have life through his name." — written → believe → life
- [RQ] What was the vision Habakkuk was told to make plain?
  → "the just shall live by his faith" (Hab 2:4)
- [RQ] Why did John write his gospel?
  → "that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ," and believing, "have life through his name" (John 20:31)

[PIONEER Prescott, GCB April 8, 1901, page 130.9] "the vision was just this one thing, The just shall live by faith. This is the key to all the visions; this is the key to all the prophets" — the key

- **Bridge:** When the spoken and written word was mocked, God acted it out.

## 5. A Sign That Pointed to the Sin-bearer — Ezek 4:4-6

- **Say:** Israel mocked the messengers, so God had Ezekiel act the message out. The prophet lay under Israel's iniquity as a sign; the Servant of Isaiah 53 bears it in fact, and justifies many.
- _2 Chr 36:16._ "they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused his prophets" — why the drama: words were mocked
- _Ezek 4:4._ "Lie thou also upon thy left side, and lay the iniquity of the house of Israel upon it" — 390 days
- _Ezek 4:6._ "thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: I have appointed thee each day for a year." — the key, stated by God
- _Isa 53:11._ "my righteous servant justify many; for he shall bear their iniquities." — the prophet only signified iniquity-bearing; the Servant actually bore it
- [RQ] What did God lay on Ezekiel as he lay on his side?
  → "the iniquity of the house of Israel" (Ezek 4:4), a day for each year (v. 6)
- [RQ] Who carries that iniquity in fact, and what follows?
  → the "righteous servant": "he shall bear their iniquities," and so he shall "justify many" (Isa 53:11)
- chain: Ezek 4:6 (day = year) → Dan 8:14 (2300 days) → Heb 8:1-2 (Minister of the true tabernacle) → Rev 14:7 (hour of his judgment)

[PIONEER Miller, WMAD 11.1] "as God in Numbers 14:34, and Ezekiel 4:4-6, had appointed each day for a year; as the seventy weeks to the Messiah were fulfilled in 490 years" — the key to Dan 8:14

- **Bridge:** The Son, the breath, the voice, the pen and the sign have all said one thing. What will you do with it?

## Decision

- **Say:** Every way God spoke this week said one thing: believe Him, and His word makes you righteous. He asks you to take His word now, and let it do what it says.
- _Rev 1:3._ "Blessed [is] he that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy, and keep those things which are written therein: for the time [is] at hand." — read, hear, keep: faith that obeys
- [RQ] When God's word says "the LORD also hath put away thy sin," what does He ask of you?
  → to believe it: "believing ye might have life through his name" (John 20:31), as David took Nathan's word (2 Sam 12:13)

[PIONEER Jones, PTUK June 6, 1895, page 356.1] "To expect the word of God to do the thing which that word says, and to depend wholly upon that word itself to do it" — faith as Christ defined it (Matt 8:8-10)
