## Revelation and Inspiration

_Week of October 24–30 · Read: Heb 1:1, 2; 2 Tim 3:15–17; 2 Pet 1:18–21; Exod 17:14; Rev 1:19; Ezek 4:1–8_

## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — Rev 1:1-3

> The last book is not "of St. John" but "of Jesus Christ": Christ is both the One revealing and the One revealed. Every revelation is a revelation of Him.

[WB]: God → Christ → angel → John → servants

- _Rev 1:1._ "The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him, to shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass" — given to be _shown_, not hidden
- _Rev 1:3._ "Blessed [is] he that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy, and keep those things which are written therein: for the time [is] at hand." — read, hear, keep: faith that obeys
- _Rev 1:9._ "for the word of God, and for the testimony of Jesus Christ" — why John was on Patmos; same pair as v. 2
- chain: Deut 29:29 (revealed things belong to us) → Amos 3:7 (secret revealed to prophets) → Rev 14:12 (commandments + faith of Jesus)

[PIONEER Smith, DAR 323.3] "Jesus Christ is the Revelator, not John. John is but the penman employed by Christ to write out this Revelation for the benefit of his church." — pioneer reads the title as written

[SOP AA 584.1] "The Lord Himself revealed to His servant the mysteries contained in this book, and He designs that they shall be open to the study of all." — open, not sealed

[Q] Isn't Revelation a sealed book?
→ the title answers it — Rev 1:1; Rev 22:10 "Seal not the sayings of the prophecy of this book" vs Dan 12:4 "seal the book"; DAR 324.1 "A revelation is something revealed"

[→] the book opens on Christ and closes on Him (Rev 22:16): the whole prophecy is His testimony

## 2. Sunday — General and Special Revelation — Heb 1:1-3

> Nature shows God's power; only the Word shows how a sinner is made righteous. God's last and fullest word is a Person, who "by himself purged our sins."

- _Heb 1:1, 2._ "God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by [his] Son" — many ways, one Speaker
- _Heb 1:3._ "upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins" — the Creator-Word is the Sin-purger
- _Ps 19:1._ "The heavens declare the glory of God" — glory, power
- _Rom 1:20._ "his eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse" — nature leaves man guilty, not saved
- _Rom 1:17._ "therein is the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith" — only the gospel reveals righteousness

| Nature (Ps 19:1; Rom 1:20) | The Word / the Son (Heb 1:2-3; Rom 1:17) |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| power, Godhead             | righteousness of God                     |
| leaves "without excuse"    | "purged our sins"                        |
| mixed with curse           | "full of grace and truth" (John 1:14)    |

[SOP TMK 38.4] "What speech is to thought, so is Christ to the invisible Father. He is the manifestation of the Father, and is called the Word of God." — Christ is God speaking

[PIONEER Waggoner, GCDB March 11, 1891, page 75.13] "[Christ,] who upholds all things by the word of his power, can by that same word create in me a clean heart" — the creating Word recreates

[Q] Isn't nature enough to know God?
→ "Nature and revelation alike testify of God's love" (SC 9.1) — but righteousness is revealed only in the gospel (Rom 1:16-17; John 1:18)

[→] the God who spoke worlds into being speaks righteousness into the believer — Ps 33:9 "he spake, and it was [done]"

## 3. Monday — The Process of Inspiration — 2 Tim 3:15-17

> Scripture is God-breathed for one purpose: to make a sinner "wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus," then furnish him "unto all good works."

- _2 Tim 3:15._ "the holy scriptures, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus." — the goal: faith in Christ
- _2 Tim 3:16._ "All scripture [is] given by inspiration of God, and [is] profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness" — all, not parts
- _2 Tim 3:17._ "That the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works." — faith that works
- _Jas 1:17._ "Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above" — the Word is that gift; unchanging Giver
- chain: Ps 33:6 (worlds by His breath) → 2 Pet 1:21 (moved by the Holy Ghost) → 1 Thes 2:13 (word "effectually worketh also in you that believe")

[PIONEER Waggoner, PTUK October 24, 1895, page 674.1] "Inspiration of God means the breath of God. Scripture inspired of God, is Scripture breathed of God." — same breath that made the worlds

[PIONEER Waggoner, PTUK October 24, 1895, page 674.3] "Whoever reads the words of the apostles and prophets as the Word of God, finds Christ." — the point of inspiration

[SOP COL 126.3] "Christ as manifested to the patriarchs, as symbolized in the sacrificial service, as portrayed in the law, and as revealed by the prophets, is the riches of the Old Testament." — Christ, every page

[Q] If the words are the prophet's own, is it still God's word?
→ received "not [as] the word of men, but as it is in truth, the word of God" — 1 Thes 2:13; how it works: 1SM 21.2 (§7)

[→] "instruction in righteousness" is not self-improvement: the same breath that inspired the page breathes life into the believer

## 4. Tuesday — Oral Messages — 2 Pet 1:18-21

> Prophets who never wrote a line spoke the same word with the same authority — and their burden was always "believe God." A spoken word from God can justify on the spot.

- _2 Pet 1:18._ "this voice which came from heaven we heard, when we were with him in the holy mount." — eyewitness to an oral word
- _2 Pet 1:19._ "We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place" — the written word confirms the heard voice
- _2 Pet 1:21._ "holy men of God spake [as they were] moved by the Holy Ghost." — oral or written, one Source

**Noah — oral preacher, righteousness by faith — Heb 11:7:**

- _2 Pet 2:5._ "Noah the eighth [person], a preacher of righteousness" — no book, full authority
- _Heb 11:7._ "By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark" — and "became heir of the righteousness which is by faith"

[SOP PP 95.1] "He gave the world an example of believing just what God says." — Noah's whole sermon

**Nathan — the word that convicts, the word that forgives — 2 Sam 12:**

- _2 Sam 12:7._ "Thou [art] the man." — oral rebuke
- _2 Sam 12:13._ "I have sinned against the LORD. And Nathan said unto David, The LORD also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die." — justified by a spoken word

[SOP PP 725.2] "In the promises of God to repentant sinners he saw the evidence of his pardon and acceptance." — David believed the word

[PIONEER Jones, ARSH November 10, 1896, page 717.6] "Will you be made righteous by it [the word of God] now? Will you live by it now? This is justification by faith. This is righteousness by faith." — 1888 voice

[→] Jesus wrote nothing, yet "Never man spake like this man" (John 7:46): the authority is in the word, not the ink

## 5. Wednesday — Written Messages — John 20:30-31

> God put the word in writing so later generations could believe and live — and so the record of past unbelief would warn us off the same road.

- _Exod 17:14._ "Write this [for] a memorial in a book, and rehearse [it] in the ears of Joshua" — first command to write; the victory kept for the next generation (v. 15, Jehovahnissi)
- _John 20:31._ "But these are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing ye might have life through his name." — written → believe → life
- _1 Cor 10:11._ "they are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come." — written for the last generation
- _Rom 15:4._ "written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope." — written to give hope
- _Rev 1:19._ "Write the things which thou hast seen, and the things which are, and the things which shall be hereafter" — past, present, future

[SOP CC 8.2] "The example of ancient Israel is given as a warning to the people of God, that they may avoid unbelief and escape His wrath." — the sin recorded is unbelief (Heb 3:19)

[SOP SC 49.3] "the gift which God promises us, we must believe we do receive, and it is ours." — how John 20:31 works

**Habakkuk — the written vision is "the just shall live by faith" — Hab 2:2-4:**

- _Hab 2:2._ "Write the vision, and make [it] plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it." — the Millerite chart text (GC 392.2)
- _Hab 2:4._ "the just shall live by his faith." — the content of the vision

[PIONEER Prescott, GCB April 8, 1901, page 130.9] "the vision was just this one thing, The just shall live by faith. This is the key to all the visions; this is the key to all the prophets" — the key

[Q] Why record the sins of God's heroes?
→ CC 8.2: to warn against unbelief; Nathan's portrayal "aided him to put them away" (CC 8.3); same grace offered us (Rom 15:4)

[→] the 1844 chart text and the 1888 message are one verse: Hab 2:2 and 2:4

## 6. Thursday — Drama, Symbols, Parables — Ezek 4:1-8

> When spoken warnings were mocked, God acted the message out. Ezekiel's sign gives the day-for-a-year key and shows a prophet bearing iniquity — the language later used of Christ.

- _Ezek 4:1._ "take thee a tile, and lay it before thee, and pourtray upon it the city, [even] Jerusalem" — a model siege
- _Ezek 4:4._ "Lie thou also upon thy left side, and lay the iniquity of the house of Israel upon it" — 390 days
- _Ezek 4:6._ "thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: I have appointed thee each day for a year." — the key, stated by God
- _Num 14:34._ "each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities" — second witness to the rule
- _Isa 53:11._ "my righteous servant justify many; for he shall bear their iniquities." — the prophet only signified iniquity-bearing; the Servant actually bore it

[SOP PK 448.1] "He [Ezekiel] was also instructed to foretell, by means of a variety of symbols and solemn messages, the siege and utter destruction of Jerusalem." — symbols as warning

[PIONEER Miller, WMAD 11.1] "as God in Numbers 14:34, and Ezekiel 4:4-6, had appointed each day for a year; as the seventy weeks to the Messiah were fulfilled in 490 years" — the key to Dan 8:14

- chain: Ezek 4:6 (day = year) → Dan 8:14 (2300 days) → Heb 8:1-2 (Minister of the true tabernacle) → Rev 14:7 (hour of his judgment) — the most holy place message
- _2 Chr 36:16._ "they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused his prophets" — why the drama: words were mocked

[SOP GC 598.3] "The language of the Bible should be explained according to its obvious meaning, unless a symbol or figure is employed." — literal unless marked

**Laodicea — God's "drama" for us — Rev 3:17-18:**

- _Rev 3:18._ "I counsel thee to buy of me gold tried in the fire, that thou mayest be rich; and white raiment, that thou mayest be clothed" — what we lack

| Counsel (Rev 3:18) | Meaning (7BC 965.1; COL 158.3) |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| gold tried         | faith that works by love       |
| white raiment      | righteousness of Christ        |
| eyesalve           | spiritual discernment          |

[SOP 15MR 94.1] "Justification by faith and the righteousness of Christ are the themes to be presented to a perishing world." — the Laodicean message's content

[Q] Were Ezekiel's acts allegory?
→ no: literal acts, God-labeled "a sign" (Ezek 4:3); the meaning is given in the text (v. 5-6) — Miller's Rules 7, 12

[→] the day-for-a-year key opens 1844 — and 1844 opens the Laodicean call to the white raiment

## 7. Friday — Further Thought — Rev 19:10

> God inspired men, not words; and the gift He renewed in the remnant is a lesser light that leads to the Bible — and through it to "the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience."

- _Rev 19:10._ "I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren that have the testimony of Jesus: worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — the gift points away from the messenger to God
- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — the last church has the gift
- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, [it is] because [there is] no light in them." — the test
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — believing the prophets = believing God

[SOP 1SM 21.2] "The divine mind and will is combined with the human mind and will; thus the utterances of the man are the word of God." — thought inspiration

[SOP 3SM 30.4] "Little heed is given to the Bible, and the Lord has given a lesser light to lead men and women to the greater light." — lesser light

[PIONEER James White, AWLF 13.4] "The bible is a perfect, and complete revelation. It is our only rule of faith and practice." — 1847, pioneer position

[PIONEER James White, AWLF 13.4] "True visions are given to lead us to God, and his written word" — what the gift is for

[SOP TM 91.2] "it [the 1888 message] invited the people to receive the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience to all the commandments of God." — what the gift lifted up

[SOP TM 91.2] "It is the third angel’s message, which is to be proclaimed with a loud voice" — present truth

[Q] Does Ellen White add to the Bible?
→ no: Bible "only rule" (AWLF 13.4); the gift leads to it (3SM 30.4); tested by it (Isa 8:20)

- [TANGENT] Gen 15:6 (Abraham believed) → 2 Chr 20:20 (believe his prophets) → Heb 4:2 (word not mixed with faith) → Rev 14:12 (faith of Jesus)

## 8. Appeal

- every mode of revelation this week said one thing: believe God, and be counted righteous — Gen 15:6; Rom 4:3
- the written vision is "the just shall live by his faith" (Hab 2:4); the last book is "the Revelation of Jesus Christ" (Rev 1:1)
- the testimony of Jesus is given to a people who keep the commandments by the faith of Jesus (Rev 12:17; 14:12)

[PIONEER Prescott, GCB April 8, 1901, page 129.3] "this grand truth, this same message, righteousness by faith, Christ in you the hope of glory." — the one message of all the prophets

[RQ] Will you take the word as God's word "now" — and let it do in you what it says?
