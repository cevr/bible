# Sabbath School — Q4 W9 (2026)

## The Test Is Christ Our Righteousness

_Quarterly: Testing the Prophets · November 21–27 · Read: Dan. 10:7–19; Deut. 13:1–5; Isa. 8:20; John 11:25; Acts 15:36–39; Jer. 28:9, 15–17_

**Memory Verse:** — _1 John 4:1._ "Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world."

**Theme: Every test of a prophet asks one question, whether the voice lifts up Christ our righteousness in our flesh and calls me to believe God, and whoever believes Him is established.**

## The road

1. Signs prove nothing; the sight of Christ empties self — Dan 10:8 _(quarterly: Sun)_

## Open

- **Say:** God commands us to test every voice that speaks in His name. Why? So that our faith rests on His word and on nothing else.
- _1 John 4:1._ "Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world." — the test exists so that faith has a sure object
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the prophet's whole message: believe God
- [RQ] In 2 Chr 20:20, what does believing the LORD do for His people?
  → "so shall ye be established": the true prophet's word ends in trust in God, not in the prophet

---

## 1. Signs Prove Nothing; the Sight of Christ Empties Self — Dan 10:8

- **Say:** A sign can come from below, so a vision's power proves nothing. What a true vision does is what it did to Daniel: it shows a man his corruption, then gives him Christ's strength.
- _Deut 13:3._ "the LORD your God proveth you, to know whether ye love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul" — the sign can come true; the test falls on the hearer
- _Dan 10:8._ "Therefore I was left alone, and saw this great vision, and there remained no strength in me: for my comeliness was turned in me into corruption, and I retained no strength." — a true vision empties the prophet before it fills him
- _Dan 10:18-19._ "he strengthened me, And said, O man greatly beloved, fear not: peace [be] unto thee, be strong" — strength imparted, not summoned
- [RQ] In Deut 13:3, why does God allow a false prophet's sign to come to pass?
  → "the LORD your God proveth you, to know whether ye love the LORD": the test is my heart, not the sign
- [RQ] What did the sight of the Man in linen do to Daniel's comeliness, and where did his strength come from?
  → "turned in me into corruption"; then "he strengthened me": nothing of Daniel's own

[WB]: Daniel → sees the Man → "corruption" → touched → "be strong"

[SOP SC 29.2] "The soul thus touched will hate its selfishness, abhor its self-love, and will seek, through Christ’s righteousness, for the purity of heart" — the true vision's fruit

- **Bridge:** If a sign cannot settle it, what does? God sends us to the written word: Isa 8:20.

## 2. The Law and the Prophets Witness One Righteousness — Isa 8:20

- **Say:** The law and the testimony are the measure of every voice. And what they testify to is one righteousness: God's, by faith of Jesus Christ.
- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, [it is] because [there is] no light in them." — the standard all inspiration answers to
- _Rom 3:21-22._ "the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets; Even the righteousness of God [which is] by faith of Jesus Christ" — what law and prophets testify
- _Jer 23:6._ "this [is] his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS." — the true prophets' headline
- [RQ] By Isa 8:20, what is a voice that does not speak according to the law and the testimony?
  → "no light in them," however much light it claims
- [RQ] By Rom 3:21-22, what do the law and the prophets witness to?
  → "the righteousness of God," which is "by faith of Jesus Christ"

[PIONEER Waggoner, WOR 59.1] "The prophets preached the same Gospel that the apostles did." — one gospel, Acts 10:43

- **Bridge:** The true prophets name Him "THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS." John's test asks where that Lord came: into our flesh.

## 3. Christ Come in Our Flesh — 1 John 4:2

- **Say:** John's test is a confession: Jesus Christ "is come in the flesh." Our flesh, where sin was condemned, so the righteousness He brings is within our reach.
- _1 John 4:2._ "Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God" — the confession test
- _1 John 4:3._ "every spirit that confesseth not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is not of God: and this is that [spirit] of antichrist" — denying Him our flesh is the antichrist spirit
- _Rom 8:3._ "God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh" — where sin was condemned
- _Heb 2:17._ "in all things it behoved him to be made like unto [his] brethren, that he might be a merciful and faithful high priest" — likeness makes the priesthood possible
- [RQ] By Rom 8:3, in what flesh did God send His Son, and where did He condemn sin?
  → "in the likeness of sinful flesh"; He "condemned sin in the flesh," the very place I lose to it
- [RQ] By Heb 2:17, why was He made like His brethren in all things?
  → "that he might be a merciful and faithful high priest": a Priest who has stood where I stand

[PIONEER Jones, ICM 1.40] "Jesus Christ came not simply in flesh, but in the flesh, the only flesh that there is in this world, sinful flesh" — 1 John 4:3 applied

- **Bridge:** A Christ who conquered in our flesh bears fruit in our flesh. That is Jesus' next test: "by their fruits."

---

## 4. The Fruit Is the Gospel in the Life — Gal 2:16

- **Say:** The fruit test doesn't ask for a sinless prophet. It asks whether the gospel shows in the life. When Peter stumbled at Antioch, the cure was the gospel itself.
- _Matt 7:16._ "Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?" — fruit reveals the root
- _Gal 2:14._ "when I saw that they walked not uprightly according to the truth of the gospel" — Peter's fault was against justification by faith
- _Gal 2:16._ "a man is not justified by the works of the law, but by the faith of Jesus Christ" — Paul's correction is the 1888 message
- _Gal 2:20._ "I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me" — the only source of good fruit
- [RQ] By Gal 2:14, what was wrong with Peter's walk at Antioch?
  → he "walked not uprightly according to the truth of the gospel": a fault against the gospel, not a failed prediction
- [RQ] By Gal 2:16 and 2:20, how is a man justified, and where does the good fruit come from?
  → "by the faith of Jesus Christ"; and "Christ liveth in me"

[PIONEER Waggoner, GTI 75.2] "It was a question of justification and salvation,—whether men were saved by faith alone in Christ, or by outward forms." — what Peter's withdrawal denied

- **Bridge:** Fruit takes a season; the time test asks whether the word comes to pass, and whom the people trusted.

## 5. Trust in a Lie, or Believe God — Jer 28:15

- **Say:** Hananiah promised peace without repentance and made a nation trust a lie. Nineveh heard a warning and believed God. The word stands, and faith in it decides the outcome.
- _Jer 28:9._ "The prophet which prophesieth of peace, when the word of the prophet shall come to pass, [then] shall the prophet be known, that the LORD hath truly sent him." — peace-prophets carry the burden of proof
- _Jer 28:15._ "The LORD hath not sent thee; but thou makest this people to trust in a lie." — false prophecy is misplaced trust
- _Jer 18:8._ "If that nation, against whom I have pronounced, turn from their evil, I will repent of the evil that I thought to do unto them." — the condition is written into the warning
- _Jonah 3:5._ "So the people of Nineveh believed God" — faith came first
- [RQ] By Jer 28:15, what did Hananiah's false word do to the people?
  → "thou makest this people to trust in a lie"
- [RQ] By Jonah 3:5, what did Nineveh do with Jonah's warning?
  → "the people of Nineveh believed God," and by Jer 18:8 the threatened evil was turned away

| Hananiah (Jer 28)        | Nineveh (Jonah 3)      |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| peace without repentance | repentance, then peace |
| "trust in a lie"         | "believed God"         |
| died that year           | spared                 |

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 15.1] "Faith is the expecting the word of God to do what it says and the depending upon that word to do what it says." — what "believe his prophets" asks of me

- **Bridge:** The remnant is marked by this very faith: they have the testimony of Jesus, and it points them to Him.

## Decision

- **Say:** Every test this week is one test: does the voice lift up Christ our righteousness, come in our flesh, and call me to believe God? The gift was given to point us there. Stop asking for signs; believe His word.
- _Rev 19:10._ "worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — the prophet points away from himself, to Christ
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the closing word: believe, and be established
- [RQ] By Rev 19:10 and 2 Chr 20:20, what does God ask of me, and what does He do?
  → "worship God" and "Believe in the LORD your God"; He does the establishing: "so shall ye be established"

## If asked

- [Q] Didn't the physical signs in vision prove the gift?
  → No. Signs can lie (Deut 13:1-3; Matt 24:24); the word decides (Isa 8:20); "John did no miracle" (John 10:41)
- [Q] Doesn't "sinful flesh" make Christ a sinner?
  → No. "Yet without sin" (Heb 4:15), "who knew no sin" (2 Cor 5:21); He condemned sin in the flesh (Rom 8:3)
- [Q] Jonah's "forty days" didn't happen. Did he fail Deut 18:22?
  → No. The threat was conditional (Jer 18:7-8); God's promise to the believer is not: "all the promises of God in him [are] yea" (2 Cor 1:20)
