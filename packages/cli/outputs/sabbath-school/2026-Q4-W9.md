# Sabbath School — Q4 W9 (2026)

## Testing the Prophets

_Week of November 21–27 · Read: Dan. 10:7–19; Deut. 13:1–5; Isa. 8:20; John 11:25; Acts 15:36–39; Jer. 28:9, 15–17_

**Memory Verse:** — _1 John 4:1._ "Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world."

**Every Bible test of a prophet asks one question (does this voice lift up Christ our righteousness and call me to believe God?), and the people who pass the test are the ones who believe Him.**

## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — The Test and the Trust — 1 John 4:1

> God commands the test, not because He is stingy with light, but because a counterfeit faith rests on a counterfeit word. You test the voice so that you can believe God with your whole weight.

- _1 John 4:1._ "Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world." — the test exists *so that* faith has a sure object
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the prophet's whole message: believe God
- _Gen 15:6._ "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." — the first man Scripture calls "a prophet" (Gen 20:7) was justified by believing what God *said*
- chain: Isa 8:20 (law + testimony = the standard) → Rom 10:17 (faith comes by hearing the word) → Heb 11:6 (without faith, impossible to please Him)

[SOP CCh 92.2] "the Scriptures explicitly state that the word of God is the standard by which all teaching and experience must be tested" — the measuring rod

[SOP PK 198.4] "Not by disciplined armies and fenced cities, but by a living faith in the God of Israel, could he hope to gain the victory" — Jehoshaphat's battle = righteousness by faith

[SOP PP 370.3] "he trusted in Christ for the forgiveness of sins. It was this faith that was accounted unto him for righteousness." — Abraham believed *Christ*

[→] every test this week is really a test of where my faith rests: on a sign, a man, or the word of God

## 2. Sunday — Role of the Supernatural — Dan 10:8

> A vision's power can come from below, so signs prove nothing. What a true vision always does is what it did to Daniel: it shows a man his own corruption and then gives him Christ's strength.

[WB]: Daniel → sees the Man in linen → "corruption" → touched → "be strong"

- _Dan 10:8._ "Therefore I was left alone, and saw this great vision, and there remained no strength in me: for my comeliness was turned in me into corruption, and I retained no strength." — a true vision empties the prophet before it fills him
- _Dan 10:5-6._ "behold a certain man clothed in linen" — the priestly Man (cf. Rev 1:13-17); the sight of *Him* is what undoes Daniel
- _Dan 10:18-19._ "he strengthened me, And said, O man greatly beloved, fear not: peace [be] unto thee, be strong" — strength imparted, not summoned
- _Num 12:6._ "[I] the LORD will make myself known unto him in a vision" — God reveals *Himself* to the prophet, not merely future facts

| Daniel by himself          | What the Man gave him   |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| "no strength" (v8)         | "he strengthened me" (v18) |
| comeliness → corruption    | "greatly beloved" (v11, 19) |
| "dumb" (v15)               | lips touched (v16)      |
| face to the ground (v9)    | set upon knees (v10)    |

[SOP SC 29.2] "The soul thus touched will hate its selfishness, abhor its self-love, and will seek, through Christ’s righteousness, for the purity of heart" — the vision's fruit

[SOP 1SAT 21.3] "Was that the way Daniel prayed to God? No; he prayed and confessed his sins." — no merit pleaded (Dan 9:5)

**Deut 13: the sign can come true and still be false:**

- _Deut 13:3._ "the LORD your God proveth you, to know whether ye love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul" — the test falls on the hearer: does love hold to God?
- _Deut 13:5._ "he hath spoken to turn [you] away from the LORD your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed you out of the house of bondage" — the false prophet pulls you away from the *Redeemer*

[SOP MM 110.2] "God’s people will not find their safety in working miracles; for Satan will counterfeit the miracles that will be wrought." — "It is written" is the safety

[PIONEER Loughborough, GSAM 307.1] "That the working of miracles is not the test of a true prophet is clearly seen by reading the scripture record of John the Baptist." — John 10:41, "John did no miracle"

[Q] Didn't the visions' physical signs prove the gift?
→ They point to a power at work, but Deut 13:1-3 and Matt 24:24 say signs can lie; the word decides (Isa 8:20)

[→] if I want a sign before I will believe, I have already failed Deut 13. Faith rests on the word.

## 3. Monday — Harmony With the Bible — Isa 8:20

> The "law and testimony" do more than measure a prophet. They witness to one righteousness, God's, by faith of Jesus. A voice that contradicts them, or preaches a righteousness of our own, has "no light."

- _Isa 8:20._ "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, [it is] because [there is] no light in them." — the standard all inspiration answers to
- _Isa 8:16._ "Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples." — law sealed in the disciples (cf. Heb 8:10)
- _Rom 3:21-22._ "the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets; Even the righteousness of God [which is] by faith of Jesus Christ" — what law + prophets *testify*
- _Jer 23:6._ "this [is] his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS." — the true prophets' headline
- chain: Gen 3:15 (first prophecy: the Seed) → 2 Pet 1:19 (day star in your hearts) → Rom 15:4 (written for our hope) → Jas 1:17 (no variableness)
- _Num 23:19._ "God [is] not a man, that he should lie" — later light never contradicts earlier light

[PIONEER Cottrell, BIC 42.2] "The law and the testimony, in this, and in verse 20, I understand to be one and the same thing-the ten commandments" — pioneer reading of Isa 8:20

[PIONEER Haskell, BHB 138.20] "One who claims to be a prophet may fulfill every other test, but if his instruction is contrary to the Bible and the testimonies, he proves himself to be false." — the decisive test

[PIONEER Waggoner, WOR 59.1] "The prophets preached the same Gospel that the apostles did." — Acts 10:43, one foundation

[SOP CCh 92.1] "The written testimonies are not to give new light, but to impress vividly upon the heart the truths of inspiration already revealed." — the gift serves the Book

| False prophets (Jer 23:16-17) | True prophets (Jer 23:5-6) |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| "vision of their own heart"   | "mouth of the LORD"        |
| "Ye shall have peace"         | "THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS" |
| flatter the sinner            | point to the Branch        |

[Q] Can a later prophet bring "new light" that sets aside the Bible?
→ No. God has "no variableness" (Jas 1:17); light unfolds the gospel already given (Gen 3:15 → Rom 3:21) and never reverses it

[→] whoever preaches a righteousness other than "by faith of Jesus Christ" fails Isa 8:20, however much he talks about the law

## 4. Tuesday — The Incarnation of Christ — 1 John 4:2-3

> The test is whether a prophet confesses Christ "come in the flesh," meaning *our* flesh, the place where sinners actually live. A Christ kept at a distance from our nature can't save us in it.

- _1 John 4:2._ "Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God" — the confession test
- _1 John 4:3._ "every spirit that confesseth not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is not of God: and this is that [spirit] of antichrist" — denying Him our flesh is the antichrist spirit
- _Rom 8:3._ "God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh" — where sin was condemned
- _Heb 2:17._ "in all things it behoved him to be made like unto [his] brethren, that he might be a merciful and faithful high priest" — likeness makes the priesthood possible
- chain: John 3:16 (the Gift) → 2 Cor 5:21 (made sin, we made righteousness) → John 11:25 (resurrection + life) → Heb 4:14-15 (High Priest, tempted, without sin) → John 14:1-3 (He comes again)

[PIONEER Jones, ICM 1.40] "Jesus Christ came not simply in flesh, but in the flesh, the only flesh that there is in this world, sinful flesh" — 1 John 4:3 vs immaculate conception

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 26.2] "it must have been sinful man that He was made like, for it is sinful man that He came to redeem." — 1888 Christology

[SOP DA 25.2] "He was condemned for our sins, in which He had no share, that we might be justified by His righteousness, in which we had no share." — 2 Cor 5:21 unfolded

| Spirit of antichrist           | Spirit of God (1 John 4:2) |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Christ far from our flesh      | Christ come in our flesh   |
| saints mediate to Him  | Priest touched by us |
| righteousness by forms         | righteousness by faith     |

[Q] Doesn't "sinful flesh" make Christ a sinner?
→ No. "Yet without sin" (Heb 4:15) and "who knew no sin" (2 Cor 5:21); He condemned sin *in* the flesh (Rom 8:3)

[RQ] If Christ conquered in my flesh, what is the gospel's answer to "I can't help it"?

[→] a prophet who lifts up Christ in our flesh brings Christ within reach: "Christ in you, the hope of glory" (Col 1:27)

## 5. Wednesday — Positive Fruits — Matt 7:16

> The fruit test asks whether the gospel is visible in a life. It doesn't ask for a sinless prophet. When a prophet stumbles at the gospel, as Peter did at Antioch, the remedy is the gospel: justification by faith of Christ.

- _Matt 7:16._ "Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?" — fruit reveals the root
- _Matt 7:17._ "every good tree bringeth forth good fruit" — the tree is made good first, then fruit follows
- _Gal 5:6._ "faith which worketh by love" — the fruit of justifying faith
- chain: Jonah 1:3 (prophet runs) → Acts 15:39 (Paul + Barnabas contend) → 2 Tim 4:11 (Mark "profitable" later) → grace restores the messenger

**Antioch: a prophet tested by the truth of the gospel — Gal 2:11-16:**

- _Gal 2:14._ "when I saw that they walked not uprightly according to the truth of the gospel" — Peter's fault was against justification by faith
- _Gal 2:16._ "a man is not justified by the works of the law, but by the faith of Jesus Christ" — Paul's correction is the 1888 message
- _Gal 2:20._ "I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me" — the only source of good fruit

[PIONEER Waggoner, GTI 75.2] "It was a question of justification and salvation,—whether men were saved by faith alone in Christ, or by outward forms." — what Peter's withdrawal denied

[SOP AA 198.1] "permitted Peter to reveal this weakness of character in order that the tried apostle might see that there was nothing in himself whereof he might boast" — no human infallibility

[SOP Mar 158.5] "Pure truth can be traced to its divine Source, by its elevating, refining, sanctifying influence upon the character of the receiver." — the fruit test applied to a message

[SOP COL 69.1] "When the character of Christ shall be perfectly reproduced in His people, then He will come to claim them as His own." — the harvest the gift ripens

[Q] Jonah fled and Peter dissembled, so were they false prophets?
→ No. The test is the trend of the life and the message (Matt 7:17). Both were restored by grace (Jonah 3:1; AA 198.1)

[→] the fruit that proves a prophet is the same fruit that proves a believer: Christ living in the flesh by faith

## 6. Thursday — Fulfilled Prophecies — Jer 28:9

> Hananiah's lie was a peace without repentance. Nineveh's escape came from believing God. Both prove the same thing: God's word stands, and faith in it decides the outcome.

- _Jer 28:9._ "The prophet which prophesieth of peace, when the word of the prophet shall come to pass, [then] shall the prophet be known, that the LORD hath truly sent him." — peace-prophets carry the burden of proof
- _Jer 28:15._ "The LORD hath not sent thee; but thou makest this people to trust in a lie." — false prophecy = misplaced trust
- _Jer 28:16-17._ "this year thou shalt die, because thou hast taught rebellion against the LORD. So Hananiah the prophet died the same year in the seventh month." — a two-month fulfillment
- _Deut 18:22._ "if the thing follow not, nor come to pass, that [is] the thing which the LORD hath not spoken" — the time test
- chain: Jer 17:5 (cursed: trusts man) → Jer 17:7 (blessed: trusts the LORD) → Hab 2:4 (just live by faith)

[SOP EW 139.1] "It has always been a characteristic of false prophets that they see visions of peace" — Hananiah's pattern, Jer 6:14

[SOP EW 139.1] "The true will boldly reprove sin and warn of coming wrath." — Jeremiah's pattern

**Conditional prophecy: the threat yields to faith — Jer 18:7-10:**

- _Jer 18:8._ "If that nation, against whom I have pronounced, turn from their evil, I will repent of the evil that I thought to do unto them." — the condition is written into the warning
- _Jonah 3:5._ "So the people of Nineveh believed God" — faith came first
- _Jonah 3:10._ "God saw their works, that they turned from their evil way; and God repented of the evil" — then the works that show faith

[PIONEER Advent Herald (Himes / Bliss / Hale, 1844), LIFIN 188.2] "Was Jonah a false prophet when he preached the time of Nineveh’s destruction? No; he had only preached the preaching that God had bid him." — 1844 pioneers on conditionality

[PIONEER Waggoner, PROLI 58.5] "this word which Ezekiel wrote, as he dwelt among the captives by the river of Chebar, two thousand four hundred and seventy-four years ago, is the word of God" — fulfilled prophecy proves the Word

| Hananiah (Jer 28)        | Nineveh (Jonah 3)    |
| ------------------------ | -------------------- |
| peace without repentance | repentance, then peace |
| "trust in a lie"         | "believed God"       |
| died that year           | spared               |

[Q] Jonah's "forty days" didn't happen. Did he fail Deut 18:22?
→ No. The threat was conditional (Jer 18:7-8); the promise to the believer is not: "all the promises of God in him [are] yea" (2 Cor 1:20)

[→] the most important fulfilled prophecy is Dan 9:24: Christ came on time "to bring in everlasting righteousness"

## 7. Friday — Further Thought — Rev 12:17; 19:10

> The remnant has "the testimony of Jesus" because the gift's work is to point to Jesus. Its proof is what the pioneers said of 1888: the testimony led them to Christ our righteousness, and they felt Him change their hearts.

- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — two identifying marks
- _Rev 19:10._ "worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — the prophet points away from himself, to Christ
- _Joel 2:28._ "your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions" — the gift promised for the last days
- _Ps 34:8._ "O taste and see that the LORD [is] good: blessed [is] the man [that] trusteth in him." — experience confirms what the word establishes
- [TANGENT] chain: Hos 12:13 (by a prophet Israel delivered) → Prov 29:18 (no vision, people perish) → 1 Thess 5:20-21 (despise not; prove all) → Rev 14:12 (commandments + faith of Jesus)

[PIONEER Loughborough, HEVI 102.9] "the Lord was to restore to the remnant church the gift of the “testimony of Jesus,” which “is the Spirit of prophecy.”" — the pioneer argument from Rev 12:17

[SOP PP 366.3] "It is the voice of Christ that speaks to us through the Old Testament." — every true prophet carries Christ's voice

[SOP 3AM 113.4] "It presented justification through faith in the Surety; it invited the people to receive the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience to all the commandments of God." — what the gift endorsed in 1888

[SOP 1888 1024.2] "We know that Christ is our righteousness, not alone because He is so described in the Bible, but because we have felt His transforming power in our own hearts." — "taste and see"

[SOP CM 125.2] "the Lord has given a lesser light to lead men and women to the greater light." — the gift serves the Book

[SOP Mar 158.4] "The very last deception of Satan will be to make of none effect the testimony of the Spirit of God." — why the test matters now

- EGW Notes further reading: HP 125 ("The Garden of God’s Promises"); UL 260 ("Mystery of the Incarnation")

[SOP UL 260.3] "Read and search the Scriptures, in which Christ is set forth as the divine object of our faith." — the further-reading payload

[Q] Why do we need a prophet after the canon closed?
→ Not to replace the Bible (CCh 92.1). The gift serves "till we all come in the unity of the faith" (Eph 4:13) and marks the remnant (Rev 12:17)

## 8. Appeal

- every test this week is the same test: does the voice lift up Christ, our righteousness in our flesh, and call me to believe God?
- the remnant are those who keep "the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (Rev 14:12): a righteousness that is *received* by faith and then *kept* in obedience
- Jehoshaphat's people won before the battle began. They believed the word and went out singing (2 Chr 20:20-22)
- decision: stop testing God by signs and start trusting His word. Let the Man in linen turn your comeliness to corruption and then make you strong
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper."
- _Hab 2:4._ "the just shall live by his faith."
