# Sabbath School — Q4 W4 (2026)

## Every Prophet Points to Christ Our Righteousness

_Quarterly: New Testament Prophets · October 17–23 · Read: Matt 16:13–16; Eph 2:20; Rev 22:6; Luke 2:36–38; Acts 21:9; Eph 6:10–18_

**Memory Verse:** — _1 Cor 12:4-6._ "Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit. And there are differences of administrations, but the same Lord. And there are diversities of operations, but it is the same God which worketh all in all."

**Theme: One Spirit speaks through every true prophet with one message: believe the Son who purged our sins, and God counts you righteous, builds you on Him, and keeps you standing.**

## The road

1. One Speaker, one subject: the Son who purged our sins — Heb 1:1-3 _(quarterly: Sabbath, Sun)_
2. One foundation: Christ our righteousness — Eph 2:20 _(quarterly: Mon)_
3. Given till Christ is formed in His people — Eph 4:11-13 _(quarterly: Tue, Wed)_
4. Clothed in Christ, the counterfeit cannot deceive — Eph 6:13-14 _(quarterly: Thu)_
5. The testimony of Jesus in the remnant — Rev 19:10 _(quarterly: Fri)_
6. Decision

## Open

- **Say:** Many gifts, many prophets, two Testaments. What is the one thing they all say?
- _1 Cor 12:4._ "Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit." — the gifts differ, but one Spirit drives them all
- [RQ] How many Spirits stand behind all the gifts?
  → One: "diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit" (1 Cor 12:4). One Spirit means one message.

---

## 1. One Speaker, One Subject — Heb 1:1-3

- **Say:** The God who spoke by the prophets finally spoke by His Son, and the subject was always the same: sins purged, received by believing.
- _Heb 1:1-3._ "God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds; Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person, and upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high;" — prophets and the Son have one Speaker; the subject is "purged our sins"
- _Deut 18:15._ "The LORD thy God will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken;" — the promised Prophet; we must hearken to Him
- _Matt 16:15-16._ "He saith unto them, But whom say ye that I am? And Simon Peter answered and said, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God." — the confession every prophet's work aims at
- _Acts 10:43._ "To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins." — the prophets' one testimony: forgiveness by believing
- [RQ] What did the Son do "by himself" before He sat down?
  → "When he had by himself purged our sins" (Heb 1:3). The work is His, finished.
- [RQ] What do "all the prophets" witness, and how is it received?
  → Remission of sins, by believing: "whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins" (Acts 10:43).

[WB]: PROPHETS → SON → PURGED SINS → BELIEVE → RIGHTEOUS

[SOP 1SM 367.1] "By faith he can bring to God the merits of Christ, and the Lord places the obedience of His Son to the sinner’s account." — what believing the Prophet brings

- **Bridge:** If every prophet preaches the same Christ, then what they lay down is one foundation.

## 2. One Foundation: Christ Our Righteousness — Eph 2:20

- **Say:** Apostles and prophets are foundation stones only because they are laid on Christ. What they lay under the church is His righteousness, not ours.
- _Eph 2:20._ "And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone;" — one building; Christ is the cornerstone that holds both walls
- _Rom 1:1-2._ "separated unto the gospel of God, (Which he had promised afore by his prophets in the holy scriptures,)" — Paul's gospel is the prophets' gospel
- _Rom 3:21-22._ "the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets; Even the righteousness of God which is by faith of Jesus Christ" — the main thing the prophets testified to
- [RQ] Who is the chief corner stone that holds apostles and prophets together?
  → "Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone" (Eph 2:20).
- [RQ] What did the prophets witness to, and how does it come?
  → "The righteousness of God which is by faith of Jesus Christ" (Rom 3:21-22), not by our record.

[SOP 5T 725.2] "The foundation of Christianity is Christ our righteousness." — what Eph 2:20 looks like as doctrine

- **Bridge:** A foundation is laid to be built on. So what are prophets given to build?

## 3. Given Till Christ Is Formed in His People — Eph 4:11-13

- **Say:** Prophets build up, urge on and comfort, and the gift stays "till" the church reaches the fulness of Christ: His character reproduced in His people by faith.
- _1 Cor 14:3._ "But he that prophesieth speaketh unto men to edification, and exhortation, and comfort." — the three tasks: build up, urge on, comfort
- _Eph 4:11-13._ "And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ: Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ:" — the gifts last "till"; that goal has not yet been reached
- _Luke 2:38._ "spake of him to all them that looked for redemption in Jerusalem." — Anna the prophetess: her message was redemption in a Person
- [RQ] How long are the gifts given?
  → "Till we all come in the unity of the faith" (Eph 4:13), to "the fulness of Christ." That has not yet come.
- [RQ] When Anna prophesied, whom did she speak of?
  → "Spake of him," to those looking "for redemption" (Luke 2:38). The gift's subject is Christ.

[SOP COL 69.1] "When the character of Christ shall be perfectly reproduced in His people, then He will come to claim them as His own." — "the fulness of Christ" explained

- **Bridge:** Where the true gift builds Christ in us, a counterfeit comes to build something else.

## 4. Clothed in Christ, the Counterfeit Cannot Deceive — Eph 6:13-14

- **Say:** False prophets copy the signs but not the gospel. The only safe armour is Christ's own righteousness, put on by faith.
- _Matt 24:24._ "For there shall arise false Christs, and false prophets, and shall shew great signs and wonders; insomuch that, if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect." — miracles prove nothing; the Word decides
- _1 John 4:2._ "Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God:" — the doctrinal test: Christ come in our flesh
- _Eph 6:13-14._ "Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand. Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness;" — "to stand" in the evil day
- [RQ] Do great signs prove a prophet true?
  → No: false prophets "shall shew great signs and wonders" (Matt 24:24). The test is the confession of Christ come in the flesh (1 John 4:2).
- [RQ] What guards the heart in the evil day?
  → "The breastplate of righteousness" (Eph 6:14), armour God gives, not armour we make.

## 5. The Testimony of Jesus in the Remnant — Rev 19:10

- **Say:** The testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy. The gift in the remnant leads to Christ and to the Bible, and its message for the end is Christ's righteousness.
- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — the two marks of the last church
- _Rev 19:10._ "worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — even the angel refuses worship; the gift points to Jesus
- [RQ] What two marks does the remnant carry?
  → They "keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ" (Rev 12:17).
- [RQ] What is the testimony of Jesus?
  → "The spirit of prophecy" (Rev 19:10): a gift that points away from itself to "worship God."

[SOP TM 91.2] "This message was to bring more prominently before the world the uplifted Saviour, the sacrifice for the sins of the whole world." — what the gift endorsed in 1888

- **Bridge:** The gift's last word is the prophets' first word. What will we do with it?

## Decision

- **Say:** The prophets, the Son, the apostles, Anna and the remnant bring one message: believe God, and He counts you righteous in Christ, builds you on Him, and clothes you to stand.
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — decide today: believe Him, and believe His prophets about Him
- [RQ] What does God ask, so that we are established?
  → "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established" (2 Chr 20:20). Not achieve: believe.

[SOP 6T 19.1] "The message of Christ’s righteousness is to sound from one end of the earth to the other to prepare the way of the Lord." — the loud cry is this message

## If asked

- [Q] Do prophets add to the Bible?
  → No: the gift is "not for a new rule of faith" (EW 78.1), and is tested by the law and the testimony (Isa 8:20).
- [Q] Did the gift of prophecy end with the apostles?
  → The gifts last "till we all come" (Eph 4:13), and the remnant has "the testimony of Jesus" (Rev 12:17; 19:10).
- [Q] Is righteousness by faith a new message?
  → No: "all the prophets" witnessed it (Acts 10:43; Rom 3:21); Abraham believed and it was counted (Gen 15:6; Gal 3:8).
