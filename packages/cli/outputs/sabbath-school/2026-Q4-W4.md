## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — God Has Spoken by His Son — Heb 1:1-3

> The same God spoke through every prophet, and finally in His Son. What He said every time was: believe Me, and I will count you righteous.

[WB]: PROPHETS → SON → PURGED SINS → BELIEVE → RIGHTEOUS

- _Heb 1:1-3._ "God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds; Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person, and upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high;" — Prophets and the Son have one Speaker; the subject is "purged our sins"
- _1 Cor 12:4._ "Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit." — the gifts differ, but one Spirit drives them all
- _Acts 10:43._ "To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins." — the prophets' one testimony: forgiveness by believing
- chain: Gen 15:6 (believed → counted righteous) → 2 Chr 20:20 (believe his prophets) → Rom 3:21 (witnessed by the law and the prophets) → Heb 11:6 (without faith impossible)

[PIONEER Jones, CWCP 13.2] "“in time past spake unto the fathers by the prophets” and who “hath in these last days spoken unto us by His Son.”" — one Speaker in both Testaments

[PIONEER Waggoner, EVCO 68.2] "Therefore when Abraham believed in the Lord, he believed in the Lord Jesus Christ." — Old Testament faith was already faith in Christ

[SOP 1SM 367.1] "By faith he can bring to God the merits of Christ, and the Lord places the obedience of His Son to the sinner’s account." — what "counted for righteousness" means

[Q] Is righteousness by faith a new message?
→ No: Gen 15:6 → Gal 3:8 ("preached before the gospel unto Abraham"); the prophets and the Son preach one gospel — Acts 10:43

[→] Every prophet this week is measured by Heb 1: does he point to the Son who purged our sins?

## 2. Sunday — Jesus as Prophet — Matt 16:13-16

> Moses foretold a Prophet whom we must hear. Jesus is that Prophet. To hear Him is to believe Him, and believing Him is the foundation of everything.

- _Deut 18:15._ "The LORD thy God will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken;" — the promised Prophet; we must hearken to Him
- _Matt 16:15-16._ "He saith unto them, But whom say ye that I am? And Simon Peter answered and said, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God." — the confession every prophet's work aims at
- _Matt 16:17._ "flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven" — the confession comes by revelation, not by human wisdom
- _Acts 3:26._ "sent him to bless you, in turning away every one of you from his iniquities" — the Prophet's work is to turn us from sin, not just to give information
- chain: Acts 3:22 (Peter: Moses meant Jesus) → John 6:14 (that prophet) → Matt 17:5 (hear ye him) → Luke 24:27 (all the prophets: concerning himself)

| Pharisee's prophet (Luke 7) | Christ the Prophet          |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| exposes sinners             | reads a heart of love       |
| keeps the woman at a distance | forgives the whole debt   |
| Simon feels righteous       | Simon sees he owes more     |

[SOP DA 412.3] "The truth which Peter had confessed is the foundation of the believer’s faith. It is that which Christ Himself has declared to be eternal life." — John 17:3

[PIONEER Jones, LOF_ATJ 15.1] "Faith is the expecting the word of God to do what it says and the depending upon that word to do what it says." — hearing the Prophet = expecting His word to work

**The Prophet is the Creator — John 1:1-4:**

- _John 1:3-4._ "All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made. In him was life; and the life was the light of men." — the Word who made the world is the Word who gives life
- chain: Ps 33:6 (by the word heavens made) → 2 Cor 5:17 (new creature) → 2 Pet 1:19 (sure word, day star in hearts)

[PIONEER Waggoner, CHR 17.1] "It was by a word that the heavens were made, and that same word holds them in their place, and preserves them from destruction." — His spoken word creates and keeps

[RQ] When this Prophet says "thy sins are forgiven," what does His word do?

[→] Hearing the Prophet means believing His word over our own record, as Abraham did.

## 3. Monday — Apostles and Prophets — Eph 2:20

> Apostles and prophets are foundation stones only because they are laid on Christ. The foundation every one of them lays is Christ our righteousness.

- _Eph 2:20._ "And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone;" — one building; Christ is the cornerstone that holds both walls
- _Eph 3:5._ "as it is now revealed unto his holy apostles and prophets by the Spirit" — one Spirit gives revelation to both
- _Rom 1:1-2._ "separated unto the gospel of God, (Which he had promised afore by his prophets in the holy scriptures,)" — Paul's gospel is the prophets' gospel
- _Rom 3:21-22._ "the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets; Even the righteousness of God which is by faith of Jesus Christ" — the main thing the prophets testified to
- chain: Isa 28:16 (tried stone, he that believeth) → 1 Cor 3:11 (no other foundation) → 2 Pet 3:2 (prophets + apostles, one commandment) → Acts 15:32 (Judas, Silas: prophets who were not apostles)

| Apostles               | Prophets                   |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| sent ones (Rom 1:1)    | spoke beforehand (Rom 1:2) |
| "now revealed" (Eph 3:5) | "promised afore"         |
| one Spirit             | one Spirit                 |
| Christ the cornerstone | Christ the cornerstone     |

[SOP 5T 725.2] "The foundation of Christianity is Christ our righteousness." — what Eph 2:20 looks like as doctrine

[PIONEER Waggoner, PTUK August 23, 1894, page 531.9] "The prophets preached the same Gospel that the apostles did." — on Rom 3:21 "witnessed by the prophets": one foundation (Eph 2:20)

[Q] Should the Bible have final authority over what we believe about Jesus?
→ Yes: the apostles' and prophets' words are the foundation, and nothing else is laid beside it — Eph 2:20; 1 Cor 3:11; Isa 8:20

[→] Build on the foundation the prophets laid, and you are building on Christ's righteousness, not your own.

## 4. Tuesday — Functions of New Testament Prophets — Eph 4:11-13

> Prophets edify, exhort and comfort. They are given until the church reaches "the stature of the fulness of Christ," which is Christ's character reproduced in His people by faith.

- _1 Cor 14:3._ "But he that prophesieth speaketh unto men to edification, and exhortation, and comfort." — the three tasks: build up, urge on, comfort
- _Eph 4:11-13._ "And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ: Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ:" — the gifts last "till"; that goal has not yet been reached
- _Rev 22:6._ "These sayings are faithful and true: and the Lord God of the holy prophets sent his angel to shew unto his servants the things which must shortly be done." — Christ is the Lord of the prophets; His word can be trusted
- chain: 1 Cor 12:10 (to another prophecy) → 1 Cor 12:28 (secondarily prophets) → 1 Cor 14:31 (all may be comforted) → Col 1:27 (Christ in you, the hope)

[PIONEER James White, PSG 5.3] "hence the perpetuity of the gifts, and their revival in the last days in great power to unite and perfect the church ready for translation" — Eph 4:13 has not yet been fulfilled

[SOP COL 69.1] "When the character of Christ shall be perfectly reproduced in His people, then He will come to claim them as His own." — "the fulness of Christ" explained

[SOP COL 328.1] "without the presence of the Spirit of God, no heart will be touched, no sinner be won to Christ." — the gift has power only from the Spirit

[Q] Did the gift of prophecy end with the apostles?
→ The text says "till we all come"; that has not happened yet — Eph 4:13; Rev 12:17

[PIONEER Loughborough, PGGC 21.1] "who will say that the Lord will not even now, as formerly, manifest those gifts for the same purpose, until probation shall end?" — the need is still here, so the gift is too

[→] A true prophet's comfort and rebuke both aim at one result: Christ formed in His people.

## 5. Wednesday — Female Prophets and Young Pastoral Associates — Luke 2:36-38

> Anna's prophecy was about Him: she "spake of him" to everyone who looked for redemption. The Spirit puts the gift on daughters and young men alike so they will preach the Redeemer.

- _Luke 2:36-38._ "And there was one Anna, a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Aser: she was of a great age, and had lived with an husband seven years from her virginity; And she was a widow of about fourscore and four years, which departed not from the temple, but served God with fastings and prayers night and day. And she coming in that instant gave thanks likewise unto the Lord, and spake of him to all them that looked for redemption in Jerusalem." — her message was redemption in a Person
- _Acts 21:9._ "And the same man had four daughters, virgins, which did prophesy." — the Joel promise fulfilled within one household
- _Joel 2:28._ "your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions:" — "afterward"; Peter places it "in the last days" (Acts 2:17)
- chain: Luke 2:25 (Simeon waiting) → Luke 2:30 (mine eyes have seen thy salvation) → Luke 1:68-70 (redeemed, by his holy prophets)

[SOP DA 55.4] "Anna also, a prophetess, came in and confirmed Simeon’s testimony concerning Christ." — a second witness to the same Christ

[SOP DA 55.5] "These humble worshipers had not studied the prophecies in vain." — faith that believes the prophecies recognizes Him

[PIONEER Loughborough, PGGC 11.2] "it was not a strange thing to the church to learn that women should share in the prophetic gift in the gospel age." — Anna prepared the way for Joel's promise

[PIONEER Smith, VEGW 13.2] "the daughters as well as the sons, the handmaids as well as the servants, are to participate." — Smith sees this already in Acts 2

**Young workers — 1 Tim 1:18:**

- _1 Tim 6:12._ "Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life" — the warfare is a fight of faith, carried out by laying hold
- chain: 1 Tim 4:12 (let no man despise thy youth) → Titus 1:4 (son after the common faith) → Titus 2:15 (speak, exhort, rebuke)

## 6. Thursday — False Prophets in the New Testament — Matt 24:24

> False prophets copy the signs but not the gospel. Their test is whether they confess Christ come in the flesh, and the only safe armour is Christ's own righteousness received by faith.

- _Matt 24:24._ "For there shall arise false Christs, and false prophets, and shall shew great signs and wonders; insomuch that, if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect." — miracles prove nothing; the Word decides
- _1 John 4:2._ "Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God:" — the doctrinal test: Christ come in our flesh
- _Matt 7:15-16._ "Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves. Ye shall know them by their fruits." — the fruit test
- chain: Isa 8:20 (to the law and to the testimony) → Gal 1:8 (any other gospel, accursed) → 2 Cor 11:15 (ministers of righteousness, disguised) → 2 Thess 2:10 (received not the love of the truth)

[SOP GC 593.1] "So closely will the counterfeit resemble the true that it will be impossible to distinguish between them except by the Holy Scriptures." — the final test is not your senses

[PIONEER Waggoner, GTI 29.2] "by leading them to trust for their salvation in that which professes to be power, but which is nothing." — the false gospel always relies on self

**The armour is Christ — Eph 6:10-18:**

- _Eph 6:13-14._ "Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand. Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness;" — "to stand" in the evil day, the judgment-hour posture

| Piece (Eph 6)        | Christ is it                        |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| truth                | John 14:6 — I am the truth          |
| breastplate of righteousness | Jer 23:6 — THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS |
| gospel of peace      | Rom 5:1 — justified, peace          |
| shield of faith      | Gal 2:20 — faith of the Son         |
| sword of the Spirit  | Eph 6:17 — the word of God          |

[SOP 6BC 1119.3] "Put on as your breastplate that divinely protected righteousness which it is the privilege of all to wear." — a gift worn by faith, not a garment we weave ourselves

[PIONEER Waggoner, GCDB March 23, 1891, page 216.12] "Having put on the whole armour which is Christ, we are complete in him." — every piece is Christ (Col 2:10; Rom 13:14)

[Q] How do I tell a false prophet?
→ Use three tests: the law and the testimony (Isa 8:20), Christ come in the flesh (1 John 4:2), and fruits (Matt 7:20); signs alone settle nothing (Matt 24:24)

[→] The person clothed in Christ's righteousness cannot be deceived by a counterfeit Christ.

## 7. Friday — Further Thought: Jesus and His Word at the Center — Rev 19:10

> The testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy. The gift in the remnant leads people to Christ and to the Bible, and its key message for the end is Christ's righteousness proclaimed with a loud voice.

- _Rev 19:10._ "worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — even the angel refuses worship; the gift points to Jesus
- _Rev 12:17._ "the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — the two marks of the last church
- chain: Rev 14:12 (commandments + faith of Jesus) → Isa 8:20 (law + testimony) → 2 Chr 20:20 (believe his prophets)

[SOP DA 826.1] "That which He had spoken, not only in person, but through all the prophets and teachers of the Old Testament, is here included. Human teaching is shut out." — the prophets' words are Christ's words

[SOP EW 78.1] "not for a new rule of faith, but for the comfort of His people, and to correct those who err from Bible truth." — the gift is under the Bible, not added to it

[SOP Ev 257.1] "the Lord has given a lesser light to lead men and women to the greater light." — the lesser light points to the greater

[PIONEER Haskell, SSP 223.1] "the gift of prophecy was long silent, it is in the Remnant church; though the law of God was long degraded and suppressed, it is again obeyed by the Remnant." — Rev 12:17 being fulfilled

[PIONEER Smith, SYNPT 302.2] "the remnant of her seed can refer to only one body of people, the last generation of Christians upon the earth." — historicist reading of Rev 12:17

**What the gift was sent to say in 1888:**

[SOP TM 91.2] "This message was to bring more prominently before the world the uplifted Saviour, the sacrifice for the sins of the whole world." — the prophetic gift endorsing Jones and Waggoner

[SOP TM 91.2] "it invited the people to receive the righteousness of Christ, which is made manifest in obedience to all the commandments of God." — faith that obeys (Gal 5:6; Rev 14:12)

[PIONEER Jones, GCDB February 13, 1893, page 243.10] "Now, that message of the righteousness of Christ is the loud cry. It is the latter rain." — 1893: what the gift endorsed is the loud cry

[Q] Do prophets add to the Bible?
→ No: the gift is "not for a new rule of faith" (EW 78.1); it is tested by the law and the testimony (Isa 8:20)

## 8. Appeal — 2 Chr 20:20

- The prophets, the Son, the apostles, Anna and the remnant all bring one message: believe God, and He counts you righteous in Christ
- Hearing the Prophet means taking His word over our own record (Gen 15:6; Rom 4:3)
- Building on the foundation means having Christ our righteousness, not our own record, under our feet (Eph 2:20; 5T 725.2)
- Standing in the evil day means wearing the breastplate He supplies (Eph 6:14; Jer 23:6)
- This is the message that lightens the earth:

[SOP 6T 19.1] "The message of Christ’s righteousness is to sound from one end of the earth to the other to prepare the way of the Lord." — the loud cry is this message

- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — decide today: believe Him, and believe His prophets about Him
