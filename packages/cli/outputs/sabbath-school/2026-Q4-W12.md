# Sabbath School — Q4 W12 (2026)

## End-Time Prophetic Revelations

_Week of December 12–18 · Read: Joel 2:28–32; Acts 2:1–21; Eph. 4:11–14; Rev. 12:17; Rev. 19:10; Acts 2:36–42_

**Memory Verse:** — _Joel 2:28, 29._ "And it shall come to pass afterward, [that] I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions: And also upon the servants and upon the handmaids in those days will I pour out my spirit."

## 1. Sabbath Afternoon — The Rain Is a Message — Joel 2:28-29

> God never sent a prophet to show off the future. He sent prophets so a people would believe Him. The last outpouring is the same: a message of righteousness by faith, poured out in power.

- _Joel 2:28._ "I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy" — no sunset clause: "afterward" reaches to the end (§2)
- _2 Chr 20:20._ "Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper." — the prophetic gift exists to produce faith
- _Rev 19:10._ "for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy" — Jesus Himself is the One testifying through the gift
- chain: Amos 3:7 (secret revealed to the prophets) → Hos 12:13 (by a prophet brought out, by a prophet preserved) → Deut 32:2 (doctrine drops as the rain)

[WB]: RAIN = DOCTRINE → TEACHER OF RIGHTEOUSNESS → LOUD CRY

[SOP TM 91.2] "It is the third angel’s message, which is to be proclaimed with a loud voice, and attended with the outpouring of His Spirit in a large measure." — "it" = justification through faith (1888)

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, GCB/GCDB 1893, page 243.10] "Now that message of the righteousness of Christ is the loud cry. It is the latter rain." — one message, three names

[→] this week asks what the outpouring looks like → Scripture: Christ lifted up, sinners justified, a people sealed to stand

## 2. Sunday — The Promise of Joel — Joel 2:23, 28-32

> Joel's rain is a teacher of righteousness. His signs are history, not guesswork, and his promise is deliverance for anyone who calls on the LORD by faith.

**The rain teaches righteousness — Joel 2:23:**

- _Joel 2:23._ "for he hath given you the former rain moderately, and he will cause to come down for you the rain, the former rain, and the latter rain" — two rains, one Giver
- chain: Hos 10:12 ("rain righteousness upon you") → Deut 32:2 (doctrine as rain) → Zech 10:1 (ask in the time of the latter rain)

[PIONEER Loughborough, PGGC 24.2] "The coming of the “former rain moderately” is called, in the margin of the text, “a teacher of righteousness, according to righteousness.”" — the KJV margin reading

**The signs keep time — Joel 2:30-31:**

- _Joel 2:31._ "The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and the terrible day of the LORD come." — fulfilled, not symbolic
- chain: Matt 24:29 (after the tribulation) → Rev 6:12 (sixth seal) → May 19, 1780

[SOP GC 308.1] "The description of this event, as given by eyewitnesses, is but an echo of the words of the Lord, recorded by the prophet Joel" — the Dark Day

**The promise is faith — Joel 2:32:**

- _Joel 2:32._ "whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD shall be delivered: for in mount Zion and in Jerusalem shall be deliverance, as the LORD hath said, and in the remnant whom the LORD shall call." — deliverance by calling, not by earning
- _Rom 10:10._ "For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness" — Paul reads Joel 2:32 as justification by faith (Rom 10:13)
- _Joel 2:13._ "rend your heart, and not your garments" — the rain falls on broken hearts

[PIONEER James White, ARSH January 7, 1858, page 69.27] "the manifestations of the gift of prophecy is with the remnant which keep the Commandments of God, and have the Testimony of Jesus Christ" — Joel 2:32 = Rev 12:17

## 3. Monday — Pentecost — Acts 2:1-21

> Pentecost was not a mood. It was Heaven announcing that Christ had been enthroned as Priest and King, and the Spirit came to lift Him up.

- _Acts 2:4._ "And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance." — the tongues carried a message
- _Acts 2:11._ "we do hear them speak in our tongues the wonderful works of God" — the subject matter was God's works in Christ
- _Acts 2:16._ "But this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel" — Scripture explains the event (Miller's Rule: the Bible is its own expositor)
- _Acts 2:21._ "whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved" — Peter's Joel ends in the gospel invitation
- chain: John 15:26 ("he shall testify of me") → John 16:14 ("He shall glorify me") → Acts 2:33 (exalted, then shed forth)

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, GCB/GCDB 1893, page 298.1] "He wants us to have now what they got at Pentecost—the personal presence of Jesus Christ" — the Spirit brings Christ, not a mood (John 16:14)

[SOP AA 38.3] "The Pentecostal outpouring was Heaven’s communication that the Redeemer’s inauguration was accomplished." — the Spirit falls from the sanctuary

[SOP GC 611.2] "so the “latter rain” will be given at its close for the ripening of the harvest" — Pentecost is the pattern for the end

| | Former rain | Latter rain |
| ------- | -------------------- | ---------------------- |
| When | Pentecost (Acts 2) | close of the gospel |
| Effect | seed springs up | harvest ripens |
| Christ | enthroned as Priest | closing His ministry |
| Message | Christ crucified | Christ our righteousness |

## 4. Tuesday — Gifts of the Spirit — Eph 4:7-14

> The ascended Christ gives gifts so that His people come to the measure of His own fulness. The gifts last until that goal is reached, and the goal has not yet been reached.

- _Eph 4:8._ "When he ascended up on high, he led captivity captive, and gave gifts unto men." — gifts are spoils of Christ's victory, given from the throne
- _Eph 4:11._ "And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers;" — prophets are a gift He gives
- _Eph 4:13._ "Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ:" — "till" = the gifts last until Christ's character is complete in His people
- _Eph 4:14._ "That we [henceforth] be no more children, tossed to and fro, and carried about with every wind of doctrine" — prophecy is ballast

[SOP TM 91.2] "All power is given into His hands, that He may dispense rich gifts unto men, imparting the priceless gift of His own righteousness to the helpless human agent." — the chief gift is righteousness

[PIONEER James White, PSG 5.3] "hence the perpetuity of the gifts, and their revival in the last days in great power to unite and perfect the church ready for translation" — "till" not yet reached

[SOP COL 69.1] "When the character of Christ shall be perfectly reproduced in His people, then He will come to claim them as His own." — Eph 4:13 fulfilled

[Q] Did prophecy cease with the canon?
→ Eph 4:13 "till" is not yet met; 1 Cor 1:7 (no gift lacking while "waiting for the coming"); Joel 2:28 "afterward"

[→] gifts → unity → Christ's fulness → harvest ripe → He comes

## 5. Wednesday — The End-Time Remnant — Rev 12:17; 19:10

> The last church is known by two marks: God's law kept, and Jesus testifying in its midst. The commandments come with the faith of Jesus, or they become legalism.

- _Rev 12:17._ "And the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — the remnant of Joel 2:32
- _Rev 19:10._ "I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren that have the testimony of Jesus: worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — defined by Scripture itself
- _Rev 22:9._ "I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren the prophets" — "have the testimony" = "the prophets"
- _1 Cor 1:6._ "Even as the testimony of Christ was confirmed in you:" — and 1:7 "come behind in no gift; waiting for the coming"

[PIONEER Uriah Smith, GCDB March 18, 1891, page 150.4] "and yet he uses the very same term, “The testimony of Jesus,” and connects it with the gifts." — Paul ties it to the gifts

[PIONEER Loughborough, HEVI 25.5] "the gift, or spirit, of prophecy will be with them to “confirm” them to the end" — the 1 Cor 1:6-8 word

**The two marks, read together — Rev 14:12:**

- _Rev 14:12._ "Here is the patience of the saints: here [are] they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." — the same people, seen from the third angel's side

## 6. Thursday — Modern Manifestations — Acts 2:36-42

> Peter preached a crucified Christ made Lord. Hearts broke, sins were remitted, and then the Spirit was given. The order is the same now: the righteousness of faith first, then the latter rain.

- _Acts 2:36._ "God hath made that same Jesus, whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ." — the sermon's whole content
- _Acts 2:37._ "they were pricked in their heart, and said unto Peter and to the rest of the apostles, Men [and] brethren, what shall we do?" — conviction is the Spirit's first work
- _Acts 2:38._ "Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost." — remission first, then the Gift
- _Gal 3:14._ "That the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith." — the Spirit through faith
- _Acts 2:42._ "they continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine" — the rain is doctrine received and lived

[SOP AA 59.2] "there was no hope of salvation except through the mercy of the One whom they had crucified. Only through faith in Him could their sins be forgiven." — the apostles' message

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, GCB/GCDB 1893, page 383.5] "Our lack of the righteousness of God, which is by faith—that is what holds it back" — why the rain waits

[Q] Will the latter rain make up for what I neglect now?
→ no: the former-rain work (repentance, remission, faith) comes first — Acts 2:38; TM 507.1

[SOP TM 507.1] "It may be falling on hearts all around us, but we shall not discern or receive it." — the rain can be missed

## 7. Friday — Further Thought — All About Mission — Rev 18:1

> The prophetic gift was given to lift up Christ. The loud cry is not a louder Sabbath argument. It is Christ our righteousness filling the earth with His glory, carried by people who rest in Him.

- _Rev 18:1._ "And after these things I saw another angel come down from heaven, having great power; and the earth was lightened with his glory." — the glory = the message of Christ's righteousness
- _Rev 14:6._ "having the everlasting gospel to preach unto them that dwell on the earth" — the frame of every angel's message
- chain: Isa 61:10 (robe of righteousness) → Jer 23:6 (THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS) → Phil 3:9 (the righteousness which is of God by faith)

[SOP 1SM 362.4] "the loud cry of the third angel has already begun in the revelation of the righteousness of Christ, the sin-pardoning Redeemer" — 1892: the cry has begun

[SOP 6T 19.1] "The message of Christ’s righteousness is to sound from one end of the earth to the other to prepare the way of the Lord." — worldwide reach

[SOP 6T 19.1] "This is the glory of God, which closes the work of the third angel." — Rev 18:1's glory named

[SOP EW 85.3] "At that time the “latter rain,” or refreshing from the presence of the Lord, will come, to give power to the loud voice of the third angel" — "while Christ is in the sanctuary"

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, GCB/GCDB 1893, page 302.7] "the heart that rests most fully upon Christ will be most earnest and active in labor for Him. That is what real faith is." — faith works

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, GCB/GCDB 1893, page 183.5] "Then what did the brethren in that fearful position in which they stood, reject at Minneapolis? They rejected the latter rain—the loud cry of the third angel’s message." — 1888 named for what it was

## 8. Appeal

- The latter rain is not a power we wait for so we can finally become ready. It is the message of Christ our righteousness, received now by faith and lived in obedience.
- Rest in Him today, so the rain does not fall around you unnoticed (TM 507.1).
- Ask in faith, as the righteous by faith, for the Spirit to lift up Christ to the world.

[SOP 1SM 363.1] "If you would stand through the time of trouble, you must know Christ, and appropriate the gift of His righteousness, which He imputes to the repentant sinner." — stand by faith

- _Zech 10:1._ "Ask ye of the LORD rain in the time of the latter rain; [so] the LORD shall make bright clouds, and give them showers of rain, to every one grass in the field."
