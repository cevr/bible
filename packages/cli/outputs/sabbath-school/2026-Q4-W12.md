# Sabbath School — Q4 W12 (2026)

## The Latter Rain Is Christ Our Righteousness

_Quarterly: End-Time Prophetic Revelations · December 12–18 · Read: Joel 2:28–32; Acts 2:1–21; Eph. 4:11–14; Rev. 12:17; Rev. 19:10; Acts 2:36–42_

**Memory Verse:** — _Joel 2:28, 29._ "And it shall come to pass afterward, [that] I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions: And also upon the servants and upon the handmaids in those days will I pour out my spirit."

**Theme: The Spirit Joel promised is a teacher of righteousness, so the latter rain is not a show of power we wait for but the message of Christ our righteousness, received by faith now.**

## The road

1. The rain is a teacher of righteousness — Joel 2:23, 32 _(quarterly: Sabbath, Sun)_

## Open

- **Say:** God promised to pour out His Spirit in the last days. What will that outpouring bring us: a display of power, or a message?
- _Joel 2:28._ "I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy" — no sunset clause: "afterward" reaches to the end
- [RQ] What does the Spirit do when He is poured out, by Joel's own words?
  → "your sons and your daughters shall prophesy" — He speaks; the outpouring brings a message from God

---

## 1. The rain is a teacher of righteousness — Joel 2:23, 32

- **Say:** Joel's rain is doctrine, and its subject is righteousness. His promise ends in deliverance for anyone who calls on the LORD by faith.
- _Joel 2:23._ "for he hath given you the former rain moderately, and he will cause to come down for you the rain, the former rain, and the latter rain" — two rains, one Giver
- _Hos 10:12._ "for [it is] time to seek the LORD, till he come and rain righteousness upon you." — what the rain carries
- _Joel 2:32._ "whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD shall be delivered: for in mount Zion and in Jerusalem shall be deliverance, as the LORD hath said, and in the remnant whom the LORD shall call." — deliverance by calling, not by earning
- _Rom 10:10._ "For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness" — Paul reads Joel 2:32 as justification by faith (Rom 10:13)
- [RQ] According to Hosea, what does the LORD rain upon His people?
  → "rain righteousness upon you" — the rain is righteousness
- [RQ] In Joel 2:32, who is delivered?
  → "whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD" — and Paul says that calling is the heart believing "unto righteousness"

[WB]: RAIN = DOCTRINE → RIGHTEOUSNESS → BY FAITH

[PIONEER Loughborough, PGGC 24.2] "The coming of the “former rain moderately” is called, in the margin of the text, “a teacher of righteousness, according to righteousness.”" — the KJV margin reading

- **Bridge:** Peter stood up at Pentecost and said, "this is that." Watch what the former rain taught.

## 2. The former rain lifted up an enthroned Christ — Acts 2:16, 33

- **Say:** Pentecost was not a mood. It was Heaven announcing that Christ had been enthroned as Priest and King, and the Spirit came to lift Him up.
- _Acts 2:16._ "But this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel" — Scripture explains the event
- _Acts 2:11._ "we do hear them speak in our tongues the wonderful works of God" — the tongues carried a message
- _Acts 2:33._ "Therefore being by the right hand of God exalted, and having received of the Father the promise of the Holy Ghost, he hath shed forth this, which ye now see and hear." — the Spirit comes from the throne
- _John 16:14._ "He shall glorify me: for he shall receive of mine, and shall shew [it] unto you." — the Spirit's subject is Christ
- [RQ] Where was Jesus when He shed forth the Spirit?
  → "by the right hand of God exalted" — enthroned in heaven's sanctuary
- [RQ] When the Spirit comes, whom does He glorify?
  → "He shall glorify me" — Christ, not the Spirit Himself, and not the people

[SOP AA 38.3] "The Pentecostal outpouring was Heaven’s communication that the Redeemer’s inauguration was accomplished." — the Spirit falls from the sanctuary

- **Bridge:** The crowd heard Christ lifted up. What did the Spirit do to them first?

## 3. Righteousness first, then the Spirit — Acts 2:36-38

- **Say:** Peter preached a crucified Christ made Lord. Hearts broke, sins were remitted, and then the Spirit was given. The order is the same now.
- _Acts 2:36._ "God hath made that same Jesus, whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ." — the sermon's whole content
- _Acts 2:37._ "they were pricked in their heart, and said unto Peter and to the rest of the apostles, Men [and] brethren, what shall we do?" — conviction is the Spirit's first work
- _Acts 2:38._ "Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost." — remission first, then the Gift
- _Gal 3:14._ "That the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith." — the Spirit through faith
- [RQ] In Acts 2:38, what comes before "ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost"?
  → "the remission of sins" — forgiveness in Christ's name comes first
- [RQ] By what means, says Paul, do we receive the promise of the Spirit?
  → "through faith" — not by effort, not by waiting

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, GCB/GCDB 1893, page 383.5] "Our lack of the righteousness of God, which is by faith—that is what holds it back" — why the rain waits

- **Bridge:** God promised a people at the end who would receive this. How will we know them?

## 4. Jesus testifies in His remnant — Rev 12:17; 19:10; 14:12

- **Say:** The last church is known by two marks: God's law kept, and Jesus testifying in its midst. The gifts stay until Christ's fulness is reached, and the commandments come with the faith of Jesus.
- _Rev 12:17._ "And the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ." — the remnant of Joel 2:32
- _Rev 19:10._ "I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren that have the testimony of Jesus: worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy." — defined by Scripture itself
- _Eph 4:13._ "Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ:" — "till" = the gifts last until Christ's character is complete in His people
- _Rev 14:12._ "Here is the patience of the saints: here [are] they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." — the same people, seen from the third angel's side
- [RQ] What is "the testimony of Jesus," by Rev 19:10?
  → "the spirit of prophecy" — Jesus Himself testifying through the gift
- [RQ] Rev 12:17 says "testimony of Jesus"; what does Rev 14:12 set beside the commandments?
  → "the faith of Jesus" — the law never stands alone; it comes with faith in Christ

| Rev 12:17 | Rev 14:12 |
| ------------------- | --------------------- |
| commandments of God | commandments of God |
| testimony of Jesus | faith of Jesus |
| the gift identifies | the message identifies |

[SOP 3SM 172.3] "faith in the ability of Christ to save us amply and fully and entirely is the faith of Jesus" — definition

- **Bridge:** That faith of Jesus is exactly what the last angel carries to the world.

## 5. The loud cry is Christ our righteousness — Rev 18:1

- **Say:** The loud cry is not a louder Sabbath argument. It is Christ our righteousness filling the earth with His glory, carried by people who rest in Him.
- _Rev 18:1._ "And after these things I saw another angel come down from heaven, having great power; and the earth was lightened with his glory." — the glory = the message of Christ's righteousness
- _Rev 14:6._ "having the everlasting gospel to preach unto them that dwell on the earth" — the frame of every angel's message
- [RQ] What does the angel of Rev 18:1 light the earth with?
  → "his glory" — and Rev 14:6 says the message is "the everlasting gospel"
- [RQ] Then what is the latter rain, the power behind the loud cry?
  → the same rain Joel and Hosea promised: righteousness rained down, now in full measure

[PIONEER A. T. Jones, GCB/GCDB 1893, page 243.10] "Now that message of the righteousness of Christ is the loud cry. It is the latter rain." — one message, three names

- **Bridge:** If the rain is a message, it can fall around us and be refused. So what will we do?

## Decision

- **Say:** God offers the rain now, as the message of Christ our righteousness. He asks us to receive it by faith today, not to wait for a power that will make us ready later.
- _Zech 10:1._ "Ask ye of the LORD rain in the time of the latter rain; [so] the LORD shall make bright clouds, and give them showers of rain, to every one grass in the field."
- [RQ] To whom does the LORD give the showers?
  → "to every one" who asks — the rain is received by asking in faith, not earned

[SOP TM 507.1] "It may be falling on hearts all around us, but we shall not discern or receive it." — the rain can be missed

## If asked

- [Q] Did prophecy cease with the canon?
  → Eph 4:13 "till" is not yet met; 1 Cor 1:7 (no gift lacking while "waiting for the coming"); Joel 2:28 "afterward"
- [Q] Is "testimony of Jesus" only our witness about Him?
  → Rev 19:10 and 22:9 define it as the prophetic spirit: Jesus is the One testifying (John 15:26)
- [Q] Will the latter rain make up for what I neglect now?
  → no: the former-rain work (repentance, remission, faith) comes first — Acts 2:38; TM 507.1
