# Reading Application

## Glossary

### Reading Preference

A deliberate, durable reader choice that changes how Bible and Writings content is presented. A Reading Preference follows the reader between web and desktop. It includes color mode, reader typeface, text size, leading, tracking, measure, Scripture layout, and visible study aids.

### Reader Typeface

The chosen family used for reading text. A Reader Typeface has a stable identity independent of the font stack used to render it on a particular device.

### Device Presentation State

A durable convenience tied to one device, window, or local file collection rather than to the reader across devices. It includes interface scale, pane dimensions, last-open contextual surfaces, filesystem recents and progress, and diagnostic controls. Device Presentation State does not sync.

### Reading Position

The canonical Bible or Writings reference where reading most recently continued. A Reading Position follows the reader between devices, but an explicit route always takes precedence when opening the application.

### Reading Route

The canonical application location currently being presented. The Reading Route determines whether Bible or Writings is active; active corpus is not a separate preference.
