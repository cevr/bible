# Righteousness by faith (1889 Principle XVIII + the 1888 message)

Scope: the pioneer frame for the film _Righteousness by Faith_. These quotes deliberately cover what
the first cut's `apps/animations/src/films/righteousness-by-faith-v1/sources.md` does NOT already have: the 1889 principle itself, the law and the
Sabbath after justification, Christ's present priesthood, the day-of-atonement setting of Zech 3, and
the latter rain / cleansing link. The Principle II note on the atonement (FP1889 147.3) is in
[nature-of-christ.md](nature-of-christ.md), because both of the `exchange` beat's problems hang on Principle II.

## Pioneer position

1889 Principle XVIII says that because "all have violated the law of God," "we are dependent on Christ, first, for justification from our past offenses, and, secondly, for grace whereby to render acceptable obedience to his holy law in time to come" (FP1889 150.6). Jones and Waggoner call that commandment-keeping, Sabbath included, God's gift received by faith and "the Third Angel’s Message" (GNT 256.4; GCDB March 8, 1897, 303.7); Ellen White sets the same message where "Christ is pleading for the church in the heavenly courts above" and in "the closing up of the great day of atonement" (Lt 57, 1895, par. 44; 5T 472.2), and Jones ties it to "the latter rain" and "the cleansing of the sanctuary" (CWCP 124.2).

## Quotes

> XVIII. That as all have violated the law of God, and cannot of themselves render obedience to his just requirements, we are dependent on Christ, first, for justification from our past offenses, and, secondly, for grace whereby to render acceptable obedience to his holy law in time to come.

— **FP1889 150.6** · unsigned (SDA Year Book), _Fundamental Principles_ XVIII (1889)

> In Christ, by faith of Christ, the believer in Jesus finds the keeping of the commandments of God, which is the righteousness of God. Thus the keeping of the commandments of God is the gift of God: it is the “free gift” of the righteousness of God “which is by faith of Jesus Christ unto all and upon all them that believe.” And this is the Third Angel’s Message: “Here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus.”

— **GNT 256.4** · A. T. Jones, _The Great Nations of To-day_ (1901). The same paragraph ran earlier in the unsigned Review editorial “The Third Angel’s Message. The Faith of Jesus” (ARSH October 23, 1900, page 680.16); Jones's book establishes him as the writer.

> But I think anybody here can see that the third angel’s message is righteousness by faith; for the Sabbath is righteousness by faith; for by it a man comes into God’s works, and those works are perfect. Therefore he gets rest by faith. … If he does not believe God, then he cannot keep the Sabbath.

— **GCDB March 8, 1897, page 303.7** · E. J. Waggoner, “Studies in the Book of Hebrews. - No. 16” (Sunday afternoon, Feb. 28, 1897), _General Conference Daily Bulletin_ (1897). The byline is at page 297. The corpus author field names the editor, A. T. Jones, not the speaker.

> The uplifted Saviour is to appear in His efficacious work as the Lamb slain, sitting upon the throne to dispense the priceless covenant blessings, the benefits He died to purchase for every soul who should believe on Him. … Christ is pleading for the church in the heavenly courts above, pleading for those for whom He paid the redemption price of His own life blood. Centuries, ages, can never diminish the efficacy of this atoning sacrifice. This message of the gospel of His grace was to be given to the church in clear and distinct lines, that the world should no longer say, Seventh-day Adventists talk the law, the law, but do not preach or believe Christ.

— **10LtMs, Lt 57, 1895, par. 44** · Ellen G. White, Letter 57, 1895. The 1888 Materials index (editorial) gives it as to O. A. Olsen, May 1, 1895. It is the paragraph right after the “most precious message” paragraph (par. 43 = TM 91.2), and it was printed as TM 92.1, where “preach” reads “teach” (1895).

> Zechariah’s vision of Joshua and the Angel applies with peculiar force to the experience of God’s people in the closing up of the great day of atonement. The remnant church will be brought into great trial and distress. Those who keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus will feel the ire of the dragon and his hosts.

— **5T 472.2** · Ellen G. White, _Testimonies for the Church_, vol. 5, ch. 53 “Joshua and the Angel” (1889). First printed 1885 as T32 228.2.

> … we know by every evidence that now we are in the times of refreshing—the time of the latter rain. And as certainly as that is so, we are also in the time of the utter blotting out of all sins that have ever been against us. And the blotting out of sins is exactly this thing of the cleansing of the sanctuary; it is the finishing of all transgression in our lives; it is the making an end of all sins in our character; it is the bringing in of the very righteousness of God which is by faith of Jesus Christ, to abide alone everlastingly.

— **CWCP 124.2** · A. T. Jones, _The Consecrated Way to Christian Perfection_, ch. XVI “The Times of Refreshing” (1905)

## Film direction

MUST SHOW

- **Justification first, then obedience.** Justification for past sins, then grace to obey "his holy law in time to come" — **FP1889 150.6**.
- **The third angel's message in its own words.** Show "the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" whenever the film names it — **GNT 256.4**.
- **The Sabbath as rest by faith, not as a work.** "the Sabbath is righteousness by faith" — **GCDB March 8, 1897, page 303.7**.
- **Christ now, in the heavenly courts.** The risen Christ pleading with His own blood, not only the cross — **10LtMs, Lt 57, 1895, par. 44**. The film already cites Heb 8:10, whose chapter opens on the heavenly High Priest (Heb 8:1-2).
- **Zech 3 with its full cast and setting.** Satan standing at Joshua's right hand, the Angel answering him, and the scene framed as "the closing up of the great day of atonement" — **5T 472.2**, with 5T 467.3 and 469.1 by refcode.
- **Where the message leads.** It runs on to the latter rain and the blotting out of sins in the cleansing of the sanctuary — **CWCP 124.2**.

MUST NOT SAY-OR-SHOW

- **Don't name the third angel's message without defining it.** Calling 1888 "the third angel's message" and leaving it at that leaves the viewer to supply a meaning — **GNT 256.4**.
- **Don't let faith push the law off the page.** The 1888 message was given so no one could say Adventists "talk the law, the law, but do not preach or believe Christ". It answered that charge without dropping the commandments — **Lt 57, 1895, par. 44** with **FP1889 150.6**.
- **Don't end Christ's work at the cross.** No picture should close Christ's work at the cross with nothing after it — **Lt 57, 1895, par. 44**, and the Principle II note in **FP1889 147.3** (see [nature-of-christ.md](nature-of-christ.md)).
- **Don't stage the robe scene without the accuser.** A private makeover with no Satan and no Advocate is not the vision as EGW applied it — **5T 472.2**.
- **Don't credit the wrong speaker.** GCDB March 8, 1897, pp. 297–303.8 is Waggoner, and p. 308.10 is I. D. Van Horn. Neither is Jones.

## Distinctives

- **Obedience is part of justification by faith.** A mainstream explainer usually stops at being declared righteous and treats the law as a mirror. The pioneers make the obedience that follows, Sabbath included, part of the same faith: FP1889 150.6; GNT 256.4; GCDB March 8, 1897, page 303.7.
- **The emphasis is Christ's present ministry.** A mainstream explainer centres the finished work of the cross. EGW's 1888 letter centres "Christ is pleading for the church in the heavenly courts above" (10LtMs, Lt 57, 1895, par. 44), and the 1889 note says the atonement is "not being completed till this work of blotting out sin is done" (FP1889 147.3).
- **The robe of Zech 3 is end-time and courtroom.** It is set in "the closing up of the great day of atonement" (5T 472.2), not as a timeless picture of conversion.
- **Righteousness by faith has an end-time horizon.** It belongs to the latter rain and the cleansing of the sanctuary (CWCP 124.2), not only to one person's conversion.

## Unverified / not found

- **Opposition in the Review volumes.**
  - Uriah Smith's Review editorials of 1888–1890 are not in the corpus. The ARSH volumes for those years hold only Jones and Waggoner items; vol. 66 (1889) has 80 Jones paragraphs.
  - His June 11, 1889 editorial appears only inside Robert W. Olson's 88IOL 8.4. Olson is an excluded 20th-century writer, so it is not used.
  - G. I. Butler's _The Law in the Book of Galatians_ (LBG, 1886) is local but predates 1888 and is not Review material.
  - EGW's own report, in RH April 1, 1890, par. 7, that some brethren feared too much dwelling on justification by faith names no one. It is not used as a record.
- **Unsigned 1900 editorial.** "The Third Angel’s Message. What Is It?" (ARSH January 23, 1900, page 56.9) ties "in verity" to Rev 14:7, the hour of judgment. It is unsigned, the writer is not established, and it is not reprinted in any Jones book in the corpus, so it is not used.
- **Loud-cry statement in `sources.md`.** The line credited to "GCDB March 8, 1897, p. 308.10 · A. T. Jones" exists, but it sits in "Righteousness by Faith. I. D. VAN HORN. (Sunday Evening, Feb. 28, 1897.)" (heading at page 305). The writer is Van Horn, not Jones.

## Missing works

- **Complete _Review and Herald_ volumes for 1888–1890**, with Uriah Smith's editorials, including the June 11, 1889 one. The installed ARSH volumes for those years are partial, so the Review-side opposition to the 1888 message cannot be quoted from primary text.
