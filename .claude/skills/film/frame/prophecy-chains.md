# prophecy-chains — the great outline prophecies (1889 Principles VI, VII, XIII)

## Pioneer position

Principle VII (FP1889 148.5) holds that "the rise and fall of empires, and the chronological succession of events down to the setting up of God’s everlasting kingdom, are outlined in numerous great chains of prophecy," and Principle VI (FP1889 148.4) that prophecy, "so far from being enshrouded in impenetrable mystery," is "to be understood by the people of God sufficiently to show them their position in the world’s history"; so Daniel 2 and Daniel 7 name the same four kingdoms, "Babylon, or Chaldea, Medo-Persia, Greece, and Rome," after which the reader stands "Down among the feet and toes," "the divided state of the Roman empire," until "the everlasting kingdom of God be set up" (DAR 532.2; FUMP 18.2). The horn "with human eyes and mouth" and the "man of sin" is "the papacy" (Principle XIII, FP1889 150.1: "the man of sin, the papacy"), holding supremacy "twelve hundred and sixty years," "538 + 1260 = 1798," and it "will continue until the second advent"; in Revelation 13 the beast "like unto a leopard" is the papacy and "the United States is the power represented by the beast with lamb-like horns," whose image and mark are fulfilled "when the United States shall enforce Sunday observance" — a mark that "signifies not a literal mark, but a public profession, or act" (DAR 141.1; GC88 356.1; GC88 578.3; TMR 108.3).

## Film direction

MUST SHOW

- Daniel 2's metal image and Daniel 7's four beasts as two views of one sequence: gold head and lion = Babylon, silver and bear = Medo-Persia, brass and leopard = Grecia, iron and the terrible beast = Rome (DAR 532.2; FP1889 148.5).
- A "you are here" marker in the feet and toes: divided Rome, some fourteen hundred years long in White's day, with the stone and God's everlasting kingdom still ahead (FUMP 18.2).
- The little horn "with human eyes and mouth" as the papacy, on a dated timeline: day-for-a-year, 1260 years, A.D. 538 (Ostrogoths driven from Rome) to 1798 (Berthier's French army takes the pope prisoner) (DAR 141.1; GC88 356.1).
- Revelation 13 as the same story continued: the leopard-like beast = the papacy; the lamb-horned beast rising "out of the earth" = the United States (GC88 578.3). Loughborough reads the two horns as "Protestant ecclesiastical power, and Republican civil power" (THB 12.2; not excerpted, since the corpus gives only a conjectural imprint "[1854?]").
- The image and the mark as future acts of that lamb-horned power, fulfilled when it enforces Sunday observance (GC88 578.3). Show the mark as a visible public choice made against known light once the test comes (TMR 108.3).
- Prophecy as history you can check, understandable and dated, "now all fulfilled except the closing scenes" (FP1889 148.5; FP1889 148.4).

MUST NOT SAY-OR-SHOW

- The little horn or man of sin as one future individual Antichrist, or staged in a rebuilt Jerusalem temple. The sources make him the papacy, a reign of "1260 years" that "ended in 1798," which continues "until the second advent" (GC88 356.1; GC88 578.3; FP1889 150.1).
- A gap in the chain, or the horn moved into an end-time future. The sequence runs straight through divided Rome to the stone (FUMP 18.2; DAR 532.2).
- The beasts as timeless symbols of empire in general, or prophecy presented as mystery. Each symbol has a named kingdom and dates (DAR 532.2; DAR 141.1; FP1889 148.4).
- The mark as a literal chip, barcode or tattoo, or a claim that everyone who now keeps Sunday already has it. The mark "signifies not a literal mark," and those who have not understood "are not referred to" (TMR 108.3). It comes when Sunday observance is enforced (GC88 578.3).
- The stone shown as the church slowly filling the earth now. God's kingdom is set up after the stone strikes the feet (FUMP 18.2; FP1889 148.5).

## Distinctives

- **Historicist, not futurist:** mainstream evangelical explainers usually treat the little horn / man of sin as a coming end-time individual. The pioneers read him as the papacy across A.D. 538–1798, continuing to the advent (DAR 141.1; GC88 356.1; GC88 578.3; FP1889 150.1).
- **Day for a year:** time, times and a half, 1260 days and 42 months are read as 1260 years, using Ezekiel 4:6 and Numbers 14:34, and are not taken as a literal future 3½ years (DAR 141.1; DAR 524.7; DAR 263.3).
- **Not idealist:** the pioneers treat prophecy as a dated, checkable outline rather than timeless symbolism or mystery (FP1889 148.4; FP1889 148.5; DAR 532.2). James White calls the claim that prophecies "cannot be understood" a form of infidelity (FUMP 3.2; not excerpted).
- **Revelation 13 names a nation:** the lamb-horned beast is the United States, not a future religious figure (GC88 578.3).
- **The mark is about worship and the Sabbath, not technology:** it is Sunday observance enforced and chosen against light, and it is "not a literal mark" (TMR 108.3; GC88 578.3).
