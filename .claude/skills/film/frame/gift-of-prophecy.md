# The gift of prophecy — 1889 Principle XIX

## Pioneer position

Principle XIX (1889): "That the Spirit of God was promised to manifest itself in the church through certain gifts, enumerated especially in 1 Corinthians 12 and Ephesians 4; that these gifts are not designed to supersede, or take the place of, the Bible, which is sufficient to make us wise unto salvation." The quoted writers say: "Will those continue till the church is perfected, ready to meet her descending Lord? So will the gift of prophecy" (James White, 1868); "the word of God as the rule of your faith and practice … not for a new rule of faith" (Ellen White, 1851); "would not constitute another Bible nor an addition to the Bible" (Uriah Smith, 1887); "a lesser light to lead men and women to the greater light" (Ellen White, 1903).

## Quotes

> XIX. That the Spirit of God was promised to manifest itself in the church through certain gifts, enumerated especially in 1 Corinthians 12 and Ephesians 4; that these gifts are not designed to supersede, or take the place of, the Bible, which is sufficient to make us wise unto salvation, any more than the Bible can take the place of the Holy Spirit; that, in specifying the various channels of its operation, that Spirit has simply made provision for its own existence and presence with the people of God to the end of time, …

— **FP1889 150.7** · Unsigned statement (corpus lists General Conference of SDA), _Fundamental Principles of Seventh-day Adventists, 1889 Year Book, p. 150, Principle XIX_ (1889)

> I recommend to you, dear reader, the word of God as the rule of your faith and practice. By that Word we are to be judged. God has, in that Word, promised to give visions in the “last days;” not for a new rule of faith, but for the comfort of his people, and to correct those who err from Bible truth.

— **ExV 64.3** · Ellen G. White, _A Sketch of the Christian Experience and Views of Ellen G. White (1851)_ (1851)

> It is well known that we have been charged with testing all men by the visions, and of making them the rule of our faith. This is a bold untruth, of which those who uttered it were not ignorant. … I still say that the Bible is my rule of faith and practice, and in saying this, I do not reject the Holy Spirit in its diversities of operations. … I believe them to be the property of the church, and a test to those who believe them from Heaven.

— **ARSH February 14, 1856, page 158.5** · James White (signed “JAMES WHITE.” at ARSH February 14, 1856, page 158.6), _Note appended to “From Bro. Bingham,” Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, February 14, 1856 (reprinted in “Note to Bro. Barr,” January 22, 1857, page 96.7)_ (1856)

> The gift of prophecy is by the apostle classed with the callings of the Christian church in Ephesians 4:11-13. … These were all given at the same time, all for the same purpose, all to cease at the same time. … Will those continue till the church is perfected, ready to meet her descending Lord? So will the gift of prophecy.

— **LIFIN 329.2** · James White, _Life Incidents (Battle Creek, 1868)_ (1868)

> But these Scriptures make provision for the operation of the Holy Spirit, not only in ordinary, but in extraordinary methods in the church to the end of time. These latter are explicitly enumerated in 1 Corinthians 12 and Ephesians 4. … Prophecies of their especial revival in the last days, are numerous. See Joel 2:28; Acts 2:17; 1 Corinthians 1:7; Revelation 12:17; etc. … But what was given in this way would not constitute another Bible nor an addition to the Bible.

— **RCASDA 76.1** · Uriah Smith, _Replies to Elder Canright’s Attacks on Seventh-day Adventists (1887)_ (1887)

> The Lord has sent his people much instruction, line upon line, precept upon precept, here a little, and there a little. Little heed is given to the Bible, and the Lord has given a lesser light to lead men and women to the greater light. O, how much good would be accomplished if the books containing this light were read with a determination to carry out the principles they contain!

— **RH January 20, 1903, par. 9** · Ellen G. White, _“An open letter from Mrs. E. G. White to all who love the blessed hope,” Review and Herald, January 20, 1903 (letter text: 17LtMs, Lt 196, 1902, par. 5)_ (1903)

> We are in the very period of time when a people were to arise who would keep all the commandments, and who would also “have the testimony of Jesus” … —the “spirit of prophecy.” … That during the last sixty years such a people have arisen, bearing just such a message, among whom the gift of prophecy has been manifested. … These writings have never placed themselves above the Bible, but they do constantly exhort to the most careful study of the word of God, pointing to it as the great standard by which our cases will be examined in the final judgment.

— **GSAM 468.3** · J. N. Loughborough, _The Great Second Advent Movement: Its Rise and Progress (1905)_ (1905)

## Key texts

Named by the statement and the writers:

- 1 Corinthians 12 and Ephesians 4 (Principle XIX).
- Ephesians 4:11-13 (James White, 1868).
- 1 Corinthians 12:28, Joel 2:28, Acts 2:17, 1 Corinthians 1:7 and Revelation 12:17 (Smith, 1887).
- Revelation 12:17 with 19:10 (Loughborough, 1905).
- Acts 10 (Ellen White, 1851).
- Isaiah 28:10, "line upon line, precept upon precept" (Ellen White, 1903).

- **1 Corinthians 12:28** — "And God hath set some in the church, first apostles, secondarily prophets, thirdly teachers, after that miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, diversities of tongues."
- **Ephesians 4:11-13** — "And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ: Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ:"
- **Joel 2:28** — "And it shall come to pass afterward, [that] I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions:"

## Film direction

**MUST SHOW**

- Prophets among the gifts "he gave" with apostles, evangelists, pastors and teachers, "Till we all come in the unity of the faith" (Ephesians 4:11-13), and "set … in the church" (1 Corinthians 12:28) (LIFIN 329.2; RCASDA 76.1; FP1889 150.7).
- Joel's last-days outpouring: sons and daughters prophesying, with visions and dreams (RCASDA 76.1). Ellen White says God "promised to give visions in the “last days;”" (ExV 64.3).
- The remnant of Revelation 12:17 that keeps "the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ", read with Revelation 19:10, "the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy" (GSAM 468.3).
- The lesser light pointing to the greater light: prophetic writings sending readers back to the Bible (RH January 20, 1903, par. 9). Loughborough adds "pointing to it as the great standard" (GSAM 468.3).

**MUST NOT SAY OR SHOW**

- Do not present Ellen White's writings as a new rule of faith, an addition to Scripture, or above it (ExV 64.3; ARSH February 14, 1856, page 158.5; RCASDA 76.1; GSAM 468.3).
- Do not show the visions as a test imposed on everyone. James White (1856) calls "testing all men by the visions" "a bold untruth" and holds them "a test to those who believe them from Heaven" (ARSH February 14, 1856, page 158.5).
- Do not say prophecy ceased with the apostles or with the closing of the canon (LIFIN 329.2; RCASDA 76.1; FP1889 150.7 "to the end of time").
- Do not write Ellen White's name into Principle XIX. The statement speaks of the gifts in general (FP1889 150.7). Individual writers apply it to her (GSAM 468.3), and so do her own statements (RH January 20, 1903, par. 9).
- Do not gloss "lesser light" beyond its sentence. The sentence gives the occasion ("Little heed is given to the Bible") and the purpose ("to lead men and women to the greater light") (RH January 20, 1903, par. 9).

## Distinctives

- Cessationist evangelical framing says prophecy ended with the apostles or the canon. These sources hold that the gifts continue "till the church is perfected" (LIFIN 329.2) and "to the end of time" (RCASDA 76.1; FP1889 150.7).
- Continuationist or charismatic framing treats prophecy as fresh revelation for doctrine. These sources say visions are "not for a new rule of faith" (ExV 64.3), and what the gift gives "would not constitute another Bible nor an addition to the Bible" (RCASDA 76.1).
- Revelation 12:17 is read with 19:10 as the mark of the last-day remnant (GSAM 468.3). James White's 1870 preface to The Spirit of Prophecy, vol. 1, signed "J.W.", makes the same reading (1SP 11.1; signature at 1SP 16.2; read, not excerpted).
