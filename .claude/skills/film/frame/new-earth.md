# The new earth

Slug `new-earth` · 1889 Fundamental Principle XXVIII (with the XXVI–XXVII sequence) · 6 verified quotes from 5 writers: Ellen G. White, James White, J. N. Loughborough, Uriah Smith, William Miller (Millerite forerunner)

## Pioneer position

“The same fire from God that consumed the wicked, purified the whole earth,” and then “we inherited the whole earth made new” (PT November 1, 1850, par. 8), for at the close of the thousand years the kingdom is complete, “the king, the subjects, the territory, the holy city being the metropolis” (TTKGG 12.1), as 1889 XXVIII has the renewed earth “with the New Jerusalem for its metropolis and capital” as “the eternal inheritance of the saints.” That inheritance is “this earth, redeemed from the curse,” “a country” of streams, plains and mountains, inherited with bodies, “substance to inherit substance,” and not by “unclothed, immaterialized spirits” in regions “beyond the bounds of time and space” (SAIN 3.1, GC 675.1, DTIS 23.1, DAR 609.3).

## Film direction

**MUST SHOW**

- Show the sequence in order (PT November 1, 1850, par. 8; 1889 XXVII–XXVIII):
  1. Fire from God falls on the wicked around the city.
  2. The same fire melts the “broken ragged mountains” and purifies the whole earth.
  3. The inheritance opens, and the whole earth is made new.
- Show the New Jerusalem, which comes down from God with Christ at the end of the thousand years (1889 XXVII; Rev 21:2), standing as the capital of the renewed earth: “the metropolis of the glorified new earth” (GC 676.2; TTKGG 12.1; 1889 XXVIII).
- Show a real country underfoot: streams clear as crystal, trees shading the paths, plains rising into hills, mountains, and people at home (GC 675.1). Houses and vineyards belong in it too: “Eden restored” (SAIN 70.4; Isa 65:21–22).
- Show resurrected people in real bodies on solid ground, “substance to inherit substance” (DTIS 23.1).
- Show this same earth restored as it first came from the Creator's hand, “very good” (SAIN 3.1). It is renewed, not replaced: “The earth is not destroyed, annihilated, and a new one created, but all things are made over new.” (DAR 705.3).

**MUST NOT SAY OR SHOW**

- Eternity as spirits floating in a cloud realm “beyond the bounds of time and space” (DAR 609.3; SAIN 3.2).
- The earth thrown away and the saved leaving it for good (SAIN 3.1).
- The new earth appearing at the second coming. In the pioneer order the saints are in the New Jerusalem in heaven for the thousand years while “the earth lies in a desolate and chaotic condition,” and the renewal comes after the final fire (1889 XXVI–XXVIII; TTKGG 12.1). Do not build this timeline from Miller, because DTIS 23.2–24.1 puts the burning and the renewal at the advent.
- The new earth as only a symbol of spiritual blessings. Ellen White warns against the fear that has led many to “spiritualize away the very truths which lead us to look upon it as our home” (GC 674.4).

## Distinctives

- **Heaven or the new earth.** The popular picture of the final destiny is heaven as a spirit realm above. The pioneers put it on this earth made new (SAIN 3.1). Smith lists the disembodied-heaven idea among the errors of Babylon (DAR 609.3). Loughborough expects readers to be startled by “a literal kingdom, consisting of literal territory” (SAIN 3.2).
- **The thousand years come first.** An explainer that already teaches new creation usually goes straight from Christ's return to the renewed earth. The pioneers put the thousand years in between. The saints are in heaven in the New Jerusalem while the earth lies desolate; then comes the final fire, and only then the renewal (1889 XXVI–XXVIII; TTKGG 12.1; PT November 1, 1850, par. 8).
- **Renewed, not replaced.** Smith reads Rev 21:5 this way (DAR 705.3): “I make all things new;” not, I make all new things. 1889 XXVIII has the new earth spring “from the ashes of the old.”
- **Miller and the Seventh-day Adventists differ on timing.** Miller put the burning and the renewal at the advent and had the meek inherit the earth during the thousand years (DTIS 23.2–24.1). James White rejects exactly this view (TTKGG 12.3). He calls it an absurdity that has the resurrected wicked come “tramping up over the fields of living green.” Use Miller only for the tangibility of the inheritance.

## Unverified / not found

- **J. N. Andrews on the new earth.** TAR 130.1 has new heavens and a new earth following the final fire, “in the earth thus made new the righteous are to be recompensed”. It supports the case but is not among the six quotes.
- **The 1850 vision in other works.** It is reprinted in EW 54.1 and ExV 35.1, and the manuscript is 1LtMs, Ms 14, 1850, par. 14. The Present Truth version is quoted here as the earliest printed form.
- **“Capital” as a word for the New Jerusalem.** The listed writers quoted here say “metropolis” (TTKGG 12.1; GC 676.2), while 1889 XXVIII has “metropolis and capital.” In this search, “capital” turned up in:
  - ARSH August 12, 1858, page 98.1, signed J. B. Frisbie, who is not on the source list;
  - SJ 185.6 (_The Story of Jesus_, attributed to Ellen G. White), where GC 676.2 reads “metropolis”;
  - the 1894 Battle Creek reprint of the 1889 statement (MBC94 16.5);
  - excluded secondary works: Froom's PFF2, the Estate's JTL28 and JTLD, and GrH_a.
- **Other leads.** DAR on Rev 21–22 (DAR 705.3 and following) and HHMLD were checked. DAR 705.3 is used above only as a supporting line.
