# second-coming-millennium — the advent and the thousand years (1889 Principles V, VIII, XXIV–XXVII)

## Pioneer position

Christ returns once, literally, visibly and audibly, with "no secret coming, no snatching away of His people by stealth" ("two comings, a private and a public one" being "wholly unwarranted in the Scriptures"), and at that coming the sleeping saints wake while "the living righteous are changed," are "caught up to meet their Lord in the air," and "ascend together to the city of God" (PTUK April 13, 1899, page 228.5; DAR 333.3; GC88 645.1; Principles V, XXIV and XXV = FP1889 148.3, 151.4, 151.5). The millennium follows the advent ("the second coming of Christ is to precede, not follow, the millennium," Principle VIII, FP1889 148.6) and is neither a "temporal millennium" nor a "mixed millennium, the literal reign of Christ on the earth … among the mortal nations" (TTKGG 9.1): "the saints are with Christ in heaven during the thousand years while the earth lies desolate," with Satan "bound" there and "none remaining, upon whom he can exercise his power," until "the saints and the city come down, and the wicked dead are raised," are judged and destroyed, and "the new heavens and the new earth" follow (DAR 696.2; GC88 659.1; Principles XXVI–XXVII = FP1889 151.6, 151.7).

## Film direction

MUST SHOW

- One return in the clouds that every eye sees and every ear hears: the archangel's voice, the trump of God, lightning from east to west (PTUK April 13, 1899, page 228.5; DAR 333.3).
- The same Jesus who ascended coming back in the same way, "as literal as was His first advent" (PTUK April 13, 1899, page 228.5; Acts 1:11).
- At that one visible coming, the dead saints raised, the living righteous changed "in the twinkling of an eye," all caught up together and carried to the city of God (GC88 645.1; DAR 333.3; FP1889 151.5).
- The thousand years as the saints with Christ in heaven, reigning and "judging the world and fallen angels" (FP1889 151.6; DAR 696.2).
- The earth dark, broken and empty of people, like Genesis 1:2 before creation (Jeremiah 4:23). Satan and his angels are confined there, "bound" because no one is left to deceive (GC88 659.1; FP1889 151.6).
- The close of the thousand years: the city descends with the saints, the wicked dead are raised and come up against it, fire from God destroys them, and a new heaven and new earth follow (DAR 696.2; FP1889 151.7).

MUST NOT SAY-OR-SHOW

- A secret or silent rapture: believers vanishing, empty clothes, crashing cars and planes, the world carrying on. The sources give "no secret coming, no snatching away of His people by stealth" (PTUK April 13, 1899, page 228.5). E. J. Waggoner also rejects a secret coming that would "take from this earth a few of His people, leaving the earth and the remainder of the inhabitants just as they were before" (PTUK June 14, 1894, page 372.4; verified by lookup, not excerpted).
- A two-stage coming, with a hidden catching-away first and a public appearing later (DAR 333.3: "two comings, a private and a public one … wholly unwarranted").
- "As a thief" shown as a stealthy, unseen arrival. It is thief-like only to those who "shut their eyes to the tokens of his approach" (DAR 333.3).
- A thousand-year reign of Christ on this earth over surviving mortal nations, a kingdom set up in Palestine, or saints living on a renewed earth during the millennium (TTKGG 9.1; DAR 696.2 with DAR 695.1).
- The world gradually converted or Christianized before Christ comes, which is the "temporal millennium" (FP1889 148.6; TTKGG 9.1).
- The "bottomless pit" as a literal shaft or underworld with Satan in a physical chain. The pit is the desolate, chaotic earth, and his binding is having no one to work on (GC88 659.1 with GC88 658.2; FP1889 151.6).
- Wicked people living through the millennium on earth. "At the coming of Christ the wicked are blotted from the face of the whole earth" (GC88 657.1; verified by lookup, not excerpted). Satan has "none remaining, upon whom he can exercise his power," and the wicked dead rise only after the thousand years (GC88 659.1; DAR 696.2; FP1889 151.4).

## Distinctives

- **No rapture before the appearing:** pre-tribulation explainers split the advent into a secret rapture and a later glorious appearing. The pioneers teach one visible, audible coming, and the catching-up of 1 Thessalonians 4:17 happens at that coming (DAR 333.3; PTUK April 13, 1899, page 228.5; GC88 645.1; FP1889 151.5).
- **The millennium is in heaven, not on earth:** premillennial explainers put Christ's thousand-year reign on earth, and postmillennial ones expect a converted world before he comes. The pioneers put the saints in heaven while the earth lies desolate (DAR 696.2; FP1889 151.6), reject both schemes (TTKGG 9.1), and call the temporal millennium "a fable of these last days" (FP1889 148.6).
- **Satan's binding:** the pioneers do not read it as a present, gospel-age restraint. It is his confinement to an emptied earth after the advent (GC88 659.1; FP1889 151.6).
- **Two resurrections a thousand years apart:** the righteous rise at the advent and the wicked after the thousand years, followed by judgment and "the purifying fires which destroy them" (DAR 696.2); they are "consumed, root and branch" (FP1889 151.7; see also FP1889 151.4).

## Unverified / not found

- **"secret rapture":** the phrase occurs only in excluded works: Froom (PFF4 1220.5, 1223.2, 1223.4), Coon (IRWHW 64.8) and EGW Estate compilation notes (JTLD 81.2, JTL6 4.1). No pioneer text uses it.
- **"rapture":** all 592 corpus paragraphs containing "rapture" were screened for nearby "caught", "secret" or "translat". No pioneer or Ellen White paragraph was found that uses the word for a secret or pre-tribulation catching-away. In Ellen White it means joy, e.g. "the rapture of that hour" (GC88 646.2). "Pre-Tribulation Rapture" appears once, in HOP 1.4, which is not a pioneer work (corpus author field: James Aitken Wylie).
- **How the pioneers handle the idea:** they reject it by description, not by that name: "secret coming", "snatching away", and "two comings, a private and a public one".
- **Other rejections found, not excerpted:**
  - GC88 625.1 (E. G. White): the manner of the advent cannot be counterfeited and is "witnessed by the whole world".
  - GC88 525.1: a "coming … at death" is Satan's "Behold, he is in the secret chambers".
  - NTMS 52.5 (J. H. Waggoner): "personal, and visible to all".
  - LDT 51.1 and SGL 31.2 (Loughborough): "secret comings" are a last-day deception.
  - SEADV 3.3 and 13.4 (James White): "personal and visible second advent"; SEADV 13.4 comments on Matthew 24:23-26 under the heading "LO, HERE! LO, THERE!".
  - ARSH January 15, 1861, page 68.7: "Thoughts on the Advent" against "a spiritual and invisible coming". The writer appears only as the author code "UrSe", which the corpus does not expand.
  - HST June 15, 1840, page 47.8 (Millerite, writer not established): "this coming cannot be secret, or figurative".
- **EMTF not used:** EMTF 39.2 rejects "any supposed spiritual, secret, or intangible coming". But the corpus assigns EMTF to A. T. Jones, while its epigraph and much of its wording match James White's SCOC (1871), and it has no byline.
- **"As a thief":** pioneer explanations found are DAR 333.3 (quoted) and ST1853 102.2 (James White, 1853: the day comes "as a thief" on those who do not understand "the times and seasons").
